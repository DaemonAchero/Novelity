The one who had come in this early morning was a tall, powerfully built young man in tight-fitting short work clothes, showing the firm, well-knit muscle of his upper body. His upper body was well developed and his legs were solid, planted on the ground like roots. The lines of his muscle were not all that pronounced. At a glance he was a fellow who trained his upper body above all. Li Shen did not mind; his smithy had stood in Red Stone Ward for many years, and such customers he had seen eight hundred and more.
"Look for what you want yourself, sir?" He pointed at the racks along the wall. Rack after rack was lined with all kinds of tools for the house and the field: hoes, kitchen knives, iron pestles, awls — a full range.
The one who had come was a lad not yet twenty, with a cold, indifferent face, not handsome, but not ugly either. It was Wei He, who had come straight here from home. "Master smith, I want something forged. A small thing."
"Forge something? What sort of thing? Let me say first, I will not do anything prohibited." Li Shen made that clear ahead of time.
"It is nothing prohibited — a small handle." Wei He took out a sketch he had drawn in advance and handed it to Li Shen. Li Shen took it and opened it out. On it, in charcoal, was a drawing of a T-shaped handle, except that the end of the handle was very sharp. "This thing… how big do you want it?"
"A child's palm." Wei He showed him roughly the size with his hands.
"A palm, is it? That will do. Are you bringing your own material, or using what I have here?"
"What you have."
"It depends on the material — cheap and dear run at different prices. And there is a lot of work on just now, so you would have to wait a month."
"…Very well." When Wei He walked out of the smithy, he felt as if his purse had been scooped hollow. He had not thought silver went so fast: for one small thing like this, with better material and a few more requirements, the price came to so much.
But this was a thing he had wanted for a long time and thought about often; spending the pay from the escort run on forging one, he could make it his killing blow. In fact what he had first thought of was gloves, but that sort of thing could not do the work of a hidden strike. And to serve as a killing blow it had to be of very high strength, which would certainly come very dear.
So he had to settle for the next best thing. By early morning the streets of Red Stone Ward already had many shops opening. Farmers carrying vegetable baskets on shoulder poles were laying out goods on the ground, no few of them. Constables in their runner's uniforms swaggered along the street.
There were also gang members with a common badge flaunting themselves about and going from place to place collecting protection money. Comparing it with where he lived, Wei He thought the bustle of the two hardly seemed to belong to one city. He came back to Stone Bridge Ward, where Mountain Fist had its yard. This place was near the Inner City, and busy enough in its way, but it had nothing of Red Stone Ward's prosperity. Here there were fewer shops and more walled houses, all of them quiet and cold; next to Red Stone Ward it was like the difference in his last life between a commercial district and an old residential quarter.
When the morning's training was done and it was time to go back and rest, Wei He was stopped by his second sister, who had come over suddenly. "Xiao He, have you got time? I want to talk something over with you." His second sister Wei Ying, who now, under Old Man Zheng's care, took all three meals along with the folk of the yard, looked a great deal better than before.
And since she was living in the room next door, her sense of safety was much greater, her heart was settled, and her skin was a good deal better than before. Standing at the yard gate, in plain grey-white undyed cloth, she still could not hide the rise and fall of her figure. "What is it, Sister?" Wei He hung the sweaty towel back on the rack and walked over to ask. Wei Ying looked about, drew him over to the wall, made sure no one was there, then spoke in a low voice.
"These past evenings, while resting, I took in some needlework on the side and saved a little money." She quietly pressed a small paper packet into Wei He's hand. "Take it and use it.
