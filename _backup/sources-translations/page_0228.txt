== Chapter 76 · Gathering (Part 2) ==
The academies around him had all come. None of the masters had come in person, but every man there stood for his own academy, and their coming was a courtesy to Mountain Fist. If the hour struck and Wei He, the man of the occasion, still did not appear, it would be a want of manners, and a grave one. Zheng Fugui looked about him and saw men already leaning their heads together and muttering.
Feiye City had gone several years without a third breakthrough. Everyone had come for a look, wondering what Mountain Fist could possibly have done to break the shackles another academy could not break, and bring up a new talent. But now, to look at things: "With all the talk that has been going round, who is to say this Wei He of theirs has really broken through? More likely his own academy blew it up out of nothing, a fake." "Still not shown his face. Likely he does not dare come out." "Should the host of the occasion not be here ahead of time?" "Do they mean to have us come, walk a circle and go, without so much as laying eyes on the man?"
Men from several academies that had no ties with Mountain Fist began to grumble among themselves. None dared say it to Zheng Fugui's face, not to a jin-entering martial master, but the whispering went on in the corners. "Master Zheng, the hour is nearly here," Jiang Su said in a low voice. She was anxious too. Wei He had eaten with her at midday. Where had the man gone?
Before long Wei Ying arrived too. She looked left and right and could not find her brother anywhere, and was seated, uneasy, close by Zheng Fugui, where she could be protected. "Master Zheng, it seems something has held our junior brother up, and the hour is nearly on us. Shall we open the ceremony first, to warm the scene up?" Zhang Lu had caught snatches of the talk about him and his face was red with it; he could hardly bear to sit still.
He had never had a bout, but men who trained their fists had blood in them, and was all that grinding at his art day after day for the sake of playing the coward when it counted? "You mean?" Zheng Fugui hardly knew what to do either, but at the suggestion he made up his mind and nodded. "Very well. Zhao Hong, get ready—" Before he had finished the words, Zhang Lu had already leapt into the middle of the ground.
In the inner yard the seats were set in a ring, with open ground left in the middle wide enough for a demonstration. Zhang Lu stepping out drew every eye at once. He cupped his fist and said loudly, "I am Zhang Lu, an inner-yard disciple of Mountain Fist. It is rare that so many friends gather under one roof. Shall we begin with a small exchange of bouts ahead of time, to warm the scene and keep it from going cold?"
"Warm the scene? Ha, I like the sound of that." A man came out from Bat Fist, who were friends of Mountain Fist, and cupped his fist slightly to Zhang Lu. "I am Cheng Xi, an inner-yard disciple of Bat Fist. Pray instruct me, Senior Brother Zhang."
