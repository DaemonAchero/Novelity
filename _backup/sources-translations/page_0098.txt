…Winter turned to spring, and the weather even gave a light rain. Though it might well turn fair again afterwards, that rain seemed to put a little life back into the parched land. The Cheng house.
In the fine, unending drizzle, Cheng Shaojiu and Wei He stood in the courtyard of the house and let the rain drift slowly down, leaving specks of white on their shoulders. Cheng Shaojiu could not settle his hands: now lifting them, now letting them fall, now clasping them behind his back and pacing. His eyes kept turning toward the inner room.
By candlelight, from outside one could see only a few figures hurrying back and forth in the room. "Push! Breathe! Breathe deep!" "Push harder!! Do not stop! Whatever you do, do not stop!" "Madam, quick, quick, quick!!"
"Ah…!" Waves of a woman's cries of pain came out of the room. Cheng Shaojiu seemed to feel it in his own body: standing in the rain and the cool wind, his brow was slowly breaking out in a cold sweat. He had never thought that his beloved second concubine should have conceived again half a year ago. Now her ten months were full and her time had come. A philanderer he might be, but he was very generous to his concubines.
His several children he doted on as well. Wei He had seen some of them, but none had seemed to matter to him as much as this one. "If anything should happen to Yun'er this time…" Cheng Shaojiu said through his teeth.
"Do not worry. I hear sister-in-law's cries are loud and her breath is strong; she should be able to get the child out without trouble." Wei He said to comfort him.
"Let us hope so…" Cheng Shaojiu nodded, but the worry did not leave his face. Just as he was about to say something more, a cry of surprise came from the inner room. "It is out, it is out!" "Scissors, quickly!"
"Sew the wound right away! Bring hot water and towels!" "Pat its bottom!" "Waa!!" At last a pitiful wail shook the sky.
Cheng Shaojiu's whole body went slack, and he made to rush into the room. Just then the door opened and the midwife stood in the doorway holding a baby wrapped in layer upon layer. "Congratulations, Master Cheng — it is a girl!" Cheng Shaojiu gave his daughter one hurried look and at once dashed in to see his concubine.
Wei He smiled a little at that; it showed how truly good Cheng Shaojiu was to his women. Since breaking through to Stone-Hide he had now had three months more, and while combining the Internal Cultivation Method he had begun the drills of the new stage, pressing toward Iron-Hide. This pass was unlike the ones before: it was a true water-mill of a pass.
Eldest Senior Brother Zhao Hong had been stuck on it five years without a tremor; Cheng Shaojiu had been stuck four, equally unmoving. Over these months, besides bitter skin grinding, Wei He had been testing out the ways of the Realm Breaking Bead. Its power to gather qi and blood plainly had to do with the nourishment and heat in what he ate; he had tried many foods of different heat, and found the Bead's progress plainly varied with them.
And as he rose to the Stone-Hide stage his body's total capacity for qi and blood rose again, and the Bead's rate of gathering was plainly slower. He understood it well enough: before, one bucket of water had been enough to break through; now it took two, while the tap ran at the same speed as before. So of course it was slower.
