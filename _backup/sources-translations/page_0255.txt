Luckily Wei He could travel far faster than other men, and the Flying Dragon Art saved him a great deal of strength. With the two arts laid over his third breakthrough in qi and blood, his qi and blood rolled on: old strength was not yet spent before new strength was born. It took him two full days, but at last he found traces, and following them he found the stockade where One Ear lived. In the dense dead-leaf woods, pale yellow and deep yellow leaves lay everywhere, spread on the ground in a thick carpet.
A careless step of Wei He's would set off a fine crunch. The mountain wind whistled through the trees, and woods with no leaves left on them could not stop the air at all. Wei He looked up ahead. Past two rough wooden watchtowers was a wall a hundred metres long, built of wood and stone together.
But the wall was only one side. The whole stockade was a triangle: the other two sides were steep cliff walls, and with the wall they made a closed space. From a distance the whole stockade was pitch black, and a smell of burnt charcoal could faintly be caught. Wei He let out a breath, and a bad thought came into his mind.
He quickened his step and closed in fast. He passed the watchtowers; they were empty, with no one on watch. Soon he reached the stockade gate. The gate was two metres and more high, made of thick timber covered with a layer of iron sheet. Half of it had been smashed to pieces, and only the other half still barely showed the outline of a gate.
He went through what barely remained of the stockade wall, and inside was a stretch of collapsed ruins. Blackened timbers stuck out at angles, pointing at the sky. In the ruins lay charred bodies curled and shrivelled, and several vultures were enjoying a feast on some of them, tearing away as they pleased. Wei He paid them no mind. He made a quick round of the stockade: bodies everywhere, burnt and warped and twisted things everywhere.
The whole stockade was a wreck, with nothing living left in it. Wei He got nothing, and came back out through the gate. From what the fire had left behind, the thing here had certainly not happened recently. Looking at the burnt ground, Wei He already knew in his heart that searching on was not the right thing. With soldiers and chaos, with everything in confusion, he could not buzz about like a headless fly; he had to look after his Second Sister Wei Ying.
The stores in the hills would run out in the end, after all, and he needed a steady supply of strange-beast meat; the little he had would soon be gone. So he, and Wei Ying, and Jiang Su and the others, all needed a settled place to live. And going to Tai Prefecture meant crossing the border of another province, and on the rough sketch Master Zheng had given them they would surely lose their way. Luckily Wei He had the escort agency's master map that Cheng Shaojiu had given him.
It was not very clear, but it marked the positions of Tai Province and Tai Prefecture precisely. From Feiye City to Tai Prefecture meant crossing the broad Yewu Mountains. On the map the range held several rivers, dozens of villages and towns, and stretch after stretch of wild dense woods. Just looking at it gave a sense of the danger. And more than that, the agency map had red dots marking a good many spots where strange beasts came and went.
Wei He counted roughly: on that road, walking without rest at the speed of an escort agency's train, by the scale on the map, half a year at least would be needed to arrive. And that was supposing no delays on the way. Not to mention that there was no telling when the Yonghe Escort Agency had drawn this route, or how long it had been out of date, or whether the villages and towns on it were still there,
or whether the range of the strange beasts had changed. All of these were problems. He weighed which was better, Yun Prefecture or Tai Prefecture. In the end he decided on Tai Prefecture.
In Yun Province even outlying Feiye City had fallen into war; things in Yun Prefecture itself were probably not much better. Most important was that the Wushi Sect Master Zheng had spoken of had the Wushi Return-to-Mountain Art that he needed. By what Master Zheng said, going to the Wushi Sect would give him a step to look down from a higher place. With the tokens ready and the connection, he and his few could go there smoothly, join the sect and be sheltered.
The Wushi Sect's strength was far, far beyond Mountain Fist's. With that, Wei He decided to make one last trip around the area, dig up the grain and meat he had hidden before and take it with him, and then leave. In truth he no longer held any hope that his parents and his elder sister were still alive.
