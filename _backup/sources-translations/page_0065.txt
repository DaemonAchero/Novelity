Of all the wards in the city, this was the one most steeped in the air of business. The Zhang family's restaurant business was of some note here too; not the strongest by any reckoning, no match for the Cheng family's standing in Stone Bridge Ward, but no small back-street concern either. "In the letter Young Master Cheng had a matchmaker bring, it says of this Wei He: at seventeen he broke through in qi and blood and became a formal disciple of Mountain Fist,
and out of his own purse bought a house with a courtyard. His character and appearance are upright; he is a man of feeling and honour, plain and reliable by nature; he has only one elder sister living, and no parents needing care; his future prospects are promising…" A maid stood to one side reading the contents of the letter carefully.
Zhang Wanruo, nearby, was as her name: slight and delicate in figure, in a pale green cotton skirt, with fine clear features and black hair to her waist. Save that going out often to mind the business had left her skin somewhat dark and her hands rough, in all else she had exactly the look of the good wife and mother many a man would choose first. Hearing the run of facts and figures, Zhang Wanruo knitted her brows slightly.
"Since he is an entered disciple of Old Man Zheng of Mountain Fist, he must be decent enough. By the way, Hongmei, do you know how long it took that Wei He to break through in qi and blood?" As one born to business she had some slight understanding of the martial line as well, and knew that the earlier a man broke through, the greater his potential. In the present state of things, force of arms was the best way to keep oneself safe, so in this matter nothing could be left to chance.
Houses and the like did not matter. The plague was fierce and the dead were many, and the great grain towns outside had suffered insect calamity; you could say that houses and such things were now the cheapest of all goods. Many people were trying to move away. The maid called Hongmei hastily dug out another letter.
"I made enquiries for you long ago, young miss. This is news I had a man get from others in the know at the Mountain Fist yard." She spread the letter and read it out word by word. "This Wei He is of a forbearing temper; outsiders cannot make out what he thinks. His breakthrough in qi and blood barely scraped through in the last two days, and only with difficulty; his potential may already be spent.
"He comes from the most chaotic and dangerous of the Outer City's wild districts. He has one second sister; his father, mother, and eldest sister are all missing. In the yard he speaks little, and keeps little company." The letter was written in a very objective, factual way, filling in all the points Cheng Shaojiu's letter had left out. When Zhang Wanruo had heard it, her brows drew tighter.
"Breaking through in the last two days — his future potential is not great. Young Master Cheng said not one word of this in his letter."
"Then, young miss…?"
"Refuse him. I, Zhang Wanruo, am not so far gone that I must marry any man of that level who comes along." Zhang Wanruo waved a hand.
Though her habit of showing herself in public and going out to do business meant few men came seeking her hand, she had not sunk to the point of marrying just anyone. This Wei He was an entered disciple of Mountain Fist, and a bare match for her in standing, but his potential still fell short. She meant to wait a while longer; after all, she had waited all this long.
"Very well, young miss. I will go and tell the matchmaker at once." Hongmei put the two letters away and turned to leave. On the other side, hearing that the meeting had been turned down, Wei He took it to heart not at all. His mind was all on how to speed up his accumulation of qi and blood; on the business of feeling and marriage he took the line of letting things run their natural course. Though the matter of heirs was a great one, with no suitable match found there was no hurry.
It was Cheng Shaojiu, rather, who had been full of confidence at the start, and after the letter was refused could not quite save face. He brought Wei He several portraits all at once. Only, since the first one he had set his hopes on had come to nothing, Wei He's interest had cooled, and he turned them all down one by one. So a little over a month passed quietly. At last the Realm Breaking Bead's progress was a hair from complete; it would not be long, surely, before it rounded off of itself.
Wei He had all his mind fixed on raising his qi and blood quickly. Once his qi and blood reached the critical point, he could break into the next stage along with it: Stone-Hide. From the treatment Xiao Ran got, one could see that between Ox-Hide and Stone-Hide there lay no small gap.
