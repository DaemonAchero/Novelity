He dropped off his horse and pulled his bundle tight on his back. "Mountain Fist, with me!" he shouted low, and without another look at anyone else he ran for where the Mountain Fist people were. While the soldiers around had not yet arrived, he reached the Mountain Fist group fast.
The disciples were huddled together, saving themselves, with no one to lead them. When they saw him, a crowd of them followed him as he charged in one direction. By now the train was in chaos too. Nothing more came from the martial masters, only the sound of fierce fighting from time to time; clearly they had been attacked first. The many guard disciples on the outer edge were the first to meet the soldiers coming up around them.
Seen from above, the black masses of soldiers on both sides were like black ants, swarming onto the grey-white train in the middle, as ants swarm over a fat white worm. Wei He lifted Second Sister down from the cart and wrapped her in thick baggage cloth. Unless it was set on fire, such cloth was hard for a man with a chopping knife to cut through, and it would keep her from being knocked about.
Then he led the people at a run for the woods on one side. Everywhere he looked there were masses of black soldiers, and his ears were full of wild, sharp war cries and screams. Wei He was at the head of the Mountain Fist group; Jiang Su and Zhao Hong guarded the two flanks, Zhang Lu and the rest guarded the rear, and they rushed at full speed for the edge of the soldiers' ring.
"Follow me!!" Wei He raised his arm and blocked two army sabers on the four iron bars bound along his forearm, then swept with one hand and let his jin out. The two soldiers' chests caved in and they were thrown back, plainly dead. The moment they fell, four more soldiers came at him with a roar, chopping.
Wei He did the same again: raised the iron bars to block, then swept across. He took down two more and badly hurt two, as easily as before. There was no time to finish them off; he rushed on towards the outside. If the ground on both sides had not been mountain woods, he would not have left his horse. On flatter ground, charging on horseback was faster than running on foot.
One arm around Second Sister, one arm sweeping, his iron-bound arm swept all before it; he did not even need to spend jin to smash his way through. But soon he met a block. A Giant-Tooth soldier, three metres tall, stood in his path.
"Die!!" The huge black wolf-tooth club came smashing down at Wei He's head. Weighing a hundred catties at least, it brought a foul wind with it before it even arrived, and the disciples behind Wei He could hardly breathe, all of them turning pale.
Pfft. At that moment a point of cold light flew from Wei He's hand and struck the Giant-Tooth soldier square in the face. It was a black poison dart, and it drove deep into his left eye. He gave a scream, the wolf-tooth club went crooked, and slammed into the ground beside him, which Wei He and the others dodged.
"Go!" A flash of blood: Wei He's fist burst the Giant-Tooth soldier's head open. The group rushed past the body and on towards the outside. Along the way Wei He broke through several more times, and each time he counted on the iron bars bound to his arms to stop the weapons around him, which saved him a great deal of strength.
He could do it; the others could not. In the confusion several Mountain Fist disciples were separated from their families, and several more simply disappeared — no one knew whether they had been lost in the crowd or killed. His ears were full of roars, screams and wild laughter.
Counting both the inner and the outer yard, Mountain Fist had some thirty-odd disciples, and close to a hundred people with the families. When they broke out, only a little over fifty were left. Zhao Hong, the Eldest Senior Brother, had gone to save his old mother, and no one knew where he was either.
