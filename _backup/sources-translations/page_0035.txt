Not just anyone was qualified to join Jiang Yan's private gathering either. The likes of Wei He, whose gifts did not come up to the mark, would be no more than chief guards for some household before long; their futures were of another class entirely. "Brother Cheng, would it not be better to bring someone else?" Jiang Yan said.
"What? Was it not said last time that getting through the door is all it takes to qualify?" Cheng Shaojiu was taken aback.
"That is so. Only, for a small circle like ours, is the threshold not a little low?" Jiang Yan said, displeased. Cheng Shaojiu understood at once: they thought Wei He's gifts too poor, and they were not satisfied. He looked at Jiang Su and Xiao Ran. Neither of them said anything, which plainly meant they thought the same.
"That Wei He broke through at the very last moment — we all saw it. Inspiring as it looked at the time, if he wants to enter our circle I think he needs to be tested a little," Jiang Yan said seriously. "Quite so." Jiang Su nodded in agreement. With her agreeing, Xiao Ran had even less reason to object.
Cheng Shaojiu paused a moment, then smiled. "Then how is he to be tested?"
"Very simply." Jiang Yan smiled slightly. "My family keeps a fierce dog. If he can beat that dog of mine with his bare hands, he passes the test."
Cheng Shaojiu had no answer to that. He tried to argue a few more words, but Jiang Yan and the others held to one line and would not be moved. With no way out, he had to go and find Wei He, who was practising attack and defence in a corner, and tell him about the test.
== Chapter 12 · Leaving the City (Part 2) ==
"A test?" Wei He was baffled.
"Just so. This No-Gap gathering is a rare chance. Jiang Yan comes from a great family, and he will bring his family's strength to bear and hire good men to
spar with us. So if you can, you should try to get in…" Cheng Shaojiu was genuinely thinking of Wei He's good. He thought that with Wei He's temperament and his understanding, it would be too much of a shame for him to be held back by slightly weak gifts. Wei He smiled. A test? A pack of kids playing their testing games.
Tempting as the No-Gap gathering sounded, he had his own arrangements. "Thank you for the recommendation, Senior Brother, but I know my own gifts are poor. I will not force my way in and ask for humiliation." He answered with a calm face.
"Come now, do not be proud about it. A chance like this — if you get some real fighting out of it, it will do you a great deal of good later on." Cheng Shaojiu pressed him.
"It is not pride. I have my own arrangements, that is all. Thank you for your kindness, Senior Brother." Wei He politely refused again. Cheng Shaojiu tried a few more times and got nowhere, and in the end went back and told Jiang Yan and the others.
"It seems this junior brother Wei knows his own limits, at least." Xiao Ran could not help laughing.
"I have seen plenty of his sort, shinning up whatever pole they can reach. Brother Cheng was probably pestered by him until he could not bring himself to refuse, and so raised it with us. But if he thinks that gets him into our circle, he is badly mistaken." Jiang Yan, copying the manner of his father and elder brothers,
said it with a composed smile. "Quite right. Brother Cheng cannot bring himself to say no; we have no such trouble." Xiao Ran agreed from the side. He had no use for these giftless wastrels either: taking so long over something so simple — if a man was that stupid, why come and learn the fists at all? Would it not be better to go home and farm and get a bigger harvest?
Jiang Su passed no judgement at all; she could not be bothered with it, and went on with her own skin grinding. Cheng Shaojiu had nothing to say and could only smile bitterly. He wanted to explain that Wei He was not that sort of man, but looking at their faces he knew the words would be wasted.
