The body bowed, both arms guarding head and face and chest. The difference was that the Mountain Fist move had none of that bouncing, springing posture. Third Senior Brother Cheng Shaojiu settled into his stance and looked at Wei He, baiting him.
"Throw a punch and try it?" He grinned.
Wei He ignored him and went on adjusting his own stance as the old man had taught, feeling out what it meant. This defensive move had five variations in all, each one for a different kind of attack. And after it came the evasive movements, made chiefly to deal with an opponent who carried a weapon: which way to dodge, how to dodge best, what to watch for once you were clear, how to strike back, how to find the weak place in a man.
And so on and so on — a great heap of forms and sequences, all of them handed down by Old Man Zheng to these newcomers of his. Forms and sequences they truly were. The task before Wei He and the others was to train them until they were reflex, so that the moment one was needed it came out like lightning, without thought.
— and then to win the day in one stroke.
"Master, I've heard Mountain Fist has an Internal Cultivation Method. Might you be teaching it?" a small-built newcomer asked suddenly. Old Man Zheng, mid-stroll, stopped short and drove a kick hard into the little man's backside.
"The Internal Cultivation Method? Temper your bones to the very limit first — that is what makes you fit to learn it. Hear one rumour and start shouting about it. What do you know, you fool? That's for training force!"
He shook his head and walked back to his high-backed chair with his feet turned out, sat down, and said no more. Wei Ying hurried to bring up the tea and the tea cakes. Ever since he had found how much finer Wei Ying's work was, Old Man Zheng had taken her on as one of the servants of the yard.
Which was why he had grown a good deal better mannered towards Wei He as well. Wei He had feared his second sister might be ill used there; but after asking carefully and watching for himself, he found the old man set little store by women and a very great store by money. That set his mind at rest.
The whole day long, Wei He and the rest trained one sequence after another: the parrying sequences and the evading sequences that belong to the defensive moves. The next day it was the attacking moves. As for the killing moves, there was not the ghost of any teaching of them, nor any sign the old man meant to teach them.
Attack and defence alone were two whole systems, enough to keep this crowd of rough, half-lettered men busy a very long time. Wei He was among the slower half of them. Of those who had come in with him, a good many had paid their money and then failed to keep at it, and left early. Others were better gifted than he and had already finished the attack and defence moves and were at work grinding their fists and their qi and blood. They were a good deal faster than him.
He had thought life would go on just so — no sickness, no disaster, quiet and steady. But before long Old Man Zheng of Mountain Fist and another martial master made ready for a simple contest between their houses. This kind of contest is called, in common speech, a fist-off, and in Feiye City it was a common thing between one martial master and another.
The rules were simple too. The masters themselves did not step into the yard; what was matched was the skill of the disciples under them, and a good many rich merchants and wealthy households from round about were invited to watch. The whole point was to show off what the masters could teach.
"It's the Bat Fist people we're matched against." Cheng Shaojiu, as third senior brother, had long since finished the attack and defence systems and now spent his days grinding at the toughness of his fists. He was already at the Ox-Hide stage.
"Bat Fist?" Wei He asked, low, as the two of them broke apart from practising counters. "Have you faced them before?"
"An old rival. Every half year or so there's a fist-off, and we take turns winning. It's about whose name carries in the wards round here." Cheng Shaojiu spoke easy.
"You're not going up anyway, so what are you afraid of? Only a few of us fight." He tilted his head towards Eldest Senior Brother Zhao Hong, not far off.
