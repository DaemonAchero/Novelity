The shock wave of the jin alone had forced the four around them to fall back and away. Now all was quiet. Zheng Fugui stood in the middle of the inner yard, his face one broad smile like an old chrysanthemum in bloom; on that wrinkled face a second spring seemed to have opened — relief, delight, ease all over it.
His hands behind his back, he looked at Wei He standing opposite him, respectful and proper, and the more he looked the more satisfied he grew. If he had not already had a grandson, he would probably be wanting to take the lad as a son-in-law. Wei He stood where he was, his face calm, and now withdrew his jin and returned to his former state. His qi and blood seemed dormant; there was not the least difference from before, and no way at all to tell that he now had the third breakthrough and had reached the Iron-Hide level.
"Your disciple broke through just now and came at once to report it to you, teacher. A while ago, when my qi and blood were growing, it happened that you were busy, and I could not report it in time. Yesterday your disciple was thinking back over the Root Picture, and something suddenly came to me; with my qi and blood surging I hurried to circulate and practise it, and sure enough, in one breath I broke clean through the shackles……"
Wei He began to describe carefully how hard he had worked lately, how bitterly he had trained — making sure to bring out that he had climbed step by step, steady and firm. He knew that what Master Zheng appreciated most was a solid foundation. Sure enough, at these words Zheng Fugui grew still happier.
"Good! Very good!" He pinched at his beard and accidentally pulled several hairs out without noticing at all. "In the outer city's wards, the reason my Mountain Fist is always the weaker one is that everyone says I cannot teach a real third-breakthrough expert. But now…… now it is different!" Thinking back over the years, he felt all his old chafing and weariness vanish at once, as if he had just soaked in a hot bath.
"Now, the moment you break through, you can already fight me to a draw with half my jin. It seems I need not worry about you in real fighting. Today you go home and rest; for the next few days try not to use your hands, and adjust your body well, let the changed qi and blood remake you." He warned him carefully. "Your disciple obeys." Wei He nodded.
In fact he had only used Mountain Fist just now, nothing else, precisely so that Master Zheng would not see through him. Master Zheng had used only half his jin — and had he not used only half his strength? If he added the Five Ridges Palm and the Flying Dragon Art, with the jin coiling and stacking and then bursting out, its power would be more than twice as great. But Wei He also knew that Master Zheng, old and body failing, was not the average of jin-entering martial artists.
After a round of praise that cost him nothing, Zheng Fugui waved a great hand and gave Wei He a whole pile of reward goods. He did not say what was inside, only told Wei He to carry it back and look for himself. Then the wildly happy Zheng Fugui took off at a run, and nobody knew what for. From the look of him, he was either going off to show it in someone's face, or going off to show it in someone's face.
The Mountain Fist hall now had one more great third-breakthrough expert; its reserves and its deterrent weight had taken another big step. That gave one more prop to the safety of every disciple of the hall. Anyone thinking of provoking Mountain Fist would first have to weigh the danger it might bring down on him. Most important of all, Mountain Fist was no longer short of hands, no longer a school with no hope of handing on its line.
The four left in the inner yard looked at Wei He, now holding this great gift packet, and for a time each of them wore a complicated expression. Jiang Yan had been momentarily cowed; now that he had come back to himself, he stared at Wei He for a long while. He thought back to when he had tried to draw Wei He in, and been refused. Thinking of it now left him stifled and sore. He felt that if he had put down more capital back then, he might by now have Wei He tied to his chariot.
A third-breakthrough expert as formidable as his own father! This was a third breakthrough…… In all of Feiye City's younger generation, the great mass of young martial talents — not one of them was not striving towards this level. And yet those who broke through were few and far between. Once this level was crossed, it meant power, wealth, pleasure — everything within reach of the hand.
All the great powers would pay a high price to draw in an expert of this level. He, Jiang Yan, if he could reach the third breakthrough, could stand equal with his father within the family. In the family council he too could have a say, could make his own voice heard.
