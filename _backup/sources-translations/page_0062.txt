This young fellow buying the house might be replaced by another tenant before long. Better to sell it for whatever price he could get; after all, with the plague lately… The old man sighed inwardly and said no more. Wei He considered a moment.
He reckoned the goods he had just drawn for the escort run, plus part of what he had saved before, and together they came to enough to pay the price of the house. "All right, this one then." He decided on the spot and bought it. That settled, he went with the old broker to the broker house and had the transfer done, then moved the goods over from his second sister's place to pay the price.
It was nearly dark before he had finished, one thing after another. Next he had to tell his second sister to come and live here too. And he would need to hire someone to help clean the place. Wei He worked out his own pay and standing: he was an escort man of the agency, but the wages he drew were not an ordinary escort man's — they were an escort captain's. That was, the same treatment as Cheng Jing.
That was probably the nub of why Cheng Jing had taken a dislike to him before.
== Chapter 21 · Proactive (Part 1) ==
"Moving house?" His second sister Wei Ying was stitching a split silk garment. Hearing Wei He bring up buying a house, her hands paused and she looked up, rather stunned.
"Mm. I bought a house nearby. We have no need to go back to the old place." Wei He nodded, serious. The room Wei Ying lived in was only a few square metres: apart from a bed there was one table and chair, and nothing else at all.
Even the window was sealed shut. It really was only a place to sleep. "But… where would we get so much money? Houses are so dear…" Wei Ying said, not quite believing it.
"They are not dear now." Wei He was silent a moment, then answered.
"…Is that so…" Wei Ying lowered her head, and for a while her needle hardly moved. But she recovered quickly, and a happy look came over her face. "Then at last we can live together. Being apart all this time, I always worried about you. Wei Ying asked.
"Now, I think. It is the noon rest right now; I will tell Senior Brother Cheng later." Wei He said.
"Mm, all right. Oh — and earlier Old Man Zheng said to go to him once you were back. He has something to tell you." Wei Ying said suddenly.
"Me? Just me?" Wei He was taken aback. In the yard he was only an ordinary disciple with nothing outstanding about him; as a rule Old Man Zheng would not single him out by name.
"Not only you. Before you came back, several others had already been called in to talk with him." Wei Ying answered.
"I know. I will go once the things are moved." Wei He nodded. Wei Ying still wanted to ask what had happened on the escort run, but Wei He fobbed her off with a couple of words. Luckily that run had been quite safe, and Wei Ying suspected nothing.
Both of them came from poor families and were quick at work, and Wei Ying's own baggage was not much; a great deal of what she used belonged to Old Man Zheng's house in the first place. In less than half an hour it was all moved over. That done, Wei He went back to the Mountain Fist yard, and before he had even gone in he saw Xiao Ran being handed up into a white carriage.
He shook his head inwardly, but his face showed nothing, and he pushed the door open and went in. In the yard, several senior and junior brothers had already begun to train hard.
