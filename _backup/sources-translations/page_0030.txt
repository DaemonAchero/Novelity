That was what he thought, but Wei He also knew that even if such a thing existed, it was not for the likes of him as he was now. Toward afternoon the yard began packing up its gear, and a few men started shooing people out; then everyone saluted Old Man Zheng and took their leave. That fixed routine done, Cheng Shaojiu took Wei He along and the two of them got into the carriage home. The carriage of Cheng Shaojiu's family was not drawn by any tall, fine horses, but the beasts were sturdy and strong. Poor in looks, good enough
in use. That was the saying. These days a household that kept a carriage was no ordinary household, and by the time they reached the Cheng house Wei He understood that well.
Sitting in the carriage he looked out through the window. A great gate of vermilion lacquer, white walls and blue-grey tiles, and before the gate two stone mastiffs, fierce enough to frighten anyone. Wei He wondered a little why they had stone mastiffs rather than stone lions, but he did not ask aloud. Cheng Shaojiu brought him in; they got down, and past the gatekeeper's fawning greetings they went straight through into the great courtyard.
The courtyard was the size of a whole schoolyard. On the right stood a flagpole, and from it hung high a white banner with the black characters Yonghe Escort Agency. The banner snapped and rippled in the wind, curling and uncurling. Cheng Shaojiu led him on, over to a corner of the yard, to a weapons rack.
He pointed to a thick wooden post driven into the ground and wrapped in ox hide, and smiled. "Give it a go?"
Wei He had just broken through; the strength in him had nowhere to go and his fists were itching. "Then — let us try."
He went up, stood before the post, and set his stance. Without warning his right fist struck from the side into the post — the first blow of the test. Then both fists came out in a chain, three blows to the second, and he kept at it without pause for a good dozen breaths before slowly easing off, drawing the fists back, standing straight, and dropping into a guard.
"Fifteen breaths at full attack — not bad, not bad." Cheng Shaojiu clapped from the side. He walked to the ox-hide-wrapped post and lifted the hide. Under it the post was covered in fist marks, some deep, some shallow. Posts like this were made of a special timber, extremely hard, far sturdier than ordinary wood. To strike fist marks through the ox hide
meant something else entirely. An ordinary man could break the bones of his own hand on it and leave no more than a faint dent. "You have broken through to Ox-Hide, but your control of force is not exact enough yet. That is what you must work at from here."
"Understood. Thank you for the guidance, Senior Brother!" Wei He nodded seriously.
"Shall we have a spar?" Cheng Shaojiu smiled, took off his outer coat to show the close-fitting short training clothes underneath, and beckoned Wei He. "Please." Wei He said nothing, took one long stride in, and threw a punch. Cheng Shaojiu blocked it aside with one hand and returned the blow at once.
He was already at the Stone-Hide level, his qi and blood thick and deep, nearly several times Wei He's, and his eyes were quick and his hands fast; move after move he blocked everything Wei He threw. Neither let his feet stop; the exchange was quick, and the dust kept rising from the ground. In the blink of an eye fifteen breaths had gone.
Cheng Shaojiu landed a quick punch square on Wei He's left shoulder and drove him back several steps. He laughed, and waved to the several house guards watching from the side of the ground. "Someone come and give my brother here a turn, let him gain some experience." At once a middle-aged man, without a word, jumped down onto the ground and came straight at Wei He.
Wei He said nothing and squared off against the middle-aged man. Unlike Cheng Shaojiu before, this man was a stranger; first he had to watch and see what he was good at, and from where he would make his first move. As a rule, men who trained the fists had heavy upper bodies and solid arms, while men who trained the legs had a firm base, light and nimble and strong at the same time.
