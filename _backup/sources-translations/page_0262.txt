“There are many dangers here. Even with the beast-repelling incense, a lot of poisonous bugs are still around. Making it here safe and sound is already very impressive,” old Wang praised.
“If I am not mistaken, young brother, you are also heading to Taizhou to find a master and learn martial arts, right?” he continued.
“That is correct.” Wei He nodded. To be precise, he really was going to learn martial arts.
“To be honest, it is the same for me. I am bringing my granddaughter there to seek a master. Practicing martial arts is very popular in Taizhou, and it is currently the most stable place among all the nearby regions.” He sighed. “If I had more choices, I would not want to leave my hometown and travel such a long distance.”
He pointed at the woman in the black veil nearby and spoke in a low voice. “My granddaughter, Zhenqi, has shown great talent since she was young and is obsessed with martial arts. Because our hometown was hit by disaster, we simply decided to move and head to Taizhou. It is a chance to look for opportunities in a bigger place.”
Zhenqi? Wei He glanced at the masked woman in black.
No matter how he looked at them, this group did not look like a grandfather and granddaughter at all. From his perspective, the core of this entire team was that masked woman. The rest of the people behind old Wang kept cautiously looking at her every now and then, as if they were taking orders from her and watching her expression.
The other party thought he could not tell, but Wei He had decades of life experience from crossing over. He could easily understand small details and micro-expressions. With a little bit of deduction, he roughly figured out who the core person was.
“Right, I forgot to introduce them. These two are fellow martial artists traveling with us. They joined us halfway.” Old Wang introduced the other two people to Wei He. “One is Xue Chengyong from Suzhou, and the other is Qian Hao from Beifu City.”
“I am Wei He, from Feiye City.” Wei He cupped his fists in greeting.
“Xue Chengyong.” One of them had a fairly handsome face with a large red mole at the corner of his mouth. He lazily raised his hand to return the greeting.
The other person cupped his fists calmly and steadily, nodding at Wei He. “I am Qian Hao. Nice to meet you, Brother Wei.”
With just a simple greeting, their different personalities stood out. Wei He instantly felt a bit of favor toward Xue Chengyong. This type of person who wore all his emotions on his face was his favorite kind—simple and straightforward, meaning he did not have to worry about too many hidden tricks when dealing with him.
Qian Hao was different. Of course, he could also be faking it on purpose, so he still needed to observe.
At this moment, old Wang shifted his attention to the Cangjian Gorge tomorrow. How to cross it and the absolute rules to watch out for were things he needed to explain first, regardless of whether Wei He, Xue Chengyong, or Qian Hao knew about them beforehand.
All three of them agreed. The most important point during the journey through Cangjian Gorge was that they could not kill the birds. Not even harming them was allowed. Otherwise, it would definitely provoke a massive group attack.
After reminding them repeatedly, old Wang discussed how to split the cost. Crossing usually required a full portion of beast bait, but since four groups were splitting it, each group only needed to pay one-fourth. This saved a lot of supplies. The journey was long, and beast bait was a precious consumable, so saving it whenever possible was important.
After discussing the plan, everyone left and returned to their respective courtyards.
From start to finish, the black-robed woman named Zhenqi never spoke a single word or made a sound. It was not until all three groups of people had left that she raised her head slightly and looked at old Wang.
“Did those people have any problems?” Zhenqi's voice was a bit hoarse, but the texture of a woman's voice could still be heard, only carrying a clear sense of coldness.
“No problems. Everyone hides things to some extent. But when you travel outside, anyone who hides nothing would have died at their own doorstep long ago and never made it this deep,” old Wang answered seriously.
“That is fine. If you find anything wrong, notify me. Acting early is better than acting late,” Zhenqi said calmly.
