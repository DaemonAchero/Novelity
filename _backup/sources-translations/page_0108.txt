This too was one of the core attractions that had kept the Seven Family League flourishing so long. Of the terms put before him, none had moved Wei He much, but the last one had truly stirred him. If he could take up residence in the Inner City, he would no longer have to worry constantly about his second sister's safety. Last time he had killed several men of the Incense Gathering Sect, and since then he had always feared their revenge.
Though nothing had come of it since, who knew whether they were gathering strength for some scheme. Now, if he could live in the Inner City… Wei He lowered his head and was silent a while. What he did not see was that not far off Cheng Shaojiu had come back again, and had happened to hear the terms Jiang Yan was offering.
Cheng Shaojiu understood it well enough: Jiang Yan had come to poach his man. From what Jiang Yan said, he had been standing not far off from the very start and had heard everything Cheng Shaojiu said to Wei He, and had then deliberately seized the gap to make his offer. Cheng Shaojiu fell silent, standing at the corner not far off, and did not show himself at once.
Only, his freshly washed hands clenched slightly of themselves, and grew hot. Wei He was silent, and raised his head to look at Jiang Yan. He drew a breath and began slowly to speak.
"Do not be in a hurry, junior brother." Jiang Yan smiled slightly and folded his fan. "Think it over carefully. If you agree, you may come and find me alone later.
"I will go back to my table first; I have a few friends waiting there." As if it were nothing to him, he turned and went. Jiang Yan knew very well that people are easily carried away, and that men of the martial arts like them, with the qi and blood running high, are the more easily moved. So a decision made in the heat of the moment, in a short space of time, is easily swayed by feeling.
Wei He as he was now, on good terms with Cheng Shaojiu, was easily swayed by the Cheng family. But once a man cooled and weighed gain and loss, he would naturally make the most rational choice. Feeling, in the end, was only a passing impulse; over the long run, interest was what mattered most. Coming to the corner, Jiang Yan happened to see Cheng Shaojiu standing there unmoving.
He smiled slightly, nodded to him by way of greeting, said nothing, and went straight past him. Cheng Shaojiu forced out a smile and nodded in return. Jiang Yan cared nothing for Cheng Shaojiu's manner: the Cheng family was on good terms with Wei He, but what of it? When ordinary people gave up gain for feeling, it was only because the price offered them was not high enough.
The Cheng family, for courtesy's sake, called him Senior Brother. And if they gave him no face? He came of the Seven Family League — would he care a straw for a little Yonghe Agency? Jiang Yan smiled slightly and walked with his page boy toward the private room. Cheng Shaojiu paused, stood there a while longer gathering his feelings, and only then went back toward the wine table. Coming back to the table, he saw Wei He standing to one side, his look complicated.
"Junior brother…" Cheng Shaojiu's lips moved; he wanted to say something, but did not know what to say. What did he have to compare with the Jiang family? It was a comparison between two altogether different levels.
"Brother Cheng…" Wei He was silent a moment, then said in a low voice, "I am sorry…"
"It is nothing… water flows downhill, men climb upward…" Cheng Shaojiu smiled and tried his best to look more at ease than he felt.
"It is not that." Wei He's face was full of compunction. "I saw you were a long time coming back, so I ordered four more plates of branch-antler deer, and ate them all…"
Cheng Shaojiu's expression froze on the instant. Four plates?? Four plates of deer meat!? Are you a pig!? He looked at Wei He, standing fixed as if spellbound, and for a moment could not get out a word. His allowance for the whole month was eaten
up… "Oh yes. Just now Senior Brother Jiang came over, wanting to pull me to his side." Wei He said calmly. "But I thought it over, and I mean to stay in the Outer City after all. Chiefly because Senior Brother Jiang has bad breath; talking with him I always feel uncomfortable all over, and quite miserable."
