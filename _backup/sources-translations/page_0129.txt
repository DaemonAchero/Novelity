"Wait!" The bald man saw how briskly he moved and his eyes narrowed at once. Could this fellow be putting on calm on purpose? He had seen men like that before, and not a few. These days the real experts were all kept and fed by some power or other — who ever went out hunting on his own? Even if one did, it was rare.
For a man on his own like the one opposite, even if he really was an expert — they had this many men, this much good gear on them; they could kill him by dark tricks and be done. The bald man had come up against an expert once before. A pity: strong as that man was, and he killed one of his brothers, he still died under the underhanded moves of the lot of them. Since that time he had understood one principle: an expert too is a man.
Being a man, he dies. So long as the plot came off, it did not matter who the man was — he could be killed. And out of that one success he had come by a martial-arts manual; then he practised hard and, groping his way along, broke through the first stage of qi and blood. On the strength of that and of his deft tricks, he had become a doughty hand among the stockade's mountain bandits.
"You are bulging all over — what have you got on you? Hand it over!" The bald man was staring at the man across from him. Under his outer robe the fellow certainly had something hidden, and not one thing or two, but a great deal. He had read a great many men in his time and he had that much sense at least. And just now the little brat had dropped his catch and turned to go without a word; a real expert would never give in that easily.
The bald man's eyes shifted and glittered, and he felt at once that Wei He's calm was forced. His dropping the catch on his back so decisively must be to hide the more precious goods still on him! The more he thought, the surer he was. "What you have hidden inside your clothes — bring it out!" His face went savage, and the brothers at his side, with fine accord, spread out in a fan
and closed Wei He in the middle of it. One after another they laughed coldly with no good intent, waving their bright chopping knives about so that they kept giving off a whistling that set a man's nerves on edge. "There is nothing in my clothes," Wei He answered. Besides the poison powder, the quicklime, the poison spikes, the leather armour over his whole body and the poison-quenched grip-spikes, he truly carried nothing.
Only the bandits opposite did not believe him, and pressed nearer step by step. "Nothing at all? You are bulging like that and you take me for blind?" One bandit came close and reached out to grab at Wei He's clothes. Wei He hesitated; he wanted no needless trouble, he did not know what strength these people had, and this was their ground, and who
stood behind them he did not know either. Yet he could not let them get their hands on him. So he took a step back and slipped away from the man's hand. "There really is nothing left on me. Elder brothers, I crouched out there for days and caught nothing but the snake in my bag; the rest, truly
there is nothing." "Nothing? I say you have something hidden in your clothes." The more the bald man looked, the more he felt that this fellow opposite was hiding his head and showing his tail, muffled up so tightly, throwing away even the goods in that great bag without a second thought, so decisively. There must be something wrong with him!
There must be something on him of greater value! His mind grew more certain still. "Mat, hold him down; the rest of you, go and search him, strip everything off him." Wei He glanced left and right and made up his mind to pull out first; this was country he did not know, and if he was going to play the hunter he had to play the part properly.
The woods around were deep and the grass thick; no one could say whether anyone else was watching from far off. If the other side had more of the band hidden away, to loose a cold dart or a cold arrow at him, that would be trouble indeed. Two fists cannot beat four hands. Wei He took another step back and brought the dry-ration pouch out from inside his clothes.
"Elder brothers, this is the last bit of food I have…" He held his voice down and set the pouch on the ground. "Dry rations? Heh. Under your clothes there is plenty more, I will wager…"
