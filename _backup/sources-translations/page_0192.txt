== Chapter 64 · Investigation (Part 2) ==
On the back side of Shaoyang Mountain, at the foot of the slope, beside a round, jade-green pool of water, two enormously fat men close to three metres tall were standing. One of them was plainly one of the two great fat men Wei He had run into that night. Apart from the two biggest, there were several dozen ordinary disciples scattered about and four white-clothed inner disciples.
Wei He measured the distance between himself and that group: a little over twenty metres. It was still morning, verging on noon. The sun beat down fiercely, and the dry grass was like a steamer basket. There by the pool, among the Shaoyang Sect men, one of the great fat men seemed to be shouting furiously, violently worked up.
Slowly, a little at a time, Wei He crept along the dry grass higher up the mountain. The mountain wind shook the grass and the woods, and the rustling it made covered perfectly the rustle of him pushing the dry grass aside. Gradually, as he drew closer, he began to feel the waves of cool air coming off the pool. 'No wonder they're all crowded in here.' Wei He understood it at once.
But what he cared about more was what these men were saying. As he drew nearer, the voices by the pool drifted over. "…Get the men from the east gathered over here, or it'll be like last time and the Seven Family League will seize their chance again! Two whole forts' worth of strange-beast bait gone! How am I supposed to explain that to the sect master?!"
Though the voice rose and fell, he could at last make out the words. 'Three times this month already, and over half the goods intercepted — when the agreement falls through you know the consequences!' "Of course I know. But what can I do? Both squads we sent out were wiped out on the road, and the inner sect only has so many men! A third of them are dead now! If more die, the whole Shaoyang Sect will be bled dry!" another voice shouted angrily.
"Those few are too fast — quicker than monkeys. We can't catch them at all! We need to find some way to block them!" "I have a way." "What way? Out with it!" "…Friend on the mountain, why not come out and talk?"
Wei He's heart went cold. Through the grass he saw that by the pool one of the great fat men had suddenly turned his head and was looking straight in his direction. "Friend, you have crossed swords with my Shaoyang Sect several times, but those were small matters. I am Zheng Yue, patrol envoy of the Shaoyang Sect, and I have a weighty matter I should like to discuss with you. If it comes off, the reward will certainly satisfy you, and
all past grudges will be wiped clean." The great fat man with the thick black brows said loudly towards Wei He, his face mild, his voice calm. The other man started slightly and turned to look as well, plainly not having noticed anyone there. The rest of the Shaoyang Sect disciples grew alert one and all and turned to stare in Wei He's direction.
For a moment there was no sound by the pool, only the whining wind threading the wooded hills. At this point Wei He could hardly be in any doubt that he had been found out. But bold as he was in his own skill, he did not rise, and stayed hidden in the grass. Holding his voice down, he spoke. "What have you to teach me, Envoy Zheng? Let us hear it."
The moment his voice came out he was ready to shift position and leave at any time. Yet the other man really did mean to talk to him, and had no intention of making a move at all. Zheng Yue spread both hands, showing he meant no hostility.
