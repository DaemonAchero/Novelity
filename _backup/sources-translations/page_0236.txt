Zhou Jihan's blade light was white with cold, and seen from far off it was like a waterfall flinging itself down, rolling and pressing on and on, with the drive of a great bear hurtling forward. It seemed that what he held was not one saber but a whole sheet of blades, so that a man could not tell at all from what angle or in what manner the attack would come. Wei He admired it in his heart: this single stroke alone covered both attack and defence — even an ordinary treacherous trick would be cut back and rolled up in that light. It was the strongest art of all the academies, and no wonder.
Any other time he would have wished to draw it out and trade moves slowly with the man. Not now. He said nothing; his right arm filled with blood in an instant, swelled and grew, and on the arm five red-cloud flowers showed bare, crawling and writhing like tattooed grubs.
"This is No-Return Mountain." Wei He drove out one fist, the qi and blood in his body surging, pouring madly into his right arm. The face of the fist turned black, the arm swelled, and the red-cloud flowers on his skin went scarlet as blood, fierce and fit to drip. Bang!!
Under the touch of that fist face the blade light broke off short and the blade itself snapped. Zhou Jihan's tiger's mouths split open on both hands; he could not believe it, and he was thrown heavily up and came down on the ground. He opened his mouth to speak, but a mouthful of rebellious blood welled up and choked him — the shaking of an internal injury. "Your strength is not small, but until you find yourself a better weapon, you had best not cross hands with a third-breakthrough master." He gave his judgement in one sentence and turned to look at the rest. "Anyone else?" Silence all round; no one spoke up, and every man was rather afraid of being picked to go on next. In that short little while Wei He had broken Iron Slaughter Fist and then every genius of Zhou Jihan's little circle of three.
What strength it showed, and no wonder: they were third-breakthrough men. Set beside the showing of Mountain Fist's inner-yard disciples earlier, it gave the crowd a fresh sense of how much the third breakthrough added. As for Wei He, this gathering, which was like some ceremony, meant little to him; it was no more than bullying children. Having stepped up into a new level, he was no longer the same as these second-breakthrough boys.
"Very well. Since no one else wishes to try, let us stop here for today." Jiang Su stepped in of his own accord to smooth things over. By now all of Mountain Fist's people had a bellyful of bad air to be rid of, and it came out of them with a vengeance. In that short little while every single inner-yard disciple had been beaten with ease, one after another; and then Wei He had come out and beaten the arrogant opponents of before just as easily.
Only now did the crowd see clearly just how wide the gap between Wei He and the inner-yard disciples was. Had Master Zheng and so many other academies not been standing by, they might well have started cheering aloud. Zhao Hong, Zhang Lu, and Jiang Yan, who had not shown his face the whole time, all had nothing to say.
Before this they had only watched Wei He trade hands with Master Zheng and thought him strong, without any measure of how strong. This time each of them had been beaten with ease, and then the man who beat them had been finished by Wei He in a single instant. Under a comparison that direct, the three of them truly felt how far the distance between them and Wei He ran. The Wei He of before, the one who never drew much notice, the one who stayed silent and did not care to talk, stood now just as silent, and simply standing there pressed hard on every man present. No one came up to challenge again, and Wei He soon drew his qi and blood back in and was set down to sit beside Master Zheng. Only once he left the ground did the mood ease, and the disciples of the other academies began going up of their own accord to try their hands against the rest of Mountain Fist's disciples.
Zhao Hong was no longer so stiff either, and went up now and then to trade moves. With the hands actually moving, everyone felt more and more plainly that Mountain Fist's strength as a whole had come clean away from Wei He's. The strength of Zhao Hong and the others was only enough, among the academies present, to top the inner-yard disciples of Bat Fist and Returning Wind Falling Leaf Legs by a little. The rest they could not match. To call them middling-to-lower, indeed, was rather flattering themselves.
After Zhou Jihan's three had gone off the ground the mood eased, and everything came back at last to the normal order of things. Wei He came out once more in the middle of it to raise a cup of tea to the company in place of wine, as courtesy required, and said a few words of thanks; after that he went down again.
