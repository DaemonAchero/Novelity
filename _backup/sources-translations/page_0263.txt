The old man warned, "Special Envoy, the others might be easy to handle, but that Wei He seems to have unusual blood qi. He doesn't look like someone at the second blood qi level."

On the other side.
Wei He returned to his courtyard and saw Jiang Su guarding the yard with a wary expression, ready for action at any moment.
"You are finally back!" Jiang Su let out a heavy sigh of relief. Without Wei He around, her heart felt uneasy.
The others also instinctively moved closer, as if staying near Wei He gave them a greater sense of security.
"Did you find anything?" Jiang Su asked.
"Not bad." Wei He explained the matters they had agreed upon. From the old man, he had also learned about many potential problems along the way, as well as the beast-repelling incense.
"I knew it. Anyone traveling this path must be relying on something. So they had beast-repelling incense..." Jiang Su nodded.
She paused, then hesitated. "Then Wei He, what do you think we should do with them? Ignore them, or make contact?"
"We do not need to care about them. We go our own way. If we find out they have bad intentions, I will deal with it," Wei He said calmly.
During his journey over the past month, he had not just been walking normally. The number of malicious travelers he had killed along the way was not small.
"Uh... surely there are not that many bad people... We should figure things out before making a move," Jiang Su shuddered. These days, Wei He killed with absolute ruthlessness. Sometimes, before she even finished her sentence, he would suddenly turn around and strike. With a single throwing dart, a human life was gone.
"Besides, people who travel out here in the wilderness must have strong skills..."
"It is fine. Only that woman in black and the old man with the surname Wang have some strength. The rest do not matter. Just prepare yourselves. If there is a problem, watch my signal and strike first," Wei He warned.
After all these days of traveling, Wei He had a clear understanding of his own strength. Among three-blood martial artists, the strength formed by the combination of his two cultivation methods was extremely powerful. Coupled with the bonus of the Flying Dragon Skill, he could move freely. In the three-blood tier, he was estimated to be in the upper-middle group.
Of course, the key did not lie in these things. When fighting, what truly decided life and death was not the strength of the martial arts, but a combination of various factors. Wei He was confident that even if someone had stronger martial arts than him, as long as they had not entered the genuine force realm, the one who survived a fierce battle would definitely be him.
"Alright, I understand," Jiang Su nodded. During this time, she had witnessed how cold-blooded her junior brother truly was. In the wilderness, at the slightest suspicion, he would deal a fatal blow. His decisive actions did not look like killing at all, but rather like a basic instinct.
"Alright, go to sleep. I will keep watch for the first half of the night, and you can take the second half," Wei He instructed.
"Okay," Jiang Su nodded.
The night passed quietly.
