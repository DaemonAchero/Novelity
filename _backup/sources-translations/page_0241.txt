"Is that so?" Wei He narrowed his eyes and stared at him a while, then looked down at the stone. He found nothing wrong with it. He looked away, up at Zhou Shun's clothes. "Junior Brother Zhou, I am a sensitive sort. So while we ride together on watch, it is better not to make any extra movements." "I understand." Zhou Shun answered with his eyes down and his manner meek.
"Good." Wei He nodded, and this time he really did turn away and ride forward. Only then did Zhou Shun fully relax. His back was damp with sweat, and he felt as tired as after a whole night of battling the girls at the Hundred Flowers Tower. He pinched the stone again and, in a special way, pressed his strength into it so that it trembled. Only so could the stone-insect be woken, and word sent to the Seven Family League. This stone-insect could carry out the exact position of every third-breakthrough expert in the train, so that the weak spots in the guards could be found.
He held the pale yellow stone between his fingers, pressed it lightly against the horse's belly and let it go. BANG!! In that instant a black palm came smashing in from the side and struck the arm that held the stone. Zhou Shun had no warning and no way to resist; the blow knocked him right off his horse.
His whole body went numb and his eyes went wide. Then he felt the stone taken out of his hand, his collar was hauled up, and Wei He's dark face appeared before him. "What were you doing just now?" "I…" Cold sweat broke out all over Zhou Shun. He could hardly say that his own academy, the whole of Bat Fist, had long ago gone over to the Seven Family League.
"Tell me. What is this you were holding?" Wei He's face was dark as he stared down at Zhou Shun on the ground. He had noticed something wrong just now, and looking closely, he saw that the man was indeed a problem. "I…" Zhou Shun opened his mouth to speak. Suddenly a man came quickly through the train from the front.
By now the train had slowly left Feiye City ten li behind. On both sides were thick mountain woods, rising and falling, running on and on. The heavy rain before seemed to have brought part of this mountain country back to life: on the hillsides, trees that had been dead and yellow were showing patches of pale green. "Senior Brother Wei." It was Zhang Lu from the academy, come back down the line. He walked up quickly, glanced at Zhou Shun on the ground, paid him no mind, and spoke low at Wei He's side.
"Master Zheng says: if something happens, get your people out at once. Do not go to the Martial Masters' Fort. Run, and scatter." Zhang Lu's face was tense, but he kept his calm as he said it low. "!?" Wei He's eyes changed. He wanted to ask more.
"Master Zheng says, do not go near the Flying Bear Saber." Zhang Lu said one more thing and meant to go on. But there was no more time. Sharp laughter came from both sides, over and over, like a horde of strange apes screaming. The oxen and horses of the train were frightened by the sound and stopped where they stood, restless and uneasy.
Once the long train stopped, the carts jammed up against one another, and getting it moving again would take a long while to sort out. "Who goes there!?" A loud male voice, full of power, came out of the train. Clearly one of the martial masters guarding the train had spoken. The real strength of the whole train was those thirteen martial masters leading it.
"Kill only the Flying Bear Saber! The rest, kneel and you live!" In that instant, with a sharp laugh, great masses of black soldiers came pouring out of the woods on both sides, and among them came huge brutes three metres tall, the Giant-Tooth soldiers. They were like giants, swinging wolf-tooth clubs that snapped the smaller trees in half, laughing as they came charging down the slope.
The laughter had clearly come from them. "The Seven Family League!?" "Fight them! All guard squads, hear the order: the Giant-Tooth men are weak in the eyes, strike their—" The voice did not finish; a dull thud cut it off.
The speaker seemed to have been struck by a surprise blow. Then came a roar full of shock and fury, and ahead, where the martial masters were, hard fighting seemed to have started too. Wei He's heart pounded. At once he remembered what Zhang Lu had just brought him from Master Zheng.
