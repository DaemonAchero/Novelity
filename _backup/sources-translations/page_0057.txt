The thing had a bird's head of black and red, with white eyeballs, and a body over two metres long; half of it was in the dark, half in the torchlight. Its two forelimbs were like a man's, with five fingers, and nails sharp beyond words, glittering silver like metal. The moment the monster had hold of the escort man's legs it pulled backwards, and the huge force threw the man down on the spot; then it dragged him into the dark, and in an instant
he was gone. "Get inside! Quick!!" Cheng Kai's roar woke the escort men out of their frozen staring. The whole party backed quickly into the bridge-head house, shut the door, and blocked the two window-holes with thick planks off the escort carts. Most of them dropped low at once, silent, holding their breath down.
One of the new men, in his fright, could not help panting hard, and in that setting it was as clear as if it were at your ear. An old hand at once reached over and clapped a towel over the new man's mouth. Wei He's heart was shaken too. That monster had moved like the wind, unbelievably fast; even he had seen no more than a shadow. If he were the one attacked, he thought, the odds of being dragged off and of breaking free might be about even.
Everyone fell silent, and for a time the house held only the two fires burning on, the firewood popping now and then in the hush and answering the moaning wind outside, which only made the silence deeper. Wei He looked to either side, and suddenly found that the man who had just been carving wood beside him was gone. Only a small bundle of his baggage was left on the ground.
His heart went cold and he looked quickly about. He remembered the man's face; in a party of twenty-odd, after these days together he had come to know most of them by sight. But look as he would, he could not find the woodcarver. The only trace the man had left was that bundle of baggage on the ground beside Wei He.
An old escort master beside him saw what he was thinking, lowered his eyes, and sighed faintly. "Life and death have no constancy…" Bang!! All at once the planks over the window-hole took a violent blow.
The huge force nearly knocked the two escort men holding the planks off their feet. Two more threw themselves on after them, and only then was it just barely held. The old escort master went white with fright; his murmured words were half spoken when fright swallowed them back. Wei He's heart leapt too, but he was a man who had seen blood and struck a cruel blow; his nerve was a good deal steadier than it had been. He forced himself calm.
"Fire!" Cheng Kai rose and shouted. Someone took a torch, lit it at the fire, and handed it over quickly. Cheng Kai reached to take it.
Bang!! The planks over the window-hole were smashed open, and a black mass came charging in. It knocked the torch Cheng Kai was reaching for flying. "Out with you!" The two escort men and Cheng Jing — three of them at once — swung their sabres from three directions.
Three choppers came down hard on the black shape and drove it back by main force. At once another man snatched up a torch and hurled it at the shape. The black shape did fear fire somewhat after all; it used the force of the blows to retreat fast, leaped out, and vanished through the window-hole. Whoo… whoo…
The men gulped air in great breaths; by the firelight every face was dead white. Cheng Jing and the other two dropped their sabres, and only then found that the webbing of their thumbs was a smear of blood and flesh — the shock of that one blow had wounded them. "It is the Black Madman… Block the window-hole and keep watch! Wait for daylight!" Cheng Kai roared. He was the one who had just snatched up the torch.
His own face was a shade pale too, but there was no fright in it, only calm and steadiness. The old hands among the escort men obeyed at once and lifted the heavy planks back over the window-hole.
