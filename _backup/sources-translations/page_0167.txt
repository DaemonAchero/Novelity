Xiao Ran sneered and drove a fist square into the middle of the sack. Bang! The bamboo weave burst apart, and the meat inside took the full weight of that force and was torn into several pieces that flew off in every direction. Wei He was too slow to block it; in the city, facing a fellow disciple, he did not dare use his underhand tricks either.
He had still been hesitating when Xiao Ran's fist struck the meat sack, and in a fluster he moved to snatch it back. But he was one step too slow. The chunks of meat scattered, flung far by that huge force, and were soon over the eaves and down into other streets dozens of metres away at the least.
== Chapter 56 · Halfway (Part 2) ==
Wei He stood where he was and did not move. Flown that far, even if he hurried after it at once he would be too late. The streets were never short of half-starved poor, and the scattered meat was certainly not to be had again.
And even if it were still there, Xiao Ran would not let him walk off easily. "What's the matter? Does it pain you?" Xiao Ran smiled and shook the meat scraps off his hand. "Aren't you the man who throws away a thousand in gold? Aren't you the rich one? Taking money of shady origin to help Cheng Shaojiu, and getting yourself a fine name out of it. Pleased with yourself?"
He came closer, step by step. "You should be glad you're still inside the ward, with people looking on — anywhere outside the city, and three moves would be enough for me to ruin you!" Xiao Ran said, closing the distance, his face cold. Wei He said nothing. He wiped his left cheek and looked at his fingers: there was a line of blood on them.
It had been a long time since he had been wounded… Wei He felt a small pang of something. He was almost dazed: the last time he was hurt had been very, very long ago…. He narrowed his eyes, and something in him quietly turned different from before. Xiao Ran truly was strong, gifted beyond most, quick to grasp things too.
Face to face, he was no match. But if it were outside the city… no, never mind outside the city — he decided to kill him here and now. In that brief exchange he had already taken the measure of Xiao Ran's style: very strong in a straight fight. But a win or a loss is easy to judge; life and death are hard to call.
A fellow of Xiao Ran's calibre was not something he had never killed — he had killed more than one. Martial skill was only one of the means to life and death, not the whole of it. Today he would teach this boy that truth. A cold flicker passed through Wei He's eyes and he was about to move. "Stop! Xiao Ran, what are you trying to do to Wei He?!"
A figure suddenly came charging in from the side, planted itself between the two and spread its arms to block Xiao Ran. It was Jiang Su, of all people. Her arrival cut short the victory speech Xiao Ran had been about to go on with. He spat on the ground and gave her a cold laugh or two.
"What? One time and you've taken a fancy to him? So concerned?" "Master Zheng strictly forbids disciples from maiming each other — you know the rule, Xiao Ran!" A flash of anger went through Jiang Su's eyes, and she forced the fury down. "And keep your mouth clean! There is nothing between Wei He and me!"
She knew, in truth, that no explanation of hers could explain it away; only a later examination by another would prove her purity. But now, knowing it was Wei He who had helped her, she would never let him be ruined just like that by Xiao Ran. She had seen the resolve in Xiao Ran's eyes. He truly meant to ruin Wei He!
For a moment all three were silent. Xiao Ran stared at Wei He without a word, then at Jiang Su, back and forth, a cold smile on his face. "Enough." Xiao Ran smiled. "Today was only a small lesson. Later on…"
