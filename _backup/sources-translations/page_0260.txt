He picked up a branch from the ground and traced a few lines in the dirt. "Let me tell you a story. Keep your voice down, so we don't make noise or..." He stopped mid-sentence and turned his head to the side. "Friends over there, you can come out now." Wei He slid one hand into his waist pouch, gripping a handful of poison darts.
He didn't have many left, and there was nowhere to get more. Every dart used was gone for good, so he tried not to touch them unless he had to. From the bushes, a few big, muscular men in rags slowly stepped out of the shadows. The leader had two bloody cuts on his face and messy hair, looking completely worn out. His eyes were fixed on the pot of soup boiling over the campfire.
"We don't mean any harm. We've been walking in the woods for too long, and we were starving. We just smelled the food and came over," the leader said in a loud voice. "You can have food, but you have to pay," Wei He raised a hand to signal Jiang Su and the others to stay quiet and let him handle it. "Sure, no problem! We have gold banknotes!" The leader nodded quickly, pulling a ten-tael gold banknote from his chest and waving it around. "Alright then. How many of you are there?" Wei He asked. "Four. Just the four of us," the man answered right away. "Good," Wei He suddenly flicked his hand.
Swish! The dart flew out, hitting someone who was creeping up behind the fire. The dart punched straight through the man's throat and stuck deep into a tree trunk behind him. Wei He moved like a shadow, leaping six meters in a flash, darting past the four men several times.
He turned back and landed right by the fire. "Let's go." He picked up his bag and led the way out. Behind him, the four men collapsed one by one. Every single one had been hit in the head by a palm strike and killed on the spot. The force was quiet, vicious, and lethal—completely different from the open, heavy style of the Mountain-Turning Fist.
Jiang Su and the others stared at the corpses on the ground, then looked at Wei He, who didn't even turn his head. A cold shiver ran down their spines. If Wei He hadn't noticed the ambush from behind, the outcome would have been... Those men were all dressed the same. They used a few people to draw attention from the front while sending one around the back. An attack from both sides could easily wipe out a group that lowered its guard.
Without delay, the others jumped up, put out the fire, grabbed their belongings, and rushed after Wei He. They hadn't walked far when the distant growls of wild beasts rang out behind them, fighting over the bodies. The group pushed forward. Right as the sun was setting, they finally found a small, recently abandoned village. The houses were still in good shape and could shelter them from the wind and rain.
This put Wei Ying and the girls in a much better mood. There was a small stream nearby where they could get water and wash themselves. After using leaves as toilet paper for over ten days straight, their bodies were starting to smell like waste. The sky slowly darkened. Inside a mud-brick house in the abandoned village, a faint firelight flickered through the cracks.
Wei He and Zhang Qi stood in the yard, waiting for the three girls inside to wash up. Stars filled the night sky, vast and deep. Suddenly, Wei He's ears twitched. He turned his gaze toward the broken gate. "Who's there?"
"Just a traveler." A long-bearded old man past fifty stood outside the gate, peering in. "Greetings, young brothers. We are a merchant team heading from Yun State to Tai State. We just got here, saw the firelight, and came to check it out. Please excuse the intrusion." "This is an abandoned village. Do as you please," Wei He said coldly.
"Thank you. My name is Wang. If you need anything, feel free to drop by the house up front." The old man carried a strong air of authority, clearly no ordinary person.
