The first stage, for one just setting out, is Clear — Clear Mountain Ridge. Only once this layer is trained through can one truly enter the door, with qi and blood grown to the point where the next step can be taken. Then comes River, the second layer, in which that grown qi and blood is pressed and changed in nature, and the breakthrough follows: that is the first breakthrough of qi and blood, and it is to step into Mountain Fist's Ox-Hide realm of qi and blood.
The third layer, Cloud, answers to Mountain Fist's Stone-Hide, the second breakthrough of qi and blood. The fourth, White, answers to Iron-Hide, the third. "Only, as it stands I have condensed just the one red-cloud flower in Mountain Fist. These past days, to build up qi and blood, I fed it all to the Realm Breaking Bead, and the skin grinding and the condensing of red-cloud flowers came to a full stop."
Condensing a red-cloud flower burns up great quantities of qi and blood. A human body makes qi and blood at every moment, and how much comes out each day is settled by one's constitution and by the quality of what one eats and drinks — like a milk cow's yield: give her poor rest, poor food and drink, and her milk runs thin.
The same holds for a martial artist. And the day's output of qi and blood is only so much: spend it on this art and you cannot spend it on that one. Training two arts at once therefore drags one's progress back badly; that is simply the way of it. So Wei He had stopped the Mountain Fist work, and the Realm Breaking Bead's accumulation had shot up at once.
"Now, at last — the third breakthrough…" Wei He sat feeling out the changes in his body. The qi and blood rolled within him; his body felt as though it had just woken from a deep and perfect sleep, brimming with spirit and vigour, his mind clear as glass, his thoughts turning with great speed and no hitch or lag in them at all. Those were only his own feelings. What was clearer still was this —
Wei He walked over to his wardrobe, picked the whole thing up and laid it flat on the bed. Then on the floor where it had stood he felt out a ring and pulled it up, bringing a square compartment up with it.
Inside lay a long rectangular hidden box made of black wood. In the box were powders of all kinds, hidden weapons, daggers, money pouches and the rest. Wei He fished out the odds and ends he had taken off Xiao Ran and went through them: a silver-edged money pouch, a small greenish porcelain bottle, a little silk kerchief with a lotus worked on it. And a book, with all sorts of
indecent spring-palace pictures painted all over it. "This boy… tsk, tsk." Wei He tossed the kerchief and the picture book into the box, took up the silver-edged pouch, loosened the drawstring and shook it out over the floor. Out fell a hundred-tael gold note, several gold leaves, two little gold beans, and a few small wooden tokens — identity tallies for the
organizations he hangs his name with, most likely. "A pauper." Wei He shook his head, picked up the gold note and sniffed at its faint scent, then gathered up the gold leaves and the gold beans and threw the rest of the tokens into the hidden box, to be dealt with at night.
"Xiao He, dinner!" Second Sister's call came from outside. "Coming." Wei He stood, took the porcelain bottle in hand, quickly put the hidden box back and lifted the wardrobe back where it had been. He let out a long breath and flexed his hands. Out of the bedroom and across the yard, he suddenly came to a halt at the spot where he practised his Five Ridges Palm.
He looked at the great black stone on which, at his full strength, his palm had once done no more than leave a pit. That stone was no ordinary rock, but a special stone laced with some mineral or other — hard, and tough with it. The same stone was used in the Mountain Fist yard; Wei He had found plenty of it up on Shaoyang Mountain and picked out a slightly larger one to carry home. The egg-shaped black stone was covered everywhere with fist prints and palm prints from before.
Because he struck it slantingly down towards the ground, the earth behind the stone had been pounded to a great hardness. Wei He looked at the palm print he had left there the day before. It was more than an inch deep, the lines of his palm still plainly visible. It looked quite formidable.
