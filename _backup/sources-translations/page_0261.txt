Looking for death. Behind the old man was a long line of people, numbering around ten in total. They carried heavy bags and looked dusty and tired. However, as one of them passed the doorway, it made Wei He's heart twitch with a slight sense of warning.
He focused his gaze and looked outside through the doorway, watching that person slowly leave, passing the entrance and walking deeper into the village. It was a tall woman with entirely black clothes, wearing a veil and a hood, walking with small, hurried steps, her long hair lowered. Strands of bright, soft black hair slipped out from the edge of her hood.
"What is it, Brother Wei?" Zhang Qi asked.
"Nothing." Wei He withdrew his gaze. The woman who had just passed by was somewhat unusual. But out traveling, were there ever a shortage of unusual people? In this day and age, how many ordinary people would dare to walk out into this wilderness? Besides, this was the main mountain path where anyone could travel; running into anything was possible.
Inside the inner room, the three girls had finished washing up and wiping themselves down. They packed up together and came out to take turns relieving Wei He and the other guy.
"I'm going outside to take a look," Wei He said. Along the way, it had been him and Jiang Su taking turns patrolling the surroundings. Now it was his turn.
Barely a few steps out the door, one of the members from the caravan saw him come out and actively walked over, cupping his fists in a salute.
This person seemed to have been waiting by the door for them to come out.
"My friend, tomorrow we reach Hidden Sword Gorge, the place shaped like a bird's nest. We must use beast bait there. If you don't mind, why don't our two groups use one portion together? That way we can save some bait. My master sent me to ask about this. I wonder what you think, friend?"
Hidden Sword Gorge? That name wasn't on Wei He's map.
But his personality was different from ordinary people. His expression remained calm, and since it was dark and hard to see clearly, the other person assumed he knew about it.
"Friend?" Seeing no reaction, the man asked again.
"To be honest, our group is traveling out for the first time, and we don't understand any of this. So, while I do have a portion of beast bait, I don't even know how to use it. Please excuse my ignorance," Wei He replied.

"To be able to travel this far on your first trip, friend, you truly have great luck..." The man was stunned for a moment, then gave two dry laughs before speaking up.
"Not at all, our luck really is pretty good," Wei He replied.
"In that case, why don't you come over to our side for a little sit-down, friend? We can chat about tomorrow's plans. How does that sound?" the man asked again.
"Then I'll intrude."
Wei He went back to the yard, told Jiang Su, and then followed the middle-aged man to the neighboring mud-walled courtyard. The two yards were separated by just a single wall—very close—so he wasn't worried about any accidents happening due to the distance.
In the center of the yard, a campfire was burning brightly. Wei He, the old man from the caravan, the woman in the black veil, and two young men sat together in a circle. The rest of the caravan members sat around the yard in twos and threes, eating dry rations and sorting through their personal belongings.
Wei He knew nothing about Hidden Sword Gorge, but high skills make one bold, so he simply came over alone to make contact with them.
This way, he could probe them a little and also learn about relevant information.
The leader here was the elderly man named Wang, the same person who had first spoken to Wei He—Wang Zichong.
At this moment, he was carefully putting away a rolled-up map he had just finished organizing, as if he had just been making notes on it.
After putting his things away, he looked up at Wei He.
"Little brother is from a nearby city, right? To dare travel out here with just a few people, you are truly extraordinary."
