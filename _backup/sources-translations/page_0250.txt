Wei He suddenly stopped dead and sprang sideways. Pfft, pfft! Two dull thuds: two throwing knives stuck exactly where he had been standing. A soft sound of surprise came from behind him.
"You can dodge even that?" A dark shape ran out of the woods at speed and stopped by a clump of bushes. Moonlight lit one side of him, and he was a big man with a full beard, holding a heavy chopping knife with silver rings. Wei He turned around and set his two down.
"Who are you?" "This humble one is the Blood Clothes Gang's Grand Protector, Yuan…" The big man never finished. A bad wind came at his head. He dodged left in a hurry, and something cut past his cheek, the force of it stinging his face.
"You—!?" Pfft. A sharp crack burst behind him, as if some powder had exploded there. Before he could turn his head, Wei He stepped out in front of him, crossed several metres in a blink and threw a punch at him.
"Five breaths." "To kill you." In the dark, Wei He's body swelled fast; he gathered his mind and held his breath, and both fists turned black in an instant, the two forces of Five Ridges Palm and Mountain Fist twining like threads around his hands.
He ran the Flying Dragon Art to its limit until the veins stood out all over his face, and the red-cloud flowers on his arms moved and coiled like blood-red tattoos. "Insolent!!" The man shook his blade, his qi and blood churning, force wound about the edge, and he cut forward. But Wei He stopped dead, two metres short of him.
Bang! Carried on by momentum, five grey columns jetted out from behind Wei He and scattered towards the man over everything. "Despicable!!" The big man roared, shut his mouth and eyes, and whirled his blade over and over, trying to fan the poisonous ash back. What a pity: a dark shape flashed.
Wei He came around his side and landed a silent palm on the man's waist and belly. Clang! The palm struck something hard, some kind of body armour by the feel of it. Now the man swung his blade with all his strength, and the edge, plainly poisoned, shone a glistening blue as it came at him in a slice.
Not knowing what poison the man had used, a wound could easily mean death. But Wei He did not fall back; he pressed in, dodged the edge by a hair with a turn of his body, and drove a palm at the man's chest. Bang! In that quick moment the two traded a palm blow.
The big man used his other hand to block Wei He's palm. What a pity: he felt a pain in the centre of that palm at once, raised it, and there was a bleeding hole in it. More than that, the numbness ran from his palm to his forearm; his whole left hand went dead. No poison could do this. Only a man whose force far outstripped his own could.
"You…!?" Shocked and furious, the big man backed off to run. But Wei He flickered, dashed in again. The two met, touched and parted, passing shoulder to shoulder. Wei He shook the blood off his hand and walked quickly away.
Not long after, the Blood Clothes Gang big man's standing body toppled to the ground. His chest had caved in, almost a hollow. It was just then the sixth breath.
