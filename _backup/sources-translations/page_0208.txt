Pff, pff, pff. Suddenly, outside the door of the small shrine, slow heavy footsteps came drifting in, sounding like boots splashing through rainwater. The three of them tensed at once, on their guard, ready for any threat.
The three were ordinary people now, and deep in mountains like these any stranger they met might mean danger. Even some small fierce beast could bring all three of them down. Before long, in their wary, watching sight, a tall, burly figure wrapped head to foot in a black rain-cape came up to the shrine and stood at the doorway without coming in.
"Guan Ye, it's me." A low male voice carried in. At the sound Guan Ye's taut little face thawed all at once; she nearly sprang up. "It really is you! Really! It's really truly you!!"
She was so happy she wanted to rush the door. "I knew I wasn't wrong! That Cheng Shaojiu boy really didn't lie to me!" She was so excited her words came out in tangles. In a desolate place like this, with nothing to lean on, to still meet a friend come to her aid — that might be what they called sending charcoal through the snow. Guan Ye moved too fast, up and out in a couple of steps, but just as she reached the doorway the newcomer came in and blocked her.
Guan Ye, small and fine at one metre sixty, ran straight into Wei He's burly one-metre-ninety frame. Her little face thumped hard into the swelling muscle and bounced back, a little stunned. She did not mind in the least; she laughed three great laughs and pounded Wei He in front of her with all her strength. "You're a good one! A good one indeed!! Hahaha! I'm changing my name to Guan Dachang from today!!"
Once there had been a man who gathered retainers far and wide and treated them with courtesy, and who lent a hand to any man in trouble; later, when trouble came to him, he was rescued by those retainers, and by this he became famous. That man was this world's Lord Mengchang, and his name was Lord Dachang. So Guan Ye now styled herself Guan Dachang, feeling that her outlay had not been wasted after all. Wei He was speechless. He caught her up by the back of her collar with one hand, lifted her whole and set her down inside the shrine, out of the rain.
He stepped into the shrine as well. Guan Qing and Yang Jie rose to their feet too. Guan Qing's face was all confusion, but from his sister's reaction he could see the man was a friend and not an enemy. Only, he knew nothing of the mix of people his sister kept company with, and could only look to Yang Jie for help. Yang Jie recognised Wei He at a glance, and a thread of alarm crossed her eyes.
"So it was Master Wei He who came to our aid!" She cupped one fist in the other and made Wei He a very solemn bow. "Master Wei stepped in, in our hour of peril, out of honour and right. A life-saving grace like this — I, Yang Jie, will remember it!" She did not say the Guan family, because she could not speak for the Guan family. But she could speak for herself. She and the Guan family were only employer and hireling, not sworn dead, and so she said that she would remember.
And Guan Qing, at her side, heard Yang Jie deliberately speak the other man's full name, and understood at once; he too cupped his fists and bowed in earnest. "Thank you for your help, sir. I am Guan Qing, Guan Ye's elder brother; our Guan family will remember this!" "There is no need for thanks." Wei He said gravely. "Guan Ye is a friend of mine. When a friend is in trouble and it lies within my power, I should step in. From the moment word reached me I hurried here, but for all my hurrying, I was still one step too late."
In truth he had arrived very early, shadowing them all the way. At first, when Chen Jun and the other two third-breakthrough masters came out, he knew himself no match and did not show himself. He had lurked and followed in secret the whole way, waiting for the Guan family and the three masters to wear each other down to a mutual ruin and for an opening to appear, and only then struck once at the key moment, letting his own strength tell exactly as far as it should.
On paper he could not beat Chen Jun the Severing Spear head-on — but that was Chen Jun in a normal state, not a Chen Jun poisoned and wounded and run more than ten li at full stretch without a rest. When a man is tired his attention drops, and Chen Jun was no different. And the Guan family had underhand devices in plenty besides, which forced him to hold himself on high alert, strung taut, for a long time.
So, with time, even a man as strong as Chen Jun would have his vigour ebb away without noticing. And when Wei He laid his poison, not only was his mask specially made, he had taken several kinds of antidote pill beforehand and deliberately held his breath — otherwise he would not have needed to retreat more than ten metres at the last.
