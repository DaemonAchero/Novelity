The reward that big man had offered this time was no small sum; if he could get all of it… perhaps he would never have to go hungry day after day again. "Xiao Si, haven't you been watching the back of this courtyard every day? Doesn't that woman ever come out?" he asked. "It's strange these days — the woman hasn't come out at all, and they draw water by their own little path at the back, with a wall along the side of it,
and we don't dare go near. We'd stand out too much…" Xiao Si answered. "Is that so? No hurry. Sooner or later she has to go out for vegetables, for oil… we wait, that's all." Wang Ziyang said viciously. "Brother Yang, I say we'd better drop it… I made enquiries, and this courtyard belongs to a big man too… if we cross him…" the shortest of the little girls could not keep from saying.
"Cross him? So what? I, Wang Ziyang, am going to do big things and make big money! If I can't even manage a little job like this, how am I ever to do the big…" Bang. A stone the size of a fist came flying from somewhere near and struck Wang Ziyang square in the forehead. He went down where he stood, twitched a few times, blood trickling from his hair onto the ground, and passed out.
The other children screamed and turned to run, but the alley mouth at the other end was blocked by several burly men too. Both ways out were sealed. The men all bore the same badge. "Bear's heart and leopard's gall indeed — coming to spy on Steward Wei's house. Do they take us of the One Heart Gang for opera players?" A
bald, burly man charged up and drove a foot into one of the youths, kicking him over hard. "Brother Yang's dead — fight them!!" The rest of the boys and girls went mad, hauling edged things out of their clothes: some had sharpened stones, some rusty scissors, some rough strips of iron. "Knock these whelps down and drag them off." The bald man waved a hand, and the gang members all round him rushed in — fists and feet and staves
doing their work — and in a few movements the weaker boys were all laid out on the ground. Wei He's figure appeared at the alley mouth, and he looked at this pack of half-grown children without expression. That was the way of the world: there was many a mean-hearted brute among the young as well. Did they really think he left his own house unguarded?
Those many little gangs and small outfits he held a post with — did they think he had hung his name on them for fun? A small favour here and there and he could have men arranged to keep an eye on things around his house. Even when Wei Ying went out for vegetables or oil he had men from those gangs watching. A few men from each of several gangs — in times like these, so long as there is food and money, there is never a shortage of men who will
put their lives at risk for a full belly and a bit of advantage. Men like these know every trick of the streets and have nothing to do most of the time, and they turn out far more useful than hired guards. "Master Wei, I saw the little bastard climbing the wall just now and brought him down with a stone. And now these whelps still dare to be lurking here with their schemes against your family — they must be mad, they've lost their wits. Rest easy, Master Wei; I'll deal with these whelps one by one after this."
Wei He nodded. He did not ask how they would be dealt with; he could not be bothered to ask. So long as they were not people he had ties to, the rest he did not care about, and could not manage so much. The bald man had the boys dragged away, and turned to Wei He with his hands folded in salute once more. "Master Wei, that business before the New Year — your stepping out to give a little fellow like me a hand, that was my good fortune. Rest easy: we will keep a close watch on
this place for you, and there will be no more slip-ups!" "Good." Wei He nodded and said no more. "And Master Wei, don't you go feeling sorry for that pack of whelps. They played on other people's soft hearts and tricked a dozen or more to their deaths. They're notorious in the wards round here. That Wang Ziyang at their head has brought ruin on more than one family — the men tricked to death, the women sold off to
the pleasure houses. Their hearts are blacker than we grown men's!" the bald man added. In his hands, that pack of children would not be coming out again. Wei He only nodded and said nothing more. He waved a hand and turned back towards his own courtyard, the bald man and his men bowing low behind him to see him off.
Creeak. The courtyard gate opened slowly. Wei He stepped in, went straight back to his own room, and as usual looked at the skin of his chest.
