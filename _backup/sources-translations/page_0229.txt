Between the different academies, men mostly called one another Senior Brother, out of courtesy, the way some men called every man they met Big Brother. "You are too kind, Senior Brother Cheng." Zhang Lu wasted no words. He stepped up face to face with him, and after each had made the courtesy due before a bout, they set their stances.
"Careful now." Cheng Xi sprang in, his arms drifting and loose as wings, wide and sweeping. That was the way of Bat Fist, made to keep a man guessing and unable to tell where the attack was coming from. A master of Bat Fist struck like a shadow, so that a man could not even tell where he was being hit. But Zhang Lu was not nothing either. Mountain Fist waits for the other man to move and then answers, and not caring what came at him, he drove one straight fist at the chest.
The fist was caught at once. The two went at each other, fierce as could be, and every eye in the place turned to them. …
………… By the Feiye River. Wei He looked darkly at the white-masked man across from him.
"You are of the Incense Gathering Sect!?" He had never imagined that the man in the white mask belonged to the Incense Gathering Sect. When he had questioned men of the sect before, he had found their house badly run and their people changed out for new ones at a great rate; with the Seven Family League of the Inner City pressing them down, he had judged them nothing much.
He had not thought to find an expert so formidable, and so unknown, among them. But then it stood to reason. The Incense Gathering Sect was a great power, one that could stand up to the Seven Family League; if they had not a few experts, how could they have held their own against it? What he could not make out was why the first to come looking for him should be the Incense Gathering Sect.
"You have stepped into the third breakthrough in qi and blood, so you know that from here the weather is different. Only with jin can a man begin to temper his own body. But that is not enough." The masked man spoke flatly. "If you joined our sect, we would pour out to you all the secret transmissions of our Scent-Seeking Jin, and it is more than twice the match of that Return-to-Mountain Jin of your Mountain Fist. The stronger the jin, the stronger the flesh you temper with it. From this moment the gap will widen and widen, until in the end you cannot imagine it."
"I thank you for the kindness, but for now I have no wish to change houses. And if one talk from you were enough to send me running off to pledge myself to a new power, a choice made so lightly, surely you would not think I meant it in earnest. So, with apologies, go back the way you came." Wei He said all this evenly.
"You still do not understand," the masked man said, shaking his head. "But no matter. One day you will come back with me and listen carefully to the sect master's teaching, and before long your heart will turn and you will take refuge in our sect." As he spoke his figure suddenly rushed forward, light as a white leaf falling, and in a breath he was in front of Wei He. Puff! He struck out with one palm. As his qi and blood surged, a faint blood-red mark showed in the centre of his palm.
At the same moment threads of a strange jin spread out from it. Wei He did not hesitate. He brought his Mountain Fist up to the full, both fists black as coal, and met the palm. He was close to blocking it, and then all at once something changed.
Halfway in, the palm turned left, then dropped, then bent right, over and over, tracing in an instant a crooked course no man could read, and like lightning it printed itself lightly on Wei He's chest. "This is Scent-Seeking." The masked man laughed long and loud, drew back, and in the blink of an eye was gone past the far edge of sight. Wei He was left standing there alone, his face dark.
He opened his collar and looked. The iron plate and the layers of ox hide padded over his chest were both pressed deep in. A clear palm print had been driven into the iron. "What a strange force in that palm…" The man's qi and blood, at the burst of it, had been nothing much, the third breakthrough and no more. And yet that one palm…
