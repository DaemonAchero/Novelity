………The sky was barely light. The great courtyard of the Cheng family.
A great banner embroidered with the words Yonghe Escort Agency strained and curled on its pole. Beneath it some twenty escort masters had gathered, all in the same grey-white long coats with grey belts and short sabres at their sides. Cheng Shaojiu stood before the ranks of escort men together with a middle-aged man who wore a small moustache. That middle-aged man was a little shorter than Cheng Shaojiu, but the fingers of both his hands were thick and great — not as if from disease, but rather
as if he had trained some special martial art. He wore escort clothes much like the rest, except that his belt was black. His face was cold and stern, and he cleared his throat. "This one is Cheng Kai, one of the three great escort masters of the Yonghe Agency. Friends on the road have been kind enough to give me face and call me Cheng Iron Hand."
He flexed his palms and tapped them lightly together, and between those palms came the sound of wood striking stone. "Those of you here — no few are old hands, and no few are new. So we keep the old rule: one old hand to one new man. First let me set out the rules of our Yonghe Agency."
He coughed, hawked a great gob of phlegm to the left, and ground it under his foot. "First: on the road, a new man follows the old hand in everything. Second: a new man must be three no's — not impatient, not grabbing, not talking."
"Third: this run is a long one, half a month there and back at the least. The route is fixed in advance. Before anyone leaves,
he must first settle all his affairs. On the way there is to be no correspondence, no leaking of news, and no talking with others at will without leave!" This Great Escort Master Cheng Kai had plainly run escorts for many years; the rules rolled off him in a stream. Wei He stood among the ranks of escort men, listening quietly.
His face was calm and he said nothing, but in his heart he understood these escort rules well and approved of them. No wonder the pay was so good: a single run took half a month, and the farther the road, the more dangerous it grew. With poor pay no one would take the work at all. Wei He understood it clearly.
Soon Cheng Kai up above finished and began assigning old hands to the new men. And he himself, with Cheng Shaojiu leading the way, came over in person. He was soon at Wei He's side. "Little brother Wei He, I will take you myself." Cheng Kai wore a smile, and his manner toward Wei He was very mild.
"I have long heard Shaojiu speak of you. I am out on the road so often there was never a chance to meet; at last I meet the man himself. I am your Third Senior Brother's uncle — by rights you should call me Uncle Cheng, how is that?" Wei He hurried to cup his fist. "Uncle Cheng. I will be relying on your care on this run."
"No matter. All these rules sound like a great deal, but after you have run a few you get used to it. Watch, listen, and learn from me, and with your quickness two or three runs should make you familiar with it." Cheng Kai said it kindly.
"Thank you, Uncle Cheng." Wei He answered at once. Cheng Shaojiu, standing to one side, flicked him a look.
Wei He caught the meaning. The two of them waited until Cheng Kai had said a few more words and stepped away a moment, then quietly went into a corner. There, in the shadow, Cheng Shaojiu took a small bottle from inside his coat. Nothing was written on the yellow porcelain; given a light shake, there was a sound of water within.
"This is the snake-venom-fang water you asked for. I got you a fair amount; if my family were not in the snake-meat trade, there would be no place to lay hands on so much."
"Thank you, Senior Brother!" Wei He thanked him at once. Taking the little porcelain bottle, he now had what the spiked handle he had ordered could be put to use with.
