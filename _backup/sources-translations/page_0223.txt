Red Stone Ward, the Zhang family's restaurant. Zhang Wanruo stared at the ledger before her, her face grim as frost: last month's takings had fallen again. Red Stone Ward's population was shrinking now, and its customers with it. The restaurant could barely go on. She had held out with all her strength, and all it had earned her was one piece of bad news after another.
"Young miss, drink a little of the white-fungus porridge…" The maid Hongmei came over with a bowl of thin gruel. There was white fungus in it, but only just: a single small pale-yellow piece floating in the middle of the broth, with a few grains of rice rising and sinking in it, so that the look of it took the appetite away. Zhang Wanruo put down the ledger and gave a long sigh.
"Hongmei, tell me — if I sold the restaurant and left Feiye City for the provincial capital, to throw ourselves on my uncle, would things be better?"
"Young miss… do not worry. There will surely still be a way." Hongmei comforted her. Selling the business and leaving Feiye City was easy to say, but in a market like this, who would be willing to take on a half-dead restaurant? And even if it were sold and they left Feiye City with gold notes in hand, who could promise that they would get out safely and reach the provincial capital?
Before, the Inner City had always kept up a basic order, and the Zhang family could still hold the situation on their thin guard force. But as the times worsened, the Seven Family League came to care less and less for the outer wards, and its secret war with the Incense Gathering Sect grew ever fiercer and more open. Order in the outer wards came more and more to rest on force. Each ward held its patch chiefly through its martial academies. And so the gangs great and small, who lived by squeezing the people, had instead become the last line of order there was.
Red Stone Ward held its ground on the Xiangzhu Fist yard here. Many a thug, and many an ill intention, held back only for fear of the eye of the Xiangzhu Fist yard, and dared not act openly or do as they pleased.
"Sister, sister!" Suddenly a short youth in a melon-skin cap came dashing through the door, shouting as he ran.
"Good news, just come in!!"
"What good news? Look at you, running so hard — go easy, slow down, mind you do not tire yourself out." Zhang Wanruo caught the youth and, with some distress, wiped the sweat from him.
"It really is good news!" The youth was named Zhang Songhao, her full brother and the Zhang family's future heir.
He said, carried away with delight: "I was playing about with my friends just now when I heard people say that in the Mountain Fist hall over in Stone Bridge Ward, a man has stepped into the Iron-Hide level and become a great expert, of a piece with Master Yao Wu of the Shanchuan Gang!" Yao Wu was the great steward of the Shanchuan Gang, to whom the Zhang family had to pay their monthly tribute, and the true power in that gang, which oversaw five wards' worth of ground.
"How formidable!" Zhang Songhao was so excited he flung his arms and legs about. "Do you know who the man is!?"
Zhang Wanruo and Hongmei both shook their heads, bewildered. Stone Bridge Ward was not familiar ground to them, and the Mountain Fist hall less so. A man who could break through to a level like Yao Wu's, an expert and a personage of that kind, was certainly not one they could have dealings with or know.
"It is the very man you were matched with before, sister! He took a liking to you back then, did he not — he even sent a matchmaker to us! It is him!"
Zhang Songhao clapped his hands together and laughed aloud. "Is it true!?" Zhang Wanruo was startled and came to her feet with a jerk. Her fine eyes went wide; she could hardly believe the news was real.
"Of course it is true! I have asked around everywhere, many times over! Hahaha!! I told them that man had once been matched with my sister, and not one of them would believe me."
"Sister, you need not worry now. He sent a proposal to our door back then, so he must like you very much. Now you need only let your tone soften a little, and he is certain to be my brother-in-law!" Zhang Songhao laughed.
