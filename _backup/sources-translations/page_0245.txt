So she had only joined a couple of days ago… "Go on, then." Wei He waved a hand at the two of them to hurry them off.
He took the iron bars off his arm and set them over the fire as a simple grill. Then he got up and felt about behind where the god statue had once stood, and dug out a small iron pot, some seasonings, and a little wrapped smoked meat.
"You… how do you even have a pot in here!!?" Wei Ying stared at him, at a loss for words. "I came here before and thought the place suited a rest, so I left a little supply here," Wei He said. He had no fewer than five such supply spots around Feiye City.
After all, he had not spent all that time out in the wild for nothing. Often the goods he took off men he had killed were too heavy to carry back to the city, so he hid them on the spot and used them the next time he came. Over time the hidden things piled up. Wei Ying had nothing to say. She could not imagine what her brother had been doing all this while behind her back.
How did hunting take a man this far out? This place was no short distance from Feiye City, and there were still things her brother had hidden here. She was silent for a while. Then Wei Ying took the iron pot, poured in water, put in dried spring onion, pickles and dried meat, tore the grain cakes into pieces and threw them in to boil.
That done, she poked up the fire to make it stronger and looked up at Wei He. "Are you… not worried about Master Zheng?" "I could break out, and Master Zheng is far stronger than me, so he could too," Wei He said calmly. "The Seven Family League's Giant-Tooth soldiers have too big a weakness; they cannot stop people. And all around were ordinary soldiers, no real fighters. So either the real fighters did not come, or they gathered in one spot to surround somebody. Put that together with what the soldiers were shouting, and it is clear: their real target was the Flying Bear Saber. And Master Zheng's opponent is too strong — even with me added, it would make no difference."
Wei Ying had not expected that, out of such confusion, her brother could calmly work out so much, and stay so steady while carrying her at a run to a place this far away. She was shaken inside. She did not know what kind of life her brother led on ordinary days, that he knew all these things so well. Thinking of it, she suddenly ached for him, and felt sad.
Pfft, pfft. A small fluffy yellow bird suddenly flew into the temple. Wei Ying's eyes lit up. "What a cute little… bird." Before the words were out, the little yellow bird's head burst open — Wei He's stone had struck it square on the head — and it fell over sideways.
"This one is rather fat." Wei He got up and picked the bird up. It was just the size of a palm. "After the feathers are off there may not be much meat, but eating it bones and all will fill a meal." "…" Wei Ying had nothing to say. Not long after, the other two came back with dry wood and branches and put them by the fire to dry.
Dried a little, they would smoke much less. "You two stay here and call me when it is cooked. I will go and keep watch outside. And take off any clothes with blood on them and give them to me." Wei He stood up as he said it. The two were not shy about it. Wei Ying had no wounds and no blood, but Ouyang Lin and Zhang Qi both had bloodstains.
The first took off her outer robe, and only then found a cut on her arm. The second had a big patch of skin scraped off his thigh, which he only noticed now. Wei He had Wei Ying tend their wounds, and built a second fire off to the side to burn the clothes. Then he stepped outside the hall.
He raised his arm and took off the outer robe he had fought in for half a day, showing the short jacket underneath, then balled it up, tied it, and threw it hard. The robe tumbled and flew, floating unevenly, and caught on some dry branches not far away. Wei He walked off to one side and waited quietly in hiding, listening for sound. The robe was soaked with blood, all of it other people's, not yet dry, and it would do nicely as bait.
