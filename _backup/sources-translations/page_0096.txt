Only then did the serving woman push the door open and begin quickly tidying the room. At a glance she saw the rather cluttered desk. "Such a mess on this desk; best clear it up." One sweep of her big hand and in a few moves she had gathered up all the crumpled papers and odds and ends on the desk into her grip.
Turning, she was suddenly dazzled: Old Man Zheng, who had only just left, was back again. "Old Man Zheng, you…?"
"I forgot something." Zheng Fugui had come back for something he had left. All at once he reached out and drew Wei He's sheet of paper from the serving woman's hand. "I keep no paper of this kind here. What is this?" Through the crumpled folds he caught a glimpse of writing within.
Zheng Fugui frowned and quickly unfolded it. Line upon line of upright, clear writing met his eye, all of it in Wei He's own hand: a request, now that he had broken through to Stone-Hide, for the drills that followed. The more Zheng Fugui read, the wider his eyes opened.
For he had a strong impression of Wei He, the disciple who last time had broken through to Ox-Hide barely in the final two days. Reckoning it up now, he could work out about how long Wei He had taken to go from Ox-Hide to the Stone-Hide stage. Less than a year! "Truly, less than a year!" Zheng Fugui was startled and delighted at once.
Startled that an excellent young talent should have appeared among his own people without his noticing; delighted that the boy knew enough to hide his light and to write to him quietly. After the Xiao Ran business he was somewhat afraid: if another Xiao Ran affair came about, he would truly want to die. "It is well… it is well." Then Zheng Fugui compared the two in his mind: Wei He's breakthrough time was far behind Xiao Ran's, and so
he would be nowhere near so conspicuous. In former days he might not have felt such relief, but after the Xiao Ran affair he understood: perhaps a man like Wei He was the truly steady sort, with hope of breaking through to Iron-Hide and yet not drawing too much attention.
"Good, good, good!" At the thought Zheng Fugui's heart steadied and all his former confusion was swept away. Recalling the contents of the letter, he could not help shaking his head. That Wei He thought Jiang Su had broken through to Stone-Hide within a year, and that her gifts were much like his own. But he had no idea: Jiang Su had not broken through to Stone-Hide within a year at all. It was that after her qi and blood were complete she had spent a full year
on the pass alone. On the pass, and nothing more! In fact, counting the time she had spent gathering qi and blood, Jiang Su had taken at least a year and a half and more. At the thought Zheng Fugui pressed down his delight, put the letter away, and without a glance at the serving woman turned and left.
First to see to the matter of Xiao Ran, then straight to look in on that boy Wei He! It struck him suddenly that he had perhaps neglected Wei He a little too much before. Could the boy be one of those talents that ripen late? It was already the next day when Wei He received word that Old Man Zheng wanted to see him.
Early the next morning he came respectfully to the door of Zheng Fugui's bedchamber, stood, and waited quietly. The sky had only just lightened, and Old Man Zheng was already up, with a lamp lit on the table by the window. Through the window one could see a figure bent over beneath the lamp, writing hard.
From time to time came the click of abacus beads. Wei He waited patiently. Except for calling out a greeting when he first arrived, he stood still and said nothing the whole time. A full half hour later the day was fully bright and the sun slowly lit the sky.
Creak. Only then did the door slowly open. Zheng Fugui stood in the doorway, and stepped slowly out.
