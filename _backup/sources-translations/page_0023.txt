As he spoke he came a little nearer, and the smile on his face dropped a notch. "Chen Biao is dead. They found him the other day — dead in his own yard." He lowered his voice, with meaning in it. In this age a dead man was a common enough thing, and nobody cared that a small thug had died.
But this Chen Biao was different, and Cheng Shaojiu knew it. So did Wei He.
"Oh." Wei He answered dully.
"And both his followers died with him." Cheng Shaojiu patted Wei He's shoulder, his face complicated.
He had formed a fresh opinion of just how ruthless this junior brother Wei of his was. Only the other day he had told him Chen Biao was hurt; now all three of them were dead. There was no such coincidence. Say it was not Wei He's doing and he would not believe it if you beat him to death. Thinking it over closely, the speed of the man's movement and the weight of his hand sent a chill down Cheng Shaojiu's own back.
Cheng Shaojiu had been in this Mountain Fist yard a long while and seen young men of every stripe, but a ferocity and a will to act like Wei He's he had met for the first time. Luckily there were men of much the same methods in his own family, so he had no particular objection to raise. "This world of ours…" he sighed.
Wei He said nothing. He knew Cheng Shaojiu had guessed it, but there was no proof of a thing like that, and no one could do anything to him.
"Never mind that. Pork has gone up again in the city lately. We who train the fists can't go short of meat — anything but pork will do for eating, of course — but it never sits right, somehow. This dried rat here, for instance." Cheng Shaojiu was back to his usual self in no time, talking of other things.
Wei He listened, and only listened, putting in a word now and then so it would show he was not wool-gathering. The two of them soon turned their attention to other matters. But they had not spoken long when Eldest Senior Brother Zhao Hong came into the yard with a wooden face, leading several women disciples of Mountain Fist.
"Master."
The women disciples were broad-shouldered and thick-waisted, all but one: she had some beauty to her, a slender figure, a full bosom, fine skin. Set beside her, even Wei He's second sister Wei Ying, standing nearby, came off the worse. Of the men working at skin grinding in the yard, most of their eyes were drawn to this one woman disciple.
There was a cleanness about the woman that ordinary poor folk did not have. Wei He noticed the group, too, and found himself looking a few extra times at the one who stood out.
"The women's yard has been broken up and merged in with us, or so they say. Not enough people." Cheng Shaojiu said it low, at his side.
"Third Senior Brother, you have a good ear for word — do you know that best-looking junior sister?" A man came up to them and asked with a grin.
"Know her? Naturally." Cheng Shaojiu nodded. "But don't set your mind on her, any of you. Her name is Jiang Su, second daughter of the Jiang family, who are well known in the ward and far from poor, and her own gift was one of the two best in the women's yard. Only Xiao Ran beats her a little, I'd say. And the chief thing is, this junior sister's temper is not a good one." At that he shook his head, plainly immune to Jiang Su's charms. Wei He listened in silence and said nothing.
All this while he had been quietly trying to find out how his eldest sister and his parents were faring. His eldest sister's side was trouble now: even the whole Black Water Gang was losing people little by little, going further downhill, close to coming apart. As for his parents, the only word he had came from Cheng Shaojiu, and that word was simply that they had indeed gone missing. Beyond that there was nothing he could do.
So his mind was nowhere near the subject of women. What he thought of all day was training his martial skill, and then, once he had a little capital to stand on, quietly searching out where his kin had gone. However things stood, his parents and his eldest sister had given him a great deal of care and support when he first came. He was a man who set no store by blood, only by what people had done for him. Blood he did not care about; this debt of feeling, he had to repay.
"Look at that — everyone who went to talk to her has come back again." Cheng Shaojiu laughed, enjoying it. "Junior sister Jiang is not in the same circle as the likes of us. Best keep your wits about you." Wei He looked over as he heard it.
