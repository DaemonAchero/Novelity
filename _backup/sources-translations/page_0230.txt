In that flash of an exchange he had in truth felt the urge to bring out the Five Ridges Palm and the Flying Dragon Art and finish the man on the spot. In the end he had pretended he could not get clear and taken the palm head-on.
There were two reasons. One, the man's qi and blood had not surged hard, and he had broken off at the lightest touch. Plainly he was testing him, with a hint of a warning and a show of force.
Two, he had killed a good many men of the Incense Gathering Sect with the Five Ridges Palm, and everything over at Shaoyang Mountain had been the Five Ridges Palm and the Flying Dragon Art as well. If word of that got out, and his identity with it, he would be in real trouble. So until he was sure he could kill this man, he would not lightly use his full strength. "Incense Gathering Sect… what a pity… count yourself lucky." Wei He's eyes dropped. He had come within a hair of using those other two arts.
Once he used them, his speed and his strength and his jin would all more than double, and with a dirty trick or two thrown into the burst of it, his chances of finishing the man were not small. Only, who was to say the other had no hidden strength of his own? All at once he came to himself: the ceremony at the Mountain Fist yard would soon begin. He turned at once and hurried for the yard.
………… Mountain Fist, the inner yard.
Bang. Zhang Lu's fist met its match once again. The two had been at it a while now and neither was giving ground; he and this Cheng Xi were much of a strength. But if the two of them were enjoying themselves, the others had begun to grow impatient.
With another dull bang the two parted again, and Zhang Lu was about to say a courteous word or two when a man stepped out of the row into the open ground, from Iron Slaughter Fist. "How long do you two mean to keep this up? Let me have a taste of Mountain Fist's best." He was short and thick-set, with a square face and a sharp eye, and about him there was an edge of keenness the others did not have.
He wore a short jacket of blue cloth, and down the middle of the back of each hand ran a black line that went up into the sleeve on his forearm. "I am Chen Jian, of Iron Slaughter Fist." Having given his name he went straight in with a fist at Zhang Lu. Cheng Xi of Bat Fist could hardly strike again now, and could only step aside, otherwise it would look as though he and Iron Slaughter Fist were setting on Zhang Lu together.
Chen Jian's fists were hard and fierce, every blow met head-on, and he had great strength with it. Iron Slaughter Fist did not burst the way Mountain Fist did, but it pressed a man down by sheer weight. The least advantage, and it came over him in layer upon layer, widening that sliver into a rout. His rhythm was quick, and he gave Zhang Lu no room to breathe; in a dozen exchanges he landed a fist on Zhang Lu's shoulder.
Bang, and he put him on the ground. "You are too good for me." A flicker of disappointment crossed Chen Jian's eyes. The moment he heard there had been a breakthrough at Mountain Fist, he had not waited for his teacher's word but had hurried over on his own, eager for a bout.
If Mountain Fist could bring up a third-breakthrough expert, then their strength had to be something. But as things looked now… as for the fellow in front of him, if he had not been holding back at the start out of caution, five moves would have been enough to put the boy flat on his back. "You are too weak. Send someone better." Chen Jian made no pretence of his contempt.
He was a man mad for martial arts. He had come running over with his hopes up, for this? An opponent who had never fought for real, who had seen pitifully little blood, who struck without committing his strength, hesitating and trailing off, with no edge to him at all. A man like that, turned loose in the wild outside the city, would not last a day if anything went wrong.
