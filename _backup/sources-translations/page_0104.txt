She was plainly still angry. Jiang Yan waved the folding fan in his hand, gave Wei He a faint smile, and turned to leave. Zhao Hong said a few words to encourage Wei He, but his manner was not different in the least — just as usual. It seemed that to him it made no difference whether Wei He had broken through or not.
Cheng Shaojiu clapped Wei He on the shoulder, and the two went out of the yard and got into the carriage outside. "Come on, let us go and drink a cup — you rascal! When did you break through? Why did you not say a word to me?" Only once he was inside the carriage did Cheng Shaojiu show his smile.
"I broke through only just now, and went to Old Man Zheng first." Wei He answered with a smile.
"The Drunken Flower Tower's Ninth Day of the Ninth vintage — today I am taking you to taste it! Call it a celebration for you. Heh, with this, you and I, brother, are slowly growing in weight. Keep on like this and one day Feiye City may well ring with our names." Cheng Shaojiu was plainly in very good spirits.
Wei He was godfather to his daughter, and of all those about him the closest to him was Wei He as well; and this proved that Cheng Shaojiu, in making friends and swearing brotherhoods, had an eye for quality. Take this news back and put it to his uncle, and it would slap him across the face until his cheeks swelled. Had it not been said that Cheng Shaojiu only kept company with worthless cronies? What now? One of his brothers had reached the second breakthrough in qi and blood!
How many men in the whole agency had reached the second breakthrough? Only three: himself, his father, and his uncle. Now let anyone dare say a word about him! Cheng Shaojiu's heart swelled; all the resentment and gloom that had piled up before seemed to pour out at once with Wei He's breakthrough. Wei He sat on the seat opposite, smiling inwardly.
This Brother Cheng was the father of four children already, and still so ill-matched to his years. Before long the carriage rolled slowly into the Inner City and stopped before the Drunken Flower Tower. Sitting in the carriage, Cheng Shaojiu sent a man to reserve a table; learning that there was a queue, he simply sat in the carriage to wait. The carriage edged slowly into a stopping place.
Cheng Shaojiu was rather moved to reflection. "In this Feiye City, every place that can truly be called a place of enjoyment is in the Inner City. And the Inner City does not allow anyone outside the Seven Family League to take up residence there. Even to join the ranks of trade you must apply for a place and a qualification; and those who trade or do business may not live in the Inner City long, but only stay a short while.
"The strictness of the rules is beyond imagining. But it is exactly these rules that let the Seven Family League know every person in the Inner City as plainly as the palm of their hand. Every man who enters the Inner City is clear to them, and at any hour they can find out where he came from and who brought him in. And so the safety of the Inner City is raised enormously."
He pointed at the Drunken Flower Tower beside them. "Take this Drunken Flower Tower. If it stood in the Outer City, the gangs would suck it dry in a moment — it could never come to such prosperity. But standing here in the Inner City, under absolute safety, set against the hand-to-mouth days of the Outer City — tell me, would a man of means rather spend his money out there or in here?"
"In here, naturally." Wei He understood as well.
"That is the way of it." Cheng Shaojiu sighed. "This is the root of why the Seven Family League can rule Feiye City for so many years. This Inner-and-Outer system alone has earned them more than anyone knows. It is really…" He broke off.
"Really what?"
"Really enough to make a man envious."
"…" Wei He had no answer. He had thought the fellow was going to say something striking. "Come on, let us get down and look at the sights about us. You have not yet taken a proper look at this neighbourhood since you came, have you?"
Cheng Shaojiu recalled it suddenly and called Wei He out of the carriage. The two got down together and made a circuit around the Drunken Flower Tower. On the streets to either side, every sort of shop and storefront stood packed together.
