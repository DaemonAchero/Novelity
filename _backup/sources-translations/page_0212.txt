Having drunk his water, he went back into the yard, took out the little leather-bound volume Guan Ye had given him and went carefully over it, committing it to memory. It was all methods for identifying every kind of poison, the cream of Guan Ye's years of study. These past days he had fallen into the habit of going through this thin little booklet every single day, page by page, and from time to time he would pick things out of the pile of medicinal materials stacked in the corner of the yard and try them.
When he had tricked Chen Jun the Severing Spear, what Wei He used was the mixture of poison powder he had got from three places: the mountain bandits, the Shaoyang Sect, and the Guan family. It was so virulently toxic that there was no antidote. At the time Wei He had been afraid the man would not be caught by it, and so he had used every means he had.
In fact that powder would poison a man even without being breathed in, only with weaker signs than when it was inhaled. Wei He had taken three kinds of antidote beforehand and had still nearly been knocked out by it in the aftermath. Luckily a heavy rain had washed all the poison powder off his outer robe, or he truly would not have dared go looking for the three Guan family people.
If it had turned out that the three were not beaten to death but poisoned to death by him, that would have been a fine injustice indeed. After reading a while he heard voices outside: Wei Ying and Ouyang Zhuang. The situation had been growing more and more dangerous lately, so Wei He had asked Ouyang Zhuang to help escort Wei Ying back and forth. The everyday buying of vegetables and rice was done in a single run by the Mountain Fist hall, and once the senior and junior brothers had escorted the goods back, each household's share was delivered to its door.
That too counted as safety. Wei Ying said goodbye to Ouyang Zhuang, opened the door and came in, and saw Wei He sitting in the corner of the yard. "Little He, I bought your favourite black mutton! There are fewer and fewer people in the market these days, and I can't work out why — fewer people buying vegetables and fewer selling them. Even the Tongcheng Gang's people are thinner on the ground."
Wei Ying came in muttering about the things she had seen that day. Wei He went up and took the vegetables and meat from her hands; the vegetable was some nameless wild green he had never seen before, the meat salt-cured. Plainly not fresh, either. But at a time like this, to be able to buy meat at all, to be able to afford meat, was already a luxury.
"I even saw someone selling white-fox grass. That stuff used to be pig feed." Wei Ying said with a sigh. "Being able to eat at all is good enough." Wei He answered. "True enough… the shopkeeper who sells rice says the rice and coarse grain now come out of the Inner City granaries, mostly to supply the Inner City; what's sold in the Outer City is Inner City people putting out their own stock, and nobody knows how long that can hold."
Wei He had nothing to say. That the Inner City held great stores of grain was no surprise; Feiye City had lasted this long precisely because of the several great granaries in the Inner City, and because grain was bought in from Su City besides. "I hear the Inner City has put out a notice — they mean to clean out the whole city before long, sweep every last thing that spreads the plague out of Inner and Outer City alike. They say the plague has started spreading inside the Inner City too." Wei Ying said in a low voice.
Wei He helped her put away the vegetables and meat, sat and talked with her a while, and only then went back to the yard. He wanted to move house too, in truth, but first, he had nowhere to go, and second, he still had the case of his parents' and eldest sister's disappearance to investigate. The investigation had been paused for want of strength enough. He meant to take it up again once he broke through into Mountain Fist, but the situation now seemed to be rotting away further every day.
Thinking of the clash between the Incense Gathering Sect and the Inner City, Wei He found it easy to suspect that this so-called great cleansing was aimed at the Incense Gathering Sect and nothing else.

That the Incense Gathering Sect could hold out so long under the hand of the Inner City's Seven Family League, and still throw up talent after talent, was simply unbelievable. Wei He could not help but think of the Hong Family Fort, which showed no sign of movement at all; he suspected the Hong Family Fort was in fact the power backing the Incense Gathering Sect in secret. Otherwise the Incense Gathering Sect, which did no production of its own, would have no source for all the grain that kept it going.
After resting a while in the yard, Wei He began eating strange-beast meat again. The wild-bear meat he had got from the Incense Gathering Sect side, set beside the wild-bear meat he had got from the Shaoyang Sect before, and with the identifying methods from Guan Ye — he had tested it once more on rats, confirmed there was no poison, and only these last few days had begun to eat it.
