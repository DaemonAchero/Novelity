== Chapter 30 · State of Mind (Part 2) ==
"Incense Gathering people again?" Wei He stirred inwardly, and a murderous light flashed in his eyes.
"Lurking by my house at a time like this — if they are not villains they mean ill. Beat them to death first and talk later!" He turned suddenly and drove one fist into each of the two men's backs. It was a sudden turn and a strike from behind: the two did not know him at all, and hurrying along with their heads down, how could they have looked for a passing stranger to fall on them out of nowhere?
And besides, the two were only ordinary members of the sect, whose reactions were far slower to begin with. There came two muffled thuds, and Wei He knocked one down with one fist and the other with the other, snapping their spines. Their bodies twisted at strange angles and their heads tilted back as they made to scream. Two sharp cracks — Wei He came up and stamped a foot on each face, crushing the scream back into the ground.
His face did not change; his feet ground and turned with force. Two more sharp cracks, and the two made no sound at all. Wei He dragged the bodies quickly to the riverbank outside the wall, threw them into a heap of withered reeds, then took one of the straw hats and put it on his head, and turned to follow the way Xu Wei had gone.
He ran all the way, and calling to mind Xu Wei's strength and quickness just now, he hesitated no longer: he pricked the Realm Breaking Bead at his chest and burst it open. At once a great rush of scalding qi and blood poured into him; his muscles swelled and filled with blood at speed, his skin broke out as if with spots, close and countless, and the backs of his fists slowly took on a grey-white colour, lustreless, like rock. In but a few breaths his frame was a size larger than it had been.
Qi and blood coursed through his whole body, surging and swelling. The killing intent in his eyes deepened and his feet went faster; in a few moves he had plunged into the lanes and alleys and was gone from sight. Xu Wei was leaning with his back to the wall, waiting for his sect brothers to bring the other incense masters and put Wei He to death. All at once he heard a faint sound of footsteps and raised his head.
"Come back already?" he wondered, and looked toward the mouth of the lane. But the very first thing to meet his eye was the powerfully built Wei He striding toward him. He hesitated a little: the man's clothes were like the Wei He of a while ago — common grey trousers and a long grey coat — but his bulk was altogether different, and on his head he wore a wide-brimmed straw hat of the standard sect pattern.
"Might I ask which incense master has come to my aid?" he asked with a cupped-hand salute. The other put a hand to the hat and drew it off downward. In the instant the straw hat uncovered his face.
