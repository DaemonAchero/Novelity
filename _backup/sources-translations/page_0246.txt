They had run a long way with blood on them, and something had very likely followed them. Sure enough. The robe had only just been thrown out when, in the dim light, several pairs of pale green eyes appeared in the dense woods. Three black wolves, coming slowly towards the bundle of cloth.
They closed in from three sides, meaning to hunt it together. Pfft. A stone struck one wolf hard on the forehead. The stone shattered, and the wolf's skull, said to be the hardest of bones, split open too. It dropped where it stood.
The other two turned to run, but it was too late. Two more stones struck their foreheads, as true as the first. All three wolves were dead. Wei He walked over, twisted off several legs on the spot, cut away two rows of loin, and only then turned back to the little hall. This was enough for them to eat. The rest he threw a good distance off: it would draw away other trouble in the night.
== Chapter 82 · Blood and Qi (Part 2) ==
"Run!! Faster!" In the mountain woods at night, the Mountain Fist group fled through the trees in a wretched state. Some had lost a shoe and did not dare stop to pick it up; their hands and legs were scraped and torn, and still they did not dare stop. They could only run on and on behind Jiang Su, who led the way.
From time to time a cold arrow or a hidden weapon flew out from behind them and struck someone down, and they did not get up. A scream, a sound of pain — then it was gone behind them and faded. The group grew smaller and smaller. Jiang Su, carrying Master Zheng on her back, ran for her life, with only one thought in her heart: escape.
They had only just parted from Wei He. The group had meant to split, some to the Hong Family Fort and some to Qingdu Sect's Ink Fort. At the first, some Mountain Fist senior brothers and sisters held posts; the second Master Zheng had spoken of before, so that if there was trouble they could ask Qingdu Sect for help. What a pity. They had not gone even half the way when the men of the Seven Family League were ready and waiting for them, laid out in ambush.
More than that, they had even spotted experts of the Blood Clothes Gang among the pursuers. Just as they were surrounded, Master Zheng came out, led them through the ring and away quickly. But Master Zheng himself was covered in blood and badly hurt; minutes after the breakout he fell unconscious. Jiang Su made her call at once: she took him on her back and ran, leading the fellow disciples with her.
Before long the enemies Master Zheng had frightened off came back and clung to their heels. Jiang Su, running with no road in mind, suddenly remembered something Wei He had once said: going into the wild gave a thin chance of life. She no longer knew where to go. The road to the Hong Family Fort and the road to the Ink Fort were both watched by many men; there was no getting through. The only way was to run flat out in the direction Wei He had spoken of.
The people around her fell one after another. Jiang Su's eyes were full of bloodshot veins as she carried her man and ran with all she had. She did not know how far she had run; she only knew she would run until there was no one left behind her.
