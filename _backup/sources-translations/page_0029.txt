But a true disciple of Mountain Fist was another matter. Wei He stepped away from the tub, and the red on his fists did not fade. The men around who had not yet brought it off, and the men still grinding, looked at him with envy in their eyes.
"How does it feel? Qi and blood up a whole level? Everything feels different?" Cheng Shaojiu came over and clapped
his shoulder.
"Mm. I never thought the gap between getting through and not getting through would be this wide," Wei He said, low.
"That is how it is. Our Mountain Fist level names may sound crude, but what fixes each level is not the hardness of the fist. That is only the outward show."
Cheng Shaojiu explained. "The fist is only a natural by-product of training the body's qi and blood. What is truly formidable is the strong qi and blood that comes with each level."

"Want to go try it out?" Cheng Shaojiu invited. "Come to my house. We keep a training ground of our own."
"Would that be proper?" Wei He hesitated.
"Nothing improper about it. You and I are brothers — what are you talking like that for!" Cheng Shaojiu thumped him hard on the back. "When we break up later, don't run off. Come back with me. Call it a visit!"
"…All right." Wei He knew that an invitation to the house was itself a kind of recognition. After all, though Cheng Shaojiu had been friendly with him before, he had never once invited him home; and now, the moment he broke through, the invitation came. It showed plainly how great the difference was between breaking through and not. After talking with Cheng Shaojiu a while, Wei He went over to his second sister.
"You've worked hard, Xiao He." Wei Ying looked at him smiling, her eyes full of gladness. With their eldest sister gone and their parents missing, this brother was the only thing she had to lean on. And now her brother had done well for himself, and she felt more secure by the day. In a chaotic age like this, what put a heart more at ease than strength?
"It's no hardship." Wei He shook his head. He meant it; he liked this feeling, the feeling of growing a little stronger day by day. In his honest heart he knew that he had never had the qi and blood to break through at all, and that without the Realm Breaking Bead he would have left with his head down like the rest who never made it, not knowing where to scratch a living, living from one day to the next.

When he had a moment he stole a look at the pattern of the Realm Breaking Bead on his chest. The black on it had all faded away, and it had gone back to its original flesh-coloured design.
Filling it again, who knew how long it would take… What a waste. Wei He sighed inwardly. To fill the bead one time he had spent the better part of a year on it, all told. If only he could find something to stand in for golden coin worm meat and speed the filling up.
