The stone was no bigger than a soybean. It fell straight down, hit the ground, and lay still. The wagon train rolled past it, wheel after wheel grinding over it, hoof after hoof stepping on it. Several minutes later, when the last horse's hooves had gone by, the pale yellow stone began to tremble in the soil.
Then the "stone" spread a pair of clear wings, rose into the air, flew a short way, and settled lightly on a long, white fingertip. It was a small pale yellow insect after all.
The one holding it was a white-skinned woman dressed all in black, with a black veil over her face. She wore a big bamboo hat, and the only thing showing under it was her eyes: cold and still, like knife blades.
"From now on, every two hundred breaths, the inside men will leave a small insect, and we can fix the position once."
"Our thanks for the trouble, Miss Zhen." A scholar in blue robes behind her stroked his long beard and spoke softly.
"You give me shelter, I repay the favour. That is all." The woman said it flatly.
"To escape all the way here from the prefectural city — Miss Zhen's skills are truly uncommon. The family heads are already on the road. Yun Tiansheng of the Martial Masters' League is bold beyond belief; he dared to work in secret with the Hong Family Fort and leak the position of the Inner City's underground meat fields. When this is done, there will be a great reward for you." The scholar spoke with his eyes narrowed.
"That won't be needed. I leave the day after tomorrow and won't stay long." The veiled woman said it coldly, and went on, following the direction of the Martial Masters' League's train.
The scholar in blue watched her go. "Five jin masters in the Martial Masters' League, and two of them are our people. Yun Tiansheng, what will you fight us with!" A cold smile came onto his face, and he hurried after the train. The Seven Family League had held the Inner City for decades. How could they look away from the huge territory of the Outer City?
Half the martial masters of the so-called Martial Masters' League had ties of one kind or another to the Seven Family League, and two of those men were hidden pieces they used to keep the Outer City in hand. The joke of it was that Yun Tiansheng knew nothing. He thought that with the Hong Family Fort behind him he could pull out safely and sit untouched in the Martial Masters' Fort.
Now four family heads were coming together. Among the thirteen martial masters, close to half were their inside men, and two of the jin masters were their hidden pieces as well. The whole Martial Masters' League could be torn apart in a moment, if they wished it. At the jin level, four family heads plus two hidden pieces made six, against the three left in the League. Six against three — there was no question how it would end.

Wei He rode his horse, now riding up beside the carriage where Wei Ying sat to check on her, now taking the two men temporarily under him and trotting back and forth along one flank of the train to look things over. In the train, those who knew him whispered among themselves. Clearly the name of this newly risen third-breakthrough man was spreading.
With so many people gathered and nothing to do but walk, gossip was everyone's favourite thing. And what had just happened in the Mountain Fist yard was the gossip everyone wanted most — especially that thick-headed Chen Jian finally getting beaten again. That was what made so many academy disciples so happy.
Chen Jian had been beaten more than once before. The man's backing was deep: he was the son of Chen Shaoya, head of the Shanchuan Gang, a jin master of the Three Gangs and Two Sects, second only to Old Man Yun of the Flying Bear Saber.
So for all his swagger, most people truly did not dare touch him. The strongest men at most gave him a quiet beating. But here was the joke: the man was a simpleton. If you fought him quietly, that suited him fine, because he loved to fight.
You could hurt him as much as you liked, and with his gift he came out of it completely fine. But you did not dare break him.
Chen Shaoya was a jin master in his own right, second only to Old Man Yun, Yun Tiansheng. The Shanchuan Gang's reach in the city was wide too, with eyes everywhere. Whoever crippled his only son would have to fight him to the death. This big man had once said in public: as long as you do not kill him or cripple him, beat him however you like.
