"It's the Giant-Tooth Army!" someone in the crowd nearby could not help crying out, his voice trembling. Wei He too was seeing giants of this absurd a scale for the first time. A build like that would count as gigantism, surely.
He was shaken to the core. Set against these men, his own height of not quite one metre ninety was that of a child. What did a man eat, to grow so huge? Wei He stared without blinking at the column, watched them pass through the middle of the street and vanish from sight; only then did he draw his eyes back, quicken his pace and hurry home.
From Cheng Shaojiu's letter he felt more and more how taut the situation had grown. He would have to make his plans early. "The Giant-Tooth Army… I never thought the Ou family would bring even them out…" In the Mountain Fist yard.
Zheng Fugui stood with his hands clasped behind his back, put the abacus back on the shelf and shut the ledger in front of him. Takings fell lower by the day now, and every time he reckoned the accounts it was a fresh pang; he had simply stopped bothering. "Master, the men of that Giant-Tooth Army are all enormous — how on earth are they raised?"
Wei He stood to one side, waiting for his teacher's answer. Thinking back on the sight just now, he still felt the shock of it. Having seen Hong Daoyuan's presence before, he had wondered then what the Seven Family League could possibly use to hold off Hong Family Fort. Now it seemed the League was no easy meat either: one look at the Giant-Tooth Army and you saw divine strength. That kind of bulk, one swing of a wolf-
tooth club, and you had a slaughterhouse of blood and wind. "The Giant-Tooth Army… is a soldier breed raised from childhood on a kind of strange-beast meat that the Ou family alone has. The soldiers bred so, as you saw, are strong in body and mighty in limb, with divine strength. Give them a little martial training on top and on a battlefield they are slaughtering beasts that nothing can stand against."
Zheng Fugui explained in a low voice. "But there is no need to worry overmuch. Strong as the Giant-Tooth Army is, it cannot beat a master of the third breakthrough in qi and blood. They are slow in their movements; for all their strength, they are no more than live targets. And because of the strange-beast flesh they eat, which carries a certain poison in it, they are generally of a violent temper, extremely quick to anger, and the least
provocation is apt to rob them of their reason. So just take care not to cross weapons with them in narrow places." Zheng Fugui said calmly. "Simply think of them as a pack of second-breakthrough men in the Ou family's service."
"I understand." Wei He nodded. "Right, you go on back now," Zheng Fugui said. "As for the Cheng Shaojiu business, he left a letter with me as well. When fortune runs so, there is nothing to be done about it." Thinking back on Wei He's throwing away a thousand taels of gold, he looked at him with a good deal more warmth than before. That name — the man who throws away a thousand in gold — had invisibly done Wei He a great service in how he was seen.
Everyone hopes that the people round him would, in his hour of need, throw all they have into helping him. Everyone hopes to have such a man at his side; everyone hopes for such a friend. And so, by extension, they feel goodwill towards that whole class of men and wish to draw closer to them. There will surely be those who think nothing of it, but however you slice it, it counts in one's favour.
Wei He went home as he was told and settled back into his ordinary daily round, while quietly beginning to prepare a fallback against a situation that might change at any moment. After the Cheng family left, the days went on as they always had. Only with Cheng Shaojiu gone did Wei He feel the Mountain Fist yard grow colder and emptier still.
The only man he could count a friend was Cheng Shaojiu; now that one man was gone, the yard, bleak as it already was, felt hollow. Time passed. In the blink of an eye another two months had gone by.
