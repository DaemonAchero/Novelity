Stone-Hide: this was the true core of the Mountain Fist yard, its highest tier. It was also the level closest to Old Man Zheng, the one that made him look at a man differently. This tier drew a true difference in kind between a man and the rest of the outer-yard disciples and followers. Wei He suddenly understood something Old Man Zheng had said to him before.
"An elite of the second breakthrough would make a middle-ranking hand even in the Seven Family League. And a middle ranker of the Seven Family League, set outside, is the high layer of a small gang."
"This is bad — Yang Zhou, your little brother is cornered again! It is the Robber Gang!" Suddenly outside the yard a half-grown boy in rags rushed to the gate shouting.
A short-haired youngster training there started in alarm. "Take me to him!" The voice, once it came out, was clear and pleasant — a girl, plainly. Only her face was filthy, her short hair a tangled mess, her body bean-sprout thin with no curves at all; no one could have told she was a girl.
"Where is he?" This girl was a newcomer who had joined the Mountain Fist yard only days ago. Her name was Yang Zhou. Wei He had some vague recollection of her — not because she was a girl, but because this Yang Zhou came from the very same Rat Alley he did, from that row of broken little houses along its edge.
Hearing the noise now, Wei He caught the two words Robber Gang as well. Something stirred faintly in him; he thought suddenly of his eldest sister Wei Chun back then — had she not been like this too, holding up all the weight of the family alone, sheltering her people and her younger brothers and sister? His heart moved, his step paused; he half wanted to go and see. Only, this Yang Zhou was just a newcomer who had barely begun learning technique, and had nothing to do with him.
Other people's business he could not be bothered with either. He lifted his foot again toward the inner rooms. He had taken only a few steps when a burst of angry shouts came from outside the yard — Yang Zhou's voice, and others cursing and bellowing. "They have come to blows outside the yard?" Wei He's step paused, and he turned about and went out of the yard.
Some of the other disciples training there stopped as well, and came out after him to see. This Mountain Fist yard was one of the best-known places in all Stone Bridge Ward — and now someone dared raise a row outside its gate? On the street outside the yard, Yang Zhou and a half-grown boy were fighting like mad, locked with several strong men.
On the ground beside them lay a frail youth, his face beaten puffy, barely breathing. The two thugs ought to have had the upper hand, but Yang Zhou and her companion kept producing one dirty trick after another until the men were fumbling and flustered, and for a moment the two sides were even matched. Just then several more men came hurrying up on the street opposite.
All of them bore the Robber Gang's black mark. The leader's face went cold; he was rolling up his sleeve to step in when he suddenly saw Wei He and the several Mountain Fist disciples standing outside the yard across from him. His face changed slightly, and a companion beside him quickly whispered a few words — it seemed they had recognised Wei He.
"Since Brother Wei is here as well, our apologies — we have disturbed you. We will be going!" Without another word the man saluted with folded fists, went over and pulled his two men away. Led by him, the whole party soon vanished into the depths of the alley. Wei He had not said a word, and the men were already gone from sight.
He was a little startled at heart. He had never thought he had grown so fearsome without knowing it. These Robber Gang fellows looked much like Chen Biao and his sort back then — idlers drifting about the neighbourhood, snatching a little money now, moonlighting as brokers now, ready to do anything at all so long as there was profit in it. And now they ran at the mere sight of him?
Wei He felt a strange pang. Back when he had no master, carrying money and running into Chen Biao and his two companions, he had trembled all over, scared half to death. Now, meeting men of the Robber Gang just the same… without a word spoken, he could make them turn and run.
