"Where is Ou Chen? Does he dare fight!?" "Drought after drought, and your Seven Family League hoards grain and will not let it go. The people cannot live. Today I, Hong Daoyuan, will act for Heaven! I will give Feiye clear skies again!!" His arm swelled; the muscles of his whole body grew quickly, as if blown up with air, and surge after surge of force gathered in his right arm like water and waves.
"Go!!" Shhk! In an instant the long spear vanished from his hand.
The spear split the air with a shriek and flew like a cannonball at the huge name board of Feiye City. "You dare!" A figure leaped down from the wall, long staff in hand, and struck the spear a heavy blow like a falling star. With a huge bang, a burst of sparks lit the air before the wall. The spear was knocked crooked, bent and sent flying wide. The figure used the force to leap back up onto the wall.
"Kill! Hahahahaha!!!" Hong Daoyuan laughed wildly, sprang up from his horse, pulled the black spear off his back and charged at the wall. Behind him a great mass of black-clad soldiers charged the wall too, shields raised. Siege carts rammed the gate one after another. Long ladders were set up one after another.
Grappling ropes and sling stones flew up onto the wall in swarms. Catapults kept throwing huge boulders at the top of the wall. Arrows scattered out and covered the soldiers. Martial experts threw short spears with all their strength, picking off the best men.
== Chapter 84 · Flame (Part 2) ==
The black soldiers, thick as a black ant swarm, charged the grey-armoured Seven Family League soldiers on Feiye City's wall. Fighters and martial experts one after another, weapons whirling, charged the gate — and the gate, which should have been sealed shut, was slowly opening.
"Reborn in paradise, cross the sea by fragrance!!" "Reborn in paradise, cross the sea by fragrance!!" Amid shout after shout, many of the Seven Family League soldiers guarding the gate pulled out grey-white cloth strips, tied them on their arms and roared,
then flung the gate open and attacked the other Seven Family League men on guard. "Destroy the Seven Families, raise Feiye!!" A great mass of black-armoured soldiers with black shields roared it out and poured in through the gate like a spring, taking the place quickly. Qi-and-blood experts of the Hong Family Fort in war armour charged in one after another, killing everyone they saw, sweeping all before them.
The Seven Family League's experts came forward to block them, and the whole scene fell into a general melee. Wei He watched from far off, looking from the hilltop towards Feiye. Firelight there rose to the sky; some building had been set alight again, and one corner of the just-greying sky was dyed red.
He, Jiang Su and Wei Ying stood on a slope in the mountain woods before a stone grave that had just been built. In front of it stood a rough stone tablet reading: The grave of Master Zheng of Mountain Fist. Below, cut fine, were Master Zheng's full name, his achievements in life, and his age. Wei He had cut all of it with his fingers. It looked rough, of course, but the conditions were what they were.
Faint shouts could just be heard in the distance, but neither Wei He nor Jiang Su moved. Wei He quietly set the bundle Old Zheng had brought out on the ground and opened it carefully. Inside were a thick letter, three dark green wooden tablets with no words carved on them, and a blackish lump of meat. He opened the letter, spread the paper out and read closely.
In it Zheng Fugui had written a great long rambling memoir, along with his arrangements for what was to follow his death. It seemed he had long expected that he might not get past this pass. The second half of the letter was about his own origins.
