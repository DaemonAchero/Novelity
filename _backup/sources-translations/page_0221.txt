The girl she had been then had never been through that business with Xiao Ran; without it she would never have come by the understanding she had now, and still less would she have grown so mature.
"Back then you had only just come in the door, and you looked slow and wooden." Jiang Su turned her head to look at Wei He, the corners of her mouth lifting a little.
"I suppose so. What was it you were going to say?" Wei He asked, puzzled.
"Nothing. Only that I feel a few things. Three years, and you are made over from the bone out — nobody would have thought the first of Mountain Fist to break through would be you," Jiang Su said, her voice low.
"I would not have thought it either." Wei He nodded. He was admiring the figure of her walking a little ahead of him — admiring, and no more; Jiang Su was not the kind he liked.
And in a disordered age like this, it was still far from a time when a man could live at peace and rest his strength.
"You are not the same now, though — you are a personage," Jiang Su joked with a smile. And it was not all joking: the standing of a third breakthrough in qi and blood was high, and in every great power of Feiye City such a man was given weight. For an expert of the third breakthrough in qi and blood is formidable alike in the power to move and the power to kill. Had he turned assassin, then even the Guan family, for all their money and all their men, could have done nothing about Chen Jun for years — they could only let him go on killing.
After all, beyond having no third-breakthrough expert of their own, the Guan family had hired no few good second-breakthrough hands. Yet between the second breakthrough and the third, one step though it was, the strength was as far apart as earth from sky. You could say that the Wei He of now, so long as he did not go courting death himself, would never want for money or beauty in the days to come: whichever power he chose to lean on, he would be well kept.
"A personage, is it? That is still a long way off. In a world like this, who can say clearly what the future holds?" Wei He smiled. "All I ask is to be safe."
"You will be safe, certainly. In Feiye City, unless it is a death grudge and a fight to the death, an expert like you is mostly beaten, not killed. After all, one only comes along once in several years — far too rare. You could say that from now on, you and the rest of us are no longer on the same plane," Jiang Su said gravely.
"Do you know?" She reached up and smoothed the hair at her temple. "Why did I choose to practise martial arts in the first place? Why did I work so hard, all to break through my strength? Why did I stop at nothing to train an art like Mountain Fist, that does its own damage to the body?"

"Was it to be strong?" Wei He guessed.
"No." Jiang Su turned her eyes away and walked slowly on. "It is because my father gave my eldest sister to a famous expert as a concubine."
She paused, then went on: "My eldest sister is weak and helpless; whenever she is bullied by her husband's family she can only bear it. The Jiang family's great estates all need that expert behind them for support. So she can only bear it."
"……" Wei He could more or less picture it.
"Eldest Sister doted on me from the time I was small; we are very close. But every time I saw her bullied, and having to go on currying favour with that man besides, my heart hurt past bearing. I told myself that if a day ever came when I reached that level, I would never let my sister be bullied again. Because then everyone would know that she has me, her little sister, at her back!"
Jiang Su's feelings had run away with her for a moment. Wei He could only walk on beside her in silence. Everyone has a story of his own — Jiang Su, Guan Ye — but unless you come closer to a person there is no learning such things. Just as in Jiang Su's eyes his own image was, most likely, no more than that of a poor boy who had broken through on luck, with perhaps a thread of good heart, of goodwill and loyalty, about him, but no more than that.
And who could know the hardship of his slipping out time and again to earn money and strange-beast meat on the quiet?
"Wei He, tell me — if our teacher had betrothed the two of us back then, and I had agreed, what would have come of it?" Jiang Su was silent a while, and in the end still asked it.
