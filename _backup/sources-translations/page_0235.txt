But the Flying Bear Saber set a far higher demand on the state of a man's qi and blood than any other academy's art did. So even with the root diagram understood through and through, he was always that one step short of breaking through. In the whole of Feiye City, he reckoned himself the nearest of anyone to the third breakthrough. And so this visit of his was meant for a true crossing of hands with a master just up into the third breakthrough, to feel out from him the way forward. Those masters who had made their names years ago had qi and blood no longer lively in them; it had settled right down to the bottom and their bodies were long since remade. Only one who had newly broken through could show plainly, drop by drop, the distance and the change from the second breakthrough to the third.
How the second state passed over into the third — that was the answer Zhou Jihan had come looking for. He put off his hesitation, stepped forward and said, "This one is Zhou Jihan of the Flying Bear Saber. I would ask for instruction from you, Brother Wei."
"This one is Hu Zheng'an of Leopard Fist; I would ask for instruction from the senior brother." "This one is He Hong of Through-Back Fist. Pray instruct me, senior brother." Almost at the same moment the other two friends at Zhou Jihan's side stepped forward with him, not a shred of fear in them, and spoke out clear and loud. There was no fright at all in the three men's eyes at what they had just seen; only more eagerness welling up.
"Very well." Wei He pulled off his outer coat without hurry and stood in the close-fitting jacket beneath it. He crossed his arms, raised them, and set them in front of him. When one is no match for an opponent, one wins by surprise, naturally. But when one stands above him, one presses him down with plain and open force.
Make him panic, make him flustered, make him afraid. Leave a shadow in his heart, so that he fears you forever. That is the real point of beating a man. Wei He let out one soft breath. The first opponent was already charging in: Hu Zheng'an of Leopard Fist, one hand hooked into a claw, body sunk low, flying at his throat like a hunting leopard loosed from a bow. Whoo… Wei He's muscles swelled all at once, veins starting up along his skin, and he pointed one hand. Mountain Fist came up from behind and overtook: the fist landed square on Hu Zheng'an's Leopard Fist.
A dull bang, and Hu Zheng'an was a kite torn loose on the wind — all the force in him blocked back on him by the hard collision — and he went down far off and lay there, unable to move. His face changed utterly; he wanted to rise, but his whole body was numb and limp and he could not get up at all. By now the second man, He Hong, had arrived.
His arms reached out loose and long, like an ape's, striking from a distance. The moves came on without a break, and his strength and speed were upright and exact with no opening anywhere — plainly a high, well-practised fist art, and one that had seen real fighting. This was more than one notch above the Chen Jian of before. Wei He blocked with one hand at speed, and took a little longer to look at it.
Only when he had seen out the whole repertoire of Through-Back Fist did he stir his jin at all. "Not bad." He Hong's face changed a little; he was just swinging both arms to repeat the splitting hang he had used before. But his arms hurt, and in a blink his sight swam and he was no longer where he had been standing.
When his wits came back to him, both arms hung limp at his sides and he could not move them at all. "Flying Bear Saber — your turn." Wei He turned the last of his gaze on Zhou Jihan. These men were strong, a notch above Xiao Ran back then, and above Chen Jian just now. Geniuses of their academies, and no wonder.
What they did not yet understand was that the distance between the second breakthrough and the third was not only a matter of how much qi and blood a man had. Zhou Jihan said nothing; the saber came out of its sheath at his back with a rasp, and the light of the blade turned to running water as he cut down at Wei He. That single stroke showed what was meant by the flying bear in Flying Bear Saber.
