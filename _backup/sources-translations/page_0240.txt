Teaching Chen Jian a lesson would have been nothing by itself. The trouble was that while he was being taught, Chen Jian had noticed something wrong. "Big Brother!!" Wei He looked back without a word. A moment ago a fine horse had come galloping up, and on it was none other than Chen Jian of Iron Slaughter Fist, the man he had just beaten in the inner yard.
The moment Chen Jian saw him, he bowed right there on the horse. "Big Brother, that move of yours just now was so good. I don't think I can break it yet!" "…" "Big Brother, give me that finger strike again, the same move as before. I think I could take it one more time!"
"Big Brother, I have a feeling you don't only train Mountain Fist. Your hands look like they have trained palm techniques too." "!?" Wei He had meant to pay this fellow no mind, but the man had put his finger on the point. He reined his horse in gently and turned to look back at Chen Jian. He had cut off this man's chance to break through at the key moment, yet Chen Jian was not angry now. He came over with a firm face, wanting to break the move he had lost to. His temper really was solid.
"You do not blame me?" Wei He asked, surprised. Others would not understand the question, but he knew Chen Jian certainly would.
"No blame. I'm young anyway, only seventeen, and I thought it over afterwards. If I hold still for now, I can go on fixing the flaws." Chen Jian answered quickly. "Otherwise, even if I break through, I would be like the ordinary third-blood people, neither up nor down. Who could I beat?" "…" Wei He was speechless. "Big Brother, tell me what that finger strike means! The force in it is so fierce!" The fellow pushed in close again with an excited face. Wei He waved a hand, too tired to bother with him. Not slapping him again was already more than he deserved, and explaining his own moves? Only a fool would do that.
== Chapter 80 · Leaving the City (Part 2) ==
While Chen Jian pestered Wei He without let-up, Zhou Shun off to the side pretended to chat with Yang Qingye and secretly let another small pale yellow insect fly out.
The little insect fell to the ground like a pebble, and the sound of it was swallowed by all the hooves of oxen and horses. Suddenly Wei He felt something and looked towards Zhou Shun. "What were you doing just now?" He narrowed his eyes, as if he had noticed something.
Zhou Shun's small movements were well hidden, but they came at fixed intervals, and repeated so many times they began to stand out. "N-nothing… nothing…" Zhou Shun's heart jumped. He put on a blank face and pretended to know nothing. "Mm. Take care. Don't let your mind wander." Wei He nodded and turned away. "Oh, yes." Zhou Shun answered in a hurry.
He let out a breath and turned to look elsewhere, pretending to keep watch. In the train there were mostly the families of the disciples; the real disciples of the academies were few. All together there were only about four hundred people. The four hundred were split into two lines, guarding the train along its length and moving slowly forward with the ox carts of baggage.
Zhou Shun relaxed. After a while he reached two fingers into a hidden pouch at his waist and pulled out a pale yellow stone. "What is this?" Wei He's voice came suddenly from beside him. Zhou Shun shook all over with fright and nearly dropped the stone.
"Senior Brother Wei!" He turned his head. Wei He, who had just been up ahead, had come to his side at some point, and was looking at the stone in his hand with interest. "I like to pick up stones and fiddle with them when I have nothing to do. Just a habit. Sorry, Senior Brother Wei." Zhou Shun explained at once.
