Chen Jian had been up Shaoyang Mountain himself once, back then, and those treacherous fat men had very nearly killed him. That trip still made his heart quail at the name of Shaoyang Mountain outside the city: the fat ones had been fearsome in strength to begin with, and their dirty tricks had come one after another with no end to them.
"You—!!" Zhang Lu had never taken such an insult in his life. He could see that the man was just a thick-headed oaf, but to say a thing like that, here, in front of a crowd…
"Zhang Lu, let me." From the Mountain Fist side Jiang Su stepped forward. Zhang Lu had only entered the inner yard recently and his strength was still small. With Wei He absent, Zhao Hong, the strongest disciple they had, could not be the one to step out at once. So she must be the one to go.
Chen Jian shrugged, unconcerned. "Let me say this first: woman or not, I will not go easy on you." "That is fine." Jiang Su was less gifted than Zhang Lu, but she had trained for longer, and in a real fight she was far his better. Zhang Lu had won his breakthrough late and had too little behind him.
With a fresh opponent facing him, the two went straight to blows without a word. The moment Jiang Su stepped in she understood why Zhang Lu had lost so fast. Chen Jian changed his moves quicker than the eye, and his strength was steady with a deep reserve behind it; after a few exchanges of fists her forearms were aching, the muscle unable to hold.
"Can any of you actually fight?" Chen Jian was getting angry. He had not come here to play at house with weaklings; he had come to try fists with the talent who had broken through to the third stage of qi and blood! Did such a disciple exist at Mountain Fist, or not? He changed his move in a flash, went around Jiang Su's arms, and drove a fist into her shoulder that sent her heavily to the ground.
"Can somebody come out who can actually fight? It is either trash or a woman. What is the point of you Mountain Fist people? And this is the second breakthrough? Why not all come at me at once?" He grew more and more irritable.
== Chapter 77 · Bouts (Part 1) ==
Mountain Fist yard. Jiang Su got up from the ground without a word. Teeth set, she would have gone at him again, but Zhao Hong behind her held her back. "I will go," Zhao Hong said seriously.
He had just been watching Chen Jian's moves, his strength and his action, and he was not sure of himself either. The man was certainly born with gifts apart; his stamina was outrageous. Every fist came with his full strength behind it, and still he did not tire. But Wei He was not here, their master's face was growing darker and darker and he said nothing, and all those men of the same trade were watching.
If Iron Slaughter Fist pressed them like this, what was Mountain Fist's name outside the city worth after today? Zhao Hong stood up calmly. He had Zhang Lu take Jiang Su back to the rear, and he walked forward step by step.
By now the disciples of the other academies were frowning and muttering. This showing of Mountain Fist was too weak; it did not look like the bearing of a place that could raise a third-breakthrough expert.
Zhao Hong wasted no more words. He stepped up and stood still, then slowly set the Mountain Fist stance. His breathing was steady, wholly unlike Jiang Su and Zhang Lu before him, and it was plain at a glance that he was a cut stronger. "This one looks more like it." Chen Jian nodded, and a spark of appetite for the fight came slowly into his eyes.
But a short while later came a burst of blows, and Zhao Hong stumbled back several steps. Where his clothes covered his chest there was a red, swollen bruise, and he was tight with pain; plainly Chen Jian had caught him with a straight punch to the chest.
He had not held out long either. Where this thick-head of Iron Slaughter Fist had come from, nobody knew, but his strength was extreme. An ordinary man throwing his full strength might manage a dozen punches or so before he tired. Chen Jian never flagged, one fist after another without pause, his stamina frightening. Add to that his rich experience of real fights and his savage way of fighting… Zhao Hong's face was red. He had held on a few dozen moves at best, and in the end he could hold no longer, and lost.
