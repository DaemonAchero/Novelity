The figure was thin from behind, and where its hands showed the veins stood out on the skin; the fingers were like a chicken's claws, dry and hard and thick with callus. "You are—?" Wei He had barely spoken when the other sprang and flung itself out through the open window, gone from sight in a blink. Wei He's face went cold. In the next instant he sprang out after it, through the window, and ran flat out in pursuit.
Someone had got into his house. Did they take him for a man with no temper at all? Walking in on him like that was a warning: the man could come into his rooms whenever he pleased and kill his family. He had guessed that once his strength was out in the open people would come peering and testing. He had not thought the testing would come so soon. The two of them ran on, one behind the other, through the cold, empty lanes and alleys.
Before long the figure ahead stopped short and stood still on a stretch of Feiye riverbank. To its right the river flowed slowly on. It turned, hands clasped behind its back, and showed a face wearing a white mask. The mask had only the two eyes; all the rest was blank white, and it looked a little frightening.
"This morning word came out of the Mountain Fist yard that another young talent had broken his shackles and set foot on the road of cultivation. My heart is much eased, and I have come for a look at him. I hope you will pardon me." The figure's voice was hoarse, as though he had changed it on purpose. He stood about Wei He's height, and the wind blowing the wide white robe against him showed how thin he was. Only, how did a body that thin keep pace with Wei He?
No. Not keep pace. Wei He's heart went cold. The other had held exactly his own speed the whole way, and that did not mean the man was as fast as he was. It meant the man was faster. He had been holding back to humour him.
"Who are you, really?" Wei He said coldly. "You do not come in by the front door. You go over the wall and through a window. Do you not think that is a want of manners?" "My apologies. My reasons are particular, and it is not convenient for me to use the front door. If I were seen, I am afraid it would bring trouble on you as well," the figure answered. "Oh?" Wei He's heart sank, and he began to guess at who the man might be.
He did not have to guess. Before long the man told him plainly who he was. "Three years, from an ordinary man to the third breakthrough in qi and blood. Your gift is truly startling. It is a pity you never found a famous teacher. I wonder whether you have heard of…"
………… Mountain Fist yard. By now the yard, which had always been so quiet, was filling with people.
There were so many that Master Zheng simply waved a hand and moved it all into the inner yard. The two courtyards were opened into each other through the inner rooms until the whole place could receive guests. The disciples met every visitor with a smile, and Zheng Fugui sat square on the chair at the centre of the inner yard, with Jiang Su and Zhang Lu, his two great disciples, one on either side of him.
Zhao Hong took the rest of the disciples to greet the visitors at the gate. "Dongshan Ward, Bat Fist, arrived!" the announcing disciple called out loudly, so that those inside would know who the visitor was and receive him properly. "Nanshan Ward, Returning Wind Falling Leaf Legs, arrived!"
"Red Stone Ward, Xiangzhu Fist, arrived!" The disciples of the three wards he was friendliest with had all come now, each one an inner-yard disciple, all of them bringing men along to offer congratulations. Zheng Fugui had put a word in with his old fellows to turn out a few people and make the scene a lively one, and to show off just how strong the talent of his yard was.
"Jiujie Ward, Leopard Fist, arrived!" "Lieshan Ward, Iron Slaughter Fist, arrived!" "Baima Ward, Through-Back Fist, arrived!"
