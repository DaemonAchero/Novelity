The young man was utterly bewildered and inwardly bemoaning his fate; the one palm strike had split the bones of his hand, and he needed treatment at once, or the arm would be ruined if it waited too long. But since he had been singled out, he had no choice but to come out. A moment later, with two screams, the yard gate swung open, a figure flashed out at speed, and in the blink of an eye was gone at the end of the street. He had not meant to kill, in fact; but the young man's palm force had been no weak thing, no worse than Xiao Ran's, and so, to be rid of future trouble,
he could only pull it up by the roots. A boy that young with palm force like that — what would he be once he grew? And what if he came looking for revenge? They were enemies already; better to kill him and have done. The night grew deeper still.
Wei He ran fast along the streets, turning over in his mind the scraps of news he had got out of his interrogation. Among them was something concerning the Guan family. The Guan family that Guan Ye belonged to was one of the very first great medicine merchants in all Feiye City. Now they wanted to pull out, and on the face of it there was nothing wrong with that.
But ever since the Guan family surrounded and killed that third-breakthrough master back then, the family had come to think too highly of itself and its business had grown greater and greater. Now, pulling out had stirred the Seven Family League's displeasure, and as the situation shifted, every hidden danger of the past might burst out all at once. From the Incense Gathering Sect side he had learned that the teacher of that third-breakthrough master they had killed — Chen Jun, the Severing Spear — had made it certain he would step forward to settle the grudge.
Chen Jun the Severing Spear was a guest retainer master of the Seven Family League in Feiye City's inner city, a man who had always kept a low profile and disliked showing his face; he was past fifty this year, a figure of the same era as Old Zheng of Mountain Fist. From the Incense Gathering Sect side Wei He had learned that Chen Jun seemed to have quietly found a special method to deal with the Guan family's poison water and poison nets, and that his chances of victory were very good. Wei He hesitated inwardly: this news was all but laid out in the open — the Guan family pulling out, and Chen Jun the Severing Spear letting it be known he would settle the
grudge — it was all being said plainly and openly. 'A pity…' Wei He wavered for a moment; as he was now, against a veteran third-breakthrough master the gap was still very large. His chances of victory were extremely low. He fell silent: the connection between him and Guan Ye was not enough to make him step out against a veteran third-breakthrough master.
'Since the Guan family dares to announce its withdrawal, it should have some countermeasure too. To have stood in Feiye City for so many years, if it could be brought down so easily it would have gone down long ago.' Wei He reasoned it out, and put his mind back on the strange-beast meat and gold notes he had just taken. The gold notes were only a few dozen taels; of strange-beast meat there were over thirty catties of wild-bear meat…
This meat was exactly the same as what the Shaoyang Sect had handed over as payment; it seemed to come from the same place. 'In a few days I'll go out of the city again; it is time I looked carefully into One Ear.' Wei He laid out his itinerary, put on more speed, and following the shadows along the street ran fast, vanishing in a moment into the night.……
