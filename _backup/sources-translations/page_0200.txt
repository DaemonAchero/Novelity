"……" Wei He had no words. All this time he had been guarding against the Incense Gathering Sect coming to his home for revenge. And now it turned out the Incense Gathering Sect lived so wretchedly… what time did they have for revenge…
"Why do you walk the streets at night?" Wei He had meant to say patrolling, but thinking the other might not understand, he put it in simpler, plainer words. After all, most of these Incense Gathering Sect followers probably could not read, and their level of culture was extremely low. "Lots of people… we can smash doors, grab people… take food, and everyone shares…" What the girl said turned Wei He cold inside.
"How strong is your altar master? Can he beat the martial masters around here?" Wei He asked again. "…Don't know… I'm from Su City, I'm eighteen this year, I like pickled radish, there are five of us in my family, my dad, my mum, my little brother, my little sister, my big sister…" That's six people, isn't it?
"The clothes I like are red, and they must have flowers on them. I came here two years ago; my family couldn't feed us, so I had to come out and find food myself. I mean to work two more years, save up more food, and go back. But the people from my village told me my mum starved to death last year. My little sister is lost too. My dad got sick and took my little brother and went off somewhere. The altar master promised me he'd help me look for them…"
Wei He was left dizzy by the flood of 'I's and 'my's, but hearing the rest of it he fell silent. The girl was still murmuring on to herself, saying all sorts of things in a thick Su City accent. He touched the mask on his face to make sure nothing was showing, then, while the girl was still groggy, turned and dashed off, vanishing into the shadows.
The Lecture Hall in Stone Bridge Ward was easy to find. It stood on the right side of the street down the middle. The Lecture Hall had originally been built for the ward's children of school age, a place to study the classics and learn to read and write. The equivalent of a primary school. The whole Lecture Hall was an ordinary, squarely built compound; what set it apart from the yards around it was that its walls were all painted black.
On the outer wall of Stone Bridge Ward each face had a huge 'Virtue' painted on it. On the mud-yellow wall the big black character was very striking, and below it were many crooked childish scribbles: chicks and ducklings, cows and sheep, pigs and dogs, fruit and vegetables, a bit of everything, all of it children's random daubings.
The sky was dark, and the moonlight was hidden behind a thin layer of cloud, giving a dim haze. Wei He came to the side gate, put out a hand to the place where the bar should be, and let out his palm force. Crack — a very faint snapping sound came from the bar. Wei He pushed the gate lightly and walked unhurriedly in.
The yard inside was empty; most of the people seemed to be out parading the streets, and only two thickset men sat dozing on the yard steps. Hearing the gate open, they merely blinked sleepily in that direction. They were not stirred in the least. Probably they took anyone who walked openly in through the gate for one of their own.
And who would set traps at the main gate of their own branch altar, where people came and went all the time? In the middle of the yard stood a great incense burner, on which three very thick brown incense sticks still burned slowly. "Who goes there!?" A man suddenly came out of the inner room. He was thin-faced, but his body was knotted with muscle; he was bare-chested, and on his chest were three incense burn scars in a triangle.
Wei He smiled slightly, his figure like a spectre, and surged suddenly forward, one palm striking at the man. "You're courting death!" The man did not fall back but came on, both hands balled into fists, meeting him head-on. His fists glowed faintly red, his qi and blood seethed and surged, and fine blue veins stood out on his face. Bang!!
The two palms clashed. Without a sound, the man's arms broke, section after section, like dry twigs. He gave a shrill scream and went flying back, crashing into the inner room again and smashing the door in.
