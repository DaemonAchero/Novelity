And Xiao Ran had lain recovering for over half a month, and could just about walk now. When he appeared in the yard now and then he could not train either, and only sat with a dark face watching the others at their practice, his look far from kind.
Another half month passed, and Wei He went on dutifully filling the Realm Breaking Bead while he waited his moment. Luckily he had spent long enough with Cheng Shaojiu to know a few simple drills of the early Stone-Hide stage, so there was no need to hurry at the outset, and little time was lost. The chief thing was that he did not know the full drills of the Stone-Hide stage, and could not well ask. That matter would have to go through Old Man Zheng.
== Chapter 32 · Turn of Events (Part 2) ==
Gradually another half month and more went by. Wei He reckoned up the times front and back: he had been stuck at the pass for two months now, and it was about
time he might quietly let Old Man Zheng know. From the day he began practising Mountain Fist to now: five months to break through to Ox-Hide, nine months to gather qi and blood, and then two months or so to break through to Stone-Hide. Beside Xiao Ran's three months it was far slower, but it was quicker than the other brothers.
It seemed that with the Bead filled, his qi and blood no longer had to be shared out, and gathered far faster. He had heard that Jiang Su had taken over a year to break through to Stone-Hide, which suited him well. Breaking through to Stone-Hide within the year, he ought to be held in more regard than Jiang Su. At last, one day at the noon rest, he went alone toward the room where Old Man Zheng rested.
Then, while Old Man Zheng was out, he slipped a sheet of paper he had written into the crack of the room's window, letting it fall onto the desk below the sill. Since he wanted to keep it quiet he would not say it to his face; once Old Man Zheng saw the paper he would understand his meaning. That done, Wei He slipped away, went back to rest, and waited quietly for good news. After that it would depend on how Old Man Zheng took it: if he cared, he would naturally call him in alone and give him the later drills; if
not, that would be all the more proof that he was unremarkable, and need not fear standing out as Xiao Ran had. Old Man Zheng's full name was Zheng Fugui. Unpleasant as the name was, he had striven and fought his whole life long, and to have come to his present position was no small thing. He had thought to raise up a few good disciples to keep him in his old age, and never imagined that Xiao Ran, on whom he had set his hopes, should have come to such a pass at that fist-off.
A stifled weight lay on Zheng Fugui's heart that would not clear, and he had not rested well these days, so that his spirits were much worse. He was old to begin with, and had taken many hidden hurts when young; now with his mind weary, his whole spirit was not what it had been. It was the noon hour, the sun outside the window bright and the light clear, but in Zheng Fugui's heart all was chill. He was reckoning in his mind who in the yard could keep him in his old age, who had the ability to inherit his Mountain Fist yard and hold together what he had built — and there was
truly no one. The eldest, Zhao Hong, was still stuck at the Stone-Hide stage, not moving at all, his gifts at their limit; at twenty-four now, he could never break through again. And without external training at the Iron-Hide stage, to think of sitting firmly in the Mountain Fist yard was not to be dreamt of.
The second, Xiao Ran — hope had barely come into view, and he did have the potential; in time he might break through to Iron-Hide. But now… this grave wound had hurt not only his body but his constitution, and the time lost with it. Whoever struck the blow was cruel indeed! At the thought of it a knife seemed to turn in Zheng Fugui's heart. But what could he do? He could not even find out who had done it. They said the Tian family — but where was the proof? And even with proof, could he stand against so
great a house as the Tian family? The Seven Family League was one trunk with many branches; touch one and you touched them all. They had ruled Feiye City for so many years, deep-rooted beyond shaking — how could an old man like him move them?
