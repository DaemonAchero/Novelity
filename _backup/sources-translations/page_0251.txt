In the Daoist temple.
Wei Ying held Master Zheng up and fed him hot soup, a little at a time. But Zheng Fugui's lips were white, with hardly any colour left. His whole body shook, his warmth ebbed away, and his eyes and pupils began to drift. Wei He, Jiang Su, Zhang Qi and Ouyang Lin all stood to one side.
"I…" The soup spilled from Zheng Fugui's mouth; he could not swallow it at all, and blood came up from his throat instead and ran down his chest. Jiang Su could not hold it in and wept quietly. Wei He's face was dark as he reached out and gripped Master Zheng's hand.
In his years at Mountain Fist, Zheng Fugui had never done them wrong. Day in, day out, it piles up; no one is a stone or a plant, and no one has no feelings. Seeing Master Zheng like this, both of them were sick at heart. "Do not grieve…" Zheng Fugui said weakly. "The Hong Family Fort, the Seven Family League — none of them wants our Martial Masters' League to live on. What they want is not a whole League, so we had to be broken apart."
He wiped the blood at his mouth and sighed. "Old Man Yun was too sure of himself. He thought the Hong Family Fort cares about its name and would surely come to the rescue. What he did not know is that coming early and coming late are two different things. When I left, there was already firelight in the city… from the Inner City side."
He raised his eyes to Wei He and Jiang Su. "Mountain Fist is finished, but never mind… The one who wounded me so badly, Old Han and I killed him together. You need not avenge me." "Besides that, I hid part of the academy's grain and meat in a deep well at the foot of Shaoyang Mountain. If you want it, go and find it yourselves."
"The Hongs, the Hongs — Hong Daoyuan would never do a thing like this. This time it must be that old fox his father. Afterwards he takes all the ill name on himself, so that with Hong Daoyuan's name for kindness the Hong Family Fort can gather up the leftovers and grow stronger. A fine scheme, a fine mind!" "And the Qingdu Sect — they agreed with me clearly enough earlier, and now they break faith too. A pity I understood it a little too late." He seemed a little better, and spoke more easily.
"Wei He." "Your disciple is here." Wei He answered at once. "I have ten thousand taels of gold, a share in the meat fields, and a scroll of the root diagram. Do you want them?" "…" Wei He was stunned.
"You are the disciple I am proudest of, so these I mean to leave to you alone. The money, I put it…" Zheng Fugui paused. "Put it…" "I do not know either whether it is still there, but that place should not go wrong. I searched a long time before I decided where to put the things."
He sighed. "A pity my daughter is no good, and my son-in-law is a wastrel too. Now that I am like this, I do not know whether they are living well." "Teacher…" Wei He wanted to ask about the money. "You need not comfort me. I am all right." Zheng Fugui raised a hand and sighed.
"When a man is old, there are always things he cannot see through. When I was young I was wild and gifted, I looked down on everything, and I was flying high in the academy. Now, old, what is left of all that?" "Teacher…" "Do not worry; I am all right, only full of feeling. When you are old, you will be the same as me.
"After this trip, I mean to find a place and rest properly. With the body hurt like this, it will be hard from now on. But the foundation is still there; I can live as an ordinary man well enough." Zheng Fugui sat up, took his meat soup and drank it off in one go.
