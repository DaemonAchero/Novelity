Among them, Old Man Yun of the Flying Bear Saber was the most ferocious of all. Before Hong Daoyuan and Ou Chen rose to fame, he had even given them pointers. People said that in and around Feiye City, apart from Hong Daoyuan and Ou Chen, the strongest man was this Old Man Yun of the Flying Bear Saber. That was no idle talk. Add the dozen or so other martial masters gathered around him, and the Martial Masters' League had become the worst headache the Seven Family League had.
When coaxing them failed, the Seven Family League changed its way and moved to drive them out. The Martial Masters' League had in fact begun its preparations long ago; this time was only the open announcement. In just two days, thirteen martial academies joined together into one long wagon train and crawled slowly out of the Outer City. It was a sign to everyone: the whole Outer City of Feiye City had now fallen into the Inner City's hands.
Inside the long train, by the afternoon the heat had eased. Wei He rode a horse, going slowly with two disciples from other academies, guarding the right flank of the train.
Of the thirteen academies in the League, five martial masters had entered the jin. The rest were third-breakthrough experts, but most of those were old, their qi and blood fading, their strength much fallen. The truly tough young third-breakthrough men in the League were only five, and one of them was Wei He, fresh from breaking through. The five were spread out along the train, each leading men to guard a part of it.
The few experts riding with Wei He came from other academies, all second-breakthrough elite. The wagon train stretched on without end, heading for a stretch of wasteland west of Shaoyang Mountain. Master Zheng said their League had long ago built a way out there: the Martial Masters' Fort, a big earth fort the thirteen academies had raised together. It could hold several thousand people, with water around it and good fields to farm.
"Senior Brother Wei, do you think we can get through this trip safely?" Riding with Wei He was a young woman disciple from Returning Wind Falling Leaf Legs, named Yang Qingye. Her family was a scholarly one, and she was far better read than the others. This time the Returning Wind Falling Leaf Legs side had pushed her onto Wei He.
Not only her. There was also Zhou Shun, a young man from Bat Fist. Both of them were put with Wei He for the same reason: their academies were poor and weak, with a gap in their ranks and no strong third-breakthrough master, and their own masters had other work to do. So they had pushed their most precious disciples onto Wei He, asking him to look after them.
It was not only Wei He. The other young third-breakthrough experts had people stuffed into their care the same way.
"Safe… no." Wei He's brows drew together. He did not believe the Martial Masters' League could walk out of the Outer City so easily. "If I were the Seven Family League, I would not let the League go this easily either. A force this big, let go so easily? There must be some other plan in it."
"That is what we all think. We just do not know what means the Inner City will use." Yang Qingye nodded too. She was the youngest junior sister of Returning Wind Falling Leaf Legs, and gifted as well, which was why she had been sent to Wei He's side for his protection. "Don't worry. My master told me that before we left the city, our League had already settled matters with the Hong Family Fort. The Seven Family League won't dare stir up trouble outside the city." Zhou Shun said it lightly from one side.
"I heard that too. And even without the Hong Family Fort, our League has five jin masters. Even the Seven Family League would not dare to start a fight for nothing." Yang Qingye had faith in that. "I talked it over with some fellow disciples. We think the most likely thing is that they will use other means, small warnings from the side." Wei He had no answer to that.
He was on a horse, and it was the first time in his life. It bounced and bounced, until his backside ached. Luckily his third breakthrough had rebuilt his body; the muscles there were strong, and this low sort of harm meant nothing to him. So a man who could not ride could still sit steady as a mountain. Wei He listened as he rode, feeling out the movement around them, watching all four sides warily.
What means the Seven Family League would use, nobody knew. The only thing he could do was stay careful. But what he never noticed was this: while Zhou Shun pretended to talk with Yang Qingye, and pretended to scratch his head, he used the motion to bring his other hand along the horse's belly and let a pale yellow stone fall softly to the ground.
