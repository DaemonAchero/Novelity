Bang!!! He thrust out both palms and met Chen Jun the Severing Spear head-on as he burst from the trees. A ring of fine grey dust burst out between them. "Quick, go!!" Guan Qing was the first to come to his senses, and hauling his sister and Yang Jie, hurried away.
Whoever that man was, this was the great chance to get away. The three of them stumbled off at speed into the distance and soon vanished slowly into the thick woods. "Who are you, sir? Now that the Guan family is beaten, why come and wade into these muddy waters?" Chen Jun stepped back, watching the man opposite with all his attention. That palm just now had been ordinary enough, but it too was third-breakthrough strength; the crux was that he himself was in poor condition, poisoned and wounded,
and to trade blows with a master of his own rank now might well let those two Guan family people escape. "…" The black-clad man bowed slowly, both palms one before the other, and took up a starting stance. "You're courting death!" Chen Jun's eyes glared wide, the chain-spear came out, and bit at the other like a venomous snake.
In an instant the two were locked together again. Bang! Bang! Bang!!
Only, for some reason, every clash between them shook out a ring of dust. There was only so much dust on a garment — where did all this dust come from? As the fight wore on Chen Jun began to feel suffocated; if he were in full strength it would be one thing, but with poison wounds on him his qi and blood moved sluggishly and a third of his strength was gone.
And there was something not right about that dust besides, so he had no wish to keep fighting; he pulled away and shot out to one side, meaning to slip past the black-clad man and go on chasing the three Guan family people. But his way was blocked again as the black-clad man flashed across. The two were locked again, spear shaft and palm striking hard, and another small ring of dust burst out.
"How is there dust again?" Chen Jun felt at once that something was wrong. He had put no force into it at all just now — he had only blocked the other's attack. Doubting in his heart, he used a feint, sweeping the spear tip across at the other. The move looked heavy and powerful, but was really light and hollow, with no weight in it.
Bang! The black-clad man's palm came again. After the muffled report another ring of fine dust burst out. This time Chen Jun saw it clearly: that sound had actually been made by the other man's mouth!
He had not even used force, the spear shaft had never touched the man — where did the dust come from?? It was simply that this man dubbed the sound with his mouth while scattering it! "You…!!?" He gaped, never having seen a man so utterly shameless. Thinking back over their exchanges, several times a bang had been out of the question, and yet the same sound had come out every time.
Now it seemed it had all been this man's mouth making the sound! "What on earth are you…!!?" Chen Jun sensed that this was bad, but alas it was too late. Suddenly his legs went weak, his head swam, and his qi and blood ran wild — he had been poisoned without ever knowing it. And not with any ordinary poison!
Wei He retreated more than ten metres at speed, clear of the drifting poison powder, and only then stood with his hands behind his back and let out a long sigh. "Today's fight has been thoroughly satisfying. A pity the senior is still one notch short in skill." "You!!!" Chen Jun was already choking with resentment, and hearing that he nearly could not draw another breath. He pointed at Wei He, lurched several steps forward, and went down on his side with a thud.
Having taken two poisons, and run at full stretch so far, and traded blows with Wei He, taking poison again and again, which only sped up his qi and blood — to hold out this long was already remarkable. And after going down Chen Jun did not even die, but only shook and twitched all over, unable to move for the moment.
