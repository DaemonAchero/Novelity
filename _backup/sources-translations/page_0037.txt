By now the escort train had come unhurried and steady through the city gate and was rolling out along the grey government road toward the far country. Wei He took the chance to walk at the edge of the train and look about beyond the walls.
Outside Feiye City. Close by the city wall lay tracts of field cut into plots, and far off among them you could see farmers driving oxen at the plough.
The fields were like bean curd cut into squares, one after another, wrapping around the government road far out to the very end of the horizon. Beyond the fields lay dense hill woods, and beyond the hills a range of mountains without end. The agency's old escort master Chen Shiniu walked on Wei He's right; he was the veteran assigned to show him the ropes.
Seeing the fresh, wide-eyed look on Wei He's face, he smiled. "Thought the whole world outside the walls was dangerous, did you? Didn't dare to step out the gate?"
"Something like that." Wei He nodded; he was a little surprised, in truth.
"Outside is dangerous enough, but not here. Within ten li of the wall, anyway, it is safe. Past ten li — heh."
Chen Shiniu narrowed his eyes at the road ahead, the wrinkles of his face swaying and jiggling loose with every step he took. "When I was young and went on my first escort run, I thought just as you do. Robbers and mounted bandits all over outside, wild beasts and poisonous vermin everywhere, dangerous beyond words!"
"But then I came out and found out: whether a place is dangerous or not all comes down to who you are."
"Uncle Chen, what do you mean, who you are?" Wei He asked when he did not understand.
"Take us. The Changfeng Agency is one of the three great agencies of Feiye City. We have squared things with every stretch of road out here, and drunk loyalty wine with plenty of great families and gang bosses. Everyone gives us a measure of face. So on this road the main thing to fear is wild beasts and vermin; guard against those and it is easy enough."
Chen Shiniu smiled. "And these fields round about — do not think they are all worked by these farmers for themselves. Nine parts in ten of them do not belong to the farmers at all."
"Then whose…?" Wei He asked, following it up.
"The lords of the Inner City." Chen Shiniu answered, his face wearing a sort of envy he could not hide.
"The Inner City — now that is a good place. Every pleasure, every fine wine and good tobacco, everything a man could want. Only, everything costs money."
"And these days money is not worth much either, I say." One of the escort men came over and shook his head. "Last year a tael of silver still bought a jar of goat-bone wine. Now a tael buys half a jar."
"Isn't that the truth. The coin gets worse by the year." Another escort man sighed along with him.
"That is why the master pays part of the wages in rice and meat now, so you lot wouldn't grumble — isn't that so?" Chen Shiniu laughed.
"The master is generous."
"Quite so, quite so, the master is a decent man." At once there was a chorus of praise for the Changfeng Agency all round.
Wei He listened in silence and said no more, only watching. All the while he kept a degree of wariness over his whole body, watching the country around him. To meet whatever this escort run brought, he had slipped a hard wooden board into his coat at chest and back, to guard against arrows and thrown stones from a distance. So long as the blow did not land on a vital spot on the head, the boards would cushion it elsewhere and cut his chances of injury a good deal.
The escort train went on. Being a short run, it could go and come back in a single day; the whole distance was no more than ten li, right up to the edge of the city's defence line. Wei He stayed wary the whole way and never once had a chance to use it. Out here everything was peaceful. On the government road you saw parties of rich young men out for a day in the country every so often.
And now and then patrol soldiers of the city passed by, leading their men. The former were well enough, no different from what one saw inside the walls. But the patrol soldiers made Wei He fall silent. Every one of them was sallow and thin, walking without a scrap of strength to him; some had weapons in their hands, and some carried nothing at all. They wore a yellow under-shirt with a shabby, crude grey leather cuirass over it that covered only chest and back, and the rest
no more. Wei He even saw that some of the patrol soldiers wore shoes with their toes poking out of torn cloth! Only the squad leaders leading them were dressed a little more decently, and even that was very little.
