== Chapter 43 · Up the Mountain (Part 1) ==
Gu-gu. A strange sound came drifting over. Wei He checked his step and looked up at the gloomy woods.
Far off a pair of pale-green eyes watched him and were gone in a flash. Wei He swallowed, drew his gaze back, and fixed his attention on the ground at his feet. The ground was stretch after stretch of dry yellowed grass with fallen leaves and twigs, and vines mixed in here and there. He pressed the grass ahead of him down with his curved knife, carefully, so that the thorns and saw-grass hidden in it would not cut
him. In woods as dense as these, once a man was cut and a smell of blood got out, troublesome hunters would surely be drawn to it. He hacked through nettles and brambles and slowly kept his guard up all around. Wei He had come this time to get used to hunting by degrees; as for whether the Shaoyang Sect's affairs had anything to do with his family's disappearance,
he was in no hurry. Even if he found out now, he would have no way to take revenge. Better, then, to get familiar with hunting first, to try killing rare birds and strange beasts and raise his strength, and only then take this business in hand. Of course, if a chance to look into it came his way along the road, that too would count as an unexpected joy.
Pu! A small sound burst suddenly out of the grass ahead. Wei He gathered his mind in, slowed his feet, and listened closely. Then all at once he stopped, and with the chopping knife in his hand gently parted the grass ahead of him on the right.
He moved very, very lightly, making almost no sound at all; beside the sound of the wind moving the grass around him it was nothing worth mentioning, lost within it. On the other side of that grass, a huge green-black python as thick as a man's arm had a black rat in its jaws and was slowly shaking it down its throat whole.
Wei He's face did not change; the chopping knife in his hand rose slowly. Shua! His burst power and his strength and speed at the second breakthrough of qi and blood far surpassed ordinary men. Return-to-Mountain Fist amplified burst power alone and set no particular store by anything else, but against an ordinary fierce beast
even that stroke of Wei He's could not be seen. A grey shadow flickered: the chopping knife flew out, the python dodged like lightning, the two happening at once — and yet the python was a beat too slow.
Pu. The chopping knife took the python squarely in the neck below the head. Not the seven-inch. A snake's seven-inch is a hold only a professional snake-handler can work; it is the key joint of the snake's cervical spine, and once gripped, the snake goes docile. That skill, though, he would leave alone.
As for the saying that to strike a snake you strike the seven-inch, in the teaching of the old professional hunter Ding Hai it was no more than a mistake passed around. A snake is spine from end to end; break it anywhere and the snake dies. The seven-inch is generally used only to control a snake. To kill one outright, the fastest place is the head. And one thing more: meet a snake and find you cannot beat it, and the first thing is to flee up the slope — a snake climbs slowly and comes down a slope like lightning, so…
Wei He looked at the python, which that one stroke had all but hacked into two lengths. It lay on the ground bleeding without stop, twisting and struggling, as though it still hoped to live a while longer. But Wei He stepped up and drove a hard stamp of his foot down on its head.
