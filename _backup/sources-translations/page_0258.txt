Under the pitying eyes of the two women, the two bear cubs scrambled out of the cave and bolted the moment they hit the open ground. They fled as if terrified of ever seeing Wei He again, their paws splashing through the wet grass before they vanished into the dense woods.
Wei He stood up, pulled out a chunk of dried giant bear dung, and crumbled a handful near the mouth of the cave.
"I'm stepping out for a bit. Wait here till I get back."
"How long will you be?" Wei Ying asked, her voice laced with anxiety.
"I'll be back when the rain stops." Without turning around, Wei He dragged the heavy rocks and thorny bushes he used as camouflage across the cave mouth, sealing it tight. He flipped up his thick fur hood and slipped into the shadows of the forest.
A light drizzle drifted through the canopy. The downpour from earlier had brought a grim sort of life back to the woods. Heavy raindrops pooled on broad leaves before dripping down, slapping randomly against the muddy forest floor.
Activating his Flying Dragon Technique, Wei He swept through the woods in a wide arc around the cave, making sure no predators or venomous beasts were lurking nearby. But he hadn't even gone a few hundred meters when a faint rustle caught his ear.
He froze. Stepping onto a thick trunk, he scampered up into the higher branches and flattened his body against a heavy bough. He pulled his camouflage cloak over his head. Instantly, he melted into the dark leaves.
A few moments later, several shadowy figures darted through the underbrush below, moving fast and showing no signs of stopping. In the pitch black, Wei He couldn't tell who they were, but he didn't care. In fact, seeing people out here was a good sign. It proved he was heading in the right direction.
Once the shadows disappeared, Wei He climbed down and pushed deeper into the woods.
Of his three martial arts, the Five Peaks Palm was still progressing at a crawl, while his Mountain Returning Fist was completely stalled out. Both techniques focused on cultivating inner force—a agonizing process that required spreading force through every inch of the body before a true breakthrough could happen.
The process was ridiculously slow. Back in Feiye City, not a single martial artist had ever tapped into inner force before the age of thirty. Even Hong Daoyuan, a prodigy with a monstrous physique, hadn't broken through until he was twenty-five, and rumors said he had taken a shortcut to get there.
Even with the twenty extra years of life granted by three bloodline breakthroughs, time was still a luxury a martial artist couldn't afford to waste.
His only real shot at immediate progress was the Flying Dragon Technique. He had been using it constantly lately, and he could feel himself closing in on the third level. He was right on the verge of maxing out the second layer.
Once he stabilized his baseline, he could use the Breakthrough Bead to force the next level. Wei He wanted to test something this time: whether breaking through a low-tier martial art would consume a Breakthrough Bead just like a high-tier one did.
He moved silently through the dark forest.
Suddenly, he stopped in his tracks. He caught the faint sound of breathing.
It was a strange, long, deep breath that drifted in and out of earshot, making it impossible to pinpoint where it was coming from.
Wei He stepped behind a thick tree trunk. He checked the bark for venomous snakes or bugs, then pressed his back flush against the wood. Reaching into his waist pouch, he silently slipped a iron dart into his grip.
Peering carefully around the edge of the trunk, he scanned the dark woods behind the tree.
== Chapter 86 · On the Road (Part 2) ==
The rain grew lighter, and pale moonlight broke through the clouds, casting a cold glow across the forest.
On a patch of wet grass behind the tree, right beside a thick, gnarled root, two massive birds covered in scruffy gray feathers were crouched over a mutilated human corpse, pecking aggressively at the rotting flesh.
The birds each had three legs. When they flexed their wings, the span reached over three meters wide. Their heads were a dull red, and their faces looked unnaturally human, except for a sharp, hooked beak.
Wei He's heart leaped into his throat. From just ten meters away, his first thought was that two men wearing feather cloaks were eating a dead body.
He frantically searched his memory, digging through all the beast records he had studied over the past few years, until a match finally clicked.
The Desolation Birds...
Wei He held his breath, his knuckles turning white around his dart.
This species was brutal, but what made them truly lethal was that their talons and beak were coated in deadly poison. They were a nightmare to fight. Their favorite way to hunt was to deliver a single, lightning-fast scratch or peck, then simply fall back and wait for their prey to die.
