== Chapter 68 · Crushing Defeat (Part 2) ==
The Guan family convoy went on and on, and soon left the city gate, gradually putting five li between itself and the city. But barely past those five li, on the great road stood three burly figures, standing straight. One of them was none other than Chen Jun the Severing Spear. The qi and blood of the three joined into one, swelling and circulating, giving off an aura and a pressure utterly unlike other men.
More to the point, behind the three, planted densely in the earth, was rank upon rank of metal javelins. The convoy slowly halted. Through the gap in the carriage curtain Guan Ye saw the Severing Spear Chen Jun blocking the way, and she quickly recognised the two men behind Chen Jun. "Tiger-Gate Hands and the Three-Element Wind-Chasing Saber!?" Guan Ye's fair face changed colour at once.
The Guan family's poison-water formation and heavy-shield formation, which she had designed, could at most deal with two third-breakthrough masters; a third… they had never considered that possibility! The Guan family, strong as it was, was only merchants, not an army or a warlord. If they built too strong a force of sworn dead, the Seven Family League would never permit it. But now… three third-breakthrough masters striking together was no less than a great power throwing all its strength against the Guan family.
It was not only Guan Ye whose face changed; Guan Qing and the two head managers changed colour at once as well. Dealing with two third-breakthrough masters was hard enough, and now there were three… "Move. Today, of all the Guan family, not one is to be left alive!" Chen Jun pulled a javelin from his back with a backhand, aimed. Whoosh!!
………… Half an hour later.
Thick black clouds, and thunder and lightning at last beginning to roll slowly across the sky. Eight or nine li from Feiye City, in a stretch of wild hill and wilderness. Chen Jun the Severing Spear, breathing hard, sprang up and gave chase to the several Guan family people fleeing ahead. Guan Ye and the others galloped on their horses, and the rest of the carriages and riders had long since been shattered and scattered by the first charge of the three masters.
The heavy-shield formation and the poison-water formation had been pinned and held off at long range by the other two masters with specially made javelins. The ablest guards at her side had been stopped one after another by the disciples Chen Jun had called in, and were fighting them now. Everything she had taken pride in was peeled away layer by layer, like an onion, leaving only herself, alone and without help. The formation that could have dealt with two third-breakthrough masters was, against a foe beyond its limit, no match at all.
The horses fled on, and had it not been that Chen Jun himself had been caught by a trap and carried poison wounds, they would surely have been overtaken long before. Suddenly one warhorse dropped to its knees and went down with a thud, having broken both legs in the fall. Guan Ye and the others, caught unawares, were thrown down in the crash. Horse and human shrieks and groans mingled, and Guan Qing, saying not a word, rolled up to his feet, seized his sister by the arm and led the others
at a run towards the side of the mountain. On the mountain the woods were gloomy, dark, nothing to be seen, which was just right for hiding. The sky was covered in black cloud and a wild wind rose, the tension and oppression thickening. Chen Jun came step by step, chain-spear in hand, to the fallen horse that still whinnied, and put it out of its misery with a casual thrust.
One sweep of his eyes found the footprints Guan Qing and the others had left. "Without horses, let me see how far you run!" In his eyes there was anticipation, and relish, and remembrance.
