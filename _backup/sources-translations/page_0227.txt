"Yunsu Ward, Flying Bear Saber, arrived!" Up to that point Zheng Fugui had only stroked his beard and smiled, betraying nothing. But when he took in the last name his face changed a little and his eyes narrowed slowly. In the Martial Masters' League, the name Flying Bear Saber carried a weight all its own. It was no ordinary academy.
Flying Bear Saber's strength was nearly a third of the whole Outer City league. Old Man Yun, the master of Flying Bear Saber, was the strongest martial master in Feiye City, and under him were his Two Tigers, Yun Daochun and Zhou Lai, both third-breakthrough experts trained by his own hand and both already holding high office in the Three Gangs and Two Sects. Of course, a small affair like this would hardly bring Flying Bear Saber's two long-standing third-breakthrough experts out to offer congratulations.
Mountain Fist, plainly put, did not have that kind of standing. And those men already held high posts and were great men of the city, busy with a hundred things; who had the time to turn out and watch a junior who had only just broken through? But it made no difference. Even if it was only a second-breakthrough disciple, that disciple was Flying Bear Saber's man.
The smile on Zheng Fugui's face paused, then took on a shade of satisfaction. Year after year Mountain Fist placed middling to low in the academy bouts, and so what? Of the academies whose arts beat his, at least half had never turned out a third-breakthrough expert either. In all the dozen-odd wards of the Outer City, the academies that had ever brought up a third-breakthrough expert could be counted on the fingers. And now even Flying Bear Saber, who had barely had anything to do with him, had sent someone to congratulate him. So Zheng Fugui was not to be sneered at! So what if he could not beat them? It did not matter. His disciples could beat their disciples, and that was enough.
Soon the representatives of one academy after another were led into the inner yard. They took their places in two rows, left and right, and it made a fine show of strength. It felt a little like the times Zheng Fugui had gone to Flying Bear Saber to watch their ceremonies. Seven academies had come out of a dozen-odd, three of them friends of his invited along to fill the scene, and the other four uninvited. At least every one of them had brought a gift.
Zheng Fugui's eyes went from one representative to the next. Bat Fist, Xiangzhu Fist and Returning Wind Falling Leaf Legs all wore smiles and were mild and courteous; they were friends. Their disciples were inner-yard men he knew, not the strongest and not the weakest, but just right. The other four were a different matter.
Leopard Fist, Iron Slaughter Fist and Through-Back Fist all looked, one way or another, to the Flying Bear Saber party to lead them. And the man Flying Bear Saber had sent was none other than the young talent everyone had been talking about, Zhou Jihan. Zhou Jihan was past eighteen, a year younger than Wei He, and he had a fine outward show of a man: sword-straight brows, star-bright eyes, a straight tall body and a steady air, with a shade of pride lurking between the brows and eyes.
To his right and left were Hu Zheng'an of Leopard Fist and He Hong of Through-Back Fist, one tall and one fat, both small talents of some name in the city. The three of them seemed to be a circle of their own around Zhou Jihan, as Xiao Ran's little circle had once been. After greeting Zheng Fugui one by one, they took their seats and waited for the hour to come round.
"Brother Zhou, do you believe anyone here has really broken through?" Hu Zheng'an of Leopard Fist wore a thin moustache and was tall and stout, his arms thick and strong, a full size bigger round than the rest of them. He had looked over Mountain Fist's other inner-yard disciples the moment he arrived, and at that one glance the last shred of hope he had brought with him was gone.
"Out of goods of that grade, someone broke through? Have they got it wrong, taken a false show of qi and blood for a real breakthrough?" Hu Zheng'an shook his head slightly. A false show of qi and blood was this: some men were born with gifts apart, their bodies unlike other men's, and in training they showed signs that ordinary men did not show.
Some men's faces went red at a cup of wine, and some went white. The body is made differently from birth, and the same thing done to two men draws two different answers. What was true of wine was true of martial training as well.
