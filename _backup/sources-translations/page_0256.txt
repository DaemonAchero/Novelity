The Shaoyang Sect mine area.
"Father! Mother! Hurry!!" Wei Chun carried the bundles and helped the two old people along every step, following the stream of fleeing miners towards Feiye City. "At last… at last we can go back…" Their mother Li Cui's face was thin and worn, though this stretch of time the eldest had held them up. The eldest, Wei Chun, was born with great strength and a big sturdy frame; with her carrying the load in the mine, the two of them had come through with no sickness and no hurt.
Their father Wei Tang, little bundle in hand, was mixed in with the stream of refugees and just as stirred. "Who knows how Little Ying and Little He are doing now, whether they live well…" "Do not worry. We will see them as soon as we are back, and they will be fine. Little He is so clever, and Little Ying keeps house so well; they can surely look after themselves." Wei Chun comforted them earnestly.
She was saying words she did not quite believe herself. Yet even such words put a little more light of hope in the two old people's eyes. In that stream there were many groups like the three of them; many a family had been taken whole to work there. Some men had worked from youth to old age, half a lifetime spent in the mine. Some children had simply grown up here, and hearing they could go out, they only looked blank, not knowing what to do. Others wept for joy; brought in only days before and now about to leave — under such a swing from grief to joy, no one stayed unmoved. Squad after squad of Hong Family Fort soldiers, strong and well built, in leather armour with weapons in hand, kept order. "Slow down, everyone slow down. Our fort lord is Lord Hong Daoyuan, the Tiger of Feiye. He has already ordered the granaries opened and grain given out for three days.
"The first thing you do when you get back is draw your grain, by the head!" "Lord Hong is kind and generous. When you get back you must remember: it was Lord Hong Daoyuan who saved you, who gave you food and drink, who let you live!" "My Hong Family Fort has now driven out the Seven Family League and taken Feiye City back. Put your hearts at rest: while Lord Hong is here, he will keep you fed one more day!"
Voice after voice shouted it out along both sides. When the Hong Family Fort attacked Feiye City, the Shaoyang Sect joined in, and the sect's best men were killed in the fierce counterattack of the Seven Family League, a heavy loss. Worse still, the Hong Family Fort turned its face and denied them: it would not admit that the Shaoyang Sect had always supplied it with ore and weapons. Instead it struck at them as it turned — Hong Daoyuan beat Ou Chen to death in front of everyone, and hammered the Shaoyang leader along with him. It was plain bridge-burning. The Hong Family Fort held that the Shaoyang Sect's foul name would eat into the bedrock of their rule, so they turned on them then and there. Not that it mattered — the Shaoyang Sect was few in number, and because it ruled with such cruelty its own people felt little belonging to the sect and were glad to see it fall. In half a day the top men were surrounded and killed, the rest split up the goods and scattered.
The mine was even less anyone's concern. Hundreds of mine workers took the chance to hurry towards Feiye City. In the confusion Wei Chun guarded the two old people and moved along towards the city. There were even Hong Family Fort soldiers sent out to escort them on the way. Everyone who had been freed sang Hong Daoyuan's kindness. Every heart was full of hope for what was to come and hunger for peace.
This was what the Hong family wanted. With Feiye City's population fallen so steeply, the first thing Hong Daoyuan had to do was build the numbers up again. And the fastest way to build numbers was to spread word of kindness and give people a mouthful of food, to draw in more refugees. Afternoon, under thick cloud.
The stream of people from the mine was like a long thin grey snake, carrying hope for what was to come, winding its way towards the Feiye City that had just been through war. And on the other side — in the mountain woods, Wei He and Jiang Su and the others, big bundles packed, were leaving along the north-west, away from Feiye City. Ahead of them lay vast mountain forest, and rolling hills that the eye could not follow to their end.
