Of course, to Wei He, this kind of strange beast was useless. Aside from using its poison, its meat wasn't even edible. He quietly backed away from his spot and headed into the distance.
According to records, as long as the Jueru bird scratched you, even a Third Blood martial artist would die for sure. Naturally, that just meant its venom was lethal—its actual combat strength was a different story. Third Blood martial artists had lightning-fast reactions and immense strength, so a bird like this probably couldn't even pull off a sneak attack. If you really wanted to compare, you'd have to see a real fight.
Either way, Wei He simply didn't want to mess with a beast like that. He might not be afraid, but the people with him were.
He moved back slowly and soon returned to the spot the others had passed earlier. Without stopping, he rushed back to the cave.
Because he found the dangerous Jueru bird nearby, Wei He rested in the cave for a bit. The next day, he led the group along an almost vanished trail, speeding up their journey. He planned to burst out of the bird's territory in one go.
Vicious beasts like this usually had their own private territory, which served as their hunting ground. As long as they left it, they would be much safer. At the same time, according to the map, going another thirty miles forward would bring them to the first village where they could resupply.
However, to Wei He's surprise, when the group reached the village, it had long been turned to ruins, ruined and abandoned for a long time.
After leaving the village, on the afternoon of the fifth day, he ran into Jueru birds again while traveling.
This seemed to be another pair. The red on their heads was even darker than the previous pair. Wei He felt a chill in his heart. Something felt wrong. According to records, Jueru birds rarely gathered so densely in one small area.
There was only one reason why Jueru birds would gather in large numbers: a nest.
There was a nest nearby that was perfect for these birds to live in. Wei He finally understood why so few people took this path. With such a deadly group of strange beasts around—and flying ones at that—anyone would choose to steer clear of this place.
Day 25 since leaving Fei Ye City.
Wei He's group built a simple little shelter in the forest out of thick branches and leaves. Working together, Jiang Su and Wei He finished it in under half an hour. A martial artist's raw power and the tough hands trained from Huishan Fist made the job remarkably easy.
The five of them rested inside the shelter, gathered around a crackling campfire and wrapped in warm animal furs. Even though everyone stank from lack of bathing, being able to live like this out in the wild was already impressive.
"We've been walking for a month, but it feels like nothing around us has changed. The scenery looks the exact same as when we first entered the forest..." Jiang Su said gravely.
"That's just how mountain forests are. But we haven't lost our way—I can still read the traces left on the ground," Wei He replied.
"Taizhou... What is Taizhou actually like? Wei He, do you know?" Jiang Su asked casually, poking the fire with a stick to make it burn brighter.
"Never been there. But we have the foundational manual, we have tokens, we have money, and we have enough beast meat for the two of us to eat for at least a month. No rush."
With the supplies Wei He brought and the prey they hunted along the way, food wasn't a problem. The key was the beast meat.
"But staying in an environment like this for so long... after a while, I just feel suffocated," Jiang Su reminded him. "I heard my dad say that when people travel for too long, everyone likes to find something to do to pass the time and relax. Otherwise, your mind breaks easily."
"What are you trying to say?" Wei He knew she was right. Staying silent for too long really put a heavy, tense weight on a person's mind.
"How about we sing?" Jiang Su suggested. "I think I'm a pretty good singer."
"I can dance," Ouyang Lin raised her hand.
"I learned how to paint," Zhang Qi spoke up.
Wei Ying hesitated for a moment. "I'll just cook and make food for everyone, so count me out." She actually felt that living like this together was more fulfilling than before.
"Singing makes too much noise," Wei He thought for a moment. "Dancing will easily attract other wild animals and beasts. Painting takes too much focus, and you'll get tired fast."
