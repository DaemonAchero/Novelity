Everyone else went together to the Hong Family Fort. Some of Mountain Fist's senior brothers and sisters were there, and they would surely be better cared for. Jiang Su went that way too: to keep the several Jiang family people with her safe, she had to go with the greater part of them. Before she left, Jiang Su gave Wei He one last deep look, then led her people away quickly. Only Zhang Lu's younger brother, a boy called Zhang Qi, stayed by the body, crying.
And one junior sister was left behind as well, standing where she was, not knowing what to do. "You are not going?" Wei He asked, surprised. He had not known that anyone trusted him that much. The junior sister was bitter inside. She had just been frightened out of her wits; in the confusion she had run after people without thinking, and by the time she came to herself in the woods, the group had split up. She was left standing there, and no one had pulled her along.
She had in fact wanted to go… Wei He read her face clearly and had nothing to say. He waved a hand. "Let's go." With one arm he picked up Zhang Lu's body and threw it up into a tree. It landed neatly across a big fork in the branches.
"When there is a chance, we will come back and bury him. Remember this place." He scratched an X on the trunk below the fork with his bare hand, then left quickly with the two of them. Second Sister Wei Ying had been let out of the furs too, and lay on Wei He's back, silent the whole way.
She knew that now of all times she must not make trouble for her brother. They ran on and on. When the two grew tired, Wei He carried one under each arm and went on running with one on his back. Luckily his third breakthrough in qi and blood had rebuilt his body and his constitution was very strong: the three together weighed only three hundred catties, not heavy at all. To him, running with that much on his back was a small thing.
The sky slowly darkened. In the broken-down Daoist temple where Guan Ye and the other two had once stopped, Wei He came in with Wei Ying on his back and Zhang Qi and the junior sister behind him. The god statue in the hall was gone; Wei He had hacked it apart, and it was now a pile of firewood in the corner.
"We rest here for now." Wei He set Second Sister down to one side and walked over to the fire pile to look it over. The wood was still fairly dry. There had been too little rain, and the mountain woods had lost most of their damp. The other two looked around the place, uneasy.
Wei Ying, sensible as always, began to open the bundle and take out dried meat, grain cakes and water skins. "Last time I brought a friend here. I did not think I would come a second time." Wei He took out his flint, crushed the dry twigs fine, struck it lightly, and the sparks caught the wood and became a flame. Thin smoke came up and blew about the little hall on the wild wind.
"Will the fire not give us away?" the junior sister suddenly asked. "Smoke this thin cannot be seen, with the night this dark," Wei He answered. "And without a fire, the night out here is more dangerous." "…I understand. Thank you, Senior Brother." The junior sister nodded quickly.
She came closer, wanting to help. "You two go and pick up some dry wood and branches nearby. Do not go far." Wei He gave the order. "Yes!" "All right." The two answered. "Wait, what was your name again?" Wei He asked as it came to him, looking at the junior sister.
"I only joined recently. Ouyang Lin." The junior sister answered seriously. "You do not look very afraid." Wei He was a little surprised. Compared with Zhang Qi beside her, this Ouyang Lin seemed to have steadied herself fast. "It is all right. I came here with my parents, fleeing the famine. We spent time in mountain woods on the road, and many people died then too."
Ouyang Lin lowered her eyes as she answered. She would certainly not say that, to get onto this fast carriage out of the city, she had given all her goods to Zheng Fugui, and that was how she had won the name of a Mountain Fist disciple who could stay.
