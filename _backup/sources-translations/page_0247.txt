"It is no use you going." Wei He shook his head. Of the people here, only he and Ouyang Lin trained at all, and Ouyang Lin was a beginner, not worth counting on. So it was left to him to carry this.
He stood up, walked out of the little hall and made a round of the temple. The three wolf bodies he had killed had been dragged off by some animal or other; all that was left were three patches of blood, with black insects of some unknown kind feeding on them. Seen from a distance by moonlight, they looked like three pools of black mud.
After one round, Wei He scattered a circle of the beast dung he had collected on himself, as a warning line. He found nothing else wrong and was about to go back. Then he heard it faintly: from the woods below the temple came sounds of running, and of fighting. It was a long way off and the sound was blurred, hard to make out. A thousand metres away, by his guess.
Wei He frowned and looked off towards the sound, but it was late, and only a few threads of moonlight came into the woods. He could see nothing. With so much already on his hands, he did not mean to go and look. There were three people in the little hall who needed watching. Ouyang Lin did not matter so much, but Second Sister and Zhang Qi had to be looked after.
Zhang Lu had been his fellow disciple after all, and at the end he had asked a favour. Since he had agreed to bring the boy out safely, he would do it. Wei He watched a while, saw that the sound was not coming closer, and only then turned back towards the little hall.

Jiang Su, carrying Master Zheng, had legs as heavy as if they were filled with lead. Only a few people were left around her, and in the dark she could not tell who they were. Every face was a dark blur. All she heard was tired breathing, with a thread of despair in it.
The pursuers behind them were like hyenas, holding on and never letting go. "You up there — put Zheng Fugui down and run, and we will let you go!" a low shout came from behind. "Hand over Zheng Fugui! Or you all die!"
"Hand over Zheng Fugui!" A jumble of shouts came from behind. The Martial Masters' League was finished, but every martial master had, besides his own methods, a steady source of strange-beast meat to feed himself. Mountain Fist's red-earth fish was one of them, and among the League's many masters there were five kinds of strange-beast meat in all. The red-earth fish was coveted too, because it was not taken by hunting in the wild; it could be got safely and steadily. Wherever a man went, a money-making road like that was the foundation that grew a family. More than that, strange-beast meat was the resource training needed most, worth more than gold notes.
So a badly hurt, unconscious martial master, and an academy crippled by the fight, were a sweet bun put right at their mouths. By now it was no longer the Seven Family League's men behind them, but the hands of the Blood Clothes Gang. After the Blood Clothes Gang helped the Seven Family League surround and crush the Martial Masters' League, its own good fighters had spread out, each chasing his own profit. The Seven Family League's target was still the Flying Bear Saber, Yun Tiansheng; they had long nursed a grudge against the League, which would not come over after so many years of coaxing.
Now that the exact news had come, that Yun Tiansheng had been working in secret with the Hong Family Fort, they struck at once, like lightning. They had been ready for the Hong Family Fort's help as well: the Su Gang out of Su City, together with Blood Clothes Gang and Shaoyang Sect experts of the Three Gangs and Two Sects, blocked and delayed the Hong Family Fort. That was why it came so late; by the time it arrived, everything was already settled.
Pfft. Jiang Su could hold up no longer and fell. She and Master Zheng, still on her back, rolled on the ground. In the dark behind them, several pursuers had used up their hidden weapons too and threw themselves on them.
