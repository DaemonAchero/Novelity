Later he had brought what remained of the family goods that had not been carried off, sold the house, and come here to learn boxing. Five years of it, and he was still at the Ox-Hide level.
== Chapter 17 · Escort Run (Part 1) ==
Li Jue was very tall, a head taller than Wei He, counted among the tallest few in the yard. Yet the muscle on his frame hardly showed; from a distance he looked thin, and could overawe no one. He was a plain, honest sort, one of the few who rose before dawn and sat up late to train like a man possessed; but his progress, for reasons no one knew, would not come.
Everyone watched Xiao Ran standing in the centre, slowly gathering his qi and blood, his two fists faintly black and a size larger than anyone else's. The sheen on those fists was dull, tinged with grey. This was Stone-Hide. At that level one's qi and blood far outstripped Ox-Hide — several times over, at the least.
Wei He had once carefully compared the gap between himself and Cheng Shaojiu at the Stone-Hide level. In endurance, in strength, in explosive force, in speed — the distance was vast. "Xiao Ran is not even nineteen yet, is he?" Someone in the crowd said it suddenly. At once the mood of the gathering turned quieter still.
"Good, good, good. Worthy of being the finest one in this yard!" Old Man Zheng laughed, tugging at his beard, plainly delighted. He rose and called Xiao Ran into the inner room; plainly he had private words for him. Eldest Senior Brother Zhao Hong shooed everyone off, sending them back to their places to carry on training. Wei He went back to his own place and pulled his collar open for a look at his chest.
The breakthrough-pearl pattern there had only reached half. And that was with him drawing the Cheng family's silver-lipped black snake meat every day. He kept feeling the rate of accumulation was a good deal slower than he had expected. 'The nourishment in silver-lipped black snake meat certainly has not changed; it must be the vessel that has. My body is stronger now, my qi and blood more vigorous, and the total energy the next breakthrough needs has grown — that much I can understand. But at this rate of saving up, who knows how long it will take.'
Wei He sighed inwardly, but with the breakthrough pearl he was already far, far stronger than the rest. So although the pearl gathered slowly, he was well content. The stir Xiao Ran's breakthrough made was not only within the yard but outside it. For many days after, merchants, wealthy households, the stewards of great families, and gangs all came to the door, all of them after Xiao Ran.
Xiao Ran turned them all away. Seeing they could not recruit him, these people did not give up but changed tack, dragging him off every day to drink and eat and hear songs. Several times carriages drew up at the gate of the Mountain Fist yard and waited for Xiao Ran to finish training before he climbed in. Many of the senior and junior brothers, seeing it, were envious beyond words.
For a while Xiao Ran rode high, and even at training there was a smile on his face. Stone Bridge Ward, where Mountain Fist kept its yard, was a small circle at best, and word of Xiao Ran soon spread through the neighbouring streets. All at once Xiao Ran, busy as he already was, had a great deal more socialising to do, and was hardly to be seen at all. Wei He saw it and felt no hurry; he kept to his own pace, day by day, bit by bit, practising and grinding his skin.
Once he became a true disciple the fees he had to pay were cut by more than half, and now and then he went to the Cheng house to drill with Cheng Shaojiu; in physique and in fighting experience he was slowly rising. Slowly, but you could see it. Above all, the breakthrough pearl went on gathering, inching forward.
Days like these, with hope in sight, left Wei He's heart calmer and calmer. But such quiet days were few. Very soon after Wei He collected the spiked handle he had ordered from the Li family smithy, word came from the Cheng house. A job had come up, and he was to be called along for the run.
Wei He himself felt that after eating the Cheng family's free rations for so long, drawing money every month and doing nothing, it was time he did some work. Otherwise he would not be easy in his own mind. …
