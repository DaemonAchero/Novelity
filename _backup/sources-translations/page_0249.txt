A junior brother was cut down by a blade. He struggled a few times, and then he was still. Jiang Su forced the chopping knife aside with all she had. Her knuckles, at the Stone-Hide stage, were covered in cuts; they could hardly take the blades any longer. She gathered her qi and blood, smashed one man back with a hard punch, and gritted her teeth as she stepped back several paces to stand in front of Master Zheng.
"Hand over Zheng Fugui and you can walk away. At this stage there is no need to throw your own life away for a man who is sure to die," one Blood Clothes Gang leader said low. "…" Jiang Su said nothing. She could no longer hear the other two. Plainly they were gone as well, and only she was left. The whole of Mountain Fist, and it had come to this…
She looked again at Master Zheng in the grass beside her. What she saw frightened the wits out of her: Master Zheng was gone. Jiang Su's heart leapt, and she was about to rush out and look when there was a dull thud. A pain shot through the back of her neck, and everything went black.
Wei He held Jiang Su in his left hand and Master Zheng in his right, one in each, Flying Dragon Art fully open, moving through the dense woods like a ghost, without a sound, going far away. The more he walked in shadow, the more silent his Flying Dragon Art became. Night owls had to practise, after all. Ever since a Shaoyang Sect expert had spotted him, he had worked hard at the parts of the Flying Dragon Art that dealt with footsteps and breath, and at drawing himself in. This time it paid off at once.
With two men in his hands, Wei He turned over, brought up the Flying Dragon Art, went up a trunk in a few moves and settled into a crouch on a big fork. From up there he watched the Blood Clothes Gang men below, torches in hand, searching everywhere.
He set Jiang Su down on the branch, gently drew a small packet from his clothes and opened it. Fine powder drifted faintly out of it and fell down below. Before long: "Where did he go!?"
A Blood Clothes Gang leader asked, knife in hand. Bang!! A dark shape suddenly burst out of the dark and knocked him flat. Right after that, in the dense woods, dark shapes one after another gave low growls and threw themselves madly at the Blood Clothes Gang men.
A thick crowd of green-eyed wolves swept past below Wei He's feet and flew at the Blood Clothes Gang men, like so many black streams running and boiling. Wei He watched the Blood Clothes Gang fall into a bloody fight, until every wolf at his feet had rushed at that crowd. He picked up his two, turned, jumped down from the fork and left at speed.
== Chapter 83 · Flame (Part 1) ==
Night, in the deep woods.
Wei He had not run far with his two when, jolted about, Jiang Su slowly came to. "Wei He…!?" By the moonlight she saw at once who carried her one-handed. "It is me." Wei He did not take his eyes off the way ahead, moving fast. The dark trees flashed by on either side of him like light on water.
"Where are we now?" Jiang Su could not help asking. "I do not know." "Is it safe around us?" "Not clear."
"…Can you say something good?" Jiang Su wiped the tear tracks from her face. "You and the teacher are both not dead yet," Wei He answered. "…"
