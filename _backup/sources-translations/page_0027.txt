"Do you know what they go off to do, those who leave?" Cheng Shaojiu came and stood at Wei He's side, his voice low.
"No," Wei He answered.
"Some go to join the small gangs. Some spent every coin they had on training, betting everything on the fists — those go to haul heavy labour, I'd guess. The ones whose families are better off go home and help out there, and some are hired on as guards for other people."
Cheng Shaojiu's face held no expression. "The guards at my family's house come out of that same stock. They have been replaced many times over by now."
"…Why replaced?"
"Because the ones before are either crippled or dead." Cheng Shaojiu clapped Wei He's shoulder, hard. He said no more, turned and
left. The two of them were friends. Lately Wei He had trained hard and still could not break through, and everyone round about had seen it. Many who once had looked at him a shade higher for his diligence now let their attitude shift a little. Wei He saw it.
He stood where he was, waiting his turn to go up for skin grinding. While he waited he could not keep from looking over toward Xiao Ran. Xiao Ran, the genius Old Man Zheng set such store by, had got through the door on the fifth day of skin grinding, shedding the outer skin of both hands with ease. And he, Wei He, was now at one month and twenty-six days, and still had not got in.
Not that skin grinding was itself passed by more than one man in several tens. That after all these years of comings and goings the Mountain Fist yard still held so few people was for this very reason: most could not pass and stay on, and so those who truly got through the door came to no more than a dozen or so in all. Wei He had expected as much. He guessed his own gifts were at best mediocre — not the worst, but not good either.
But a guess is only a guess, and a man will always hope for a lucky break. What if he got through it on his own, after all? And then the facts were laid before his eyes, and only then did he see clearly how cruel reality was.
With two days left. Once again Wei He saw two men pack their things and go. He saw his second sister's anxious eyes on him, and he saw the looks of the people around him slowly going sour. Early in the morning, Wei He was the first to begin skin grinding. He stood alone before the wooden tub, looking at the black sand in front of him.
Not far off, Xiao Ran came into the yard surrounded by people, cold-faced with a thread of arrogance in it, and greeted junior sister Jiang Su, who had come over from the women's yard. Xiao Ran, Jiang Su, and a senior brother named Jiang Yan had become a little circle of their own, the three of them. Xiao Ran's gift was beyond other men's; Jiang Su's family was well off, her gift was not poor, and she had beauty besides. Jiang Yan's family had deep wealth, and were the richest household of
Stone Bridge Ward, where Mountain Fist stood. The three of them kept their distance from the rest, and had no intention of mingling with others at all. Wei He brought his eyes back and saw Third Senior Brother Cheng Shaojiu watching him from one side. Cheng Shaojiu made a fist of encouragement at him from a distance. His attitude had not changed in the least for Wei He's failure to get through the door.
A junior brother beside him could not hold back his puzzlement. "Third Senior Brother, that Wei He has not got in even now; he is probably a lost cause. Why do you still—"
"What you mean to say is, he is a man who will leave sooner or later, so why do I treat him the same as before?" Cheng Shaojiu asked in return.
"That is the meaning of it. Most of the people who come to this place have no money at home.
Most of them come with one thought: to pour out their strength here and learn a set of skills, and then find a better road. Plenty of them spend every coin they have and still cannot get through the door here, still cannot get in after all this time — and after that there is probably no future to speak of." The junior brother shook his head.
"That is how the saying goes. But what do you think I, Cheng Shaojiu, make friends for?" Cheng Shaojiu said, smiling. "Because other people
have a grand future, and so I go and befriend them? That is not friendship. That is calculation." The junior brother had nothing to say at once.
