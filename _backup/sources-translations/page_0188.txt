Another of them carried a damaged manual on him, the Duxin Art — a palm art too, its highest reach only the second breakthrough of qi and blood, of no value. Wei He simply kept it as a collector's piece, tossing it in with the rest into the hidden box. After resting a little he got up and went to the Mountain Fist yard, going about his fist training and skin grinding as if nothing were amiss, the same as ever.
After the third breakthrough he did not dare drive his qi and blood and strength out to the full, for fear Old Zheng saw it. As it happened, Old Zheng did not show his face in the yard the whole day, and Wei He put his mind at rest and turned to puzzling over the red-cloud flower of Mountain Fist. By his earlier analysis, whatever art one breaks through with becomes one's foundation.
Other arts trained afterwards have their effect greatly weakened. That meant that once a poor art was laid down as the foundation, it could never be made up for later, and a man stayed behind others for ever. Since a second art could not be used to replace the foundation, it meant one could only follow the main art all the way to death; for other arts, their foundations incomplete, were even harder to break through in.
While Wei He practised his moves, getting his body used to its present strength and qi and blood, his mind kept working over the analysis. 'Unless I give up the foundation and start again from nothing, retraining everything, can the foundation be remade — and that is far easier said than done.' Only now did Wei He feel how fearsome the Realm Breaking Bead was. He need not care whether a foundation was complete or not: so long as his qi and blood were full and the accumulation reached the threshold, he could break through directly.
That meant he could swap out his foundation whenever he liked. Everything for the breakthrough: so long as he could break through, any art would do to sprint with. He could not help feeling that in this world, things were truly unfair to other men. With that his mood settled, and the surges left over from his return from Shaoyang Mountain slowly eased.
Along the way he even gave Ouyang Zhuang a pointer or two on faults in his moves. Training done for the afternoon, he changed his clothes and came out of the yard, and saw a black-clad household servant waiting outside the gate. Seeing him come out, the servant hurried up. "Might you be Master Wei He?"
"I am. What is it?" Wei He's qi was plentiful now, and his voice carried weight. "My master is the Guan family. The eldest young lady invites Master Wei to dine at the house, and begs Master Wei's answer, that I may go back and report." The servant bowed low and respectfully. The Guan family? Guan Ye? Wei He's heart stirred, and he agreed on the spot. "Lead on."
The servant brought him to a carriage waiting not far off. Unlike the old carriage Cheng Shaojiu had kept, this one was far newer. Plainly the Guan family was much better off. To keep a carriage at a time like this spoke of very deep pockets. The carriage went briskly along the way, at no small speed, and soon reached Red Stone Ward. Deep inside the ward it stopped before a broad, white-walled mansion.
The mansion took up a great stretch: half an entire street hung with the character for Guan. Two gruel sheds stood at the gate with men handing out gruel, laying up merit, and long queues stretched before both. Wei He looked them over twice, then followed the servant in through the gate, threading the walkways between the gardens, round and about, this way and that, until at last, in a pavilion among rockeries and plantings, he met Guan Ye.
Guan Ye was boiling something in a fine porcelain pot; the smell of medicine came off it strongly, with a hint of mint in it, bracing to the mind. "Come, come, hurry over!" She looked up at Wei He and waved him over at once. The weather was hot, and she wore only a pale green long dress, her two slender white forearms bare, her hair done up in two great buns with all manner of ornaments and ribbons.
Seen from a distance she was lovely: red lips, white teeth, a face like peach blossom, skin like jade. Wei He was a little taken aback; with the sunlight reflecting down, those two arms were white enough to dazzle the eyes. He lowered his gaze a little and came forward, sitting down across the porcelain pot from her without ceremony.
