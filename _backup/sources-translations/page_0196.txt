"Ghoul, make your move!" Zheng Yue, one of the Shaoyang Sect's only three patrol envoys, sprang up out of the convoy and gave chase to the green shadow. But he was more than one notch too slow and could not catch him at all; he could only trail behind, eating his dust. The green shadow killed his way along at the front, all but invincible, and Zheng Yue could do nothing but glare from behind, burning with rage, shouting and bellowing. Only, he did not know why the Ghoul still would not make his move, when it had plainly been agreed. He had brought the follow-up payment with him too.
Why had the Ghoul taken the deposit and then not come for the rest? This was plainly only a matter of blocking Chen Zhao a little, nothing hard for a man of the Ghoul's speed. Yet now… the two of them, one chasing, one fleeing, and along the way the transport convoys were sprinkled with fire-oil one after another.
The green shadow circled round again, came back along the mountain wall, and all at once there was a red-hot steel briquette in his hand. "What a fool; he never learns a thing. Heh heh." The green shadow doubled back at speed and threw the lit briquette at the first transport cart. Suddenly a fleck of cold light flashed.
It was not aimed at him, but into mid-air. Bang! The red-hot briquette burst apart, spraying a great fan of red sparks. The sparks went up like fireworks, and down below by the transport cart a figure had all at once appeared.
Wei He looked up at the green shadow, and at once kicked off the ground, his figure shooting up like a flying dragon. Both palms went straight into the Five Ridges Palm, borrowing the Flying Dragon Art's momentum, and he drove them squarely at the other man. Bang!!! The two palms slammed into the green shadow's right leg.
The two of them used the force to part. Wei He planted his feet lightly on a cart roof, and with a flick of his hand scattered a handful of poison darts. Without waiting for the result he raised the Flying Dragon Art, his figure like a spectre, tapped his way across several carts, snatched up a bundle from the last one, and sprang away.
Pff!! A great cloud of quicklime and poison powder burst out. In the blur of it there was no sign of him left. The green shadow, Chen Zhao of the Wind-Chasing Legs, was held up for a moment by that. He was just about to go on with his flight when
a flight of poison darts came after him and joined with the flying knives thrown by the two Shaoyang Sect patrol envoys to ring him in. Bang! The robe-ball opened out again, and Chen Zhao blocked every dart and knife, but he had lost his best chance of getting away. "Damn it!!" Frantic with rage, he looked off towards the place where the great cloud of poison powder still drifted. That man's sneak-attack — timing, speed, force —
had all been excellent, exactly right. The man had made him pause for one moment, and together with the Shaoyang Sect envoys behind had cut off his line of retreat. The man's palm force was ordinary, carrying a faint sense of shattering, on the weak side; but his timing was exact and his force precise, so that every scrap of his strength told to the utmost.
"Chen Zhao, your death!" The two Shaoyang Sect patrol envoys came at him one from either side, and on the other side yawned a sheer cliff. This time it was a dead end. The three crashed together on the spot and fought in a knot; after a dozen-odd moves the Shaoyang Sect disciples suddenly cried out.
"Envoys, your travelling packs are gone!!" "What!!?" Patrol envoys Zheng Yue and Shangguan He were throwing everything into the kill; hearing this, their faces changed at once. The Shaoyang Sect's martial arts required eating in great quantity to guarantee plenteous power and qi and blood.
The deeper one's power, the more one had to eat. To lighten the load on their stomachs and guts, third-breakthrough masters at envoy level all carried a great pack of strange-beast jerky with them, replenishing meat whenever and wherever, so as to keep their qi and blood abundant and keep up their huge daily intake.
