Wei He likewise roused his qi and blood and roared. "Hundred-Step Divine Fist!" He lashed out a foot; before the other man's palm-strike could land he drove his boot hard into the man's belly. That the leg is longer than the arm is an unshakable principle.
"You—" The kick sent the fat man off course, so that he grazed past Wei He's side. But Shaoyang Palm was not only strong in force; it had insights into nimbleness at close quarters too. In the instant he slid past, the fat man's palm turned direction with great speed, palm becoming fist, and smashed at Wei He's back. Peng!
That fist changed too fast; Wei He had not looked for it and was struck on the spot. But as it turned out, the fat man gave a muffled grunt — the spot his fist landed on was in fact a plate of iron. That punch of his dented the iron plate, but the rebound left his own bones aching. Wei He gave no ground: he spun and lunged in, both fists like a squall of wind and driving rain, hammering at the fat man without pause.
Beaten back again and again, the fat man grew anxious and hot of heart and met him in a straight clash. Fist and palm crossed between them, qi and blood surging. Before ten breaths had passed, the fat man's face went blue and he toppled over on his back.
The poison on the grip-spikes and the poison of the powder had finally taken effect… Wei He panted like an ox; that fight had drawn his fighting power out to its very limit. This man before him was the greatest enemy he had met since he first went out into the world. Had it not been that for this hunt he had made complete preparations beforehand — if he had met this man in his ordinary state, which of them lived and which died
was truly hard to say. He looked at the fat man on the ground, twitching all over and gradually going still, and reached back to draw a poison spike, then flung it at him with all his strength. He had never trained in hidden weapons, but at a distance of only a few metres, throwing with all his might so that the spike struck the man — that he could still manage. He was a man of the second breakthrough in qi and blood, after all, and his body outdid ordinary men's in every way.
Chi. The instant the spike left his hand the fat man sprang up nimbly, dodged it, and flung a fistful of darts at Wei He. Not one, but a whole fistful — a dozen or more at least — coming at Wei He straight for the face.

This came utterly without warning. That great handful of darts was suddenly upon him.
Unfortunately for him, Wei He had been on his guard all along: he turned side-on away from several darts, and those he truly could not dodge he blocked with the iron plates he wore. Never with arm, leg or foot would he let them touch. When the fat man saw that this last hidden hand had failed, his heart went to dead ash; he staggered a few steps and fell to the ground once more. This time he did not move at all.
Wei He quickly drew another poison spike and flung it hard into him. Pu — the spike drove deep into the fat man's belly, and still he did not stir, showing no movement at all. Only then did Wei He let out his breath, and picked up a stone again to throw with all his strength; it struck the fat man on the brow, and he watched the blood run slowly out and the man's skull cave in.
Only then did Wei He move in slowly and begin to deal with the body. The fat man had a good haul of useful things on him too: a pair of metal gloves, five poison darts left, one pouch of poison powder remaining, a leather money pouch, and a dry-ration pouch and a water skin apiece. Once he had searched everything out and slashed the wounds to ruin, Wei He pulled out quickly.
He would have liked to drag the bodies away as well, but there was no time: the corpses he had left before had likely been found by now. Faintly he could hear movements coming from up the mountain — he did not know whether beasts or men, but there were a good many of them at least. Whatever they were, this was no place to linger. He knew very well that the men of the Shaoyang Sect generally liked to come out a few to a group.
