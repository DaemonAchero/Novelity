"Oh my, this really is a great joy!" Hongmei was startled and delighted both. "And to think that this Wei He, Master Wei, had the Cheng family come with his proposal, and has now turned out so formidable!" She looked at her own young miss.
"Young miss, this really is joy sent from heaven. With Master Wei's achievements now, the time will come when you are the wife of a personage like Master Yao Wu! Oh, by then the family business and the rest of it will not matter at all, and you need not fret every day over how to plan and arrange things."
"Just so, just so — sister, I have already promised them all; when the wedding comes I shall have my good friends over to drink, and it has been so long, so very long since any of us touched wine. This is just the chance!" Her brother Zhang Songhao was brimming over too. "And then they will see for themselves what a real expert looks like!" He clenched his fists, his face full of expectation. "Say something, sister! Why are you sitting there in a daze by yourself?"
"Just so, young miss; this news is a joy from heaven. How is it you do not look the least bit glad?" Hongmei put in, surprised, from one side.
Zhang Wanruo was silent and slowly sat down again. "You are thinking too well of it. He has soared now, and I am afraid he will not look at me again."
"How could that be? Back then it was Wei He who made the proposal himself; it was you who would not agree, sister. At worst we go to the matchmaker ourselves now, and would that not settle it?" Zhang Songhao said carelessly.
"Just so, just so. Young miss, you do not know — many a man, once he has risen, keeps a fondness for the woman he liked before. You need only take the least bit of initiative, and it will drop into your hand." Hongmei laughed along with him.
Zhang Wanruo only gave a bitter smile. Watching the two of them chatter on in front of her about a fine future, her heart grew emptier and emptier.
They did not know what the third breakthrough in qi and blood stood for, nor what Master Yao Wu stood for; but if they did not know, was she ignorant of it too? At that rank, with that strength, in the Outer City of Feiye City as it was now, he had only to crook a finger and there would be no end of people wishing to cling to him.
Among those people there were plenty prettier than she. 'No fear where there is no knowledge' — that was just what the saying meant. Zhang Wanruo said no more, only turned her face to the setting sun beyond the window, sinking lower and lower, with both loss and resolve in her heart. Perhaps if she had agreed back then she could have untied her present difficulty. But one who pinned all hope on another's help would never make anything of himself in the end.
As for having refused Wei He back then, she might feel some regret and loss over it, but not much, and no repentance. For at any time at all, faced with what Wei He had to offer then, her nature would never have chosen to agree. 'Am I to be unable to get through this because I have no one's help? I do not believe it!' The feeling in Zhang Wanruo's eyes faded little by little, and the steadiness she had had before came back into them.

"Yes — back then we laughed at you for your poor birth, and for being so utterly devoted after one small kindness from Cheng Shaojiu. And looking at it now…… the ones who should be laughed at are us," Jiang Su said, mocking herself. She raised a cup of tea and drained it in one draught.
"Utterly devoted?" Wei He smiled. "I was only returning a peach for a plum."
"You two dealt with each other as equals. Senior Brother Cheng said so at the time, and we did not believe him; now I do," Jiang Su nodded. "Back then, nobody could see that you, Wei He, had hills and valleys in your breast."
