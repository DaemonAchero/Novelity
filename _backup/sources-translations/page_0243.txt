Jiang Su was hurt, but only a light wound on the arm, which was all right. Zhang Lu was a different matter. His back had taken two chops in a row, and poison was on one of those blades. The group had broken out of the ring and was hurrying along a mountain slope. "I… can't…" Zhang Lu, held up by others, gasped for air, his face white.
"The blade… was poisoned…" Despair showed in his eyes. "My father… is still waiting for me… What a pity… I let him down…" They set him down gently. He had no strength left and was covered in blood. Wei He came over quickly and looked at the wounds. The blades had indeed been poisoned, but the poison was not the worst of it. The cuts went too deep: through the wound in his back you could see the moving organs. "Do you have anything else to say?" He took Zhang Lu's hand. Master Zheng and Zhao Hong were not here, so he was the highest.
"Senior Brother Wei… my younger brother…" Zhang Lu gripped Wei He's hand tight and tried to say more, but the wound had nearly cut him clean through, and he could not hold on. "Brother!" A young man beside him cried out, half a sob in it. Zhang Lu could not catch his breath. His head tipped to one side, and he was gone. Wei He stood up without a word. He had seen many dead men, but Mountain Fist, the people around him, had always been the clean ground in his heart.
But now that clean ground was slowly stained with blood too. In that press, with enemies on every side, to shield the few people near him had already been a great deal. The other directions had to look after themselves. That was why Zhao Hong was lost, why Zhang Lu was dead, why Jiang Su was wounded.
== Chapter 81 · Blood and Qi (Part 1) ==
Wei He looked at the Mountain Fist disciples around him, their faces full of fear. Many of them were family, not disciples. Many were hurt and bleeding, some lightly, some badly.
"Senior Brother Wei, where do we go now?" Ouyang Zhuang, who knew Wei He best of them, stepped out and asked it low. "Everyone is hurt. We need a place to rest and treat wounds." Wei He said it gravely. Unlike the panic around him, he had been through a great many fights, no fewer than several dozen in the past three years.
This small battlefield was his first, but he could still hold on to his calm. "If you trust me, bring your people and follow. We cannot stop. Move!" Wei He wasted no words, took the lead, and the group set off in another direction. But after a few steps, people halted.
"Senior Brother Wei, this road leads into the wild. Where are you taking us?!" "We have to get to a town. We need wound medicine! Otherwise we will all die!" "Let us go back. There are so many earthen forts around us. Somebody will take us in! Is the Hong Family Fort not the strongest of them? We can throw ourselves on them!"
Voice followed voice. These disciples had had little to do with Wei He, and though Master Zheng had given his order, they began to doubt. "Wei He, we truly cannot go into the wild. There are too many strange beasts and poisonous insects out there, and no food or water. We would be on our own for everything. Most of the people here would not live." Jiang Su said it too, caught in the middle.
"The other directions, I suspect, will be blocked. Going the other way now gives us the best chance of living. Whoever wants to come, come; whoever does not, do as you like." Wei He said it calmly. He could not explain why he suspected it. Instinct told him there would most likely be a block that way as well. But they were right as well. Everyone here needed wound medicine, or once the wounds swelled and the fever came, in an age with no such medicine there was only luck, or waiting to die. He had no right to decide for these people, so the choice was theirs. Soon the group split. Only a few were willing to go with Wei He into the wild, away from the chaos.
