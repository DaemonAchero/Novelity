And some had a little property at home. Wei He knew nothing of this conversation; he was giving his whole mind to skin grinding. Fist after fist drove into the sand without pause. On the strength of the medicine he meant to carry the checkpoint in one push, and step into a new level.
Time ran on. But by the time his turn was up the red on his fists had faded away quickly again. Only if that redness held and would not go, and then fixed itself so, could a man get through the door and step into the Ox-Hide level. Standing at the edge of things, Wei He breathed in great draughts, and unlike the other men who had not got in, his face showed nothing particular.
No gloom, no disappointment. He simply stood there. After a while he raised his head and saw Wei Ying by the firewood shed, looking this way from far off, her eyes full of hope. In the end Wei He sighed, inwardly. He was finally going to use the Realm Breaking Bead.
However it was, by the look of it this step was one he could not cross on his own. So… When Wei He's turn came, he looked once more at his second sister, then rolled up his sleeves, walked up, and stood before a wooden tub.
He made a fist, then loosened it, tightened, loosened, several times over. Wei He closed his eyes and drew one deep breath in. Bang. He drove both hands into the tub with force; the cold sand with its coarse feel stung the surface of his fists.
And in that very moment, at a turn of his thought, the balloon the Realm Breaking Bead had become burst with a soft pop. An unseen current ran from his chest swiftly through his whole body. Wei He had never felt anything like it. All five of his senses seemed to turn bright at once, and the bustle of the sounds around him came to his ears.
He smelled the odd smell of the sand in the tub, and from the medicine tub beside him the thick scent of the herbs came drifting. His hands began to warm, then to burn, then to burn scalding hot. But Wei He kept his eyes shut and finished his course of practice, blow after blow. Time went slowly by, and at last his time was up.
"Next." He heard a senior brother call out at the side. He opened his eyes and looked at his own two fists. They were red through and through. Though the actual result was not yet proven, he could feel that his hands were like a pair of thick ox-hide gloves drawn over them.
Tough, and heavy. "He's through." Not far off, Old Man Zheng narrowed his eyes, looked, and nodded slightly. His gaze swept over the skin-grinding disciples in the yard all along, so he caught Wei He's change at a glance.
Though the working of his fists had just ended and they were red through, Old Man Zheng's experience was no small thing — far beyond what ordinary disciples could match — so naturally he saw at once that Wei He was through.
"Through?!" The senior brother who had just been speaking low with Cheng Shaojiu about Wei He was taken aback a moment; he looked at Wei He, and astonishment rose in him.
"Well, there it is — made it right at the last few days after all." He could not help sighing it out. Cheng Shaojiu clapped his hands gently, but only went through the motions; there was no sound. Wei He looked over at his second sister. Wei Ying had her hand over her mouth, a joyful smile on her face. He let out a heavy breath, and felt the weight on his chest rush out of him and empty itself all at once.
Then he looked at Third Senior Brother Cheng Shaojiu, saw the soundless clapping, and could not help shaking his fist at him in return. Having brought off this checkpoint, only now did he count as a true disciple of Mountain Fist; before this he had been no more than one of the paying ordinary rabble. Old Man Zheng never even looked at that kind of disciple. They were as many as a man could want, and a good many of them came and went every month.
