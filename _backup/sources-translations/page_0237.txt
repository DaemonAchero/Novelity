He had only just hurt men; for him to stay on the ground after that would only make the mood more awkward. So Wei He simply took Second Sister Wei Ying and left, and the rest he left to his fellow disciples. The two of them strolled along the river road home. Wei Ying had still not come back to herself, nor woken all the way out of the sight of her little brother laying one man after another low in his glory.
She had been at home all this time and had heard little enough of how her brother was coming along — and Wei He's pace of breakthrough had been a little too fast besides. Apart from the way the other disciples treated her better and better when she went out to buy food, Wei Ying had felt nothing. Not until just now, when she truly watched that tall, towering figure in the inner yard beat down one man after another as easily as eating or drinking.
Only then did it come home to her. Her little brother had at last made a great leap forward, changed through and through, gone up another storey. "Little He… you…" Wei Ying stopped with the words half out, watching Wei He walking slowly ahead of her. "Me what?"
"…You… you are stronger and stronger…" Wei Ying's face was complicated. "If Father and Mother and Eldest Sister knew what you have come to today, they would be too excited to get a word out."
== Chapter 79 · Leaving the City (Part 1) ==
Father, Mother, Eldest Sister… True enough. Now that his strength was rising, once the move was made, it would be time to set out and investigate. "…Do not worry. I will find out the truth," Wei He said calmly.
"Mm…" Wei Ying had no real measure of how strong her brother was; she only felt that he grew more and more formidable, as if step by step passing a great many others. "By the way, Little He, your body is so strong now — could you not take a few more wives, and have a few more children? Then the Wei family would branch out for certain."
"…A few more children?" Was that not rather a leap of subject? "Mm, I hear plenty of martial men, once they take a wife, can have five at the least. You are stronger than most of them, so surely you would have more, would you not? Would twenty be all right?" Wei Ying said, a little hopeful. "Do you take me for a pig? Twenty!?" Wei He was speechless.
"And then? What are you really saying? Has someone come to you to make a match for me?" "Ah…" Wei Ying was hit square; her face went awkward. "Well, there are a few… they all look quite good to me…" If her brother took them all, and each bore one child a year, then in three years the whole Wei family would grow strong! Their parents were gone, and where parents are absent an elder brother stands for a father and an elder sister for a mother; such things were naturally for Wei Ying to think of for her brother.
And of course the main thing was that her brother had the means and could keep them. "Are we not moving house very soon?" Wei He had nothing to say. "That is so, but if they married in, would they not simply move along with us?" Wei Ying argued. "There is no hurry for now. Things outside are in such disorder; until we find a settled place, there is no more to be said about it," Wei He said, shaking his head.
He knew in his heart that word had got out and people meant to ride the wind of the academy's withdrawal, to win shelter for themselves and leave Feiye City with them. It was likely not only him: every disciple of every academy was probably being pestered the same way. Once the academies left, after all, the last floor under the Outer City's order would be gone, and there was no saying what the whole Outer City would turn into.
Not long after Wei He went home and took up his hard practice on the Five Ridges Palm and the Flying Dragon Art again, word of what had happened spread fast: how he had beaten Chen Jian before them all, and brushed aside Zhou Jihan and his two friends with ease. The news that a third-breakthrough disciple had appeared in Mountain Fist's yard was confirmed as well, and spread swiftly through the dozen-odd wards of the Outer City.
Even the Seven Family League had word of it. But the retreat of the Outer City's Martial Masters' League was their own work, forced by them. The League's very existence was too much trouble: a force of a dozen-odd martial masters with their hands joined was something even the Seven Family League as a whole found thorny to face.
