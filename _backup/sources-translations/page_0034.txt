Wei He went to his place and took off his outer coat, about to begin training, when the sturdy woman who did the sweeping brought him a cup of hot water of her own accord and gave him a warm smile. Not far off his second sister Wei Ying looked his way several times, smiling all over, plainly in a very good mood. It was not the same feeling as before at all. It was as if he had all at once come within the straight regard of a great many people.
"Well? Did you think it over?" Cheng Shaojiu clapped Wei He's shoulder from behind.
"I've thought it over. Your family's price is the best. But I still have my own training to do, and I can't spend long hours helping you." Wei He said it plainly.
"Of course. We'll tell you in advance when there is something; all we ask is that you come quickly when you are needed. The rest of the time you're free."
Cheng Shaojiu said it smiling, and nodded.
"All right then. I'm in." Wei He answered firmly.
"Good! You are my brother. Don't worry, I'll never treat you badly." He said it seriously. Wei He was glad, too. So long as there was enough Silver-Kissed Black Snake meat, he could cut the time it took to fill the Realm Breaking Bead by a great margin.
Cheng Shaojiu talked with him a while longer, in good spirits, and only after a long while went back to his own place and began training. Wei He noticed that this Third Senior Brother of his, who knew everyone, had at some point begun talking with that little circle of Xiao Ran, Jiang Su, and Jiang Yan. It looked as if he had slipped into that circle without anyone marking when.
Since coming to the yard, Jiang Su had naturally formed a little circle with herself at its centre. Few were the people she thought highly of; among them were Xiao Ran, whose gift was excellent, and Jiang Yan, whose family was wealthy, and now Cheng Shaojiu had been added. The four of them together had become a small new circle of their own.
Just then the several of them were talking in low voices. "Speaking of which, my uncle has come back from Zaiye this time and brought a share of a local speciality, cloudstone iron. If any of you are interested, you might come to my place and look it over. And since we were discussing No-Gap training together last time, shall we settle the time and the method for it now?"
The one speaking was, surprisingly, Jiang Yan, who rarely said anything in the yard. He had a mild face and soft brows, giving a plain, unjarring impression, neither sharp nor pushy. No one would guess he came from one of the families of the Inner City here in Feiye City. The No-Gap he spoke of: first, it trained each man's ability to guard against ambush and against thrown stones; second, as core disciples of Old Man Zheng of
Mountain Fist, it was the private gathering that could take on missions of better value. The Jiang family came from the Inner City, and the Inner City and the Outer City were two entirely separate regions. This too was one key reason Jiang Su looked on Jiang Yan with special favour.
"As to No-Gap, I have no objection. But I want to bring a junior sister along — that's all right?" Jiang Su was the first to answer. She never let slip a thing of this kind, especially a chance to strengthen herself. "Of course it's fine." Jiang Yan nodded. "I have no objection either." Xiao Ran was cold-faced, but his eyes would drift toward Jiang Su without his meaning them to.
It was plain the drunkard's mind was not on the wine. He paused, and then, remembering something, added as well: "Besides that, I need to bring one person along as well."
"Fine." Jiang Yan nodded, smiling. Cheng Shaojiu, standing near enough by now, had a stirring in his own eyes too; looking at Jiang Su at his side, he made no secret of his appreciation
of her. "That No-Gap you spoke of last time — I have no objection either." He paused, and went on: "I'll bring junior brother Wei along." He pointed with his hand at Wei He, not far off. "This brother of mine is generous in temper and firm in will; there will be something of him in the future."
Jiang Yan glanced at Wei He and recognized him as the man who had only just broken through at the last few days. He frowned a little. The people they brought were junior brothers and sisters who had got through long ago, or got through the Ox-Hide level without trouble. Wei He met the standard, true, but this potential of his seemed a little too low.
