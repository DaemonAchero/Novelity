That upper body was like armour: hard, with a sheen of metal about it. "Quite so. Since this is a squabble among our young, better that we let them settle it themselves." Suddenly another figure appeared just as abruptly behind Chen Jian. He stood a head taller than Chen Jian, broad and powerful, the muscles on him standing out clear as tree roots. It was Wei He.
Wei He stepped forward into the inner yard. Whoosh, a ring of wind came off him and blew away behind him, and threads of an invisible pressure of qi and blood spread out from him over the whole gathering. "I am Wei He." His face was calm as he looked about him. "I am a little late. My apologies."
That invisible weight brought the inner yard, so noisy a moment before, to silence at once. The Mountain Fist disciples who had been about to keep shouting found themselves swallowing, and said no more. The inner-yard disciples of the other academies felt it too: a pressure wholly unlike that of the Mountain Fist disciples they had just been watching.
A jin-entering master was too far above them for the comparison to mean much; the third breakthrough in qi and blood was close to their own level, and so they knew that level well and felt it more keenly. It was just because they understood how much qi and blood a man held that, the moment Wei He entered the inner yard, every one of them fell quiet without meaning to.
Only then did Wei He's flat gaze come slowly down and settle on Chen Jian in front of him. "You. What were you saying just now?" Chen Jian was in no right state at all. His eyes were shot through with blood, and now, at this moment, he felt to the bone the strength in front of him, qi and blood wide open, pressing down with all its weight.
He was not afraid. There was no trace of fear on his face at all; there was only fervour. "This feeling… yes, this is it… ha ha ha! I am excited, so excited! Excited until I am—" "about to burst!!! Ha ha ha ha ha!!"
In that instant he gave a wild roar, his whole body's qi and blood surged, and he burst out with all his strength. His toes drove hard into the ground, and a fearsome force rolled through vein and muscle and bone like an explosion, wave after wave of it passing into his right fist. "Take this move of mine! Baleful Yin Dragon!!"
All his strength, all his vigour, all his frenzy gathered into that one fist. Chen Jian felt his own qi and blood rising, lifted suddenly and all at once within this single punch. Under that huge pressure, heavy with ill will, it seemed that something in him was changing in kind. In the root diagram of Iron Slaughter Fist, those strange patterns of colour seemed to take on a special meaning in that moment.
He swung the fist out, and all at once a wordless understanding came to him. This fist… he… he had caught it at last! Caught the last light of the breakthrough! Ha ha ha ha ha!
He would at last, at last— Pff! Without warning a sharp pain shot through Chen Jian's body. His half-mad attention was dragged back by force and focused again on what was in front of him.
"This… what is this!?" His eyes went wide and he stared, stupefied. His fist was only half swung; half the distance to the other man still remained. And yet from his chest came steady waves of pain.
Wei He's right forefinger was resting lightly against his chest. "You…!?" Bang!!!
