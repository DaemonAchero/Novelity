It turned out that the Mountain Fist yard he had opened had first been an outer branch, set up by a power called the Wushi Sect to screen disciples. Later the Wushi Sect itself moved away, taking off a great many resources and experts and giving up a small, out-of-the-way post like Feiye City. But Zheng Fugui had not wanted to go then: he had already married in Feiye City and had a daughter, and he would not leave. So he took leave from the sect for a time, changed the name to Mountain Fist, and no longer kept watch there as a Wushi Sect disciple.
The Wushi Sect had always screened men quietly and acted with great reserve. So at the start few people knew that Zheng Fugui was the man in charge of the Wushi Sect's outer-disciple post. And after the Wushi Sect moved away and gave the place up, fewer still knew his real identity. The letter said that Mountain Fist was in fact the Wushi Sect's way of screening for the art: anyone who reached the third breakthrough in qi and blood could take a token and enter the Wushi Sect. And the step from the third breakthrough to forging force was very important, because the force a man forged decided how complete and how strong his body would be later. So those three tokens were the marks of passing the test back into the Wushi Sect. He could take one, go to Tai Prefecture, go up to the Wushi Sect, get the whole Wushi Return-to-Mountain Art and train his force to fullness.
The letter warned him carefully: if he should happen to break through and reach the entry to force, then he must get back to the Wushi Sect before he began forging his body. Last of all, the letter set down what concerned the meat fields and the red-earth fish. Zheng Fugui said that the strange-beast red-earth fish he had caught had been drawn on until its life was nearly spent, and it was close to failing. So he had taken the strange seed and let the meat field wither.
As soon as another strange beast was caught, the strange seed could be planted in it and a meat field made again. The letter warned carefully that the black lump of meat was the strange seed; the way to use it was to let it touch strange-beast blood and fix it to a wound on the beast, and a meat field would form. On no account was it to be used on a person, or to touch his own blood. "Three tokens, a letter and a strange seed, worth ten thousand in gold — he did not say it wrong."
When Wei He had finished, he handed the letter to Jiang Su. Of the people here, only he and Jiang Su were inner-yard disciples. The rest… some had vanished, some had been killed, some had turned traitor. Jiang Yan, for one, had gone missing from the very start, and without question had gone home to the Jiang family. Jiang Su took the letter and read it through closely.
"What… do you mean to do?" She looked at Wei He, at a loss for a moment, with a trace of sorrow still in her eyes. "There is one more thing I have not done. When it is done, I go." Wei He said it calmly. "I will come with you!" Jiang Su said it seriously. "Me too!" "Same for me!"
The other two spoke up quickly, afraid of being left behind. "No need. We will pick a place to meet and go together then." Wei He meant to look into One Ear. Feiye was no longer a place to stay, so before he left he had to find out the truth about his parents and his elder sister. "I have a place where I can settle you. Jiang Su, you can help watch over my sister. I will be back before long."
Wei He explained that he was going to look into his parents and his elder sister, and that he would be quicker alone, so the others said no more. "All right. I will take them somewhere and keep them hidden," Jiang Su nodded. Now only a handful of Mountain Fist was left; it was a bleak thing, but better than those other yards that had been broken and scattered for good. Several yards had not a single man left; the gangs had carved them up between them.
"You — take care," Jiang Su said, low. "Do not worry. Rest and heal well." Wei He nodded calmly. To other men, the present state of things might be hard and dangerous. To him it was nothing more than a few more man-shaped wild animals about, no different from hunting before. With that he took the others out of the city, towards the ground where One Ear moved.
First he settled Jiang Su, Second Sister and the other two in one of his hidden hunting caves. In it were the stores of grain, meat and water he had taken by force; he had hidden supplies everywhere just for the chance of a day like this. However chaotic things became, he had a living point everywhere, a way to live in every place. That was what it meant to be ready. Having warned the others properly, Wei He ran flat out, and by what he had learned before he began searching the ground where One Ear often moved.
Ten li outside Feiye City was where One Ear often moved, only no one knew where he actually hid. The mountains were deep and wide and could hide countless things; to hunt down a band of mountain bandits like that would truly not be easy in a short time.
