And they had no use for Wei He besides. Better to let it go than to bring a man in to be humiliated; and if in the heat of it tempers flared and trouble came of it, that would be a nuisance too. Only, he sighed inwardly, feeling the loss of the chance for Wei He's sake.
After all, this No-Gap gathering was a good chance to gain experience against skilled hands. Through the No-Gap gathering they even had ties to the core disciples of other martial masters. For seeing all kinds of methods and broadening a man's fighting experience, it was of enormous help. A pity…
Wei He felt no such pity. Once he had settled things with Third Senior Brother Cheng Shaojiu, and with no proper work yet started on the Cheng family's side, he took on a short escort job as well, meaning to go and broaden his experience first. It was a temporary hire by the Changfeng Escort Agency, and the pay was good: five hundred taels one way, plus a stone of rice and ten catties of smoked pork. One day
and it was done. The job was to escort goods from the Inner City out to an earthen fort near Mingde Temple beyond the walls. The reason he took it was that the road passed Mingde Temple, and that was where his parents had gone missing. One way or another he meant to look into it a little.
Going out of the city alone, he feared danger; travelling with the Changfeng Agency's big train raised the margin of safety a good deal. Wei He had asked around, too. Escort work was not like in the dramas of his last life, where every convoy came to grief. For a big agency like Changfeng, trouble once in ten runs was already rare. Many bandits and mountain robbers gave them a measure of respect, and let them through for a toll.
So he did not think trouble would find him this time; the odds were small. And it suited him besides to see with his own eyes what the country beyond the walls, always spoken of as so dangerous, was actually like. Three days later, Wei He put on the short outer cape he had been issued by the Changfeng Agency. On the grey cape were the two characters Changfeng, looking thrifty in cloth and handsome with it.
After all, they were only hired for the occasion; there was no making each man a fitted uniform. This cape, a kind of short cloak, fitted tall and short, fat and thin alike, and could be taken back after every run — one effort for lasting gain.
The wide arch of Feiye City's gate house passed slowly over Wei He's head. He walked behind the long tail of the escort train, a rough short knife at his waist — not for fighting, but for clearing brush and weeds and noxious insects. From ahead came the endless creak and rumble of wooden wheels.
Wei He fished a water pouch out of his coat, then a paper packet, and took out a small golden-brown piece of dried meat, which he put in his mouth and chewed carefully. This was the Silver-Kissed Black Snake meat he had just drawn from Senior Brother Cheng. Dried though it was, and with less goodness in it, a piece the size of a palm was enough for one meal.
It truly held off hunger. These last days Wei He had drawn a month's rations without a shred of shame — that packet in his coat. By Cheng Shaojiu's reckoning it would last a month and a half at least. He could never have imagined that at Wei He's rate of eating ten days would see it gone.
Wei He drank some water, swallowed the dried meat, and pulled his collar open to look at his chest. There was a clear sign of black coming back on the Realm Breaking Bead; an area the size of a grain of rice had begun to change colour. This Silver-Kissed Black Snake meat was remarkable indeed. Eating golden coin worm meat alone, it would have taken him two weeks at least to reach this point.
And now three days had brought the change of colour. How much of a gap had been closed, there was no telling. Not bad, not bad — keep this up and it might be two months to fill it again. Wei He's heart was full of expectancy. Having felt what Ox-Hide level qi and blood were like, he was all the more curious about the stages of qi and blood that came after.
