"Still, that time did me good. After it my father beat me soundly, and I came to see that wine and women really are poison to training: if a man does not keep the number of times and how often under control, he will have cause to regret it later. So from then on I put myself under Master Zheng's gate, into this yard of nothing but men, keeping clear of wine and women and working hard at the martial arts. And now it has proved what I thought then…"
He ate a mouthful of food, and Xiao Ran laughed again. "You all think that because I go out to feast every day lately — hah, even while I eat and drink I never forget my training, wherever I am. Did you really think I knew nothing? That I did not know wine and women drag your progress down? I have even bought special tonic medicines besides."
"It seems Brother Xiao's present achievements are not all natural talent. Talent alone, without grit, would never bring a man to Brother Xiao's
level." Jiang Su said with feeling. "I have never had such experience from childhood — is that perhaps why my talent falls short?" She could not help laughing at herself as she said it.
"Talent is inborn; that is not for me to help. But if a man can take in a few utterly devoted followers to guard him on every side, is that not just as comfortable? Why does Susu not ask Brother Shaojiu for a pointer or two? Even Senior Brother Jiang calls Brother Shaojiu's methods remarkable, and in this he must be no slouch." Xiao Ran said with a smile. Hearing it, Jiang Su — though she was of a strong-willed temper and held that a man's own strength was what mattered most — could not but be stirred at the thought that if she could take in a few men for her own use, her sense of safety
would rise greatly as well. Besides, it had been she who was asking Cheng Shaojiu in the first place, and the subject had been turned aside by the other two; so now she turned her head this way. Only, the others had been talking away so merrily that they had not noticed Cheng Shaojiu's state.
Looking over now, she saw that Cheng Shaojiu was bent over his dish and cup, saying not a word, as though he had heard nothing of what they had just been saying. "Senior Brother Cheng? You have not answered the question I asked before." Jiang Su said.
"Oh — just now I was only minding the food; my belly was empty. My apologies, apologies; I have been a little rude." Only then did Cheng Shaojiu come to himself and look up,
apologising again and again. "Has Senior Brother Cheng any good method…" Jiang Su went on, and repeated what the others had said.
"A good method…?" Cheng Shaojiu lowered his head again, and was silent a while. He set his chopsticks out level and laid them across his spoon.
Then he raised his head again. "In fact there is a thought I have carried in mind all along."
"What thought?" Jiang Su asked.
"It is only that I hope that one day I may make friends in every place under heaven, and that wherever I go I may turn spears into jade and silk — settling every trouble without
raising a hand." Cheng Shaojiu said with a smile. "So my own view is that I have no particular designs on anyone. It is enough to treat men with sincerity. Wei He — I count him a friend, so he counts me a friend; there is nothing more to it."
The moment he said it, the expressions on Jiang Yan's and the others' faces slowly closed up, for there was something off in the taste of it. Everyone knew that Cheng Shaojiu had friends far and wide and could hold a few words with men of any station. Saying this, his meaning was: I, Cheng Shaojiu, count Wei He a friend — a friend the same as you.
Was that not to set them on a level with the likes of Wei He, and put them side by side? "Brother Cheng seems a little drunk." Jiang Yan's smile did not change as he folded his paper fan. "Let us lay this subject aside for now. Shall we go and look at the several fine things newly come to the Hundred Treasures Tower? In truth, now that silver coin cannot be used and everything must be changed into gold leaf, it is a good deal inconvenient."
Jiang Yan was the first to rise, and called the restaurant waiter over to settle the bill. But after the scene just now, the three of them all had their thoughts about Cheng Shaojiu. The four split into two pairs, two ahead and two behind.
