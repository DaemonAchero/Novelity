A flicker of doubt crossed Jiang Su's eyes, but she said nothing, and went to one side and stood quietly. Soon Cheng Shaojiu, Jiang Yan, and Zhao Hong arrived one after another. With Wei He, and with Xiao Ran absent through his wound, that made six in all — the core disciples of Mountain Fist as it now stood. The five stood in a row before Zheng Fugui, quietly awaiting his word.
Zheng Fugui coughed a few times, picked up his teacup and sipped. His old eyes, with crow's feet at the corners, went once over the five of them. He waited a while, and only then spoke slowly. "Today I have called you here because there is a matter I must set before you…" He broke off, set the cup down unhurriedly, and let out a breath.
"Ah, yes." He looked suddenly at Jiang Su, as if remembering something. "Jiang Su, how old are you this year?"
"Nineteen, Master."
"Nineteen… not so young either." Zheng Fugui pinched his beard. "Let me put it this way: your junior brother Wei He is eighteen now. He is an honest, plain sort, with decent potential and decent strength, and would make a fine husband. Why do you two not make a match of it, the better to support each other?" These words stunned everyone present into stillness. Jiang Yan had long had his eye on Jiang Su, and at this he almost forgot the fan in his hand; it dropped with a smack to the floor behind him.
Cheng Shaojiu was swallowing and nearly choked on it, and bent his head, drawing breath after breath. Zhao Hong was the steadiest of them; he only blanked for a moment and then was as before, unmoving. As for the two concerned, colour rushed to Jiang Su's face — not from shame but from anger. "Master, me and Wei He?! You — Junior Brother Wei's temper and character are not the sort I like at all, and long ago I swore
an oath that I would marry only a man stronger than myself. He will not do!"
Wei He was startled too, and spoke up quickly. "Thank you for your kind intent, Master, but this disciple truly has not the least thought of that kind toward Senior Sister Jiang — only respect. I beg your understanding."
Honestly, a woman with her eyes above the clouds like Jiang Su could be given to him and he would find her a burden. The partner he pictured ought at least to be like his second sister: gentle, virtuous, a good woman for keeping a home. This Jiang Su was too calculating, too strong-willed; perhaps one of those soft, fair-faced boys would suit her better. Jiang Su, at his side, shot him a sidelong glance and thought: 'The boy has some self-knowledge after all; he knew to refuse of his own accord.'
Both of them had been startled by Zheng Fugui's wild manoeuvre, and both refused in haste. Zheng Fugui waved a hand. "Is this not a fine match? Wei He broke through to Stone-Hide today, with good potential, and Jiang Su is no worse — beauty and family both. Is this not that thing… that what-do-you-call-it match?"
"A match made in heaven." Cheng Shaojiu bent over and prompted him in a low voice.
"Ah — a match made in heaven."
"…" Cheng Shaojiu smiled, straightened up again, and looked back at Wei He. Suddenly the look on his face went blank. What had the master just said?
Xiao He — broken through — Stone-Hide? The smile froze on his face. He thought he must have misheard, and cast his mind back carefully. "No, Master — did you just say Wei He has broken through? A second breakthrough in qi and blood?" Cheng Shaojiu hurried to ask.
"Did I not say so?" Zheng Fugui glanced at him. "That is right. He is now at the same stage as the rest of you."
A moment ago the others had thought they had misheard; now it was said again, and at once Jiang Yan and Jiang Su were both struck dumb. Wei He — that fellow who had always been Cheng Shaojiu's little hanger-on? He could reach Stone-Hide too?
