These days, a girl who stayed out a whole night did not come away with a good name. Only once she had left did Wei He, behind the door of the little Wei courtyard, shut it for good and turn into his own inner room. There was still the Flying Dragon Art manual to alter, so that it would not fall into another man's hands and do him good for nothing. As for how to alter it, he need only take the key parts and recast them in words only he could read.
Pinyin, for instance… English, Chinese characters, and the Latin he had once taught himself purely to show off. That way no one but himself could make sense of it. While Wei He sat over the manual, over in the Mountain Fist yard Jiang Su had arrived early and put on the look of one beginning her practice. She meant to deceive her family into thinking that she had been at the Mountain Fist
yard the whole time, training and resting with her junior sisters. She had already squared it with an honest junior sister she knew well. But as she practised now, her mind kept going back over everything that had happened the night before. Before long Xiao Ran arrived too, and Wei He as well — all of them were there.
Jiang Su swept a glance over them. Xiao Ran's eyes flickered and he avoided her without meaning to. Wei He's face was wooden, empty of expression. Jiang Su was bitter at heart; she understood well enough that it was Xiao Ran who had drugged her — but then she thought of how he had kept watch over her the whole night through, and a sweetness welled up in her again.
Just then a junior brother who hung about Xiao Ran ran up, all eagerness. "Senior Brother Xiao, how about coming again tonight? You won a good bit of money off us last night — how can you just take your winnings and walk off like that?" "Beginner's luck, beginner's luck." Xiao Ran smiled, a little awkwardly.
The night before, afraid of being caught up in it, he had hurried off to the Drunken Immortal Lodge in the Inner City and gamed there all night with a few of his hangers-on, going back only in the morning — proof that he had been out the whole time and had done nothing. That place was a fine spot for eating, drinking and pleasure; unlike Hundred Flowers Tower, the girls there could be taken back to your room as you pleased. Last night, to vent his fire, Xiao Ran had had three without a pause.
But that one answer turned Jiang Su cold, sweetness and all. The Drunken Immortal Lodge…?! That was a quarter of an hour's walk at least from where she had woken. If Xiao Ran had stayed in the Inner City the whole time, then who had been keeping watch over her last night? Could he have slipped back in the middle of it, once or twice?
At that thought Jiang Su sprang a step closer to Xiao Ran. "Junior Brother Xiao, you were at the Drunken Immortal Lodge all last night?!" Her voice was harsh, with a thin edge of chill in it. Xiao Ran's conscience was none too clear to begin with, and his heart jumped at that. "That's right — I was with a few of the other junior brothers, playing all night…" he answered honestly without thinking.
"Then I…" Jiang Su smiled a bleak smile. "You? Last night you drank yourself drunk and went straight home, didn't you?" Xiao Ran blinked and collected himself fast. Watching his eyes flicker, hearing the want of any confidence in his answer, Jiang Su only grew colder inside. She whipped her head round and looked at Wei He.
The place she had woken was right beside Wei He's house. She had her guess already; she simply would not and did not wish to think in that direction. Wei He was only one of Cheng Shaojiu's hangers-on, a mud-legs and nothing more. She had never thought much of even Cheng Shaojiu — and Wei He was a good deal less again.
And yet, as things stood… "Senior Sister Jiang, what are you looking at Wei He for? Don't tell me that last night…" Xiao Ran had noticed it too, that she was staring at Wei He, and a sourness welled up in him. Had Wei He really got his hands on her last night?
The goods that he, Xiao Ran, had not even laid a hand on — and this fellow named Wei to have taken the whole of her? "Still, fair enough: the way you looked last night, Senior Sister, any man would have been stirred…" The sourness churned in Xiao Ran, and the words coming out of his mouth were none too decent.
