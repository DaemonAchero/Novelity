Wei He's heart settled. The wealth a third-breakthrough master had piled up over many years — surely it would not disappoint him? With chopsticks and a small knife he carefully untied the drawstring of the money pouch, then tipped out everything inside. Clatter. A crisp ring.
Out of the pouch came a few pitiful gold notes, and a few thin soft gold leaves, and — a block of pink transparent stone, like amber and like crystal at once. Wei He's gaze passed over the gold notes, only a few hundred taels' worth; gold notes too were marked for value by thickness and size, and if they were damaged they fell in value.
So how much a note was worth needed no reading of digits: a rough look at its size and completeness told you. Passing over the notes, he fixed his attention on the pink stone. Wearing his gloves, he picked it up with the chopsticks and studied it closely. It was only the size of a quail's egg, irregular in shape, with cloud faintly congealing inside, and a very fine look to it.
"If a third-breakthrough master would keep it in his money pouch together with gold notes and such, then perhaps this is a thing of value too, something that can pass as currency. Put it away first and think later." Wei He knew he still lacked much experience and understanding of the levels above him, and so he had no intention of dealing with the thing rashly. That done, he sorted through the remnants with a touch of disappointment: after waiting so long, the things he had fished off Chen Jun turned out to be this little.
Everything seen to, Wei He sat cross-legged on the bed, regulating his qi and blood, and turned the root diagram of Mountain Fist over in his mind. Lately he had felt something stirring, as though he had come to another understanding of that root diagram. Since stepping into the third breakthrough with the Five Ridges Palm, he had gained a deeper knowledge of third-breakthrough force.
As the days piled up, he gradually understood what so-called force really was, how it came to be, how it moved. And so, turning back to the Mountain Fist root diagram, Wei He had a vague, nameless sense of it. He was a modern man to begin with, with wider knowledge and a richer imagination than the people of this age, and now he had the third-breakthrough force to compare with, setting it beside the way Mountain Fist's qi and blood moved at present.
Wei He had a feeling that he might be able to break through into Mountain Fist's third breakthrough without the Realm Breaking Bead. "The Realm Breaking Bead ought to be spent somewhere more urgent — breaking the Five Ridges Palm through to a higher level, say!" That foreboding grew ever stronger in his heart. These days, as he went on feeling out the root diagram, he had a vague sense of having caught hold of the root of Mountain Fist.
Root diagram, root diagram: its root lay in depicting how Mountain Fist's third breakthrough ought to move. The true meaning of that landscape painting was not the landscape it painted, but the qi and blood it painted. Wei He kept pondering it, then rose and slowly began to practise Mountain Fist in the room, qi and blood filling both fists, a grey-white slowly surfacing. As he practised the moves, picture after picture from the root diagram turned in his mind into a special
scroll of his own qi and blood's flowing. Where it should be fast, where slow, where fine and thin, where it should charge — the flowing, turning, circulating and dividing of qi and blood, under the understanding the root diagram brought him, quickened, and quickened, and quickened again. Wei He's spirit gradually gathered into one, everything drawn to a single point, wholly absorbed in feeling and governing the movement of qi and blood within his body.
He felt that if the tracks of that flow were drawn out whole, they would make another landscape painting, much like the root diagram. He did not know how much time had passed. Then, suddenly, a thunderclap at his ear.
Boom!!!! He gave a violent start, and all the qi and blood in his body rushed of itself towards his two fists, like two great flood-tides, unstoppable, irreversible. Puff!
A muffled report, as though something had burst through. Wei He felt a pain in both hands, and then the qi and blood flowed back, at more than twice the speed of before. The rushing circulation heated his whole body fast, and his skin grew scalding.
