Instead, he walked slowly to a black iron pillar rooted in the ground against the wall. "That — is jin force!" Shh! In an instant his right hand flashed out and his five fingers drew lightly across the pillar, thick as a man's arm. Without a whisper of sound, a deep finger mark stood out in the hard iron.
With nothing but his fingers, and not a sound to it, he had cut a mark into hard iron!! Jiang Yan's heart lurched. He had never seen his father show his strength fully in front of him; he only knew that his father was strong, but how strong had never been a thing he had any measure of. Only now did he truly understand what a gulf lay between the third breakthrough of qi and blood and the second.
He walked quickly over and put out a hand to stroke the mark. An inch deep at least! And the cut was smooth beyond anything, as if made with a knife. At his full strength he could just manage to bend an iron pillar like that — and take injury from the recoil himself besides. This was far, far beyond anything he could do.
"The third breakthrough of qi and blood demands talent, and perseverance, and qi and blood driven to a man's own limits; only then can he break the shackle and step into new country. Do you understand now?" Jiang Donggui said, mildly. "Your son understands…" Jiang Yan nodded gravely. "No wonder the third breakthrough adds twenty years to a life…" "It sounds like only one step more than the second, but that one step is a change of nature, a rising up, a breaking of one's own fetters."
Jiang Donggui swept out another palm, sideways. Pff. The iron pillar before him was cut clean through where it stood; the upper length was lopped off and flew away to roll along the ground. …
………… Outside the city: the Qingdu Sect's Ink Fort. The Three Gangs and Two Sects all had earthen forts of their own outside the city; not as large as Hong Family Fort, but still a place to base out of, and a way out if it came to that.
So it was with the Qingdu Sect's Ink Fort: it was both the sect's headquarters and the power standing behind Five Star Town on one side. Set against this place, the Qingdu Sect's presence in Feiye City amounted to no more than a feeler stretched out from here — a way for the Qingdu Sect, as one of the Three Gangs and Two Sects, to make its presence felt. The Ink Fort was round as a whole, high walls standing all about it pierced with firing slits, and simple watchtowers within.
There was room inside for a hundred households and more, and all who lived there belonged to the Qingdu Sect's elite, along with their families. All round the Ink Fort lay fields of yellow-green in plot after plot, with diversion channels letting river water flow slowly along their edges. It was the afternoon now, and the men of the Qingdu Sect were outside the fort, practising the martial way in massed formation under the lead of their teaching instructor.
Square formation after square formation stood in orderly array, perfectly neat. Before long a shrill whistle sounded from the Ink Fort, and a black hawk stooped out of the sky and dropped in through its walls. Soon the main gate swung open, and several men came out of their own accord to stand waiting outside it.
The one at their head was Zhou Yan, Outer Hall Master of the Qingdu Sect and one of its four hall masters. Zhou Yan was past forty, ruddy of face and with a touch of the scholar about him. Seeing one man riding up alone along the road far off, he went forward himself to meet him. "Brother Zheng, it has been a long while. Keeping well, I hope?"
The newcomer rode a black horse, his frame stooped and his face gaunt — none other than Zheng Fugui, who held the Mountain Fist yard day in and day out. "Young Zhou Yan. A long while indeed." Zheng Fugui swung down from the horse and checked its hooves carefully, easy only once he was sure it had not trodden on something and hurt itself. That black horse had been with him many years; it was thirty-five years old now.
Every time he went out he was careful with it, afraid it might hurt itself with one careless step. Zhou Yan was in no hurry either; he stood to one side smiling and waited. Only once Zheng Fugui had finished his inspection and was sure all was well did he slowly speak.
