"You and I are brothers. No need for that. But this stuff is not cheap; sold outside it would fetch at least this much, so go easy with it." Cheng Shaojiu said it low, and held up five fingers. Wei He understood. It was only because Cheng Shaojiu was the young master of his own house that such a thing could be had at cost price, and very low cost at that.
That was the only reason he could get the venom from the Cheng house. He had meant to pay for it, but Cheng Shaojiu waved a great hand — no money needed, it is a gift to you! He had always spent silver like running water and thought nothing of so small a sum. Wei He could only accept the kindness and keep it in mind. And if quenched poison was to take effect at once and sway a fight on the spot, it had to be a violent poison indeed.
And the silver-lipped black snake itself was just such a snake, venomous to the extreme; the poison water refined from it was used now and then by the Cheng family's own people, and very seldom sold outside. That Cheng Shaojiu had given Wei He a whole small bottle free was already a very great favour.
Take that little bottle outside and you could not dream of getting it for under five hundred taels — and even then there was no market for it at any price. With the poison in hand, Wei He joined Cheng Kai's party; after a short rest they took the escort carts and filed slowly out of the Yonghe Agency, heading for the city gate. Once through the gate, the party raised the great banner.
Two horses in front, two horses behind. In the middle, the escort carts, drawn by two horses, guarded on every side by the escort men. Wei He followed Cheng Kai at the very front; Cheng Kai rode while Wei He walked alongside. Riding was not for ease but for a longer view; so the men on horseback were the escort masters with the best eyes.
The party did not stop once on the road and set a good pace; by half a day they had passed the ten-li limit. On the way Wei He even saw Mingde Temple, where he had been before. The temple showed and hid among the hill woods, blurred in the mist of the deep hills. Below the hill worshippers came and went in an unbroken stream, seemingly not affected in the least by the killing he had done there.
"They say inside ten li is one place, and outside ten li another. That saying is not wrong either." Cheng Kai, on horseback, turned sideways to talk with Wei He. "Has little brother Wei ever been beyond ten li?" he asked with a smile.
"No." Wei He shook his head. "Uncle Cheng, why is it so dangerous beyond ten li?"
"Because of mountain bandits, and robbers." Cheng Kai sighed. "Beyond ten li is the line that Hong Family Fort and One Ear have tacitly agreed on. Inside ten li the Hong family's power reaches, and it is very safe. But beyond ten li is One Ear's range. You may pass through, yes — for a price."
"Hong Family Fort, One Ear?" It was the first time Wei He had heard it put that way.
"Does it seem strange? Why, when guarding the border ought to be the governor's troops' duty, is it Hong Family Fort that holds it?" Cheng Kai went on.
"Hong Family Fort was at first only a local clan of substance, a family that had held office for generations with an excellent name and reputation. But later Feiye City had a change of city governor, and for reasons no one knows he fell into conflict with the Hong family. The two sides came to great odds, and in the end, after much mediation, each gave ground: the Hong family withdrew from the Inner City and built themselves an earthen fort outside, called Hong Family Fort.
"But because the Hong family had done favours for too many people, and its web of connections is vast, the governor within the city could not move against them, and could only quietly let it stand."
"And who is this One Ear?" Wei He pressed.
"One Ear is a famous great mountain bandit round here, very strong, with many men and stout horses. Within a hundred li around he is this." Cheng Kai raised a thumb.
"Because when the fort was being built he clashed with Hong Family Fort several times, and One Ear came back badly beaten, so the two sides agreed on a line. That is the inside-and-outside-ten-li." He paused, and seemed to recall something else. "That reminds me, I should warn you too. Beyond ten li there is not only One Ear; there are also the Two Sects of the Three Gangs and Two Sects you hear about. If you meet them in days
to come, you must not offend them lightly; you must give way." "All right." Wei He nodded. Cheng Kai did not go into which names made up the Three Gangs and Two Sects, and Wei He was not in a hurry; having a rough idea of it, he would hear the rest sooner or later.
