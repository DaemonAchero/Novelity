"This is not pork?" Wei He guessed; he had tasted not the least bit of pig in it.
"Of course not. This is game — the flesh of a deer called the branch-antler deer, and a great tonic." Cheng Shaojiu wore the look that every man understood.
"Er…" Wei He indicated that he was still a child.

== Chapter 36 · Treatment (Part 2) ==
Wei He nodded; in fact he had felt it himself even without Cheng Shaojiu saying so.
As soon as the meat went down, cooked with spices, he felt a faint warmth in his belly. He quickly ate another ten slices or so, each no thicker than a fingernail, and to his surprise felt full. "How much is this meat a catty?" Wei He could not help asking. Though he knew he certainly could not afford it, a little now and then to taste should be no problem.
After all, when he ate Silver-Kissed Black Snake flesh before, he had needed at least three times this much before feeling full. Now he had eaten only a few dozen slices in all, together no bigger than a child's fist, and he was full? "This meat… the market price is about four taels of gold a catty of raw flesh." Cheng Shaojiu said low. "Chiefly it is a price without a market, for no one sells it.
"The moment a hunter takes one, it is bought up."
"Four taels of gold?" Wei He was taken aback. Had they not priced things in silver before? And then in grain and dried meat? Why gold now?
"Here in the Inner City everything is priced in gold leaf and gold notes. That is the true hard currency. Not like silver, which any house can mint privately — who can tell how much real silver there is
in a lump of it?" Cheng Shaojiu explained. He paused. "A few days ago I saw at the rice shop that one tael of gold will buy fifty catties of rice. Work out for yourself what this meat is worth."
"Fifty catties…!" Wei He was a little startled. Only now did he grasp the true price of this meal.
Fifty catties of rice could feed a household a long while… For a middling family it was all right, no more than a month or so of food money; for the poor, changed into mixed-grain flour it was… And four taels of gold… Looking at the fine dishes on the table, Wei He's feelings were complicated for a moment.
Without noticing it, he had climbed to the level of eating dishes this costly. For a while his chopsticks moved a good deal faster. "Hey?? Leave me some!" Cheng Shaojiu caught on at once. The two of them flew at it with their chopsticks, and in a few moves a whole plate of meat was gone.
Of course, for all the contest, Cheng Shaojiu still let Wei He have most of it; after all, he had asked him out expressly. When they had eaten and drunk their fill, a dish of toothpicks was brought. Each took one and sat picking at their teeth. "Xiao He, now that you have made your second breakthrough in qi and blood, it is time we raised your terms here as well." Cheng Shaojiu had long since settled in his own mind
what to offer Wei He. The Yonghe Agency as it stood could not in truth offer anything to satisfy Wei He: at his stage a man lacked neither money nor grain, but lacked things like Silver-Kissed Black Snake flesh, good things that gathered qi and blood quickly. Yet such things were usually each house's treasured secret, never let out. So he had settled on it: to hire Wei He for part of
his time. Nominally he would hang his name at the agency and draw an income, without hindering him from joining anywhere else. He put the idea to Wei He, and Wei He agreed.
