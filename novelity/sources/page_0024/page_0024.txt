And so it was: the several senior and junior brothers who had gathered round her were sent off with a cold face by the new junior sister Jiang Su. She looked right and left, then went off alone to a corner and began skin grinding. Why a girl would choose a martial art like Mountain Fist that ruined the skin, nobody could say. Cheng Shaojiu and a few other senior brothers gossiped in whispers a while, and finding nothing more to say, drifted apart of themselves.
Wei He paid it no mind. He rested a while, then went up again for his turn and began a second round of skin grinding. These last days he had noticed that the last little bit of black on the Realm Breaking Bead over his heart was close to complete, and so there was an expectancy in him and his training had real force behind it. Only, the more he looked for that last of the black, the slower it seemed to come.
Time raced on. Day after day of grinding went slowly by. The black on the bead grew denser and denser, and that last patch of blank grew smaller and smaller. And the skin-grinding stage deepened little by little. By Old Man Zheng's rule, two months were all a man was given for skin grinding; fail to bring it in and get through the door inside two months, and after
that there was no succeeding at all. It meant a man's gift was not up to the mark, and further training would win him little. There were six other senior and junior brothers grinding skin alongside Wei He; counting Wei He, seven.
Wei He put his head down and trained, not a word out of him, not a sound. Then one morning Third Senior Brother Cheng Shaojiu, who was rarely quiet, had very little to say. It was out of the ordinary. Wei He had just taken off his outer coat and was rolling up his sleeves to begin when he saw two of the brothers he had been grinding skin beside these past weeks, fully dressed,
with their bundles in their hands, slip out quietly by the side door.
"They're not training, at this hour…?" one of the men beside him said, astonished.
"They're done. They won't train any more, ever." Cheng Shaojiu said it flatly. The men around him understood, and fell silent.
Only one junior brother who had come in this month still looked puzzled and uncomprehending. Not far off, Old Man Zheng had begun, in his showing-off manner, to explain the system of Mountain Fist's moves to the newcomers, laying about him with a willow switch from time to time. The new lads had no choice but to bear it, and one of them hurried into the inner room to fetch snacks and bring them out.
Old Zheng was holding back and dragging the pace out, the same as ever — the very scene when Wei He himself had come in. Wei He counted up the time he had had for grinding, in silence. Half a month left; if he could not bring it off by then, there was nothing for it but to leave with his head down. At the thought he rolled up his sleeves without a word, went to a tub, and began the day's skin grinding.
Cheng Shaojiu, uncommonly, said no more than he had to, and went off by himself to a corner to work on live drills with a partner. The great yard was divided into three: one part for the newcomers, one for skin grinding, one for fighting practice. Lime lines on the ground separated them, clear as day. And time went on, one day after another.
The two-month limit drew nearer and nearer. Soon two more men, unable to bear the weight on their minds, left early. And new men joined the skin-grinding stage in their place. Wei He worked on in silence, but the redness always faded from his fists quickly; he never once reached the Ox-Hide stage.
Only at Ox-Hide could a man truly stay on and learn what came after; without that foundation there was no training Mountain Fist, and staying was useless. And soon there were five days left to him.
In his own house. In the afternoon Wei He came back from the yard and sat on a stool a while to rest. He was just getting up to eat when the wooden door clicked and swung gently open. Second Sister Wei Ying slipped in, a small bundle in her hand — a small grey bundle.
