Li Jue raised a hand in greeting too, by way of familiarity. Many of the people in the yard had left. Of those remaining, a good number were newcomers. Wei He did not come often and had not been paying attention. Seeing it now, he could not help thinking of the shifting situation in the city of late. He sighed inwardly.
"Is Master Zheng here?" "Not yet. But he should be soon; the old man usually comes around this hour." Ouyang Zhuang said with a smile. "Then I'll go in first." Wei He nodded, crossed the outer yard and went into the inner room. There had originally been three sturdy women working here; now only one was left bustling about.
Seeing Wei He come in, the sturdy woman showed him a friendly, ingratiating smile. Wei He nodded back and went into the inner yard. There, Jiang Yan was talking in a low voice with Zhang Lu about something. Jiang Su was silently practising her moves to one side. Zhao Hong stood in a corner with a letter in his hand, apparently reading it slowly.
Seeing Wei He come in, Jiang Yan's expression did not change and he went on talking to Zhang Lu. Zhang Lu was not acquainted with Wei He at all and showed no reaction. Zhao Hong only raised his head and nodded to Wei He by way of greeting, then bent to his letter again. He had been quietly learning to read of late, and by the look of it he was doing well.
Only Jiang Su slowly stopped her movements and walked over to Wei He. "It's been a long time since you came." She looked at Wei He with something complicated in her face. Ever since Xiao Ran's death, from her misunderstanding of Wei He to her making it up to him afterwards, and all the changes in between, her view of Wei He was now far more than that of a hanger-on of Cheng Shaojiu's.
The impression of Cheng Shaojiu had slowly thinned in her mind, and in its place the image of Wei He had grown deeper and deeper. "Where is Master Zheng? Not here yet?" Wei He asked casually. "Mm. Nobody knows what Master Zheng has been busy with lately; he looks very tired. I asked him, but he did not explain." Jiang Su nodded. "Then I'll wait a while." Wei He was in no hurry. Since breaking through in Mountain Fist, with the Five Ridges Palm stacked on top of it, the two arts' third-breakthrough qi and blood
layered over each other. The depth of the qi and blood in his whole body was simply stronger than that of every third-breakthrough martial artist he had ever met. Even those patrol envoys of the Shaoyang Sect did not feel as deep in qi and blood as he did now. It was only because he had rested at home for several days, his qi and blood completely under control and showing no sign of anything amiss, that the few people present had not caught on.
Qi and blood is such a vague, elusive thing: when it is moving and at work, a man looks heroic and full of life, his whole body giving off great heat, his presence overwhelming. But when it is still and his breath is level, there are few who can easily tell how strong another man's qi and blood is. The big fat men of the Shaoyang Sect excepted, of course — that build, and one look tells you his qi and blood will not be weak.
So Wei He also understood why Master Zheng liked to sit in the outer yard and watch all the disciples train: because in the work of circulating force, grinding skin and feeding moves, every disciple's progress was plain at a glance, perfectly clear. Wei He found a corner and leaned quietly against the wall, waiting. Jiang Su had meant to go on training, but seeing that Wei He had come she suddenly lost the will; before, she had been able to practise with all her attention, and
now she was distracted without meaning to be. She held on a little while, found she could not, and stopped. Zhao Hong, before long, finished the letter, put it carefully away in a leather pouch, and then went back to his usual place and began his routine practice of moves.
Grinding fist moves into conditioned reflex and instinct is no easy thing. Repeatedly pondering a move's meaning and where it applies, then improving it into a move that suits oneself better — all of that takes a great deal of time and energy. The reason Zhao Hong could, at the same Stone-Hide level, hold down everyone else was that his pondering over moves went far beyond the others'.
Watching his practice now, something came clear to Wei He as well. Master Zheng was not here anyway, so he simply watched his senior brother practise and waited quietly.
