"Besides — the Incense Gathering Sect… if it really was the Incense Gathering Sect who took my parents, then weren't they off carving Buddha statues at the Mingde Temple at the time? Isn't that the Incense Gathering Sect's own branch altar? How could they rob themselves?" Wei He sensed something wrong. Then his heart steadied. 'This trip has fixed the level of these third-breakthrough masters' strength. I really am still far short; better to keep honestly to hard training at home for a
while and wait for the Realm Breaking Bead to fill once and break through before I make another move… Besides, I ought to find a chance to let my qi and blood show, little by little, so that the teacher has some hope. Otherwise he will only waste away further…' And the breakthrough into Mountain Fist would need a transition too; it would not come at one step.
Having settled that, Wei He calmed his heart and took up his old life again day by day, quietly accumulating qi and blood, training, tempering. Every day he ate great quantities of the Shaoyang Sect jerky he had stolen; that strange-beast jerky helped greatly to shorten the time it took to gather qi and blood.
As time went on… another month and more slipped past. The Realm Breaking Bead's progress flew — with no need to spend qi and blood on it, it was a new sight almost every few days. Six meals a day, all strange-beast meat with other vegetables and coarse grain, and only Wei He's present digestion could have stood it; the Realm Breaking Bead
gave him another hidden benefit besides: he could eat anything at all, his powers of digestion strengthened. With all that eating and eating, he still had not shown a sign of indigestion? And on the other side, in the fist yard, after Xiao Ran's death more and more disciples quietly left over time. The yard grew emptier and emptier.
"Hey, boss, the Guan family's pulling out — did you know?" During morning training Ouyang Zhuang suddenly nudged Wei He lightly and said in a low voice. "The Guan family?" Wei He's movements checked. He had indeed not gone to the Guan family lately, shut up in hard training and stuffing himself with strange-beast meat.
The strange-beast meat from before was nearly all used up, and hidden among it there had even been a piece of wild strange-beast meat. The result was pleasing: his Realm Breaking Bead had reached three-fifths. Very fast. Clearly the quality of the meat had a great deal to do with how quickly qi and blood accumulated.
What martial artists call qi and blood in fact comprises two things, qi and blood; the circulation of qi and blood consumes the body's various trace elements and the energy of its cells and organs. What the Realm Breaking Bead absorbed was, to put it more precisely, that energy. And now, with his growing grasp of his own body, Wei He felt his own condition more and more precisely.
"Isn't the Guan family doing fine? Why pull out?" Wei He asked with a frown. "I don't know, but I hear they wanted to leave before and the Seven Family League, on the inner-city side, wouldn't let them — impounded a great lot of their medicinal materials, so it dragged on a while. Now they have finally settled it and mean to pull out." Ouyang Zhuang was well informed, with a crowd of dubious friends, and he paid close attention to such gossip himself.
So the news he got was more accurate than Cheng Shaojiu's had been before. "When people pull out they leave quietly — why is it this Guan family's going makes such a great show?" Ouyang Zhuang was puzzled. Wei He's heart stirred. He seemed to guess something. "Do you know when?"
"Don't know… three days from now, seems like… I'm not a Guan, am I? Aren't you on very good terms with their eldest young lady, Senior Brother Wei?" Ouyang Zhuang gave him an ambiguous smile. Bang. Wei He smacked him on the forehead with one palm.
"No nonsense. Get back to training!" Ouyang Zhuang yelped with pain, and had no choice but to go and train honestly. Nothing for it: his skill fell short and he could only take the bullying. Wei He, for his part, packed up his things, meaning to make a move.
