Wei He understood. Without the medicinal formula, this art had little to offer him. With this kind of external skill, if the formula made for it were missing, a man training it in earnest would, over time, end up injured or crippled. He set the booklet down and went to look over the rest of what he had taken.
Then something in him checked. 'This art… it needs no root diagrams, and only at a breakthrough does it call for a tempering formula to drive it on. So could my Realm Breaking Bead stand in for that formula at the breakthrough, and break me through directly?' The possibility struck him all at once and his heart began to pound.
If that possibility held, then this Five Ridges Palm was, one could say, the martial-arts manual best suited to him! For this art asked less in sheer quantity of qi and blood than Mountain Fist did, though its gates were harder to break through. He pressed the guess down for now and went on looking over the rest. Apart from a pile of powder pouches, the richest pickings were a pair of metal gloves and a money pouch.
Only the joints of the metal gloves were shielded with metal plates; all the rest was leather. Even so it was enough. A little repair and they would win him no small advantage in an ordinary exchange — unless he ran into someone with grip-spikes like his own. He tossed the gloves aside and turned to the money pouch. Inside was a stack of gold notes printed with a pattern like lotus flowers.
On the gold notes were printed four large characters: Money Reaches Heaven. Wei He drew one out to look at it and pinched it. This was the first time he had ever held a gold note; at the other places he hung his name with, he always asked for grain and meat. 'It is not paper… this is… some kind of leather?' Wei He was startled to find that the gold note was in fact made from the hide of some animal,
and carried a faint clean scent of its own. 'This thing might, who can say, have some use in itself… not merely stand in for gold…' Wei He guessed inwardly. He took all the gold notes out and counted them, and found with a start that there were a thousand taels here.
By the rate Cheng Shaojiu had once given him for gold against white rice in the Inner City, a thousand taels was no small sum at all. He noticed that at the foot of every gold note were the words Issued by Damao Money House. He did not know where Damao Money House was, though.
Having tasted the sweetness of it, Wei He understood at once why there were so many mountain bandits and robbers in this age. Rob one man and a fellow came away with this much money and this much good gear; if a man could have an income like that every day, it was past imagining… But he knew too that even if he went to Shaoyang Mountain every day, he could not meet a Shaoyang Sect fat man carrying a fortune every time.
He put the money and the various pouches away and rested well content. From that day on he kept hunting and shifting his ground: not Shaoyang Mountain, but other parts of Feiye City's country. For more than a month on end he lived in a round of hunting by day, coming back in the afternoon to train, and skin grinding.
At the same time, whenever he had a moment spare, he began to practise what was in that Five Ridges Palm manual. Fed on plentiful meat, the growth of Wei He's Realm Breaking Bead quickened again, and soon came to three tenths. His qi and blood had likewise begun to gather the substance of the first red-cloud flower. Even so, beside the Realm Breaking Bead, the grinding of skin and the gathering of flowers was plainly far slower.
From the look on Old Man Zheng's face when he examined him, Wei He could tell well enough that his rate of qi-and-blood growth was not what anyone would wish for. Old Man Zheng had first taken Wei He for a genius, because of how quickly he had broken through; in the end… as time went on, Wei He's true quality showed itself. His rate of qi-and-blood growth was nothing like what a genius should have.
To speak precisely, his rate of growth was less than a third of Xiao Ran's back then — a great deal less. Little by little, the hope that filled Old Man Zheng turned to disappointment. Fortunately, just as he was sinking in heart, Xiao Ran's fortunes took a turn after all.
