Zheng Fugui smiled. Hands clasped behind his back, he strolled about the inner yard, working his feet from time to time. "In this Feiye City, the Seven Family League and the Hong Family Fort are the strongest. Next come the Three Gangs and Two Sects, and after them come us — the great martial academies. And last of all, the little gangs and little sects. The Robber Gang and its like."
He pointed at Wei He and went on. "In this Feiye City, the very strongest man is in fact the head of the Ou family of the Seven Family League — Ou Chen, the city governor of Feiye City. Before Hong Daoyuan rose, Ou Chen was this." He held up his thumb. "Ou Chen…" Wei He set the name away in his mind, on the same shelf as Hong Daoyuan.
"Ou Chen's whole body is Money Jin — vicious, poison-mean, very formidable. You could say that touch him and you are crippled, take his blow and you are dead. He has reached the level of jin-refining. Once skin and flesh are tempered to the limit, jin comes out of itself, shaped to whatever art you have trained. Ou Chen's jin is the Money Jin handed down from the Ou family's forebears. Wholly overbearing." Zheng Fugui sighed. "Master Zheng, then how is jin-refining trained?" Wei He asked, curious.
"Jin-refining? Wait until you break through Iron-Hide, then comprehend the Mountain Fist intent in full and join the two together to lift your qi and blood a storey higher; after that, jin will come out of you. Though the Return-to-Mountain Jin of our Mountain Fist is still a good deal weaker than the Ou family's Money Jin. Yet we have our own advantage: it is far easier to train to completion than Money Jin!" Zheng Fugui said proudly. "Of the hundred-odd people in the Ou family, high and low, exactly one has truly mastered Money Jin."
"Then Master, how many does our Mountain Fist have?" Wei He asked, curious. "Our Mountain Fist…" Master Zheng fell suddenly silent, and paused. "There used to be a good many." "…" "For now, you need only know this: so long as your master still lives, Mountain Fist will not fall." Zheng Fugui was proud again.
Old as he was now, with the years carrying off a great part of his qi and blood, he had once been a jin-refining master; his qi and blood might have declined, but he could still handle several third-breakthrough experts without trouble. Wei He had more or less worked that out, and at heart he was rather startled at the strength of the seedy old man. A jin-refining master… it seemed that was the very top of the strength of all Feiye City.
"Then Master, are the other martial academies the same as our Mountain Fist?" Wei He asked again. "Not necessarily. But to be fit to report in at the governor's office and open a martial academy, a man must reach the very top of the third breakthrough in qi and blood at the least." Zheng Fugui nodded. He stole a glance at Wei He.
"None of that from you, you rascal. Bring out your nine red-cloud flowers and lock your qi and blood down first. Bring out the nine-spots red cloud flower, reach the Iron-Hide level, and you will lock your qi and blood away from decline in good measure. It will stretch your qi-and-blood prime out to forty years of age. Only after that are you fit to think about breaking through into the jin level." "Your disciple understands." Wei He nodded. Hearing it from Old Man Zheng, he had a rough picture now of this stretch of the city's martial world.
"In any case, know this much: you are no runner of errands now. Down at the bottom you count as having some strength. In an ordinary little gang or little sect, the strongest man is only the top of the second breakthrough." "Enough. Do not reach too high. Again!" Zheng Fugui said heavily. 'The phrase is "reach too high", not "reach too far"…' Wei He could not help correcting him in his heart.
He had no time to think further: across from him Zheng Fugui's fist was already coming at him. Under that ceaseless hammering at the moves, comprehending the scroll's intent was the fastest way to train them. That part rested wholly on understanding, on the mind's power to grasp. The skin grinding and the gathering of qi and blood that came after rested wholly on talent. Those three together — that was how Stone-Hide was cultivated.
After more than an hour of private coaching inside the inner yard, Wei He came out and happened to see Cheng Shaojiu, newly changed out of his clothes, yawning, speaking with the newcomer in his charge. Seeing Wei He, Cheng Shaojiu raised a hand and waved it at him. "Morning, Xiao He."
"Brother Cheng, what is this? Why are your eyes so black?" Wei He asked, startled. Under both of Cheng Shaojiu's eyes was nothing but swelling and dark rings; plainly he had slept far too little the night before. At the question he waved a hand at once and sighed. "Do not bring it up. Last night I was drilling my moves hard when I suddenly had an insight, and in my delight I kept at it half the night."
