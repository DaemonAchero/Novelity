He slept through to dawn, and when he rose he did not go to the Mountain Fist yard first; he went early to the agency and drew his own share of the pay. Then he took it home and hid it away, and only then changed his clothes and went off to the Mountain Fist yard. Thud.
Wei He parried Cheng Shaojiu's fist, ducked his head, and swung a right hook back at him. That fist too was parried with ease. The two traded back and forth, their fists now pausing, now lashing out all at once. Driven by qi and blood, both men moved far faster than ordinary folk.
Before long Wei He could not hold his ground; a hook knocked him lightly aside, and he staggered back two steps, nearly falling. "Enough. What has got into you today? Your fists are a lot heavier than usual." Cheng Shaojiu wiped the sweat from his brow and asked. Today's Wei He was a good deal trickier to deal with than usual; his punches were thrown and drawn back with far more decision and bite. If Cheng Shaojiu had not practised a few times at the small gathering, he might not have been able to keep up with Wei He's reactions.
Many a time he thought Wei He would throw the third move of Mountain Fist, and Wei He would suddenly skip up and use the fifth. That lack of rhythm was thoroughly unpleasant to deal with. Wei He stepped back a pace and wiped his sweat too. He was thinking back to his fight with the fellow who had been tracking the black-letter worm: the man's eyes had been blinded and his strength was nearly gone,
and still he had lasted so long. "Third Senior Brother, in real fighting, how should a man handle it? If you meet an opponent far stronger than you, how do you get out of that?" he asked suddenly.
"Heh… That, now — come to my training ground later and I will go through it with you in detail." Cheng Shaojiu was slightly taken aback; he had not seemed to expect
the question. But Wei He's behaviour of late had made him understand that this junior brother had reached the stage where it was due. Every man who trained the martial arts, once his moves were practised smooth, began to think about this question: in a fight, how do you fight? "Master Zheng has explained it to us — what to do against all kinds of opponents, in all kinds of surroundings, in all kinds of situations; there are set patterns in Mountain Fist for
each. But some things are not enough just to talk about. When we are done here, I will go through it with you." Cheng Shaojiu said it in a mysterious way. Wei He had a small realisation: in this yard many of the senior and junior brothers had secrets of their own — private little techniques, as a rule experience and training tricks worked out over years of pondering.
But few would choose to pass them on. If Third Senior Brother meant to teach him this, then perhaps… Wei He's heart stirred. He turned to drinking water and resting, then swapped partners for more drilling. He did not bring the matter up again.
Cheng Shaojiu also made as if nothing had happened and went off to drill with the stronger senior brothers and sisters. Eldest Senior Brother Zhao Hong happened to be there, so he went over to pair up with him. All that day, as Wei He trained, the fight with the tracker kept echoing in his head. He saw clearly that although their qi and blood were far apart in level, it made no sense that by the end, with the tracker's qi and blood both failing and his eyes
blinded, he still could not bring the man down quickly. It was only by a sneak attack that he had won. At noon he ate, rested a while, and, unable to sleep alone, stood before the wooden post and turned the matter over.
Toward evening, when the yard broke up for good, Cheng Shaojiu took him along, and they rode the carriage as before to the Cheng residence. The two took off their outer coats and faced each other on the training ground; there was no one else around. Only then did Cheng Shaojiu speak with a smile. "I saw you out of sorts all day. Have you been chewing on that the whole time?"
"Yes." Wei He nodded; there was nothing to hide. "To be frank with you, to get a feel for how escort agencies work I took a job with the Changfeng Agency. When we reached the destination and I was out wandering about on my own, I ran into a robber — and I felt that in real fighting I am lacking far too much."
