== Chapter 18 · Escort Run (Part 2) ==
Once past ten li the country beyond looked the same as ever. Wei He looked out over it. It was midday then. Sunlight fell, lighting up the woods far off on either hand. Along the government road there was little farmland — nothing but weeds.
Weed patches taller than a man were everywhere, but in clumps, not joined into one sheet. What he had not expected was that although Cheng Kai spoke of danger, the road was calm the whole way. The first day they travelled on to a village. The village was called Fan Family Village, and at its edge stood a roadside inn.
Cheng Kai and the innkeeper seemed well acquainted; greetings were exchanged, and the whole crew stayed the night. Early the next morning, before the sky had lightened, they set off quickly. And so it went: a day's travel, a village, a night at an inn to rest, and round again. Wei He as a rule shared a room with Cheng Kai; the room had two beds, and he slept in his clothes by the window, the better to watch for trouble at any hour,
but nothing unexpected came up. All was calm. The route, the stops, even the resting places had all been arranged in advance. It quite stripped the escort run of any mystery for Wei He. As for the supposedly dangerous country outside the city, he was coming to have his doubts.
The evening of the seventh day. The train was nearly at its destination, among a stretch of barren hills beside a great stone bridge. The men lit fires, brought the escort carts and horses close to the bridge-head house, and made a half-circle round them. The lot of them rested in the house. The mountain wind whistled, and the wavering firelight sent their shadows to and fro.
The great stone bridge stretched on for several hundred metres, and at either end of it stood a watch house. These watch houses seemed left over from the days when the bridge was kept up, but they were of great age and long since abandoned. And the bridge itself, once several hundred metres long, was broken and rotten everywhere from long want of repair. The bridge-head house was round as a drum, seven or eight metres tall, and broad — seventeen or eighteen metres across.
Inside it was bare and empty, and the twenty-odd men crowded in about two fires, talking and laughing. The cold wind that blew in endlessly through the two window-holes could not damp the warmth so many bodies made. Wei He sat in a corner, silently poking the fire with a thick branch to make it burn brighter. To his left several men were bragging and joking in a dialect he could not follow.
To his right, one man was carving wood with a small knife, and another dozed against the wall. Cheng Kai, as great escort master, had settled the night watches outside and now came in too. He exchanged a few words with the escort men round the other fire. Soon several men rose and went outside. Before long the faint sound of fists and feet striking — smack, smack — came drifting in.
"They have started again. Come on, let's go watch!" Several escort men perked up at once and headed for the door. Beside Wei He, the three who had just been carving wood and dozing leapt up and went out as well. The men who had been joking in dialect got up too, and one of them saw Wei He sitting motionless with a blank look, and laughed.
"Come on, come on — brother Wei has trained the genuine Mountain Fist. Go out and have a bout, and let the brothers see what you can do!" He came over and tugged at Wei He's sleeve.
"What?" Looking baffled, Wei He was pulled to his feet and followed the others out of the bridge-head house. Just behind the house there was an open patch that looked to be where they did their cooking and split firewood.
Two escort men were facing each other, slowly circling. Someone held up a torch to light them. Others shouted at random, cheering one or the other on. The spot backed onto the hill face and was shielded by the bridge-head house, so the mountain wind barely reached it — a natural dead corner.
