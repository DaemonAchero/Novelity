Five Ridges Palm asks far less in qi and blood than Mountain Fist. At the east gate of Feiye City. A column of blood-spattered escort men came slowly into the Outer City with battered escort carts and two limping nags, making for Stone Bridge Ward. The escort men were one and all glassy-eyed, bandaged here and there about the body, and some of them had even lost an arm.
Captain Wang, who led them, looked ruined, his lips white with no colour in them at all. He had held himself up by main force to get back. Now that he saw they were in the city at last, Captain Wang could hold out no longer: his knees folded and he pitched forward onto the ground. "Captain Wang!"
"Captain! What is wrong with you!?" "Bring water, quickly!" The escort men around him were shocked and came running. Before long word reached the Yonghe Escort Agency's chief escort master, Cheng Zhengxing, who hurried over and, with a crowd of escort men, swiftly took the convoy's wounded
away. Be that as it may, a good many common folk had gathered to watch. Battered as the Outer City was, plenty of poor people still scratched a hard living in it. Many of them barely got by on a trade, on odd jobs, on farming land outside the walls.
What they earned was whittled down by the head tax they had to pay, leaving only enough to keep body and soul together. Now they had seen the Yonghe Escort Agency — a name famous in several wards — come to grief. Plenty of them thought at once of selling the news for a reward. The Yonghe Agency had no few rivals watching it, and first-hand news like this, delivered in good time, would surely earn a little grain by way of thanks.
It was said that the goods the Yonghe Agency had been escorting on this run were no small matter, and the cargo extremely precious. Losing this run, people feared, would mean danger for an agency already shaken by much trouble.
== Chapter 47 · The Tide Turns (Part 1) ==
Day had barely broken. Wei He turned over and sat up in bed. His face was weary, his brow covered in sweat, and the vessels at his temples jumped and throbbed.
"It has been a long time since I dreamed… I never thought I would dream of the old days again." As he recalled the many things of his previous life, they now sat behind a blurring pane of glass, like a flower in mist or the moon in water, impossible to see clearly. The scenes of this life, by contrast, ran like a recording, vivid before his eyes, ready to be called up at any moment.
