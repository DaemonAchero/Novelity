"How did Senior Brother Xiao come to be hurt, anyway? With Old Man Zheng along, could he still be hurt?" someone muttered low.
"Old Man Zheng is strong, but no one has ever seen him put his hand out. How do you know he is stronger than Senior Brother Xiao? The man is old, after all, and qi and blood fail with age." another whispered back.
"Did they not say they went to the fist-off a few days ago? Can it be…!?"
"Who knows… the fist-off… heh, I have heard the older brothers say that no fist-off has ever left a man gravely hurt."
"Stop your wild guessing." Jiang Yan snapped coldly. "Mind your own training." Wei He drew his attention back as well, unmoved within, and went on slowly with his own practice. The yard settled back into quiet, but though everyone seemed calm, there was a knot of curiosity pressed down in each of them.
Xiao Ran had never been well liked in the yard, and so on hearing that he was hurt — gravely hurt — some of those present were secretly pleased. Not long after, Ouyang Zhuang arrived too, though his look shifted about and he came over to Wei He's side pretending to be calm. "Senior Brother Wei, I have just been to Taihe Hall… Senior Brother Xiao is there being treated. Would you like to know how he came by his
wound?" he said low, all mystery.
"…" Wei He said nothing and went on with his own work, building up the fire and heating the sand and gravel used for skin grinding, to cleanse them. Ouyang Zhuang blinked, waited a while and found that Wei He made no move, and at once grew impatient.
Compared with Cheng Shaojiu and Old Man Zheng his patience was worse yet; he could not hold it in for more than a few moments before he began to shake out all his so-called inside news. "Brother He, you have too much patience for a man! I tell you, I asked the house guards at home. At the fist-off yesterday both the Zhao family and the Tian family of the Seven Family League took a fancy to Xiao Ran on the spot, and both invited him to join their house in some post."
"And the upshot… that fellow Xiao Ran…"
"Speak properly." Wei He gave him a glare. Such loose ways of naming people, with no sense of who was senior — if others heard it, it would do Wei He no harm, but Ouyang Zhuang would surely get a name for dishonouring his seniors, which would serve him badly later on.
"Oh… all right, all right. Senior Brother Xiao. Satisfied?" Ouyang Zhuang pursed his lips, unconcerned. He was young and cared for none of it. "I tell you, Xiao Ran refused the Tian family and chose to join the Zhao family. And the upshot… today word came out that he had been ambushed and gravely hurt, and by a masked man at that. Tsk tsk… This Zhao family and this Tian family have always been rivals; but for the agreement within the Seven Family League there would never
be any league at all. Senior Brother Xiao has had pure misfortune fall on him. Pitiful… too pitiful."
"The way I see it, if he had chosen the Tian family, the Zhao family would surely have struck as well. Nobody is willing to see a young elite like him — a talent with such potential — join his own rival.
"In fact the Zhao family would certainly have been on their guard against an ambush, but the Tian family moved too fast; there is no guarding against a thief for a thousand days, as you would say…" After that he said a few more things that smacked of gloating, no doubt because in daily life Xiao Ran wore an air of being first and holding the whole world second, and was offhand with everyone, which had annoyed Ouyang Zhuang a good deal.
But Wei He had already stopped listening. His head was full of what Ouyang Zhuang had just said. Xiao Ran, being too great a talent, had been courted by two families at the fist-off, and the upshot… he accepted one, and was ambushed by the other, and was gravely hurt…
So being a talent carried its risks after all. He had thought the risks small; now it seemed… they were not small at all… A great tree that grows clear of the wood must bear the wind and rain from every quarter alone. 'Is this the price of towering above the forest?' Wei He thought soberly. He had been meaning to make his progress known quickly, thinking he might even earn the name of a young talent.
But now it seemed… 'Best to keep it hidden a while longer. No hurry…' Wei He had a bad feeling about it. Being a talent was not so easy a role to fill.
