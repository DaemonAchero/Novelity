Strength and burst power like this — even he could not manage it. This had to be an expert of the second breakthrough in qi and blood! Against an expert like that, even in a straight clash, he was far from a match. Yet what he had never expected was that the other man, who could plainly beat him, had chosen to play a dark trick. You are already this strong and you still stoop to dark tricks — have you no shame at all!?
The bald man wanted to weep and had no tears; his heart was dead ash. An expert was not fearful; what was fearful was an expert who threw in dark tricks as well. This was simply leaving a man no road to live! Quicklime in his face, both eyes burning in agony, the bald man's mind flashed through thought after thought — but he had not much time left to recall and regret anything.
Pu. A fist took him squarely in the brow and sent him flying backwards, blood running from every opening of his face; he struck the trunk of a great tree, fell, and did not move again. Silence came back over everything around them.
"I truly meant to go… ah…" Since he had struck, he could only wipe this band out to the last man. There was no helping it; that was the way of this world. Wei He drew back his fist, picked a few stones up off the ground, and hurled them one after another, hard, at the corpses around him. Every one of them must have a good one laid on his brow to make sure.
Once he was sure they were all dead beyond doubt, he began to search the bodies. These ordinary mountain bandits turned out to carry a good deal of useful stuff: three pouches of what looked like knockout powder, four pouches of poison powder, five dry-ration pouches and water skins. A few gold beans — a unit of currency smaller even than gold leaf.
There was also one small pouch of a special sort, and a small sallow booklet. "What is this?" Wei He first pinched the little pouch carefully, opened it and looked in from far off: inside was a light-grey medicinal powder, and what powder it was he did not know — it would need identifying when he got back. He carefully tied the pouch shut again and tied it up together with the poison-powder pouches.
Then he threw the pile of food and drink into his waterproof pack, kept apart from the python meat, and last of all picked up the little booklet taken off the bald bandit. Its paper was sallow and its corners long since badly worn, and on the cover, in crooked writing, were a few large characters: Ledger of the Li Family Medicine Shop. "A ledger?" Wei He flipped it open quickly with the point of his knife.
Inside it was no ledger at all, but a set of training diagrams with forms drawn in full. Writing on the left, figures on the right, word by word, described with real precision. As for why the cover said ledger — probably the bald man's crude disguise. Wei He had no time to leaf through more; he put it away quickly and set about dealing with the corpses.
He heaped all the bodies together and slashed the wounds made by his fists to ruin with the knife, so that no one could judge from the style of the martial art that it had been Mountain Fist. At the same time, the smell of blood that slashing the wounds released would draw beasts quickly to gnaw the bodies, which would make the injuries and the cause of death that much harder to tell. That done, Wei He left the spot quickly, sweeping his footprints away with a large branch as he went.
After he had gone some distance he was about to throw the branch away when a great fat man in white clothes came walking towards him head-on. This was the road down the mountain. This was Shaoyang Mountain.
White clothes, a great fat man. Wei He's heart gave a sharp jump and he looked the man over closely. The fat man was over one metre eighty tall and more than a metre across, almost like a ball. But what was strange was that on his face and hands, wherever skin showed, there was none of the white plump softness of an ordinary fat man — instead it was all
solid muscle the colour of flesh. The impression he gave was of a fat man made of scarcely any fat at all, of nothing but stacked muscle. It was only that his build was spindle-shaped, so that from a distance he looked like a fat man.
