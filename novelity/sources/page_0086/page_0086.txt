That was the way of poor households, to save the lamp oil; now that the two of them had a little to spare they still kept up this good habit from before. Sitting on the step under the eaves at the house door. Wei Ying picked up her bowl and looked at her brother.
"Just now, when Sister Zhang came over to chat, she told me something. I thought — it has nothing to do with you, but I still wanted to say it to you."
"What is it?" Wei He looked up, puzzled. "Another family they want to match you with?"
"What? No." Wei Ying said, flustered and annoyed. "It is that Li Huanhuan in your yard. Sister Zhang told me to keep an eye on you — not to get too close to
her."
"Li Huanhuan?" Wei He took a moment to recall whose name that was. She was the new girl who worked for nothing in the yard, the one who had taken over from his second sister Wei Ying in looking after Old Man Zheng. She was passably pretty, and though her figure was not the equal of his second sister's, she had her own curves about her.
His impression of her was not deep, since his whole mind was on bitter training, and Li Huanhuan mostly liked to hover and busy herself about the disciples of good family, so the two had had little to do with each other. "What about her?" Wei He asked, and bent his head to take up a chopstick-load of tomato fried with egg.
"She… Sister Zhang says she has dealings with a good many of the men in the yard…" Wei Ying felt that this was like speaking ill of someone behind her back, and did not like the taste of it.
"Dealings?" Wei He was taken aback, and then understood.
"Mm. Bad dealings, whatever they are. Sister Zhang has seen her come out of different disciples' rooms more than once. You had better be careful; do not get
tangled up with that Li Huanhuan." Wei Ying said low. "Whether it is true or not, it is better to keep your distance."
"I know." Wei He thought back and saw that it was so: Li Huanhuan did greet some of the brothers rather too closely. In a yard of sweating, muscle-bound men who drove themselves at their training every day, with the qi and blood running high, if the woman truly meant to se-
-duce a man, she could very easily have her way. Wei He noted it in his mind, meaning to watch and take care later. He raised his bowl and went back to shovelling his food, but before he had taken many mouthfuls there came knocking at the courtyard gate. Thump, thump, thump.
"Is anyone there?" A deep male voice came in from outside.
"Who is it?" Wei He rose and went to the door.
"I am the brother of your neighbour Xu Chun next door. Open the door, if you please." the man said loudly. Wei He's eyes narrowed. He paused, then turned and signalled to Wei Ying to go into the inner room and hide.
Wei Ying understood at once, rose quietly, went quickly into the inner room and shut the door. Wei He was the only one left in the courtyard. He stood before the gate, put out a hand and gently drew back the bar, and pulled it open. A powerfully built man stood outside.
The man wore a full beard, a white upper garment, and his sleeves were rolled up, showing thick forearms. Seeing Wei He open the door, he narrowed his eyes and swept them over him. "Forgive the disturbance. My elder sister is Xu Chun; I am Xu Wei. Some days ago my sister was savagely beaten at home, and now her eyes are blind, so that she cannot see and can hardly see to her own living.
"So I have come to ask the neighbours whether they heard any sounds from next door that day." The man's breath came long and slow, his heartbeat unhurried, and the lines of his muscles were remarkably smooth, with no bulk about them. Above all, his eyes swept left and right as though… as though he were looking to see whether anyone else was about!
The alarm in Wei He's heart rose another notch. "Sounds from next door? I heard nothing. I am about to rest. If there is nothing else, you may go." he said, feigning impatience, and made as if to close the door.
