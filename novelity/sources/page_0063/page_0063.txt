Old Man Zheng was half leaning in one corner, seated in a rosewood armchair whose armrests were polished to a shine, and one of the brothers was already waiting before him. Wei He went over; Old Man Zheng did not speak, only flicked his hand slightly to point out a spot. Wei He walked over and stood, waiting quietly. After a while two more disciples came in and likewise stood to one side and waited.
Last of all Eldest Senior Brother Zhao Hong came in. "Everyone is here." Old Man Zheng said mildly. "Hong, tell them the general situation. Since they have come under my gate, they must have a clear picture in their heads. The state of things in this city grows more wrong by the day, and everyone must be careful."
"Yes." Zhao Hong bowed his head respectfully.
Only then did Old Man Zheng pick up a pickled chicken foot from beside him and begin to gnaw it unhurriedly, as though meaning to watch and put in a word here and there. Zhao Hong cleared his throat and looked over the four brothers present. "I have said this twice before; today I will say it again for those of you who have not heard it."
He paused, glanced at Old Man Zheng, and finding he had no wish to correct him, went on. "The other disciples who have not entered the gate do not count as men of our Mountain Fist; they are only apprentices. Apprentices, apprentices — their life and death is not within our protection. Even if they walk out and about, no one will take them for men of our Mountain Fist. But you are different: you have all entered the gate, and you have all broken through once in qi and blood."
Step outside and you can stand for Mountain Fist too. So today, here, I will set out the rules for everyone and speak of what is good and what is not." All four of them, Wei He included, nodded to show they were listening closely. Zhao Hong nodded slightly, satisfied, and went on. "In this city, apart from the small gangs and little sects that change hands every two or three days, and need not be heeded, what you
really have to watch for are the Three Gangs of the Three Gangs and Two Sects. The Three Gangs are the Shanchuan Gang, the Blood Clothes Gang, and the Tongcheng Gang. These three great gangs have great numbers, but the outer members need not be paid much mind; only when you meet core members should you be a little careful. Do not provoke them lightly. In a moment I will tell you the marks by which the core members of the three gangs can be known.
"Also, when in your daily rounds you pass houses where the sickness is, do not linger; keep as far off as you can. The plague in the city has grown worse and worse lately…" He went on with a set of cautions about the plague. Wei He listened quietly on the side, right to the end. Zhao Hong described at length what marks the plague left on those who caught it.
Only then did Old Man Zheng cut in, unable to help himself. "Enough, enough. Come straight to the last and most important point."
"Yes." Zhao Hong nodded quickly. "Feiye City has the Three Gangs and Two Sects, but all of those are in the Outer City. The Inner City is quite different from the place where we live. Most of the businesses and land there are held by seven families, and so they are commonly
called the Seven Family League. This Seven Family League, and Hong Family Fort outside the city — this is what I wanted to say — are the powers in Feiye City that must not be provoked. When you are out and about, if you meet men of the Inner City's Seven Family League or of Hong Family Fort, give way as much as you can and do not come to blows."
The Seven Family League… Wei He's heart stirred. He recalled that on the escort run Great Escort Master Cheng Kai had also spoken of the grudge between Hong Family Fort and the city governor's
family. Likely one of the seven families in that League was the governor's family. After a long rambling spell of it, the several of them had plainly found out Eldest Senior Brother Zhao Hong's failing: a man who said nothing as a rule, but once he opened his mouth, droned on and on.
In the end Old Man Zheng again could not help cutting him off, and sent the four of them straight out to train. Going out the door, Wei He looked back and saw Eldest Senior Brother with his head down, being scolded by Old Man Zheng. He could not help finding it a little funny. Back in his own corner of the yard, Wei He saw that Cheng Shaojiu was already waiting there ahead of him.
