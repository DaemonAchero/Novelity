Cheng Shaojiu clenched his teeth, and never knew how he had got back to his room to rest. The next day he went about in a daze; telling himself he would go and think of a way, he changed his clothes and headed off in the direction of the Mountain Fist yard. There he stood blankly in his own corner, staring at the iron-sand basin with its charcoal fire lit under it, and did not move a muscle. The senior and junior brothers who were usually all brother this and brother that with him said not a word, and none of them came over to greet him.
And the three of them — Xiao Ran, Jiang Yan, Jiang Su — had long since drifted away from him, and had little time for him now. Looking around, he knew in his heart that everyone must have heard about the agency. "Brother Cheng." All at once Wei He's figure came up to him.
"I heard yesterday that the agency is in trouble. If there is anything you need, just say the word." Cheng Shaojiu and he were sworn friends, and he himself hung his name with the Yonghe Agency; now that the agency was in trouble, he feared Cheng Shaojiu could not hold things together, and had come over at once to ask. Cheng Shaojiu raised his head and looked at Wei He.
He opened his mouth to ask about money, then laughed bitterly to himself. Seven hundred taels of gold, no less… and he knew exactly how little his junior brother Wei He earned: at most forty taels of gold a month, all told, from the places he hangs his name with. Where would a man find that kind of money?
The words were on the tip of his tongue; Cheng Shaojiu was silent a moment and swallowed them again, then put a smile on his face. "It's nothing, I can handle it. Don't worry, Xiao He." Better to keep it to himself than to say it out and give Wei He something to worry about too. This was his own Cheng family's business, after all, and had nothing to do with Wei He.
Even if he did say it, Wei He could do nothing but worry; so better not to say it. "Rest easy." Cheng Shaojiu clapped Wei He on the shoulder again and smiled. "If there is anything you need help with, say it straight. You and I are brothers, and I am still Cheng He's godfather. Don't keep it all bottled up," Wei He said gravely. He had been robbing men of late and done well at it, and his own earnings he had saved without spending much, so that he had put away a good fifteen hundred-odd
gold notes, and was just thinking how he might be able to help. "I know. Rest easy." The more Cheng Shaojiu heard him talk like that, the less he wanted to drag Wei He down with him. His own family's business, he would handle himself; how could he drag a brother into it? "Only, this month's pay will have to be suspended," Cheng Shaojiu said with a bitter smile.
"That's all right. Your side comes first." Wei He nodded. Seeing that Cheng Shaojiu really did seem to be all right, he slowly set his mind at ease. "You really are all right?" "Really, no problem." Cheng Shaojiu showed him a brilliant smile.
He knew the people around them were all watching him; and the more such a moment, the less he could show fear or panic. His uncle had already fallen; if he too showed fear and let his true state be seen, then the old rivals of the Cheng family would not stand by gaping. They would come like starving wolves, and with the Cheng family weakened they would fall on it from every side and tear off its flesh.
== Chapter 49 · Pulled Back (Part 1) ==
The Yonghe business: after Wei He had asked the man himself, he went round to the agency too, to see Cheng Zhengxing, who had been taken ill.
It was a pity he could not help with the agency's troubles either. Cheng Zhengxing was an old man of the third breakthrough in qi and blood; old as he was, his foundation was still there, and his strength was no trifling thing. And this business had laid even a man of the third breakthrough low. Now it came down to what Cheng Shaojiu could do. Wei He asked again and again, and every answer he got was that there was no problem.
Cheng Shaojiu seemed to have a hold on it. Wei He let his worry ease a little and threw himself back into his hunting life on the mountain. He had now halted the gathering of red-cloud flowers, and Five Ridges Palm was brought to its perfection; all that was lacking was for the Realm Breaking Bead to be brought to perfection once more.
