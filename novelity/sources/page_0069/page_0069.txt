The Inner City. Flower Boat Street. In the Inner City there were no wards as such, only different districts — five great districts in all. Among them Flower Boat Street was a gold-swallowing pit in the truest sense, and the busiest, most bustling place in all of Feiye City.
From the Outer City to the Inner City was almost two worlds apart. A great flood of carriages and horses and jostling heads ran ceaselessly along Flower Boat Street. On either side the shops and storefronts were all red lantern and green wine, and every sort of trick for drawing custom had been put up before their doors.
In the air above hung all kinds of floating lanterns, tied with silk threads and written over with advertisements. Gambling dens, taverns, houses of song and dance, jewel towers, oriole halls — they were to be seen at every turn. Amid that din of voices it hardly seemed like the dark of evening at all. On the second floor of the Cloud Fall Restaurant.
Cheng Shaojiu sat by the window, his face faintly reddened by the lantern light from outside. There was a flicker of excitement in his eyes as he watched the flower boats parading below. The flower boats were a procession in which the loveliest flower girls of the place sat on boats and were carried through the streets to show off their talents. The boats were carried by men, decked about with countless fresh flowers, and so they had come to be called flower boats.
"Big as I am, this is the first time I have ever seen the flower boats parade in the Inner City! A trip well worth making, well worth making!" Cheng Shaojiu could not help exclaiming. At his table sat Jiang Su, Jiang Yan, and Xiao Ran. Jiang Su had a smile at the corners of her mouth and gave nothing away; she only watched the extraordinary bustle outside, and there was a warmth in her eyes as well.
Such splendour was seldom seen even by her. Xiao Ran had been here before and was rather more used to it, though watching the flower girls of such delicate beauty, a hint of desire showed in him, and his gaze tended to rest on the sensitive places that showed and then did not. Only Jiang Yan was plain of face, a white paper fan in his hand, his hair bound with white cloth, and at the tail of the fan and on the hem of his robe gold-set jade
ornaments — plainly a man of a different temper from the ordinary young master. He and Cheng Shaojiu were both dressed as young gentlemen today, long sleeves and long robes, plain white cloth bound over their hair; but in dress and ornament the two were set apart at a glance. "Speaking of which, Brother Xiao Ran has broken through successfully this time, and we have never once properly celebrated it for him. So I have invited the three of
you to the Cloud Fall Restaurant, and taken the chance for a small gathering of us all." Jiang Yan said with a smile.
"You are too kind, too kind. It was only luck, a lucky breakthrough, ha ha…" Xiao Ran's mouth curved, and he raised his cup and drained it with Jiang Yan. "My thanks to Senior Brother Jiang for the feast!"
"Brother Xiao has limitless potential. There may come a day when I have some request to make of you; what is a little feast like this between us now?" Jiang Yan said politely. After the two of them had gone through their courtesies, Jiang Su drew her gaze back from the side and spoke up.
