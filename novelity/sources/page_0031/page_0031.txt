But all of that was Old Man Zheng's talk. This was the first time Wei He had truly faced an opponent. "Hei!" The man lunged forward, both hands actually coming for Wei He's shoulders to seize them. Sudden as the move was, in Wei He's eyes it was not quick. He realized of a sudden that since his qi and blood had reached the Ox-Hide level, his reactions and responses, and the keenness of all five senses, had risen by varying degrees.
It seemed to be a passive effect of stronger qi and blood. Wei He shifted his body aside and slipped the lunge. Startled as he was, his body answered far quicker than the other man's, and he gave way as easy as that.
Then his right fist tapped the man lightly on the back. Bang. The man went down as easy as that. "Two more," Cheng Shaojiu laughed from the side.
Two more guards came up at once. Wei He slipped them with ease and tapped each one down. He was well measured about it: once he had found that his qi and blood were stronger and his strength likely greater, he held back from striking back with full force, and put the men down with half his strength.
"Not bad, not bad! Your real fighting is a good deal better than mine was." Cheng Shaojiu came over clapping. "Well? Do you feel the change after breaking through?" he asked, smiling. He helped up the several guards who had been put down and sent them off.
"I feel it." Wei He nodded.
"There it is." Cheng Shaojiu smiled. "Old Zheng at Mountain Fist isn't going to teach you any real fighting. If you want to fight, you can come train here any day of the week. My uncle's escort agency has plenty of men."
"You are too kind, Senior Brother." Wei He had not finished before Cheng Shaojiu said, smiling:
"Don't be in a hurry to refuse. You have properly got through the door now, and the qi and blood of an Ox-Hide level man is far fuller than an ordinary person's. If you
put on leather armour, then even two or three strong men with weapons in hand would not get near you. So… your standing is different from before as well. Do you understand?" Cheng Shaojiu winked at him.
"Standing?" Wei He did not follow.
"Just so. Come with me." Cheng Shaojiu led Wei He deeper into the house.
Through a large room with doors at either end was another training ground, hung with sandbags of every kind. A few men here and there, bare-shouldered, were drilling among the sandbags. "This is the training ground; if you come and work for me, it is all yours to use. Besides that, board and food free for the month, along with a base payment of one stone of white rice, ten catties of meat, and a hundred taels of silver.
And if there is an escort job now and then, fifty taels for a short run, a hundred to five hundred for the long ones. What do you say?" Cheng Shaojiu's sudden offer took Wei He by surprise, though not beyond reason. He had had a guess or two in mind before ever coming here, and the sparring just now had served to prove what he could do. Once qi and blood reached the Ox-Hide level, a man was indeed different from ordinary people.
"You can go home and think it over well, and compare it against what other people offer you. Having broken through and got in, you count as a true able hand now, and before long plenty of people will come asking for you and naming prices." Cheng Shaojiu smiled.
"Is that so?" Wei He was a little at a loss.
"Of course. In times like these, any martial master with some skill is watched. Train up a few good hands and they will be hired away in the blink of an eye." Cheng Shaojiu's smile faded and he sighed. "This world of ours — you know it as well as I do. Nobody has it easy. Have you heard that outside the city, Hong Family Fort is up to something big? Nobody is allowed to pass along that stretch of the government road by them any more."
"Hong Family Fort?"
"Mm. The greatest of the powerful families outside Feiye City is the Hong family. They built themselves an earthen fort out there, called Hong Family Fort; it just about counts as a small city." Cheng Shaojiu nodded and explained.
