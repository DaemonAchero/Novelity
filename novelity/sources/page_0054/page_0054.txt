Cheng Kai saw that Wei He had come out too, and walked over, smiling. "This is the entertainment we hold on every run."
"Entertainment?"
"That is right." Cheng Kai stroked his little moustache and said with a smile, "On escort we are not allowed wine and not allowed gambling, and on the road
there is nothing to do but chat. Often a man is not even allowed to speak loudly or for long. And after a while he cannot help feeling pent up." He paused and went on, "So every time we come near the destination we hold a bout among ourselves. For one thing, since wine is barred, a fight wakes you up and gets the qi and blood moving.
For another, it settles where each man stands among us. Who is strong, who is weak — better that everyone has some idea, so that when trouble comes no one is at a loss." Wei He understood at once. All this talk came down to one thing: wherever there are men, rank cannot be escaped.
Just then a great shout came from the middle of the ground. Two escort men had crashed together. One was a tall escort man with an iron staff; when he swung it there was method in every move — attack and defence, advance and retreat, all in measure, not a thing out of place.
The other was short and solid, bare-handed, his punches quick and fierce, with a whistling power to them. His force looked good too. After the two had grappled close a while, the one with the staff had too little distance to work in, and he was not quick enough to open the gap either; in the end he lost a move, took a blow on the shoulder, and yielded.
"Bai Shengfeng's horizontal-slant staff is reckoned decent even among our lot. He once made a name with it, one man and one staff,
knocking down five toughs in Nanshan Ward at one go. And still he loses to Li Er." Cheng Kai gave his judgement. Wei He watched. These two were only run of the mill; he could not judge their force, but their speed of hand and speed of reaction were plainly nothing special.
By his reckoning they were only a little above common folk, with some method in their fighting and that was all. Then, after Li Er had won a bout, he was not yet satisfied and fought again, and won again. Only now did this man's strong point show: his endurance was long, and two bouts in a row left him not the least tired.
"Well? Do you want to go up and try? Li Er's Nine Mountain Fist is no weak thing; even I have to keep my wits about me to win."
Cheng Kai smiled over at Wei He.
"Good." Wei He said little, tossed off his cloak, and stepped up a few paces into the ground. "Wei He." He cupped his fist in salute. "Li Er." The short, thick-set Li Er had a fierce look, and in the firelight a scar could be seen through his left eyebrow. Muscle lay on him in slabs,
slab upon slab, and the look of him was fierce indeed. When the two had saluted, they stood still where they were. Wei He was watching closely: he had seen two bouts already and had some idea of Li Er's ways, but now that he was actually in the ground, he was in no hurry.
