"Will you listen properly for a moment…" Wei He's voice rose without his meaning it to.
"And what use is listening? Even if you talk a flower into bloom it will not cover the fact that you are that kind of man. Enough of your nonsense!" Cheng Jing led off with a high-raised leg and brought it down. Whup!
The blur of her leg flashed down like an axe chopping. That strike was not a whit slower than Wei He's own. So she too had broken through once in qi and blood!
== Chapter 19 · Ideas (Part 1) ==
Under the torchlight, Cheng Jing drove her leg down with a dull sound of air torn open. A fighter who had broken through once in qi and blood like himself, her leg work was square and straight, her movements unhurried and composed; though it was half a surprise attack, her posture and force were precise
in every degree, and in every respect formidable. Caught off guard, Wei He knew he had let her words goad him and had lost the first move. He had no choice but to block before him with both fists, guarding head and chest. Bang!
The long leg, trailing the reek of mud from her shoe sole, slammed hard into Wei He's forearms. A leg's force is as a rule far greater than an arm's: even though Cheng Jing was a woman and a little weaker, the strength of her leg still beat Wei He's two arms. The kick landed square and drove Wei He back several paces.
"Is that all!" A flicker of contempt crossed Cheng Jing's eyes, and she was about to press her advantage when suddenly a gust of wind whistled past not far off. Ah!
An escort man standing on the outer edge gave a sudden shriek, fell forward on the ground, and clawed at it desperately with both hands, tearing up great clods of earth and turf. It did no good; some vast force in the darkness dragged him swiftly into the dark, and in a moment the cry had gone far off and then cut off short.
All fell quiet again. The escort men forgot the bout and hurriedly raised torches to light the place. But all the light showed were the finger-scratches left in the ground and a pool of fresh blood. "What was that thing!" "Old Sun has been dragged off!"
"How can that be?! Sun Zhu's strength is one of the best among us!"
"I saw a black shape drag him off by both legs and run backwards! I tried to hit it with something, but it was too fast — I had not even taken it in before…" That escort man was shaking all over, whether from fear or from regret that he had not saved the man in time.
Wei He and Cheng Jing forgot their bout and hurried over. By then Cheng Kai had crouched down at the spot to look. His face was cold and stern, his eyes grave; he reached out and felt the ground behind the claw marks and the blood, then brought his fingers to his nose and sniffed. "It is the Black Madman! Everyone get inside, quick!!" His face changed all at once and he shouted.
Ah!! Before his words were out there came another scream from the other side. Another escort man was seized by the legs and vanished into the dark in an instant. "Die!!" One of the new escort men flung his torch at the black shape.
But he was a good deal too slow. The torch only had time to light a small part of the black shape's body. In that fleeting glimpse they made out a strange creature whose head was black mingled with red.
