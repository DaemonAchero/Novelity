Time trickled by. Before long, a set of dull, heavy footsteps came from the inner room. Everyone perked up, eyes on the door of the inner room, waiting for Master Zheng to come out. Soon Zheng Fugui, in a grey-white unlined gown and black mandarin jacket, his hands behind his back, came slowly into the inner yard.
The edges of his cloth shoes still carried a little black mud, and there was no telling where he had been so early in the morning. Zheng Fugui still had his sleepy, bleary look; he drifted into the inner yard and looked up at the few of them. His somewhat clouded eyes made a circuit — seemingly looking at the people, but in fact seeming to be thinking of something else. He was rather out of sorts, his mind elsewhere.
Lately Zheng Fugui had been like this, coming to the hall sometimes and not others. The wrinkles on his face had gradually multiplied, and even his favourite tea cakes and snacks he only picked at before leaving them. Of late the situation in Feiye City had been changing, and the conflict between the Incense Gathering Sect and the Inner City had grown and grown. For all that the Incense Gathering Sect was a wildly muddled organisation, living strength still poured into it in great quantities.
And some power was quietly backing it in the shadows, so that it could stand against the Inner City's Seven Family League. Zheng Fugui had lately been running about because of this too, investigating, trying to grasp the situation. A few days ago he had gone to see his daughter; the girl still would not take advice at all and insisted on doing what she wanted. Zheng Fugui had argued with her bitterly, to no effect.
He had in fact come to Feiye City decades ago and quietly built up his holdings. Now in his fifties, he still had not much to his name — all of it because of this daughter. Every bit of wealth and resource he earned was squandered wholesale by the spendthrift girl. And it was precisely because his daughter was such a spendthrift that Zheng Fugui had formed his daily habit of counting every copper and pinching every penny.
The daughter had a child of his now — his grandson — and he had thought that with a child she would settle her heart. But no, still the same as ever. She took charge of nothing, running out day after day to gad about. Nearly thirty, and still so feckless. Zheng Fugui could harden his heart against anyone, but this daughter alone…… petted from childhood, and now…… it was too far gone to mend.
These days, besides busying himself over a way out, what troubled Zheng Fugui most was his daughter's side of things. At least half his energy went to wiping up after her. The things that girl stirred up…… the very thought gave Zheng Fugui a headache.
Were it not for clearing up her messes, he would not have worn himself as ragged as this. Only after he had this daughter did he understand that in this world not everything can be solved with force; there is much that no amount of force can do…… Coming back to himself, Zheng Fugui looked at the few disciples in the inner yard.
"It is rare that all of you are here today; it happens I have some things to announce." The several of them stopped what they were doing and gathered round, listening quietly. Whatever else he was, Zheng Fugui was a martial master who had entered the jin, so even Jiang Yan did not dare be careless with him. He was a Jiang family man, but the Jiang family had more than one junior; measured against a jin-entering martial master, his standing fell far short.
Unless his father came, then perhaps they could just about meet as equals. The few of them came close and waited quietly for Zheng Fugui to speak. "In a few days, the Mountain Fist hall means to move outside the city. Those of you willing to go as well, make your preparations. Those who are not, tell me in advance."
They were all taken aback. They had foreseen that the hall might have to move, but not that the day would come so suddenly. As for the move, Jiang Yan naturally could not go. Zhao Hong and Zhang Lu would certainly go. And Jiang Su — that would depend on her choice. The Jiang family was no small household after all.
As for Wei He, he thought a moment and decided to go along. With Master Zheng, a jin-entering martial master, about, it was safer than dragging Second Sister around on his own.
