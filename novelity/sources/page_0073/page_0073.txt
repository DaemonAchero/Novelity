== Chapter 25 · Trouble (Part 1) ==
Wei He paid all of that no mind.
Once he had set his second sister up in the bun shop, he went back to his old rhythm of bitter martial training. In a blink it was the start of winter. Still no rain had come; everywhere the ground was parched and cracked. In the small courtyard of the Wei house.
Wei He, stripped to the waist, fists guarding his head, faced a sandbag hung from the great tree trunk and hammered at it blow upon blow. Each punch was like a heavy mallet, one after another, without pause — thirty punches in a single breath. No punch was quick, but every one was uncannily steady and heavy. After that burst of full effort, deep fist prints stood out all over the sandbag.
Wei He picked up the waterskin beside him and took a slow sip of warm water, holding it in his mouth and swallowing it little by little. Resting, he looked down at the Realm Breaking Bead pattern on his chest. When he had looked that morning, one last small gap remained; now it was utterly complete. 'Full at last.'
A stone fell from Wei He's heart. He had feared that the Bead might fill the first time and not the second. At the speed it accumulated — slow as a snail — he could hardly have borne it. But it was well, and at last it was full. On a thought he tried the same method as last time, using his mind to pierce the Bead at his chest as if pricking a balloon.
It was no use. The Bead stood firm as ever. 'Just as I thought… I must wait until the qi and blood are complete as well.' Wei He sighed inwardly and tried no more. His qi and blood had only just passed the middle stretch; he could feel the thickness in his body, but it had not come to the point of being filled to the brim.
In truth, at an ordinary pace, of the disciples in the Mountain Fist yard ninety-nine out of a hundred could only take the slow water-mill way of gathering qi and blood, however briskly they went about it. He had broken through to Ox-Hide at the start of the year; barely half a year had passed since — how could he have ground up to the top so soon? Only a man like Xiao Ran, born with a strong body and an extraordinary gift, could break through in one rush.
For everyone else, that stretch of gathering qi and blood was never less than a year and a half. At the thought Wei He pressed down the restlessness in him and grew quiet. "It has been barely half a year for me, and I have already gathered over half my qi and blood. In fact that is a fairly quick pace. Judged by native gifts, most of the men in the yard are at much the same stage as I am."
Among the Ox-Hide disciples of his own generation — the several who had broken through at the same time — some gathered qi and blood slower than he did and some faster, but the gaps were not great. Plainly his was the normal pace. Wei He was by habit a man of few words who watched closely; now, calling it to mind and making the comparison, he grew easy in his heart.
When his daily practice was done Wei He put on his clothes, and in unusually good spirits decided to go out and see how his second sister's business was doing. The bun shop had been open some ten days now, and it was the morning, the best hour of all for selling buns. Wei He went out and walked along the riverside street toward the Mountain Fist yard. The Wei Family Bun Shop stood between the Mountain Fist yard and the small Wei courtyard, convenient for minding.
Before the bun shop just now Wei Ying was spinning about with work, selling buns and rushing to set a new batch in the steamer at the same time. Queue after queue of people stood in a long line before the shop, waiting their turn.
