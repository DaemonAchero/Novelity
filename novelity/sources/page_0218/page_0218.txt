He looked around at the several of them and smiled. "You may pair off and fight, so that I can see your progress. The winners fight last, and for the final winner your master will set up a reward."
"Master Zheng, is setting it up that way not a little unfair?" Jiang Yan stepped forward. "However the bouts are arranged, Senior Brother Zhao Hong comes first and holds down everyone else. Doesn't the reward then certainly go to Senior Brother?" Zheng Fugui shook his head inwardly: Jiang Yan's birth was uncommon and his aptitude passable, but his outlook was a little small. "Who else thinks it unfair?"
"Your disciple thinks it unfair too." Zhao Hong stepped out too and said calmly. He was a man of indifferent temper by nature; since Jiang Yan had called him out, he simply stepped up and said it straight. "Anyone else?" Zheng Fugui's brows drew together. "Your disciple thinks it unfair too." This time it was Wei He, one step forward, his face calm. "Oh?" Zheng Fugui narrowed his eyes at Wei He; he had not expected Wei He to step up.
Only, he had not been paying attention just now; now that he looked closely, his eyes suddenly widened a little. "You!?" His body gave a shudder. Suddenly he threw a fist forward. The punch was unbelievably swift, its power bursting; the fist was pitch black, wrapped in threads of jin force.
The fist closed on Wei He at speed; its wind lifted the hems of the clothes of everyone around, and the strong force sprayed out a stinging, needle-like breath that made Jiang Yan, Zhao Hong and the other two wince about the face. In the flash of a spark. Bang!!
Wei He raised one hand and caught that fist. "Teacher, is that enough?" The hand that had caught the fist was pitch black, like black iron. "Not enough!!" Zheng Fugui's face trembled, and in his eyes was a wild joy beyond belief.
"Again!!" He laughed madly, closed in and struck his two fists together. Both men opened their jin force to the full and crashed together with a boom. Boom!!! Dull, violent iron fists colliding; in a short moment the two traded dozens of blows.
The watching Jiang Yan, Zhao Hong, Jiang Su, Zhang Lu — even standing near was beyond them; they were forced back and away. All four looked on appalled at the two figures in the yard. An outrageous guess they could hardly believe gradually rose in their hearts. A few breaths later.
A bang. The two sprang back and each took his stand. "Teacher, is it enough now?" Wei He's two fists were wholly black, the muscles of his body visibly swelling a size, even his face showing fascia and blood vessels; he looked terrible to see.
Zheng Fugui looked at his own hands, going faintly numb, then suddenly threw back his head and laughed. His laughter was free and full, as if he meant to let out all the resentment pent up over these many years.

Wei He had broken through. And broken through to the third breakthrough in qi and blood! When that guess was admitted in person from Master Zheng's mouth, the few in the inner yard woke as from a dream, as though all that had just happened were an illusion.
But reality is no illusion; it is real. However hard they found it to believe, the fact was the fact. Zheng Fugui had crossed hands with Wei He at the Iron-Hide level, the two exchanging dozens of moves swift as lightning, without a win or a loss.
