In no long time he vanished at the far end of the stone path down the hill. Just then a man shot quickly out of a clump of bushes on the right of the path. It was Wei He, who had just hidden himself. He glanced in the direction the man had gone, rose, and let out a breath. "Clever little tricks, though." A voice came suddenly from behind Wei He.
== Chapter 14 · Mingde (Part 2) ==
Wei He started violently and by reflex hacked backward with his knife.
The blade missed and thudded into a tree trunk, where it stuck fast. Wei He let go of it at once and swung a Mountain Fist hook at the temple of the man behind him. "A mediocre fist art, and a more mediocre man to train it." The man smiled coldly and tapped his right foot lightly on the ground, then stabbed it forward like a viper, quick as lightning.
As he moved, the qi and blood in his whole body surged; his breathing, his heartbeat, his lung-sounds were all much louder than Wei He's. Plainly this man's qi and blood were a stage above Wei He's. The thrust of his foot came without a sound, low and vicious, straight at Wei He's vital part below the waist. In the exchange, though he struck second, he was faster and his force heavier.
If that kick landed, the result would be an end to any sons or grandsons. Puff! In that very instant Wei He's fist opened wide, and a handful of white lime inside it was thrown straight into the man's face. He had not looked for such viciousness from Wei He, and this time his eyes took the lime square on. The pain was fiery.
But this man's will was firm, and his right leg did not stop for an instant; it thrust on, as if he meant to trade ruin for ruin with Wei He. Only, with his eyes blinded he could not follow up and change his move, and the thrust was evaded at the last instant as Wei He leaned back. An end to his sons and grandsons, by a hair.
"You low, despicable man! If you have any guts, fight me one to one!" The man could not hold back from cursing him loudly.
His right leg swept in chain kicks every which way, thudding dully against the trees around. Wei He stepped back a few paces, took the last of his lime powder from his belt pouch, and flung it hard at the man's head from afar. But it was just that throw — the sound he made gave him away, and the man closed in with a sweeping leg. Shh.
The shadow of the leg lashed at his right arm. Behind him stood a tree that took a man's arms to reach around; there was no retreating. Wei He steadied his heart, gathered up his qi and blood, drew his right arm in, and met it with a Mountain Fist straight punch. Thud. Fist and leg struck.
Wei He went over sideways, rolled five or six turns on the ground, then quickly got up, holding his hand. He felt the spot where his fist had met the other's leg swell and ache; the bone was fine, but the flesh would be bruised for sure. The man showed no reaction at all, and came after him still following the sound, lashing out again in wild sweeps like a battle-axe.
Wei He put up Mountain Fist, calmed his heart, kept his noise down, and gave ground. Now and then the man caught up with him and they traded a couple of blows. The man had been bursting with all his strength for some while, and with his damaged eyes he could not put out even close to his real power now; only six or seven parts of his strength was in use.
Wei He, careful and wary, and relying on the other man not being able to see, now and then used his chopping knife as a fist to trade blows with him, drawing blood. After this had gone round a dozen times or more, the man finally began to run out of strength: his breath came faster, his heart beat quicker, and sweat poured from him in quantity, soaking his clothes. Only then did Wei He turn it about and press him step by step, forcing him to keep up the attack he had begun.
The moment the man eased off, Wei He went straight in and attacked with Mountain Fist. Mountain Fist had little advantage in move-for-move counters, but in bursting power it had something of its own.
