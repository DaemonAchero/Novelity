"Senior Sister Jiang asked me to bring this over for her." The sturdy junior sister dropped her bundle honestly on the step and turned and ran. Wei Ying had only just opened the door; before she could say anything there was no sign of the girl. She stuck her head out and looked left and right after her a while, still could not find her, and shut the door with a sigh. "Xiao He, that big brother from last time has brought meat again…"
"…" She might not look it, but she was most certainly a girl… Second Sister, really… Wei He was speechless. Still, he knew Wei Ying did not say it on purpose; she truly did believe the person was a man… And to be fair, had he met the girl for the first time himself, with that rough, bluff, hearty voice of hers, and given that in the boxing yard men and women alike had their pecs well
developed, it would be quite normal not to be able to tell whether the person was man or woman. "Understood. It's from Senior Sister Jiang — just take it in." Wei He answered from inside the room. He had told Wei Ying a few days before that he was going into closed seclusion; unfortunately Wei Ying had no idea at all what closed seclusion meant.
Every so often she still came running over with a question or two. Things like 'Do you want your chamber pot changed?' 'I'll take your quilt out to air; you need rest even when you're tired from seclusion.' 'Didn't you only just have your seclusion this morning?'
'Aren't you going to change your clothes?' 'Come out and wash — I've heated the water for you; eat a little something after and then go back to your seclusion.' That sort of talk, over and over, until it took all Wei He's eloquence to make clear to her what closed seclusion actually meant. Only now had Wei Ying stopped disturbing him so much.
"This is the third time already… Your Senior Sister Jiang is very good to you…" Outside came the rustle of Wei Ying opening the bags. "My, it's that strong-smelling meat again — the wrapping alone looks expensive. Next to the pork jerky I bought before, the colour is ever so much nicer…" Wei Ying had set to dealing with the new meat, talking all the while about how it compared, in detail, with the meat she usually saw.
Ever since Wei He had gone quiet in seclusion she had fallen into the habit of murmuring on like that, half to herself and half, it seemed, for him to hear.
== Chapter 58 · The Way of the World (Part 2) ==
In his room, Wei He looked out through the crack of the window and saw that this too was strange-beast meat. These past days Jiang Su had sent him twenty catties of strange-beast meat, one kind after another, without a break. Her connections and channels far outstripped his: branch-antler deer was not the only strange-beast meat she could buy.
Pieced together from here and there, she had somehow scraped together so much good stuff. Wei He had had his prejudices about the woman; seeing how hard she was working to make amends, his temper had slowly cooled. He turned from the window and pulled open his collar to look: the Realm Breaking Bead at his chest still wanted only a very little.
He sighed, picked up another dried turnip and began slowly tearing and chewing it. 'Still, the breakthrough is only days off now… there's a good deal more strange-beast meat than I reckoned on, and with that I can get the most out of my digestion: every hour of the day, as soon as one lot is digested, straight on to the next.' He was eating six meals a day now, taking strange-beast meat in without let-up.
He had eaten a little when a scream came from Wei Ying outside. Bang — the door flew open and Wei He shot out, and saw a figure toppling off the top of the courtyard wall and falling outside it. Right after came the sound of feet stumbling away at a run.
Wei He could clearly see that a good deal of blood was left on the sharp stones along the top of the wall. He had bought those sharpened stones for the wall himself and had them set all along the top — precisely to guard against thieves.
