But a long wolf howl came drifting from far behind him, and at once it wiped the thought away. Plainly the bloody fight just now had drawn in no few beasts that had caught the smell of blood. Whether that corpse was even still there was now in doubt. Wei He pressed down the restless urge in his heart.
== Chapter 15 · Real Combat (Part 1) ==
"Either way, go back first and think later. The hidden power behind all this is probably still fixed on tracking the demon woman, with no time
for anything else." Wei He thought it over, gritted his teeth, and turned back after all. In the hill woods, by the moonlight, he soon came to the place again, and saw a pack of wild wolves gathered round the corpse, making a feast of it. From a distance he could see only that the body had been hacked apart and was being gnawed at without pause by the hungry pack.
Each of these wolves was a body long as a man and more, with green light in their eyes and long black hair over their whole bodies — a frightening sight. Wei He watched until the remains were carried off in the wolves' jaws and a pack of mole-like creatures flung themselves on the bloodstains on the ground to lick them up. Only then did he quietly turn again and hurry down the hill.
When he got back to the escort train, the men were only just gathering to head back at a quick pace. With night come there was a good deal more danger, but with no goods on them the party moved much faster; and with no goods the odds of being robbed were far lower besides, so the escort men all looked a good deal easier. Wei He pretended to have been out wandering about the area; his whereabouts were unclear, but few men would pay him any mind.
The old escort master who had walked with him did take some notice, but he deliberately made as if he knew nothing. These days every man swept the snow before his own door and let the frost lie on his neighbour's tiles; meddling too much only brought trouble on yourself. The party waited a while longer, then raised torches and set off back at an easy pace. Only, it struck Wei He as odd: parties rarely travelled at night, and did Feiye City not shut its gates at night either?
That was another curious thing. On the way back the men talked and laughed; the goods they had been carrying seemed to have been a great nuisance, and now that they were rid of them everyone was much relaxed. The road back passed with frights but no harm, and they came to the foot of Feiye City.
The gate was indeed shut; all was black save for torches moving to and fro along the wall. But soon someone in the party shouted a few words up. The gate slowly opened a crack and let the train file in. Wei He pressed down his astonishment and said nothing; only once he was through the gate did he let out a breath.
"What is it? Finding it strange?" Chen Shiniu, walking with him, said with a smile. Now that they were about to break up, he made no bones about it and asked straight out.
"A little." Wei He nodded. Was it not said that beasts roamed at night and it was very dangerous?
"There's a saying that beasts come out thick at night and a man is easy prey, so you travel by day. The saying has something to it — only you have to look at
where you are." Chen Shiniu smiled. "This run of ours never goes more than a stone's throw from the city. Robbers are scarce, and the beasts keep even further off. So this job is really very easy."
If that was so, then why would anyone spend money on an escort over such a short distance? Would it not be the same to carry it over yourself?
In the end Wei He never put this doubt into words. However strange the run had been, he collected his pay and left, and that was that. Soon the chief escort master leading the party gave his word: each man was to go to the main agency office the next day, in daylight, to draw the wages for this run. Then they all went their ways.
Wei He lost no time at all in heading home. The Incense Gathering Sect men he had run into that night before were still fresh in his memory. Back home, he washed up quickly, lay down in his clothes, and sank into a heavy sleep.
