Cheng Shaojiu drank a cup of wine and explained. "Your parents were invited to Mingde Temple to do stone carving. With so many masons going together, you cannot be the only one looking into the matter, surely? Why not find the families of the others and look into it together?" "I have looked for them. Eleven masons in all, and the other families want nothing more to do with it." Wei He said calmly. "The Shaoyang Sect… that sect has few men, but those who go abroad from it are said to be very formidable. Do not provoke them lightly. Xiao He, if
you must look into it, you had best bring it up with Master Zheng. His information is far readier than ours." Cheng Shaojiu warned him. "I understand." Wei He nodded. "Ah, there is one other channel." Cheng Shaojiu suddenly clapped his hands. "Did you not agree to be an outside guest retainer for the Qingdu Sect? Every month you still draw your allowance from the Qingdu Sect. Why not go and ask them. The Qingdu Sect and the Shaoyang Sect are both outside the city, both of the Two Sects,
and have crossed paths a good many times. Their information is bound to be readier than ours." "The Qingdu Sect… many thanks for the reminder, Brother Cheng." Wei He nodded. Had Cheng Shaojiu not said it, he would never have known that the Qingdu Sect and the Shaoyang Sect had crossed paths. At once he brought to mind that Steward Bai of the Qingdu Sect, the one who had come to invite him last time.
He was said to be second in charge of the Qingdu Sect's holdings in Feiye City. His surname was Bai, his given name Honghou. The man's talk and bearing gave one the impression of a mildness that was always smiling. But a man who could hold this seat, and who carried a second breakthrough in qi and blood besides, could certainly not be simply a mild man. After taking his leave of Cheng Shaojiu, Wei He wasted no time at all; the first thing he did was go to the Qingdu Sect's station in Feiye City.
The Qingdu Sect's station was in Jiujie Ward, a ward a good way off from Mountain Fist, with Nanshan Ward to be crossed on the way. By the time Wei He found the station it was already dusk, and the sun was near to setting. Jiujie Ward was a little more ordinary than Stone Bridge Ward — not so lively, and not so desolate either. On the street one could still see here and there the stubs of incense sticks not yet burned out, planted in clusters in the dry mud at the roadside.
The Qingdu Sect's station stood in the busiest stretch of Jiujie Ward, a general store on its left and a quiet gambling house on its right. The station was a two-storey little building, with two burly men in green head wraps standing outside the gate. Wei He took out the green cloth strip that marked him a guest retainer and tied it on his arm, then walked towards the entrance. The two men on the gate glanced at him, bowed slightly with folded hands, and said nothing.
Wei He bowed in return with folded hands and pushed open the half-closed wooden door. Inside was a small hall of vermilion red; a dozen or so sturdy men and women sat apart — some chatting idly, some playing chess, and some actually arm-wrestling over drinks… As Wei He came in, only a few glanced over at him and looked; the rest paid him no attention at all and went on with their own business.
Wei He noticed that the people here were, basically, all guest retainers like himself, all with strips of green cloth tied on their arms. He looked left and right, then picked out a young man leaning by a wooden pillar, staring blankly out of the window, and went up to him. "This brother, may I ask whether Steward Bai — Bai Honghou — is in? I have business to consult him on." Wei He said with folded hands. "The steward is on the second floor. You look unfamiliar, brother; did you come in this year?" The man smiled. His face was broad and amiable, only
his mouth was very wide and his skin very pale, so that one could not forget him at a glance. "Mm, I joined not long ago." Wei He nodded. "My name is Jin Xiufeng. Anyone who can hang his name with the Qingdu Sect is no ordinary man in this Feiye City. Let us be better acquainted from now on." The man said with a smile.
"This one is Wei He. We will talk when there is a chance." Wei He nodded. Taking his leave of this wide-mouthed man, he went up the stairs to the second floor, and there by a window he soon saw Bai Honghou, alone, slowly practising his handwriting with a brush. Bai Honghou saw someone come up, recognised Wei He as the one newly taken on, and remembered him.
"Little brother Wei, this is a rare visit. Sit, sit." He pointed at a row of seats on the empty second floor, and set down his own brush and came over. Wei He was a new man of the second breakthrough in qi and blood; every year he took a few such into the sect. In the same way, every year a few of the old guest retainers would drop away for one reason or another.
To keep the numbers steady, once there was a gap he made sure to take new men on in good time. Wei He was a new man taken on to fill such a gap. "Brother Wei has come this time — what is it for?" he asked with a smile.
