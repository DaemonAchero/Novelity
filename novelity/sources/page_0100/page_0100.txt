The horses were well enough, all as it should be, but the riders on their backs had qi and blood failing one after another, and some of them breathed raggedly, now heavy, now light, as if they were gritting their teeth against something. "There is the smell of blood!" Wei He narrowed his eyes and his nose moved; he had caught a faint scent. The thirteen riders galloped off along the street and vanished at a street end leading toward the Inner City.
Wei He drew his gaze back and told Old Huang to drive on. 'These riders are no common men. Their qi and blood may be failing, but from a distance their bearing and breath alike speak of long training; likely they have all practised the martial arts.' he guessed. The rain fell slantwise.
Wei He let the carriage curtain fall. "Drive on." Old Huang answered him.
"Those lords of the Inner City — a spell ago, when they went out, they were all in black cloaks, and now coming back… ai…"
== Chapter 34 · News (Part 2) ==
"Did you see them that while ago?"
"Mm, I did. I was oiling the carriage and saw them from the roadside; there were twenty-odd riders in the party, every one of them a fierce sight." Old Huang answered. "Before that, at the city gate, I saw a notice. It said the strange beasts outside the city were running wild over the fields, and the Seven Family League of the Inner City was leading a party out to hunt them. Each of the seven houses sent
its experts, gathered into one party." Old Huang took out a waterskin and drank. "By the look of them today, I fear it came to nothing." Wei He said nothing and asked no more. The carriage went slowly forward through the night, heading toward the small Wei courtyard.
The wheels threw up strings of rainwater as they passed pedestrians under umbrellas, and went by a man in blue at the roadside. He carried a white paper umbrella with a finely wrought red plum blossom on it, strolling at his ease. Through the crack of the window Wei He caught one brief glimpse, and dimly made out that on the sleeve above the hand holding the umbrella there was embroidered a small, finely worked 'Hong' character. His heart tightened. He opened the window quickly and put his head out to look back, but the man in blue was nowhere to be seen.
The street was bleak and bare, with only a few people hurrying along under umbrellas. The unlit lanterns before the shop doors hung swaying in the wind, and the cold quiet was the deeper for it. Wei He drew his head back and sat up straight, and all at once there was a heavy weight in his heart. He did not know why, but his instinct told him it was like black clouds pressing down, with the storm ready to break at any moment.
"Perhaps I should go and ask Old Man Zheng; he may know something." Wei He settled on the thought: tomorrow he would go and ask Old Man Zheng. As a great martial master of note in Stone Bridge Ward, Old Man Zheng's connections and information were far beyond his own. And that man in blue… the Hong family…
Wei He pressed down the tangled thoughts, closed his eyes, emptied his mind of everything, and drew a deep breath. Home again, Wei Ying heated water for him, and after a bath he fell asleep quickly. The next day, before the sky was light, he went straight to the inner room where his master Old Man Zheng lodged. He knew that Old Man Zheng usually rose very early, only that every morning he first went over his accounts in secret.
"The riders last night? Ah, the Seven Family League's riders…" Setting down his abacus, Zheng Fugui stroked the long beard on his chin. "Do you know, I have just learned from an old brother of mine that Hong Daoyuan of Hong Family Fort went out for an outing yesterday, taking his lovely wife and young son to Mingde Temple to burn incense.
"And they say the party chanced on a sudden attack by fierce beasts, and he put his own hand to it, killing more than ten of the beasts without a scratch on himself."
"That…!" Wei He's heart tightened; putting one thing with another, he had a bad feeling. Zheng Fugui smiled.
