A feeling of thorough, drenching comfort spread through his whole body. Raising both fists, Wei He saw at a glance that the knuckles had turned grey-black. He knew: Mountain Fist's third breakthrough in qi and blood had come through! "With the force of a third breakthrough as my foundation, knowing what force really is, and coming back to feel it out and study it again — with conditions this good, I have already left I don't know how many people behind me. Even if my qi-and-blood aptitudes are a little poor, in insight and thinking I am second to no one!"
Wei He straightened up, feeling the two transformed kinds of qi and blood inside him, the Five Ridges Palm's and Mountain Fist's, running sharply distinct and yet blending and harmonising. The Five Ridges Palm's third-breakthrough qi and blood was the calmer; Mountain Fist's third-breakthrough qi and blood the more violent.
One moving, one still, each making up for the other — and at this moment it gave Wei He a strange sense of growing great. He could feel his total qi and blood swelling and multiplying fast under this second breakthrough. "A pity no other art is at the verge of perfect completion, or I could ride this breakthrough on through in one breath, and perhaps study closely the key to the jin one level above."
Wei He sighed. He spread both hands; as his qi and blood circulated, Mountain Fist and the Five Ridges Palm produced two kinds of force at once, and the two forces were like a millstone, pressing and tempering the bones of his arms and palms without cease. The tempering gave him a strange tingling itch.
"By Master Zheng's explanation, after every breakthrough in qi and blood a period of rest is needed, so that the body can complete its change the faster. These days I needn't spend qi and blood on Mountain Fist, so I may as well put all my strength into raising the Flying Dragon Art." The Five Ridges Palm still showed no hope of a fifth-realm breakthrough, and there was no telling how long it needed to reach the verge. Mountain Fist had only just broken through, so its verge was far off. These two arts took too long; by the time they filled up, the Realm Breaking
Bead might well have filled twice over. So the nearest one was only the Flying Dragon Art. "All the better. I meant to test, besides, what happens when I use the Realm Breaking Bead to break through a lower-tier art after I have risen to a higher stage — will it still be used up all at once, as before, or will it spend only part and leave some progress behind?" Wei He did not, in fact, have any very complete understanding of the Realm Breaking Bead, the card he held in his sleeve. The thing filled its progress slowly, after all, and was too precious; he did not have many chances to experiment with it. After the breakthrough,
Wei He felt out Mountain Fist's qi and blood as it circulated, and the stir of this flow was far greater than the Five Ridges Palm's. "Mountain Fist rates only middling even in Feiye City, and it is already this much stronger than the Five Ridges Palm — I really do not know how many divine arts and peerless skills there are in this world." A faint anticipation rose in Wei He's heart.
The third breakthrough alone could add twenty years of life; what then of stronger arts? Higher realms? Perhaps one day there would even be a day of long life and lasting sight. He went out into the yard and slowly practised Mountain Fist's moves, quickening the circulation of his qi and blood, speeding the body's remaking. Three days in a row he stayed at home, until he could control the qi and blood of his whole body completely and felt the body's remaking gradually settling and slowing,
and only then changed his clothes and set out for the boxing hall.
== Chapter 72 · Hope (Part 2) ==
The Mountain Fist hall's main gate was a little shabby now, and nobody came to repair it. Wei He pushed the door open and went in; the yard was somewhat quiet, but a dozen or so people were still there, practising skin grinding without a word. This skin grinding was in fact the grinding out of each person's different physical endowments. Who was strong, who weak, who had the better foundation and bone, who healed faster — all of it was plain in the skin grinding.
Looking at these senior and junior brothers and sisters now, Wei He could judge in an instant whose cultivation and strength ran deeper. He also began to understand a little of how Master Zheng felt, sitting in the yard every day. "Senior Brother Wei, you've come?" Ouyang Zhuang spotted him from a distance and hurried over to greet him.
