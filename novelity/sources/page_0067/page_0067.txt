He knew that Senior Brother Cheng was a married man, and not married only — he had taken two concubines as well, and now a third was coming. His wife had borne him a son, and each of the two concubines a daughter. "By the way, Senior Brother — between me and Cheng Jing, who is stronger?" The question came to Wei He all at once. Their bout last time had been broken off by that monster and never carried through.
He had his own doubts on the point. "Cheng Jing? That Cheng Jing from your escort run?" Cheng Shaojiu recalled her with a start. "She is no ordinary sort. If you went up against her, I doubt you would do. Inside thirty moves you would lose."

"Who would lose?" Wei He asked.
"You." Cheng Shaojiu pointed a finger. "Cheng Jing has trained at the martial arts since she was small — eight years now — and her qi and blood have already come to the very edge of the second break-
through, and she may break past the limit at any hour. Beside you as you are now, she is a good deal stronger." Wei He was silent. But the qi-and-blood pass could only be ground down and endured little by little. The martial arts — and in this world above all — were never a light thing. "Anyway, you had better put your back into it. Accumulating qi and blood is only a slow water-mill sort of work; as long as a man is willing to take the bitter effort, he can build it up. The crux is this bar-
-rier." Cheng Shaojiu put away his qi and blood, and the grey on his two fists slowly faded. "The Stone-Hide pass, the second breakthrough in qi and blood — many cannot get past it. Some reach the qi-and-blood stage early, and then stick here and simply cannot go through. Three years stuck, some of them. The longer they stick, the more fretful they grow, and the more fretful, the longer they stick; and so it goes round and round. Worse still, as the years climb one upon another, once a man's qi and blood pass
their peak at twenty, they begin to slip slowly down. And then the fretting is worse still." Cheng Shaojiu gave a sigh. "That is why Xiao Ran draws so much attention. He is so young, and has such enormous potential ahead of him." Wei He understood. After a silence he spoke again.
"Then, Senior Brother — how strong is Xiao Ran now?"
"He…" At the mention of Xiao Ran a complicated look came over Cheng Shaojiu's face. "At our private gathering last time, I gave it everything and lost to him by two moves."
"…" Wei He was shaken inwardly. This Xiao Ran ran wild out of doors every day and hardly trained at all when he came back, and yet he still had this much
strength. It was simply frightening. "Enough, let us not talk of him. A talent like Xiao Ran is not for the likes of us to compare with. Talents have talents to measure themselves against; we ordinary folk still go one step at a time and lay a solid foundation. If you and I can break past the Stone-Hide limit in our lifetime, and grind Mountain Fist up to the master's level, we shall already be very formidable indeed."
Wei He nodded in agreement. So far as he knew, Cheng Shaojiu had been stuck for three years between Ox-Hide and Stone-Hide, at this second breakthrough in qi and blood. And Cheng Jing had been stuck at this same pass four years ago, and was stuck at it still. Pass, stick — the reason those two words had been taken for these stages was that ordinary people were so often barred out and stuck at them; and it was for that very reason that the stages were
named so. "Lately many of the brothers in the yard have found places of their own and have the money and grain to keep their training up, and everyone's qi and blood is growing none too slowly. You had better hurry and put in the effort too." Cheng Shaojiu reminded him.
"What Senior Brother says is right…" Wei He nodded.
"All right, no more talk with you. I have to go to the Inner City in a while. A gathering on Jiang Su's side." The moment Cheng Shaojiu mentioned the Inner City the corners of his mouth turned up and he looked altogether pleased with himself, which drew a little scorn from Wei He. Lately he was always hearing him speak of the Inner City, the Inner City — what was so good about the place, that it should put a full-
of-spring look on the face of a rich young master the moment he mentioned it? As if he had read Wei He's thought, Cheng Shaojiu waved a hand impatiently.
