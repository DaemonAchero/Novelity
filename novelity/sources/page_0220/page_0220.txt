After all, in his Jiang family only the family head was a jin-entering martial master, and only three had the third breakthrough. One of them was his father. And the key was that his father Jiang Donggui had more than one child…… brothers and sisters of the second breakthrough like himself, there were four more. The second breakthrough still looked like an expert to ordinary people, but in the Inner City, with strange-beast meat piled up in heaps, such people were everywhere.
Among the children of the Seven Family League, all it took was training in martial arts, and many were at this level. Only the third breakthrough could not be heaped up on resources alone. It also took insight, bone and resolve. Not one of the three could be lacking. And Jiang Yan knew very well that for the last dozen years and more, every third-breakthrough expert of the younger generation had come out of an outer-city martial academy.
In the Inner City, not one. That was a false prosperity heaped up of resources and experience. Some things cannot be pushed into a qualitative change by outside means. The more a man relies on resources and experience to help him break through the second time, the harder it is for him to break through the third.
"……" A faint ache rose in Jiang Yan's heart; he knew very well that he was jealous. On what grounds was it that he, with strange-beast meat piled up in abundance and his father helping and instructing him every day, still could not break through? A pity — whatever he felt now, Wei He's breakthrough was already a fact. Suddenly he seemed to think of something and raised his head towards the other few.
Senior Brother Zhao Hong said nothing. Long after Master Zheng left, he practised on for a while, then left quietly. He spoke to no one, but by the look of him he had taken a heavy blow; after all, he had watched Wei He being brought in, and knew that it was almost three years now, and that Wei He had come on by leaps and bounds — seemingly ordinary, but looking back from here, extremely steady. Zhang Lu, by contrast, felt little; he and Wei He had never had much to do with each other, and even without this breakthrough Wei He was his senior brother, and
now he had merely become a more formidable senior brother. He cupped his hands and went up to offer his congratulations, his mood entirely normal. The young man clearly did not yet understand just what a third-breakthrough expert meant; he had not yet been beaten over the head by the failure of a breakthrough barrier. Back then Xiao Ran had failed two breakthroughs in a row, and his heart, which had been higher than the sky, was brought down with a brutal blow by reality. Only then did he learn
how hard life is. After Master Zheng left, Wei He stayed on in the inner yard and, as usual, practised Mountain Fist for a while. Watching his senior brother practise just now had given him more insight, so now he consolidated it. It was nearly noon.
He changed his clothes, wiped off the sweat, and was about to leave. "Shall we walk a while?" Jiang Su suddenly said from behind him. Wei He paused, then nodded. "All right."
The two of them had a complicated relationship. Though Jiang Su had gone for a chastity check a while ago and proved she was still untouched, it had in the end broken the hearts of no few of her suitors. Only now she did not care about such side branches. The two left the Mountain Fist hall one behind the other; they did not go to a tavern or the like, but strolled along the Feiye River near the hall. Down the empty street beside the river they walked slowly.
After the heavy rain the Feiye River ran swift and turbulent; sparse figures along the bank were catching fish and shrimp and pulling waterweed. One heavy rain seemed to have brought a little life back to the outer city's wards. Jiang Su wore a grey-black fitted outfit — long trousers, short skirt, arm guards, knee guards, the whole set grey-black, showing off her figure while covering the clear lines of her muscles.
Compared with before, her breasts seemed more conspicuous; her long hair, by contrast, had been cut short, only to her shoulders. Black hair, black clothes, black eyes, and the fine figure trained out by long practice — in peacetime she would surely have drawn many eyes. A pity that the people passing now and then were all busy looking for something to eat by the river, with no leisure at all to admire beauty.
