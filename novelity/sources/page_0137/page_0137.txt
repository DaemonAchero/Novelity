In the days that followed he went back and forth through the hills and woods around Feiye City, learning the lie of the land everywhere and hunting without pause. Most of the time a whole week brought him only a little game; had he lived off hunting alone he would long since have starved. But Wei He's purpose had always been to temper his strength and his ability to live in the wild. In that time he met mountain bandits several times, and dealt with them all with ease.
The strongest of them was a hand of the second qi-and-blood breakthrough, and that one seemed to have broken through by some trick; his total qi and blood stood at the second breakthrough, but it fell well short of Wei He's. In a fist-off, Wei He beat him to death. In that time he also met flung stones, bows and arrows, throwing knives and other ranged attacks, and his reaction speed, far beyond ordinary men's, let him slip them all. When he happened on a hunter out after the same game, he would pay in ordinary kill for the man's experience and learn from him.
Little by little Wei He also found himself a hidden little cave in the hills to serve as a second base. It had probably been a bear's den once, long since abandoned. Once he found it he fitted it out and kept it as a concealed temporary post. He named the little cave Natural Dwelling, because the housing estate he had lived in in his previous life had carried that very name.
By that he meant to keep the old days in mind. Two months slipped by in a blink. Wei He's Realm Breaking Bead had at last gone past the halfway mark, and his qi and blood had formally gathered one ninth of a red-cloud flower. That pace was cold enough to chill the heart: not only did Wei He himself feel helpless over it, Master Zheng had at last taken his gaze off him for good
and set it on Xiao Ran. For Xiao Ran, healed of his grave injuries, had made good all the qi and blood he had lost and climbed back to the level of eight red-cloud flowers, his standard from before he was hurt. And this time Xiao Ran would try again for the third breakthrough in qi and blood — for the Iron-Hide level.
On the other side, perhaps because his qi-and-blood foundation was itself at the second breakthrough, Wei He's progress in Five Ridges Palm was brisk. The stages below the second breakthrough of qi and blood needed no tempering at all; they broke through of themselves. In two months he trained on to the third stage of Five Ridges Palm. Five Ridges Palm has five stages in all — clear, river, cloud, white, mist — namely Qing Mountain Ridge, River Mountain Ridge, Cloud Mountain Ridge, White Mountain Ridge and Mist Mountain Ridge.
Each stage answers to one tempering of qi and blood, and Wei He had already broken through in one breath to the third stage, the Cloud Mountain Ridge level. His palms, building on what Mountain Fist had made of them, went one step further — but only grew a little harder and firmer. What disappointed him was that the gain was not great.
Nor was that all: this side-training dragged heavily on the gathering of red-cloud flowers for his own Mountain Fist. No wonder one seldom heard of a man training a second main art; its gain was extremely limited, and it wasted qi and blood and time and strength — the loss outweighed the gain. Yet this very grind had let him catch hold of something, faintly, that was key.
The Wei family's little yard. Before a great green boulder that several men could not have spanned with their arms. Wei He wore only a tight grey vest, which he had asked Second Sister to help him cut down and resew: it took up sweat and washed easily, and it also covered the Realm Breaking Bead mark on his chest.
Wei He already stood about one metre eighty-five, a good deal taller than before. At nineteen, beside the boy who had first walked into the Mountain Fist yard, he carried now a streak of maturity, and more steadiness and a killer's quiet besides. Standing before the great boulder, he raised both hands level. 'There is no one to teach me the moves of Five Ridges Palm, only my own imagining, so they are of little use. But its methods of training have something worth taking.
So long as I can break through the third stage and reach the fourth, I will set foot in the third breakthrough of qi and blood. And with the third breakthrough to my name, going back to train Mountain Fist will surely take half the effort for double the result!' The key Wei He had found was this: because he trained Five Ridges Palm with qi and blood above his own level, its stages had broken through for him with ease. So if he could use Five Ridges Palm to break through first, reach the third breakthrough in qi and blood, and then return to Mountain Fist, he would surely
save a great deal of strength. 'For anyone else this method is an impossible task — it would even drag a man's cultivation badly down. But I have the Realm Breaking Bead, and the bead's progress runs faster than the gathering of qi and blood. Most crucial of all, Five Ridges Palm, beside Mountain Fist, seems to ask far less of the qi and blood a man must gather.'
