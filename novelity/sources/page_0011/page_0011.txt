He picked up a willow switch from behind his chair and laid into the lot of them with it. The switch cracked and snapped, and the ring of men scattered, yelping and baring their teeth. Only Wei He was left.
"All right. Now, where were we?" Old Man Zheng scratched his hair.
"We'd got to how we're different from ordinary people." Wei He answered at once.
"Mm. Right. That's the place." The old man dragged it out, slow enough to make a man want to slap him. He drew a long breath in and let it out slowly. "Why do we train? Isn't it just so we can beat other people?"
"Yes, yes." Wei He nodded.
He was curious, in fact — curious whether the martial arts of this world were anything like those of the life he had left behind. In that life, the martial arts of Huaxia had withered as they failed to keep up with the times, until a pack of sanda fighters had broken them apart with ease. Scattered, routed, no army left.
That Huaxia's martial arts had never been able to fight, from ancient times to now — that Wei He did not believe. Every dynasty had thrown up masters without end; among a billion-odd people there were always some who trained hard and long. They could not all have been worthless. The likeliest reason they still lost to sanda was that they had walled themselves in and lost the one thing that mattered: real combat.
So he was more than a little curious about what the martial path in this world actually amounted to.
Old Man Zheng dawdled a while longer, then finally stopped holding back.
"Training the martial arts means training to beat a man. Beat him, and the purpose of the training is met. So martial arts are the skill of beating others and killing others by every method there is." He summed it up.
"I don't know how others see it, but that is the foundation of my Mountain Fist." Old Man Zheng stroked his beard. "And beating the other man comes down, in the end, to who has more strength, who is faster, who strikes true — and who can take more punishment!"
"The master's insight is profound! Only an old hand like the master could put it that way!" Wei He hurried to praise him.
Old Man Zheng smiled. "The principle is simple. But how do you make yourself stronger, faster, more accurate, harder to put down? That is what training is for. That is the whole purpose of what we do. But…" His tone turned.
"No man can be everything at once."
They said Old Man Zheng had earned a xiucai degree in his youth, and it showed in the way he spoke. He sighed.
"A man's energy is limited, and his talent cannot be split in all directions. So being able to train one direction until you are formidable is already very good."
He looked at Wei He. "My Mountain Fist trains the hands. The fists."
"A rich man can wear good gloves to strengthen his punch and protect his hand. You have no money." Old Man Zheng said lazily. "So — train. Harden the fist, build the strength, and your blows will land hard."
He stood and pointed at a tall, dull-faced man. "Zhao Hong, you're in charge of your junior brother. Start him on strength."
"Oh…" The man answered honestly.
Wei He looked at him, compared his build with the others around him, and had the feeling that this Zhao Hong was probably quite strong.
"Junior brother, my name is Zhao Hong. I'm the eldest senior brother here. I'll start by teaching you how to build strength. But building strength needs meat, so you'll have to find a way to eat better first."
"I understand!" Wei He nodded seriously. "Eldest Senior Brother, if I may — have you ever heard of an internal cultivation expert?"
"Internal cultivation? What internal cultivation? Never heard of it." Zhao Hong shook his head.
You could see the sort of man he was — plain and honest. Tall, heavily muscled, obedient and straightforward. In a courtyard like this he was probably sent running errands all day.
"You just got here today, so start by lifting stone with me."
"Lifting stone?" Wei He felt a little cheated. All that money, and he was to be dragged off to lift rocks?
