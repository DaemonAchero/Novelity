"They say that in that skirmish Hong Daoyuan's spear maimed two of One Ear's best generals outright, and then two more strokes killed the exotic warhorse One Ear prized most. One Ear himself was nearly shaken to death on horseback, and the exotic horse he rode had all four legs smashed and went down on its knees. His strength is simply outrageous!"
"…Are all the formidable military commanders in the army this strong?" Wei He could not help asking.
"Armies and officers have to be judged by what power keeps them. Where troops are raised by a great family or a great sect, the gap between them and ordinary soldiers is enormous. As for the officers, many come out of the gangs or from among martial masters — all different. I have only heard my uncle speak of these things, and I do not know the details either.
"In any case, think about it: if the army's officers were not strong, would the Three Gangs and Two Sects in the city not have thrown everything into disorder long ago?" Wei He saw the sense in it. They paid a little money and got their chance to train; the officers would hardly be worse off — better methods, better food and clothing and lodging, better arts for cultivating qi and blood.
That they were each one of them monstrously strong was only natural. "They say Hong Family Fort keeps a Black Wind Cavalry — clouds follow the dragon, the wind follows the tiger — raised for the Tiger of Feiye, Hong Daoyuan himself; the least a man can be to apply to join is one who has broken through once in qi and blood. They are extremely formidable!" Cheng Shaojiu said in admiration. "This trip has broadened my view." Wei He felt it deeply too.
He had once thought that with his training as it was he counted for a little something; seeing this today, he understood why everyone stood so in fear of Hong Family Fort. Yet plainly all of it was still far beyond him. Only, this was the first time he had truly seen what an elite soldier was.
"But do not think too much on it. If you truly cannot break through, with your wit you could carve out a career even without the martial arts. That much I believe!" Cheng Shaojiu said to comfort him. He knew Wei He had lately begun preparing to break through his pass. But that pass was desperately hard, and with Wei He's potential… "At the worst, come to the agency and let the two of us brothers join forces; in time we shall not be weaker than my uncle and his like!" Cheng Shaojiu said, all seriousness.
"I understand." Wei He nodded, warmed at heart. But he had also heard that Cheng Shaojiu had said as much to more than one man; the Yonghe Agency had by now taken in a good many of his so-called boon companions and sworn friends, most of them idle eaters who did no work, which had angered his father and uncle badly. The two of them went home, and for days after neither slackened in the least, training at skin grinding and building qi and blood the whole day long.
But Cheng Shaojiu was by nature a man who could not sit still, and before a month was out he was running off to the Inner City again. Wei He was different: roused by what he had seen that night, he buried his head and trained, his whole mind set on breaking through to a higher stage. The gap in sheer force in this world was a little too wide. If the terror of that Hong Daoyuan was real and not boasting, then he was truly savage beyond measure, with the valour of ten thousand men.
Set before such a man, he would be refuse, finished by a single spear-thrust. Luckily his own qi and blood were only a little short of complete. Grinding and enduring as he did, after another two months and more, at last he brought the last of his qi and blood to the full. And so, with the Realm Breaking Bead and his own qi and blood complete at once, Wei He could at last properly break through his pass.
He was in no haste to break through at once, however: if he could try to break through by himself, that would save one round of filling the Bead, and would it not be better to keep it for the next stage? As it happened, the fist-off mentioned before began just then. Old Man Zheng took the three strongest disciples — Eldest Senior Brother Zhao Hong, Xiao Ran, and in third place Cheng Shaojiu — with him to the Inner City.
Wei He was only an ordinary Ox-Hide man, still far behind, and so he stayed in the yard like the other brothers. But he had little mind now to take part in a fist-off; he buried himself in the pass he had to break. Nor was he alone: in the yard there were four others whose qi and blood like his were complete, and the four of them in the same way flogged themselves every day, mad to break through the pass as soon as they could.
Once a man broke through, his standing changed at once and entirely; only at Stone-Hide did a man count as truly elite. "They say that during the fist-off Xiao Ran's qi and blood reached completion! He can begin pushing at Iron-Hide any time now!!" A low voice suddenly broke in on Wei He's full concentration.
