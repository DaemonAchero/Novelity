He looked left and right once more. Something was plainly hidden here. The fort was not large: inside there were only a few tables and chairs, a cage, some torches on the walls, and a bronze furnace. He went to the torches and checked them one by one. Having made sure every torch was sound, he went on to the tables and chairs.
Soon the tables and chairs had passed too, and the bronze furnace was all that was left. Wei He went close to the furnace and studied it. He gathered up his qi and blood and slowly ran a hand over its surface. "Eh?" Something struck him. On the bronze furnace there was not the least stain, not a scratch, no smell of incense ash.
Could it be that… A thought flashed through Wei He's mind. He quickly looked over the tables and chairs again, and the inner walls all round. The walls were the same. Wei He moved slowly, a little at a time, around the inside of the fort, and soon came back to the tunnel.
He knelt on one knee before the tunnel, ran a hand over the ground, and put it to his nose to sniff. A faint rank smell and the smell of blood came off it. "Can this be—!?" Something stirred in Wei He. Bang!!
All at once the fort's gate was smashed hard open. Several white-clothed fat men came in at a stride, holding up shields. Behind them a huge fat man three metres tall with a spiked war-hammer filled the gateway entirely with his enormous belly. "Where's the man?" The fort was empty now; there was no one at all. Only a string of corpses lying across the ground.
"Wind Guard caught no scent — the man's run!" a crowd of white-clothed fat men outside answered loudly, leading green-eyed wolf-hounds. "A pack of useless things!" The big fat man smashed his hammer into the ground; the men around him went numb in the soles of their feet and fell back several steps, not daring to make a sound.
== Chapter 62 · Up Again (Part 2) ==
Wei He was already running by then, halfway up the mountain slope. From how new the bronze furnace was, and the tables and chairs, the torches, the inner walls — all of them new — he had judged that the fort must have been built very recently.
And even the tables and chairs were new, with no tracks of feet on the ground around them. Below the cage ran a pitch-black tunnel, and at its mouth hung a strange beast smell. The whole arrangement put him in mind of one particular structure: a decoy cage. So he had made his decision at once and pulled out fast.
"Halt!" Suddenly, out of the black woods ahead, a squad of white-clothed fat men came straight at him. Two huge fat men close to three metres tall led them, each with two of the kind of white-clothed fat man Wei He had killed before at his back. Meeting on a narrow road, with pairs of green-eyed wolves on either side.
Wei He's keen senses caught it at once: the qi and blood in those two great fat men ahead rolled like a flood, splashing and roaring, utterly absurd. That strength and quality… far beyond the second breakthrough of qi and blood. The fat men's eyes fixed on Wei He, one and all.
Especially the two great fat men at the very front: merely resting their gaze on him made Wei He's skin tighten and sting. Those two were certainly at the third breakthrough of qi and blood! His heart thumped; he knew things were bad. The far side of Shaoyang Mountain really was dangerous. A good thing he had never dared come this way on a risk before, or he would have been finished long ago.
"What are you doing going down the mountain alone?" the big fat man on the left asked. He was looking at the figure opposite, white-clad and round as a ball. In the darkness he could not see him very clearly either, but if the man wore his own sect's clothes, and was that fat, then he was one of their own.
