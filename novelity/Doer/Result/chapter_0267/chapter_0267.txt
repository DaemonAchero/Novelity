The group inside the ruined temple wore fine clothes and had guards with them, but the young man and woman at the center could not hide the deep worry in their eyes.
A campfire burned brightly, driving away the chill and drying the ground around it. They had clearly been here for a while, or the wet soil would never have dried so completely.
"Brother... when will Uncle and the others get here? We've been waiting for two days. Even if this place is hidden, what if..." The girl spoke in a quiet, anxious voice.
"Don't worry, they'll be here soon. They probably just got delayed on the road." The young man forced a relaxed tone, though his eyebrows remained tight.
His name was Lian Shuihan. He and his sister, Lian Youran, were top disciples of the Purple One Sect. Not long ago, tragedy struck their sect. Their master was critically injured and died just days after returning.
Their long-time enemy, the Nature Sect, struck immediately. The Purple One Sect was crushed and scattered. Senior masters fled in all directions, carrying the sect's rare treasures with them.
The brother and sister were one such fleeing group. They had come to this spot to meet their grandfather, Li Chong, a famous master known as the Silver Lock Across the River. They had agreed to meet here for safety. With their sect destroyed, his protection was their only hope.
Their biggest fear was that the enemy would find them before their grandfather arrived. Two days of waiting had left everyone on edge. Outside, snow fell heavily, but the cold weather was nothing compared to the chill in their hearts.
"Brother... when is Grandfather really coming? Should we just head to Jinzhou on our own?" Lian Youran urged softly. Something felt wrong to her.
"No need to rush. I contacted Grandmother earlier, and they will definitely come for us. It's remote here, and Old Lord Mountain is full of dangerous beasts, so delays are normal," Lian Shuihan reassured her.
"But..."
Before Lian Youran could finish, a guard raised a finger to his lips. "Shh."
"Young Master, someone is approaching!"
The guard was Uncle Zhao, the most loyal captain of the Lian family guards and a high-level martial artist. He was practically family to them. Any other guard would have run away long ago instead of risking their life here.
The rest of the guards instantly gripped their weapons, put on their gloves, and spread out in defense. They had survived several ambushes on the way here and were completely on edge.
As the group held their breath, a tall, heavily built figure in a black cloak walked slowly into the ruined temple. The hood covered most of his face, leaving only a pale chin visible.
It was Wei He, who had just arrived at the temple.
He glanced at the Lian siblings, cared little for what he saw, and walked straight to a corner to sit cross-legged and wait. With his keen eyes, he could tell the group looked wealthy on the surface but were exhausted and desperate underneath. But he was only here to meet You Fu for a spar. He had zero interest in their troubles.
Wei He sat in silence, completely motionless.
The Lian siblings let out a quiet sigh of relief. They knew he was not a pursuer from the Nature Sect. Members of the Nature Sect always wore a light blue V-shaped mark on their left arm so they could recognize each other in battle. This man had no mark and ignored them entirely, meaning he was likely just a traveler passing through.
The group relaxed slightly and went back to resting around the fire, though no one felt like talking anymore. The siblings were lost in thought about their future. Living under their grandfather's roof would never be as comfortable as home, and with their parents missing, they could rely only on themselves.
"Young Master, Miss, that man is huge," Uncle Zhao whispered. "Someone with a frame like that is no ordinary person. He is definitely a trained warrior. Be careful."
"Of course," Lian Shuihan said quietly. "The mountains are full of wild beasts. No non-warrior would dare walk here alone."
As he spoke, hurried footsteps sounded outside.
BOOM.
The half-broken door was kicked open. Two broad-shouldered, middle-aged men stepped inside and scanned the room.
One wore white robes with a deep scar near his eye. Both wore the blue V-shaped mark on their arms. They were masters from the Nature Sect.
"So this is where you were hiding," the scarred man sneered, fixing his eyes on the siblings. "How does it feel for the prized disciples of the Purple One Sect to sleep in a trashy old temple for days?"
The moment the two men entered, the Lian group went stiff. The guards rushed forward to block the young master and miss, while Uncle Zhao drew his short blade, his expression deadly serious.
"Gongsun Hong...!" Lian Shuihan's heart pounded, but as the oldest, he had to step up.
"Where are your elders?" Gongsun Hong asked with a cold smile, scanning the shadows. "You really think you can survive these woods on your own? You think the black-eared wolf packs are just a rumor?"
Gongsun Hong was cautious. Many masters had escaped the destruction of the Purple One Sect. Since these two kids were important figures, it made no sense for them to be unguarded. He suspected the elders were hiding in the dark, using the kids as bait to draw him into a trap.
In reality, the siblings were terrified because they truly were alone. They had been separated from their elders days ago, which was why they had been hiding in this filthy, freezing temple, too scared to leave.
Gongsun Hong was one of the three great masters of the Nature Sect, a true Bone-Forging warrior. If he realized they had no backup, they were dead.
Sweat poured down Lian Shuihan's forehead as he tried to think of a way out, but his mind went blank.
Lian Youran, however, looked toward the black-cloaked giant in the corner. Anyone who could travel these dangerous woods alone had to be strong. If she could drag him into this fight to block Gongsun Hong, it might buy them a chance to run. She didn't care if the stranger survived or not.
Her eyes flickered toward Wei He as a dark plan formed. The Nature Sect wanted two things: to kill all survivors, and to steal the Purple One Sect's treasures and secret manuals. The treasure mattered more to them. If she could make Gongsun Hong believe the treasure was given to that stranger...
Lian Youran took a deep breath, ready to speak.
CRACK.
Crunching footsteps sounded outside in the snow. They stopped right at the doorway.
"Little Friend Wei, sorry to keep you waiting," a deep voice called out through the wind and snow.
Wei He stood up, shook the dust off his cloak, and walked toward the entrance.
"Senior, you doing me this favor is already more than enough. I only just arrived myself."
He strode forward, completely ignoring everyone else in the temple.
He walked straight toward Gongsun Hong and his partner, who were blocking the doorway. The two Nature Sect warriors glared at him coldly as he approached. To avoid trouble, they decided to strike first. The moment he stepped close enough, they would kill him with a single palm strike.
"You!" Gongsun Hong started to speak, but then he caught a glimpse of the face beneath the hood.
His face went pale. In a panic, he shoved his partner aside and scrambled out of the doorway.
The moment he cleared the path, his throat began to itch and burn. Gongsun Hong panicked, shoving a detox pill from his pouch into his mouth. But as soon as he swallowed it, his arm went numb and itchy.
"Master Wei, spare my life! Please spare me!" he shrieked, falling to his knees and slamming his head on the ground. "I was blind! I didn't know it was you! Please, spare me!"
His partner looked confused. "Gongsun, are you crazy? What are you..."
Before he could finish, his vision blurred with white patterns. The world spun upside down.
THUD.
The partner collapsed, holding his head, his body stiff. He gasped for air like a fish out of water. Within seconds, his chest stopped moving. His eyes went blank and his breathing ceased. He wasn't dead yet, but the poison had paralyzed his lungs and heart. He was trapped, waiting for death.
Gongsun Hong clutched his own neck, feeling his breathing grow heavy and his hands lose feeling. Luckily for him, the detox pill began to work just enough to keep him conscious. Wei He didn't even look at them as he walked past and left the temple.
Gongsun Hong held his breath, terrified to make a sound, kneeling motionless on the cold floor. He knew that if he drew that monster's attention again, he was dead.
Two Nature Sect experts—one a High Master, the other a Bone-Forging Master—were instantly defeated without a fight. One was dying, the other was begging on his knees.
The Lian family was frozen in terror. Uncle Zhao and the guards swallowed hard, quietly backing away toward the rear wall, trying to get as far from the door as possible.
The only sound left was the crackle of the campfire.
Lian Youran's heart beat wildly in her chest. She realized how close she had come to death. If she had gone through with her plan to frame the stranger, they would be lying dead on the floor right now beside the Nature Sect warrior.
Yet, as the fear wore off, she looked at Gongsun Hong kneeling on the floor.
The terrifying man who had just threatened their lives was now kneeling like a dog, too afraid to move.
A surge of dark, vengeful pleasure filled her heart. Even if she hadn't been the one to do it, seeing the Nature Sect suffer brought her pure joy.
