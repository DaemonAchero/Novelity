Steeling his nerves, Wei He pulled a pale yellow tile from his poison pouch.
He crushed the block with his fingers, turning it into fine powder that drifted down onto his clothes. The dust quickly merged into the fabric and vanished.
This powder could neutralize several deadly mists that his current arts could not block. He had prepared it in advance to survive the toxic canyon air.
Next, Wei He pulled a long, thin black metal rod from his back.
At the far end was a neat metal claw. Whenever he squeezed the handle, the claw clamped shut with strong force. This tool allowed him to handle dangerous objects without touching them directly.
Wei He tested the grip, then stepped into the thick fog and entered the canyon.
Hiss...
Phew...
Just as his foot hit the ground, he froze. A faint, strange sound reached his ears.
A sudden sense of dread rose from the depths of his heart.
Wei He instantly went on high alert. At his current level, his body's instincts naturally warned him of deep danger.
His real combat strength was already on par with top Viscera Refining experts. Yet, his body was warning him just like it used to back when he was weak.
This meant the thing living in this canyon was far beyond his expectations.
To stay safe, Wei He stopped breathing completely, refusing to let the local air enter his lungs.
He used the long metal claw to probe the ground ahead, slowly clearing a path. Step by step, he hiked deeper into the dark ravine.
The ground here looked wrong.
Outside, the dirt was dark, covered in grass, shrubs, and roots. But inside the canyon, the soil turned a pale, sickly gray. It was filled with tiny white grains and felt unusually wet.
Walking on it felt like stepping into deep mud.
After moving forward a bit more, his metal claw hit something hard.
He leaned closer to check. Through the fog, he saw a black stone tablet stuck sideways in the mud.
Wei He moved nearer. Simple words were roughly carved into the stone.
‘5th year of Dinggan... I can no longer control myself... Yezi... if fate allows, let us meet in the next life.’
Wei He frowned and looked closer.
Thick moss grew inside the deep cuts of the letters, showing that a long time had passed.
The grammar was not the modern language of the Great Yuan Empire. It was far older.
Before Great Yuan, there was the Wu Dynasty. The ancient text on the stone matched the writing style of the old Wu era—a time that ended over a thousand years ago.
That ancient era was known for terrifying chaos.
"Has this stone been here for a thousand years?!" Wei He thought, shocked. He did not expect this lonely canyon to hold ties to a lost dynasty.
The message on the stone felt chilling. It read like a final goodbye between two people driven to madness.
"5th year of Dinggan... what does that mean?"
His suspicion grew. Wei He automatically spread his defensive energy through his body, ready to react to any sudden attack.
He had always been curious about the Mind Sensing Realm. From what he knew, that level was completely different from everything below it. It was like stepping into a whole new realm of existence.
He had come to this dangerous place specifically to test his limits and understand the strength of top experts like Zhu Chen.
Leaving the stone behind, Wei He pushed deeper into the fog.
The path sloped downward. As he went deeper, tall stone walls rose around him. The canyon was shaped like a massive funnel, with all sides sloping down toward a single center point.
Wei He slowed his pace.
This place felt like the hunting ground of a terrifying beast. There were no animals around. Not even a single bug crawled on the rocks.
As he went lower, the dirt and stone turned completely white, as pale as fresh snow.
Wei He quietly activated his Five Elements Art, ready to spring back at any second.
He knew he was walking straight into the den of an elite beast.
Even though he trusted his power, he reached into his poison pouch and pulled out a small blue glass vial. Inside was a thick, oil-like liquid.
It was a special substance he had created by accident. It burned far hotter and longer than normal lamp oil.
He placed the bottle in an easy-to-reach spot on his belt, got his flint ready in his other hand, and kept moving down.
Two hundred meters deeper, the natural light faded. The fog grew so thick he could barely see his own hands.
Wei He stopped. Looking back, even the white mist looked gray in the dim light.
Hiss...
A sudden, sharp sound of breathing echoed right next to him.
His entire body went rigid. He focused all his senses on the dark mist around him.
The breath was unusually long. The creature inhaled for over a full minute before stopping.
Phew...
Then came the exhale. The steady gust of air was so loud it masked the faint sound of running water nearby.
Wei He held his metal rod, using it to feel out his surroundings. The mist was strange; it blocked his senses, leaving him blind to anything beyond ten meters. Beyond that distance, everything felt like thick cotton.
Suddenly, Wei He’s hand stopped. He froze all movement.
He raised his head, held his breath, and stared directly to his right.
Through the thick fog, a dark gray shape emerged.
It was a head that looked like a giant fox made of black smoke. Two pale white eyes stared down at him.
The fox head was as big as a watermelon. Except for its glowing white eyes, its entire form was pitch black.
The man and the beast stood perfectly still, locked in silence.
Time ticked by.
Driven by curiosity, the fox slowly drifted closer to Wei He. Its body began to show through the mist.
Strangely, its body was entirely wrapped in dark fog, making it impossible to see its real fur or flesh.
The fox got closer and closer.
Ten meters.
Eight meters.
Six meters.
Four meters.
Two meters...
Wei He stood face-to-face with the creature. He turned his body to confront it directly.
Swish!
Suddenly, a second fox head popped out from the side. It looked identical, pitch black with glowing white eyes.
Then, a third fox head emerged from the other direction.
Three giant fox heads stood side-by-side, hovering three meters in the air, all staring down at Wei He at the same time.
As the fog cleared further, the outline of the monster finally came into view.
Wei He stared, finally realizing what kind of beast he was truly facing.
Outside the Wang Manor, inside a quiet nearby house.
A pale young man stared out the window with a bitter, hateful look.
"The Wang family trapped me and damaged my organs. It took me until today to recover from those injuries. Once my Master arrives, I will make them pay in full. But for now, I'll take a little interest."
Across the street, Wang Shuyi stepped out of the grand gates. She wore tight hunting clothes that highlighted her fit body, holding a riding whip as guards gathered around her.
As the spoiled young lady of the Wang family, almost everyone in the city treated her with respect.
Her older brother, Wang Shaojun, had recently taken an official post and was now praised as the top young fighter in the city. With his protection, people constantly tried to flatter her, making her even more arrogant.
"She has no official title and no duties. Leaving the city at this hour... is she going hunting?" the pale man muttered to himself.
He noticed she only brought three or four guards. His eyes flashed with cold intent. He knew his chance had come.
To normal people, those guards were terrifying martial masters. Having them as bodyguards showed immense wealth and power.
But to him, they were nothing.
He had walked right into the main Wang Manor before and escaped alive, taking only minor injuries from a surprise attack. These few guards could never stop him.
The pale man smiled coldly and turned toward the door.
He decided to kidnap the girl and present her as a gift to his junior brother. Once his brother had his way with her, a weak martial artist like Wang Shuyi would never escape their grip.
As for offending the Wang family? He didn't care.
In this entire city, only Zhu Chen was strong enough to make him cautious. Everyone else was weak.
He was the famous Meteor Thief of Taizhou. In a direct brawl, he might not be the absolute strongest, but when it came to speed and escape skills, few could ever catch him.
Deep inside the canyon.
Wei He remained motionless, staring back at the three fox heads.
He felt three sharp, cold gazes fixed right on him.
Even worse was the sound coming from beneath the dirt. A deep, heavy heartbeat echoed like a muffled drum.
Thump...
Thump...
Thump...
Suddenly, the heartbeat stopped.
Wei He sensed danger and quickly looked down.
BOOM!
The ground shattered beneath his feet.
A massive black mouth lined with sharp teeth burst out from underground, snapping straight at Wei He.
Wei He released his inner energy, launching himself into the air and flying off to the right.
At the same time, the three fox heads shot forward through the black mist. Their necks stretched out like long, strike-ready snakes, snapping at him from three different angles.
From high above, Wei He looked down and finally saw the full body of the creature.
It was a massive, ten-foot-tall fox with three heads. Its entire body was deep black, lit only by six glowing white eyes, and its tail was actually a massive black mouth that looked just like a giant python.
