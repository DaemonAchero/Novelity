Standing in the snow, Wei He’s mind buzzed with a thousand thoughts. He stayed silent for a long time until his body fully settled into the Viscera Refining stage.
Entering this new realm meant his internal organs, already strengthened by his earlier force, had a solid base. Now came the next step: pushing that internal strength even further. He needed to form a thin shield of force around his organs to protect them from critical damage.
After that, he would complete the second refinement, choose a sense, and sharpen it. Finally, he would use that sense to read the outside world and lock it in.
"Locking the sense... What kind of things will I actually see?"
Wei He felt a mix of curiosity and quiet dread.
Most martial artists viewed this step with uncertainty. No one could guarantee their body talent was enough to reach the Sense Locking stage. Even someone as gifted as Wang Shaojun couldn't be sure.
Even with the right talent, you had to pray the rare beasts needed for your specific art weren't extinct. The truth of true martial arts was simple: you ate and absorbed elements from these beasts, making yourself stronger, faster, and less human.
Wei He snapped back to reality and turned toward the Old Master Temple.
It was an odd place. A Taoist temple filled with wooden Buddhist statues. Worse, someone had chopped the heads off every single statue.
Wei He walked up and stopped at the entrance.
Inside, the Lian family group huddled together, too terrified to cause trouble. On the other side, Gongsun Hong from the Natural Order Sect had already snunk away in the chaos.
"That Gongsun Hong ran off while you were fighting!" Lian Shuihan said, his voice trembling. "If you want to hunt him down, you can find him at the Natural Order Sect!"
Wei He ignored him. He just stared up at the temple's ancient wooden sign.
The wood was faded from years of wind and snow, but the carved letters carried a sharp, deadly intent. Only a true master could spot it.
"Senior, we are disciples of the Purple One Sect," Lian Youran said, stepping forward with a gift box. "You saved our lives. Please, take this tiny gift..."
Before she could finish, Wei He turned and walked away without a word. Within seconds, his figure faded into the snowstorm.
The Lian family stood frozen in the cold.
Lian Youran panicked and took a few quick steps after him. If she could build a connection with a killer like this, her family wouldn't have to fear the Natural Order Sect. If she could become his student, even better. She trusted her rare talent, her pretty face, and her body. Few men could say no to a desperate, beautiful woman.
But the chance was gone. He was already out of sight.
"Miss!" Uncle Zhao rushed to her side. "When he was fighting earlier, I heard him mention the Thousand Poison Sect. We cannot mess with them. They're rising fast and brutal. They would crush our sect like a bug."
"You think he's from the Thousand Poison Sect?!" Her eyes lit up.
"Miss... that sect is pure evil. We shouldn't go near them..."
She tuned him out. Now that she had seen real power, she had no interest in hiding under her grandfather’s weak protection. Going straight to the Thousand Poison Sect sounded far better.
"Youran, it's not that simple," her brother, Lian Shuihan, stammered from behind. His voice was filled with terror. "We're... we're poisoned!"
"What?!"
She spun around. Red spots were breaking out all over her brother’s face. The other guards had them too, thick and ugly like smallpox. She touched her own face and felt the raised bumps.
"Take the antidote powder! Quick!" Uncle Zhao barked.
They swallowed the pills, but it did nothing. The red spots only turned darker.
A chill ran down Lian Youran’s spine. The man hadn't even looked at them. He hadn't said a single word to them. Yet, they were all infected just by standing near him.
Lian Shuihan looked at the dead bodies in the snow and then at his poisoned men. He bit his lip hard.
"We change plans. We go to Xuanjing city and find the Thousand Poison Sect!"
They didn't even know what this poison was. If it was a unique brew, only the Thousand Poison Sect had the cure.
Lian Youran felt a sudden wave of nausea. If she had been even slightly rude to that monster, they would all be cold corpses by now, just like the dead fighters on the ground.
Wei He didn't care about the Lian family. He hadn't targetted them; they were just collateral damage from his attack on the Natural Order Sect.
Back at the Thousand Poison Sect, he locked his doors and trained in secret, ignoring the rest of the world.
Winter faded into spring. Two years flew by.
Wei He spent every day training. He pushed every single martial art he owned to the Bone Forging stage, creating bone force for each. But he couldn't push them further. Bone Forging meant reshaping your skeleton into specific forms. His body was already set into the Cloud Gathering Bone structure. It couldn't be changed again. So he stopped.
Instead, he focused entirely on strengthening his internal organs. Step by step, he broke through twelve small barriers in the Viscera Refining realm, his physical power growing steadily every day.
To keep a low profile, Wei He ordered his men to dump their toxic drug businesses. The wealth they had saved was already enough to feed the sect for thirty years.
The headquarters in the Black House Mountains had grown into a small, fortified mountain city, housing thousands of people. Fields of crops stretched across the cleared woods, making the sect entirely self-sufficient.
Under Wei He’s strict command, the sect pulled back from the world. They cut off outer connections, keeping only a few secret spies to track outside news.
Slowly, the name of Wei He, Master of the Thousand Poison Sect, began to fade from memory.
With foreign armies invading the land, major wars breaking out daily, nobody cared about a quiet sect hiding in the mountains anymore.
A soft spring breeze blew through the swaying willow trees.
It was the annual Root Gathering Festival in Xuanjing City. Families set out simple food offerings for the Harvest God, praying for peace and full bellies for the coming year. Crowds flooded the streets, celebrating at local markets and temples.
High inside a red tower in the Far Chess District, the Wei and Wang families held a quiet private party.
After years of surviving together, the two families were like one. Wei He had given his son Wei An to Wang Shaojun as a godson, while Wang Shaojun’s daughter became Wei He’s goddaughter.
Inside the main hall, Wan Qingqing and Wang Shaojun’s wife, Xie Xue, watched over the playing children.
The elders, Wan Ling and Wang Shaojun’s parents, sat nearby talking softly. Every now and then, Wang Shaojun’s father gave Wan Ling a lingering look, earning a sharp pinch to the ribs from his own wife.
Overall, the room was full of warm laughter.
"The provinces have declared independence, and the enemy army has halted at Yizhou for now," Wang Shaojun said, taking a slow sip of wine. "It feels peaceful, but it's just an illusion."
"Did you hear about the Zhou family?" he asked softly, looking out the window at the crowded street below.
"You mean Zhou Xingtong getting wrecked in a fight six months ago?" Wei He asked, frowning.
"Yes. Word just got out," Wang Shaojun sighed. "His right arm was torn completely off, and his blood power is ruined. His martial arts path is dead."
Wei He stayed quiet for a moment. "He was at the Bone Forging stage, right?"
"He actually made it to Viscera Refining. But it doesn't matter now. A ruined body, massive blood loss, and a missing arm. He'll never reach Sense Locking." Wang Shaojun shook his head.
"Why bring this up?"
"We are thirty now, Wei He. It's time to think about the future."
"What future?"
"It's a dark world out there. I have good talent, but pushing further in martial arts will take decades of hard training. And even then, there's no guarantee I'll ever break through."
Wang Shaojun sighed again. "So why waste all my years on a gamble? I'm building real power instead. In a few days, I'm heading to the provincial capital to take over as Chief of the Trade Division. Come with me."
Wei He looked into his friend's eyes and stayed silent.
He knew what this meant. Wang Shaojun was giving up the path of martial arts. He was choosing political power and military strength to protect his family and secure his future.
Taizhou was currently split between two giant powers: the Wushi Sect and the Taian Army.
The Taian Army had absorbed the remnants of the broken imperial court, growing massively in size and high-level fighters. Even top rebel groups didn't dare poke them now.
The Wushi Sect was too distant and arrogant to rely on, so the Wang family joining the Taian Army was the smart play.
