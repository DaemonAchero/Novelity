On the carriage ride back, Wei He stared at the thick stack of gold notes in his hand.
Ten notes, each worth a thousand taels.
Ten thousand taels of gold!
"What bold generosity," Wei He thought, slightly stunned.
This was the first time he had ever held so much money. It was also the first time he realized he might never be broke again.
Xiao Heng, a martial artist at the Grand Completion of Force Entry, only carried a few thousand taels on him. And this was real gold, not the standard coins used in Taizhou.
Around here, small everyday purchases relied on a strange bronze-colored coin. They called it the horn coin, or simply the big coin. One tael of gold equaled ten gold leaves. One gold leaf equaled ten gold beans. And a standard gold bean was worth ten big coins.
In Xuanjing City, ten thousand taels of gold could buy three thousand pounds of low-grade mutated beast meat. Even if he bought high-quality meat like the First-Line Green fish, it would still buy a full thousand pounds.
This was enough to cover all his expenses at Wanqing Hall for over ten years.
It was pure luxury.
At the same time, he felt completely confident he could clear up Rushui Shop's trouble. The client had paid this price targeting a Three-Blood martial artist. That meant a Three-Blood strength was enough to solve it. Since the job stayed within the Three-Blood limit, he could handle it.
Wei He tucked the money away.
"Now I have enough cash, and the Five Spirits Breath Technique is done. Time to test it out."
If the technique worked, he would take this money, use the Breakbound Pearl to its limit, and stuff himself with beast meat.
Training the Rain-Gathering Cloud-Accumulating Technique was painfully slow. With so much time on his hands, he could easily train other martial arts to make his body tough.
In his previous fights, he had already tasted the rewards of combining styles.
The Mountain Returning Fist, Five Peaks Palm, and Flying Dragon Technique had boosted his arm strength and explosive power. He could even trade heavy blows with Grand Completion Force Entry fighters without taking damage.
That sparked an idea. If he found complementary styles to toughen his whole body like his arms, how strong would he become?
He never worried about getting stuck at bottlenecks when learning multiple styles. With the Breakbound Pearl, bottlenecks did not exist.
Wei He also realized another point. What if he reached Force Entry later on? If he filled his whole body with these combined, complementary forces, how terrifying would that be?
Every style's Force had a unique trait. Some excelled at defense, some at counter-attacks, some at parrying, and others at stealth. If he could master them all and layer them over his entire body, would any enemy even be able to hurt him?
It would be like wearing layer upon layer of heavy armor. Unstoppable.
Of course, there were plenty of hurdles to overcome, but the Breakbound Pearl made it possible. He used to lack money, so he could only dream about it. Now, with money in hand, the dream was real.
Back home, Wei He went to the Thousand Bats Pavilion and bought a huge pile of water bear meat. It was the cheapest kind, but it filled the belly fast.
Even though it was cheap, it still cost three taels per pound. That was a bit cheaper than the beast meat in Feiye City, but not by much.
Since First-Line Green fish meat was in short supply, water bear meat was the best option. It had many impurities that usually messed up future breakthroughs, but it was cheap and let his stomach work at maximum power.
Ever since the Breakbound Pearl boosted his digestive system, nothing upset his stomach. He had even tried eating mild poison once, and it did nothing to him.
It was incredible.
After eating beast meat six times a day for three days, Wei He felt the color of the Breakbound Pearl shift slightly.
He never dared to eat like this before. Back then, he only received less than twenty pounds of First-Line Green fish meat a month. Eating like this would have wiped out his monthly supply in days.
Now that he had mastered the first level of the Rain-Gathering Cloud-Accumulating Technique and stabilized his base, it was time to collect the second level.
Inside Wanqing Hall.
Wan Qingqing was quietly teaching a female disciple the details of triggering the Rain-Gathering Force. She explained everything from blood changes and force ignition to precise control. Each step required endless practice.
Hall Master Wan Ling sat nearby, quietly reading a bamboo scroll.
Paper books were common these days, so bamboo scrolls were usually ancient texts. Wei He had looked into it before. The bamboo used here was not ordinary. It was a tough, rot-proof material that resisted moisture and bugs. It was very expensive.
Wei He walked to the open door and waited quietly. He had to wait until the female disciple finished her questions and got her answers before taking his turn.
Once the disciple thanked the master and left, Wei He stepped inside.
"Disciple Wei He greets the Hall Master and Chief Senior Sister."
He bowed respectfully to Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing.
"I heard you received funding from the Purple Bamboo Society. That is good news. I hope you use these resources to train hard and take another step forward," Wan Qingqing said with a smile.
"Thank you, Senior Sister. I am here today to collect the second level of the Rain-Gathering Cloud-Accumulating Technique," Wei He replied.
"That was fast. But it makes sense. You already had a solid Three-Blood foundation from your old style, so you understand this stage better than most. Moving fast is normal," Wan Qingqing said, understandingly.
She was a Grand Completion Force Entry master currently trying to forge her bones, so assessing Wei He's state was easy for her.
Wan Ling nodded slightly from the side. Wei He's progress was fast, but still within reason.
"Wait here." Wan Qingqing stepped into the back room and returned with a pale yellow booklet.
"This is the second level. Moving from the first to the second level is crucial for building your foundation. The nine branch halls have such detailed steps for the Three-Blood stage precisely to refine blood and strengthen the force produced."
She gave him a serious warning.
"Force is the foundation of all foundations. You must take it seriously. A strong force makes your future path smooth. But if your force is weak, even if you reach Force Entry later..."
Wei He nodded and took the booklet carefully.
"Thank you for the guidance, Senior Sister."
He bowed to Wan Ling once more and turned to leave.
"Wait." Wan Ling suddenly shifted her eyes to him, her face showing doubt.
"Did you... clear out your old cultivation?" she asked in surprise.
Wei He's heart skipped a beat. This was the moment of truth. He was about to test if he could fool a master at or above the Bone Forging level.
It all depended on the Five Spirits Breath Technique.
"How did you notice, Hall Master?"
"Before this, the extra styles you trained were clear as day. Clearing them out is fine, but it harms your body. Take good care of yourself from now on," Wan Ling said calmly.
"Yes. I understand," Wei He said, bowing his head.
"Focus on nourishing your blood, regulating your breath, and calming your mind. Do not lose your temper or stir up your blood for the next two years," Wan Ling added.
"Yes."
Wei He let out a silent sigh of relief. The Five Spirits Breath Technique actually worked.
Carrying the second level booklet home, Wei He sat down to study it carefully.
The second level was simple enough. It was all about refining the body's seven openings. The seven openings held seven different types of blood. He needed to use the blood stamp from his technique to match their flow and replace them.
It was slow, tedious work, and it stood as a huge obstacle for most Wanqing Hall disciples. Out of more than ten inner disciples, only three had broken through to the third level. And those three included Wan Qingqing herself.
Now that he proved the Five Spirits Breath Technique worked, Wei He visited the Heavenly Edge Building again. This time, he dove into the records and notes on the first floor to study the side effects of mixing different martial arts.
Right then, a letter from Rushui Shop arrived.
He took their money, so it was time to do the job.

Xuanjing City. Yubei Town.
Rushui Shop was well known across Yubei Town and nearby areas. It looked modest, but it ran fifteen separate stores, each with its own loyal customers.
After some adjustments, the recent turmoil in the shop had calmed down. But ever since Lin Xiaoxiao took charge, fresh troubles popped up.
Several stores faced constant vandalism and theft, ruining goods over and over. The local guards came a few times but found nothing. Desperate, Lin Xiaoxiao had to seek outside help.
That was how her deal with Wei He came to be.
Inside the second store, employees were tidying up goods on the shelves. Lin Xiaoxiao and Wen Lian stood by a counter, looking over the latest damage report.
"They did it again yesterday. These thugs have zero respect for the law!" Wen Lian slammed her hand on the counter in anger, scaring the nearby workers.
"It is fine. I notified him," Lin Xiaoxiao said calmly.
"You really believe he can fix this? The guy you hired only knows how to hide and train. What can he do here? Solve a crime? You are dreaming," Wen Lian snapped.
Thinking about her friend's foolish move still made her blood boil. Ten thousand gold! Just given away like that. She treated money like trash.
Lin Xiaoxiao shook her head without saying a word.
"Listen to me. If you hired someone with good talent, strong backing, and wide connections, they could bring a crowd to guard every store at night and catch the culprits in a few days. But you picked him. No background, no talent, no skills. What can he do? He will just stand here with us and stare at the wall!"
Wen Lian kept nagging. She was simply angry that her friend paid so much for someone who seemed useless.
"Even Lin Zhao only gets two thousand gold a month! What kind of deal did you make back then? And look at what he said: 'the more the better.' Is that how a normal person talks? Does he think money grows on trees? You wake up before dawn and work until midnight every day. You worked so hard for this money, yet he takes it without a second thought. Does he have a conscience?"
Lin Xiaoxiao patted Wen Lian, telling her to quiet down.
"Don't pat me! I tell you, even if he stood right here, I would say it to his face. If he fails to solve this, see if you regret it later! You handed over the cash so easily. Once you run out of money and the stores get wrecked, you will learn your lesson. Just don't come begging me for money then. Even if I go crazy or die, I will not lend you a single coin!"
"We need five hundred taels for the next stock shipment," Lin Xiaoxiao said, staring at her.
"Five hundred taels! Why don't you just rob a bank? I only have four hundred left!" Wen Lian pulled four hundred-tael gold notes out of her purse with practiced speed.
"Good enough," Lin Xiaoxiao took the money and went back to her calculations.
"Where was I?" Wen Lian lost her train of thought.
Lin Xiaoxiao shook her head.
"Anyway, you got ripped off big time! Luckily you only signed a three-year contract. Otherwise, you would be crying with nowhere to turn!" Wen Lian grumbled.
"Boss, Mr. Wei from the Heavenly Ink Gate is here," a female clerk walked up and whispered.
"Got it," Lin Xiaoxiao nodded. She dragged Wen Lian along to greet him at the entrance.
