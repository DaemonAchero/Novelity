Wei He turned and jumped into the air, rushing toward a distant spot outside the city.
Far away from Wandu City lay the Jushui Gorge, where his old friend, the Three-Headed Beaver, lived. He wanted to use its power to test how strong he really was.
Back then, the creature had fought Zhu Chen to a standstill, but Zhu Chen was hurt much worse. That meant the beast was stronger.
Trees blurred past as Wei He flew forward at high speed. His mind was not on the battle ahead, but on the Sensing notes from You Fu.
'Strength at the Sensing Stage is not about how many hyper-sensory perception traits you have, but how many times you have completed the Sensing process. The more times you finish it, and the longer you hold it, the stronger you get. However, having more hyper-sensory traits shortens the time between each Sensing cycle. The risk is higher, and the danger comes faster.' Wei He reviewed Zhu Chen’s information carefully, planning his path.
'The Sensing count determines true power, and that count is driven by my hyper-sensory traits. I have the Break-Through Bead to bypass bottlenecks, but I do not know if it works on Sensing. I should complete one normal Sensing cycle first to check before trying to speed up the process.' He could not risk a mistake. Failing the Sensing process meant mutating into a monster. Look at the Three-Headed Beaver. All the meat of wild beasts eaten during past cultivation sat in the belly like a time bomb. Fall short here, and you became a mindless beast forever.
Even with the Bead to help him, he preferred not to gamble recklessly.
Swoosh!
Wei He stopped dead on top of a tree canopy, looking straight ahead.
A deep gorge wrapped in thick white fog lay before him. Warning stone tablets surrounded the area, placed by his men to keep outsiders away.
"Zhu Chen only went through Sensing once," Wei He thought, stepping forward with care. "The beaver did it once too. It only won because its body mutated, giving it massive strength and fast healing. If I can beat this thing, it proves I am top-tier among those at the first Sensing stage."
He moved step by step without hiding his presence, giving the beast a clear warning.
The white fog grew denser, turning into a pitch-black haze where he could barely see his own hands. Step by step, he slowed down, reaching the center of the gorge.
Through the heavy mist, three dark fox heads stared down at him with cold, dead eyes.
"Long time no see," Wei He said softly.
Last time, he had to look up at the monster. Now, at nearly three meters tall himself, he stood eye-to-eye with the creature.
"Still using the same old trick?" Wei He smiled and stomped his foot down hard.
Boom!
The ground shattered. The beaver's snake tail, trying to sneak up from underground, was pinned under his heel, unable to move.
Wei He used simple Sea-Covering Force, but the sheer weight of it made the beast shriek in pain.
Hisss!
All three fox heads lunged down at him at once.
"Too slow."
Wei He stepped back just enough to dodge, then drove his index finger straight into one of the heads.
Bang!
Multiple layers of force exploded inside the fox head—twisting, piercing, and bursting all at once. Before the monster's active force could block it, the kinetic shock tore deep into its flesh.
The beast howled as a bloody hole blew open on its head. Yet, within seconds, the flesh squirmed and healed back together. It roared and lunged again, faster this time.
Wei He dodged easily, striking back each time. He was far faster than the creature, dodging every hit while dealing steady damage. But the beast healed too fast for normal strikes to matter.
After testing his speed, Wei He decided to face its active force head-on. Ten layers of bone force inside his body gathered into his arms. The two blood sacs from his Whale-Flooding Skill opened up, pumping massive amounts of blood into his limbs. His arms swelled to double their normal size, turning a dark, bruised purple.
"Come!" Wei He barked.
ROAR!
The monster brought its three heads together and smashed down from above. Wei He threw a full-power punch to meet it.
Hoom!
The shockwave blew the white fog away in giant circles.
Wei He’s heavy bone force slammed directly into the beast's active force and razor-sharp teeth. The active force tried to shred his arm, but the sheer density and wild effects of ten different bone forces ground it down. Unable to break through, the creature was dragged into a raw contest of strength and force.
They stood locked in place, equal in raw power.
Knowing his bone force could not burst forever, Wei He suddenly stepped to the left. The beaver crashed hard into the empty ground, smashing dirt and rocks everywhere.
Wei He slapped the snake tail away, leaped back out of the fog, and landed outside the gorge.
The test was done. He could match it in a short fight, but a long battle of attrition was foolish. The best way to kill it was poison. His True Poison Method was far deadlier than any drug, and he could easily use it to finish the job.
Standing by the warning stone, Wei He looked back at the foggy gorge and sighed.
"Sensing... This world is getting stranger and stranger."
Without high power, he could not protect anything. The Wandu Sect looked grand, but two Sensing experts from the Wushi Sect could wipe it out in a day.
He thought about leaving Taizhou, but every other region had its own monsters—the Wanfei Palace in Jinzhou, heavy warlords in Bianzhou, and strong sects everywhere else. Moving changed nothing.
Inside the Wushi Sect, on the Jiu-Zhen Peak.
Dozens of disciples practiced their martial arts in absolute unison on a massive stone platform.
Yan Jiuzhen, Head of the Jiu-Zhen Hall, sat on a high white rock facing the sea of clouds. Though over fifty years old, his face remained smooth and young.
"Hall Master," a disciple said, bowing low. "Two reports came in today."
"Speak," Yan Jiuzhen said, eyes closed.
"First, Master Zibai sent a request for help from Dongzhou regarding the Dragon Blood. The three Peaks may send the Duanzheng Peak to handle it."
Yan Jiuzhen sighed. "So many resources wasted on one drop of blood. If I knew it would drag on like this, we should have given up early."
"The second matter," the disciple continued, "Huang Siwei and Xu Zhou have set a debate date at Tai Lake on April fifth. The Sect wants us to decide who to send."
"Huang Siwei represents the old academies, while Xu Zhou leads the new movement," Yan Jiuzhen said, opening his eyes. "This is a direct clash between two massive factions. I will speak to my senior brother about it later."
"There is one last thing," the disciple added quickly. "Su Zhao of the Farmer Faction was brought to the Wandu Sect by the Extermination Alliance. Senior Brother Lu agreed to give Wandu Sect one month before bringing Su Zhao back to our mountain."
Yan Jiuzhen frowned. "Why wait a month?"
"To avoid direct conflict with the Alliance, and to try to bring Wandu Sect to our side—"
"No need," Yan Jiuzhen cut him off coldly. "Since our Jiu-Zhen Hall handles this, tell Lu Yunbai to take the man within three days. Kill anyone who stands in the way."
"But the Wandu Sect..."
"If they resist, wipe them out."
