Deep in Black House Mountain, outside the Cave of True Art.
Lei Wu listened to the others, then walked toward the cave.
A few fighters from Jin Prefecture tried to block him. Lei Wu gave a soft push, sending them flying into the distance.
"He hit us! A expert from Tai Prefecture is here!"
"Get him together! Drive these savages out!"
"Kill them!"
The Jin Prefecture fighters shouted in a mess.
"Shut up!" Lei Wu roared. His inner power flared out, pressing down on everyone around him. The noise died down instantly.
"Look who's acting big. Ah, it's Lei Wu."
A young girl stepped out from the crowd.
She wore golden bells on her wrists, a light blue martial suit, and a white cloak with a soft fur collar that made her skin look pale white. She held a thin flower branch in her hand, smiling pure and sweet like a girl next door.
Unlike the others, her official language was clear and proper, showing she came from a good background.
"Yue Xiaodie?!"
Lei Wu froze. A flash of fear crossed his eyes.
Not just him—the fighters around them all stepped back in fear. The noisy crowd fell dead silent.
"I heard a treasure was here, so I came to look. But after all this time, you found nothing? Fighting over a basic True Art? Don't you know nobody dares practice a True Art without a master?"
She smiled, letting the bells on her wrist spin softly with a pleasant ring.
"Yue Xiaodie..." Lei Wu bit his lip. He looked around and saw she was alone.
She was famous for her cruelty in Jin Prefecture, but with so many people watching, if he backed down now, his reputation would be ruined.
He took a step forward and raised his voice. "Miss Yue, our Tai Prefecture group found this cave too. We should share it. What do you think?"
Yue Xiaodie was already walking toward the cave.
She ignored him completely, not even turning her head.
The guards at the entrance saw her and panicked.
"It's so small inside, and so many people are crowded together. Don't you feel it's dirty?" Yue Xiaodie stopped, a look of disgust on her face. "Little Red, clean it up."
Before anyone could react, a red blur flashed through the trees, passed over the crowd, and shot into the cave.
The red figure wore a big red robe. It was short, like a child, but fast as lightning.
Screams and curses instantly erupted from inside.
"Who is that?!"
"Watch out!"
"Kill her!!"
Screams, panic, and the sound of people vomiting blood turned the cave into total chaos.
Ten seconds later, the red figure flashed back out, landing just behind Yue Xiaodie.
It was a short dwarf wearing a black mask. His skin was pitch black, dried like old meat. Blood dripped from his fingertips onto the dirt.
"Miss, all done."
"Good." Yue Xiaodie walked in, stepping across the bloody ground without care now.
The Tai Prefecture fighters who wanted to push forward earlier didn't dare say a word.
Lei Wu stood there, tall and strong, but cold sweat soaked his forehead. He didn't dare move.
He tried to step back slowly, but a pair of cold eyes locked onto him.
It was the red-robed dwarf.
His small eyes stared straight through his mask, full of kill intent.
Lei Wu froze, crying inside. He only spoke up to save face, and now this happened...
Inside the cave.
Yue Xiaodie walked over the blood-stained ground without a care. She went deep to the end and saw words carved into the stone wall.
There was no title, only dense rows of a technique manual.
"A nameless True Art? Interesting..." She read through it, her eyes lighting up with mischief. She traced the words in the air with her fingers.
Then she picked out a few key words and reached out.
Carving on stone with bare hands was easy for anyone at Three-Blood level. By pushing out inner power, the fingertips acted like chisel blades.
Yue Xiaodie was skilled at faking handwriting since childhood. Her copies looked identical to the original.
After changing key words, she took out a small bottle and sprayed liquid on the wall.
The fresh cuts quickly corroded under the potion, looking just as old as the original text.
Done with her work, she turned to leave.
"Let's go, Little Red. Nothing worth seeing." She fake-yawned.
The dwarf stayed put, staring fixedly at Lei Wu.
"Oh? Did he offend you?" Yue Xiaodie paused, then smiled. "Then gouge his eyes out. A big man staring like that gives me the creeps."
Whoosh!
The dwarf shot forward like a bullet, aiming straight for Lei Wu.
Lei Wu was terrified. The dwarf's speed was far beyond his own.
He pushed his power up and threw a hard palm strike to block.
The red blur shifted mid-air, dodging the palm, and clawed straight at his face.
"Ah!!"
Lei Wu screamed, feeling a sharp pain across his eyes.
He stood frozen, mind going blank.
Yet the pain wasn't as bad as he thought.
He opened his eyes. The dwarf was gone.
His eyelids were scratched, but he wasn't blind!
The dwarf was already back next to Yue Xiaodie, crouching low, staring fiercely into the forest.
"Sharp hearing."
A voice spoke from behind Lei Wu.
Before Lei Wu could turn, Xue Dan and the others nearby paled and scrambled several steps back.
"The Sect Master of Myriad Poisons?! Why is he here?!"
"I thought he had a duel with the Lishan Sect? Why isn't he preparing?"
"Is he here for the True Art too?"
Fearful whispers reached Lei Wu's ears.
The Sect Master of Myriad Poisons?!
Lei Wu's heart sank. He turned around quickly.
A huge figure over two meters tall stood nearby, long hair falling over his shoulders, wearing a black cloak.
The story of him poisoning hundreds of Scarlet Bright troops was already confirmed by official wanted posters.
Plus, his fight at Tianya Tower where he killed Yan Junshan alone had forced the Lishan Sect to send another master for a duel. The whole area was talking about him.
Lei Wu forced a smile. Grateful to be alive, he quickly stepped aside.
Luckily, Wei He seemed to be in a good mood. He ignored Lei Wu and walked straight toward the cave entrance.
Lei Wu and Xue Dan looked at each other, sighing in relief.
The government wanted posters described Wei He as a ruthless monster with a strange temper who poisoned people over the smallest things.
They were lucky he was in a good mood today.
Looking back, they watched Yue Xiaodie.
She was just as cruel and unpredictable in Jin Prefecture. Nobody knew her true background, but she killed people on a whim.
Some were killed just for looking at her twice. Others were butchered because they made too much noise near her.
She was a true demon.
Now, two ruthless monsters were facing each other. If they fought, it would be a show.
Wei He didn't care what the crowd thought.
He saw the cave the hunter mentioned. But he frowned—too many people were already here.
He had traded a free medical treatment for this location. With so many people around, he felt he took a loss on the deal.
Should he... kill them all? That way, the art on the wall would belong to him alone.
The thought shocked even himself.
He was a good person at heart. Why did he have such a cruel idea?
'It must be from poisoning too many people lately... my mindset is slipping...' Wei He sighed in his heart.
'Whatever. I'll just leave a mark.' He shook his hand slightly, releasing a invisible, faint scented dust into the air.
It was a harmless mark for now. But if needed, a simple trigger could turn it into deadly poison.
The moment the scent spread, the dwarf next to Yue Xiaodie growled in distress, glaring at Wei He.
"Hmm?" Wei He turned his head, meeting the glare of both the dwarf and Yue Xiaodie.
"You can notice my poison?"
The Jin Prefecture fighters grew excited.
They didn't know Wei He and didn't care about Tai Prefecture rumors. Seeing him stand tall against Yue Xiaodie made them grin.
"This is going to be good. That savage is going to lose his eyes!" a young man with a silver headband laughed, holding a pipe.
"Last time, the leader of the Yuejiang Gang made a drunk joke, and Yue Xiaodie cut off his tongue and arm. Now this guy... tsk tsk," a Jin fighter said, feeling relieved.
"The savages seem to fear him. He must be strong. Maybe he can last a bit longer."
"In Tai Prefecture, only Wushi Sect is worth mentioning. Anyone else meeting Yue Xiaodie is dead meat."
"Good thing he's taking her focus. We can use this chance to leave."
The stronger fighters had already copied the text and left. The ones staying were either here for drama or lacked the power to enter the cave.
Wei He ignored the chatter and kept his eyes on Yue Xiaodie.
