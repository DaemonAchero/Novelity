“Baiyu Sect… It’s the Baiyu Sect!”
Tie Lang and his three men looked toward the screaming. They spotted the female priest right away.
“Baiyu Sect. She carries a sword on her back but fights with bare hands. That is their mark. And the pink petal mark on her face… It's those lunatics!”
Tie Lang’s face turned heavy. He wanted to back off.
The Gujian Sect was just a pack of stray dogs. The female priest of Baiyu Sect was a real tiger.
They were not on the same level.
Even their godfather, Zhou the Benevolent, would not mess with her easily.
“Retreat!” Tie Lang said coldly.
“But Big Brother, the Chongsheng Cup!” a man next to him shouted in panic.
Tie Lang stayed silent. He looked around.
“Is she all alone?” His eyes flickered.
Gao Ling was dead. The Gujian Sect men stopped, staring at the female priest with killer eyes.
The rules in the Gujian Sect were brutal. If they ran back now, the punishment hall would cut off their tongues and limbs. It was worse than death.
Their sword style focused on stabbing. Simple, direct, and quick to learn.
So the sect never ran short of low-level men like them.
“She’s alone! Kill her!!”
Someone yelled first.
The crowd went wild.
Gao Xiu was crushed by grief. His brother was dead, and his mind snapped.
“Kill!!”
He roared, charging straight at the female priest.
Puft!
Cold light flashed.
He collapsed right in front of her. A petal-shaped wound showed on his chest.
The strike pierced straight through his heart and came out his back. Simple, direct, deadly.
No one even saw how she moved.
“The Baiyu Sect of the capital… truly deadly…” Tie Lang’s eyes shrank. He was the fastest and strongest here, so only he caught her movement.
She poked out one finger first, then pushed her own elbow with her other hand.
Using both hands, she broke through Gao Xiu’s defense, tore his skin and bone, and destroyed his heart.
Gao Xiu died before he even knew what hit him.
She was just too fast.
“Retreat!” Tie Lang didn't waste another word. He took his men and left fast.
This was beyond their power. They had to go back and get backup.
With their leader dead, the Gujian Sect men lost their nerves. They backed off slowly, not daring to step forward.
The female priest didn't care. She turned around, walked to Gao Ling’s body, and searched him. She pulled out a small black silk box.
Click.
She opened it. Inside sat a small green jade cup, the size of a human eye.
Ancient texts were carved all around the cup, making a full poem.
Vicious beasts were carved on the base—snakes, dragons, bulls, and sheep.
“The cup is here! Kill!!”
Shadows jumped out from the dark corners. They formed a square, trapping the female priest in the center.
“Kill!!”
These men were all dressed like common laborers.
, The female priest kept her cool. She stood up and closed the box.
“The Chongsheng Cup is fake,” she said softly.
“Fake? Then where is the real one?” a sharp voice called out from the dark.
“I don’t know,” she answered.
“Kill her! Take the cup first. Who knows if she is lying!” the sharp voice yelled.
The crowd rushed in.
A few moments later.
The snow fell harder. The ground was painted red, covered with bodies.
Puft.
The female priest slowly pulled her hand out of the last old man’s heart. She shook off the blood.
“I told you, it’s fake.”
Looking down at the dead old man, her voice remained cold.
Now, she had to find the real Chongsheng Cup. Master Zhuang was killed by the Gujian Sect.
If the Gujian Sect had the real cup, they wouldn't come here to kill Zhou’s adopted children.
If Zhou the Benevolent got the cup, he wouldn't stay here either. He would have run away first thing.
So… Master Zhuang must have swapped the cup himself.
Her eyes turned sharp. She knew where to look now.
When Master Zhuang was in Feiye City, who did he meet? Where did he go?
Those people and those places were the key.
Before reaching Feiye City, he had no time or chance to swap it.
Only during his quiet days in Feiye City could he find a craftsman to make a fake cup.
Knowing her path, the female priest turned and walked toward the city gates.
At Jianxia Gorge.
The birds in the sky were lured away easily by the beast bait.
Taking this chance, the wagon team drove into the gorge, moving fast.
When the bait ran out, someone threw another piece far out.
The birds swarmed toward the new target.
The wagon team kept going and passed Jianxia Gorge safely.
After moving a distance past the gorge, Fang Zheng and his people finally relaxed a bit to take a break.
Wei He and his group were ready to part ways with them.
“Chun’er, you really don’t want to come with us?” Wan Qingqing smiled at Fang Yongchun, who came to see them off.
She and Wei He both liked this smart little girl. They checked her talent and found it was decent, so Wan Qingqing wanted to take her as a disciple.
Too bad…
“Thank you, Sister Qingqing. I want to stay with my stepfather and the others.” The girl bowed politely. To her, learning martial arts was simple—just pay money to join a local school.
“What a pity…” Wan Qingqing sighed.
Wei He smiled beside her.
“If you like her, leave her something to remember you by.”
“Fair enough.” Wan Qingqing took out a token from her sleeve and tossed it. It stuck right into the ground in front of Fang Yongchun.
“This is my personal token. If you ever want to find me, bring this to Wanja Inn in Fusong Town, Xuanjing. Just show it to them.”
“Thank you, Sister Qingqing!” The girl was thrilled. She knew this was a rare chance, so she picked it up quickly.
Heavy snow fell, piling thick on the ground.
The two teams split up, taking two different paths forward.
Wei He had only six people, a leopard, and a horse.
The triangular leopard pulled the wagon for a while, but they swapped it back for the horse.
The leopard shook the wagon too much. Even after many beatings, it was still not as steady as a horse.
Wei He just switched back.
Because of this, the Three-Poison Monk was sent to ride the leopard, handle scouting, hunting, and chores.
The driver role fell to Bai Dou, who was just conquered.
Only Wei He would use a Bone-Forging master as a driver.
Since his own power was far above Bone-Forging, he didn't care.
Past Jianxia Gorge was a vast sea of trees.
It was the same forest on the border when Wei He left Yun Prefecture.
He sat cross-legged in the wagon, practicing his skill, looking out the window to enjoy the view.
“Is this Yun Prefecture?” Wan Qingqing leaned against him and asked softly.
She had never been here.
“Past this forest is Yun Prefecture. Back when I left, I only had my second sister and a few martial brothers with me…” Wei He recalled.
“You were only at Three-Blood back then, right? Crossing this huge forest with so few people, facing dangerous beasts.” Wan Qingqing sighed. “It must have been hard.”
“It was,” Wei He nodded. “But that’s all in the past.”
He came back for two reasons: to pick up his second sister, Wei Ying, and to find out what happened to his eldest sister and parents.
If they were alive, he wanted to see them. If dead, he wanted their bodies.
And if they were killed, who did it?
Some blood had to be spilled.
He also remembered the Seven Allies, who broke his school and killed Master Zheng…
Hong Clan, Martial Masters Union, Xiangqu Sect, Shaoyang Sect. And his old companions: Guan Ye, Cheng Shaojiu, Jiang Yan, Jiang Su.
Memories and faces flashed in his mind.
“Back then, plague hit the city. Second sister was kind, so she opened a shop selling steamed buns. She made cheap buns out of coarse grains so poor people could trade for them.”
Wei He smiled.
“Looking back, it was silly. We were lucky a crowd didn't loot the shop or pass the disease to us.”
“Sister did that?” Wan Qingqing asked in surprise.
“Yeah. Our home was damp and cold, with mold all over the walls. Rats ran around at night. They were starving, so if you weren't careful, they’d bite your foot and give you the plague.
Too many people starved or died of sickness in the city. Herbs ran out… A rat bite meant death soon after. So we had to be very careful.”
“So harsh…” Wan Qingqing had never heard of such things. She gently held Wei He’s hand.
“That wasn't even the worst part. At night, we had to lock the door early. If the Xiangqu Sect parades saw you awake, they’d drag you out to join them, taking your money. If you refused, they ruined your family,” Wei He said. “Every night, I heard their chanting outside.”
Wan Qingqing gasped. Growing up in the Tianyin Sect, she had never seen such a brutal life. Just listening to it was chilling.
“Later, I’ll take you to see where I used to live, and where I started martial arts… Then we’ll pay our respects to my master.” Wei He said in a deep voice.
“Without Master Zheng, there would be no me today.”
“Okay!” Wan Qingqing nodded firmly. She really wanted to see where her husband grew up.
