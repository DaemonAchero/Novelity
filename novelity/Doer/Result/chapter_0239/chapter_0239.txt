No matter what, Sect Master Wei, we have to fight you today.
You Rong stood out from the other You family members. He lost his usual rich-nobleman look, replaced by a cold, sharp energy.
Swish!
He angled his red-tasseled spear down. A twisting force hummed around the tip.
"Wei He, do you know what kind of mess your actions will cause?" Wang Yehe looked at him, sighing. He seemed almost sorry to see Wei He end up like this.
"Mess?" Wei He narrowed his eyes. "When Zhu Chen set a trap for me, he didn't care about the mess. Now that he lost, I’m supposed to care? Why should I?"
Wang Yehe had no words. He had seen what Zhu Chen did. He just didn't expect Wei He to strike back so hard and fast.
If it were him, he would have just forced the Wan Du Sect to break up, then recruited Wei He and a few top guys to keep things quiet.
Too bad he never understood what Zhu Chen really wanted, or why they needed to drag so many martial artists to the capital.
"Uncle, if you're done talking, move," Wei He said calmly. "For Shaojun's sake, I won't kill you."
Wang Yehe sighed again. He didn't move. He lowered his right hand, energy gathering on his palm. He was ready to fight.
"Let me test the Wan Du Sect's power today," You Rong said, his eyes turning fierce as his spear trembled.
Zhou Rong had been trying to stay hidden in the background, hoping Wei He wouldn't notice him.
Seeing the talk fail, he sighed, stepped forward, and ran his family's star-crossing power. Layers of force bloomed like flowers over his hands.
The three took positions around Wei He. Other fighters spread out to guard the outer ring.
Three Viscera-Refining level forces circled in the air like burning fires, stirring up heavy gusts of wind.
"Kill!!!"
The three moved at once.
Spear, palms, and finger attacks rushed in like illusions, completely boxing him in.
The combined force crashed down like a solid wall of pure pressure right in front of him.
Wei He's eyes lit up. His power surged wildly—combining bone forces, light force, iron skin, five-element force, and the massive strength of his main technique all at once.
Power flowed from his body into his arms, pushing straight to his palms.
"Heart Severing!"
He roared and charged head-on, throwing his deadliest strike.
The four clashed instantly.
BOOM!!
An invisible wind exploded from the center.
Clear waves of force shot out in all directions, knocking back the nearby guards several meters.
Wei He's palms pierced straight through the weak spot in their combined attack. He forced his body through the wall of wind.
Unleashing his full speed, he flashed toward You Rong and slammed a palm down.
Pfft!
Before the palm even hit, You Rong spewed a mouthful of blood and flew backward.
Wei He froze for a second. I haven't even hit him yet. Why is he spitting blood?
Before he could react, Wang Yehe and Zhou Rong both spat blood too, flying back and crashing through the roof.
Silence fell over the roof.
Wei He stood there, looking at his hands, then at the rest of the three families' people standing around dumbfounded.
The remaining men looked at each other. They charged forward, screamed halfway, and threw themselves off the roof.
Wei He understood.
If they didn't fight him, their superiors would punish them later. But if they really fought him—a man who beat Zhu Chen—they knew they would die.
So they played this trick.
They fought, got "hurt," and let him get away. Now they had a good excuse for the higher-ups.
It was just a show put on by the top fighters of the three families.
Wei He shook his head and leaped toward the General's Manor.
Since he knew where the three families stood, he knew what to expect at the manor.
When he arrived, half the soldiers were gone. Jiang Yan and his core men had already run off.
Wei He didn't care. He killed the few soldiers left behind, kept one alive to ask where Jiang Yan went, and chased him out of the city.
With his speed, even a mutated horse couldn't outrun him, let alone a low-level martial artist like Jiang Yan.
Before the group could even make it past the city gate, Wei He caught up, forced out the names and locations he wanted, and beat them all to death.
Killing over ten people in public was a huge deal in Xuantian City.
But with the three families covering it up, and Wei He keeping the fight small, he just searched the bodies and walked away.
Over the next ten days, Wei He and the lightly wounded members of his sect hunted down every single hideout of the Sun-Seeking Willow-Night network.
With Wei He's speed, once he got a location, he could cross twenty miles in ten minutes.
One by one, every hideout in the region was wiped out.
The spies scattered and hid underground, abandoning all their bases.
In just a few days, over a hundred spies were dead.
That was more than half of their total network in the city.
Wei He also hunted down the military generals who had taken part in the attack on his sect.
Family after family was wiped out completely. He left no one alive. His methods were brutal and terrifying.
Wei He never claimed to be a good guy. He just wanted to live his life.
But people kept coming out to use him or push him around.
During this slaughter, he didn't just kill spies; he also killed some "elite" guards from the three families.
Both sides understood the game. When Zhu Chen attacked the sect before, the three families held back.
The You family hid, the Zhou family let people pass, and Wang Shaojun even helped him.
So Wei He returned the favor. He helped them get rid of these "elites"—who were actually traitors inside their own families.
After all, when the higher-ups came to investigate, a few minor injuries wouldn't be enough to explain why Zhu Chen died and all the spies were killed, while the three families remained totally untouched.
To protect themselves, the three families made the exact same choice.
However, this wave of killings showed the three families just how terrifying Wei He really was.
He was too fast. Even faster than before.
Combined with his record of injuring three top experts in one move right in front of Zhu Chen, his reputation shot straight to the top.
Within days, Wei He was named the number one fighter in all of Xuantian City.
Once the last remaining spies were hunted down or driven off, the city went quiet.
Wei He had no interest in killing innocent people to vent his anger. Because he spared the weak, the public couldn't decide if he was a demon or a hero.
In Taizhou Capital, inside Tianshui Inn.
Guests were having breakfast on the first floor.
A storyteller stood on a small stage, speaking loudly about the recent tragedy in Xuantian City.
"Over half a month ago, at the General's Manor, three top experts fought the Wan Du Sect Master, Wei He!"
"The battle shook the ground and blocked out the sun! Dozens of holes were blasted through the roof of the manor!"
"Wei He’s poison skills were terrifying! With a single move, clouds of deadly poison filled the air! When he struck, not only were the three experts hurt, but the men around them collapsed before they could even get close!"
As the crowd listened, a man with a blade on his back shouted out, "Wait, I heard that big fight happened at the Zhu Manor, not the General's Manor!"
"Correct!" the storyteller called out. "There was a fight at the General's Manor, and another at the Zhu Manor! And don't forget the final battle in the Black House Mountains! Who won and who lost is still a mystery, but..."
The storyteller snapped his fan shut.
"Zhu Chen has vanished, and Wei He entered the city for revenge, killing hundreds of people! That is a fact! And that battle at the General's Manor was what proved Wei He is the strongest man in Xuantian!"
"The strongest in Xuantian? That's a huge title!" a man at a table said.
"He must be strong. That city used to be ruled by the Heavenly Seal Sect," another replied.
At a nearby table, a few men and women in grey robes with Taoist hair buns were eating breakfast.
Their meal was simple—cabbage and egg porridge, wild greens, and a small dish of dried meat.
One of the female disciples looked surprised when she heard the name Wei He.
"What is this Wan Du Sect? And this Wei He... which character is his name?" she asked softly.
"It means 'harmony,'" a companion whispered. "I heard his sect came from the old Heavenly Seal Sect. After it was destroyed, the branches split up, and his group took the lead."
"The man is a genius," another said with envy. "In just a few years, he went from a beginner to a master, then pushed straight into the Viscera-Refining level. Now no one in Xuantian can match him."
The female disciple listened, and her doubts faded.
It sounded like the man who had traveled to Taizhou with her before, but it couldn't be him. That man had terrible talent. There was no way he could reach such a high level so quickly.
The Viscera-Refining level was something only senior elders could reach.
"Forget about him," one disciple said. "At least three of our elders left the mountain this time. I wonder what's going on."
"I heard a lot of experts are heading to Yishuang County. Even our elders went there."
The woman named Jiang didn't care much about the news. She knew her own limits; her internal power was still low.
She lowered her head and ate her food. It was better to just go back to the mountain and train. The world outside was getting more chaotic by the day.
