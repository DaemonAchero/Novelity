A wave of screams burst out.
More than a dozen people around suddenly clutched their throats, their faces showing pain. After just a few seconds, they slumped to the ground.
Wei He stood in place, looking with surprise at the woman in the white dress.
"Interesting. What kind of powerful poison-repelling item do you have on you?"
He had already used his two most lethal types of martial master mixed poison on the person in front of him, yet it only made her head feel slightly dizzy.
"Miss, can you let me see it?"
"You...!" The woman in white saw her subordinates fall to the ground. Clearly, they had taken poison-repelling medicine in advance, held their breath, and worn face masks, yet they still fell victim.
Knowing she had underestimated Wei He, she was about to speak when she was shocked to see the figure opposite her suddenly enlarge.
While she was speaking, Wei He closed in once again, striking with a palm.
"You forced me to do this!!" The woman in white finally let out a roar, her internal force contracting and then suddenly exploding along a specific trajectory.
"Coffin! Open!!"
With a low shout, her whole body's internal force suddenly expanded by nearly double. She rushed forward again, slashing out with a sword.
Yet at this moment, Wei He was already dozens of meters away, about to disappear into another patch of woods.
That guy's surprise attack just now was just a fake move!
His movement technique was free to advance or retreat, almost ignoring inertia. In that exact fraction of a second, he changed direction, turned around, and leaped far away.
Only the woman in white was left standing in place. She had just activated her secret technique, sacrificing her vital energy and internal organs in exchange for greater and more powerful strength, but she hadn't expected...
Watching Wei He, whom she could no longer catch up to, the woman in white felt a stuffiness and oppression in her chest. She could no longer hold it back, and a mouthful of rebellious blood surged up.
Puff!
She opened her mouth and spat out a mist of blood, fiercely slashing her sword toward her side. The blade cut through empty air, whipping up a gust of wind that tilted the grass on the ground.
"Ahhhhhhhh!!! Wei He, you brat, next time we meet, I will kill you!!"
The internal injuries brought on by activating the secret technique and the pent-up anger collided together, deepening her injuries.
Yet fierce as her words were, she also knew that when she and Wei He fought in actual combat, they were evenly matched. To truly fight to the death would be hard, hard, hard!
She was a grand master at the peak of Organ Refining, supremely talented, and had served as the master of the Returning Wild Goose Pagoda for less than forty years. Although she had not yet reached the realm of the previous pagoda master, she was already a rising star within the sect.
She had never expected to suffer a secret loss in such a small place!
At this thought, the woman felt even more stifled in her heart.
Cough, cough...
Just because she had exerted force to burst forth, the poison she had taken earlier began to rage violently again. She did not dare to lift the secret technique immediately, fearing the opponent would pull a U-turn, but she also had to suppress the poison in her lungs right away. She immediately turned and walked away.

Below Tianya Pavilion.
Zhou Rong continuously pointed out his Flying Star energy, pressing the old man with the smoking pipe to continuously retreat, facing danger several times.
If not for multiple draws forced by a style of mutual destruction, and if not for Zhou Rong's unwillingness to suffer even the slightest injury and his desire for a complete victory to take down his opponent, the old man with the smoking pipe would likely have been defeated long ago.
Even so, following this trend, he would not be able to hold on for much longer.
On the other side, Mother Zeng had to deal with Yan Haochun and an unknown Bone Refining elder from the Zhou family all by herself, making the situation even more difficult.
In the open space in front of Tianya Pavilion, strands of internal force churned the air currents, stirring up gusts of wind that made the surrounding dozen meters a battlefield of swirling air and whistling sounds.
Two figures kept circling Mother Zeng, palm and fist striking respectively from two directions, continuously lashing out with bone strength.
Although Mother Zeng also resisted with bone strength, she was already old and frail. Even though bone refining had extended her prime, how could she withstand the combined exhaustion of two experts of the same level now?
In just a few dozen moves, gradually, even with a desperate struggle, she could no longer reverse the declining trend.
Finally, after another dozen or so moves.
Yan Haochun spotted an opening, flew up, and fiercely kicked Mother Zeng in the side of the waist.
This kick carried the explosion of his bone strength. If it landed, it would surely shatter Mother Zeng's protective internal force and cause her to be instantly defeated.
In this situation, defeat meant death.
However, Mother Zeng was currently exchanging palms with another person and had no time to relieve the pressure or dodge, so she could only watch helplessly as this kick rapidly approached.
Bang!!
In an instant, a figure rushed over from the side and struck Yan Haochun's kick with a palm, knocking it off course.
Yan Haochun was also startled. The person who suddenly darted out was so fast that he hadn't noticed them at all.
With his kick beaten back, he quickly retreated and looked up again.
He saw a woman in green martial attire, with pretty features and a heroic spirit, solemnly raising her hand to block the side of Mother Zeng.
"Who are you!?" Yan Haochun asked in surprise.
"Wan Qingqing! Wan Qingqing of the Wanqing Sect is here!" Not far away, a commotion stirred among the crowd.
"Didn't they say she has a chest big enough to run a horse across? Height close to three meters? Eyes like copper bells? This... the gap is way too big, isn't it?"
"Can you believe storytelling? But the Wanqing Sect really came! Truly audacious, they dare to show their faces directly while the government is still issuing a wanted notice."
"Since the Wanqing Sect has appeared, doesn't that mean the other branches will be showing up soon too?"
Waves of clamor continuously drilled into the ears of everyone present.
Yan Haochun came to his understanding. Looking at the spirited and heroic Wan Qingqing opposite him, a trace of admiration flashed in his eyes.
"To step into Bone Refining at such a young age, your talent is truly not weak. What a pity, you are going to die here."
"Mother Zeng, are you alright?" Wan Qingqing had arrived not long ago. Seeing the critical situation, she had no choice but to show herself and clear the siege.
Hearing Yan Haochun's words, her pretty face turned cold and stern.
"I didn't expect a great sect like the Lishan Sect to produce nothing but boasters of exaggerated words."
"Be careful, these two are very tricky, and there are others waiting in secret all around." Mother Zeng was panting like an ox, covered in sweat, clearly showing that their recent exchange had cost her an untold amount of mental energy.
"I know." As her voice fell, the old man with the smoking pipe on the side could no longer hold on.
Bang, bang, two muffled sounds.
The old man with the smoking pipe took two kicks to the chest in succession, his face turned pale white, and unable to catch his breath, he collapsed heavily to the ground.
"Old man!" Mother Zeng rushed over, helped him up, and checked his injuries.
Zhou Rong stood indifferently in place, not pursuing.
Given his strength and status, making such a move was already somewhat bullying the younger generation.
"One last word, step aside."
"The Tianyin Sect has become history. To persist any further is just a meaningless death with no purpose."
Mother Zeng and her companion remained unmoved, guarding the entrance of the building, supporting each other as they stood up and glared fiercely at him.
"If you have the ability, break through yourself!"
"Fine!" Zhou Rong finally grew impatient, his figure surging forward as he quickly strode toward the main entrance of Tianya Pavilion.
The expressions of Yan Haochun and You Ce changed, both worried that Zhou Rong would hog the prize for himself.
Yan Haochun followed closely behind, rushing toward the entrance of Tianya Pavilion.
As for the white-haired Bone Refining martial artist beside You Ce, without needing to be told, he had already rushed toward Tianya Pavilion.
This time, Mother Zeng and her companion did not try to stop them. One was overly exhausted with weak limbs, while the other was heavily wounded and unable to move.
Only Wan Qingqing stepped forward alone, blocking the doorway.
"Three of you, stay your steps." Wan Qingqing gritted her teeth and spread her arms wide. "This place is the vital ground of my Tianyin Sect!"
"Little girl, have you thought this through? Standing in our way means you alone are resisting the combined might of our three families." Zhou Rong said indifferently. "Do you know that if I want to, I can take you down at any moment and make your life worse than death!"
"What kind of meaning is there in bullying a junior!?" In the crowd nearby, a figure leaped up and landed lightly on the side.
This person was dressed in white clothes and white trousers, with long hair draped over the shoulders and a pair of golden silk gloves on both hands. It turned out to be the head of the True Forest Courtyard, Xiao Qingyu.
"Zhou Rong, didn't you say before that my Tianyin Sect wouldn't dare to show their faces?" Xiao Qingyu said coldly.
"Just you alone?" Zhou Rong shook his head slightly. "Not enough."
"What if you add me?" Another person slowly walked out from the crowd. It turned out to be Vice Sect Master Xie Yan.
She held a small white shield, her face stern as she scanned the audience. Her strength in the Organ Refining realm left no one daring to face her head-on.
A smile suddenly appeared on Zhou Rong's face.
"Still not enough!"
This sentence was not spoken by him, but came from the other side, out of the shadows of Tianya Pavilion.
In the shadows, a man wearing a tall crown and wide robes with flowing large sleeves walked out step by step.
This man's eyebrows were similar to Yan Haochun's, slanting upward into his temples, but his gaze was completely different.
If Yan Haochun's temperament was that of a wealthy young master, then his temperament was that of an eagle eyeing its prey.
Even though this man had hair and a beard as white as snow, he still gave off the powerful vigor of someone just reaching his prime.
"Yan Junshan...!" Xie Yan's expression changed.
One of the top three masters of the Lishan Sect, arguably the pinnacle expert beneath the sect master, ranked third.
Among Organ Refining martial artists, this man's strength was among the very best. He had killed a handful of peers at the same level, making him somewhat abnormal.
It wasn't just her whose expression changed; Wan Qingqing and Xiao Qingyu both knew in their hearts that they had fallen into a trap.
This time, Tianya Pavilion was very likely an encirclement and ambush targeting the Tianyin Sect.
Now, Yan Junshan alone could counter at least two Organ Refining experts, while the rest—Zhou Rong at Organ Refining, along with Yan Haochun and the You family—made two more Bone Refining experts.
Their strength had completely surpassed that of the Tianyin Sect side.
On the Tianyin Sect's side, Xie Yan was in Organ Refining; Wan Qingqing, Xiao Qingyu, and Mother Zeng were all merely in Bone Refining; and the old man with the smoking pipe was injured and unable to fight, rendering him equivalent to nothing.
Xie Yan's expression flashed repeatedly, and she immediately turned and sprinted away, choosing to flee without a fight.
However, she had not run far before she was intercepted and blocked back by two experts joining forces.
"Since Sect Master Xie has come, why not stay and rest awhile?"
Both of them wore Chijing Army standard-issue leather armor. One held a hook, and the other held a short spear, cooperating with tacit understanding. Although they were only in Bone Refining, their combination forced Xie Yan to retreat repeatedly.
"The two garrison commanders of the Water Punishment Division...!" Xie Yan's expression changed slightly as she recognized the two men.
Among the three elite divisions of the Chijing Army, the garrison commander of the Metal Punishment Division was Zhou Xingtong, while the garrison commanders of the Water Punishment Division were these two brothers, Zhao Zhong and Zhao Cheng.
Their martial arts were linked in mind, merging into one. Their strikes were like those of a single person, possessing formidable power comparable to Organ Refining.
At this moment, the encircling force possessed two major Organ Refining experts and four Bone Refining experts.
They surrounded one Organ Refining and three Bone Refining members of the Tianyin Sect. Six against four meant zero chance of winning.
In this tiny place of Tianya Pavilion, nearly half of the apex martial arts experts of Xuanjing City had gathered.
Figures surrounded Xiao Qingyu, Wan Qingqing, and the others from all directions—those were the Chijing Army annihilation battle array and firearm array that had been set up in ambush long ago.
"Today is the day the remnant scum of the Tianyin Sect are completely wiped off the roster!" Yan Junshan said coldly.
"Such big words."
Suddenly, another person walked out from the other side of Tianya Pavilion.
"Yan Junshan, if you want to make a move against my Tianyin Sect, I'm afraid you'll have to consider Senior Zhou Rong's opinion first." That person sighed leisurely.
"What do you mean?" Yan Junshan shifted his gaze to look at Zhou Rong not far away.
Just as Zhou Rong was also bewildered, a miserable man's cry suddenly burst forth from inside Tianya Pavilion.
"Dad!! Dad, save me!!"
"Brother, vicious people have broken into the house, grabbing people wherever they see them! You must save little sister!!"
"My child, do not hesitate. Don't mind our lives, kill!"
"Rong brother... we shall surely meet again in the next life!"
A flurry of weeping, shouting, and roaring gradually grew from soft to loud, drifting into Zhou Rong's ears.
He was struck as if by lightning. He had already recognized those as the voices of his own elderly parents, wife, and younger sister.
"How... how could this be!!?" He stood frozen in place, utterly failing to react for a moment.
He hailed from the Zhou family, one of the three major families! This person actually dared to... actually dared to capture the people in his household!!
Simply lawless and audacious!
Zhou Rong trembled all over, his blood surging upward. His internal force began to twist and turn into chaos due to his overly agitated emotions, stirring up dust on the surrounding ground to flutter and fly about.
"You!! You!!?" He pointed at the person in the shadows, his face turning dark red, almost unable to speak.
"Senior Zhou, for the sake of your wife, children, and parents, please make sure to hold back Senior Yan Junshan." That person slowly walked out from the shadows, his stalwart body and robust muscles unable to conceal the chilling intent radiating from him.
It was none other than Wei He, who had just hurried over from the Wanqing Sect.
At this moment, the surrounding spectators were also in an uproar.
With this sudden twist of events, nobody expected that the Tianyin Sect, which had already been completely surrounded, still had this card up its sleeve.
"Not only that, Young Master You, please calm yourself as well." Wei He smiled as he looked at You Ce on the side.
"What, I don't believe you'd dare to run wild in my You family's estate!" You Ce said coldly, completely unafraid.
"The You family's defenses are strict, so naturally I could not," Wei He smiled. "It's just that what I want to talk about is not your family, but Young Master You yourself."
"What do you mean!?" You Ce involuntarily took a step back, merely being looked at by Wei He's gaze gave him a bone-chilling sensation.
"Hasn't the young master noticed that something is wrong with your body?" Wei He smiled.
"...!!?" You Ce's expression changed. He rapidly mobilized his vital energy to inspect his whole body, but the moment he moved his vital energy, a wave of acute pain surged up from his chest and abdomen.
He couldn't help but open his mouth and spit out a mist of blood forward.
"You!!?" The white-haired Bone Refining elder of the You family also changed his color at this moment, quickly returning to You Ce's side to rapidly tap several blood meridian nodes on his body.
"Good methods...!" The elder looked fiercely at Wei He. "Truly worthy of the name Poison Demon."
"Senior is too polite. Now I have already taken over the Wanqing Sect, thanks to my master's great kindness in passing on the position of sect master."
Wei He smiled.
"So from now on, please address me by the name of the Wanqing Sect Master. Titles like Poison Demon are far too low-grade. I really don't like them."
"If I don't like something, I will become unhappy. And once I'm unhappy... people will die."
As soon as these words fell, even the surrounding onlookers felt a chill permeate their bones, growing terrified in their hearts and hastily backing off far away for fear of being implicated and poisoned.
This kind of method where one is poisoned without making a sound was simply hair-raising.
"Wan Qing Sect! I think it should be renamed the Wan Poison Sect in the future!" Yan Junshan said coldly. His gaze remained tightly locked onto Wei He, the murderous intent growing increasingly intense.
"Senior Yan, words can be spoken carelessly, but things cannot be said carelessly." Wei He's gaze turned cold.
"People will die."
