In Feiye City, anyone who could break through three rounds of blood refinement was a famous master.
Most of these masters were in their thirties or forties. Very few were young.
They were already top leaders in the major gangs and sects. Breaking through to this level was never easy.
When Jiang Su and Xiao Ran failed, nobody laughed at them. People thought it was normal.
Especially Xiao Ran. He was only seventeen years old. He still had three full years ahead of him. With his talent, he had a high chance of breaking through the bottleneck and entering the next level.
Jiang Su was different. She had damaged her blood foundation. Resting and fixing it would take at least half a year. Reaching perfection again would take another half year.
That was over a year lost. She was getting closer to twenty years old. Her potential was fading.
Lately, Wei He often saw Jiang Su in the yard.
Her face grew cold and hard. She trained like a madwoman to toughen her skin, shoving handfuls of costly medicine and herbs into her mouth every day.
She was playing with her life.
After Xiao Ran failed, he also came to the yard sometimes. He acted much calmer. The failure made him grow up, and he was no longer as arrogant as before.
Still, the third blood refinement was extremely hard to break through.
Inside the city, on the second floor of Baihua House.
Xiao Ran and Jiang Su sat across from each other. Jiang Yan was not there, and Cheng Shaojiu was not there either. Only the two of them were present.
A maid poured wine for them and silently walked away.
Xiao Ran looked at the quiet second floor. He remembered his failed breakthrough, and a heavy bitterness filled his heart.
He picked up a cup and drank it in one gulp.
"The third blood refinement is much harder than I thought. At the final moment, I felt a huge gap. My blood is at perfection, but the wall is still too far away."
Jiang Su sat opposite him. Her face was pale. Failing to break through had hurt her foundation badly.
Compared to Xiao Ran's talent, she was far behind. One single failure had already damaged her body.
She felt even more bitter than Xiao Ran.
"Junior Brother Xiao, do not dwell on it. You are young and still have many chances. But I only have this one..." She paused, then continued.
"Those who reached three blood refinements are all famous figures who have ruled the city for years. They hold power in big gangs and keep everyone in line. If it were easy for us, the third level would be worthless."
She was trying to comfort herself.
"That is true. But I still feel unwilling. I keep thinking I might make it," Xiao Ran nodded.
"Besides, think about it," Jiang Su said with pride in her voice. "If even you and I failed, who else in the yard could move forward? If Senior Brother Cheng were still here, there might be hope. But the rest? That Senior Brother who wasted years? The lazy Jiang Yan? Or Wei He, who can barely break through Stone Skin?"
"I heard the teacher took a newcomer into the inner yard recently," Xiao Ran sighed softly. "His name is Zhang Lu. It took him only six days to break through Stone Skin..."
Jiang Su opened her mouth to say something comforting, but no words came out.
"..." She raised her cup and drank it clean.
Life was like this. If you stopped moving, others would not wait for you.
Stopping meant falling behind. Even moving too slow meant falling behind.
A sense of danger grew in both of their hearts. Failing to break through meant standing still, and standing still meant wasting potential. As time passed and they got closer to twenty, their talent would lose its value.
Meanwhile, newcomers were chasing them from behind, step by step.
Xiao Ran knew this best. Everything he had—his status, his treatment, his resources—came from people valuing his potential. If his potential was no longer special and others surpassed him, everything he owned would be stripped away.
He did not want that.
Cup after cup went down.
As Xiao Ran drank, his frustration grew. Suddenly, his hand stopped.
He looked at Jiang Su across the table. Her face was flushed, her skin was fair, and her body curves were clear under her clothes.
His throat felt dry and hot. A dark desire stirred inside his heart.
Both of them were bitter and stressed. Jiang Su, seeing her hope fade after her injury, drank endlessly. In her fuzzy mind, she had no idea how much she had swallowed.
She only knew Xiao Ran kept ordering more wine.
What she did not notice was that Xiao Ran only drank a lot at the start. Later on, he only poured a tiny drop into his own cup.
As they kept drinking, nine out of ten cups went into Jiang Su's stomach.
Jiang Su felt her head spin, and her eyes grew blurry.
"Senior Sister, you are drunk. Let me take you back," Xiao Ran stood up and said.
"I... I am not drunk! I can still drink!" Jiang Su shook her head. The wine made the heavy knot in her chest feel a bit lighter.
Xiao Ran's eyes flickered.
"Alright, one last cup together, then we go. You are drunk, and walking back alone might be dangerous."
"Okay... the last... last cup." Though Jiang Su was drunk, her martial artist body kept her from passing out completely.
For the last cup, Xiao Ran filled both glasses to the top.
As he poured Jiang Su's drink, a pinch of faint green powder dropped from his sleeve without a sound.
It was an aphrodisiac powder he had made in the past for fun. Once a woman took it, her mind would go cloudy, her body would lose all strength, and a fire would burn inside her that she could not control.
The powder slipped into the wine, and Xiao Ran gave the cup a gentle shake. It dissolved instantly.
"Cheers!"
"Drink!"
Their cups clinked, and they downed the wine.
After this last drink, Jiang Su stood up, muttered a few words, and prepared to head downstairs with Xiao Ran.
The drug was too strong, and the warm wine made it spread faster. After taking just two steps, her body began to burn violently.
She realized something was wrong.
She drank often and knew what being drunk felt like. Even if she drank too much today, it should not feel like this.
The heat was wrong. It turned from warmth into a scalding fire across her entire skin.
Jiang Su sensed danger. She turned her head to look at Xiao Ran.
Xiao Ran looked calm, but the corner of his mouth was curled into a slight smile.
He reached out to hold her.
"Senior Sister, you are drunk. Let me hold you."
"Xiao Ran... you...!" A dark feeling hit Jiang Su. Her body grew hotter and hotter, her legs rubbed together, and a strange urge surged within her heart.
She knew she was in trouble. Turning around, she rushed down the stairs as fast as she could.
Even though she was drunk, a martial artist had strong endurance. As a second blood refinement fighter, she forced her blood to surge, suppressing the drug and the wine for a short moment as she ran outside.
"Senior Sister! Wait for me!" Xiao Ran saw things going wrong and chased right after her.
If he succeeded tonight, he could force her to stay silent later. But if he failed, it would bring him big trouble.
Jiang Su ran out of the tavern. Passersby dodged her in fear as she hurried toward the outer city.
She knew Xiao Ran well. Once he decided to strike, he would go all the way without mercy.
She liked Xiao Ran, but she never wanted to give herself to him like this. If she lost her purity here, her reputation for the rest of her life would be destroyed.
Her only choice was to run back to the Huishan Martial Yard and ask their teacher for help.
Xiao Ran was strong, the strongest in the inner yard. Night had fallen, and ordinary disciples could not stop him. Only Master Zheng could.
Jiang Su ran with all her strength, but she overestimated her endurance. The moment she left the inner city and entered Shiqiao Town, her steps began to stumble, and her speed dropped fast.
Before reaching the yard, the heat boiled inside her, and her entire body was burning. She was about to collapse.
Xiao Ran caught up from behind. He grabbed her arm, keeping a polite distance without acting out of line.
"Senior Sister, what is wrong with you? What happened?" Xiao Ran asked with deep care, looking completely confused.
Jiang Su looked at him, and doubt crept into her mind.
'Was it not him who drugged me?' Her mind shook.
'Did I blame him by mistake?' "Are you alright, Senior Sister?" Xiao Ran held her arm with a noble look, his concern appearing completely real.
"Senior Brother Xiao, Senior Sister Jiang, why are you two still here at this hour?" A few martial brothers from the yard happened to pass by and called out to them.
It was late, and this street was close to the Huishan Martial Yard. Some disciples had just finished training and were heading home.
"We had some wine. I am taking Senior Sister Jiang back," Xiao Ran replied casually.
The others suspected nothing. Jiang Su's small circle was always distant from others, and people often saw them together.
Seeing that Jiang Su did not argue, the disciples waved and walked away.
Jiang Su did not argue because her mind was fading fast. Heat consumed her body, and she wanted to tear her clothes off.
Her vision grew blurry, and her thoughts turned to chaos.
Xiao Ran held her and walked toward the Jiang family home. On the way, they met several other disciples finishing their work, and he greeted them naturally each time.
He had already made up his mind. Once he cleared this street, he would find a quiet spot and take her.
He would enjoy himself completely and vent all the frustration from months of training without women.
Losing purity would hurt blood refinement progress later.
However, Jiang Su's foundation was already broken. Her future potential was tiny, and her chances of breaking through were almost zero anyway. A little more damage did not matter.
It was better to let him use her. Maybe after venting his desires, his mind would clear up, and he could break through to the next level.
When that time came, he could just give her some compensation.
A dark fire burned inside Xiao Ran. Smelling the faint scent of a woman on Jiang Su, his heart grew even more restless.
To focus on breaking through, he had not touched a woman for six months. Now that he let his desires loose, he could not control himself.
After greeting two more disciples while holding Jiang Su, Xiao Ran reached a street by the river. Jiang Su had completely lost consciousness, only groaning and stumbling along as he pulled her.
Xiao Ran looked at the dry grass by the riverbank. He was about to drag her inside.
As he pushed the tall grass aside, he felt something and turned his head.
In front of a small yard nearby, a person stood straight and quiet.
The person's eyes were sharp, reflecting the moonlight from the sky. He stared straight at them, completely still.
It was Wei He.
"Junior Brother Wei..." Xiao Ran let out a fake laugh, slowly pulling his hand back from the grass. Excuses ran wildly through his mind.
Wei He looked at him and Jiang Su.
He had actually run into the two of them on his way back and noticed something was wrong with Jiang Su.
He did not like either of them, so he had no plan to stick his nose into their business.
The problem was, Xiao Ran did not go far or near. He chose to do his business right here.
"Senior Brother Xiao..." Wei He said, word by word, with a calm face. "What are you planning to do in the tall grass right in front of my door?"
Xiao Ran: "..."
Wei He: "..."
The two stood in silence, looking at each other.
