Cheng Shaojiu squeezed his fists tight. He had no words to say.
He stayed silent for a long time, then turned around, left the escort agency, and walked back to the Huishan Boxing Academy.
He actually knew everything. He knew about his uncle's situation. On the surface, he acted like he did not care, but he understood the whole picture.
The agency's business was affected, but it was nowhere near failing. His uncle just made it sound terrible so Cheng Shaojiu would stop bringing people into the agency.
His family never met his loyal brothers and sisters for the exact same reason.
Everyone thought he only made bad friends. Everyone thought he was wasting his life away. But who truly understood what he was thinking?
He, Cheng Shaojiu, treated people with pure honesty. He believed others would do the same for him.
Only true hearts could win true hearts. Sure, his friends looked like nothing special right now. But if a real crisis hit, the ones who would step up to back him were far more reliable than the escorts under his uncle.
Yet his uncle did not see it that way.
Walking down the familiar road, Cheng Shaojiu soon reached the boxing academy again. To keep anyone from noticing his trouble, he had to bury what just happened deep inside his heart.
Inside the yard, many martial brothers had already arrived. They were practicing on their own.
Cheng Shaojiu spotted his good friend Wei He right away. The two exchanged glances and were about to speak.
"Something bad happened!" A new junior brother suddenly kicked open the yard gate and rushed inside.
"Senior Brother Xiao Ran was badly hurt in yesterday's sparring match!"
" What?!"
The crowd in the yard froze first, then showed faces of doubt and disbelief.
Who was Xiao Ran? He was the rising star of the academy, Old Man Zheng's favorite treasure, and a big expert at the Stone Skin level.
Now someone suddenly ran in saying he was badly hurt?!
"It's true!" Another person stepped through the door shortly after and spoke in a deep voice. "Teacher gave an order. Third Senior Brother, Xiao Jue, Zhang De, Zheng Niu, and Xu Anshan, you five follow me. Everyone else stay and guard the yard!"
This guy was a foot soldier who always followed Zhao Hong closely. He often delivered messages for the teacher and the First Senior Brother.
Hearing him speak like this, the crowd started to believe it.
After all, no matter how bold this guy was, he would not dare to fake the teacher's words.
The five named brothers immediately stepped forward. Led by Cheng Shaojiu, they hurried out of the yard with the runner, heading toward the street district with the most doctors and herbalists.
Wei He did not even get the chance to tell Third Senior Brother about his breakthrough.
He watched almost half the crowd leave in a rush.
He let out a quiet breath.
"How did Brother Xiao get hurt anyway? Old Man Zheng was with him, so how could he get hurt?" Someone asked in a low whisper.
"Old Man Zheng is strong, but no one has seen him fight. How do you know he is stronger than Brother Xiao? Besides, he is old, and his blood energy must be fading," another person argued softly.
"Didn't they say he went out to spar a few days ago? Could it be?!"
"Who knows. Sparring... Heh, I heard older senior brothers say before that no sparring match ever beats someone into a heavy injury."
"Stop guessing wildly," Jiang Yan snapped in a cold voice. "Focus and practice your own work."
Wei He also pulled his attention back. His heart felt no waves at all as he went back to practicing slowly.
The yard slowly returned to calm, but while everyone seemed quiet, a heavy cloud of curiosity pressed down on their minds.
Xiao Ran was never popular in the academy. So hearing that he was hurt, and heavily hurt at that, some people present even felt a quiet joy at his bad luck.
Not long after, Ouyang Zhuang arrived too. His eyes flickered nervously, but he pretended to be calm as he walked over to Wei He.
"Brother Wei, I just went to Taihe Hall. Brother Xiao is getting treated over there. Do you want to know how he got hurt?"
He whispered with a secretive face.
"..." Wei He stayed silent and kept doing his own thing. He lit the fire pit and started heating up the sand and gravel used for skin grinding to clean them.
Ouyang Zhuang blinked. After waiting a moment and seeing no reaction from Wei He, he grew anxious.
Compared to Cheng Shaojiu and Old Man Zheng, he had far less patience. He could not hold back for long and dumped out all his so-called inside news at once.
"Brother He, you are way too calm! Let me tell you, I asked my family's guards. In yesterday's match, Xiao Ran was spotted right on the spot by both the Zhao family and the Tian family from the Seven-Family Alliance. Both sides invited him to join them and take up a position."
"And then... that guy Xiao Ran..."
"Speak properly," Wei He glared at him.
This kind of rude, disrespectful addressing could hurt Ouyang Zhuang's future if others heard it. Wei He would be fine, but Ouyang Zhuang would get a name for disrespecting his seniors.
"Oh... fine, fine, Senior Brother Xiao, happy now?" Ouyang Zhuang curled his lips, not caring at all.
He was young and did not give a damn about these rules.
"I am telling you, Xiao Ran rejected the Tian family and chose to join the Zhao family. The result? News came out today that he was ambushed and heavily hurt by a masked man. Tsk tsk... The Zhao family and the Tian family have always been bitter enemies. If not for the agreement inside the Seven-Family Alliance, they would not be in an alliance at all.
Brother Xiao really walked into a trap meant for others. So miserable... way too miserable."
"If you ask me, if he chose the Tian family, the Zhao family would have attacked him just the same. Nobody wants to see a young, talented fighter with huge potential join their rival.
Actually, the Zhao family would have kept guard to prevent him from being ambushed, but the Tian family moved too fast. You can't guard against a thief every single day, right..."
He kept talking with joy over Xiao Ran's downfall. It was probably because Xiao Ran usually acted like he was the ruler of the world, ignoring everyone and making Ouyang Zhuang very annoyed.
However, Wei He could no longer process the rest of the words.
His mind was completely filled with the news Ouyang Zhuang just dropped.
Xiao Ran, because he was too talented, received invitations from two big families during a match. As a result... he accepted one, got ambushed by the other, and was now heavily injured.
A genius... turned out to carry great risk too. He used to think the risk would be small, but looking at it now... the risk was huge.
A tall tree that grew outside the safety of the forest had to face the wind and rain from all directions alone.
'Is this the price for standing out from the crowd?' Wei He thought with a heavy heart.
He originally planned to show his progress quickly, thinking he might earn the title of a small genius.
But now...
'I should hide it for a while longer. I cannot rush...' Wei He felt something was very wrong.
Being a genius was not an easy job.
Still, he understood that it was hard to hide his condition from his teacher, Old Man Zheng. That was fine too. Only if Old Man Zheng knew his progress could he get more resources pushed his way.
His safety would be guarded as well, and he could smoothly get the next training methods without being held back.
But as for everyone else, he would keep them in the dark.
Wei He made up his mind. It was better to delay a bit longer before showing his breakthrough. That way, he would not bear the weight of being a genius and could deal with things more easily.
If Old Man Zheng saw through it on his own, Wei He would not need to say it first. But if he did not notice, Wei He would find a chance to show it to him alone.
Ouyang Zhuang was still babbling beside him with joy over Xiao Ran's misfortune, saying a lot of things.
Wei He gave lazy replies now and then while continuing his practice.
Gradually, the whole day passed. Old Man Zheng and the others never showed up.
First Senior Brother Zhao Hong, Cheng Shaojiu, and several core disciples did not return either. They were clearly all guarding Taihe Hall.
It was rare for the disciples in the yard to get such hot gossip, so none of them were in the mood to practice properly.
Wei He, on the other hand, urged Ouyang Zhuang and made sure both of them finished their daily lessons seriously.
"Brother Wei, you are really too calm..." Ouyang Zhuang said weakly.
"..." Wei He said nothing. He slowly dropped his sweat towel into hot water, washed it, wrung it dry, and put it over his face.
The sky grew dark. It was almost time to pack up and leave.
Only then did a group of people push through the yard gate.
First Senior Brother Zhao Hong walked right at the front, followed by Cheng Shaojiu. Behind them were two disciples carrying a stretcher.
Lying on the stretcher was Xiao Ran, his face pale as paper.
Xiao Ran had no blood color on his face or lips. He was unconscious, clearly hurt extremely badly.
Old Man Zheng walked at the very back with a walking stick in hand, his face dark and ugly.
His usual lazy, slow steps now carried a heavy weight and pressure.
Different from what Wei He expected, Old Man Zheng had no mind to check on the rest of the people in the yard. With a cold face, he followed the group directly into the inner room.
Moments later, loud scolding came from inside the room. A disciple ran out quickly with a prescription, likely going out to buy medicine.
Wei He looked calm. He packed his things, pinched the gloating Ouyang Zhuang beside him, and led the foolish kid out of the yard gate.
"Ouch! Brother Wei, why did you pinch me?!"
"What did you call me?" Wei He stopped his steps.
"Brother Wei?" Right after Ouyang Zhuang said that, his ear was pinched hard again.
He instantly screamed in pain.
"Senior Brother Wei! Senior Brother! Senior Brother, my good Senior Brother, I was wrong!"
He quickly begged for mercy.
Only then did Wei He pull back his hand. "Let's go."
"Oh..." Ouyang Zhuang rubbed his ear, looking confused and helpless.
The two packed their things and left the yard.
Wei He originally planned to bring up his breakthrough to Old Man Zheng on his own, but he put that thought down for now.
Seeing Xiao Ran's end showed him how dangerous it was to be a genius. So Wei He decided to act like someone who was not that talented.
He had to find another time to tell Old Man Zheng.
After Xiao Ran was heavily hurt, Old Man Zheng used all his connections to search for the attacker everywhere, but he found nothing at all.
Instead, he wasted a lot of time, energy, and money.
This went on for half a month.
Old Man Zheng left early and returned late every day. After still getting no results, he came back to the yard with an ugly face.
Unlike before, he used to love taking a slow sip of tea and chewing on chicken feet.
Now he had no mood for chicken feet at all. His eyes looked like they were staring at the people in the yard, but his gaze was empty, not focused on the yard at all.
Everyone could see that Xiao Ran's injury hit him very hard.
Xiao Ran rested for half a month and could barely walk on the ground now. Occasionally, he appeared in the yard. He could not practice, so he just stood there with a dark face, staring at others practicing with unpleasant eyes.
Another half month passed. Wei He continued to save up his Breakthrough Beads while waiting for the right moment.
Luckily, he had spent a lot of time with Cheng Shaojiu and knew a simple practice method for the early Stone Skin stage, so he did not need to rush at first. No time was wasted.
The main problem was that he did not know the full practice method for the Stone Skin stage, and it was hard to ask around. He still had to go to Old Man Zheng for that.
