"The Cult..." Qian Xiao's face twitched.
That word carried a massive, terrifying weight. No one knew when they first appeared, but one thing was certain: wherever the Cult went, chaos and death followed. Even the Great Yuan Empire at its peak couldn't wipe them out. Now, a mere gang of bandits like the Meteor Bandits had tied themselves to such monsters.
Qian Xiao stood frozen, unable to speak.
"Think about your next move carefully," You Fu said, knowing how grim the situation was. He turned his gaze to Wei He. "Sect Master Wei, is there something else? Speak freely."
Wei He said gravely, "Can we speak in private?"
"Of course." You Fu nodded at Qian Xiao.
Qian Xiao cupped his fists respectfully and walked out to ponder his options.
Once they were alone, Wei He spoke in a low voice. "Alliance Leader, do you know the details of the Black House Mountains? My Ten Thousand Poison Sect suffered heavy losses and plans to settle there to recover. But we know little about the area. If there are other dangerous places like the Three-Headed Ferret's territory, could you warn me?"
You Fu had a rough face, but he was patient. "Sure. There are three major death traps in those mountains. The Three-Headed Ferret is just one."
He narrowed his eyes, pulling up old memories. "The Three-Headed Ferret of the Gathering Water Canyon, the Spawn Worms of the Cloud Burst Swamp, and the Grass Pythons of the Hollow Vine Forest. Watch out for these three."
He went on to explain their locations and danger levels. Though You Fu clearly held back some details, the main picture was clear.
Wei He was deeply grateful. In times like these, information was life. Exploring blindly would have cost him too much. He owed You Fu a real favor now.
"One more thing," Wei He added. "What exactly is the Sense Engraving Realm? When I fought Zhu Chen and that Three-Headed Ferret, things felt... wrong."
He paused, then promised, "I know I am pushing my luck, but if you guide me, my sect will owe you two favors. If you ever need us, as long as it doesn't break our moral code or destroy our sect, we will do whatever you ask."
"Knowing about Sense Engraving too early isn't good for you. It might slow your progress," You Fu said, shaking his head.
"I've already found clues. Knowing only half the truth troubles my mind," Wei He pressed. "The stone tablet mentioned Fixing the Sense... how does that relate to the beast? Please, clarify."
Wei He's raw power aside, his movement speed was so fast that even a Sense Engraving expert like Zhu Chen couldn't catch him. Add his lethal poison mastery, and his promise held huge value. You Fu didn't fear poison, but he had disciples and family who might need a master healer one day. Making friends with a rare talent was always good business.
That was why You Fu treated him so well.
"Fine. I will make it simple," You Fu sighed. "Forging Bones builds your foundation. Refining Organs strengthens your mind. All of it exists so that when you reach Sense Engraving, you won't be twisted by the raw reality of the world."
"Twisted by reality?" Wei He's heart skipped a beat. He sensed deep danger. Back in his past life, he had read plenty of cosmic horror stories. Playing them in a game was fun; living in one was a nightmare.
"Yes," You Fu nodded. "At Sense Engraving, your five senses grow exponentially. You must pick one sense and fix it. You push that single sense beyond normal limits until it pierces standard reality and touches a deeper truth. That process is Sense Engraving."
"Engraving a sense..."
You Fu let out a breath. "This world is far larger and darker than we think. We experience it through our five senses, but normal senses are too weak. Ancients sought power by force-expanding their perception."
"At first, it worked. Their heightened senses unlocked hidden rules of nature, granting them godlike power. New sensory input forced their bodies to adapt, triggering massive changes."
You Fu asked, "When you feel freezing cold and have no extra clothes, what do you do?"
Wei He suppressed his inner alarm. "I move around to generate heat, and I eat more."
"Exactly. It's the same for Sense Engraving," You Fu said. "Your mind and body adapt to survive. But every so often, every master must fix their chosen sense again. If you fail, your perception warps. The mutated beast flesh you consumed in the past will awaken and mutate your body and mind."
"Succeed, and you get a few quiet years. Fail, and you turn into a monster."
He looked at Wei He. "With your talent, you might reach this level one day. When you do, remember this: hold onto who you are. Never forget yourself."
"I understand..." Wei He murmured. He paused, then asked, "The Three-Headed Ferret... was it once..."
"Yes. It was a Sense Engraving martial artist who ate the flesh of an immortal beast. Before his lifespan ran out, his sense fix failed. He mutated and survived like that until today."
You Fu's voice grew stern. "Sect Master Wei, the world holds terrifying secrets. Be careful of what you perceive. The beast flesh inside you is only a secondary trigger; the real driver of mutation is stimulus from the outside world. The stronger your base foundation, the easier you will handle external pressure, and the longer your cycle between sense fixes will be. Keep that in mind."
Wei He etched every word into his memory. He stepped back and bowed deeply. "Thank you for the guidance, Alliance Leader."
This was a major favor. Wei He handed over two special tokens from the Ten Thousand Poison Sect as proof of his promise. Without wasting a second, he left Yishuang County and headed for a nearby black market.
The news was critical: a stronger foundation meant a safer transition to Sense Engraving. He needed to hide away and focus purely on grinding his basics.
On the river barge.
After meeting with Qian Xiao, You Fu stood alone on the upper deck, watching the morning sun rise over the water.
"A-Yan. Tell the Lotus Branch to tone it down. Don't ruin our main plans," he ordered quietly.
Mo Shiyan stepped out from the shadows behind him. "Master, I sent word earlier, but the Lotus Branch split into factions a century ago. Those Meteor Bandits are just rogue descendants using speed techniques to act wildly. Words won't stop them."
"If they don't listen, stop covering for them," You Fu said, his face cold and dark.
"Understood."
"Has your junior sister returned?"
"She is still in Yun Province," Mo Shiyan smiled. "With Uncle protecting her, her safety is guaranteed. His poison and martial skills are unmatched there."
"Good. She mentioned Wei He before. Having met him today, I see he is indeed capable. Great potential and sharp instincts. Pity his path is already set." You Fu sighed lightly. "Still, he is ruthless to enemies yet loyal to his allies. A man of real character."
"I'll see if we can bring him to our side," Mo Shiyan said.
Thud! Thud! Thud!
Hooves pounded along the rough mountain highway. The steep trail hugged the cliffs; a single slip meant falling into the abyss.
Wei He rode hard. He had spent two days at the black market converting stolen jewels and antiques into over a dozen rare beast cores to stock his sect's vaults. Since the loot came from the Sun-Seeking Gang, he didn't care about getting low prices—he just wanted quick resources.
As he rode, his mind kept returning to You Fu's warning.
If he's telling the truth, the raw reality of this world is horrifying.
Just perceiving the world could trigger mutation. How dark was the environment out there?
If a strong foundation is the only protection, my Organ Refining stage must be rock solid. It's a shame I lack the core manual of Nine Canopy Strikes. I can't forge the absolute body frame, so I have to use other arts to bolster my Cloud Rain Force.
Still, he was content with his progress.
He felt the Break-Through Bead in his chest. It was already two-thirds full. One-third more, and he could force another breakthrough. Meanwhile, his improved talent had naturally pushed another technique to the Force stage.
His strength was growing, his path was clear, and he had avenged himself against Zhu Chen. All he wanted now was a safe place to hide his sect and sit out the growing turmoil of the world.
