The old man with the braided beard, a master of the Iron Blood Fist, ran over to Chen Jian. He checked his star student and sighed in relief when he saw no major damage.
What he didn't realize was that Wei He's interruption cost his disciple a rare, golden chance to break through. Losing that momentum meant his future attempts would be twice as hard.
Seeing that his student was fine, the old master didn't waste any words. He grabbed Chen Jian by the collar and pulled him away. The mood was ruined, and staying would only bring more trouble. He had rushed here to stop Chen Jian from acting foolishly. Now that he saw they were in the wrong, leaving fast was the smart choice.
Wei He watched them leave. Once Master Zheng calmed down and offered a small smile, Wei He felt at ease. He didn't waste time and walked straight to the center of the ring.
"I am Wei He. Thank you all for coming. There was a small mix-up earlier, but it is cleared up now."
"If anyone else wishes to challenge me, step up."
His voice echoed softly, but no one laughed. After what happened to Chen Jian, everyone knew Wei He was far from friendly. A single finger from him sent a grown man flying ten meters into a wall, leaving him breathless. Most inner disciples here knew they couldn't pull off a feat like that.
At first, the visiting masters looked down on the Mountain-Returning Fist because of Chen Jian's rampage. But Wei He's arrival changed everything. His thick blood energy pulsed through the air, pressing down on the crowd like a heavy weight.
Most people backed off, but a few held their ground.
Zhou Jihan calmed his racing heart and closed his eyes. He replayed Wei He's attack in his mind. That finger strike was as fast as lightning, perfectly accurate, and hit with crushing power. His speed and resolve were on a totally different level compared to the other disciples.
Just like Chen Jian, Zhou Jihan needed a spark to break through his own limit. He understood his martial art's manual, but the Flying Bear Blade put a heavy demand on one's blood energy. That was why he remained stuck at the edge of a breakthrough.
In the entire city, he believed he was the closest to reaching the Third Blood Stage. He came today specifically to fight a fresh Third Blood warrior, hoping to feel the exact moment of transformation. Older masters had already settled into their power, so their energy was quiet. Only a fresh breakthrough could show the exact gap and shift between the Second and Third Blood stages.
Without hesitation, Zhou Jihan took a step forward.
"Zhou Jihan of the Flying Bear Blade. I ask for a match, Senior Brother Wei."
"Hu Zhen'an of the Leopard Fist. I ask for a match as well."
"He Hong of the Through-the-Back Fist. Please guide me, Senior Brother."
At the same time, Zhou Jihan's two friends stepped forward, their voices loud and firm. They were not scared by Wei He's power. Instead, their eyes burned with excitement.
"Very well," Wei He said softly.
He pulled off his outer jacket, leaving only his tight undershirt. He crossed his arms and raised them to his chest.
When you face a stronger enemy, you rely on tricks. But when you are the stronger one, you crush them with pure power. You make them panic. You make them fear you. You leave a shadow in their minds that lasts forever. That was how you truly beat someone.
Wei He took a light breath.
The first attacker, Hu Zhen'an, lunged forward. His fingers formed a claw, and his body stayed low like a hunting leopard, aiming straight for Wei He's throat.
Whoosh...
Wei He's muscles expanded rapidly, dark veins popping across his skin. He thrust his hand forward.
His strike was faster. A heavy punch smashed directly into Hu Zhen'an's claw.
BANG!
With a dull thud, Hu Zhen'an flew backward like a broken kite. The raw force slammed into him, knocking him to the dirt. He tried to get back up, but his entire body was numb and useless.
The second challenger, He Hong, arrived right after. His arms whipped out long and fast, moving like a wild ape. His attacks flowed together smoothly with great power and speed, showing real combat skill. He was much stronger than Chen Jian.
Wei He blocked the incoming strikes with one hand, watching carefully. He waited until he saw every variation of the style before unleashing his inner force.
"Not bad," Wei He said.
He Hong's face paled. He raised his arms to repeat a heavy chop, but a sharp pain shot through his shoulders. The world spun around him. By the time he realized what happened, he was on the ground, his arms hanging limp and lifeless.
"Flying Bear Blade, you are next," Wei He said, turning his gaze toward Zhou Jihan.
These fighters were strong, noticeably better than Xiao Ran or Chen Jian. They were true geniuses of their schools. But they failed to understand one thing: the gap between the Second and Third Blood stages was not just about the amount of energy you held.
Zhou Jihan said nothing. He drew his blade with a sharp sound, sending a flash of steel toward Wei He.
That single swing showed the true meaning of the Flying Bear style. The steel flashed like a crashing waterfall, carrying the weight of an angry bear. It felt like he was swinging a dozen blades at once, hiding his true attack path.
Wei He approved of the move. It covered both defense and attack. In a normal fight, he might have taken his time to play around. But not today.
Without a word, Wei He's right arm swelled with blood. Five Nine-Cloud Flowers appeared on his skin, squirming like red insects.
"This is the Mountain-Returning Fist."
Wei He threw a straight punch. His blood energy surged into his right arm. His fist turned dark, his arm swelled, and the flower marks burned blood-red.
BOOM!!
Fist met blade. The bright sword light vanished instantly as the steel snapped in two.
Zhou Jihan's hands tore open from the force. He flew backward and hit the dirt hard. He tried to speak, but a mouthful of blood blocked his throat, trapped by internal damage.
"You have good skill," Wei He said calmly. "But until you find a better weapon, do not fight a Third Blood master."
He turned to the rest of the crowd. "Anyone else?"
The arena was dead silent. No one said a word. Every disciple pulled back, terrified that Wei He might call them out. In just a few moments, Wei He had crushed the Iron Blood Fist and wiped out a team of top talents. This was the true power of the Third Blood Stage.
Comparing his strength to the defeat of the other inner disciples earlier, everyone finally understood the massive gap between stages.
To Wei He, this whole gathering felt meaningless. It was like an adult picking on children. Since stepping into a new realm, he was no longer on the same level as these kids.
"Alright. Since no one else wishes to fight, we will end it here," Jiang Su said, stepping forward to ease the tension.
The Mountain-Returning Fist disciples finally let out a long breath. They had just watched their inner disciples get beaten one by one, only for Wei He to step in and crush the arrogant invaders. It was clear as day how far ahead Wei He had gotten. If Master Zheng and the other guest masters were not present, the disciples would have cheered out loud.
Zhao Hong, Zhang Lu, and Jiang Yan stood in silence. Earlier, watching Wei He spar with Master Zheng gave them a vague idea of his power. But now, seeing the men who easily beat them get crushed by Wei He in seconds, the gap became painful to face.
The once quiet and unremarkable Wei He was still a man of few words. But now, just standing there, he radiated a terrifying pressure.
With no more challenges, Wei He pulled back his blood energy and sat beside Master Zheng. Once he left the floor, the heavy pressure lifted. Disciples from other schools began stepping up to spar with the Mountain-Returning Fist crew again.
Zhao Hong loosened up and joined a few matches. As the fights continued, the brutal truth became clear: the overall strength of the Mountain-Returning Fist was far behind Wei He. Zhao Hong and the others were barely stronger than the lowest-tier schools present. Calling themselves average was generous.
After Zhou Jihan's group lost, the mood settled back into a normal, polite event.
Wei He stepped forward once more, raising a cup of tea to toast the guests. He offered a few words of thanks before stepping down. Having just injured several young talents, his presence only made the atmosphere stiff.
He decided to leave early, taking his older sister, Wei Ying, with him. He left the rest of the event to his senior brothers.
The two walked side by side along the river road heading home.
Wei Ying was still in shock. She couldn't wrap her mind around the way her younger brother had dominated those powerful fighters. She spent most of her time at home and knew little about his martial arts progress. Besides, Wei He had advanced far too fast.
Other than noticing that the fellow disciples treated her much nicer at the market, she hadn't felt any real difference until today. But seeing that massive, powerful figure drop top fighters like it was nothing brought the reality crashing down. Her little brother had truly transformed into a powerful figure.
"Little He... you..." Wei Ying started, looking at his back as he walked ahead.
"What is it?" Wei He asked.
"...You are getting so strong..." Wei Ying said with a mix of awe and sadness. "If Mom, Dad, and Big Sister were here to see this, they would be lost for words."
