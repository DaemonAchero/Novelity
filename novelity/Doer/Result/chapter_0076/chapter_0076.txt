The outer circle was full of people from other martial arts schools. None of the senior masters had shown up yet, but these visitors represented their respective schools. They were giving the Return Mountain Fist school face.
If the main event arrived and Wei He—the star of the show—was still missing, it would be a major insult. A severe one.
Zheng Fugui looked around. People were already leaning in and whispering to each other.
Feiye City had not seen anyone reach the Third Blood Refinement in years. Everyone came to see how Return Mountain Fist managed to break through the wall that blocked everyone else. They wanted to see this new genius.
But right now...
"People were talking so big before. Who knows if this Wei He guy actually broke through? Maybe the school is just blowing hot air to make themselves look good."
"He still hasn't shown up. Is he scared?"
"Isn't the host supposed to be here early?"
"Did Return Mountain Fist bring us all out here just to stand around and look at an empty floor?"
Members from schools that were not close with Return Mountain Fist began to murmur among themselves. They did not dare say it directly to Zheng Fugui, a fighter who had unlocked Jin power, but the bad mouth behind his back was loud enough.
"Master Zheng, time is almost up," Jiang Su whispered, her voice tight with panic. She had eaten lunch with Wei He at noon. Where had he gone?
Moments later, Wei Ying arrived. She scanned the crowd, looking for her little brother, but he was nowhere in sight. Anxious, she was guided to sit right next to Zheng Fugui so he could keep her safe.
"Master Zheng, Wei must have gotten held up by something," Zhang Lu said, his face flushed red. He could hear the mockery around them and could hardly stomach it. "Since time is up, why don't we start with a few warm-up fights to set the mood?"
Zhang Lu had never fought a real life-and-death battle, but a man who trained every day had hot blood. Why train day in and day out if you were just going to back down when it counted?
"You mean..." Zheng Fugui was in a tough spot. Hearing the idea, he immediately nodded. "Alright. Zhao Hong, get ready..."
Before he could even finish his sentence, Zhang Lu bolted into the center of the field.
The seats for the inner disciples formed a large circle, leaving a wide open space in the middle for demonstrations.
Zhang Lu’s sudden move drew everyone’s eyes.
He cupped his fists, bowed, and shouted, "I am Zhang Lu, an inner disciple of Return Mountain Fist. Since so many friends are gathered here today, why don't we start with a quick sparring match? It will warm things up so we don't just sit here in the cold."
"Warm-up? Heh, I like that."
A man stepped out from the Bat Fist group, a school that was friendly with Return Mountain Fist. He bowed lightly to Zhang Lu.
"I am Cheng Xi, an inner disciple of Bat Fist. Senior Zhang, please go easy on me."
Disciples from different schools often called each other "Senior Brother" out of plain politeness, the same way people call strangers "brother" on the street.
"Senior Cheng, you are too kind." Zhang Lu wasted no time. He moved face-to-face with his opponent. They exchanged basic pre-fight ceremony and took their stances.
"Watch out!" Cheng Xi darted forward, his arms spreading and swaying like bat wings. His movements were wide and unpredictable. This was the signature style of Bat Fist—it made it hard to tell where the attack was coming from.
A Bat Fist expert moved like a shadow, making it almost impossible to see where they were hitting.
But Zhang Lu was no fool. Return Mountain Fist relied on counter-attacks. Ignoring the trickery, he drove a straightforward punch straight for the man's chest.
Cheng Xi blocked it.
The two traded blows at high speed. The fierce fight caught the attention of everyone watching.
By the Feiye River.
Wei He had a dark expression on his face as he stared at the person wearing a white mask across from him.
"You are from the Xiangqu Sect?!"
He never expected this mysterious white-masked person to belong to the Xiangqu Sect.
During his past interrogations, he found that the Xiangqu Sect was chaotic and suffered from high turnover. Pressed hard by the Seven Alliance in the inner city, he assumed they were not a big threat.
He had not expected them to hide such a powerful, mysterious master.
Then again, it made sense. The Xiangqu Sect was a large force that could openly fight the Seven Alliance. If they did not have a few strong fighters, they would have been wiped out long ago.
The only thing he could not figure out was why the Xiangqu Sect was the first to track him down.
"Now that you have reached the Third Blood Refinement, you should know your path is changing," the masked man said casually. "Once you have Jin, you can slowly refine your body. But that is still not enough. If you join our sect, I will teach you the secret Xiangqu Jin without reserve. It is more than twice as strong as your Return Mountain Jin. The stronger your Jin, the stronger your body becomes after refining. From now on, the gap between you and others will grow wider and wider. So wide you won't even be able to imagine it."
"I appreciate your good intentions," Wei He said calmly. "However, I have no plans to change my school right now. Besides, if I joined you based on just a few words, you wouldn't believe I was truly loyal anyway. So, I am sorry. Please go back."
"You still don't get it," the masked man said, shaking his head. "It doesn't matter. Once you come back with me and listen to the Sect Leader's words, you will change your mind and join us."
He lunged forward. His body drifted like a white leaf, landing right in front of Wei He in the blink of an eye.
Pfft!
He struck out with a palm. As his blood boiled, a small red mark showed up on his palm.
At the same time, a strange strain of Jin power spread through the air.
Wei He did not hesitate. He poured everything into his Return Mountain Fist. His fists turned jet black as he met the attack.
Just as he was about to block the hit, a sudden change happened.
The oncoming palm jerked left mid-air, dipped down, hooked right, and twisted again. In a flash, it traced a strange, zigzag path and lightly slammed straight into Wei He's chest.
"This is the Xiangqu Palm," the masked man laughed out loud. He pulled back and melted away, disappearing into the distance in seconds.
Wei He stood alone on the spot, his face dark as night.
He unbuttoned his shirt and looked down. The iron plate and layers of ox hide he wore under his clothes were crushed inward.
A clear palm print was pressed deep into the iron plate.
"What a strange palm technique..."
The man's blood power was only average for the Third Blood Refinement when it broke out. But the line of his strike was bizarre, and his Jin was totally different from normal styles.
In that brief split second of contact, Wei He almost gave in to the urge to use his Five Ridge Palm and Flying Dragon Skill to kill the guy on the spot.
In the end, he pretended he could not dodge and took the strike head-on.
There were two reasons for this.
First, the man did not put much strength into his blood, and the hit was quick to leave. It was clearly just a test and a show of force to warn him.
Second, Wei He had used the Five Ridge Palm to kill quite a few Xiangqu Sect members before. Up on Mount Shaoyang, he had used both Five Ridge Palm and Flying Dragon Skill.
If news leaked out and his identity was exposed, he would be in real trouble.
So, unless he was one hundred percent sure he could kill the man, he would not use his full power easily.
"Xiangqu Sect... Pity... Lucky you," Wei He muttered, his eyes cast down. He had almost used his other two techniques just now.
Once he activated them, his speed, power, and Jin would jump by more than double. Combined with dirty tricks, his chances of killing the masked man were high. But who knew if the other guy was holding back power too?
Suddenly, he realized something. The event at the Return Mountain Fist school was about to start!
He spun around and hurried toward the school grounds.
Inside the Return Mountain Fist martial arts yard.
Bang!
Zhang Lu clashed fists with his opponent once more, neither gaining an inch.
They had been fighting for a while now. He and Cheng Xi were evenly matched, neither able to get the upper hand.
While the two were having fun in the ring, the crowd watching was getting impatient.
With another dull bang, Zhang Lu and Cheng Xi backed away from each other. Just as Zhang Lu was about to say a few polite words to end it: "How long are you two going to drag this out? Step aside and let me see what Return Mountain Fist can really do."
A man from the Iron Fiend Fist school stepped forward into the ring.
He was short and stocky, with a square face and sharp eyes. He had a harsh, mean edge to him that set him apart from the rest.
He wore blue cloth clothes. A black line ran down the back of each of his hands, stretching all the way up into his sleeves.
"I am Chen Jian of Iron Fiend Fist."
The moment he said his name, he threw a heavy punch straight at Zhang Lu.
Cheng Xi had no choice but to step back. If he stayed, it would look like he and Iron Fiend Fist were ganging up on Zhang Lu together.
This Chen Jian fought with raw, brutal force. Every hit was a hard clash, and his physical strength was massive.
Iron Fiend Fist did not have the explosive burst of Return Mountain Fist, but it relied on overwhelming pressure. Give them a tiny advantage, and they would stack hit after hit on you until the pressure crushed you completely.
Chen Jian's fighting rhythm was fast and relentless. He choked the air out of Zhang Lu. In less than ten moves, his fist hit Zhang Lu hard on the shoulder.
Bang! Zhang Lu was knocked flat on the ground.
"Good fight," Chen Jian said, disappointment showing in his eyes.
The moment he heard someone here had broken through, he had rushed over without waiting for his master's order, eager for a real fight.
If Return Mountain Fist could raise a Third Blood Refinement master, their disciples had to be impressive.
But now... looking at the guy on the floor, Chen Jian felt that if he had not held back at the start, he could have flattened him in five moves.
"You are too weak. Get someone who can actually fight," Chen Jian said, making no attempt to hide his disgust.
He was a fighter obsessed with martial arts. He had come here full of drive, only to end up fighting garbage like this?
This guy had never even been in a real fight. He had barely seen any blood. His moves lacked follow-through, his attacks were hesitant, and he had zero killer edge.
Put a guy like this in the wild outside the city, and if anything went wrong, he would not last a day.
Chen Jian still remembered when he went up Mount Shaoyang. He was almost killed by those sneaky fat guys.
That trip still gave him nightmares about Mount Shaoyang. Those fat men were not just crazy strong; they had endless dirty tricks hidden up their sleeves.
"You!!" Zhang Lu had never been humiliated like this before. He could tell Chen Jian was just a blunt brute, but to say that in front of everyone was too much.
"Zhang Lu, let me handle this," Jiang Su said, taking a step forward from the Return Mountain Fist side.
Zhang Lu was new to the inner yard and still lacked strength.
With Wei He away, Zhao Hong—the strongest remaining disciple—could not just jump in right away.
She had to step up.
Chen Jian shrugged lazily. "Let me state this first. Even if you are a woman, I won't pull my punches."
"Fine by me," Jiang Su said. She might not be as talented as Zhang Lu, but she had been training longer and had far more real fighting experience. Zhang Lu had broken through fast, but he simply had not had time to build up experience.
With a new opponent in the ring, the fight started immediately without another word.
The moment Jiang Su traded hits, she understood why Zhang Lu had lost so fast.
Chen Jian changed his moves lightning fast. His strikes were heavy, and his stamina was endless. After just a few clashes, her forearms throbbed with sharp pain, her muscles reaching their limit.
"Is there anyone good here or not?" Chen Jian barked, his temper rising. He did not come here to play house with these weaklings. He came to fight the genius who broke through to the Third Blood Refinement!
Did Return Mountain Fist actually produce a Third Blood Refinement disciple, or was it all a lie?
With a swift shift in movement, he slipped past Jiang Su's guard and slammed a heavy fist straight into her shoulder, throwing her hard to the dirt.
"Can you bring out someone who can actually fight?! You send me absolute trash or women. Is Return Mountain Fist a joke?? Is this all your Second Blood Refinement fighters have to offer?? Why don't you all just come at me at once!" His anger boiled over completely.
