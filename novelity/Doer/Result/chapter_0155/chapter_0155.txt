The Wei family courtyard on the West Mountain was quiet.
Outside the wall, the faint sounds of new Wanqing Sect disciples practicing their martial arts echoed through the air.
The new government reforms hit every industry hard. The old ways of making money broke down, and new ones took their place. Some people lost everything. Others rose to the top overnight.
Because of this, the faces inside the Wanqing Courtyard changed too.
Old acquaintances whose families went broke had to leave quietly because they could no longer afford the training. New faces showed up to learn martial arts for self-defense, backed by their newly rich families.
In just a few short months, almost all the faces in the courtyard were different.
Dawn was just breaking.
Wei He sat at the desk in his room. He gently put out the oil lamp and looked out the window at the light rain, falling silent for a long time.
From the next room, he heard the faint, calm breathing of Wei Ying. She seemed to be having a sweet dream, smacking her lips now and then as if eating something delicious.
She said Zhen Qi had taught her a special way to sleep to make her skin better, build her health, and keep sickness away. Wei He wanted to check it out for her, but Wei Ying turned red every time she brought it up and refused to explain.
Assuming it involved some private body parts, Wei He did not push her. Seeing his second sister looking healthier and full of energy was enough to put his mind at ease.
He was only worried about Zhen Qi.
So much time had passed since he last saw her, back when she brought his sister home. She had unlocked her Force back then too. He did not know how strong she really was now, but she acted like a wealthy young lady, which was enough to scare off most people.
At least she was safe. That was much better than the dangerous life they used to live.
Wei He watched a black swallow fly past the window, remembering his days in Feiye City. He suddenly felt an urge to go back to Yun State and see how things were.
But he knew he was not strong enough yet. Traveling that far was a massive risk on its own.
Even if he went back, what could he do? He was powerless against the massive strength of the Hong Family Fortress.
'When I am stronger, when my power is high enough, I will go back and check...' Wei He thought to himself.
In truth, when he left Feiye City, he had already lost hope that his parents and eldest sister were alive. Looking back now was just him missing his old life.
Wei He pulled his gaze back, gently closed the window, and stood up from the desk.
He walked over to the wardrobe and lifted it with both hands. Beneath it, he pulled a ring hidden in the floor.
With a soft creak, a hidden hatch opened below the ring.
Wei He used his Force to hold the hatch cover and opened it upward.
Inside the hidden space sat all the items he had gathered recently, all placed neatly.
There were the goods from Zhou Yugui’s group, as well as the stash he just took from Zhou Shun.
Zhou Yugui, the two martial masters from Fushan Courtyard, and the Night Eagle Tower gang had left behind eighty thousand in cash notes. There were also some poisons, knockout drugs, hidden weapons, and partial secret manuals for Three-Blood martial arts.
From Zhou Shun, he got five thousand in cash notes, a black jade pendant, and a map peeled off Zhou Shun's back.
The map was stuck tightly to the skin on Zhou Shun's back, looking just like real human skin. On it was a drawing of the nearby mountains with three points marked out. Those were likely the locations of the three flesh farms Zhou Shun mentioned.
Wei He put the map away, planning to hunt them down when he had time.
He grabbed the cash notes, packed the rest away, reset the hidden compartment, and stood up.
He had a sparring match set with Wan Qingqing today, and time was running out. He needed to leave.
Right now, the Wanqing Sect only had three martial masters. Wan Qingqing was the face of the group. Yao Hansheng was unreliable and loved to wander around drinking.
That left Wei He as the most calm and grounded master, so many disciples liked coming to him for advice.
Whenever they asked, Wei He gave quick, direct tips.
He trained in many martial arts at once, and his overall cultivation was a level higher than his main Rain Cover Force. Because of this, his insights were often clearer than even Wan Qingqing's.
Over the past few days, Wei He's reputation within the Wanqing Sect grew higher and higher.
Wan Qingqing wanted to spar with him mainly because she was confused by the strange freezing feeling she felt during their last fight.
Usually, even though the fourth layer of Rain Cover Force used cold Eye of Quanyin birds to break through, it was extremely rare for Force to gain an icy trait because of it.
Wei He's performance last time made her suspect that he had gained a cold trait during his breakthrough.
Only people with an extreme match for the martial art could trigger this special effect.
Because of this, Wan Qingqing had been asking Wei He to spar more and more often.
Wei He packed his things, changed into tight martial arts gear, and walked out the door.
To separate themselves from the old Tianyin Sect, the new Wanqing Sect changed their uniform to deep blue long clothes and pants.
Following the taste of their female leader, a faint cloud pattern was embroidered on the left chest and back of the clothes. It represented the Cloud-Gathering Rain-Covering Art of the Wanqing Courtyard.
The women's clothes were a bit different. They were tighter to show off the figure, while shrinking and holding in the solid muscles of female fighters to create softer, prettier lines.
To keep the lower half from being too tight and showing private outlines, popular lotus skirts were added to cover the thigh area.
Wei He walked out of his room and saw the disciples gathered in an open forest clearing nearby.
Inner disciples, along with a few outer disciples and newcomers who followed them, made a crowd of over fifty people. Standing together, the place looked quite alive.
Over forty of them were women, while only a dozen were men. More than half of them were over thirty-five years old, leftovers from the old nine-year retirement system of the inner courtyard.
Under Wan Ling's design, the old members used their experience to guide the new and weaker disciples, explaining moves and sparring with them one-by-one.
As soon as Wei He came out, Wan Qingqing, who was explaining a move to a young girl nearby, walked over quickly.
"Still on for today?" Wan Qingqing did not know her mother had already brought up a marriage proposal to him once.
She openly asked Wei He to fight again.
Wei He nodded. "Sure. Fifteen minutes from now, at the old spot."
He also wanted to fight high-level masters in the Bone Forging stage.
Bone Force was extremely explosive and powerful. He wanted to get used to it so he would not be caught off guard when he faced such experts in the future.

Xuanjing City, Yuanqi District.
Yuanqi District was the area with the most gardens in Xuanjing City.
There were many chess gardens left over from peaceful times. Back then, Yuanqi District was a place set aside by the city for people who loved playing chess.
Chess clubs were everywhere, and public chess parks could be seen all over, looking like small parks.
Inside the gardens were flowers, ponds, pavilions, and stone paths. The environment was pleasant and great for walks.
Ever since chaos broke out across the land, the area lost its popularity.
Fewer people came here. After all, most could barely get enough food to stay alive, so who had time to play chess?
As a result, it became a quiet place for rich youngsters to relax.
Lin Xiaoxiao picked up a small stone and placed it gently on the edge of a small pond. The stones she picked earlier were already built into a small ring.
She wore a plain white dress with a pure black crystal pendant on her neck. Her long hair fell down to her waist, with a silver ribbon showing inside.
"I knew you were here again." Wen Lian stepped closer from behind, glaring at a few hired guards who tried to step in.
The guards were startled by her sudden approach, but once they saw it was Wen Lian, they backed away again.
"Looks like Rushui Workshop is doing well lately. Every time you are in a good mood, you come here to stack stones," Wen Lian said with a smile.
She always wore a red short dress with a tight belt. Underneath, she wore tight black pants and brown tall boots, showing no skin while looking sharp and attractive.
"It is alright," Lin Xiaoxiao stood up and adjusted her long hair.
"Fuzui Restaurant is being fixed up recently, so business is down. Since I have nothing to do, I just wander around all day," Wen Lian said lazily. She squatted down nearby, grabbed a twig from the ground, and poked at the floating leaves in the pond.
"Not resting?" Lin Xiaoxiao asked. Wen Lian looked tired, as if she had not slept well.
"Don't want to. By the way, why is Rushui Workshop pulling back lately? You pulled back all the markets you expanded into before. What happened?" Wen Lian asked in surprise.
"He suggested it," Lin Xiaoxiao answered softly.
"He?" Wen Lian was stunned. "That Wei He you hired from Wanqing Courtyard? No, it should be called Wanqing Sect now. Are you serious? Doing everything he says, how can you grow your business?"
"He is very capable," Lin Xiaoxiao answered seriously.
"Capable of what? If he were really capable, he wouldn't make you hide and pull back everywhere. With the new policy out, now is the time to grab market share. If you don't push forward now and wait until others take everything, aren't you being stupid?" Wen Lian pouted.
"..." Lin Xiaoxiao shook her head slightly and stopped talking.
"Let me tell you, guys like him do nothing except take money happily. What else has he done? Rushui Workshop only had a little trouble at the start, and you got lucky and walked away fine.
Nothing has happened to Rushui Workshop for so long now. You think that's his work? That was just your good luck!"
Wen Lian pouted.
"You don't think your luck will last forever, do you? Relying on a guy who does nothing but take money, Rushui Workshop will collapse sooner or later. Why not switch to someone else soon while your business is still good?"
Lin Xiaoxiao shook her head without a word.
Even though Wei He only showed up once in a while and did not manage daily things, Rushui Workshop had been very stable up until now.
So she trusted Wei He deeply. She always felt safe as long as he was around.
Wen Lian poked the leaves in the water with her twig for a bit, then stood up.
"If you ask me, you should hire someone from the Huguang Courtyard, the strongest group right now. In the Tianyin Sect, Wanqing Courtyard was the weakest. Now that Rushui Workshop is doing well and making good profit, you should change someone quickly before it gets hard to do so later."
"No need. Things are fine as they are," Lin Xiaoxiao shook her head.
Wen Lian was speechless.
"Then how about sponsoring one more?"
"..." Lin Xiaoxiao hesitated. "I need to discuss it with him first."
"...You are driving me crazy!" Wen Lian completely gave up.
If she discussed it with him, wouldn't that tell him Rushui Workshop was making more money now? That would give him a chance to ask for more, especially since their contract was not over yet.
"Speaking of the contract, back when you signed it, the Tianyin Sect was the witness and guaranteed your safety. Now that the Tianyin Sect has fallen apart, nobody cares about that contract anymore. Doesn't that mean you can..."
"He is very good..." Lin Xiaoxiao shook her head.
Things were great as they were. Her younger brother was learning martial arts under Wei He, coming back healthy and growing stronger. The business was steady and quiet. Everything was going smoothly.
She had no big ambition. As long as she could live peacefully like this until her brother grew up, that would be enough.
