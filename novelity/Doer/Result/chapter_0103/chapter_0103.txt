Before long, Wang Chong slowly woke up. Every inch of his body hurt like hell.
Then it hit him. He reached out with a weak, trembling hand to feel for the gold notes in his shirt. Empty. Tears immediately filled his eyes.
"You're finally awake," Wei He's voice came from the side.
Wang Chong lifted his head. At least his neck was still intact.
He looked at Wei He—the inner disciple he had mistook for a naive greenhorn. The guy was lying on the ground just like him. His face was pale as paper, blood wiped across his mouth. He looked badly hurt.
Wang Chong stared, speechless. Something felt completely off.
When he was knocked out earlier, his mind went blank. He knew nothing after that. Because of it, he messed up and missed his deal with Xiao Heng.
He had no idea what happened next.
Wei He had already hidden the cash. He raised his head and looked back at Wang Chong with dark, deep eyes.
He was thinking: If this guy tries anything stupid, I'll just crush him with one slap to save the trouble.
"Lord Wei... Boss Wang..." Yang Xue slowly woke up too. "What just happened?" He was totally lost, confused about how he got knocked out out of nowhere.
"A thief dosed us and ambushed us," Wei He said, having already prepped his story. "I fought him off, but I got hurt and fainted too. In the end, we all got cleaned out..."
Yang Xue panicked and quickly searched his clothes. His face dropped. All his gold was gone.
That was gold! Far more valuable than paper cash! It was at least three months' worth of his training budget!
Yang Xue wanted to cry. Talk about bad luck—getting robbed just by eating barbecue? Weren't they supposed to be constables?
"Ruthless..." Wang Chong couldn't hold it back. He stared at Wei He and forced out the words.
Clearly, he didn't realize how bad his injuries were. If he knew he was permanently broken, he might have jumped up right then to fight Wei He to the death.
Though he probably couldn't jump even if he tried.
Wei He just sighed. If Wang Chong weren't wearing that constable badge, he would have killed him on the spot.
Taking this detour was just to avoid unnecessary trouble.
Whether the government office believed it didn't really matter. He just needed to give the outside world a decent excuse.
Wang Chong was ruined. He had zero value left.
Wei He, on the other hand, was still a Three-Blood martial artist and an inner disciple of the Heavenly Seal Sect. Even if they suspected him, anyone could see who mattered more.
Soon, another group of constables from Xiongshan Town arrived, led by a different captain.
Everything went just as Wei He expected. Hearing that their own captain was dropped by cheap poison was an embarrassing scandal. They had no interest in digging deeper. They roughly wrote down a report about an ambush by unknown thugs, handed it in, and closed the case.
As for Wang Chong? Just a low-level captain with no backing. Exactly as Wei He predicted: nobody cared, and nobody gave a damn.
Once it was done, the three had no interest in hanging out anymore. They split up and went home.
Wang Chong only got carried back because his fellow constables took pity on his past fairness.
Of course, the promise of two taels of gold per person upon arrival was the real reason.
Yang Xue headed home with a bitter face, left to figure out how to explain this loss to his family.
Wei He turned down the offer for a ride. He dug up his hidden gold notes and headed straight for the Heavenly Seal Sect.
With this haul, he could finally train in peace for a while.
One thousand taels of gold, plus the Water Bear meat he hoarded over two months of patrol duty, was enough to sustain him.
Wanqing Yard cost seventy taels of gold a month. One thousand taels would last him over a year.
The first thing Wei He did when he got back was find Senior Sister Wan Qingqing to pay off his debt.
Wan Qingqing was the daughter of the yard master, Wan Ling, so she lived in the inner court.
Early the next morning, Wei He put salve on his arm, drank some blood-circulating medicine, and went to the yard.
Men and women had to keep boundaries. Martial artists didn't care much for minor rules, but basic manners still applied.
He intentionally waited until the sun was fully up before heading over.
Because inner court yards had few disciples, formal lessons were held only twice a week at fixed times and places.
On normal days, every disciple had one extra chance to ask for guidance. Once used, they had to wait until next week.
That was the rule of Wanqing Yard.
When Wei He arrived, he saw two female disciples pulling Wan Qingqing aside in the courtyard, asking questions.
Because of the master's strength and the nature of their martial art, Wanqing Yard had mostly female disciples. Out of a dozen or so, more than half were women.
"The key to the first layer of the Overcast Cloud Art lies in the word overcast," Wan Qingqing explained carefully. "You need enough Blood Seal built up. Once you have enough, release it all at once to cover your whole body. If anything goes wrong halfway, all your effort goes down the drain. So make sure you prepare well."
"Senior Sister, how much do we need to build up before we can cover our whole body?" a short female disciple asked.
"For women, look at your cycle. If..." Wan Qingqing lowered her voice, clearly avoiding male ears.
A moment later, the two female disciples left with bright red faces.
"Junior Brother Wei? Come in," Wan Qingqing called out with a smile when she spotted him at the gate.
Wei He answered and walked in.
Yard Master Wan Ling was often away, so Wan Qingqing usually taught in her place. Wei He was used to this scene.
Today, Wan Qingqing wore tight grey-white training clothes. Long legs, slim waist, full chest—her curves were on full display. Her pale arms were bare, smooth like lotus roots.
Her long hair was tied up high, but her face carried a gentle smile as she looked at Wei He.
That focused stare could easily make someone think she liked them.
In fact, a few male disciples had picked this yard solely because of her beauty and gentleness.
Wei He was not one of them.
His face stayed calm. He knew this was just how she treated everyone—warm and friendly to all, special to none.
"Greetings, Senior Sister," he cupped his hands.
"What brings you here, Junior Brother?" she asked directly.
"I'm here to pay back the money." Wei He pulled out a twenty-tael gold note from his pocket and offered it with both hands.
He didn't add any extra, just the exact amount he borrowed. He knew her personality.
Last time someone tried to pay back extra, she threw them out. So he returned only what he owed.
"Not bad. You found a way to make money quickly," Wan Qingqing smiled, taking the note. She caught sight of his injured arm. "What happened there?"
"Got hit during a fight by mistake. Just a minor injury. Thanks for asking, Senior Sister," Wei He replied.
He was about to say more, but someone else arrived at the gate.
"Senior Sister! Sister Yun and Brother Zhou invited you to West Mountain to view the flowers!" an outer disciple called out from the entrance.
He was just a messenger. He stood at the gate, afraid to step inside, looking cautious.
Wei He didn't get it at first, but then he saw the gentle Wan Qingqing's face darken instantly.
"Tell them I thank them for the offer, but my hands are full," she said, taking a deep breath.
These people kept coming to act as matchmakers, all sent by that guy from the Lishan Sect.
She had fallen for it a few times before. Every time she went, they just nagged her to accept his marriage proposal. It always ended badly.
Honestly, if the guy pursued her openly, she might respect him more. But using pressure tactics from all sides to force her hand? Not a chance.
Once the messenger left, her face softened a bit.
"Sorry you had to see that. Anything else?" She looked at Wei He.
"Nothing else. Thank you for helping me before, Senior Sister," Wei He thanked her again.
"No need. We are from the same sect. We should look out for each other," she smiled.
"Yes." Wei He nodded, turning to leave.
"Oh, right. Junior Brother Wei, try not to wander too far from the city these next few months. Things are tense. Rebel armies are getting close to Jin Province. War might break out, so be careful."
"Jin Province? Got it. Thanks for the warning."
"Don't worry too much," she added. "General You from Xuanjing City is already moving troops. They'll crush it soon."
"Understood."
Wei He nodded and took his leave.
After staying in Tai Province for this long, he had a general idea of how things worked here.
Back in Yun Province, his news was blocked because he was trapped in the small Huishan Boxing Academy. Here, it was different.
The Heavenly Seal Sect was far larger. Except for a few, most disciples only showed up for class once a week and went straight home.
Many disciples were locals from Xuanjing City. Their families owned businesses, served as officials, or belonged to wealthy clans.
With so many people, news inside the sect moved way faster than back at Huishan.
As Wei He walked back to Heavenly Seal Town, he slowly pieced together where the sect stood near Xuanjing City.
Xuanjing City belonged to the Three Great Clans. Their roots and power ran through every single corner. Many locals only heard rumors of the distant Heavenly Seal Sect, but they truly feared the Three Clans right in front of them.
Compared to these clans that could pull out thousands of armed guards at any moment, a few hundred members in the Heavenly Seal Sect really didn't look like much.
Whatever. That's way above my pay grade right now. Better focus on training, Wei He thought.
He had enough cash now. He could finally lie low for a while.
Back home, Wei He focused on solidifying his newly broken-through Flying Dragon Technique. With its boost, his speed surged to a new level.
He stopped going out altogether, staying home every day to practice his martial arts and push the Overcast Cloud Art with everything he had.
This core art of Wanqing Yard was brutally hard. It made sense for a technique that required Three-Blood strength just to start. Progress was dead slow.
Not long after, the body of "Human-Faced Tiger" Xiao Heng was found in a filthy ditch. The news spread across the city and reached Heavenly Seal Town.
People threw out all kinds of guesses about how he died, but not a single one hit the truth.
Wei He knew exact truth, but he kept his mouth shut. He only felt it was a pity he couldn't loot Xiao Heng's body.
He reminded himself internally: Next time something like this happens, never underestimate how tough a Force-user really is.
