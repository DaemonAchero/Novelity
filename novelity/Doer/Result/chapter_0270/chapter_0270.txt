“Have you really made up your mind?” Wei He asked softly. “With your natural talent, if you keep trying, you might still break through to the Sensate Realm. But if you give up now, there really is no hope left.”
Wang Shaojun smiled.
“Life is unpredictable. I can’t gamble decades of my life on an uncertain chance. I can’t give up everything else just to bet on that.”
He added, “Did you know that Taizhou has two new geniuses who are just as good as me? Mo Suanyun from the Mo family, and Bai Hao from the Qiuchi Sword Sect. Both are under twenty years old, yet they have already mastered the Martial Master level. They’ve been challenging the young masters of the three big families in Xuanjing, and they haven't lost once.”
“The Mo family is just a small family that moved here from outside. The Qiuchi Sword Sect is also just a medium-sized faction. Why worry about them?” Wei He frowned.
“I went to a gathering for the younger generation two days ago and saw Bai Hao. He’s very similar to how I used to be. His father is a top expert in the Nest Extermination Alliance. He’s no weaker than me. His strength is genuine,” Wang Shaojun said with a smile.
He looked closely at Wei He again.
“So, how about it? We’re brothers. Come help me.”
It was a choice.
Right now, Wang Shaojun was being favored by the state governor. Following him would lead to a different path.
However, once he took that path, his time for martial arts training would drop sharply.
In fact, Wang Shaojun’s cultivation was already regressing because he spent so much time on government work.
Wei He knew how strong Wang Shaojun’s protective force was. At this close distance, Wei He’s keen senses could tell that his friend’s protective energy was a tiny bit weaker than it was six months ago.
Even though it was just a tiny drop, Wang Shaojun was still in his prime. This shouldn't be happening.
The only reason was that he was neglecting his martial arts to focus on politics.
“I’m sorry,” Wei He said after a moment of silence. “I prefer to rely on my own strength. I will only walk one path.”
Wang Shaojun stared into his eyes, but he didn't look surprised.
“I actually guessed this before I came. You wouldn't follow me to the capital city. It seems I was right.”
Wei He didn't answer. He just felt a bit emotional inside.
No matter how good a friend was, there would always come a time to part ways.
Now that Wang Shaojun was being transferred to the capital, there were fewer and fewer familiar faces left in Xuanjing.
After today, it might be years before they met again.
“When are you leaving?” Wei He asked suddenly.
“In three days,” Wang Shaojun answered cheerfully.
Wei He took the wine jug from a nearby maid and filled both of their cups.
“Any last words before you go?”
Wang Shaojun thought for a moment, and the smile on his face slowly faded.
After a long time, he spoke.
“I don't know if my path will end in success or failure. If the Wang family gets into trouble in the future, don't come to save me. If you care enough, just help me keep a single bloodline alive. The same goes for you.”
“...Good!” Wei He raised his cup.
The two clinked their glasses and drank it all down.
...
Wang Shaojun left. Along with him went most of the Wang family's elite fighters, leaving only a small portion of the clan behind in Xuanjing. Wang Zhihe was also transferred to the capital city.
The Zhou family was slowly weakening because Zhou Xingtong was badly injured.
The new powerhouses in Xuanjing were the rising Qiuchi Sword Sect and the Mo family.
With the old forces hiding away or moving out, these two factions became the new rulers of Xuanjing City because they had Viscera Refining experts backing them.
Wei He returned to his sect. He had already cleared most of the twelve barriers of the Viscera Refining Realm.
In two years, with the help of the Breakthrough Bead, his progress had far surpassed Wang Shaojun, and even beat old experts like Xie Yan.
He had officially entered the middle stage of Viscera Refining.
It was evening.
Wei He went for a walk outside the mountain forest with his wife and son.
Two-year-old Wei An couldn't run steadily yet, but he was already rolling around and shouting in the woods.
Wei An was chubby and fair-skinned. He took after his mother, with pretty, cute features that almost made him look like a girl.
Unlike his cute looks, this kid had been hanging out with Jiu Ying since he was a baby, so his habits were a bit strange.
Dusk fell.
Wei He and Wan Qingqing walked slowly behind, while Wei An ran around happily in short sleeves and shorts.
Neither of them was worried about his safety. At this distance, Wei He could easily protect the boy no matter what happened.
Besides, the kid had been soaked in expensive medicinal baths since he was tiny.
On top of that, Jiu Ying and Wei Wei had secretly used the True Method to lay a foundation for him. His body was tough, his blood was strong, and he was immune to hundreds of poisons.
Even though he was only two, he was already a nightmare for all the wild beasts in the surrounding forest.
The little guy spent every day following Jiu Ying around, ordering the elite fighters of the Thousand Poisons Sect to mess with the local beasts.
When the animal packs saw him, or even felt him coming, they would turn around and run.
“This kid is getting wilder... I wonder what he’ll grow up to be,” Wan Qingqing said worriedly as she watched her son.
Wei An was currently holding a small, triangle-headed green snake with one hand, yanking it straight out of the grass.
The poor little green snake had its tail stepped on. Before it could even see what happened, it turned around and bit him.
Then a flying stone crushed its head.
Even if the snake had bitten him, Wei An would have been fine.
His immunity to poison was different from Wei He and Jiu Ying.
Since he was too young to cultivate force energy, he couldn't practice the True Method properly.
However, just because he couldn't cultivate didn't mean they couldn't use it to make him stronger.
To help him grow up safely in this toxic forest, Wei He and Jiu Ying came up with a method. They used external tools to forcefully give him the effects of the True Method.
They combined poison skills with the True Method and found out why traditional poison skills shortened one's life and ruined their looks.
It was because traditional poison skills involved self-harm. They forced the body to constantly suffer damage and rebuild itself. That was the root cause of a shortened life.
To fix this, they just needed to reduce the damage and the number of times the body had to adapt.
So, Wei He and Jiu Ying worked hard to create a mini version of the True Method just for Wei An.
That led to the current situation.
“Speaking of which, will Xiao An’s body affect his ability to make friends later?” Wan Qingqing asked with concern.
“Don't worry. Once he adapts, his body will naturally hold the poison in,” Wei He comforted her.
“What about his education? Should we hire a tutor from outside, or teach him inside the sect?”
“Education... let's check inside the sect first. If that doesn't work, we can hire someone from outside. The world is too chaotic right now. It's best if everyone stays inside.”
“I really hope we can keep living like this,” Wan Qingqing sighed. “Playing chess, drinking tea, eating snacks, taking walks, growing flowers... having nothing to worry about.”
“We will. As long as we are careful, everything will get better.”
“Anyway, some sect members brought silk from the capital yesterday. I plan to make you a new robe. You’ve been wearing the same old clothes for a long time. As the sect master, you should dress properly.”
“Thank you, my wife.” Wei He reached out and placed his hand on her waist.
Since reaching the Viscera Refining stage, his body had grown again, close to three meters tall.
When he opened his hand, a single palm was almost as wide as Wan Qingqing’s waist.
The two no longer slept in the same bed because the size difference was too big, and Wan Qingqing couldn't handle it.
Because of this, Wei He was recently researching how to increase his body density without making himself any taller.
“Dad! Mom! Look! See what I caught!”
Wei An ran back cheerfully, hiding his hands behind his back with a big smile.
“What is it? Can you show Mom?” Wan Qingqing bent down and asked softly.
“This! Haha!” Wei An thrust his hands forward.
Two purple centipedes were wriggling wildly in his hands, trying to escape, but it was useless.
“Look! Two little cuties!” Wei An didn't notice his mother's face turning pale. He excitedly rolled the centipedes into a ball, rubbed them, tied them in a knot, and swung them around.
“These are bigger than the ones I played with at Grandpa Jiu Ying’s place! If I kill them, we can make soup!” The boy spoke clearly. Though his voice was childish, he was far smarter than a normal two-year-old.
Wan Qingqing stood frozen, staring at the many-legged purple centipedes.
She looked at her son’s innocent smile, then looked at the centipedes biting his hand without even breaking the skin...
“Wei He!” she screamed.
“What on earth did you do to your son?!”
Amidst her loud anger, Wei He picked up his son and ran.
Wan Qingqing didn't know about the mini True Method they made for Wei An. She only thought he was doing basic poison resistance training.
If she knew about this dangerous method, she would have never allowed it.
Now that the truth was out, Wei He couldn't explain it right away. He had to wait for her to calm down first.
After a lot of talking, Wei He and Jiu Ying finally made Wan Qingqing understand their good intentions.
Living in a forest full of poison meant Wei An needed strong protection, or a single mistake could cost him his life.
This wasn't just for Wei An. Other children born to couples in the Thousand Poisons Sect went through similar training, though without the help of the True Method.
After calming his wife down, Wei He returned to his private training ground.
He had already cleared eight of the twelve barriers of the Viscera Refining Realm. His internal organs were more than half-strengthened.
Because of this, his understanding of the five senses grew deeper.
According to the information he got from You Fu, entering the Sensate Realm required fixing one of the five senses: sight, hearing, smell, taste, or touch.
Most people chose touch.
Touch was easier to apply to the whole body, and easier to shut off when needed.
One could choose to strengthen touch on just a small area, like a single hand, a leg, or a small patch of skin on the back.
The other senses were different. You had to pick one eye or both eyes, one ear or both ears. Taste left you with no choices at all.
Because touch was the most common choice, there was more information available for it.
Wei He thought it over. To lower risks and make the best use of the knowledge he had, he decided to lock his sense of touch, specifically targeting his left hand.
He would sharpen the touch sensitivity of his left hand until it reached a super-sense. That was his next plan.
