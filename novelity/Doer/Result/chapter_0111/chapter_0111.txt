Xiao Yurong’s Tiger Claw force was made to rip through an enemy's power. Against a weaker fighter, it would crush them in a single move. Against a stronger one, it could soften the blow, letting him escape alive.
"So the drugs really aren't enough anymore," Wei He sighed. After testing two Force-Bound masters, he knew normal poison barely affected people at this level.
Scheming and trickery were just a waste of time. Killing them directly was much faster.
"Die!" Furious, Xiao Yurong poured out all his force.
He smashed through the window and lunged at Wei He. Silver light flashed from his claws, aiming straight for Wei He's vital spots.
Wei He used his Flying Dragon technique, stepping back quickly to dodge.
He instantly drew power from his two main skillsets, Mountain Return Boxing and Rain Cover Seal. A thick layer of force covered his hands.
The two forces twisted together around his arms. As he blocked the blows, he remained completely unharmed, effortlessly shutting down Xiao Yurong's tearing force.
Xiao Yurong was far weaker than Xiao Heng, the Human-Faced Tiger. His hits were strong, but they lacked that terrifying, deep-vibrating shockwave. His speed, power, and reactions were a level below.
Still, he was a true Force-Bound martial artist, a whole realm higher than Wei He. Before his recent breakthrough, Wei He might have been pushed back.
But not today.
After thirty moves, Wei He knew exactly where he stood. Crossing the first layer of his new technique had boosted his raw power by a huge margin. Now, he was actually pressing Xiao Yurong down.
"Leader Xiao, why so angry? I mean no harm. I only came to find the truth," Wei He said calmly. "Do we really have to fight to the death?"
"Screw you! No harm?! Then why did you drug my drink?! Do you think I'm an idiot?!" Fire burned in Xiao Yurong's chest. "One of us dies today! Rip!"
He slammed his claws down like a tiger pouncing on its prey. Cold wind sliced through the air like blades, rushing straight at Wei He.
Whoosh!
Halfway through the strike, Xiao Yurong froze.
His claws stopped just an inch from Wei He's chest. But that single inch was a gap he could never cross.
It wasn't that he stopped on purpose. Wei He’s right palm was already resting quietly right between his eyebrows.
"Leader Xiao, I really had no bad intentions," Wei He sighed. "If you were as obedient as the Black Water Gang and just stayed asleep, we wouldn't be here now."
"You...!!!" Cold sweat poured down Xiao Yurong’s face. That gentle palm had instantly shattered his defensive force.
"You can't kill me! The Zhou Family is behind me!" he screamed. He kicked off the floor, trying to leap backward.
Wei He moved like a shadow. He closed the gap in a flash and slapped his palm onto Xiao Yurong’s head again.
Thump.
"You...!!" Xiao Yurong choked out his last word. His eyes stayed wide open as his body dropped to the floor.
Even in death, he didn't understand. Why did this fight happen? Why was he poisoned? Why was he dying to this stranger? He hadn't done anything unusual. He just took some money to do a small job, like he always did...
"Hitting the head is definitely faster," Wei He muttered, looking at the body. "Otherwise, he would have run away like the last guy. I guess even Force-Bound masters die if their heads get smashed."
He leaned down and quickly searched the corpse. He found a pouch with over three thousand gold notes inside.
Wei He smiled.
"Right, he mentioned the Zhou Family. Not that it matters. They just took some money to lend him their name. As long as the gang keeps paying its monthly fee, a big family doesn't care who runs a tiny gang like this."
The Zhou Family was one of the three ruling families in Xuanjing City. They backed dozens of small gangs. If you paid them, they ignored you. If you didn't, you died.
Wei He had already checked this before coming. He knew what he could get away with. Xiao Yurong had simply been stupid enough to think he actually mattered to the Zhou Family.
Now came the loot.
Wei He walked back into the main hall. Bodies were knocked out all over the floor. The outer guards had already run away when the fight started, leaving the place dead quiet.
Moving fast, Wei He searched the bodies one by one.
Moments later, he walked out of the building carrying a large sack. He had found the exact thing he wanted most: the manual for the White Jade Technique.
Xiao Yurong had claimed this skill made him immune to poisons. Since Wei He dealt with poisons all the time, he was very interested in it.
He had pulled the manual out from a hidden spot beneath the tiger-skin chair, along with a manual for the Tiger Claw style. From the six gang leaders in the hall, he grabbed another two thousand gold notes, plus a bunch of poisons and drugs.
Combined with what he stole from the Black Water Gang, he had made over eight thousand gold notes on this single trip. A massive harvest.
Wei He used his Flying Dragon movement and vanished into the night.
This investigation had opened up a whole new way to get rich. It also proved his true strength. Unless a Force-Bound master was exceptionally strong, they were no match for him now.
Besides, he had a good reason to rob them. The White Snake Gang attacked first. With the Heavenly Seal Sect backing him, the Zhou Family wouldn't risk a fight over a dead nobody.
Add in the ten thousand gold notes from Lin Xiaoxiao, and Wei He was richer than ever.
It was time to pick his next martial art. With a steady supply of rare beast meat, his breaking-bead would fill up fast. He could choose skills that perfectly complemented his weak points.
Should he pick raw defense, speed, energy redirection, or stamina?
Yubei Town, Lin Manor.
Lin Xiaoxiao held a small pot, carefully watering the flowers in her garden.
Her favorite thing to do was water unopen flower buds, figure out what nutrients they lacked, and feed them. Seeing a flower bloom bright and beautiful gave her pure joy. If it didn't bloom well, she would sit and analyze what went wrong.
A servant came in and handed her a letter.
She took it, opened it gently, and read it line by line.
It was from Wei He. He said the problem was fixed, and no one would trouble her shop anymore. If she needed help again, she could send a letter to the Wei house near the old locust tree by Heavenly Seal Lake.
Lin Xiaoxiao let out a soft breath and carefully folded the paper.
"Xiaoxiao!" Wen Lian burst into the garden loudly. "Watering flowers again?"
She wore a bright red leather outfit with a fur shawl that showed off her sharp curves. Aside from her fierce eyes, she was the complete opposite of the quiet Lin Xiaoxiao. One was like a delicate lily, the other like a hot chili pepper.
Lin Xiaoxiao looked up, silently asking why she was here.
Having known each other for years, Wen Lian understood the look immediately.
She huffed. "I was worried about you! That guy you hired isn't reliable. So today I begged my father, and he agreed to introduce an expert from the Jianzhe Temple! You're coming with me this afternoon to meet him."
"No need," Lin Xiaoxiao said, shaking her head.
"Why not?! Jianzhe Temple is one of the Five Great Sects too! Sure, it's not as big as the Wushi Sect, but it's ranked higher than the Heavenly Seal Sect. Besides, Heavenly Seal's reputation is terrible right now. Everyone in the city is looking for help elsewhere. My father got us this connection. Even if he's just a regular inner disciple, he's way stronger than that Wei He guy!" Wen Lian urged.
"I..." Lin Xiaoxiao tried to speak.
"I know, I know! You're worried about the money you already paid him," Wen Lian interrupted. "Forget the money! Fixing the problem comes first. That Wei He definitely can't handle this. If we delay, you lose money and reputation every single day. Do you know how long your father worked to build your shop's name? So..."
Wen Lian kept rambling on and on.
Lin Xiaoxiao quietly pulled the folded letter out from her robe and opened it. She held it out to Wen Lian, but her friend pushed her hand away.
"Wait, let me finish! If you're tight on cash, I mortgaged one of my restaurants today to get you a loan. You can just pay me back later. Last time, my father's problem was solved by the Jianzhe Temple too. They are way more professional than Heavenly Seal. Plus, I had people analyze your situation yesterday. The ones targeting your shop are almost certainly the Bai Family. They've always been your biggest rival..."
She kept breaking down her theories about who planned the attack.
Lin Xiaoxiao held the paper out again.
"Read," she said seriously.
"Read what? Listen to me, this is more important! Is there anything more urgent than saving your business? I'm running out of time today, I just came to tell you this," Wen Lian said impatiently.
"This is..." Lin Xiaoxiao started to explain.
Suddenly, Wen Lian gasped.
"Oh no! I forgot I'm supposed to have tea at the Chen Shop this afternoon! I'm late! I have to run!" She turned around and sprinted off.
"Get ready! We're going together later! I set everything up for you!" Wen Lian yelled as she vanished out of the garden.
Lin Xiaoxiao stood alone by the flowerbed, holding the open letter, completely speechless.
