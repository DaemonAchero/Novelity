"Do you really think I killed him?" Wei He laughed cold. "Yuchi Yan was a Viscera Master. Even if I wanted to kill him, it would take time. I came here for one thing: to get back my Tianyin Sect secrets. Why would I waste time killing him?"
"Only my brother knew where the hidden vault was!" Yuchi Yuansha yelled, her eyes red with rage. "You used poison when he refused to speak. You murdered him!"
"Ridiculous." Wei He did not lose his temper. He had expected this trap.
"Do you even know what kind of poison it takes to down a Viscera Master? Their senses are razor-sharp and their bodies resist almost everything. Even with the strongest toxin, it wouldn’t work this fast—"
"Shut up! Your words mean nothing! You die tonight!" Yuansha snapped, cutting him off.
She raised her hand. "Kill him!"
BOOM!
A heavy sound tore through the air, like a balloon bursting or a thick log snapping in two.
Wei He’s calm face instantly changed. His internal force surged, sending thick bone force to his palms as he whipped around and grabbed at the air behind him.
BANG!
A sharp, black bullet the size of a finger smashed directly into his palm.
It spun at insane speed, carrying pure destruction. It ripped through his outer force layer in a split second, hit his bone force, and slammed into his right palm.
CLANG!
The bullet bent slightly and dropped to the ground.
Wei He didn't even have time to feel the sting in his hand. Down below, a heavy hail of crossbow bolts rained down toward him.
BOOM!
That sound rang out again.
Wei He didn't dare block it raw. He kicked off the edge and dropped inside the tower.
He used to doubt the rumors that a small team of riflemen could kill bone-forging martial masters. Now, seeing they even made sniper rifles like this, those dead masters didn't die in vain.
Luckily, these sniper bullets weren’t too fast. They hit hard, but as long as he dodged instead of taking them head-on, they couldn't kill him.
As for the poison on the bullet, he wiped a bit of matching antidote powder over his palm and suppressed it easily. Poison was never a threat to him.
Too bad the wind is blowing the wrong way, Wei He thought, dropping down another floor.
He reached into his pouch and pulled out a handful of dark green pills.
He didn't want to waste time fighting these people. They were clearly played by Yuchi Qi and the woman. They thought he was the killer.
Still, he was surprised at how fast the real killers worked. Yuchi Yan was a Viscera Master with strong body force. Dying this quickly meant something far darker was going on.
"Are you truly bent on fighting me to the death?" Wei He called out.
Silence met his words.
"The real killer drew you here to trap me. It's a distractor. They have another goal. If I were you, I wouldn't leave all your elite troops stuck here."
His voice echoed. He blocked another wave of bolts and bullets, his protective force holding strong. His massive blood energy gave him far more stamina than any normal martial artist.
But Yuansha was blinded by rage.
She was no real military commander. With the body found and three main guards confirming they just fought Wei He, she was sure of it. Even if Wei He didn't kill her brother directly, he was part of it.
She had only one thought: revenge.
"Sniper team, ready!" she raised her hand.
Those sniper rifles were lethal, but bullets were rare and costly. They were made to hunt high-level martial arts masters. But with her brother dead, she didn't care about the cost anymore.
On the other side of the city.
Yuchi Qi and Tang Huanhuan darted across the rooftops of Xuanjing City.
They were heading straight for the south granary.
All the city's food stores were gathered there. Huge piles of grain from local towns and far-off lands were kept in this single zone. It was the official military supply—the lifeblood of the Red Crimson Army.
It also held over fifty meat farms and heaps of dried food.
The two landed near the main gate.
The guards stepped forward and bowed deep. "Vice Commander."
The guards here had long been replaced by their own spies.
Tang Huanhuan nodded. "Is everything set?"
"Yes. Ready on your word."
"Yuchi Yan is dead. Once this place burns, they won't have a leader. The front lines will collapse," Tang Huanhuan said softly.
"Our job is done," Yuchi Qi smiled.
"That fool Wei He really thought we were working with him," Tang Huanhuan sneered.
"He knew we had our own plans," Yuchi Qi laughed. "He just had no choice. Alone, he couldn't pull it off."
Wei He was drawing away most of the Yuchi family's power. Elite guards were swarming his location to stop him from running. Without him taking the heat, they could never have set this trap so easily.
"Signal the men. Move," Tang Huanhuan ordered, swinging her hand down.
"Tonight, the Red Crimson Army ends."
Back at the Yuchi estate.
Wei He dove out of a tower window, crushing green pills in his palm. A massive cloud of toxic powder burst into the air, riding the wind down toward the soldiers below.
"Watch out!" a commander yelled.
The soldiers held their breath and hurled prepared buckets of water into the air, washing away most of the cloud.
But that single second was all Wei He needed. He burst through their line at full speed.
Just as he was about to break free, Yuansha slashed her blade straight at him. The three main guards lunged alongside her, with four more bone-forging masters closing in from behind.
Wei He had already used up half his force. Facing Yuansha's wild slash, he felt the heavy bone force coating her blade. Another Viscera Master.
The Yuchi family didn't rule this city on luck alone.
CLANG!
His palm hit her blade. The raw impact threw both of them back, but they recovered instantly and struck again.
"Think about it," Wei He said fast. "Why did the real killer lead you to me? The more troops you waste here, the easier it is for them to hit their real target."
"I'll ask you after I break your legs!" Yuansha roared, her blade wrapped in thick, crushing force.
She raised her sword to split his head.
BOOM!
A massive explosion shook the ground from the south.
A red glare lit up the night sky. Thick black smoke rolled into the air as bright fire roared upward, followed by a second deafening blast.
BOOM!
Flames ripped through the dark, swallowing every other sound in the city.
The whole city went quiet under the blast. The guards stopped, turning their heads toward the fire in shock.
Taking advantage of the chaos, Wei He moved like a ghost, slipping past the frozen gunners and vanishing into the night.
"The granary!" Guard Li roared, snapping back to his senses.
His shout woke Yuansha. Her face turned pale. Looking at the burning sky, her mind went completely blank.
"Put out the fire!" she screamed two seconds later.
The soldiers snapped out of it and rushed off in squads. No one cared about Wei He anymore.
Just as Wei He had said, someone had set them up to hit the granary.
"The man who framed me was Yuchi Qi," Wei He’s voice echoed from a high roof in the distance. "His master is Tang Huanhuan. They are your real killers."
He was already safe, yet he stayed to leave a warning. It made his words far more believable.
Yuansha grit her teeth, looking toward his voice, but she couldn't order a chase. Saving the food came first. If the granary burned, the army was finished.
Cold sweat ran down her back. Her brother was dead, and the army's future was ruined.
"Report! Yuchi Qi is missing!" a soldier ran up, breathless.
The family members huddled together in fear. With the second master dead and the food burning, ruin was coming for the Yuchi family.
Yuansha closed her eyes in pain. Wei He was telling the truth. The real killer used her to distract everyone while they destroyed the city's lifeline.
She squeezed her fists until her knuckles turned white, filled with regret.
"Order full search!" she snapped, opening her eyes. "Find Yuchi Qi! Put a price on his head across all three houses!"
Outside the city walls.
Tang Huanhuan and Yuchi Qi rode two donkeys down the dirt road, moving away at a lazy pace.
They looked back at the burning city with cold smiles.
"The food is gone. The army is done," Tang Huanhuan laughed.
"They're probably scrambling to fight the fire right now," Yuchi Qi added. "And that Poison Master Wei He will probably take the blame for the fire too."
"He knew the risk," Tang Huanhuan said softly. "His sect was already blood enemies with the army anyway. Too bad for him, he had to take the fall for this too."
"Yes, too bad," a voice agreed from above. "I really miscalculated this time."
On a roof nearby, Wei He stood watching the fire.
A shadow flickered, and Wang Shaojun landed right beside him.
"Why call me out so late?" Wang asked, his voice dark as he looked at the flames.
"Nothing much," Wei He turned around with a cold smile. "Just got played by someone. Want to help me hunt down a few cult masters?"
Wang blinked. "You mean... the fire was their doing?"
"Either them or another cult. Does it matter?" Wei He said.
"The Yuchi family is hunting everywhere. Do you have a lead?"
"A small one," Wei He grinned. "I slipped a little something onto the kid earlier, just in case."
He never relied on scented poisons. Tang Huanhuan was too strong to mark, but Yuchi Qi was careless.
"What's the plan?" Wang asked seriously.
"They messed with my sect," Wei He turned away, his eyes cold. "They think they can walk out of this city alive?"
