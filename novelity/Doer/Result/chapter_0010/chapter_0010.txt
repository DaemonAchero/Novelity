"Should we celebrate when we get back?" Wei Ying walked up, straightened his hair, and suggested.
"Tomorrow. Senior Brother Cheng invited me to his place later," Wei He replied.
"Alright. Just remember to bring a gift."
"I will. Sis, rest well and don't stay up late."
"I know, I know. Go on, I can take care of myself," Wei Ying smiled, her eyes and face full of joy.
Wei He looked at her. He suddenly realized that his every move and tiny change directly affected the people around him.
If he failed, his sister would have to leave this safe place and go back to that awful environment.
Now that he joined the sect, she could stay here with him, living a tiring but peaceful life.
His situation decided her fate.
With a heavy heart, Wei He went back to his corner and practiced the basic moves of the Mountain Fist.
He secretly checked the mark on his chest. The black color was gone, back to normal skin color.
"I don't know how long it will take to fill it up again. What a waste..."
Wei He sighed. It took him half a year to charge the bead this time.
"If only I could find something to replace the Money Bug meat and speed it up."
He knew that even if such a thing existed, he couldn't afford it right now.
In the afternoon, workers started cleaning up the yard. A few men sent everyone away, bowed to Old Zheng, and said goodbye.
After the routine was done, Cheng Shaojiu pulled Wei He onto a carriage.
Cheng Shaojiu's carriage was not drawn by fancy horses, but they were strong and useful.
In these tough times, only rich families had carriages.
Wei He understood this when they arrived at the Cheng house.
He looked out the carriage window.
Big red doors, white walls, and blue roof tiles. Two stone guard dogs stood at the entrance.
Wei He wondered why they used stone dogs instead of lions, but he kept quiet.
Cheng Shaojiu led him inside. The guards greeted them politely as they walked into the main courtyard.
, The yard was as big as a school playground. On the right stood a flagpole with a black-and-white flag that read 'Yonghe Escort Agency'.
The flag flapped loudly in the strong wind.
Cheng Shaojiu led him to a weapon rack in the corner of the yard.
He pointed at a wooden post wrapped in cowhide on the ground and smiled.
"Want to try a set?"
Wei He had just broken through and felt full of energy. His fists were itching for action.
"Sure, let's try."
He stepped up to the post and took a stance.
He struck first with a quick right punch from the side.
Then he unleashed a rapid chain of punches, throwing three punches a second. He kept going for over ten seconds before slowing down and taking a defensive stance.
"Full power for fifteen seconds. Not bad, not bad," Cheng Shaojiu clapped.
He walked to the post and lifted the cowhide.
Underneath, the wood was covered in deep fist marks.
This was a special hard wood, much tougher than normal timber. Leaving marks through cowhide meant serious power.
If a normal person hit this, they would break their bones and barely leave a scratch.
"You broke through to the Cowhide level, but your power control needs work. That's what you should focus on next."
"Understood. Thank you for the advice, Senior Brother!" Wei He nodded.
"Want to spar?" Cheng Shaojiu smiled. He took off his outer coat, revealing short practice clothes, and waved at Wei He.
"Please." Wei He didn't waste words. He stepped forward and threw a punch.
Cheng Shaojiu blocked it with one hand and counterattacked fast.
He was already at the Stonehide level. His inner energy was several times stronger than Wei He's. His eyes were sharp and his hands were fast as he blocked attack after attack.
They moved fast, kicking up dust from the ground.
Fifteen seconds passed in a flash.
Cheng Shaojiu landed a quick punch on Wei He's left shoulder, knocking him back a few steps.
He laughed and waved to the guards watching nearby.
"Someone come practice with my brother here so he gets some experience."
A middle-aged man jumped into the field right away and charged at Wei He.
Wei He stayed quiet and watched him closely.
Unlike Cheng Shaojiu, this man was a complete stranger. Wei He needed to see what he was good at and how he attacked.
Fist fighters usually had strong upper bodies and thick arms. Kicking experts had stable lower bodies and moved fast on their feet.
Old Zheng had told him this before, but this was Wei He's first time facing a real enemy.
"Hey!" The man lunged forward, grabbing for Wei He's shoulders with both hands.
The attack was sudden, but in Wei He's eyes, it wasn't fast.
He realized that after his energy reached the Cowhide level, his reactions and senses had improved significantly.
It seemed like a passive effect from boosted blood energy.
Wei He stepped aside and avoided the grab easily.
Though caught off guard, his body moved much faster than the attacker.
He tapped the man on the back with his right fist.
Bang.
The man fell flat on his face.
"Two more!" Cheng Shaojiu laughed from the side.
Two more guards jumped in.
Wei He dodged their moves easily and knocked them both down one by one.
He controlled his strength carefully. Seeing how much stronger he became, he didn't use full force, knocking them down with just half his power.
"Great job! Your real fight skills are way better than mine when I started," Cheng Shaojiu came over, clapping.
"How is it? Do you feel the difference after breaking through?" he asked with a smile.
He helped the guards up and dismissed them.
"I feel it," Wei He nodded.
"That's the point," Cheng Shaojiu laughed. "Old Zheng won't teach you real combat skills at the hall. If you want to fight, come to my place. My uncle's agency has plenty of men."
"You are too kind, Senior Brother," Wei He began, but Cheng Shaojiu cut him off with a smile.
"Don't refuse so fast. You are an official member now. Cowhide level energy is much higher than normal people. If you wear leather armor, two or three armed thugs won't even get close to you.
So... your treatment is different now. You get it?" Cheng Shaojiu winked at him.
"Treatment?" Wei He was confused.
"Yes, follow me." Cheng Shaojiu led Wei He inside.
They passed a big room with doors on both sides and reached a training field full of sandbags.
A few shirtless men were training among the sandbags.
"This is the training area. If you come work for me, you can use it anytime.
Plus, free food every day, a base pay of one stone of white rice, ten pounds of meat, and one hundred taels of silver a month.
If you go on escort missions, short trips pay fifty taels. Long trips pay one hundred to five hundred taels. How about it?"
Cheng Shaojiu's offer surprised Wei He, but it made sense.
He had guessed something like this before coming.
The fight earlier proved his worth. Reaching the Cowhide level made him different from regular people.
"You can go home and think about it, then compare it with other offers.
Now that you broke through, you are a real expert. Many people will come to hire you soon," Cheng Shaojiu smiled.
"Is that so?" Wei He felt a bit lost.
"Of course. In these times, people watch strong fighters closely. Once a martial artist trains up, someone hires them right away."
Cheng Shaojiu stopped smiling and sighed. "Times are tough, as you know. Nobody has it easy. Did you hear about the Hong Family Fort outside the city? They made a big move and closed the main road near them."
"Hong Family Fort?"
"Yes. The biggest powerful family outside Feiye City is the Hong family. They built an earthen fort outside, calling it Hong Family Fort. It's almost like a small city," Cheng Shaojiu explained.
"Don't look at how rich the inner city looks. The real powerful families build forts outside. You will understand when you see more. Anyway, let's not talk about that now. Come, I'll show you the best perk here!"
Cheng Shaojiu laughed and pulled Wei He toward another yard.
Soon, the two sat facing each other in a small room.
A black wooden table stood between them with two dishes and a soup.
The two dishes were stir-fried pork with bamboo shoots and cold celery salad.
The soup was soybean stewed with pig feet.
"Brother, if you join me, your best daily perk is this." Cheng Shaojiu smiled and pointed at the pork dish.
Green bamboo shoots were mixed with crispy pork slices, dry red pepper rings, and garlic slices. It looked delicious.
"Isn't this just fried meat?" Wei He looked up, confused.
"It's not normal meat. Try a piece," Cheng Shaojiu said mysteriously.
Wei He picked up his chopsticks, took a piece, and chewed it.
The taste was plain and dry without many spices, nothing like how good it looked.
However, the meat was tough, fresh, and didn't taste like pork.
Soon after swallowing, a warm burning sensation spread from his stomach.
"How is it? Feel the heat in your stomach?" Cheng Shaojiu said proudly. "This is a special item only my Cheng family has."
He picked up a piece of meat and explained, "This is Silver-Beaked Black Snake meat. It boosts your energy greatly. One pound keeps you full for two days. It works even better when soaked in liquor.
Fighters burn far more energy than normal people, so eating it every meal is fine. It builds up your blood energy fast. Most importantly, you can't buy it outside. My family keeps it exclusive."
"Silver-Beaked Black Snake..." Wei He took another piece and noticed the difference.
The snake meat had black skin and elastic meat inside. Tiny blood vessels as thin as hair were visible. Up close, it almost looked like a work of art.
"So, as brothers, I won't treat you bad. Go home and think it over. If you agree, give me an answer tomorrow morning. How about that?" Cheng Shaojiu said seriously.
"Alright!" Wei He nodded.
