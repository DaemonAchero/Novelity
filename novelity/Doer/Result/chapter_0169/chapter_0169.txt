Xuanjing City. Central Ring District. Baisheng Tavern.
"So, a few days ago, the Red Sight Army surrounded and wiped out the Tianyin Sect. Most were killed or injured. Of the famous Nine Sons of Tianyin, only a few got away. It was a complete bloodbath. Horrible to look at."
"As for those remaining Nine Sons, they should just call them the Nine Fools of Tianyin! Commander Yuchi Zhong of the Red Sight Army clearly set a trap to slaughter them all. Yet, every single one of them went there bringing only their primary disciples, and they got wiped out in one single sweep!"
"That night, the wind blew through the pitch black dark, and heavy rain poured down like mad..."
"Old Yu, there wasn't a single drop of rain those days. Can you at least make your lies sound real?" A man couldn't help standing up to cut off the old storyteller.
Old Yu was a healthy old man who had lived in Xuanjing City for a long time. His origin was a mystery. He always loved to boast and tell stories in taverns and wine shops.
He was over seventy years old. His only joy was hunting for rumors and strange tales, then broadcasting them in public to gather a crowd and feel good about himself.
Many times, his wild mouth offended people. But even if he offended powerful, rich families, he never got into trouble.
Over time, everyone knew in their hearts that this old man had strong backing behind him.
"Lies? When have I, Old Yu, ever lied to you? Let me tell you, there was indeed no rain for the Tianyin Sect that day. But the rain inside human hearts? Now that is the real heavy rain. A flood! A downpour! It could raise the water level of the Jing River outside the city by a huge mark!"
Old Yu wore a simple gray cloth robe and a strange gray-black skullcap. He held a brass gong in his hand, looking quite out of place.
"Stop babbling and move to the next part! How did the rest of the Nine Sons actually escape? That Shangguan Ji is known as the number one fighter in Xuanjing. What happened to him in the end? How did he do against Commander Yuchi Zhong?" A large man sitting nearby shouted to urge him on.
The people around them were all waiting with high hopes. They wanted to know who was truly the top fighter in Xuanjing after Yuchi Zhong and Shangguan Ji fought again after so many years.
"Speaking of Shangguan Ji, we have to talk about something from the past," Old Yu sighed. He picked up a cup of wine from a table next to him and took a heavy gulp.
He let out a comfortable breath, picked up a fried peanut, and popped it into his mouth.
"Shangguan Ji... people call him the Fire-Eyed Golden Ape! That nickname was earned with real fists back during the Five Sects Tournament.
Word says that when he pushes his Tianyin Nine-Strike Art to the absolute limit, his eyes turn burning red, and his whole skin turns a faint gold. He moves like the wind, quick and agile, just like a giant ape."
He paused for a moment, then continued, "Back then at the Five Sects Tournament, the battle at Tianhe Peak set the power and status of the five big forces in Taizhou. The Tianyin Sect used that victory to press down on our Xuanjing City and became the top power on this land.
Sadly, ever since the old sect leader fell sick and bedridden, the Tianyin Sect lost the strength to stand against the other four sects.
The Fire-Eyed Golden Ape is strong, but he is not strong enough to hold up an entire sect on his own. So over these years... Shangguan Ji had to live a life of constant yield and hard struggle. It was all because the Tianyin Sect held a position it no longer had the raw power to match."
"Why do you talk so much trash? We want to hear who escaped that night! I heard the Red Sight Army lost a lot of men," a young wealthy man called out loudly.
"Heh heh... as for that part..." Old Yu smiled slyly and looked around. The crowd was slowly getting drawn in by the secrets he was sharing.
Seeing more and more people gathering, his mood grew better. He stopped keeping them in suspense.
"That night, under a dark, windy sky... the Red Sight Army sent out the Gold Penalty Division, one of their three elite units, to hunt them down and kill them."
"Speaking of the Gold Penalty Division, their commander Zhou Xingtong is a top fighter from the Zhou family, known as the Bronze Man of Xuanjing. He was born with skin like bronze, safe from blades and spears, untouched by fire or water. On top of that, he was born with immense brute force."
"The Zhou family, the You family, and the Wang family each have their own strong figures. But in recent years, Zhou Xingtong has pushed down all three families. Even the eldest son of the You family, You Rong, openly said he did not want to fight him.
Yet, a top fighter like this led several martial master assistants and over five hundred elite troops of the Gold Penalty Division..."
"Pure trash! Can you be a bit more real, Old Yu? The Gold Penalty Division only has one thousand men in total. Commander Zhou only brought two hundred men that night! My cousin is an officer left behind at the camp!" Someone below immediately shouted back to fight his words.
"Fine, fine. I, Old Yu, got it wrong. It was indeed two hundred men. But even with so many men, mixed with strong soldiers of the second and third blood stages, led by martial masters... they were still slaughtered by just one branch of the remaining Tianyin Sect! Blood flowed like a river, limbs were torn apart, and bodies covered the ground. It was horrifying..."
In an instant, everyone's mood was pulled in by his words. They all urged him to keep going.
They all wanted to know what really happened that made a top fighter like Zhou Xingtong return in failure and lose so many men.
"To talk about this matter, we must mention a branch of the Tianyin Sect that people were not familiar with before," Old Yu smiled and said. "That is the Wanqing Yard."
At a table in a corner of the tavern.
A few men and women in fine silk clothes sat quietly, listening to Old Yu's story. Not only were they wearing rich clothes, pearls, and jade, but several powerful guards with strong presence were standing by their side to protect them.
The leader was a man with a small mustache.
While listening to the story, the man had a gentle smile on his face.
"I did not expect to run into such an interesting thing during this trip back home. The Tianyin Sect has been an old power here for many years. To think such a big thing happened to them."
"Indeed. When I was little, I often saw halls of the Tianyin Sect all over the streets. Back then, you could see their people everywhere, wearing the same uniform.
I remember over ten years ago, their uniform was white mixed with gray, with a white mark on both arms. Different marks meant different branches," a woman sitting beside him said calmly.
"In the blink of an eye, the world changes. The rise back then, the fall today. Highs and lows follow each other, always like this." The man nodded, feeling a little sad for them.
"Is brother thinking about what happened to Qinglin?"
"I feel a bit touched, but that matter has passed, so there is no need to think more about it. Returning this time, we are only here to rest and recover. We won't care about other business," the man said with a light laugh.
"That is good. Right now, the Governor is pushing new rules. He opened schools, set up the Industry Bureau, built up the Literature Bureau and the Craft Bureau, and set up training offices. His moves are huge. For you to lead one whole department shows how much he trusts and expects from you," the woman said seriously.
"It is hard. Starting a new department with no men and no money, only given the power to act as I see fit. How can I pull it off? What gives me headaches every day now is where to dig up talent and where to invite men of both skill and virtue," the man shook his head.
"Can't you also look for talent while taking your break during this trip home?" The woman smiled.
"That is true. It is just that I have not been back for many years, so I don't know how things are in Xuanjing City today."
"How hard can that be? Just listen more and look more. Listening to all sides brings clarity."
The two looked at each other and smiled, saying no more.
By this time, Old Yu was reaching the key point of his story. More and more people gathered around him.
SLAP!!
Old Yu struck his brass gong hard, making a loud ring.
"Look at Wanqing Qing, the head senior sister of Wanqing Yard! She threw out a single palm, shaking the earth and sky! Her giant hand, wide as a fan, was heavy as an elephant's foot, pressing down hard onto Commander Zhou Xingtong!
At the same time, Wanqing Qing shouted out loud: Do you know why the Rain-Covering Power of my Wanqing Yard is called Rain-Covering Power?"
"Why?" Everyone asked in curiosity.
"Covering rain and turning clouds, wild flames reaching the sky! Of the nine strikes of Tianyin, only Wanqing stands supreme!" Old Yu called out, holding his head high as he recited.
"Good!"
"Great! Here is a reward!!"
The people around were shocked by this big momentum. They opened their purses and threw out reward coins one after another.
Old Yu continued, "Now, Commander Zhou Xingtong tried his best to hold on, but he was still no match for Wanqing Qing. Though Commander Zhou was ten feet tall and built like an ox, he was still not as strong as Wanqing Qing!"
"Wanqing Qing was small in body, but one palm strike from her carried the force of an elephant and five bulls! It was beyond compare, impossible to block!
She let out a wild roar and threw out strike after strike with both arms. She smashed the trees in front of Black House Mountain until they burst, dirt flew everywhere, and rocks shattered. Even a thousand-year-old giant tree was broken by a single punch from her!"
Pfft.
A group of women sitting at a nearby table heard this and could not help letting out a low laugh.
However, this laugh was too quiet, and Old Yu's voice was too loud, so no one else heard it.
Yet, the table of rich-clothed people nearby heard it clearly.
The man looked over toward them and caught sight of the woman who was looking down and laughing softly.
The woman had long, narrow eyes, pale fair skin, an oval face, a small nose, and red lips. Her vibe was attractive, mixed with a hint of sharp boldness.
With just one look, the man's eyes went blank for a moment.
"Brother?" The woman beside him quickly tapped him awake. She followed his line of sight and happened to see the woman who was laughing softly.
That refined face with a touch of bold charm made her line her brows slightly.
In her memory, women with this kind of vibe were all dangerous figures.
Boldness, especially in a woman in this age, meant absolute confidence in oneself.
A man like her brother, who was honest and gentle in matters of love, could not handle a woman with such a vibe.
Thinking of this, she tapped the man again.
"I know. I understand," the man came back to his senses, nodded, and closed his eyes.
His name was Zhao Zheng, a son from a big family in the capital city of Taizhou. When he was young, he studied in Xuanjing for a time. Later, he moved to Taizhou with his family and settled down there.
After that, he passed his exams, won titles, and served as an officer in many places. Now returning home, he was called by the Governor to serve as the chief of the Industry Bureau.
He never thought that as soon as he returned, he would see...
Even with his eyes closed, the image of that woman kept floating up in his mind.
That quick look in that single moment, her laughing face felt like a blade, carving itself deep inside his heart.
Meanwhile, a fight broke out on Old Yu's side.
Someone was causing trouble, saying Old Yu was talking pure nonsense and making up lies.
The two sides started arguing right away.
"Wanqing Qing is the head senior sister of Wanqing Yard. I saw her once from far away! She was gentle and brave, guiding her junior brothers and sisters using skill and speed. You turned a young girl into a giant monster who can run horses on her arms and smash stones on her chest? Even if you are making up a story, have some common sense, will you?!"
"Hearing a bit of rumor and then adding your own lies to twist the facts... Old Yu, you can't do this. I've seen Wanqing Qing too, she is a very gentle girl," a scholar nearby shook his head and spoke up for them.
"That's nothing! The battle at Black House Mountain was clearly won because the hidden Wei He made his move to kill Zhou Xingtong, You Muxuan, and the rest!
With his own power alone, he poisoned over three hundred men! In the end, both he and Zhou Xingtong were hurt, and both sides fell back! Right now, the Red Sight Army has set up guard posts and watchtowers at Black House Mountain just to watch out for this Poison Master Wei! Yet you turned real facts into this trash?" A muscular woman stood up and spoke in anger.
Once these words came out, the crowd grew even more interested, breaking out into loud noise.
Even storybooks wouldn't dare write like this! One person poisoning over three hundred men, and elite soldiers of the Red Sight Army at that! Led by the Bronze Man Zhou Xingtong himself, with several martial master assistants!
This was... simply insane!
