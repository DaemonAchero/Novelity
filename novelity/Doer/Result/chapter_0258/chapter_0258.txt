Wei Chun took a breath and tried to calm her heartbeat. When she passed by earlier, she had caught a glance at the portrait. It was her master, Old Man Zhuang.
Someone is looking for Master, and they're working with the guards.
She was terrified she’d be caught, but she kept telling herself that the Star-Worship Cup was buried far away. As long as she stayed cool, they would be safe.
Knock! Knock! Knock!
"Anyone home? Open up!"
"Who is it?" Wei Chun called out, her voice tight.
As she stood, her stomach dropped. The voice sounded just like the guards on the street.
"Guards! Open the door!"
Wei Chun panicked, but she forced herself to walk over. Behind her, her parents, Wei Tang and Li Cui, came out. They looked terrified, holding hands tightly.
Seeing them so scared made Wei Chun harden her resolve. She couldn't mess up. If she did, her whole family was dead.
She opened the door.
A guard in a red-and-black uniform stood outside, hand on his sword. He snapped open a scroll. It was Old Man Zhuang.
"Seen him?"
Wei Chun hid her terror and looked at the drawing. "No, sir. What did he do?"
"None of your business!" The guard barked, turning around to leave. He was annoyed at having to search house by house in the freezing cold.
As he moved to the next door, the heavy weight on Wei Chun’s chest finally lifted.
"Chun'er, what was that about?" Wei Tang whispered.
"Nothing, Dad. Just looking for a criminal," she said, shutting the door.
A massive manhunt swept through Feiye City. The authorities put up a huge reward for any info on Old Man Zhuang's past movements.
Half a month passed. They traced where he had lived and walked, but the Star-Worship Cup was nowhere to be found.
The fake cup had already been destroyed by Taoist Baiguan.
Crash!
In the Feiye City Enshrinement Hall, a tea cup shattered against the floor.
Bai Honghou, a high-ranking master in a red robe, glared at Taoist Baiguan.
"Baiguan! You're searching for clues, fine! But why are your people digging up my family's backyard? You think the Bai family is hiding criminals?!"
Bai Honghou was a powerhouse from the Qingdu Sect. After the Hong family took over Feiye City, his sect had merged into their ranks. He wasn't the strongest, but he represented most of the red-robed masters here.
Taoist Baiguan sat quietly. "You got a problem with that?"
"I—"
Slick.
A sword tip pierced straight through Bai Honghou's right palm. Blood sprayed everywhere.
"Argh!" He screamed. Before he could even draw a weapon, he was already bleeding.
The blade pulled back, dripping red.
"You're too loud," Baiguan said, sheathing her sword. She closed her eyes, acting like nothing happened.
"Report!"
A guard burst inside just as Bai Honghou was clutching his bloody hand, teeth gritted in rage.
"Someone claimed the reward! They saw someone meeting Old Man Zhuang!"
Baiguan snapped her eyes open. "Who?"
"A street beggar. He said every few days, he saw a giant woman enter that ruined house."
"Bring him in!" Bai Honghou barked. He couldn't wait to get rid of this crazy woman. He couldn't beat her in a fight, and her background was too strong. He just wanted her gone.
Moment later, a filthy, reeking beggar was dragged in. He fell to his knees.
"You saw someone?" Baiguan asked coldly.
"Yes! But is the money real?" The beggar yelled, risking his life for a payday. He was starving anyway.
Baiguan threw a heavy purse at his feet. "Who was it?"
"A huge, muscular woman. Tall as a tower! If I see her again, I'll know her!"
"Good!" Baiguan smiled. Tall, muscular women were rare. Narrowing it down would be easy. Finally, a real lead.
"Listen up," Baiguan ordered, looking down at Bai Honghou. "Find every tall woman near that house. Bring them all here for him to check."
Bai Honghou swallowed his rage and bowed his head. "Understood."
Heavy snow fell over the main road.
A plain white carriage moved slowly toward the gates.
"Feiye City... my old home," Wei He muttered, looking out the window.
The carriage looked normal, but the escorts were scary. Two giant female martial artists, the brutal monk Sandu, the needle master Bai Dou, and a dozen elite guards followed behind.
Wei He looked at the stone city walls. It was much smaller than Xuanjing City, but it brought back memories.
"My childhood is here. Where I first learned to fight. I miss it," he sighed.
"Can you still find your old friends?" Wan Qingqing asked gently.
"They probably fled to the capital years ago. Everyone was starving back then. Staying meant dying." Wei He looked closely at the busy streets. "Looks better than it used to, though."
"Let's visit your old house first," Wan Qingqing smiled. "Maybe you'll bump into someone."
"Yeah," Wei He nodded. "Besides, to find Zhenqi and my second sister quickly, I need the local bosses to work for me."
"What's the plan?"
Wei He stood up and stepped out of the carriage. Cold wind blew his hair.
"When I was young, the powerful people here were like unbeatable mountains to me. Now that I'm back, I wonder if those mountains are still that tall."
He looked at the city gates. "I want to see if anyone in this city can still stand in my way."
Wan Qingqing smiled and picked up her jade flute. "A grand return?"
"No. Just settling old debts."
Swish!
Wei He shot into the air like a bolt of lightning, vanishing into the snowstorm toward the city.
"Madam... should we follow?" a female disciple asked.
"Slow down and keep moving," Wan Qingqing said softly.
She knew why he went ahead alone. He was going to clear out all the threats first so nobody would attack her carriage or put her unborn child in danger.
As for how he would clear them out? She trusted him completely.
Inside the Grand Eagle Tower.
A middle-aged woman in a white Taoist robe walked in, holding a black sword.
On the first floor, Hong Daoyuan sat waiting, flanked by two masked bodyguards. Incense filled the room, and red prayer ribbons fluttered under the roof.
"Daoist Baiyi from the White Jade Sect," Hong Daoyuan said, staying seated without moving an inch. "Welcome to my city."
"We agreed on thirty rare pills for the Star-Worship Cup," Daoist Baiyi said coldly. "Is the deal still on?"
"Your sect is one of the strongest in the province. I trust your word," Hong Daoyuan smirked. "But finding it takes time. I can't dig up the whole city overnight."
"That cup holds big secrets. Don't play games with me," she warned.
"Watch your mouth," Hong Daoyuan's eyes went dark and cold. As the ruler of this city, he feared no one. "Some threats are dangerous to say out loud—"
BOOM!
The wall behind him exploded.
A dark figure burst through like a giant bat, bringing a terrifying shockwave.
A heavy hand reached straight for Hong Daoyuan's face!
"Enemy!" The bodyguards drew their swords, slashing with all their strength.
Hong Daoyuan grabbed his spear to block while leaping back.
SNAP!
Swords broke. Spear snapped.
The giant hand slammed onto Hong Daoyuan's face and smashed him straight into the floor.
CRASH!
The wooden floor shattered into a massive crater. Dust and stones flew everywhere.
The intruder stood by the pit, his black cloak fluttering. He turned his eyes toward the stunned Daoist Baiyi.
"Where is Hong Daoyuan?" Wei He asked quietly.
He needed a local boss to find his family. The Hong family was the biggest power here, so they were his first stop.
Besides, he really wanted to test if one of his old "mountains" was actually strong at all.
