The Black Water Gang? A while back, they were wiped out and taken over by the Angle Snake Gang. So now..." Cheng Shaojiu shook his head, showing he couldn't help with that one.
"As for the other guy, that thug named Chen Biao, I actually heard something about him from my cousin."
Cheng Shaojiu came from a rich, powerful family and was well-informed. In the towns around Feiye City, he was considered a big deal.
On top of that, he was generous with his money and loved making friends. Because he knew people from all walks of life, he always had the latest news.
"What did you hear?" Wei He asked in a deep voice.
"Just that the street thugs over in Rat Alley have all joined this new Angle Snake Gang. Right now, they're expanding their territory every day, fighting and killing all over the place. It's real chaotic. But why are you asking about a small-time thug? Do you have beef with him?" Cheng Shaojiu glanced at Wei He.
"It's nothing. Thanks though. If you can keep an eye on Chen Biao for me, I'll finish your homework from last time."
Wei He offered a trade.
The main reason he could get close to Cheng Shaojiu was that this guy hated doing his schoolwork. His dad had hired a private tutor for him who gave him homework every few days. That gave Cheng Shaojiu a massive headache. He was the type who loved to play and hang out with friends; he couldn't sit still and study.
So when he found out Wei He could write homework for him, he immediately asked for help.
Surprisingly, Wei He's ghostwritten work was great and even got praised by the tutor. Even if the praise was just about the neat, strong handwriting, it was way better than anything Cheng Shaojiu could do himself. So the two of them often traded favors like this.
"Deal! I was waiting for you to say that. Say no more. Chen Biao, right? I'll keep my eyes glued to him!" Cheng Shaojiu slapped his own chest and promised.
"Thanks, Third Senior Brother." Wei He nodded.
"Wei He! Master wants you inside!" A short boy suddenly ran out from the inner house door and shouted toward them.
"Got it." Wei He put down his stone block and quickly went into the inner house.
Inside, Old Man Zheng sat shirtless while an old pharmacist applied herbal plasters to his back. The whole room smelled heavily of Chinese medicine.
Seeing Wei He enter, Old Man Zheng didn't lift his head, only flicking his eyes over. "Go buy me five ounces of Sappanwood, five ounces of Dahurian Angelica root, White Peony root, and six drams of Figwort. Pay for it first; I'll pay you back when you return."
"Yes, Master."
Because Wei He could read and do math, Old Man Zheng often gave him these kinds of errands.
Grabbing the cloth medicine bag, Wei He went out through the side door and walked down the street toward another town section.
The section where the Mountain-Turning Fist Academy was located was close to the Inner City, so it was quite prosperous. But the shop he needed to buy medicine from was in a section near the Outer City.
The dozen or so sections in Feiye City were like self-contained villages. Each had its own houses, shopping streets, and unique traits. All sections wrapped around a huge central plaza, and each connected to it. The Inner City lay beyond that plaza.
The sun had just come up. Wei He walked quickly through the streets.
The shops and stalls on the roadside weren't open yet. Only a few steam bun shops had their wooden doors half-open, with people vaguely seen carrying supplies inside.
After crossing a stone bridge between two sections, Wei He arrived at an old, run-down herb shop.
A plaque hung above it with four words: Zhao's Herb Shop.
Just as he was about to knock, a rustling sound came from the street on his right.
"Pass into Paradise, smell the incense to cross the sea..."
"Pass into Paradise, smell the incense to cross the sea..."
Someone was chanting softly, leading the way. Right behind, a huge crowd joined in, chanting together.
Wei He's heart tightened, and he knocked fast on the door.
Knock, knock, knock.
The shop went quiet. Soon, the wooden door opened slightly, and a middle-aged man wearing a black round hat pulled him inside.
"Get in, quick! The Incense Sect people are here again!"
It was dark inside the shop, with light showing only through the gaps in the door and windows.
Wei He and the middle-aged man stayed quiet, standing near the door to wait. They waited until the chanting outside slowly moved away and vanished completely.
Only then did they breathe a sigh of relief.
"These Incense Sect guys are coming around way too often lately. Who knows when this will end?" The middle-aged man let out a long breath.
"Manager Qian, do you know who those people were..." Wei He had seen these strange groups before. Even though meeting them was scary, they hadn't caused him trouble yet.
"That's the Incense Sect. It's a cult that started spreading through the city recently. Whenever they spot someone, they force them to burn incense and give money. At first, they gathered a lot of poor people and gained power. Then they used half-robbery methods to get money and split it among themselves. That's how they grew so big. I don't know why the government isn't stopping them." Manager Qian sounded helpless.
"If you run into them head-on and don't hand over some cash, they'll send people to block your door every single day. You won't be able to do any business at all."
Wei He had nothing to say to that.
He bought the herbs he needed, checked the price, packed everything up, and left the shop.
On his way back, he saw the Incense Sect crowd again from far away.
There were so many people—at least two hundred—forming a long line like a giant snake.
Right now, these people were facing off against a few shouting city guards.
The cult leaders up front were being pushed around, looking like they were about to be arrested and dragged off. Among the rest of the cult, a dangerous vibe was rising.
Wei He took one look from afar and didn't dare look again. He quickly rushed back toward the martial arts yard.
Before he got far, a sharp scream came from behind him.
"Murder!!"
Then came a mess of angry curses, loud shouts, and painful screams.
Wei He moved faster, breaking into a run.
He had only practiced the Mountain-Turning Fist for three months. He barely knew the basic moves and hadn't even done a real fight yet. If he got dragged into this, there was no telling what might happen.
He ran all the way back to the yard in one breath, delivered the herbs to the inner room, and settled the money. When Wei He walked out, he realized his back was covered in sweat. He couldn't tell if he was scared or just tired from running.
As someone living a second life, he knew better than anyone how destructive these cults could be once they grew.
"What happened to you?" Third Senior Brother Cheng Shaojiu looked at him, feeling it was strange that a simple medicine errand made him sweat this much.
"Maybe I just ran too fast," Wei He said softly. "Let's get back to it."
He walked to his spot, picked up his stone block, and went back to training.
By evening.
After finishing his training, Wei He drank his herbal soup, put on his clothes, and got ready to pick up homework to write. Going around to collect homework for cash was his steady side hustle.
Only today, he felt strangely uneasy.
The image of that long line of Incense Sect cultists kept flashing through his head.
"I need to get stronger as fast as I can." He subconsciously touched the Break-Through Bead mark on his chest.
The bead had been gathering power for so long, but it was only half black. The other half was still its original color.
After leaving the study hall, he passed by an herb shop on his way home.
Thinking back to buying medicine for his Master, Wei He had a thought and turned into the shop.
The shop was filled with a sharp, choking smell of herbs.
A middle-aged woman in her nineties, wearing a black-and-gray long dress, was pressing down hard on a mortar, grinding herbs. Nearby, an employee lazily wiped down the counter with a rag.
"Boss, do you have any ready-made recipes for daily nourishment?" Wei He asked.
"Ready-made nourishment recipes?" Hearing this, the woman fixed her hair and put down her tools. "I can tell you're an amateur right away. People get their recipes from doctors or pharmacists. Who comes straight to our shop asking for one?"
Her voice was raspy, sounding less like a woman and more like an old man.
Wei He was startled by her voice, but he quickly reacted.
"I've been feeling like regular food doesn't keep me full long enough, so I wanted to see if there's anything I can eat once that keeps me full for a long time."
"Meat then. Meat keeps you full best. I have some fresh wild game here, but it's a bit pricey. See if you want it."
The female boss turned around and had the employee bring out a thin, black strip of meat from the back room.
Thump.
She tossed the meat onto the table.
"White-Spotted Black Snake meat. Ten taels of silver per pound. Fixed price."
Ten taels...
Wei He's heart sank. It was expensive; the money he brought couldn't buy much of it.
"This is fresh snake meat. I also have wild boar, wild beef, yellow monkey meat, gold coin bugs, centipedes, scorpions..." The female boss listed off a long list of random meats. Some were dried; some were fresh.
Wei He thought it over.
He bought a small piece of each kind, adding up to a large pile. He spent all the money he had saved over a long time in one go.
When he walked out, he was carrying a bundle the size of a human head, filled with all sorts of dried and fresh meat.
Carrying the meat, he returned to his home in Rat Alley.
He started messing around with how to cook it. His second sister, Wei Ying, lived in a room near the martial arts academy, so he was the only one living in this old house, which made it nice and quiet.
After a lot of work, Wei He turned every piece of meat into paste, cooked it, and put it into different bamboo tubes as bowls.
Twelve kinds in total.
He started testing them one by one. By watching the Break-Through Bead on his chest, he checked which meat gave him the best results for his money, hoping to speed up how fast the bead filled up.
He ate two portions a day and tracked the results. Soon enough, Wei He found the meat that charged the Break-Through Bead fastest for the same weight.
It was the gold coin bug meat.
This meat kept him full for a long time and was packed with energy. It was usually used for sick people who needed deep nourishment. The price wasn't too bad either. It was slightly more expensive than the snake meat, but still worth it for now.
Once he figured out the gold coin bug meat, he went back to his daily grind of building strength.
In the blink of an eye, half a year passed.
With his steady effort, the Break-Through Bead was finally almost completely black, just a tiny bit away.
Wei He had also grown from his original five-foot-seven frame to nearly six feet tall. His body had built much tougher lines, with smooth, tight muscles joining together.
He finally passed the raw strength phase and began practicing actual combat moves. He was now officially one of those big guys fighting in the yard every day.
Reaching this step, Old Man Zheng finally started teaching him a special skin-hardening technique for the Mountain-Turning Fist, meant to make the skin on his fists thick and tough.
