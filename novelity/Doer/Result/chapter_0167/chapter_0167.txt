“You poisoned me!?” Zhou Xingtong yelled in a mix of shock and rage. He whipped his head around and glared at Wei He. How could this poison penetrate even his Detox Pearl?
“If you say so.” Wei He’s shadow flickered again. Zipping through the trees in sharp zigzags, he suddenly landed right on Zhou Xingtong’s left flank.
He slammed two quick palms into his side.
Before the man could react, Wei He shifted to the other side and struck his back with two more palms.
Then he shifted again, keeping up a relentless sequence of strikes.
Zhou Xingtong was already slowing down, and his speed was nowhere near Wei He's to begin with. Now, he couldn't keep up at all.
He spun around again and again, wildy swinging the invisible whips attached to his arms. But other than shredding nearby tree bark and bushes, it was completely useless.
One was lightning-fast, the other sluggish. And Zhou Xingtong was only getting slower by the second.
“I don’t know what you’re using to block poison, but if you think poison is all I have, then next year’s today will be the anniversary of your death!”
Wei He slammed another palm hard into the back of Zhou Xingtong’s head.
BANG!!
The massive impact exploded a cloud of fine, powdered poison from Wei He’s palm, coating the man’s hair.
At this point-blank range, he refused to believe this guy could hold out.
In the thirty-plus strikes he had landed so far, every single one had carried a different toxic powder or liquid.
He carried such a wide variety of high-purity poisons for two reasons. First, to test which poison his opponent was most vulnerable to, so he could target them next time. Second, to make it easier to mix custom poisons on the fly.
Many lethal mixtures degraded quickly. Exposure to light or air ruined their potency, so they had to be mixed right before use.
To solve this, Wei He had designed a honeycomb-like poison pouch on his back, combining his own ideas with the Nine Shadows design. It allowed a poison master to unleash their full power.
And right now, it was working.
The thirty-plus poisons Wei He had dumped into Zhou Xingtong were mostly cold-attribute or neutral poisons, specifically chosen to compound the slowing effect of his own icy force.
Crack... Crack...
Zhou Xingtong’s skin grew colder and icier by the second.
He pushed his blood and inner force to the limit, but it was useless. A massive mix of special cold poisons was seeping into his body in every way possible.
His biggest mistake was relying too much on his innate ability—his Bronze Skin and Iron Bone.
Earlier, he had concentrated most of his defensive inner force into those invisible whips. His protective barrier had been smashed by Wei He over and over, and now it was basically useless.
He hadn't cared at first, assuming his natural tough skin alone would block the attacks effortlessly.
He never expected that while Wei He couldn't break his skin, he could still paint it with deadly poison.
“Good... Very good!” Zhou Xingtong had not felt this humiliated or enraged in a very long time.
Even on the battlefield fighting against the Xiangqu Cult rebels, or facing Cult of the Disturbed Gods masters far above his rank, he had always relied on his tough body to tank through everything. At worst, he would suffer light injuries.
They might beat him, but nobody could kill him.
But now...
He suddenly felt a long-lost sense of fatal danger. It was a feeling he hadn't experienced since he was a kid, back before his divine strength and tough skin had fully developed.
Zhou Xingtong took a deep breath. He jammed his fingers hard into two blood channels right below his shoulders.
“I’ll show you what I can really do!”
He thrown his head back. The inner force across his entire body began to spin and swell at insane speeds.
“Myriad Dragon Sound!!”
His inner force condensed into three glowing rings around his throat.
Suddenly, Zhou Xingtong opened his mouth and let out a wild roar straight at Wei He.
ROAR!!!
An inhuman, beast-like roar of dragons and tigers exploded outward, covering a full 360 degrees around him.
The hairs on Wei He’s neck stood on end. He had already retreated the moment he saw the man charging up, but he was still a fraction of a second too slow.
Sound moved too fast. In the blink of an eye, the shockwave caught him. His body went numb, his blood churned violently, and his inner force shattered and failed.
ROAR!!!
The first ring at Zhou Xingtong’s throat faded away, and the second ring swelled in size. A second massive roar shattered the air.
Waves of sound hit outward with him at the center. Everything within a hundred meters was blasted by the deafening sound.
The grass, ground, rocks, and dirt all trembled violently.
Masses of leaves rained down from the trees.
As the second roar finished, the third ring instantly activated.
This time, it wasn't an outward wave. He aimed it directly at Wei He.
The sound focused into a single point.
ROAR!!!
He roared with everything he had. A cone of sonic power, far stronger than the first two waves, slammed directly into the dizzy and disoriented Wei He.
“DIE!!” Zhou Xingtong stomped the ground, rushing forward while the stun effect was still active. He launched a full-power palm right at Wei He’s head.
He closed the distance of tens of meters in a split second.
The shock effect of the Myriad Dragon Sound was far deadlier than a normal roar.
Wei He couldn't shake off the dizziness for several seconds. By the time his mind cleared, blood was dripping from his nose and ears, and he had taken internal damage.
Zhou Xingtong was now only a few meters away.
In a panic, Wei He threw himself into a side roll, leaping behind a massive tree.
BOOM!
The tree trunk was blasted completely through. Zhou Xingtong’s giant hand punched straight through the center of the tree, grazing right past Wei He’s ear.
It almost took his head off.
'Terrifying! If he had used this sonic attack from the start, I wouldn't have even had the chance to run!' Wei He’s heart pounded.
He kept dodging as the numbness and stun slowly wore off, and his speed began to pick up again.
However, the internal injuries to his organs caused by the soundwaves couldn't heal right away. His overall speed was noticeably slower than when the fight started.
Yet, after dodging a few more exchanges, Wei He noticed he wasn't the only one suffering.
Zhou Xingtong’s face was turning pale, and his attacks were losing their fierce momentum.
Clearly, that massive attack had taken a heavy toll on the user, too.
It wasn't that he hadn't wanted to use it earlier. That move hurt the user just as much as the enemy, and he would never use it unless he was backed into a corner.
Zhou Xingtong was furious and increasingly anxious.
The Myriad Dragon Sound was a special secret technique he had looted off a dead Cult of the Disturbed Gods master.
Secret techniques were different from normal cultivation arts. They didn't generate inner force, but they allowed a fighter to use their existing inner force in bizarre, unexpected ways to make up for the limitations of their main style.
The trade-off was that these techniques almost always self-damaged. To maximize killing power, they forcibly twisted the natural flow of inner force by any means necessary.
Because of this, secret techniques were rarely used. But when they were, they served as devastating finishing moves.
The Myriad Dragon Sound was Zhou Xingtong’s hidden area-of-effect crowd control move. It could stun and daze an opponent for over ten seconds.
Yet Wei He had recovered in just three seconds, completely throwing off his calculations.
He didn't know that Wei He possessed the Whale Flood Technique, which gave him a massive pool of vital blood and boosted his body’s natural resistance, allowing him to cut the stun duration drastically.
A massive pool of vital blood didn't always show flashy benefits on the surface. But at key moments, like training low-level martial arts or recovering from negative status effects, it worked wonders.
The battle fell into a deadlock once more. One chased, the other dodged.
Wei He was bleeding slightly from his eyes, ears, nose, and mouth.
Zhou Xingtong’s face grew paler by the minute. The cold poison seeping into his system, combined with the self-inflicted damage of his secret technique, was becoming too much even for his body to handle.
Yet, the worse he felt, the stronger his urge to kill Wei He became.
Wei He was clearly weaker than him in raw power. If they fought head-on without tricks, he could beat this guy to death in less than ten moves.
The problem was that the guy refused to clash directly. He just used his speed to circle around, drop poison, and use unknown methods to slow him down.
If Zhou Xingtong hadn't trained in the Flying Needle Stance and the Myriad Dragon Sound specifically to counter fast opponents, he would have been forced to retreat empty-handed long ago.
But now...
BOOM!
Zhou Xingtong snapped another small tree with a palm strike. He took a deep breath, gathering his inner force for a second Myriad Dragon Sound.
Three rings of force began to form at his throat.
Before he could finish, a violent fit of coughing burst from his lungs. The itching pain was unbearable.
The inner force shattered mid-charge, completely ruining the move.
Cough, cough, cough...
Zhou Xingtong let out a choked roar, trying to force the cough down. But Wei He seized the moment, darting in to slam three consecutive palms right onto the back of his ribcage, right over his lungs.
The combined force of the strikes, backed by the immense power of the Whale Flood Technique, instantly shattered the blood and inner force Zhou Xingtong was using to suppress the cough.
COUGH, COUGH, COUGH!!!
With his inner force disrupted, Zhou Xingtong knew he was in deep trouble and scrambled backward.
It was too late.
A sharp pain stabbed through his lungs. He couldn't hold it back and spat out a mouth full of bloody phlegm.
“AAAAARRRRGH!” He roared in pure frustration, swinging a wild palm at Wei He, but it hit nothing but air.
The two separated again, returning to a tense standoff.
Wei He landed lightly on a high tree branch. Zhou Xingtong was breathing heavily, leaning against the thickest tree trunk in the area.
For a moment, neither of them moved.
Wei He had seen that sound attack clearly. If the man used it again at close range and stunned him for too long, he would be as good as dead. He didn't dare push forward blindly.
On the other side, Zhou Xingtong was boiling with rage and caution. The guy was too fast, and the poison inside his own body was too potent. Even with the Detox Pearl, it was hitting him hard. If he hadn't undergone strict poison-resistance training in the past, he would have collapsed already.
In the dead of night, moonlight filtered through the dense canopy, casting pillar-like beams onto the ground around them.
Wei He and Zhou Xingtong stood completely still, looking like two stone statues.
Wei He was waiting for the poison to take full effect. Zhou Xingtong was waiting for his reinforcements to arrive.
SWISH!
Suddenly, a dark shape burst out from behind Zhou Xingtong, lunging straight for his back.
It was a massive, black-furred wolf, easily as tall as a grown man.
The beast was sized like a wild bull, kicking up a gust of foul wind as it pounced. It was even more terrifying than a tiger.
This was a Black Lion Wolf, an exotic beast with a thick lion-like mane, cheetah-like speed, and a bloodthirsty, fearless nature.
Smelling the heavy blood on Zhou Xingtong, its eyes had turned blood-red with hunger. It hadn't eaten in a long time.
It pounced completely without a sound from his blind spot.
SPLAT!
Before the Black Lion Wolf could even land, a massive hand grabbed its head mid-air and slammed it violently into the side of a nearby tree.
THUD.
Zhou Xingtong caved the monster's skull in with one hit. But before he could pull his hand back, a massive broken tree trunk came flying straight at his face.
Wei He didn't come close. Instead, he used his raw strength to rip up broken tree trunks and throw them like giant missiles from a distance.
His physical strength wasn't quite equal to Zhou Xingtong’s, but it was close enough.
BOOM!
Zhou Xingtong smashed the flying trunk to pieces with a palm strike, but blocking it still drained his energy and stamina.
Soon, tree trunks, wooden stumps, and heavy boulders came raining down on him one after another.
Zhou Xingtong refused to back down, grabbing whatever was around him and throwing it back.
The fight instantly shifted from close-quarters combat to a chaotic, heavy-ranged barrage.
...
...
...
A thick fog began to roll through the forest.
Wang Shaojun darted through the trees like a ghost, pulling anti-toxin pills from his pouch and swallowing them to fight off the poisonous mist.
A palm-sized spider with colorful petal patterns dropped from above, trying to bite his neck.
Before the spider could even touch him, his protective inner force slammed into it, popping the creature like a bubble into a splash of green juice.
On the ground, a few horned leopards and giant wolves smelled blood and looked up toward the trees.
Before they could even react, Wang Shaojun had already leaped across the treetops and vanished into the deep forest.
“Almost there... I'm getting close.” Wearing a mask, he tightened his grip on his two daggers.
After a brief thought, he stopped mid-way to strip off everything that could link him to the Wang Family.
He hid his jade pendant and tied up the hem of his robe where the Wang insignia was embroidered.
After double-checking that nothing could give away his identity, Wang Shaojun pushed forward at full speed, following the tracks left on the ground.
