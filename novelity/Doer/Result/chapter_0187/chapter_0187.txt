Dusk was falling.
Deep inside the woods near Qingqi County, Jiuying was running for his life. Two figures were right on his tail.
Blood dripped from his body. Their quick clash earlier had left him badly hurt. The chasers knew his moves inside out and countered everything he did. In just a few minutes, he had been caught completely off guard. If his footwork hadn't been slightly faster than theirs, his head would already be separated from his shoulders.
Jiuying stomped on a low bush, launched himself across a tree trunk, and whipped his right hand back. Three poisoned needles shot out in a tight triangle.
The two behind him were ready. They dodged effortlessly, leaving the needles to hit nothing.
A bitter, rusty taste filled Jiuying's mouth. Throwing those needles had forced his internal power, flaring up his wounds.
He ran for a few more miles, but a cold thought slowly crept into his mind.
The two pursuers were keeping up, but they weren't closing in. Several times they had the chance to corner him, yet they deliberately held back. They kept just enough distance to press him without forcing a final blow.
Jiuying wasn't a fool. You don't survive a lifetime of enemies by being stupid. Back when he made a name for himself, he had poisoned plenty of masters and pissed off half the martial world.
Are they using me as bait? he thought. If so, they must be targeting Wei He.
He was a lone wolf now. The only person around who mattered enough to be threatened through him was Wei He.
Puk.
Jiuying spun around mid-air, grabbed a thick branch, and planted himself on a fork in the tree. He turned to face them.
Sure enough, the two chasers slowed down. Because his stop was so sudden, their momentum pushed them forward a few extra steps before they could halt.
"Heh. To think a day would come where I, Jiuying, am used as a soft persimmon," he said, his voice cold. "If you think you can use me to pull Wei He out, you're dreaming."
"Whether it works doesn't depend on your mouth. It depends on how he reacts," a cold voice called out.
The Master of Guiyan Tower leaped out from the trees and landed lightly on the grass. She didn't bother denying it. At this stage, secrecy didn't matter. There was nothing Jiuying could do to change things anyway.
"It's useless," Jiuying sneered. "Wei He and I are barely acquaintances. He won't walk into a clear trap for me. You're underestimating him."
Now that he had confirmed their goal, he stopped running. The Tower Master and her two men stood on one side, while he held the other. Between them ran a small river, crossed by an ancient stone bridge. The bridge was dark, curved, and covered in moss. Its arch mirrored perfectly in the clear water below.
"Whether he comes or not isn't up to you," the Tower Master sneered again. "Look. Isn't he already here?"
She turned her gaze toward the bank.
Under a weeping willow, a towering figure had appeared like a ghost. The air displacement from his arrival set the willow leaves swaying gently. He wore a dark cloak over a muscular frame, with long black hair falling over his shoulders. Just standing there, he had the presence of an unshakeable mountain.
"We parted in a rush last time," Wei He said, looking over. "I didn't expect the Tower Master herself to come block my way."
One glance told him everything. The Tower Master and two martial masters had hunted Jiuying down, using the noise to draw him out.
"Last time, I didn't get to ask your name," Wei He said. He leaped into the air, his toes lightly tapping the river's surface before landing solidly in the middle of the stone bridge.
"Dead men don't need names," she replied coldly. She gave a subtle signal, and the two martial masters behind her began to slip back.
Their job was done. The target was here. They had seen Wei He slaughter people before, and lower-tier masters—even those focused on speed—were no match for a top-tier monster like him.
Wei He turned to face her, his expression flat. "Plenty of people want my head. Let's see if you have the strength to take it."
Behind him, Jiuying's lips parted, wanting to speak. But seeing the quick hand signal Wei He made behind his back, he choked the words down.
"Watch out," Jiuying muttered. Knowing he would only be a burden, he turned and vanished into the thick woods.
Neither Wei He nor the Tower Master moved to stop him. Once again, it was a one-on-one showdown.
"You escaped last time because I was careless," the Tower Master said, slowly drawing her short sword and pointing it at the ground. "This time, you won't be so lucky."
"You really think you can keep me here alone?" Wei He raised an eyebrow.
"Of course not."
A deep, smooth voice echoed from the side.
A figure drifted down from above like a falling leaf, landing gracefully on a piece of driftwood in the water. The newcomer wore purple robes, held a jade flute, and had long hair framing a face with a bright red mark between the eyebrows. The features looked almost feminine, save for the subtle curve of the chest and the faint scent of a woman.
"Bishui Asura from the Lishan Sect," Wei He said quietly. Having read through the records of the Tianyai Tower, he recognized the technique immediately. "I didn't expect the second-in-command of Lishan to show up."
He wasn't entirely surprised. Ever since he beat Yan Junshan to death, he knew Lishan wouldn't let it slide. A master at the peak of Organ Refining was a rare asset for any faction.
"That's not all," the Tower Master sneered, clapping her hands softly. "We know how fast you are. We came prepared."
To the left of the bridge, an old woman stepped out without a sound. She had pure white hair and eyebrows, wearing a vivid red dress. Though old, her eyes were sharp and alive, looking as if a young person's eyes had been transplanted onto an aging face.
It was Elder Ruan Qinghong of the Luanshen Sect. Known as the Thousand-Armed Poison Demon, she was an expert in both leg techniques and poison arts at the peak of Organ Refining. She was the real killer move planned for today, brought specifically to counter Wei He's poison setup.
"Three of us sending you off," the Tower Master said. "Is that enough respect for you?"
"More than enough," Wei He said calmly. "The Thousand-Armed Poison Demon was famous decades ago. I'm honored."
He didn't flinch. "Though, life and death aren't decided by reputation. We still have to fight."
The moment the words left his mouth, he exploded forward.
The Tower Master didn't expect him to strike first without another word. She raised her short sword and slashed down straight toward his head. A bright white beam of sword light cut through the air.
Surrounding the blade was a dense mesh of thread-like energy—her signature Thousand-Flaw Force. It subtlely twisted and pulled at an opponent's attack, causing them to miss while making her own strikes hit with absolute precision.
The blade fell.
Wei He twisted his body, dodging the edge while his Rain Force canceled out her twisting energy. They had fought before; he knew he was slightly faster, and their base strength was roughly equal. Last time, he had relied on his hidden Bone Force to catch her off guard.
On the stone bridge, the two blurred together. Swords, fists, and palms clashed at terrifying speeds. To an average fighter, they were just shadows.
Wei He turned on his speed burst, his massive physical strength fully unlocked. He moved like a ghost across the narrow space. The Tower Master held nothing back either, matching him move for move everywhere except in raw power.
Seeing the stalemate, Bishui Asura and Ruan Qinghong exchanged a look and leaped into the air. One with a jade flute, the other with bare hands, both striking toward the spots Wei He would have to dodge into.
Two massive waves of force slammed down.
Just as the three-way trap was about to close, Wei He stomped his foot. His force exploded downward, blasting a hole straight through the stone bridge.
He dropped through the hole, dodging the three attacks instantly. Clinging to the underside of the bridge like a gecko, he shifted his position.
Boom! Boom! Boom!
Three quick punches punched back up through the stone deck, meeting their attacks head-on. The stone bridge exploded into shards, looking like dynamite had gone off.
An ordinary Organ Refining master would have been crippled by the combined shockwave. But Wei He didn't drop into the river. He burst up through the stone on the opposite side, landing right behind the Tower Master.
Bang!
The Tower Master turned and slashed blindly, but hit nothing. Wei He floated backward, flipping through the air and landing at the foot of the bridge.
The movement defied all logic. Even Bishui Asura and Ruan Qinghong felt their hearts skip a beat.
You couldn't predict where he was going. He fought when he wanted, and slipped away when he wanted. It didn't feel like a three-on-one ambush—it felt like the three of them were taking turns trying to touch him.
"What a bizarre movement technique," Ruan Qinghong muttered. She finally understood why the Tower Master feared this man.
Terrifying strength, explosive speed, flexible footwork, and deadly poison.
"No wonder Yan Junshan died by your hands," Bishui Asura said, his voice cold. "To have this level of power at your age... if we let you grow, you'll become a disaster. You die today."
