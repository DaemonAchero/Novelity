Xiao Heng did not know that Wei He was actually even more shocked.
Ever since Wei He learned how useful quicklime was, he had spent a lot of time studying these kinds of drugs. He combined insights from Guan Ye's notes with a huge collection of knock-out powders and poisons to test them out.
By now, he had made twenty-eight different types of low-odor knock-out drugs. Yet Xiao Heng, the Human-Faced Tiger, only swayed a little before returning to normal.
Only now did the people at the nearby food stalls notice someone had fallen.
A few tried to get closer to see what happened. But once they felt the bad vibe between Wei He and Xiao Heng, they turned around and ran.
Other stall owners quickly hid in dark corners to avoid getting caught in the fight.
The busy street cleared out in seconds.
"Kid, you're pretty smart," Xiao Heng sighed. "Why not join us? Great families rule the world now, and warlords are taking over everywhere. It's the perfect time to build something big together."
"Sorry, senior," Wei He lowered his head. "I'm doing fine at the Heavenly Seal Sect. I don't plan to leave."
"What a pity..." Xiao Heng shook his head. "Then I have no choice but to beat you to death here today."
"Senior," Wei He raised his head. "Do you have money?"
"Money? A few thousand taels. Why?" Xiao Heng was confused.
"Nothing. Just deciding whether to run or fight." Wei He pulled off his official uniform top and threw away his fake sidearm.
"Haha, interesting." Xiao Heng smiled.
His family technique, the Five-Weariness Three-Injury Skill, made sure that once he struck, his enemy's internal organs would take heavy damage within ten moves.
The Five Weariness meant wearing down the five main organs: heart, liver, spleen, lungs, and kidneys.
The Three Injuries meant damaging the blood, energy, and spirit.
The highest tier of Three Injuries was mostly an exaggeration, but harming the five organs was easy for him.
As an Advanced Force master, every strike Xiao Heng sent out carried hidden force that shook and damaged organs. It was hard to spot and crept up on you. Stay in the fight too long, and you were done for.
Wei He had been right about his internal injury earlier.
If Xiao Heng had taken him seriously, or if Wei He didn't have three different Blood Refinement martial arts boosting his body, he wouldn't have just escaped with broken tiny blood vessels.
Each time a martial art hit the Third Blood level, part of the blood transformed into Mark Blood.
All three of Wei He's arts reinforced his hands. This made his hands far stronger than anyone else in his sect. That was why he could hold his ground against Xiao Heng.
Neither fighters from the Wushi Sect nor the Heavenly Seal Sect at his level had hands as strong as his.
One art gave one boost. Three non-conflicting arts boosted his fists, palms, and fingers separately.
This was why Wei He never gave up any of his three skills.
"Hahaha... interesting! Very interesting!" Xiao Heng looked at Wei He. He had never met anyone like this before.
A Third Blood fighter actually thought he could fight and beat an Advanced Force master?
"Heavenly Seal Sect, Wei He. Please instruct me." Wei He raised his hands in a fighting stance.
This time, he really had no more tricks. The drug powder earlier was all he had on him, and he had thrown it all under the cover of the chili smell.
He decided to use this chance to test his full power in a clean fight.
"Fine! I'll teach you!"
Xiao Heng stepped forward quickly. Both palms swung at Wei He's shoulders like he was going to scoop up an object.
If a regular person used this move, their chest would be wide open. But Xiao Heng kept force flowing through his chest, so he was far from defenseless.
Wei He didn't rush in blindly. He used his Flying Dragon Skill alongside his Third Blood burst to step up his speed.
Then he pushed his Mountain Return Fist and Rain Cover Cloud Gathering Skill to the limit. His blood surged, turning both fists pitch-black while nine Nine-Cloud Flowers showed up on his arms. His arms swelled up and slammed forward.
BOOM!!
A cloud of white dust exploded between them.
Before the dust settled, they exchanged more than ten fast moves. Every hit was a hard clash of force.
Wei He's two types of Third Blood force could barely block Xiao Heng's strikes.
However, an Advanced Force master had endless energy that lasted way longer than a Third Blood fighter's power.
More importantly, an Advanced Force master could launch force from anywhere on his body. Wei He could only do it through his hands.
It was like a fight where one man wore full armor from head to toe, while the other only had armored arms and was naked everywhere else. They were not on the same level.
Besides, Third Blood bursts didn't last long, and Xiao Heng's strikes were damaging his organs.
Soon, Wei He couldn't hold on. After a hard elbow block, he fell back several steps. His face flushed red, and blood began to seep out of his arm skin.
This time, he was truly hurt.
He now knew his limits.
"Stop!" Wei He raised his hand and yelled as Xiao Heng charged at him again.
"I agree to join! Senior!" Wei He shouted.
Xiao Heng stopped two meters away and looked at Wei He with a smile.
"To last this long against me, you're pretty good for a Third Blood fighter."
"You praise me too much," Wei He coughed a few times. "Senior, aren't you dizzy?"
"Dizzy?" Xiao Heng was confused.
Then he realized what was happening. He looked down at the clothes Wei He had thrown on the ground. The middle part was soaked with something that was giving off a faint smell.
His face changed instantly.
"You!" He tried to rush forward to finish Wei He off.
But the dizziness hit him for real now. The world spun around him, and he lost his balance.
Taking this chance, Wei He moved fast. He used his Flying Dragon Skill and rushed forward in a low stance.
He gathered all three forces from Five Ridge Palm, Mountain Return Fist, and Rain Cover Cloud Gathering Skill into his right hand, then slapped it forward.
The silent effect of the Five Ridge Palm worked wonders. By the time Xiao Heng noticed, it was too late. He could only gather his force to protect his body.
Thump thump thump thump thump!!
Wei He landed five palms in a row straight on Xiao Heng's waist and stomach.
The first three hits felt like striking cotton, as the force was brushed off. But the last two hits broke through the defense and smashed into Xiao Heng's waist.
Pain brought Xiao Heng back to his senses. He gathered all his power into one palm and struck back at Wei He.
BOOM!!
This was their first real clash at full strength.
Wei He's three forces twisted together like three poisonous snakes biting forward. Xiao Heng's force was like an angry rhino charging straight in.
Wei He's forces broke instantly. He flew backward and landed lightly on the ground. He stepped back several times, cracking the stone floor under his feet with every deep footprint.
He was using his Flying Dragon Skill to disperse the force.
Just then, Wei He felt a numb and full feeling in his legs. Under the pressure of that full-power hit, his Flying Dragon Skill finally reached complete mastery. Small blood vessels opened up entirely.
Wei He acted fast. He bent over to hide the changes in the Realm Breaking Bead on his chest, then focused his mind.
A surge of hot energy poured out from the bead straight into his legs.
The heat stimulated his legs, making them swell up, harden, and fill with blood.
Two new dragon marks showed up on the inside of his legs. This was the sign of breaking through to the fourth level of the Flying Dragon Skill.
The fourth level was called Crossing.
Wei He tapped his toes, and his speed shot up. He skimmed across the ground for several meters, passing right by Xiao Heng.
He was much faster now, taking Xiao Heng completely off guard.
Pfft!
Wei He landed a full-power palm strike right on Xiao Heng's injured waist, tearing it apart completely.
Still, an Advanced Force master was tough. In that split second, Xiao Heng managed to strike Wei He's arm back.
They touched and separated like lightning.
Xiao Heng turned around and ran into the dark without looking back. Even with a ruined waist, he moved fast as if nothing had happened. The life force of an Advanced Force master was truly amazing.
Wei He didn't dare stay either. He quickly cleaned up the scene.
He wiped away all traces of his drugs and poison. He threw the soaked clothes into the river, knowing the poison would wash out soon.
After finishing all this, he sighed.
Should he stay or leave now?
Wang Chong was clearly a traitor. He had brought them here for food even though he was hurt, which felt off. Wei He had already noticed strange signs back in the alley.
Putting it all together, Wei He had kept his guard up.
The problem was that he had no proof Wang Chong was a traitor.
Wei He looked at Wang Chong and Yang Xue lying on the ground.
He walked over, pulled out all the money and items from Wang Chong's pockets, and checked his injuries.
This guy was fine. His injuries were light, and the blood he threw up was all an act.
Wei He thought about it. Even without proof to get rid of Wang Chong, he should still take some payment.
He slapped Wang Chong hard in the chest.
Crack!
A loud snapping sound rang out. At least five or six ribs broke, and his internal organs took damage.
Wei He then grabbed his limbs and twisted.
With a snap, Wang Chong's arms snapped, and his legs suffered deep fractures. Fake injuries turned real.
For a martial artist, a hit like this meant he would never fully recover. His strength was ruined. He was done.
"Stop practicing martial arts. Go back home and farm," Wei He patted Wang Chong's head, sighed, and stood up to leave.
Now he understood why Xiao Heng could hunt down You family members so easily. He had an insider helping him.
And turning a Third Blood captain into an insider wasn't something Xiao Heng could pull off alone. If Xiao Heng had that kind of power, he wouldn't have been pushed so hard by the You family at first.
"Oh, right." Wei He remembered something and ran back to search Yang Xue's pockets too.
If he wanted to fake a robbery, he couldn't leave anyone out.
The money from both men added up to quite a lot. Wang Chong, who was just complaining about being poor, had over one thousand taels in gold notes.
Yang Xue had much less, barely fifty taels in total.
