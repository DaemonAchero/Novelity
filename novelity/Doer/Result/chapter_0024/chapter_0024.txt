The group laughed and talked, shifting their conversation to the Katori Cult, which had been causing trouble in the outer city lately.
They also brought up the unusual movements from Hong Family Fort outside the city walls. These were two major forces.
Cheng Shaojiu mentioned the terrifying monster his convoy ran into. In a single second, it dragged off a strong, skilled guard.
Everyone gasped, amazed and shocked by the story.
Hearing about the monster made Jiang Su remember something.
"Senior Brother Cheng," Jiang Su asked, " did you say you met that black monster alongside your junior brother, Wei He?"
"That's right." Cheng Shaojiu nodded, taking a bite of braised pig ear.
"Wei He is always following you around," Jiang Su said casually. "I've been a bit curious. His background is plain and his talent is average, maybe even weak. He's no different from the other low-level disciples. Why do you treat him so well?"
She only respected those with talent or rich family backgrounds. She could not understand why Cheng Shaojiu would lower himself to befriend someone like that.
Before Cheng Shaojiu could answer, Jiang Yan waved his paper fan and chuckled.
"Susu, you don't get it. To win someone's loyalty, you have to help them when they are at their lowest. Senior Brother Cheng is building Wei He's absolute loyalty."
"Loyalty?" Jiang Su thought about it.
"Exactly," Jiang Yan said. "Think about it. Wei He is poor, quiet, and doesn't talk much. What does he know about the world? But people like him care the most about favors and kindness. If Senior Brother Cheng spent this effort on a rich person, would he get the same devotion? Wei He would take this kindness to heart. If used right, Senior Brother Cheng might get a guard who is willing to die for him in the future."
Jiang Yan sounded very experienced. He had seen many elders in his family use the same trick, so he easily saw through Cheng Shaojiu's actions.
Xiao Ran and Jiang Su suddenly understood. Compared to Jiang Yan, their families were far less powerful, so they were not as sharp about these social tactics.
"I think Senior Brother Cheng learned this from his father," Jiang Yan laughed. "Looking at Wei He now, he seems fully devoted. The trick is almost complete."
"Now that you mention it," Jiang Su said, "my father won over his two bodyguards the same way years ago."
"My family did that too," Xiao Ran added with a laugh. "My mother brought back a servant girl who followed every single order without question. It was great until I played too rough one day and she jumped down a well. That almost scared me to death."
He paused, still feeling a bit nervous about the memory.
"Though that was a good lesson. My dad beat me up badly after that. I realized drinking and women ruin martial arts training. If you don't control yourself, you will regret it later. So I joined Master Zheng's place, stayed in this all-male courtyard, avoided women and booze, and focused on training. Turns out I was right."
Xiao Ran took a bite of food and smiled. "You see me going to parties every day, but even while eating and drinking, I never stop training. Do you think I don't know that drinking hurts progress? I even bought special herbs to boost my body."
"So your success isn't just talent," Jiang Su said with respect. "Talent without hard work wouldn't get you this far."
Then she laughed at herself. "I never had those experiences when I was young. Is that why my talent feels lacking?"
"Talent is born, nothing we can do about it," Xiao Ran smiled. "But getting a few loyal guards to protect yourself is just as good. Susu, why not ask Shaojiu for tips? Even Brother Jiang says he is good at this."
Jiang Su was proud and believed personal strength mattered most, but having a few loyal men for safety sounded great.
She turned to Cheng Shaojiu. Earlier, the others had interrupted her, so she had not gotten his answer.
Only now did she notice that Cheng Shaojiu had been silently eating and drinking the whole time, as if he hadn't heard a word.
"Senior Brother Cheng? You haven't answered my question," Jiang Su said.
"Oh, sorry," Cheng Shaojiu looked up, apologizing repeatedly. "I was just really hungry and focused on eating. My bad."
"Do you have a good method for winning people over?" Jiang Su asked again, repeating what the others had discussed.
"A method...?" Cheng Shaojiu lowered his head and went quiet.
He neatly put down his chopsticks and rested them on his spoon. Then he looked back up.
"I actually have one simple idea," Cheng Shaojiu smiled gently.
"What idea?" Jiang Su asked.
"I hope that one day, I can make friends everywhere I go. Wherever I walk, I can turn enemies into friends and solve problems without fighting."
He continued calmly, "So I don't have hidden motives toward anyone. I just treat people with honesty. I treat Wei He as a friend, so he treats me as a friend. That's all there is to it."
Hearing this, the smiles on Jiang Yan and the others slowly disappeared.
His words felt wrong to them.
Everyone knew Cheng Shaojiu was friendly with people from all social classes. But by saying he treated Wei He like a true friend, he was putting them on the same level as a poor loser like Wei He.
"Brother Cheng seems a little drunk," Jiang Yan said, his smile returning as he folded his fan. "Let's drop this topic. How about we check out the new items at the Treasure Building? To be honest, using gold leaves instead of silver coins nowadays is really annoying."
Jiang Yan stood up first and called the waiter to pay.
However, after that conversation, the three of them looked at Cheng Shaojiu differently.
They walked out in two groups. Xiao Ran and Jiang Yan walked behind, lagging a bit behind the other two in the crowded street.
Xiao Ran looked at Cheng Shaojiu's back. "I didn't know Shaojiu was so... pure."
"You mean naive, right?" Jiang Yan sneered. "There is no equality in this world. You treat people with honesty, and they backstab you. I've seen it many times."
"You've seen it yourself, Brother Jiang?" Xiao Ran asked.
"Of course. Shaojiu stayed inside the city for too long, so he thinks too well of human nature. People like Zhou Yishi and Wei He look obedient to him now, but if I offer them enough money, none of them will stay loyal to him," Jiang Yan said coldly.
"Makes sense," Xiao Ran nodded. "I believe you."
"So, Shaojiu is wasting his time. That's not how you control people," Jiang Yan unfolded his fan and walked faster. "At first, I thought he was clever. But after hearing him out... treating everyone with honesty makes that honesty worthless, just like silver coins now. If he treats the poor and the rich the same way, the poor will feel thankful, but the rich will feel insulted. Over time, rich people will stay away from him. Can a bunch of poor trash help him climb higher? How many people can he even meet in one lifetime? Who holds more power in this world, the poor or the rich? He is throwing away gold for rocks. Just watch. If he keeps this up, he will regret it."
Xiao Ran learned a lot from this. He admired Jiang Yan even more, feeling Jiang Yan was on a completely different level than Jiang Su and Cheng Shaojiu. He quickly followed him.
Cheng Shaojiu had no idea that behind his back, Jiang Yan and Xiao Ran had already decided he was not worth connecting with seriously.
...
Time passed. A week went by quickly.
The weather grew hotter. Even though it was autumn, the air showed no sign of cooling down.
As soon as Wei He got his pay, he took his second sister, Wei Ying, around the area to find a spot for a steamed bun shop.
A bun shop didn't need much space, but it needed a spot with heavy foot traffic.
At first, Wei Ying didn't know the shop was for her. Wei He told her he was helping a junior brother set up a small business. She felt envious of that brother, but she still gave helpful advice and ideas.
In the end, Wei He picked a shop based on her advice, settled the price, and bought it right away.
When she saw her name on the land title, she realized the shop was hers. She was moved to tears.
Wei He was now considered a senior disciple in the courtyard. He no longer trained directly under Cheng Shaojiu. Instead, they each took a new student to guide, which was the duty of senior disciples.
His status had changed. After a brief chat with Master Zheng, Wei Ying was allowed to leave the courtyard to prepare her new shop.
Once Wei Ying left, another disciple saw the chance and brought a new girl into the courtyard to work for free, just for food and housing. This kept the girl safe and helped the disciple get closer to Master Zheng. It was a smart move.
Those benefits were worth far more than a small wage.
However, Master Zheng had gotten used to Wei Ying's careful work. With the new girl around, he kept missing Wei Ying. Every time the new girl made a mistake, he would complain and talk about how thoughtful Wei Ying was.
When Wei Ying heard about this, she began visiting the courtyard every few days to care for the old man and keep him happy.
Because of this, many people in the courtyard started looking at Wei He differently.
Jiang Su, in particular, believed Wei He had planned everything from the start. They thought he used his sister to gain favor with Master Zheng to get extra care.
Under rumors spread by Jiang Su and others, many disciples changed their view of Wei He. They began to think he was overly scheming and dangerous to get close to.
