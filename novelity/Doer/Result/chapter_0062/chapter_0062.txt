Wei He was already halfway down the mountain.
Earlier, he noticed the copper burner, the tables, the chairs, the torches, and the inner walls were all brand new. The fort had clearly just been built.
There were no footprints on the surrounding ground either.
Underneath the cage was a pitch-black tunnel, and a strange beast smell drifted from its opening.
All these signs pointed to one specific setup: a trap.
He had made up his mind instantly and fled without hesitation.
"Stop right there!"
A group of fat men in white robes suddenly emerged from the dark forest ahead.
Two huge fat men, nearly three meters tall, led the way. Behind each of them were two smaller white-robed fat men—the same kind Wei He had killed before.
It was a narrow road. On both sides, pairs of green eyes glowed in the dark—a pack of wolves.
Wei He's sharp senses immediately picked up the truth. The blood energy inside those two giant fat men was roaring like a flood. It was insanely strong.
That level of power was far beyond the Second Blood refined stage.
The fat men all locked their eyes on Wei He.
When the two giant leaders looked at him, the sheer weight of their gaze made Wei He’s skin prickle with pain.
These two were definitely at the Third Blood refined stage!
His heart beat fast. He knew he was in deep trouble. The back of Mount Shaoyang was truly a death trap. It was a good thing he had never dared to come here before, or he would have been dead long ago.
"What are you doing going down the mountain alone?" the giant fat man on the left asked, staring at the round figure in white.
It was dark, so he couldn't see clearly. But since the person was wearing their sect's robes and was this fat, he had to be one of them.
"This disciple lost something while on patrol," Wei He replied quickly, muffling his voice. "I came back to look for it."
"You..." The giant fat man on the right looked puzzled. They were all fat, but few were as perfectly round as the guy standing in front of them.
"How did you get so round?" he couldn't help asking.
"Reporting to the elders, I was born with this body type..." Wei He acted helpless.
"You're so round, won't you just roll down the mountain instead of walking?" The giant fat man laughed out loud.
The other fat men around him burst into laughter too.
"Go on then. A signal was sent from the top just now. Find your stuff and get back quickly. That old dog Chen never gives anyone a break."
seasonal The two giant fat men led their group up the mountain at full speed.
Wei He continued walking down. After a few steps, he gradually picked up his pace, moving faster and faster.
In the end, he was sprinting.
He tore off his white robe and threw it away, exposing a ring of inflated leather bags strapped around his waist.
He opened a valve on the bag, and air hissed out.
This was special gear he had designed specifically for the Shaoyang Sect, made to help him disguise himself in emergencies. It was a bit too round, though. He would need to tweak the design next time.
The Shaoyang Sect was unique. Every member was extremely fat, making it almost impossible for outsiders to impersonate them.
But Wei He had run into them many times on the mountain. After watching them enough, he learned how to play the part.
To his annoyance, he hadn't managed to capture a living target this time. It seemed he had stumbled into an ambush set up by the Shaoyang Sect and almost got caught in the crossfire.
Still, he didn't leave empty-handed.
Wei He reached for his waist. Before leaving, he had looted most of the valuables from the fat men he had killed.
"I'll have to wait for the next chance to go up again. Bad luck this time."
However, this trip had helped him spot his own flaw.
His true foundation was still the Five-Ridge Palm, not the Mountain-Returning Fist.
Even though he had reached the Nine Clouds Flower stage with the Mountain-Returning Fist, his blood foundation hadn't fully changed. It was still rooted in the Five-Ridge Palm.
To put it simply, he was currently a Five-Ridge Palm Third Blood expert wearing the skin of the Mountain-Returning Fist.
On the outside, he looked like a Mountain-Returning Fist Third Blood master, but on the inside, he was still using Five-Ridge Palm.
When he used the Five-Ridge Palm, his blood energy flowed smooth and unbroken. But with the Mountain-Returning Fist, his body felt stiff and blocked in certain places, lowering his power.
He guessed that the force generated during the Third Blood breakthrough was different for each technique, resulting in different physical changes to his body.
Did that mean the Nine Clouds Flower he had formed was fake?
Did his Mountain-Returning Fist need another push from the Breakthrough Bead to truly fix his foundation?
Wei He felt this was the most likely reason. Ever since he reached the Nine Clouds Flower, his Mountain-Returning Fist couldn't move forward anymore. Unlike Master Zheng, he couldn't take the next step to feel out the hidden force. Something was missing.
Once a warrior reached peak Third Blood, they could push their blood energy to its limit to mirror the mindset of the core diagram, searching for that hidden force. Then, by understanding how force converted, they could practice it, master it, and complete it.
In other words, after Third Blood, Master Zheng had no fixed moves left to teach. Reaching the hidden force was different for everyone. Everyone had a different body, a different state of mind, and a different understanding of force.
According to Master Zheng, even if two masters used the exact same Mountain-Returning Fist, the force they produced would still be different in power, range, and focus.
Looking at it now, his Nine Clouds Flower had only increased his total blood energy. Without the mind match from the core diagram, his realm remained incomplete. He still needed to hone it.
Wei He walked all the way back to Feiye City. He waited in the shadows outside the city walls for half the night until the sky turned pale.
The city gates opened.
He ripped off his night suit, put on a face mask, and walked through the gates with his head down.
Back home, he got a good night's sleep before sorting out his loot. As expected, fat men were secret millionaires.
From the two fat men, he found two bags of poison powder, two tubes of poison water, nearly six hundred taels in gold notes, and a scattered pile of gold leaves and gold beans.
One of them was also carrying a partial secret manual called the Mind-Crossing Skill, which was a palm technique. It only went up to Second Blood, making it useless to him.
Wei He kept it purely as a collectible and tossed it into a hidden slot.
After a short rest, he got up and headed to the Mountain-Returning Fist Academy, acting completely normal as he practiced his fist moves to toughen his skin.
After reaching Third Blood, he didn't dare use his full blood energy or physical power, fearing Old Zheng might notice something.
Fortunately, Old Zheng didn't show up at the academy all day.
Wei He relaxed and began to carefully study the issue with his Nine Clouds Flower.
Based on his earlier thoughts, whatever technique you used to break through became your main foundation.
If you tried to practice other techniques later, their effects would be greatly reduced.
This meant that if you built your foundation on a weak technique, it would be almost impossible to fix later. You would always trail behind others.
If secondary techniques couldn't replace your foundation, you had no choice but to stick to your main path until the end.
Otherwise, with an incomplete base, breaking through with other techniques would only be harder.
As Wei He practiced his forms to adapt to his current strength, his mind kept analyzing the problem.
'Unless I give up my foundation and start all over from scratch, there's no way to rebuild it. But that's far too hard.' Only now did Wei He realize how powerful the Breakthrough Bead truly was.
He didn't need to care whether his foundation was broken or not. As long as his blood energy was full and reached the breaking point, he could break through directly.
This meant he could switch his foundation whenever he wanted.
Everything served the breakthrough. As long as he could break through, any technique would do to build up speed.
He sighed in his heart. This world was truly too unfair to everyone else.
Calming his mind, he let the thrill of his return from Mount Shaoyang fade away.
Along the way, he even pointed out a few mistakes in Ouyang Zhuang's practice moves.
After finishing his afternoon practice, he changed his clothes and walked out of the academy. Suddenly, he saw a servant in black waiting outside the gate.
Seeing him come out, the servant quickly stepped forward.
"May I ask if you are Master Wei He?"
"I am. What is it?" Wei He spoke with confidence now that his strength had grown.
"My master is from the Guan family. The eldest young lady invites Master Wei to a dinner at our residence. Please give me an answer so I can report back," the servant said, bowing respectfully.
The Guan family? Guan Ye?
Wei He's heart moved, and he agreed right away.
"Lead the way."
The servant led him to a waiting carriage nearby.
Unlike Cheng Shaojiu's old carriage, this one was much newer and far more luxurious.
Clearly, the Guan family was much wealthier.
To be able to keep a horse carriage during times like these showed just how deep their family pockets were.
The carriage moved smoothly at a good pace, quickly arriving at Red Stone Town.
Deep inside the town, it pulled up in front of a large white-walled mansion.
The mansion was massive. A sign with the name 'Guan' hung over half the entire street.
There were two soup kitchens set up by the entrance, serving porridge to the poor. Long lines had formed in front of both.
Wei He glanced at them twice before following the servant through the main gate. They walked along pathways between gardens, twisting and turning until they reached a gazebo set among artificial rocks. There, he saw Guan Ye.
Guan Ye was cooking something in a fine porcelain pot. A rich herbal scent filled the air, carrying a hint of mint that refreshed the mind.
"Come over, quickly!" She looked up, saw Wei He, and waved at him.
It was hot outside. She wore a simple light green dress that exposed her smooth, pale forearms. Her long hair was tied up into two large buns with ribbons and pins.
From a distance, with her red lips, white teeth, and glowing skin, she looked truly striking.
Wei He froze for a moment. The sunlight reflecting off her pale arms was almost blinding.
He lowered his eyes slightly, walked over, and sat down across from the porcelain pot without standing on ceremony.
"I have something good here. I called you over so we could share it. It's a rare treat," Guan Ye said in her usual casual tone.
She cooked the mixture carefully for a long time, starting with medium heat, dropping to low heat, and finally letting the leftover heat from the red coals warm it.
A maid soon brought over a basin of cool well water.
The porcelain pot was placed inside the basin to cool down.
"Do you know how much this pot costs?" Guan Ye asked proudly, pointing at the container once she caught her breath.
"No idea."
"One thousand gold pieces!" Guan Ye held up a single finger.
"That expensive?!" Wei He was startled. How many people would he have to rob to get that much money?
Bandits had been getting rarer and poorer lately. He had only earned a few hundred gold pieces today because of pure luck. This was truly...
"Good things should be shared with friends. To be honest, out of everyone in your yard, Jiang Yan is the only one I somewhat respect, and that's only because of the Jiang family behind him. People like Cheng Shaojiu and Jiang Su aren't worth my time."
Guan Ye stood up, took a fan from a nearby maid, and began fanning the porcelain pot herself.
