The two chased and dodged around the backyard. Before long, they smashed the rockery wall to pieces, leaving the whole area in total ruin.
At their level, almost everything they hit crumbled. Nothing could stop them.
Lin Shiling had not expected Wei He to break through his limit. The longer she failed to take him down, the angrier she grew, throwing one deadly move after another.
Wei He used his superior speed to dodge every strike just in time.
However, as the fight went on, he felt something strange. It seemed like a pair of hidden eyes was quietly watching him.
Thanks to the massive blood energy from his Whale Flood Art—far beyond anyone at his level—Wei He’s senses were much sharper than normal martial masters.
That was why he could pick up on that faint, distant gaze.
This made him even more cautious. He did not dare make any risky moves and could only use the fourth layer of his Rain Cover Force to keep dealing with Lin Shiling.
Lin Shiling was using a tight grappling style. Her moves were meant to catch and lock, yet every hit carried the power of a mountain-splitting punch.
What should have been a joint-locking technique turned into a heavy slap, turning her grappling style into a joint-crushing style instead.
After a few fast chases, her slower speed kept her from catching him. She grew even more furious.
Whoosh!
She grabbed a wooden support pillar in the backyard and pulled with all her force.
With a loud snap, the pillar broke off. She swung it like a massive spear straight at Wei He.
The swing covered almost the whole yard. There was no room to dodge.
Wei He leaped into the air, tapped his toes on the flying pillar, and soared up to avoid the strike.
Just as he reached the top of his jump, a sharp wind howled behind him.
A shadow shot out from the distance like a large bird, reaching a claw straight for the back of his neck.
Before the hand even reached him, a huge wave of force whipped up a wild wind that crushed down over a wide radius.
The force was so strong that even before it hit, it pressed Wei He's protective Rain Cover Force into a deep dent. Its power went far beyond anything he had imagined.
Wei He could barely breathe. Caught completely off guard, his body instinctively wanted to release its full strength to defend itself.
He had been ambushed. All his internal force was focused on his hands to block Lin Shiling, so he had very little protective force left on his body.
If that claw landed, his fourth-layer Rain Cover Force would fail completely, and his life would fall right into someone else's hands.
"Zhou Shun! How dare you!" a voice suddenly shouted.
A figure in green leaped up, stretching an arm out at incredible speed to slide right between Wei He and the attacker.
BOOM!
The two forces collided, and both fighters pushed back.
The figure in green wrapped an arm around Wei He and carried him safely down to the ground.
It was Wan Ling, the head of the Wanqing Hall, wearing a long green dress.
She had a tall, slender figure with rich curves, showing no obvious sign of explosive power.
Yet, cold, thread-like streams of force swirled around her like a protective windstorm.
Wei He stood steadily by her side, his heart still pounding in fear.
This was the closest he had ever come to death.
He looked up at the attacker. The man had a calm face, a goat beard, and wore a blue robe like a scholar.
It was Zhou Shun, the Deputy Sect Master of the Heavenly Seal Sect.
"Zhou Shun, have you no shame, ambushing a junior?" Wan Ling’s chest heaved with rage, her face flushed red.
"As a Deputy Sect Master, you don't set an example for the nine halls. Instead, you sneak around to attack a member of my Wanqing Hall. Even if we take this to the Sect Master, we are in the right!"
"Wan Ling, I was merely testing the boy's reactions," Zhou Shun said calmly.
His son, Zhou Yugui, had been missing all night. The last lead Zhou Shun found showed that his son had come to this branch to handle a situation. In the end, not only did his son vanish, but several other martial masters from the Night Hawk Tower and Fushan Hall had also disappeared.
Even for the Heavenly Seal Sect, such a massive loss had to be investigated to the end.
Martial masters were the core strength of any faction.
Without the top leaders taking action, these martial masters were the ones who held down entire territories. They were completely indispensable.
Now, three martial masters from the sect were gone, along with two from the Night Hawk Tower. This was no small matter.
"My son Yugui and two martial masters from Fushan Hall went missing near here. Last night, they were drinking at the inn right across from this branch. I came here to ask this young man if he saw where they went," Zhou Shun said in a flat tone.
Wan Ling froze for a moment, then quickly realized what was going on. Why would they be drinking across from her branch in the middle of the night? Their intent was obvious.
Still, the disappearance of so many martial masters was indeed a major event.
She turned her eyes to Wei He to ask him.
Wei He put on a look of shock and alarm.
"Three martial masters went missing near me? Senior, I have been inside the branch the entire time. Apart from being attacked earlier, I haven't met anyone else. You don't actually think I attacked those three martial masters, do you?"
Wei He said earnestly, "Setting aside whether I even have the power to harm three martial masters, even if I did, wouldn't they have had the chance to run or scream for help?"
"Perhaps you had accomplices," Zhou Shun replied calmly.
"Does Deputy Sect Master Zhou really think someone of my weak strength is qualified to partner up with experts of that level?" Wei He asked back.
Zhou Shun also felt it was unlikely. He stared closely at Wei He for a moment, then without a word, he leaped up and vanished over the distant rooftops.
By now, the morning light fully filled the sky.
Wan Ling watched Zhou Shun leave and let out a sigh of relief.
"Things are changing in the sect. Wei He, pack your things right now and go to Hansheng. I will go ahead first."
"Yes!" Wei He nodded quickly. He knew something big must have happened to make Hall Master Wan Ling rush over like this.
The black-robed men who attacked last night were another sign that something major was going on in the Heavenly Seal Sect.
Wan Ling leaped into the air. The speed boost of her Rain Cover Force showed its true power—she moved so fast she became a blur, far faster than Zhou Shun. In an instant, she disappeared over the rooftops.
Wei He felt a chill in his heart. He knew that even if he used all his strength, he could never match that speed.
He did not know her exact realm, but it was far beyond what he could reach right now.
He looked down at Lin Shiling, who was still standing in the yard.
"Senior Sister Lin, forgive me for speaking bluntly, but could your brother's disappearance be connected to Deputy Sect Master Zhou's son? Why did they vanish at this exact time and not earlier?
Besides, think about it. Our Wanqing Hall is weak. Even if we had the strength, we wouldn't dare provoke both Fushan Hall and Deputy Sect Master Zhou at the same time."
"..." Lin Shiling did not truly believe Wei He could kill her brother either. She had only thought Wei He might be the closest person to the truth, so she rushed here to catch him first.
Yet, after their fight, she had failed to take him down quickly.
"Wait! Didn't you shatter your inner power before? Your force doesn't look like someone who lost his strength," she suddenly realized.
"You arrived at a bad time, Senior Sister Lin. Just a few days ago, I was lucky enough to break through to the fourth layer of the Rain Cover Force. I have now made up for part of the force I lost," Wei He said in a deep voice.
Lin Shiling felt this made sense. She nodded slightly and was about to speak.
Suddenly, her expression shifted. Her eyes widened, and her mouth opened slightly.
Seeing her face, Wei He knew something was wrong. He spun around immediately.
A figure in blue was standing silently behind him, reaching out with a hand to grab him.
The move looked simple and without form, but it pushed a vast wave of swirling air forward like a solid wall, rushing right toward Wei He.
It was impossible to stop, and impossible to dodge.
Wei He felt surrounded by raw force. The energy mixed with the rushing air, locking his body in place as he tried to move away.
The protective Rain Cover Force around his body rapidly broke apart under the heavy pressure.
Wei He held back the urge to use his Whale Flood Art. He also did not use the freezing trait of his Rain Cover Force. He simply gathered all his force to block the spot where the attack was coming from.
He was gambling. He was betting that the man would not dare kill him in broad daylight, right in front of Lin Shiling and so many sect members.
Zhou Shun's palm came to a sudden stop right in front of Wei He, less than an inch from his chest.
The heavy force and air current slammed hard into Wei He's body.
BANG!
With a dull thud, Wei He flew backward and crashed hard into the broken rockery on the ground.
The rocks shattered, kicking up a large cloud of dust.
Wei He almost threw up a mouthful of blood, but he forced it back down. He knew right away that he had taken a serious internal injury.
"Wei He, you are very calm. You think you did everything perfectly, but unfortunately, you left a clue at the scene that you cannot hide. A deadly clue," Zhou Shun said coldly.
Wei He felt a shock in his heart. He quickly went over how he had cleaned up the scene, thinking carefully about every detail.
At the same time, he kept a look of confusion and anger on his face.
"Deputy Sect Master Zhou still refuses to believe me?"
"I am giving you one chance now. Tell the truth. Otherwise, even if your master Wan Ling comes back today, she won't be able to stop me from killing you." Zhou Shun's face was calm, but his eyes grew sharper and sharper.
His son had gone missing right near here, and Wei He was the only person who might know something. Therefore, he was certain that Wei He was hiding a secret from him.
"Deputy Sect Master Zhou, I truly do not know! Even if you beat me to death, I still won't know! I was attacked myself last night. You are blaming me, but I don't even know who to ask!" Wei He said in a heavy voice.
He simply stood up and spread his arms wide.
"I know I am innocent! If Deputy Sect Master Zhou still doesn't believe me, then go ahead and beat me to death. I, Wei He, will never fight back!"
He raised his head and took a deep breath. All the poison bags on his body were ready. The moment the man attacked, he would blow them all up to create a poisoned area. At the same time, his Whale Flood Art was ready to burst.
"I want you to know that even if I had the power to touch your son, I would never do such a thing to a fellow member of our sect!
Since the day I was born into this world, everything I have done has been clean and honest! My conscience is clear!"
"Good!" Zhou Shun was slightly moved. "Do you dare swear to me in the name of Taishi?"
"I, Wei He, swear in the name of Taishi that I have done nothing to harm Master Zhou Yugui. If I break this oath, may lightning strike me down, and may I die a horrible death!"
Wei He swore the oath without a second of hesitation.
As a practical person who had even chopped up and burned the statue of the Taishi ancestor in a temple for firewood, why would he care about a simple oath?
Zhou Shun looked deeply at Wei He. He had seen many people in his life. When most people did something wrong, they would instinctively show signs of guilt.
That was why he had lied about the deadly clue earlier, trying to trick Wei He.
The result did not change.
Then, he launched a sudden attack to put Wei He under a deadly threat and force the truth out of him, but the result remained the same.
Finally, he forced him to take an oath.
Swearing in the name of the Taishi ancestor with such a harsh curse was serious, yet Wei He had sworn instantly with an open and honest look.
In all his years, Zhou Shun had seen fewer than five people who looked as open and upright as Wei He did right now.
"Fine. I will trust you this once. If you find any clues about my son Yugui, remember to tell me right away. You will be rewarded well!" Zhou Shun said in a deep voice.
In truth, he did not believe Wei He had the power to kill Zhou Yugui either. Right now, he still thought Zhou Yugui was merely missing.
"Brother Zhou is a senior in our sect, and a good friend to my Senior Sister Wan Qingqing. If I find any clues, I will surely report them!" Wei He said firmly.
"Also, if I may speak freely, Brother Zhou is very strong. Normal martial masters are no match for him. Senior, you should look for the small group of experts who can defeat him easily without giving him a chance to run or call for help," Wei He added seriously.
Zhou Shun nodded slightly.
That was indeed true. Turning his thoughts this way and using the process of elimination, there were very few experts near here last night who could easily defeat Yugui.
Eliminating the rest made it easy to narrow down the target range quickly.
"It seems I blamed you by mistake. So, in your view, what is most likely to have happened to Yugui?" he asked seriously.
"That depends on why Brother Zhou came here, whether he had companions, whether he had trouble with those companions, or if any top experts passing by last night made contact with him," Wei He said quickly.
Zhou Shun was instantly left speechless. He could hardly admit that his son had come here just to take over this branch from Wanqing Hall first, could he?
Such things could be done in the dark, but bringing them out into the open violated the rules.
Even though Sect Master Shangguan Ji was a quiet man who rarely interfered, he still did not allow anyone to break the rules in public.
