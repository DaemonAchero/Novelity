Returning to the branch, Wei He felt light and relaxed. Suddenly, everything around him looked much better.
He slept deeply through the night. Early the next morning, Lin Xiaoxiao brought her younger brother over to pay their New Year respects.
Outside the branch gate, Wei He had someone bring out a long string of red firecrackers. The string stretched along the ground before being lit with hot charcoal.
The loud popping and crackling instantly caught the attention of everyone nearby.
Local kids ran out to watch the excitement, while adults rushed after them to keep them at a safe distance.
Not far away, a few martial arts masters in fine robes were passing across the rooftops. The sudden noise startled them, forcing them to pause their light-foot steps for a moment.
Lin Xiaoxiao covered her ears, her pretty face watching the exploding fireworks with a hint of nostalgia, lost in her own thoughts.
She looked much fuller and healthier than she did before making her deal with Wei He. Her complexion was bright and fresh.
Standing beside her was a boy about half her height. He wore a tiger-skin hat and heavy clothing, making his round face and round body look like a green rubber ball from afar.
When the noise finally died down, Wei He cupped his hands toward the neighbors.
"Sorry for the noise! Happy New Year!"
"Master Wei is too polite!"
"Master Wei, we rarely see you around. Stop running up to high places all the time! We all want to visit you, but we can never find the chance," someone joked.
"Work has been piling up. Yubei Town has seen a lot of trouble lately, so I've been busy from dawn till dusk. Let's definitely get together when I find time!" Wei He laughed.
Of course, he had no intention of wasting time socializing with them.
These neighbors were mostly wealthy merchant families. They only wanted to get close because they saw he was older and still single, hoping to climb the social ladder through him.
At his current level, average people no longer mattered to him.
In truth, money and regular beast meat could only buy the loyalty of Third Blood fighters at best.
Beyond that level sat Martial Masters. Armed with protective Qi, normal weapons could not even scratch them. They could crush ordinary soldiers with ease.
No matter how many commoners gathered, they were just sheep to a Martial Master. Only trained martial artists and military formations posed a real threat.
Basic resources held little value for people at this level. After all, a Martial Master earned as much every year as a wealthy family.
Hearing Wei He's words, the neighbors refrained from pushing further.
The Heavenly 印 Sect's Wanqing Courtyard only had four Martial Masters. Even though rumors claimed Wei He was the weakest because he had lost his power before, he was still far stronger than a Third Blood fighter in the eyes of regular people. He was still a true Martial Master.
After exchanging a few quick pleasantries, Lin Xiaoxiao and her brother presented their New Year gift. It was a bright red paper box decorated with patterns of lucky deer, egrets, and mountain spirits forming a large circle.
"We know each other so well, why bother with formalities?" Wei He smiled. He had a worker take the box and pointed toward the door. "Come inside first."
Lin Xiaoxiao smiled without speaking. She was not worried about offending him, as they both knew each other's temperament well.
Inside the branch, Zhuang Ping had already set up the feast.
The courtyard was mostly quiet. Most members had returned home for the holidays, leaving behind only the orphans. They set up several big tables in the yard to celebrate together.
After drinking a round of toasts with his men, Wei He walked back to Lin Xiaoxiao's table.
"How is business lately?"
"It's fine. The small issues were already cleared up thanks to you," Lin Xiaoxiao answered briefly.
"The city might get messy soon. Be careful, and don't hesitate to spend money if it settles trouble," Wei He warned her.
"Understood," Lin Xiaoxiao nodded.
The balance in their relationship had shifted the moment Wei He became the branch leader. Backed clearly by the Wanqing Courtyard, his position removed almost all of her business troubles.
"Also, find more sources for your perfume raw materials. Don't rely on just one route, as prices might swing soon," Wei He added.
Lin Xiaoxiao kept nodding, listening closely like a student listening to a teacher.
After a moment, she suddenly spoke up. "Brother Wei, the Purple Bamboo Gathering is starting again this year. Do you have anyone good to recommend?"
"The Purple Bamboo Gathering..." Wei He chuckled. That was the exact event where he first met her.
"By the way, what happened to that genius who joined at the same time I did?" he suddenly recalled.
Back then, a genius named Lin Zhao from the Gasification Courtyard had joined alongside him.
Lin Xiaoxiao shook her head. "Still stuck at Third Blood. I heard he was sent out on a mission."
"I wonder what kind of talents will show up this year," Wei He mused with slight interest.
A new genius had recently emerged in the Wanqing Courtyard. People claimed her talent was a once-in-a-century gift. She was an eccentric girl who liked cross-dressing as a man, going by the name Sun Qi.
Her family background was mysterious and deeply powerful. However, Wei He never bothered to make contact. He was busy gathering blood energy and studying medicine, leaving no spare time for anything else.
"Are you going?" Lin Xiaoxiao asked.
"No. Besides the New Year greeting, you have another reason for coming today, right?" Wei He smiled softly.
Lin Xiaoxiao blushed slightly, but she stood up with firm resolve and pulled her brother up with her.
"Brother Wei, please check if my little brother can become your apprentice."
She was never good with words, and this was as far as she could push herself. Yet the hope and pleading in her eyes were clear.
Wei He narrowed his eyes, studying the boy in the tiger-skin hat.
"Why not join the main gate? The Heavenly Seal Sect takes in plenty of disciples, so they won't turn him away."
"He can't get in. His bone structure and comprehension are both below average," Lin Xiaoxiao said bluntly.
"Then why do you think I would take him?" Wei He shook his head. With poor structure and slow learning, what was the point of training?
Even with his own poor structure, his sharp mind paired with his secret cheat allowed him to rise. This kid had neither. He was a lost cause.
"I don't need him to become strong. I just want him to build a healthy body and live a long life," Lin Xiaoxiao said seriously. "Even an unofficial disciple is fine."
Wei He fell silent for a moment.
"Let's get this straight first. I won't teach him any Heavenly Seal Sect martial arts."
"That's fine," Lin Xiaoxiao agreed immediately.
"Alright then. Unofficial it is," Wei He nodded.
It was about time he gathered a few followers anyway. A lone fighter lacked reach, so building a circle of disciples was necessary to cover all bases.
Lin Xiaoxiao quickly forced her reluctant brother to the ground. The boy knocked his head on the floor nine times in the highest sign of respect. He then offered a cup of tea. Wei He took it, drank a sip, and finished the ceremony.
Because of their close relationship, he skipped the usual formal process of inviting witnesses and sect elders. He had no time for empty displays.
The boy's real name was Lin Yuan, nicknamed Little Tiger. After the bow, he went home to pack his things before moving into Wei He's place for training. He would spend half of every month here, starting from the very basics.
While Wei He could not teach sect moves, his mastery over multiple martial arts made basic fists and footwork easy to pass down.
Having achieved her goal, Lin Xiaoxiao took her leave.
Soon after, Zhang Lingxu and Meng Qing arrived together with gifts to pay their respects.
Then came Hu Zicheng. Terrified by Xiao You's recent downfall, he acted with total submission, treating himself like Wei He's loyal lackey.
Back when Wei He was only at Third Blood, Hu Zicheng had already feared him. Now that Wei He had mastered Force, the man lowered himself even further, visiting to discuss setting up a martial arts hall.
Later, store managers, supervisors from Yuxing Market, and various merchants under Wei He's domain arrived one after another to pay their respects.
After a long wave of visits, the place finally quieted down by the afternoon.
However, the peace did not last long before a message arrived from the main sect.
Head Wan Ling wanted him back.
Since Wan Qingqing had left, the position of Chief Disciple was open, and a replacement had to be chosen.
Wei He hurried back to cast his vote.
This time, he finally met all the Martial Masters of the Wanqing Courtyard who were absent last time.
Aside from Yao Hansheng, there were two others: Ding Xiaohe and Bai Rui. Along with Wei He, there were exactly two men and two women.
The four held a short meeting. Wei He had no interest in competing. Since he was just a newcomer who had previously lost his power, the title went to Yao Hansheng, the strongest among them.
Ever since his painful defeat against the military general, Yao Hansheng had trained in total isolation. His combat skills had grown far sharper. If Wan Qingqing had not broken through her limits, the outcome between them would have been uncertain.

Zhongling Manor.
Zhou Yugui returned once again to Shanhe Building. He had checked the site of Yan Jiuhai's ambush multiple times already.
However, this time was different. He brought along Xiahou Ji, an old detective from the magistrate's office who claimed a ninety percent case-solving rate over decades of work.
Xiahou Ji was greedy and lustful. Despite his flaws, his skills were undeniable, forcing Zhou Yugui to pay a heavy price to hire him.
Since the ambush, Yan Jiuhai had fallen into a deep coma. This caused Yan Junshan to view Zhou Yugui and his father with deep hatred, nearly attacking them on several occasions.
Taking advantage of the tension, the Lishan Sect put immense pressure on the Heavenly Seal Sect's disciples. In a short period, many sect businesses took heavy hits, causing profits to plunge.
Sect Master Shangguan had to personally visit the Lishan Sect to negotiate and cool down the conflict.
Even so, the sudden attack made Lishan's top fighters constantly squeeze the Heavenly Seal Sect in the shadows. The already fading power of the Heavenly Seal Sect slid further into decline under this pressure.
Zhou Shun and his son knew Yan Junshan was simply venting his anger. It was his own son who brought about the trouble, yet he suspected the Heavenly Seal Sect of setting a trap. Still, they were powerless to argue.
Xiahou Ji had already walked upstairs to examine the second floor carefully.
The old man stood under five feet tall, strangely short, but his sharp eyes missed nothing.
Before long, he let out a sudden gasp.
"Young Master Zhou, I found a clue."
Zhou Yugui's spirit lifted as he stepped forward.
"What did you find?"
"It's a good thing Shanhe Building was sealed off right after the attack, so no customers ruined the scene," Xiahou Ji said quickly. "According to the statements I collected, the attacker wore a large black cloak covering his whole body, paired with a mask to hide his identity.
That brings up a question: why hide so thoroughly? There are two main possibilities.
First, he wanted to prevent anyone from remembering his face.
Second, his body or identity carries distinct features that cannot be exposed.
I lean toward the second option. The attacker did not just cover his face; he wrapped his entire body. That means his physique is unusual enough to give him away instantly."
