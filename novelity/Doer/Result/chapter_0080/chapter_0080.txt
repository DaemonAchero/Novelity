When Chen Jian kept bothering Wei He, Zhou Shun was nearby. Zhou Shun pretended to talk with Yang Qingye while sneaking out another small yellow flying bug.
The little bug fell on the ground like a tiny pebble. The heavy noise of horse and ox hooves hid the sound.
Wei He suddenly felt something wrong and looked back at Zhou Shun.
"What were you doing just now?"
He narrowed his eyes, clearly sensing something.
Zhou Shun tried to hide his movements, but he kept repeating the same action after a set time. Doing it too many times made him stand out.
"N-nothing..." Zhou Shun’s heart skipped a beat. He acted confused, as if he knew nothing.
"Right. Be careful. Keep your eyes open," Wei He said with a nod, turning back around.
"Oh, okay," Zhou Shun answered quickly.
He breathed a sigh of relief and looked around, pretending to inspect the area.
Most people in the supply train were family members of the disciples. The actual martial arts academy disciples were few.
All together, there were about four hundred people.
They formed two lines to guard the supply carts, moving forward slowly with the ox carts.
Zhou Shun relaxed. After a while, he put two fingers into a hidden waist pouch again and pinched out a tiny yellow stone.
"What is that?" Wei He’s voice suddenly sounded right next to him.
Zhou Shun jumped in shock, almost dropping the stone.
"Senior Brother Wei!" He turned his head. Wei He, who was in front just a moment ago, was now standing right beside him. He was looking closely at the stone in his hand.
"I just like picking up stones to play with. It is a bad habit, sorry Senior Brother Wei," Zhou Shun explained quickly.
"Is that so?" Wei He narrowed his eyes and stared at him for a moment. He looked down at the stone, but saw nothing wrong.
He moved his eyes to Zhou Shun’s clothes.
"Junior Brother Zhou, I am a jumpy person. Since we are guarding together, do not make extra movements."
"I understand," Zhou Shun replied with his head low.
"Good," Wei He nodded. This time, he turned around for real and rode his horse forward.
Zhou Shun relaxed completely. Cold sweat soaked his back. He felt more tired than spending a wild night with girls at the whorehouse.
He pinched the stone again. He used a special method to make his inner energy vibrate slightly. Only this could wake up the stone-bug and send a signal to the Seven-Family Alliance.
This stone-bug could send out the exact positions of all Third Blood masters in the guard team. It helped the enemy find the weak spots in their defenses.
He held the yellow stone and pressed it gently against the horse’s belly to release it.
BANG!!
A black hand suddenly shot out from the side and slammed into Zhou Shun’s arm.
Zhou Shun was caught off guard. He could not fight back at all. The heavy hit knocked him right off his horse.
His whole body went numb. His eyes went wide with shock. Then he felt someone snatch the stone from his hand.
Next, someone grabbed his collar and pulled him up. He found himself looking into Wei He’s dark, angry face.
"What were you doing just now?"
"I..." Cold sweat broke out all over Zhou Shun. He could not tell him that his entire academy, the Bat Fist, had already surrendered to the Seven-Family Alliance.
"Tell me, what is this thing in your hand?" Wei He looked down at Zhou Shun with a dark face.
He had felt something was wrong earlier. Now that he checked closely, he caught him red-handed.
"I..." Zhou Shun opened his mouth to talk. Suddenly, someone rushed over from the front of the cart train.
The team was now ten miles away from Feiye City. Thick mountain forests stood on both sides, rolling on without end.
The heavy rain earlier seemed to bring some life back to the mountains. The yellowed trees in the woods were slowly turning light green.
"Senior Brother Wei," the person was Zhang Lu from the academy. He ran back quickly and glanced at Zhou Shun on the ground. He ignored him and whispered into Wei He’s ear.
"Master Zheng said if anything goes wrong, take our people and run right away. Do not go to Martial Fort. Scatter and run," Zhang Lu said in a low, nervous voice, though he tried to stay calm.
"!" Wei He’s eyes changed. He wanted to ask more.
"Master Zheng said, stay away from Flying Bear Blade," Zhang Lu added. He wanted to say more.
But it was too late.
Sharp laughter echoed from both sides of the forest. It sounded like a swarm of strange apes screeching together.
The horses and oxen pulling the carts stopped in fear, stepping around nervously.
Once the long line of carts stopped, it got jammed immediately. Getting them moving again would take a long time.
"Who goes there?!" A loud, strong male voice called out from the team.
It was one of the martial masters guarding the train.
The true strength of the guard team were those thirteen masters leading the way.
"We only kill Flying Bear Blade! Kneel down and we will spare your lives!"
A sharp laugh exploded. Swarms of dark soldiers flooded out from the forests on both sides. Mixed among them were giant soldiers, three meters tall, known as Giant-Teeth.
These Giant-Teeth were like giants. They swung heavy studded clubs, snapping small trees in half as they rushed down the mountain with loud laughter.
The strange screeching earlier came from them.
"Seven-Family Alliance?!"
"Fight back! All guard teams listen to me! The eyes are the weak spots of the Giant-Teeth, hit their..."
Before the master could finish, a dull impact cut him off.
The speaker seemed to be hit by a sudden attack.
Then came loud roars of anger and shock. A fierce battle had broken out in the area where the masters were.
Wei He’s heart beat wildly. He instantly remembered Master Zheng’s words from Zhang Lu.
He jumped off his horse and tied his travel pack tightly to his back.
"Viscera-Returning Fist, follow me!"
He growled out low. He stopped caring about the others and sprinted toward the Viscera-Returning Fist members.
Before the enemy soldiers could close in, he made it to his group.
A group of disciples were grouping together for safety, totally lost. Seeing him arrive, they immediately followed him and charged in one direction.
The cart train had fallen into complete chaos.
No words came from the masters' side, only constant sounds of heavy fighting. They were clearly locked in a hard battle.
The outer guard disciples were the first to clash with the rushing soldiers.
Looking down from above, the two black waves of soldiers looked like ants. They swarmed the long grey line of carts in the middle.
It looked like ants attacking a big white worm.
Wei He grabbed his second sister from a cart and wrapped her in a thick cloth cover. As long as it was not set on fire, normal blades could hardly cut through it. This kept her safe from bumps and hits.
Then he led the group and charged into the forest on the side.
Everywhere he looked were black crowds of enemy soldiers. His ears were filled with wild screams, shouting, and painful cries.
Wei He took the lead for the Viscera-Returning Fist group.
Jiang Su and Zhao Hong guarded the sides. Zhang Lu and the other disciples covered the back. They rushed toward the edge of the enemy line.
"Stay close to me!!"
Wei He raised his arm, using the four iron bars on his forearm to block two incoming sabers. With a swift swing of his hand, he unleashed his inner force.
The ribs of the two soldiers collapsed inward. They flew backward, dying on the spot.
As soon as those two died, four more soldiers charged at him with their blades raised, shouting in rage.
Wei He did the same thing. He raised his arm to block with the iron bars, then swept his hand across.
He killed two more with ease and heavily injured the other two. But he had no time to finish them off and kept rushing outward.
If both sides were not thick forests, he would not have left his horse.
Riding a horse on flat ground was much faster than running on foot.
Wei He held his second sister with one arm and swept his other arm around. His arm with the iron bars destroyed everything in its path. He did not even need to spend much inner energy to smash through.
But soon, he hit a big wall.
A Giant-Teeth soldier, three meters tall, stood right in front of him.
"DIE!!"
A giant black club crashed down toward Wei He’s head.
The heavy club weighed at least a hundred pounds. It whipped up a harsh wind before it even hit. The wind blew so hard that several disciples behind Wei He could barely breathe, their faces turning pale with fear.
Pfft.
Right then, a cold light shot out from Wei He’s hand. It hit the Giant-Teeth soldier right in the face.
It was a black poison dart. It sunk deep into the Giant-Teeth's left eye.
He let out a painful scream. His swing went wild, slamming into the ground to the side. Wei He and his group dodged it easily.
"Move!"
A flash of blood sprayed out. Wei He smashed his fist into the giant’s head, bursting it open.
The group quickly ran past the body, charging toward the outer edge.
Along the way, Wei He broke through several enemy lines. He relied on the iron bars tied to his arms to block weapons, saving a lot of his strength.
He could handle it, but the others could not.
In the chaos, several disciples got separated from their families. Many others vanished into thin air, and no one knew if they were lost or dead.
Roars, screams, and wild laughter filled the air.
The inner and outer disciples of the academy added up to over thirty people. Counting their families, there were nearly a hundred. But after breaking out, only fifty people remained.
Zhao Hong, the senior disciple, disappeared while trying to save his mother.
Jiang Su was hurt, but it was just a light cut on her arm, so she was fine.
Zhang Lu was not so lucky. His back was slashed twice. One of the cuts was laced with poison.
The group finally broke through the ring of enemies and ran quickly up the forest slope.
"I... I can't make it..." Zhang Lu gasped for air as someone held him up. His face was dead pale.
"There is poison... on the blade..." Despair filled his eyes. "My dad... is still waiting for me... I guess... I let him down..."
They laid him down gently. He was covered in blood, with no strength left in his body.
Wei He walked over quickly and checked the wounds. The blade was indeed poisoned, but the poison was not the main problem. The cut was too deep. One could see his organs moving through the open wound on his back.
"Do you have any last words?" Wei He took Zhang Lu's hand.
With Master Zheng and Zhao Hong gone, Wei He was the leader here.
"Senior Brother Wei... my little brother..." Zhang Lu squeezed Wei He's hand, trying to say more. But the cut on his back almost sliced him in half. He could not hold on any longer.
"Brother!" A young boy beside Zhang Lu cried out, tears running down his face.
Zhang Lu failed to catch his next breath. His head tilted to the side, and his breath stopped.
Wei He stood up in silence. He had seen plenty of dead people, but the Viscera-Returning Fist Academy had always been a safe place in his heart.
Now, even this safe place was getting stained with blood.
Surrounded by enemies on all sides, he was strong enough just to protect the few people right next to him. The rest had to survive on their own.
That was why Zhao Hong was lost. That was why Zhang Lu died. That was why Jiang Su was hurt.
