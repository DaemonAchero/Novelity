The armies were facing off. For ordinary people, the atmosphere was suffocating.
For the Wanqing Sect on Black House Mountain, it changed almost nothing.
Wei He cultivated every day, checking his apprentice's progress when he had free time.
Lin Xiao’s younger brother, Lin Yuan, used to train in the basics without drawing any attention. Now he had finally unlocked his First Blood.
Over at the boxing yard, a few kids stood out as natural talents after months of brutal training. One of them had even broken through to Second Blood. Wei He took him in alongside Lin Yuan as a named disciple.
Two months flew by.
The rebel army fought a few quick battles with the Tai’an Army. They gained no ground. With their supply lines stretched thin and food running out, they finally pulled back.
the crisis over Xuanjing City ended.
Power shifted fast. A new faction emerged: the Wang family at the center, backed by the You family and the Wanqing Sect. Together, they took control of Xuanjing and the lands around it.
Behind them stood the Tai’an Army.
The old status quo, once split between the Three Great Families and the Heavenly Seal Sect, was completely gone. All opposition was crushed into a single voice: the Tai’an Army.
During this post-war quiet, a messenger from the Wanfei Palace of Jinzhou arrived.
He was just an ordinary messenger. But he brought the final missing piece Wei He needed: the Cold Charm Technique of the Yuanbing Yard, part of the Nine Emblems of the Heavenly Seal.
"How did you get this?" Wei He frowned, looking at the messenger.
"Sect Master Wei, the Shangguan family isn’t just made of Shangguan Ji and Shangguan Jue," the messenger said. He had blonde eyebrows and a plain face, but his eyes were completely calm.
"So you didn't just find the Shangguan family..." Wei He felt a chill. The Wanfei Palace had strange, deep roots.
Getting the Cold Charm Technique so easily to complete his Nine Emblems felt unreal. But that was the true power of a giant faction. One word from the top, and countless people below threw away their lives to make it happen.
"Getting the full Nine Emblems is impossible for us," the messenger smiled. "But for just the Cold Charm Technique? Shangguan Jue wasn't the only one near it. Now, as agreed, will you release Princess Yue?"
"Princess?" Wei He sneered inside. The Wanfei Palace was arrogant to call Yue Xiaodie a princess. But he didn't care. A deal was a deal. Releasing Yue Xiaodie posed zero threat to him.
"Guards."
"Here, Master!" A disciple stepped in and bowed.
"Take him to Yue Xiaodie. Then send him off." Wei He stood up and left. He had no time to waste.
His Silkworm Force was rebuilding his foundation, letting him form cloud patterns at a terrifying speed. He finally understood how prodigies like Wang Shaojun felt. He had already condensed nine cloud patterns, past the halfway mark. This rapid growth made him resent every wasted second.
"Thank you, Sect Master Wei," the messenger said as he was led to the dungeon.
Wei He didn't care if the messenger found Yue Xiaodie slightly fatter than when she was first captured.
He returned to the pond and resumed training.
The Nine Emblems were complete. He studied the Cold Charm Technique, connected it with the other sub-branches, and ran them all at once.
Inside his body, the blood force of all sub-branches fused into a dense sphere. It spun fast, woven together like a net of blood lines.
Wei He plunged deep into his training. He forgot to eat and sleep, converting his primal blood into emblem blood like a man possessed.
His total primal blood was over 4.5 times that of an ordinary Bone Forging master. His natural talent was mediocre, but after the Whale Flood Technique and multiple resets, his blood pool had expanded massively.
He made the completed Nine Emblems manual accessible to all high-ranking members of the sect. They were too old to switch tracks, but they could train new disciples with the full version. None of them expected Wei He to be this generous.
Outside, peace returned.
With the rebels gone, the Wang family took charge, working with other factions to suppress the plague. People shifted their focus to surviving the disease.
The Wanqing Sect moved back to the Heavenly Seal Sect's old site, the Thousand Bats Pavilion, and rebuilt the Tianya Tower. They opened their doors publicly to recruit talented disciples, finally stepping out of the shadows.
Wei He remained locked in his cultivation. The complete loop of the Nine Emblems was far stronger and faster than training the Rain Force alone. Without the core master manual, he used the Rain Force as the base and the other techniques as support, evolving it into the Ocean Force.
The benefits were huge. Training became one seamless routine. It saved energy, and his blood force grew at a speed that shocked him. Supplied with endless resources, he felt himself getting stronger every single day.
He was addicted to this power. If nothing interrupted him, he would have trained like this for a full year until his next breakthrough.
Then, news broke that forced him to stop.
Zhen Qi and his second sister, Wei Ying, officially announced their marriage.
At the same time, Wang Shaojun was getting married too.
The loud sounds of horns, gongs, drums, and flutes echoed through the city.
The Wang family held a massive wedding at their largest manor in the Central District. Guests showered them with gifts as Wang Shaojun, dressed in a red groom outfit, went through the traditions with his veiled bride.
The Wang family was at the peak of its power. Countless factions came to gain favor. The manor was packed, and the storerooms overflowed with presents. Children in red robes ran around, scattering flowers and fruit for good luck.
Wei He sat at the table reserved for the Wanqing Sect, watching Wang Shaojun smile and toast his guests from afar.
"Time flies. Even this guy is getting married..." Wei He murmured.
"It has been three years, and Master Wang is twenty-four now," Wan Qingqing said softly beside him. She watched the happy scene with envy in her eyes. Growing up raised by a single mother, she always craved a complete family.
"True. The war is over, so a wedding is good for morale." Wei He looked around.
Around their table, a large empty space had formed naturally. No guest dared to step close to the Wanqing Sect.
It was the weight of his reputation.
Even though the Wang family tried to suppress the rumors, people still talked in secret. Rumors about Wei He's deadly poisons had spread even further. People stopped exaggerating the number of his victims and started exaggerating the quality.
Unsolved murders and mysterious deaths near Xuanjing were all blamed on the Wanqing Sect. If a powerful fighter died, Wei He was named the killer.
The reasons given were absurd: the victim looked at Wei He the wrong way, swallowed too loudly near him, bumped into a Wanqing carriage, or hurt an outer disciple.
Combined with the sect's heavy use of poison, the fear was real.
"People love to exaggerate rumors. Don't mind them," Wan Qingqing said gently.
"I don't care. It's just..." Before Wei He finished, Wang Shaojun walked over with a cup of wine.
"Old Wei, you sure know how to clear a room," Wang Shaojun teased, clearly in a great mood.
Wei He stood up, raising his cup. "You kept this quiet. You never mentioned your bride before."
Wang Shaojun gave a helpless laugh. "I always treated her like a younger sister. Who knew... But she’s loved me for a long time, and I don't dislike her. Being together feels natural."
"As long as you're happy," Wei He smiled. "I brought you a special wedding gift. Make sure to open it alone later." He winked.
"You... what kind of weird stuff did you give me?" Wang Shaojun looked suspicious. He remembered Wei He mentioning a potion meant to enhance bedroom performance.
"Just open it. Tell me how it works after you try it," Wei He chuckled.
Wang Shaojun pointed at him, lost for words. He downed his wine and turned to leave before Wei He could say anything worse.
"By the way, it’s for your wife," Wei He added from behind. "It boosts her physical stamina short-term."
Wang Shaojun froze, realizing he had been teased. But a warm feeling washed over him.
In this world, if a martial artist was too strong, a weak wife could die giving birth to his child. Wang Shaojun was at the Viscera Forging level, while his wife was only at Third Blood. The gap was dangerous. The medicine was exactly what they needed.
"Thanks!" Wang Shaojun called back. Then he paused and looked between Wei He and Wan Qingqing. "Even I'm married now. When are you two going to pull the trigger?"
"I..." Wei He started to speak, but felt a soft hand hold his left hand tightly.
He stopped. Next to him, Wan Qingqing’s face was flushed red. She lowered her head, her eyes darting away in silence.
