Wang Shaojun took the two young girls and went back.
Another outpost belonging to the Returning Goose Tower had been wiped out even before the two went to Qianhe Mountain Manor. The two Bone Forging Hall Leaders there had no chance to escape. Working together, Wei He and Wang Shaojun took them down one by one.
That night, inside Qianhe Mountain Manor, long after Wei He left with the Mica Bugs.
The corpses lying all over the ground were giving off a faint rot smell. The blood had dried into a dark red crust. Swarms of flies buzzed everywhere, while scavengers circled above the walls, afraid to land.
As darkness fell, a gray figure landed on top of the wall, scaring the birds away. The figure jumped down and landed right next to He Qianhe’s body, crouching down to search the corpse.
"It’s gone."
"We missed it," another shadow landed behind him.
"Yeah. There are poison marks on the wall. Check the bodies on the ground to see who did this."
"Got it."
The two searched the corpses, but the injuries only made them more confused.
"Fusheng Energy from the Returning Goose Tower, and some unknown poison."
Soon, one of them stood in front of the Feather Hall Leader's body, which Wei He had blasted apart. The strange damage made his eyes narrow.
"What force is this? The insides exploded and turned the organs to mush. Is it the Spring Rain Technique from the Wang Family?"
"No," the other shook his head. "This wasn't an internal blast. It was a hit from the outside that crushed and exploded everything. It’s raw strength, not internal energy."
"Looking at our records, only two people around Xuanjing have this kind of force: Iron Man Zhou Xingtong and Poison Demon Wei He. Both have innate monstrous strength."
"Zhou Xingtong is still recovering from his wounds, and Poison Demon Wei He is a wanted man. Why would they suddenly fight here? Where did they get the news?"
"Whatever the case, report this to the top first."
Without another word, the two leaped over the wall and vanished into the night.
Shortly after they left, a person in white flew over the wall and landed lightly on the training field.
The intruder was a woman, wearing a veil and holding a jade flute. She looked cold and beautiful. Seeing the dead killers from the Returning Goose Tower, her eyes turned sharp.
In a single stride, she crossed tens of meters and knelt next to the Feather Hall Leader's body.
"Nullified the bone force first, then used pure physical strength to crush and blow up the flesh. What a move..."
She stood up, her gaze icy. "Come out."
Two masked people in white appeared behind her and dropped to one knee. Both wore black masks with a copper goose mark on the bottom, a sign of a Returning Goose Tower martial artist.
"Find every martial artist nearby with monstrous strength. Bring me the list," she ordered softly. "Whoever killed my disciple will pay in blood!"
The two nodded silently, jumped up, and vanished over the wall.

Inside Xuanjing City, at the Lin Manor.
Lin Xiaoxiao sat in her study, quietly copying key information from the account books. Though she looked calm, she was worried deep down.
"Still writing?" Wen Lian pushed the door open, feeling bad for her friend.
"It's so late. Go to sleep." She snatched the brush from Xiaoxiao's hand and pulled her up.
"I’m not done yet..." Xiaoxiao tried to resist, but Wen Lian had been practicing martial arts recently and had grown stronger.
"You look restless. Why keep writing?" Wen Lian saw right through her.
"Still worrying about Heavenly Ink Sect? Don't be. Boss Wei is strong. Even the Gold Execution Department couldn't catch him. The Red Crimson Army won't dare mess with him now."
"He poisoned over a thousand people in one go. They say Commander Zhou Xingtong was so angry he almost got paralyzed and is still stuck in bed. So many houses in Xiongshan Town are hanging white mourning banners now."
"A single general's fame is built on ten thousand dead bodies... One man gets famous, but so many lives are sacrificed. What a terrible world..."
Wen Lian had grown up a lot since her family abandoned her. Now, she relied on Xiaoxiao to survive, but her talent for business helped Xiaoxiao's shops thrive.
When Heavenly Ink Sect fell, she knew trouble was coming. She sent large bribes to the Water and Fire Execution Departments of the Red Crimson Army right away. Since those two departments hated the Gold Execution Department, they accepted the gifts and promised not to touch the shop.
Then, she worked with Xiaoxiao to pack up and prepare to move to the provincial capital. The convoy was ready, leaving tomorrow under the protection of experts from the Big Red Gang.
The Big Red Gang was second only to the top five factions in the capital, and they had deep ties with government officials. That was why they dared to protect the shop’s move.
"Things are different now. We need to leave fast so we don't become a burden to Boss Wei," Wen Lian said seriously.
"I know..." Xiaoxiao nodded.
"Good. Now go to sleep."
Xiaoxiao lowered her head to leave, but caught something falling outside the window. She stopped and looked closely.
White snowflakes were drifting down through the gap in the window.
She walked over, opened the window, and reached out her hand.
"It's snowing..."
BANG!
Wei He struck out a palm, making the airflow swirl around his hand.
By a small waterfall in Black House Mountain, he moved like a dragon. Every strike created distortion in the air, sending snowflakes flying. The falling snow was pushed back by his energy, forming a clear ring around him.
Half a month had passed since he helped Wang Shaojun.
During this time, Wan Ling's injuries got a bit better. With Wei He's anti-poison medicine, she helped scout the surrounding area.
The biggest problems around Black House Mountain were the poison mist and toxic beasts. But if someone could solve the poison issue, the place was not much of a threat to a Bone Forging martial artist.
Wan Ling was at the peak of Bone Forging. As a former leader, her internal energy was deep. As long as she avoided the heavy mist and strong beasts, she could move freely.
With her and Wan Qingqing taking charge of the branch, Wei He had more time to cultivate. He only stepped in when the sect needed help with poisons.
After finishing a set of Heart Breaking Seals, Wei He pulled back his hands and stood still.
The area around him was dead silent. Nobody dared to live near his place. Even though he saved them and everyone was willing to help him, no one dared to get too close once they learned he was a poison expert. Accidents happen, and if poison leaked out by mistake, it would be fatal.
After training, Wei He took out a wooden box from a leather bag by the pond. Inside the dark red box was a yellow sponge, holding rows of pearl-white eggs.
He picked up an egg about the size of a soy bean, popped it into his mouth, and swallowed it. It tasted sweet and sour, like a cherry.
This was a Mica Bug egg.
As the egg reached his stomach, a burning heat spread through his body. If a normal person ate this, it would burn their throat and cause terrible stomach pain. But for Wei He, it was perfect. One egg gave him as much energy as eating water bear meat five times.
To feed the Mica Bug, he just needed to mix meat powder with a special recipe every day. He followed the old records, and it worked well. A single Mica Bug could lay eggs for ten years.
Settling his mind, Wei He closed his eyes and practiced the Heart Breaking Seal again, turning his blood energy into internal force.
He felt new energy growing inside him. As the pressure built up, a tiny bit of force slowly pushed into his bones and reached the marrow. It caused a faint itch deep inside his bones that he couldn't scratch.
"My Bone Forging speed is still too slow. At this rate, who knows how long it will take to reach peak Bone Forging."
Wei He frowned, remembering the fast training method Wang Shaojun told him about.
"Using pressure, training underwater might actually work. But I need to handle breathing and energy consumption."
He looked toward the deep pond next to him.
The pond under the waterfall had dark blue, ice-cold water. It matched his force perfectly.
"Worth a try."
Wei He grabbed a heavy stone, ran toward the pond, and jumped in.
SPLASH!
He sank straight down. Bubbles floated up, and his sight turned dark blue. The weeds near the shore swayed like wild beasts. Above him, the bright surface rippled with waves.
Wei He looked down into the dark, bottomless depth of the pond.
He covered his body with internal force to shield himself while holding the stone to sink deeper. The slime and dirt on the stone washed away in the water.
The freezing water and growing pressure forced his shield to drain faster, but the pressure also pushed his force inward.
He felt the force pushing into his bones much faster now. It was a small change, but it was working.
"I need to go deeper."
Wei He felt excited and let the stone drag him further down into the dark water. As a martial artist, he could hold his breath for over ten minutes without a problem, so he had plenty of time.
