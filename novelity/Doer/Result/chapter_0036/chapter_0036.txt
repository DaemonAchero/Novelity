Wei He nodded. Even if Cheng Shaojiu hadn't said anything, he felt it himself.
Once the meat went down his throat with the spices, his stomach warmed up.
He quickly ate a dozen more slices. Each slice was only as thick as a fingernail, but his stomach already felt full.
"How much is a pound of this meat?" Wei He asked.
He knew he couldn't afford it, but buying a little to taste once in a while should be fine.
When he ate the Silver-Kissed Black Snake meat before, he needed three times this amount to feel full.
Now, he only ate a few dozen slices. Put together, it was only the size of a child's fist.
Yet he felt full already?
"This meat... the market price is about four taels of gold for a pound of raw meat," Cheng Shaojiu said in a low voice. "The main problem is that nobody sells it. As soon as wild game is hunted, people buy it instantly."
"Four taels of gold?" Wei He froze. Wasn't silver used for prices before? Then it changed to food and dried meat. Why was it gold now?
"In the inner city, gold leaves and gold notes are used. That is the real money. Silver is different because families can forge it themselves. Who knows how much real silver is in it?" Cheng Shaojiu explained.
He paused: "A few days ago, I saw at the rice shop that one tael of gold can buy fifty pounds of rice. Calculate yourself how much this meat is worth."
"Fifty pounds...!" Wei He was shocked. He finally understood the true cost of this meal.
Fifty pounds of rice could feed a family for a long time. For a middle-class family, it was a month of food money. For poor people, if changed to coarse flour...
And four taels of gold...
Looking at the food on the table, Wei He felt complex emotions.
Without realizing it, he had reached the level of eating such expensive food.
His chopsticks moved much faster.
"Hey! Leave some for me!" Cheng Shaojiu reacted.
Their chopsticks moved like lightning, and they finished the plate of meat in a few bites.
Cheng Shaojiu let Wei He eat most of it, since he was paying for the meal.
When they were full, a small plate of toothpicks was brought over.
They each took a toothpick and picked their teeth in their seats.
"Little He, now that you broke through Blood and Qi a second time, we should raise your pay today," Cheng Shaojiu had already planned for Wei He.
right now, Yonghe Escort Agency could not give Wei He what he wanted. At his level, he did not lack money or food. What he lacked were good things like the Silver-Kissed Black Snake meat that could quickly build Blood and Qi.
However, such items were secret treasures of big families and were not shared. So he decided to hire Wei He for just part of his time.
Wei He would register with the agency, take a salary, and still be free to join other places.
When he explained his idea, Wei He agreed.
"The monthly fee will be paid as usual. We will only call you when we need your help, with no other rules. It won't be more than twice a year," Cheng Shaojiu smiled.
"No problem," Wei He nodded.
"Actually, at your level, you can register with several places. They just want your name. Small gangs love this. They pay you a fee, and when they need help, they can use your name to guard the gang and draw in new members," Cheng Shaojiu said with a laugh.
"It works like that?" Wei He thought of his past life, where experts passed high-level exams and rented out their certificates to make passive income.
He didn't expect to enjoy such benefits here too.
They rested for a while and drank a few more cups.
Cheng Shaojiu soon felt the need to pee, so he stood up to use the bathroom.
As soon as he left, Wei He listened to the woman in green play the pipa in the restaurant.
"Junior Brother Wei, what a coincidence. I didn't expect to meet you here."
Suddenly, a familiar figure walked up to him and blocked his view.
Wei He looked up. It was Jiang Yan.
Jiang Yan held a folding fan and wore white clothes. A servant boy followed behind him. He looked refined and smiled warmly.
His appearance was indeed good. He was handsome, tall, slim, and came from a strong family. It was easy for him to attract women.
"Senior Brother Jiang," Wei He stood up and held his fists together, showing basic respect to a senior brother.
"Drinking and eating here alone?" Jiang Yan looked at the table and knew someone else was there.
"Good. I came to say hi and talk a bit before leaving. Since I saw you here, Junior Brother, now that you broke through, why stay with the Cheng family? My Jiang family needs people lately.
If you agree, I can give you ten pounds of blood-nourishing medicine every month, equal to this Flower-Deer meat.
I will also give you one hundred second-grade Blood Pills, ten packages of Blood Powder, and fifty taels of gold."
The servant boy behind Jiang Yan changed his expression slightly. This offer went beyond what a normal second-breakthrough expert got. Just the ten pounds of medicine equal to Flower-Deer meat was rare.
It could save a lot of time in gathering Blood and Qi. If offered outside, countess martial artists would join willingly.
Even in this restaurant, the Flower-Deer meat was not sold every day. It was offered once a month, with limited amounts per person.
This alone made it the restaurant's top dish.
"Besides, you are my fellow junior brother. I must take good care of my own," Jiang Yan opened his fan to cover his chin and smiled.
"I will add more for you. Senior Brother Cheng cannot offer this price. If you join, I will give you free living rights in the inner city. You and your sister won't need to worry about danger. With the Seven-Family Alliance and officials patrolling the inner city, nothing bad will happen.
Of course, if you accept, you cannot register with other forces outside. But do not worry. After joining my Jiang family, you can manage small gangs in the outer city and take a share of the profit.
At that time, with ten men under you carrying the Seven-Family Alliance badge, no one in the outer city will dare to mess with you. Wouldn't that be great?"
Among all these benefits, the right to live in the inner city was the key.
Only the Seven-Family Alliance had the power to let outsiders live there for a long time.
Living in the inner city was a special right that even Cheng Shaojiu did not have.
To Jiang Yan, it was an easy spot to get. Though limited, it was worth less than one hundred taels of gold to him.
To Cheng Shaojiu and others, this spot was the most valuable thing.
Because money could not buy it.
This was one of the main reasons the Seven-Family Alliance stayed strong for so long.
Wei He was not interested in the earlier offers, but he truly wanted the last one.
If he could live in the inner city, he wouldn't need to worry about his second sister's safety.
He killed several people from the Fragrance Sect last time and kept worrying about revenge.
Though nothing happened yet, who knew if they were planning something in secret?
Now, if he could move into the inner city...
Wei He lowered his head and stayed silent.
What he didn't see was that Cheng Shaojiu had returned nearby and heard Jiang Yan's offer.
Cheng Shaojiu understood that Jiang Yan was stealing his man.
From Jiang Yan's words, he had been standing nearby from the start and heard what Cheng Shaojiu told Wei He.
He waited for Cheng Shaojiu to leave to make his offer.
Cheng Shaojiu stood quietly around the corner, not showing himself right away.
His freshly washed hands tightened slowly and felt hot.
Wei He stayed silent, then looked up at Jiang Yan.
He took a breath and prepared to speak.
"Don't rush, Junior Brother," Jiang Yan smiled and closed his fan. "Think it over carefully. If you agree, come find me alone later. I will return to my table now. I have friends waiting."
He turned and walked away like he didn't care.
Jiang Yan knew that people were easily moved by emotion, especially martial artists whose blood boiled easily.
Decisions made in haste were easily guided by feelings.
Right now, Wei He had a good relationship with Cheng Shaojiu, so he was easily influenced by the Cheng family.
However, once a person cooled down and weighed the gains and losses, they would make the smartest choice.
Feelings were only temporary. Over time, profit mattered most.
At the corner, Jiang Yan saw Cheng Shaojiu standing still.
He smiled, nodded to him without a word, and walked past.
Cheng Shaojiu forced a smile and nodded back.
Jiang Yan did not care about Cheng Shaojiu's attitude. The Cheng family had a good relationship with Wei He, but so what? Normal people gave up profit for feelings only because the offer wasn't high enough.
The Cheng family called him Senior Brother to respect him. If they didn't, he came from the Seven-Family Alliance, so why would he care about a tiny Yonghe Escort Agency?
Jiang Yan smiled slightly and led his servant boy toward the private room.
Cheng Shaojiu paused, waited at the spot for a moment to calm his feelings, and then walked back to the table.
When he reached the table, he saw Wei He standing with a strange look on his face.
"Junior Brother..." Cheng Shaojiu opened his mouth, wanting to say something, but didn't know what to say.
How could he compete with the Jiang family? They were on two completely different levels.
"Brother Cheng..." Wei He stayed quiet for a moment, then said softly, "I am sorry..."
"It's fine... Water flows down, and people move up..." Cheng Shaojiu smiled, trying to act normal.
"No," Wei He looked apologetic. "I saw that you didn't come back for a long time, so I ordered four more plates of Flower-Deer meat and ate it all..."
Cheng Shaojiu's face froze instantly.
Four plates? Four plates of deer meat? Are you a pig?
He looked at Wei He, standing there like a statue, unable to speak. His whole food limit for the month was wiped out...
"Oh right, Senior Brother Jiang came over just now to recruit me," Wei He said calmly. "I thought about it, but I still plan to stay in the outer city. Mainly because Senior Brother Jiang has bad breath. Talking to him made my whole body feel uncomfortable."
