“A tense situation?” Wei He walked into the wooden cabin, brought out a tea tray, and poured two cups of warm tea.
Wang Shaojun followed him inside. Both men sat opposite each other on the floor.
“Did you hear something?” Wei He raised his cup and drank it in one sip.
“Just a few things,” Wang Shaojun sighed. “When the Heavenly Seal Sect collapsed, I noticed something strange. Now, I see the exact same pattern with the Lishan Sect. I suspect…”
He paused for a second. “I suspect a single force has been targeting the Wushi Sect all along.”
Wei He was confused. “If they want the Wushi Sect, why strike the Heavenly Seal Sect and Lishan Sect first?”
“There are five major powers in Tai Prefecture, led by the Wushi Sect. The other four seem independent, but they act as meat shields. Their territories surround the Wushi Sect like a wall,” Wang Shaojun explained in a low voice.
“If the Wushi Sect falls, new factions will bring their own puppets to claim the land. The existing powers won’t allow that. To protect their own gains, they will naturally group up under the Wushi Sect.”
“How can you be so sure the enemy will bring their own factions?” Wei He asked, instantly catching the core issue.
“The Wushi Sect sits high above everyone else, ignoring common affairs. In all nineteen provinces, who else cares less about profit than them?” Wang Shaojun countered.
Wei He said nothing. He knew Wang Shaojun had secret information he couldn't share.
“Forget it. The Lishan Sect can handle their own mess. Let’s just watch,” Wang Shaojun took a sip of tea and smiled. “Now that you’ve mastered the Silken Force, what are your plans for the future?”
“Plans? What plans?”
“At our age, the highest we can reach is Shangguan Ji’s level. Our martial path ends there. It’s time to plan for other things,” Wang Shaojun said directly.
“I’m happy with a peaceful life. As long as things stay quiet, that's enough.” Wei He smiled. He was satisfied with his current life.
“Do you really think this peace will last?” Wang Shaojun shook his head. “The world changes constantly. Right now, Grand Yuan is falling apart. Disasters happen everywhere, and warlords rule each region. War is coming.”
He looked straight into Wei He’s eyes.
“If you wait until the enemy armies arrive, it will be too late to prepare.”
“What are you getting at?” Wei He asked flatly.
“With your talent, you should join the provincial government with me. Serve the Governor. It’s better than wasting your life drifting along,” Wang Shaojun said firmly. “We must take control of our own lives.”
Wang Shaojun had built his argument carefully. First, he showed that even strong sects were unsafe. Then, he warned of upcoming wars. Finally, he offered the solution: join the strongest power in Tai Prefecture, the Taian Army of the provincial governor.
Wei He held his teacup without drinking.
“Brother Wang, you’d make a fine lobbyist.”
“Old Wei, think about it. Do we really have another choice in this mess?” Wang Shaojun shook his head.
Wei He remained silent.
“I know what you care about—peace, martial arts, family,” Wang Shaojun continued. “You think being strong is enough to protect your family forever. But no matter how strong you get, how many years will it take to reach the Viscera Refining stage? How many years to reach the Mind Sensing stage? And even if you reach Mind Sensing like Shangguan Ji, look how he ended up.”
He looked gloomy. “When I was younger, I thought Mind Sensing was easy. Now that I’ve reached Viscera Refining, I realize how terrifying it actually is. If I can reach it in thirty years, I’ll be lucky.”
“Thirty years? That doesn’t sound like you,” Wei He said, surprised.
“When you reach my level, you’ll understand. Mind Sensing means recording the five senses without losing yourself. Once you enter that realm, you are no longer human. A dark terror hides within it.” Wang Shaojun drank his tea in one gulp.
Wei He memorized every word, feeling a strange chill behind them.
“Are you scared?” Wei He asked.
“A little…” Wang Shaojun sighed. “Anyway, think about it. Neutral people die first when war starts. By the way, when is your engagement?”
“In a few days,” Wei He replied. “Don’t forget a gift.”
“You won’t be disappointed.” Wang Shaojun smiled.
They drank a few more cups before Wang Shaojun left.
Wei He sat alone for a long time.
If a genius like Wang Shaojun needed thirty years to reach Mind Sensing, the path was clearly brutal. Even with the Breaking Orb, Wei He didn't know how long he would be stuck. He really needed a backup plan.
After a moment, Wei He put away his thoughts and went back to training.
His progress with the Nine Kills Technique was fast. Five sub-branches had already reached the Three Bloods stage. Once all branches reached this level, he would use the Breaking Orb to break through at once. Meanwhile, his Cloud Pattern Bone progress was steady. Everything was going well.
He only needed time.
Wei He trained until evening. After dinner, as he prepared to continue, his second sister, Wei Ying, arrived with Zhen Qi.
“If I don’t visit, you never come see us, do you?” Zhen Qi complained.
Ever since her relationship with Wei Ying became public, she spoke more freely around Wei He.
“Keep quiet, Zhen Qi,” Wei He said coldly.
“Alright, stop arguing every time you meet,” Wei Ying said, pulling Zhen Qi’s sleeve softly.
“Fine. We came for real business,” Zhen Qi held Wei Ying’s hand and straightened her face.
“What business?” Wei He looked up.
“Your sister wants to return to Yun Province to look for your parents and big sister,” Zhen Qi said. “Life is stable now, and you have no trouble here. It’s good to look for family.”
Wei He thought for a moment, then shook his head. “I can’t leave.”
His presence kept the local area safe. If he left for too long, trouble would brew.
“Yun Province is far,” Wei He added. “I will send guards with you.”
“No need, I have my own people,” Zhen Qi refused. “We just came to let you know.”
Wei He didn't drop the matter. The Wanqing Sect had taken in many skilled martial artists. Sending a few guards was easy.
After agreeing on the details, the two women left.
Wei He returned to his strict routine. He worked on his leg techniques, the Nine Kills Technique, and the True Method all at once.
Half a month later, Wei He successfully pushed all Nine Kills sub-branches to the Three Bloods stage.
However, bad news arrived from Qingqi County.
Jiu Ying had collapsed into a coma.
Fortunately, Jiu Ying had trained a group of apprentices beforehand. They handled basic medicine, treatment, and quarantine without panic, keeping the plague from spreading further.
Still, more than half the population of Qingqi County had already died from the sickness.
Wei He was shocked by the news. Jiu Ying was a powerful martial master with far stronger vitality than normal people.
He immediately set off to see him.
Qingqi County.
Inside a courtyard used for treating patients, Wei He found Jiu Ying, who had just woken up.
The room smelled heavily of incense, mixed with the stench of sickness and old medicine.
Jiu Ying leaned against a wooden bed, covered in a white blanket. His face was pale and lifeless.
Seeing Wei He enter, he smiled and waved his assistants out. The apprentices hesitated, but Jiu Ying forced them out.
Once they were alone, Jiu Ying exhaled heavily.
“Your martial arts improved again.”
“You look like you’re about to die,” Wei He replied.
“Is that how you talk to an elder?” Jiu Ying grunted. “I can live decades more! I was just tired. You think I’m done?”
“Heh. If people knew the famous poison master Jiu Ying passed out from saving plague victims, they’d think you were an impostor,” Wei He said.
“I just wanted to keep my promise,” Jiu Ying shook his head.
He only cared about saving his old friend’s last descendant. Saving the others was just an extra task.
“Since you are here, I want to ask you something,” Jiu Ying said.
“What is it?”
“Can I pass the True Method to someone else?”
“You helped complete the True Method. Pass it to whoever you want,” Wei He said calmly.
“Generous,” Jiu Ying sighed. “Without your resources and ideas, I couldn't have finished it.”
Jiu Ying clapped his hands softly.
The door opened, and a young child with a tied ponytail walked in. It was Yuan Bao.
“Wei He, this is my new disciple,” Jiu Ying patted the boy’s hand. “Take care of him in the future.”
