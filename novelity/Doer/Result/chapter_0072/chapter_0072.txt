The main gate of the Return Mountain Fist Academy was worn out, but no one bothered to fix it.
Wei He pushed the door open and stepped inside. The yard felt cold and quiet, though about ten students were still silently practicing their skin-refinement techniques.
This training was meant to test a person's raw limits. Who was strong, who was weak, who had better bone structure, and who healed faster—everything became clear during this process.
Looking at his fellow students now, Wei He could instantly tell who held superior strength.
He finally understood why Master Zheng liked to sit in the yard every day.
"Senior Brother Wei, you're here?" Ouyang Zhuang called out from a distance and hurried over to greet him.
Li Jue raised a hand in greeting as well, showing familiar respect.
Many people who used to be in the yard had already left. Most of those remaining were fresh faces.
Wei He did not visit often, so he had not paid much attention before. Seeing this now, he could not help but link it to the shifting situation in the city.
He sighed inwardly.
"Is Master Zheng here yet?"
"Not yet, but he should be soon. He usually arrives around this time," Ouyang Zhuang said with a smile.
"I'll head inside first then." Wei He nodded, passed through the outer yard, and walked into the inner house.
There used to be three servant women working in the house, but now only one remained.
When she saw Wei He walk in, she offered him a warm, pleasing smile.
Wei He nodded back and stepped into the inner yard.
Inside, Jiang Yan was whispering to Zhang Lu. Jiang Su practiced her moves in silence. Zhao Hong stood in a corner, holding a letter and reading it line by line.
As Wei He entered, Jiang Yan kept his face neutral and continued talking with Zhang Lu.
Zhang Lu did not know Wei He at all, so he gave no reaction.
Zhao Hong simply raised his head, nodded as a greeting, and went back to his letter. He had been learning how to read lately, and it seemed he was making good progress.
Only Jiang Su stopped her movements and walked over to him.
"It has been a while since you last came," she said.
She looked at Wei He with a complex expression. Since Xiao Ran died, she had misunderstood Wei He, then tried to make it up to him. After that series of events, her view of him had completely changed. He was no longer just Cheng Shaojiu's follower.
The memory of Cheng Shaojiu was fading from her mind, replaced by a deeper, sharper impression of Wei He.
"Where is Master Zheng? Still not here?" Wei He asked casually.
"Yeah. Master Zheng has been busy with something lately. He looks exhausted. I asked him about it, but he didn't explain," Jiang Su nodded.
"I'll wait then." Wei He was in no rush. After breaking through the Return Mountain Fist, he had stacked it with the Five Mountains Palm. Stacking the blood energy of two complete arts three times over made his internal power thicker than any three-layer blood artist he had ever met.
Even the patrol envoys from the Shaoyang Sect felt weaker than him right now.
He had rested at home for days to control his blood flow and hide any abnormal signs. Otherwise, the people here would have noticed it immediately.
Blood energy was an abstract force. When active, it generated intense heat and a dominant presence. But when a fighter stayed calm, few people could judge their true depth.
Except for those massive fighters from the Shaoyang Sect, of course. With bodies like that, anyone could tell their power was monstrous.
Because of this, Wei He realized why Master Zheng enjoyed watching the disciples practice. When they moved and struck, their true progress was laid bare.
Wei He picked a corner, leaned against the wall, and waited quietly.
Jiang Su tried to focus back on her training, but seeing Wei He stand there ruined her focus. She tried to push through it for a bit, gave up, and stopped.
A moment later, Zhao Hong finished his letter, folded it carefully into a leather pouch, and returned to his spot to drill his techniques.
Turning martial moves into muscle memory was hard work. Understanding the point of a technique, using it in real combat, and refining it to fit one's own body took massive amounts of time and energy.
The reason Zhao Hong could overpower everyone else at the same Stone Skin level was that he understood his moves far better than they did.
Watching him train, Wei He gained fresh insights of his own. Since Master Zheng had not arrived, he quietly watched his senior brother practice.
Time slowly passed.
Heavy footsteps sounded from the inner house.
Everyone perked up and looked at the exit, waiting for Master Zheng to show up.
Soon, Zheng Fugui walked into the inner yard. He wore a plain grey shirt, a black jacket, and held his hands behind his back. Dark mud stuck to the edges of his cloth shoes. No one knew where he had been so early in the morning.
Zheng Fugui still looked half-asleep. He drifted into the yard and looked at his disciples. His cloudy eyes scanned the group, but his mind seemed far away.
He had been like this lately, showing up whenever he pleased. Deep wrinkles carved into his face. Even his favorite tea snacks sat mostly untouched.
The situation in Feiye City was getting worse. Conflicts between the Incense Cult and the inner city grew hotter every day. The Incense Cult was chaotic and disorganized, yet countless fresh fighters kept joining its ranks.
On top of that, a hidden force was supporting them from the shadows, letting them go head-to-head with the Seven Families Alliance.
Zheng Fugui spent his days running around, investigating and tracking these shifts.
A few days ago, he visited his daughter. She refused to listen to his warnings and insisted on doing things her way. He begged her, but it changed nothing.
He had lived in Feiye City for decades, quietly building up his fortune. Yet at over fifty years old, he had barely anything left to his name. All because of her.
Every bit of wealth and resources he earned was wasted by his reckless daughter. That wastefulness was the exact reason Zheng Fugui had become so cheap and stingy in his daily life.
Now she had a son of her own. He thought having a child would make her settle down, but she stayed the same, running wild every single day. She was nearly thirty, yet completely irresponsible.
Zheng Fugui could be cold and hard to anyone else, but not her. He had spoiled her since she was small, and now the damage was done.
Besides planning a way out of the city, his biggest headache was dealing with her. Half his energy went into cleaning up her messes.
Just thinking about the trouble she caused gave him a migraine.
Only after having this daughter did he realize that strength could not fix everything in this world. Some problems remained completely out of reach, no matter how powerful a fighter grew.
Shaking off his thoughts, Zheng Fugui looked at the disciples in the yard.
"It is rare to see all of you here today. Good. I have something to announce."
Everyone stopped training and gathered around to listen.
No matter what, Zheng Fugui was a true Force Martial Artist. Even Jiang Yan did not dare show disrespect. Jiang Yan belonged to the Jiang family, but he was just one of many young members. His status was nowhere near a true Force master. Only his father could stand on equal footing with Zheng Fugui.
The group stepped closer, waiting for him to speak.
"In a few days, the Return Mountain Fist Academy will move outside the city. If you want to come along, get ready. If not, let me know in advance."
Everyone froze. They expected the academy to move eventually, but they did not think the day would come so fast.
If it moved, Jiang Yan obviously could not leave the city.
Zhao Hong and Zhang Lu would definitely follow.
As for Jiang Su, it depended on her choice. Her family held real weight in the city.
Wei He thought it over for a moment and decided to go along. Staying with Master Zheng, a true Force master, was far safer than running around alone with his second sister.
"Also, I have not tested your skills in a long time. Let me see how much you have grown," Zheng Fugui continued.
He looked around at them and smiled.
"Pair up and spar. The winners will fight each other, and I will give a reward to the last one standing."
"Master Zheng, isn't that unfair?" Jiang Yan stepped forward. "No matter how we fight, Senior Brother Zhao Hong is the strongest. He will beat everyone. Doesn't that mean the reward is already his?"
Zheng Fugui shook his head internally. Jiang Yan had a good background and decent talent, but his mindset was too narrow.
"Does anyone else think it is unfair?"
"I also think it is unfair," Zhao Hong said calmly, stepping forward. He was naturally quiet and distant, but since Jiang Yan called it out, he spoke his mind.
"Anyone else?" Zheng Fugui frowned slightly.
"I think it is unfair too," Wei He stepped forward, his face dead calm.
"Oh?" Zheng Fugui narrowed his eyes at Wei He. He did not expect Wei He to stand up.
He had not paid close attention earlier, but as he looked closer now, his eyes widened slightly.
"You?!"
His body trembled. Suddenly, he threw a punch straight forward.
The strike was blindingly fast, packed with explosive power. His fist turned pitch black, wrapped in tight threads of force.
The blow rushed toward Wei He, the wind whipping the clothes of everyone nearby. The stray force stung the faces of Jiang Yan, Zhao Hong, and the others like sharp needles.
In the blink of an eye— BANG!
Wei He raised a single hand and caught the strike.
"Master, is this enough?"
The hand gripping the fist was solid black, like forged iron.
"Not enough!" Zheng Fugui's face twitched, his eyes bursting with wild joy.
"Again!" he roared, throwing himself forward with both fists.
Both men released their full force and crashed together.
BOOM!
Heavy, violent iron fists traded blows. In a matter of seconds, they exchanged dozens of strikes.
Jiang Yan, Zhao Hong, Jiang Su, and Zhang Lu could not even stay near the fight. The shockwaves forced them back.
All four were horrified, watching the two shadows clash in the center of the yard.
An unbelievable, terrifying thought slowly filled their minds.
A few seconds later— A sharp blast echoed out.
Both fighters shot backward and stood firm.
"Master, is it enough now?"
Both of Wei He's arms were solid black. His muscle mass expanded slightly, and dark veins bulged across his face, making him look terrifying.
Zheng Fugui looked down at his numb hands, then threw his head back and laughed out loud.
His laughter was pure and wild, as if washing away years of buried frustration.
