Under the waterfall, inside the deep pool.
Wei He sat at the bottom with heavy stones tied to his body. Tiny bubbles rose from his nose one by one.
He was trying to merge all his martial arts together.
He had wanted to do this for a long time. He wanted one single framework to hold every skill that complemented each other.
If he viewed all his techniques as one single art, he could train them all continuously.
For other fighters, doing this was useless.
But for Wei He, who possessed the Break-Through Bead, this was a massive simplification.
With this, he could split his martial arts into three main categories: core, power-boosting, and support.
He had eight core techniques alone. His support arts included White Jade Skill, Righteous Method, Whale Flood Skill, and several others.
He had so many martial arts that practicing every single one each day took far too much time.
That was why he decided to fuse them.
He would remove the redundant, overlapping parts of each technique and combine them into one to save time and energy.
With the Autumn Deer Method, he could test different training ideas safely and fix any mistakes.
Even if a test went wrong, only his earlobe would rupture, and it would heal quickly.
At the bottom of the pool, force rolled through Wei He's body. His blood cycled and gathered at his earlobe.
He came up with one fusion idea after another, but rejected them just as fast when problems showed up in his simulations.
He had been at this for over ten days.
Luckily, he was finally finding a rhythm.
He chose the Covering Rain Force as his primary foundation, using every other martial art to power it up.
When he cultivated the hidden forces from the other Sky Seal branches he had collected, they boosted the Covering Rain Force way beyond its old limits.
With this concept, his tests began to take shape.
All his force-based martial arts gathered into one branch.
All his body-strengthening arts—Whale Flood Skill, Righteous Method, and White Jade Skill—merged into another.
Though it was just simple addition minus the overlap, the meaning for Wei He was huge.
"All force arts center around the Eight Sky Seal techniques, with Covering Rain at the core. Gathering them like this is like rivers flowing into the sea.
Let's call this power-boosting framework the Cloud Sea Code. Any new force I cultivate will stack on top to empower Covering Rain Force.
Calling the boosted result plain Covering Rain Force doesn't fit anymore. Let's call it Ocean Covering Force instead."
Wei He named it casually.
Next was his blood-refining and poison-resisting branch, led by Whale Flood Skill and supported by Righteous Method and White Jade Skill.
"Righteous Method already covers most of what White Jade Skill does, so I can drop White Jade. Let's name this part Whale Flood Righteous Method."
This was a full cleanup of his arsenal.
Ocean Covering Force, Cloud Sea Code, and Whale Flood Righteous Method.
These three were his core, booster, and support.
Future techniques could be thrown into these categories. Everything became clean and clear instead of a messy pile.
With the three main systems set, other techniques like Autumn Deer Method and Five Spirits Breath Secret were sorted under secret skills, which also counted as support.
Sitting deep underwater, Wei He finalized the layout. He ran a simulation with the Autumn Deer Method and found no major flaws.
Then, he used his own body to continue refining the Cloud Bone Structure.
