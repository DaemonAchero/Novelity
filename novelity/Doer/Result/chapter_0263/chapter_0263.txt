Rat Alley.
It was a broken, run-down lane.
Wei He walked through it with a calm pace. Soon, he reached the front of his old family home.
The small house was gone. In its place stood a completely renovated building.
Red-painted doors, white walls, and black tiles. The single window was replaced with thick, decorative security bars. A heavy bronze lock hung on the main gate, sealed with two pictures of door gods.
"Is someone living here?"
Wei He's heart skipped a beat. Could his parents and oldest sister be back? Or maybe Zhen Qi and his second sister fixed the place up.
He looked around. The surrounding houses were still falling apart.
This was a slum. When the new city district opened, most people moved there for the better work and benefits. To the poor, survival came first. A roof over their head was enough.
Wei He stood silent for a long moment.
An old man with a hunched back slowly walked out from a side alley, leaning on a cane.
He stopped and looked closely at Wei He's face.
"Young man, are you looking for someone?"
He spoke with a heavy local accent. Having lived here for years, Wei He understood him instantly.
"Yes," Wei He turned and asked politely. "Does the Wei family still live here?"
"The Weis..." The old man paused. "A while ago, two young women came and repaired the house. They were quite pretty. I don't know if they're Weis, but if you wait, they usually check the place in the late afternoon."
"Thank you, elder," Wei He nodded. "Have you always lived in Feiye City?"
"About thirty years," the old man sighed. "I used to live on the next street. But the local power beat up the Xiangqu Cult, and those high-and-mighty fighters smashed the entire road. I had no choice but to move here."
"I grew up here too," Wei He glanced at the sky. There was still time before sunset. "Are the big families near the central square still around?"
"A few are gone, replaced by newcomers. The old lecture hall was torn down for a new hall that sells jewelry. The market at Red Stone Town is gone too, replaced by restaurants and teahouses."
The old man said his name was Wang. He had two children working as street cleaners in the new district.
He was talkative and mentioned that life was much better now than during the famine. His face was full of life and his voice was clear, showing he ate well.
This gave Wei He a clear idea of the Hong family's control. The city was ten times better than when he left.
The Hong family wiped out the Seven-Alliance and betrayed the Xiangqu Cult. They relied on demonic factions behind the scenes, but to the common people, the result was all that mattered.
Seeing the old man grow tired, Wei He said goodbye.
He waited longer, but Wei Ying didn't show up. Thinking they wouldn't come today, he decided to leave. He planned to return later with Wan Qingqing.
Qingqing and the others were staying at a mansion provided by the Hong family. She was a Bone-Forging martial artist herself, accompanied by Bai Dou and three expert fighters. They were safe.
After the long journey, Qingqing needed time to rest and wash up, which was why she stayed behind.
Wei He turned to leave the alley.
"Wait, young man!" the old man called out from behind.
"What is it, elder?" Wei He turned around.
"If you're looking for the Wei family, check the new district. People from this area were moved to Baisha Lane."
"Baisha Lane..." Wei He thanked him and walked out.
Now that Zhen Qi and Wei Ying were back, visiting Baisha Lane made sense. The Hong family was busy handling the cult's aftermath, so relying solely on their search might take time.
The street outside was quiet. Snow covered the ground, and ice hung from the roofs. The recent chaos had scared people into staying indoors.
Wei He walked down the empty street. A team of Hong family guards followed him secretly from a distance. They were there to serve him and keep brainless fools from causing trouble.
As he walked, two veiled women approached from the front.
Even through the veil, Wei He recognized his second sister, Wei Ying, from her posture and presence.
Zhen Qi and Wei Ying had just finished exploring nearby. They stopped in shock when they saw him.
"Xiao He?" Wei Ying asked softly.
She thought he was still in Taizhou, busy with his sect.
"It's me, Second Sister, Zhen Qi," Wei He smiled and walked over.
"Show some respect! Call me Sister Zhen Qi!" Zhen Qi snapped. "Your sister and I are partners, so I'm your elder too."
"You can't beat me in a fight," Wei He smiled.
"I am her partner!" Zhen Qi glared at him.
"Say one more word and I'll hit you," Wei He warned playfully, raising his fist.
She took his sister away for so long without a word, making him worry. Now she wanted to act tough? His fist wasn't soft.
Zhen Qi choked on her words and shut her mouth, knowing she was in the wrong.
Wei Ying chuckled and gently pulled Wei He's hand down.
"You came at the right time, Xiao He. We've been looking for our parents and oldest sister with no luck."
"Don't worry. Qingqing is here too, along with a few useful servants. We have plenty of hands."
Wei He paused mid-sentence and looked to his right.
A guard stepped out from the shadows and handed him a note.
Wei He opened it. His eyes narrowed.
Wei Tang, Li Cui, and Wei Spring found. Living at No. 761, Baisha Lane, New District.
Below were their jobs, followed by a note about Wei Spring: Wei Spring is involved in the Star Cup incident. She is suspected to be a disciple of Master Zhuang, the previous owner of the Star Cup.
"Found them?" Wei He's heart raced with joy.
When he first transmigrated to this world, he was weak and helpless. The love of his parents and sisters helped him adapt. He had assumed they were dead. Now, they were alive and in this very city.
Then, he focused on the trouble mentioned at the bottom.
"The Star Cup..."
The artifact had drawn the attention of every major force in Feiye City. It was tied to a secret treasure. If martial artists were fighting over it, the reward was more than just gold.
"What is it, Xiao He?" Wei Ying asked.
"Good news." Wei He passed the note to Zhen Qi, as Wei Ying couldn't read well.
Zhen Qi read it aloud, her face lighting up. "Is this real?"
"They wouldn't dare lie to me," Wei He said.
"Then let's go now!" Zhen Qi urged.
Wei Ying's eyes turned red with excitement. "Let's go!"
Wei He signaled a guard to prepare a carriage. Soon, the three boarded and headed straight for the new district.
Feiye New District, Baisha Lane.
Wei Tang fanned the fire under a pot, boiling medicine for his wife, Li Cui.
The sores on her body were growing worse. With no other choice, he had bought cheap herbs from a doctor.
Knock. Knock. Knock.
Someone knocked on the door.
Wei Tang coughed and stood up cautiously. Night had fallen, and his eldest daughter wasn't back yet. He had to be careful.
"Who is it?" he called out.
"Is this Master Wei Tang's house?" a young man's voice answered from outside.
