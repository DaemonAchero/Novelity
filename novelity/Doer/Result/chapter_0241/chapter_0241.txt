A massive woman stepped inside. It was Yu Qingyan, the Master of the Lishan Sect and head of the Yu family.
She looked a bit rough. Bandages wrapped her body under a pink coat as thick as a blanket.
She ignored Wei He. Everyone in the cabin was standing up anyway.
"Greetings, Sect Master Yu."
"A pleasure to meet you. I am Xiang Chun, Master of the Feifeng Sect."
"I am the current heir of the Purple Moon Sword line. Greetings."
"Li Dongcheng from the Wangyue Pavilion in Sanwu City. My father brought me to see you two years ago."
The experts greeted her one by one, and Yu Qingyan nodded back to each of them.
The people here were top leaders from various regions. Even the weaker ones had powerful factions behind them. She could not afford to be rude.
Mo Shiyan stepped forward and guided Yu Qingyan to a seat on the left of the Alliance Leader.
Yu Qingyan never even looked at Wei He. Frowning, she sat down, exchanged a few words with Alliance Leader You Fu, and closed her eyes to rest.
She was clearly exhausted. The Lishan Sect headquarters in Juxia City was currently in deep trouble, yet she still traveled all this way. She cared deeply about this meeting.
Since she said nothing, Wei He stayed quiet. He had a small grudge with the Lishan Sect, but it was just minor friction.
He had killed Yan Junshan, a Viscera Refinement expert, but the Yan and Yu families were rivals within the sect. Yu Qingyan had no reason to avenge him. At most, she might scold Wei He as a sect leader.
Besides, she looked far too drained to care about small matters right now.
Wei He remained still. Soon, a few more people entered the cabin.
Time was up. The heavy doors shut, and the servants left.
You Fu stood up with a wine glass.
"Thank you for traveling so far to join us. Let us skip the small talk. You came here to stop the Nest of Taizhou."
He looked around the room. Some people looked calm, others confused or worried.
"It seems many of you still do not know what the Nest is."
His voice was clear and sharp as he looked at every face.
"So let me explain what the Nest actually is."
Wei He sat straight and listened closely. Back in Xuanjing, he would eventually face the next wave of imperial experts sent to replace Zhu Chen. Understanding the bigger picture was his main goal. Only then could he guess the government's plans and prepare his own moves.
You Fu spoke again.
"The Nest is a secret plot by the Dayuan Court to deal with the coming crisis. My spies brought back some news. I cannot share the minor details, but I can tell you their main goal."
He let out a heavy sigh. Though he was a bald, one-eyed brute, he sounded like a tired old man.
"They are gathering martial artists from everywhere. Using a strange technique, they drain their force to create a new type of soldier. They call them Puppet Masters."
"Puppet Masters?" Yu Qingyan snapped her eyes open. "How strong are they compared to normal troops or martial artists?"
"They are made from martial artists," You Fu said darkly. "The Court uses mind control to wipe their memories and turn them into loyal flesh puppets."
The room erupted into mutters.
Martial artists were rare and deadly. Once they unlocked internal force, their bodies became as tough as leather armor. If you put them in heavy steel plates, gave them spears, and marched them together, they would be unstoppable.
"Puppet Masters..." Mo Shiyan whispered. "If the Court builds a real army of them, they could crush the rebels and take back the land."
"Precisely," You Fu nodded. "The Nest brainwashes these men and stockpiles them. It has become a dragon's lair. Deadly and dangerous."
The room went cold. Knowing their missing allies were alive was good news, but learning they were turned into thralls made it far worse.
"Have you fought these Puppet Masters yourself, Leader?" someone asked.
"I have," You Fu said seriously. "One-on-one, a Puppet Master is weaker than a normal martial artist. But when they fight as an army, they are far more terrifying."
The light mood was gone. Everyone knew what would happen if a weak empire got its hands on a force like that.
"Do you have a plan?" Yu Qingyan asked, staring at You Fu. "You called us here, so you must have a way to deal with it."
"I do," You Fu said. "I need all of us to work together to smash the Nest in the provincial capital. We must wipe it out."
Wei He listened silently, unimpressed by the call to action.
First, no one here trusted each other. Second, no one knew if You Fu was telling the whole truth. Lastly, Wei He did not believe a few puppet soldiers could save a dying empire. The problems of Dayuan ran far deeper than that.
You Fu then signaled Mo Shiyan to show the evidence.
Mo Shiyan listed off several reports and had her men haul out a corpse.
It was a dead Puppet Master. The body looked human, but its skin was a strange, deep red.
Wei He glanced at it once, then lost interest.
After some debate, You Fu asked for their decision.
"Everyone here hates the Court. That is why you came. Now, we plan to march on the capital and crush the Nest. Who will join us?"
"I will!" a warrior with braided hair roared, jumping to his feet. "The governor killed my parents and stole my home! I will have my revenge!"
"Count us in!"
"We go together!"
Representatives from various groups shouted their support. They came here to fight anyway.
Wei He frowned and spoke up.
"My Thousand Poison Sect is broken. I was badly hurt fighting Zhu Chen, and I have not healed yet. Forgive me, Leader, but I cannot join this attack. However, I can provide my sect's special medicines to help."
His refusal stood out in the excited room.
Shortly after, two other leaders spoke up, saying their forces were crippled and could only offer supplies, not men.
"I understand," You Fu said calmly. "The Thousand Poison Sect lost ninety percent of its people fighting Zhu Chen. You have done enough. Your medicine is a big help."
He then excused the other two groups with valid reasons, easing the tension in the room so no one held a grudge against them.
Wei He watched him closely. You Fu was sharp and handled these rough, stubborn fighters with ease. He truly deserved to lead this alliance.
The discussion continued into the night. They promised money, troops, and supplies. Wei He handed over funds and a batch of anti-poison pills. Poison and antidotes were his sect's specialty, and these pills would give the attackers an edge.
By dawn, they finally agreed on a plan and secret passcodes.
The regional situation was tense, so everyone rushed off as soon as the meeting ended.
Yu Qingyan never said a word to Wei He. Wei He did not bother talking to her either.
He stayed on the boat a bit longer. Another middle-aged woman named Qian Xiao stayed behind as well.
"Sect Master Wei, Leader Qian. Is there something else?" You Fu stood on the upper deck, looking out over the quiet river.
Qian Xiao stepped forward, her face grim. "Leader, I need an answer."
"Speak."
Qian Xiao was over forty and looked like an ordinary woman from the market, but the sharp glare in her eyes showed she was a killer.
"I heard the Meteor Bandit came to Taizhou. Did your men fight him?"
"The Meteor Bandit... he is nothing," You Fu said softly. "It is the man behind him you should fear."
"Tell me!" Qian Xiao hissed. "He ruined my daughter. If I do not kill him, my family has no face left in the martial world!"
You Fu sighed. "Behind the Meteor Bandit is a branch of the Demon Faction. His master is one of their top killers, and he is in Taizhou right now. Those people are ghosts. I cannot trace them, and neither can you."
