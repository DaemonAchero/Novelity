Zhen Qi wanted to save the guard earlier, but the Dragon-Tailed Serpent moved too fast. Even she couldn't react in time.
It was Wei He who punched the snake and stunned it for a second, giving her the chance to leap forward and slice off its tail.
Now, Zhen Qi noticed Wei He glancing at her over and over, his eyes filled with suspicion.
She let out a cold snort.
"What are you looking at?"
"Miss, have you ever seen a small black bug that looks just like your Tracking Bug?" Wei He asked in a low voice.
As he spoke, he stared closely at her face, watching for any tiny change in her expression.
He had almost died from an assassin lured by those Black Word Bugs back then, and he still held that grudge in his heart.
"Black..." Zhen Qi felt a chill in her heart, but her face stayed completely calm. "So what if I've seen it? So what if I haven't?"
She hated lying and looked down on it, which was why Old Wang usually talked for her.
Being asked about the Black Word Bug in public pushed her to her limit.
"...I see." Wei He couldn't read her face through the veil, and her eyes revealed nothing.
"Either way, thank you for saving my sister." He cupped his hands again to show respect.
In his mind, he was almost sure that this woman was tied to the Black Word Bug.
If she really was the witch who owned those bugs, he remembered the first time he saw one. It had crawled out of the corpse of the thug Zhao Deli.
It was hard to say she didn't kill Zhao Deli and his son.
Killing a thug like Zhao Deli was fine since he deserved to die, but his son was just a child. Anyone who could kill a kid was truly ruthless.
Wei He believed in cutting roots to kill weeds, but meeting someone as cold as himself did not feel good.
Once he confirmed her link to the black bugs, he raised his guard.
Zhen Qi closed her eyes to rest and stopped talking.
Both groups rested for a bit. They used the dead men's personal items to build simple grave mounds in the woods. Then, in silence and lingering shock, they set off again.
It seemed their bad luck had run out.
The rest of the trip went smoothly. They killed the wild beasts they ran into or lured them away with meat bait.
The beast-repelling incense helped a lot too, keeping wild beasts far away and making the journey much safer.
After what happened earlier, Old Wang warmly invited Wei He and Qian Hao to join his group and travel together.
Walking as three groups together, especially with Wei He's strength, left a deep impression on him.
In a strange and dangerous land, having a stronger expert raised everyone's chances of survival.
The three groups merged into one. With three Blood Augmentation experts—Zhen Qi, Wei He, and Old Wang—guarding them, the group would be fine as long as they didn't run into crazy beasts like the Absolute Bird.
After fighting side by side several times, the tension among the three groups slowly broke down.
In the blink of an eye, another month passed.
They were now halfway through the Night Crow Mountain Range between Yun State and Tai State.
Heavy rain poured down, mixing with the howling wind and rustling tree leaves outside the broken temple, sounding like ghosts crying and wolves howling.
After leaving Yun State for over two months, Wei He and his group were much more mature than when they started.
The pride on Jiang Su's face was long gone. She was now lying on the ground, blowing into the campfire through a homemade bamboo tube.
Beside her, Wei Ying used bone needles and gut thread to sew different animal pelts together into rainproof cloaks for everyone.
Zhang Qi leaned on a piece of rotten wood nearby, using a charcoal pencil to draw a map.
Ever since Wei He found out Zhang Qi could read, he gave him this task.
Wei He stood in an empty spot nearby. His legs trembled slightly as blood and force vibrated through his leg muscles at a specific rhythm.
He was taking advantage of the spare time to practice the Flying Dragon Technique.
In another corner, Old Wang and Zhen Qi took their own spot and built their own fire.
Zhen Qi was reading a book carefully, fully focused.
The rest of the merchants and guards had lost their act. They no longer looked like merchants or guards at all.
Each of them had calm, focused eyes and moved like trained fighters. They were skilled, quiet, and clearly part of a single secret group.
In the last corner, Qian Hao sat alone wrapped in animal skin, eyes closed as if asleep.
He didn't talk much along the way, but he helped out during chaotic fights, so no one saw him as dead weight.
Wei He kept trembling his legs, using force to train his blood and drive the Flying Dragon Technique forward.
Today, his Flying Dragon Technique finally reached full mastery of the Second Layer.
This martial art was far harder to train than he expected.
Even his Five Peaks Palm and Mountain Returning Fist had visible progress, yet this last bit of the Flying Dragon Technique dragged on for over a month.
This was only the Second Layer, yet it was already this troublesome.
"At least it is finally maxed out." Wei He calmed his mind, pushing away random thoughts to focus.
With a single thought, the break-through bead on his chest—which had been full for a long time—suddenly shivered.
Surges of blood rushed out from his chest and poured straight into his legs.
In just a few seconds, hidden beneath his pants, Wei He's legs swelled up with blood. The muscle fibers grew thick, tough, and strong at high speed.
His tendons and bones were strengthened too, as if weeks of training were squeezed into a few breaths.
Soon, Wei He felt a purer and stronger force of blood flow back from his legs, joining the blood network in his whole body.
The total amount of blood in his body rose another step.
At the same time, his body felt lighter. His legs felt weightless yet grounded.
"Is this the Third Layer of the Flying Dragon Technique, Dash?"
Wei He pulled his collar open as if to catch some air, glancing down at the pattern on his chest.
The black mark was now one-third smaller.
"Breaking through the Flying Dragon Technique took one-third of the bead's energy... Looks like not every skill eats up a full bead. Now I know how it works."
Wei He let out a sigh of relief. It seemed the bead followed the rules of energy and wasn't random.
Feeling his blood level rise again, Wei He felt satisfied and walked back to his fire.
He noticed Wei Ying was no longer where she had been sitting.
Wei He frowned and looked around, quickly spotting his second sister by the other team's fire.
She was whispering with Zhen Qi, one talking while the other listened.
For some reason, ever since Zhen Qi saved Wei Ying, Wei Ying had made an animal pelt coat and given it to her as a gift one night. The two started talking and never stopped.
These two were from completely different worlds, yet chatting became a regular habit for them.
Every few days, Wei Ying would sit with Zhen Qi to chat for a while.
No one knew how they had so much to talk about.
At first, Wei He kept a close eye on Wei Ying out of fear that something bad might happen. But over time, seeing that Zhen Qi meant no harm and had saved his sister, he slowly let his guard down.
Still, he had no idea how two women with nothing in common could get along so well.
A short while later, Wei Ying walked back in a good mood.
"Look, Sister Zhen Qi gave me this poison-clearing salve. She said rubbing it on keeps mosquitoes and poison bugs away, and it's good for the skin too."
She held a small pot of light green paste inside a semi-clear jar with gold letters on the outside. It clearly wasn't cheap.
"Stay away from that side from now on," Wei He said with a frown.
That Zhen Qi was likely linked to the Black Word Bug. He felt he needed to warn his sister.
"Don't worry, Sister Zhen Qi isn't who you think she is. She is actually very nice, she just..." Wei Ying tried to explain, but a loud cough from Zhen Qi cut her off.
She quickly closed her mouth.
"Alright, I'll stop talking. I'm going back to sewing the coats."
Wei He looked back at Zhen Qi. She gave a cold snort and turned her head away.
"Brother Wei," Old Wang called out with a smile from the side.
"You don't need to worry, Brother Wei. I'm sure you can tell we aren't a real merchant team, but we aren't bad people either. Spending this much time together, some things can't be hidden."
"..." Wei He stayed quiet. If it were just one or two people, he might doubt their acting. But with this many people, it was impossible for every single one to be an actor.
From how the rest of the group acted, he could tell they were not cruel or bloodthirsty. They didn't look like people who killed for fun.
"Brother Wei, why don't you come over and chat? Earlier, I saw you practicing your martial arts. But I am a bit confused. Aren't you a fist user?" Old Wang asked.
"I also practice leg techniques," Wei He answered calmly. There was no need to hide this. During past fights, besides hiding his Five Peaks Palm force, his Flying Dragon Technique and Mountain Returning Fist were already exposed.
"You train in leg techniques too? Is this leg technique part of your fist style?" Old Wang frowned.
"No." Wei He felt the old man was crossing the line with his questions.
"Don't mind me, Brother Wei. I just have some advice I want to give you ahead of time." Old Wang stroked his beard.
"When it comes to blood techniques, it is best to stick to just one. Otherwise, once you reach Tai State, it might lead to bad results."
"Oh? What do you mean by that?" Wei He was puzzled.
Old Wang stroked his beard, looking back on his memories.
"My strength is average, but I've lived a long life and seen a lot. My words might sound harsh, so please don't take offense, Brother Wei."
"Please speak, Old Wang." Wei He nodded. Back at Hidden Sword Gorge, Old Wang had stopped him from killing to prevent the Absolute Bird from going wild at the sight of blood.
He understood why later on.
Because of that, he held no bad feelings toward the old man.
"It goes like this," Old Wang recalled. "My master once said that blood art is like a person; it has different levels. The most basic blood is called Source Blood.
It is the foundation of foundations, which can be grown through basic exercise and daily living.
Later on, after learning martial arts and breaking through to the Second Layer of blood, the Source Blood starts to split into different martial properties.
This is called Stamp Blood, blood with a mark.
Different arts create different Stamp Blood. At first, you won't feel it. But once you reach the Force level, the forces created by Stamp Blood will differ. If you are lucky, they help each other. If you are unlucky, they clash."
