Taizhou Prefecture, Black Cloud Inn.
Inside a secluded, detached small building near the corner of the inn compound, four people sat around a cheap, freshly bought map: Wei He, Jiang Su, Wei Ying, and Zhang Qi.
Silence fell over the room after Wei He finished sharing his experience at the Wushi Sect.
"We... we traveled all this way," Wei Ying said softly, her voice heavy with grief. "Linlin didn't even make it... and this is the result?"
They had started this journey with high hopes for the Wushi Sect. None of them expected things to end like this.
"The Wushi Sect is massive and powerful," Jiang Su said coldly. "They only care about geniuses and people with real value. It's normal for them to ignore people from small, distant towns like us."
After enduring so many hardships, her face remained as cold as it was back at the Rebounding Mountain Fist Academy. But inside, she was far tougher now. She was no longer the clueless girl who would panic at the first sign of trouble.
She looked up at Wei He.
"Wei He, what do you plan to do?" She knew he was a man of few words, but he always had strong ideas. He must have a plan.
"I..." Wei He took a deep breath and pressed his hand lightly against the map. "Taizhou Prefecture isn't just a single city. It's a huge region made of many forts, small cities, and towns. The main city is the center, surrounded by satellite cities and countless towns. It's at least ten times bigger than Feiye City."
"What are you getting at?" Jiang Su asked, sensing his train of thought.
"I'm saying that while the Wushi Sect is the strongest power here, they aren't the only option," Wei He said gravely. "I asked around. The Wushi Sect is number one in Taizhou, followed by five other major factions. These five factions were once glorious. Times changed, and the Wushi Sect took the top spot. So..."
"But what about our main martial arts technique?" Jiang Su asked, cutting straight to the core problem.
The Rebounding Mountain Fist they practiced was actually called the Wushi Rebounding Mountain Art. Only the Wushi Sect had the complete style. More importantly, only the Wushi Sect had steady access to the specific mutated beast meat needed for higher levels of training. That was why the Wushi Sect was so arrogant.
Their monopoly on the full technique and the matching meat made them the best place to train. Even if someone wanted to leave the sect, it was nearly impossible to find similar beast meat elsewhere to make up for it. Using their vast wealth and power, the Wushi Sect had long since claimed or driven away all mutated beasts tied to the technique. They refused to let outsiders touch them.
Wei He had already gathered this information. Bribing people with gold made open secrets like this easy to get. In fact, all major factions did the same thing. They locked down the specific beast meat required for their core techniques to prevent their secrets from leaking.
At lower levels, you could eat any meat to boost your blood vitality. But at higher levels, the type of meat mattered. Eating the right meat over time sped up power cultivation, boosted the chances of breaking through bottlenecks, and adjusted the body to fit the technique better.
"So we don't really have a choice, do we?" Jiang Su said plainly.
"What do you want to say?" Wei He looked up, expecting her answer.
"I just don't want to be weak anymore." Jiang Su stood up and pulled back a strand of hair near her face. She walked to the window and looked outside.
The setting sun painted everything crimson. Merchants, travelers, drivers, and commoners flowed down the street like water. Their faces showed things rarely seen in Feiye City: hope, stability, and a future.
The trade here was far richer than in Feiye City, and the society was far more stable. It was the perfect place for her to build a life. Feiye City was destroyed, and her family's faction had lost its gamble. She wasn't just living for herself anymore; she carried the hopes of her remaining family. She wanted to gain power and return to Feiye City to retrieve them.
"Leaving the Wushi Sect means starting over from scratch. Wei He, I don't have that kind of time," Jiang Su said, turning back to face him. "I've spent almost ten years practicing the Rebounding Mountain Fist. How many decades do I have left in life? I can't afford to gamble a second time."
Wei He stood up as well and looked at the others.
Wei Ying seemed to sense what was coming. She kept her head down and clutched her dress tightly, staying quiet.
Zhang Qi was different. He raised his head and looked directly into Wei He's eyes.
"Brother Wei, I feel the same as Sister Jiang. I don't want to leave either."
"...What do you mean?" Wei He asked calmly.
Regarding Zhang Qi, Wei He only took care of him because he promised Zhang Lu he would bring him to safety. Arriving in Taizhou Prefecture meant that duty was fulfilled. Zhang Qi was free to choose his own life. But as a junior he had brought along the entire way, Wei He still had to ask.
"My brother died in Feiye City because he was too weak. The Seven-Alliance was destroyed because it was too weak," Zhang Qi said earnestly. "In this chaotic world, being weak is a sin. The weak are just bugs!"
He stared fixedly into Wei He's eyes.
"Brother Wei, I want to become strong! The strongest! Stronger than anyone else! That's the only way to survive... I don't want to rely on others all the time. I don't want to rely on you or Sister Jiang anymore. I want to rely on myself."
Wei He had no words. He couldn't count how many times he had been left speechless like this. Whether it was Jiang Su or Zhang Qi, every one of them had a reason that left him silent.
Zhang Qi didn't stop. He stood up and continued, "From what I can see, the Wushi Sect is the strongest. If I want to become the strongest, that's where I must go. It's the fastest path!"
"You guys..." Wei He started to speak, but stopped.
Things had come to this point. There was nothing left to say. They hadn't experienced what he went through during his visit to the Wushi Sect. Besides, they were only temporary traveling companions. He had no right or obligation to control their lives. It was normal for them to make their own choices.
Wei He knew that parting ways was now bound to happen. Jiang Su and Zhang Qi both had their own thoughts and goals. They weren't his followers. They just wanted to build the lives they wanted in this chaotic world.
"Zhang Qi and I will both apply to the Wushi Sect," Jiang Su said directly. "We aren't as strong as you, so we'll sign up as low-level recruits and take the tests. If we fail, we'll figure something else out later."
"I understand," Wei He said.
This was their choice. Everyone had their own life, and he couldn't interfere.
He walked over to the rough map and ran his fingers across the paper, feeling its crude texture against his skin.
"Do you have enough money?" he asked.
"Yes," Jiang Su nodded.
"My brother left me plenty. It's enough," Zhang Qi added.
"When are you leaving?" Wei He drew back his hand and asked softly.
"Tomorrow morning. There's no time to waste," Jiang Su replied.
She had always been decisive, and the long journey from Yunzhou to Taizhou had made her even more practical. Independent, self-reliant, and capable of holding her own ground. She was never a weak woman, proven by how she took night watches alone without a single mistake. It was only that Wei He's strength was too bright, casting a shadow over her.
"I'm getting older, and breaking through to the Third Blood stage is hard now. But the Wushi Sect has deep roots, so there might be other ways to fix that! This is Taizhou, not Feiye City. I'll find a way!" Jiang Su said with conviction.
Wei He nodded.
With things stated so clearly, further words were useless. The room fell into silence.
A moment later, Jiang Su and Zhang Qi said their goodbyes and returned to their rooms to pack for tomorrow's registration.
Only Wei Ying remained, sitting by the table. The room that was full of life just a moment ago was now empty except for the two of them. She knew parting was near. Having spent almost a year together every single day, she was used to having them around. The sudden change filled her heart with sadness.
"Don't worry. I'll handle everything," Wei He said calmly.
He felt the sadness too, but his willpower was strong enough to push it down. Every feast must come to an end. A day of parting with Jiang Su and Zhang Qi was bound to come; no one expected it to happen so soon.
"I... I'll go rest in my room for a bit," Wei Ying said, standing up with a low voice.
"Go ahead. Don't worry, they're just going to the Wushi Sect. We might see them again in the future," Wei He comforted her.
"I know..." Wei Ying nodded. She kept her head down, walked out with small steps, and gently closed the door.
Wei He was left alone in the room, standing silently in front of the map.
Outside the window, the evening sun sank lower, throwing long shadows across the floor.
After a long time, he took a charcoal pencil and gently drew five circles on the map. These five circles represented the five major factions of Taizhou besides the Wushi Sect: the Lishan Sect, the Danyang Sect, the Tianyin Sect, the Jiangzhe Temple, and the Taizhou State Army.
Sizzle.
The sound of cooking oil came from a neighboring house, breaking his train of thought. The rich smell of sour and spicy shredded pork drifted in through the window.
Below the window, a few young men from rich families rode past on horses, dressed in fine clothes. They laughed and chatted in the local Taizhou dialect about the girls at a place called Night Waterfront.
Wei He watched them ride away, then packed up the map and stepped out of the room.
He had rented this entire small building using gold bills. Gold was very valuable to normal people. Renting this place for ten days only cost five taels of gold. The building was quiet and isolated, perfect for avoiding unwanted attention. The only downside was that it was hard to get fresh news here.
Wei He left the building and entered the main hall of the Black Cloud Inn. Waiters ran back and forth between tables, serving food and drinks to guests.
Without wasting time, Wei He walked straight to the innkeeper.
"Sir, besides the Wushi Sect, where else in Taizhou can someone learn martial arts? To be honest, I was just rejected by the Wushi Sect's entrance test."
Wei He spoke directly and slapped a few gold nuggets onto the counter.
The innkeeper was just a normal person, and gold like this was a huge extra income for him. He wasn't a martial artist. The gap between commoners and martial artists was huge because martial artists spent vast amounts of money on their training and food.
