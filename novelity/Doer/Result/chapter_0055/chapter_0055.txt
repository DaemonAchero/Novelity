Wei He watched the whole drama unfold with a strange expression.
Last night, he had only stepped out to take a break while revising his secret manuals. He never expected Jiang Su to go through so many emotional swings in her head.
Even if he knew, he would not care.
He helped Jiang Su purely out of basic duty toward a fellow student. As for anything else, he never gave it a thought.
Seeing Jiang Su and Xiao Ran break up, Wei He did not care much about either of them, but he still felt a bit of cheap joy at their bad luck.
Soon, he pushed it out of his mind and headed into the inner yard. He needed to find Master Zheng to get his monthly share of Red Land Fish.
Now that he no longer had Silver-Kiss Black Snake meat, he relied on this fish to build his blood energy.
At first, he thought about hunting wild beasts himself for meat. But now he was close to breaking through. The Break-Through Pearl was only a tiny bit away from being full.
His Five Ridge Palm blood energy was already enough. He could break through the third blood energy level of Five Ridge Palm at any time and step into the fourth realm.
Once his power jumped to that level, hunting would be much safer and easier.
So he kept enduring.
Unlike his fellow students, he did not get stuck at bottlenecks. He only needed the Break-Through Pearl to fill up to break through.
Waiting a bit longer did not matter.
He stepped into the inner yard.
Master Zheng was talking in low, heavy voices with two tall men in rich silk robes.
The three made no effort to hide their voices. Their cold snorts echoed through the whole yard from time to time.
One of the two men was tall and wild, with a full beard and two iron rulers strapped to his back.
The other had sharp leopard eyes and a thin goat beard. He dressed half like a scholar and half like a warrior, looking like a general wearing a scholar's long robe.
"Please leave, you two. What my Return-Mountain Punch said before still stands," Zheng Fugui said, his tone hard as iron.
"Old Zheng, why be so stubborn? In times like this, only those who adapt can survive for long," the man with leopard eyes said softly, stroking his beard.
"Hmph. I know what your Zhao family is planning. Save the nonsense and leave," Zheng Fugui sneered.
The two men did not get angry. This was not their first time coming to persuade the Return-Mountain Punch sect. It was just the stormiest visit so far.
They cupped their hands toward Old Zheng, turned, and walked away without looking at anyone else.
Wei He stood by the inner yard gate and watched them leave. His heart sank a little.
Rolling blood energy surged from the two men. As they brushed past him, he felt their long, powerful breathing and the thick, heavy pulse of blood in their bodies.
They were only two men, yet they gave him the same heavy pressure as a huge wild beast.
Wei He was no longer a rookie hunter. He had run into big wild beasts on the mountain before, like gray bears and black tigers.
Beasts in this world were much larger than the ones in his past memory.
Gray bears stood over three meters tall. Giant tigers measured three meters long without even counting their tails.
They looked like they grew on steroids, possessing immense power and speed. Wei He never dared to fight them head-on and always dodged early whenever he spotted them from afar.
He had confidence in his strength, but he was not stupid enough to face such monsters recklessly. Especially after he saw a gray bear smash a big bluestone into pieces with one slap—the very same kind of stone he used for punch practice—he became even more careful.
Even if he hit a stone that size with all his power, he could only leave a fist mark at most, not crack it apart.
Using poison could kill them, but then he could not eat the meat. So it was better to avoid them for now. Once he broke through and felt sure of his odds, he would come back to hunt them as food stock.
Only after the two men left did Wei He walk slowly into the inner yard.
"Master Zheng," he said in a low voice.
Zheng Fugui felt heavy and annoyed inside, but hearing the voice, he did not want to pass his bad mood to his inner student.
He pulled his thoughts together and forced a smile toward Wei He.
"What is it, Xiao He? Is something wrong with your training?"
"Nothing wrong. It is just... Master Zheng, I came to collect my Red Land Fish for this month," Wei He said with a helpless look.
He did not want to ask for meat every month like this, but he had no choice. He could not hunt wild beasts on his own yet.
And after seeing those giant beasts on the mountain, he doubted if he could hunt any non-poisonous wild beasts safely.
It was better to stay quiet and act after breaking through.
"Go find Aunt Red to get it," Zheng Fugui looked at Wei He. He wanted to ask about his progress, but remembering the bad result from last time, he lost all drive to ask.
'If I give all the Red Land Fish to Xiao Ran, could he go one step further?' My own share, plus Wei He's share, all given to Xiao Ran, maybe...
The thought popped into Zheng Fugui's mind. But after a short pause, he let it go.
Xiao Ran had backing from so many places that he did not care about a few small fish.
As for Wei He, without the Cheng family supplying him beast meat, he could not afford to lose this bit of blood energy.
Zheng Fugui waved his hand weakly, telling Wei He to leave.
He placed all his hopes on Xiao Ran now. If Xiao Ran broke through, the Return-Mountain Punch sect would finally have a strong future.
Also, if a Iron-Skin expert at the third blood energy level stood guard at the sect, he could leave to handle other matters instead of being stuck in one place every day.
The two men from the Zhao family came from the second and third branches. Both were experts at the third blood energy level, skilled in team fighting. Their brutal power was famous across the entire Seven-Family Alliance.
If those two worked together against an old man like him whose body was half in the grave, he might not even win.
Wei He walked out of the inner yard holding a bag. He went home first, then headed straight for the inner city.
His Break-Through Pearl was just one small step away from being full. To fill it up faster, he decided to spend his money on Flower-Branch Deer meat and eat it all together to break through.
He had saved money for a long time to get this small stash. It was enough to buy a whole month's share of meat, bring it home, and eat it slowly to push for a breakthrough in one go.
He walked down the increasingly quiet streets, following the main road that cut through the city.
Soon, Wei He entered the inner city. Even this rich area seemed slightly affected by the troubles outside.
It was said that everything in the inner city was self-made. Feiye City had two special gates used only for the inner city to ensure plenty of supplies.
Though called an inner city, its size kept growing and now took up almost half of Feiye City.
The outer city grew more run-down by the day. A huge number of people slowly became servants for the inner city.
Those who could not find work either left for far places or joined small forts and outside gangs. The rest barely survived by running tiny businesses.
By now, true city dwellers were getting fewer and fewer.
Wei He smoothly bought a month's share of Flower-Branch Deer meat from Zuihua Tower.
This meat took almost all his savings. Business had been bad lately, and the bandits outside the city seemed to make less money too.
Because of that, Wei He only managed to save this little bit.
Carrying the meat in a woven bamboo bag, Wei He walked back toward the outer city's Stone Bridge area.
Everything was ready. His Break-Through Pearl was just a hair away from full. By his own guess, it would take half a month at most.
Eating all this Flower-Branch Deer meat together with the fresh Red Land Fish would just about fill it.
The deer meat alone could cut down his blood energy training time by half a month.
This showed just how powerful wild beast meat was.
Sometimes, Wei He wondered how nice it would be if he had reborn into a rich family in this world. He could eat beast meat whenever he wanted, instead of fighting for every single meal like he did now.
Holding his bag, Wei He walked fast toward home.
Yet just a few steps out of the inner city, a man stepped out from an alley on his right, blocking his path.
"Long time no see, Senior Brother Wei. You just came out from the inner city. What were you doing?"
The man was Xiao Ran, whom he had just seen earlier.
Xiao Ran had a half-smile on his face, but his eyes were full of anger and annoyance as he stared straight at Wei He without moving.
He stood right in the middle of the path Wei He wanted to take.
"Junior Brother Xiao? Why are you blocking my way? Is something wrong?" Wei He said calmly. Though he spoke lightly, he already guessed why Xiao Ran blocked him.
The man failed his attempt last night, felt mad about it, and now came to pick a fight to vent his rage.
However...
Wei He gently put down his bag and prepared to speak again.
Suddenly, the shadow in front of him flashed. Xiao Ran leaped forward and threw a direct punch straight at his chest using the Front Mountain path.
The Front Mountain path was the fastest striking move in Return-Mountain Punch, famous for its sudden start and high speed.
With Xiao Ran striking first, his fist turned into a grey streak that flashed right in front of Wei He's chest in an instant.
Bang!
Wei He raised his elbow, forcing the punch upward so it missed its target.
He immediately used Return-Mountain Punch to fight back face to face.
Right there on the open street, two pairs of fists turned greyish-white in a short moment.
Both of them were going all out.
Soon, Xiao Ran let out a cold laugh. He threw a hook punch, but shifted its path three times mid-air, coming through Wei He's guard at a strange angle and brushing past his cheek.
It almost hit Wei He right in the face.
Wei He breathed fast and stepped back several paces using the force. He took a small loss in that short clash. Not only was his face scraped, but his arms were also swollen and aching. His blood energy was far weaker than Xiao Ran's in a head-on clash.
When the other man used blood energy to boost his body, his physical strength and fist power were far above his own.
He truly lived up to his name as a top genius.
This natural gift of body strength was indeed hard for normal people to match.
In a direct fight, he really could not beat Xiao Ran. The gap was not small.
Wei He calculated the difference in his mind and had to admit the other man's power and gift.
"Junior Brother Xiao, is that enough?" he asked calmly.
"Not enough!"
Seeing Wei He's calm face made Xiao Ran burst with rage inside.
Thinking about how Jiang Su was almost his, yet ended up slipping away while this guy got off easy, he felt as disgusted as if he had swallowed a fly.
Anger surged in his heart. Spotting the bag of meat on the ground beside Wei He, he rushed forward in a quick stride.
After two heavy thuds, Wei He was pushed back a few steps. Just as he caught his balance, he saw the meat bag get kicked into the air.
Xiao Ran sneered and punched hard right into the center of the bag.
Bang!
The woven bamboo exploded. The meat inside took the full hit of the heavy power, ripping into several pieces that shot off in different directions.
Wei He could not stop it in time. Facing a fellow student inside the city, he did not dare to use hidden poisonous moves easily.
While he was still hesitating, Xiao Ran smashed the meat bag with one punch. Feeling a rush of panic, Wei He stepped forward to grab it.
Sadly, he was one step too late.
The chunks of meat flew far away under the strong force, sailing over the rooftops and landing on other streets tens of meters away.
