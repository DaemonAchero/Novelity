Deep in the Mountains The third year of the Qing Sect, October, Autumn.
Li Tong of the Xiangqu Cult pressed the Xuanjing region again. After a two-week standoff with General Yao Xiong of the Tai'an Army, he launched a sudden strike on Chiyu City and crushed its defenders. But at the final moment, Taoist Shi Jing, the Dao Child of the Wushi Sect, ambushed Li Tong. Severely wounded, Li Tong retreated.
Rustle.
Wei He crumpled the letter into fine ash and tossed it into the trash bin.
"Troubled times..." He sighed, threw on a loose robe, and walked out.
Beyond the wooden building lay a wide open field. Over a hundred new recruits stood in neat formation. They punched and kicked in unison, shouting with each strike.
Leading them were the survivors of the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect. Only a hundred of these veterans remained, but their loyalty ran deep. Using them as instructors to teach the basics and instill loyalty was the best approach.
Wei He turned left, crossed the hallway, and leaped from the side of the building. His movements were lightning fast. In a flash, he cleared the open field and the wooden huts, using a rooftop for a final boost to vault into a bamboo forest.
Past the forest, a broken cliff rose upward. On top sat a smooth, round stone platform where several elders of the sect were gathered.
Wan Ling, her injuries slightly improved, was among them. Beside her were Xiao Qingyu, who was still badly hurt, and Du Han, who looked grim. They were discussing something, but turned to face Wei He the moment he arrived.
"Sect Master," Du Han said in a deep voice. "We have hidden in the Black House Mountains, but our food is running low. The poison fog prevents us from farming much, and wild food is scarce. We need to buy supplies from outside. We were just discussing where to go."
Du Han had not fought in the recent battle. Driven by guilt, he was eager to prove his worth.
"The Xiangqu rebels are right outside, and the Tai'an Army is hunting deserters," Wei He said softly. "We cannot travel freely without drawing attention. That leaves us few choices."
"What about Jinzhou?" Du Han suggested. "Yue Xiaodie was ransomed back to the Wanfei Palace earlier. We could use her as a contact."
"Her family was extorted, and you want to trade with the extortionist?" Xiao Qingyu scoffed.
"Anyone else would refuse, but Jinzhou is different," Du Han replied seriously.
"No need to go to another province," Wei He asked. "Are there options nearby besides Xuanjing?"
"To the west and north are wild forests," Du Han analyzed. "To the south is Xuanjing, but the rebels just left, so we cannot buy there yet. That leaves the east. Five hundred miles east is Rongcheng, controlled by the Purple Moon Sword Sect."
"Purple Moon Sword Sect..." Wei He narrowed his eyes, recalling a young man from the alliance meeting. That youth was about his age and claimed to be the current heir of the sect, holding average strength among the alliance leaders back then.
"We can reach out to them," Wei He mused. "Send an official letter in my name to ask about buying grain. It might work."
"Understood," Du Han nodded.
"Sect Master," Wan Ling spoke softly from the side. Since the final battle with Zhu Chen, she was no longer as casual with him. Around others, she addressed him formally as Sect Master, only acting as his mother-in-law and master in private. "While traveling outside, we heard new rumors about you in Taizhou."
"Why care about rumors, Master? They mean nothing," Wei He said.
"This rumor summarizes the top martial artists in Taizhou quite well," Wan Ling said. "I suspect someone is pushing it intentionally."
"Oh?" Wei He paused. "Tell me."
"Someone compiled all the top fighters in Taizhou into a book," Wan Ling explained. "There are no rankings. Instead, it is divided into Gold Wind and Jade Dew. The Gold Wind section lists the top men, while the Jade Dew section lists the women."
"I heard of this too," Du Han added. "It details their backgrounds, martial styles, and personalities. Whoever made this has deep intelligence gathering."
"Ignore it," Wei He said. "Someone is just trying to exploit the chaos. Their motives do not concern us. We only need to buy food, hide deep in the mountains, and rebuild our strength."
The others agreed.
With Wei He's reputation, the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect had gained fame across Taizhou. It was now listed among One Sect, Four Paths, and Seven Heroes.
The One Sect was the Wushi Sect. The Four Paths were Danyang Gate, Lishan Faction, Jianzhe Temple, and the Extermination Alliance.
The Ten Thousand Poisons Sect was one of the Seven Heroes—the seven strongest surviving factions outside the top five. These seven had fought back fiercely against the government's raid on martial artists. Most had suffered heavy losses, but many war talents had emerged. A few had achievements matching Wei He, capable of defying Mind Sense masters.
Though far from the past glory of the Heavenly Seal Sect, this recognition was fair for their current size.
"Taizhou has thirteen cities," Wei He ordered. "We will visit multiple places if needed. We will find food."
"Understood."
"One last thing," Wei He paused. "Tomorrow, I will marry Senior Sister Qingqing. The sect will celebrate."
The three were stunned, then overjoyed. In these dark times, a grand wedding could boost morale.
The wedding took place deep in the mountains. There was no fine wine or lavish meat, only wild fruits and simple dishes. But Wei He wanted this wedding to settle the hearts of his people. After so much struggle, the sect had finally found peace.
The celebration lasted three days. No outsiders were invited. Only Wang Shaojun was informed, and he briefly stopped by to leave a gift without even meeting Wei He. On the other hand, Wanfei Palace sent a gift signed by Yue Xiaodie.
After the wedding, Wei He and Wan Qingqing became husband and wife, easing the sect's grief. Red decorations briefly replaced the black mourning clothes.
Afterward, Wei He left daily chores to Wan Ling, Xiao Qingyu, and the elders. Besides practicing martial arts, he spent his time with his wife. He knew that in this chaotic world, only strength could protect what he held dear. He pushed his training relentlessly.
The sect's experts managed to capture and trade for mutated beasts, establishing nearly twenty meat fields. For just two hundred people, this was more than enough.
The food supply ran into a minor issue along the way, but Xie Yan handled it herself, easily crushing the bandits who tried to rob them. Meanwhile, they established a trade route with Wanfei Palace. With both the Purple Moon Sword Sect and Wanfei Palace as suppliers, they were no longer cheated on prices.
Everything settled into a routine. The sect adapted to the quiet life in the mountains, ignoring the outside world and focusing solely on cultivation.
Over a year passed in the blink of an eye.
Using the Breaking-Bound Bead and his refined body, Wei He finally broke through the final bottleneck of the Heavenly Seal Nine Strikes, pushing all nine force layers into the Force Entry stage.
The nine layers of force joined into a complete cycle, wrapping around his body like heavy armor.
Around the same time, Wan Qingqing began morning sickness. A diagnosis confirmed she was pregnant.
With his martial breakthrough and his wife's pregnancy, Wei He hosted another feast. This celebration was far livelier, as their supplies were much richer now.
Having secured their hold in the area, the sect's influence spread to nearby strongholds and hidden factions. Outlaws and hermit sects in the Black House Mountains all came to deal with them. Slowly, the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect built a formidable name in this region.
Music played as female disciples performed graceful sword dances, drawing loud cheers from the guests. At nearby tables, disciples drank, laughed, and caught up with visiting friends. Farther away, disciples hung colorful floral strings between the trees, creating a festive view.
Wei He gave a brief toast on stage before joining Wan Qingqing to greet the guests. As he reached the final table, a familiar figure stood up.
"Old Wei. Long time no see."
The man was handsome, with pale skin and an elegant yet subtle air.
It was Wang Shaojun, whom he had not seen in over a year.
"Old Wang..." Wei He was surprised. He had not seen Wang Shaojun since leaving Xuanjing. Even at his wedding, Wang Shaojun had only dropped off a gift and left.
