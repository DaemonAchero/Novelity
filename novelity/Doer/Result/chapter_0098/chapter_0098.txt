Wei Ying recognized him. He was the only son of their neighbor, the Wang family—Wang Qubing. She had seen him when they first moved in.
Wei Ying locked eyes with Wang Qubing. His creepy gaze made her skin crawl, so she turned away and hurried her step.
She hadn't gone far when a sharp pain shot through her butt.
It felt like something had hit her hard.
She turned around and saw a jagged rock covered in wet mud rolling to a stop on the ground.
Her butt burned with pain. She glared at Wang Qubing and saw him flashing a smug smile. He raised his hand toward her and squeezed his fingers together in a crude grabbing motion.
"Big! Hahaha, so big!" he laughed foolishly.
Embarrassed and angry, Wei Ying hurried forward to get away from him.
This wasn't the first time. Being new to this unfamiliar place, she didn't want to bring trouble upon herself, so she had kept quiet.
Her younger brother had told her that this place was near the Heavenly Seal Sect. It was right by a grand sect, completely different from a remote town like Feiye City. She had to be careful everywhere she went.
So she swallowed her shame and rage, kept her head down, and moved faster.
"Big... so big... hahaha..." The stupid voice kept trailing behind her.
A middle-aged man with a smoking pipe walked out next to Wang Qubing. The man chuckled and patted his son on the head. Instead of stopping him, he looked at Wei Ying with the same dirty smile and let out a sleazy laugh.
"Come on, time to go back and eat," the man said, patting his son's head again. They both turned back toward their yard.
As soon as they turned, they crashed straight into a solid wall.
No, it wasn't a wall. It was a man's chest.
A massive, towering guy over six feet tall stood there. He was shirtless, with thick muscles bulging all over, looking down at them coldly.
He was built like a bear, easily twice as wide as the two of them combined.
Father and son hit his chest hard and bounced right back to where they started, dizzy and dazed.
"Is it big?" the muscular man asked.
"...Big, hehe," Wang Qubing grinned like a fool. "So big!"
"I'll show you big!"
SLAP!
The giant swung a heavy palm across his face. The slap twisted the boy's face, drew blood, and sent a few teeth flying out of his mouth.
Before Wang Qubing could even hit the ground, the man grabbed him by his long hair, pulled him back, and slammed his knee straight into his stomach.
THUD.
Wang Qubing groaned in agony, curling up on the ground like a shrimp. His face swelled up fast like a steamed bun, completely ruining his features.
The big man picked him up by his collar again.
"Now you're bigger than me."
He tossed the boy aside and turned his eyes to the pale-faced father.
"My brother-in-law knows people in the Inner Court of the Heavenly Seal Sect! You dare touch us?!" the father screamed in terror.
"Shut up," the muscular man said coldly. "I am from the Inner Court."
He grabbed the father's collar, yanked him close, and started beating him mercilessly.
Beating people up was an art, and Wei He had always believed that.
Especially now that his strength had grown immensely. With three blood martial arts under his belt, all focusing on hand techniques—fist, palm, and finger—his raw power was terrifying.
The three blood forces merged into inner energy, boosting his hands to a level he couldn't even measure.
He only knew that his current blood energy was far greater than when he first broke through. It was at least three times stronger.
Even though standard training guides claimed that mixing different blood forces would mess up future breakthroughs, it didn't matter right now. At his current level, his sheer blood force was far superior to anyone else's.
After a brutal beating right on the street, Wei He left the father and son lying on the dirt, unable to move, battered and swollen like two bloody pigs. Only then did his mood improve a little.
Releasing them, Wei He cracked his knuckles and looked around. The passing townsfolk kept their heads down and hurried past, not daring to look at him.
In nearby yards, a few neighbors peeked through cracks in windows and doors, but nobody stepped out to stand up for the two creeps.
"You... just you wait! I'll call people to beat you to death when I get back!!" the father groaned from the dirt, still trying to act tough.
"Beat me to death?" A cold glint flashed in Wei He's eyes. He yanked the man up by the neck.
CRACK.
His hands moved like lightning. With a few quick snaps, he broke both of the man's arms, then both of his legs.
The father screamed like a dying pig, thrashing around, but he couldn't break free from Wei He's grip.
Beside him, Wang Qubing was almost wetting his pants in fear. He cowered against the corner of the wall, trembling and afraid to move an inch.
"Alright. Tell me, who in the Inner Court are you calling to beat me to death? Let me see if I know him," Wei He said as he threw the man back onto the ground with a cold smile.
He had already looked into the neighbors before moving in. The father's brother-in-law did know someone in the Heavenly Seal Sect, but that person was just an Outer Court grunt.
In truth, many people in Tianyin Town had some small connection to the sect.
But connections were totally different in strength.
The Outer Court and the Inner Court were two completely different worlds.
Outer Court members were just trainees, while those in the Inner Court were on the path to becoming true core disciples.
The entire Inner Court had less than a hundred disciples in total. Meanwhile, hundreds of people cycled in and out of the Outer Court every single year.
No matter how often the Outer Court changed, the disciples of the Nine Inner Courtyards stood firm like solid rocks.
Their status was night and day.
The father was in so much pain that he couldn't speak another word, lying on the ground groaning in agony.
Wei He didn't bother with him anymore. He walked over and broke both of Wang Qubing's arms as well.
Satisfied with his work, he turned around and walked after Wei Ying.
Wei Ying hadn't walked too far. She was pressing her hand against the mud stain on her skirt, trying hard to wipe it off.
But the dark mark wouldn't come out.
"Sister," Wei He called out from behind.
Wei Ying flinched and turned around quickly to face him. "Why are you back so early?" She forced a small smile.
"I officially joined the Inner Court today, so I came back early to celebrate together," Wei He smiled back.
"I see... Then I should hurry and buy some groceries. I haven't bought anything yet. Go home and rest, I'll be back soon," Wei Ying said quickly.
"It's fine, I'll go with you," Wei He said gently, making no mention of the mud stain on her back.
He unknotted the coat tied around his waist and draped it over her shoulders, covering the dirty spot completely.
"Look at you, coming out so early without wearing enough. It's getting colder every day."
"What about you? You're walking around shirtless," Wei Ying countered.
"I train in martial arts, so it's different," Wei He said as he tied the coat straps for her. With a quick glance, it looked just like a short cloak.
Wei Ying suddenly realized that he had already noticed the mud mark. Seeing her brother's thoughtful action, she looked at him quietly.
"Let's go back and change clothes first," she decided firmly, giving up on buying groceries for now.
"I tripped earlier and got my clothes dirty. You saw it, didn't you?" She admitted it herself with a light smile.
It wasn't a fake smile. As long as her brother was back, she felt truly safe.
Wei He didn't point it out either.
The two walked back to their courtyard. They didn't go back out for food. Instead, they used the ingredients at home and cooked a hearty meal together.
After eating his fill, Wei He took out a small manual and sat down in the courtyard. Listening to the faint groans coming from next door, he slowly flipped through the pages.
The manual explained in detail how to refine blood force into inner energy once a warrior reached the Three Bloods stage.
At this stage, a warrior's blood force would transform into specialized Seal Blood based on their martial art.
Different Seal Blood generated different types of inner energy.
The Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Art worked the same way.
This manual detailed the first layer: how to refine and expand the Rain-Covering Seal blood force until it covered the entire body.
When every part of the body—except the seven facial orifices—was filled with this special blood force, the first layer would be complete.
"No wonder the power gap at the Inner Energy stage is so massive. The blood force difference alone is huge."
"Just as Senior Brother Kong Ran said, if the inner energy you cultivate is weak, you'll still be useless even after reaching this stage. Even if your body is full of inner energy, a strong Three Bloods warrior could still beat you."
"If someone lands a heavy blow and your inner energy can't block it, having it all over your body won't save you."
Wei He realized the truth.
The human body was complex and delicate, filled with vital weak spots.
If your inner energy couldn't protect your lethal points from a real attack, having it spread everywhere else was useless. Enemies would just hit where it hurt most and kill you instantly.
He thought it through, then turned the page to read about refining blood force across the body.
This part was explained clearly.
'Erupt your blood force, form the Refining Seal, and push it with full force at your limbs while taking the catalyst medicine. This will speed up the transformation.' "The Refining Seal plus the catalyst medicine will speed up the process. The method itself is simple enough."
Wei He looked closely at the diagram of the Refining Seal.
The hand gesture was strange: the pinkies touched, the thumbs touched, and the middle three fingers pressed against each other.
The manual noted that if possible, one should consume the matching catalyst medicine right before training with the seal.
Doing so made the resulting Seal Blood far stronger and made future breakthroughs much easier.
"The catalyst is the meat of the Thread-Line Green Fish... but that stuff is expensive..."
Wei He sighed inwardly.
"I need to find a way to make money."
He pondered for a moment.
Taizhou was different from Yunzhou. There weren't many bandits or outlaws around here, so he couldn't just hunt criminals for quick cash.
On top of that, martial arts were everywhere here. Powerful masters were everywhere, and running into the wrong person would mean serious trouble.
Wei He had already explored the surroundings in his free time.
There were mountains near the Heavenly Seal Sect, but wild beasts were rare. Most of them had likely been hunted down by the massive crowd of martial artists long ago.
"It seems I have to do this the normal way..." Wei He considered his strengths.
His advantage was that his combat power was top-notch among those at his level. Plus, he had a deep understanding of poisons.
"Maybe I should ask Yang Guo. She seems to know how to make money." Wei He realized that once he tried to do things legitimately, he had no idea how to earn big money.
His old tricks like doing homework for others were totally useless at this level.
He didn't need small change. He needed a fortune.
"The Inner Court doesn't have many disciples, but every one of them is strong—at least Three Bloods. Maybe I can start from there."
After finishing his thoughts, Wei He chatted with his sister for a bit. He then walked next door to check on the Wang family, making sure the father and son were completely broken and terrified before heading off to the Thousand-Bat Water Pavilion to find Yang Guo.
"Making money?" Yang Guo was inside the martial arts hall where Senior Brother Kong Ran had tested the newcomers earlier.
She sat cross-legged on the floor, letting out a lazy yawn.
"What I'm doing right now is a job. Want to join? Evaluating new recruits."
"I just joined the Inner Court today. I don't think I'm qualified yet," Wei He shook his head.
"Fair point. This job requires you to be at the peak of Three Bloods at minimum. I barely qualified myself," Yang Guo nodded.
She paused to think. "Honestly, making money as an Inner Court member isn't that hard. We're Three Bloods warriors, after all, and everyone needs fighters. I have a few options you can pick from."
"Thanks," Wei He listened intently.
"First, working for the local government on patrol duty. Maintaining order. It has some danger since you'll clash with desperate outlaws sometimes. You might even run into crazy strong targets. Because of that, the pay is high."
"However, they don't pay in cash. They pay directly with dried beast meat. Whatever they have, they give. You'll need to sell that off and buy the specific beast meat used in our courtyard," Yang Guo explained.
"Any others?"
"Second, taking on tasks posted by the elders in the sect. The pay isn't as high as patrol work, but you get to show your face to the elders and build a good impression."
"Third, taking jobs posted by wealthy families and big clans nearby. The difficulty varies. You can check the details at the administration office."
Wei He fell silent. All these methods sounded like they would take way too long.
He really wanted to ask if there was a faster shortcut to get rich quick.
