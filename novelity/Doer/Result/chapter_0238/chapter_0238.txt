Wei He made full use of his freakish strength. He picked up a boulder weighing hundreds of pounds and hurled it with terrifying speed.
Zhu Chen dived left to dodge the rock, but he missed a thick root sticking out of the ground.
His shoulder slammed hard against it, sending him tumbling to the dirt.
Running at full speed had reopened his wounds. Blood squirted out like a fountain.
"Wei He, you coward! Fight me like a man!" Zhu Chen roared, his inner energy surging.
No words came back. Only a massive, square headstone whistled through the night sky, spinning fast.
BOOM!
The earth shook.
A shower of blood exploded from the impact zone. Zhu Chen was buried underneath, crushed and motionless.
Wei He landed softly forty feet away, eyes fixed on the rock.
He didn't walk over. Instead, he pulled an oil flask from his pouch, tossed it forward, and struck a flint with his other hand.
Pfft!
The oil splashed all over the stone, and the spark ignited it instantly.
Wei He stood there, cold and silent, watching the burning oil drip down and set the stone and the surrounding dirt ablaze.
A few minutes passed. The fire spread over twenty feet wide.
BOOM!
The boulder shattered into pieces. Unable to take the heat any longer, a desperate Zhu Chen burst out and charged straight at him.
At the same time, Wei He drifted backward like a ghost.
One chased, one retreated, keeping the distance between them at forty feet.
To play dead, Zhu Chen had let himself catch fire. He had even burned his own open wounds with the heat to stop the bleeding.
Combined with the medicine kicking in, he was somehow in better shape than before.
"Come on! Kill me! Isn't that what you wanted?!" Zhu Chen screamed as he gave chase. "Your Poison Sect has hundreds of bodies, all dead! I'm right here! One breath left! You still don't have the guts to finish me?!"
Wei He said nothing. He just kept backing away smoothly.
The two darted through the night, but after a few minutes of screaming, Zhu Chen finally slowed down.
He knew he was dead. Yet he kept shouting curses anyway.
If you only heard his voice, you would never guess it belonged to a dying man.
"Kill me! Fight me face to face!"
"You martial artists are a plague! You ruin the court and terrorize the people for your own greedy desires! You do nothing for this country!"
"I wanted to wipe you all out from the start! By now, nine out of ten warriors in this city are dead! Hahaha!"
"You parasites! You think killing me changes anything? Keep dreaming!"
Zhu Chen's mind was slipping away as he raged.
"His Majesty is chosen by Heaven! He will crush all rebels and bring back peace! You traitors, you parasites, you will all die! Die, die, die! Hahaha!"
Wei He remained silent, dodging and stepping back, watching him coldly.
Zhu Chen was blind now. His eyes were blank, his pupils dilated. He swung his arms wildly, using only the feeling of the wind on his skin to guess where to strike.
"DIE!"
Zhu Chen stumbled hard and collapsed.
The poisons in his system and on the flying daggers had fully taken hold. He had lost seventy percent of his blood along the trail.
Surviving this long was already a miracle. No doctor could save him now.
Wei He stopped moving and stared down at him.
"If you had treated me with kindness at first, I would have returned it," Wei He said softly. "You brought this on yourself."
"You... traitors..." Zhu Chen gasped, his voice raspy and weak. "You will die terrible deaths..."
His mind was fading fast. He couldn't even hear Wei He anymore.
"His Majesty... will reclaim the world..."
"The Empire... will rise again!"
BANG!
Wei He moved like lightning, appearing behind him and driving a heavy fist into the back of Zhu Chen's head.
The skull caved in with a sickening crunch. Brain matter and blood sprayed everywhere.
The body shivered on the ground, pinned by the force of the strike.
"Long live... the Empire!" Zhu Chen choked out his last words, then went completely still.
Wei He backed away. He had deliberately struck from behind, fearing a final desperate counterattack.
It was unnecessary. The man was already blind, poisoned, and bleeding out.
He was a complete lunatic, Wei He thought, staring at the corpse.
Revenge felt good, but a cold chill ran down his spine.
Until his last breath, Zhu Chen remained blindly loyal to the court.
If a top-tier grandmaster was willing to go this far for the Emperor, how many more fanatics were still hiding in the imperial court?
The Empire was falling apart. Everyone knew that.
Local governors already ran their territories like private kingdoms, ignoring central orders.
Yet, under such circumstances, a die-hard imperial loyalist had appeared here in Tai Province. That made the motives of the top provincial officials very suspicious.
The men running this province were no fools. So why did they launch a sudden attack on the four major martial sects?
First they hit the Tianyin Sect, now the Lishan and Danyang Sects.
Why stir up internal chaos when rebel armies were already marching toward the border?
Wei He didn't know the answer. All he knew was that this province was no longer safe.
Other provinces might be even worse. His best option was to hide the surviving members of his sect deep in the Black Mist Mountains and wait out the storm.
Wei He stepped forward to search the body, but the eunuch's belongings had already been melted by acid. There was nothing left.
Making sure the man was dead, Wei He tossed the corpse into the flames and vanished into the dark.
He had to wipe out every single Sun-Hunting Leaf outpost in the city tonight, before a new commander arrived to take over.
BOOM!
The heavy red gates shattered into pieces, crashing into the courtyard.
"Who's there?!" several elite guards yelled, rushing out with weapons drawn.
Before they could take five steps, a dark blur flashed past them.
Thump! Thump! Thump!
A dozen guards froze mid-stride like statues.
Swish!
Wei He stopped at the entrance of the main hall. Behind him, all twelve guards collapsed together, blood spurting from their throats.
These low-level martial artists couldn't even track his movement speed.
This was the Zhu Estate, the local headquarters of the Sun-Hunting Leaf network.
"Kill him!"
A man and a woman in nightwear burst from the hall, swinging their blades in tandem. Their swords glowed with sharp energy, aiming straight for Wei He's neck and heart.
"Foolish," Wei He muttered, flicking his fingers.
Clang! Clang!
The blades snapped in half and flew backward, impaling both attackers through the chest. They flew off their feet and hit the floor, dead before they landed.
"Who dares attack the governor's estate?!"
A white-haired elder rushed out from the inner court, swinging a massive double-edged sword down at Wei He's head.
"Too slow."
Wei He shifted slightly, effortlessly avoiding the heavy blade. He slammed a open palm straight into the elder's shoulder.
BOOM!
The elder's upper body burst into a cloud of red. His remains smashed into a tiger painting on the wall and slid down, lifeless.
"Guard the estate! Help!" shouts echoed from the streets outside.
Wei He's eyes turned cold. He leaped upward, punching a hole through the wooden ceiling, and landed on the roof.
"The Poison Sect is handling business here! Get lost!" he roared.
His voice boomed like thunder across the night sky, packed with raw energy.
The approaching soldiers froze in their tracks.
The Poison Sect was notorious. Over the past few months, rumors of them wiping out entire families had spread everywhere.
Nobody dared to step foot into the estate. They just stood outside in the dark, listening to the screams coming from within.
A few servants tried to climb the back wall to escape, but Wei He caught them in seconds and crushed them.
In less than two minutes, all eighty people inside the estate were dead.
Wei He jumped down from the roof and headed toward his next target.
The network had one more base in the city—the Commander's Mansion.
That was where Zhu Chen usually worked, and his most trusted aides were stationed there.
Those men likely held the master list containing every hidden outpost in the province.
The captain of those guards was a man named Jiang Yan. Wei He needed him alive.
As he leapt across the rooftops, three figures suddenly rose out of the shadows, blocking his path.
It was You Rong from the You family, Zhou Rong from the Zhou family, and Wang Yehe, the father of his friend Wang Shaojun.
The three major families had joined forces to intercept him.
"Master Wei," You Rong said, raising his spear. "The Zhu Estate is already gone. Why not show mercy and spare the rest? They were only following orders."
"Are you standing in my way?" Wei He asked, his voice cold.
The three men went dead silent.
