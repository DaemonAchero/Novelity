Back from the Wang family estate, Wei He immediately set to work gathering intel on the Lishan Sect and the remaining local factions.
Only a dozen or so gangs were left in Xuanjing City. Not too many, not too few.
Many of them were tied to the Three Great Families, and some were backed directly by them. Hitting these gangs meant striking at the foundation of those families. The pushback would be massive.
Yet Wang Shaojun seemed completely confident in the plan. Wei He could not understand why. There had to be something deeper going on that he knew nothing about.
A few days later, Wei He got engaged.
As the Sect Master of Wanqing, he kept the ceremony simple. He invited only Wang Shaojun, his father Wang Yehe, and a few close friends. With Wan Ling sitting as his elder, Wei He and Wan Qingqing shared a simple toast and bowed to seal their commitment. They would pick a better date later for the actual wedding.
Around this time, a phantom thief appeared across several cities in Tai Prefecture, stealing rare treasures.
If it had ended there, it would have been fine. But the thief soon turned his eyes toward secret martial arts manuals. He actually succeeded in stealing core techniques from several mid-sized sects.
Enraged, these sects pooled their money to place a bounty on his head and sent out their elite fighters to hunt him down. People started calling him the Meteor Thief, simply because he once claimed his blade was named the Meteor Cutter.
While the Meteor Thief drew all the attention, the Incense Gathering Cult tried to spread its influence again in secret, only to be crushed without mercy.
Under these tense conditions, the first purge officially began, led by the Wang family.
Just as the envoy had said, the primary force was not the locals, but the elite operatives from the provincial capital, including men from the Sunseeker and Night Willow factions.
In Nong County, far from the main town, a sprawling complex of buildings stood at the base of a mountain.
Wang Shaojun and Wei He sat on horseback, leading a large squad of the Red Sun Army, looking down at the compound from a distance.
A man stood ahead of them.
He was pale, clean-shaven, and dressed in black official robes. His hair and eyebrows were pure white. An ungraspable aura surrounded him. Though Wang Shaojun and Wei He could see him clearly, their senses registered absolute emptiness, as if no one was standing there at all.
His name was Zhu Chen, one of the executors sent by the Sunseeker organization.
Wang Shaojun had originally planned to handle the operation with just the Wang family, but the Sunseekers moved terrifyingly fast. As soon as the secret order went out, their men rode through the night to take command.
"Many years ago, when I was just a child," Zhu Chen spoke, his voice high-pitched and sharp like an eunuch’s, "I saw the Dragon Shadow and Phoenix Wu factions at their absolute peak, crushing their enemies. Back then, they were unstoppable. No one dared stand in their way."
"You jest, Lord Zhu. No one can stand in our way today either," Wang Shaojun smiled.
"You don't understand, Shaojun. Having seen that era, looking at the world now is just disappointing. I've grown more disillusioned with every passing year."
Zhu Chen raised one hand and opened his fingers.
Instantly, dark figures darted out from both sides of the Red Sun Army, rushing toward the buildings below. They moved with terrifying speed, at least as fast as the Three-Blood leg-technique martial artists Wei He had seen in the past.
"This is the headquarters of Night Eagle Tower," Zhu Chen said coldly. "We issued an ultimatum, yet they refused to kneel. They chose death."
"Kill them!" he ordered.
The Red Sun soldiers surged forward like a tide. Bowmen and shield-bearers split into formation. A rain of flaming arrows whistled through the night, falling over the compound.
Night Eagle Tower was prepared. Several figures leaped into the air, throwing out massive, light-gray nets. With a sharp twist, the nets created a vortex, catching the burning arrows in mid-air.
"Hah!"
The fighters roared, pulling the nets tight into a single massive fireball and hurling it back toward the army. The burning sphere, over ten meters wide, tumbled through the air straight at the army formation. The soldiers could feel the scorching heat and crushing pressure long before it hit.
"It has been a while since I last fought. I can't let the other teams outshine me," Zhu Chen smiled softly.
He waved his hand. Five soldiers stepped forward, groaning as they carried a massive horse-slaying saber toward him.
The flaming net crashed down toward the troops, getting closer by the second.
"Cut!"
Zhu Chen grabbed the saber and leaped into the air, rising like an arrow straight into the massive fireball.
Flash!
The massive net of fire froze mid-air, floating silently.
Then, without a sound, it shattered. The flames went out completely. Burnt scraps rained onto the ground, puffing thin trails of black smoke.
"Bing'er! Run!" a voice screamed from inside the compound.
Several figures leaped into the sky, fleeing in all directions. Among them was a white-haired old man in black outfit. His inner force whirled around him like a tornado as he scaled the outer wall, charging straight at Zhu Chen.
Pfft! Pfft! Pfft!
The old man kicked off the ground three times, his power surging higher with every step. By the third step, his inner force turned into a visible gale, swirling in a one-meter zone around his body.
His Viscera Refining power roared like a meat grinder. Anyone stepping into that space would be torn to shreds instantly.
"The Eight-Direction Cloud Cut. Impressive. No one in Xuanjing would ever guess that a mere Night Eagle Tower housed a master like you," Zhu Chen said, raising his heavy saber. "Too bad..."
He closed his eyes and held the blade horizontally.
"Slash."
He opened his eyes. The saber turned into a line of silver light, bursting forward.
The silver line flashed right through the old man.
The roaring gale around the old man froze, then dissolved into nothing. He kept his forward momentum for a second, then stopped dead in his tracks, unable to move another inch.
"So... fast..." the old man muttered, reaching up to touch his neck.
His body swayed, and his head rolled off his shoulders, hitting the dirt.
Screams and bloody roars echoed continuously from inside the Night Eagle Tower complex.
"Go clean up the runners. Leave no survivors," Zhu Chen commanded, looking back at Wang Shaojun and Wei He.
He had just killed an elite Viscera Refining master in a single strike, yet he wasn't even out of breath. His expression remained completely calm.
"Yes, sir," Wang Shaojun and Wei He replied together.
That single strike was still ringing in their minds. It was too fast, too brutal.
Both of them tried to simulate the attack in their heads, but neither was confident they could dodge it, let alone block it head-on.
Taking a team of elite fighters each, they split up to hunt down the escapees. Wang Shaojun went west; Wei He went east.
Wei He looked solemn. He knew Zhu Chen was strong, but not this strong. He even wondered if the man had reached the Mind Sense Realm. That strike held total control over his inner force—every drop of power was focused into the blade with zero waste, cutting through his opponent's force instantly.
Deep in the forest.
Wei He, Xie Yan, and Xiao Qingyu tracked the fleeing targets at high speed.
"Who is this Lord Zhu? How can someone that strong be completely unknown in Tai Prefecture?" Xiao Qingyu asked, unable to contain her shock.
Wei He shook his head. "His speed, strength, and force are far beyond us. All I know is that he comes from the provincial capital."
He was curious too. Zhu Chen was just one of three envoys sent to Xuanjing. That single slash placed him at the absolute peak of Viscera Refining, if not higher.
Though Wei He doubted he was in the Mind Sense Realm—masters of that level were not so common. He was likely at the absolute ceiling of Viscera Refining.
If all three envoys were this powerful...
Up ahead, sounds of battle broke out.
A squad of soldiers had already cut off the escape route. The military had prepared tight blockades around the perimeter, setting up battle arrays led by martial masters and supported by Blood-refinement warriors.
Trapped in the center were three young people—two men and one woman. They looked similar, clearly siblings.
The woman wielded twin curved daggers, her sharp force slicing through the incoming soldiers' spears as she fought desperately to protect her brothers.
Click.
A line of riflemen stepped forward, aiming their weapons.
Wei He's team landed softly at the edge of the trees, watching the fight unfold.
"Those three are the children of the old Tower Master," Xiao Qingyu said with a sigh. "If Heavenly Seal Sect was the largest faction in the light, Night Eagle Tower was the ruler of the dark. Now Heavenly Seal is scattered, and Night Eagle is being erased. It all comes to nothing in the end."
"End it," Wei He said softly.
Xie Yan and Xiao Qingyu stayed quiet.
Wei He raised his hand, then let it drop.
Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!
A volley of gunfire erupted. The three young fighters, their inner force already drained by the troops, were instantly cut down.
"Move on. Next group," Wei He said, turning back toward Zhu Chen's position.
Night Eagle Tower was gone. Its master dead, its senior experts wiped out by the Wang family and the Wanqing Sect.
When the news spread, all of Xuanjing shook.
