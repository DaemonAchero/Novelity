The pitch-black forest was dead quiet.
Wei He ran through the trees with two people in his hands. Under the heavy bouncing, Jiang Su slowly woke up.
"Wei He...!?" She looked up in the moonlight and saw him holding her with one hand.
"It's me," Wei He said. His eyes stayed fixed on the path ahead as he moved like a phantom through the dark.
"Where are we?" Jiang Su asked.
"I don't know."
"Is it safe here?"
"Not sure."
"...Can you tell me any good news?" She wiped the tears from her face.
"You and Teacher are still alive."
Jiang Su went silent.
Wei He suddenly froze, then leaped sideways.
Thump! Thump!
Two flying knives slammed into the exact spot where he had just been standing.
A voice came from the dark behind them. "How did you dodge that?"
A dark figure rushed out of the woods and stopped near a bush. The moonlight lit up one side of his body—a large man with a thick beard, holding a heavy steel blade ringed with iron.
Wei He turned around and set Jiang Su and his teacher on the ground.
"Who are you?"
"I am the First Guardian of the Blood Robe Gang, Yuan..."
Before the big man could finish his sentence, a violent gust of wind hit him in the face.
He ducked to the left as fast as he could. Something grazed his cheek, the sharp wind burning his skin.
"You!?"
Pop!
A light sound snapped behind him. Some kind of powder exploded in the air.
Before he could turn around, Wei He stepped forward, closing the gap in less than a second. A fist punched straight toward his throat.
"Five breaths," Wei He whispered in the dark. "To kill you."
Wei He's muscles swelled violently under his skin. He held his breath as his fists turned pitch-black. Two different fighting forces wrapped around his hands like iron threads. His internal power ran at full strength, veins popping across his face. The nine red flower marks on his arms writhed like living blood tattoos.
"Arrogant brat!!" the big man roared. He swung his large blade forward, sending a wave of power toward Wei He.
Unexpectedly, Wei He suddenly stopped two meters away.
Bang!
Five streams of gray dust shot out from behind Wei He, blasting straight into the man's face.
"You dirty coward!!" The big man screamed, shutting his eyes and mouth. He spun his blade madly to blow the poison dust back.
A black shadow flashed.
Wei He slid to his side and silently slammed a palm into the man's waist.
Clang!
The strike hit something hard—some kind of heavy inner armor hidden under his clothes.
The big man swung his blade back in a rage. The edge glowed with a sickly blue light, dripping with lethal poison.
Wei He did not retreat. He leaned sideways, barely dodging the blade, and smashed his open palm into the man's chest.
Bang!
Their hands met in midair. The big man blocked the hit with his free palm.
A sharp pain shot through his hand. He looked down and saw a bloody hole straight through his palm. The numbness spread instantly up his arm, going completely dead in seconds.
This was not poison—it was raw, overwhelming force tearing through his flesh.
"You...!?" Terrified, the big man tried to jump back and run.
Wei He moved faster, rushing forward like a shadow.
They crossed paths for a fraction of a second.
Wei He shook the blood off his hand and kept walking without looking back.
A moment later, the big man stopped dead in his tracks. Thump. He collapsed to the ground, his chest crushed completely flat into a hollow cave.
That was the sixth breath.
Inside an old, broken temple.
Wei Ying helped Master Zheng sit up, feeding him small sips of warm soup.
Master Zheng's lips were pure white. His body shook constantly, his skin growing colder by the minute, his eyes losing their light.
Wei He, Jiang Su, Zhang Qi, and Ouyang Lin stood silently around him.
"I..." Zheng Fugui tried to speak, but the soup spilled out of his mouth. He could not swallow. A heavy mouthful of dark blood bubbled up from his throat, staining his chest red.
Jiang Su started crying quietly.
Wei He's face turned grim. He reached out and squeezed his master's cold hand tightly.
Over the past few years, Master Zheng had taken care of them and taught them everything. They were not made of stone—watching him die like this hurt deeply.
"Don't cry..." Zheng Fugui said softly. "The Hong Family Fort, the Seven Alliances... none of them wanted our Martial Sect to exist. They want us broken and scattered."
He wiped the blood from his lips and sighed.
"Old Man Yun was too foolish. He thought the Hong Family cared about their reputation and would send help. But he didn't understand—saving someone early and saving them late are two completely different things. When I left, fires were already burning inside the city."
He looked up at Wei He and Jiang Su.
"Our school is gone. But it's fine... the bastard who mortally wounded me was killed by me and Old Han. You don't need to avenge me."
"Also... I hid some meat and grain in a deep well at the foot of Mount Shaoyang. If you want it, go get it."
"As for the Hong Family... Hong Daoyuan is too young for this. It was his old fox of a father. Taking all the blame himself so his son can step up as a savior and gather the survivors... what a clever plan."
"And the Qingdu Sect... they promised to back us, but they stabbed us in the back. I realized it too late."
His spirit seemed to jump back to life. His speech became surprisingly smooth and clear.
"Wei He."
"I am here, Master," Wei He answered quickly.
"I have ten thousand taels of gold, a meat field, and a secret training chart. Do you want them?"
Wei He froze.
"You are my best disciple, so I will only leave these to you. The gold is hidden in..." Zheng Fugui stopped. "It's hidden in..."
"I don't know if it's still there, but that spot should be safe. It took me a long time to choose that place." He sighed. "My daughter is weak, and my son-in-law is useless. I don't even know if they are still alive."
"Master..." Wei He wanted to ask where the gold was.
"Don't comfort me, I'm fine," Zheng Fugui waved his hand. "When you get old, you start seeing things clearly. When I was young, I was arrogant and talented, looking down on everyone. Now that I am old, what do I have left?"
"Master..."
"Don't worry about me. After this, I'll find a quiet place to rest. My inner body is ruined, so I can't fight anymore, but I can still live like a normal person."
Zheng Fugui sat straight up and drank the remaining soup in one gulp.
"I wanted to leave the gold to my daughter, but if I gave it to them, someone would steal it or they would waste it. Some things are a curse for ordinary people." He let out a long breath. "So I hid it all in that place. It's the safest spot I know."
He looked at Wei He and Jiang Su.
"I swore I would become someone great in this life. But after fighting all my life, what do I actually have left?"
"Oh, right... I forgot to tell you where the place is, didn't I? Look at my memory..." He laughed softly and slapped his own head.
"That place is right..."
He reached into his shirt and pulled out a thick bundle wrapped in animal skin.
"Right here!"
Zheng Fugui looked at Wei He and Jiang Su's dumbfounded expressions and burst into loud laughter.
He waved the bundle in the air. "Look at you two... so easily fooled!"
As he laughed, blood suddenly poured out from his eyes, nose, and ears.
Wei Ying covered her mouth and turned her head away, unable to watch.
The laughter died down slowly. Zheng Fugui's eyes stayed wide open, his hand still gripping the animal skin bundle with all his strength.
He sat there completely still, his breath gone.
Wei He and Jiang Su stepped back, knelt on the floor, and knocked their heads against the ground.
"May Master rest in peace," Wei He said calmly.
"May Master... rest in peace," Jiang Su sobbed uncontrollably.
The campfire flickered. On Zheng Fugui's pale face, there was a faint look of relief, mixed with a final, silent regret.
Whiz!
An arrow shot high into the sky, flying over the Feiye River and landing hard on the wooden gates of Feiye City.
Thud!
A lazy guard opened his eyes, stuffed a dry piece of bread into his mouth, and looked outside.
From the top of the ten-meter wall, he looked into the distance.
The land outside was dead and dry, full of cracked earth. On the main road ahead, a massive black cloud was moving toward them.
It looked like a swarm of locusts, or maybe...
"Enemy attack!!!" the guard screamed, pulling the alarm rope wildly.
Inside the inner city, where the fires had just been put out, soldiers began running everywhere.
Before the smoke even cleared, squads rushed up the steps toward the top of the walls.
The heavy city gates were locked tight. Heavy stones, boiling oil, and logs were dragged up the walls in a endless stream.
Suddenly, a wave of arrows rained down from the sky, pinning dozens of guards to the ground.
War drums roared outside.
Three massive generals rode their armored horses to the front of the city.
A young general pulled his horse forward, staring up at the crowded walls. He raised his hand, and a soldier handed him a heavy iron spear.
"The people of Feiye suffer enough! I do not wish to harm the innocent!"
He held the spear tightly and turned his horse around, then shouted at the top of his lungs.
"Ou Chen! Where are you!? Do you dare to fight me!?"
"During this massive drought, your Seven Alliances hoard all the grain while the people starve! Today, I, Hong Daoyuan, will bring justice to this land!"
The muscles on his arm expanded rapidly, surging with terrifying strength.
"Go!!"
Swish!
The spear disappeared from his grip instantly.
It ripped through the air with a deafening screech, flying like a cannonball straight toward the city's main sign.
"You dare!"
A figure leaped down from the wall, holding a heavy iron staff. He slammed the staff into the incoming spear with incredible force.
Bang!
A burst of sparks exploded in the air. The spear bent completely and bounced away, while the figure used the force to jump back up onto the wall.
"Kill them all! Hahahahaha!!!" Hong Daoyuan roared with laughter. He leaped off his horse, drew the black spear from his back, and charged the wall.
Behind him, thousands of dark soldiers raised their shields and rushed forward. Battering rams struck the main gates, while siege ladders slammed against the stone walls.
Massive boulders flew through the air from catapults, smashing into the defense towers above, while deadly short spears flew up to kill the elite guards.
