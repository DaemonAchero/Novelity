The stone bridge shattered with a loud explosion, snapping in two.
Fu Yanling, the Master of Returning Goose Tower, was exchanging blows with Wang Zhihe when Wang Yehe ambushed her from behind. She spat out blood on the spot, her internal force breaking.
She screamed. All her power and secret techniques exploded at once. The bridge under her feet collapsed completely. She plummeted straight down and hit the water with a heavy splash.
"After her!" Wang Zhihe barked.
He leaped into the river first. Wang Yehe and several Wang family martial masters followed right behind him, diving into the water.
Wang Shaojun landed softly in front of Wei He.
"How was that?" he asked with a smile.
"Impressive," Wei He gave a thumb up. "Returning Goose Tower is done for. Now we wait and see how the God Disturbance Sect reacts."
"They won't find it easy to step in," Wang Shaojun explained. "They already have their hands full dealing with the Taoist and Buddhist factions. They can't spare much power here."
"What about the guys you fought?" Wei He suddenly asked.
"Over there." Wang Shaojun pointed behind him.
Four bodies lay scattered in a clearing, each with a bloody hole torn through their chest. It was clearly the work of the Wang family's Spring Rain force.
"Your grandfather went after her. You're not going?" Wei He asked.
"No need. I stayed behind just to keep you company. Pretty good treatment, right?" Wang Shaojun chuckled.
He was telling the truth. Wei He was involved in too many things, and the government had not officially recognized him yet. It was not wise for the Wang family to mix with him in public. Keeping Wang Shaojun as the middleman was the best choice.
The two began checking the bodies for loot.
The four top assassins of Returning Goose Tower were all Bone-Forging masters. Losing them was a fatal blow to the tower. Meanwhile, Bishui Xiuluo of the Lishan Sect had fled the moment she saw things go bad. Her quick escape left Fu Yanling wide open, allowing Wang Yehe to land that sneak attack. Success and failure really hung on her actions.
Once they gathered the spoils, Wei He, Wang Shaojun, and the master from Jiangzhe Temple split everything into three equal shares.
Wei He claimed the items from Ruan Qinghong, who was now severely injured and unconscious on the ground.
"He's still alive. What should we do with him?" Wang Shaojun asked, looking between Wei He and the monk.
The master sent by Jiangzhe Temple was Monk Jingmie, head of the Enforcement Hall. He stood nearly three meters tall, with hands like tree trunks and palms like giant fans. He looked like the monster soldiers Wei He had once seen back in Feiye City.
Standing next to this giant, Wang Shaojun, who was over five feet tall, looked tiny between the two massive men.
"This man is a master of poison," Monk Jingmie suggested. "Normal guards cannot handle him. It is best if Sector Master Wei takes charge of him."
"Makes sense," Wang Shaojun agreed.
Pfft!
Wei He stepped forward and slammed his palm onto Ruan Qinghong's head. Power surged out, crushing his skull instantly. The poisoned master died before he could even wake up.
"Why lock him up? Isn't killing him easier?" Wei He withdrew his hand and shook off the blood.
"..."
"..."
The other two stood silent.
Monk Jingmie looked at Wei He closely, thinking to himself: Such ruthless nature. The rumors are true. As the Master of the Myriad Poison Sect, he really is cold-blooded.
Looking at Ruan Qinghong, a infamous poison master who had terrified people decades ago, now dead without a sound in a random forest, Jingmie felt a sudden, strange trace of sadness.
Wang Shaojun, however, was used to Wei He's style.
"Fine, I knew you'd do that. Next, we need to hunt down the rest of Returning Goose Tower. Are you ready? I can assign a few hideouts to you."
"Sure," Wei He nodded.
After this battle, Returning Goose Tower was ruined. At most, a few survivors would run away from Taizhou. A major threat was gone.
After trading information, they prepared to leave.
As they turned to go, Monk Jingmie called out to Wei He.
"Sector Master Wei, forgive my intrusion, but I wish to offer a piece of advice."
"Speak, Master," Wei He said.
"All life is precious. Please think twice before you kill."
Jingmie turned and left, his large frame vanishing into the dense trees.
Wei He shook his head inwardly. He knew the monk was warning him about his fierce nature.
"Buddhists are always like that," Wang Shaojun comforted.
"It's fine," Wei He sighed. "I just wanted to say, I already thought it through before striking. A martial artist needs a firm mind to strike without hesitation."
"The monk's hits seemed heavy, but he avoided vital spots. Wasn't he just following his own path?"
"Then what is your path?" Wang Shaojun asked, curious.
"Peace," Wei He smiled and turned to leave.
Wang Shaojun watched him walk away into the dark with Xie Yan, then shook his head and led his own men off.
In that moment, Wang Shaojun wasn't thinking about the God Disturbance Sect, but the poor children he had seen in Quanye. Martial arts feuds were pleasing him less and less.
Now, the Tower Master was wounded and missing. Two hall masters were dead, and four top assassins were gone. The remaining lower-level killers scattered like dust. A force that once held a third of the strength of the three big families was erased by the wind just like that.
Over the next half-month, the Wang family, the Myriad Poison Sect, and Jiangzhe Temple destroyed over ten secret hideouts, taking vast wealth and killing over a hundred assassins.
With this, the Myriad Poison Sect built a firm reputation. People now saw it as the strongest power right below the big three.
Wei He's reputation as a ruthless poison master was stuck for good. Whenever people ran into his disciples, they always saw them using dark poisons, making the label run even deeper.
Life settled down. News soon arrived from the frontlines.
General Yuchi Zhong had fought over ten battles with the rebel army at East Tile Lake. Both sides suffered losses, and they were now stuck in a standoff.
Huge supplies of grain and meat were sent to the front line day after day. Though the war was stuck, the main military kept the rest of Taizhou safe. Peaceful life went on as usual. Only families with sons in the army worried for their loved ones.
In comparison, the destruction of Returning Goose Tower was just a small splash in the water, quickly forgotten. Everyone's attention was fixed on the war news.
"It's true," Jiu Ying said, coughing as he crushed herbs with a pestle inside a hidden house in Black House Mountain. "No matter how big a mess we make, normal people don't care. They only care about war, rising prices, and whether their family will die."
He sighed. "The world of martial arts is too far from normal people. Li Tong is leading four hundred thousand troops, bringing along countless evil masters. If I weren't caught up in it, I wouldn't care either."
"Your health is getting worse," Wei He said darkly from the side.
"Probably from the old injury..." Jiu Ying shook his head. "I tried to fix it, but it didn't help much. I'm just getting old, and my body is full of old wounds. Anyway, forget that. I have an idea on how to merge poison with internal force."
He smiled. "Writing this book allowed me to review everything I learned in my life. I actually gained new insights."
"Oh?" Wei He perked up. "How?"
If he could use deadly poison and internal force together, it would boost his future power greatly.
Jiu Ying smiled. "Remember the theory I told you before?"
"Of course," Wei He frowned. "Use the body as the base to raise the limit of what it can endure. Then, use strong poison to stimulate growth. But how far must the body grow to ignore lethal poison? That method requires my body to be far stronger than my enemy's. Only the poison I survived can harm them."
"If my body is already that strong, why would I bother using poison? I could just punch them to death."
"True for others, but maybe not for you."
Jiu Ying put down his tools, walked to the corner, and pulled a paper scroll from a sealed jar. He handed it to Wei He.
"I wrote this two days ago. Take a look."
Wei He opened the scroll. Yellow vertical lines held neat rows of words mixed with simple drawings.
"In my lifetime of study, poison is an external evil," Jiu Ying explained. "Where there is evil, there is good. Strengthening the body to push out evil is the right path. But what if we keep the evil inside, and release it to hit enemies when needed?"
"How do we store it?" Wei He asked.
"Store it in your internal force," Jiu Ying answered. "Internal force sits on the body's surface, away from veins and blood. You can use external poison to stimulate your inner energy. The stronger the poison, the stronger your force becomes. You just need to stop the force from flowing back into your body at the start."
"Makes sense," Wei He nodded. "This fits the framework I thought of before. Move step by step from weak to strong poison. As long as we don't damage the body's core, physical strength will grow fast."
"Yes," Jiu Ying said quickly. "I split your framework into three stages: Gather, Absorb, and Melt. I also grouped suitable poisons into a clear path. Each person's path will be different based on their body."
"What will you name this technique?" Wei He asked. "If it works, it will be a brand new system outside normal martial arts."
"It boosts blood, builds physical power, and adds lethal poison to attacks," Jiu Ying thought for a moment after coughing again. "How about calling it the Nine Shadows Old Man Art?"
Wei He looked at him with a deadpan face.
