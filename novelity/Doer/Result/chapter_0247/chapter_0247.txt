The Trap "Sect Leader Wei... spare me... please..." Yun Hu choked out, forcing his shattered body off the dirt.
His spine was snapped and his inner power had completely collapsed. He could barely move. But his strong cultvator body kept him alive, turning every second into living hell.
"You spied on the women of my sect, and you ask for mercy?" Wei He’s voice was dead calm.
He flicked his finger. A thin grey line shot out, driving straight into the open wound on Yun Hu’s arm.
Yun Hu screamed. The grey line squirmed like a living parasite, digging deep into his flesh.
"What did you put inside me?!"
"Stay here."
Wei He didn't even look at him. He leaped into the trees and vanished in a flash to hunt down the other thief. He didn't care if Yun Hu ran. He trusted his poison completely.
Yun Hu’s heart filled with bitter regret. His footwork was dead silent and completely hidden. How did Wei He spot him so easily?
He didn't know that to Wei He, the scent of the soul-tracking powder on his skin burned like a bright lantern in the pitch black. No matter how fast he was, he could never escape.
In another part of the forest, Wei He tore through the woods like a hurricane. A single leap carried him fifty meters.
A monkey eating a red fruit felt a cold breeze on its neck. It looked up, only to see a dark blur zip past. Before it could blink, Wei He was already gone.
If the wild beasts couldn't react, Yun He stood no chance.
Yun He and Yun Hu were the "Meteor Bandits." Yun Hu did most of the dirty work, while Yun He usually trained under their master. He only came out to hunt people when he was bored.
He never wanted to target Wan Qingqing of the Thousand Poisons Sect, but his master needed living test subjects for drugs.
Now, he was running for his life.
Yun He’s blood boiled violently. Every time his feet kicked off a trunk, thick branches snapped like twigs.
Run! Run! Run!
Sensing extreme danger, Yun He pushed his body past its limits.
Wei He wasn't even a Master Level yet, but the moment Yun He saw him, horror gripped his soul. Yun He had a natural gift for sensing danger—that was why his master took him in. His gut told him one truth: if Wei He caught him, he was dead.
Swish! Swish!
Yun He moved so fast he seemed to fly right above the ground. Yet, the ice-cold pressure behind him only grew heavier. It was closing in.
Dammit! They said Wei He was only at the Organ Forging stage! What kind of monster is this? He’s twice as terrifying as the elders in the Wang family!
Yun He panicked. He kept changing directions, trying to throw Wei He off. But no matter how many turns he took, the death aura stayed right on his tail.
Suddenly, a wide river appeared ahead. Yun He jumped, trying to cross to the other side.
While he was still in mid-air, a harsh wind blew behind him.
Flash!
Yun He’s eyes went wide. A broad, muscular figure was already standing right where he was supposed to land.
It was Wei He.
Yun He twisted his body in panic, crashing awkwardly onto the muddy riverbank meters away.
"Your footwork is better than the last guy. Interesting," Wei He said, a cold glint in his eyes.
"You can't kill me!" Yun He roared, throwing a dozen dark iron balls into the air.
Puff! Puff! Puff!
Thick smoke exploded, filling the air with deadly knockout gas. Yun He sprinted to the left under the cover, but his vision blurred again.
Wei He appeared right in front of him, completely ignoring the toxic gas.
Trapped, Yun He put everything into one final strike. He channeled all his power into his right palm. A sharp, poisoned dagger slid quietly out of his sleeve, hidden between his fingers.
"Single-Qi Scale!" Yun He yelled, slapping his palm forward while throwing a splash of black water with his other hand.
The black water reeked of rot. The moment a few drops hit the grass, green smoke rose, burning holes straight through the leaves.
Wei He’s expression didn't change. He threw a simple palm strike.
Just as his hand was about to hit the black water, it shifted like lightning, missing the splash by a hair and aiming straight for Yun He’s left shoulder.
Yun He dropped his shoulder, changing his move to dodge.
To his horror, Wei He shifted his attack again mid-air. His hand bypassed Yun He’s defense completely, landing softly on the center of his chest.
BOOM!
Shockwaves exploded from Yun He’s chest. He spat out a mouthful of blood and flew backward.
Yun He tried to gather his strength in mid-air, but his vision went black. A large hand darted toward his arms like lightning.
CRACK! CRACK!
Both of his arms snapped. He crashed heavily onto the ground, unable to get back up.
"Good footwork. You actually dodged one move," Wei He said, landing softly.
The Meteor Bandits were only annoying because they were fast. Against slower foes, they were ghost-like. But against someone faster than them, they were helpless.
Wei He caught his breath and walked toward Yun He.
Swoosh!
A stone zipped past his face, cutting through the trees to his left.
Wei He stopped and turned toward the river.
An old man with a pale face and a hooked nose stood by the water's edge.
"Young man, you've punished them enough. Leave it at that. Give people a way out, and you might live longer."
"Are you talking to me?" Wei He smiled softly.
"Of course," the old man frowned. "Don't forget how your Heavenly Seal Sect was wiped out. Know your place."
"The Heavenly Seal Sect..." Wei He took a step forward. "It's been a long time since anyone dared to use that name to threaten me."
"Let's see..."
The muscles in Wei He's right arm swelled rapidly, his power layering over and over until his arm grew twice its size.
"...what gives you the right to talk down to me!"
BOOM!
The ground exploded beneath Wei He’s feet. He turned into a dark phantom, darting through the air like a bolt of black lightning straight at the old man.
He slammed his huge palm down from above.
The old man raised both arms to block, his power gathering along his forearms like twisting snakes.
BOOM!
A wave of poisonous air exploded around them.
The old man spat out blood and fell back, his arm muscles torn and bleeding. Shocked, he tried to retreat, but Wei He was right on top of him.
BANG! BANG! BANG! BANG!
They traded dozens of blows in seconds. Palms clashed, fingers struck, and the sound of colliding power shook the forest, raining leaves down like a storm.
Suddenly, the old man shifted like a ghost, appearing at Wei He's left side. He jabbed a finger directly into Wei He's lower back.
He shifted again, appearing on the right, driving another finger into Wei He's side.
The old man moved like a shadow, striking Wei He’s body again and again, pouring his lethal energy straight through Wei He’s defenses.
Phoo!
The old man leaped ten meters back, taking a long, deep breath to recover his strength.
Wei He looked down at his body, his face cold. "What kind of movement is that?"
Even with his explosive speed, he couldn't keep up with the old man's movement. It was unnaturally fast.
"Wind Walker," the old man said coldly. "You've taken my Five-Step Vein Sealing Strike. You are already a dead man. I told you to leave a way out, but you wouldn't listen..."
"Five-Step Vein Sealing Strike..." Wei He took a step forward.
"One."
"Two."
"Three, four, five."
Wei He opened his hands.
"Six."
He took another step forward.
"It doesn't seem to work," Wei He said, looking up.
The old man's face pale. He tried to fall back, but he was a second too late. His shock had cost him everything.
Wei He lunged forward. His hand twisted through the air like a darting bird, dodging the old man's guard and locking onto his arm.
BOOM!
Wei He slammed the old man straight into the dirt.
The terrifying force, combined with Wei He's raw speed, smashed the old man like a sack of rocks. Mud and gravel flew everywhere.
"You dare!" the old man roared.
His arm twisted unnaturally, releasing a burst of power that freed him from Wei He's grip. He flipped up and kicked his legs out like a pair of razor-sharp scissors, aiming straight for Wei He’s neck.
BANG!
Mid-kick, Wei He caught both of his legs with a single hand and slammed him down again.
BOOM!
The old man lost his balance and hit the ground hard. Before he could recover, Wei He leaped into the air, driving his elbow straight down into his chest.
CRACK!
The old man’s eyes went blank as blood spurted from his mouth. His chest was crushed into a bloody hole.
