"What do you mean...?" Wei He was interested now. The Yuchi family seemed to have deep problems of its own.
"I've been waiting for a chance," Yuchi Qi said strictly. "I thought it would take longer, but since Sect Master Wei is here, this is our best shot."
"How so?" Wei He looked at her. "What are you planning?"
Yuchi Qi stopped to think, but the black-veiled woman beside her raised a hand to block her and spoke first.
"We heard you've been looking for the remaining secrets of the Nine Stamps Secret Techniques. There aren't many strong fighters in Xuanjing, but you are one of them. So here is my offer: help us, and you'll get the location of the vault."
The veiled woman waved her hand. A small breeze scattered a faint smell in the air.
"I heard Sect Master Wei's poison skills are quiet and deadly. Please, keep those smells to yourself. It ruins our trust."
"...Impressive," Wei He nodded.
This was the first time someone spotted his poison so fast. Her martial arts must make her senses very sharp. He knew normal Bone Refinement or Viscera Refinement fighters wouldn't notice it. Even Wang Shaojun might miss it. But she caught it instantly and blew it away. She clearly knew poison well.
"My bad. It's a habit. I like to stay safe when there's danger," Wei He smiled faintly.
The two women frowned slightly, but they understood his meaning.
"May I know your name, miss? I am Wei He."
"Tang Huanhuan," the woman replied coldly. She turned to Yuchi Qi. "Enough talk. Since Sect Master Wei is here, let's take this chance to get your revenge!"
"Yes!" Yuchi Qi's eyes filled with dark hatred and bloodlust.
"Sect Master Wei," Yuchi Qi said carefully. "The secret manuals are in the vault. Only Yuchi Yan knows where it is and how to open it. Help us catch Yuchi Yan, and we will force him to talk."
Wei He was surprised. Yuchi Qi was part of the Yuchi family, yet she was planning to betray her own grand-uncle?
"Don't ask why. Yuchi Yan is a beast. What he did to me..." Yuchi Qi looked down, her eyes turning red with shame and anger.
Tang Huanhuan gently pulled her close and patted her back.
The story was half-said, but the dark meaning was obvious.
"He also tricked my grandfather into giving him all the power!" Yuchi Qi added with hate. "I'm going to kill this trash for my family!"
"Aren't you afraid I'll leak this?" Wei He asked.
"Who would believe you?" Yuchi Qi asked back. "As long as I keep quiet, will they believe a family member or an enemy like you?"
"Makes sense," Wei He agreed.
"Besides, if you ruin this now, everyone will guard the manuals even tighter. You won't get another chance. Work with us, and we both win."
"Fair enough." Wei He nodded. "So, what's the plan?"
He was tempted. They clearly had their own secret goals, but he didn't care as long as he got the manuals. Once he got what he wanted, he would leave.
"It's simple," Yuchi Qi lowered her voice. "My master and I can handle Yuchi Yan, but he has three top fighters with him."
"Three fighters?"
"Yes. Li Quan, Ou Kaiming, and Li Ruying. They have served my grandfather for decades. All three are at the Bone Refinement stage, and their teamwork is so good they can fight a Viscera Refinement master together. If you draw them away, we can capture Yuchi Yan."
"Why should I trust you?" Wei He cut her off. "If I move first, what proves you'll keep your end of the deal?"
"What do you want then?" Yuchi Qi asked.
"Simple. Let me put a little poison in you, and I'll trust you," Wei He said calmly.
"No way!"
"Sect Master Wei, we both need something here," Tang Huanhuan said calmly. "If we team up, we win. If not, we both get nothing. With your strength, you can just walk away if things go wrong. Why not take a gamble? You have nothing to lose."
Wei He thought about it.
She was right. Unless he was strong enough to force his way in and beat Yuchi Yan directly, he had to take some risks. He wasn't strong enough to run wild in Xuanjing City yet. He could kill Yan Junshan alone, but fighting two Viscera Refinement masters at once was different. One mistake could kill him.
He looked at his own limits and nodded.
"Fine. I'll gamble. But I need something upfront if you want my help."
The two women looked at each other.
"What do you want?" Tang Huanhuan asked.
"Can you get whatever I ask for?"
"As long as it's not too rare, yes," Tang Huanhuan said quietly, showing off her background.
Wei He thought for a moment. "Honestly, I don't need much right now."
"How about this?" Tang Huanhuan pulled a small gold tube from her leg bag. "Black Gold Marrow. You're a poison master, so you can check if it's safe. It's unique to my Jinyu Hall. It fixes broken bones fast, even for regular people."
Jinyu Hall? Wei He noted the name in his mind.
"Fine," he agreed. "But I can't check its power right now. This alone isn't enough."
"Then take this too." Tang Huanhuan threw a dark sphere at him.
Wei He caught it. It was a mutant beast core.
"A mid-tier core?"
"Unused. Good for three charges," Tang Huanhuan said. "Is that enough?"
"Enough," Wei He smiled.
In the front hall.
The meeting was ending, and people were leaving.
"Vice Commander Xie, please wait," Yuchi Yan called out to Xie Dong.
"Oh? What is it, Master Yan?" Xie Dong was a tall man with a full beard and sharp eyes. He looked like an ordinary martial artist, but Yuchi Yan knew better.
Before leaving the city, Yuchi Yan's older brother had warned him to watch out for Xie Dong. Xie Dong looked like a vice commander of the Chijing Army, but he was actually a spy sent by the provincial capital—a master strong enough to match Yuchi Zhong.
"If it's not urgent, can we talk tomorrow?" Xie Dong asked, surprised to be called back.
Once everyone left, Yuchi Yan spoke slowly.
"I heard a rumor recently. They say you have ties with that secret group in the Capital. Is it true?"
"The group in the Capital?" Xie Dong laughed. "Are you asking if I'm with the Dragon Shadow and Phoenix Wu?"
"The Great Yuan Empire is falling apart now," Yuchi Yan said seriously. "That group isn't as scary as it used to be. What are your plans?"
"I used to belong there," Xie Dong smiled. "Are you asking because of that treasure rumor?"
"...You joke, Vice Commander. Who wouldn't want such a treasure? But I know my limits. I'm just curious," Yuchi Yan sighed.
