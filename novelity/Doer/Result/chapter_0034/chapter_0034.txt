"Did you see them the other night?"
"Yeah, I saw them. I was greasing the carriage wheels by the road. About twenty riders came through, looking like absolute demons," Old Huang replied.
"Back at the city gates, I saw a notice. It said wild beasts were destroying fields outside, so the Seven-Family Alliance sent out a hunting party. Every family sent their best fighters," Old Huang took a sip from his water skin and continued, "Looking at things today, it seems they failed."
Wei He stayed quiet and stopped asking.
The carriage rolled slowly through the dark, heading toward the Wei family courtyard.
Rain splattered off the wheels, passing umbrella-carrying pedestrians along the way. It also passed a man dressed in blue by the roadside.
The blue-robed man held a white paper umbrella painted with a delicate red plum blossom as he walked.
Wei He caught a fleeting glance through the window gap. On the sleeve near the man's hand, he saw a small, finely embroidered symbol for the Hong family.
His heart sank. He quickly opened the window and leaned out to look back, but the man in blue was already gone.
The street was cold and empty, except for a few rushed pedestrians under umbrellas.
Extinguished lanterns swayed in the wind outside the closed shops, adding to the chill.
Wei He pulled his head back inside and sat up straight. A heavy feeling washed over him.
He did not know why, but his instincts told him a storm was brewing, like thick dark clouds pressing down from above.
'Maybe I should ask Old Zheng. He might know something.' Wei He made up his mind to visit Old Zheng tomorrow.
As a famous martial artist in Stone Bridge Town, Old Zheng had connections and information far beyond Wei He's reach.
And that blue-robed man... the Hong Clan...
Wei He pushed down his uneasy thoughts, cleared his mind, and took a deep breath.
When he got home, Wei Ying boiled water for him. After taking a bath, Wei He fell asleep quickly.
The next morning, before dawn broke, he went straight to the inner room where his master, Old Zheng, lived.
He knew Old Zheng usually woke up early to secretly handle his account books.
"The riders from last night? Ah, the team from the Seven-Family Alliance..."
Putting down his abacus, Zheng Fugui stroked his long beard.
"Did you know? I just heard from an old friend that Hong Daoyuan of the Hong Clan went out to the outskirts yesterday. He took his wife and young son to Mingde Temple to offer incense. The rumor says their group ran into wild beasts, but Hong Daoyuan stepped up himself, killed over a dozen beasts, and came out completely unharmed."
"What?!" Wei He's heart tightened. Connecting the dots made him feel uneasy.
Zheng Fugui chuckled.
"The Seven-Family Alliance and the Hong Clan have fought openly and secretly many times. The Alliance lost most of those fights. Now that Hong Daoyuan has gathered enough power, he is just waiting for the right moment. What does the Seven-Family Alliance have to stand against him?"
He stood up, placed the abacus into a cabinet slot, and pulled out a thin ledger from another spot.
"Do you know what kind of place Mingde Temple really is?"
"I don't know... Isn't it just a place to burn incense and pray to Buddha?" Wei He shook his head.
"Xiao He, let me teach you something today," Zheng Fugui smiled. "In this world, any martial hall or temple built outside the city walls that manages to survive out there is never simple."
"...Does Mingde Temple have another identity?!" Wei He felt another chill. He had even killed people at Mingde Temple before to cover his tracks...
"That's right. Mingde Temple is actually a branch altar for the Incense Seduction Cult. The Hong Clan going there to offer incense makes their intentions clear, doesn't it?" Zheng Fugui sneered. "The Seven-Family Alliance is panicking now."
"Master, if a real war breaks out, what should we do?" Wei He asked with worry.
"What are you afraid of? Even if they fight, they fight each other. What does that have to do with us? The Seven-Family Alliance aside, the Hong Clan has a good reputation. They won't harm common people for no reason."
Zheng Fugui spoke calmly.
"Besides, our Return-Mountain Fists has people on both sides. There are members in the Hong Clan and members in the Seven-Family Alliance. No need to rush."
Wei He thought for a moment and asked, "What about the Incense Seduction Cult?"
"The Incense Seduction Cult? Just a messy, scattered group. They can't cause real trouble. At most, they just incite the poor," Zheng Fugui dismissed it without care.
Wei He wanted to say more, but Zheng Fugui raised a hand to stop him.
"Alright, since you are worried, I will make your breakthroughs public in a bit. That way you can choose which side you want to join. How about that?"
"Master, what do you mean...?" Wei He was confused.
"A few days ago, people from the Hong Clan reached out to us martial masters," Zheng Fugui explained. "We teach many students and hold great influence, so they cannot ignore us. Whatever they do, they must consider our stance."
"So your intention is...?" Wei He felt his tension rise.
Clearly, it was time to pick a side.
"What intention could I have?" Zheng Fugui grinned, showing his yellow teeth. "You all have your own fortunes, backgrounds, and choices. Which path you take depends entirely on you."
"Alright, stop overthinking this. It is still early. Just focus on practicing your martial arts. In a moment, I will call the core disciples inside and spread the word about your progress. Otherwise, relying only on that bit of snake meat from Cheng Shaojiu, how long would it take for you to make progress?"
Wei He nodded, knowing his master was right.
At his current level, the Silver-Kiss Black Snake meat was no longer enough to build up his blood vitality.
The speed was too slow.
The monthly allowance of snake meat could not even support his normal training, let alone fill up his breakthrough bead.
"Stone Skin makes you an elite user of Secondary Blood Vitality. You could be a small leader in the Hong Clan or a mid-level figure in the Seven-Family Alliance. Rich families would treat you as an honored guest, and you would be a core member in the Three Gangs and Two Sects. So pick carefully."
Wei He nodded heavily.
He knew Master Zheng was pointing all this out so he wouldn't get taken advantage of.
Soon, Zheng Fugui called a sturdy servant woman outside to bring in the disciples waiting in the courtyard.
Before long, daylight broke through.
The first person to enter was Jiang Su.
The girl wore a tight white outfit that showed off her fair arms and full chest, making her stand out.
No one knew how she trained, but her chest looked even larger than when she first arrived.
Her face was flushed red with a healthy glow from long-term training. As she walked in, she brought a strong scent of female charm into the room.
When Jiang Su stepped inside and saw Wei He standing to the side, she paused in surprise.
This was Master Zheng's private bedroom. Disciples who were not close to him were never allowed inside.
And yet...
Doubts flashed in Jiang Su's eyes, but she stayed quiet and stood to the side.
Soon, Cheng Shaojiu, Jiang Yan, and Zhao Hong arrived one by one.
Together with Wei He, and excluding the injured Xiao Ran, there were six of them in total. These were the core disciples of Return-Mountain Fists.
The five of them stood in a row in front of Zheng Fugui, waiting quietly for his orders.
Zheng Fugui coughed a few times, picked up his teacup, and took a sip.
His old, wrinkled eyes scanned the five of them. After a short pause, he spoke slowly.
"I called you here today to tell you something..."
He stopped, calmly set down his teacup, and let out a breath.
"Ah, right."
He suddenly turned to Jiang Su, as if remembering something.
"Jiang Su, how old are you this year?"
"Responding to Master, I am nineteen."
"Nineteen... not young anymore," Zheng Fugui tugged at his beard. "How about this? Your junior brother Wei He is eighteen. He is honest, capable, and strong. He is a good man. Why don't you two get together and support each other?"
His words shocked everyone in the room.
Jiang Yan had always liked Jiang Su. Hearing this, he almost dropped his fan, letting it slide behind his back onto the ground with a loud snap.
Cheng Shaojiu was swallowing his spit and almost choked, ducking his head to cough.
Zhao Hong stayed the calmest, only freezing for a moment before returning to normal.
As for the two involved, Jiang Su's face turned red instantly. Not out of shame, but out of anger.
"Master, me and Wei He?! You... Junior Brother Wei's personality is not my type at all. Besides, I swore long ago that I would only marry a man stronger than me. He won't do!"
Wei He was also startled and quickly spoke up.
"Thank you for your kindness, Master, but I only hold respect for Senior Sister Jiang and have no romantic feelings toward her. Please understand."
To be honest, Wei He found high-and-mighty women like Jiang Su exhausting.
His ideal partner was someone like his second sister, gentle, kind, and focused on home life.
Jiang Su was too ambitious and competitive. A softer, weaker man would suit her much better.
Jiang Su glanced at him from the side, thinking to herself: 'At least this kid knows his place and rejected it first.' Both of them were frightened by Zheng Fugui's sudden proposal and refused immediately.
Zheng Fugui waved his hand.
"Doesn't it sound like a good match? Wei He broke through to Stone Skin today and has great potential. Jiang Su is talented, pretty, and comes from a good family. Isn't this what they call... what kind of match?"
"A match made in heaven," Cheng Shaojiu leaned over and reminded him softly.
"Ah, yes, a match made in heaven."
"..." Cheng Shaojiu smiled, stood back up, and looked at Wei He again.
Suddenly, his smile froze. What did Master just say?
Xiao He broke through to Stone Skin?
The smile on his face vanished. Thinking he heard it wrong, he replayed the words in his mind.
"Wait, Master, did you just say Wei He made a breakthrough? A Secondary Blood Vitality breakthrough?" Cheng Shaojiu asked hurriedly.
"Didn't I just say that?" Zheng Fugui looked at him. "Yes, he is at the same stage as you all now."
The others had also thought they misheard, but hearing it confirmed stunned both Jiang Yan and Jiang Su.
Wei He, the guy who was just Cheng Shaojiu's little follower, actually reached Stone Skin?
Jiang Yan frowned and looked closely at the calm Wei He, but his expression softened shortly after.
Cheng Shaojiu's weak follower just got lucky? That wasn't bad.
Wei He came from a poor background and had a sister to take care of. Now that the Cheng family was losing power, Jiang Yan could win Wei He over to make him a loyal servant.
Jiang Yan quickly made up his mind.
On the other side, Jiang Su felt heavy and upset, biting her lip without saying a word.
'So what if he reached Secondary Blood Vitality? Why try to arrange a marriage for me right away? With Wei He's talent, Stone Skin is probably his limit. He just got lucky. Does Master really think he is a genius?' She felt bitter and unhappy with Master Zheng too.
Making her marry Wei He was like dragging her down to the level of Cheng Shaojiu's follower.
She barely looked up to Cheng Shaojiu, so why should she be matched with his follower? Had Master Zheng gone senile?!"
