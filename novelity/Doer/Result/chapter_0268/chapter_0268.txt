"Friend Wei, I hope you have been well."
Outside the Taoist temple, a tall figure stood quietly in the snow. He was about the same height as Wei He.
The man had one eye, a shaved head, and wore a gray robe laced with silver thread. He looked ferocious, but his eyes and posture were surprisingly calm.
"You look good too, Senior. It seems the trouble in the provincial capital was settled cleanly," Wei He smiled.
"There was some trouble at first, but it was resolved after I brought back the targeted medicine from you," You Fu chuckled.
One was the master of the Extermination Alliance, a man of terrifying strength. The other was the master of the Myriad Poison Sect, a master of poison.
Both were top figures in Taizhou.
If people found out these two villainous giants were meeting in a deserted mountain, it would cause widespread panic.
You Fu’s actions moved half the martial art forces in Taizhou. His power was proven in the extermination battle.
Wei He was the biggest poison supplier in Taizhou. He had wiped out thousands of elite soldiers on his own.
They stood ten meters apart in the snow, keeping their distance.
"It seems I owe you another favor," Wei He sighed.
"No need to be polite between us," You Fu smiled.
"Then how about a fight for a hundred breaths?" Wei He proposed.
A hundred breaths were enough to test his current combat power.
"Good."
The quiet returned.
Neither spoke. They calmed their breath and readied their bodies.
Snowflakes spun through the air, dropping past them. Not a single flake touched their clothes.
BANG!
They vanished.
The snow between them exploded, leaving a deep crater several meters wide.
Wei He’s muscles swelled. His vitality surged as he coated his palms in the Sea-Covering Force, striking out like lightning.
You Fu blocked every blow. Wei He’s force could not penetrate his defense.
At the same time, You Fu’s active force was blocked by Wei He’s thick layer of internal energy.
Active force had destructive power, but Wei He’s energy wall was too thick to pierce quickly.
Both were masters of technique. They moved fast, refusing to get locked in a deadlock.
As long as they didn't stall, Wei He wouldn't be worn down.
Snow whipped around them. Wei He’s strength was beyond what You Fu expected.
You Fu had guessed how Wei He fought the old eunuch, Zhu Chen. He assumed Wei He used speed to lead the eunuch into the territory of the Three-Headed Fox, acting as support to take him down.
Now, fighting him directly, he knew the truth.
He had unlocked his senses twice, placing him two levels above Wei He. Yet, Wei He matched his speed.
It made no sense.
He began to suspect Wei He was a Mixed-Blood. Mixed-Bloods were strong without training; martial arts were just an extra boost for them.
"Sun Finger!"
You Fu switched tactics, coating his index finger in force and thrusting it forward.
His force was called True Sun Energy, focusing on heavy vibrations.
He wanted to test Wei He, so he added a trace of vibration to the strike.
Buzz!
Ripples of force spread from his fingertip, pushing toward Wei He’s chest like a wall.
It started as a finger strike, but turned into a crushing palm technique. It hit like a heavy hammer against a temple bell, sending shockwaves through the air.
Wei He stayed calm. He took a deep breath, turning on all the effects of the Sea-Covering Force at once.
Power, speed, explosion, pierce, rotation, defense, and ice. Everything stacked.
This was his Tower State.
His blood pumped faster. His body expanded a full size.
He brought his palms together, gathering massive energy into his right palm, and struck out.
ROAR!
A sharp whistle cut through the air.
The strike met You Fu’s Sun Finger head-on.
The two forces clashed for a second, then exploded.
BOOM!
They separated instantly, both knocked back several steps.
"Great power!" You Fu laughed. "I thought you killed that old eunuch with tricks, but I underestimated you."
"You flatter me, Senior. I just had a small breakthrough recently." Wei He calmed his trembling blood and muscles.
In that clash, his energy felt like a hard block of tofu. It blocked the hit at first, but when the vibration effect kicked in, it became hard to hold.
After sense-unlocking, the effects of internal force became much stronger.
If he didn't have three defense-focused forces, he would be injured.
Though Wei He still had his bone force and Whale Power unused, You Fu was holding back his cards too.
Wei He knew he was not You Fu’s equal yet.
"Still want to go?" You Fu asked.
"No need. I still have work to do," Wei He shook his head.
He estimated he would need another fighter of his level to stand against You Fu.
Compared to You Fu, the eunuch Zhu Chen was weaker.
"A good fight ends before injury. I see why you asked to meet," You Fu smiled. "In Taizhou today, your power is close to Shangguan Ji."
"Is that so?" Wei He felt relieved. "How do I compare to Yu Qingyan and Yao Zixuan?"
Yu Qingyan was the master of the Lishan Sect. Yao Zixuan was the master of the Danyang Gate.
"Slightly weaker, but you excel in movement. You don't need to fight them head-on."
"Thank you for the answer."
"Which sense do you plan to lock onto first? Different techniques require different choices," You Fu said. "Picking the right one can make your progress much faster."
"No rush..." Wei He asked for details, quickly understanding the process.
To reach the sense-unlocking stage, one had to pick a sense and train it until it transformed.
This stage had five levels, matching five sense-locks.
Passing each lock increased strength, but the process grew harder every time. It also awakened the beast blood inside the practitioner.
"Remember, when you are close to breaking through, stay calm. Control your emotions no matter what you see or feel," You Fu warned.
"I understand," Wei He cupped his hands.
"Anything else?" You Fu asked.
Wei He asked a few more questions about improving perception, and You Fu explained everything clearly.
When they finished talking, You Fu turned to leave.
He glanced toward the Laojun Temple, paused, but did not wipe out the people inside.
Wei He closed his eyes to process the lesson. Inside his body, the Silk Force vibrated rapidly, shaking his internal organs.
This was not an injury. His Silk Force was breaking through!
This step meant moving from Bone-Forging to the Viscera-Training stage.
Layers of bone force leaked from his bones, blending with the energy around his body.
Viscera-Training meant getting full support from the internal organs to sustain a stronger defensive force.
The force coming from his organs made his defense thicker, stronger, and faster.
His Sea-Covering Force shook as its strength grew.
The core of his force leveled up, and bone force spread evenly across his entire body.
He had entered the Viscera-Training stage.
It was only the starting level of the Rain-Covering Cloud Art, and the stat boost was small, but it was a step forward. He did not use the Breakthrough Bead. It happened naturally.
The Silk Force and his improved talent allowed him to cross over smoothly.
Wei He stood in the snow, feeling the difference between the two stages.
He had thought about stopping the process to use the Breakthrough Bead for a perfect leap, but he let that idea go.
A natural breakthrough was already perfect.
He could save the energy of the Breakthrough Bead for something else, like refining his organs later on.
The Viscera-Training stage of his art covered eleven areas across the organs, requiring them to resonate together at the end.
To reach perfection, he had to complete all twelve steps.
He needed to use his force to strengthen each organ, wrapping them in energy like clouds. Only then could he choose a sense to lock onto.
This process was also a way to understand his own body and find his strongest sense.
