"Lianzi... are you really okay?" Lin Xiaoxiao looked with concern at Wen Lian, who sat quietly beside her.
The lighting in the room was dim. Wen Lian’s face was swollen like a pig's head. She sat on the edge of the bed, silent and motionless, staring blankly at a porcelain bottle with lotus and bird carvings on the table.
"Don't worry. I specifically asked Brother Wei for help this time. He used his personal connections to get you out. I transferred the two meat farms you asked me to manage over to him. You don't mind, right?" Lin Xiaoxiao asked carefully.
Wen Lian slowly turned her blank gaze toward her closest friend.
She had been knocked out and kidnapped. After that, she was beaten endlessly, fainting several times. In the end, her own family didn't come to save her. Instead, it was her best friend, Lin Xiaoxiao, who tried everything to get her back.
What broke her heart even more was that when she went home, her father was terrified. He thought she had escaped on her own and actually wanted to send her back to her captors.
"Xiaoxiao... do you think I am very stupid?" Wen Lian suddenly spoke up.
"No, you are just a bit stupid, not very stupid," Lin Xiaoxiao answered honestly.
"..."
Wen Lian thought her friend would take the chance to comfort her, but instead...
Her heart took another heavy blow. Looking at her friend, she couldn't hold back her tears anymore and burst into a loud sob.
"Why am I so unlucky... Why did I have to meet that bad guy? Why? I was beaten non-stop after getting caught... He didn't care at all that I was a girl. I thought I was going to die before I could ever come back..."
Wen Lian cried messily, covered in tears and snot.
Lin Xiaoxiao could only move closer, gently hugging her friend and patting her back so she could cry it all out.
After crying for a long time, Wen Lian slowly stopped. She curled up in her friend's arms, sobbing softly.
"It's okay. I'm here. Everything is fine," Lin Xiaoxiao comforted her softly. "It was all thanks to Brother Wei's help this time, or I wouldn't have been able to save you from Han Yuzhong."
"Brother Wei..." Wen Lian immediately thought of that martial artist from Wanqing Court whom her friend had sponsored.
She used to feel that with her friend's wealth and status, she could have found a much better and stronger martial artist.
Hiring Wei He always felt like a waste of money and a downgrade.
But now... she never expected that the person who ended up saving her was the very man she used to despise.
"Later, you should come with me to thank Brother Wei," Lin Xiaoxiao said seriously.
"Actually, many times when the business at Rushui Shop suddenly got better, I suspected that Brother Wei was secretly helping behind the scenes."
"..." Wen Lian was speechless.
Looking at how things turned out, she had truly misunderstood Wei He in the past.
Even though she had been rude to him multiple times, he was still willing to lend a hand and didn't hold a grudge. That was true generosity.
She looked at her best friend Lin Xiaoxiao, then looked at her current miserable self... Suddenly, she understood why Lin Xiaoxiao always had good luck and could turn dangers into safety, while she herself ended up like this...
The issue with Wen Lian was just a minor detail to Wei He.
Most of the time, solving a problem did not require him to fight alone.
If he had acted personally to save Wen Lian, he might have had to take risks.
However, for someone at Wang Shaojun's level, it only took a single word to fix it effortlessly.
Martial power was often just a tool.
With Wen Lian gone, Han Yuzhong no longer had any worries. After confirming that the Wen family had no real connection to the Wang family, he tested the Wang family's stance. Once he knew there would be no trouble, he showed no mercy and struck the Wen family hard.
The Wen family, which had flourished in Xuanjing City for decades, saw its many businesses shut down. In a single night, their grand manor was raided and seized.
During the era of the new administration, various new departments urgently needed achievements. The Chijing Army also needed huge amounts of grain and beast meat.
After the Wen family was seized, all their assets were quickly confiscated under various charges, gathered together, and sent straight to the Chijing Army camp.
Wen Shang and his family fled into the night, their whereabouts unknown. Wen Lian was left alone at Lin Xiaoxiao's house, losing everything overnight.
Such events were common during the political shift. The Wen family's business channels were quickly swallowed up by rising merchants, giving birth to a new batch of wealthy figures.
Wei He, however, had no time to care about these trivial matters.
Uchi Zhong, the Commander of Xuanjing City, invited the eleven branches of the Tianyin Sect to gather at the Commander's Manor.
Wan Ling decided to bring Wei He and Wan Qingqing along, leaving Yao Hansheng behind to guard the sect.
This meeting was the first time the Tianyin Sect gathered together again after its split.
Judging from the previous meetings Uchi Zhong held with the Xuanjing Chamber of Commerce and local elites, the situation looked grim.
This old Commander had forced Shangguan Ji to watch the Tianyin Sect split and even personally cleaned house. Now, he was finally stepping onto the stage himself to show his true intentions.
In the evening.
Inside the Commander's Manor located in Yuanqi District, lights shone brightly. Soldiers of the Chijing Army, armed with blades and shields, were everywhere, constantly patrolling and guarding the grounds.
Inside the manor's Yushan Hall.
Uchi Zhong had white hair and a healthy, rosy face. He sat at the main seat.
His shoulders were unusually broad, and his long arms hung down past his knees like an ape's. Even just sitting there, his massive upper body gave off a faint, ferocious aura.
While this fierce vibe might scare ordinary people, it was not that special to martial masters.
There were plenty of martial masters with stronger presence than Uchi Zhong, but no one could tell that this old man was actually the second strongest fighter in Xuanjing, second only to Shangguan Ji.
Below the main seat, two rows of chairs were set up on both sides.
Sitting there were the Nine Sons of Tianyin, Vice Master Xie Yan, and Shangguan Ji, who stood out almost as much as Uchi Zhong.
Without his armor, Shangguan Ji looked like an elegant scholar. He was quiet, his eyes calm as water. He looked quite young, and compared to the late Zhou Shun, his aura was much more reserved.
The rest of the Nine Sons of Tianyin had all kinds of appearances.
Some looked like blacksmiths, covered in soot with strong muscles.
Some had plain faces, looking like old rural farmers.
Some were handsome, elegant young masters with noble looks.
One was a muscular giant with a soft, flirtatious gaze.
Another was a white-haired old woman holding a golden bowl like a beggar.
The strange looks and styles of the Nine Sons fully reflected the unique nature of their nine different martial arts.
Wan Ling was among them as well.
She wore a bright yellow dress with green silk sleeves on her arms and a black belt tied around her waist. Her long hair was pinned up, revealing a delicate white neck.
Her dark eyes were bright and gentle. After leaving the Tianyin Sect, she actually looked in better shape than before.
Without the stress of her daughter's illness, her peaceful mind allowed her to return to her gentle and serious self.
Two people stood behind her: Wan Qingqing and Wei He. Both wore the tight outfits of the Wanqing Branch with their long hair tied up.
Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing shared similar pretty faces. They looked lovely and gentle, less like mother and daughter and more like sisters.
One was mature and full, while the other was pure and fresh, drawing plenty of attention. In comparison, Wei He became a total background character.
His ordinary face made him blend in completely next to two beauties.
Wei He didn't mind at all, though.
He never liked showing off or taking the spotlight. Staying low-key fit his style much better.
"Commander, why have you called us here? Please speak directly," Shangguan Ji, as the Sect Master, spoke up first with a calm face.
He was over sixty years old, but thanks to his high martial arts level, he looked at most in his thirties or forties. His voice was clear and sharp, leaving a deep impression.
Uchi Zhong picked up his teacup and took a small sip.
"Master Shangguan, I remember we sparred once many years ago. I lost by a single move back then. I have great respect for your sect's martial arts."
"You flatter me, Commander," Shangguan Ji replied humbly. "We martial artists are only good at small-scale fights and one-on-one combat. We cannot compare to your skills in fighting wars and defending the nation on the battlefield."
"Master Shangguan is too modest. Alright, let's skip the idle talk. I invited everyone here today because our Chijing Army recently fought off the invading rebel forces and protected the side of Taizhou, which cost us a massive amount of supplies."
Uchi Zhong got straight to the point.
"Now, our military rations are running low. Even with the full support of the You, Zhou, and Wang families, the poor harvests everywhere mean our granaries are falling short. This could cause great trouble. So..."
The Commander scanned the room with his eyes.
"So, I heard that all of you are famous figures of goodwill in Xuanjing City. On behalf of the soldiers of the Chijing Army, I hope to receive your support."
He just wants money and food, doesn't he? Wrapping it up in such nice words.
Before coming, the Nine Sons of Tianyin had already heard that Uchi Zhong was constantly holding gatherings to collect donations from local elites.
Now, he was finally targeting the Tianyin Sect.
As a major force that had been in Xuanjing City for over a century, the Tianyin Sect had a vast number of fighters and ran many meat farms, allowing them to hoard huge amounts of grain.
Compared to regular wealthy families, they were far better off in terms of resources.
Many people had already guessed that Uchi Zhong would move against the Tianyin Sect sooner or later.
"Commander, my Arc Light Branch is willing to donate five thousand stone of rough rice and one thousand jin of dried beast meat."
"My Gas Transformation Branch is willing to donate four thousand stone of rough rice and eight hundred jin of beast meat."
"My Yuan Ice Branch is willing to donate four thousand stone of rough rice and seven hundred jin of beast meat..."
"My Zheng Lin Branch is willing to..."
The leaders of each branch spoke up one after another. Soon, it was Wan Ling's turn.
Wan Ling followed the pattern of the others and offered four thousand stone of rough rice and six hundred jin of beast meat.
Only Shangguan Ji and Xie Yan offered more, giving ten thousand stone of rice and one thousand jin of meat each.
This was a massive contribution that wiped out half of their total savings.
The Tianyin Sect had accumulated these resources over many long years. Giving away half at once showed how much pressure they felt.
One stone of rice was about one hundred jin, enough to feed a family of three for more than two months.
In a time of food shortages, these supplies were even more precious.
This was all the savings the Tianyin Sect had built up over so many years in Xuanjing City.
Giving away so much grain and meat pained Wan Ling's heart. But she knew that if she didn't grit her teeth and pay up, they wouldn't get through this easily.
"I can see everyone's sincerity, but... it's not enough," Uchi Zhong shook his head gently.
"My Chijing Army protects the land and keeps the Xiangqu rebels away from you. So..."
Except for Shangguan Ji, everyone else's expression changed slightly. He wanted to strip them of everything they owned.
Yet, as strong as the Tianyin Sect was, they could not fight the Chijing Army.
Even a powerful figure like Zhou Shun was forced to flee, injured after dozens of guns fired at him at once.
If it were them... they looked at the hundreds of armed soldiers surrounding the area...
Standing in the back, Wei He watched the Nine Sons of Tianyin being forced to offer more money and grain.
Seeing their suppressed anger and frustration, he sighed quietly to himself.
No one dared to resist. Look at the dense ranks of strong soldiers around them, the gun barrels on their backs, and the army generals hiding in the dark.
Just among the nearby fighters, there were over ten martial master level presence. Several veteran generals held long, deep breathing styles, openly showing off their powerful force to intimidate everyone.
Only after everyone pledged more support did Uchi Zhong show a smile.
"Alright, thank you all for your strong support of the Chijing Army. Next, I have long heard that the masters of the Tianyin Sect possess incredible skills. Last time I only sparred with Brother Shangguan, and my generals have been eager to see your skills for a long time. Why don't we have a few spars to add to the fun?"
Since he opened his mouth, no one dared to say no.
The Nine Sons of Tianyin agreed one after another.
"If we main leaders fight personally, things might get heated and lose grace. It would be better to let the younger generation step up. That way, we can entertain the host without hurting our relationships," Shangguan Ji suddenly suggested.
He could tell that Uchi Zhong wanted to use force to scare the Tianyin Sect. Having the younger generation fight instead offered a softer buffer.
Uchi Zhong stared at him, paused for a moment, and then smiled.
"Fair enough. Brother Shangguan makes sense. Let the younger ones try their hands."
"We have also been wanting to see the skills of the Tianyin Sect," a young officer in white armor called out from the side.
Martial artists in the military were partly trained by the army itself and partly came from major families, like the Three Great Families.
The Tianyin Sect's members, on the other hand, mainly came from small-to-medium families and merchants. The two sides lived in entirely different worlds.
One belonged to the military, while the other belonged to the martial underworld. They ran on completely different tracks.
Even though both sides practiced martial arts, if military experts fought underworld fighters, who would be stronger...
This was inherently unfair.
Even so, it was still better than having the Nine Sons of Tianyin step into the field themselves.
