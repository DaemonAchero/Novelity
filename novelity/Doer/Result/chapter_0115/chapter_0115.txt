The dinner finally came to an end. Everyone was full and satisfied.
Zhang Husheng, the fat junior, raised his glass for a few final toasts to thank everyone for showing up. With that, the gathering officially ended.
One by one, the disciples said their goodbyes.
Outside the Xiaoyue Building, people dressed in Inner Court robes of the Heavenly Seal Sect boarded their carriages and left. A few fast movers vanished into the dark night in the blink of an eye.
Wei He had a great chat with Chen Huan, Sun Yi, and the others. After breaking up downstairs, he stood alone, looking toward Wan Qingqing from afar.
As the Senior Sister, she was among the last to leave and was saying goodbye to two junior sisters.
Wei He stood by the roadside, waiting quietly.
He walked over only when Wan Qingqing finished her conversation and turned to step onto her carriage.
"Senior Sister Wan. I'm Wei He. Do you have a moment?" he asked calmly.
Wan Qingqing recognized him. This junior brother was a bit special in the Inner Court.
After all, he was a Three-Blood warrior who transferred from the Wushi Sect. His starting line was already higher than most.
"Ah, Junior Brother Wei. Is something the matter?" Wan Qingqing smiled warmly.
She had heard about him during previous chats. Rumor said he killed two Force-Grasping Martial Masters all by himself.
She didn't really believe it, though.
She knew very well how strong a Force-Grasping Martial Master was. Even if two Wei Hes fought together, they wouldn't stand a chance.
Besides, he had already drained his own martial arts. Even if he had clever tricks, how could he block so many gang members on his own?
As for the rumor that he poisoned everyone to win, that was even less believable.
How much poison would it take to knock out a true Martial Master?
Having reached the peak of Force-Grasping herself, Wan Qingqing knew this better than anyone. Only after crossing this threshold could one be called a Martial Master—someone qualified to teach disciples and pass on martial arts.
At this stage, a master's entire body was protected by circulating force, giving them massive resistance against poisons and toxic powders.
She had tested it herself. With her strong body, it took enough poison to knock out dozens of normal people just to make her feel a slight dizziness.
To work on a master, a poison would need a blend of over ten different self-boosting toxins.
So, she simply laughed off those rumors.
Still, Wei He stopping her now made her curious about what he wanted.
"Go ahead, Junior Brother Wei. Are you troubled by those rumors?" she asked with a smile.
"No," Wei He said. "Actually, I planned to visit your court after the party anyway. I've finished training the second layer of the Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Skill, and I need the next manual."
"You finished the second layer?" Wan Qingqing was stunned, then smiled. "Stop joking, Junior Brother Wei. You just took the second manual from me half a year ago, right?"
Among the Wanqing Court disciples, some did break through in half a year—like her, or those two inner disciples who had now reached the third layer.
But they were different. They were recognized geniuses from the start, with endless resources and total focus on training.
Meanwhile, Wei He had drained his martial power before...
"Junior Brother Wei, if I remember right, you drained your past skills, didn't you? You wiped out all your Wushi Sect arts, correct?" Wan Qingqing asked.
"Yes," Wei He nodded.
"Then let me ask you again. Have you really finished the second layer?" Wan Qingqing asked seriously.
"Not 'training in it', but fully completed!"
A cold night wind blew past. The two stood by the carriage. Though they were two meters apart, the mood between them grew tense and solemn.
"Yes, it is done," Wei He nodded. "The control part of the second layer is hard, but I trained similar martial arts before, so it was familiar ground."
The smile on Wan Qingqing's face slowly faded away.
She stepped forward and reached out her hand.
"Do you mind?"
"Go ahead." Wei He reached out his arm, letting her press her fingers against his forearm.
The martial arts of the Heavenly Seal Sect were all centered on the hands. To test Wei He's progress, a quick touch on his arm was all it took.
Wan Qingqing's fingers were ice-cold and smooth. As she pressed down, a needle-like force pushed out from her fingers.
This force instantly triggered Wei He's internal blood energy. A large amount of Seal Blood automatically rushed to the spot she pressed.
A moment later, Wan Qingqing pulled her hand back. She looked up at Wei He, feeling both shocked and sorry for him.
"You really reached the second layer. I will give you the third manual when I get back."
She was shocked by his talent. He was smarter than she thought, or perhaps his body fit the Heavenly Seal Sect's skills perfectly. His speed was fast enough to match her own back then.
Many inner disciples spent years stuck between the first and second layers without breaking through.
Yet Wei He had only joined two years ago, and he had already crossed a wall that held others back for years.
If he had joined the Heavenly Seal Sect from the very start, he might have been a top genius on her level by now.
What a pity...
Wan Qingqing felt sorry in her heart. He had drained his power before.
Draining one's power damaged the foundation of martial arts, the body's bones, and even one's lifespan. Even if he broke through and reached Force-Grasping, his protective force would be weak. He might not even defeat a strong Three-Blood warrior.
He had destroyed his natural foundation.
"Thank you, Senior Sister," Wei He said, cupping his fists with respect.
"I will tell my mother about this. Maybe she can check your body later and find a way to fix it," Wan Qingqing added.
Wei He's heart froze. His disguise skill, the Five Spirits Breath Hiding Art, could fool people from the outside, but if a master examined him up close, he would definitely get exposed.
His mind raced. He quickly put on a pained, reluctant, and embarrassed look on his face.
"No need!" he said in a low voice. "I only drained my skills, I am not a cripple. I won't trouble Master with this."
He acted like a proud man who refused to show his weakness to anyone.
"But..." Wan Qingqing wanted to say more, but seeing his expression under the moonlight, she thought she understood his pain. Her eyes grew even softer.
Reaching Force-Grasping with a ruined body would bring him humiliation rather than pride.
"Alright, I understand..." She nodded seriously. "Do you need me to keep this secret for you?"
He broke through so fast, yet his foundation was broken from draining his power. He could have been a hidden star, but now, he was bound to fall short.
"No need to hide it on purpose. Just treat it normally. It's no big deal," Wei He said in a deep voice, pretending to be strong.
"...Junior Brother Wei, don't lose heart. History has seen great men who drained their skills yet turned their fate around through sheer willpower. As long as you reach the Bone Forging stage, you'll have a chance to rebuild your Seal Blood. Bones are the source of blood, so..."
She comforted him softly.
Wei He had no words for this, so he just tried his best to look like a man hiding his pain behind a brave face.
They chatted a bit more before parting ways, agreeing to meet again inside the Wanqing Court.
Wei He watched her carriage leave, then turned toward the dark streets.
On his way back, his tall 1.9-meter frame, muscular build, and cold eyes warned everyone to stay away. Nobody dared to cause him trouble.
As he walked, he pondered his situation. His three old martial arts—Fierce Tiger Fist, Five Ridge Palm, and Flying Dragon Art—were starting to fall behind.
He had to find a way to fix this.
First, he could not drain his skills again. Draining power ruined the body's foundation.
Since he couldn't drain them, he could just leave them idle. No matter how many arts he trained or how much they slowed down his breakthroughs, his Breakthrough Pearl could force its way through any limit anyway.
Plus, keeping them in his body gave him an extra layer of power.
However, Wei He had always had an idea in mind—an idea about how the Breakthrough Pearl actually worked. He needed to save that thought for later when the time was right.
He ran fast, moving at a high speed. In less than an hour, he arrived at the entrance of Wanqing Court by the Heavenly Seal Lake.
After waiting for a short while, Wan Qingqing finally arrived.
Without wasting words, she stepped off her carriage, nodded at him, walked inside, and quickly brought out a book scroll to hand over.
"This is the third manual. It contains the Master Chart for the third layer. Do not let anyone else see it. Leaking it is a severe crime in our sect and will bring disaster."
She warned him carefully.
"Understood. Thank you, Senior Sister!" Wei He took the manual with both hands. Barring any accidents, this was his foundation for Force-Grasping.
He had traveled thousands of miles from Feiye City and Yun Prefecture to Wushi Sect just to take this step into Force-Grasping.
He then left Wushi Sect for Heavenly Seal Sect for the exact same reason.
Everything he did had been stuck at this final hurdle.
Now, the key to that step was placed right into his hands.
Wei He suddenly felt a heavy weight in his heart.
"Junior Brother Wei," Wan Qingqing said, looking at him with a serious face. "It took me a year and a half to master the third layer. This layer focuses on comprehension and control, turning the Seal Blood across your body into force. The key is to follow the divine intent of the Master Chart..."
Standing under the moonlight, she carefully explained her insights and key points to Wei He.
Wei He listened quietly, taking every word to heart.
More than half an hour passed quickly. Wan Qingqing finished her explanation and said goodbye.
Before leaving, she comforted him once more, telling him not to worry about having weak force after reaching Force-Grasping. As long as he aimed for Bone Forging, he would have another chance in the future.
Wei He gave a quiet nod in response.
Once back in his room, he opened the third layer of the Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Skill right away.
The core of the third layer was refining.
Refining Seal Blood into internal force was what people called Force Conversion.
The manual explained methods to refine blood into force, along with various problems one might face during the process.
It also noted that the Rain-Covering Force trained by each person was unique. Some were strong, some weak, some covered the entire body, and others covered only small spots.
Some forces had strong piercing power, while others were soft.
At the very end of the manual was the Master Chart.
The picture showed heavy rain falling from the sky, crashing onto the foggy surface of the Heavenly Seal Lake.
The lake surface was completely empty.
It was a visual guide showing what the web of refined force should look like as a whole.
Wei He studied it carefully for a moment, then put the manual away safely.]
