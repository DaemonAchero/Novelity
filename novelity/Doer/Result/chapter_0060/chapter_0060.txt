Jiang Yan, Jiang Su, Senior Brother Zhao Hong, Wei He, and the newly promoted Zhang Lu had all arrived.
They stood quietly to the side with their heads bowed, looking at the corpse in the middle of the room.
"Teacher, how did Junior Brother Xiao die?" Zhao Hong asked in a low voice.
Zheng Fugui stayed silent for a moment. He took a deep breath and said slowly, "Someone used extreme palm strength. The first strike broke his arm. The second strike hit his chest. His ribs collapsed, piercing his heart and organs."
He paused.
"The killer's palm strength is very strong. It should be a master at the Third Blood Refinement stage. But these force marks... I cannot think of anyone in the city who excels at such explosive palm strikes."
"Third Blood Refinement masters are all famous figures. Why would they kill my son? There was no grudge! My son was not even qualified to offend such a master! What on earth happened!? Master Zheng, tell me, what happened!?"
Xiao Ran's father, Xiao Zhongcheng, stood up and asked every word with anger.
His words made it clear that he believed Xiao Ran's death was caused by Zheng Fugui's enemies, and his son was just collateral damage.
It was reasonable for him to think so. Even Zheng Fugui himself suspected the same thing right now.
To put it simply, although Xiao Ran had strong potential, his potential dropped after failing his breakthrough. Being beaten to death like this was not jealousy of his talent. It was a targeted revenge murder.
A Second Blood Refinement user had no way to offend an old Third Blood Refinement master on normal days.
Zheng Fugui remained silent, unable to reply.
Fortunately, Xiao Ran's father was still rational. He knew the man in front of him was a Force Martial Master, a top expert in the city who was stronger than ordinary Third Blood Refinement users, so he did not dare to act recklessly.
He was just angry. Suppressing his rage, he left the martial academy slowly with two servants carrying his son's corpse.
In these chaotic times, even a strong person like Xiao Ran could die at any moment.
Zheng Fugui stood quietly in the yard next to the empty spot where Xiao Ran's body had been. He said nothing and remained silent.
A dark anger raged inside his heart. He swore he would find the true killer and avenge his favorite disciple.
But no matter how he swore, Xiao Ran could not come back to life.
The other disciples dispersed quietly. Some went to practice, while others went to rest.
Jiang Su stood to the side, looking at where Xiao Ran used to stand. Her mood was complex.
This guy, whom she had once liked and hated, had died in the blink of an eye.
It was a feeling hard to describe.
Now in the academy, there was no one left with the hope to break through to the Third Blood Refinement stage.
She knew her own situation well. Giving all her beast meat to Wei He meant she had already given up on breaking through.
These days, she clearly felt her blood refinement recovery getting slower. It meant her body recovered poorly after being damaged.
With such damaged potential, trying to push for the Third Blood Refinement was too hard.
As for Zhang Lu, it would take him a long time to reach completion. Whether he would fail like Xiao Ran in the future was also unknown.
The situation was getting harder. Whether the Huishan Fist Academy could wait until Zhang Lu grew up was a mystery.
She raised her head and looked at Wei He. Wei He had also been threatened and blocked by Xiao Ran before, so they had a grudge.
However, Wei He's expression right now was no different from the other brothers. He looked like he had no big emotions, no hatred, no joy, only a subtle mix of sorrow.
It was as if he felt pity and sadness for Xiao Ran's death.
Jiang Su wanted to speak, but after thinking for a moment, she stayed quiet. She turned back to her spot and continued grinding her skin to condense her blood into new Nine Cloud Flowers.
After her failed breakthrough, she had lost one flower and had to refine it again. Although she had no hope left, reaching full blood refinement could still boost her real battle power. In this increasingly chaotic world, strength was the best protection.
Wei He did not waste time either. He went to his spot and started condensing Nine Cloud Flowers. Now he did not need to save energy for the Break-Through Bead, so he could use his blood refinement freely and finally continue his Huishan Fist progress.
Ouyang Zhuang was sighing beside him, looking like his father had just died.
"What happened to you?" Wei He asked.
"Nothing, I just lost an old customer. It will be hard to make money in the future," Ouyang Zhuang said helplessly.
"An old customer?"
"Yeah, the drawings I... well, I cannot say." The kid quickly shut his mouth, almost leaking the secret.
Wei He instantly thought of that erotic painting and felt speechless.
"Just practice, do not delay."
"Ah, yes!" Ouyang Zhuang quickly started heating his sand basin. But right after setting it up, he raised his head again.
"Senior Brother Wei, can I leave a bit early later?"
"What is it?"
"A junior brother plans to leave. Before he goes, he wants to invite everyone for a meal." Ouyang Zhuang said in a low voice.
"Go ahead." Wei He nodded.
"Okay..." Ouyang Zhuang nodded.
Wei He said nothing more and began grinding his skin carefully to condense the Nine Cloud Flowers.
The quality of this Third Blood Refinement was clearly much higher than the Second level. His fists struck the iron sand repeatedly. The hot sand kept grinding his skin. Every time he finished a round and put his hands into the medicine basin, he could feel blood power constantly gathering at his upper arms.
Everyone's Nine Cloud Flowers formed at different places. For Cheng Shaojiu, it was on his back. He had not seen Jiang Su's, but Senior Brother Zhao Hong's was on his chest.
Wei He's own flowers were on his upper arms.
High quality blood power, super fast recovery speed, and a body that felt much clearer after breaking through to the Third Blood Refinement made his blood flow much faster without friction or delay.
Sometimes, Wei He could even clearly feel the progress as a Nine Cloud Flower took shape step by step.
Slow, steady, and unstoppable.
He practiced in one go until the afternoon ended. Wei He felt his right upper arm burning hot, as if something was forming, but when he pulled up his sleeve, he saw nothing.
Master Zheng went out in the afternoon. When he came back near the end of the day, his face was dark and he looked very unhappy.
After finishing his practice, Wei He planned to pack up and leave, but a junior brother called him into the inner room.
Master Zheng was summoning them.
Zheng Fugui warned all the inner disciples one by one. He told everyone to stay in groups as much as possible, avoid traveling alone, stop going out at night, and stay safe.
He said nothing extra, but Wei He could sense that something bad was about to happen.
After returning, several days passed, and the Huishan Fist Academy acted as if nothing had happened.
Except for Master Zheng looking a bit older, everything else stayed the same as before.
Fortunately, there was the newly promoted Zhang Lu, whose talent was not weaker than Xiao Ran's. This seemed to give Master Zheng a new glimmer of hope.
Wei He did not pay attention to these things anymore. Now that he had reached the Third Blood Refinement, practicing Huishan Fist again allowed his blood power to condense much faster than before.
In just four days, he condensed the second Nine Cloud Flower. One must know that each flower was harder to form than the last, requiring more blood power each time.
Yet he was able to reach the second flower so easily and quickly. This proved that his previous strategy was completely right.
Use the dark path first, then the righteous path, combining the strange with the normal!
Not only that, Wei He also started practicing the Flying Dragon Skill in his spare time. Powered by his strong blood supply, this leg technique also broke through rapidly.
In about ten days, it reached the second layer.
The Flying Dragon Skill required condensing blood-red dragon marks on the legs. It worked on the same principle as the Nine Cloud Flowers, where the dragon marks showed the layer of the skill.
This technique was extremely difficult and had very high demands on talent and mind. If a breakthrough failed, the blood power would backfire and strike the heart, making it extremely dangerous.
The skill had six layers in total. Each extra dragon mark meant going up one layer. Step, Walk, Run, Cross, Sweep, and Fly.
Six layers in total, and each layer matched a certain level of speed.
The highest level of this martial art was only the Third Blood Refinement, but it belonged to the very rare category of leg techniques.
According to what Old Zheng had said, any leg technique that could generate Force was quite precious.
That was because truly powerful leg techniques allowed one to attack or defend at will, keeping a person undefeated by nature. Coming and going like the wind made joining or leaving a battlefield extremely fast.
Wei He paid special attention to this Flying Dragon Skill. Even though it had no path to Force after the Third level, it would still be great if he could force his way up using the Break-Through Bead.
Training backwards from the Third Blood Refinement allowed him to break through two layers of the Flying Dragon Skill very easily, and his speed increased a lot. However, the progress slowed down after that.
Although he still moved forward steadily without obstacles, the speed was much slower.
Because of this, he grew even more wary of other Third Blood Refinement masters.
Looking at it this way, the older a Third Blood Refinement master was, the more likely they were to spend a lot of time using their blood power to learn a second or even third martial art.
Given that a Third Blood Refinement master got twenty extra years of life, experts at this level were completely on a different tier compared to Second Blood Refinement users.
No one knew how many techniques an expert at this level had practiced. The older they were, the stronger they might be.
Ordinary blood boiling only boosted basic physical strength and reaction time. But if blood power was condensed into proper martial techniques, the power was totally different.
After all, the blood power generated each day was limited, and the body could only store so much.
Martial techniques were different. Once condensed successfully, they required very little blood power to maintain.
In theory, the number of techniques a person could condense was unlimited.
About a month after reaching the second layer of the Flying Dragon Skill, Wei He finally condensed every single Nine Cloud Flower of the Huishan Fist without any trouble. He fully reached the level of nine iron-skin flowers.
Master Zheng had no time to check his cultivation lately. He seemed busy with other matters, leaving early and returning late every day. Other martial masters often visited him, as if discussing or preparing something.
Wei He was in no rush to show off his strength. His speed of condensing was too fast. Checking him two months ago showed only one flower, and now all nine were full. That speed was far too abnormal.
He decided to wait a while longer before showing it. Besides, he wanted to train his Flying Dragon Skill a bit more. This leg technique increased stamina and speed, making it clearly superior to Huishan Fist and Wuling Palm. It could serve as his core skill.
Completing all nine Nine Cloud Flowers meant his iron skin was complete and his Huishan Fist had reached its peak.
The next step was to figure out how to gain Force.
Reaching the Third Blood Refinement naturally allowed a person to touch upon Force, but that was not true Force mastery. Only when Force spread across the entire body to withstand normal blades and spears would it count as real Force mastery.
However, that belonged to the next realm. Wei He could not wait that long. Now that he had achieved the Third Blood Refinement, he needed to quickly investigate what happened to his parents and eldest sister.
It was time to visit Mount Shaoyang again...
