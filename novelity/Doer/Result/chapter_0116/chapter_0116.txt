Wei He decided to deal with his leftover problems.
He spent five days throwing money around to buy information about Xiao You. With the steady income from Rushui House and the silver he robbed earlier, he still had plenty of money left. Buying extra meat was nothing to him.
In those five days, he hired people to ask around, drugged sources for truth, and tracked down former White Snake Gang members. He quickly found out where Xiao You was.
When her father was alive, Xiao You had decent talent and trained in martial arts. But she was only at the First Blood level and had not made any Blood Ink yet.
After learning her father died, she felt pure hatred. She took the money her father hid, paid off a big name in Fushan Yard, and joined the Heavenly Ink Sect outer courtyard.
The outer courtyard was her safest choice.
Every sect had a rule against disciples killing each other, and Heavenly Ink Sect was the same.
Because she was in the sect, Wei He could not attack her out in the open without breaking the rules.
She was smart to do this.
Wei He really could not attack her directly, so he chose another way.
Fushan Yard was the closest yard to Thousand Bat Water Pavilion. It was one of the Nine Yards, just like Wanqing Yard.
It was also closest to the outer courtyard.
In a small yard in the outer courtyard, Xiao You wore a tight white outfit that showed off her curves.
She practiced the Heart Lock Ink technique, a basic skill for outer disciples.
Sweat dropped from her forehead as muscles in her arms swelled up. She had never wanted to get strong this badly.
She wanted to make the killer feel the pain of losing family. She wanted to push him into a corner until he gave up in despair.
She gave up her body and spent a huge sum of money to connect with a big figure in Fushan Yard.
She did not care if she was just a toy to him. She only cared about revenge.
The dark clouds covered half the moon, making the light fade.
"Why are you still training?"
A tall, strong man stepped out of the inner room without a shirt.
He had sharp eyebrows like swords. His blood energy rolled wildly around him like waves. He was a master from the inner courtyard at the Three Blood level.
"Brother Cheng, I feel uneasy," Xiao You stopped and said with a frown.
The man was named Hu Zicheng. He was an inner disciple she worked hard to seduce.
Thanks to his connections, she joined the sect and became an outer disciple.
"Are you worried about Wei He?" Hu Zicheng laughed. "He would not dare to strike here. If he kills another disciple, the rules will destroy him. Besides, sect elders guard this area. He is just a Three Blood like me. How could he kill you?"
"You are right. Thank you, Brother Cheng." Xiao You felt much better hearing this.
Wei He could not touch her here.
Given enough time, she would make Wei He feel the same pain of losing family.
She would make him regret everything he did.
She heard he had a sister living in Tianyin Town...
Hatred flashed in her eyes.
Just then, someone knocked on the door.
"Is Senior Hu inside?" It was the voice of a junior brother from the outer courtyard.
"Is that Dongzi? What is it?" Hu Zicheng walked to the door and blocked the view so the boy could not see Xiao You.
"Senior Hu, here is a letter for you."
Hu Zicheng took the letter, tore it open, and read it quickly. But after a few lines, his face changed, and he read it again carefully.
Xiao You stood far behind him and could not see what was written.
She only saw him standing still at the door, reading the paper over and over. Even after the messenger left, he did not move.
After a long time, she got worried.
"Brother Cheng?"
"It is nothing." Hu Zicheng smiled, hid the letter, and turned back. "You take a rest, Youyou. I need to go out and handle something. I will be right back."
"Okay," Xiao You said, acting submissive.
Hu Zicheng quickly put on his coat, grabbed his things, and left. He vanished into the dark night.
Xiao You stood at the door alone, watching his back as he left.
"To defeat an enemy, you isolate them, weaken them, break their spirit, and waste their energy."
In the Wei family yard at Tianyin Town, Wei He stood with his hands behind his back. He looked at the half-moon in the sky with a calm face.
He had already spread the word that he reached the Second Layer and was close to gaining force.
He also spread another rumor: someone else actually killed the two gang leaders back then.
Xiao You had some talent, but she was only at First Blood. She relied on her father's money and her looks.
With just that, the people she could attract were limited.
Wei He easily found out about her and Hu Zicheng.
Hu Zicheng was greedy. His potential at Fushan Yard was drying up. He thought Wei He was stuck too, which was why he decided to help her.
Now that Wei He spread the news of his breakthrough, he wrote a letter explaining the pros and cons clearly.
Hu Zicheng knew what to choose.
Should he pick Xiao You, a girl with no backing and only a bit of money and looks? Or should he pick Wei He, who was about to gain force and had far more money?
They were not related, so the choice was simple.
As for the rumor that someone else killed her father, it was meant to confuse her and drain her mind.
A person might make impulsive choices out of anger, but only if they knew their target.
If she was not even sure who the real killer was, her hate and drive would fade.
Without strong will, her actions would slow down.
Over time, her chance would pass, and she would lose the drive to do anything.
A short while later, Wei He walked out of his yard and went to the lake.
A shadow moved quickly from afar and landed lightly on the rocky shore.
The two men stood face to face.
It was Hu Zicheng, who was just with Xiao You a moment ago.
He looked at Wei He with uncertainty. Wei He looked dangerous. His body was larger than Hu Zicheng's.
Wei He's eyes were peaceful and calm, showing he planned everything.
Looking at him and thinking of the letter, Hu Zicheng felt nervous.
"Junior Wei, I joined the sect earlier than you. Can I call you that?" He bowed with joined hands.
"Of course. Senior Hu, do not worry about small things. I invited you here because I heard you have a link with Xiao You, so I wanted to clear things up." Wei He spoke sincerely.
"Oh? How will you clear them up?" Hu Zicheng narrowed his eyes.
"I heard Senior Hu uses the Hidden Sleeve Sword from Fushan Yard, and it is very strong with Fushan Ink. I want to test my skill against yours tonight." Wei He said directly.
He had guessed Hu Zicheng's nature. Now he just needed to add more weight to the scale.
He needed to make Hu Zicheng see how dumb it was to offend Wei He for a weak girl and a little money.
Then the choice would be clear.
Hu Zicheng was smart and understood right away.
"Please go ahead."
He was already shaken. He just wanted to check Wei He's power so he had a better reason to walk away.
Without more words, both men stepped forward.
Wei He waved his hands, poking out ten fingers to create a wall of palm prints like heavy rain.
Hu Zicheng held his hands half-closed. Force burst from his fingers, forming a heavy wall to block the rain.
Pfft.
Not long into the fight, Hu Zicheng felt pain in his arms. It felt like a strange force was sinking into his bones.
Rain-Covering Force should not feel like this.
It was simply that Wei He was too strong. The raw force shook his arms and hurt him.
After twenty moves, they stopped using basic sect moves and stepped back to where they started.
A moment later, Hu Zicheng bowed.
"I have learned much. I was wrong about Xiao You." He said no more, turned, and left.
He walked far away until Wei He's shadow vanished in the night. Only then did Hu Zicheng slow down and catch his breath.
He wiped his forehead. His hand was wet with sweat.
Only he knew that even though the fight looked calm, Wei He could have killed him at least six times.
Several times, he felt force graze his throat and forehead. That terrifying feeling made his blood run cold.
Their real battle experience was too far apart. Compared to Wei He, he was just a weak flower in a greenhouse.
"Wei He... I can never make him an enemy!"
Fear filled Hu Zicheng's eyes.
Looking back, he saw through the simple trap Wei He set for Xiao You.
His fear of Wei He grew even deeper.
Simple, direct, cruel, and precise.
He could not handle this man's mind or skills.
Hu Zicheng made his choice. He took Xiao You's money, but the enemy was too strong, so no one could blame him.
He chose not to go back to the outer courtyard. He headed straight for his other home in Xuanjing City and vanished into the night.
Over the next few days, Hu Zicheng kept an eye on Wei He and Xiao You.
Xiao You came looking for him many times, but every time he thought about her, he remembered Wei He's face from that night.
That mild face hiding a dark intent to kill scared him. He pretended he was not home and refused to see her.
He acted like he never took her money or met her.
At his age, he only cared about buying property and planning for his future. He did not want to make an enemy of a talented fellow disciple for no reason.
As for Xiao You, she had a bit of talent, money, and looks, but unused potential was not real strength.
Hu Zicheng backed away.
