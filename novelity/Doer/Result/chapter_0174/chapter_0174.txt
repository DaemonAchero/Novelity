The water grows deeper and deeper.
The light all around grows darker and darker.
Wei He can barely see anything in front of him now. He can only see a small spot of pale white light on the surface of the water far above.
By now, the surface has shrunk to the size of a washbasin, and the light shining down is growing even dimmer and weaker.
The water temperature all around is turning bone-chillingly cold.
Yet Wei He feels fine. When he broke through, he used the cold-shaman bird eyeball, which carried an even more intense chill than the ordinary spring-bird eyeball.
Because of this, his Rain-Covering Qi inherently gained a trace of coldness. This trace of coldness gave him a strong resistance to the cold.
So this bit of chill is nothing to him.
The light overhead grows dimmer, and the water pressure all around grows heavier.
Wei He gradually feels the pressure pressing against his body begin to increase, slowly squeezing his protective Rain-Covering Qi.
The speed at which the penetrating force seeps into his bone marrow is getting faster and faster—almost several times faster than normal. But the consumption of his internal energy is also several times greater than usual.
Finally, he feels the pressure pushing his protective energy to its limit, and a negative pressure starts pressing against his skin and muscles.
Only then does Wei He swim slightly, using one hand to support the stone and the other to grab the rocky wall of the deep pool at his side.
The stone wall is slippery and covered in all kinds of aquatic plants, but when Wei He stabs his fingers—reinforced with internal energy—into the wall, his five fingers sink deep into the stone, gripping it tightly and holding him in place.
Wei He simply stops right here, quietly closing his eyes to regulate his breathing.
This spot is at least several hundred meters deep below the surface of the pool. The light from the surface is reduced to just a single dot.
Pitch darkness surrounds him.
Wei He clings quietly to the stone wall and reaches behind his waist to take out an animal lung.
It is a brown, balloon-like bubble filled with air from the outside.
It can provide him with oxygen.
He originally prepared this thing as a backup measure against wide-range poison attacks that could pollute the air.
He never expected to use it here.
Time ticks by slowly.
Suddenly, not far away, a dark red object the size of a washbasin slowly floats up from the even deeper waters below.
Wei He pauses, then looks over carefully.
Using the tiny bit of light from above, the dark red creature slowly reveals its full form.
It is actually a huge red meat butterfly!
An underwater creature that looks just like a dark red butterfly!
It gently flaps its red wings, dragging a few flower-stamen-like tentacles from its tail.
Its body turns and swims softly, moving closer to Wei He.
Wei He quickly gathers all his internal energy to guard against any possible attack from this creature.
To his surprise, the red butterfly does not attack him. Instead, it floats up and down in front of him, gently flapping its wings.
Swish.
Suddenly, patches of blue fluorescent patterns light up all over the red butterfly's body.
A large number of patterns—resembling patches, arcs, and text symbols—flicker with a faint blue light, growing bright and dim on its body.
Only now, aided by the light, does Wei He see what the red butterfly's head looks like.
It is a red, mushroom-like protrusion covered in countless dense, tiny red eyes.
The red butterfly, glowing with blue light, swims around Wei He a few times, seems to lose interest, and turns to swim away in another direction.
The light on its body quickly fades, and it soon vanishes into the gloomy depths of the water.
"What was that?"
In the Tianya Tower of the Tianyin Sect, Wei He has read a massive amount of historical records regarding strange beasts.
Over the years, he has also made records of every strange beast he has ever seen.
Yet none of those records match the strange beast he just saw.
He suddenly recalls a travel journal he read long ago, where the author wrote a single sentence: "When I truly stepped into the uninhabited world, I suddenly understood that to this world, perhaps we are the outsiders."
At this very moment, Wei He feels an inexplicable resonance.
The number of strange beasts in this world is massive, and their species are endless. What people can detect is only limited to the areas around their own settlements. As for remote places, countless strange beasts and insects remain undiscovered.
Sighing inwardly, Wei He readjusts his state and continues the practice of his penetrating force.
Half an hour later, Wei He surfaces from the pool, ending his training for the day.
Starting from today, he will dive to this depth every day, relying on the all-around water pressure to increase the penetration pressure of his internal energy and accelerate his bone-forging progress.
However, the consumption of nutrients also increases greatly. Wei He's appetite is much bigger than before—helped entirely by the Breakthrough Bead, which greatly boosts his digestive and absorptive capacity.
If an ordinary martial artist tried this, their food supply alone could not keep up.
Such special training requires enduring not only high water pressure, but also the bone-chilling cold of the pool.
Fortunately, the effects are truly good, and his bone-forging progress is advancing at a speed visible to the naked eye.
Added to that is the endless consumption of massive amounts of strange beast meat eaten without worrying about digestion speed. Wei He estimates that stepping into the bone-forging stage should be very soon for him.
Bone-forging is divided into four limbs, the torso, and the most dangerous skull.
What is forged is the bone, but what the internal energy actually penetrates is the bone marrow—improving the hematopoietic function of the bone marrow.
Therefore, the final hurdle of bone-forging is actually the skull.
This difficult barrier has trapped countless martial artists, and a moment of carelessness can lead to injury.
But Wei He is not afraid. By the time he reaches that point, his Breakthrough Bead should have already reached perfection.

One year later...
Central Town, Xuanjing City.
Wei He sits on the second floor of a small teahouse, watching the Chijing Army troops slowly march past below.
He sits there openly; no one recognizes him, and no one has the mind to arrest him.
"The Chijing Army going to war this time is something they had to do." Across from him, Wang Shaojun picks up his wine cup and takes a light sip.
"How so?" Wei He asks calmly.
He knows that recently, his friend became the ruling official of the Quanye Dao in Xuanjing, gaining the position of Deputy Dao Master, on equal footing with the county magistrate, and with dozens of people under his command.
"It is not because of the pressure from the Tai'an Army, nor for any other reason, but simply because the Xiangqu Sect's general Li Tong captured Bianzhou and led an army of four hundred thousand to approach our Taizhou. Driven by the circumstances, Yuchi Zhong has no choice but to move." Wang Shaojun sighs.
"Four hundred thousand..." Wei He falls silent.
"They claim four hundred thousand, but the real number is probably around two hundred thousand. However, this rebel army is no longer the mob of before. After several major battles and years of grinding warfare, they are definitely seasoned veterans. And the Chijing Army... their real number will not exceed one hundred thousand. This battle... will be difficult." Wang Shaojun shakes his head.
"What about the other garrison troops in Taizhou?" Wei He asks.
"You think there is only Li Tong's rebel army? And do you think holding troops to secure one's own power is just talk? Unless the enemy is at the front door, who is willing to use their own strength to help others drain their resources?" Wang Shaojun asks in return.
"If they lose..."
"If they lose, Xuanjing will fall into the hands of the Tai'an Army. By then, you can openly show your face and enter the city." Wang Shaojun smiles.
"So, I should hope they lose?" Wei He shakes his head.
"Let's not talk about this. The poison I asked you to figure out last time—do you know if the antidote is ready yet?" Wang Shaojun asks.
"You mean that Evil Dragon Powder?" Wei He frowns slightly. He has no idea where Wang Shaojun found that poison; its complex ingredients and stubborn toxicity are the hardest he has ever seen in his life.
"Right, it is a fatal poison that spread from the demon faction. So-called fatal poisons can reportedly only be unlocked by the leader of the Nine Li demon faction. It is a method they use to control the experts under them," Wang Shaojun explains.
"So, you took in an expert poisoned with this, and you want to help him detoxify and recruit him for your own use?" Wei He instantly understands.
"Brother Wei knows me best." Wang Shaojun nods with a smile. "I am currently using the slow-acting medicine you gave me earlier to suppress the toxicity and buy him time, but keeping this up is not a solution."
"Evil Dragon Powder is only troublesome, not impossible to solve. It just takes time." Wei He shakes his head. "Besides, I still have to focus on my martial arts cultivation. I don't have time to invest heavily in this."
"Then how do you still have time to go and cause trouble for Zhou Xingtong?" Wang Shaojun sneers in return. "Half a year ago, you and Zhou Xingtong fought outside the city again; don't think I don't know. Three months ago, you fought him again. That is just what I know about—who knows how many times you two have fought?"
Wei He falls silent.
It is true. After breaking through the Iron Ridge Garment, he was unwilling to stop and went to fight Zhou Xingtong again.
The outcome was the same as last time: neither side could do anything to the other. Only this time, both sides were wary of each other, so they did not fight to the point of severe injury like before.
Afterward, every time Wei He thought of a potentially useful new idea or new poisoning technique, he would go pick a fight immediately.
As a result, that fellow Zhou Xingtong was not just sitting around either; he also prepared new methods to deal with Wei He.
With both sides going back and forth, they remain at a stalemate.
In a head-on fight, Wei He cannot win. In speed, Zhou Xingtong cannot catch him. Neither can do anything to the other.
"Let's drop this. Did you know that Shangguan Ji has retired?" Wang Shaojun drops his sneer and says in a deep voice.
"Oh?"
"I got the news the day before yesterday. He left Taizhou with his father, a concubine, and his younger brother—Shangguan Jue, the head of the Yuanbing Courtyard. No one knows where they went."
"What about the Thousand-Bat Water Pavilion and the Tianya Tower?" Wei He narrows his eyes.
"What do you think? Right now, those places have been sealed off by the government yamen. The Chijing Army has no time to deal with it, as they are in a rush to fight Li Tong. Yuchi Zhong is the commander, and Zhou Xingtong is the lead general. They have no time to care about such petty matters."
Wang Shaojun starts to laugh. "The only branches of the Tianyin Sect still left are your Wanqing Sect, the Chidi Sect, and the Zhenglin Sect. What are your plans?"
"The Thousand-Bat Water Pavilion and the Tianya Tower are just dead objects." Wei He is silent for a moment before speaking slowly.
"So?" Wang Shaojun looks at him with a half-smile.
"Even though they are dead objects, the Tianyin Sect is now falling apart, and my Wanqing Sect stands alone at the top. We must turn the tide, and those things rightfully belong to us." Wei He looks up.
"Hehe, you are still as shameless as ever. Aren't you afraid the other two sects will object?"
"The Thousand-Bat Water Pavilion is fine, but the Tianya Tower holds a massive amount of martial arts manuals. We can never allow them to fall into other people's hands."
"What if they don't agree?"
"They won't have a choice," Wei He says calmly.
He has a feeling that his four-limb bone-forging is about to reach perfection in these few days, and for the final skull barrier, he can probably use the Breakthrough Bead to skip past it directly.
In that case, the bone-forging stage—which is extremely dangerous for ordinary martial artists—will be a flat path for him.
Once bone-forging is completed, he originally planned to find a chance to beat Zhou Xingtong to death. Unfortunately, since the Chijing Army is now on an expedition, who knows when he will return.
Wei He already has a clear idea in his mind.
He plans to use the Wanqing Sect's Rain-Covering Gathering Clouds Skill as his main practice, constantly breaking through, supplemented by complementary techniques to strengthen his Rain-Covering Qi.
Until he strengthens the Rain-Covering Qi to its absolute limit.
The Tianyin Nine-Strikes True Skill originally belonged only to the Shangguan family—it was their family's martial art, and outsiders could not get the true teachings.
Since that is the case, he will not beg for the true teachings. Instead, he will walk a path of his own.
The Rain-Covering Gathering Clouds Skill is also a true skill. He refuses to believe that with the power of the Breakthrough Bead combined with his own cleverness, he cannot carve out a path to the heavens!
"It seems you already have a plan now?" Wang Shaojun smiles and asks again.
"I have some plans." Wei He says flatly. "When I go back this time, I will challenge my teacher, Wan Ling, and seize the position of Wanqing Sect Master."
"Good." Wang Shaojun nods heavily. "If you need any help, just say the word."
Clink.
The two raise their cups in a toast and drink them down in one go.
