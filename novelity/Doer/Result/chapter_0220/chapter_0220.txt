Wei He brewed all seventy-two poisons into a lethal net and handed them to Wang Shaojun. With his payment and materials in hand, he returned to Thousand Bat Pavilion.
He had moved half his people back here. Old Lady Zeng and her partner also returned to Tianya House to run their old business.
He did not care how the Wang family planned to bait the Meteor Bandit. Once back, he locked himself away with Wan Qingqing to cultivate. At the same time, he used his men and money to search everywhere for clues about the Whale Breath state and the Painting of the Moon and Whale.
Two months passed in the blink of an eye.
As Wei He’s birthday drew near, his Golden Moon Five Elements Skill finally mastered Jin force. His leg technique fully recovered and grew even stronger than before.
His old Flying Dragon technique was strong, but it stayed at the Three Blood level. Now, his leg force complemented his main power, adding a new layer of force outside his Rain-Covering Jin.
This new layer boosted his explosive speed and impact. Defensively, his guard force grew stronger by thirty percent. He could now take blows from high-level Organ Refining masters.
His Cloud Patterns also grew to fourteen.
By this time, the small and medium factions around Xuanjing were almost completely wiped out. Those who survived had long joined the Three Great Families, just like the Wanqing Sect.
Everyone thought the long chaos in Tai Prefecture was finally over. The plague was under control, political power was stable, and the rebel army was pushed back. It seemed time to rebuild.
Inside the General’s Mansion, in a small meeting room.
Zhu Chen stood with his hands behind his back. The red sunset bathed his body, casting his face in deep shadow.
He stared at Wang Zhihe. "General Wang, you do not need to know the reason. Just follow orders."
Wang Zhihe always looked calm and smiling, like a kind old man. But now, his smile slowly vanished.
"Sir Zhu, the situation just stabilized. Xuanjing cannot take more trouble."
"Then send all the captured Martial Masters above the Jin force level over there. The gap above is huge," Zhu Chen said softly. His sharp voice sounded creepy in the quiet room.
"I..." Wang Zhihe opened his mouth.
"You should understand, General Wang. We are running out of time," Zhu Chen interrupted. "The news brought by Lord Xie proves that our time is short."
"Sir Zhu... are you not afraid of falling into Hell when you die?" Wang Zhihe’s lips trembled.
"Fear?" Zhu Chen turned his face to the sunset. "Life and death mean nothing. Look at Great Yuan. It is like this dying sun. All we can do is carry out His Majesty’s will."
"Will..." Wang Zhihe took a deep breath. "I understand."
"When the sun sets, it rises again. But if Great Yuan falls, where is our next dawn?" Zhu Chen said coldly.
In a barren rocky mountain.
Wei He leaped like an arrow, crossing dozens of meters in a flash.
In the stone forest ahead, a bald man surrounded by troops let out a roar. "Imperial dogs! Die!"
Their fists met.
BOOM!
A shockwave exploded between them, pushing air out in all directions.
The man’s Bone-Forging force shattered instantly under Wei He’s Rain-Covering Jin.
The man tried to burn his blood force for a final desperate attack. But Wei He’s fingers flicked fast, tapping key points on his body to smash his gathered energy.
Wei He snapped the man's channels, paralyzing him, and threw him back. "Take him."
The soldiers and Wanqing Sect fighters caught the man and threw him into a drug-soaked sack to stop him from escaping.
"Do we keep hunting?" Wan Qingqing walked up, her face filled with worry. Her relationship with Wei He had grown much closer lately.
"Sir Zhu wants ten Martial Masters every month," Wei He said with a frown.
"Ten a month..." Wan Qingqing fell silent.
"I cannot change orders from above. I can only pick bad people to catch," Wei He said.
They had sent dozens of fighters over the past few months, but the superiors were still not satisfied.
"What if we fail to fill the quota?" she asked softly.
"I don't know. But nothing good," Wei He shook his head.
Zhu Chen was like a mountain pressing down on them. Wei He and Wang Shaojun had asked Old Man Wang to negotiate, but it failed. The Night Hawks were everywhere, filled with high-level experts.
Last month, Wei He even found that the Night Hawks had masters who were good with poison.
"Let's go. The bandits here are cleared out," Wei He raised his hand.
Over a hundred men shifted formation and left the stone forest. Wei He now commanded a squad of three hundred elites, built to hunt down martial artists.
Control inside Xuanjing City grew strict. With so many masters captured or killed, the demand for meat fields and food dropped, causing market prices to fall. To regular people, things looked better.
"I will lead the team to deliver them. You should rest," Wan Qingqing sighed.
The Wanqing Sect relied completely on Wei He now. Even with two Organ Refining masters in their sect, they were nothing before Zhu Chen. That man was terrifyingly strong—he had killed the former leader of the Night Hawk Building in a single move.
"Alright." Wei He needed time to think.
He needed more power. The stronger he was, the more control he had over his own life.
Most small factions were gone now. Though Zhu Chen looked calm during handovers, Wei He felt the man viewed him differently than the Wang family. The Wanqing Sect was still just a small faction in Zhu Chen's eyes.
A cold sense of danger grew in Wei He's heart. He feared Zhu Chen would turn on them one day.
He had broken through to the Golden Moon Five Elements Skill using the Breakthrough Bead. Now he only needed four more Cloud Patterns to reach mastery. Then he could jump to the sixth level of the Rain-Covering Cloud Skill and rebuild his foundation.
He could not afford any mistakes right now.
After Wan Qingqing left with her team, Wei He led the rest back to Thousand Bat Pavilion.
On the road, merchant caravans and travelers shrank back in fear when they saw the army banners and Wei He's figure.
The Wanqing Sect already had a bad name for using poison. Now that they were capturing and killing people everywhere, their scary name was enough to make crying children go silent.
Wei He felt complicated. To outsiders, he was a monster taking over territory. Only he knew he was just fulfilling secret orders.
If this went on, all the shame and hatred would land on his shoulders and his sect. He worried the court would kill his sect later just to calm public anger.
The Wang family had deep roots to protect them, but he did not.
"Sect Leader, how long do we have to keep catching people?" a disciple asked softly, his veins bulging on his hands as he gripped his reins tightly.
"I don't know..." Wei He said. "Move more of our people to Black House Mountain. Be ready."
"Yes, sir."
Wei He sighed. The deeper he stepped into this world, the less control he had. He had to protect Wan Qingqing, Wan Ling, and the sect. He could not lose.
A fancy carriage passed by them on the road.
The curtain was slightly open. Inside sat a beautiful, striking woman. Her eyes met Wei He’s for a second. She held a thin fan over her lips and smiled playfully at him.
"Whose carriage is that?" Wei He asked after they passed.
"It belongs to the Shuyun House in the Middle Ring district," a female disciple answered. "They are gathering famous women to recreate a lost song, the Snowy Rhyme of Ink Forest. That woman must be a musician they found."
"Snowy Rhyme of Ink Forest..." Wei He's heart stirred. "Find out who she is."
"Yes!" A soldier rode off to follow the carriage.
With Wei He's power in the city, checking a musician's identity was simple.
"Sir, Young Lady Zhang came looking for you again a few days ago," a guard whispered.
"I won't see her. The past is done. We are strangers now," Wei He said coldly.
"Understood."
"Does Qingqing know?"
"Not yet."
"Tell her everything clearly later," Wei He ordered.
"Yes, sir."
Wei He looked at the outline of Thousand Bat Pavilion in the distance, whipped his horse, and rode fast toward home.
