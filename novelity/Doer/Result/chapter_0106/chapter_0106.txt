It was true. After all this time, Wei He knew he couldn't keep using martial arts practice as an excuse to dodge marriage forever.
He knew he wouldn't stay stuck at the Third Blood level. But getting stronger didn't mean it would be easier to find the right partner.
Beside him, Zhao Wanzhu spoke up again.
"Junior Brother Wei, if I remember right, your hometown is Yun Province?"
"Yes."
"Yun Province is a good place. It's rich in minerals. My elders often mention that its silver and iron mines are the richest in all of Great Yuan. I always wanted to visit, but now that I'm older, I don't have the chance anymore." Zhao Wanzhu's words sounded a bit fake, but they weren't off-putting.
"Senior Sister Zhao, just tell me what's on your mind," Wei He said directly.
"It's nothing, really. I just heard Yang Guo mention you and got curious. By the way, you joined not long ago, so you probably haven't joined the Purple Bamboo Society yet?" Zhao Wanzhu smiled.
"Purple Bamboo Society?"
"You might not know, Junior Brother Wei. Most disciples in the inner courtyard come from local wealthy families. They don't have to worry about resources for cultivation.
"But a small portion is different. They either came from far away, grew up poor, or lost their family wealth. Those disciples rely on the Purple Bamboo Society to find suitable partners to cooperate with."
"Cooperate?" Wei He roughly guessed what she meant.
"That's right. Nearby, new wealthy merchants rise up all the time. They need power to back them up as they grow, so they fund promising fighters. Under the Purple Bamboo Society's management, both sides sign a contract and choose each other."
Her words made it crystal clear to Wei He what this group was.
He had heard before that many inner disciples had sponsors behind them. They never worried about money or resources and could just focus on training. It turned out to be true.
"With your conditions, you can totally try joining the Purple Bamboo Society. You're practicing multiple arts, so your force might become messy and block your future breakthroughs. But even so, a potential Force Fighter combined with our Heavenly Seal Gate name is enough to draw plenty of investors," Zhao Wanzhu advised.
Wei He was currently desperate for money. To him, wasn't this just like taking a side job with a small gang back in Feiye City?
His interest was sparked right away.
"Then how do I join, Senior Sister?"
"The Purple Bamboo Society is just a meeting spot hosted by my family," Zhao Wanzhu giggled. She pointed a fair finger at herself. "Guess how old I am this year?"
"You look young, Senior Sister. Twenty-five at most," Wei He answered honestly.
"You really know how to talk, Junior Brother Wei," Zhao Wanzhu laughed. "I just turned thirty-two last month. I've been in this Wanqing Yard for six years now."
"Uh... I really couldn't tell." Wei He meant it.
Her skin and looks made her look twenty-seven at most. You couldn't tell she was thirty-two at all.
"Did you know, Junior Brother, that my son and daughter are already old enough to run errands?" Zhao Wanzhu couldn't stop laughing.
She had given birth before turning twenty. Now her twin son and daughter were over ten years old, practically mini-adults.
Wei He showed surprise once again.
While chatting, Zhao Wanzhu also took the initiative to talk with Qian Rui at the same table. They had a great conversation too.
Soon, she got agreement from both Wei He and Qian Rui. She made identity tokens for them to place in the Purple Bamboo Society, waiting for sponsors to pick them. She even promised that if multiple people liked them, the highest bidder would win.
She seemed to run the Purple Bamboo Society professionally. She knew almost every disciple inside out, not just in Wanqing Yard, but in other yards as well. As she talked, she casually dropped news from other branches.
Wei He quietly lowered his opinion of her. Clearly, her knowing his background earlier was just a business routine.
"Actually, besides the Purple Bamboo Society, the Yuan Seal Society is another place to make money," Zhao Wanzhu hinted suddenly, then dropped the topic.
Qian Rui raised his head, his interest hooked.
"May I ask what the Yuan Seal Society is?" Like Wei He, he was an outsider who came to Heavenly Seal Gate for its reputation, and he was just as broke.
"The Yuan Seal Society works differently from ours. It's another way to make money, but there's a rule. You can only join if things don't work out at the Purple Bamboo Society," Zhao Wanzhu giggled mysteriously. Her eyes drifted toward Qian Rui's handsome face. "That way of making money is very different from normal methods."
Wei He had a guess. He realized Zhao Wanzhu might be involved in something dirty.
In Heavenly Seal Gate, the women of Wanqing Yard grew strong and had great bodies because of their martial arts. As long as they didn't look bad, there was always a market for them among rich nobles. Zhao Wanzhu was likely running that kind of business.
Keeping his mouth shut, Wei He went back to eating. Rare beast meat was served nonstop.
This meal was far more luxurious than anything in Feiye City. They could eat as much rare beast meat as they wanted. Zhou Xingsu's wealth was one reason, but Xuanjing City also had plenty of meat farms and no shortage of supply.
A short while later, Yard Leader Wan Ling showed up briefly. She seemed busy, so she said a few comforting words to everyone and left in a hurry.
After everyone ate and drank their fill, the feast ended, and they headed home.
Wei He and Qian Rui said goodbye to Zhao Wanzhu and walked back separately.
On the way back, Zhao Wanzhu's words gave Wei He a lot of hope. He had long heard that disciples without background could get sponsored and never lack resources. Now it was finally his turn.
Before leaving, Zhao Wanzhu had given him a quick breakdown of the deal. Once sponsored, he would get a fixed monthly allowance of resources. In return, he had to step up and solve trouble for the sponsor when needed.
A Third Blood fighter from Heavenly Seal Gate, and a future Force Fighter, was a strong title anywhere, even in Xuanjing City.
Back home, Wei He continued his quiet training while waiting for news, taking on odd jobs in between.
He even took a delivery task like Yang Guo did. A round trip took over a month and earned him a few hundred gold pieces. It was good money, but it wasted too much training time.
Fortunately, after that month, Wei He's Realm Breaking Pearl filled up again. It was finally ready to break through the Cloud Rain Art.
By the shore of Heavenly Seal Lake, on an empty stony beach.
Wei He and Yang Guo stood face to face.
Both had black fingertips and full blood energy, fully focused for the fight.
Though Heavenly Seal Gate was split into nine yards, every yard trained the hands. However, each yard's hand techniques had unique traits and flaws.
Yang Guo practiced the Shrinking Mist Art from the Arc Light Yard.
"Be careful. My Arc Light Yard focuses on heavy power and explosive force. If you fail to block, my slip-up could hurt you," Yang Guo said seriously, taking the fight seriously.
"It's fine. Let's start," Wei He nodded.
Though his first level of Cloud Rain Art wasn't at the Force level like hers, his foundations from other arts had already strengthened his hands to a scary degree. He had even traded blows with a Force Master like the Human-Faced Tiger Xiao Heng before without taking damage.
"Then watch out!" Yang Guo charged, her feet slicing a straight line across the stony beach.
Her burst of speed in that instant was no slower than Wei He using his Flying Dragon Art at the fourth level.
It only lasted a second, starting fast and slowing down as she reached him. But it was still impressive, considering she hadn't practiced any other arts alongside her main study.
Wei He forced himself not to use the Flying Dragon Art. He raised his hands and clashed with her using the Rain Cover Seal.
Clang! Their hands collided. Fingers pulled tight like blades, they attacked each other at lightning speed.
In a single breath, they exchanged seven or eight moves.
Wei He felt a numb, electric power leaking from her hands into his. He knew this was the Arc Light Force. Taking too many hits from it would numb the hands and drain power. It was a vicious force meant to damage tendons and muscles.
His Cloud Rain Force was different. It excelled at finding tiny gaps like flowing water, pouring in to crush the enemy faster than other forces. But that only worked if he could find a opening in the first place.
This was why Wanqing Yard was weaker than the rest. If the opponent left no opening, or if the user failed to catch it, the Cloud Rain Force was useless.
After fifty moves, Wei He was caught off guard and hit in the middle of his chest by Yang Guo. He admitted defeat.
Both stopped their moves and stepped back.
"You're still better," Wei He exhaled, slowly relaxing the blood energy in his hands.
"You're already crazy strong. You blocked me for so long at just Third Blood," Yang Guo praised. "I'm a Force Fighter! My force flows without end, yet you held out this long."
"Maybe I just naturally have better stamina," Wei He said calmly.
"Maybe. Also, didn't you learn other martial arts? Why didn't you use them?" Yang Guo asked.
"My other arts are rusty now. Even if I used them, they aren't as strong as Cloud Rain Force, so there was no point," Wei He explained.
"Fair enough. It just feels like a waste. If you had practiced our gate's martial arts from the start, you might be a Force Fighter by now," Yang Guo sighed.
"It's fine," Wei He sighed along, pretending to be disappointed.
"By the way, Wei He, I might not be able to spar with you often from now on." Yang Guo suddenly fixed her long hair, took off her forehead ornament, and wiped the sweat from it.
"Why?" Wei He paused.
He had very few friends in Heavenly Seal Gate, and Yang Guo was one of them. Hearing this caught him off guard.
"I got engaged." Yang Guo looked up with a big smile. "I need to watch my boundaries now. My fiancé's side isn't happy about me sparring with men all day, so... I need to keep my distance."
"...Makes sense." Wei He understood.
It was true. If he were her fiancé, he wouldn't want his future wife body-contact sparring with other men every day either. That concern was normal.
