"Let's drop this topic. Bone Refining and Viscera Refining—what's the real difference between them? Now that you've reached Viscera Refining, do you feel any change in your body?" Wei He suddenly shifted the conversation.
He was very curious about this realm. Now that his close friend had stepped into it, he wasn't going to let it pass without asking.
"Bone Refining is about penetrating force deep into the bone marrow. The key here is penetrating force," Wang Shaojun said freely, hiding nothing. "Viscera Refining, however, isn't about using brute pressure. You must first master Silk Force. It's a force as thin as silk that flows gently along your blood vessels into your internal organs, connecting the inside with the outside. This stage is mostly similar across different methods. The difference lies in the techniques and how you blend force inside and outside. It demands high control and a huge amount of total force."
Wei He listened and gained a quick grasp of it.
"Is there a way to speed up Bone Refining?"
"Yes, but it's very trouble," Wang Shaojun said. "You need an expert with the exact same attribute of force to press against your protective force from the outside. Long-term, stable pressure. It can't stop, day and night, twice a month. It can speed up the rate of force penetration."
He paused and continued, "There's another way. Legend says training deep underwater has an accelerating effect."
"So, the stronger and more abundant your force is, the faster it penetrates?" Wei He understood.
"That's the logic."
"Got it." Wei He quickly scanned the state of his inner force.
Indeed, under careful sensing, after his Rain Force was upgraded, the speed at which his force seeped into his bones was much faster than before.
It was simple: human muscles and skin could only hold so much force. Once full, adding more created pressure that pushed force into nearby areas. Bones were the first to be invaded.
"Alright, time's up. Let's go," Wang Shaojun suggested.
"Okay."
The two stopped talking and left the chess garden one after the other.
...
Fifty miles from Xuanjing City, Heshan County.
A thin drizzle was falling.
On the edge of the county stood a white villa called Qianhe Villa.
The owner, He Qianhe, was famous throughout the county for his mastery of the Dingyan Palm.
Yet right now, He Qianhe sat in the main chair, white-haired and pale. Around him gathered the villa's top fighters and disciples.
On the ground lay the fallen guards and disciples who had fought earlier.
Everyone stared in fear at the dozen people standing in the martial grounds by the main gate.
Those people all wore black diamond masks.
The leader wore a mask marked with a silver goose. Below him were copper goose masks, and the lowest were white goose masks.
"He Qianhe, will you still not hand over the item?" the Silver Goose mask said in a low voice. "Or do you really think you can keep it? You should know that my Returning Goose Tower is only the beginning. The news is out. More masters and factions will come."
"I didn't expect the Hall Master of the Flying Feather Hall to come in person," He Qianhe gritted his teeth. "But this is Taizhou, this is Xuanjing City! I don't believe you can act without law!"
"Xuanjing City's Tianyin Sect is breaking apart right now, who has time for you?" Silver Goose laughed softly. "Forget it. Since you are stubborn, I will give you a quick death. Do it."
He waved his hand gently.
More than ten black masks charged forward at once.
"Kill!"
The Qianhe Villa disciples rallied their strength, using Dingyan Palm to fight back.
However, the gap was too wide. In skill and real combat experience, the disciples were no match for the killers of Returning Goose Tower.
One by one, people fell bleeding to the ground.
He Qianhe could not help them, as he was already locked in a fierce fight with Silver Goose.
As one of the few Bone Refining martial artists near Xuanjing City, He Qianhe had been famous for years, but he was old and past his peak. Holding off the enemy took all his power.
His three sons and two daughters were fighting back frantically.
He Yuxin was the youngest of his five children, but the most talented. His white robe was stained with blood. Watching his uncles and sisters drop dead in pools of blood, his normal playboy demeanor vanished. He fought like crazy, gathering his Three-Blood force into his fists to smash at the black masks.
His fourth sister, He Yiru, was behind him, desperately shielding the children of his older sister and second brother.
Fewer and fewer allies remained. People fell dead continuously, while hardly any black masks were lost.
The clean Qianhe Villa was slowly painted red.
Suddenly, a sharp scream rang out.
"Father!!"
The eldest daughter, He Wanqing, watched in horror as her father was hit squarely in the chest.
Bang!!
Blood sprayed everywhere.
He Qianhe's protective force shattered, and he flew back, crashing hard into the middle of the grounds.
"Grandpa!" "Grandfather!"
Two small, pretty girls ran over crying. Luckily, no black mask attacked them on the way. The two seven-year-old girls held onto He Qianhe, sobbing bitterly.
"Pitiable. A warrior thinking he can wash his hands in a golden bowl and retire to the countryside—how ridiculous," Silver Goose stepped forward, his tone full of disdain. "Martial conflicts never end. He Qianhe, your biggest mistake was greed."
Silver Goose flashed forward and struck the head of He Qianhe's eldest daughter on the right.
Her skull cracked, and He Wanqing fell dead on the spot.
Step by step, he killed the remaining fighters with ease.
Qianhe Villa collapsed completely. Disciples scattered, running in terror, while others fell to their knees begging for mercy with tears flowing.
The remaining black masks showed no mercy, cutting everyone down and finishing them with poisoned weapons.
Only two He family members, He Yuxin and He Yiru, along with the two little girls, were left alive.
Because He Yuxin and his sister had decent looks and body structures, a black mask tied them up and stuffed rags in their mouths. They planned to take them back, ruin their martial arts, and sell them.
"Two small ones left."
Silver Goose chuckled, flashed forward, and appeared before He Qianhe, looking down at the two little girls.
"I don't like your eyes," he looked at the hatred in their eyes and raised his palm violently.
He Qianhe tried to raise his hand with his last bit of strength to block it.
He Yuxin and He Yiru widened their eyes, the ropes cutting into their flesh as they struggled to save them.
"Sigh..." A low sigh drifted over.
A voice echoed from outside the villa.
Silver Goose quickly pulled back his hand and retreated ten steps. With every step he took, a steel needle quietly appeared in the floor tile beneath his feet.
"Who's there?!" He looked up towards the gate.
The gate stood wide open. Two figures were standing on the ground right behind He Qianhe.
The person on the left wore a silver mask, tall and well-dressed like a young master out for a walk.
The person on the right was heavily built, wrapped in a large black cloak. That face...
"Poison Demon Wei He?!" Silver Goose froze the moment he saw the second man.
"Hmm?" Wei He was surprised. He thought he had disguised himself enough that no one would recognize him.
His relaxed expression instantly turned dark. His eyes grew dangerous as he looked at Silver Goose.
"You shouldn't have called out my name."
"I told you that disguise was useless," Wang Shaojun smiled beside him.
"He probably has disguise recognition skills," Wei He said.
"I bet he doesn't."
"Also, I hate that nickname." Wei He took a step forward, his body darting to the left.
Flying Dragon Skill level five burst out at full speed, boosted by the swift effect of Rain Force.
He covered the distance in a flash.
In the blink of an eye, he appeared right before Silver Goose.
"Didn't you say you'd coordinate with me?!" Wang Shaojun yelled from behind.
Bang!!
With a loud crash, Wei He tilted his head to dodge the enemy's palm and slammed his right palm into Silver Goose's chest.
Whale Force erupted. A brute power equal to Bone Refining quickly offset the opponent's force, followed by pure physical power blasting into his chest.
Pfft!!
Silver Goose's chest exploded like a balloon, spraying blood and shredded meat everywhere.
He was still in a palm-striking posture, but his midsection was stripped of flesh, leaving only a bare frame of bones.
"You...!!" Silver Goose didn't even understand how he died. He had clearly blocked the force...
His eyes behind the mask stared wide open in shock, fear, and confusion before his life faded completely.
Wei He pulled his hand back, took the pouch from the man's waist, and checked it.
"Not here." He tossed it to Wang Shaojun behind him.
"Fine, you win," Wang Shaojun said helplessly. "Also, stop stealing my kills. You made it way too bloody. It ruins the elegance."
"I only care about results," Wei He said coldly.
The remaining black masks saw that things were bad and tried to slip away silently.
Yet not a single person escaped the grounds.
The moment they jumped to the top of the wall, they collapsed and stopped breathing.
Wang Shaojun was shocked inside. He didn't know why Wei He used poison so casually. Wasn't he afraid of poisoning himself? Even with antidotes, taking too many was bad for the body.
He didn't know that Wei He had trained in White Jade Skill just to handle poison, reaching the second layer. It gave him high resistance to two types of poison.
As long as he used poisons aligned with his high resistance, he suffered almost no harm.
Soon, all the black masks on the ground were dead.
He Qianhe had breathed his last breath. Only the young master He Yuxin, the fourth daughter He Yiru, and the two young girls remained.
Wang Shaojun untied the ropes. The brother and sister rushed to He Qianhe's body, crying loudly.
The family held each other and wept bitterly.
Wei He didn't want to deal with the emotional scene, so he began searching the bodies for loot as usual.
The Silver Goose mask was a Bone Refining master from Returning Goose Tower. Wei He had tested his power just now: even a Bone Refining master would die in one hit if caught off guard by him.
Every move he made now carried the force level of Bone Refining, packed with raw power. He was like a Bone Refining master who was always using bone force.
Add to that the massive power of Whale Force, and Wei He had a clear sense of his current strength.
Stronger than normal Bone Refining, never fought Viscera Refining, but still couldn't win against Zhou Xingtong in a head-on fight.
That monster's hide was too thick to pierce even with Bone Refining force. If you couldn't break his defense, no attack mattered.
After finishing with the loot from the killers, Wei He returned carrying a large bundle wrapped in cloth.
Wang Shaojun had handled the He family's situation.
He Yuxin and He Yiru lay dead on the ground, having taken their own lives. Only the two little girls, He Wei and Xiang Ling, were left crying violently.
"I've accepted both of them as my maidservants," Wang Shaojun sighed. "Qianhe Villa used to be a famous estate. What a shame... I didn't expect it to end like this."
"Let's go." Wei He didn't bother commenting on his action.
"Wait. I agreed to take them in, and in return, they gave me this." Wang Shaojun opened his hand to reveal a delicate small black metal cage.
Inside the cage rested a dark purple, semi-transparent flying insect with double wings.
The insect looked a bit like a dragonfly, with a fat belly and a large compound eye shining with colorful light.
"Is this... a Spirit Insect?!" Wei He was stunned.
"So you do know it," Wang Shaojun sighed. "This is a Mica Bug. Its eggs are as valuable as high-grade spirit beast meat, making it an incredible tonic. One Mica Bug can produce a huge amount of eggs continuously, acting like a portable meat farm."
"A magnet for trouble," Wei He shook his head.
"Take it," Wang Shaojun threw the cage over.
Wei He caught it and stayed silent for a moment.
"Then I won't stand on ceremony. I really need this." He had plenty of meat farms, but because of the wanted bounty from the Chijing Army, he was severely short on food to feed them, so meat production had plummeted.
A portable meat farm like the Mica Bug, which required little food but gave high returns, was perfect for him.
"Don't mention it," Wang Shaojun smiled. "Do I look like someone who lacks these things?"
"By the way, this Mica Bug seems to hold some secrets. Watch it carefully later. A single Mica Bug wouldn't bring ruin to the whole Qianhe Villa," he warned.
"Yeah, I know," Wei He nodded.
With this job done, he needed to focus on training to break through his Rain Force.
Zhou Xingtong and the Chijing Army were still hunting him and the Wanqing Sect. To live peacefully under the sun again, the best way was to get strong enough—so strong that the enemy had no choice but to accept him, or be wiped out entirely.
