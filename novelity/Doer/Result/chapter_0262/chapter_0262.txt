A silver crossbow bolt shot straight at Wei He from behind.
Clang!
Wei He caught the bolt with one hand and snapped his wrist back. He pumped his heavy force into the weapon, sending it flying right back where it came from.
A scream echoed from the distance as the shooter’s head exploded.
That was just the start. All around him, enemy archers fired off their bolts, but every single shot hit thin air.
Wei He moved like a ghost. In the blink of an eye, he cleared tens of meters and landed right in front of the archers.
"Block him!"
A few men in red robes leaped into the air, bursting with energy. They were all master martial artists.
Bang! Bang! Bang!
Three heavy hits landed on Wei He at the same time, but they could not even scratch his protective barrier.
Wei He gave a casual shake of his body. The three masters paled as they were sent flying backward. Thin red lines spread across their palms like a web of bloody veins.
"The Xiang取 Sect, the Hong family elites... who else?" Wei He scanned the crowd, his voice totally flat.
"Us! Quick, hit him together! His energy is running dry, don't let him catch his breath!"
Two women in white robes rushed Wei He with long swords, fast as lightning. Their blades trembled with lethal energy. They were high-level masters from the White Jade Sect, capable of unleash deadly special traits.
"Boring."
Wei He did not even bother looking at them. He casually slapped a nearby fighter.
The man screamed as he spun through the air, crashing right into the two swordswomen. All three slammed into the ground together, their internal energy canceled out instantly.
Within seconds, the heavy poison coating Wei He’s force invaded their bodies. They could not move a muscle.
That single strike terrified the crowd. Two top-tier masters could not even handle a guy Wei He threw at them.
On top of that, the invisible poison powder Wei He had scattered earlier began to kick in. Groups of fighters dropped to the dirt, rolling around in agony from the unbearable itching and pain.
The rest of the Xiang取 Sect and guard hall fighters froze. They formed a tight circle, watching Wei He with absolute terror.
Snow flurried through the air. Over a hundred men surrounded one single man, too scared to step forward, yet too terrified to run away. A few started dragging the poisoned fighters away from the scene.
The remaining guards looked pale and defeated. If they attacked, they would end up poisoned and broken. If they ran, the Hong family would butcher them later.
"Where is Hong Daoyuan?" Wei He asked with a small smile. "If you've given up, come talk to me."
He leaped up and landed lightly on a nearby roof. The crowd jumped in fear, terrified he was about to strike again.
In just minutes, the masked leaders of the Xiang取 Sect were all flat on their backs, poisoned. More than half of the Hong family's top fighters were down too, leaving only a dozen still standing. The white-robed sect members were taken out in one move.
Three major factions had teamed up, yet they lost in less than ten minutes.
Far away in a high tower, Hong Daoyuan and his father, Hong Yi, watched the fight from a hidden window. Both men were grim.
"Why did this Poison Sect Master come all the way from Taizhou?" Hong Yi asked, panic in his voice. "What does he want? Why hit our Hong family the second he arrives? We never crossed him!"
"I don't know," Hong Daoyuan shook his head. "But even my master lost to him. His poison skills are beyond anything we can fight. We can't defeat him."
"So what do you mean...?" Hong Yi frowned.
"We work with him," Hong Daoyuan said coldly. "If we can't beat him, we make him one of us."
"But we just tried to kill him..."
Hong Yi looked at his son's calm, firm face and instantly understood.
Just like when the Hong family joined the Chaos God Sect, the sect did not take Hong Daoyuan in just for his talent. They wanted control of the entire Feiye City. They wanted the city's power and money.
The Poison Sect was no different. That meant they would not ruin Hong Daoyuan, the city's ruler.
"What about the poison in your body...?" Hong Yi asked nervously.
"It doesn't matter," Hong Daoyuan said calmly. "The only way he will trust me is if I carry his poison. Getting poisoned is necessary."
"You suffer too much..." Hong Yi sighed.
"It's just poison, not death. Don't worry, Father. The Chaos God Sect is broken anyway. My masters hide their real moves and boss us around. The Poison Sect might actually be fairer."
Hong Daoyuan looked at the dying elder on the ground, whose face had turned yellow.
"Besides, this works out. If stronger experts from the Chaos God Sect show up later and beat the Poison Sect, I can just use this poison as an excuse. I'll say Wei He forced me."
This was the first time the Hong family had seen a true top-tier powerhouse.
Soldiers couldn't stop him. Master fighters couldn't break him. One man was holding hundreds of elite warriors hostage just by standing there.
Watching the poisoned men roll on the ground until they stopped breathing, Hong Daoyuan knew he couldn't hide any longer. The losses were getting too high.
Wei He's scariest weapon wasn't his speed or his martial arts. It was his poison. One wave of poison had downed a hundred elite men. Training that many men took too much time and money. The Hong family couldn't afford this loss.
Hong Daoyuan opened the window, jumped down, and walked toward Wei He.
Wei He turned his eyes toward him.
"Are you happy with this outcome?" Wei He asked.
"Master Wei, forgive me. This wasn't what I wanted. Everything was..." Hong Daoyuan began to apologize, his voice sincere.
Suddenly, the world blurred.
Wei He's massive frame appeared right in front of him.
Bang!!!
A single palm strike hit his chest.
Hong Daoyuan felt his defensive energy snap like paper. He tried to step back, but it was too late.
A hand grabbed his right arm.
Crack.
The bones in his arm shattered like dry twigs, crushed into powder in seconds.
Hong Daoyuan kept a blank face. Since his plan had failed, he knew he had to pay a price. The man wouldn't kill him, and this injury would heal soon...
Pfft!
Hong Daoyuan's eyes went wide. He looked down slowly at his own caved-in chest.
"You...!!?"
He could not believe Wei He had just landed a fatal blow out of nowhere.
"Honestly," Wei He smiled, pulling his hand back. "I was just going to break your arm."
"But the look in your eyes just now was too calm. It made me feel like you were still plotting against me. So I thought... I'll just kill you and save the trouble."
Hong Daoyuan's mind shattered. He had thought he controlled everything. Wei He wasn't supposed to kill him.
Yet, simply because Wei He didn't like his look, he killed him on the spot. He didn't care about the Hong family's revenge, nor the chaos it would cause.
Hong Daoyuan's sight faded. His body fell backward.
He had miscalculated. He thought he was too useful to die.
"Daoyuan!!!" Hong Yi screamed from the tower.
He tried to throw himself out the window, but two Hong family guards grabbed him tight, keeping him back.
The guards froze. The Hong family went mad with rage. The Xiang取 Sect members stood paralyzed.
Hong Daoyuan was the bridge holding all three factions together. Now he was dead.
"Retreat!"
The remaining Xiang取 Sect members ran first. They were only here to assist. With Hong Daoyuan dead, the mission was over. The guard hall members fled next. Soon, only the Hong family and the city troops remained.
Wei He held Hong Daoyuan's corpse by the neck, watching the chaos calmly.
Suddenly, he tossed the body into the air.
His right hand struck out like lightning, tapping several points on Hong Daoyuan's body. He forcibly pulled the shattered chest bones back into place. Then, he injected steady pulses of energy into the frozen heart, forcing it to jump back to life.
Gasp!!
Hong Daoyuan took a violent breath, his mouth wide open.
He breathed in and out rapidly, finally opening his eyes. Under the shocked stares of everyone around, he clutched his chest and stood up slowly.
This time, the calm mask on his face was gone. The cold touch of real death had terrified him to his core.
Was that... death?
He realized then that Wei He really would kill him on a whim. If Wei He had made one mistake during that quick fix, he would be dead for good.
Wei He had literally used his life as a test!
"It actually worked?" Wei He was a bit surprised himself. He had only discussed this theory once before—that if the main organs weren't destroyed, a heart stopped by shock could be restarted with force. He hadn't expected it to work on the first try.
"Find the people I asked for. Handle the rest yourself."
Having proven his theory, Wei He was in a good mood. The mess here was settled.
"Yes! I won't disappoint you!" Hong Daoyuan bowed, completely broken and terrified.
Seeing that the man was now fully tamed, Wei He said nothing more. He leaped into the air and vanished into the heavy snowstorm.
With the Hong family helping, finding his second sister and Zhenqi would be easy. Tracking down his parents and oldest sister might take more time. Now, it was time to meet up with Wan Qingqing and check on his old home in Mouse Alley.
