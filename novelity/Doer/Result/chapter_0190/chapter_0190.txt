He walked up to his friend's courtyard at the edge of town. Before he could knock, a violent coughing fit echoed from inside.
Jiu Ying frowned. He grabbed the iron ring on the door and knocked.
The door creaked open. An old woman with snow-white hair, Sun Li, stood there wearing an apron. Her face was full of worry, her sleeves rolled up as if she had been busy working.
When she saw Jiu Ying, a look of immense relief washed over her face.
"Uncle Jiu, please look at my old man! It was just a mild cough before, but now..."
The family trusted Jiu Ying completely. In the past, whenever anyone felt unwell, Jiu Ying would write a simple prescription for free, and they would recover quickly after taking the medicine.
Now, her husband's cough was getting worse. The local doctors were useless, so she put all her hopes on Jiu Ying.
"Didn't I give you a prescription last time? Did he take it properly?" Jiu Ying walked in, his brows furrowed.
"Three times a day, exact dosage! Please, go check on him."
"Okay." Jiu Ying picked up his pace and entered the inner room.
On the bed lay an old man with gray hair. It was the same old man he usually played chess with.
Wearing a gray hemp shirt, the old man leaned against the bedhead, slowly sipping a bowl of hot water.
Seeing Jiu Ying, he forced a smile. "You're here? Too bad I'm sick, or we could play a round."
"Old Sun, what happened after you took the medicine?" Jiu Ying asked coldly.
"It felt better the first day. But by the second and third day... cough, cough! Cough!" Before he could finish, another violent coughing fit hit him.
His face turned bright red, his throat rattling with heavy phlegm that he couldn't spit out.
Jiu Ying took his pulse, checked his tongue and eyes, then examined his chamber pot.
"Old Jiu... tell me the truth," Old Sun said from the bed, looking resigned. "Am I going to make it?"
"You're fine. I can cure this," Jiu Ying said in a deep voice.
Suddenly, he heard Sun Li coughing outside as she worked. Hidden beneath her cough was that same subtle sound of heavy phlegm.
"I'll write a prescription to clear the phlegm. Don't worry, it's just a minor illness. You'll be back on your feet soon."
"Good. I'll leave it to you. To be honest, I went to see Doctor Chen, the best doctor in the county, a few days ago. Even he was coughing like crazy! A doctor who can't even cure himself... what a useless old fool!" Old Sun complained.
"Alright, get some rest. I'm waiting for you to recover so we can play chess again," Jiu Ying comforted him with a light laugh.
"Don't worry. My body is tough. Once I get better, we'll head up the mountain. I'll prove to you I wasn't bragging! When it comes to gathering herbs, I've been ranked fifth in Qingqi County for years!" Old Sun laughed.
"Deal. I'll hold you to it." Jiu Ying wrote another prescription and turned to leave.
As he reached the courtyard gate, Sun Li stopped him.
"Uncle Jiu..." Her eyes were filled with fear and dread. "Is he..."
"Don't worry," Jiu Ying said gravely. "I will cure him."
"...Okay." She nodded. "Thank you. Here are some..."

Heavenly Seal Lake.
A small wooden boat drifted slowly across the water.
Wei He crouched at the edge of the boat, watching small fish swim in the dark depths of the lake.
The battle at Guiyan Pagoda was over. After wiping out several enemy strongholds, the Heavenly Seal Sect had taken massive spoils. Though they suffered some losses, it served its purpose as a trial by fire.
The Wang Family and Sword Temple took over the remaining cleanup. Wei He only had to warn his disciples to stay cautious.
Life quickly returned to peace.
In his free time, when he wasn't training, Wei He would return to the Thousand Bat Waterside Pavilion at Heavenly Seal Lake to relax.
He had sent people to maintain the pavilion. Fearful of his ruthless reputation, no one dared to trespass.
Those strong enough to fight Wei He couldn't survive his poisons. Those who could survive his poisons had just been killed by him.
By now, the name of Wei He and his Myriad Poison Sect echoed across Xuanjing.
Wei He stood up and looked toward the distant shore.
Laughter drifted through the mist. A small group of people was walking along the lake.
They were young men and women in fine martial attire, accompanied by armed guards. They clearly came from rich, powerful families.
"Look, Xiao Yin! There's a boat out there!" a young nobleman suddenly shouted.
A pretty girl beside him followed his line of sight toward the water.
"It's just a small boat. Someone is standing on it. It's so cold today, yet he's standing alone on the water. How poetic."
"Or maybe he got dumped?" The young nobleman laughed.
"Watch your words, Brother Xu!" A calm young man nearby spoke up sternly.
"This used to be Heavenly Seal Sect territory. Even if it's empty now, an old master might be visiting out of nostalgia. Mocking him like this is dangerous."
"What's there to fear? I'm just joking. Besides, on a freezing day like this, sitting alone in the middle of a lake... if he isn't heartbroken, he must be out of his mind," Brother Xu said dismissively.
"Brother Xu..." The calm man tried to warn him again, but Xu shoved his advice aside impatiently and walked away.
"Chen Rui, you're such a coward," the pretty girl sneered before following Xu. "You get scared of everything. If you're that terrified, you should just hide at home."
"You guys..." Chen Rui frowned. He turned back to look at the lake.
His heart skipped a beat. The boat in the middle of the lake was empty.
He whipped his head around and froze.
A tall figure was standing right on the shore, just a few steps ahead of the group.
The man was built like a tower, wearing a black cloak. His long black hair fell over his shoulders, and his skin was deathly pale.
He stared at them with cold, emotionless eyes.
"Watch out ahead!" Chen Rui screamed.
Xu turned around in confusion.
He found himself staring straight at Wei He, whose finger was raised, pointing directly at his forehead.
"Protect the master!"
The guards snapped out of their shock. They drew their blades and lunged forward to save him.
Xu and the girl were paralyzed with fear. They were less than three meters away from Wei He. His finger was about to flick forward.

BANG!
Xu let out a blood-curdling scream and flew backward, crashing onto the ground. His face turned pale, blood trickled from his mouth, and he blacked out immediately.
Seeing this terrifying display, the guards froze. They knew they had hit a brick wall.
The distance between the boat and the shore was huge, yet this man had heard every word they said.
If they rushed him, it would be suicide.
Trembling in fear, the guards formed a wall in front of the unconscious Xu, checking his wounds without daring to make a move.
"Black cloak, long black hair, seven feet tall..." Chen Rui's eyes went wide as realization hit him. "You... You are Master Wei He of the Myriad Poison Sect!"
Wei He's cold gaze swept over them.
In a single glance, he had already read their physical states.
The guards were at the Second Blood Refinement. The youths were only at the First or Second Blood Refinement—just kids in their teens who had barely started martial arts.
Up close, this Chen Rui was dressed far more plainly than the rest, yet his cultivation was at the Second Blood Refinement, making him the strongest of the young group.
"We are disciples from the nearby Bright Moon Boxing Academy! Please show mercy, Senior!" Chen Rui bowed deeply, sweating profusely under Wei He's gaze.
"Academy disciples..."
Looking at the youth's raw, inexperienced face, Wei He was reminded of his own days back at the Grounding Fist Academy.
He stayed silent for a long moment. Losing interest, his body blurred. In the blink of an eye, he crossed the water and landed back on his wooden boat.
The boat drifted slowly into the fog, vanishing from sight.
Without realizing it, he had become the terrifying senior in other people's eyes.
