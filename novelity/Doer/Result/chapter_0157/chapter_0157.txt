Lin Xiaoxiao knew why they couldn't control the situation.
Before coming here to beg Wei He, she had already thought about this. As a businesswoman, how could she not understand the unique situation of the Wen family?
Yet, she still came.
It wasn't just because she had no choice. It was also because of her bond with Wen Lian.
"Wen Lian owns two meat fields of mutated beasts. I can decide to transfer one of them, the North Map Cave Wolf field, to you," Lin Xiaoxiao said seriously.
"North Map Cave Wolf..." Wei He froze. That was a mid-tier mutated beast.
The ratings for mutated beasts came from a system created a thousand years ago by Emperor Yuan, who used martial arts to build the nation. Though a bit outdated now, the standard was still very accurate.
A mid-tier meat field was not simply equal to several low-tier ones. You could not trade them like that.
Wei He had already received a top-tier meat field from Zhou Shun. But with good things like this, the more he had, the better.
He was training in several martial arts at once and needed to store energy in his Breakthrough Bead. His needs far exceeded normal fighters.
"One is not enough," Wei He thought for a moment, then shook his head.
"Then I will transfer both to you!" Lin Xiaoxiao said firmly.
"Deal," Wei He agreed right away.
This meant that, adding Zhou Shun's four meat fields from the map, he would have six in total.
Besides, he still kept one mutated seed he got from the bat cave with Jiuying.
With this much profit, it was worth stepping in once.
"Thank you!" Lin Xiaoxiao bowed deeply. She knew how dangerous this task was.
Han Yuzhong was not a martial artist. He was an official. Behind him stood not just a single sect, but the entire Xuanjing City.
She knew clearly how much it would affect Wei He if he was exposed.
That was why she was willing to pay such a big price for Wen Lian. Two meat fields yielded more meat than even some small martial schools could get.
For Wei He to get it all by himself was extremely generous.
"You said Wen Lian has already been taken away?" Wei He asked after a brief pause.
"Yes. Before I came, I got news that she was taken to Han Yuzhong's house," Lin Xiaoxiao answered quickly.
"No time to waste." Wei He stood up. "I will go there right now. Stay here and do not go anywhere, so you stay safe."
"I brought some..." Before Lin Xiaoxiao could finish her sentence, her vision blurred, and Wei He was already gone.
A short time later.
Yuxing District, Xuanjing City.
Wang Shaojun walked gracefully into the private room wearing a white robe with silver thread. He looked out at the completely dark sky.
"Why look for me at this hour?"
"I need your help with a small matter. Once it is done, you get a Water Bear meat field," Wei He said calmly.
"Speak like a normal person," Wang Shaojun sneered.
If they really calculated the trades between them, was a single Water Bear meat field even worth mentioning?
The banned poison materials they dealt with before were mostly very expensive. Among them, the mixed poisons useful for Martial Masters were worth far more than a single Water Bear meat field.
He did not care about this at all.
"It is very simple, just a small thing." Wei He briefly mentioned Wen Lian's situation.
Wang Shaojun nodded. He called a servant from outside the door, gave a few instructions, and sent him to deliver a message. The matter was settled.
"It is indeed a small thing. A word from me is enough. But if the timing is too late, do not blame me."
"It depends on her luck. Leaving that aside, I have a few meat fields. I want to trade them for higher-tier ones. Do you have any contacts?" That was Wei He's main goal in coming here.
If he got Wen Lian's meat fields and then found Zhou Shun's fields on the map, he would have six fields. That didn't even count the one-third meat yield he could get from Jiuying's side.
Right now, he was rolling in money. He was not short of cash!
So, the main point was how to turn these resources into things more useful to him.
"That is easy," Wang Shaojun nodded. "But I do not suggest you trade them. On the market, you can trade low-tier for mid-tier, but top-tier ones are very rare. Because there are so few of the last two, they are priceless."
"It's fine. As long as I know you have the contact," Wei He agreed.
The two ordered some food and drinks and had a small meal.
Their relationship was no longer hidden. As one of the Three Great Martial Masters of the Wanqing Sect, the news of Wei He breaking through the fourth layer of Rain-Covering Force was slowly spreading.
The rumors about him losing his inner force were covered up by this new breakthrough.
The public impression of him was now buried under his title as one of the Three Great Martial Masters of the Wanqing Sect.
By now, Wei He was building a real foundation in Xuanjing City.
Jiuying, Wang Shaojun, Lin Xiaoxiao, Hu Zicheng, and his sect, the Wanqing Sect.
His connections with these people formed a network.
Though the network was not large, they could trade resources and help each other out.
Wei He could use resources from all sides to fill his own gaps.
For example, when Wang Shaojun needed special poisons or cures, if Wei He could not do it himself, he would talk with Jiuying and quickly find a fix.
With Jiuying, a poison expert, backing him, Wei He's poison skills improved fast.
In terms of poison use alone, Wei He could rival Jiuying by using modern, creative thinking and wild ideas.
But in terms of deep professional knowledge, he was still far behind Jiuying.
If he needed official help, he could ask Wang Shaojun. Wang Shaojun was a young master of the Wang family, holding a high status, known as one of the Three Young Masters of Xuanjing City.
The Wang family was one of the top families in the government of Xuanjing City. They had vast connections, plenty of money, and plenty of people.
On the other side, Lin Xiaoxiao and Hu Zicheng gave Wei He a stable basic enterprise.
Lin Xiaoxiao provided money and normal resources to handle daily small issues.
Hu Zicheng ran the martial school to train disciples and guards for future use.
Once these guards grew up over time, they could solve the problem of Wei He not being able to handle everything at once.
They were useful for taking care of his family and friends, gathering news, and managing his businesses.
He couldn't do every little thing himself or waste his relationship with Wang Shaojun, could he?
At night.
Coming out of Yuxing District, Wei He split up with Wang Shaojun and did not go back right away.
Instead, he walked slowly along the sidewalk toward the city exit.
Wen Lian's matter seemed very serious to Lin Xiaoxiao, but to Wang Shaojun, or even to Wei He, it was just a small issue.
As long as the profit was enough, it could be solved easily.
Even for Wei He himself, he could solve it secretly with a bit of drug.
He didn't even need lethal poison. Just a little drug to make the guy impotent would take care of it in a roundabout way.
It was just safer to let Wang Shaojun use official power.
Suddenly, a clatter of horse hooves came from the street ahead, pulling Wei He out of his thoughts.
He looked up.
On the street ahead, people on both sides backed away to clear a path for a carriage line.
The group consisted of three carriages. The front and back were pulled by two horses each, while only the middle one was pulled by four horses.
These carriages were light yellow, carved with fine cloud patterns and peacock shapes on the outside.
A character for "Xie" was embroidered on the carriage windows and the drivers' clothes.
Wei He narrowed his eyes, staring at the middle carriage.
Someone was sitting inside. Even across ten meters, Wei He could feel a strong, fire-like inner force from him.
It felt as if it was jumping and burning.
The person sat inside the carriage, his inner force like a distant storm, sending out soft whistling sounds just by moving.
It was a strange feeling. Even though Wei He's ears heard nothing, he could feel how powerful the inner force inside was.
He noticed a faint smell of blood coming from the sides of these carriages.
Combined with the strong inner force released without care by the person inside, this was clearly a carriage line that had just been attacked.
"That is Old Mister Xie Minzhi from the capital," Wang Shaojun said softly, appearing next to Wei He out of nowhere.
"Even though his strength has faded a lot, regular Martial Masters still cannot mess with him. None of these big figures who travel between states are simple," Wang Shaojun whispered.
"Why are you back again?" Wei He asked in confusion.
"The matter you asked for is done. I do not need your meat field. I am not short of those. I need you to do me a favor."
"What favor?"
"The Returning Goose Tower assassins we dealt with last time brought along a few troublesome people. It is not easy for me to let my family know, so I can only turn to you," Wang Shaojun lowered his voice.
"You trust my strength that much?" Wei He was surprised.
"What do you think?" Wang Shaojun sneered. He didn't care about others, but why act like this in front of him?
"I can do it, but if they are too strong, I cannot win," Wei He thought about it and agreed.
"Do not worry. You just stand by, use poison, and back me up. I will handle the rest myself." Wang Shaojun did not expect Wei He to be the main fighter anyway.
"Alright then." Wei He had no reason to refuse.
Anyone who made Wang Shaojun act like he faced a great enemy must be a Bone Forging level Martial Master.
He also wanted to test his skills and see how strong the Bone Forging level really was. Although he had sparred with Wan Qingqing many times, that was just training. Wan Qingqing was just a sect disciple, not on the same level as assassins with real combat experience.
"Thanks. I will send someone to tell you the time later. Once it is done, what do you want?" Wang Shaojun generously let Wei He set his price.
"Save it for now. I will ask when I need something." Wei He did not need anything urgently right now.
"Do you want me to introduce a beauty to you?" Wang Shaojun suddenly gave a playful smile.
"Do you think now is a good time to start a family?" Wei He asked back.
"So you will just wait forever and never start a family?"
"..."
"I think your Senior Sister Wan is very good. By the way, at yesterday's feast, a younger guy mentioned that his younger brother's wife knows you. Her name is Yang Guo. Do you know her?"
"I do. We met in the past." Wei He paused, feeling a bit touched.
"Is that so? I thought he just found out we were friends and tried to act close on purpose," Wang Shaojun realized.
"..." Wei He stayed silent.
"Now neither you nor I am young anymore. We should plan for the future. I plan to build a Hundred Spring Building in the south of the city to run a gambling house, chess club, and trade rare treasures. Are you interested in taking a share?" Wang Shaojun suggested.
"Didn't you say you wanted to pass Shangguan Ji?"
"That is for the future. By the time I reach Shangguan Ji's age, I will surely be stronger than him. But right now, we still have to take one step at a time." Wang Shaojun seemed to have grown up quite a bit.
"Even if you and I have the talent to reach that level, how much time will it take in between? My rough guess is that reaching Shangguan Ji's level before I turn fifty would be very good. Could it be that you will never marry or have children until you are fifty?" His words left Wei He speechless.
"So we are gathering wealth to prepare for our future family businesses?" Wei He asked.
"As long as you understand." Wang Shaojun glanced at Wei He. "To put it bluntly, even though I am far above normal people, even with smooth sailing and good luck, reaching Shangguan Ji's level is my limit. Not to mention you, your talent is worse than mine.
Yet even someone like Shangguan Ji is limited everywhere and lacks freedom. In this world, relying on martial arts alone won't get you far."
"You really see things clearly," Wei He sighed in his heart.
"Of course I see it clearly. Shangguan Ji is impressive in Xuanjing City, but looking at the whole Tai State, there are four other big sects on his level, as well as army generals. Not to mention the Wushi Sect standing high above everyone else," Wang Shaojun said coldly.
Wei He fell silent.
This was a difference in vision. While he still viewed Master Shangguan Ji of the Tianyin Gate as the peak of martial arts, what Wang Shaojun saw went far beyond a single Shangguan Ji.
"Let us go back."
Ever since the Zhou Shun incident, Wang Shaojun had slowly started to view Wei He as a true partner.
He really did not want Wei He as a rival or an enemy.
So the best way was to make him a friend.
Since their cooperation was getting closer and Wei He was the type to return kindness with kindness, their relationship naturally grew closer.
After parting ways, Wei He returned to the West Mountain. Then he sent Lin Xiaoxiao back down the mountain.
A few days later, Lin Xiaoxiao sent news that Wen Lian had been saved.
However, even though she was saved in time and remained unharmed, she broke off ties with her family.
That was because her father, Wen Shang, actually wanted her to go back on her own to find Han Yuzhong.
Under pressure from the Wang family, Han Yuzhong let her go, but he had backing as well. He released her only because he did not want to offend the Wang family.
Besides, the attitude shown by the Wang family did not look like they cared much about the Wen family.
It was impossible for Wen Shang to use their name to scare others off.
After a big argument at home, Wen Lian finally ran away and joined her best friend, Lin Xiaoxiao.
