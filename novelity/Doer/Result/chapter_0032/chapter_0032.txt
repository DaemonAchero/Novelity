Another half a month went by. Wei He calculated the time in his head. He had been stuck at the bottleneck for two months now. It was time to quietly inform Old Zheng.
From the day he started practicing the Rebound Fist until now, it took him five months to break through to Ox Hide, and nine months to build up his blood and qi. The last step took him about two months to break through to Stone Hide.
Compared to Xiao Ran, who broke through in just three months, he was much slower. But he was still faster than the other disciples.
It seemed that after the Breaking Orb was filled, his blood and qi were no longer drained by it, so his progress sped up a lot.
He heard that Jiang Su took over a year just to break through to Stone Hide. So his speed was just right. Breaking through within a year meant he should get more attention than Jiang Su.
Finally, during a noon break, he walked alone toward Old Zheng’s room.
Taking advantage of Old Zheng's absence, he slipped a letter he had written through the gap in the window. The paper dropped onto the desk right under it.
Since he wanted to stay under the radar, he chose not to speak face-to-face. Old Zheng would understand everything once he read the letter.
Done with this, Wei He left quietly, heading back to rest and wait for the news.
Everything now depended on what Old Zheng thought. If the old man cared, he would naturally call him in private and give him the next stage of the training method. If he did not care, it proved Wei He was unremarkable, which meant he did not have to worry about attracting dangerous attention like Xiao Ran.
Old Zheng’s full name was Zheng Fugui. The name sounded cheap, but he had fought hard his entire life to reach his current position, which was quite impressive.
He originally thought he could train a few good disciples to take care of him in his old age. He never expected that Xiao Ran, the one he had high hopes for, would end up like this after the last sparring match.
Zheng Fugui felt a heavy weight in his chest. He had not slept well lately, and his spirit was completely drained. He was already old, carrying many old battle injuries. Now that his mind was exhausted, his energy dropped sharply.
It was noon. Bright sunlight streamed through the window, but Zheng Fugui’s heart was freezing cold.
He ran through the names in the yard. There was no one who could protect him in his old age, no one who had the skill to inherit the Rebound Fist Hall and hold down the fort.
The oldest disciple, Zhao Hong, was still stuck at the Stone Hide level, unable to move forward. His talent had reached its absolute limit. At twenty-four years old, he had no chance of making another breakthrough. Without the external martial arts of the Iron Hide level, he could never keep the Rebound Fist Hall stable.
The second disciple, Xiao Ran, was his brightest hope. He truly had the potential to reach Iron Hide in the future. But now... this heavy injury harmed not just his body, but his vital age and talent. Whoever did this was truly ruthless!
Thinking of this, Zheng Fugui felt a sharp pain in his heart.
But what could he do? He could not even find out who did it. People said it was the Tian Family, but where was the evidence? Even with evidence, how could he fight against such a massive family?
The Seven Families Alliance stood together. Touching one meant fighting them all. They had ruled Feiye City for so many years with deep roots. How could an old man like him stand against them?
The third disciple, Cheng Shaojiu, had decent talent and broad connections, but he lacked willpower. He wasted his background and talent for nothing. Otherwise, he might have had a shot.
Then there were Jiang Yan and Jiang Su.
Jiang Yan was just here for fun. He came from the Jiang Family, one of the Seven Families in the inner city. Coming to the Rebound Fist Hall was just a random choice for him. Even Zheng Fugui did not dare to speak harshly to him, let alone anyone else. A person from the Jiang Family would never take care of an old man like him.
As for Jiang Su, he did not rely on her either.
Jiang Su was a woman and would marry sooner or later. Her talent was about the same as Jiang Yan's. Though she had reached Stone Hide now, pushing to Iron Hide was nearly impossible.
Looking at her progress, it took her over a year just to cross the bottleneck to Stone Hide. She was far behind Xiao Ran.
Having taught students for many years, Zheng Fugui knew who had hope and who did not. To outsiders, these top disciples were all core figures he valued. But in his own eyes, all of them put together could not match Xiao Ran, who had a real chance at Iron Hide.
"Sigh..." He was fifty-four this year, covered in old wounds and diseases. In a few years, he might not even be able to teach anymore.
When that time came, what would he use to protect the property he spent his life building? His head?
Zheng Fugui sighed in despair.
Xiao Ran’s injury had crushed his last bit of hope. To reach Iron Hide, one had to break through to Stone Hide within a year. Up until now, only Xiao Ran met this requirement.
Now, he was powerless. His daughter’s side was a complete mess, leaving him to carry everything on his own.
Returning to his bedroom, Zheng Fugui put his hands behind his back and looked at the empty room. Thinking of Xiao Ran lying on the sickbed, his heart felt lost and sad.
He walked to the bed, sat down heavily, and reached out for the pipe on the nightstand. But after a second, he pulled his hand back.
He remembered the small black dog he raised when he was young, named Iron Stick. Whenever he was sad, Iron Stick would notice, run to his legs, and rub its head against him to comfort him.
Unfortunately, Iron Stick had died of old age long ago. A dog only lived for ten-plus years, even with his best care.
Zheng Fugui sighed, stood up slowly, and walked to the desk to start his daily bookkeeping.
He calculated every bit of income and expense across all his properties every single day. Only then could he know if someone was cheating him or stealing his money. This money was his safety net for old age, so he could not afford a single mistake.
He checked the books once in the morning and once at night.
Outsiders thought he was sleeping in just because he came out to the yard late. Ridiculous. He was doing his accounting! These brats knew nothing.
Zheng Fugui pulled out his chair to sit down, when his eyes caught a piece of paper on the desk, right under the window gap.
"Hmm? What is this? Did I bring paper out yesterday?" He could not remember clearly, so he reached out to unfold it.
Knock, knock, knock.
Suddenly, a rapid knock sounded at the door.
"Old Zheng! Xiao Ran is having spasms all over. It looks like his injuries are acting up again! Please come quickly!"
"Okay!" Zheng Fugui’s face turned serious. He stood up quickly and opened the door.
Outside stood an old maid responsible for cleaning the inner yard.
"Clean my room, change the blanket and bedsheets! I will rest right after I come back!"
"Yes, sir." The maid nodded repeatedly.
Zheng Fugui nodded and rushed off.
The maid stepped into the room and began cleaning quickly. She immediately noticed the messy desk.
"This desk is so messy, let me clean it up." With a sweep of her large hand, she grabbed the crumpled paper balls on the desk.
Turning around, her eyes blurred. Old Zheng, who had just left, suddenly returned.
"Old Zheng, you?"
"I forgot something." Zheng Fugui grabbed the paper Wei He had left right out of the maid’s hand.
"I do not use this kind of paper here. What is it?" Through the wrinkles, he caught a glimpse of handwriting inside.
Zheng Fugui frowned and quickly opened it.
The handwriting inside was clear and neat. It was written by Wei He himself, asking for the next training method after breaking through to Stone Hide.
Zheng Fugui’s eyes widened as he read.
He had a clear memory of Wei He, the disciple who barely broke through to Ox Hide on the last two days of the deadline.
Calculating the time in his head, he realized how long it took Wei He to go from Ox Hide to Stone Hide.
Less than a year!
"It is really less than a year!" Zheng Fugui felt a mix of shock and joy.
He was shocked that a talent had appeared under his nose without him noticing. He was joyful that the kid knew how to keep a low profile and write to him secretly.
After what happened to Xiao Ran, he was terrified. If another Xiao Ran incident happened, he would truly want to die.
"Good... this is good." Comparing the two, Zheng Fugui realized Wei He’s breakthrough speed was far behind Xiao Ran’s, which meant he would draw much less unwanted attention.
Normally, he would not feel this relieved. But after Xiao Ran’s fall, he understood clearly.
A person like Wei He was the truly cautious type. He had a chance at Iron Hide without drawing too much eyes.
"Good, good, good!" Thinking of this, Zheng Fugui felt relieved. The darkness in his heart faded away.
Remembering the content of the letter, he shook his head with a slight smile.
Wei He thought Jiang Su had broken through to Stone Hide within a year, assuming their talent was similar. But the kid had no idea. Jiang Su did not break through to Stone Hide in a year. It took her a whole year just to cross the final bottleneck after building up her blood and qi.
Just the bottleneck!
Including the time to build up blood and qi, Jiang Su spent at least a year and a half.
Suppressing the excitement in his heart, Zheng Fugui hid the letter, ignored the maid, and turned to leave.
He would handle Xiao Ran first, then go straight to see the kid Wei He!
He suddenly felt that he had ignored Wei He too much in the past. Could this kid be a late-blooming genius?
It was the next morning when Wei He received the news that Old Zheng wanted to see him.
Early in the morning, he walked respectfully to Old Zheng’s bedroom door, stood straight, and waited quietly.
The sky had just turned bright.
Old Zheng was already awake. A lamp burned on the desk by the window. Through the glass, a shadow could be seen bending low, writing fast. From time to time, the clicking sound of an abacus filled the air.
Wei He waited patiently. Aside from his initial greeting, he remained completely still and silent.
A full hour passed.
The sky turned bright, and the hot sun slowly lit up the land.
Creak.
The room door opened slowly.
Zheng Fugui stepped out, walking slowly around Wei He in a circle.
"You kid... not bad!" He slapped a hand onto Wei He’s shoulder. "You have seen blood, haven't you? It seems my old eyes missed your real worth."
The more Zheng Fugui looked at him, the more he liked what he saw.
Wei He had decent talent. Though far behind Xiao Ran, he was still above average and had a shot at Iron Hide. More importantly, he was calm and extremely cautious. He might actually be the one to protect him in his old age.
"It is all thanks to Master’s teaching," Wei He said, bowing his head.
"Good, good." Zheng Fugui thought for a moment. "As for your matter, I will teach you the method first, then make it public in two months. That way, we avoid trouble. Any problem with that?"
"I will follow Master's arrangements completely," Wei He agreed quickly.
