“Alright, since you’ve decided to stay, I’ll hand in your identity plaque later. Vice Door Master Yan Hui is in charge of managing the deacons,” Wan Qingqing instructed.
Wei He nodded. He took the box and turned around to look at the others.
About a dozen inner court disciples stood in the courtyard. Most of them knew he had crippled his own martial arts before, so they didn’t envy him much. Some even looked at him with quiet disdain.
Among the people Wei He recognized, Qian Rui—who had been dragged into the Purple Bamboo Society alongside him back then—kept his head down, hiding his expression.
Sun Yi, who had entered the sect from the outer court with him, smiled at him. It looked genuine, but his eyes were complex. Even if Wei He had restarted his training, he was still a Force Master now. They were in different worlds.
As for Chen Lin and Zhou Xingsu, who always saw themselves as geniuses, one had his eyes closed to rest, while the other whispered to a pretty junior sister beside him. Neither cared about a Force Master who had lost his power once. They were confident they would become strong Force Masters sooner or later anyway. Since they weren’t close with Wei He, they were just here to go through the motions, like attending a filler dinner.
Besides these Three Blood disciples who were now behind him, Wei He looked at two others who had reached the third layer just like him: Yao Hansheng and a tall woman he didn’t know. Both merely nodded at Wei He without saying a word. Wei He remembered Yao Hansheng because back during their first gathering, Yao’s stories of fighting military experts of the same level had shocked everyone. Wei He had kept that name in mind.
“Wei He,” Master Wan Ling finally spoke.
She smiled slightly. Her tired face looked a bit brighter now that the inner court had gained another Force Master.
“Master,” Wei He bowed quickly.
“You lost your power once, but you must be a perfect fit for the Rain Cover Force to train this fast. You reached the Force level in just two years. By the way, I heard you aren't married yet?” Wan Ling asked out of nowhere.
Wei He’s scalp went numb. He was at a key point in his martial path and had no time for marriage. The last thing he needed was an arranged match.
“Your disciple is devoted to martial arts and still young. I have no plans for that yet,” Wei He quickly declined.
“Devotion to martial arts is good, but you cannot forget to carry on the Wei family line. Continuing the bloodline is vital,” Wan Ling smiled. “I heard your parents are gone. Since I am your master, I must take up this duty for you.”
She paused. “I have a sister with a daughter. She is beautiful, lively, and the daughter of the Yuzhong County Magistrate. You should meet her sometime, Wei He. What do you think?”
Backing out of an arrangement made by a master was as bad as disobeying one's parents. Although Wan Ling asked politely, if Wei He refused, it would be seen as treason against his master. Getting dragged out and beaten up by her would be totally normal.
So even though he hated the idea, he had to nod.
“Thank you, Master.”
“Good. I know you have an older sister at home. If she wants, I can help find a good match for her too,” Wan Ling said softly.
“That... should depend on my sister's own wish,” Wei He said quickly.
Hearing this, Wan Ling’s eyes grew even softer. In this day and age, men who respected women like this were rare.
“True. Bring your sister over when you have time, and I’ll speak with her properly,” she said.
“Thank you, Master. I will,” Wei He replied.
Leaving the Wanqing Court, Wei He felt a heavy mix of emotions. He was twenty-one now, and staying single at this age really was rare in this world.
Now that he had reached the Force stage, his immediate path was cleared. Progressing further would require long, slow grind. To him, Force felt a lot like inner power. The thicker and purer it was, the stronger it became. Different forces also had different traits.
'Isn't this just like inner power?' Wei He thought.
Regardless, marriage was now a huge mountain blocking his path, and he had to deal with it. Otherwise, repeated marriage pushes would ruin his martial training.
On his way out, no one came to bother him. Since he had reached the Force stage after losing his power once, everyone seemed to have been warned by Wan Qingqing not to congratulate him, so they wouldn't hurt his pride. Wei He was glad for the quiet.
He walked home slowly. Leaving the Thousand Bats Pavilion, he circled around Tianyin Lake, which led straight to Tianyin Town.
Halfway there, through the misty lake shore, he spotted a familiar figure.
Yang Guo stood next to a man in fine clothes, talking quietly. Sensing someone, Yang Guo turned her eyes and saw Wei He. She raised her hand to greet him, but remembered something, froze, and put her hand down, leaving only a faint smile.
Wei He gave a calm nod and walked past them. They were once good friends, but now that she had made her choice, he would respect her path.
“Do you know that guy?” the man asked in confusion.
“Yeah. Just a younger disciple I used to know, but we haven't met in a long time,” Yang Guo replied, not wanting him to misunderstand.
“Alright.” Seeing her reaction, the man dropped the topic.
Yang Guo was already preparing for her wedding and paid little attention to sect matters anymore. She rarely came back these days, so she naturally had no idea that Wei He had broken through to become a Force Master.
“We should come here less in the future. Just a quick visit once in a while is fine. It’s too remote here,” the man whispered.
“Yeah, I know,” Yang Guo nodded.
Becoming a Force Master brought not just open benefits from the sect, but also hidden perks.
After Wei He rested at home for three days, Wan Qingqing sent someone with his job assignment: Guard Master of Yubei Town.
Yubei Town happened to be where Rushui Square was located. Clearly, this was a favor Master Wan Ling had fought to get for him, making it easy for him to look after his sponsor.
Standard rules dictated that each post had five outer disciples and one Force Master. The nine towns of Xuanjing City were guarded by nine Force Masters in total.
Wei He was given one month to report for duty, so he wasn't in a rush.
Since reaching the Force stage, he noticed his stomach absorbed food much faster. He could chew down a one-pound piece of dry beast meat without water in minutes, and digest it completely ten minutes later.
As a Force Master, Wei He knew his body well. He clearly felt this jump in digestive speed.
This was great news. It meant his Breakthrough Bead would charge up much faster, helping his upcoming plans.
Light rain fell on and off for half a month.
Wei He had grown used to training by the lake lately. Every day, the clear notes of Wan Qingqing’s flute drifted across the water. The pure sound helped him focus and train much faster. Wei He guessed she was using her Force through the music, turning it into a special technique.
Sitting cross-legged on a large rock surrounded by mist, Wei He kept a calm face and eyes half-closed. The Seal Blood inside his body woven itself into strange, thread-like structures.
His Whale Flood Art had finally hit its first bottle-neck.
'Training multiple martial arts at once is exhausting, and it slows me down,' Wei He thought with a frown. But having no solution for now, he pushed the thought down.
'At least the Whale Flood Art reached its limit.' He felt a bit relieved.
For anyone else, no matter how talented, breaking this bottleneck required consuming the heart blood of a special beast called the Peng Fish. But according to his research, the Peng Fish had been extinct for hundreds of years. This proved that the creator of the Whale Flood Art lived even earlier than that.
'Luckily, I don't need Peng Fish blood.' With a thought, the Breakthrough Bead in his chest burst open.
A wave of scalding heat rushed through his entire body.
The bead he had saved for so long now played its crucial role. The hot energy flooded into the threads of Seal Blood from the Whale Flood Art, forcing them to grow tougher and more elastic, while automatically weaving them into a blood pouch.
From activating the bead to forming the blood pouch, Wei He’s body didn't move an inch. Nothing showed on the outside.
Yet inside, a total change was taking place. Massive amounts of blood flooded into the new pouch, storing up endlessly.
The first layer of Whale Flood Art was complete!
His total blood capacity began to grow, rising every single second. This transformation would last a whole month. By then, his total blood volume would be twice that of a warrior at the same level.
This was the power of the first layer.
With so much blood drawn into the pouch, Wei He’s lips turned slightly pale. But soon, his fast digestion and blood creation converted nutrients into fresh blood to refill his body.
“Next comes stacking Force. Relying only on Rain Cover Force is barely enough for offense. Rain Cover Force isn't even top-tier among the nine courts, let alone against powerful forces outside.”
Wei He had already planned his next steps.
He planned to try them one by one to see which forces could complement the Rain Cover Force. This was easy for him. Once a martial art reached Three Blood, it would produce Force. Whether the new force fought with his current one or boosted it would be clear right away.
Backed by heavy funding and a steady supply of beast meat, Wei He had both the resources and time to experiment. The hardest part of learning multiple martial arts was overcoming bottlenecks, and the Breakthrough Bead solved that completely.
'The other problem is that after reaching Three Blood, every martial art creates Seal Blood. Seal Blood can't be removed. How do I handle it before it turns into useless buildup in my body?' That was what Wei He was really thinking about.
Listening to the flute music, his mind grew calm and peaceful as he searched for a fix.
'Seal Blood can't disappear because it's already changed permanently. In that case, is there a martial art that can eat all these Seal Bloods as food, absorb them, and turn them into one single type of blood?'
