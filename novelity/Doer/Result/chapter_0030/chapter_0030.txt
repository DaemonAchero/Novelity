"Katori Sect again?" Wei He thought, eyes flashing with cold intent.
"Hiding near my home right now... even if they aren’t criminals, they mean no good. Better kill them first!"
He turned around fast and punched both of them in the back with full force.
It was a sudden ambush from behind.
The two men didn't even know him. They were just walking along, never expecting a random passerby to strike out of nowhere.
On top of that, they were just low-level sect members with slow reactions.
Two dull thuds echoed out. Wei He’s fists smashed them to the ground, snapping their spines instantly.
Their bodies twisted into weird angles. Just as they opened their mouths to scream, Wei He caught up and stomped hard on their faces.
Crack! Crack!
The screams were forced back into their throats.
His face remained cold. He twisted his heels with heavy force. Two more snaps, and both men went limp and silent.
Wei He dragged the corpses over to the river bank behind the wall and tossed them into the dried reeds. He grabbed a straw hat, put it on, and chased after Xu Wei.
Running at top speed, he recalled Xu Wei's strength and reaction speed earlier. Without second thoughts, he triggered the Break-Through Pearl in his heart.
A massive surge of scalding blood rushed through his body. His muscles expanded rapidly, engorged with blood. Tiny red dots spread across his skin, and his fists turned a dull, grayish-white—rough and lifeless like solid stone.
In just a few seconds, Wei He’s frame grew a size larger.
His blood surged like a raging river. With killer intent burning in his eyes, he sped up and vanished into the alley.
Xu Wei was leaning against a wall, waiting for the sect members to bring a leader to finish off Wei He.
Hearing light footsteps, he raised his head.
"Back so fast?" He looked toward the mouth of the alley, slightly confused.
When the figure came into view, he saw a heavily built man walking toward him.
He hesitated. The man’s clothes were similar to Wei He’s—just plain gray pants and a shirt—but the body size was totally different. Plus, he wore the wide-brimmed straw hat typical of the Katori Sect.
"May I ask which leader has come to assist?" Xu Wei cupped his hands in respect.
The man reached up, grabbed the straw hat, and pulled it down.
The moment his face was revealed— Pfft!
A handful of quicklime was thrown straight at his face.
Xu Wei was caught off guard. Though his reactions were fast and he immediately threw his hands up, closed his eyes, and retreated, a bit of the powder still hit his eyes, burning his vision into a blur.
"You!"
His blood boiled. Sensing a foul wind rushing at him, he closed his eyes and threw out a Black Lotus Palm to block the attack.
The moment he struck, he realized something was wrong. His right palm slammed directly into a sharp spike.
He groaned in pain as blood burst from his hand.
"You!!" Furious and panicked, he opened his mouth to scream for help. But another cloud of quicklime came at him, covering his face and landing right inside his open mouth.
"I...!!" Pain tore through Xu Wei. Mumbling through the burning sensation, he swung his palms blindly ahead.
Once again, his palms hit the exact same spike.
Slash! Slash! Slash!
Three strikes in a row hit the spike. Xu Wei’s hands went completely numb, losing all feeling. Terrified, he turned to run.
Thud!!
A heavy fist slammed into the back of his head, knocking him out cold on the spot.
Wei He walked over, stepped on his neck with practiced skill, and gave it a hard twist.
Crack.
Clean and done.
Clap, clap, clap...
Faint applause sounded from the entrance of the alley.
"Nice moves." A man stood at the alley entrance, looking at Wei He with a relaxed expression.
"You hit hard and kill without hesitation. Cruel, sharp, and fast."
Before he could finish his sentence, his vision blurred. Wei He, who was just a short distance away, lunged forward like a ghost and threw a fist straight at his face.
The man panicked. He raised his arms to block and tried to step back to run.
It was too late. The fist crashed into his arm with the terrifying force of a second-stage blood surge. Like a heavy sledgehammer, it shattered his bone with a sharp crunch.
The man screamed and fell backward. He tried to shout for help, but he was a second too late.
Wei He stepped in and kicked him hard in the side of the head.
Snap!
His neck snapped sideways. His eyes remained wide open, staring in disbelief as he died.
Wei He quickly grabbed both bodies and hauled them over to the dry reeds. He threw all the corpses, including the earlier ones, one by one into the Feiye River.
The river was wide and deep. Even though the water level had dropped, the current was still fast. The moment the bodies hit the water, they vanished.
It was the perfect spot to get rid of evidence.
After clearing his tracks, Wei He walked slowly back to his yard.
That last guy... if Wei He hadn't noticed his legs shaking and small beads of sweat on his forehead, he might have been fooled by the act.
Too bad... after reaching the Stone Skin stage, his senses were sharper. Combined with his cautious nature, he wasn't someone who fell for cheap tricks.
That man probably knew he couldn't run in time, so he tried to play a master who had nothing to do with the Katori Sect just to talk his way out.
Too naive.
Wei He entered his yard calmly.
Wei Ying had been alerted by the noise outside. She stood in the yard holding a wooden stick, looking around on high alert.
Seeing Wei He come in through the side door, she let out a long sigh of relief.
"Little He, where did you go? I heard noises outside and got scared. I tried to find you, but you weren't around."
She lowered her guard and rushed over.
Then, she noticed small spots of blood on his chest.
"Ah! Why is there blood on you?!" She panicked.
Wei He kept a straight face and pinched his own nose hard.
"Ah... I just had a nosebleed..." He tilted his head back, letting a drop of real blood leak out.
"Oh no, you really are bleeding! What happened...!" Wei Ying worried immediately.
"Hold on, I'll go get something to plug it." She turned around quickly to search for cloth.
After a moment of panic, she plugged his nostrils and finally relaxed.
"How did you get a nosebleed? Is the weather too dry?"
She made a mental note to cook something cooling for him tomorrow, thinking the dry weather was causing him to overheat.
Wei He sat down, feeling the changes in his body after reaching Stone Skin and breaking through his blood capacity a second time.
His blood flowed smoothly and strongly through his veins. Under the push of this greater force, his body was changing every single second.
His body held more strength now. His skin was tougher, his explosive power was higher, his senses were sharper, and his movements felt lighter.
'So this is what a breakthrough feels like...?' He pulled his collar open and looked down at his chest. The pattern of the Break-Through Pearl was empty once again.
Emptying it for the second time left a hollow feeling in his heart.
'I wanted to take my time and break through on my own... but this world doesn't give me that choice...' He sighed internally.
Early the next morning.
Wei He woke up early and headed straight to the Mountain-Returning Fist Academy.
He had no intention of hiding his new strength. In times like this, showing high potential was the only way to get more resources from the academy.
Hiding his strength forever would only leave him without enough nourishment to grow strong.
Creak.
He pushed the door open and stepped inside.
Li Jue and two other disciples were already there, moving their training equipment around.
Wei He walked over to lend a hand, then looked around for Cheng Shaojiu and Ouyang Zhuang.
It turned out he had arrived too early.
Cheng Shaojiu hadn't shown up yet, and Ouyang Zhuang wasn't there either, likely taking a day off or working a side job.
Instructor Zheng had led a team out to fight a few days ago, so things were back to normal. He probably wouldn't show up until noon.
Wei He rested for a bit. Since no one was around, he didn't waste time and began training on his own.
It wasn't about showing off. Reaching the Stone Skin stage meant his training methods, blood building, and skin toughening techniques all had to change.
He needed to inform Instructor Zheng to get the next phase of the technique.
Unless he wanted to stay stuck in place, he had to report his progress.
Meanwhile, Cheng Shaojiu had no time to worry about anything at the academy.
He was currently arguing with his uncle over family matters.
Cheng Zhengxing, the head of the Yonghe Escort Agency and Cheng Shaojiu's uncle, had no sons of his own. He treated Cheng Shaojiu like his own child.
In the past, whatever this young master wanted to do—even using family money to feed lazy friends—he didn't mind.
Business was good back then, money was plentiful, so feeding a few extra mouths was fine.
But now, business was down, yet the boy kept bringing his useless friends home to feed them.
Calling it "loyalty" was taking things too far.
Cheng Zhengxing had argued with Cheng Shaojiu about this many times, but the young man just refused to listen.
Inside the main hall of the Yonghe Escort Agency.
Cheng Zhengxing tried to put on a stern face, but looking at his only nephew, he couldn't bring himself to be harsh.
"Shaojiu, the agency isn't doing well right now. You need to think about the family. Those friends you brought in... just feeding them every month is a huge cost for us." Cheng Zhengxing tried to reason with him.
"Uncle, didn't I already tell Zhou San and the others to leave? I've already sent many people away! The ones left are my true, loyal brothers. If I kick them out during such a hard time, where are they supposed to go?" Cheng Shaojiu replied angrily.
He understood the family was facing money problems. When the monthly allowance was cut, he helped explain it to them.
He even convinced some of his friends to leave.
But now he was being asked to kick out every single one of them during their hardest times. Wouldn't that make him a fake friend who turns his back on brothers?
"You know what happened to the agency, and with the way things are changing outside, our business has fallen through the floor. If you keep acting so stubborn, our family really won't survive this," Cheng Zhengxing sighed.
Who could have guessed that a thriving escort agency would drop to this state so fast?
If only Shaojiu had made friends with wealthy young masters like Jiang Yan, those connections could help them right now.
As for those low-born friends of his, no matter how many he had, what use were they? They couldn't do a single thing to help the family get through this crisis.
