Yubei Town, Tianyin Hall.
The sky was just turning grey when Wei He sat up from his bed.
He walked over to the wooden shelf in his bedroom. He carefully searched through his old reading notes, reviewing what he had memorized before.
Reviewing old knowledge brings new insight. It also helped lock the memories in his mind, so he made it a daily habit.
As he gathered more belongings, the secrets he carried grew heavier.
Wei He felt more and more that he needed a hidden room—a private place to store his secret items.
Take this branch hall, for instance. Once he left, anyone could slip into his bedroom. They might discover his work on poisons and knocked-out drugs, or even find his lost secret manuals.
Even with encryption and safety measures, another issue remained.
The room was simply too small. Many of his tests required long periods of time and space to complete.
Now, his knowledge of medicine had hit a wall. He needed a lot of tests to move forward.
Practicing his hidden martial arts also required a larger, safer place. Staying in the branch hall was too risky. It was too easy for someone to walk in on him.
‘Maybe I should build a hidden training ground and test lab?’
Once the thought crossed Wei He's mind, he could not push it down.
He read his books for a while and updated his notes on the current martial arts system.
He gathered the new information he had come across recently, writing down anything useful.
Then, Wei He started his daily training routine.
The Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Skill came first.
Next was the Whale Flood Art, followed by the Iron Ridge Armor used to strengthen his Rain-Covering Force.
The Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Skill went slowly as usual. The speed of turning blood marks into internal force was plain and average.
He had only finished the third layer. He was still far from finishing the fourth layer, not even halfway there yet.
As for the Whale Flood Art...
Wei He frowned.
He was starting the third layer of the Whale Flood Art, but his progress was strangely slow. It seemed like there was a problem with the blood marks.
The Whale Flood Art worked by weaving blood marks into different blood pouches. This stored more blood energy and raised the body's maximum blood limit.
However, after finishing the second layer, he needed to enter a state called "Whale Breath."
Only in this state could he weave the next layer of blood pouches.
‘It is clear that the third layer of the Whale Flood Art will bring a big jump in power. But this Whale Breath state... I have no clue how to reach it. The manual explains it in a confusing way. And since it is not a normal bottleneck, I cannot use the Breakthrough Bead to force it open.’
Wei He felt a slight headache.
Still, his current strength was enough for now. He was in no rush and could take his time to figure it out.
Standing up, Wei He slowly reached out and looked at his fists.
His fists used to be dark and hard. Now, they were slightly swollen with dark red spots. This was the payback from beating two martial masters to death earlier.
‘Pure physical power is strong, but force works both ways. Without enough defense, killing others will hurt my own body from the shock. It seems I have to wait for the Rain-Covering Force to boost my defense before this gets better.’
Now that Wei He's blood energy had grown, he no longer felt dragged down by his poor natural talent.
The boost from the Whale Flood Art allowed his talent to finally catch up with his fellow disciples. He was no longer at the bottom.
In real combat, his burst of raw power could take out an Internal Force Master in a single punch.
When unleashed, this force was even stronger than his Rain-Covering Force.
Yet, Wei He knew very well that the Whale Flood Art was not made for fighting. It was made to extend life.
That was why the Whale Flood Art did not touch on internal force at all. It was all about boosting blood energy.
More blood energy meant more strength, but raw strength alone did not make one invincible.
If his body could not take the force he put out, he would suffer just as much as his enemy. That made it useless.
He practiced the Whale Flood Art for a bit, but found no clues.
Wei He switched to the Iron Ridge Armor.
The Iron Ridge Armor was already at the second blood level. Once the Breakthrough Bead was full again, he could push into the third blood level. Progress was smooth.
After all, Wei He had already practiced many third-blood martial arts. The main hurdle for the third blood level was understanding internal force deeply, which was no longer a problem for him.
Finally, after finishing his daily training, Wei He pulled out the mixed poisons he got from Wang Shaojun and began to study them.
There were three types of mixed poisons he had traded for. All of them could attach to internal force, flowing with it to cover the body surface.
These poisons had different toxic levels and worked at different speeds. To fool the sharp senses of strong fighters, most of them had almost no smell, no color, and looked clear as water. They were extremely hard to spot.
Suddenly, a thought hit Wei He.
Since Wang Shaojun could get these drugs, then Yan Jiuhai, who was connected to Wan Qingqing, might be able to get them too.
Yan Jiuhai had a bad name. If he poisoned Wan Qingqing to force a marriage, both the Tianyin Sect and Lishan Sect would likely accept it once it was done.
Wei He had looked into the matter and knew a bit about Wan Qingqing's current situation.
Normal martial masters rarely saw drugs that could affect internal force users. They could easily drop their guard.
At this thought, Wei He picked up his pen and quickly wrote a letter to Wan Qingqing. He mentioned that he found a new poison that could affect martial masters, warning her to watch out for poisoned food or drinks.
Wei He had also asked around about Yan Jiuhai over the past few days. Just as Wang Shaojun said, the man was cruel and did not care much for women. It was unlikely he was trying so hard to marry just for a pretty face.
His true goal was clearly not simple.
As for Zhou Shun and Zhou Yugui, they were pushing Wan Qingqing hard to force the marriage. Were they not afraid she might hold a grudge and turn Yan Jiuhai against them later?
There was likely a deep secret hidden behind all this.
Wei He quickly finished the letter. Besides basic greetings and asking about martial arts, he wrote about the clear, odorless poison that worked on martial masters, telling her to stay alert.
As long as Wan Qingqing was on guard, the chance of her getting poisoned would drop.
Then again, Wei He thought he might be worrying too much. Wan Qingqing might have seen things like this before.
He sealed the letter and had Ding Yang send it to the Tianyin Sect.
Next, he just had to wait until October to head back to the Tianyin Sect and see what would happen.
If it was within his power, he would step in and help.
The long, peaceful days were coming to an end.
Wei He had a feeling that quiet days like these would be rare in the future.

A few days later.
Near Xuanjing City, at Yanshan Mountain.
Yellow leaves covered the ground, rolling in the wind.
Wan Qingqing wore a plain white training outfit. Under her mother Wan Ling's eyes, she was practicing her martial arts step by step.
She did not release her internal force. She kept it hidden within her palm attacks.
A white shadow moved back and forth in the falling leaves, her palms cutting through the air with soft thuds.
To a normal person, this looked like a fight dance.
To Wan Ling, however, every palm strike showed its exact reach and power.
That range and power were the true core of this palm technique.
Wan Qingqing moved like a flying butterfly, fast one second and heavy the next.
Soon, a dry yellow leaf fell in front of her palm.
The dry leaf made no sound as the palm force touched it. It shattered into dust, scattering like sand in the wind.
"That is enough, Qingqing," Wan Ling finally spoke up. "You have mastered the Thousand Words Palm well enough. If you use it in a fight, it will make your internal force much more active for a short time. Your attacks and defense will both grow stronger."
Wan Qingqing slowly stopped her movement. She turned, brought her hands together like holding a ball, and closed her palms.
Phoo!
She exhaled a long breath of hot air that lasted for over ten seconds.
"Mother, how strong is Yan Jiuhai really? Is there truly no news about him?" she asked softly after catching her breath.
"I do not know either. But among the young generation, your strength is right behind the very best. Yan Jiuhai has been kept hidden, never showing himself. No one knows his talent or understanding. Even if he has power, he should not be your match," Wan Ling shook her head.
"That is good," Wan Qingqing relaxed a bit.
Suddenly, a white bird swooped down from above. It slowed down and landed on Wan Ling's shoulder.
Wan Ling expertly took a small bamboo tube from the bird's leg, opened it, and pulled out a scroll of paper.
She opened the paper, and her brows pulled together in a frown after a single glance.
"Qingqing, get ready. The set time was moved up. We leave now for Zhongling Manor," Wan Ling said.
"Moved up?" Wan Qingqing paused, then nodded. "Fine. The sooner it ends, the better. I am ready anyway. I will make Yan Jiuhai give up for good."
"Good. Go back and pack your bags. We leave in a moment," Wan Ling said.
"Yes, Mother," Wan Qingqing agreed.

Late September.
Rain.
Outside Xuanjing City, Taizhou Prefecture lay straight to the north. The other three sides were large areas of wild mountains and forests.
The Jing River flowed through them like a ribbon.
The people in the city split these wild mountains into two areas. One was called Yanshan, shaped like an inkstone. The other was called Yangshan, shaped like a goat.
Deep inside Yangshan Mountain.
A large, tall figure moved fast through the woods. He left almost no footprints on the ground, leaping mostly from tree trunks and branches.
The figure had long hair over his shoulders and wore a black face mask. Standing two meters tall, he was a rare sight even in Xuanjing City.
Normal martial artists were usually around 1.75 meters. Those who were taller usually practiced special body skills, making their strange bodies easy to spot.
Yet this figure had a well-shaped body with no odd spots, despite his height.
Soon, the figure slowed down and jumped softly onto a patch of open ground covered in fallen leaves.
On the open ground stood a stone table covered in dry leaves.
The table had chessboard lines carved into it. On both sides stood wooden stool stumps of just the right height.
Beside the stone table stood a grass hut. It looked long abandoned, surrounded by thick weeds that almost blocked the door.
The figure let out a soft sound of surprise and walked up to the stone table.
Light filtered down through the trees, shining on his face. It was Wei He, who was supposed to be staying at the Tianyin Hall.
Ever since his breakthrough, his training had caused too much noise. He felt staying at the Tianyin Hall was no longer safe or easy, so he decided to build a hidden base outside.
The wilds of Taizhou were quiet, with few wild beasts around. So he had come out of the city to look for a place.
‘I did not expect to find a place where someone used to live. Looking at this chessboard, people must have played chess here before.’
Wei He wiped away the fallen leaves, showing the deep lines carved into the stone below.
The lines were rough, looking as if they were drawn with human fingers.
Wei He did not mind it. Any third-blood martial artist who trained in palm or finger techniques could easily write on stone.
He turned his gaze toward the grass hut nearby.
It was dead quiet. There were no sounds of bugs or birds around, which felt strange.
He walked closer, his internal force flowing through his body as he stayed alert for any sudden danger.
He had only come to find a spot for a temp base, but stumbled upon this place by chance.
To live alone in these wild mountains, the original owner was clearly no easy target.
