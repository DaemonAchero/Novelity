March had arrived, and a soft spring breeze stirred the willows.
Inside Fushan County, Xuanjing Region, what should have been a peaceful day was shattered by a brutal street battle.
On one side stood government guards in official uniforms. On the other was a smaller group wearing grey robes and blue belts.
Each grey-robed fighter had the character "Jue" embroidered on their sleeve. They belonged to the Decisive Hall, a minor local faction in Xuanjing.
The hall itself was not very powerful, but it had a strong master.
Or at least, a strong nominal master: Sun Zhu.
Sun Zhu had incredible talent. As a child, he bought a cheap street pamphlet and somehow mastered its broken martial art. Later, he trained under Master Yu Huoshan for twenty years before mastering Jin energy. He spent years hunting wild beasts in the mountains, studying their movements to refine his style.
By thirty-eight, he reached the Bone Forging stage. He pushed further into Viscera Refining, but poor foundations forced him to stop there.
Now in his fifties, his martial arts were completely perfected.
Blood ran like rivers through the streets as Decisive Hall members fell one by one. Overwhelmed by numbers, more than half were killed. The survivors finally dropped to their knees and surrendered.
Leading the government forces was a middle-aged man with faint blue eyes.
His name was Zhan Heng, known as Green Frost Fist. He was one of four top fighters sent by Liu Ye to assist Zhu Chen.
Zhan Heng came from outside the province, but his talent was exceptional. He trained under Zhang Dongyang, the top master of the capital, as his head disciple. When Zhang Dongyang died, Zhan Heng surrendered all his fellow disciples to Liu Ye, becoming his secret agent to survive.
"A-Heng, do you think Sun Zhu will show up?" a masked man beside him asked softly.
"If he doesn't, Decisive Hall is finished," Zhan Heng said coldly.
"Let's see if he truly cares about them," the masked man smiled.
"I checked his past. He is cold, but he keeps his promises. He swore to protect this place. He will come," Zhan Heng said calmly.
Nearby, a tall figure watched the slaughter from the shadows.
The wind blew back his hood, revealing a pale, calm face. It was Wei He, who had just left the Ten Thousand Green Sect.
He hid his breath and stood still. His cloak matched the dark wall behind him, making him completely invisible.
Wei He had traveled alone with only dried meat and his Mica Bugs. The bugs provided enough resources for now, but his Breaking Realm Bead still needed two or three months to charge.
He knew Zhu Chen's plan. Once Decisive Hall fell and Sun Zhu was captured, the government would come for him next.
He had to make sure Sun Zhu survived. The more time he gained, the more he could charge the bead and raise his strength.
Wei He waited quietly in the dark, watching the bloody scene.
The remaining fighters grew fewer. Finally, a small core group of five or six tried to break through the encirclement.
Covered in blood and hit by hidden weapons, their body-guarding Jin energy was almost spent.
Zhan Heng pulled on black gloves and stepped forward to finish them off himself.
After one step, he felt a sudden chill and looked back.
Less than ten meters away stood a man in a straw raincoat and bamboo hat, wearing a plain mask.
The man's breath was barely audible. His eyes were hidden behind the mask.
Shing.
He pointed a thin iron sword toward the ground and walked forward slowly.
"Hall Master Sun Zhu. You actually dared to come alone," Zhan Heng said, raising his right fist.
Frosty Jin energy spread over his fist, chilling the air around him. His Green Frost Fist specialized in freezing cold.
"Zhu Chen sent you to die?" Sun Zhu asked softly.
"Die?" Zhan Heng sneered. "We'll see who dies."
He stepped forward, pushing all his Jin energy into his right fist, and struck out wrapped in thick white mist.
He closed the distance in a flash.
Pfft.
Sun Zhu dodged by a hair's breadth.
His black sword vibrated, and a flash like lightning cut through the air.
Zhan Heng raised his left arm to block, but the sword was too fast.
Slash!
The two crossed paths and swapped positions.
A red line opened across Zhan Heng's chest, spilling blood onto his clothes and armor.
He looked down at his wound.
"Fast sword... sharp energy!"
"Still a fraction too slow. Otherwise, you would be dead," Sun Zhu said, shaking a drop of blood from his blade.
Behind him, four government soldiers suddenly collapsed, blood spurting from their necks.
In that single instant, Sun Zhu had sliced through Zhan Heng's defenses, cut his chest, and killed four attackers, all without taking a single hit.
"You really are one of the strongest masters in Xuanjing," the masked man walked out from the side, locking Sun Zhu between himself and Zhan Heng.
"Just the two of you?" Sun Zhu asked coldly.
"Let's find out," Zhan Heng smiled.
All three moved at once. To an outsider, three shadows flickered rapidly across the street, vanishing and reappearing.
Their powerful Viscera Refining Jin energy distorted the light and air. Every movement stirred violent gusts of wind.
Wei He watched silently.
With three top masters fighting, normal soldiers dared not get close. Anyone who stepped in would be crushed instantly.
Sun Zhu's sword was blazing fast, cutting through the air like sharp lightning. Combined with his slicing Jin energy, he forced the other two to stay on the defensive.
Soon, Zhan Heng and the masked man changed tactics. They overlapped their Jin energy, striking Sun Zhu's defenses in turns.
Their combined energy was too thick for Sun Zhu to cut through in one hit. The heavy impacts forced his sword to slow down.
The two fighters worked together perfectly, pushing Sun Zhu backward through the street. Storefronts shattered, wooden walls broke apart, and support pillars snapped like twigs under their stray attacks.
Sun Zhu was losing ground.
These two were far more dangerous than the three elderly Viscera Refining masters he had fought in the past.
Suddenly, Sun Zhu flashed five sword shadows at once.
The sharp black energy slashed through his opponents' sleeves, forcing them to jump back.
Instead of following up, Sun Zhu turned and ran.
"Stop!"
The two chased after him immediately. Within seconds, all three vanished down the street.
After running a few hundred meters, Sun Zhu turned a corner and stopped dead.
Standing in front of him was a two-meter-tall man in a heavy black cloak, wearing a simple ghost mask.
Behind him, Zhan Heng and the masked man rounded the corner, stopping in surprise as they saw the giant figure standing in their path.
