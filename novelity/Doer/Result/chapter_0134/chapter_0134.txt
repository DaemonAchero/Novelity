Creak.
Wei He pushed open the wooden door. Surprisingly, the inside was clean and tidy, as if someone had just swept it.
There were pale yellow wooden tables and chairs, hanging basket flowerpots on the walls, and a stone bed with a half-open blanket.
"This is a good spot." He took a careful walk around, making sure there were no traps, poisonous powders, or hidden threats. It was just a normal thatched hut.
Maybe an expert lived here before. But since it was empty now, it belonged to him.
Behind the hut was a vegetable garden. Overgrown with weeds, but easily cleared for use.
With the location settled, Wei He headed back to the Heavenly Seal House to move his things.
Keeping his vital possessions there was becoming too dangerous.
When he reached his desk, he found a black letter waiting for him.
It was sealed with red wax and addressed to him directly.
By the design, Wei He knew it was the private code he and Wang Shaojun agreed on.
He tore it open and read it fast.
It was short, but it made him frown.
"Wan Qingqing moved early? She left three days ago for Zhongling Villa? Is that where the fight is taking place?"
In the letter, Wang Shaojun warned him to act fast. Yan Jiuhai was a dangerous opponent, not someone to mess with.
Wei He closed his letter, rubbed his hand, and the paper turned to powder, drifting away through his fingers.
"Zhongling Villa... the neutral estate near Taizhou City?"
Zhongling Villa was not a martial sect. It was a hundred-year-old family estate independent of Taizhou's five big sects.
It owned the famous Peacock Lake, known across the province. They said at dusk, the water flashed with a thousand vivid colors. It was a famous sight, drawing crowds of travelers every day.
The Villa took advantage of this, running inns and restaurants. They were wealthy merchants with political ties and deep connections in the martial world.
Because they had friends everywhere, the Villa often served as a neutral ground to settle disputes and host big events.
Over time, resolving conflicts here became an unwritten rule.
Wei He fell silent for a moment.
"Zhuang Ping."
"Here, sir," a voice came from outside. It was Zhuang Ping, the house manager, always waiting by the door.
"Pack my bags. Our Senior Sister in the Inner Court is getting engaged. I am going to Zhongling Villa."
Wei He did not bother hiding his movements. He could not hide them anyway.
Wan Qingqing had not made a huge scene, but news still leaked. Fellow disciples, especially those from the Wan Qing Hall, would be heading there too.
"Understood, Boss Wei," Zhuang Ping answered fast.
Wei He gave his orders, turned around, and opened a coded lock on a hidden box in the corner of his study. He pulled out a set of pitch-black robes and a face mask.
Since he was recognized last time just wearing a mask, he realized his massive body frame gave him away. This time, he made a custom oversized robe that hid his entire body shape, paired with the mask.
If he was going to help Wan Qingqing, it was best to hide his identity so he could go all out.
Zhongling Villa was not just a simple compound. It was a large region with two mountains and a lake.
The mountains were named Mount Zhong and Mount Ling. The lake was the Peacock Lake.
Along the lake and up the slopes stood numerous pavilions for guests and residents.
It was dusk. Halfway up Mount Zhong, inside the Green Jade Pavilion.
Bird-shaped lanterns burned bright like balls of fire, lighting up the second-floor banquet hall like day.
Zhou Yugui fanned himself slowly. He wore a white robe under a black cloud-pattern silk cloak, looking like an educated scholar.
"Brother Yan, are you confident about this?"
He looked toward the balcony, where a man in purple stood with his back turned.
"Confident? About what?" the purple-robed man replied. His voice was deep and dull, as if nothing in the world mattered to him.
Zhou Yugui smiled, walked to the balcony, and looked down the mountain.
At the foot of Mount Zhong, a large open plot had been rented out by the Heavenly Seal Sect. Workers were setting up the arena for the marriage tournament.
Banners of the Heavenly Seal Sect were planted along the perimeter, forming a square.
In the center sat a square stone stage, surrounded by workers setting up tables and chairs for the guests.
"Tomorrow is the big day. I have talked to the Chief Disciples of the nine halls. Not that it matters much. Wan Qingqing is the strongest among them anyway. Only two Chiefs in the nine halls can fight her evenly."
"Are those two Xie Ruseng and Chen Han?" the man in purple asked.
"Yes. The One-Qi Finger and the River Hawk. They have quite a reputation," Zhou Yugui laughed.
"Besides those two, none of the other Chiefs matter," the man in purple said.
"I spoke to those two already. So you only need to beat anyone else from the nine halls who tries to ruin the rules. Though I doubt anyone will be stupid enough to try," Zhou Yugui smiled.
"Wan Qingqing's Yin-Scale Blood can balance my Extreme Sun Technique. If this succeeds, my father and I will remember your help, Brother Zhou." The purple-robed man turned around, revealing a stiff, yellowed face that looked like dry wood.
"You are too kind, Brother Yan. We have been friends for years. Hearing that your bone-tempering went wrong worried me deeply. Helping you now is just doing my duty as a friend," Zhou Yugui said seriously.
"You truly understand me, Brother Zhou!" Yan Jiuhai's face remained stiff, but his tone sounded moved.
"Then we wait for tomorrow's fight."
"Actually, it might not take that long," Zhou Yugui smiled suddenly, fanning himself slowly.
"What do you mean?" Yan Jiuhai asked, surprised.
"Maybe tonight, the beauty will give her heart and body to you on her own," Zhou Yugui winked, wearing a mysterious smile.
"Brother Zhou, what are you saying?" Yan Jiuhai was even more confused.
"Do not overthink it. It might not happen. But I know Wan Qingqing best. We grew up together. She cares about her friends and family more than anything. Forced into a corner like this, she might make a choice."
"Then I will wait for good news," Yan Jiuhai said, a hint of expectation in his voice.
He had strong self-control and stayed away from women, but if he could break through his martial arts and enjoy a beauty at the same time, he would not refuse.
On the other side of the estate, inside the Pavilion of a Hundred Charms on Mount Ling.
Wan Qingqing was chatting quietly with her close friend.
Wan Qingqing, Shu Yumo, Zhou Yugui, and You Bufan grew up together as childhood friends.
Since childhood, Wan Qingqing was closest to Shu Yumo. Among the four, Shu Yumo came from the weakest family. Whenever she ran into trouble, she came to Wan Qingqing, and Wan Qingqing always protected her.
Wan Qingqing treated Shu Yumo like a younger sister, caring for her and shielding her. Their bond was deep.
Shu Yumo came from a merchant family, nowhere near as powerful as the Three Big Families.
She was soft and fragile, like a delicate porcelain doll with snow-white skin that looked like it would break at a touch.
She came tonight because Zhou Yugui asked her to push Wan Qingqing into making a choice.
To force Wan Qingqing to change her mind, Zhou Yugui gave her a bottle of special Thousand Flower Nectar.
Shu Yumo could not refuse him.
She could not stand up to Zhou Yugui's pressure, whether through family background or strength.
Besides, she wanted to help Wan Qingqing make up her mind.
The Heavenly Seal Sect was trapped under the subtle pressure of the Three Big Families. The Yan Family of the Lishan Sect was far superior to the Wan Family in strength and status, almost rivaling half of the Heavenly Seal Sect itself.
If Wan Qingqing missed such a powerful family, she would regret it for life.
In Shu Yumo's eyes, Wan Qingqing was just young and naive, dreaming that she could win everything by relying on herself.
She did not know how weak a single person could be when real trouble came.
Waiting until you needed help was too late.
So tonight, she had to set this deal in stone for her friend. Even if Qingqing hated her later, she would eventually understand it was for her own good.
The two sat and talked for a while. They grew thirsty.
Shu Yumo shifted the topic.
"Qingqing, the fight is tomorrow. Are you confident you can beat Yan Jiuhai?"
She picked up the wine jug gently and poured a cup for each of them.
The drink was low-alcohol nectar made from flowers. For martial artists, their bodies would flush it out in fifteen minutes, so it was harmless.
"Seventy to eighty percent. I will make him back off," Wan Qingqing said firmly.
"Qingqing, will you really not reconsider? If you anger the Lishan Sect, your mother will face even more pressure," Shu Yumo warned softly.
"I trust my mother," Wan Qingqing fell silent for a second.
"If you ask me, what is wrong with marrying Yan Jiuhai? If he wanted me, I would say yes without thinking twice. I really do not get you, Qingqing. How do you know it will not work if you never give him a chance?" Shu Yumo whispered.
"I just want to pursue the life I choose," Wan Qingqing answered seriously. "My mother wasted her life like that. I do not want to make the same mistake."
"But we are women. We have the right to rely on others. Why not live a comfortable life? Why make things so hard for yourself?" Shu Yumo asked, confused.
"Maybe because I want to live on my own terms," Wan Qingqing thought for a moment and replied.
"Fine then." Shu Yumo raised her cup. "Even if I cannot understand, I hope you get what you want tomorrow. Victory to you."
Wan Qingqing raised her cup too, smiled, and clinked it against hers. She lifted it to her lips.
Just as she was about to drink, she saw Yumo staring at her blankly with her cup still in hand.
"Why are you not drinking?" she asked.
"Oh, seeing you drink just reminded me of when the four of us used to steal wine back then," Shu Yumo smiled, her heart skipping a beat. She was terrified inside.
She had taken the antidote beforehand, but what if it did not work?
The drug in the drink was heavy, made specifically to paralyze martial artists.
She was a weak regular person without any martial training. If she swallowed it, her body might not take it.
Now that Wan Qingqing called her out, she knew she could not dodge it. She downed the drink in one gulp and showed the empty bottom of her cup.
"Alright," Wan Qingqing's slight doubt cleared up.
She raised her cup and tilted her head back.
"Wait." She froze mid-motion.
"Did Bufan come?"
"You Bufan... he said he would arrive tomorrow," Shu Yumo's heart leaped into her throat as she watched Wan Qingqing swing the cup around. She grew anxious.
"Tomorrow..." Wan Qingqing smiled, remembering the letter Wei He sent earlier.
She hesitated, then put the cup back on the table.
"Never mind. I have a fight tomorrow. I should stay sharp and skip the drink so it does not affect my state.
Let us save this cup for tomorrow after the fight as a victory toast. What do you think?"
Shu Yumo froze, a forced smile breaking across her face.
"Alright then. If you say so, we drink tomorrow. You sound very confident, Qingqing."
"Fairly confident. If nothing unexpected happens," Wan Qingqing nodded.
