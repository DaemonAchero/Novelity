“Run!! Run!”
Deep inside the dark forest.
The survivors from the Return Mountain Fists rushed through the woods in complete chaos. Some lost their shoes along the way, but they didn’t dare stop to pick them up. Branches ripped their skin and left blood everywhere, but no one slowed down.
They just kept running, following Jiang Su as she led the way.
From time to time, dark arrows flew out from the shadows behind them. A few people took hit after hit and slammed into the dirt, never getting back up. Their screams echoed briefly before fading away into the distance.
The group grew smaller with every passing second.
Jiang Su carried Master Zheng on her back, running like a desperate animal. She had only one thought in her mind: live.
Just a while ago, they had split up with Wei He. The plan was for different groups to head toward Hong Fortress and the Ink Fortress of the Qingdu Sect. The former had senior apprentices from the Return Mountain Fists working there, while the latter was a place Master Zheng had mentioned before as a safe haven if things went wrong.
Unfortunately... they hadn't even made it halfway before men from the Seven Families Alliance ambushed them.
To make matters worse, they even spotted elite killers from the Blood Clothes Gang in the enemy ranks.
When they were surrounded, Master Zheng had forced his way through to open an escape route for them. But he was covered in blood and heavily injured. Just minutes after breaking out, he lost consciousness.
Jiang Su made a quick choice. She picked him up on her back and ran with her remaining martial brothers.
It didn't take long for the enemies to snap out of their shock and chase them down like hounds.
Desperate and lost, Jiang Su suddenly remembered what Wei He had told her before: if you run into the deep wild, you might find a sliver of hope.
She had nowhere else to go. The roads to Hong Fortress and Ink Fortress were already blocked by guards. Her only chance was to run in the direction Wei He had pointed out.
Her companions fell one after another. With bloodshot eyes, Jiang Su carried Master Zheng and ran with everything she had. She lost track of time and distance. She just kept moving until the sounds behind her died down.
Slash.
The blade sliced through the wolf leg with ease, cutting it into neat strips. Salt was rubbed over the meat before it was placed over the fire to roast.
Wei He, Zhang Qi, Ouyang Lin, and his second sister, Wei Ying, sat in a circle around the camp. All four stared at the sizzling, greasy wolf meat over the flames.
"Keep roasting," Wei He suddenly said. "I'm going outside to check."
He stepped out at fixed times to look around, making sure no hidden danger was creeping up on them.
"Brother Wei, let me go," Zhang Qi offered. His eyes were still red and swollen, but his spirit had recovered. The death of his older brother, Zhang Lu, had made him tougher.
"You won't be able to handle it," Wei He shook his head.
Only he and Ouyang Lin knew martial arts here, and Ouyang Lin was just a weak beginner who couldn't be counted on. He had to handle it himself.
He stood up and walked out of the small hall to patrol the area around the temple.
The corpses of the three wolves he had killed earlier were gone, dragged away by unknown wild beasts. Only three pools of dark blood remained on the ground, swarming with strange black bugs. Under the dim moonlight, they looked like three patches of black mud.
Wei He did a full circle and scattered beast dung around the perimeter to act as a warning line. Seeing nothing unusual, he turned to head back.
Suddenly, he caught faint sounds of running and fighting coming from the forest below the temple. The noise was weak and distant, probably a kilometer away.
Wei He frowned and looked into the dark. Night had fallen completely, and only a few pale shafts of moonlight pierced the thick trees. He couldn't see a thing.
Since it was late, he preferred to avoid trouble. He had no intention of checking it out.
He had three people to protect inside the temple. Ouyang Lin didn't matter as much, but he had to keep his sister and Zhang Qi safe. Zhang Lu had been his martial brother, and on his deathbed, he had asked Wei He to bring his younger brother to safety. A promise was a promise.
Wei He watched for a moment. Seeing that the sounds weren't getting any closer, he turned and went back inside.
Jiang Su carried Master Zheng, her legs feeling heavy as lead. Only a few people were left at her side, their faces smeared with dirt and blood, making them impossible to recognize in the dark.
The air was filled with the sound of heavy, hopeless breathing.
The pursuers behind them were like hyenas, locking onto them without letting go.
"You ahead! Drop Zheng Fugui and run! We'll let you live!" a deep voice shouted from behind.
"Hand over Zheng Fugui, or all of you die!"
"Give us Zheng Fugui!"
Shouts rang out from the darkness.
The Martial Master Alliance was broken, but behind every martial master lay a stable source of special beast meat to fuel their training. For the Return Mountain Fists, it was the Red Land Fish. Out of all the masters in the alliance, there were only five types of this special meat, and the Red Land Fish was heavily craved by many.
This wasn't meat hunted in the wild by chance; it was a resource that could be harvested safely and consistently. Wherever you went, having a source of wealth like this was enough to build a powerful family foundation.
More importantly, beast meat was the most vital resource for martial artists, far more valuable than gold notes.
Because of this, a heavily injured, unconscious martial master and a destroyed martial school were like fresh meat delivered right to their jaws.
The people chasing them now were no longer from the Seven Families Alliance, but men from the Blood Clothes Gang. After helping the alliance crush the Martial Master Alliance, the top fighters of the gang had scattered to hunt down the spoils.
The main target for the Seven Families Alliance was still the Flying Bear Sword, Yun Tiansheng. They had long held a grudge against the Martial Master Alliance for refusing to join them. Once they confirmed that Yun Tiansheng was secretly dealing with Hong Fortress, they struck hard and fast.
They had even planned for Hong Fortress's reinforcements. The Su Gang from Su City, along with masters from the Blood Clothes Gang and Shaoyang Sect, moved in to block and delay Hong Fortress. By the time Hong Fortress arrived, the battle was already over.
Thump.
Jiang Su's legs gave out, and she collapsed, rolling into the dirt along with Master Zheng.
In the dark behind them, the pursuers had run out of thrown weapons and charged in.
"Bitch, keep running!"
A man holding a heavy cleaver swung straight for Jiang Su's neck. He was at the Second Blood level, his inner power surging as his full force broke out.
Jiang Su knew the truth. There were no real top masters left among the pursuers. The real trouble came from these Second Blood gang leaders, who acted like relentless hyenas. They were the ones who lacked beast meat and couldn't break through to become stronger, which made their greed for it far worse than anyone else's.
Jiang Su rolled up and engaged two armed leaders at once. The three of them dodged and struck through the dark forest.
She was already injured and dizzy from blood loss. Fighting so hard now only made her wounds rip open further. But with Master Zheng lying unconscious nearby, she couldn't run.
"Take this!"
Catching a tiny gap, Jiang Su threw a heavy punch and forced one man back a few steps. But she was running on empty; the strike only pushed him back a few meters before he recovered.
"She's out of strength! Kill her!" the man screeched in joy, throwing himself forward again.
More men from the Blood Clothes Gang rushed up from behind, surrounding them.
Jiang Su and the remaining survivors were trapped in a desperate struggle. Cut after cut opened across her body. Yet, because she had dragged down and killed a Second Blood leader earlier despite her injuries, the enemies didn't dare push her too hard. They stayed back, teaming up to wear her down.
They acted like real hyenas, circling the prey and taking quick bites whenever she turned her back.
"Little Su... just run..." Zheng Fugui mumbled faintly, waking up in weakness. "Leave me..."
Jiang Su said nothing, gritting her teeth as she fought on. At this point, running away wasn't even an option anymore. She had wanted to drop everything and flee many times along the way, but somehow, she had held on until now.
Wei He stood outside the temple with his eyes narrowed, looking down into the dark valley.
He thought he heard a sharp scream earlier. It made him frown.
The woods at night were deadly. Wild beasts roamed the area, and worse, so did mutated beasts. Everyone knew this, yet people were actually fighting down there.
"Idiots looking for death..."
Wei He stared toward the sound, but it was too far and too dark to see a thing. He turned back toward the small hall, having no interest in getting involved with people seeking their own demise.
Suddenly, a faint shout drifted over through the wind.
"Hand over Zheng Fugui!!"
Wei He froze right as he lifted his foot. He turned around instantly and shot straight toward the sound.
Slash.
Another blade cut across Jiang Su's back.
She swung a fist behind her, but it missed.
The men around her refused to clash head-on. They just circled her, waiting for her to rest before swinging a blade. They wanted to exhaust her to death. No one wanted to risk their life, in case she dragged them to the grave with her last breath. Getting a clean kill without taking damage was always the best choice.
Three Second Blood leaders kept Jiang Su trapped while the rest hunted down the other two Return Mountain disciples.
Flash after flash of cold steel sliced through the dark, leaving deep wounds on the survivors.
Puk.
One of her martial brothers was hacked down. He twitched a few times in the dirt before going still.
Jiang Su forced away a blade coming from her right. The skin on her knuckles was torn to shreds, barely able to take any more hits. She squeezed out her remaining blood energy, threw a hard punch to push a man back, and retreated several steps to stand guard in front of Master Zheng.
"Hand over Zheng Fugui and you can walk away," a Blood Clothes leader said in a cold voice. "There's no need to throw your life away for a dead man."
Jiang Su stayed silent. She could no longer hear the breathing of the other two disciples. They were gone. She was the last one left.
The entire Return Mountain Fists... was really...
She glanced toward the grass where Master Zheng was lying.
That single look made her heart drop into pure terror. Master Zheng was gone.
Panic surged through Jiang Su. As she turned to look around, a blunt force struck the back of her neck. Pain shot through her mind, and everything went black.
Wei He held Jiang Su in his left hand and Master Zheng in his right. Moving at full power with his Flying Dragon Art, he glided through the pitch-black woods like a ghost, leaving no sound behind.
Spending so much time in the shadows had made his footwork lighter than ever.
After being spotted by that Shaoyang Sect master last time, he had put hard work into sharpening the soundless movement of his art. It paid off completely tonight.
Holding both of them, Wei He leapt up, using his movement technique to scale a massive trunk in a few swift steps, alighting on a thick branch.
From high above, he looked down at the Blood Clothes Gang searching with torches below.
He set Jiang Su down on the branch, pulled a small paper pouch from his shirt, and gently opened it. A fine powder drifted out from his fingers, falling into the night air.
Down below, the gang was growing restless.
"Where are they?!" a leader yelled, brandishing his blade.
Bang!!
A massive black shadow burst from the dark brush and slammed him straight to the ground.
Low roars erupted throughout the woods. Dozens of shadowy forms charged recklessly at the gang members. A wave of green, glowing eyes flooded past the base of Wei He's tree, surging toward the hunters like a rushing dark river.
Wei He watched cold-headedly as the gang got swallowed by the wild wolves.
Once the last wolf rushed into the chaos, he picked up both bodies, dropped lightly from the tree, and vanished into the night.
