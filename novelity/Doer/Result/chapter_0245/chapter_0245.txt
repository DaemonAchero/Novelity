Dusk fell.
At the Wang manor, carriages rolled into the back courtyard one after another. The heavy gates slammed shut behind them.
Guards stood watch on all sides, eyes scanning for onlookers. The oppressive silence hung thick in the air. Even a stray mosquito buzzing through the yard was sliced in two by a flick of invisible force. No sound was allowed to disturb the senses of those inside.
A curtain was pulled back.
A towering man, over six feet tall, stepped down from the carriage. He wore a dark cloak, his shoulder-length black hair loose. His face was pale and flawless, but his eyes shone like dark gems.
A group was already waiting nearby.
At the front stood the current head of the Wang family, Wang Yehe. Beside him were six senior members, both men and women. Among them was Wang Shaojun.
"We have to trouble you again, Sect Master Wei," Wang Yehe said solemnly.
"No need for formalities," Wei He replied with a faint nod. "Shaojun and I are old friends. This is nothing."
Ever since Zhu Chen died, Xuanjing City was tied up dealing with assassins from the alliance. The city guard's top fighters were busy fighting them off across the region, leaving no time for other matters. For now, the Wang family controlled everything in Xuanjing.
They led Wei He inside. The doors closed, and lamps lit up the grand hallway.
Wang Yehe walked ahead while Wang Shaojun matched Wei He's pace. Torches flickered along the wooden corridor, casting distorted shadows on the walls.
"Based on our intelligence, the Meteor Thief's footwork has two main traits," Wang Yehe explained as he walked. "First, he can make people ignore him. He blends into his surroundings so well that he becomes almost invisible."
"Invisible? Is it a matter of color?" Wei He asked.
"More than that," Wang Yehe said. "It has to be something else. If it were just color, our experts would have spotted him already."
They reached a spacious meeting room equipped with fresh tea.
They sat down, and servants immediately served fruit and pastries. Wang Yehe raised his hand, dismissing them. Only four key members remained, sitting quietly on the side along with Wang Yehe and Wang Shaojun.
"Second," Wang Yehe continued, his voice heavy, "his movement is completely silent. He moves like a ghost."
An elderly elder added, "I was less than ten feet from him once. He walked right behind me, and I felt nothing. It was a dead silent night. His footwork is beyond imagination."
"How fast is he?" Wei He asked.
"Faster than any of us," Wang Yehe shook his head. "Every time he's spotted, he vanishes in an instant."
"Bring in all the witnesses," Wei He said calmly.
"Right away." Wang Yehe whispered an order, and a guard left immediately.
Wei He was no longer the unknown junior he once was. His title as Master of the Thousand Poison Sect was built on a mountain of bodies. He had killed officers of the Crimson Sun Army, elite spies, and powerful martial artists. No matter how arrogant the Wang family was, no one dared to act up in front of him.
Soon, the witnesses arrived, including two young women who had been abducted by the thief: Wang Yini and Wang Shuyi.
Eight people entered one by one, and Wei He questioned each of them.
Finally, it was Wang Shuyi's turn.
She stepped inside and saw Wei He sitting on the cushion. She froze. She hadn't expected the grand guest to be him.
In the past, she had mocked him to his face, telling him not to act proud just because he knew the Wang family.
Now, her feelings were a mess. Wei He's gaze felt like sharp blades cutting into her.
The higher she used to look down on him, the more embarrassing this moment was. Back then, she treated him like trash. Now, even her father treated him with deep respect.
She bit her lip and lowered her head, letting her hair cover her face.
Wei He asked her the same basic questions as the others, but she felt like he was targeting her.
After a detailed interrogation, she and the others were dismissed. Relieved, they bowed and hurried out.
Only Wang Yehe, Wang Shaojun, and Wei He remained in the room.
"Is there a way to lure him out again?" Wei He asked.
"There might be," Wang Shaojun said darkly. "When he escaped last time, he vowed to come back and violate my cousin, Wang Yun."
"If we use Yun'yun as bait, he will show up," Wang Yehe added. "But she is under heavy guard right now. We must ask her opinion first."
"We can try," Wei He nodded. "No rush. Let's look at the clues first."
...
Wang Shuyi walked slowly down the hallway in her pale red dress.
She used to be the young mistress of the Wang family, one of the four great beauties of Xuanjing, courted by countless suitors. Now, her reputation was ruined.
Her body had been taken, her purity stolen. Her status in the family had crashed overnight. Wherever she went, people looked down on her. Even the servants whispered behind her back.
She was dirty now.
She bit her lip, but her mind involuntarily drifted back to the night she was taken, remembering the wild passion with the masked man. The overwhelming pleasure and the feeling of being held so tightly still made her crave more.
'No... Sect Master Wei is a master of poison and footwork. If he is involved, my lord will be in danger.' Fear took over her thoughts.
'I have to warn him.' Her love for the bandit made her hate her restrictive family even more. She only wanted to be with her lover, indulging in endless pleasure.
Thinking of this, she touched a small object inside her waist pouch.
She hurried back to her private courtyard, locked the door, and sent her maid away, ordering no one to disturb her. Inside her room, she shut the windows tight.
She pulled out a tiny, fingernail-sized yellow block from her pouch.
She snapped off a small corner, poured some water into a cup, and dropped it in. The yellow piece dissolved instantly into the liquid.
Moments later, a nimble green bird landed quietly at her window sill.
She opened a crack, let the bird inside, and attached a tiny note to its leg. But as she was about to release it, she hesitated.
The manor was under lockdown. Not even a fly could leave. If she let the bird go, the guards would shoot it down.
She took the note back and placed the bird into a small cage in her room.
Knock, knock, knock.
"Who is it?" she asked quickly.
"Miss, it's Xiao Shun. The master wants you at the Fanying Hall. The guest has more questions."
Her heart skipped a beat, but she replied quickly, "I'll be right there."
She checked the bird one last time, combed her hair, and stepped out.
Inside Fanying Hall, Wang Yini was already standing nervously before Wei He, Wang Yehe, and Wang Shaojun.
Wei He sat silently, listening to her recount her ordeal.
Wang Shuyi stood to the side and waited. When Yini finished, it was her turn to repeat her story. They had retold these events dozens of times, so the words came easily.
Wei He listened intently, searching for any hidden detail about the Meteor Thief. But after listening to her full account, he found nothing new. The Wang family had already cleared every useful clue.
"That's enough. You may go," Wang Yehe sighed, seeing that Wei He had found nothing.
"Yes," both women bowed and turned to leave.
Just as they reached the door, Wei He spoke up.
"Miss Wang Shuyi, do you like using Ruili incense?"
Wang Shuyi froze for a second, then realized he was talking about the yellow block she had just dissolved.
Her heart raced, but she kept her face calm and nodded. "Yes. Is there anything else, Sect Master Wei?"
Wei He stared at her for a long moment.
"You..."
He stopped himself.
"Nothing. You may leave."
Wang Shuyi bowed slightly and walked out smoothly.
She let out a quiet breath of relief.
This Wei He had a terrifying sense of smell. The scent of Ruili was extremely faint, barely noticeable to anyone except trained carrier birds. Yet he had picked up on it.
Wei He found it strange as well. The scent of Ruili was weak and rarely used in perfume pouches. It was odd that she carried it.
"Wait," Wei He's voice came from behind her again.
Wang Shuyi stopped in her tracks.
"Did you really not see the face of the man who violated you?"
