A few days later, news of the Wu army’s disastrous attempt to raid the Xuanmiao Sect spread everywhere.
General Xie An was killed in action. Four Buddha Masters, including Lord Dingyan of the Lifo Temple, fell with him.
The remaining troops broke and retreated. If the Xuanmiao Sect had not been so small in numbers, the Wu army would have been completely wiped out.
This sudden raid ended as fast as it began. It started out of nowhere, and it ended just as strangely.
With every high commander dead, the low-ranking officers had no choice but to lead the retreat.
When the news got out, it shocked the state of Wu and embarrassed the Great Yuan Coalition.
The Coalition had just offered to help the Xuanmiao Sect evacuate. Instead of running, the sect fought alone and crushed the Wu strike force head-on.
This shift in Haizhou forced Wu’s high command to take the sect seriously. Not knowing what they were up against, the Wu forces pulled back near Zhentao City to regroup and wait for reinforcements.
That very night, assassins struck their camp. They killed the guards at key nodes and stole the core pieces of the defense array.
The Wu army had to retreat again.
Even then, they lost hundreds to a thousand men every single night.
This nightmare went on for over ten days until the Wu army finally retreated to the border of Haizhou. Only when they reached their main stronghold did the killings stop.
By now, everyone knew the truth: the Xuanmiao Sect was hunting them, forcing them out of Haizhou step by step.
Left with no choice, the Wu army dug in at the border, relying on their 300,000-man camp to wait for help.
Meanwhile, State Teacher Modo and Grand Marshal Gongsun Ru were locked in a standoff against the Great Yuan Coalition at the Sishui Plains, commanding 600,000 men.
With both major armies stuck, Haizhou finally found a moment of peace.
...
Xuanmiao Sect, Inner Mountain, Yunsian Platform.
"Huan Songzi."
"Wei He."
"Greetings, First Senior Sister."
Surrounded by the True Masters of the three branches, Wei He and Huan Songzi bowed to Yuanduzi on the terrace.
The two had just returned from the deep sea. Looking at the broken buildings, scattered arrows, and bloodstains, they knew the Wu army had already been here.
How the sect drove them back remained a complete mystery to them.
In fact, even Senior Masters like Qingmeizi and Baihezi were out of the loop. They used to complain about the grandmasters being away, but Yuanduzi had handled this massive crisis all by herself.
The Wu army had come with massive force, only to run away with their tails between their legs. No one knew what trick First Senior Sister had used.
In the end, after Yuanduzi lured Lord Dingyan and his men into the pocket realm, she completely locked the entrance. Nobody outside saw what really happened inside.
"Don't worry about the details," Yuanduzi said gently, seeing the confusion on their faces. "The outcome is all that matters."
"First Senior Sister is right," Huan Songzi agreed. "What about Chen Songzi...?"
"Chen Songzi and the others are guarding the Liyuan Abyss. They didn't have to fight," Yuanduzi smiled.
Seeing Wei He's puzzled look, Yao Wan sent him a secret voice transmission.
'Chen Songzi is Huan Songzi's wife. They're inseparable.' Wei He understood instantly and quickly spoke up. "First Senior Sister, what about my family...?"
"They are all safe. I moved everyone deep into the mountain before the fight. They've already returned home. However... your house will need some repairs."
"That's a small matter, as long as they are safe," Wei He let out a sigh of relief.
His family was his biggest concern. With his wife, child, and parents here, a slip-up would have meant total disaster. Tianhai Island was surrounded by water; if it fell, there would be no escape.
"First Senior Sister, how exactly did you beat those Buddha Masters?" Qingmeizi asked, still doubtful. He couldn't figure out what kind of trap could keep four Buddha Masters and their secret leader dead in the pocket realm.
They were a sect, not a military camp. They didn't have array power to borrow.
"It was thanks to the three Grandmasters," Yuanduzi sighed with deep emotion. "Before they left, they gave me secret plans for this crisis. I simply followed their instructions. It is nothing impressive."
She pushed everything onto the three Grandmasters. They were Grandmasters, after all—capable of anything. Even Baihezi and the others didn't fully know the limits of a Grandmaster's power.
The crowd remained skeptical, but they had no proof to doubt her.
Yuanduzi then gave a half-true account of how close the fight was, claiming the Grandmasters had left her powerful, single-use weapons. Combined with the star array and a surprise attack, the damage went off the charts.
It sounded fishy, but nobody could disprove it.
Wei He was speechless. As he walked down the platform with the others, Huan Songzi patted his shoulder.
"It's not the first time something like this has happened. As long as we survived, why overthink it? You're just giving yourself a headache."
Wei He had no reply to that.
His easygoing senior brother had a point. The result was good, and that was enough.
For Wei He, his biggest haul from the trip was the Black-Striped Whale oil. He had an entire whale's worth of oil now—enough to last him a long time.
He planned to hole up in his cave and focus entirely on training. He wasn't going anywhere until his strength leveled up.
The war between Great Yuan and Wu was building toward a final showdown. In times like these, raw power was the only thing you could rely on.
Leaving the platform, Wei He went all in on his cultivation.
Half a year passed in the blink of an eye.
During those six months, the two armies remained at a stalemate, though small clashes broke out constantly. Both sides suffered wins and losses, waiting for the right moment to strike a decisive blow.
Wei He lived a simple, strict routine, moving only between his home and his cave. Most of his time went into converting his True Force into the second layer of Mind-Storing Force.
It was a slow, grinding process, but he also trained in the Whale Surge Technique. With the whale oil, he finally started the fourth and final layer.
The fifth layer of Whale Surge was purely theoretical; even its creator had only reached the fourth.
In Great Yuan, once a True Force warrior reached the Viscera Refining stage, they stopped training raw muscle and focused entirely on True Force.
When True Force evolved into Mind Perception, it became Real Force. Real Force offered no physical boost; in fact, its harsh nature could even erode the user's body over time.
Because of this, many True Masters were no stronger physically than a Viscera Master. Their true terror lay in their lethal force and insane healing speed.
Wei He was the sole anomaly.
By stacking the Whale Surge Technique, he had built a body as tough as a Blood Warrior while walking the path of a Force Warrior.
BOOM!
Inside the martial field on Yunsian Platform, Yuanduzi watched silently from the side.
Two figures clashed violently in the arena.
It was Huan Songzi and Wei He, and both were taking it seriously.
Huan Songzi's body flickered as he entered the Fully Realized state. Black, snake-like energy swirled around his limbs. Behind him, the faint phantom of a dragon-headed turtle flashed briefly, boosting his moves with his innate talent.
Wei He didn't hold back either. Entering his Tower-Climbing form, a sea of Real Force burst out of him. His force was lighter in color than Huan Songzi's, but the sheer volume wrapped him up until he was almost invisible.
In terms of quantity, his force completely dwarfed Huan Songzi's.
Every time they collided, a chunk of Wei He's force shattered, but his pool was so deep that it didn't bother him at all.
For a short while, he actually fought Huan Songzi to a standstill.
With another heavy crash, the two separated and landed on their feet.
Wei He had a fresh palm print stamped onto his chest. He had lost.
"Unbelievable... If First Senior Sister hadn't told me, I never would have guessed your talent was this terrifying!" Huan Songzi said, breathing heavily as he stared at Wei He.
"Your Real Force reserve is at least three times mine! It's insane! I thought the top genius of the Jinlian Sect was a one-of-a-kind monster, but you..."
At this point, Wei He had used every bit of his baseline strength.
Just as he had guessed, without using his hidden trumps, he could go toe-to-toe with Huan Songzi for a while. But once a true expert went all out, he was still outmatched.
"Wei He's hard limit right now is the Fifth Step of the Fully Realized state," Yuanduzi said calmly.
She looked at Wei He. "Your force is massive, but it only works against opponents below the Fifth Step. Do you know why?"
Wei He straightened up and bowed. "Please enlighten me, Senior Sister."
"Because once a master reaches the Fifth Step, their force pool explodes in size as well. They won't be far behind you in quantity. When that happens, your volume advantage vanishes, while the quality of your force remains far weaker than theirs. You would lose instantly."
Wei He nodded. He knew Yuanduzi was aware of his real strength, and this Tower-Climbing form was indeed his current cap. Her evaluation was spot on.
"That's still terrifying," Huan Songzi grumbled, shaking his head. "He's only at the First Perception! Senior Sister, give the rest of us a break, will you?"
