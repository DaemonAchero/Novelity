The Wanqing Sect.
"This is my first time visiting your place, isn't it?"
Wang Shaojun walked alongside Wei He, taking in the sector grounds. Two subordinates followed closely behind him.
Both men looked calm, but the muscles beneath their black robes were tight. They were ready for a fight at any second.
After all, this was the infamous headquarters of the Wan Poison Sect.
Rumors about the sect master, Wei He, poisoning thousands of people were still spreading through the city. People said so many died back then that every house hung white banners and everyone wore black cloth in mourning.
Wang Shaojun noticed his men's tension. He found it funny, but also felt helpless.
That was the power of rumors. Even Bone-Forging martial artists could not help being affected by them.
Wei He walked ahead. His massive body was now nearly two and a half meters tall.
Along the path, members of the Heavenly Seal Sect stopped and bowed to him. The look in their eyes showed how much they trusted and believed in him as their leader.
"I haven't fully organized this place yet," Wei He said with a faint smile. "I'm just leading everyone in name for now. Calling me the Sect Master doesn't really count."
"It's good you were here to hold down the fort this time. That Jinyu Hall seems to be a branch of the Chaos God Cult, just like the old Returned Goose Tower. But it looks much stronger than the tower was. I still don't know what to do with Tang Huanhuan."
The blond man from Jinyu Hall had given Wei He a strong warning. The man was wounded, poisoned, and fell off a cliff during their fight. It was clear that Jinyu Hall was far tougher than Returned Goose Tower.
"Why not just kill her?" Wang Shaojun asked, surprised. "Are you keeping her for the New Year?"
"Killing her is a waste," Wei He sighed. "It's not easy to catch a live Viscera-Refining master. I want to squeeze everything out of her first."
"If she was uninjured, Jinyu Hall might trade with you. But now that she's ruined, she has no value to them," Wang Shaojun said, shaking his head.
"Not necessarily. The main technique she trains is still very valuable," Wei He said.
"What? You want to rebuild the Horizon Tower?" Wang Shaojun was speechlessly amused. "They say Horizon Tower was built piece by piece just like this."
"Maybe. Anyway, we're here. This is where I live."
The four men stopped in front of a small wooden house by a pond.
The house was too small for all of them, so the two men brought out stools and sat in the open space outside.
"You're always busy, so you wouldn't come here just to hang out," Wei He said calmly. "Speak up. What is it? No need to hide things between us."
"You guessed it," Wang Shaojun laughed. "Someone from Jin Province asked me to bring you a message."
"Oh? What force? What are they offering?" Wei He instantly became interested.
"The biggest sect in Jin Province, the Wanfei Palace. It's a power formed by strong martial artists who act like businessmen. They control an entire province, so... you get the idea." Wang Shaojun raised an eyebrow.
"Are they arrogant?" Wei He smiled.
"A bit," Wang Shaojun said simply. "They want you to release the prisoner. If you don't, they will send people to clear up all the trouble."
"Experts? How strong?" Wei He smirked.
"Very strong. Stronger than both of us right now," Wang Shaojun replied seriously, without a smile.
"Interesting," Wei He shook his head. "Then let them come. Tell them this: at worst, we die together."
Looking at the situation, Wei He was truly ready to take his sister and leave if things went wrong.
The situation in Xuanjing was already turning bad. He could easily run hit-and-run attacks in the Black House Mountains and poison everything in his path. The mountains were full of deadly poisons anyway. He wanted to see just how tough this Wanfei Palace really was, and how many men they could afford to lose.
As for the Wanqing Sect, he could break it up and hide his people deep in the mountain range.
Wanfei Palace would have to waste their men and time trying to hunt him down.
Since people already saw him as a demon, he saw no reason to hold back. If he couldn't kill the top experts, he could still slaughter everyone else beneath them.
"You're really going all out?" Wang Shaojun was speechless.
"I have to," Wei He smiled. "I can't step back now. Besides, my Wanqing Sect is already broken and ruined. How much worse can it get?"
Wang Shaojun had to agree with that.
Poison experts were always the kind of trouble major forces hated to provoke. Without a proper counter, they were impossible to guard against.
Especially a master like Wei He, who killed Bone-Forging martial artists as easily as butchering chickens.
Even a massive force like Wanfei Palace was a province away, and they were still held back by the Wushi Sect. How many men could they actually send into Taiming Province?
With the Wushi Sect around, Wanfei Palace could not act freely in Taiming. That gave Wei He plenty of room to maneuver.
"Still, you need to tell me about the Sense-Marking realm above Viscera-Refining. What level is it really?" Wei He asked. "I've only read a few descriptions in books. I don't know how strong it actually is because I've never seen a real one."
"Sense-Marking... is simply training your five senses to surpass human limits and enter a new realm," Wang Shaojun explained simply. "Experts at this level are essentially the top leaders of their own factions. People like Yuchi Zhong, Shangguan Ji, and the masters of Danyang Gate are all at this stage."
"At this realm, it is split into five parts: eyes, ears, mouth, nose, and touch. Enhancing these five senses brings a massive leap in martial strength. I don't know the exact details, but I noticed that top experts at this level all seem to focus on a specific sense."
"Focus?"
"Yes. Like Shangguan Ji from your Heavenly Seal Sect. People call him the Fire-Eyed Golden Ape. You don't think that's just a nickname, do you? His eyes are the main sense he trained."
Wei He instantly understood.
"If it was just making their senses sharper, it shouldn't boost their power by that much. They must have other secrets."
"That's true. But we aren't qualified to touch that level yet. As for those top experts, we'll deal with them later," Wang Shaojun sighed.
The peak of Viscera-Refining was the limit for the Three Great Families. Sense-Marking was a level reserved only for the Five Great Forces.
If the Three Families had Sense-Marking experts, they would be part of the top forces too.
The two talked quietly by the pond for a long time. When it grew dark, Wang Shaojun left with his men. He also took the new poison-locking drug Wei He created to test it out.
The last batch sold very well, so Wei He decided to produce it regularly.
It cost about three taels of gold to make a single vial. A finger-sized vial could be used on five people.
Wang Shaojun bought fifty vials at once for thirty taels of gold per vial. This was a friendly discount because of how much Wang Shaojun had helped him.
If it were anyone else, Wei He would charge at least three thousand taels per vial. After all, the recipe was a secret.
Thanks to the rich poison resources in the Black House Mountains, he could make large batches easily.
After seeing Wang Shaojun off, Wei He went to check the interrogation of Tang Huanhuan.
He recorded all the martial arts squeezed from her, added them to his collection, and used them to broaden his knowledge and find new ideas.
Now, among the nine styles of the Heavenly Seal Grand Skill, he only needed the final one.
Sadly, Shangguan Jue from the Yuanbing Hall was missing. Wei He had only obtained the entry-level part of her Cold Charm Art, while the rest was lost.
"Forget it. I'll focus on building my foundation first. My current strength isn't enough against the stronger enemies I'm facing."
Wei He knew his situation well. The forces he was pissing off now were all at the Viscera-Refining level or higher.
Just staying at the Bone-Forging level was no longer enough.
This was the price he had to pay to live freely without backing down.
Finally, he checked on the plague situation. Jiuying seemed to have controlled the illness for now.
The patients he treated didn't die as quickly, but the treatment consumed a massive amount of herbs.
The small fee the patients paid was far from enough to cover the costs.
Jiuying's goal was to study the plague, find a cure, and save specific people he cared about—his friend's grandchildren and his benefactor's family.
Meanwhile, some people in Qingqi County were secretly hoarding herbs to profit from the dying.
The local government was useless. The guards and officers were abandoning their posts because too many people were infected.
At the same time, Xuanjing City isolated the surrounding counties, setting up military guard posts to block anyone from crossing.
Under these conditions, Wei He had to supply herbs endlessly, which made him frown.
It was not that he was greedy, but this was not his job. He was using the Wanqing Sect's resources to feed strangers in Qingqi County.
He could do it a few times, but doing it forever would ruin his own sect.
He was no saint. His disciples risked their lives gathering herbs in the dangerous mountains. The Black House Mountains were not a back garden, and the wild beasts inside were far from harmless.
Wei He thought for a moment and gave an order.
The herbs sent before were a donation, but from now on, Qingqi County had to buy them at fair prices.
He would not scam people in a crisis, but he would not play the fool either.
As for those hoarders making money off the dead? Send men to wipe them out and take their stock.
He was not the government. He only cared about what he liked and disliked.
After clearing up these matters, Wei He focused entirely on cultivating the Cloud-Gathering Bone Structure.
He also used the Autumn Deer Skill to test for a suitable leg technique. The Shadowless Illusion Skill he used before was still not a good fit.
He had to keep searching through the collection from Horizon Tower.
Right now, he had too many techniques to practice.
He had eight styles of the Heavenly Seal Grand Skill to train, plus the Zhengfa Skill, White Jade Skill, finding the Whale Breath state for the Whale Flood Skill, and using the Autumn Deer Skill to find a matching leg technique.
Fortunately, the Iron Ridge Armor and the Overturn Rain Force did not conflict with each other, nor did they clash with the other sub-branches of the Heavenly Seal Skill.
This saved him a lot of trouble. Otherwise, he would have to waste time finding another complementary skill to fix it.
Time passed slowly in training and waiting.
In the blink of an eye, August arrived.
Over the past two months, Wei He used the Silkworm Force to raise the cloud patterns of his Cloud-Gathering Bone Structure to the fourth level. His training speed was vastly faster than before.
There were eighteen cloud patterns in total. At this rate, he estimated he could complete it in one or two years. The power of the Silkworm Force truly lived up to its name.
Soon after, Wanfei Palace tried to send people over, but the Wushi Sect blocked them.
The Wushi Sect was arrogant. Ever since the major event two hundred years ago, they looked down on all other forces. They didn't even care about Wanfei Palace, which controlled a whole province.
According to Wang Shaojun's intelligence, the Wushi Sect believed Wanfei Palace was using an excuse to meddle in Taiming Province. Without a word, they fought Wanfei Palace at the border and beat them back.
Wei He and Wang Shaojun looked at each other in surprise, but after thinking it through, it made sense.
Taiming Province was the Wushi Sect's territory. No one wanted a powerful, dangerous outsider wandering around their home.
Wanfei Palace claimed they were only catching a few rats, but who would believe that?
Either way, this bought Wei He valuable time to cultivate.
He kept Tang Huanhuan, Yue Xiaodie, and Yuchi Qi locked up like livestock.
Meanwhile, friction between Jinyu Hall and the Wanqing Sect grew. Disciples from both sides fought and killed each other in the shadows.
However, under Wei He's training, the Wanqing Sect members used their martial arts together with poison techniques, holding their ground and trading blows with the enemy.
Wei He treated these fights as real combat practice for his men.
During all this, Wei He paid no attention to the minor skirmishes outside.
He was deep in seclusion, fully focused on rebuilding and integrating his own system of martial arts.
