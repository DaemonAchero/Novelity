Clang!
A heavy metallic clash echoed through the air. A deep blood trail opened across his fist.
Just as he thought he had successfully blocked the strike, the blade of dark energy in front of him shattered. Dozens of dark smoke streams burst out from inside, howling past him.
In a split second, the smoke surrounded him and the other masters nearby.
Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom!
After a series of heavy blasts, the men flew backward, slamming hard into the ground.
Under the crushing force of his massive energy, Zhao Xu and the masters had no chance to fight back. One of them even tried to use a secret technique to boost his power.
Too bad for him. Regardless of what technique he used, Wei He was like someone holding a high-pressure water cannon, spraying force directly into his face.
It did not matter what move or force they used. As long as their energy was weaker than his, they were instantly wrapped by his vast power and frozen in place.
Without the absurd raw strength and resistance of the Blood-lines from the Wu Nation, these martial masters who relied purely on energy were completely dead in the water once they were overwhelmed. A mere few thousand pounds of physical strength could never break free from this pressure.
This sudden attack sent panic through the remaining monks of the Wushi Sect, who were already watching nervously from nearby.
Just as Wei He was ready to sweep the entire area, the rest of the Wushi Sect monks panicked like scared birds. In just a few breaths, they turned and ran off in all directions.
Wei He was speechless.
The unity of this Wushi Sect was truly something to behold.
Only Zhao Xu and a few other masters remained on the ground, completely frozen and unable to move.
Wei He walked over to Song Shizhen, lifted him up by the collar like a sack, and swung him in front of Zhao Xu's face.
"So, tell me. You kidnapped my godson. How are we going to settle this debt? What is my compensation?"
Wei He was only here to start a fight in the first place. Whether he actually cared about this godson depended entirely on if the guy was useful to him.
His excuse to pick a fight was crude, but it got the job done. He was not very experienced at this yet anyway, but he would get better with practice.
Beside him, Song Shizhen had been focusing on decoding his blueprints. When he finally looked up, he was shocked to see the crowd of Wushi Sect members running away for their lives.
The leaders who had been so arrogant were taken down by Wei He in a flash, completely useless.
"Senior Brother Wei, this place is extremely important to our Wushi Sect," Zhao Xu threatened, realizing soft words would not work. "If you stop now, the Three Peaks might not take extreme action. But if anything really goes wrong here, I guarantee that the masters of the Three Peaks will never let this go."
"Then let them come," Wei He said casually. "If they have the guts, tell them to come find me at the Xuanmiao Sect mountain gates. I will be waiting."
Anyone could talk big.
By now, almost all the Wushi Sect members had fled. Only a confused Song Shizhen was left, along with Zhao Xu and his men, who were stuck on the ground.
Wei He did not bother with them anymore. He walked straight up to the entrance of the Shadow Beast Ruins.
He had only read about these Shadow Beast Ruins in the ancient books of his sect.
Inside these ruins, one could find an item called the Shadow Beast Dial. This single object was the core reason why the great sects could keep their holy land arrays running for a long time.
Otherwise, if they had to rely solely on hunting real beasts for their cores to power the arrays every single day, how many people would they need to send out to hunt non-stop?
On the pale white door of the ruin, faint grey-black marks crawled continuously across the surface, like living creatures.
This was what the ruin door looked like under true vision.
Strands of black force kept leaking out from the cracks of the door, carrying a thick, dangerous vibe.
Wei He looked left and right, but he could not see where the suppression array was hidden.
An array capable of suppressing the aura of a Shadow Beast Ruin like this had to be extremely valuable.
"Since you grabbed my godson, I will give you a chance to make up for it," Wei He said, turning back to Zhao Xu.
"Please speak, Senior Brother Wei! As long as we can do it, we will do it!" Zhao Xu was overjoyed, seeing a sliver of hope.
If paying a small price could get rid of this terrifying visitor, that would be the best outcome.
"It is very simple..." Wei He smiled slightly.
A moment later.
The entire suppression array around the Shadow Beast Ruin had completely vanished. Along with it, every single valuable item on Zhao Xu and his men had been stripped clean.
After taking their things, Wei He walked back to the entrance of the ruin.
Ever since he reached his current level, he had never stepped inside a ruin like this.
It was said that the Shadow Beast Dial inside was the most valuable item in the whole place.
Since he was already here, he was not going to leave empty-handed.
That Shi Heng guy had been acting so arrogant earlier. This was a good chance to teach him a lesson in person, to let him know that out in the real world, nobody was going to pamper him.
Thinking of this, Wei He looked up at the sky.
It was noon, and the sunlight was bright.
Yet, despite how bright the sun looked, the light shining down felt cold, carrying almost no warmth at all.
"What happens if someone goes in without breaking the array first?" Wei He asked.
Zhao Xu was sitting behind him with a gloomy face. Everything on his body had been taken, even the array plates were gone.
Right now, he only hoped Wei He would stay here a little longer. Once Shi Heng arrived, he would at least have an answer to give.
He hesitated for a second, but when he felt Wei He's cold glance sweep over him, a shiver ran down his spine, and he quickly answered.
"If you force the door open, only two things can happen. One, you fail to open it. Two, the array gets smashed, the ruin destroys itself, and everything inside disappears, turning it into a normal empty cave."
"Self-destruction?" Wei He was surprised. Such an advanced setup?
Driven by curiosity, he closed his eyes and opened them again a second later.
Everything around him transformed back into the true world view.
The surrounding forest turned grey, and the sky went dark, covered in thick lead clouds.
From time to time, living things drifted past in the wind, making strange noises.
Wei He looked up at the Shadow Beast Ruin in front of him.
The pale white door and the stone bat sculptures were nothing special.
But down on the ground beneath his feet.
Wei He narrowed his eyes and stared hard at the exact spot he was standing on.
The ground was not normal black soil, nor was it loose gravel or yellow mud. It was a massive, long spine.
They were standing right on the middle of this spine. The pale bones were covered in a layer of greyish-white skin and flesh.
Perhaps because too much time had passed, the ribs extending from both sides of the spine were connected by layers of dried-out flesh.
This flesh was so dry that it had turned into a semi-transparent material, almost like paper.
Looking through these thin layers, Wei He could see giant black-red bugs, as long as an arm, crawling beneath his feet.
They moved slowly through the gaps between the ribs, wiggling their fat heads and squeezing through the folds of their skin as they burrowed around.
If it were just this, Wei He would not have been so shocked.
What truly shocked him was that between these ribs and giant bugs, there were finely crafted metal spikes neatly hammered into the bone. Simple mechanical gears were also set into the structure, replacing missing pieces of bone.
Following the layout from his feet, Wei He moved his gaze back up toward the entrance door.
Only then did he realize that the door sat right where the head of this massive spine would be.
If the ground beneath them was the main body of a giant, then the door was the giant's head.
Wei He stared at the entrance door.
Behind him, Zhao Xu was still opening and closing his mouth, saying something.
But Wei He had no interest in listening to his noise.
Stepping closer, Wei He looked up at the plaque hanging above the door.
He suddenly realized that it did not look like a normal sign at all. It looked much more like a scanning device from the sci-fi movies in his past life.
A device meant to scan identity.
Sure enough, as he got closer, the strange patterns on the plaque began to shift and move, rearranging themselves.
Soon, after shifting for a short moment, the patterns settled back into their original shape. Everything went still again.
Clearly, after scanning him, Wei He did not meet the requirements to enter.
"Not letting me in?"
Wei He was not surprised at all.
He raised his hand and slammed a single palm right onto the door.
"Stop!" Zhao Xu shouted frantically from behind.
But it was too late.
Wei He's palm landed softly against the pale white door.
Boom!
A low, heavy thud sounded out. The entire door shook violently, and a huge cloud of dust and powder fell from above.
A shallow handprint appeared on the surface of the door.
"Phew..." Zhao Xu let out a breath of relief. Luckily, the door was extremely tough. Even a top master would not be able to destroy it in a short time.
However, when he saw Wei He raise his hand a second time, preparing for another strike, his heart leaped back into his throat.
"Senior Brother Wei!"
His voice was instantly swallowed up by a deafening roar.
BOOM!
This time, Wei He used his full, true power. His complete force gathered on his hand like a massive ball of swirling smoke before he slammed it forward.
The terrifying sphere of power exploded right against the door.
A massive impact hit the door, and for a second, everything seemed frozen in place.
The endless stream of energy rushed into the door like it was sinking into a bottomless pit, completely disappearing.
Then, in a flash.
Crack.
Starting from where Wei He's palm had landed, cracks began to spread out in every direction.
A dense network of cracks, looking like a spiderweb, covered the entire door in the blink of an eye.
Crash!
With a heavy sound, the entire door crumbled into a pile of small stones, scattering all over the floor.
And the creepy, dangerous aura that had been drifting out from the Shadow Beast Ruin vanished completely the moment the door shattered.
In Wei He's eyes, he could see thick, endless black smoke pouring out from the broken doorway at top speed.
Rolling dark smoke surged out from the shattered entrance, rising straight into the sky.
Before long, the smoke thinned out, grew smaller, and completely disappeared.
Zhao Xu and his men watched this scene with wide eyes and numb scalps, not daring to utter a single word.
It was not until they saw Wei He pull his hand back with a satisfied look that they snapped back to reality, trembling all over.
The Shadow Beast Ruin... was actually forced into self-destruction?
A rare ruin that could have brought them massive rewards, a place so rare that only one might be found in a hundred years, was destroyed so easily just like that.
The men from the Wushi Sect felt their mouths go dry and their heads ring. They could not tell if their heads were ringing from the force of Wei He's attack or from the sheer shock of what just happened.
Just thinking about it made them shiver. If the higher-ups in the sect found out about this, the trouble and anger would be unimaginable.
Wei He took one last look around. Seeing that there was nothing else left to do, he picked up the still-dazed Song Shizhen, leaped high into the air, and vanished from the spot.
Since he could not find Shi Heng, he would simply destroy what Shi Heng wanted most.
Anyway, as long as it was something the Wushi Sect wanted or tried to do, he would ruin it whenever he ran into it.
If he were not in a hurry to help his own sect move, he would have stayed here a bit longer to wait for Shi Heng to return so he could beat him up face to face.
Still, this was enough. Losing a Shadow Beast Ruin that held real weight even to the Wushi Sect would be a massive blow to Shi Heng.
Shortly after Wei He left.
A figure suddenly shot out from the nearby forest like a rocket, arriving near the entrance in the blink of an eye.
The figure slowed down, revealing his face.
It was Shi Heng from the Wushi Sect, who had rushed back at top speed after receiving the urgent signal.
When he saw the destroyed door of the ruin and the empty spots where the expensive array plates had been stolen, Shi Heng's vision went dark. He almost passed out right there.
"Where is Wei He?!"
He grabbed Zhao Xu, who still had not recovered, lifting him almost completely off the ground.
"He... he left."
"Useless trash!" Shi Heng roared, throwing a furious punch at the stone bat on the side.
The hard stone bat exploded into tiny dust particles in an instant.
"Wei He! You ruined my good thing and now you run?! You coward who only dares to cause trouble when I am not around, do not let me catch you!"
The anger and humiliation in Shi Heng's heart were bursting out of his chest.
Wei He had taken advantage of his absence to strike, destroying the Shadow Beast Ruin, injuring several real masters of the Wushi Sect, and stealing the expensive array plates.
"Wei He, I will have my revenge against you if it is the last thing I do!"
Shi Heng let out a wild roar and slammed another heavy fist into the ground.
Ripples of shockwaves pushed the dirt and rocks outward, silently digging a deep hole several meters wide into the earth.
