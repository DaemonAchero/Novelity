Meng Jin's cultist girl shivered. Something felt wrong. She looked toward the door.
Wei He stood there, watching her with a calm face.
"Go on. Keep talking." He stared at her, a hint of violence in his eyes.
Both she and Xu Chun trembled. They stood up right away and walked out with their heads down.
If Wei He wasn't here, it would be fine. But with him around, they didn't dare preach so openly.
They originally saw that the Wei family had a little money and sold buns almost at a loss to help people. They thought if they pulled Wei Ying into the cult, they could make a good profit.
They never expected Wei He to return so fast after leaving.
The two women squeezed through a small gap beside Wei He and basically ran away. They vanished around the street corner in seconds.
Wei He stayed silent for a moment, then looked at the nervous Wei Ying.
"Sister, don't meet with Xu Chun again. She's not a good person. She belongs to those night cults."
"I... I understand..." Wei Ying pinched her clothes, feeling lost, like she had done something wrong.
"Alright, I'm going back to train. If anything happens while you're alone, just shout. The fist school and our house are nearby. People will hear you. Got it?" Wei He reminded her.
"Okay, okay." Wei Ying nodded quickly, not knowing what to do.
She had heard her brother mention how evil and dangerous the Incense Cult was. Only now did she realize she was just in danger.
But thinking about it, she still felt Xu Chun didn't seem like a bad person. Xu Chun had even given her a hand towel earlier. Could a bad person be that generous? Why would a bad person give away free things?
"Alright, I'm off." Wei He had actually returned because he smelled the Incense Cult's joss stick scent on that towel while wiping his hands.
These days, most people could barely afford thin porridge. Who had the extra money or time to burn incense all day?
No one was that free except those cult members.
So he kept a sharp eye out. When he left earlier, he didn't go far. He stopped, hid, and watched in secret.
He planned to watch for a bit and go back to train if nothing happened.
He didn't expect those two to target his sister right after he left.
He was afraid his sister was too naive and lacked experience, making her easy to trick.
After driving the two away, Wei He left the shop. He looked back at his sister.
She was still standing at the entrance, watching him from afar.
Wei He waved at her to go back and rest.
He watched her pause, turn around, and start kneading dough with yeast before he finally walked away.
He walked back along the street and soon reached a black door.
He pushed it. It wasn't locked. He paused, then slipped inside through the open door.
Xu Chun and the cult girl stood in the yard, talking in low voices. Next to them sat a ceramic burner with a leftover stick of incense.
That's right, this was Xu Chun's yard, not his.
After fleeing in panic, the two women went straight back to their own yard. They didn't expect the door to open the moment they got back.
They turned quickly to look at the entrance.
Whoosh!
Instead of a person, a huge cloud of white lime dust hit their eyes.
Wei He threw the lime, dashed forward, and dropped between them.
He pulled out a hard wooden stick from his clothes and began beating them blindly.
A dozen breaths later, both women were screaming on the ground, unable to move, covered in bruises.
Wei He threw another handful of lime onto their faces to make sure they couldn't see him. Then he put away the stick, ran into the inner room, took two long strips of dried meat, and left. He even shut the door behind him.
The Incense Cult was strong now. It was best not to mess with them if he could avoid it.
But Xu Chun lived too close to him, and she was trying to trick his sister. He had to act.
So he planned to come beat her up every few days and steal something, pretending to be a starving bandit.
After a few times like this, she would have no choice but to move out.
Besides, with their heads covered in lime, they couldn't see who he was anyway.
He walked out the front door. It was the hottest time of the day.
The street was empty.
Wei He had picked this time precisely because no one was around.
He turned and slipped into his own house next door, closing the door behind him without a sound.
A short while later, a crowd heard the noise at Xu Chun's house and rushed into her yard. Amid a mess of shouting, they carried the two injured women away to find a doctor.
In his own yard, Wei He stripped to his waist and started hitting the sandbag punch by punch.
Soon came a knock on his front door.
His heart moved, but his face stayed calm. He walked over and opened it.
"Who is it?"
A few men and women holding sticks and kitchen knives stood outside. They looked fierce and were about to demand if he had heard or seen what happened next door.
But when the door opened, they saw a solid, muscular man standing inside. His muscles made him look twice as big as their skinny group.
The woman leading them softened her tone immediately.
"Little brother, did you hear someone getting beaten next door just now? Did you see anyone run out?"
"No. Anything else?" Wei He looked annoyed, as if he would hit them if they wasted more of his time.
Scared by his look, they didn't dare speak for a moment.
Bang!
He slammed the door shut.
The crowd outside jumped in surprise, but none of them dared to say a harsh word.
When the leader saw the door open, she had spotted the black belt outfit from the Huishan Fist School drying in the yard.
That was the main reason her tone changed.
The formal black belt disciples from the fist school were all strong fighters. Any single one of them could easily take on five or six regular people.
Plus, these disciples often had ties with rich and powerful families. They were often hired as guard captains or temporary bodyguards.
They were not people to mess with easily.
Before long, the crowd looked at each other, unable to vent their anger. They had to go knock on other doors.
Soon, shouts and noise filled the neighborhood again.
Wei He ignored them, calmed his mind, and went back to training.
After that beating, Wei He didn't see Xu Chun for over ten days.
He felt safer about Wei Ying's side too, and she went back to running the bun shop every day.
Days flowed by like water.
Slowly, food prices in the outer city seemed to settle down. People started using mixed flour made from ground grains and beans as a basic trade item. Barter trade spread everywhere.
Trading mixed flour for white rice, meat, and other goods slowly formed a market.
Wei He noticed that no matter how bad things got outside, the food at the Huishan Fist School remained steady. They gave out the same amount of rice and meat every day.
There was only one new rule: no taking food outside.
Old Zheng still looked calculative and unhurried as usual.
He spent all day lazy in his armchair, chewing on chicken feet. No one knew where he kept getting so many chicken feet.
Most of the winter soon passed.
Wei He's internal blood energy grew stronger, slowly moving toward completion.
It was slow, but steady. Since he already had the Breaking Bead ready, he only needed to push his blood energy to the limit to break through his skin level and take another step forward.
Hope was right ahead. Along with the tiny progress he felt every day, it gave Wei He the same satisfaction as playing a game in his past life.
In the blink of an eye, winter was over. Yet the weather didn't feel cold at all. It was still hot.
This strange weather made the river water level drop by more than half. The river became muddier and muddier.
Food on the market grew scarcer.
The monthly grain and meat Wei He got from the Cheng family began to drop too. Clearly, the Yonghe Escort Agency was facing tough times as well.
Cheng Shaojiu talked to him about it a few times. The agency used to get many jobs, but most were escorting living people away.
And most clients paid with goods instead of cash. Now the jobs were dropping fast, as almost everyone who could leave the outer city had left.
Those left behind either didn't want to leave or couldn't leave. Business became bad.
However, Cheng Shaojiu guaranteed that Wei He's share wouldn't be cut short.
Wei He also told his sister to limit the daily sale of buns and mantou, and to start hoarding grain and meat.
But it seemed every family was doing the same thing. Any food or meat on the market was bought up the moment it appeared.
Later, Wei Ying didn't even dare open her shop. The moment she opened, her entire stock was wiped out in minutes.
Her dream of running a bun shop came to an end.
Every day in Stone Bridge Town, people fell dead in corners and sewers, staying still forever.
Stray dogs disappeared completely, likely beaten to death and eaten.
Splash!
A big tub of muddy river water was poured into an even larger wooden basin.
A thin filtering cloth was placed inside the basin. Two strong women pulled the cloth from both sides, letting the muddy water drip down slowly.
They were filtering the water.
The weather was dry, and the river was getting shallower and dirtier. Without filtering, it couldn't be used at all.
Nearby, another person scooped out the filtered water and poured it into joined bamboo tubes filled with charcoal. The water slid down section by section into another clean basin.
The resulting water was clean.
Wei He picked up a filled basin of water, poured in mixed medicinal powder, stirred it, and added warm water. It became a basin of warm medicine water used for skin toughening.
He picked up the basin and walked outside, returning to his usual spot.
He wasn't in the corner anymore. He was now in the middle section along the left wall.
He wasn't standing next to Cheng Shaojiu either. Instead, he trained beside a newcomer he was guiding.
The newcomer was a strong youth with natural yellowish hair named Ouyang Zhuang.
His family seemed to have some money, so he had been a bit spoiled when he first arrived.
But after getting beaten up a few times in harsh sparring, he stopped acting up.
Before, he didn't even call Wei He "Senior Brother." Now, seeing Wei He from afar, he would hurry over to help carry basins and gear.
Just like now.
When Wei He walked out with the basin, Ouyang Zhuang ran over in quick steps and took it from him.
They walked together toward their training spot.
"Senior Brother Wei, did you know the number of people in the yard has dropped quite a bit lately?" Ouyang Zhuang asked in a low voice.
"Dropped?" Wei He had been focusing entirely on building up his blood energy to prepare for the breakthrough, so he hadn't noticed other changes.
Hearing this from Ouyang Zhuang, he scanned the area. Sure enough, both old members and newcomers were missing in large numbers.
