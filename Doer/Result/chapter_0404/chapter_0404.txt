Pfft.
Wei He pointed with one hand, catching the red line mid-air.
The line snapped back the moment it touched him, zipping away into the dark.
A massive dark shape burst from the shadows. Standing over three meters tall, it looked just like the other pus lizards—only much bigger. This brute was clearly the leader of the pack.
"A whole pack?" Wei He looked the monster over. Seeing it was just a bigger lizard with no special tricks, he lost interest.
He broke off a tree branch, pumped his True Force into it, and whipped it forward.
A sharp whistle split the air. The branch sank into the giant lizard's side in a flash.
Then— BOOM!
A burst of True Force exploded inside its belly.
Blood and shredded meat flew everywhere as Wei He's force flooded the creature's body. Two seconds later, the giant beast slammed to the ground, dead.
Wei He cut out its core and kept moving. He killed four more lizards along the way before finally reaching the beach.
This was his first time seeing a real sea beast invasion.
Under the dark sky, countless pus lizards crawled out of the ocean, covering the shore. Some roared at the sky, some clawed at the sand, and others shook their heads, looking dazed.
Wei He stood at the edge of the woods and looked out. There were dozens of lizards on his stretch of beach alone, and many more as far as the eye could see. Monsters kept pouring out of the dark water without end.
"Impressive," he muttered.
Wei He stepped back into the trees. Instead of fighting, he decided to map out the island.
With his current power, he could easily handle any beast in this light-wind zone. The sheer number of lizards was no threat to him.
After a quick loop, he estimated that at least a thousand monsters had come ashore, wrapping around the whole island.
Deep inside the island, there seemed to be a huge network of tunnels. At the center, Wei He found underground caves of all sizes, likely dug out by different beasts.
He did not go inside right away. Instead, he went back to his camp to rest and stay in his high-sense state, preparing for his Sense Lock. To play it safe, he planned to finish three locks before going into the caves.
Days bled into one another.
Wei He settled down on the island. During this active period, swarms of monsters washed ashore every night, hunting for anything living. Except for trees, they ate whatever meat they could find.
Soon, thirty days had passed.
Whenever Wei He got hungry, he caught fish in the sea. When he was thirsty, he drank the water he brought with him, or bought fresh water from travelers passing by during the day. When pushed, he distilled seawater. It lacked minerals and was bad to drink long-term, but it worked fine for a short stay.
Finishing three Sense Locks was easy for him now. The process went smoothly, and he completed the full month of training without a hitch.
Wei He could feel his vast pool of True Force adapting to a deeper level of energy. As his senses reached into the deeper layer of the True World, the energy there was slowly pulled in, refined, and merged into his body.
Only now was he a true master of the Third Sense Lock.
With his breakthrough done, he turned to his real goal: finding the Dragon Lock Wood.
Over the past month, Wei He had searched every inch of the island. Apart from the ravenous lizards, there was only black trees, dirt, and stone, along with a few dark green plants. None of them were what he needed.
Yet the clues he bought clearly said someone had seen Dragon Lock Wood here.
He packed his gear and walked to the center of the island, stopping before a greenish lake. Next to the water stood a wall of black stone, riddled with caves of different sizes.
These caves were the last place left to check.
Wei He did not hesitate. He had completed his Sense Lock and collected plenty of monster cores over the past month. Those weak lizards were like helpless babies to him. He had already gathered over a hundred pounds of low-grade black star stones without even trying hard.
"The lizards come up to this point, but none of them dare to step inside these caves. Something is definitely down there."
Wei He picked a larger cave, flashed forward, and vanished into the dark.
Inside, it was pitch black. Wei He lit a whale oil lamp and pushed deeper.
At first, the tunnel sloped down gently. But after a hundred meters, it plunged downward at a sharp angle, as if heading straight for the seabed.
Holding his lamp, Wei He sped up, running at over a hundred meters per second.
Dark stone walls blurred past him. Wei He stayed sharp, watching for any sign of danger. While his reaction speed was not as fast as the top masters above the Fifth Step, his reflexes were still far better than standard experts. At this speed, a single mistake meant slamming straight into a stone wall.
He ran for several minutes until the tunnel flattened out, then began to climb steeply, almost like scaling a mountain.
A few more minutes passed. If Wei He were not so used to long, dull training, he would have grown restless by now.
Finally, a faint light appeared ahead.
Wei He picked up his pace and burst through the opening.
SWOOSH!
Outside lay a dark, foggy forest. Tall, straight trees stood like spears driven into the ground, each rising twenty to thirty meters high.
But what truly shocked Wei He was the figure standing right in front of him.
It was a giant, black, human-like creature nearly ten meters tall. It looked like a tree monster from ancient myths, covered in thousands of dark tendrils that dragged along the ground like dirty mops, dripping foul, sticky slime. The creature's entire body was wrapped in these tendrils. Every time it moved, slime flew everywhere.
Wei He swept his eyes across the clearing, and his gaze landed on what lay behind the beast.
Between the trees stood a plant with light-gold bark shaped like a coiling dragon. Its leaves were like heavy golden needles, hard as iron.
It was the Dragon Lock Wood!
Right beneath it grew a white flower with five gold-edged petals. Golden mist floated gently from its center, showing its rare value.
By now, the foul tree monster had spotted Wei He.
It looked down, raised a massive foot, and stomped on him.
BOOM!
The heavy foot smashed down where Wei He had been standing, digging a hole over a meter deep and throwing up dirt and stone.
Wei He dodged to the side and sized up the monster's raw strength.
"Not bad," he thought, keeping a calm face. Without using his secret skills, he might not be able to take this thing head-on. "Time for hit-and-run."
He leaped into the air.
Raising his right hand, he gathered True Force into a gray, cloud-like shape and slammed it onto the monster's head.
BOOM!
The blow hit full-force. Half of Wei He's total force surged into the tree monster, throwing its head back and blasting away chunks of tendrils.
Yet Wei He was surprised. The strike he put half his strength into only forced the beast back a single step. The monster shook its head, having lost only a few vine-like roots from its face.
ROAR!
It let out a thunderous roar and swiped its arms forward.
SWISH! SWISH! SWISH!
A dense rain of sound cut through the air as thousands of tendrils shot out, reaching for Wei He like a massive net.
Wei He pushed his force even higher, no longer holding back.
Dark patterns spread across his skin as his body expanded, growing back to his full three-meter height.
Mid-air, he spun his hands, shaping the force on his arms into sharp blades to slash through the incoming tendrils. Taking advantage of the gap, he kicked off the air and landed back on the ground.
As he fell, he noticed something odd. The wind here was much stronger than on the rest of the island.
Wait... this wind! We are right next to the Strong Wind Zone!
Wei He suddenly realized the truth. No wonder this beast was so brutal—it was likely a monster from the high-risk zone.
Before he could think further, he threw himself to the left.
BOOM!
A cluster of tendrils slammed down, stabbing deep into the dirt where he had just been standing.
"Again!"
Wei He felt his blood boil. He kicked off the ground and leaped back up.
This time, he put all his force and physical power into one strike. He threw a heavy downward punch that carried bits of his old martial art styles.
To Wei He, raw force was everything. Techniques and moves were secondary. As long as your force was strong enough, plentiful enough, and fast enough, a simple punch could unleash terrifying power.
He unleashed his full strength. Dense, dark gray threads of force wrapped around his fist, forming a giant black fist four to five meters wide. Carrying the weight of a mountain, it crashed into the tree monster's head.
KABOOM!
A deafening explosion echoed through the forest. Broken wood and tendrils blasted outward, mixed with scattered bits of dark force.
The tree monster reeled back several steps. Its entire upper body caved in, and half its head and chest were blown to bits. Wei He's force clung to the open wounds, hissing as it corroded the flesh.
Just as Wei He thought it was over, the tree monster planted its feet. A burst of energy exploded from its chest, blowing away Wei He's lingering force.
Then, the scattered pieces of wood and tendrils flew back toward its body, like iron filings drawn to a massive magnet.
Within a second, the monster was fully healed.
ROAR!
It roared in fury and slammed a giant fist into its own chest.
PUFF!
The self-inflicted blow unleashed a thick cloud of dark gray dust. The powder spread at high speed, covering hundreds of meters in an instant.
Wei He was about to rush back in when the dust caught him mid-leap. His body suddenly felt insanely heavy, as if he were carrying ten thousand pounds on his back.
"What is this stuff?!"
Before he could react, two massive wooden hands closed in on him.
They slammed together from both sides like a giant trap.
BOOM!
Raw force clashed against raw force.
Wei He threw his arms wide, desperately shoving against the huge wooden hands to stop them from crushing him.
Now he understood why Master Yuan Duzi always warned him never to enter the Strong Wind Zone carelessly. If every monster in that zone was this absurd, how did the Great Yuan Empire survive back then?
Wait... Great Yuan had Grandmasters in the past, yet disasters were everywhere back then too—droughts, floods, plagues. All of them had traces of beasts behind them. It wasn't that there were no Grandmasters; even the Grandmasters couldn't stop them.
Stuck between two forces, Wei He was slowly getting pushed back. The monster's physical strength was higher than his, and the wooden hands were grinding closer and closer.
"Damn it!"
Wei He realized a normal master could never handle this beast. Their forces were roughly equal, but he was losing in pure physical power.
He made a quick decision.
He activated a tiny pulling force from his spirit. The sudden burst of pull gave him just enough space to slip right out of the trap.
With his spiritual force active, Wei He moved with incredible ease. He vanished and reappeared without momentum, popping up on the left, then the right, landing full-power strikes on the tree monster every time.
Each time the monster was smashed apart, it healed itself and chased after Wei He in a rage, raining down tendrils everywhere.
Heavy explosions echoed endlessly. Trees fell in rows, and the ground was ripped apart into deep craters.
Both fighters were fully locked in. One was nimble and hard to hit; the other had an insane healing factor. The fight turned into a battle of endurance.
At first, Wei He thought the healing must have a limit. But after three straight hours of fighting, his body ached from using so much force, yet the beast healed up once again.
I won't beat this thing unless I find out how it heals, Wei He realized. He had destroyed every part of its body, but it simply reformed every time.
He glanced over at the Dragon Lock Wood. Since he could not kill the monster now, he would draw it away, then sprint back to grab the treasure!
Forming a plan, Wei He leaped up and shattered the monster's left arm with a heavy blow. Using the bounce to jump far away, he landed deep in the woods.
The tree monster was completely blind with rage. After taking a beating for three hours, it was furious despite its ability to heal. It stomped after him with huge strides.
One chased, one fled, crashing through the forest. Trees fell and the ground exploded in their wake as they vanished into the distance.
Right after they left...
The ground exploded in the deep forest nearby. Two figures leaped out and dashed straight for the Dragon Lock Wood and the golden flower.
They had been hiding in the shadows for a long time, drawn by the noise of the fight. Recognizing the monster, they stayed hidden and waited for an opening.
This Undying Tree Monster was a high-tier beast that had recently followed the Strong Wind Zone to these islands. Two powerful masters from the Sea Alliance had been badly injured by it before.
These two hidden fighters had only come to hunt normal beasts, but they stumbled upon this rare chance. The strange man had fought the monster head-on and pulled it away.
It was a free gift from the heavens. Without a second thought, they moved at full speed to snatch the treasures before anyone noticed!
