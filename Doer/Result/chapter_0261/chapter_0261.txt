People always view the world through the lens of their environment.
Take the Hong family right now. Hong Daoyuan might know when to submit, but this whole show was likely a conspiracy hatched by the rest of his clan.
It looks like the Yueyin Sect experts are raiding the Hongs, Wei He thought, looking at the three elders of the cult. But why attack at the exact moment I make my move?
"You think combining forces gives you a chance to win?" Wei He asked.
The lead elder, a man with a goat beard, sneered. "Since you guessed it, there's no need for talk. You should have stayed in Xuanjing and enjoyed your easy life. Coming to Feiye City was your own death wish!"
"Kill him!"
The three elders split, turning into three blurs. They lunged at Wei He from three directions like curved swords or twisting spears, using their palms as the lethal tips.
In the martial world, you could rarely guess someone's true strength until they actually moved.
The moment they struck, the Yueyin Array activated. Three invisible currents of force merged into a single trap, completely sealing off Wei He's escape routes. Because their internal forces fused together, attacking any single elder meant fighting the power of all three at once.
Against three combined foes, a normal fighter would be at a total disadvantage. Anyone else would fall back and look for another opening.
Not Wei He.
His Overlord Force was reinforced by over ten layers of internal energy, making his defensive shroud absurdly thick. He had been looking for a worthy target to test his current limits anyway.
The Mind Sensation realm masters were too dangerous and hard to find. The Viscera Refining masters below him were too weak—even those at the late stage couldn't survive a single casual hit from him.
Looking at the three elders, Wei He's eyes lit up. They might be just right.
He raised his hand and gathered his energy. Without activating any special effects, he simply threw a plain, unadorned palm strike forward.
BANG!
A heavy explosion echoed through the room.
Wei He didn't budge an inch. The three elders, however, bounced off him upon impact. Their faces flushed red as their internal blood surged, and their formation shattered instantly.
"What immense power! Could he be at that level?!" The goat-bearded elder gasped, his face pale.
"No! The energy signature is wrong. He's not there yet!" the second elder spat through grit teeth. "As long as he hasn't reached that level, we can drain him to death! I don't believe the three of us can't outlast him. Besides, the Xiangqu Cult men outside will join us soon. Attack!"
Uniting their minds once more, the three elders formed the array again. Merging their internal force into a unified wave, they swarmed Wei He from different angles.
This time, they didn't hold back. They activated their special energy traits.
The air in the hall vibrated. Threads of distorted force erupted from their palms—a trademark of the cult—wrapping tightly around Wei He. The technique locked him in place, slowing his movements and making it impossible to dodge.
Classic Mind-Disruption style, Wei He thought. Having fought the cult so many times, he knew their tricks inside out.
Unless a martial artist possessed divine natural strength or an immense reserve like his Whale-Hong Art, this twisting force would mangle their movements and make fighting a nightmare.
This was the core strength of the Mind-Disruption Sect. Their base energy could branch into various unique styles. Some focus on distorting a target's moves while slowing them down. Others focus on snapping a fighter's posture at a critical moment.
The three elders were using the slow-down variant, much like the master of the Guiyan Tower had used before.
Too bad for them.
The moment Wei He felt the drag, he triggered his body's blood sacs. His physical strength exploded, easily tearing through the binding effect.
The three elders orbited him like violent gusts of wind, attacking from all angles like zig-zagging lightning. Yet, every single strike was effortlessly batted away by Wei He.
After dozens of exchanges, the elders realized something was wrong. Wei He wasn't taking them seriously at all. He was using them as punching bags to test his moves.
"You arrogant brat! DIE!"
Feeling utterly humiliated, the goat-bearded elder roared as dark purple veins bulged across his skin. He activated a secret technique, causing his speed and power to jump by several notches.
The three of them funneled all their combined energy into his arms, compressing it into his palms before slamming down at Wei He from above.
"DIE!"
With three forces merged and boosted by the secret art, the twisting effect reached its absolute peak. Even Wei He felt his arms lag slightly.
Knowing his normal physical limits now, he stopped holding back.
He raised his hand and used the Heart-Break Seal's cold-blooded variation, packing it with his multi-layered Overlord Force.
His current force contained two main strength-boosting traits: the White Jade technique from the Jiujiang Academy, which doubled his strength, and the Five Elements art, which added a massive flat bonus. Combining both at once, his raw physical output instantly tripled.
This was Wei He's first time measuring his own true combat power from an objective angle.
Now he understood why Hong Daoyuan, a natural-born genius with terrifying raw power, had been flattened by him in a single move.
Without special effects, Wei He was already as strong as three late-stage Viscera masters combined. Once he activated his traits, his strength alone tripled, not to mention his other passive traits like armor-piercing, rotation, and short-burst speed.
His Overlord Force had evolved into a Swiss army knife of destruction. If his former master, Shangguan Ji, were here to see this, his jaw would hit the floor.
The original martial art of the Shangguan family was meant to be flexible, letting the user swap between different force traits depending on the enemy. But you could only use one at a time.
Wei He had forced the arts to stack.
The style was meant for agility and deception, but he had turned it into a monster that combined nine arts into one, giving him the power of nine masters fused together. This was no longer the original art. It was something else entirely.
The elders' full-power strike crashed directly into Wei He's Heart-Break Seal.
The seal itself was just a mid-tier technique, average at best in terms of power gathering or muscle utilization. But backed by his stacked Overlord Force, a heavy, rolling wave of energy smashed into the elders' combined palm.
Two massive forces collided.
BOOM!
The three elders flew backward, smashing three massive holes through the thick tower wall behind them. The brick structure collapsed, and the three tumbled out onto the outer plaza, coughing up blood, their faces dead pale.
Wei He stepped back twice to steady himself.
Combined energy still loses to pure, single-body power, he noted. The three elders had pooled their strength with a secret technique, but friction between their different energies created internal drain. Their total power wasn't a three, but maybe a two-and-a-half.
Wei He was different. His force pushed forward like ocean waves, fully integrated inside his body without energy loss.
The result was clear.
"Three late-stage masters at their absolute limit, using a secret art to combine their power... and they are still a bit weaker than me. My current limit is probably equal to four Viscera Refining masters fighting as one."
He calculated his stance. Still, he couldn't be sure if he could fight a Mind Sensation master. Their energy operated on a living, qualitative level—it was a change in nature, not just volume. Without fighting a real one, he couldn't gauge his chances.
"Kill him! That's the demon from the Ten Thousand Poison Sect! Watch out for poison!" a voice shouted from outside the tower.
BANG! BANG! BANG!
The windows around the hall shattered simultaneously. Dozens of masked figures leaped inside, wielding curved blades as they charged at Wei He. Over thirty men flooded the hall in seconds.
What surprised Wei He was their eyes. Every single assassin shared the exact same gaze: pure, unhinged madness and murder.
"KILL!" they screamed, hacking down at him.
"Just low-level thugs," Wei He muttered.
He stepped forward, his body turning into a lightning-fast blur as he zig-zagged through the crowd.
When he reappeared, he was already standing outside the hall's main doors.
Behind him, the masked men froze in place, then collapsed to the ground one by one. Every single one had a bloody finger-hole pierced directly through their heart.
He had killed over thirty men in a matter of seconds using pure finger pressure.
Before he could take in the outside view, a thick metallic net dropped toward his head from above. At the same time, over ten poison sprayers fired a cloud of liquid toward him from behind the net.
"Boring. Always the same tricks."
Wei He ducked back to avoid the net and liquid, then burst forward through a gap in the spray. Distorted energy surged around his body as he breached their line.
He didn't even bother to swing his fists. He simply took a casual walk through the crowd.
A fine mist of lethal poison powder and toxic energy spread naturally from his body. The masked assassins instantly clutched their throats and dropped like flies.
A few stronger ones managed to endure the poison and landed strikes on Wei He's back, but they couldn't even dent his energy shield.
His defense had held against three top-tier elders using a combined secret technique. These low-level cult members didn't stand a chance.
"Retreat!"
The cult members fled as fast as they had arrived, realizing their attacks were useless.
Within seconds, the open ground outside the tower was littered with motionless bodies.
