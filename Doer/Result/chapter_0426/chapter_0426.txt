Gongsun Shan watched the three Xuanmiao Sect members receiving congratulations from the surrounding Masters. Thinking of his own Wushi Sect's lack of young talent, his heart turned sour.
This was a genius that should have belonged to the Wushi Sect. Yet, the talent raised by their own branch was thrown away and given to the Xuanmiao Sect instead.
To make matters worse, the Wushi Sect now had to beg the Xuanmiao Sect for help.
Facing all this, Gongsun Shan could only feel bitter. There was nothing he could do.
His heart burned with anger, and a dark glare flashed in his eyes. He swore to himself that once he returned, he would investigate what happened back then. A top genius was lost all because some greedy disciples broke the rules and showed no mercy.
He wanted to go back right now and kill everyone involved!
Yet, he still had to force a fake smile and join the crowd to congratulate the three Xuanmiao Sect Masters.
Twin Eyes Islands.
On the island guarded by the Xuanmiao Sect.
The star formation was wide open. Daoist Cai Menghuan, Wei He, and Hong Chan—who had just been saved—sat together in front of Ancestor Xiao Ling for questioning.
The beast tide was still going on, though it was much weaker than at the start.
The others were stationed outside. Working with the star formation, holding out for a short while was not a big problem.
The main issue was that this incident was too shocking.
Xiao Ling had no choice but to call all three team leaders to question them face-to-face.
"So, you are saying Cai Menghuan took the main attack, Wei He did a sneak attack, and together you forcefully killed the heavily injured Thousand-Face Demon Lord?"
Xiao Ling stared at Cai Menghuan and Wei He with a solemn face.
He knew Cai Menghuan’s true strength. He was a Daoist successor who had survived a sneak attack by the Thousand-Face Demon Lord before. Just staying alive back then proved how strong he was.
Though he was only at the Three-Step Quanzhen level, his real power had reached the absolute peak of Quanzhen.
When he used all his hidden cards, he was no weaker than the peak Buddha Masters of the Guangci Sect.
He truly deserved his title as the Xuanmiao Sect Daoist successor.
With him tanking at the front against a heavily injured Thousand-Face Demon Lord, holding on for a short time was not a problem.
Xiao Ling believed they could survive against the demon lord together.
But killing him on the spot?
That was...
Xiao Ling understood Cai Menghuan, but he did not know Wei He well.
So his gaze fell mostly on Wei He.
"You..." Xiao Ling was curious. What kind of trump card allowed Wei He to take a Master's counterattack head-on?
However, asking such a thing directly was inappropriate. Everyone had their secrets.
For Wei He to fight way above his level and help Cai Menghuan kill a Master showed his hidden secrets were massive.
Adding his secret status of being valued by Yuan Duzi, Xiao Ling pushed down his questions and smiled.
"No matter what, you accomplished something unheard of. Teaming up to kill a Master... I have already sent news back to the sect using the Golden Falcon.
After today, you two will be famous across the world. Be ready for what comes next."
"I would rather not have this kind of fame," Cai Menghuan said with a bitter smile.
Wei He said nothing. Fame meant nothing to him. He only cared about hard training and getting stronger.
Unless fame brought real benefits, he did not care at all.
Speaking of which, he had previously sealed the Thousand-Face Demon Lord and turned him into a ball of flesh.
The mental energy feedback from that single act had fueled his Fifth Layer Mysterious Lock Technique at an insane speed.
Now it was more than half complete. That single fight saved him five to six years of painful effort.
Based on his past progress, the mental energy from one Master was equal to sealing hundreds of Quanzhen-level beasts!
At this rate, in two more years, he would hit the peak of the fifth layer and finish it completely.
After the Fifth Layer Mind Sensing came the true Quanzhen level.
He was finally reaching this barrier. Soon, he would enter the realm of true experts among the Realized Masters.
And his actual combat power was already far beyond ordinary Quanzhen fighters.
"Since you three are heavily injured and a large beast tide is still coming, we are on our own. All nearby shipping routes are cut off.
I have some powerful healing pills here. Take them. Hold out until the tide ends, and the sect will reward you well," Xiao Ling said with a smile.
Looking at Cai Menghuan and Wei He now, their value was completely different from before.
Especially Wei He.
Everyone thought he would peak at low-level Quanzhen. Yet the light he shone now was blinding. No one even cared about his future limits anymore.
Even if Wei He stayed at his current strength forever, he was already a key pillar for the Xuanmiao Sect.
Not to mention how high he might reach if he actually broke into Quanzhen.
As for the last person, Hong Chan, she had been knocked out and thrown into a corner by the Thousand-Face Demon Lord. He had taken her form to ambush the two, but failed.
She was still paralyzed by the demon lord's residual poison and could not fight at all.
So the island's defense fell entirely on Cai Menghuan and Wei He.
Cai Menghuan had dragon blood in his veins, making poison useless against him.
Wei He had his core techniques and was a master poison maker himself, so he feared nothing.
After taking the healing medicine, both felt much better.
Xiao Ling nagged them with endless warnings before finally letting them leave.
If the beast tide had not drained their forces, he would never have let the two out of his sight.
The two left the central stone house and walked onto the open ground outside.
Right as they were about to part ways and return to their own defense zones, Cai Menghuan suddenly asked a question.
"Junior Brother Wei, do you know about Weiyuan?"
"Weiyuan?" Wei He stopped, not knowing what the name meant.
"My bad. If you haven't heard of it, let's talk about it another time," Cai Menghuan replied seriously.
"Alright," Wei He nodded.
From Cai Menghuan's tone, this "Weiyuan" seemed to be a very important topic.
"The Great Yue Dynasty grows stronger every day. Soon, we will have to support the next generation. Please look out for me in the future, Junior Brother Wei," Cai Menghuan said gravely.
Wei He nodded and watched him walk away.
It seemed even the Daoist successor felt the heavy pressure of the coming storm.
The Great Yue Dynasty had created Blood Weapons. Their national power was soaring, and their experts were growing stronger.
The pressure on the Xuanmiao Sect was getting heavier by the day.
Right now, the Xuanmiao Sect relied almost entirely on Senior Sister Yuan Duzi alone.
If she showed even a tiny sign of weakness, the Great Yue Dynasty would strike down hard like lightning.
Wei He understood this clearly. He, the Wei family, and the Sect of Ten Thousand Poisons were now completely bound to the Xuanmiao Sect.
If the Xuanmiao Sect fell, his family would be dragged down and destroyed with it.
'As expected, using every single second to gain power is the only true way to survive in this world.' At that moment, his drive to grow stronger grew even firmer.
Yet the Great Yue Dynasty had countless experts and many Masters. How strong did he need to be to stay safe and hold the line?
A clear answer formed in his mind.
He needed to become as strong as Senior Sister Yuan Duzi—or even surpass her!
Only then could he stabilize everything and keep his family, masters, and friends safe in this world.
Still, Wei He felt a trace of worry. Given his natural talent, he would peak at low-level Quanzhen and get stuck forever. After that, even with the Breakbound Bead, it would be useless. The bead broke through bottlenecks, but if he could not make any daily progress at all, he would never even reach a bottleneck.
What should he do then?
'Looks like it's time to find a way to improve my natural talent.' Pushing those thoughts aside, Wei He walked quickly toward the zone guarded by his Lock Mountain branch.
The sky was dark and gloomy at noon, looking like it was about to pour rain.
Along the shoreline, swarms of white crabs as tall as a person waved giant claws, rushing frantically at the guarding disciples.
These crabs were covered in Truemeat Qi. Even ordinary Realized Masters without Mind Sensing could deal with them easily.
The star cores they dropped were just tiny black rocks.
Wei He did not bother to attack. He sat at the back to oversee the defense.
He thought about his future road and direction.
He knew about the Martial Scriptures. Great Yue was completely banning martial artists who used True Qi.
Driven by Blood Weapons, Trueblood warriors had a massive advantage over True Qi warriors.
While Trueblood required strong bloodlines, True Qi required natural talent. Both systems had strict requirements.
Yet, while anyone could try to train in True Qi, the resources and comprehension needed along the way were far harsher than Trueblood.
Looking at the sea boiling with giant white crabs, Wei He suddenly felt a sense of aimlessness.
Ever since coming to this world, he had fought his way up through endless hardship and faced countless enemies.
Reaching his current level, he suddenly lost his drive.
Great Yue might threaten the Xuanmiao Sect in the future, but Senior Sister Yuan Duzi was still holding everything down. Things were safe for now.
Wei He could not feel any crushing pressure right in front of him, nor did he need more strength for daily life.
His current power was already enough to protect his family for decades to come.
A strange wave of loneliness hit him. He did not know what to do next.
Because of his continuous training, his wife, child, and parents were drifting further and further away from him.
As for the rest of the world, he had no deep ties to anyone else.
What did he want to do? What could he do? What would he become in the future?
A series of questions popped into his mind.
He suddenly understood why so many Realized Masters in the sect went out to explore the True Realm and seek deeper truths.
Perhaps compared to this small world, the vast and endless realm outside—packed with endless secrets—was what they truly wanted to understand.
Pfft!
Suddenly, a Lock Mountain disciple nearby was caught off guard. A grayish-white line pierced his chest, blowing half his body into bloody mist.
The white line looped in the air and darted straight at Wei He!
"Sky-Turning Ray!?" Wei He recognized it instantly.
It was the same ray beast he and Cai Menghuan had blocked days ago.
The messy thoughts in his mind vanished instantly. His focus snapped sharp. He waved a hand, sending five Gravity Pythons swirling around him.
A small Gravity Net formed in an instant.
Just as the ray crashed toward him, the gravity net hit its body, slamming it down and slashing its speed.
'No matter what, I'll break through to the peak of Mind Sensing first!' Wei He's heart settled, and he locked his focus on fighting the Sky-Turning Ray.
