Feeling her visitor rush into the distance, Yuan Duzi closed her eyes and fell into thought.
She had only agreed to meet with the Tide people for one reason. They had brought her a gift she simply could not turn down.
For nearly forty years, her cultivation had been stuck. True Qi had reached a dead end in her body. Whether as a Grandmaster or at the limit of her True Body, her physical frame was completely maxed out. It could hold no more power.
Now, Tide had offered her a new path forward.
There was a catch. To make this breakthrough, she would be left extremely weak for a while. In a tense time like this, she had to wait for the right moment.
Clang... Clang... Clang...
The deep, heavy chime of a bell echoed across the island.
Outside the cave, a quick rustling sound filled the air as robes snapped in the wind. A huge wave of True Disciples were hurrying off to their morning class.
Xuanmiao Sect had grown into the single largest sect in Yuanxi. Up in front was the Haining Alliance, but that was a coalition of many factions, not a single brotherhood.
Deep in his cave, Wei He sat cross-legged. He slowly pulled himself out of his trance.
In just a few short hours of deep meditation, he had effortlessly broken through the fifth layer of the Xuan Lock Technique.
He had hit the fifth level of Sense Lock.
This step did not bring a massive physical change, but it was the bedrock for everything to come. It was the crucial key to reaching Full True later on.
To complete Full True, a warrior needed ultimate perception across all their senses.
Wei He had already unlocked Touch, Sight, and Hearing.
After absorbing his second heart, the blood of the True Beast worked its magic. Unlocking his Smell and Taste went shockingly smooth. He just needed a little time to adapt, and his foundation would be completely set.
Knock. Knock. Knock.
Someone tapped lightly on the heavy stone door.
"Master, the time you set has arrived."
"Is there news from Senior Brother Cai?" Wei He asked, his voice low.
"Yes," Lou Shenyue replied quietly from outside.
"Good."
Wei He stood up slowly. "Tell the Wei Manor that I am stepping out. I do not know when I will return."
"Understood." Ever since her past injuries, Lou Shenyue had become far more grounded and calm.
Wei He packed a few crucial traveling items and walked over to the entrance.
Rumble.
The massive boulder rolled to the left, letting warm, bright sunlight spill across his face.
This journey back to the Great Yue Empire would be his very first trip across the ocean in all these years. He was going to rescue the remnants of the True Qi sects, and if time allowed, maybe he would drop in on a few old friends.
"Anything else?" Wei He glanced at Lou Shenyue.
"Yes," she said, keeping her head lowered respectfully. "In a few days, your disciples face their entry trials for the sect."
"Run it strictly by the book," Wei He said. "Do not give them an easy pass just because they learn under me."
"Understood." Beside serving as Wei He's guard, Lou Shenyue also worked as an inspector for the Moral Hall. She held real power over accepting outside warriors into the sect, which was why she asked.
After Wei He gave a few more instructions, the bell down the mountain rang twice more. He looked up at the sky, adjusted his gear, and set off.
He walked up to the dark cliff behind the mountain.
Cai Menghuan was already waiting. The man had covered himself in shiny trinkets and tokens gifted by various women. He looked less like a warrior on a deadly mission and more like a rich young man out for a casual picnic.
"That is all you are bringing?" Cai Menghuan stared at the small bundle slung across Wei He’s back, then glanced down at the two massive wooden crates sitting on the ground beside his own feet.
"You brought all that? Are you moving your entire house?" Wei He said, completely speechless.
"Well... I brought some gifts for my little sisters back in Great Yue. We haven't seen each other in so long," Cai Menghuan said with a helpless shrug.
How many little sisters do you even have? Wei He cursed silently in his mind. Why do you have 'sisters' everywhere?
Wei He honestly believed that the only reason this guy had not been beaten to death yet was his raw, terrifying talent and strength. Anyone weaker living like this would have been chopped into pieces long ago.
"Don't look at me like that. They really are just sisters," Cai Menghuan quickly blurted out.
"With the way you act, even if they are just sisters, you are going to get killed one of these days," Wei He said coldly.
"Ah..." Cai Menghuan had no answer to that.
They waited a moment longer. Soon, a figure in a black dress glided down from the high rocks above, landing without making a single sound.
It was Yuan Duzi.
She wore a sleek black outfit with long sleeves and trousers. Her long, dark hair fell over her shoulders, and a dark blindfold still covered her eyes.
"Are you ready?" Yuan Duzi asked, her blindfolded gaze sweeping over them.
"Yes!" the two men answered at once.
"Speed is everything this time," Yuan Duzi said. "We are only taking a tiny group of top fighters to pull off the rescue. Great Yue won't be able to predict our movement today, so the overall risk is low. Still, if you run into real trouble, crush this."
She flicked her wrist. Two dark, withered twigs fell into their hands.
Wei He had used one before. Snapping it would summon their First Senior Sister instantly.
"I might not trust everyone in the sect, but the two of you can handle this mission. As long as you don't run into the absolute top-tier powerhouses, you will be fine."
Yuan Duzi paired them up because they had worked together to slay the Thousand-Faced Demon King. One was raw defense, the other was lethal offense. Their synergy was seamless. Even against a full Grandmaster, they would not fall instantly.
As long as they could buy a little time, she could step in and save them.
Together, their power sat at the absolute peak below the Grandmaster level. Taking them along was like bringing half a Grandmaster.
With herself and Ancestor Yan Wujiu striking at the same time, this rescue mission would go off without a single hitch.
"We understand," Wei He and Cai Menghuan replied together.
"Good. Let us move," Yuan Duzi nodded. "This time, we do not take a ship."
Before they could even wonder what she meant, Yuan Duzi raised her hand.
WOOSH!
A crushing gust of wind blasted down onto their heads.
Three giant white birds with wingspans several meters wide swooped down. They circled directly over the group, their heavy wings churning the air.
The birds had bright red crowns, pure white feathers, and long, thin legs. They looked almost like legendary cranes. Yet, their bodies were far too massive, and their beaks were lined with rows of razor-sharp teeth. They were clearly deadly beasts.
"Up," Yuan Duzi said, leaping lightly onto the back of one giant crane.
Cai Menghuan and Wei He jumped up too, landing smoothly on the remaining two beasts.
Cai Menghuan looked back at his huge crates left on the ground, his face twisting in pain. "If I knew we weren't taking a boat, I wouldn't have packed so much..."
"Did you honestly think we were going on vacation?" Wei He sent his voice straight into Cai Menghuan's ear with True Qi.
The three cranes spun upward, slicing through a layer of cold, damp mist.
In seconds, they soared high above the entire island.
Looking down from the sky, Jujun Island looked like a giant round cake that someone had taken a few big bites out of.
Wei He took a deep breath, his heart beating with fresh excitement. This was his first time flying this high since coming to this world.
The crane beneath him was cruising at an altitude of several thousand meters.
Under the bright blue sky, the three giant cranes cut through the air, speeding toward the distant mainland of Great Yue.
Freezing winds whipped against Wei He's body, bending his protective True Qi layer out of shape. The cranes moved at a terrifying speed. They were not breaking the sound barrier, but they were easily covering over two hundred meters every second, matching Wei He's maximum sprinting speed.
Wei He stared straight down.
In the blink of an eye, Xuanmiao Sect shrank into the distance. Below them, the land vanished, replaced by a boundless, deep blue ocean.
A few ships crawled across the water like tiny black ants.
"This is your first time climbing to such an altitude," Yuan Duzi's voice echoed back from the front crane.
"Now, open your senses and step into the True World."
Wei He and Cai Menghuan traded glances. They knew their Senior Sister would never say something like this without reason.
Taking a deep breath, they closed their eyes, shifting their perception into super-sense.
Flash.
Both opened their eyes at the exact same moment.
The blue sea below had completely vanished. In its place stretched a ocean of lead-grey ash.
A thick layer of living grey powder covered the surface of the dead water. It squirmed, boiled, and twitched, like endless layers of insects crawling over one another.
High up in the sky, countless tiny glowing specks floated down like soap bubbles, tossed around by the howling wind.
Those specks were falling from somewhere even higher up.
Wei He tilted his head back, searching for where those specks were coming from.
The moment he raised his eyes, Yuan Duzi's voice slammed straight into his head.
"Watch out!"
Directly ahead of them, a gigantic creature over a hundred meters long was slithering straight toward them through the air.
It looked like a massive sea serpent. Its body was thick, rough, and dark grey, covered in heavy rock armor. Color patches of grass and strange flowers grew out of the stone cracks on its back.
The serpent had golden spiral vortexes inside its eyes. Thick, sticky liquid dripped constantly from its gaping jaw.
"It is a Sky Beast. Ignore it. As long as I am here, it cannot harm you," Yuan Duzi said calmly.
Sky Beasts... Wei He had read brief mentions of these creatures in old texts.
They grew to a massive size, but they were stupid and surprisingly weak. More importantly, they were completely worthless.
When a Sky Beast died, its body dissolved into useless gas. It carried no core inside. They rarely attacked living things on their own, floating through the sky like a living natural disaster.
"Sky Beasts only live in the high clouds, and you cannot spot them from a distance," Yuan Duzi warned. "This one is small. If you run into a truly massive one, its back might carry ancient ruins or lethal monsters. If you ever run into one on your own in the future, be extremely careful."
"Yes!" both men answered.
This trip was opening their eyes to things they had never dreamed of. Even detailed sect scrolls rarely contained information about creatures like this.
Yuan Duzi was personally teaching them secrets that ordinary True Disciples would never learn. She was treating them as her true successors.
"Sky Beast ruins are far more dangerous than anything you find underground," Yuan Duzi added. "When Grandmasters vanish for months at a time, most of them are out exploring Sky Beast ruins. If you ever want to try it yourselves, wait until your cultivation is far higher."
