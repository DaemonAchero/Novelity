At that moment, seeing Wei He’s calm face nearby, Jiang Su felt a sudden surge of random anger in her heart.
‘Putting on a show! Just a lucky kid whose path in life has barely begun. Do you really think you can run on luck forever and keep acting like a genius? Sooner or later, your true colors will show.’
She couldn’t fully explain what she was feeling, but it was like taking a bite of porridge and finding a dead rat inside. The sheer disgust in her chest was beyond words.
Zhao Hong cared the least. In his eyes, looking at the time Wei He took, he probably just got lucky this time too.
After all, this guy struggled hard just to break through to the Leather Skin level before, so this time was just good luck.
Maybe it was because Master Zheng was stressed and in a terrible mood over the news about Xiao Ran.
Wei He’s breakthrough just happened to bring a little good news, so the teacher took a slightly higher view of him.
Besides, given Zhao Hong's nature, he didn't care much about these things anyway. Whether others broke through or not made no difference to him. It wasn't him making the breakthrough.
Zheng Fugui was only bringing it up in passing. If he could bring two people closer together, great. If not, it didn't matter.
Seeing that neither of them wanted to, he dropped the topic.
"Spread the news. Having another good junior brother means you can look out for each other in the future. Don't let him get taken advantage of."
"Yes," Cheng Shaojiu quickly spoke up. He was genuinely happy for Wei He.
Just as he spoke, he noticed Jiang Yan was actually agreeing at the exact same time.
His face changed slightly, and he quickly added, "Master, don't worry. My Cheng family will definitely treat junior brother well!"
Jiang Yan turned his head and gave him a faint smile.
"Third Senior Brother, I heard your family has been having some trouble lately. In terms of compensation, you might fall a bit short, right?"
"No need for Junior Brother Jiang to worry. No matter how bad things get, we can still guarantee Brother Wei's compensation," Cheng Shaojiu said in a deep voice.
Jiang Yan's tone gave him a bad feeling.
Jiang Yan smiled and said nothing more.
If his Jiang family offered a price, how could a small Yonghe Escort Agency in the Outer City compare?
His Jiang family didn't lack an elite who had achieved a second blood-gas breakthrough, but looking at Master Zheng, he seemed to value Wei He quite a lot.
In that case, recruiting Wei He to win Master Zheng's favor was undoubtedly a very good deal.
"Alright, everyone leave. Remember to go outside the city less often lately," Zheng Fugui warned, waving his hand for everyone to step out.
"Yes," several people quickly bowed and filed out of the room one after another.
The sunlight in the courtyard was burning hot. The light rain from earlier was completely gone, evaporated in a flash by the sun.
The ground was covered in dry, rising dust.
Stepping out of the room, Jiang Su didn't even look at Wei He and the others. She strode away and left the courtyard in a flash without looking back.
She was clearly still furious.
Jiang Yan fanned himself with a folding fan, smiled slightly at Wei He, and turned to leave.
Zhao Hong said a few words of encouragement to Wei He. His attitude was no different from usual.
It seemed that to him, whether Wei He broke through or not didn't matter at all.
Cheng Shaojiu patted Wei He, and the two walked out of the courtyard and got into the carriage outside.
"Come on, let's go have a drink! You kid! When did you break through? Why didn't you tell me?" Cheng Shaojiu finally smiled after sitting inside the carriage.
"I just broke through too, so I came to Master Zheng first," Wei He answered with a small laugh.
"Today I'm taking you to try the September Ninth aged wine from Zuihua Tower! Consider it a celebration for you. Haha, with this, our influence as brothers is slowly growing.
If we keep going like this, who knows, maybe our names will actually mean something in Feiye City in the future."
Cheng Shaojiu was clearly in a very good mood.
Wei He was the godfather to his daughter, and Wei He was also his closest friend. So this proved that he, Cheng Shaojiu, had great taste when making friends and making brothers.
When he brought this news back to his uncle, it would slap him right in the face and swell up his ego.
Didn't they say he only hung around with useless friends? How about now? One of his brothers had reached the second blood-gas level!
How many people in the entire escort agency had reached the second blood-gas level? Just him, his father, and his uncle.
Let's see who dares to talk trash about him now!
Cheng Shaojiu felt high in spirits, as if all the built-up frustration and annoyance from before vanished with Wei He's breakthrough.
Sitting on the seat opposite him, Wei He chuckled in his heart too.
This Brother Cheng was already a father of four, yet he was still so unrefined.
Soon, the carriage slowly drove into the Inner City and stopped in front of Zuihua Tower.
Sitting in the carriage, Cheng Shaojiu sent a servant to book a table. Upon learning they had to wait in line, they simply stayed in the carriage to wait.
The carriage slowly moved into a parking spot.
Cheng Shaojiu was full of emotion.
"In Feiye City, the places where you can truly enjoy life are all in the Inner City.
And non-members of the Seven-Family Alliance are not allowed to live in the Inner City. Even those coming in to do business must apply for a spot and qualification.
Also, merchants and traders cannot stay in the Inner City long term. They can only stay for a short time.
The rules are so strict it's hard to imagine.
But it is precisely these rules that allow the Seven-Family Alliance to know the population of the Inner City like the back of their hand.
Every single person who enters the Inner City is crystal clear to them. They can find their background and who brought them in at any time, anywhere.
Because of this, the safety of the Inner City is greatly improved."
He pointed to Zuihua Tower off to the side.
"Take this Zuihua Tower for example. If it opened in the Outer City, it would be bled dry by various gangs in a moment. It could never reach this level of prosperity.
But opening in the Inner City, under absolute security, compared to the living day-to-day survival of the Outer City, tell me, would rich people prefer to spend their money outside or here?"
"Here, of course," Wei He understood instantly.
"That's the truth," Cheng Shaojiu sighed. "This is also the root of why the Seven-Family Alliance has ruled Feiye City for so many years. Just this Inner and Outer City system alone has made them an unknown fortune. Truly..."
He paused.
"Truly what?"
"Truly makes a man jealous."
"..." Wei He was speechless. He thought this guy was about to say something shocking.
"Let's go, let's head down and look at the view around here. You haven't taken a close look at the surroundings since you got here, right?"
Cheng Shaojiu suddenly remembered and called Wei He down from the carriage.
The two came down together and walked around Zuihua Tower.
The streets on both sides were completely filled with all kinds of shops and stores.
Jewelry, antiques, dried goods, clothing, snacks, they had everything.
The crowd moved in waves, and everyone was dressed neatly at the very least. At a glance, they were clearly not poor people.
This level of prosperity made it hard for Wei He to believe that this was the same place as the Feiye City in the Outer City.
In the Outer City, even the most prosperous parts of Shiqiao Town and Hongshi Town were not as lively as this place.
In those places, you could still see beggars and disabled people. In some corners, you could even see dead bodies that hadn't been dragged away in time.
"These people... what kind of people come into the Inner City?" Wei He couldn't help asking.
Did Feiye City have this many rich people?
He looked at the people walking out of jewelry shops and medicine shops carrying large and small bags, feeling deeply confused in his heart.
"They aren't all from the Outer City of Feiye City. The Outer City is only a part of it. Most come from nearby rich family strongholds. You know about Hong Family Fortress, but do you know that Feiye City doesn't just have one Hong Family Fortress?
Outside the city, there are about a dozen small fortresses built by various rich families. Some rely on Hong Family Fortress, while others are members of the Seven-Family Alliance."
"I see," Wei He understood.
"Heh, every single fortress is like an independent small town, filled with members of the same clan protecting the surrounding fields.
Compared to them, those towns in the Outer City of Feiye City might not even live as well as they do," Cheng Shaojiu shook his head and said.
As they were talking, two scholars he knew passed by and greeted him.
He quickly smiled and greeted them back.
Wei He looked left and right at the surroundings.
He hadn't noticed before, but looking closely now, he found that the guards patrolling in the corners and the sturdy men wearing clothes with the Seven-Family Alliance mark seemed much more numerous than before.
These people all had sharp, vigilant eyes scanning the area.
Wei He glanced at them and quickly moved his eyes away to avoid causing any misunderstanding.
On the other side, after Cheng Shaojiu finished greeting the people, the servant waiting in line ran over to report that Zuihua Tower had a table ready.
Only then did the two walk back.
Zuihua Tower was different from Baihua Tower. This was a proper place to drink wine and eat real dishes.
The biggest difference was not that there were no pretty young girls serving, but in the quality of the food.
The moment they sat down, Cheng Shaojiu smoothly ordered a whole list of dish names and asked for a pot of good wine called September Ninth.
"Today is to celebrate your successful breakthrough! It's rare for us to come here, so let's have a drink together!" Cheng Shaojiu smiled and lightly tapped the table.
"Just one cup will do," Wei He smiled back.
"Alright, alright, I know you're careful and afraid of affecting your training," Cheng Shaojiu laughed.
While waiting for the food, he talked about the main features of the Inner City.
Good food, good wine, beautiful women, and sweet dreams.
"I can understand the first three, but what is the last one, sweet dreams?" Wei He asked out of curiosity.
"Sweet dreams means the gambling house, the Sweet Dream Hall. It means a place where dreams come true. Legend says someone went in and made a massive fortune overnight, living a glorious life!" Cheng Shaojiu laughed. "Of course, just listen to those stories and don't take them seriously."
Soon, dishes came up one by one.
Small roasted lamb chops, river fish and mushroom soup, twice-cooked pork with green and red peppers, braised rabbit meat, and five-color shredded wood ear fungus salad.
Four dishes and one soup, each portion served in a large bowl.
The rice was fragrant pearl rice sprinkled with black sesame and crushed peanuts.
The rice was as white as snow and gave off a rich aroma.
Wei He looked at the line of food in front of him, recalling the people in the Outer City who starved to death in the gutters and were dragged away.
He suddenly had a strange feeling of confusion, as if everything he saw before was just an illusion.
"Help yourself!" Cheng Shaojiu sat opposite him and pointed his hand. "I'm getting a taste of the good life thanks to you today. This meal doesn't come cheap!"
"Please!"
Wei He picked up his chopsticks, grabbed a piece of twice-cooked meat, and put it into his mouth.
The meat was extremely chewy, rich, and delicious. Surprisinly, thick juice even seeped out from inside the meat.
"This isn't pork?" Wei He guessed. He couldn't taste any flavor of pork at all.
"Of course not. This is wild game, the meat of a deer called the Branch Deer. It's a great tonic," Cheng Shaojiu gave a look that every man understood.
"Uh..." Wei He indicated that he was still just a kid. "Is this Branch Deer the kind of deer with tree branches growing on its head?"
"More than that. The Branch Deer has a strong body, usually as big as a domestic ox. It has sharp teeth in its mouth and a violent temper, often fighting lions and tigers. It's one of the few fierce beasts."
Cheng Shaojiu laughed and said, "Just eat. This meat is much stronger than silver-kiss black snake meat, and it has special effects. It's the best thing for growing your blood-gas."
