Cheng Shaojiu went all out from the very first move, using every bit of his Stone Hide power.
His blood surged, making his muscles swell slightly. His fists turned a chalky gray-white. He moved as fast as the wind, snapping his arms forward like loaded crossbows.
Bang! Bang!
Wei He blocked both strikes.
Wei He was a master of surprise attacks himself, so he knew the signs all too well. Seeing Cheng Shaojiu’s overly aggressive posture, he sensed a feint and caught the real target: a sharp strike aimed at his lower belly.
After pushing Cheng Shaojiu’s arms aside, Wei He resisted the urge to throw a handful of blinding lime powder. Instead, he drove his knee straight up.
Cheng Shaojiu was wide open after having his guard broken. It was too tempting. But remembering this was just a friendly spar, Wei He forced himself to hold back and focus on normal combat.
He pumped his own blood power, and his fists turned a faint gray-white as well.
Under the moonlight, the two clashed violently. A battle between two Stone Hide fighters was far beyond what normal people could handle. The impact of their fists sounded like heavy drums. Sand and dust kicked up around them, hiding their figures in a swirling cloud.
Cheng Shaojiu grew more alarmed as the fight went on.
Not long ago, he could easily suppress Wei He. But now, he could not overwhelm him with raw blood power, and his experience was barely enough to keep up with Wei He’s counterattacks.
Cheng Shaojiu had trained in martial arts for years longer than Wei He, yet the gap between them had narrowed to almost nothing.
Dull thuds rang out endlessly across the training ground. Cheng Shaojiu shouted with every blow, while Wei He remained completely silent. Their fighting styles were total opposites.
Soon, a heavy thud echoed out.
Wei He stumbled backward out of the dust cloud. He took over ten steps back before finally stopping himself. Gasping for air, he looked down at his chest, where a gray fist mark was stamped on his clothes.
"As expected of you, Brother Cheng. I lost."
The dust settled, revealing Cheng Shaojiu standing in his original spot with a smile.
"You're already incredible. You just broke through and you're already this strong. Your foundation is rock solid. No wonder Master Zheng values you so much."
"You're flattering me, Brother Cheng," Wei He said, still analyzing how he lost. "I never imagined that final combo could be used like that. I learned a lot today."
Wei He had gone all out, yet he was still broken down in a direct clash. It was clear that even with his breakthrough, he was still far behind Cheng Shaojiu.
Both were at the Stone Hide level, but their blood power was vastly different. A veteran at the peak level like Cheng Shaojiu was on a completely different tier than a beginner like Wei He.
In a full-power clash, Wei He's knuckles ached, and his stamina drained rapidly. After trading more than ten blows in a row, his strength gave out, causing his defeat.
"Alright, it's getting late. I'll have Old Huang take you back," Cheng Shaojiu said.
"No need. I'll reflect on the fight for a bit and walk back myself. It's too late, and it won't be safe for Old Huang to walk back alone," Wei He declined.
"Fair enough. Be careful." Cheng Shaojiu nodded and walked off the training field.
Once he reached a dark corner out of sight, Cheng Shaojiu raised his hand and looked at his slightly reddened fist. A complex look flashed in his eyes.
If he hadn't used a secret ultimate move designed by his uncle at the last second, he might not have won.
The gap between them was tiny now. Wei He fought with extreme caution, never wasting a drop of energy. He was nothing like him. Without that secret move, if the fight had turned into a battle of attrition, it would be hard to say who would have won.
Without realizing it, the weak junior brother he used to beat with ease had grown into a dangerous opponent.
Thinking about the years he had wasted, Cheng Shaojiu felt a sudden urge to throw everything away and focus purely on martial arts.
"Starting tomorrow! No more slacking!" Setting his mind, he took large strides and vanished into the inner house.
On the other side of the field, Wei He was still analyzing Cheng Shaojiu's final attack.
That double-fist combo wasn't just a basic assault. The fists traveled along strange, contradictory paths. They looked like they were targeting the ears and shoulders, but mid-flight, they shifted. Using an elbow strike to knock away the opponent's guard, it followed up with a full-power swing right to the chest.
As he practiced the move, he noticed someone standing quietly at the edge of the training ground, watching him.
Guards and escort leaders passed by here all the time. Even if someone stopped to watch, they usually left quickly. But this person had been watching for a long time.
Realizing she had been spotted, the figure walked over.
Under the moonlight, the shadow revealed itself to be Cheng Jing.
She was dressed much more practical this time. She wore a gray-white combat suit, tight black pants tucked into tall boots, and two sheathed daggers strapped to her outer thighs. Judging by the travel pack on her back, she had just returned from an escort mission.
She stopped nearby and stared at Wei He with a complicated expression. She didn't speak, just stared at him with a mix of envy, shock, and conflict on her pretty face.
"You...?" Wei He blinked, having no idea what she wanted.
Whoosh!
Cheng Jing suddenly lunged forward. Raising her right leg high, she threw a vicious side kick straight at him.
"What are you doing?!" Taken off guard, Wei He raised his arms to block.
If he hadn't broken through his blood power, he might not have stopped this ambush. His reactions wouldn't have been fast enough. But now at the Stone Hide level, his enhanced senses, reaction speed, power, and explosive strength were far superior to hers.
He blocked the kick with ease, even though it was a surprise attack.
However, blocking the move only fueled her emotions further.
Her face flushed red. Gritting her teeth, she launched kick after kick at Wei He without stopping.
Bang! Bang! Bang!
Fists and feet collided as they moved rapidly across the field.
Cheng Jing threw twelve continuous kicks in a single breath. Every single one was blocked by Wei He's airtight defense.
"Is that enough?" Wei He growled.
"Not yet!" Cheng Jing stepped back and threw a full-power flying kick straight at his chest.
Driven purely by built-up emotion, she held nothing back.
Normally, people saved some strength during friendly spars. Full-power strikes created massive recoil, and if you weren't careful, the shockwave could injure your own body even if you hit the target. It was also impossible to stop yourself from accidentally killing the other person.
But Cheng Jing didn't care about any of that right now.
She threw everything into a straight kick aimed at Wei He’s belly, her eyes red as her foot tore through the air.
"Enough!" Wei He was finally pushed over the edge.
He shifted his feet, dodged the kick from the side, grabbed Cheng Jing's high ponytail, and slammed her head-first into the dirt.
Bang!
Caught off guard, Cheng Jing crashed heavily to the ground. Wei He immediately jumped on her and rained down a barrage of heavy punches.
Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!
A moment later.
The two sat side by side at the edge of the training field, staring silently at the crescent moon in the sky.
Cheng Jing's pretty face was swollen like a pig’s head, covered in bruises and red marks. Luckily, Wei He knew the boundary between men and women and avoided hitting her sensitive spots.
Even so, she finally understood how massive the gap was between a normal fighter and someone with a secondary blood breakthrough. Even a fresh breakthrough like Wei He could beat her effortlessly, let alone veterans who had been at that level for years.
"Did something happen on your escort mission?" Wei He asked.
"..." Cheng Jing stayed silent, pointing her bruised face toward the sky and staring blankly at the moon.
"Do your feet still smell?" Wei He asked.
"Ahhh!!" Cheng Jing threw a punch at Wei He's face, only to get punched right back in the cheek.
Thud.
She collapsed again.
"Can't you fix that temper of yours? Who's going to marry you if you stay like this?" Wei He stood up and sighed. Was this woman insane?
Lying on her back, Cheng Jing stared at him blankly. Suddenly, she burst into tears.
Tears streamed down both sides of her face. She shook violently as she cried, as if pouring out every last bit of her strength.
"What are you crying for?" Wei He was speechless. "You're the one who ambushed me first."
He didn't want to waste time on this crazy woman and turned to leave.
"You think I want to cry?! You think I'm crying because I lost to you?!" Cheng Jing screamed from behind him, sobbing hysterically.
"If I don't break through soon, I'll be forced to marry someone! You think I want this?! You think... I worked so hard for so many years! Why him and not me?! Do you have any idea how miserable my life is?!"
Wei He went quiet.
His footsteps stopped. Turning around, he walked back to Cheng Jing and stood over her.
"Are you here to comfort me?" Cheng Jing opened her swollen eyes to look at him.
Wei He threw a punch right into her face.
Bang!
"I'm here to bring you back to reality."
After landing the punch, he felt a wave of relief washing over him. He turned and walked away.
"Nobody in this world is going to spoil you. You think you're miserable? There are plenty of people living worse lives than you. At least your parents are still alive. Stop whining."
Throwing his jacket over his shoulder, Wei He felt the gloom and worry in his heart fade away.
He left Cheng Jing lying on the ground alone, one of her eyes swollen shut.
"You hit my face again?!"
She wanted to cry even more now.
This escort mission had been a nightmare. Though they didn't run into the dark maniac from last time, they were ambushed by bandits twice. They lost half of the cargo before barely making it back alive.
Fortunately, that loss fell within the contract's risk margin. The client was frustrated, but they accepted the situation.
However, the moment she returned, she received news that her mother was critically ill. On top of that, Wei He, who used to be weaker than her, had suddenly broken through and passed her by.
Add to that the growing pressure on the escort agency, and her emotions finally snapped.
Lying on the ground, Cheng Jing remembered the fight just now. No matter how hard she attacked, she couldn't break through Wei He's defense at all.
That sense of powerlessness was identical to the fate she was currently facing.
"I'll remember this beatdown! Just you wait, Wei He, I will pay you back one day!"
She made up her mind. To change her fate, she would start by curing her smelly feet!
