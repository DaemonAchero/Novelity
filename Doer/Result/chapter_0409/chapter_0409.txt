Clatter...
A dozens of balls rolled across the ground. Round, oval, or spiked like iron armor—it didn't matter.
Even spines made of reinforced steel snapped, bent, and forced themselves into perfect spheres.
Looking at the ground covered in fish-balls, Wei He felt a strange taste in his mouth.
His martial path seemed to be straying into a very bizarre direction.
With a wave of his hand, the balls tumbled off the edge and splashed into the sea.
'Now that sealing power is unlocked, I can build my gravity net.' The past few years were not wasted. A year ago, Wei He designed a trick specifically for ultra-fast Perfect Realm masters: the Gravity Net.
By using the instant trigger of sealing power, he could cast a max-range gravity zone in the air around him.
This net was just an upgraded, expanded version of his protective Qi.
Once an enemy touched the gravity net, they would be sealed and slowed down heavily.
Right now, Wei He's Qi could produce nearly forty thousand pounds of gravitational force.
That was more than half of his own raw physical strength.
And as his Return-True Qi grew, this pull would only get stronger.
Wei He glanced left and right to make sure no one saw him making balls.
Those strange fish only existed in the True World, invisible to normal people. As for the True Masters, they were always in a rush and had no time to snoop around.
"Ah! Was that... a ball?"
A familiar voice suddenly came from behind him.
Wei He froze. He slowly turned around...
Of course.
It was Huan Songzi, the laziest man in the whole sect.
The two looked at each other in silence for a moment.
"Brother Huan Songzi, didn't you go outside on a mission with sister-in-law?" Wei He felt his face go stiff.
Huan Songzi didn't answer. He just walked in a circle around the spot where Wei He did his test.
"Tsk, tsk. Is that a new trick from your spirit visualization?" He looked wildly curious.
He had caught a glimpse from far away and found it fresh and exciting.
True Masters lived long lives. Spending all that time purely on cultivation was a waste. So, most of them looked for fun to pass the time.
"Come on, show me again!" Huan Songzi found no balls left on the ground, so he smiled at Wei He.
"I still can't control it well. I can't show it right now, sorry," Wei He refused directly. He didn't want to be watched like a monkey in a circus.
"That's not cool. I am a top expert at the Sixth Step of Perfect Realm. A few words from me can save you years of detours. Quick, show me your ball!" Huan Songzi was hooked and kept nagging.
"..." Wei He said nothing, turned around, and walked away.
Huan Songzi refused to give up and chased after him.
They went all the way from the sea back to the dock. No matter what Huan Songzi said, Wei He stayed cold and refused.
He nagged for a long time until someone ran over to call him away for business.
Before leaving, Huan Songzi made Wei He promise to show him next time.
Wei He knew Huan Songzi loved to play, but he didn't expect him to be this clingy.
After finally shaking him off, Wei He rushed back into his cave to continue his closed-door training on the gravity net.
This was the key to whether he could fight Sixth Step experts.
To put it simply, except for being insanely fast, Sixth Step experts didn't scare Wei He at all in other areas.
A Sixth Step body held a maxed-out amount of Return-True Qi.
Wei He also had a maxed-out amount of Return-True Qi. Plus, his body was pushed by the Whale Flood Technique and his blood sacs, making his capacity much larger than normal True Masters.
Much, much larger. His total Qi far exceeded the Sixth Step.
Wei He guessed that even if his Qi quality was slightly lower, as long as he could stop their ghostly speed like he did with Huan Songzi, he had a real chance to win.
The key right now was building the net.
Back inside the cave, Wei He ate quickly and sat in the center.
Traces of Return-True Qi gushed out from his body, wrapping around his skin and clothes step by step.
Next, he expanded his protective Qi outward.
Little by little, it pushed out.
The bigger the net grew, the more Return-True Qi he had to dump into it.
The cost grew sharply with distance.
Inside the dark cave, he controlled his Qi while reaching out to activate the star array on the floor.
Grey rocks covered the ceiling and walls.
Scattered furniture sat in the corners. Ever since his last training ruined the room, Wei He had Lou Shenyue clear out the cave.
With his protective Qi, sleeping on the floor felt no different from a bed. As long as he had basic food and water, he was good.
Sitting in the center, a layer of grey-black Qi slowly covered Wei He's body.
He had to push his protective Qi out first before moving to the next step.
Soon, his protective Qi reached its thickest limit. Then, Wei He began to release Qi away from his body.
At first, he didn't release much. Just a tiny thread of Return-True Qi floated into the air.
This thread looked like a grey string, flying out from his hand and floating away slowly.
Very soon.
One meter, two meters, three meters...
Just past three meters, the thread began to spread out, lost control, and broke down into basic air and waste.
'Is three meters the maximum range of my Return-True Qi?' Wei He realized.
'Barely enough. Many True Masters in the sect can do this. Looks like the Gravity God doesn't boost range much.' With a thought, he pulled the thread back.
Ever since Return-True Qi fused with inner air, it became a half-living energy.
After pulling it back, Wei He started releasing tiny threads again.
Grey threads wove together, quickly forming a crossed net that trapped Wei He in the middle.
"My targets are mostly humans, so the mesh holes can be a bit bigger..."
Wei He waved his hand and released more Qi.
Fist-sized net layers floated out, stacking up around his body.
However, even though he prepared for this move for years and even asked Yuanduzi for advice to train his control, he still felt overwhelmed.
By the third net layer, he lost control.
"...This is far from what I planned," Wei He felt dissatisfied.
Before this, he could control over ninety threads to form four net layers around him.
Now, he could only make two.
Spread out, two nets could barely cover less than a meter of space.
'When the Qi and gravity get stronger, control actually gets harder...' Wei He calculated carefully. The maximum number of threads he could control right now was less than thirty...
Any thread beyond thirty would break down automatically.
'Thirty is the limit...' Wei He went silent, staring at the one-meter net he created.
The expected three meters shrank to just one meter...
Was this the gap between dream and reality?
"..."
He thought for a moment.
"Looks like there is only one way left..." Wei He took a deep breath and let it out slowly.
"To reach the three-meter limit, hit hard enough, and slow down Sixth Step experts or even stronger ones..."
"The only way is..."
"Make them thicker!"
Ten minutes later.
The three-meter max net was finally done...
Wei He sat on the floor, watching the heavy network floating and whipping around him. He was speechless for a long time.
He reached his goal and created a three-meter gravity domain.
But...
A grey-black python as thick as a man's arm slowly slithered past his shoulder.
"This is completely different from what I wanted..."
Wei He had no words.
Around him flew twenty-five grey-black Qi cables as thick as arms.
No, they were no longer threads. They were Qi ropes.
The half-living Return-True Qi, once thickened to this size, looked like a bunch of giant pythons flying and coiling around him.
"Whatever. As long as it works, who cares," Wei He sighed.
At least within three meters, his Return-True Qi covered everything.
The gravity released by this energy would stop anyone trying to burst through.
To build this net, Wei He even trained in control techniques before, though he later erased them to boost his blood power. But the result was there.
He stood up slowly and tried walking around with the gravity net active.
In a still pose, he could control it easily. But moving around was a different story.
He needed time to get used to it.
Still, Wei He was sure that if he fought an expert like Huan Songzi again, as long as he popped the gravity net in time, Huan Songzi could never land a cheap hit on him.
Three meters was short, but it was enough time for an expert of his level to react.
His own reaction speed was not slow; it was just far behind those at the Fifth Step or above.
'This way, if anyone wants to pass through my three-meter domain, they must use their own Return-True Qi to fight mine off. That drags them into a pure battle of Qi volume. And when it comes to raw Qi, I'm not scared of anyone!' Wei He felt satisfied.
Even though his style was looking a bit off now.
Long hair, black clothes, pale skin, and twenty-five giant grey-black pythons slithering around him—he looked nothing like a righteous cultivator.
But for safety and power, he never cared about style.
"Since it already looks like this, I might as well turn them into real pythons."
Wei He gave up on looking normal.
He shaped the Qi cables to give them snake heads and tails. Scales were too hard to control, so he skipped them. But adding heads was easy.
Soon, the thick cables became real grey-black pythons...
"This can scare people, and maybe make sealing easier. As for whether they bite, no one wants to find out," Wei He felt pleased.
Watching twenty-five giant snakes flowing around his body, a deep sense of safety surged in his heart.
"Right, since I am adding stuff, I should add the True Method deadly poison too. Return-True Qi corrodes too slowly by itself. If I layer it with lethal poison, one touch means death. It will make clearing crowds a lot easier."
