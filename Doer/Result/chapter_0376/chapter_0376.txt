Xiangxing Tower was open for business, so clients came and went every day. The banquet hall beside the tower was often rented out to rich merchants and government officials for drinking and dining.
"You are saying a very strong person from the Xuanmiao Sect arrived? Named Wei He?" Shangguan Tong asked. Her face was soft and handsome, her body tall, and her chest muscle well-developed. Her aura was as sharp as a sword drawn from its sheath.
If someone did not know her secret, no one would guess she was actually a woman dressed as a man.
She had led the White Jade Crown for over a hundred years. She never married and hid her real identity for one reason: to prove she was not weaker than any man, nor weaker than anyone else.
"Yes, Master. Just as you thought, it is the same Wei He we met back in Feiye City," a woman in a white dress carrying a sword said in a deep voice from the floor behind her.
"The Thousand Poison Sect?" Shangguan Tong whispered to herself.
Back then, the White Jade Crown suffered a small loss in Feiye City. Once she found out, she sent experts to investigate. But by then, the Thousand Poison Sect had already left Yun State and moved far away to Tai State.
At that time, the White Jade Crown was busy expanding its own territory. She did not have enough manpower to spare, so she put the matter aside, planning to take revenge later.
She never expected the world situation to change so fast. In the blink of an eye, more than half of Yun State was swallowed whole by the Guangci Sect.
Even the land of Yun State was lost, let alone the White Jade Crown.
Because the White Jade Crown was made entirely of women, and many were beautiful, several were caught by the Wu army and forced to become military comfort women.
Furious, Shangguan Tong worked closely with the official army and killed many experts from the Wu army and Guangci Sect. She completely forgot about Wei He and the Thousand Poison Sect.
She did not expect that Wei He, the master of the Thousand Poison Sect, would appear before her eyes again in this way.
"Where are the people from the Xuanmiao Sect now?"
"Reporting to the Master, they have returned to their base and show no sign of leaving the city."
"...Interesting... That Wei He could capture Ling Xian'er in a single move. His strength must be very terrifying. It has only been a few years, yet he has grown to this level..."
Shangguan Tong felt a bit troubled.
She asked herself honestly: if she fought Ling Xian'er, she could not defeat her instantly like Wei He did.
Winning was one thing, but an instant kill was a completely different level.
However, she roughly calculated the limit of Wei He's power. No matter how strong he was, cultivation needed time to build up.
It had only been a few years since Feiye City.
In such a short time, even if Wei He reached the Perception stage, he could not be at a very high level.
"Besides, rumor had it that Wei He defeated two True Masters of the Wushi Sect while only at the Organ Refining stage. That proves he can fight above his level. Now that he is a True Master, his stage cannot be high. Even if he is a genius, he has at most reached the First Perception, or likely not even that," Shangguan Tong judged quickly in her heart.
"Looking at it this way, Wei He could defeat Ling Xian'er at the Transformation stage because of his ability to fight above his level, combined with a sudden explosive technique. He caught Ling Xian'er off guard, and she was caught after making a mistake."
Thinking of this, Shangguan Tong calmed down.
Then, she continued to guess.
"So Wei He is just bluffing. He acts like a top master on purpose to scare the other forces and suppress the Three Faces hiding in the dark. That way, he lowers the chance of female True Masters from the Xuanmiao Sect being killed. If so, why is he bluffing instead of leaving?"
Her mind turned, and she suddenly understood.
"It seems the Xuanmiao Sect has more experts coming later! This master of the Thousand Poison Sect is just waiting for backup!"
The thought of more Xuanmiao Sect experts coming made Shangguan Tong feel wary.
"Fine. As long as this Wei He does not pick a fight with me, I can let him go."
"Master, someone from the Wushi Sect has arrived. It is Young Master Qian Zhan," a guard behind her reported.
"Qian Zhan is here?" Shangguan Tong's eyes lit up.
Although the Wushi Sect took heavy losses when the Wu army attacked its sacred grounds, its three main peaks still had deep foundations.
Setting aside the disabled masters, the three main peaks had more and stronger active True Masters than both the Xuanmiao Sect and the Wanfei Palace.
And this Qian Zhan was a close friend of hers among the three main peaks.
"Bring him in quickly." Shangguan Tong was just wondering how to handle the city's situation. The Three Faces were far too arrogant, giving her a headache, and now the Xuanmiao Sect sent backup.
Qian Zhan came at the perfect time.
If she could use the power of the Wushi Sect...
Thoughts raced through her mind.
Soon, a gentle young man in green robes appeared in the small hall, holding a refined ancient short sword with a bright smile.
Qian Zhan was a True Master from Yuya Peak of the Wushi Sect. When he met Shangguan Tong a few years ago, he saw through her real gender.
During a chance meeting, he saw her in female clothes and was stunned by her beauty. He decided right then to make her his cultivation partner.
"Tong'er, it has been a long time. I traveled to Haizhou and went out to sea to find this ancient sword from a ruin. Didn't you say you needed a short sword for self-defense?"
He handed the short sword over gently.
Shangguan Tong took it and sighed. No real joy showed on her face.
"Why are you so sad, Tong'er?" Qian Zhan asked in confusion. Seeing her troubled look, he felt pained.
"To tell you the truth, an evil thief called Three Faces is running wild in the city..."
Knowing he had just arrived, Shangguan Tong explained the matter of the Three Faces in detail.
At the same time, she worried that stronger experts from the Xuanmiao Sect might come, making the situation impossible to control.
After all, the forces behind the White Jade Crown could not stand against the Xuanmiao Sect.
Hearing this, Qian Zhan relaxed his brows.
"I thought it was something serious. Why worry about such a small matter, Tong'er? Leaving aside that the Xuanmiao Sect has already left the allied forces, even Wei He, who leads them now, actually had ties with my Wushi Sect in the past."
"Oh?" Shangguan Tong looked confused. "Please tell me more."
"It was like this. Back then, Wei He joined a branch of my Wushi Sect, and that branch built his martial foundation. Without that, he would never have reached such heights and would have died in Feiye City long ago. Later, through chance, he rose step by step and joined the Xuanmiao Sect. I did not expect him to become a True Master now. And his first teacher was taught by my father's disciple back then. What a coincidence," Qian Zhan said with a light laugh.
"Do not worry. If high experts from the Xuanmiao Sect arrive later, I will speak to Wei He for you. Since there is no deep hatred, he will surely not make things hard for you."
Qian Zhan knew a little about Wei He's past, but not everything.
In his eyes, even if Wei He ignored their past connection, he would not be so reckless as to disrespect the Wushi Sect to his face.
The Xuanmiao Sect was currently facing the Wu army, so he believed they would not dare to make more enemies.
Besides, others might fear the Xuanmiao Sect, but his Wushi Sect did not.
Most importantly, if he could perform well in front of his beloved and leave a great impression, he might be allowed into her room soon and become her partner.
So even if Wei He did not give him face, he would force him to give it!
"Then everything depends on you, Young Master Qian..." Shangguan Tong let out a heavy sigh of relief.
"Easy, easy." Qian Zhan was overjoyed, happy to have a good chance to show off.
"Speaking of which, the Mid-Summer Festival is in a few days. I wonder if Tong'er has time..."
BOOM!!
Before Qian Zhan could finish, a loud crash from downstairs cut him off.
"Who goes there!?"
The guards of the White Jade Crown rushed forward. But in just one moment, a powerful force knocked them back, crashing them hard onto the ground.
A broad, strong man with a plain face walked in slowly, wearing a Taoist robe.
"Is this the White Jade Crown?"
The man looked up at Xiangxing Tower.
"Wei He!? What are you doing here?"
On the upper floor, Master Shangguan Tong leaped down gracefully and landed on a blossoming pink flower tree.
"I do not recall having any connection with the Xuanmiao Sect. Of course, if you want to trade information about the Three Faces, my White Jade Crown is happy to share. But Brother Wei, your attitude does not look like a trade."
Shangguan Tong stood with her hands behind her back, looking down at Wei He from above.
Wei He did not reply. Instead, he looked around.
The rich merchants and officials who were having a banquet just now were now watching with great interest.
Many of these people had some martial arts foundation.
No matter where you go, people always love to watch a show.
Or maybe these people believed that even if a fight broke out, they would not be hurt?
Wei He looked back at Shangguan Tong up in the tree.
"I give you three seconds. You had better come down. I do not like looking up when I talk to people."
His voice was calm, as if stating a plain fact. But what he said caused a big stir among the crowd around them.
The Xuanmiao Sect was a big sect and indeed powerful, but the White Jade Crown also had background forces. Moreover, everyone saw that the person from the Wushi Sect had good relations with the White Jade Crown.
No matter how unwise the Xuanmiao Sect was, they would not make enemies so blindly, right?
After all, both Xuanmiao and Wushi came from the same Taoist roots.
Shangguan Tong felt a chill in her heart. She looked down into Wei He's eyes. There was no sign of joking in his gaze.
"One."
"Two."
A sudden wave of panic hit Shangguan Tong's heart. She quickly jumped down and landed steadily on the ground.
Whatever the case, she did not want a direct clash with the Xuanmiao Sect.
No matter how strong the White Jade Crown was, it was no match for the Xuanmiao Sect.
"Brother Wei, what is the meaning of this?" Shangguan Tong asked with a dark face.
"Are you the Master of the White Jade Crown, Shangguan Tong?" Wei He looked at the person in front of him.
"I am." Shangguan Tong glanced at her injured subordinates nearby, holding back her anger to question him.
Unexpectedly, her vision blurred. The man suddenly charged forward and reached out to grab her.
"You...!?!"
Before Shangguan Tong could react, she felt her protective qi break instantly like a dam crushed by a flood.
Her entire body went numb, and a large hand moved to grab her throat.
"Stop!!"
A figure flew down from the upper floor, throwing a punch straight at Wei He.
The fist shadow was heavy like a mountain, carrying deep and righteous force. Faint patterns were visible on his arm.
"Wushi Mountain Returning Fist?" Wei He's face remained unchanged.
He raised a palm and slapped forward.
Layers of True Force swept forward like a roaring tidal wave.
Before his palm even hit, the opponent's fist shadows collapsed completely.
Qian Zhan's face changed sharply. He felt his True Force melt away like snow under heat.
"Someone from the Wushi Sect dares to act wild in front of me?!" Wei He added thirty percent more power to his hand.
With overlapping palm shadows, he broke through the protective force and struck Qian Zhan hard on the chest.
BOOM!!
His chest caved in, and he was sent flying backward, crashing right back into the room he had just come from.
Wei He scanned his surroundings. Seeing no other female True Masters around, he grabbed Shangguan Tong by the neck, leaped up, and vanished in the blink of an eye.
The situation in this city was far too mess. It was better to clear the field first.
As for investigating, that was just an excuse.
As long as he captured and gathered all the female True Masters in one place, the Three Faces would have no choice but to come to him if he wanted to strike again.
When that time came, it would simply be a matter of whose methods were stronger.
