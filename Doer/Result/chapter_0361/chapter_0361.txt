Wei He packed his belongings, getting ready to hunt the Black Thread Whale.
According to Lou Shengyue, he needed specific materials as bait for this trip.
Since he was traveling far out into the open sea, he also needed to bring the Xuan Lock Manual given by the sect for his cultivation.
Though he didn't have any servants, Lou Shengyue handled all these chores for now.
Before leaving, Wei He met Chang Xuezhong one last time for a farewell meal.
Next, he went to find Yao Wan, but her servant informed him that she had already left for a distant place.
Finally, he visited the Cloud Altar to say goodbye to Yuan Duzi.
Deep within the fog-wrapped Cloud Altar, darkness surrounded everything. Only the central building emitted a faint light, standing out clearly.
Inside the territory of the Lock Mountain Lineage, Wei He saw his Senior Sister. As the current head of the Xuanmiao Sect, Yuan Duzi was talking with a middle-aged man and woman who both carried powerful aura.
Seeing Wei He approach from a distance, a soft smile touched the corner of Yuan Duzi's lips.
She knew about Wei He's talent, progress, and actual combat strength through Yao Wan, and she found it all impressive.
Even from her standard, she held high expectations for him.
Compared to Wei He, the other talents of his generation in the Xuanmiao Sect fell far short.
If Wei He's cultivation weren't still too low to guarantee his entry into Quanzhen, she would have already trained him as a candidate for Sect Master.
"Come here," Yuan Duzi called out, waving her hand gently.
The two people beside her turned to look at Wei He. Sharp scrutiny flashed in their eyes, quickly replaced by friendly, warm expressions.
"These two are current Sect Seeds, as well as your senior brother and sister, Xuan Zhen and Xuan Ning. You should get closer with them in the future," Yuan Duzi introduced warmly.
The middle-aged man stepped forward with a smile.
He wore a white Daoist robe and had a handsome face. His eyes were ordinary; if he were outside the mountain, he would look no different from the common folk.
Yet as Wei He walked closer, he felt a strange, subtle threat from this man.
"Junior Brother Wei He, I've heard of your reputation for a long time. Meeting you today, you truly live up to it. If you ever need anything in the future, just send word to me," Xuan Zhen said softly.
"Thank you, Senior Brother Xuan Zhen," Wei He greeted him respectfully with a bow.
Anyone who could make him feel threatened was definitely no weakling.
On the other side, the woman with long, cascading hair had a stern demeanor. One look was enough to tell she was a strict, principled person.
"My name is Xuan Ning. If you have any doubts about your cultivation while Senior Sister Yuan Duzi is away, you can come to me. I cultivate in the sect year-round and am usually here, so don't hesitate."
"Thank you, Senior Sister Xuan Ning. I won't hold back," Wei He said seriously.
This Senior Sister Xuan Ning seemed to be at another extreme. Her aura was faint and barely noticeable, almost nonexistent.
As a Seed expert who carried the future hope of the entire Xuanmiao Sect, she was definitely not weak.
Since she wasn't weak, it meant she hid an extremely terrifying trump card.
"Alright, you two may leave now. Follow my arrangements for what we discussed earlier, and do your best," Yuan Duzi said softly.
"Yes."
Xuan Zhen and Xuan Ning answered together immediately.
"Then we shall take our leave. Senior Sister, please take care of your body and do not overwork," Xuan Zhen said gently.
"Don't worry," Yuan Duzi nodded, watching them turn and walk away.
On the martial arts field built from white jade, only she and Wei He remained.
"Tell me, are you planning to head out and hunt the Black Thread Whale?" Yuan Duzi looked at Wei He with a faint smile.
"Nothing escapes your eyes," Wei He nodded. "The Lou family couldn't get the whale themselves, so I have to go personally."
"It's good to go out more. Given the current situation, even if you hadn't planned to leave, I would have given you a mission to travel around," Yuan Duzi smiled.
"Senior Sister... did something happen again?" Wei He lowered his voice unconsciously.
"Some things happened, but don't worry, I am still here," Yuan Duzi said, her smile fading.
"Alright, let's not talk about that. Since you're heading out, I won't send anyone to protect you this time."
She reached into her wide sleeve and pulled something out.
It was a black, bent willow branch.
"Take this to protect yourself."
"This?" Wei He was startled as he took the branch.
He examined it over and over in his hand, but it looked no different from an ordinary willow branch except for being black.
"You can't see its secret yet. Just keep it on you at all times," Yuan Duzi chuckled.
"Thank you, Senior Sister," Wei He said, storing it away solemnly.
"Go on. Make sure you prepare well. The sea area you're heading to is close to the Gale Zone, which is dangerous.
Countless dangers lurk in the Realm of Reality. Even Real Persons like me, or even Grandmasters, cannot guarantee survival there.
Once you step into the Gale Zone, you'll face countless terrifying True Beasts of all strength levels. Do not lower your guard."
Yuan Duzi warned him carefully.
Wei He nodded heavily.
"Senior Sister... are even you afraid of the Gale Zone?" he asked after a moment of hesitation.
"Of course," Yuan Duzi nodded. "The Gale Zone has swallowed Grandmasters more than once. Long ago, during the peak of Great Yuan, a massive disaster broke out there. Back then..."
Her mind seemed to drift back into the distant past.
Back then, Great Yuan was packed with powerhouses. Grandmasters united, expanding aggressively outward, until that disaster struck...
"Alright, enough of that. We can talk more when you return. Off you go..." Yuan Duzi stopped herself.
Wei He bowed and took his leave.
He walked along the lonely bridge leading away from the Cloud Altar.
Turning back, he looked once more at Yuan Duzi, who stood there all alone.
The entire Xuanmiao Sect only had a dozen or so Quanzhen experts, and this place rarely saw visitors.
The vast Lock Mountain region of the Cloud Altar alone could hold hundreds of people, yet usually only a few came in and out.
The other two regions were the same.
A sudden sense of loneliness washed over Wei He. He shook his head to clear his mind and walked away with long strides.
Two days later.
A large ship flying the banner of the Xuanmiao Sect slowly left Dragon Bay, heading toward the deep sea.
Waves slammed against the hull with rhythmic thuds.
Wei He stood at the bow, looking into the distance.
The endless ocean sparkled like crystal jade.
The clear waves churned and rolled constantly, full of life.
Massive, pure white seabirds circled overhead, crying out into the sky.
White clouds drifted slowly across the blue sky.
Wei He took a deep breath, filling his lungs with the salty ocean air.
"So this is the real sea..." Even though he had been at the Xuanmiao Sect on Heavenly Sea Island for a long time, this was his first time feeling the true breath of the deep ocean.
"Is this your first time going out to sea?" Lou Shengyue asked with a smile at his side.
"This is my second uncle. He often travels the sea and arranged the last hunt for the Black Thread Whale. He will be your guide this time." She pointed to a white-haired man with a warm demeanor standing nearby.
"Junior Brother Wei He, I am Lou Chengxun, Dao name Suiguang. I've heard so much about you, and seeing you today, you truly live up to your name."
The white-haired man looked to be at least fifty years old.
The protective force of his True Force from the Mind-Sensing Stage flowed around his body. Judging by his demeanor and tone, he was clearly a peaceful and friendly person.
"I am Wei He. I haven't decided on a Dao name yet. Greetings, Senior Brother Suiguang," Wei He bowed properly.
Even though his true strength was already at Quanzhen, on the surface he was still at the Perception-Fixing Stage. He had to follow sect rules and basic manners.
"I feel ashamed. Our Lou family intended to send the whale oil directly to your cave abode, but we lost over a hundred men and still failed..." Lou Chengxun said guiltily.
"It's fine. I will check it out myself this time and see who this Daoist Sanyun really is," Wei He replied carelessly. Being able to locate the whale was already good enough.
He had assumed this task would take years to find a breakthrough, just like the Bright Moon Whale Chart.
"Speaking of which, Junior Brother Wei should have completed your first Perception-Fixing process by now. Back when you were still in the Viscera Tempering stage, you fought two crippled Real Persons from the Wushi Sect at once and forced them back. Now, you could probably beat two regular Real Persons like me, right?" Lou Chengxun probed casually.
He had been stuck at his first Perception-Fixing stage for ten years, trapped by a bottleneck in his core art.
Everyone's talent, bone structure, and comprehension had limits.
The quality of the bone structure forged during the Bone Tempering stage set that ceiling.
That was his case. The base he forged back then was decent, but it had hit its absolute limit now.
Even among top-tier bone structures, there were vast gaps in quality.
Just like a building foundation, you couldn't build infinitely higher. If you ignored the base and kept building up, the whole thing would eventually collapse and crumble to dust.
Lou Chengxun knew his own limits.
His bone structure was only good enough for the second Perception-Fixing stage. Going any higher would bring danger.
That was why he felt complex emotions looking at Wei He.
A person like Wei He, who built a terrifyingly solid base during Viscera Tempering, had a ceiling far higher than his own.
If his comprehension kept up, he might even reach the high-tier Perception-Fixing stage in the future.
"What a pity. Back then, I took the easy way out and didn't aim for a perfect bone structure. Now I'm stuck with this result. Even top-tier bone structures have their limits..." Lou Chengxun sighed.
Wei He didn't know what to say to that.
"Senior Brother Suiguang, don't be discouraged. As the saying goes, where there's a will, there's a way. As long as you work hard, there's always hope."
Empty encouragement didn't cost anything, so he dished it out freely.
"You make a good point, Junior Brother," Lou Chengxun sighed and changed the topic. "This journey will take at least five days. If the wind favors us, it'll be faster, but head winds or wild winds will slow us down significantly."
Wei He nodded.
"So a round trip will take more than half a month if everything goes smoothly?"
"Correct. That's how long it took us last time," Lou Chengxun nodded.
He looked at Wei He, a thought suddenly crossing his mind.
"By the way, Junior Brother Wei... do you know how to swim?"
