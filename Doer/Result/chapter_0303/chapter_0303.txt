The crowd scattered on the street.
There were no loud screams or shocked cries. People were already used to martial artists fighting in public.
Wei He glanced around. The street was empty. People had fled in all kinds of ways with smooth, practiced movements. Clearly, this was not their first time.
Now, except for the circle of dead bodies around him, not a soul was in sight.
"To dare cause trouble on Wanfei Palace turf, the Kaori Sect must have really gone mad."
Holding a dead body, he turned and walked back into the hall.
Inside the hall, a crowd stood around Lu Weisheng's body. They looked at each other in panic.
The Song brothers and Wang Zhui hid in a corner, keeping as low a profile as possible.
The rest of the visitors huddled together near the experts of the Wan Poison Sect, looking tense and alert.
When Wei He came back through the door, every eye turned to him.
Lu Weisheng's men grew nervous.
Now that Lu Weisheng was dead and their backer from the Kaori Sect was killed on the spot, an even more dangerous unknown lay ahead. It all depended on how Wei He handled them.
The Song brothers and Wang Zhui breathed a sigh of relief, knowing they were safe.
However, Song Shixiong knew he had brought trouble to Wei He. He hurried over with a fake smile.
"Greetings, Foster Father! Please accept my bow!"
Without another word, he dropped to his knees and banged his head on the floor a few times.
This shameless behavior made his brother, Song Shizhen, blush with shame. He could barely watch.
Wang Zhui turned his head away, unable to look.
Wei He found it amusing. In all his years of living, he had never seen anyone so thick-skinned.
Yet, Song Shixiong was flexible. He knew how to use everything around him to get out of trouble.
With such quick thinking and ruthless methods, he would surely make something of himself in the future if his luck held out.
"You three, follow me," Wei He said calmly. He turned to leave.
"Master Chen, please wait!" Boss Xiao Jingyun of the trading firm called out. Sweat covered his forehead as he held back his fear.
Even though just standing before Wei He terrified him, he had to speak up to save his own life.
"Boss Xiao? What else is there?" Wei He asked in surprise.
Knowing his real identity and still daring to speak up, this man had some nerve.
"May I ask, Master Chen, how do you plan to deal with us?" Xiao Jingyun took a deep breath to steady his voice.
"Do you think I will kill you all to silence you?" Wei He smiled. "This is Jin Zhou, and I am not the mad killer rumors make me out to be. I am a generous man. I treat people with kindness and warmth. So, there is no need to fear."
No need to fear, my foot! If he was really so kind, where did the rumors of him slaughtering thousands come from? Did they sprout from the ground?
"Thank you, Master Chen!" Though terrified inside, Xiao Jingyun quickly cupped his hands in thanks.
Hearing this, the others quickly chimed in as well.
Wei He's words meant he would not harm the people present.
"Of course," Wei He suddenly changed his tone. "I hope all of you keep today's events to yourselves. Do not spread a word."
"Rest assured, Master Chen! We will never say a word!" Xiao Jingyun promised at once.
"That is right! Master Chen can rest easy! If anyone opens their mouth, I will skin them alive!"
"Having seen Master Chen's true face today, how could we dare to spread rumors?"
"We swear, whoever talks will die by a thousand arrows!"
The guests scrambled to give their word.
Right now, over a dozen martial masters stood around them, watching like tigers. If anyone acted out of line, a deadly palm strike would follow immediately.
In such a grim situation, no one dared to bet that Wei He would not kill them to keep the secret.
"I trust your words, but I trust my medicine even more," Wei He said with a smile, pulling out a small green porcelain bottle.
"This is my own Ten-Thousand Green Poison-Locking Pill. Take one pill each, and I will let you leave."
"Master Chen... is this... not a bit much...?" Sweat poured down Xiao Jingyun's face as he forced a smile.
"Once you take the pill, we are all on the same side," Wei He added.
"Boss Xiao, if you think the people backing you can save you, feel free to try." He gave Xiao Jingyun a cold look.
Xiao Jingyun had factions backing him too, but Wei He did not care who they were.
In the Jin Zhou area ruled by Wanfei Palace, outside of the top experts at the Mind Sensing level, he had nothing to fear.
With Yue Xiaodie and the Master of Wanfei Palace backing him, as long as he did not break the general rules of trade or cause massive chaos across the state, killing a dozen people was a minor matter.
Sweat dripped down Xiao Jingyun's forehead. He stared at Wei He and swallowed hard. He wanted to speak, but he had no idea how to escape this trap.
"Alright, hand out the medicine. From now on, we are on the same team," Wei He smiled.
He handed the bottle to his men and let the martial masters handle the rest.
His eyes fell upon Song Shixiong and the other two.
"You three, come with me."
Terrified by what had just happened, the three did not dare to resist. They meekly followed Wei He out of the hall.
Soon, inside a private room of a nearby teahouse, Wei He sat cross-legged, quietly brewing green tea.
Song Shixiong and the other two stood obediently by his side.
The Song brothers were somewhat prepared, having seen Wei He's terror back in Gray Cypress Town.
But Wang Zhui was different.
He came from a rich family and had never stepped into the dark underworld.
Usually, the worst things he saw were street fights, dirty business tricks, or local bullies.
Seeing people killed on the spot, and dozens controlled with poison right before his eyes, truly opened his world.
Look at Boss Lu Weisheng. One wrong word, and he ended up a corpse on the floor.
Inside the private room, Wang Zhui did not dare to say a word. Facing Wei He alone brought overwhelming pressure.
The man sitting before him looked average in size, yet he radiated an indescribable feeling of danger and unknown power.
"Speak. Where is the rest of the text from this book?" Wei He tossed the Selection of Heaven manual onto the floor.
The pages opened right to the secret technique at the end.
Song Shixiong forced a smile and spoke quickly.
"To let Foster Father know, the text on this book was treated with my family's special liquid. It only shows up when washed with the same solution used on the Bright Moon Whale Map earlier."
"..." Wei He looked at Song Shixiong, then glanced at the quiet Song Shizhen behind him.
"Later, you will apply the solution to bring out the text. Also, where did you three learn that strange movement technique?" he asked again.
"Oh, that! We found a broken fragment of a movement technique in the Wang family library. That was what it recorded. If Foster Father wants it, I can write it down from memory right now!" Song Shixiong answered fast.
Wei He looked at him closely, then threw a small medicine bottle to the floor.
"No need. Take this and cure the poison in your bodies."
The three were shocked. Looking at the small yellow porcelain bottle rolling on the ground, none of them knew when they had been poisoned.
Song Shixiong picked up the bottle and went silent for a moment. Then, he smiled, pulled the cork, and popped a pill into his mouth.
He passed the bottle to his brother and Wang Zhui. Hesitating for a moment, the two swallowed the pills as well.
"Foster Father, were we really poisoned just now?" Song Shixiong could not help asking.
"My inner force and the martial art I practice are naturally laced with deadly poison. When I killed Lu Weisheng and those Kaori Sect brats earlier, it affected you," Wei He explained briefly.
"Then... that cure just now..." Song Shixiong breathed a sigh of relief.
"Actually, I lied to you. That was not a cure. It was poison," Wei He suddenly laughed.
"..." The three felt a chill run down their spines. Their hearts pounded in terror.
"Look at how scared you are..." Wei He shook his head. "It was just a joke."
"...Foster Father, your joke is a bit too terrifying..." Song Shixiong wiped his sweat and forced a laugh.
"Alright, hurry up and process the Five-Turn Dragon Breath. Since you asked me to save you, you cannot leave me empty-handed, right?" Wei He smiled softly.
Only then did the three breathe a sigh of relief.
They finally understood what a true master of the evil path was like.
The way he acted was unpredictable and twisted. Normal people could not take the shock.
Soon, Song Shixiong brought out the hidden text of the Five-Turn Dragon Breath, and Wei He copied it down.
Afterward, the three took the manual and left the teahouse.
Wei He stood by the window, looking down as the three walked away and blended into the crowd.
In his hand, he held the handwritten copy of the Five-Turn Dragon Breath.
Just as Song Shixiong had said, this secret technique could boost physical strength by more than five times.
However, the price was draining a whole day of future energy.
After using this secret art, one would fall into extreme weakness for an entire day.
Even so, this secret art was insanely powerful.
Because it lasted for a full hour!
"Five-Turn Dragon Breath... it truly lives up to its name." Wei He carefully read through the copied notes.
The notes stated that training this art required the long-term use of a material called the Birth Phase Fruit.
This item was not a fruit, but a lump growing on the head of a rare beast called the Three-Eyed Dove.
Only by drinking the fluid inside this lump while practicing could one master the Five-Turn Dragon Breath.
"Just in time. I can see if I can get this material at the upcoming gathering." Wei He felt satisfied.
If he could master the Five-Turn Dragon Breath and pair it with a breakthrough in the Whale Inundation Art, he would gain another deadly trump card.
By now, poison was slowly becoming just a side tool.
Against masters of the Mind Sensing realm and true Daoist masters, poison worked far too slowly.
If you were weaker, you were weaker. By the time the poison took effect, you would have died a hundred times over.
But if he could master the Five-Turn Dragon Breath, it would give him one more powerful tool.
Packing up his things, Wei He turned and left the teahouse.
Half a month passed like flowing water in the blink of an eye.
The Three Sun Festival was a special folk festival in the Great Yuan to ward off evil.
Whenever this festival arrived, people bought three types of flowers with the word "Sun" in their names to form the "Three Suns" to ward off evil and pray for safety.
Legend had it that the Three Sun Festival was also the day with the strongest sun energy of the year.
Various districts in Weifang City held celebrations. Some people even dressed up as ghosts and monsters, only to be beaten by skilled martial artists playing heroes.
Taking advantage of the lively mood, Wei He left home early, carrying his prepared gear.
Leaving the city, he arrived at the Qixia Villa outside.
During the past half month, he had already sent men to clear out the location of Qixia Villa.
So he headed straight there without delay.
