Things were not as bad as Wei He had thought.
After killing the men, Wei He turned around and hid. He stayed hidden until early the next morning.
Once it was quiet outside, he stepped out to check the area.
The island was peaceful, no different from before.
There was no Grandmaster, and no assassins.
Everyone was doing their usual routine, patrolling the area and clearing out any stray sea beasts.
However, Daotzi Cai Menghuan had sent a message saying he was lightly hurt. He was resting in his room, so the two factions had to manage their own business for now.
"Wei Senior, did anything happen to you last night?"
Inside a simple stone house on the island, Zhao Yin sat across from Wei He, looking at him with a strange expression.
She had found some clues last night. The main leader, Daotzi Cai Menghuan, had been attacked. Hong Chan, the leader of the other faction, was attacked too.
Since two leaders in a row were targeted, Wei He from the Lock Mountain line likely ran into an attack as well.
"Nothing much. Someone tried to assassinate me, but I beat them back. How is Senior Cai doing?" Wei He asked back.
He had killed two people on his side, both of whom were leftover targets from last time. Since the enemy set up such a strong trap for him, Cai Menghuan must have faced even bigger trouble.
"Senior Cai is fine. The assassins brought a Grandmaster at first, but our Founder was waiting for them and blocked the hit. But the enemy had another trick. Shortly after the Founder chased them away, Senior Cai got hurt," Zhao Yin said in a deep voice.
"Hurt?" Wei He paused. He thought Cai Menghuan was extremely strong, maybe even as strong as him.
"Yes. The assassins used explosives. A huge amount," Zhao Yin nodded.
"..." Wei He gasped in his mind. For people at their level, normal amounts of explosives were useless, but huge amounts were a different story.
If there were enough of them...
Wei He felt tense.
"Also, I have some doubts about this whole trip," Zhao Yin continued. "How did the assassins know our exact route? How did they plant explosives on this island in advance? It feels like they knew our destination from the very start."
"True," Wei He agreed. "That four-winged giant lizard last night was probably lured here on purpose to distract the Daotzi."
"So everyone is checking their own teams now to see if our fears are true," Zhao Yin said with a serious look.
No one who reached this level was stupid. Even if constant training made some people slow-minded, they would not make silly mistakes when it came to basic safety.
"How bad is Senior Cai's injury?" Wei He asked again.
"It is not serious. He dodged the main blast in time, so it is just a light injury. It will not affect the Beast Tide Competition," Zhao Yin replied.
"That is good," Wei He nodded.
"I will take my leave then. I will let you know as soon as I hear anything new." Zhao Yin stood up.
He was assigned by Wei He to keep an eye on the other two teams.
On the Lock Mountain side, a few people suffered minor injuries last night, but nothing major. The assassins mainly targeted Daotzi Cai Menghuan and Wei He himself.
Especially Wei He.
After hearing Zhao Yin's report, Wei He felt a sense of danger. Cai Menghuan actually faced a Grandmaster assassin on their very first day here. They still had a long time ahead of them...
It was clear from Zhao Yin's words that Xuanmiao Sect might have used the Daotzi as bait to draw out the assassins.
The bait worked, and the Grandmaster assassin was driven off, but Cai Menghuan still got hurt. No one expected the enemy to use explosives.
Shortly after Zhao Yin left, another visitor arrived.
This time, the visitor made Wei He stand up with a serious, respectful expression to greet him.
"Disciple greets the Founder."
On the open ground outside the stone house, Wei He cupped his hands and bowed deeply to Grandmaster Xiao Ling.
"No need for formalities." Xiao Ling looked Wei He up and down. "I came because of the attack earlier. Tell me the truth. How many assassins were there, and how strong were they?"
Wei He was already prepared and answered without hesitation.
"There were three. Two of them could enter the Full True Ethereal State and were extremely strong. If not for the trick Senior Sister Yuan Duzi gave me, I am afraid I would not be alive to see you today!" His eyes turned heavy, showing a look of relief and luck.
"...Two Ethereal State assassins, and the third one could not be weak either. Wei He, you managed to escape safely from such a team without using the First Line Array. I am impressed," Xiao Ling said.
Xiao Ling had heard of Wei He's name before, not from other Real Persons, but from Yuan Duzi. Yuan Duzi cared a lot about Wei He and told Xiao Ling to protect him before this trip.
Xiao Ling remembered that. Now, it seemed Wei He was keeping some secrets.
However, every Real Person had their own hidden tricks. As long as it did not harm the sect's interests, it did not matter.
As for Wei He's claim about using Yuan Duzi's hidden trick to drive off the assassins, Xiao Ling did not doubt it. That was the only logical explanation. Otherwise, a four-time Sensate killing three Ethereal State assassins would be way too wild.
Still, not getting killed right away by an Ethereal State opponent was already impressive.
"I believe you are a good child. If you face trouble in the future, use this to contact me directly." Xiao Ling tossed a small, fine silver wood badge.
Wei He caught it cleanly. Before he could ask anything else, Grandmaster Xiao Ling was already gone.
Wei He was shocked by the speed. He knew a Grandmaster was far beyond his reach. He could not even keep up with an Ethereal State opponent, let alone a Grandmaster.
Still, the Founder's attitude showed he now had a rough idea of Wei He's strength.
Wei He had thought about hiding the truth, but hiding facts in a situation like this could cause big trouble later. So he chose to tell the truth. He had already shown part of his strength in internal sparring anyway, so showing a bit more did not matter.
After all, when fighting the assassins, he only used his normal full power plus three Gravity Pythons. Compared to his full power of twenty-five Gravity Pythons, that was nothing.
During the following days, Wei He stayed inside to train his Sensate state, waiting for the beast tide.
The other Real Persons of the Lock Mountain line moved around freely. Real Persons were a loose group by nature, and no one liked being controlled all the time. Since Wei He liked to train alone, the Lock Mountain group became even looser.
After the attack, everyone kept their whereabouts a secret and used their own ways to stay safe. They only gathered during regular meetings. Other than that, no one knew where anyone went.
Wei He did not care. He focused entirely on speeding up his training.
At his level, he was about to touch the second type of Ghost Wind—the Entangling Wind, which was deeper than the Nightingale Wind.
Sensate training required a full month of continuous high-awareness state, so as long as the beast tide did not arrive, he was in no rush. He simply ran his Star Array and trained alone every day.
Over a month passed in a flash.
Around the island, small groups of sea beasts started coming ashore every morning and evening, trying to attack humans.
When real sea beasts attacked normal humans, they usually brought various seaside illnesses—sometimes sudden diarrhea, sometimes sudden deafness, or spreading certain bacteria. The symptoms were strange and varied.
Luckily, the island was prepared with various cures.
As more sea beasts came ashore, the group from Xuanmiao Sect finally moved to the coast to guard the area.
Splash.
A black sea beast the size of a half-grown seal was stabbed through the head by a Lock Mountain Real Person and fell into the water.
The Real Person bent down, cut open the beast's body, and dug out a dark Star Stone.
Along the coastline under the sunset, seven Real Persons spread out in a line, dealing with the sea beasts that kept coming out of the water.
Wei He sat in the back with Zhao Yin, supervising the situation.
In the distance, beasts were killed one after another, falling into the water with loud splashes. About one or two beasts appeared every second.
This speed was trouble for normal people, but for Real Persons, it was nothing. Their attack and movement speeds were too fast, so these beasts were just free kills.
Wei He narrowed his eyes in the back. As a leader, he did not care about these common beasts.
"According to the rules, our competition with the Sea Peace Alliance depends on the number of clear sea beast Star Cores we get," he said calmly in a low voice.
"Yes," Zhao Yin nodded beside him. "Beasts with clear Star Cores are as strong as four-time Sensates up to Two-Step Full True. And beasts at this level are the main force of the real tide."
She gave a helpless smile.
"So, normal Real Persons are not much use in this kind of beast tide. In the end, we have to take the front line ourselves."
"As long as you understand," Wei He nodded. On the Lock Mountain side, only he, Zhao Yin, and Meng Chunhan were at or above Three-Step Full True.
In truth, only the three of them were qualified to compete.
"I will do my best, though I cannot match your strength, Leader Wei," Zhao Yin sighed.
The failed investigation of the recent attack made everyone hold back a bit of strength while fighting beasts, just in case something happened again. Because of this, everyone on the beach was saving their energy, and their attacks were getting a bit slow.
BANG!
A sudden heavy sound rang out. A ugly black sea beast as tall as a two-story building rose from the water.
ROAR!
The beast looked like a woolly mammoth, covered in long fur, with four snow-white horns under its mouth like sharp blades.
Its eyes were blood-red as it charged straight at the Lock Mountain Real Persons on the shore. Its speed was just as fast as the Real Persons.
Taken by surprise, two Real Persons failed to react in time and were knocked flying, rolling across the ground.
The mammoth beast roared and charged toward Wei He and Zhao Yin in the back.
At the same time, two more giant mammoths appeared from another direction, breaking through the defense line and charging at the two of them.
Behind Wei He and Zhao Yin were the island's defense structures. If they failed to stop them and the defense was broken, the other sea beasts would rush in through the gap.
That would be a real disaster.
