Chang Xuezhong remembered Wei He's insane combat power and shook his head with a smile.
"True... but when you're out in the world, never lower your guard." He turned around, reached out his hand, and sent out a wave of force.
It pulled an object from the back shelf straight into his hand.
"I used this when I first left the mountain to anchor my senses. It will watch over your surroundings while you rest. Take it."
He passed the object to Wei He with a sincere look.
"Thank you, Senior Brother!" Wei He accepted it without hesitation.
This journey to anchor his senses was also a patrol mission for the disciples of the Xuanmiao Sect. Besides clearing out mutated beasts in the Fire Pool secret realm, he needed to check on several sect outposts along the border.
He was not the only one. Every newcomer in the sect had to do this, including Ouyang Luo, who had advanced alongside him.
"I heard that when Ouyang Luo found out you were leaving to anchor your senses, he immediately asked to leave too. He refuses to fall behind you. It's almost funny. He might be talented, but he can't match you at all."
Chang Xuezhong laughed as he mentioned the man.
Wei He smiled and said a few words in response.
For over a year, he and Chang Xuezhong had gotten along well in the inner mountain. They had grown quite close and were now true friends. Learning that Wei He was about to leave, Chang Xuezhong had come specifically to buy him a farewell meal.
After eating and drinking their fill, Wei He said goodbye to Chang Xuezhong and left the tavern.
When he reached the Longwan Docks, Zhou Muqing was already waiting on a small boat.
Seeing him arrive, she cupped her hands in respect.
"Master, everything is ready."
Zhou Muqing was wearing warrior robes with swords strapped to her back. An eyepatch covered her eyes, showing she had hyper-sensed her vision.
She stood on the small boat as the sea breeze blew against her, making her robes stick tight to her waist and showing off her slender figure. Compared to a year ago, she looked much more calm and steady.
As she watched Wei He walk closer, she felt a wave of emotion.
Top experts like them were all proud people. Before entering the blessed land, every single one of them believed they were rare geniuses who would easily break through into the True Realm.
But now... you only saw the gap when you compared yourself to real monsters.
In over a year, Wei He had not only finished hunting his innate talent, but he had also hyper-sensed his eyes and was now ready to anchor his senses.
The process was simple: endure the ghostly winds below the mountain for a month straight. During this month, hyper-sensed mode had to stay active every second.
It sounded easy, but it was brutally hard.
Zhou Muqing looked at herself. She had entered the blessed land at the same time as Wei He, yet she had only just finished her talent hunt. Now she was only starting her second phase of eye hyper-senses.
She remembered how hard it was just to fight a First-Anchor beast, needing every trick and item in her book to slowly grind it to death. Meanwhile, Wei He had gone out once and casually killed a Human-Faced Bird leader that was as strong as a Second-Anchor beast.
The gap between them was ridiculous.
"When we are outside, just call me Master. Skip the formal titles," Wei He commanded as he stepped onto the boat.
"Yes," Zhou Muqing replied, pulling her thoughts back. She pushed her force into the water, sending the small boat gliding away from Tianhai Island.
Lost among the many ships moving in and out of the island, their little boat was completely unremarkable as it headed toward Zhentao City.
Standing at the front of the boat, Wei He looked out over the vast blue sea. His mind finally relaxed a little.
Over the past year, his life had been safe and stable, but it was also suffocating.
His Senior Sister, Yao Wan, had guarded him day and night without rest. She even secretly watched over him while he cultivated. Because of that, Wei He never dared to reveal any of his hidden trump cards.
Fortunately, he was finally free.
Yao Wan had only promised Master Sister Yuanduzi that she would guard him for one year. Now that the year was up, he could finally move freely.
A smile crept onto Wei He's face.
"You seem to be in a good mood, Master," Zhou Muqing said softly beside him.
"I've been training hard in the inner mountain for a long time. Getting out for fresh air feels good," Wei He nodded. "The Xuanmiao Sect is great, but it's too quiet. The three branches stay separate, and the disciples keep to themselves to train or do missions. There isn't much interaction."
"That's true. Inside the mountain, people stick to small groups formed around their teaching sisters or leading brothers. Everyone else keeps their distance, so there aren't many real bonds," Zhou Muqing agreed.
"Now that we're out, I can finally see how the world has changed," Wei He said softly.
"Are you homesick, Master?" Zhou Muqing smiled.
"Maybe."
Wei He shook his head and stopped talking.
The boat cut through the surging waves like a motorboat, pushing up white foam as it sped toward the Zhentao City docks.
The shipping lane between Zhentao City and Tianhai Island was packed with small boats. Some powerful warriors even crossed the sea relying purely on lightfoot skills.
Halfway through their journey, Wei He's eyes suddenly flicked toward a brown ship rushing past on their right.
The side of the ship had the word "Xuanmiao" written on it in huge letters. A squad of sharp-looking warriors stood on deck, radiating a faint smell of blood.
The man leading them was wearing the robes of the Qianliu Branch.
The Qianliu Branch was solely responsible for external security. Unless you were part of the high-level management, other branches had no right to question them.
Wei He was just a newcomer getting ready to anchor his senses, so he had no right to pry either.
Still, seeing this group made his heart sink.
The people sent out to protect the sect's safety were no weaklings. Yet this group of experts couldn't even hide the blood scent on their bodies when returning.
Clearing away a blood scent only took a tiny push of internal force. The fact that they couldn't manage even that meant only one thing: they were completely exhausted and pushed to their absolute limits.
"Master," Zhou Muqing whispered, thinking the same thing as worry flashed in her eyes.
"It's fine. Where we're going is just the outer edge. It's safe," Wei He said calmly.
The ships passed each other.
Wei He didn't look back. He stared at the passing commercial and passenger ships, lost in thought.
Zhou Muqing wanted to say more, but seeing his quiet face, she kept silent.
The boat soon reached the shore.
They grabbed their luggage. Wei He didn’t inform his family about this trip to save them the worry.
For a normal expert, anchoring senses was extremely dangerous. But for him, his massive store of True Force meant he could easily last a month, or even a year without trouble.
More importantly, without Senior Sister Yao Wan watching over him in secret, he could finally test his true limits.

"Kill!"
On the border of Haizhou, a squad of experts wearing Xuanmiao Sect gear were locked in a brutal battle against a group wearing light blue shirts and brown leather vests.
Inside the dense forest, forces exploded as experts collided. Giant trees snapped and shattered on contact.
In the middle of the battle stood a massive man. He was three meters tall, built like a mountain of flesh. His pale skin reflected the bright sunlight.
This was Xu Dingchun, the White-Robed Buddha, who was the top leader sent by the Guangci Sect to control Haizhou.
Xu Dingchun had trained under King Chiye, one of the two great elders of his sect. His body-hardening martial arts, powered by True Blood, were terrifyingly strong. Even inside his sect, he was ranked among the top fighters.
However, he had a terrible temper and zero social skills. He was sent to this remote, troublesome place only because he had pissed off his colleagues and superiors back home.
"I've fought the Qianliu Branch many times now. Too bad... your force is useless against me," Xu Dingchun sneered at the tall man panting in front of him.
The man had high cheekbones and sunken eyes. He looked exhausted, but his blocky muscles made him look softer compared to the giant Xu Dingchun.
This was Zhao Mengheng, a veteran expert leading the Qianliu squad. He had been patrolling the border when he found suspicious clues and tried to report back to the mountain.
Unexpectedly, the junior brother he sent for help vanished without a trace. And for some reason, all their signal flares failed to work.
The forest looked normal, but they were already completely surrounded by these Guangci Sect cultists.
"Junior Brother Quan! I don't think we're making it out today..." Zhao Mengheng rubbed his throbbing wrists, a wave of despair rising in his eyes.
"In a moment, I will use a secret technique to blast an opening. You must... get this message back to the sect! These Guangci cultists have been planning this for a long time! Tell the sect that most of our border posts have already fallen! Go...!"
Zhao Mengheng sounded tired but absolute. He used his arm to block his teary-eyed junior brother behind him.
"Senior Brother Zhao!" The young man named Quan cried out, his vision blurring. "We leave together!"
"Fool! Don't ruin the sect's critical mission! Run!!" Zhao Mengheng roared as he charged forward.
"Die, cultist!!"
Zhao Mengheng poured all his True Force into his attack. He was no ordinary expert; he was a rising star in the Qianliu Branch who was right on the verge of reaching the Full True Realm.
In the entire Qianliu Branch, only three people were this close to reaching the Full True Realm. If he survived and broke through, the sect would gain another top-tier powerhouse.
Sadly, he wouldn't get the chance.
Zhao Mengheng focused all his True Force into a single sharp point, firing off his branch's secret technique: the Mountain-Splitting Tiger Spike. He burned through his force like fuel, unleashing every ounce of power he had left.
"I don't care who you are, die with me!!"
The air around him twisted for several meters. His white True Force gathered into a massive, one-eyed white tiger that roared and lunged straight at Xu Dingchun.
BOOM!
Waves of force blasted outward in circles.
Zhao Mengheng brought both hands together, striking Xu Dingchun directly in the chest. The massive energy shook like a white tiger biting hard into his heart.
"What a shame... Why do you Xuanmiao Sect fools always use this move?"
Xu Dingchun took the strike head-on, completely unharmed.
He grinned, his fat pale face making him look like a creepy Buddha statue.
SPLAT!
He slammed his giant palms together.
His massive hands clamped down on Zhao Mengheng's head like a vise, crushing it into a bloody mess.
The thick barrier of True Force protecting the head was forcibly blown apart by sheer, raw strength.
"You Dayuan people love playing with fancy True Force. No matter how much force you have, it can't beat my two hands pressing together," Xu Dingchun said casually, tossing Zhao Mengheng's corpse aside.
He turned his eyes toward the remaining disciples.
He had already hunted down five groups trying to escape from different posts. Zhao Mengheng had been the strongest of this lot, almost reaching the Full True Realm. His force had been close to taking a physical form.
Unfortunately, almost reaching it wasn't the same as actually being there.
