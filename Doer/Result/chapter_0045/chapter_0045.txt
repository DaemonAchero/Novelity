That last move came out of nowhere.
A heavy shower of darts flew straight at him.
Luckily, Wei He was already on guard. He twisted his body to dodge a few, and for the ones he couldn’t avoid, he blocked them with the iron plates on his body. He made sure not to let them touch his arms or legs.
Seeing his final trick fail, the fat man lost all hope. He stumbled a few steps and fell to the ground again.
This time, he didn't move at all.
Wei He quickly pulled out another poison spike and threw it hard at him.
Puk!
The spike sank deep into the fat man's belly, but he stayed completely still.
Wei He finally breathed a sigh of relief. He picked up another heavy stone and threw it right at the fat man’s forehead. Blood slowly leaked out, and the fat man's skull caved in from the strike.
Only then did Wei He slowly walk over to clean up the body.
He pulled quite a few good things off the fat man.
A pair of metal gloves, five leftover poison darts, a bag of poison powder, a leather coin pouch, and bags for food and water.
After taking everything, Wei He chopped up the fat man's wounds to hide the cause of death, then quickly left.
He wanted to drag the body away, but time was running out. The bodies he left behind earlier might already be found.
He could faintly hear faint noises coming from the mountain, though he couldn’t tell if it was wild animals or people. Whatever it was, there were a lot of them.
Either way, he couldn’t stay here any longer. He knew very well that people from the Shaoyang Sect usually traveled in groups.
Wei He left fast, carrying his loot as he headed down the mountain.
He found the spare clothes he had hidden earlier in a patch of wild grass. After changing into them, he grabbed a fresh burlap sack and stuffed all the looted items inside.
The bag was meant for hunting prey, but now it served a different purpose.
Wei He sprinkled odor-masking powder over himself, wet a towel with water from his pouch, and wiped the blood off his face and body. He fixed his messy hair, grabbed the bag, and rushed toward the gates of Feiye City.
He had gained a lot from this trip, but he had also brought big trouble on himself.
A whole nest of bandits plus a Shaoyang Sect member—he had killed them all. That was seven lives in total.
Wei He felt a wave of mixed emotions. Without realizing it, he had started treating human life like trash.
Not too long ago, he was just a timid kid whose hands would shake after hitting someone too hard.
Now...
He walked all the way back to his house and dropped the bag onto the dirt in the yard.
It was already noon. His second sister had gone to the Huishan Punch Academy to help out and visit Old Zheng.
Since losing the steamed bun shop, Wei Ying often went to check on Old Zheng when she had free time.
With Zheng Fugui's daughter far away, he was growing closer to the two siblings every day.
Since his sister wasn't home, Wei He didn't waste any time. He pulled out the python, nailed it to a tree trunk, and began skinning it with his knife.
The snake wasn't poisonous. Once skinned, it could be boiled into soup or roasted over an open fire, both of which were tasty.
He skinned it, gutted it, washed it several times with clean water from the barrel, and set it to simmer.
He rubbed salt over a portion of the meat and hung it in the yard to dry under the hot sun, turning it into jerky so it wouldn't spoil.
After working for a while, the python soup was finally ready.
Wei He didn't take the pot off the fire. Instead, he kept it warm over hot coals. He scooped out a spoonful, blew on it to cool it down, and took a small taste.
A faint gamey flavor mixed with salt, ginger, and green onions filled his mouth.
"It still smells a bit wild, but it is really fresh."
He quickly fished out a pile of thick meat chunks to cool off. Then he went to the inner room, mixed some chili peppers and spices, and made a dipping sauce.
The python was huge, and its meat was thick.
Wei He dipped the large chunks into the sauce and stuffed them into his stomach. It was spicy, numbing, savory, and incredibly satisfying.
If he hadn't run out of charcoal, he would have made a second pot right then and there.
By the time he finished, he guessed he had eaten three pounds of pure snake meat.
For a python over two meters long, that was just a tiny slice of its body.
The rest would be enough to feed him for a long time.
He felt warm and comfortable all over. Leaning back in a cane chair under the shade of a tree in the yard, he picked up a cup of cassia seed tea and took a slow sip.
"This snake meat tastes great. Too bad it's not a spirit beast, so it doesn't give an instant boost like the flowered deer meat. But it should still be way better than normal farm animals. With a snake this long, if I eat it slowly, it'll last me half a month."
Having tasted the sweet rewards of hunting, Wei He felt full of energy. He planned to rest a bit, test the waters, and then head out to hunt again.
Of course, before going out next time, he had to find out what the Shaoyang Sect was doing after losing one of their members.
He rested for a bit.
Then, Wei He pulled out the small booklet he had looted from the bag and read it carefully.
The booklet recorded a martial art technique called the Five Ridge Palm.
This technique trained the hands. According to the text, when mastered, both palms would become as hard as iron, and a simple strike could leave deep handprints in metal.
The Five Ridge Palm was really just another name for the Five Finger Mountain Palm.
Wei He flipped through the details and found that the manual was incomplete. It was missing the special medicinal bath formula needed for breakthroughs.
"Makes sense. Who would be dumb enough to carry a full manual on them? This part focuses on moves and forms, which they probably kept around to practice. If it were me, I'd definitely keep the secret recipe somewhere else too."
Wei He understood.
Without the recipe, the technique wasn't worth much.
For external martial arts, practicing without the right medicine would only leave you crippled or injured over time.
He put the booklet down and moved to check the rest of his loot.
Suddenly, a thought struck him.
'This technique... doesn't need a core diagram. It only needs the medicinal bath during breakthroughs. Does that mean my Break-Through Bead can replace the medicine and force a breakthrough on its own?' The moment he thought of this, his heart pounded violently.
If this worked, then this Five Ridge Palm technique was the absolute best fit for him!
That was because this martial art didn't demand as much blood energy as the Huishan Punch, even though its bottleneck was much harder to cross.
Suppressing his excitement, he kept looking through the rest of the items.
Aside from a bunch of powder bags, the best items were a pair of metal gloves and a money pouch.
The metal gloves only had iron plates over the knuckles while the rest was leather, but that was plenty.
With a little repair, they would give him a huge edge in a normal fight, unless he ran into someone else using hand spikes like he did.
He tossed the gloves aside and checked the money pouch. Inside was a stack of gold notes stamped with a lotus pattern.
Printed across the notes were four large words: Money Commands The Gods.
Wei He pulled one out and felt it between his fingers.
This was his first time holding a gold note. When he took jobs from other places, he had always asked for grain or meat instead.
"This isn't paper... is this... some kind of animal skin?" Wei He was surprised to find that the gold note was made from hide, and it carried a subtle, sweet scent.
"This thing might have its own special use... not just as a stand-in for gold..." Wei He guessed.
He pulled out all the gold notes and counted them. To his shock, there was a whole thousand taels here.
Based on what Cheng Shaojiu had told him about the trade rates for white rice in the inner city, a thousand taels of gold was a massive fortune.
He noticed that at the bottom of every note were the words: Issued by Damao Bank.
He just didn't know where this Damao Bank was located.
After getting such a huge reward, Wei He finally understood why there were so many bandits around these days.
Robbing one rich target could yield this much cash and gear. If someone could pull this off every day, the wealth would be beyond imagination.
Still, he knew that even if he went to Mount Shaoyang every day, he wouldn't always run into a fat target carrying this kind of cash.
After packing away the money and bags, Wei He rested up, feeling fully satisfied.
From that day on, he kept hunting, but he changed his spots. He stayed away from Mount Shaoyang and hunted in other areas around Feiye City instead.
A month passed like this.
His daily routine consisted of hunting in the morning, then coming home in the afternoon to train his martial arts and refine his skin.
At the same time, whenever he had free time, he began practicing the moves from the Five Ridge Palm manual.
With plenty of meat to feed his body, the growth of his Break-Through Bead sped up again. It soon reached three-tenths full.
His blood energy was also slowly forming the solid shape of his first Nine-Cloud Flower.
However, compared to the speed of the Break-Through Bead, his progress in refining his skin and forming the flower was still far too slow.
Wei He could tell from Old Zheng's face during checkups that his blood energy growth was disappointing.
At first, Old Zheng had treated Wei He like a rare genius because of how fast he made his first breakthrough. But...
As time went on, Wei He's true talent began to show.
His blood energy growth speed was nowhere near that of a real genius.
To be precise, his speed was less than one-third of what Xiao Ran had shown back then.
This caused the high hopes in Old Zheng's heart to slowly turn into disappointment.
Luckily, just as the old man was feeling down, things took a turn with Xiao Ran.
"Master Zheng!! Xiao Ran... Xiao Ran is...!!"
On this day, inside the inner yard, Wei He was listening closely as Old Zheng explained combat tricks for the Stone Skin stage of the Huishan Punch.
Suddenly, Jiang Su dashed inside, panting for breath as she yelled, "Xiao Ran, he recovered!!"
"Xiao Ran? Recovered??" Master Zheng froze for a second before asking in disbelief.
"Did you just say Xiao Ran recovered? What did he recover? Tell me clearly!"
Jiang Su glanced at Wei He and Zhao Hong standing nearby, then turned her full attention back to Master Zheng.
She said every word with total seriousness, "Master Zheng, Xiao Ran got his Stone Skin blood energy back! He is out in the outer yard right now. I just tested him myself, and there's no doubt about it. It's definitely second-stage blood energy! I'm sure of it!"
Master Zheng stood glued to the spot. After listening to her, he pulled at his beard, unable to speak for a long moment.
The disappointment he felt over Wei He's slow progress suddenly flared back up into a fresh spark of hope.
He tugged at his beard repeatedly, paced back and forth a few times, and then stopped.
"Let's go! Take me to see him!"
"Yes!" Jiang Su took the lead right away, guiding Master Zheng out toward the outer yard.
They left Senior Brother Zhao Hong and Wei He standing where they were.
The two traded a look, then quickly followed behind.
Even though neither of them liked Xiao Ran as a person, they had to admit the guy possessed terrifying talent.
When Wei He arrived at the outer yard, he saw a crowd of disciples gathered around Xiao Ran in a tight circle.
Master Zheng stood right across from him.
The old master placed his hand on Xiao Ran's shoulder, closing his eyes as if sensing the energy inside him.
Moments later, Master Zheng slowly opened his eyes.
"He actually... fully recovered!" His hands trembled as he patted Xiao Ran on the shoulder over and over.
"You... did very well!"
