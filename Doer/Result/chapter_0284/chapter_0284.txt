Wei He watched with his own eyes as the bloody hole in Song Ting's abdomen closed up at a speed visible to the naked eye, turning into a thin line of red, stopping the bleeding.
Although it did not fully heal, this level of muscle control was enough to ensure he would not be dragged down by blood loss or poison before the fight ended.
Yes, Wei He knew very well that under such a technique of controlling muscles and skin, his potent poison could hardly play any role.
Because poison, no matter what, always needs a medium to spread and diffuse.
For a human body, the usual medium is blood, the circulating force.
To ordinary masters, this might be extremely fatal.
But to Insight-level powerhouses, their flesh and inner force had already transcended the realm of ordinary people.
Coupled with their extremely high resistance.
Wei He had already verified this fact back then from Zhu Chen.
Secondly, the muscle control ability Song Ting displayed was enough to cut off many blood vessels around the wound and keep the poison from spreading.
"Impressive!" Song Ting let out a long sigh. "I haven't been injured in a long time. I didn't expect that coming down the mountain today, I would actually get hurt..."
He looked with pity as Wei He charged at him.
"Without any moves or realm, just relying on a crude pile-up of strength and speed effects, you managed to reach this level. Your talent is truly terrifying... What a pity... If only you hadn't made an enemy of my Wushi Sect, how wonderful that would have been."
He truly felt a surge of appreciation for talent.
If he could take a genius like Wei He as a disciple and pass down everything he had learned in his life.
Just think, with Wei He's terrifying talent, combined with his own powerful realm of actual flow, and the deep cultivation of the Returning Truth force.
If Wei He could break through Insight, even within the Three Main Peaks, he might one day touch that legendary dream realm in the future.
"What a pity..." For the sake of the sect's interests, the more hostile a genius Wei He was, the more thoroughly he had to be taken down.
After all, Su Zhao still needed the poison locked in his body to be lifted.
"Xiange, make your move." Song Ting finally let go of his pride and asked Song Xiange to join forces with him.
This was the first time in decades, ever since stepping into Settled Insight, that he had teamed up with someone to deal with a guy who wasn't even at the Insight level.
"Wouldn't it have been better to do this sooner?" Song Xiange raised his arm and began to unwrap the bandages around it.
Song Ting made the exact same move.
With a shake of their inner force, the bandages on both men's arms instantly came loose, exposing the muscle and skin underneath.
Marked clearly on that skin were identical symbol-like imprints.
"Shadow Ending Step, Second Stage. Watch out!" Song Ting dodged Wei He's charge and turned to stand firm. As the bandages were removed, his already bulky frame swelled and expanded even further with rushing blood.
Inner force rapidly spun and surged around his legs.
Boom!!
The ground suddenly exploded into a large pit, and Song Ting and Song Xiange vanished from the spot at the same time.
"Secret Kill: Empty Cloud Flying Crane!!"
Both of them moved one after the other, their Returning Truth force naturally condensing into the shape of a white crane spreading its wings to fly.
Accompanying the blurred form of the white crane force was a terrifyingly sharp and piercing Returning Truth force.
The Returning Truth force, which was already terrifyingly lethal, seemed to gain true vitality at this very moment.
It felt as though it had truly come alive, turning into two real white cranes, genuine living creatures.
Their bodies wrapped inside the white crane force, they dashed toward Wei He in an instant.
Their hands acted as the white crane's wings, simultaneously flapping down from both sides toward Wei He's body.
"If you want me dead, you'll have to die too!!!"
Wei He roared, feeling a fatal crisis.
The strength these two erupted with in an instant had surpassed the Three-Headed Civet, bringing him a threat that felt even stronger than You Fu!
He knew this had to be a special secret technique that only Insight-level fighters could use, capable of drastically boosting actual combat power in an instant.
Knowing it was one thing, but he had seen secret techniques used by Insight-level experts before.
Zhu Chen had used one back then, and it hadn't seemed this powerful, had it?
Could this be a unique, true ultimate strike belonging only to the Wushi Sect?
Two deadly threats rushed toward him rapidly. In the split second of a spark, Wei He roared and used his very last trump card.
Ocean-Covering Force and the Poison Righteousness Dharma Manual, all supported by the Whale Flood Technique, operated and contracted at full power.
He had never imagined that there would come a day when he couldn't react simply because his opponent's speed was too fast.
Right at this moment, he truly understood how those past opponents, whose speed was inferior to his, felt when fighting him.
Invisible!
Untouchable!
Unable to perceive anything!
He could only feel goosebumps rising across his skin, his heart contracting violently, and his blood flowing at full capacity, frantically reinforcing his body's defense everywhere in an attempt to block everything.
"I won't die!!!"
Wei He roared in his heart.
The inner force inside his body suddenly condensed into a bizarre pattern model and exploded instantly.
"Secret Technique: Myriad Green Extreme Profound Force!!!"
The secret technique he had cultivated in the past as his last line of defense was finally put to use at this moment.
This secret move could drastically strengthen the power of his entire body's inner force while increasing its piercing and sharp lethal damage.
Originally, this was a secret technique meant for offense, but Wei He had no other choice left.
He gathered the power of the secret technique, channeling it all over his body to defend against any possible point of attack.
Puff! Puff!!
Finally, two soft sounds rang out.
The figures of Song Ting and Song Xiange appeared simultaneously on the left and right sides of Wei He.
Their right fists landed with pinpoint accuracy on both sides of Wei He's neck.
Massive Returning Truth force poured crazily into Wei He's body, breaking through his protective force, rushing inside, and destroying every tissue it touched.
Yet, under the Ascending Tower state, the inner force inside Wei He's body was varied and diverse. With all effects turned on to maximum, layer upon layer of obstruction caused the two men's force to sink into mud, capable of damaging only a portion of his blood vessels and muscles before losing all further momentum.
Instead, because they had forcefully broken into Wei He's protective force, the sharp piercing effects enhanced by his secret technique cut into the skin and flesh of their own forearms.
A dense mass of sharp bloody holes appeared on both of their forearms.
"Fall back!!"
The two rapidly retreated, their expressions changing drastically. They felt a thread of numbness and itching crawling up their forearms toward their bodies.
Hurriedly controlling their muscles to close their blood vessels, they reached out and dug out those patches of flesh to prevent the poison from spreading.
"Impressive... truly as hard to handle as a hedgehog." Song Xiange had been watching from the sidelines earlier and hadn't truly felt what Song Ting experienced.
Now that he had stepped into the fight himself, he understood why Song Ting had gone back and forth with Wei He for so many rounds.
It wasn't that they couldn't beat him, but that they didn't dare to get hurt.
"What kind of cultivation art does this kid practice anyway? Such heavy poison? Even we can't take it. If this were someone below the Insight level..." Song Xiange now clearly understood why Wei He had been able to instantly kill the inner court head in just a few moves.
Thud.
Wei He dropped to one knee, blood seeping from both sides of his neck, leaving the flesh mangled and blurred.
Had it not been for the sharp, thorn-like force enhanced by his secret technique, his cervical vertebrae would probably have been broken straight through, killing him on the spot.
The spot the opponents chose was also extremely tricky; they didn't go for the harder skull, but instead chose to sever the connection between the head and the body, which was the neck bone.
The neck bone was much easier to break than the skull, and even if it didn't break, it easily cut off blood vessels and caused a person to lose consciousness.
"Die!!" Fighting to this point, Wei He's eyes turned crimson as he stood up and charged at the two men first.
The secret technique was still taking effect, and taking advantage of this moment meant he was at the peak of his power.
So...
"Leave!"
Unfortunately, Song Ting and Song Xiange did not grant his wish. With ugly expressions, they retreated without fighting, leaping backward rapidly to distance themselves.
The injuries on their bodies were not heavy, but if they stayed and looked at Wei He's aggressive stance, if the fight continued, even if they could crush him, they would end up heavily wounded themselves.
By that time... their conditions might not hold out long enough to make it back up the mountain before mutating.
Weighing the gains and losses just a little, they reached a decision.
The two turned and left without the slightest hesitation.
If they were martial artists with an intense sense of sect honor, they might have chosen to stay right then, risking heavy injuries just to trap the enemy.
However, they had trained bitterly for so many years, and to throw it all away just for two inner court heads was impossible, whether viewed from the perspective of sect interests or personal survival.
Therefore, retreating was the only choice.
Besides, on the other side, someone else was still present.
The two vanished in the blink of an eye.
In the forest on the other side, a tall and bulky figure slowly walked out.
Bald, one-eyed, with a gentle temperament—it was none of other than You Fu, the Nest-Destroying Alliance leader who had sparked this entire incident.
You Fu wore a wide cloak, his gaze complicated as he stared at Wei He, whose neck was bleeding.
"Wei He, after today's battle, you can truly be considered to have entered the view of the Wushi Sect's higher-ups..."
"Congratulations." He raised the wine flask in his hand, offering a distant toast.
"Congratulations to me?" Wei He turned around. His three-meter-tall Ascending Tower state made him look like a terrifying, ferocious beast at this moment.
The blood spots spattering down his neck from his wound added even more to his fierce aura and killing intent.
"I said back then that the favor I owe you will only be repaid on the premise that it does not harm my relatives or any of my other interests."
Wei He's bell-shaped eyes stared fixedly at You Fu.
"Now I have done even more than expected. So."
He reached out a hand.
Bang!
His palm clenched into a fist.
"I'll return Su Zhao to you. The supplies left behind in the sect will be sent to the alliance to clear the debt of this favor. Everything else between you and me is settled!"
You Fu looked complexly at Wei He. He had originally intended to come and offer timely assistance, but he never expected that Wei He, entirely on his own, would drive away two First-Settlement Insight experts from the Wushi Sect.
Even though it was mutual exhaustion, this kind of record... had already surpassed Zhu Chen, the Three-Headed Civet, and Shangguan Ji, placing him truly on a level close to his own.
To reach such a realm with a mere Visceral Training cultivation.
He had watched very clearly from the side: a Wei He operating at full power was definitely not a Blood-Corrupted entity.
He was a genuine, real martial arts genius.
All of Wei He's strength was built upon inner force and martial arts secret techniques.
As for the traits unique to Blood-Corrupted beings—innate divine strength, copper skin, and iron bones—he didn't have a single one.
The trace of godly strength erupted by the Whale Flood Technique didn't even come close to the level of a Blood-Corrupted being.
"If I said I never expected things to develop this way from the start, would you believe me?" You Fu sighed.
"What do you think?" Wei He didn't waste any more words, turning around and striding away.
He was afraid that if he didn't leave soon, once the secret technique faded, the side effects would kick in, and combined with his fresh wounds, he might plunge into a state of weakness.
And to fall into weakness in front of You Fu, he could not guarantee his own safety.
"After this battle, as long as you don't actively provoke them again, the Wushi Sect won't come looking for trouble with you anymore. That bunch of mountain hermits—none of them are willing to fight to the death with us folks down the mountain. Wei He, you..."
Before You Fu finished speaking, Wei He's back was already out of sight. His voice trailed off into a sudden silence.
He had miscalculated. He hadn't expected Wei He to be willing to completely disband the Myriad Poison Sect, cutting himself off entirely from the vortex of conflict and escaping.
As the master of the Myriad Poison Sect, Wei He had proven his strength. If the Wushi Sect wanted to take him down, either higher-tier powerhouses had to make a move, or the sect itself would suffer heavy tendon and bone damage.
Right now was the critical period of the Dragon Blood contest and the Taihu Dao Discussion; the Wushi Sect simply couldn't afford to be distracted. The local forces they could deploy would not exceed the scope of Song Ting and the other.
Therefore, relying on himself, Wei He had carved out a path of survival for the Myriad Poison Sect.
"What a pity..." You Fu let out a long sigh, turning around and quietly fading away into the shadows.
