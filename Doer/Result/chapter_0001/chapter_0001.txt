The sky was dim white, completely clear of clouds.
Feiye City, Yun Province, Great Yuan.
Screech!
A bird with white feathers and a black beak flapped its wings, flying over the land and diving downward.
It passed over the thick, old city walls and towers. The bird slowed down, landing softly on the highest dark-red tower in the city. It preened its feathers while looking down.
Inside the city were groups of gray buildings and different towers. Pale yellow houses and courtyards with curved roofs were everywhere.
Right in the middle sat a huge square plaza.
The ground was made of wide stone bricks over a meter across. Large, clear patterns of strange beasts were carved into them.
This open space was used as a daily market. Right now, it was packed with crowds of people who had come to watch and listen.
In the center of the square, on a crude wooden stage, a priest in white robes with gray hair held a whisk and spoke in a loud voice.
"Those who study deeply, read this ten times, and the five emperors will guard you..."
"You students still have bad air inside you, your body is not yet pure..."
"Oh! Here it comes!!" someone whispered in the crowd.
Soon, another priest came forward holding a wooden bowl. He pulled out handfuls of copper coins, walked a few steps, stopped, and threw them down toward the crowd.
He did not throw many, but it still made the people below bend down to grab them.
Wei He squeezed through the crowd, looking from afar at the old priest teaching on the high stage.
He was 1.7 meters tall, with light fuzz on his upper lip and a young, plain face. He wore a set of short gray hemp clothes and black cloth shoes that were still in decent shape.
Looking at the old priest on the stage, he paused for a brief second. Then, carrying the fried oil cake he had bought, he walked toward home.
Pushing through the crowd, he had to endure all kinds of bad smells coming from around him.
Sweat, smelly feet, bad breath, and even a faint stink of waste.
Wei He looked down, saw a stepped-on pile of dog poop on his right, and picked up his pace.
He left the square, turned into a narrow street, and passed through several alleys filled with yellow dust and garbage.
Soon, Wei He returned to his only home in this city.
The home sat in a small alley, just one room in a row of broken, run-down shacks.
Outside the door hung old clothes to dry, showing faint differences in color between men's and women's.
Wei He moved a broken wooden stool blocking the door and stepped into the dark room.
"Second Sister, I am back," he called out.
In the corner of the room, a thin woman stood up.
Her sleeves were rolled up as she washed clothes. Her hands were wet. She used her forearm to push back her hair, looking at Wei He with a smile.
"Why are you back so early today, Xiao He?"
"The work finished early, so I came back first," Wei He explained.
"Is the repair work on the Mu family's main house done too?" She walked to the window. The faint light fell on her, showing a somewhat neat face. Though her skin was rough, her figure was shapely and nice.
Her name was Wei Ying, Wei He's second sister.
Wei He had an elder sister named Wei Chun, who was the main pillar of the family. She worked in the Black Water Gang in the city.
This was the main reason the Wei family could live in relative peace around here.
"Finished long ago. I even helped them fix a few guest rooms nearby," Wei He replied.
He had been in this world for several months. He went from feeling lost at first to accepting it calmly, and now to working hard every day just to get by. It had only taken him three months.
Three months was fast.
Wei He's parents were still alive, and he had two elder sisters.
His big sister Wei Chun treated him very well, his second sister Wei Ying was also good to him, and his parents saved all the best things for him.
Because people favored sons over daughters and the family was too poor for birth control, his parents had two daughters before finally having Wei He.
This made the whole family value him deeply.
"Big Sister is not back yet. Little brother, be careful when you go out these days. When I went out to find sewing work earlier, I heard another home was robbed in the middle of the night," Wei Ying said in a low voice, her eyes full of worry.
"Not back yet? How much did that family lose?" Wei He's expression changed slightly.
"Half of the pay they just got. What was left is not enough for them to live on... And in the Rat Alley across from us, someone was robbed yesterday too. They fought back a little and lost a finger."
"Stolen in the night again? Didn't those thieves leave recently?" Wei He asked.
"I do not know... Maybe a new gang came to fight for territory." Wei Ying sighed, picked up a piece of clothing, took out a needle and thread, and started sewing.
"Anyway, when you are outside, endure everything and be careful. If you run into robbers, just give them what you have. The family is still here, so we will not let you starve." Wei Ying warned him seriously.
"I know," Wei He nodded.
His expression was somewhat blank.
Coming to this world and being reborn as Wei He, an ordinary young boy, he was not completely without help.
It was just that this help could not be activated right now.
It was a bead, or rather, the mark of a bead printed right in the middle of his chest.
From the faint memories left in his mind, the bead was called the Realm Breaking Bead.
Its ability was simple.
It broke through martial art realms.
When it gathered enough energy and his own body reached its limit, it could help him break through a level of a martial skill.
He knew this because when he crossed over, the bead left a bit of information in his head, like a planted memory.
As for whether it was real, that remained to be seen.
After the Realm Breaking Bead gathered enough energy, it would change from a skin-like clear color to pitch black.
Right now, even though three months had passed, Wei He still had not seen the mark turn black.
At most, the color had darkened just a little.
Makes sense. Right now, he barely ate enough to fill his stomach each meal. He had no extra energy to save up for the bead.
The energy this bead needed came from his daily meals, taking extra food and turning it into vital energy to slowly absorb.
At first, Wei He held onto hope. But after three months passed, the excitement brought by hope had slowly turned into calm acceptance.
He lay on the bed with his clothes on to rest. There were only three beds in the house: one for his parents, one shared by his two sisters, and one for him.
In a house smaller than sixty square meters, three beds had to squeeze into one spot. The rest of the space held the stove, table, and chairs, making it very crowded.
Yet this was already a middle-class income for the common people around here.
After sleeping for a short while, Wei He got up, planning to walk around outside.
He had the Realm Breaking Bead. He wanted to find a decent martial art to train in, then use the bead to break through and grow stronger.
But right now, he could not find any martial arts at all. Big Sister was in the Black Water Gang only because she was ruthless, wild, and hit hard.
And the Realm Breaking Bead showed no movement at all.
Feeling helpless, Wei He walked around the city every day after finishing work.
He was small and wore old clothes, looking like he had no money, so no one tried to cause trouble for him.
When he got up, Second Sister Wei Ying was already asleep on the only table.
Wei He left quietly, shut the door, and walked right out of the alley.
Cough, cough...
Just as he stepped out of the alley, a burst of coughing came from the right side outside.
He looked up and saw Sister Zhang, a neighbor who often looked for work with Second Sister Wei Ying. She held her mouth with her hand, head down, coughing constantly.
"Xiao He, stand back." Someone pulled him from the side.
The person pulling him was a short old man holding a pipe.
He wore a white shirt that smelled of sweat. He pulled Wei He out of the alley mouth and took a few more steps away.
Then he lowered his voice, his face looking bad.
"A sickness has spread in the city recently... That Zhang family girl got it somehow. Ever since she came back a few days ago, she has been coughing non-stop. You must be careful not to catch it. Tell your second sister to be careful too."
"Thank you, Grandpa Wang." Wei He felt a chill in his heart. He had heard about the plague earlier. They said it was spreading in the north of the city, but now it had reached his side...
During these three months, he had tried hard to use his modern thinking as a cross-world traveler to help his family make money.
Sadly, every attempt ended in failure.
No matter the method, everything in this city came down to one point.
Gangs.
The lower areas were divided into territories, with different gangs running different places.
Whether opening a shop or running a small business, you had to pay the gangs. Without a strong backer, the moment your business did a little well, you would be targeted.
The result was either handing over the business and working for them, or paying a huge sum every month and earning only a bit of hard effort money.
This was a bad age where any common person who had a little money would be targeted.
Wei He did not stay. He left the alley quickly and passed through the small paths in the city. Soon, he reached an old gray courtyard.
Clear sounds of reading came from the courtyard, mixed with the low scolding of an old teacher.
Above the main gate hung a board with three red words: Scripture Hall.
Wei He walked along the wall, found a good spot on the ground, sat down, and leaned against the wall to listen quietly.
The Scripture Hall was where rich families in the city sent their children to learn.
It was said that the teachers inside were all scholars who had passed official exams.
"I have mastered most of the words here. In any place, words and knowledge are the true foundation of control."
Wei He looked down and used his finger to write on the dusty ground, practicing the words he had learned before.
"Wei He!" Suddenly, a child with pigtails ran up to him quietly and called out.
"This is today's work." The pigtail child pulled out a roll of paper hidden in his shirt and threw it into Wei He's arms.
"Same rule, half a silver coin for a hundred words," Wei He said calmly. Silver was not worth much these days. If he did not charge more, he could not save any money.
"Fine, fine, fine! Hurry up, the teacher wants it tomorrow morning." The pigtail child lowered his voice, took out a piece of silver from his shirt, threw it to Wei He, and ran off quickly.
This was doing homework for others.
It was a small business Wei He picked up while secretly learning here.
The pigtail child was named Li Bian, the only son of a rich merchant in Feiye City who spent money freely.
Wei He put away the silver and did the math.
"This way, I am close to having enough money to learn Mountain Back Fist from Old Man Zheng."
Since he had a cheat code, he would not give up on finding a way to activate it.
The information from the Realm Breaking Bead said it broke through martial art realms. Wei He had tried training in basic fighting moves under his big sister, but it was useless.
Later, from his big sister, he learned that a small group of people in the world held a special method called internal training.
Those people were the real masters of the world. Just like in the Black Water Gang where his big sister worked, there were experts like that.
However, internal training methods were not passed on easily. Wei He had searched for over a month through his big sister's connections before finding a place nearby that taught it, which was Old Man Zheng's place.
But the fee for that place was not cheap.
