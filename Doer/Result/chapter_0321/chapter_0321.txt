After receiving his share of the sect's resources, Wei He was kicked out of Mo Ming Temple by Yao Wan. He quickly got to work on the Thousand Mountains Ten Thousand Locks Skill.
This basic manual of the Lock Mountain branch was also called the Xuan Lock Skill.
Yao Wan had handed him a roster. It listed all the official senior brothers, sisters, and elders of the Lock Mountain branch.
He was expected to memorize every single one. Interestingly, the hierarchy in Xuan Miao Sect was not based on raw strength, but strictly on when you joined.
Wei He found his own name on the list, placed in the fourth generation. Right above him were Yuan Duzi and the rest of the third generation.
Above them were the three founding ancestors of the three branches, who made up the second generation.
Leaving Mo Ming Temple, they passed through the same pitch-black zone as before. Guided by Yao Wan, Wei He stood once again before the Xuan Cha Jade Wall.
"We start here."
Yao Wan stood by the jade wall and snapped her fingers.
Snap.
The scenery around them trembled slightly, as if something had just twisted out of shape.
Wei He noticed two new side paths branching off to the left and right.
Where there used to be only one path leading into the inner mountain, there were now three.
"This...?" He blinked, wondering if his eyes were playing tricks on him.
"You aren't seeing things. This is the real inner mountain that you will normally use," Yao Wan said coldly.
"Inner mountain disciples usually stick to these three paths. The middle one leads to your quarters. You can figure out the other two on your own."
"Senior Sister... didn't you say newcomers are only allowed in Mo Ming Temple?" Wei He asked, surprised.
"That's right. All of this counts as Mo Ming Temple. You couldn't go anywhere else even if you wanted to," Yao Wan replied casually. "Alright, use your token to claim your room."
She pointed to the middle path.
The stone steps stretched straight up the mountain, bordered by wooden railings on both sides. Bright light filled the air, birds sang, flowers bloomed, and thick mist drifted around. It looked like a perfect hideaway for a hermit.
Wei He looked up and saw Taoists moving back and forth on the stone steps. Their movements were lightning fast, vanishing in the blink of an eye.
Nobody was taking a slow walk up there.
Since it was his first time here, he decided to take it step by step, taking in his surroundings as he climbed.
"Senior Sister..." He wanted to ask something else, but when he turned around, Yao Wan was already gone.
She had slipped away without making a sound.
Wei He shook his head inwardly. Why did everyone in this sect love coming and going like ghosts? Every one of them seemed a bit... casual.
He felt that was the best word for it.
Yuan Duzi was like this, and so was Yao Wan. They just threw the necessary items and files at him, as if saying, "Here, take this and go cultivate," before turning around and running off.
Clearing his mind of idle thoughts, Wei He climbed the steps one by one.
The staircase was short, only about a hundred steps.
Along the way, empty martial arts grounds lined both sides. People had planted fruit trees near the path, complete with signs warning against picking the fruit.
Some of the railings had words carved into them.
Things like: "The Buddha Poisoner was here," "Jiu Fang Sect's Shen Que leaves a mark here," and "The Five Heroes of Qi Mountain swore brotherhood right here."
Reaching the top of the steps, Wei He waved his sleeve and sent out a burst of force, pushing back the cloud of mist in front of him.
Directly ahead was a massive, brownish-yellow cliff face that looked just like a giant honeycomb.
The cliff wall was full of small cave openings. Some were blocked off with stones, while others remained wide open, cold wind blowing continuously through their dark interiors.
From a distance, Wei He could see people stepping in and out of these hollow spots from time to time.
This was clearly the living area for the inner mountain disciples of Xuan Miao Sect.
"..." He was completely speechless.
He pulled out his token. The characters on it had shifted into a twisting script that read "Xuan Miao." The two characters wriggled across the surface, forming an arrow that pointed toward a spot ahead.
Wei He leaped into the air and dashed toward the arrow's direction. Soon, he reached a cave opening on the cliff face.
Inside, it was pitch black. There was a bed, a desk, an oil lamp, a bookshelf, and a single chair. Nothing else.
The entire cave was only about ten square meters. It was tight and cramped. Aside from sleeping and meditating, you couldn't do anything else here.
A slip of paper was stuck to the wall nearby.
'Tao follows nature. If you want something, make it yourself.' "...This place... really is casual..." Wei He thought, totally speechless.
Though, maybe this wasn't all bad.
Wei He suddenly understood why the sect allowed each of them to bring two personal guards inside.
He looked at the rough floor and the few piles of bird droppings scattered around. He shook his head without a word.
Since he was here, he had to make the best of it.
He flicked his wrist, sending out a wave of force that swept across the ground, rapidly cleaning up the trash, dust, and loose stones.
After a thorough sweep, the inside of the cave looked much cleaner. It then occurred to Wei He that he needed a block of stone to use as a door.
He walked out of the cave and looked around until he found a slope of hardened lava.
Using his bare hands, he dug out a large block of volcanic rock. He trimmed and shaped it until it looked enough like a door, then brought it back to install it. It would have to do for now.
He could leave the details to his guards later.
Back inside the cave.
Wei He blocked the entrance with the rock door, lit the oil lamp, and pulled out the manual Yao Wan had given him to study it closely.
The Xuan Lock Skill, also known as the Thousand Mountains Ten Thousand Locks Skill, was a strange and unique art.
Although it was listed as a basic foundation manual, practicing it could constantly revitalize one's force, slowly converting all the force in one's body into true Realized Force.
It also continuously boosted the body's natural resistances while offering a slight bump to overall defense.
However, this manual provided almost no boost to strength, speed, or combat power.
In other words, even if you mastered it completely, the manual clearly stated that it offered very few special effects for actual fighting.
If you wanted to kill an opponent, you had to rely on secondary martial techniques, specific combat moves, or innate abilities.
Wei He had received the full manual, containing all twelve layers.
The sect didn't seem worried about the manual leaking out.
He studied it for a bit and guessed there must be some anti-theft mechanism he didn't know about yet.
Putting all distractions aside, Wei He focused completely and began trying to cultivate the Xuan Lock Skill.
The very first line for entering the Xuan Lock Skill was: Open your hyper-sense and absorb true qi.
According to the manual, true qi was the aura of the real world.
The true qi in the outside world was filled with heavy impurities and mixed with ghost winds. But inside a blessed land like Mo Ming Temple, one could slowly breathe in true qi and draw it into the body to strengthen oneself.
'I remember back in my past life, people said true qi was built up from within through practice. I didn't expect that here, true qi is actually absorbed from the outside world.' Wei He sighed inwardly. After skimming through the entire manual once, he realized he could only understand the first few layers. The deeper he read, the less sense it made. By the end, it turned into total gibberish.
The text degraded into a chaotic mess of squiggly lines that didn't look like words at all.
He figured this was probably the anti-theft system at work.
Without wasting any time, he sat cross-legged on the bed. Following the entry steps for the Xuan Lock Skill, he focused his mind, calmed his breathing, adjusted his pulse, and activated the hyper-sense in his left hand.
Suddenly, Wei He felt a spark in his heart.
The hyper-sense in his left hand opened up.
After practicing hyper-sense for so long, he could now maintain this state for short periods.
Usually, he could hold it for about an hour at a time.
Nourishing the hyper-sense required a constant flow of force to moisten and stimulate the nerves.
Before this, he had used his own mixed forces.
Now, he switched over to the Seven Subtle True Force he had just brought to full mastery.
Pure Seven Subtle True Force slowly seeped into his left palm.
A cool sensation, like shedding a layer of heavy clothes, washed over his heart directly from his left hand.
He closed his eyes and paid close attention to the changes in his left hand.
He temporarily shut off all other senses.
Only the nerves in his left hand remained active, constantly sending back a flood of sensory signals.
'It really is different from the outside world!' Wei He spotted the difference right away.
There was no ghost wind.
Not even a trace of it here.
He raised his left hand and gave it a gentle wave.
It felt like moving his hand through a cool breeze. The hyper-sensitive nerves in his left hand didn't feel any stinging pain or damage.
It felt incredibly comfortable.
This was Wei He's first time using hyper-sense inside a blessed land.
Now he finally understood why people outside fought to the death just to get into a place like this.
This feeling of peace was far too pleasant.
He opened his left hand and wiggled his fingers freely.
There was zero resistance. He didn't have to worry about the ghost wind freezing his hand solid. It felt just like normal life without hyper-sense.
After practicing for a while, Wei He felt his throat grow dry. Getting thirsty, he left the cave to find a nearby water supply.
It didn't take him long to locate one.
Someone had installed a water pipe there, and a sign standing next to it read: Water Station.
The water station sat inside a small grove of trees to the left of the honeycomb cliff.
When Wei He walked over, the area was dead quiet and completely empty. An old monkey was sitting right beside the pipe, carefully combing its hair.
Wei He stepped forward.
He twisted the pipe handle, but not a single drop came out.
He looked over at the old monkey, feeling puzzled.
This was clearly the water station, so why wasn't there any water?
The old monkey gave him a peaceful glance, completely unbothered, and went back to combing its hair with its fingers.
Wei He was speechless, but he didn't dare make a reckless move. A monkey living in the inner mountain was bound to be different from the ones outside. If it belonged to some senior brother or sister, causing trouble would be a massive headache.
He glanced around to see if he had missed a valve or switch.
Sure enough, he found a line of carved text on the stone wall to the right of the pipe.
'Tao follows nature. If fate wills it, the water will come.' Wei He: "..."
What kind of joke was this!
No water at all? Did they really think everyone was an immortal who could pull water out of thin air with a snap of their fingers?!
He turned on his heel and left to look for other places.
Next was the mess hall.
'Tao follows nature. If fate wills it, the food will cook itself.' A pile of raw meat and vegetables, hard as rock, sat stacked on the ground. They looked like they had been air-drying for ages.
Then came the clothing depot.
'Tao follows nature. If fate wills it, clothes will present themselves.' Inside a bamboo tray, a few fat white silkworms were lazily chewing on leaves.
A thoroughly speechless Wei He walked back to his cave.
He finally understood why there were so few people around here and why nobody stuck around.
Aside from practicing, everyone had probably run down the mountain to have fun in the outside world.
No water, no food, and only one set of clothes with no way to wash them. The longer you stayed here, the worse you smelled, and the hungrier you got.
Left with no choice, he had to make a trip out to have his family pack up some supplies, preparing everything he needed for food, clothing, shelter, and travel.
Then he went to Moral Palace to get an inner mountain guard token for Zhou Muqing, tasking her with handling his regular supply runs.
Only then did things finally settle down.
On top of that, he handed the job of picking the second guard over to Zhou Muqing as well.
He told her to gather a few candidates so he could make the final cut himself.
This time, he laid his cards on the table. He was going to use this spot as a bargaining chip in exchange for the resources he desperately needed.
Wei He put out the exact list of resources required for the next level of the Whale Flood Art, leaving it for them to hunt down.
The fourth layer of the Whale Flood Art required a special state known as 'Sky Meets the Sea.' To enter this state, one had to burn through oil made from the Black Line Whale.
