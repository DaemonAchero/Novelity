"Prime Blood..."
Wei He’s heart stirred. If it were anything else, he might be worried. But having practiced the Whale Flood Art, Prime Blood was the one thing he had in endless supply. If the Seven Wonders True Art really depended on Prime Blood, then this time...
After chatting with the three for a while, he took his leave and headed out to explore the surroundings.
Although the Xuanmiao Sect was not as grand as the Wushi Sect, it was still one of the major Daoist sects. Owning a blessed realm in a time of war like this was proof of its power.
Stepping out of the Moral Palace, he was surrounded by mountain forests. In the distance lay a sea of rain and fog. The sunlight filtering through the chilly mist seemed stripped of all warmth.
Around the Moral Palace, disciples dressed in Xuanmiao Sect robes walked back and forth. It looked lively on the surface. But unlike other places, the people here looked cold and calm. Even when acquaintances met, they only whispered a few words before quickly walking away.
Throughout the entire Xuanmiao Sect, mountain trails ran in every direction. On both sides of the roads, large ornamental stones were set up, carved with warning sayings in red ink. Below each quote was a name. Wei He had never heard of most of them, but they were likely past experts of the sect.
He faced up the mountain and walked along the path. But when he reached a checkpoint, he was stopped by a guard.
"To go up, show your inner mountain token."
The guard sat cross-legged on the side. He had been meditating with his eyes closed, opening them with a flat voice only when Wei He drew near.
"My apologies," Wei He nodded. "May I ask where I can send a letter?"
"Half a mile west of the Moral Palace, inside Dragon Bay," the Daoist answered calmly. Seeing that Wei He had no intention of pushing forward, he closed his eyes again.
Wei He bowed and stepped back.
Along the way, every person he saw seemed cold and detached, with almost no noise or clutter. This place was different from any sect he had seen before.
Standing on the steps leading up, he looked higher into the mountain. Wrapped in clouds and fog, the steps looked like a ladder to heaven, stretching beyond where his eyes could see.
Exhaling lightly, Wei He turned around and walked back toward the Moral Palace.
Soon, west of the palace, he found the place the guard mentioned: Dragon Bay.
Dragon Bay was a harbor within the inner gates of the Xuanmiao Sect. The place was bustling, filled with small stalls set up by the Daoists themselves, forming a small market.
On the semi-circular bay, there was also a golden beach.
On the sand, some Daoists had buried most of their bodies, seemingly training their arts. Others stood half-submerged in the sea, throwing punches. Some even stood with their hands behind their backs, roaring at the ocean, trying to look like high-minded masters.
Wei He could never understand the point of all that shouting. Did they not feel foolish? But then he remembered that many martial artists were influenced by their arts. Perhaps their minds were suppressed for too long and needed an outlet. It was understandable.
Walking a lap around Dragon Bay, Wei He quickly found a place for sending letters called the Swallow Post. It was a two-story stone building right by the beach.
Daoists constantly entered and exited the building, clearly there to send or receive mail.
Once inside, Wei He quickly wrote a letter to his family. He gave them the address here and told them to pack up quietly and make their way over. With the Support of the Wanfei Palace, he was not worried about their safety. Since Xia Linghua had placed a bet on him, he would protect them to protect his investment.
Besides, Wei He controlled many experts under the Wandu Sect. As long as a Realm Master did not show up to kill them, there would be no big issue.
Besides, it would take time for the Xiangqu Sect and the Luanshen Sect to learn that their two Realm Masters were dead. He had been extremely careful when dealing with the bodies.
Inside the stone building, after sending the letter, he saw emergency news notices posted on the wall.
"Excuse me, what are these for?" Wei He pointed to the paper slips on the wall.
"Oh, those are free updates about the outside world," the clerk folding paper behind the counter replied with a smile. "We live in the mountains, but we cannot be completely cut off from everything. To keep everyone informed about the changing world, the masters and ancestors of the inner mountain set up this free post."
"Can these updates be trusted?" Wei He asked, surprised.
"Of course. They are all verified. Most of them are not secrets anyway. It is just that because of the distance and poor communication, many people do not know. But locally, these events are common knowledge. No need to worry about truth or lies," the Daoist replied.
He answered smoothly, clearly used to being asked this.
Wei He understood. He turned around and stood in front of the wall, reading the notes alongside a few other Daoists.
One piece of news caught his eye immediately.
'March 15th: General Zhong Sunchong of the Wu Army, leading 150,000 troops, captured Yang Zhou. Governor Xu Han died in battle. The capital of Yang Zhou was sacked and washed in blood for three days.' 'Four True-Blood Generals of the Wu State, led by Gongsun Tan, surrounded the headquarters of the Xiangqu Sect, slaughtering over 3,000 members and declaring the Martial Ban once again.' 'Ten Thousand Layers Temple, a sacred Buddhist site, was burned and destroyed by troops, suspected to be the work of the Wu Army.' "The Wu State goes too far!" a Daoist nearby could not help but shout in anger.
"The Wu State is different from us. We were always like fire and water," another man sighed. "Now that Dayuan is weak, Wu is taking advantage. There is nothing to complain about."
"In the end, Wu focuses on Blood Corruption, backed by force. Their True-Blood system and our Dayuan Force system are not on the same path at all. They do not rely on resources the way we do. It is a pity. If this were a few centuries ago during the previous dynasty, when Dayuan warriors were at their peak, how could a small state like Wu dare to act so wild?"
Wei He’s mind stirred as he listened.
"May I ask, Senior Brother, what is the deal with this True-Blood system versus our Force system? Is True-Blood the same as the Blood Corruption system?"
The Daoist turned to look at Wei He.
"That is correct," he sighed and continued. "The True-Blood system is the higher realm of the Blood Corruption path. Wu and Dayuan walk completely different roads. They dig into the potential of their own bloodlines from start to finish. So in Wu, bloodlines define noble families, and noble families define social class. The hierarchy there is brutal. Your bloodline decides your entire future."
Wei He felt a chill. That was complete social stagnation.
It was the most extreme divide. The children of the high class had their bloodlines refined, growing stronger and holding onto the top. The descendants of the lower class stayed lower class forever, never able to catch a glimpse of the top.
"Our Force system is different," the Daoist explained. "Dayuan was built on martial arts. Foreign Species, Force, True Realm, one layer after another. Comprehension, bone structure, and innate blood energy, these three decide a person's potential and future. Compared to Wu, it is far more fair."
Wei He strongly agreed. That was true. He was an example of someone climbing up from being a commoner. And there were plenty of people like him around.
"However, even that does not work anymore," the Daoist’s face darkened. "The strength of Wu's True-Blood Generals is terrifying. When Yizhou’s Kong An city was slaughtered earlier, Tang Yan alone killed three Xiangqu Sect Realm Masters who joined forces to assassinate him."
"Did no stronger Realm Master step in?" Wei He frowned.
"Do you think Realm Masters grow on trees?" The Daoist shook his head. "Tang Yan is just one True-Blood in Wu. For this campaign, Wu has deployed eight True-Blood Generals. Among them is Supreme Commander Gongsun Ru."
He glanced at Wei He’s robe.
"You must be new. Come here more often in the future to keep an eye on the outside. Winds are rising out there. Wu’s ambition is clear, while local lords only care about themselves, even fighting among themselves. The future of Dayuan... sigh..."
He was in no mood to speak further, so he turned and walked away.
Wei He felt just as heavy-hearted. He did not know how giant sects like Xuanmiao Sect would handle themselves in this chaotic war, or what place they would occupy.
For some reason, he felt a sense of aimlessness about his future.
The blessed realms stood high above, chasing ultimate truths. But no matter what, they still belonged to Dayuan's Force system, and sat right at the top of it. Now, Wu’s Martial Ban was digging out the very roots of Dayuan’s Force system.
This meant that all the great sects of Dayuan would eventually clash with Wu.
Looking at the current state of affairs, Wu’s True-Blood system actually held the upper hand in direct head-on fights against Dayuan's Force system. It was hard to imagine how strong Wu truly was.
Having sent his letter, Wei He was no longer in the mood to wander around. He returned to the Moral Palace and went back to his own small courtyard.
He stayed in the yard for over ten days. Every day, besides practicing the Whale Flood Art, Wei He went to the Swallow Post to check on the situation outside.
After giving him over 10,000 pounds of raw strength, the third level of the Whale Flood Art slowly added a few thousand more pounds. Now, it had finally reached saturation.
Wei He did a complete calculation. So far, the three levels of the Whale Flood Art had given him just over 20,000 pounds of raw power. Added to his original physical strength after reaching the Organ Refining stage, his pure physical strength was close to 21,600 pounds.
It was roughly sixteen times what he had originally estimated.
Over 20,000 pounds of force was equal to over 10,000 kilograms, or more than ten tons. That was equivalent to a single giant boulder about two to three meters long, wide, and high.
It sounded impressive on paper, but in reality, it was just okay.
Wei He calmed his mind and resumed his previous experiments, trying to create a True Art suited for himself. This kind of attempt did not consume much energy from the Realm-Breaking Bead. The energy he used to push through the Whale Flood Art had already recovered.
Time flew by like running water.
In the peaceful life, the background check period soon passed.
"Third Yard, your background checks are done. Here are your technique manuals."
An old Daoist with white hair and a white beard arrived at the yard holding a stack of pale yellow books. He handed out the gear to the four of them: Wei He, Zhou Muqing, Liang Huyao, and Shangguan Xing.
"If there is anything you do not understand, you can go to the Ningxin Hall every seven days to attend lectures. Those who fail to enter the threshold within a year must leave the mountain. Once you enter the threshold, you must pay for your own expenses. If you need resources, go to the Dening Hall to accept tasks. There are plenty of missions there to earn what you need," the old Daoist explained calmly.
"All of you are restarting your cultivation, so I will not repeat the warnings. The Seven Wonders True Art must not be passed to outsiders. The rest depends on your own luck and skill."
"May I ask for Senior Brother's Daoist name?" Liang Huyao asked respectfully.
Standing right in front of them, this old man showed no signs of blood flow at all, looking like a dead body. It was clear he was no ordinary person.
"This humble Daoist is Qianhezi. Is there anything else you wish to ask?" the old man said calmly.
"Does Senior Brother know how many people are in our training batch?" Liang Huyao asked cautiously.
"All the newcomers who arrived in the past half year make up three yards. Each yard has four people. Do the math yourself," Qianhezi replied coldly.
"Does that mean we must outperform the others and reach a certain rank to..."
Before Liang Huyao could finish asking, the old Daoist cut him off.
"No. As long as you master the Seven Wonders True Art, no matter how many people there are, everyone can take a master and enter the blessed realm."
"Understood. Thank you, Senior Brother," Liang Huyao bowed.
"No need." Qianhezi turned around and left the yard, heading to the next spot.
"Everyone, shall we head back to our rooms and study it?" Liang Huyao turned to look at the others.
"See you later then," Shangguan Xing said briefly, being the first to enter her room.
Wei He and Zhou Muqing also split up and returned to their own bedrooms.
Holding the thin booklet, Wei He stepped inside his room and quickly flipped open the first page.
The Seven Wonders True Art started with body forging, just like the Wushi Return-to-Mountain Fist he had practiced before, climbing upward step by step. Once a practitioner reached the seventh layer, they could take a master and enter the mountain.
Wei He flipped through the book. Upon reaching the Bone Forging stage, this True Art required rebuilding the bone structure, which was quite troublesome.
However, with the Prime Blood from his Whale Flood Art, it was nothing to worry about.
