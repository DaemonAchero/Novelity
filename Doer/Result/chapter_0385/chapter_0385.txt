Breaking the Barrier "Hurry up. Don't keep the senior True Persons of the Wushi Sect waiting," Miss Tao urged softly.
Song Shizhen felt helpless. Since he had already agreed, he had no choice but to follow through. He only hoped that the experts from the Wushi Sect would be easy to deal with. If he ran into a short-tempered tyrant, his future would be miserable.
With another sigh, Song Shizhen followed Miss Tao out of the city. They walked through endless stretches of stone forests.
Miss Tao led the way, occasionally telling him to move faster.
"Miss Tao, do you know what kind of ruin this is?" Song Shizhen asked. He found it hard to imagine what sort of place required someone to break a formation just to enter.
"I don't know either," Miss Tao shook her head. "And don't ask about things you shouldn't know. Just do your job."
Song Shizhen did not dare to say another word. Soon, they arrived at the guard perimeter set up by the Wushi Sect.
After verifying their identities, they passed through the outer patrol of Daoists and walked deeper inside. Before long, they saw Shi Heng lounging on a long bench.
"Ask for whatever you need. As long as you can break the formation and find the entrance, everything else will be fine," a goat-bearded old man beside Shi Heng instructed. Shi Heng himself did not bother to look up.
"I understand. I will do my absolute best," Song Shizhen answered seriously.
The old man nodded with satisfaction. "Good. Take him over."
A Daoist quickly led Song Shizhen to the side of the main ruin gate. Someone handed him a stack of drawn formation diagrams.
The mechanism was like a modular maze. He needed to fit the right pieces together to clear a safe path to enter and exit. However, there were countless possible combinations for a puzzle like this.
Song Shizhen looked at the stack of papers in front of him, took a deep breath, and threw himself into solving the formation.
While Song Shizhen worked, Shi Heng waited for a short while before getting impatient and walking away, leaving a few men behind to guard the area.
The Shadow Beast ruin itself held no danger for normal people. Only those at the True Person level, who possessed True Perception, could sense the hidden threats. If an ordinary person came here to rob the tomb, they would not run into anything unusual. At most, they would feel that this tomb was colder than others.
Of course, if a person could not sense the danger, they could not find the Shadow Beasts or obtain the precious Shadow Beast Star Plates inside.
Not long after Shi Heng left...
"So this is where the ruin is?"
High up in a large tree canopy in the distance, Wei He crouched among the dense leaves. He concealed his presence and stared at the site from afar.
From his vantage point, he could see a Daoist sitting near the ruin gate with a cup of wine, barking orders to the man beside him. A perimeter of guards wearing Wushi Sect robes surrounded the entire area.
Wei He examined the massive gate of the ruin. The doors seemed to seal something dark and dangerous. Even from this distance, he could see traces of corrosive Qi seeping through the cracks of the gate.
Naturally, this was visible only through his ultra-perceptive vision.
He scanned the Wushi Sect guards. Among them were three True Persons, and all three appeared to have unlocked ultra-perceptive vision as well. It seemed most True Persons from major sects possessed this ability.
"This place... the Wushi Sect values it so much, so there must be great treasures hidden inside. But how should I step in?" Wei He thought to himself.
Since the Wushi Sect claimed this place first, charging in to loot it openly would give him a bad name. Furthermore, the conflict regarding the Three Faces had just ended. If he made a loud, public move now, it would intentionally spark a war between the Xuanmiao Sect and the Wushi Sect.
"I need to find someone to figure out what this ruin is actually for. Only then can I target the right spot." Wei He scanned the crowd, his eyes lingering on each member of the Wushi Sect to pick a target. "I also need a good excuse to interfere."
Wei He knew a simple disguise would not fool a True Person. The three True Persons here were not beginners in the Mind-Fixing realm. Their senses were sharp. Treating them like fools would only make him look like a fool.
Thoughts raced through Wei He's mind. Soon, he thought of a way.
If he used Song Shizhen as his excuse to jump into the conflict, Shi Heng would have nothing to say. It would give him a valid reason to fight.
In reality, Wei He did not care much about what the Wushi Sect had found. He simply wanted to teach Shi Heng and his men a lesson. The fact that he had guided Song Shizhen in the past served as a perfect excuse.
Having made up his mind, Wei He cast one last glance at the ruin and leaped down softly to the ground.
He did not bother hiding anymore. He walked straight out of the forest and headed toward the ruin.
The Wushi Sect guards spotted him instantly. Two Daoists stepped forward with stern expressions.
"Stop right there! This area is temporarily closed to the public. Take a detour."
Wei He remained expressionless. He raised a finger and tapped the air.
Whish!
His body flashed, passing right between the two guards in a split second.
The two Daoists froze. They stood rooted to the spot, completely silent and unable to move. Every muscle in their bodies was locked tight by his Return-to-Truth Force.
If Wei He had already mastered the fourth layer of the Mysterious Lock Force, he would not have needed to use so much raw power. A single strand of Mysterious Lock Force could easily seal a person's entire cultivation. Instead, he had to use a massive wave of force to crush their movements.
Of course, what seemed like a massive amount of force to the guards was barely a fraction of Wei He's total power. Even so, this small portion of corrosive Return-to-Truth Force was enough to cripple their strength and leave them badly injured for a long time.
Wei He had no intention of killing them. Although the Wushi Sect was annoying, he did not need to slaughter everyone he met. He was simply in a bad mood and wanted to ruin their plans.
Passing the two frozen guards, Wei He walked deeper in. The remaining guards quickly noticed that something was wrong, and several more rushed toward him.
Among them, one Daoist caught sight of Wei He's face, and his expression instantly changed. He raised a hand to stop the others and whispered something urgently to them.
The moment he spoke, the faces of the Wushi Sect members turned pale. They looked at Wei He as if they were staring at a monster.
Wei He smirked. They had recognized him.
"Interesting."
Wei He kept walking until he reached the spot where Shi Heng had been sitting earlier.
The old Daoist who had been drinking wine earlier stood up with a heavy heart and walked over to greet him.
"Zhao Xu of the Wushi Sect greets Senior Brother Wei of the Xuanmiao Sect," the old Daoist cupped his hands. "May I ask what brings Senior Brother Wei to this place?"
Zhao Xu's heart pounded in his chest. Ever since Shi Heng almost clashed with Wei He earlier, Zhao Xu had looked into Wei He's background. When he found out that Wei He had been rejected by the Wushi Sect's Inner Court years ago, Zhao Xu had cursed his own sect in his heart.
After leaving the Wushi Sect, Wei He had taken over the Heavenly Seal Sect, renamed it the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect, and eventually joined the Xuanmiao Sect. In just a few short years, a plain martial artist had transformed into an exceptionally strong True Person in the Mind-Fixing realm.
How could the useless elders of the Inner Court throw away such a genius back then?
It was not the first time the Inner Court made such a mistake, but this time, Zhao Xu truly wanted to curse them out loud. If Wei He had stayed on the mountain, he would be a star disciple of their Three Main Peaks right now.
Looking at the current situation, it was clear that Wei He still held a deep grudge over what happened back then.
Zhao Xu had also discovered that during the feud between the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect and the Wushi Sect, an Outer Court head was killed. The Inner Court sent several junior True Persons to hunt Wei He down for revenge, only for Wei He to beat them up and drive them back.
The whole mess was a giant headache.
"How do you know who I am?" Wei He asked with a calm smile, looking at the old man with interest.
"After I returned earlier, I checked our old records and learned a bit about what happened back then," Zhao Xu answered softly. "Senior Brother Wei, are you still unhappy about the Outer Court head from back then? If you wish, I can do my best to investigate the matter thoroughly when I return to the sect. I promise to give you a satisfying answer."
Zhao Xu was not as reckless as Shi Heng. As long as he could complete this mission without outside interference, making a few empty promises did not hurt. Even though his status was lower than Shi Heng's, he still held plenty of sway over Outer Court affairs.
He might not belong to the top family factions on the mountain, but he was still a True Person of the Three Main Peaks with strong backing. The words he spoke were not empty lies.
"Oh? If you put it that way, you are making things difficult for me," Wei He raised an eyebrow. "I originally planned to take a look around, and if your attitude was slightly bad, I would destroy this entire place. I didn't expect you to be such a sensible person."
Wei He's blunt words made Zhao Xu's heart skip a beat.
"Senior Brother Wei must be joking. The Wushi Sect and the Xuanmiao Sect both belong to the Daoist lineage. Even if we are not formal allies, there is no need to treat each other as enemies."
"In that case, can you tell me what this ruin is actually for?" Wei He took another step forward.
Zhao Xu groaned internally. He had already sent a secret message to call Shi Heng back. Wei He had proven himself unstoppable in Yunzhou City earlier, and Zhao Xu knew he was no match for him alone.
"This ruin... is a unique place my Wushi Sect stumbled upon," Zhao Xu quickly made up a lie under pressure. "We plan to study a rare True Beast inside. Because this beast is extremely dangerous, we must seal off the area to prevent it from escaping..."
"Is that so?" Wei He nodded. "Then why do I feel like this place looks more like a Shadow Beast ruin?"
Zhao Xu froze. He never expected a newly risen True Person like Wei He to recognize a Shadow Beast ruin. He opened his mouth to make up another excuse.

Do you think my Xuanmiao Sect is weak and easy to bully?"
