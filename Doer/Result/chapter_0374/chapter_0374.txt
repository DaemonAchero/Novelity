So... she was taking a gamble.
She gambled that Three Faces would not leave the city over just a few of them.
"Then we can pretend to leave," Gong Huan said, his voice cold and hard. "We go into hiding and wait for backup."
If he did not catch the killer, he would never forgive himself for the rest of his life.
"From now on, the two of us do not separate for even a single step!"
"I understand..." Zhao Yuexi went quiet for a moment, then nodded.
The three of them got up to leave, but beautiful women in pink dresses stepped out from the surrounding crowd.
They wore unusual clothes. Long skirts below, but white tight-fitting vests on top, like fighting gear. They wore long riding boots and heavy makeup. They looked like young ladies from rich families who just returned from a trip.
The woman leading them had a pretty face and a lively feel. She was clearly not a young girl anymore, but deep in her eyes, there was a rare look of innocence.
She spun a small bamboo ring around her fingertips, over and over.
"I am Ling Xian'er of the Wanfei Sect," she said. "Greetings to the two Grandmasters of the Xuanmiao Sect."
Snap.
The bamboo ring in her hand stopped spinning.
"Why leave in such a hurry, Grandmasters?" Ling Xian'er asked softly. "Three Faces is far too wild. Why don't we join forces and solve this together? Our Wanfei Sect and your Xuanmiao Sect are neighbors. We have worked together many times and share good ties. If we team up, we have a better chance of catching the killer."
"I must decline..." Gong Huan said.
Normally, this proposal made complete sense.
But right now, the Coalition had abandoned the Xuanmiao Sect. The Wanfei Sect was part of that Coalition. Their relationship had hit rock bottom.
More importantly, Gong Huan had found a hidden trace of energy on Yang Ling's body—energy that belonged to the Wanfei Sect.
The Wanfei Sect practiced the Taisui True Scripture. Their energy was thick, powerful, and kept them looking young. It had very distinct traits.
The killer hid their traces very well, but Gong Huan still found a tiny hint. He recognized it immediately as the energy of the Taisui True Scripture.
"Our sect's backup will arrive very soon. You do not need to worry about us, Senior Sister," Gong Huan said. He forced down his grief and held his hands out in respect.
"Very well..." Ling Xian'er wanted to keep the body so they could search for clues, but looking at the two, she knew it was a bad time to ask.
She pressed her thoughts down and waved her hand, telling her people to open a path.
Gong Huan took the body. Together with Zhao Yuexi and Han Xiangqi, they quickly moved through the crowd and headed toward the city gate.
A short while later, Gong Huan's waist shook. A signal bug was vibrating.
His face stayed calm, but he was thrilled. He pulled out the signal bug and turned around, running in the direction the bug was pointing.
Signal bugs always came in pairs. Once you released one, it flew straight toward its partner at full speed.
That meant the person coming was holding the twin bug he had left back at the sect.
When the bug took off, both Gong Huan and Zhao Yuexi were overjoyed.
They carried the body and rushed after the bug, leaving a confused Han Xiangqi behind them.
A few moments later.
Gong Huan landed inside a quiet alley and looked deep into the shadows.
Two people stood there.
One was a big, broad man. He wore a simple Daoist robe, but his large muscles stretched the fabric tight. Long black hair fell over his shoulders. He had a chest like a bear and a back like a tiger, and his eyes shone with a sharp light.
The other was a woman. Her energy was weak. She did not even have the unique vibe of a Grandmaster. She was likely just his servant or helper.
"Senior Brother...? Is it really just the two of you?" Gong Huan paused, feeling unsure.
The joy he felt moments ago faded away.
"That's right," the man said. "I am Wei He. I just left the Inner Mountain, and I came here specifically to settle this murder case. Did someone else die just now?"
Wei He did not bother to waste time with explanations. He asked right away.
"Yes! It was my wife, Yang Ling. She was killed by that scoundrel just moments ago!" Gong Huan's eyes turned red as he spoke in a low voice.
Wei He looked at the body in his arms. The corpse was slowly turning into black smoke, though the process was slow.
This was what happened to a Grandmaster. When they died, they slowly sank into the True Reality, away from what normal humans could see or feel.
In a way, Grandmasters were no longer the same species as ordinary people.
"Do you have any clues?" Wei He asked.
"I didn't find the killer... but the marks on her body are connected to the Taisui True Scripture of the Wanfei Sect..." Gong Huan answered.
He started to feel a glimmer of hope. This Wei He really seemed to be the backup sent by the sect.
Facing the dead body of a Grandmaster, Wei He's face did not change. He spoke with a strong, natural confidence.
It was clear this man trusted his own strength completely.
Because of this, Wei He was not just here to escort them back. He was really here to investigate and solve the case.
"Can I see the body?" Wei He asked.
One of his good friends, Yue Xiaodie, belonged to the Wanfei Sect. He had forced her to tell him about the Taisui True Scripture before, so he knew a bit about their basic martial arts.
Gong Huan went quiet for a second. Then he put the body down and pulled back the cloth wrapping it.
Wei He stepped forward and looked closely at the corpse.
The main wound was on the neck. Someone had cut her head off with a blade.
Along the edge of the wound, there were marks of opposing energies clashing. It was clear Yang Ling did not die defenseless.
She had pushed her energy to the limit, but the killer had still cut her down face-to-face.
The killer's strength was at least at the Transformation Realm or higher.
After all, Yang Ling herself was a Transformation Realm Grandmaster.
Wei He took a quick look. There were indeed traces of energy from the Taisui True Scripture.
"You are right. It really is the energy of the Wanfei Sect."
"Then, Senior Brother Wei, what do we do now?" Zhao Yuexi asked from the side.
"Let's go," Wei He shook his head.
"?" The others looked at him, confused by his response.

"...!?" The four of them stood frozen. They never expected Wei He to say something like that.
"But... but..." Gong Huan wanted to argue. No matter how hot-headed he was, he knew a faction like the Wanfei Sect was not something you could easily mess with.
"No buts. Lead the way."
Wei He did not care to explain. He looked around, grabbed Han Xiangqi with one hand, jumped into the air, and flashed toward the city center.
On the other side of town.
Ling Xian'er of the Wanfei Sect stood by a water well, checking the area for any clues left behind.
She wanted to find something the killer had dropped.
Sadly, the area was too messy. People came and went constantly. Even if there were tracks, they were buried under countless new footprints long ago.
There was no way to track anyone from this spot.
"What a shame..." She sighed and looked up at the sky.
The sun was high. It was already past three in the afternoon, the hottest time of the day.
"There are no clues here. Let's head back for now and wait for our next move," Ling Xian'er sent a voice message to her group, frowning.
A Grandmaster from the Xuanmiao Sect had died. This was a massive deal. The Xuanmiao Sect would never let this go.
Ling Xian'er ran through the names of the inner mountain Grandmasters in her head. She picked out the ones who liked to travel outside and tried to guess who might come this time.
She looked around at the crowd. Many martial artists were watching, drawn by their own courage.
The common folk, however, walked past quickly, terrified of getting caught in the crossfire.
Three Faces... actually had the power to kill a Grandmaster.
It seemed they needed to rewrite their plan to hunt them down.
"Senior Sister, the Xuanmiao Sect people are back," a junior sister whispered beside her.
"Hmm?" Ling Xian'er turned her head to look toward the city gate.
To her confusion, she saw no one coming from that direction.
"Where are they?" Ling Xian'er asked, puzzled.
Whoosh.
She froze mid-breath.
Less than two meters away from her, a figure appeared out of nowhere.
It was a man. A massive, heavily built man.
He had a calm face, pale skin, and quiet eyes. He looked like an ordinary, easygoing martial arts instructor.
Yet, standing in front of this plain-looking man, Ling Xian'er felt as if a hand had reached out and squeezed her heart. She could not move a muscle.
"My name is Wei He," the man said, offering her a gentle smile.
After years of hard training, he was no longer as short-tempered as he used to be.
Long, quiet training had taught him to endure loneliness, sharpened his mind, and made his character far more stable.
"Senior Brother Wei..."
Tap.
Ling Xian'er's body shook. She felt a heavy hand fall onto her right shoulder.
A massive, invisible wave of energy rushed over her, wrapping around her in an instant.
Her own strong defense energy had barely started to react before it was crushed completely.
She tried to lift her arm to fight back, but her body was numb and useless.
A single finger pressed lightly against the skin of her shoulder, sending waves of numbness through her.
That energy moved like a spiderweb. In the blink of an eye, it shattered her own power and took complete control of her body.
"Senior Brother Wei... what is the meaning of this!?" Ling Xian'er panicked.
She was one of the Ten Flowers. Even if she was ranked ninth, her power was at the Third Sensing Realm.
Yet the man in front of her... had crushed all her energy in a single move.
The terrifying amount of energy he had was like a vast ocean with no end in sight. You could not even tell how much he really had.
The Xuanmiao Sect... what kind of monster did they send this time!?
Ling Xian'er was terrified, unsure of what to do. She did not remember a single detail about this man. She had memorized every registered name in the Xuanmiao Sect, but this man was definitely not on that list.
Was this Wei He a newcomer!? Or was he an old monster they had kept hidden away!?
By now, the other women of the Wanfei Sect realized what was happening. They panicked and quickly surrounded Wei He.
"Let Senior Sister Xian'er go!"
Led by a white-haired woman, over ten beautiful women surged with energy. They glared at Wei He, completely surrounding him.
The air turned tense in an instant.
Wei He's expression did not change. He squeezed his hand on Ling Xian'er slightly, keeping her still, and freed his other hand.
He spread his five fingers open.
Inside him, a massive sea of energy began to roar and surge like crashing ocean waves.
