During the Heavenly Shade Disaster, daytime stays normal. But once the sky goes dark and night falls, pure chaos and terror creep out from the real world.
Nine winds, nine fires, and all kinds of deadly beasts and unknown threats appear. Any single one of them can easily kill a person without leaving a trace.
To Wei He, the way these True Realm experts lived and talked—as described by Duan Chaosheng—felt less like immortal gods and more like a bunch of hermits hiding from a catastrophe. They relied on external tools just to survive the force of reality. They either hid in safer corners of the real world, living off pills and elixir, or built their own safe havens to huddle together, resist the danger, and live in peace.
To them, the outside world was extremely dangerous. Many accidents and natural disasters that seemed ordinary to common people were actually far from random.
The two sat and chatted for a long time. From Duan Chaosheng, Wei He learned a lot of basic knowledge about the True Realm. For example, Jin Prefecture belonged to the Wanfei Palace. The relationship between the True Realm and the ordinary world was like the roots of a giant tree and its leaves. The mortal world was simply the root that fed and supported the True Realm hermits.
As they talked, a woman in a green dress walked into the main hall, followed by two maidservants. She clapped her hands softly to draw everyone’s attention.
"Thank you all for your years of support for our Wanfei Palace. This time, we have a new batch of Sixty-Four bottles of Hundred-Flower Mind-Settling Dew. Each bottle boosts your resistance against the first two layers of Ghost Wind and lasts for one year per person. We will sell them through an auction. Starting price: ten Liang of Star Stones per bottle."
Star Stones?
Wei He was puzzled, but no one else looked surprised. Clearly, this was the common currency among True Realm experts. He quickly decided he wouldn't buy anything today. He was just here to open his eyes and look around. Besides, Duan Chaosheng had mentioned this small gathering happened once a year anyway.
With his mind set, Wei He relaxed. Only then did he have the mental energy to check his surroundings.
He noticed the walls and floor of the hall were covered in strange, winding patterns. He could faintly feel living energy flowing through these lines, serving a hidden purpose. Remembering Xia Linghua's living energy from before, Wei He formed a vague guess.
Unfortunately, his main art, Nine Strikes of the Sky Seal, was at too low a level. Reaching the Mind-Sensing state was already his absolute limit. As for creating his own technique, his lack of understanding about the real world kept him stuck in slow exploration.
Soon, the green-dressed woman started the auction for the first item: a piece of form-fitting inner armor glowing with a faint purple light, made for women.
The crowd showed great interest and started bidding right away. In a flash, the price shot past eighty Liang of Star Stones. It was bought by a red-bearded old man. Watching the old man smile with joy and try to put the woman's armor over his clothes was quite an eyesore.
Next was a long robe made of some kind of feathers. Another round of fierce bidding began. Wei He just sat on the side, watching the show. This time, a Taoist old man with the look of a legendary immortal bought it.
"Even the old monks from the Wushi Sect are here? Sects with safe havens really have deep pockets, tsk tsk..." Duan Chaosheng whispered in admiration.
Wushi Sect?
Wei He’s heart stirred, and he took an extra glance at the old Taoist. He hadn't expected to run into people from the Wushi Sect here.
From Duan Chaosheng's scattered words, he learned that Jin Prefecture belonged to Wanfei Palace, the surrounding areas were controlled by Wushi Sect, and Yun Prefecture belonged to White Swallow Sect. Yizhou was under the Xiangqu Cult, but Yizhou was now occupied by the Wu army. The True Realm fighters of the Xiangqu Cult tried to resist, but they were crushed in direct combat.
Without top-tier power to keep them in check, the enemy country's masters roamed free in Yizhou. Many generals under Li Tong were assassinated, forcing him to accept the Wu country's demand for a duel between generals on the front line, which ended in a tragic defeat.
One item after another was sold, until they reached the Hundred-Flower Mind-Settling Dew.
This stuff was said to boost resistance against early-stage Ghost Wind. Wei He watched with envy. He was close to entering the Mind-Sensing state and needed this badly, but he was completely broke. He didn't own a single Star Stone.
"Brother Chen, you’re not buying anything?" Duan Chaosheng asked from the side.
"To be honest, I just broke through my realm and came here to expand my horizons. I don't have a single penny on me," Wei He said with a bitter smile.
After chatting with Duan Chaosheng for a while, Wei He could tell that while this guy had some age on him, he had lived in a simple environment and been bottled up for too long. Once he got out, he couldn't stop talking. In short, he was a chatty talker on the rebound. Wei He could use this to dig up news.
Hearing Wei He self-deprecatingly admit he was broke, Duan Chaosheng's eyes flickered, and his warm tone slowly cooled down.
When Wei He walked in earlier, he looked calm, impressive, and held a special token from Wanfei Palace, making Duan Chaosheng think he had a powerful background. Who would have guessed...
"Just broke through? Ah, that makes sense. Brother Chen is an independent rogue, right?" Duan Chaosheng asked.
"Yeah, I am a rogue. No masters or elders to guide me, so I have to rely on myself for everything," Wei He admitted with a nod. At the True Realm level, he was flat broke and had nothing worth stealing anyway, so being honest was fine.
"It really is hard for rogues to get Star Stones. I have a few rogue friends who can only rely on hunting real beasts to get them. One mistake and they risk mutating into monsters. It's tough," Duan Chaosheng sighed.
Once he heard Wei He had no master, he knew he had misjudged him. The token Wei He held was probably just a basic special invite token.
Wanfei Palace had two types of gathering tokens. One was a high-end token for clients with powerful backgrounds. The other was an ordinary invite token, usually given to plain rogues who had special talents but no backing. Though these people had unique skills, some weren't even true True Realm experts, so their status was lower than normal fighters.
Realizing Wei He only had a basic token, Duan Chaosheng lost all interest in talking. He turned his head and started chatting with another man on his other side.
Faced with such realistic behavior, Wei He had nothing to say.
The small gathering ended in less than an hour. The sixty-four bottles of Hundred-Flower Mind-Settling Dew were snapped up almost instantly. It was an eye-opening sight for Wei He.
Since Duan Chaosheng ignored him, Wei He turned to talk to an old man sitting on his other side.
There were quite a few rogues like him at the event, and this old man was one of them. His name was Sun Tanguang, a native of Yun Prefecture, making him a fellow countryman of Wei He.
After a short chat, Wei He learned that Sun Tanguang had also entered using a basic invite token. According to the old man, besides the two of them, there were three others—two men and one woman—who came in with the same invitation.
It had to be said that once someone reached the True Realm, their appearance froze at the moment of their breakthrough. Their internal organs were refined, filling them with vibrant energy and striking charisma. Unless someone deliberately made themselves look ugly, almost no one looked bad.
Since they were all rogues, they waited together until Wanfei Palace announced the end of the meeting.
Everyone stood up in groups. Wei He walked out of the hall with Old Man Sun, alongside the other invited rogues.
The rogues were kept separate from the established True Realm members and didn't interact much. It was clear that rogues were slightly pushed aside, but fortunately, they were eager to make friends among themselves.
"Brother Chen, us rogues live far apart from one another. Since it's rare for us to meet, we plan to gather for a private meeting afterward. Why don't you join us?" Sun Tanguang offered kindly as they walked outside.
Wei He's heart skipped a beat. None of these rogues had made a bid during the auction. Hearing this, he realized the Wanfei Palace gathering wasn't the main event for rogues like them. This private follow-up meeting was likely where the real trades and chats happened.
"Sounds good. When and where will it be?" Wei He asked, genuinely interested.
"In half a month, on the Three-Sun Festival. At the Sunset Cottage south of the city," Sun Tanguang gave him the address and date.
"Alright, I will be there on time," Wei He cupped his fists.
Leaving the courtyard, Wei He looked up at the sky. Dusk was approaching.
To his right, Duan Chaosheng walked off with two other people and climbed onto a nearby ox cart. The guy had clearly hooked up with targets he deemed valuable. Other participants either had their own carriages or leapt away over rooftops, disappearing in the blink of an eye. At their level, their movement speed was far beyond normal martial artists. To ordinary people, they would look like flickering ghosts or gods.
Wei He turned back to look at the Wanfei Palace courtyard. From the outside, it looked like a simple residence of a rich merchant. Who would guess that many powerful True Realm experts gathered here?
Sighing inwardly, Wei He hailed a horse carriage and took a seat to head home.
He suddenly understood what Xia Linghua, the Master of Wanfei Palace, meant by giving him this token. It wasn't to let him buy things, but to show him how tough life was for rogues in the True Realm. She wanted to open his eyes so he would realize how valuable her trade offer was, making him value his deal with Wanfei Palace even more.
Understanding this, Wei He settled his mind. He had absorbed too much news today and needed time to process it all. Once back, he could use the intelligence network of the Myriad Poison Sect to gather more information about the surrounding areas.
Back home, Wei He sent people to officially contact Wanfei Palace, paying even closer attention to the deal Xia Linghua offered.
Creating a custom poison-locking technique wasn't hard for him. What he could get in exchange depended on his own skill.
Half a month went by quickly under daily hard cultivation. Wei He finalized his research plan for Wanfei Palace's custom poison lock. Through the Myriad Poison Sect's network, he also uncovered a few clues about Star Stones.
However, regarding Xia Linghua's main condition—a testing spot for a Safe Haven—Wei He had zero clues. Xia Linghua hadn't given him any details, clearly leaving him to figure it out on his own.
So, Wei He pinned his hopes on the upcoming rogue gathering to see if he could gather information about Safe Havens there.
Cultivating alone at home, his progress with the Sea-Covering Force was as slow as a snail, even though his Whale Flood Technique advanced steadily thanks to his comprehension of the Moon and Whale picture. His research into the real world remained stuck in a bottleneck. For now, he could only maintain his super-sense state for a brief moment. Any longer, and his left hand would freeze solid into a block of ice.
He also couldn't push forward with creating his own martial art. Lacking higher-level information about the real world, he had no overall grasp of what the Nine Winds actually were. Without that, he couldn't build the framework for a new technique.
Wei He desperately needed a complete system to fill the gaps in his knowledge and guide his path forward.
