"Moving?"
Wei Ying was stitching a torn silk dress. Hearing her brother mention buying a house, her hands stopped. She looked up, startled.
"Yeah. I bought a place nearby. We don't have to go back to our old home," Wei He said seriously.
Wei Ying’s room was tiny, just a few square meters. It held a bed, a small table, and a chair. Nothing else. The only window was nailed shut. It was merely a place to sleep.
"But... where did we get that kind of money? Houses are so expensive..." Wei Ying doubted him.
"Not anymore," Wei He said after a quiet pause.
"...Is that so..." She lowered her head. The needle in her hand stayed still for a moment.
She quickly pulled herself together, a genuine smile breaking across her face.
"Then we can finally live together. I was always worried about you while we were apart. You wouldn't let me go over to clean your place before, saying it was dangerous. But now the neighbors are all talking about you. Everyone knows you're Old Man Zheng's student. They say you're strong and doing great! If Mom, Dad, and Big Sister knew..." Her voice trailed off at their names, but she quickly pushed the thought away.
"When do we move?" she asked.
"Right now. It's lunch break. I'll tell Senior Brother Cheng later," Wei He said.
"Alright. Oh, right. Old Man Zheng said to see him once you got back. He has something to tell you," she remembered.
"Me? Just me?" Wei He was surprised. He was just an average student in the yard with nothing impressive about him. Old Man Zheng rarely called him out by name.
"Not just you. A few others were called in before you came back."
"Got it. I'll go right after we move."
Wei Ying wanted to ask what happened during the escort mission, but Wei He brushed it off with a few vague words. Since the trip had been safe overall, she didn't suspect anything.
Both were born poor and worked fast. Wei Ying didn't have much to pack anyway, and the new place already had basic things left behind by Old Man Zheng. Within an hour, everything was moved.
After setting things up, Wei He returned to the Return-Mountain Fist academy. Before stepping inside, he saw Xiao Ran getting into a white carriage. He shook his head inwardly, kept a calm face, and pushed the door open.
In the yard, several new students were already training hard. Newcomers always worked themselves to the bone. Only later would they learn that relentless training without proper rest ruined progress, leading them to find their own rhythm.
Wei He didn't know many people here. The only familiar face was Li Jue. He gave Li Jue a distant nod in greeting and walked straight into the inner room.
The inner room was long and rectangular. Old Man Zheng reclined in a corner, sitting on a polished mahogany chair. A student was already waiting in front of him.
When Wei He walked in, Old Man Zheng didn't speak. He just flicked his hand, pointing to a spot. Wei He walked over and stood quietly.
A moment later, two more students walked in and stood waiting alongside them. Finally, Senior Brother Zhao Hong entered.
"Everyone's here," Old Man Zheng said flatly. "Hong'er, fill them in. Since they entered my line, they need to know what's going on. Things in the city are getting worse. Everyone needs to be careful."
"Yes," Zhao Hong answered respectfully.
Old Man Zheng picked up a dish of marinated chicken feet and began chewing slowly, looking like he was just there to watch and add a word or two if needed.
Zhao Hong cleared his throat and looked at the four students.
"I've said this twice before. Today is for those who missed it." He paused, glancing at Old Man Zheng. Seeing no sign from the old man, he continued. "The other students who haven't entered the inner line don't count as part of our Return-Mountain Fist. They are just apprentices. We don't protect apprentices in life or death situations, and no one outside views them as our people. But you four are different. You've entered the line, and you've all broken through your Blood Energy once. Outside, you represent our name. So today, I will set the rules and tell you how things work."
Wei He and the other three nodded, listening closely.
Zhao Hong nodded in satisfaction. "In this city, ignore the small gangs that rise and fall every few days. What you really need to watch out for are the Three Gangs. They are the Mountain-River Gang, the Blood-Robe Gang, and the City-Pass Gang. They have huge numbers, but you don't need to worry about outer members. Just watch out for core members and don't provoke them. I'll tell you their core symbols later. Also, stay away from houses hit by the sick. Don't linger near them. The plague in the city is getting worse..."
He listed several warnings about the plague. Wei He listened quietly until the very end.
After Zhao Hong rattled on and on about plague symptoms, Old Man Zheng couldn't take it anymore and cut him off.
"Enough, enough. Get to the point."
"Yes," Zhao Hong said quickly. "Feiye City has the Three Gangs and Two Sects, but they are all in the Outer City. The Inner City is completely different. Most of the businesses and land there are controlled by seven families, known as the Seven-Family Alliance. The Alliance and the Hong Family Fort outside the city are the forces you must never mess with. If you encounter people from the Seven-Family Alliance or the Hong Family Fort, back off. Do not fight them."
The Seven-Family Alliance... Wei He thought back to the escort trip, where Escort Leader Cheng Kai had mentioned tension between the Hong Family Fort and the City Guard families. One of the Seven Families was likely the City Guard family.
After a long lecture, everyone realized that Zhao Hong, who usually stayed quiet, was actually a rambler once he started talking. In the end, Old Man Zheng interrupted him again and sent the four out to train.
As Wei He walked out, he looked back and saw Zhao Hong getting scolded by Old Man Zheng with his head bowed. He found it a little funny.
Back at his corner of the yard, Cheng Shaojiu was already waiting for him. They set up their gear and prepared to practice skin-grinding.
"I heard about what happened on the trip. Hard work," Cheng Shaojiu whispered.
"It's fine. I took the job, so it was my duty," Wei He shook his head. He had taken the Cheng family's pay for a long time, so facing danger on the road was expected.
"Just be careful out there," Cheng Shaojiu sighed. "Honestly, I wanted to go on an escort trip for a long time, but my dad won't let me. What's the point of me training so hard every day?"
Wei He was silent. After a moment, thinking back to the creature in the dark, he spoke in a low voice. "Senior Brother, do you know what the Black Madman we ran into actually was?"
"Black Madman is just a general name for wild beasts that hunt at night. I asked my dad about the two who died that night. Even he didn't know. It seems to be a kind of beast that never appeared before," Cheng Shaojiu shook his head.
Wei He fell silent. He had thought the lands outside weren't as dangerous as rumored, but a single trip had claimed two lives.
Cheng Shaojiu lowered his head and punched into the sand basin violently. After a while, he looked up suddenly.
"Xiao He, how old are you?"
"Seventeen..." Wei He answered, confused.
"Seventeen... How about I find you a good girl to marry?" Cheng Shaojiu blurted out.
Wei He couldn't follow his train of thought.
"Just trust me! I promise to find you a gentle, dutiful girl!" Cheng Shaojiu slapped his chest.
In these times, many people married and had children at seventeen or eighteen. Wei He was at that age, but he had been too busy training to care about marriage.
Wei He remained silent. Senior Brother Cheng seemed distracted lately, which was bad for his martial progress.
Neither spoke after that, concentrating fully on their training.
After training for a while, Wei He wiped his sweat and pulled open his collar to check the mark of the Breakthrough Bead. It had grown again. After the half-month trip, it had progressed from half-full to well over two-thirds. It had gained about a quarter of progress.
He acted like nothing happened and kept training hard. According to the information in his mind, the Breakthrough Bead could only be used when he hit a bottleneck, allowing him to break through in one go. Therefore, he had to train his Ox-Hide tier Blood Energy to its absolute limit. When his Blood Energy hit that wall, he could use the bead to push through.
He cleared his mind and focused on grinding his skin.
Growing Blood Energy wasn't enough on its own; he had to expand his body's capacity to hold more of it. That was the purpose of skin-grinding. Sadly, his talent was limited, far behind geniuses like Xiao Ran. He could only push forward step by step.
Time flowed slowly.
Wei He and his sister moved into their new home. He invited Third Senior Brother and Li Jue over for a meal to show them the place. After that, nothing unusual happened.
He woke up early and returned late every day, training his martial arts and polishing his body. Days dripped away like water from the bamboo pipe in the yard.
As for Cheng Shaojiu's offer to introduce a girl, Wei He ignored it completely. He didn't even have time to fix his own life, let alone worry about marriage.
Besides, the world was getting worse.
Prices on the street changed daily, becoming chaotic. Fake silver coins flooded the market, making silver worth less and less. Many shops and stalls stopped accepting silver altogether, switching to cloth, meat, and rice as currency.
Prices rose so high that carrying enough silver for a basic purchase required a heavy sack, making people hate using it even more.
Looking back, Wei Ying had worked so hard before to earn a few dozen taels of silver, which had definitely been a loss. Wei He told her not to take silver for future work, demanding only rice and meat instead.
In the blink of an eye, another month passed.
"Almost there, almost..."
Wei He pulled his hands out of the hot stone sand and quickly plunged them into a basin of medicinal liquid beside him.
He felt his hands cool down while the hot Blood Energy inside his body rapidly balanced out, growing slightly stronger.
A wave of comfort washed over him.
He could feel his Blood Energy take another step forward. He was now midway through the Ox-Hide tier. This speed wasn't great, but it wasn't terrible either. Plenty of people around him trained at the same pace, and quite a few were faster.
Among the ten or so Ox-Hide disciples, his progress was strictly average.
