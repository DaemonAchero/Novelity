"Want to change locations?" Shi Heng narrowed his eyes, staring at Wei He.
Wei He's earlier move had caused enough of a stir to make Shi Heng take him seriously.
The Xuanmiao Sect had badly injured Shi Heng's junior brother. With so many eyes on them, Shi Heng couldn’t just let it go without saving face.
Still, even though Wei He had dealt with Three Faces, Shi Heng didn't think the guy stood a chance against him.
If Wei He really thought Shi Heng's surface strength was all he had, how could Shi Heng have tricked and killed so many rogue martial artists over the years?
"Works for me," Wei He said, sizing Shi Heng up. The man seemed to have solid brainpower and strength, but Wei He would only know his true limit after they actually fought.
The Wushi Sect had taken two hits from him in a row, but that didn't mean they were weak. After all, Wei He hadn't met any of the actual Grandmasters from the three main peaks yet.
Of course, as for who would win?
Wei He didn't believe anyone short of the Buddha Master or Quanzhen rank could defeat him.
Even if he faced the Buddha Master or someone five steps into Quanzhen right now, he would still have to fight them to know the outcome.
Both men were fully confident, ready to move to a new spot and settle this for good.
"Hold on! It's a misunderstanding! All a misunderstanding!"
Suddenly, a kind-looking old man with graying hair stepped out from the group of rescued hostages.
Hands behind his back, the old man took quick, leaping steps. In the blink of an eye, he stood between Wei He and Shi Heng, blocking them both.
"Since Three Faces is caught, there is no need for the Wushi Sect and Xuanmiao Sect to at each other's throats. You are both outstanding talents. If our Great Yuan martial artists keep bleeding each other dry, the Wu barbarians will destroy our nation sooner or later."
He turned toward Shi Heng. A subtle trace of force gathered in his palm, forming a strange symbol.
"Grandmaster Shi Heng, don't you have that important matter to handle? Staying here wasting time isn't very wise, is it?"
Shi Heng's expression shifted slightly. He caught the hidden symbol as it flashed in the old man's hand. He let out a cold snort and turned around immediately.
He would give the old man face today. Otherwise, to protect the honor of the Wushi Sect, he would have definitely taught Wei He a lesson.
A mere trash who hadn't even reached Quanzhen was just lucky enough to beat a few weaklings above his level. Yet he dared to act arrogant in front of him.
He truly didn't know how deep the water was!
Without another word, Shi Heng turned and walked away.
The other Wushi Sect Grandmasters with him wanted to speak, but knowing Shi Heng's foul temper, they kept quiet and followed him closely.
Wei He frowned, looking at the short old man in front of him.
The old man had a warm, smiling face, looking very friendly. But Wei He knew pure friendliness couldn't have chased a guy like Shi Heng away.
"What guidance do you have, old sir?" Wei He asked calmly.
"I wouldn't dare guide you," the old man chuckled. "However, Grandmaster Wei He resolved this matter so fast because you have other things to handle, right?"
"Indeed," Wei He nodded. There was nothing to hide.
Now that the Three Faces case was settled and the killers were caught, he needed to attend an old friend's birthday party. After that, his main focus was getting the clue for the Three-Eyed Dove, finding the Birth-Fruit, and learning the Fifth-Turn Dragon Breath.
Then, he would complete his second Perception.
"I wonder if Grandmaster Wei still remembers Jiuying?" The old man's smile vanished. He spoke using sound transmission straight to Wei He's ears.
Wei He reacted instantly. "You people... are the ones who were backing Jiuying back then?"
He remembered how Jiuying had used his Sect's channels to give massive support to those secret forces.
When those people tried to contact Wei He later, he rejected them.
In the end, they only took Jiuying's disciple and disappeared without a trace.
He didn't expect to run into them here today.
"Now the Wu nation presses our borders, the people suffer, and the allied forces are falling apart. Our Yuan nation is on the brink of total destruction. Grandmaster Wei, do you really not want to do your part for this situation?" the old man asked seriously through sound transmission.
"I don't."
Wei He's face remained completely flat.
Though his body belonged to Great Yuan, his soul came from another world. He felt zero loyalty to Great Yuan.
Whether it was Feiye City back then or Taizhou later on, the entire Great Yuan was filled with corruption, heavy taxes, and ruthless oppression, on top of natural disasters. The common people could barely survive.
A Great Yuan like this was better off destroyed.
The old man froze, choked by Wei He's cold refusal. Then he realized what kind of man he was dealing with.
He had checked Wei He's files in advance. But face-to-face, he realized this man was far harder to deal with than the files suggested.
"Can we talk somewhere else?" the old man continued to transmit his voice.
"I have no time. Since Three Faces is caught, the matter is closed," Wei He said coldly. He turned his attention to the Jin Chi and Mo Yilian brothers.
The two had been captured by Shi Heng. They looked calm now, carrying a bit of that old man Qi Lianzi's fearless attitude.
"Release everyone else. Three Faces is captured, so you can all go back. Later, I will find an open space and execute them publicly," Wei He ordered.
Soon, members of the Poison Sect moved in, untying everyone and giving them the antidote.
Only then did the hostages realize they hadn't just had their martial force locked, they had also been heavily poisoned.
Instantly, as the crowd was freed, they looked at Wei He with deep fear. Everyone ran as fast as their legs could carry them, swearing in their hearts never to meet Wei He again.
Before long, the large crowd scattered completely.
Yue Xiaodie wanted to whisper a few words to Wei He before leaving, but under the glares of Ling Xian'er and her senior sisters, she had no choice but to leave with them.
Shangguan Tong held in a belly full of rage, but she didn't dare blow up.
Seeing that even the Wanfei Palace stayed quiet, she was even more terrified to cause trouble. She left in a hurry.
Moments later, only the Xuanmiao Sect members remained in the courtyard.
Gong Huan, Zhao Yuexi, and Han Xiangqi—a descendant of the Xuanmiao Sect Grandmaster Huang Yue, who had been protected the whole time—all gathered in the yard.
Gong Huan and Han Xiangqi stared at the captured Jin Chi brothers with pure hatred, wanting nothing more than to slaughter them on the spot.
However, Wei He still had use for the two, so he naturally wouldn't let them die yet.
"I ask, you answer."
Wei He walked over to Jin Chi and Mo Yilian. The two were staring at the spot where Qi Lianzi's body had been.
By now, most of the corpse had already dissolved into black smoke.
"There's nothing to say. The winner takes all. Just kill us," Jin Chi said calmly, as if his life didn't matter at all.
"Did you join the Guangci Sect of the Wu nation?" Wei He asked.
"Yes," Jin Chi replied without expression.
"Are there any other members of Three Faces left?"
"...I don't know," Jin Chi answered carelessly.
Wei He asked a few more questions, but the two kept acting tough, giving fake or useless answers.
Knowing where things stood, Wei He didn't bother wasting more breath. He had already reached his goal for this trip anyway.
The so-called Three Faces were just top-tier bandits with movement techniques faster than ordinary martial artists. Compared to regular criminal trash, they were just stronger.
Nothing more.
Next, Wei He looked over at Han Xiangqi, who was still visibly emotional.
"Where is the Birth-Fruit?"
Han Xiangqi had already been told that she needed to hand over the Birth-Fruit of the Three-Eyed Dove as payment for this Grandmaster's help.
To avenge her grandmother, paying a fruit she didn't even know the use of was something she was more than happy to do.
She nodded right away.
"The Birth-Fruit is inside my family's treasure vault. I can't get it out right this second, but once things here are settled, I can take you there. The vault isn't far from here."
"Good. Handle the rest yourselves. Gong Huan, I leave these two in your care. Don't let them die early. They still need to be executed in public later," Wei He instructed. Not bothering to say more, he turned and flashed away.
The Three Faces case, which could have dragged on for a long time, had been crushed by his sheer force in a matter of minutes.
Now, it was time to visit his old friend in the provincial city.

A few days later...
Yuanzhong Town, Tianxiang Restaurant.
The restaurant was decked out in banners, colorful cloths, and flower baskets to celebrate a birthday.
Although Guan Yi couldn't host a grand feast at her main family home, the capture of Three Faces meant she was no longer grounded.
So she rented out a whole restaurant outside to throw her birthday party.
From the third branch of the Guan family, everyone showed up except for her irresponsible father, Guan Yude.
Her oldest brother Guan Wei, second brother Guan Fulin, and third brother Guan Qing were all present.
Guan Yi was the youngest sister and loved making friends. Her three brothers always tried their best to take care of her.
Aside from the four of them, several other friends from the Guan family had arrived. A clerk was writing down the guest names and gifts line by line.
"Second Branch, Guan Ziran: One Dingshu inkstone, thirty pearl beads."
"First Branch, Guan Xinyu: One Hundred-Flower Coral sculpture."
"Boss Yu from Chenghuan Street: One pound of premium Wind-Sand Gold."
Friends of the third branch arrived one by one, dropping off gifts before going upstairs to take their seats.
Guan Yi sat at the main table on the second floor, looking quite unhappy.
In the past, she had spent countless amounts of money to make friends. Looking around today, she never expected this result.
All the networks she had worked so hard to build, all the brothers and sisters of the martial world she had trusted—when it came to her birthday today, they didn't bring a single gift. They just walked right in to eat and drink for free.
Guan Yi was speechless as she watched the large tables in the corner of the second floor, packed with her so-called friends.
There were plenty of people—at least seventy or eighty sitting up here, not counting the dozen tables filled downstairs on the first floor.
Yet out of all these people, only a handful had actually given gifts.
The vast majority were just freeloaders who didn't care about their own pride at all.
To make matters worse, the second floor was extremely noisy, filled with loud drinking, bragging, and cheap talk. There wasn't a moment of peace.
Guan Fulin shook his head inwardly, feeling bad for Guan Yi as he saw her disappointed eyes.
Guan Yi was in her thirties now, but she still held onto foolish hopes. She had always believed that if she kept making friends endlessly, one day, if just one of them made it big, they would repay her far beyond what she had spent.
Sadly, reality was cruel.
These people were all shameless leeches. Anyone who had the thick skin to show up just to eat and drink for free clearly had no bottom line.
Forget about getting favors back if they ever got rich; even now, they wouldn't even remember her kindness.
They were probably calling her a fool in their hearts right now.
