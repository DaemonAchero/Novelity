Taoist Linhua took a deep look at Qingye.
"You are not afraid of offending the Masters of Xuanmiao Sect?"
"Fear? Of course I am afraid. But the truth is, our bond with Xuanmiao Sect is just a matter of trade. If not for the heavy price we offered, why would they agree so easily to help us move?" Qingye spoke with a cold, clear mind.
"Besides, given our past with them, we will most likely never see them again after this deal is done. Offending them a little changes nothing," she said with a faint smile.
"And it is only a few messengers. Can offending them mean we offend all of Xuanmiao Sect? That is too much of a stretch. When the time comes, we will just say the token was lost by mistake, and the others had to split up. They will have no words to say."
Her words hit the exact spot Taoist Linhua worried about.
"Very well. We will do as you say. Ask the Master from Xuanmiao Sect to step in, test the waters, and hold off any ambush in Yubian Town. We will take the hidden goods while they are busy, then leave together."
"Master is wise," Qingye bowed her head.
"Since you brought this up, you, Lu'e, and Huangxing will act together. We split into two teams. You three will place the token near Yubian Town, then spread out and wait for them to come. The rest of us will wait near the hidden spot. Once the Masters from Xuanmiao Sect show up, send the signal at once. We dig up the goods and go," Taoist Linhua ordered.
"Yes, Master," the three women replied at once.
"If you run into the Xuanmiao Sect Masters, just tell them you got lost from the main group. They will have nothing to say," Taoist Linhua added.
"Yes." The women agreed.
The group split into five smaller teams, waiting for the right time to strike.

Bang.
A heavy thud echoed inside Yubian Town.
A spiked club, as thick as a man's thigh, smashed hard into the dirt, carving out a small crater.
Dust kicked up as the iron spikes dug deep into the soil, locking the heavy weapon upright.
Huang Zhen wiped his bald head. He glanced at his two companions who were leaning against the wall, taking a nap.
"I say, how much longer are we going to wait here? The report said the Wushi Sect hid their treasures here. They will come for it sooner or later. But look at the time. Why is no one here yet?"
"What is the rush? We of True Blood are not as good at hiding as those of True Force. Even if they are here, could you even tell where they are?" one man opened his eyes and spoke in a cold tone.
"Bodhisattva Haizhu, say something, will you? If we just sit here, what if they never show up?" Huang Zhen snapped at the third person in frustration.
The third person was a massive giant, five meters tall, sitting cross-legged by the corner of the wall.
A red mark cut down between his brows. His thick muscles looked like solid bronze, hard as stone, with faint copper lines running across his skin.
More than ten thin gold rings hung from his ears. Each ring was the size of a fist, crowded together, pulling his earlobes down to the length of a palm.
"If they do not come, it saves us work. The hiding spot is right next to us. If anyone comes near, we will know," Haizhu opened his eyes and said.
Though all three were at the Bodhisattva rank, they were no ordinary men in the Hall of Buddha.
Huang Zhen and Haizhu were both top-tier Masters with terrifying skill.
In fact, Haizhu had reached the strength of a Buddha Lord over ten years ago. He only hid his power because he did not want to rank up.
After all, a higher rank brought more resources, but it also brought far too much trouble.
There were many hiding spots for the Wushi Sect's treasures. The place they guarded was only one of the smaller ones.
Beside the two of them, there was one more person named Zhou Lan.
He was the most unknown of the three.
Zhou Lan had been named a Son of Buddha five years ago. Rumor said a top Buddha Lord Master stood behind him.
Because of this, whenever there was an easy task that gave free credit, his name would be on the list.
It was the same today. This waiting trap was an easy job. The hidden goods here were not the main treasure of Wushi Sect, so any enemy coming here would not be too strong.
It was the best place for Zhou Lan to steal some quick credit.
Huang Zhen and Haizhu knew this very well. But they could not afford to mess with his background, so they gave his backer face.
"They are here," Huang Zhen suddenly sat straight, his voice dropping low as he looked toward the edge of the town.
Bodhisattva Haizhu heard the soft steps too. He stood up with a serious face and patted down the folds on his robe.
The three walked out of the yard.
This hiding spot was inside an old, broken yard in town.
They walked through the arch gate on the wall and looked toward the restless woods in the distance.
Two figures were rushing straight toward them.
"Wait? That is not Wushi Sect? Those clothes... they are from Xuanmiao Sect!?" Bodhisattva Haizhu sensed something wrong.
Looking from afar, this was not what they expected.
Huang Zhen and Zhou Lan were shocked too. Why would people from Xuanmiao Sect show up here?
While they stood shocked, Cai Menghuan and Wei He, who had just rushed over, were equally shocked.
They followed the signal from the token and ran straight to the spot. Yet they saw no one from Wushi Sect, only three big men in the light blue robes of the Great Yue Court.
One of the big men stood out like a mountain. Even sitting down, his five-meter frame gave off a wild, heavy pressure that chilled the heart.
"Junior brother, what is going on?" Cai Menghuan asked in confusion. "Why is the token signal coming from them? Could it be that these three are actually from Wushi Sect, just pretending to be experts from Great Yue?"
Cai Menghuan sure had a wild mind.
But Wei He took one look at the fierce eyes on the other side and knew things had gone wrong.
"No, they are real Great Yue men. As for why the token is in their hands, do you even need to ask?"
Wei He frowned tight.
"We came too late... All the people from Wushi Sect are dead."
He let out a sigh.
Cai Menghuan froze, then realized that this was indeed the most likely case.
"What a waste... If I knew... we would have run at full speed... What a waste..."
He used the word waste twice, then traded a quick look with Wei He.
Whoosh!!
The two spun around and ran away without stopping for a single second.
Since the Wushi Sect people were dead, why stay here? It was better to fall back to the ship and leave Great Yue before more hunters showed up.
"You think you can run!?" Haizhu, Huang Zhen, and Zhou Lan were unsure of the power of the two Xuanmiao Sect men at first.
Without a fight, no one could tell how strong an enemy was.
But seeing the two turn and run, the three instantly thought their massive size and aura had scared the enemy away.
After all, Haizhu's five-meter height carried insane pressure.
Any True Blood expert with that body was at least a Bodhisattva.
Gaining full confidence, the three charged out to hunt down Wei He and Cai Menghuan.
Once the pursuers were led away, Qingye and her group sent out the signal, telling Taoist Linhua to start digging up the real treasure.
The group was in high spirits. With Xuanmiao Sect drawing away the deadly hunters from Great Yue, they had gained a safe window of time without any worries.
Every Wushi Sect disciple in this group, including Taoist Linhua, felt relaxed.
They moved into the broken yard and began to dig at top speed.
"It is all thanks to Qingye's good trick this time. With these riches, our start across the sea will be much better than the rest of our peers. Qingye has done a great deed," Taoist Linhua said through voice transmission while swinging a shovel with inner force, clearing away the dirt.
"I did not expect things to go this smooth. It really is thanks to Qingye. Otherwise, if we returned empty-handed, we would not even afford basic pills to keep our cultivation up out there on the sea. A sect's holy land is not that easy to rebuild," Lu'e smiled.
"Indeed, Qingye has always been calm and reliable. At a time like this, her worth shines even brighter. We truly owe it to Junior Sister Qingye," another disciple praised.
Knowing they could split the treasure buried under the ground, everyone felt great. Even the hard task of moving across the sea felt easy to accept now.
Qingye took a soft breath, a faint, proud smile showing on her face.

On the other side.
Cai Menghuan and Wei He were running back at full speed, but Haizhu and the other two were right on their heels, refusing to give up.
The two sides moved like wind through the woods.
"Junior brother, those three behind us still dare to chase. Want to test their strength?" Cai Menghuan sent a quiet voice transmission.
Word said the True Blood system of Great Yue had grown much stronger thanks to the refinement of blood tools.
Cai Menghuan had only heard other Masters talk about it, but had never faced it himself.
Now that he ran into them, it was normal to test their blade.
"They came to set a trap, so they must have a way to call for help nearby. If we fight, we must kill them fast," Wei He replied.
"Then, I take two and you take one?" Cai Menghuan offered. "Let us see who finishes first!"
"You are the senior brother, you call the shots," Wei He smiled.
The two shared a glance, stopped dead in their tracks, turned around, and split to the left and right.
Streams of black gas burst out, covering the whole forest in a flash.
"Thunder Step, Shadow Flute!!"
Cai Menghuan's body blurred out of sight. The copper flute in his hand exploded into a wall of sharp dark shadows, swallowing both Haizhu and Huang Zhen whole.
Clearly, he picked the two strongest targets due to Haizhu's massive frame.
Wei He turned back, five thick black force pythons floating around his body.
"Go!"
He pointed his finger. The five giant pythons swooped down, roaring as they slammed into Zhou Lan, the last man standing.
Zhou Lan was shocked out of his mind. He was barely at the entry level of True Force, nowhere near a match for Wei He's five force pythons.
In a single clash, the black pythons flew back. All that was left on the ground was a pile of clothes and crushed bone bits.
On the other side, as the dark force mist cleared, Haizhu's massive body was covered in blood. He shot away into the distance like a cannonball, running for his life.
He moved so fast that even Wei He was shocked.
That speed was almost as fast as the mist state.
His skin turned dark red, a clear sign that he had used some self-harming secret skill to boost his speed.
Senior Brother Cai Menghuan's face flushed red as he pulled back his force.
"Junior brother, how are you this fast!?" He looked at Wei He in shock.
He had used a secret skill just to speed things up, yet he was still slower than Wei He.
He looked Wei He up and down.
"No wonder you are built like a beast, you really get things done fast!"
"..." Wei He stood silent. This senior brother was good in every way, except he had no filter when he spoke...
"If I knew you were this strong, I would have given you the big guy with the best body!" Cai Menghuan sighed again.
