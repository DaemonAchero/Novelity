Wei He scanned the crowd. He didn't recognize a single top fighter from the other two branches.
He walked over to the fourth row and stopped right at the spot marked with his name.
Most people around him were top-tier Xuanjin experts. He was the odd one out, standing there with only a low-level Perception limit.
Even so, no one dared to pick a fight with him. His cultivation level was low, but his combat power was famous across the entire sect. Everyone knew he had insane natural talent, massive blood reserves, and a few secret encounters that made him a monster in battle.
Behind Wei He stood the ordinary masters of the Xuanmiao Sect.
Line after line of masters stood divided into three groups, matching their three branches. A quick glance showed over a hundred people in total. This was the true hidden strength of the Xuanmiao Sect.
Lou Shengyue, who worked as a heavy laborer, had barely scraped into the Engraving stage last year. She hadn't even reached her first Perception stage or unlocked any hunting talents. Still, an Engraving master was an Engraving master. She was allowed to stand here, though she had to stay at the very back with the other laborers.
After a short wait, everyone settled down.
Looking down from above, the outer disciples filled the grand training grounds, split into three giant squares representing the Ethics Palace, the Heart Palace, and the Virtue Palace. Hundreds of people wore identical, fresh light-blue Taoist robes. Men and women wore the same color, though the women's robes were tighter at the waist to fit their figures.
The only difference between the three groups was the woven crest pinned on their chests, showing which faction they belonged to.
Wei He stood on the high stage right in front of the main grounds. The platform was almost ten meters tall, giving him a full view of the entire area.
In the center of the grounds were the three light-blue blocks. Because the disciples had never trained in formations, the "squares" looked more like three giant eggs. The organizers were trying their best to straighten the lines, but the strong outer disciples only made the formations look flatter and messier the more they tried to fix them. The only good thing was that everyone took it seriously. No one dared to run around or make noise.
On both sides of the grounds sat various guests and reps invited from the mainland. Wei He was surprised to spot a group of foreigners with blond hair and blue eyes.
Small wooden nameplates sat in front of their seats. Right next to them were representatives from the Haining Alliance, as well as delegates sent by various independent grandmasters.
A moment later, an old, white-haired Taoist with a legendary look jumped onto the main stone platform. He started talking and going through a long list of pointless chatter.
He started by announcing that the gathering was held for the primary Seed, Cai Menghuan. The old man went on and on about Cai Menghuan's life, his best moments, his combat records, and his noble character.
Then came the real deal.
"By unanimous decision of the top elders, Cai Menghuan is officially named the Dao Son of the Xuanmiao Sect as of today! He will also gain partial control over the sect's Star Formation!"
The moment those words left his mouth, a quiet buzz rippled through the crowd. But things settled down just as fast.
"Dao Seed" and "Dao Son" sounded similar, but they were worlds apart.
A sect could have many Dao Seeds. It just meant someone had high potential and might become a key figure later on. But a Dao Son was the chosen heir. It meant he was guaranteed to be the next sect leader. If Master Yuandu stepped down or died tomorrow, Cai Menghuan would take full control of the sect. As long as the three branch ancestors stayed in seclusion, he would call all the shots.
Cai Menghuan flew up to the platform and accepted the official symbol of the Dao Son: a plain, black wooden ring that looked completely unremarkable. He gave a short speech, thanking his teachers and Master Yuandu for their guidance, then stepped down.
Right away, the energy radiating from the other Dao Seeds shifted. They were clearly hit hard by this.
Wei He didn't care who became the sect leader. Instead, his eyes drifted toward the foreign group with blond hair and blue eyes.
A wooden sign in front of them read: The United States of Seankla.
What caught Wei He's attention was their clothing. Their outfits looked a lot like modern Western suits. Some held binoculars, staring up at the high platform. A few ladies in fancy dresses held fans, hiding their mouths as they whispered and laughed. They were clearly curious about the ceremony.
Seeing this brought back old memories.
Wei He missed his past life in the modern world. But he knew this world took a totally different path, especially after seeing those weirdly built firearms before. He cleared his mind and waited quietly for the event to wrap up.
This ceremony was purely meant to lock in Cai Menghuan's place as the Dao Son. As for why they chose this specific timing, there was probably more going on behind the scenes.
Soon, the ceremony ended. The top elders hosted a banquet for the invited guests. Wei He and the lower-ranked masters weren't forced to go, so they were free to leave.
Taking Lou Shengyue with him, Wei He took a quiet walk by Dragon Bay to clear his head before heading back to cultivate.
He was right at the edge of his current bottleneck. Once he finished converting his force, the remaining hurdles meant nothing to him. He could simply use the Breakbound Bead to smash right through them.
For other masters, every single Perception stage was terrifying. They spent every day worrying if they could break through, if they would get hurt, or if their force would backfire. They had to prepare for every bad outcome.
But Wei He was different. As long as he kept training and making progress, he could break through every time. With the Breakbound Bead, bottlenecks were the last thing he feared. His only real fear was having talent so bad that he couldn't make any progress at all. Without progress, he wouldn't even reach a bottleneck in the first place.
"Junior Brother Wei!"
A voice echoed directly into his ears just as he was about to head back to his cave.
The night was dim. Thin, veil-like moonlight fell over a figure walking toward him.
"Long time no see. Care for a walk?" the person asked softly.
Dragon Bay was dead silent, save for the constant crash of the ocean waves.
Wei He looked up, took a glance at the man, and nodded. "Sure."
As the man walked closer, the moonlight revealed his face. It was Huan Songzi, whom he hadn't seen in a long time.
"I heard you've been working hard lately," Huan Songzi said warmly, slapping Wei He on the shoulder.
"So, how is it going? Terrible, right?" He chuckled. "I told you not to waste your time. Look at you, still stuck at the second Perception stage. See? It's useless."
Wei He stayed quiet, though he really wanted to punch him. If he wasn't unsure about beating the guy without using his secret techniques, he would have slapped that smirk off his face on the spot.
"Let me tell you something," Huan Songzi continued. "At your age, you act like an old man, doing nothing but training all day. Where is the fun in that? You need to enjoy life. Look at me! I played around my whole life, and I still made it to the Xuanjin level, didn't I? Go with the flow! Endless hard training goes against the natural way of things."
"Senior Brother... can you get to the point?" Wei He said, totally done with the talk.
"Fine, fine. I just didn't want you to feel bored," Huan Songzi's smile faded slightly. "Yao Wan isn't here right now. Someone from the Yao family caused trouble and messed with one of my descendants. The matter was brought to me. I'm not close with Yao Wan, but I heard you two have a good relationship, so I came to ask what you think."
Yao Wan used to be a rising Xuanjin expert listed on the Dao Registry. But now, both her legs were paralyzed, and her nerves were damaged. She couldn't cultivate properly anymore, and her natural talent was ruined. Her future was completely gone.
Because of that, the Yao family's status had crashed. The only reason they survived was that Yao Wan had helped many masters in the past, giving her good connections. People gave the Yao family a break out of respect for her.
But now, the Yao family had crossed Huan Songzi.
Wei He was caught off guard. Huan Songzi was usually a lazy guy who laughed off everything and didn't care about anything. For a guy like him to personally track down Wei He over a family matter meant this wasn't a small problem. It was serious.
By coming here, Huan Songzi was giving Wei He face. The hidden message was clear: If I go after the Yao family, are you going to stand up for Yao Wan?
Wei He understood instantly. Whatever the Yao family did to Huan Songzi's people, it was bad.
Now that Yao Wan was disabled, her strength and future were gone. She still had connections, but far fewer people were willing to back her up now.
A quiet sense of sadness crept into Wei He's heart. Still, Yao Wan had taken good care of him in the past...
Wei He looked at Huan Songzi, who was waiting for an answer, and let out a soft sigh. "Senior Brother, can you tell me what actually happened with the Yao family?"
Asking this meant he was willing to shoulder part of the weight for her.
Huan Songzi patted Wei He's shoulder with force. "With that single line from you, what else can I say? Don't worry, it was just a fight between the younger kids. One of my descendants was beaten into a coma. But for your sake, as long as the Yao family comes forward, apologizes properly, and gives a full restitution, I won't press it any further."
"Thank you, Senior Brother," Wei He nodded.
"Forget the polite talk. To be honest, among all the masters of your generation, you're the one I respect the most," Huan Songzi said, though the words sounded a bit hollow.
Wei He didn't mind. He knew these top Xuanjin masters didn't care about his ability to fight above his level. His displayed combat power was decent, but it only matched a five-step Xuanjin level—the same as Huan Songzi. What they actually cared about was the fact that Master Yuandu favored him.
"Alright, I'll take off now. Make sure to talk to the Yao family and clean up the mess, then we'll call it even," Huan Songzi smiled.
"Will do," Wei He agreed.
"I'm off to get some drinks!" Huan Songzi waved his hand and disappeared into the darkness.
Wei He watched him leave. Once again, he felt the cold truth: the rise and fall of every inner master directly decided the fate of their families outside.
If Yao Wan still had her strength and potential, Wei He wouldn't need to step up at all. As a registered expert, she could have just brought her people over, apologized, paid up, and resolved the issue herself.
Sadly, the current Yao Wan didn't have that kind of leverage anymore.
She was still a master, but without a future, she had no hope left. If she stayed inside the holy grounds, she might be fine. But the moment she kept stepping outside, she would eventually turn into a monster.
Master Yuandu's cold attitude toward her lately made that crystal clear.
That was why Huan Songzi came to Wei He. If Wei He refused to step in, Huan Songzi wouldn't give Yao Wan any face, and he would crush her family.
Wei He sighed. He turned to look at Lou Shengyue, who was walking up to drape an outer robe over his shoulders.
"Let's go back," he said softly.
"Yes," Lou Shengyue hesitated, wanting to say something, but reading his cold expression, she held her tongue.
The two turned around and walked slowly toward the inner mountain's holy ground. Behind them, two sets of footprints—one deep, one shallow—stretched all the way from the beach to the mountain path.
In just half a month, Wei He would push his third-layer force to its limit. Normally, when an ordinary master hit this wall, they either had to spend years grinding or hope for a lucky break to force a breakthrough. Grinding meant wasting two full years, and even then, over half of the masters in the Xuanmiao Sect failed when trying to break through the Perception stage. A failed attempt meant permanent damage to their foundation, making the next try even harder.
Fortunately, Wei He didn't have to worry about any of that.
This incident only made him more determined to keep pushing forward.
