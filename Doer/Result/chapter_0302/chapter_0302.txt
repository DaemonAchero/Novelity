“It really is that!” Lu Weisheng’s eyes gleamed brighter as he saw the book. The iron balls in his palm spun faster, showing his growing excitement.
Legend said the Heaven Select Tome did not record mortal martial arts, but a special path to immortality.
No one could fully understand it before, so it kept drifting through the underworld. But recently, Wang Zui’s grandfather got obsessed with old books and accidentally found a new version.
To be exact, this book was a re-interpretation written by an unknown master based on the rumor in the world. It should be called the Heaven Select Tome Commentary.
Lu Weisheng learned from a spy in the Wang family that most of the notes were useless. Except for the very end. It described a secret technique called the Five-Turn Dragon Breath—the master’s ultimate skill.
It could ignite human potential and burn blood energy in a split second, exploding with terrifying force. Its power could reach five times the user's normal strength. That was why it was named Five-Turn.
Seeing his men struggle to catch the three, Lu Weisheng frowned in annoyance.
"Use the iron sand."
Iron Sand Hand Chen Shou smiled grimly. He pulled out a handful of iron sand. It was laced with poison that could paralyze a target instantly.
Even with their strange movement skills, a wide spray of poison sand would definitely hit them. Without defensive force, the three were bound to fall.
Chen Shou raised his hand, ready to throw.
The three turned pale. They wanted to run out of the exhibition hall, but the thick crowd blocked their path.
Seeing Lu Weisheng dare to use a wide-area weapon here without caring about other guests, a intense sense of danger rose in their hearts. If they didn't escape now, they were dead.
Bang! Bang! Bang!
Heavy steel plates suddenly slammed over the doors and windows from the outside, blocking them completely.
Their last hope of escape was gone.
In deep despair, Song Shixiong’s mind raced, trying to find a way out.
Suddenly, his eyes caught a figure in the crowd. He recognized the man instantly, his face lighting up with joy.
"Follow me!"
He growled, darting through the crowd like a fish. The other two followed closely. Before they could ask, Song Shixiong threw himself to the ground, kneeling right in front of a man standing by the wall.
"Master! Save us!!!"
"!!??"
Wang Zui was completely stunned, clueless about what Song Shixiong was doing.
His younger brother, Song Shizhen, quickly realized what was happening, but he wasn't as thick-skinned. They had agreed before: a painting in exchange for martial arts, money, and safe transport. The deal was done back then.
Yet Song Shixiong’s loud shout froze everyone—including Lu Weisheng’s men who were chasing them.
Chen Shou paused, unsure if he should throw the sand. Iron Bead Monk shifted his eyes toward Lu Weisheng, waiting for an order.
The rest of the men surrounded the three and the man looking at the painting.
Wei He frowned, turning around to look at Song Shixiong on the floor.
"I am not your master. You have the wrong person," he said coldly.
Their old deal was finished. He had noticed the brothers using some strange new footwork earlier. Since they were being hunted again, they must have caused more trouble.
He preferred a quiet life. If he stepped in for every mess, he would never have peace in these chaotic times.
"Master! I would never mistake you! Please, for the sake of our time together, save your disciple!" Song Shixiong pleaded, his voice breaking into tears.
No one knew how he mustered such sorrow in three seconds. Anyone watching would think: if this guy survives today, he will become someone terrifying in the future.
"I said, this has nothing to do with me. I am not your master," Wei He said calmly.
"You are wrong, Master! A teacher for a day is a father for life! I have always seen you as my real father!"
Song Shixiong shouted with deep emotion. As he spoke, he suddenly pulled a small booklet from his clothes and shoved it into Wei He’s hand.
"Master, this is the Heaven Select Tome! It holds the Five-Turn Dragon Breath technique! It can boost your full power by five times in an instant! I risked my life to get it just to give it to you!!"
He blurted it all out quickly.
Wei He froze.
His Whale Flood Art was an ancient martial art. Even at the second level, it only increased his blood energy and power four times. This Five-Turn Dragon Breath could boost power by five times at once?
This thing...
Wei He held the book, curious. He was about to open it when a voice cut in.
"Boss Chen, can you hand over the book to me? I am willing to pay five hundred taels of gold!"
Lu Weisheng stared intensely at the booklet in Wei He’s hand. The book was made of special material, fireproof and waterproof. He was sure it was real.
"Is this real?" Wei He asked in surprise. He flipped open the book. It was filled with strange symbols he couldn't read.
The last few pages used ancient script to describe a technique called Five-Turn Dragon Breath.
Except... only the name was there. The pages after were completely blank.
"This..." Wei He wasn't sure if it was meant to be like this or if the book was a fake.
His hesitation made Lu Weisheng impatient.
"Boss Chen, I know you are a retired officer with decent skills. But I am set on getting this book. Are you sure you want to stand in my way?"
He had let Wei He off earlier, but the man showed no gratitude. Now he even dared to touch the treasure right in front of him.
A murderous intent rose in Lu Weisheng’s heart.
Wei He looked at Song Shixiong kneeling before him, then down at the book.
"If I say I knew nothing about this before, would you believe me?" Wei He looked at Lu Weisheng.
"Since you put it that way, just hand it over and we can settle this peacefully," Lu Weisheng said, his tone softening slightly.
"I wasn't finished," Wei He said calmly. He narrowed his eyes. "Before, I didn't know. That was fine."
"But now that I know, I find it quite interesting."
A faint smile appeared on his face.
If he combined the Five-Turn Dragon Breath with his Whale Flood Art, he wondered how strong he would become.
"Are you playing me?!" Lu Weisheng’s face changed drastically.
Wei He didn't waste any more words. He wasn't talking just for Lu Weisheng to hear. Outside the hall, there were many others who came for this book.
Clap! Clap!
Wei He clapped his hands twice.
"Men."
BOOM!! BOOM BOOM BOOM!!
All the steel plates were smashed open. Dark figures flooded into the hall, surrounding everyone inside.
These men wore plain clothes, but every single one had defensive force surging around their bodies. They were all Martial Masters!!
The hall erupted into chaos as the guests panicked.
Lu Weisheng’s eyes widened, his blood almost running cold.
What was going on!?
He looked around. His own men quickly pulled back to protect him. Meanwhile, the incoming group of unfamiliar Martial Masters numbered at least ten!
Where did so many Martial Masters come from?
And this man in front of him... who was he!?
"Who on earth are you?!" Lu Weisheng blurted out.
He finally realized this man wasn't some ordinary retired officer. He was a giant shark hidden among small fish!
"I was wondering who it was. The grand Master of the Thousand Poison Sect, Brother Wei, actually chose to hide in Weifang City as a rich, idle man."
A voice echoed into the hall from outside. It sounded neither clearly male nor female, shifting close and far, making it hard to locate.
The crowd was already terrified, but hearing the name "Thousand Poison Sect" made their skin crawl.
The sector's reputation for poisoning thousands of people wasn't just a rumor. They were ruthless, vicious demons who ignored all rules.
Wei He ignored the terrified looks around him. He put away the book. Most of the time, he preferred not to kill unnecessarily. But every time he tried to show mercy, someone always forced his hand.
"Since you know who I am, why show yourself? Do you think I don't dare to kill?"
A single book could draw out so many experts. It seemed people had forgotten his name after he stopped killing for so long.
Crack.
The wooden floor under his feet shattered.
In a blur, he was already right in front of Lu Weisheng.
One palm attack!
BOOM!!!
Lu Weisheng’s two bodyguards, Iron Bead Monk and Iron Sand Hand Chen Shou, instinctively raised their arms to block.
Both of their arms snapped instantly. Behind them, Lu Weisheng shuddered. A sharp pain hit his chest, and a large cloud of blood exploded out from his back.
As Lu Weisheng collapsed backward, Wei He had already leaped out the window.
His wide sleeves unfolded like a giant bat as he hovered down. A wave of iron sand sprayed out from his hands—it was the very sand he had taken from Chen Shou earlier.
A group of figures in green rushed toward him, but the sand hit them, sending them crashing to the ground.
A woman in a red dress dashed toward him at high speed, swinging double blades. The blades whistled with sharp force, aiming for his arms.
Pfft! Pfft!
Two dull thuds sounded. The blades stopped right at the surface of Wei He’s skin, unable to cut through.
The woman looked up in disbelief. She had used all her strength. With her power, even a peak Viscera Refining expert wouldn't dare take this hit head-on, yet...
"People always think rumors are exaggerated. But there are always exceptions."
Pfft.
Wei He grabbed the woman's neck and lifted her up.
"Another piece of trash from the Incense Gathering Sect."
CRACK.
He snapped her neck effortlessly.
To him, living quietly before was giving face to the Wanfei Palace, following Jinzhou’s rules, and hiding from the Wushi Sect.
Now, his identity was exposed. But with his current standing, as long as he didn't ruin Jinzhou’s business environment, Xia Linghua wouldn't bother him.
After all, the Thousand Poison Sect could bring her far more profit than loss.
