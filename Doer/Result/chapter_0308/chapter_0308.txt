Taking advantage of the moment they were stunned by his transformation, Wei He slammed his foot into the ground and launched himself at Zhao Qing.
He did not know high-level moves, but with his current power, even a simple attack possessed terrifying force.
As he reached out, his five razor-sharp nails sliced through the air with a piercing screech. His massive hand aimed straight for Zhao Qing's head, threatening to crush it like a melon on contact.
"Come at me!" Zhao Qing laughed, stepping forward instead of retreating.
He ran the Three-Flower Golden Cone Power, driving its innate sharpness to a lethal edge. At the same time, he unleashed his secret skill, Lengfeng Kill, boosting his power's piercing ability even further.
Then came the true strength of a complete Real Realm warrior—the raw edge they had over the disabled martial artists of the Wushi Sect.
With a low roar, Zhao Qing activated the Genuine Power inside his body, forming three invisible power cones near his side. Each cone was the size of a fist. They floated unseen in the air, spinning toward Wei He's arm.
At the same time, Zhao Qing grinned savagely. He pressed his palms together to form a point and stabbed directly into Wei He's descending palm.
They collided in an instant.
Screech!
A harsh grinding noise tore through the air.
Wei He's arm was wrapped in layers of enhanced power, but three of those layers were shredded almost instantly by the invisible cones.
However, the remaining layers held firm, eroding the piercing force of the attack. Since the cones were not driven by Zhao Qing's physical body, their damage was limited.
Even so, the attack worked, disrupting the flow of Wei He's internal power.
Puff!
Zhao Qing's hands struck Wei He's huge palm.
Their wild energies smashed together and exploded outward, whipping up a fierce gale.
Blood spots welled up on Wei He's palm. Grunting, he was forced back several steps, his protective energy torn open by force.
Zhao Qing's face flushed red as he also stumbled back. That full burst of energy had nearly drained him completely.
"A pity, but I win!" He marvelled at Wei He's true strength, but the urge to kill him grew even stronger.
The holy mission of the Sacred Sect had to sweep away all obstacles!
"Die!" Ximen Xing struck from another angle, hurling a cloud of greyish-white powder with his other hand.
Zhao Qing alone could match Wei He. Without a hundred moves, no clear winner would emerge. Now with Ximen Xing joining in, Wei He was forced into a steady retreat. Even with all the heavy buffs from his Tower Form, he was barely blocking their attacks, stuck on the defensive.
Yet despite pushing him back, the pair did not rush the kill. They were wary of hidden trump cards.
At their level, who did not have a game-changing secret move?
They pressed forward steadily. If not for the extreme toughness of Wei He's Tower Form and his thick layer of protective power, he would already be a bloody mess.
After fighting for a while, Wei He shifted the battle toward the marsh.
He noticed that the powder thrown by Ximen Xing was chilling his body and slowing him down. If this dragged on, he would be in deep trouble.
If he used his newly broken-through Whale Flood Technique, his strength would surge, taking them both off guard.
But this was not the place for it.
The main road was nearby with too many travelers. If he exposed his trump card here, word would spread. If the Katori Sect or Chaos God Sect sent more people afterward, he would lose all room to maneuver. Once targeted, he would be dead meat.
So, he could not do it here!
Making a quick decision, he left a deliberate opening and took a blow from Ximen Xing on his left shoulder.
Bang!
A massive impact along with overlapping Genuine Power slammed into him.
Wei He's body shuddered as dizziness hit his brain. Forcing down the pain, he used the shockwave to fly backward, tapping his feet lightly against the ground.
With a swift whoosh, he shot off like an arrow, heading away from the main road.
"Trying to run!" Having tested Wei He's strength, Ximen Xing and Zhao Qing stopped holding back and gave chase at full speed.
All three moved fast, reaching a cluster of small hills deep in the plains in no time.
This was the very spot Wei He had chosen earlier—the hill where Qixia Cottage stood.
Without hesitation, he leaped straight into the forest.
Zhao Qing and Ximen Xing followed right behind him.
Trees blurred past them.
Though Wei He was fast, the white powder on his body slowed him down terribly. The dust had turned into a thick frost. The intense cold went past his natural resistance, heavy enough to choke his blood flow and internal power.
"Wei He, you've been hit by my Ice Bone Dust. Your movement is ruined. You're dead even if you keep running," Ximen Xing called out from behind, his voice cold.
"Seeing how hard you've trained, if you yield to us today, I can spare your life."
The three dashed through the forest at breakneck speeds, turning sharply to dodge trees at the last second.
Wei He stayed silent, ignoring the words as he pushed toward the back of the hill.
His speed, however, was dropping noticeably.
Even Zhao Qing, who had weaker footwork, was easily catching up now.
"Still refusing to give up? You won't cry until you see your own coffin!" Zhao Qing snorted.
"Go!"
He pointed a finger, sending three gas cones shooting straight toward the back of Wei He's head.
The three cones lined up, stacking their force to make up for their lack of individual penetration.
Puff! Puff! Puff!
The cones tore through layer after layer of Wei He's defense, breaking apart one by one as they did so.
Soon, the first cone exploded.
Then the second.
The third cone was half-melted when it finally tore through the last layer of Wei He's thick armor.
Puff!
Wei He jerked his body aside, dodging the final cone by a hair's breadth.
He dodged one side, but could not dodge the other.
Ximen Xing flashed forward, slamming both fists into the open space beside Wei He.
With a thunderous crash, two ring-shaped shockwaves erupted from his hands.
The sheer force squeezed the air together and blew apart.
Wei He raised his arms to block, but his three-meter-tall body was knocked back several meters by the blast.
Bang!
His back slammed into a large tree trunk.
The tree shattered, sending leaves showering down.
Shua!
Zhao Qing gathered three more gas cones, linking them like a long sword aimed right at Wei He.
"One last chance. Surrender or die?"
Back against the tree, Wei He slowly raised his head.
"This spot looks good enough."
"What do you mean?" Before Zhao Qing could finish, his vision blurred.
Bang!
The tree behind Wei He blew apart, and the ground exploded.
His weak-looking body erupted with terrifying speed. A wave of wild, scorching blood energy burst out from him, generating a hot wind that swept across the clearing.
Mud and rocks flew everywhere, blocking Zhao Qing's view.
In that exact instant, a massive hand reached through the dust, crushed the three gas cones, and grabbed Zhao Qing by the arm.
He swung!
Bang!
Zhao Qing was slammed into the ground by a force he could not fight.
The dirt caved in with a loud crash.
Wei He spun, driving his elbow down hard.
Crack!
Zhao Qing's arm snapped. Before he could even scream, Wei He's elbow slammed into his head.
With a wet squelch, his face caved in completely, spilling red and white across the dirt.
Wei He let go of the corpse and stretched his neck, looking over at the stunned Ximen Xing.
"See? Life is full of surprises."
"You had your fun hitting me earlier. Now it's my turn."
Shua!
The ground exploded under his feet as explosive force launched him forward at higher speeds.
His skin was dark red, his three blood sacks pumping vast amounts of stored blood through his veins.
The endless power made him feel stronger than ever before.
Pure physical force was one thing, but combining it with his internal power and the boost from Tower Form meant he could easily beat three of his past selves.
Speed and power were everything.
Even in martial arts where internal power ruled, when the gap in raw speed and strength grew too large, the outcome was already set.
"Could you be...!" Ximen Xing tried to speak, but he was out of time.
A palm as big as a fan was already at his face.
He pulled all his power together, triggering the second level of the Real Realm's special trait: Armor Form!
Dense layers of internal power formed a heavy shield around his body.
He brought both hands together in a full-power guard, firing off his Secret Technique, Double Step, to boost his strength once more.
With all these desperate buffs stacked together, Ximen Xing's arms swelled up, turning deep red.
Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!
Their four arms clashed rapidly. The raw power and grinding energy made leaves fall like rain.
Ximen Xing's face grew redder as his arm bones groaned under the pressure.
He possessed great strength, but he was no Abomination.
I need to run! The thought crossed his mind.
Once a warrior lost his resolve and hesitated, his strikes lost their power.
And against someone stronger than him, that meant only one thing.
Wei He grinned. His right hand smashed aside Ximen Xing's guard, while his left palm shot straight into his open chest.
Heavy power surged forward like a tide.
"Die!!"
The palm landed square on Ximen Xing's chest.
Like a cannonball, the blow shattered the armor layer, crushed skin, muscle, and bone, blasted through his organs, and burst out his back.
Puff!
Blood sprayed into the air.
Ximen Xing grabbed Wei He's arm with both hands, staring down at his caved-in chest in disbelief.
"I... accept... this..."
He still had hidden moves left, but Wei He had exploded too fast, leaving him no time to use them.
More than that, he never expected Wei He to unleash damage on par with a Second Mind-Fixing Realm warrior.
Top experts at that level had nearly completed their physical refinement. They met the standards for major sects and rarely roamed the outside world, let alone lived in seclusion.
Yet he had run right into a monster like Wei He.
From the sudden burst to the double kill, Wei He had taken only fifteen seconds.
Chii.
He pulled his hand back and wiped away the blood.
The ten thousand pounds of force from the Whale Flood Technique was indeed impressive compared to a normal Viscera Training fighter.
Against a Real Realm expert, though, it still lacked that tiny edge needed to end things instantly.
At the end of the day, Real Realm warriors were on a different level. Their bodies had been hardened, gaining boosts to speed, power, recovery, and defense.
Looking down at Ximen Xing's corpse, Wei He sighed.
He did not enjoy killing. But in this world, if he did not kill, others would kill him.
He leaned down and quickly searched the bodies, pulling out their belongings.
One was from the Katori Sect, the other from the Chaos God Sect. Their identity tokens made their origins clear.
Alarm bells rang in Wei He's mind.
He had too many enemies. The harder he hit back, the deeper the grudges grew. Now that he had killed a Real Realm warrior from both sects, they would not let it rest.
This was a major grudge now.
Unlike killing minor branch members, losing a Real Realm warrior—a proper one, not a disabled one like those in the Wushi Sect—was a heavy blow to any faction.
And he had just killed two.
"Trouble... I need to leave right away, or quickly find a powerful backer."
Wei He's thoughts naturally drifted to the recommendation spot Palace Master Wan Fei had promised him.
