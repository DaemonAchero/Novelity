The Wushi Sect didn't care about geniuses. Anyone who made it here was already a genius, just to different degrees.
As heads of the Inner Court, the several leaders present had seen countless talents come and go. Yet rarely did anyone disturb their minds the way Wei He just did.
If they had taken him in back then, things might be very different today.
Qing Ye couldn't stop the thought from rising in her chest.
What a shame...
She felt pity for the deaths of Yan Jiuzhen and Song Qinghu, but even more so for Wei He, who was about to die.
The Masters of the Three Main Peaks were furious. They were coming down the mountain to hunt him. There was no way Wei He would survive.
"Senior Brother... After all these years, are you still using this way to make sure I never forget you?" Qing Ye sighed quietly. She turned and used her movement technique to leave.
Unlike the cold indifference of the inner and outer courts, the Three Main Peaks moved much faster than expected.
Two hours later.
Two people walked down from Jade Tooth Peak. They took no followers and headed straight toward the Black House Mountains, right in the direction of the Ten Thousand Poison Sect.
Light and shadow flickered through the thick forest, washing over the moving convoy.
Wei He sat quietly at the very back of the last carriage.
the horses pulling the cart were no ordinary animals. They were half-beast horses mixed with special monster blood.
These horses were much taller, stronger, and more muscular than normal ones. Their stamina was incredible, their senses were sharp, and they were smarter than normal livestock.
Wei He sat on the side of the carriage with the door wide open, his legs dangling in the air. Listening to the idle chatter and the noisy laughter of the children in the convoy, he let out a soft sigh.
He ran his fingers over the rough wood of the carriage.
"Without realizing it, I've become the kind of person I used to hate the most."
He was just a normal guy who wanted a quiet, simple life.
Yet even a simple wish like that was impossible to reach.
Every single moment, he had to plan for every possible threat that might come his way.
Every single day, he spent endless hours training his martial arts. No holidays, no fun, almost no friends. Just hard training, over and over again.
"Master, the Jinzhou border is just ahead. I can see the boundary marker," a guard by his side said softly, approaching with caution.
This guard had just been released from the dungeon. His body was filled with locked poison, and his spirit was completely broken. He was terrified every time he saw Wei He.
In fact, ever since last night's battle...
No one in the entire Ten Thousand Poison Sect, from top leaders to fresh disciples, looked at Wei He without fear.
Killing people was easy. But killing them until everyone was terrified of you—that took real skill.
"How long until we reach Weifang City?" Wei He asked casually.
"At our current pace, about a month," the man answered carefully. "We are already moving fast. Every cart is traveling at more than twice the speed of a normal convoy."
"Move faster," Wei He said simply.
Suddenly, his ears twitched. He pushed off with one hand and dropped lightly to the ground.
"I'm going to stretch my legs. Keep moving. I'll catch up soon."
Leaving those words behind, he used his movement technique. With a few quick leaps, he vanished into the dense woods.
He moved so fast that most of the people in the convoy didn't even notice he was gone.
Their Sect Master moved like a ghost anyway, appearing and disappearing all the time. It was nothing new. Even if someone noticed, they wouldn't care. They were used to it.
Wei He went back about a mile, stopped by the side of the road, and sat cross-legged on a large flat stone.
From far away, he could feel someone chasing after him, and they were moving extremely fast.
After he killed two court heads right in front of everyone last night, only one group of people would dare to chase him down so openly.
It had to be the experts from the Three Main Peaks of the Wushi Sect.
This time, things would be different. These were not soft targets like the ones before.
This was going to be a hard fight.
Wei He slowed his breathing and readied his body.
His fight against Yan Jiuzhen and the other two didn't even test his real strength. Those three were like soap bubbles—they popped the moment he touched them.
But this time, the Wushi Sect would send real Visceral Sensing experts.
It was March, just after winter. The leaves in the forest were dark and dead. Cold wind howled through the trees like a eerie instrument.
Before long, Wei He heard two figures rushing down the main road.
No horses, no carriages. They were running purely on their own two feet.
Both wore pure white Taoist robes, but their sleeves were cut short. Beneath the short sleeves, their arms were wrapped in layers of grayish-white cloth bandages.
The cloth was covered in ancient symbols written in faint red ink. From a distance, they looked mysterious and strange.
Soon, the two spotted Wei He waiting by the road and stopped immediately.
One was tall and thin, the other was short and fat. They looked no different from ordinary monks you would meet on the street.
Yet only Wei He could feel the massive blood energy and power waves rolling off their bodies.
It felt like standing next to a burning steel furnace, with waves of intense heat crashing into your face.
Right now, these two were like two open furnaces, showing off their power without holding back.
The tall, thin monk held a scroll with a drawn picture. He unrolled it, took a look, and then looked at Wei He.
"Wei He of the Ten Thousand Poison Sect?" he asked in a sharp voice.
"That's me. And you two are?" Wei He stood up and slapped the dust off his robe.
"Song Ting."
"Song Xiange."
They answered short, one after the other.
"You killed two of our Inner Court heads. You've got strength, and you've got guts. Young man, if I were in your shoes, I might not have had the courage you showed," the tall old man, Song Ting, said loudly.
"Brother, why talk so much? Finish him off so we can get back up the mountain! If we stay too long, we'll get into trouble!" the other man, Song Xiange, said impatiently.
"What's the rush? It's not often we get to come down the mountain. We should do things right," Song Ting replied.
"What do you want to say?" Wei He was happy to stall for time. He was also studying these two experts from the Wushi Sect.
Truth be told, he hadn't met many people at the Visceral Sensing level, so he knew very little about it. Now that he had the chance, he wanted to observe them as much as possible.
"I just want to ask, why did you have to kill them?" Song Ting asked curiously. "You could have just captured them. That way, you wouldn't have completely ruined things with our Wushi Sect and created an absolute death grudge."
He didn't seem to care about the deaths of Song Qinghu and Yan Jiuzhen at all. He was simply curious about the answer.
Wei He went silent.
"What, is it hard to say?" Song Ting asked in surprise.
"No... nothing like that," Wei He sighed. "To be honest, I didn't plan to kill them... I just didn't expect them to be unable to block even a single hit..."
Song Ting went silent this time.
"Alright, Brother! Why talk so much? Just kill him and get it over with! Come on! I need to get back early to train my mind!" Song Xiange urged again from the side.
"You're quite an interesting fellow," Song Ting looked at Wei He with regret. "I heard you used to belong to a branch of our Wushi Sect. It's a pity your luck was bad and you failed to join the Inner Court. Otherwise, things might have turned out very differently today."
He took a few steps forward, unbuttoned his top robe, and tossed it onto a nearby tree branch.
"The Ten Thousand Poison Sect comes from the Heavenly Seal Sect. Speaking of which, I fought with Shangguan Ji back in the day. Ever since his father passed away, he went downhill every day, losing his spirit step by step.
At our level of martial arts, if you don't push forward, you slide back. And sliding back means danger. He just never understood that. What a waste..."
"Brother! Stop talking!" Song Xiange shouted in annoyance.
"Fine, fine. Young man, I'm not like those weak fools you fought earlier. Be careful, my hits are heavy," Song Ting warned seriously, appearing to take a liking to Wei He.
"Song Ting... Song Ting..." Wei He suddenly remembered something. He had read many books at the Tianyai Building and knew a bit about the older generation of masters in Taizhou.
The name Song Ting was famous eighty years ago. Back then, he was recorded in the local history as a top-tier powerhouse.
According to the records, he was once the head of the Seven Courts in the Inner Court.
"Are you... the third strongest master in Taizhou eighty years ago, the Mountain-Shaking Fist, Song Ting?" Wei He realized, his eyes widening.
"Haha! It seems I still have some fame left. I'm glad someone still remembers an old man like me," Song Ting laughed happily.
"In that case... I'll leave you with one breath of life!"
The moment the words left his mouth.
Song Ting took a heavy step forward and bent his body like a bow.
His muscles bulged and expanded rapidly. His dark skin turned a deep, reddish-black.
In less than two seconds, he transformed from a tall, thin old man into a giant muscular monster over two meters tall.
"Shadow's End Step."
Song Ting twisted his foot. In an instant, his massive body turned into a blur and charged straight at Wei He.
Boom!
A series of rapid, heavy footsteps merged into a single explosive sound because of his terrifying speed.
Wei He felt his eyes blur, and the man was already right in front of his face.
He didn't dare drop his guard. The power he had gathered exploded instantly. His locked blood energy rushed out, and his body expanded rapidly, returning to his three-meter tall form.
Boom!
Both hit out at the same time. One used the Mountain-Returning Fist, the other used the Heart-Breaking Seal.
Fist met palm. One force was quick and strange, while the other was heavy and tough. They were evenly matched.
After one strike, both took several steps back to catch their breath.
"You've got some skill!" Song Ting laughed. "That's not True Force, yet you can block mine. Interesting."
"You're not bad either, senior," Wei He felt relieved. This was what a real Visceral Sensing expert should look like.
If everyone was as weak as Yan Jiuzhen, he would be deeply disappointed with this level. So much risk, so little gain. It wouldn't make any sense.
He tested the strength of the other man's active force. It couldn't break through his defensive force, but it definitely felt dangerous.
Comparing them, this Song Ting was a bit weaker than You Fu, but stronger than Zhu Chen. He was probably about the same as the Three-Headed Civet.
"Again!"
Without another word, they stepped forward and clashed head-on.
Both were experts in direct combat. The force of the Mountain-Returning Fist was known for its sheer weight. The fist style was solid and complete, built for both offense and defense.
Only now did Wei He truly understand why the Wushi Sect was so strong.
Song Ting's True Force wasn't crazy strong, and its special effect only added extra weight, which wasn't super high.
The moves of the Mountain-Returning Fist were not that fancy either; they were pretty standard.
Yet even though none of these things looked impressive on their own, when put together, they created a powerful mix. Song Ting's attacks felt like a moving mountain.
Every step forward or backward was like a mountain shifting place.
His attacks were heavy and fierce, and his defense was completely airtight.
Wei He tried using his speed to circle around him, searching for a weak spot or an opening to strike. But after spinning a few times, he couldn't find a single flaw in Song Ting's stance.
A simple set of fist moves, backed by the heavy True Force, formed a defense as tough as an iron wall.
In the end, Wei He had no choice but to trade blows with Song Ting face-to-face.
Fist against palm, trading hits directly. True Force clashed head-on with Ocean-Covering Force, grinding against each other.
Heavy booming sounds echoed through the forest without stopping. Neither side could get the upper hand.
Song Ting couldn't break Wei He's defensive force, and Wei He couldn't break through Song Ting's iron-like defense either.
Just as Wei He was thinking about using his secret arts, Song Xiange on the side finally made his move.
