By the edge of Tianyin Lake.
"So, I hope you understand. I probably won't be able to come see you much anymore," Yang Guo said, putting her hands together in an apologizing gesture.
She had actually seen the man beforehand and was very pleased with him. He matched the partner she had always dreamed of. Most importantly, he was rich.
So, for the sake of her ideal life, she cut off all ties with other men. Though leaving her friendship with Wei He behind felt a bit tragic, she still chose to break it off for her own future.
"I understand. I wish you both the best," Wei He nodded. He actually liked Yang Guo too.
She had a straightforward personality. She was clear about what she wanted and never played games.
He had thought that if he was ever pushed too far by life, he would just pursue Yang Guo, get married, and start a family.
Sadly, life changes fast.
Before he even made a move, her heart already belonged to someone else.
They talked a little more by the lake, and then said their goodbyes.
There was no regret. They were just pure friends, without a single bit of romance between them.
Yang Guo walked away freely, and Wei He felt no grief.
He watched her fade into the distance, disappearing into the thick white fog along the shore.
Left alone, Wei He raised his hands and formed the seal for the Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Skill once more.
The Breakthrough Bead in his chest was completely full. It pricked him gently, and a wave of heat immediately flowed through his body.
The heat circled inside him and quickly gathered at his hands.
Soon, his hands turned dark black. But this time, the black color on his fingers was slightly lighter.
This was the special trait of the Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Skill. When you start, your fingers turn pitch black during practice. But as your power deepens, the black color fades until your hands turn pure, jade white.
Wei He felt more heat crowd into his hands. It grew heavier and heavier, making him feel as if he was holding a massive iron ball.
After a few deep breaths, a sharp pop echoed in his ears.
The heat exploded like a flash flood. It surged out from his hands, rushed across his body, and covered every inch of his skin, except for the seven facial openings.
He had mastered the First Layer.
He had decided not to hide it anymore. He would just break through his main skill at a normal speed.
As for his excuse, he had already prepared one.
A Skill-Dispersing Technique.
This was a special technique created to clear away conflicting martial arts. It harmed the body and damaged a person's core blood and energy. People said it left many bad side effects.
Of course, he did not plan to actually clear his martial arts. He was going to fake it.
To figure out how the master from the Wushi Sect had seen through his extra martial arts at a single glance, Wei He had been busy lately. He had spent his time going in and out of the Tianya Building at the Tianyin Sect.
It was the largest library in the entire Tai Province.
Except for the secret martial manuals that cost money, most books were free to read. It was the best benefit for Tianyin Sect disciples.
From those books, Wei He had found what he needed.
Many pages mentioned that any warrior who practiced multiple styles would show abnormal blood flow. This abnormality showed in their energy, spirit, and muscle swelling.
Different martial arts caused blood to gather in different parts of the body. Wherever a person trained most, blood naturally gathered there.
And top martial artists had sharp senses. They could easily spot these blood clusters.
Wei He had studied this hard and quickly found a fix. He got his idea straight from the Skill-Dispersing Technique.
"First layer done. I need to claim the second layer now. Luckily, as long as I break through a layer within three years, I get the next one for free. Good thing it costs nothing," Wei He thought to himself. If he had to spend more money now, he was totally broke.
If he had not paid for a full year of Azure Fish meat in advance, he would not even have a meat ration this month.
He sighed and checked his remaining two hundred gold notes. That was the money he made from delivering messages.
To normal people, two hundred gold notes was a huge sum. But for a martial artist, it was only enough for a few months of basic supplies.
He was running out of money again.
Wei He pondered where he could score some quick cash.
Leaving the lake, he did not head straight to Wanqing Court to get the second layer of the skill. Instead, he went to the library first: Tianya Building.
Tianya Building was located inside the Thousand-Bat Water Pavilion. It was the most valuable spot in the entire sect.
It held not just martial manuals, but also countless logs and notes. It was a complete encyclopedia.
Wei He knew the way well. He walked in and stood before Tianya Building.
The building sat right in the center of the water pavilion. Thick stone walls surrounded it, with patrol guards walking along the top to catch book thieves.
There were dedicated fire guards, bug clearers to keep the humidity down, and a floor keeper.
The floor keeper was a mute old woman named Zeng. No one remembered her full name, so everyone called her Granny Zeng.
When Wei He reached the front, he gave his request to the inner disciple on duty. He received a small wooden tag that let him read on the first floor for one day for free.
As he walked through the main door, he caught sight of Granny Zeng sitting in a small pavilion nearby. She seemed to be playing chess with someone.
She never hid her fierce power, but she rarely cared about the disciples coming and going.
She was nothing like the secret, friendly old masters in storybooks who hid their strength and acted nice every day.
Wei He shook his head, clearing the silly thought from his mind.
Unless their skills were weird, what kind of top master would pass up a good life just to do useless chores every day? It made no sense.
Holding the wooden tag, he stepped into the first floor.
The first floor was a massive round hall. The bookshelves were set up in concentric circles, packed tight with books.
All the books were made from pale yellow paper. It showed that the papermaking skills of this world were quite good.
Wei He handed his tag to the timer at the entrance.
He washed his hands in clean water, wiped them dry with a towel, and pressed his palms into a bowl of white powder to soak up all moisture.
His hands became completely dry and smelled faintly nice. Only then did he change into special shoes and enter the hall.
Tianya Building had three floors. The first floor held general notes, while the second and third held real martial arts that required payment.
With only two hundred gold in his pocket, Wei He did not plan to go upstairs. He just strolled around the circular shelves on the first floor.
There were few readers inside. All of them wore inner disciple robes, mostly men. The hall was as large as two basketball courts, but only five people stood scattered around, reading carefully.
Wei He returned to the shelf he had checked before.
A label on the shelf read: Breathing Control, Skill Dispersing, Force Conversion.
This meant the books here focused on those three areas.
Wei He scanned the titles: Turtle Breathing Technique, Energy Hiding Method, Sleeping Lotus Skill, Mountain Leaf Sleep Technique, Drunkard Skill Dispersing, Tower Force Conversion, Iron Force Conversion, Dingtuzhi Skill Dispersing, Liu's Skill Dispersing, and more...
The famous Turtle Breathing Technique was the first thing to catch his eye.
He did not pick it up. He had flipped through it before, and it only taught simple ways to spot the skill, not how to train it.
This was how Tianya Building worked. The first floor never held actual training methods. It only had introductory texts to tempt disciples into paying to go upstairs.
It also let disciples read the effects first so they could head straight for their target on the upper floors.
The seniors who built this place had certainly put a lot of thought into making money.
Wei He reached out and pulled a thin booklet from the shelf. The title read: Five Spirits Breathing Control Method.
It recorded a practical trick designed to hide blood flow from outsiders.
That was exactly what Wei He wanted.
He flipped through to check its location, price, and description.
'Five Spirits Breathing Control Method: Created by the Five Spirits Hermit. Used to balance blood energy and hide oneself in the world. It can adjust blood energy and force, heal injuries caused by hidden force, and treat irregular health issues, stomach pain, and mouth sores...' Wei He took the booklet, grabbed a random Skill-Dispersing text, and went to the front counter.
A worker there would go up to the second floor to get the actual manuals for him.
A short while later, Wei He stepped out of Tianya Building with two copied manuals.
He touched his empty money pouch and felt a wave of sadness.
Still, he had secured his excuse for future breakthroughs. Fortunately, these two skills were cheap enough for him to buy.
Carrying the Five Spirits Breathing Control Method and the Dingtuzhi Skill Dispersing, Wei He hurried home and started training.
He was smart, and the Five Spirits method was not hard. After just two days, he mastered the basics.
It was a simple trick that just took constant practice until it became second nature.
The core idea was to smooth out blood flow, reduce internal friction, and stop others from reading his movements.
Once Wei He understood the logic, practice became easy.
Half a month later, when he stepped out of his house again, all signs of his secondary martial arts were gone.
Only the thick, heavy blood energy of the Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Skill remained on his body.
This was another trick he had worked out: using the heavy blood energy of his main skill to mask everything else.
As long as his main skill stayed active, it covered up his other traits.
With the Five Spirits method hiding his breath and his main skill masking his extra traits, Wei He was ready. He decided that in a couple of days, once news arrived from the Purple Bamboo Society, he would head to Wanqing Court to collect the second layer of his skill.
He had done everything he could. Success now depended on the results.
