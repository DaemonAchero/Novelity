"It's fine, it's fine..." Chang Xuezhong recalled Wei He's background. He knew Wei He could already fight Visceral Sensing experts back in the Visceral Refining realm, making his actual combat ability extremely fierce.
But that was Visceral Refining. Not now.
In Visceral Refining, there were always a few people with exceptional combat power for various reasons. But that didn't mean they could still fight above their level after entering the True Realm.
In the True Realm, no one knew what kind of abilities a True Person or a True Beast possessed without actually fighting them.
"Where is your Profound Lock Force at right now?" Chang Xuezhong asked carefully after a short pause.
Normally, asking a question like this was like asking about someone's monthly income. It was bad manners to ask so casually.
"I just need to grasp your specific situation so I can tailor our next itinerary. By the way, that True Beast is called a Human Ghoul. It's essentially a special entity born from the Burial Ground. It can confuse people's minds, and on the outside, it manifests as a kind of unknown toxic gas."
"Toxic gas?" Wei He understood. He remembered the numb, itchy feeling he got when the beast grabbed his arm.
"I see." He nodded. "Don't worry, Senior Brother. I understand. As for my cultivation, I think I've just reached the First Layer."
"First Layer? No wonder!" Chang Xuezhong was shocked at first, then understood.
The First Layer required filling the entire body with True Returning Force.
Wei He was likely born with natural talent and an unusually massive amount of Original Blood, which allowed him to reach the First Layer so quickly.
"That makes sense. Your files mentioned that you were born with immense physical strength, so having strong Qi and blood is normal." Chang Xuezhong found a logical explanation.
Wei He looked at him, feeling a bit confused.
"Senior Brother, is my level considered strong? Where do I stand among peers of the same level in our sect?"
"It's decent. Having enough force lets you hold out longer outside, and it keeps you safer during Sensory Fixation. After all, when people reach Sensory Fixation, everyone tries their best to extend their endurance limit. Your level... is upper-middle class."
Chang Xuezhong suppressed his tongue, managing to swallow the word "top-tier" and replacing it with a more modest evaluation.
"Alright..." Wei He nodded in partial understanding.
Yet, he remained confused in his heart.
He had only used one-tenth of his Profound Lock Force. That was already considered upper-middle class?
He naturally didn't realize how massive his expanded Original Blood was after stacking the Whale Flood Art, True Method, Cloud Smoke Art, and other techniques.
Wei He was no fool. Having led the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect for so long, he could tell Chang Xuezhong wasn't fully speaking his mind.
A guess formed in his mind.
'It seems the total amount of True Returning Force I converted is even larger than I thought. I must use it carefully in the future and not overdo it.' He understood the principle that a tall tree catches the wind.
Exceeding others by a small margin was fine and reasonable.
But exceeding others by too much would inevitably expose the existence of the Whale Flood Art.
Techniques like the Whale Flood Art were not particularly rare. Since even a secular force like the Heavenly Seal Sect could collect it, it was clearly not an extinct, secret technique.
The critical issue was that cultivating it required large amounts of extinct mutated beasts. He had used the Boundary Breaking Bead to replace those beast remedies to complete the technique.
That was the key.
Exposing the Whale Flood Art meant exposing the power of the Boundary Breaking Bead.
Once Wei He figured this out, he made up his mind. In the future, he would only show the force he had just burst out with—roughly one-tenth of his Profound Lock Force.
As for the rest, it was better to keep it hidden.
Originally, he had planned to disperse his Rain Cover Force back into Original Blood as well. But to cover his tracks, he kept this technique as a disguise.
At the same time, the special effect of the Rain Cover Force was extreme speed, which could make up for the True Returning Force's lack of speed.
"Alright, this Human Ghoul's talent is illusion and confusion. Do you want it?" Chang Xuezhong pulled out a purple bead from somewhere and pressed it against the beast's corpse to draw something out.
"How strong is the illusion?" Wei He asked with a frown.
"Didn't you just test it? It made your hand feel itchy and numb like you were poisoned. If you truly believed it, you would definitely be poisoned. It's a type of phantom poison. If you don't believe it, nothing happens," Chang Xuezhong explained.
Wei He raised his hand. There were indeed red spots on his left hand. This ability felt a lot like a scam turning into reality.
"Can this talent be used over an area?" he asked.
Chang Xuezhong picked up the bead, looked at it, and shook his head.
"No, it requires close physical contact and can only be used on one target at a time."
"Then forget it." Wei He thought for a bit and gave up. "This ability has too many conditions. Let's find another one."
"It's already quite good, much more useful than the ones on the mountain. Paired with your poison skills, you could even restrict experts stronger than you in close combat. If you trick them successfully, they will definitely be poisoned. Most importantly, talent abilities cost nothing to use and can be cast once a day. Used well, it's a guaranteed win once a day!" Chang Xuezhong advised.
The ability was indeed decent.
However, Wei He didn't want a poison-related talent. Poison was just a side tool. What he truly wanted was a talent ability that could boost his overall combat power.
Seeing Wei He's unmoving expression, Chang Xuezhong knew his mind was set and stopped trying to persuade him.
"Alright then. What a waste..." He looked at the beast's corpse on the ground, shook his head, and stood up.
Absorbed talents could only be used by the hunter. Others couldn't use them.
Otherwise, if Wei He didn't want it, he could sell it and make a fortune.
"Next one," Wei He said.
"Yeah."
"By the way, Senior Brother, are there no Star Stones?" Wei He asked suddenly.
"There are, but very little. Only three or four ounces. Do you want them?" Chang Xuezhong said casually.
Three or four ounces wasn't even enough to buy a single Profound Heart Pill.
"Besides, you used too much force earlier and crushed the Star Stones. They're probably scattered all over the corpse now..." Chang Xuezhong said helplessly.
"Alright..." Wei He noted down another key point. 'Looks like I must be careful while hunting in the future. I can't smash the Star Stones...' "Yeah, there's no helping that. But usually, a True Beast's Star Stone is located in its fatal spot. Just mind your force when hunting. True Returning Force can offset the corrosion of their bodies, so you don't need to smash them to pieces with brute force."
"Got it..."
The two briefly packed up. Chang Xuezhong removed some useful materials from the Human Ghoul, left the stone bridge, and headed to the second location.
The second investigation spot was a desolate mountain valley three hundred miles away.
The valley originally had no name. Because it was near Feiyun Manor, people often called it Feiyun Valley.
The two rode their horses at full speed.
They finally reached their destination before nightfall.
Inside the desolate, dark, and lonely valley.
A layer of dim mist drifted around.
Roar!
A massive black turtle with two heads rushed swiftly toward Wei He's position.
The giant turtle was over two meters tall and more than ten meters long. Its body was covered in black armor, and its four eyes glowed with a faint green light. When it charged, it felt like an armored tank with a fierce, terrifying momentum.
"This is a Two-Headed Turtle. The sect has hunting records on it. Its talent ability is Skin Hardening. It can significantly boost physical strength for a short time. It's a purely defensive talent," Chang Xuezhong introduced via voice transmission from behind.
Wei He stood at the front with a vigilant look.
After his last experience, he knew the danger of touching a True Beast at close range.
So this time.
He decided that no matter what he touched, he would blow it up with a single punch first.
He opened his eyes, but saw nothing except a patch of pale yellow mist in front of him.
However, his left hand in the hyper-sensory state could feel a massive entity charging straight at him.
In the outer world, many True Beasts existed in an invisible state.
Ordinary people couldn't touch them at all.
Only in the hyper-sensory state could the True Beasts materialize.
This was caused by a massive difference in perception.
Amid heavy, thumping footsteps, the Two-Headed Turtle slammed into Wei He.
However, Wei He dodged to the side and threw a sudden punch with his left hand.
Bang!
His fist struck the side shell of the Two-Headed Turtle, instantly creating a loud impact.
The shell caved in rapidly and shattered, with large cracks spreading everywhere.
The Two-Headed Turtle screamed in pain. A full third of its shell was shattered by this single punch.
Before it could roar again, another fist slammed down right on its head.
Bang!
The dust settled.
The giant Two-Headed Turtle crashed to the ground, lifeless.
Chang Xuezhong shook his head slightly from a distance.
This Junior Brother was truly worthy of Senior Sister Yuan Duzi personally taking action to accept him as a disciple on behalf of their master.
Looking at him now, he was truly incredible.
The Two-Headed Turtle had such thick skin, yet it was beaten to death purely with True Returning Force.
To possess such deep True Returning Force right after entering the Inner Mountain put him on par with those direct disciples who possessed immense Qi and blood.
Direct disciples cultivated the sect's core techniques from a young age, wasting not a single drop of Original Blood. By the time they entered the Inner Mountain, all their Original Blood would be converted into True Returning Force.
That was why direct disciples in the Inner Mountain usually had much stronger True Returning Force than outer disciples.
After all, their Original Blood wasn't wasted on practicing other martial arts.
Yet as an outer disciple, Wei He managed to possess such powerful True Returning Force even while practicing multiple techniques. It showed he was born with naturally vast Original Blood.
Chang Xuezhong shook his head helplessly.
Looking at the dead Two-Headed Turtle lying on the ground, he walked over to check the talent and collect the Star Stone.
This Two-Headed Turtle was considered tough among low-level True Beasts, yet it fell after just two punches against Wei He's massive strength and force.
Even if he took action himself, without that kind of natural strength, he could only grind it to death using True Returning Force. He couldn't knock it down in two punches like Wei He did.
Even though both could achieve a quick kill, Wei He saved so much effort.
True Returning Force was meant to resist the Ghost Wind, not just for hunting True Beasts.
If he used up too much, it would be disastrous if he ran into the Ghost Wind.
"I don't think you need my help at all. You could easily hunt alone down the mountain," Chang Xuezhong couldn't help sighing.
Wei He smiled, sensing his Senior Brother's mild frustration, but said nothing.
"The talent is Skin Hardening. It suits you well, useful for both defense and offense. However, your skin might turn black," Chang Xuezhong said.
"How strong is it?" Wei He asked.
"The strength depends on your own physique. The better your foundation, the higher the strength. The exact effect can only be seen after you use it."
"Let's store it for now," Wei He thought for a moment, pulled out an Amber Bead, pressed it against the Two-Headed Turtle, and began absorbing it.
A talent ability was just an ability. How strong it was depended on how he used it after absorbing it.
If used well, Skin Hardening could produce good results in direct combat.
True Beasts and True Persons fought using active forces like True Returning Force, which all had penetrating power. Wei He wasn't fully satisfied with just Skin Hardening alone.
"That works too. Store it first. When you find a better one later, you can decide whether to swap it. The Amber Bead can release stored talents anyway," Chang Xuezhong agreed.
Compared to the last time he helped a newcomer hunt, Wei He saved him so much trouble this time.
Wei He's own strength was already comparable to experts at the First Sensory Fixation level, only one layer behind him. He was far ahead of other new disciples.
Combined with his natural strength, fighting these low-level True Beasts was simple. A few punches was all it took for one beast.
Wei He quickly retrieved the Star Stone. The Two-Headed Turtle's Star Stone weighed eight ounces, which was pretty decent.
The two then continued toward the next location.
