"Senior misunderstood. He just didn't take me seriously and didn't want to waste time," Wei He replied. "To be honest, I also learned leg techniques. That helps me absorb a lot of the force when moving fast."
"Leg techniques?"
Before the words fully left his mouth, You Zhuang lunged forward. His hand shot out like a bird's sharp beak, pecking hard toward Wei He’s right shoulder.
Wei He was already prepared. He brought up his Return Mountain Fist and Rain Cover Seal, meeting the blow head-on.
Unexpectedly, You Zhuang shifted his grip effortlessly, changing direction and speeding up from another angle.
This burst of speed was far beyond any Three-Blood martial artist. He didn't even leave an afterimage.
Whoosh.
You Zhuang’s right hand stopped, hovering right in front of Wei He’s left shoulder.
"Not bad. Your blood energy is much stronger than others, and your reactions are fast," he nodded in evaluation.
"However, next time, remember not to force it. If Xiao Heng truly set his mind on killing you, you would be dead within ten moves."
"Thank you for the guidance, Senior." Wei He nodded, though cold sweat soaked his back.
If You Zhuang had actually landed that hit, Wei He would be dead or severely crippled.
This You Zhuang was terrifyingly strong. He was at least an Intent-level master, miles ahead of Xiao Heng. His true strength was a bottomless pit.
Even if Wei He stacked the blood energy burst from his Five Mountains Palm, it would be useless. The gap was just too wide.
Without reaching the Intent level, a fighter could not cover their entire body in inner force. A single scrape would hurt; a single solid hit would ruin you.
"It isn't real guidance. You are strong for a Three-Blood fighter, but you train in too many styles. That makes breaking through extremely hard. You should know that blood seals cannot be easily undone," You Zhuang said, shaking his head slightly.
"Still, your real combat skills are good. Interested in joining the You family? Our First Young Master gathers talented people. If you join, your monthly resources will be at least double what you get now at the Sky Seal Sect."
"Thank you, but I still have trivial matters to deal with, so..." Wei He knew the rules of these wealthy clans. Once you signed on, you were bound for years. The benefits were great, but only for people who had already hit their limit.
He didn't plan to stay a Three-Blood fighter for the rest of his life.
"No matter. If you ever change your mind, come to the You family estate and have the guard tell me. Young Master You Rong loves meeting capable people. Brother Wei stays calm in danger and fights well. You are no ordinary man," You Zhuang said seriously.
"Thank you." Wei He nodded.
He noticed Wang Chong standing nearby. Hearing the conversation, Wang Chong had a faint look of envy on his face.
It seemed either the terms were amazing, or the You family genuinely treated their people well.
The You family group didn't stay long and quickly pulled back.
Only then did the town's Head Constable, Wang Yuanling, finally arrive.
Wei He briefly explained what happened, then left with Wang Chong to treat their injuries.
This Wang Yuanling was an Intent-level master, but he was overly cautious and never took risks. Whenever something like this happened, he made a lot of noise but did very little.
After shouting a few orders, the Head Constable rushed away, seemingly terrified of being dragged into the mess.
He was great at squeezing money out of people, but when it came to desperate killers, he dumped the work on his subordinates. He wouldn't even touch a Three-Blood criminal, let alone a dangerous master like the Human-Faced Tiger.
The old constable had held his rank for years precisely because he understood how to survive. He was past the age of hot-blooded bravery. Making money and enjoying life were all that mattered to him now.
Wei He and the other two headed toward Baohe Hall, the official medical station. To get there from the crime scene, they had to cross three different streets: Youshan Road, Qushan Road, and Fengtu Road.
Qushan Road sat right next to a docking area for black-awning boats on the Xuan Water River.
A long line of dark boats tied together covered half the river's surface, stretching far into the distance.
Inside one of the black-awning boats.
"That little constable killed my man, and he actually managed to block a dozen of my moves. He has decent strength..."
A man in gray sat cross-legged inside the cabin, his voice low and suppressed.
Opposite him sat a hunched figure.
Hearing this, the figure shook its head slightly.
"So what do you plan to do?"
"I didn't manage to kill him just now, so I'll wipe him out on my way back." The gray-robed man looked up, revealing a cold, sinister face. On his right cheekbone was a light red, coin-sized circular birthmark.
It was the Human-Faced Tiger, Xiao Heng, who had just escaped the You family.
"That constable uses Sky Seal Sect martial arts. He's only at Three-Blood with average finger strength, but he must have trained in leg techniques too. His speed and reactions are fast. Now that I know his tricks, he will die within ten moves next time."
"He is just a small bug. You Zhuang took action earlier, so be careful not to run into him again," the hunched figure warned in a deep voice.
"Don't worry. He killed my man. I can't kill You Zhuang, but you think I can't even handle a small constable?" Xiao Heng sneered. He was a ruthless man who held deep grudges. He never spared low-level constables, let alone someone like Wei He who got in his way.
"Alright, they are coming. Kill him quickly and leave. Don't linger."
"I know."
"...Remember, do not cause any extra trouble."
The voices faded into silence. Soon after, a dark shadow slipped silently out of the boat, disappearing into a distant vessel moving down the river.
On the riverbank.
Yang Xue supported Wang Chong as they walked slowly alongside Wei He along the pedestrian path.
As night fell, the three walked steadily with lanterns in hand.
Down at the dock, some boatmen were busy tying up their boats so the river current wouldn't sweep them away.
Yellow lantern light dotted the river surface, making the night look quiet and peaceful.
Some shops along the street were opening up for the night market.
Xuanjing City had no night curfew, so the dark hours were actually when the city was most alive.
Street vendors selling barbecue started setting up stalls along the bank, bringing out simple grills.
The rich scent of grilled fish and shrimp brushed with sauces and spices floated through the air, making the three men salivate.
"Want to grab a bite?" Wang Chong looked at Wei He and Yang Xue.
"Aren't you going to treat your wounds first?" Wei He asked in surprise.
"It's nothing, I know my own body!" Wang Chong waved his hand carelessly.
"I don't like fish," Wei He shook his head.
"Grilled fish is fine, no bones! Trust me!" Wang Chong urged, pulling both of them toward the stall near the black-awning boats.
That exact spot was where Xiao Heng was hiding.
Neither Wei He nor Yang Xue wanted fish, but Wang Chong insisted and dragged them along anyway.
"Wait!" Wei He suddenly stopped in his tracks. "Something is wrong!"
He frowned, sniffing the air carefully.
"What's up?" Wang Chong and Yang Xue looked at him, confused.
"Do you... smell something?"
"Smell what?" Wang Chong looked around, puzzled. "The food?"
"I don't smell anything out of the ordinary. Brother Wei, did you hit your nose earlier?" Yang Xue checked his surroundings, catching no strange scents either.
"No... that's not it."
Wei He frowned. "Don't you think this fish smells way too strong? Spices are expensive. If they use this much, won't they lose money?"
"Oh, that? Don't worry, these vendors all have secret recipes. If it doesn't smell good, who would buy it? People could just grill fish at home," Wang Chong chuckled.
"Makes sense," Wei He said.
The three walked up to the stall.
"Boss, three grilled fish, a pound of grilled shrimp, and three side dips!" Wang Chong ordered skillfully.
The owner was a balding, middle-aged man wearing an apron. With a humble smile, he called out a loud response and briskly pulled three fish out of a bucket, getting ready to gut them.
"Hold on," Wei He blurted out.
"What now?" Wang Chong and Yang Xue looked at him again.
"Mine doesn't need scales removed!" Wei He said grimly.
"Oh, I thought it was something serious. Stop jumpy, will you?" Wang Chong grumbled.
"Brother Wei, can you list all your requests at once?" Yang Xue was getting annoyed too.
"Fine," Wei He nodded. "Boss, make mine extra spicy!"
"Coming right up!" It was the owner's first order of the night, so he worked with full energy.
Soon, a sharp, extra-spicy aroma filled the air.
"Man, this smells great..." Wang Chong took a deep breath, feeling satisfied.
"Smells good? It should," Wei He said darkly.
"!??" Wang Chong froze, not catching his drift.
Before he could react, Yang Xue collapsed to the ground with a soft thud.
"Attack!"
Suddenly, a gray figure burst out from a nearby black-awning boat.
The shadow lunged straight at Wei He. His foot shattered the stone paving on the bank like a massive python leaping into the air.
Yet the moment he landed, he realized Wang Chong, who was supposed to back him up, was now red-faced, clinging onto the wooden stall table, unable to move.
The other young constable lay unconscious on the ground. Even the stall owner had slumped sideways, completely passed out.
Wei He stood with his hands behind his back, watching him quietly, as if he had been waiting all along.
"What did you do!?" The gray figure revealed his face. It was Xiao Heng, the Human-Faced Tiger who had just fled.
"Nothing much. I just used over twenty kinds of knockout powder to pass out Brother Wang. Looks like my drugs are getting less effective..." Wei He sighed softly.
Using so much drug powder exhausted him mentally. After all, poisoning them without Wang Chong noticing was no easy task.
"You knew?" The Human-Faced Tiger was shocked.
"No," Wei He said. "I just decided to try it out and see what happened."
"..." Xiao Heng stared at Wei He's calm expression, feeling as though his intelligence was being dragged through the dirt and stomped on.
"Fine, I actually guessed something was off," Wei He admitted, seeing the look on his face. "He was badly injured, yet he couldn't wait to eat barbecue. How greedy would someone have to be to not wait a little longer? At first, I only suspected it, so I used a few slow-acting poisons. If nothing went wrong, I could just secretly give them the antidote later.
But as things felt stranger, I raised the dosage and mixed in more types."
"Weren't you afraid of poisoning the wrong people?!" Xiao Heng was shocked and furious.
"Not really. I only used knockout powders. At worst, I'd just apologize later," Wei He said indifferently.
"You bastard..." Xiao Heng had never met someone so ruthless and underhanded. Killing intent flared violently in his chest. If he didn't kill an enemy like this immediately, the future trouble would be endless!
Without another word, he charged forward in a burst of speed.
However, after taking just one step, his head spun with dizziness, forcing him to halt.
This knockout drug was so potent that even he almost succumbed to it.
