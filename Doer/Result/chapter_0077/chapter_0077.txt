Inside the Huishan Martial Arts School.
Jiang Su said nothing. He dragged himself up from the dirt, teeth clenched, ready to charge again. But Zhao Hong grabbed him from behind.
"I'll go," Zhao Hong said gravely.
He had watched Chen Jian's moves. To be honest, he was not sure he could win either. The guy was a freak of nature, with stupidly high stamina. He threw every single punch with full strength, yet never seemed to tire.
But Wei He was away. Their master's face was getting darker by the second.
And peers from other schools were watching.
If Tiesha Boxing pushed them into a corner today, how could Huishan Boxing keep its reputation outside?
Zhao Hong stood up calmly.
He had Zhang Lu help Jiang Su to the back, then walked forward step by step.
The watching disciples were already frowning and whispering. Huishan Boxing looked way too weak today. This level did not look like a place that could train Three-Blood warriors.
Without a word, Zhao Hong took his stance.
His breathing was heavy and stable, completely different from Jiang Su and Zhang Lu. Anyone could tell he was a step above them.
"Now this one looks decent," Chen Jian said, a hint of fight rising in his eyes.
A few moments later, fists collided in a rapid blur.
Zhao Hong stumbled back, his face pale. Beneath his clothes, his chest was red and swollen. A single straight punch from Chen Jian had caught him square in the middle.
He could not hold out for long either. This brute from Tiesha Boxing seemed to come out of nowhere, but his raw power was terrifying.
A normal fighter would tire after throwing a dozen full-power hits. Yet Chen Jian showed no sign of slowing down. Punch after punch came like an endless wave. His stamina was unnatural.
Add his rich combat experience and vicious style to the mix...
Zhao Hong’s face flushed red. He managed to hold on for a few dozen exchanges, but in the end, he broke and fell.
Yet if Zhao Hong was frustrated, Chen Jian was even angrier.
Chen Jian spat hard on the ground, his face full of disappointment.
"Fuck! What a waste of my time!"
"What the fuck did you just say!?" A Huishan disciple could not hold back anymore and stepped out, his face burning with rage.
"It's none of your business!" Chen Jian barked back.
"Were you just cursing at us!? Apologize!!" the disciple roared.
"Heh, did I name you? Did I say I was talking about you? I'm not apologizing, so what?" Chen Jian laughed out of pure anger. "If you got the guts, step up! One on one! Winner gets to be the loser's dad! You dare?"
"Let me at him!!" The disciple tried to rush forward, but his companions grabbed him tight and held him back.
"Heh, trash is trash. Of course, I don't mean Master Zheng. I wouldn't dare insult an Inked Blood master. I just feel bad for him. A grand master, yet all his students are useless junk," Chen Jian mocked on purpose.
"Shut your damn mouth!!"
"I won't! Come on, kill me! I came here today just to let you guys kill me!" Chen Jian slapped his chest and sneered.
Sitting in the main chair, Zheng Fugui’s face turned a dark red. He gripped the armrest so hard his fingers sank deep into the wood.
He had long heard that Tiesha Boxing recruited a gifted brute, a idiot genius who pissed off many martial schools while leaving them helpless.
He just never thought he would see it with his own eyes today.
This Chen Jian had a strong background and insane talent. He lived for fighting. If not for his trash mouth, he would have built a vast network by now.
Looking at him now, it was no wonder so many schools hated his guts.
The courtyard was in total chaos.
Zhou Jihan and his two companions were speechless. Chen Jian was strong. He had fought Zhou Jihan before and lost, never coming back for a second try.
Back then, Zhou Jihan thought he was just another random challenger.
Who knew this guy was actually an absolute lunatic?
"Chen Jian is strong, but is he really unstoppable? Does Huishan Boxing have no better inner disciples?" Hu Zheng'an asked, confused.
"Among all the martial schools, Huishan Boxing ranks average, maybe even on the lower end. What do you think?" He Hong replied coldly.
"A total farce. A complete mess," Zhou Jihan said. He felt there was no point in watching anymore.
The whole place was a circus now, turned into a screaming match between Huishan disciples and Chen Jian.
Two Tiesha disciples tried to pull Chen Jian back a few times, but he slapped both of them away.
It seemed this guy was an absolute tyrant back at his own school too.
"If Senior Brother Wei was here, you wouldn't be acting so brave!!" Ouyang Zhuang from the outer yard roared in anger.
"Right! If Senior Brother Wei was here, one slap would break you!"
"You're only tough because Senior Brother Wei isn't around!"
"Are you guys for real!?" Chen Jian lost his temper too. "I came here specifically to fight that Wei guy! But he's nowhere to be found! You guys were the ones who wanted a warm-up match! Now that you lost, you start bragging about a guy who doesn't even dare to show his face? Fine! I'll stand right here! Two against one! I'm giving you a chance! If you beat me, I'll kneel and apologize! If you lose, I don't need you to kneel, just admit you were wrong! Every single one of you! I, Chen Jian, am reasonable like that!"
Bang!
Zheng Fugui could no longer hold it in. He snapped the wooden armrest with a single palm and stood up.
Even if people called him a bully, he was going to beat this brat today and teach him some manners!
"Brother Zheng, why get so angry? This is just kids playing around. Why should you and I step in?"
A figure suddenly appeared in the yard, standing not far from Zheng Fugui.
It was a long-bearded old man dressed similarly to Chen Jian.
His beard was braided and wrapped around his neck. His body was packed with thick muscle, especially his chest, which looked like metallic armor.
"He's right. Since it's a fight between us younger ones, we should solve it ourselves."
Another figure materialized right behind Chen Jian.
He was a head taller than Chen Jian, built like a wall, with thick muscles wrapping his body like tree roots. It was Wei He.
Wei He stepped onto the inner yard ground.
Whoosh...
A soft breeze burst out from behind him, spreading outward. A heavy, invisible aura of blood force bled from his body into the air.
"I am Wei He," he said calmly, looking around. "Sorry I'm a bit late."
The invisible pressure instantly crushed the noisy courtyard into dead silence.
The Huishan disciples who were shouting a moment ago swallowed hard, unable to make another sound.
The inner disciples from other schools felt it too. It was a completely different pressure from the others.
An Inked Blood master was too far away for them to gauge, but Three-Blood was right next to their level. They understood this strength better than anyone.
Because they knew how terrifying this amount of blood force was, everyone fell silent the moment Wei He stepped inside.
Wei He’s cold gaze slowly shifted downward, landing on Chen Jian.
"What were you saying just now?"
Chen Jian was in a completely different state now. Blood vessels popped in his eyes as he felt the full weight of the terrifying blood force pressing down on him.
He felt no fear. Not a single trace of fear showed on his face.
There was only wild obsession.
"This feeling... Yes... Hahaha! I'm so excited! So excited I'm about to..."
"EXPLODE!!! HAHAHAHAHA!!"
With a roar, his blood force surged to the limit.
He stomped his foot into the ground. A terrifying power exploded through his veins, muscles, and bones, rushing like a tidal wave into his right fist.
"Take this! Dark Dragon Punch!!"
All his strength, all his energy, all his mad obsession pooled into this single blow.
Chen Jian felt his blood force rising to a new level, breaking through right in the middle of this punch.
Under this massive, malicious pressure, his body seemed to be transforming.
The strange, colorful patterns from the core chart of Tiesha Boxing suddenly made sense in his mind.
As his fist swung out, an epiphany struck him.
This was the punch...
He... he had finally caught it! The final spark of breakthrough!!
Hahahaha!!
He was finally about to...
Pfft!
Suddenly, a sharp pain shot through Chen Jian's chest.
His crazy focus was pulled back instantly, forced to look right in front of him.
"This... what is this!?"
His eyes went wide as he stared in shock.
His fist was only halfway through its swing, still a distance away from his target.
Yet pain was already bursting from his chest.
Wei He's right index finger was pressing lightly against his sternum.
"You...!!?"
BOOM!!!
An explosive wave of power detonated from Chen Jian's chest, throwing him into the air.
With a loud crash, Chen Jian slammed hard into the stone wall behind him, spiderweb cracks spreading across the brick.
He felt as if a wild rhino had hit him head-on. The blood force that was rapidly ascending in his body was instantly shattered by the blow, losing all momentum.
The breakthrough he had just grabbed... was shattered like glass!!?
"My bad. I didn't think you were this weak. I used too much force," Wei He said.
He pulled back his hand and walked straight toward Master Zheng.
Zheng Fugui had just gone through an emotional rollercoaster. The rage in his heart completely vanished the moment Chen Jian was crushed.
He took a step forward, a look of pure relief spreading over his face. He was about to speak.
Suddenly, his chest felt tight, and his head spun. He nearly collapsed back into his seat.
Luckily, Zhao Hong noticed in time and quickly supported him.
On the other side, Chen Jian had finally reached the edge of a breakthrough, only for Wei He to shatter his blood force with a single finger, throwing him back to square one.
The pain, rage, and grief in Chen Jian's heart were beyond words. With his blood force churning wildly inside him, although he wasn't fatally injured, his breath was trapped, leaving him unable to utter a single word.
