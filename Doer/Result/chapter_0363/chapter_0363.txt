The ship drifted on the water.
Wei He looked into the distance and saw a small black island rising above the sea.
The black-striped whale wasn't going anywhere else. It swam straight toward that island.
"Something is wrong," Wei He said in a deep voice. "Does this whale really like to play near islands? Shallow water surrounds it. A beast this size can barely move here."
Lou Chengxun nodded along.
"Something is definitely wrong. This must be the work of Daoist Sanyun. I wondered why we couldn't find the whale for so many days. He must have spotted us early and kept the beast away on purpose."
He seemed to figure out the truth.
As a Real Person, his eyesight was sharp. He could already make out the outline of the island far off.
He saw human figures standing there.
One of them had a black cross marked between his eyebrows. It was Daoist Sanyun himself.
"Junior Brother Wei, what do we do now?" He turned to Wei He.
Wei He was their main force today.
This was a master who had beaten back two Real Persons back when he was only at the Viscera Refining stage. Now that he had achieved his first sensing, his power was far greater than before.
Beating Lou Chengxun would be easy for him.
The Lou family estimated that this rare genius had already reached the Transformation Realm. An average expert at the second sensing stage would stand no chance against him.
On top of that, Senior Sister Yuanduzi valued Wei He greatly. He definitely held hidden trump cards. In a real fight, he would be much stronger than anyone else.
That was why the Lou family felt safe sending Wei He out with their two Real Persons. Otherwise, after their own masters failed last time, going back out would just be asking for death.
Wei He narrowed his eyes and thought for a moment.
"No rush. We don't know their true strength. If we rush in, we might fall into a trap."
He paused and added, "We wait."
The enemy was using the whale to lure them here. That meant they must have set a trap ahead of time.
"If they really are luring us, then the moment we turn around and leave, they won't be able to sit still," Wei He said calmly. "If I were the one setting the trap, seeing my prey turn away right before stepping into my mouth, I wouldn't be able to hold back."
"True. But this sea is wide open. Even if they set a trap, so what? As long as we turn the ship around, we can easily get away. What good is their trap?" Lou Chengxun asked, puzzled. "With our eyesight, we can spot their setup from far away. A trap here is meaningless."
"How do you know it's meaningless?" Wei He asked back.
"What do you mean?" Lou Chengxun was surprised. "From this distance, as long as we see it early and turn away, isn't their trap useless?"
"Senior Brother, do you know that if we can see them, they can see us too?" Wei He said flatly.
Lou Chengxun still looked confused.
Wei He said calmly, "Don't you know? For true masters, once they see you, they can catch you."
"And a distance like this takes only a second to cross."
Right as he spoke the words, the water far off blew apart.
Three figures stepped onto the water and charged straight toward the ship, riding the waves.
They turned into three black lines. Their speed was much faster than the ship.
Water splashed beneath their feet as they kicked off the surface over and over.
"Here they come," Wei He sighed.
With so many witnesses around, he couldn't hide his full strength anymore. But it didn't matter. Sooner or later, some of it had to come out. It was only a matter of how much.
"Senior Brother, guard my back," he ordered. He leaped off the ship's edge and dropped into the sea.
Splash.
His feet hit the water, but he stood on it like solid ground. He charged straight at the three enemies.
If they were going to fight, he would test their strength first. He would focus on full defense first, then decide how much offensive power to show.
That was what Wei He needed to think about right now.
In the blink of an eye, the three black dots grew larger. The leader was the bald, burly man with the black cross mark on his forehead.
The two men behind him looked different from each other, but both were bald, muscular monks. Even with bare hands, their heavy muscles gave off a wild, dangerous threat.
"Wei He of the Xuanmiao Sect?"
As they closed in, the black-cross man spoke up. His voice was deep, but his Great Yuan tongue sounded clumsy and strange.
In a flash, a thought struck Wei He's mind like lightning.
"Men of the Wu Nation?!"
His face changed completely. He turned around and ran.
The Wu Nation had been plotting here for a long time. They went out of their way to use the whale to lure him in. This was a trap built just for him.
If it were any other force, he wouldn't care. No matter how strong a small island force was, it had limits. But the Wu Nation was different!
If he didn't run now, he would be a fool.
Wei He didn't think twice. He turned and fled.
Yet on this endless sea, where could he run besides back to the ship?
The black-cross man was a bit surprised, but he calmly raised his hand. "No need to panic. He has nowhere else to go. He can only rely on that ship. As long as we take the ship, everything is ours."
Wei He had turned and run before they even clashed.
The Lou family members on the ship were struck dumb.
When Wei He got close and sent his voice over, Lou Chengxun realized the Wu Nation was behind this setup. His face turned pale with fear. He screamed at the sailors to turn the ship around and escape.
The ship turned in a hurry.
Wei He burst out of the water with a heavy splash and landed on the deck.
His expression was dark.
That Daoist Sanyun was just bait. This wasn't the first time the Wu Nation had set an ambush.
Last time, they controlled Zhou Muqing beside him to lure him out, and he ran into a Wu Nation master at the Quanzhen level.
Luckily, the enemy misjudged his true strength. He managed to kill that man instead.
That time, he was lured out alone and killed a Quanzhen master. So he couldn't be sure how the Wu Nation rated his current power.
If they rated him as a true Quanzhen master, things would get ugly. He couldn't win against every master at that level.
Yao Wan had told him that in real combat, his power was close to a Quanzhen master of the fifth step.
After the fifth step, Quanzhen masters went through a big jump in power. Both the quality of their force and their innate gifts improved greatly.
In the Wu Nation, this level matched a high-rank Bodhisattva. In Great Yuan, a person like that could hold a major position in any sect.
Wei He had checked his own limits. Going all out, he could barely draw with a fifth-step Quanzhen master.
And among the Wu Nation forces that entered Haizhou, there were at least seven people at this level or higher.
"What do we do now?" Lou Chengxun looked at the three figures closing in fast, his heart cold with fear.
At his level, he couldn't tell how strong the enemies were. But looking at their physical traits, traits that only Bodhisattvas above the Arhat level possessed, he knew he couldn't fight them.
The ship finally finished its turn. Every sailor used all their muscle, pulling the oars with everything they had.
The large ship sped up.
Wei He and Lou Chengxun watched as the three enemies fell slightly behind, failing to catch up by just a bit.
Seeing that the enemy couldn't close the gap, they breathed a small sigh of relief.
A martial arts master was slower on water than on land. Luckily, they noticed the trap early. Or rather, luckily Wei He realized something was wrong and ran away instantly.
If they had been caught and held down, no one on this ship would have survived.
Watching the three enemies fall further back, Lou Chengxun and Lou Shengyue relaxed a bit.
"Our luck is good. The wind is with us, so the ship is at top speed. Otherwise, we couldn't have outrun them," Lou Chengxun said, feeling a weight lift off his heart.
Wei He stayed quiet, but he also let out a breath.
Safe for now. Luckily, his reaction was fast enough to escape the tiger's mouth.
Otherwise, this trip would have been deadly.
The group stood at the edge of the ship, looking back.
After chasing for a while, the three enemies saw they couldn't keep up. They slowed down and stopped.
The black-cross man looked up at Wei He from far away. He suddenly raised his arm.
Roar!!
A white shadow split the air. It was a giant bird with a wingspan of six meters. It dived down, grabbing his arm with its pale yellow claws and pulling up.
Its huge force pulled him straight off the water.
With a long cry, the giant bird carried the black-cross man and rushed toward Wei He's ship.
"You want to leave? Not that easy!!"
Black veins burst out across the man's skin. Dark spots like scales covered his body.
While still in midair, his body swelled up a full size larger.
The white bird held him and dived forward at extreme speed. It quickly caught up to the ship and threw him down onto the deck.
The black-cross man rolled in midair and pounced straight down at Wei He.
No one on the ship expected a sudden turn like this. Lou Chengxun's first move was to pull Lou Shengyue back. He moved his niece to a safe spot before turning to look at Wei He.
Wei He was also stunned, but he didn't run. He stood his ground on the deck, looking up at the approaching enemy.
The man was coming for him.
Wei He could see it in the man's eyes the moment their gazes met.
The distance between them shrank in a split second.
Suddenly, Wei He's entire body expanded. Streams of invisible force turned into gray-black smoke, swirling around him.
He raised his right arm, let out a wild roar, and threw a heavy punch upward.
At the same time, the black-cross man's right arm grew layers of dark scales. His fingers bent like hooks, the sharp tips glowing with a faint purple light as he clawed downward.
"Die!!"
BOOM!!!
The two clashed in an instant.
Between the fist and the claw, layers of force burst apart under crushing physical power.
Over a hundred thousand pounds of force poured down from the man's arm like a falling mountain.
This power was even greater than Wei He's when he boosted himself with the Whale Surge Art.
This was the true backing of a Blood-Corrupted warrior, someone who dug out their natural gifts step by step to reach this point.
Though Wei He had reached the third layer of the Whale Surge Art, he was not a Blood-Corrupted warrior himself. Even now, his physical strength was less than thirty thousand pounds.
In truth, he only had an ordinary bloodline. To reach a level close to a Blood-Corrupted warrior with an ordinary body was already shocking to the world.
Only now did he understand why the creator of the Whale Surge Art viewed it as nothing more than a life-extending technique.
The massive power it added seemed huge to Wei He, but to the generals and high masters of the Wu Nation, it was just child's play.
The heavy power on the man's arm pressed down like a mountain, making Wei He's bones creak and snap.
The huge gap in power turned his face red. His muscles shook nonstop.
The deck beneath his feet cracked and broke into a large hole.
Bang!!
Before falling through the deck, Wei He twisted his body and smashed through the ship's rail. To bleed off the crushing force, he was thrown off the deck by the hit and crashed into the sea with a loud splash.
