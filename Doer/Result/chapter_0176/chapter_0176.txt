Wei He closed his eyes and calmed his breath. He felt a wave of heat hit his skull like a heavy hammer.
Bang!!
His head spun for a moment and his vision blurred. But this blur quickly began to fade.
In its place was a clear and open feeling connecting his brain and his whole body. A trace of inner force flowed smoothly through his bones without stopping, forming a complete cycle.
"Done...!" Wei He felt neither sad nor happy in his heart. He gently threw out a palm.
A dark current rushed out silently from his palm, hitting some water weeds on the front left. The water weeds broke one by one and were torn apart. The invisible bone force also left a deep dent on the stone wall underwater.
The water suddenly became turbid.
Wei He let go of his hand and swam upward.
Once you truly reach the Bone Forging realm, you are not much stronger than ordinary martial artists. The main use of this realm is to prolong life, extend the peak state of a martial artist, and enhance blood production. It also builds a foundation for the future Organ Refining realm.
Compared to ordinary martial artists, Bone Forging just adds another layer of bone force explosion. Other protective forces and secret skills still need to be practiced and improved by yourself.
With a splash, Wei He came out of the water and onto the shore. His body force trembled, shattering all the water droplets on his clothes and hair into water vapor that drifted away. Suddenly his whole body became much drier.
He looked up at the sky. Then he sensed the-breakthrough pearl. The full scale accumulated by the breakthrough pearl was now completely used up. Accumulating it again would take three months.
"It is about time. I should go to the Tianya Tower." Wei He had investigated beforehand. Shangguan Ji only took a few secret manuals from the Tianya Tower, but most of them were still there. With Granny Zeng, the tower guard, around, ordinary people had no ability to go in as petty thieves.
But now that Shangguan Ji had left, no one knew how long Granny Zeng could still protect it. So there was no time to lose.
After reaching Bone Forging with the Rain-Covering and Cloud-Gathering Art, to reach Organ Refining, he needed to master Silk Force. This was a realm where inner force was controlled down to the most minute detail, handled thread by thread.
It was not something that could be done in a short time. Even a genius like Wang Shaojun had been stuck for three years. There were countless others who could not break through, and more than half of the Nine Sons of Tianyin failed to break through, such as Wan Ling.
When you reached this stage, it was time to start competing on talent.
"At this step, in the whole Xuanjing City, my realm can be considered a somebody," Wei He sighed in his heart.
Thinking back to the past, he rose from ordinary, learning fists step by step, all the way up to now.
"I just do not know how I compare to true Organ Refining masters." Wei He had a sense of expectation in his heart.
In addition, the complementary skill Iron Ridge Clothing made up for the weakness in defense of his Rain-Covering Force. Next, he planned to go to the Tianya Tower to look again and see what complementary secret manuals he could find, strengthening whichever aspect he found.
Complementary techniques could not be forced and he could not afford to be picky.
"Although the techniques in the Tianya Tower are only at the entry level, they are more important to me than anything else. I must get all of them."
Wei He packed his things, did not stop at all, and went straight to Wan Ling's bamboo house in the Wanqing Sect camp.
On the edge of Heiwu Mountain, among the many bamboo houses of the Wanqing Sect, the largest two-story bamboo house was the home of Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing. This place not only served as a residence, but also held the Wanqing Sect's two meat fields, as well as a large amount of stored grain and medicinal herbs. Therefore, the space had to be built larger.
When Wei He arrived, Wan Ling was softly guiding a female disciple in martial arts moves. Seeing him coming over, Wan Ling nodded slightly, signaling him to wait a moment.
Wei He stood not far away and waited for a while. Until the lecture ended and the female disciple left, he walked forward.
"That person just now... seems to be a newcomer?" Wei He asked with some surprise.
"Well, outside this Heiwu Mountain, there is a small mountain village nearby, as you know. Most of the people inside are children of herb pickers who are good at climbing mountains and ridges. I took a few from them as new blood for the sect," Wan Ling smiled and said.
A year of getting along was enough for her to slowly get used to this strange way of getting along with Wei He.
"You have worked hard, Master," Wei He nodded. "I came this time to retrieve the martial arts secrets in the Tianya Tower. Master must know about the Tianya Tower and the Thousand Bats Water Pavilion, right?"
"......I know. But isn't it now... a bit early?" Wan Ling hesitated.
She always liked to hesitate and delay, and even now, she still could not change this bad habit. In essence, although her realm was high, she was a person who was not good at fighting.
"Not early. I came this time with a bold request, and I hope Master will agree," Wei He said in a deep voice.
"Tell me. If it is reasonable, I will grant it." Wan Ling nodded seriously, seeing Wei He's solemn attitude.
"I want to take the Tianya Tower, but with my status in the sect, it is hard to convince the public. Du Han, Xiao Qingyu, and Xie Yan might all show up this time," Wei He said calmly.
"You..." Wan Ling stared closely at Wei He and suddenly understood what he meant. Then she paused and suddenly showed a relieved smile.
"You... finally said these words..."
This time it was Wei He's turn to be stunned.
"What does Master mean by this?"
"I have been waiting for you to say this." Wan Ling smiled, "Actually, as early as when you saved the entire Wanqing Sect, I had the intention to hand the position over to you. But you probably felt your realm was not enough back then, so you could not convince the public, right?"
"......Master's insight is sharp." Wei He nodded.
Although his poison was strong in actual combat, the martial arts realm was the foundation. To take the position of the leader of the Wanqing Sect, he at least needed to be in the Bone Forging realm. Otherwise, as just an entry-level martial artist, how could he teach martial artist disciples?
"It seems that you have finally broken through, which is why you came to find me?" Wan Ling guessed.
"......" Wei He was speechless.
"In this way, I can finally rest. In the past year, although you were not the leader, you have been doing the job of the leader. You have worked hard, Xiao He," Wan Ling said softly.
"Master exaggerates," Wei He was silent for a moment before answering.
Wan Ling smiled slightly and took a cyan-green metal waist token from her waist. A large cyan character was engraved on the front of the token, and on the back was an ice-blue spring bird spreading its wings to fly.
"This is the leader token forged from cold iron. I will give it to you today, and I will announce this news to everyone later. Everyone will be happy. After all, your breakthrough in realm is a great thing!" Wan Ling smiled.
Wei He took the token.
"There is no rush for now. I will go to Tianya Tower first. It is not too late to celebrate after I bring back the books."
"I will let Qingqing go with you," Wan Ling said.
"Okay."
No matter what, Wan Qingqing was also a Bone Forging martial artist, which was considered high-end military power in the entire Xuanjing City and held a certain weight. Wei He did not refuse.
"By the way, Xiao He," Wan Ling said suddenly again, "Have you ever thought about the future?"
"The future?" Wei He was stunned.
"Yes... the future." Wan Ling smiled, "We will talk about it after you come back."
She knew Wei He's aptitude and also knew that he had unlearned his martial arts to reach this level. Both Qingqing and Wei He had actually reached the limit. Going further might take more than ten years of luck to enter the Organ Refining realm, but that would no longer have much meaning.
With ten years of time, it was impossible to put everything into martial arts alone. Life was limited and martial arts were boundless. A person always had to think more for themselves.
So at this time, it was also time to start a family and arrange for the future. If she had looked down on Wei He a bit because of his background in the past, now the more she looked at him, the more she felt he and Qingqing were a match.
Wei He seemed to understand the meaning. He nodded gently.
"There is no rush for this. Let's talk about it when I come back."
"Okay."

Thousand Bats Water Pavilion.
By the surrounding Tianyin Lake, the sky was getting late, and many people were already standing there.
The Shangguan family quietly left, leaving behind a mess to attract and divert attention. I have to say this method was very effective. The Thousand Bats Water Pavilion was fine as it was just a historical building, but the Tianya Tower was different.
Inside the Tianya Tower, various books collected by the Tianyin Gate for hundreds of years were stored. The number of martial arts classics among them was counted even in the entire Taizhou.
Among the five major forces, the Tianya Tower had the first collection of books, and that was not just for show.
At this time by Tianyin Lake, waves of people who knew martial arts ran over to watch the excitement.
The martial arts sect that once dominated Xuanjing had completely collapsed, and even its landmark buildings had become meat for various forces to fight over.
The feeling of desolation made people sigh.
At this time, beside the Thousand Bats Water Pavilion, in front of the Tianya Tower.
An old woman in black with white hair coiled up was sitting at the door of the tower leaning on a cane with a gloomy face, saying nothing and moving not at all. She was Granny Zeng, the tower guard and the last remaining master of the Shangguan lineage of the Tianyin Gate.
When Shangguan Ji left, he asked her to go together, but unfortunately she refused.
Like her was a skinny old man holding a smoking pipe next to her. The old man squinted his eyes and swept his gaze over the groups of people standing separately in front of the tower.
The first group of people was led by Yan Haochun, consisting of people from the Lishan Sect and others, numbering about ten people. The number was small, but each was an elite, including at least five martial artists, led by Yan Haochun, a Bone Forging martial artist.
The second group was the You family, one of the three major families in Xuanjing, led by the second young master You Ce, with more than twenty people. Among them was an old man with white hair and unusual momentum, who was obviously also in the Bone Forging realm.
The third group was a group of masters from the Zhou family, led by Zhou Rong, the fourth elder of the Zhou family. This Organ Refining peak martial artist over fifty years old had the highest realm present.
At this time, Zhou Rong's face was cold, and his eyes stared jealously at Yan Haochun's side. Obviously, the appearance of the Lishan Sect also gave him a sense of crisis.
Apart from these three waves of people, the many crowds surrounding them also mixed with many greedy and malicious gazes. Without thinking, one could know that countless greedy people were hidden here, just waiting for a chance to swarm forward and grab the secret manuals.
"Zeng Yuhong, do you really think you can guard the Tianya Tower all by yourself? Aren't you overestimating yourself?" Zhou Rong, as a prominent master among the three major families in Xuanjing City, had a status comparable to Zhou Shun of the Tianyin Gate in the past.
He had indeed fought with Zhou Shun before, and it was a draw. He belonged to the top masters in Xuanjing second only to Yuchi Zhong and Shangguan Ji. With his strength, status, and age, there were no more than four people in the entire Xuanjing City who could be mentioned on par with him, so he had the qualification to speak in this tone.
Zeng Yuhong was Granny Zeng's real name.
Hearing this, she sneered.
"This old woman is old and doesn't have many days left to live anyway. Zhou Rong, if you want things, come up and get them yourself. I never planned to guard the Tianya Tower all by myself. However..."
"However, the Tianya Tower only allows people from the Tianyin Gate to enter. This is the rule!" The skinny old man beside him took over the conversation and smiled.
"Whoever breaks the rules, old Zeng and I will fight one on one, and we can still break even." The skinny old man lifted his smoking pipe and gently tapped it against the door frame.
Crack.
The metal-clad door frame made a slight sound, dented, and showed cracks. This person was obviously also a master. Judging by his tone, he was probably not inferior to Granny Zeng, and was likewise in the Bone Forging realm.
Two Bone Forging martial artists, although they were seventy or eighty years old and their blood and qi had declined a lot, if they fought desperately, no one could be sure what secret skills they had mastered.
"Why must you do this?" Yan Haochun sighed, "Two elders, even if you insist, only people from the Tianyin Gate can enter. But right now, at this very moment, do they dare to show their faces? Even if you let them in, do they dare to enter?"
"The Tianyin Gate has long become a thing of the past. Your persistence, two elders, is admirable, but this persistence is meaningless except for wasting lives."
Yan Haochun continued: "Du Han, brother Du of the Chidi Gate, has made it clear that he will not show up. Vice Sect Master Xie Yan's whereabouts are unknown. Do you think Xiao Qingyu of the Zhenglin Sect and Wan Ling of the Wanqing Courtyard dare to show up? Why suffer like this?"
Zeng Yuhong and the skinny old man remained silent.
