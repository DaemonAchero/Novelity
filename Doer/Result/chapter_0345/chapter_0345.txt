Many years ago, Wei He heard about True Persons, Sense Inscription, and Reality.
Back then, he thought everything was just like the immortal cultivators and magic tricks he heard about in his previous life. He thought the real world was a strange, magical place filled with daoist magic and demons.
Now, he knew he was wrong.
Click.
The door opened slowly.
Wei He looked straight ahead. Blood vessels spread around his pupils like moving smoke, swirling without form.
Light poured in from outside, burning his eyes a bit. He blinked and let his vision adjust.
A long time ago, he touched many things with his left hand. In his hyper-sense state, those things felt completely different from what he imagined. Because of that, he already had a few guesses.
He stepped out of his quiet room.
Inside, his strong Hai Zhen Qi was constantly spreading out, pushing away anything that did not belong to it. That was why he could not see anything strange while inside.
Only after coming out...
Wei He blinked and pulled the door all the way open. A light breeze drifted in across his cheek, bringing a cool, fresh chill.
The Wei family house had moved to Tianhai Island. Though the island was big, space was limited, so they were given a simple, square courtyard.
There were a dozen rooms inside. They housed Wei He’s family, Wan Qingqing and her son, and a few old servants.
Wei He’s quiet room sat at the very back of the square, right next to the study.
Outside his room was a curved hallway connecting all the rooms in the house. A simple shade structure covered the top like a grape trellis. Green climbing vines grew over it, making a refreshing walk.
Some vines bore small fruits, but most were strong, wild creepers. They covered the top of the hallway, breaking the bright sunlight into tiny, shining spots.
Wei He stood at the door. He expected to see the curved hallway dotted with light. He expected to smell flowers, green leaves, and warm sun. That kind of environment always put his mind at ease.
Instead, the scene before him shocked him completely.
Looking back, he finally understood why the True Persons of the Inner Mountain rarely left their holy lands. He understood why Chang Xuezhong advised him to plant as few herbs and trees as possible.
Now, he got it.
Wei He looked out from his door.
The heavy green vines over the bright hallway were now like thick green sludge. They wiggled, flaked, and dropped dark green mud onto the floor continuously.
Every vine was covered in a thick layer of moving, swaying mold. Like mushroom spores, they kept puffing tiny dark-green dust into the air.
Now, the top, floor, and walls of the hallway were covered in this thick dust and green sludge. Wei He could almost hear the quiet squelching sound of the sludge squirming around.
He took a breath. A light puff of dust drifted toward his face, trying to get into his nose and mouth. But before it touched him, a clear barrier blocked it.
Wei He realized what it was. He looked down at his hands and saw a thin, invisible layer covering his skin.
That was his protective Qi.
"To think it looks like this..." Wei He said nothing more.
He smiled bitterly and shifted his eyes past the hallway.
Beyond the path was the courtyard wall and a small garden surrounded by four rooms.
The garden was entirely grey and black. The small pond, the flowers, the rippling water, and the fresh green grass were all buried under a thick layer of dark, evil powder.
The powder was like volcanic ash, falling silently from the sky like endless snow.
Wei He looked up. He saw only a thick grey cloud. Light pushed through it, looking like the sun after a heavy rain.
"Wei He, you finished your training?" a familiar voice called out.
Wei He looked over.
A person was walking toward him down the hallway. Her entire body was covered in a layer of wiggling, weird fur. The fur acted like living creatures, fitting tightly around her like a suit. It was mostly red and black.
Wei He focused his eyes. Through the thick layer of living hair, he recognized his wife and former senior sister, Wan Qingqing.
Wan Qingqing walked up to him and waved her hand in front of his face. "What is wrong? Still out of it?"
"No... I am fine," Wei He said softly.
Looking closely at his wife, he saw a thin layer of natural force around her body, blocking most of the wiggling fur. But even so, some of the living hair still slipped inside her barrier, crawling across her smooth skin like tiny bugs or a skin disease.
"My mind is still on my training. I just need a moment," Wei He said in a deep voice.
He looked down at his own hands again. His skin was clean and white, with nothing on it. Clearly, his protective Qi had a purifying effect that hers lacked.
Giggle...
Suddenly, a little girl's laugh sounded near his ear. Even without using his full hyper-sense, he could hear it clearly.
Before he could hear what Wan Qingqing was saying, a pale white frost swept in fast from outside the courtyard.
The frost spread quickly, reaching Wei He and covering him in a second. His left hand immediately felt ice-cold.
"Ying Xiao Wind!"
Wei He realized what it was. This was one of the biggest dangers in the real world, the Ghost Wind!
The white frost hit Wan Qingqing too. The thick layer of living fur on her body froze hard and crumbled to the ground.
"What happened?" Wan Qingqing had no idea. She looked at Wei He in confusion, wondering if he needed to go back inside to train more.
"Nothing..." Wei He replied quietly.
He clearly saw that as the wind blew past Wan Qingqing, it pulled thin streams of white smoke out of her body. They floated away, invisible to normal senses.
Wan Qingqing felt nothing at all. Even her protective Qi could not stop it.
"Go check on our child first. I just unlocked hyper-sense in my eyes and need to adapt," Wei He said simply.
"You unlocked it?" Wan Qingqing was surprised. Since coming to Tianhai Island, she had learned a lot about these basic concepts. Most people here were from the Xuanmiao Sect, along with their helpers and families, so secret knowledge was much more common here.
"Yes. I plan to find our sect brothers in the Inner Mountain to learn more," Wei He nodded.
He felt uncomfortable with everything around him. Even though he had guessed what these wiggling, crumbling things were, seeing them was a different matter.
"Alright... Will you come back today? An'er has been working hard on his training. I want you to take a look..." Wan Qingqing said softly.
"I will try to come back soon. I cannot promise today... Don't worry, I will check on him when I have time," Wei He said gently, reaching out his hand.
A gentle wave of Hai Zhen Qi flowed over Wan Qingqing's long hair, washing over her body.
With a soft rustle, the wiggling fur turned to dust and dropped to the ground. The invisible wind could no longer pull white smoke out of her.
Wei He understood now. His Hai Zhen Qi acted like an absolute barrier and a cleanser, wiping away these weird creatures.
Wei He closed his eyes and turned off his hyper-sense. His massive Hai Zhen Qi instantly sealed his eyes.
Sealing required a special technique recorded in the Xuan Suo Art, known as the Phantom Armor.
Each time a cultivator sealed a sense, the Phantom Armor became more complex, needing rarer materials and higher skills. It also demanded strong Xuan Suo Qi. Only high-quality Qi could form a powerful enough armor to seal a hyper-sense organ.
Wei He was not naturally good at the Phantom Armor technique. It took him a long time to learn its complex steps.
However, he had an advantage over other True Persons: his cost for making mistakes was close to zero.
Other True Persons had limited Qi. Once they ran out during practice, they had to wait hours to recover. Wei He did not need to wait.
When he practiced the seal, if he failed, he tried again immediately. He failed hundreds of times, but practice made perfect. His deep pool of Hai Zhen Qi, combined with the energy around him, refilled faster than he could spend it. Because of this, his appetite had grown much larger recently.
Closing his hyper-sense eyes, Wei He let out a long breath.
He said goodbye to his wife and jumped onto the courtyard roof. In an instant, he cleared the house and rushed toward the sect.
He had read many records before, but none were about hyper-sense eyes. The Xuanmiao Sect was known to have the most experience with this.
As he left the Wei house, Wei He felt that the entire Tianhai Island was much colder and quieter than usual.
