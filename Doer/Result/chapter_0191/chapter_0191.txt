Bang! Red, yellow, white, and blue. Fireworks exploded in the dark sky. Tiny sparks drifted down, dying out before they even hit the ground.
On the roof of a small tavern in Xuanjing City.
Wei He, Wei Ying, and Zhen Qi sat around a small wooden table. No one recognized him in the dark. Just like the normal folks around them, they were only here to watch the festival lights.
"So pretty," Wei Ying looked up in awe. She dressed much better now. The dirt of her past village life was gone. Her yellow dress showed off a nice figure.
Zhen Qi wore a light blue dress. It was the exact same style, custom made. She was not as curvy, but her cold face and sharp aura made up for it. Her eyes only looked soft when she watched Wei Ying. To everyone else, she was like ice.
"We have been in Taizhou for years now," Wei He said.
"Yes. So when are you getting married? You keep avoiding it," Wei Ying complained. Her only dream now was for him to have kids and grow their family tree. But Wei He did not care about that at all.
"There is no one suitable," Wei He shook his head. Wan Qingqing crossed his mind. But he knew he only respected his old senior sister. There was no love there.
"It is a festival today. Many rich girls are finally allowed outside. Look around. If you like one, we will go propose tomorrow," Wei Ying pushed.
"Why rush?" Wei He sighed. He was drowning in work. He had to study his Silk Force, collect the Nine Strikes art, perfect his poison, and upgrade his martial skills. He had zero time for romance. Just sitting here to watch explosions in the sky was a waste of precious time.
"This is once a year," Zhen Qi added coldly. "Rich girls hide at home. They do not fight like us. Miss this chance, and you will only get to pick from fake paintings drawn by matchmakers. Think about it."
"She is right! Pick at least ten targets tonight. You are getting old. A man must marry!" Wei Ying put her hands on her hips. "Look around. This tavern is not cheap. The girls here have money."
She leaned in close. "Look at the girl in the lotus skirt behind me."
Wei He glanced over. Five people sat there. Four looked like boring elders. The girl sat with her head down, hiding behind a pink fan. She looked shy and a little drunk. Pale skin, normal body.
"Well?" Wei Ying waited.
"Fine." Wei He nodded slowly.
"That is one," Wei Ying quickly wrote it down in a notebook with charcoal. Then she kept hunting for more.
Unmarried girls wore their hair down with bells. Married women wore their hair up with pins. It was an easy hunt. Wei Ying searched hard. Zhen Qi just smiled and drank her wine, watching the dreaded Wei He surrender to his sister.
No one here would ever guess the truth. The man sighing at his sister was the feared master of the Myriad Poison Sect. If word got out, people would think it was a joke.
But Wei He knew the grim reality. His sect had some good fame right now, but the Chijing Army still wanted him dead. They were bitter enemies. His wanted poster was kept quiet for now, but if he showed his true face, the army would hunt him down like a dog.
Wei Ying found five targets. She planned to spy on their families tomorrow. She did not know her brother's dark secrets, but she knew he was a boss in this city now. He had the power to pick anyone.
The fireworks slowly died. The sky went black.
They paid the bill and walked downstairs. Suddenly, cold words from the second floor made Wei He stop.
"Over three hundred families. Dead in five days. It is a bloodbath."
"I knocked on their doors to check records. No answer. I broke the doors down, and the smell hit me. The bodies were already rotting!"
"Are you talking about Wanfu and Jin'an?" someone asked.
"Yes. The old wars left too many unburied corpses. It started a plague. People die in droves. We are lucky our city is safe."
"The offices were paying huge money to hire men to go out there. I almost went. Thank the gods I stayed!"
"Who knows when this curse will end."
Wei He listened closely. No familiar places were named. He relaxed a bit. His life was finally stable. He did not want more blood and chaos. His sect was weak. Rival factions and armies watched them like hungry wolves. The Chaos God Cult could send killers at any moment. He could only trust himself. Never rely on others. Always be ready to kill. That was his only rule.
"What is wrong?" Wei Ying asked.
"Nothing." Wei He faked a smile.
They went out and got into their carriage. On the dark road, ox carts slowly moved out of the city gates. A strong smell of medicine bled from the wood.
Near the gate, a driver yelled at the guards. "This is for the plague! Hurry!"
"Go!" The guard looked grim and waved them out.
Wei He watched the carts pass. The heavy shadow of death in the air reminded him of his bloody past in Feiye City.

Qingqi County.
Dirty stalls lined the streets. Clerks poured dark soup for the long lines of sick people. The medicine was not free, but the lines were still endless. Soon, the giant iron pots were scraped clean.
Only one small bowl was left.
The clerk looked at the three people next in line. A father, a mother, and a five year old boy. All three coughed heavily. Their faces were grey. Death was clinging to their skin.
"One bowl left. The next batch comes in two days," the clerk frowned.
Three sick people. One cure. Who gets to live? The soup could not kill the plague, but it pushed the Reaper back for a little while.
The father picked up the bowl. He hid his own hunger and looked down.
"Give it to Dong'er."
"Yes. To Dong'er." The mother coughed blood. Her eyes were going blind, but she forced a smile. She knelt and put the bowl to the boy's lips. "Drink it all."
The boy did not understand death. He just obeyed and drank the bitter soup.
Nearby, Nine Shadows watched and sighed. He turned away and walked into a dark alley. He stopped at a small yard and knocked on the wood.
The door creaked open. Old Widow Sun peeked out.
"Uncle Nine. You came?" She stepped back to let him in.
Nine Shadows walked in. "How is Old Sun?"
Silence. He looked back. The old woman was shaking.
"He passed away last night." She let out a long breath. "When your time is up, you cannot hide."
"Did he not take my medicine?" Nine Shadows felt his throat go tight.
"It helped the pain. But his body was just too weak." She tried to smile, but tears fell down her wrinkled face. "Do not blame yourself. You did your best."
"He took too many heavy beatings when he was young. No money to heal. That is just fate." She coughed, her face turning numb.
Nine Shadows could not speak. He walked into the hall and looked at Old Sun, resting in a cheap wooden coffin. He had seen mountains of dead bodies before, but today, his chest felt heavy.
"I will not last much longer either." The old woman crouched to burn fake paper money in an iron pot. "Uncle Nine, you are the best doctor here. When I die, please. Keep my grandsons alive."
Nine Shadows turned to look at the dying woman. He nodded slowly.
"I will."
"Thank you." She smiled, looking like a heavy chain had just been taken off her neck.
