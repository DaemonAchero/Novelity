The relationship between the Xuanmiao Sect and the current Dayue Dynasty is a bit delicate.
Because of the standoff between Yuanduzi and Moduo, the Dayue Dynasty did not openly surround and kill the people of the Xuanmiao Sect. They also ordered the experts below that as long as the people of the Xuanmiao Sect did not cause trouble, they were not to provoke them.
Therefore, on the side of Dayue, some hidden liaison points still remained. At least until Yuanduzi and Moduo completely decided the winner and loser, these hidden points were safe.
This was the reason why the Xuanmiao Sect dared to brazenly sail boats to pick up the people of the Dao Sect. Otherwise, did they really think the many naval warships of the Dayue Dynasty were soft?
It was just giving face to the Xuanmiao Sect and not chasing them out to sea to kill them. Both sides had a strong tacit understanding. As long as it was not done openly, everyone just turned a blind eye in secret. After all, the one with a grudge against Yuanduzi was the royal family, not everyone underneath. If not for this, no one wanted to fight against the strong power backed by Yuanduzi.
"Eldest Senior Sister, I have some things... I want to go to the Dayue mainland for a while..." Cai Menghuan said with some embarrassment.
"..." When Wei He looked at him, he immediately connected it to the two boxes of things this guy brought for his younger sister before setting off. Good grief, the things he brought alone filled two boxes, and goodness knows how many dozens of younger sisters he had in the mainland.
Hearing this, Yuanduzi also thought of this, and a trace of helplessness flashed in her eyes.
"Menghuan, some things must be restrained. Do not live up to the sect's expectations of you, and do not live up to the innate bone structure and comprehension given to you by the heavens," she gently persuaded.
"Disciple understands! Disciple... this is the last time!" Cai Menghuan gritted his teeth and lowered his head to promise.
"Last time... I do not know how many times I have heard these words from you," Yuanduzi said helplessly.
Her gaze turned to Wei He.
"In that case, Wei He, you can accompany him on this trip. At the critical moment, be sure to drag him back by force."
"Yes!" Wei He nodded heavily.
"Remember, the things on both of you can guarantee ten breaths of safety after being broken, and they must be used at the most critical places. In addition, be careful not to reveal the martial arts characteristics of the Xuanmiao Sect. Do not be too brazen," Yuanduzi instructed.
"Yes!" the two quickly replied.
"That is good. Go. Go and return early. If there is anything, you can use the hidden points to send messages." Yuanduzi waved her hand with a tired heart, signaling Cai Menghuan to scram quickly.
Cai Menghuan felt as if he had been granted a pardon. He quickly pulled Wei He, leaped off the boat, boarded a small wooden boat, and hurried toward the Dayue land.
"Senior Brother Cai, just how many female confidantes do you have in the Dayue mainland? I just want to ask, with such a long distance, how did you hook up with them before?" Wei He stood at the stern of the boat and could not help asking.
"What do you mean by hook up? That sounds so ugly. I just cannot bear to see the girls sad..." Cai Menghuan shook his head and sighed.
"The hardest thing in this world to accept is the kindness of a beauty..." He wore a deeply emotional expression.
"Junior Brother, although you are married, you really do not understand this reasoning at all in this regard." He looked at Wei He with some pity. "Do you want me to teach you a few moves?"
"No. I will pass." Wei He quickly resolved. Constantly revolving in these romantic battlefields to the point where even martial arts could be easily distracted and dragged down—such a life was exhausting, what was good about it?
"You." Cai Menghuan sighed helplessly. "All day long you only know cultivation. Have you not heard that combining work and rest, tension and relaxation, allows you to reach a higher level?"
"..." These words sounded very correct, but coming from Cai Menghuan's mouth, they felt strange.
Wei He was too lazy to bother with him. After getting along more, he knew that this guy Cai Menghuan looked serious on the surface, but actually was deeply repressed inside. When facing girls throwing themselves into his arms, he would verbally refuse, act shy, and decline, but in reality, he was playing hard to get.
"This time I still have to trouble Junior Brother to accompany me on this trip. Also, does Junior Brother have any places you want to visit?" Cai Menghuan asked.
Mentioning this, Wei He thought of the Yunzhou cemetery where Teacher Zheng was buried many years ago. Since he was back, he would go and take a look. With his current speed and footwork, running back and forth would be very fast. And some old friends from back then... he wondered how Wang Shaojun was doing now. And Zhenqi, who almost became his sister-in-law...
"There are indeed some places and some people I want to see. At that time, we can split up and then agree on a meeting place to gather, how about that?" Wei He suggested.
"Okay." Cai Menghuan nodded quickly. At this moment, he was thinking that it would be convenient to act alone, and he could just check if any of those younger sisters of his had given birth to a son or daughter for him.
The two of them harbored different thoughts, rowing the boat with true qi, cutting through the wind and waves, and incidentally changing their robes into ordinary everyday clothes. Once the clothes were changed, the two instantly transformed into ordinary commoners commonly seen on the road. At most, the material of their clothes showed that their family background was quite good.
Cai Menghuan was a scholarly and gentle young master from a wealthy family. Wei He was a strong and powerful martial artist with some foundational skills. With his physique, pretending to be a low-level true blood martial artist was really not a difficult task. Coupled with the Whale Flood Scripture he practiced and his astonishing divine strength, relying solely on his physical strength and power, he could contend with a Buddha-level limit Buddhist child. Pretending to be a low-level true blood had no flaws at all.
Handling some details, the two quickly landed ashore from another coast in Haizhou. The shore was filled with messy reefs and was open and uninhabited.
Wei He took out the spoils of war he had just scavenged from his pocket—various miscellaneous items belonging to Zhou Lan, whom he had killed in seconds, were among them. Among them were identity documents, duty tokens of the Guangci Sect, some gold notes, star stones, and a light yellow, somewhat aged true blood martial arts booklet named the Guangchan Kung.
Wei He divided a small portion of the items and gave them to Cai Menghuan. He kept a portion for himself, and after agreeing on a meeting time and place, the two separated.
Cai Menghuan went straight to the current Zhongzhou. Wei He planned to go to Xizhou of Dayue at this time, which included Yunzhou of the former Dayuan.
Leaving the coastline of Haizhou, Wei He was just about to unfold his full speed. Not long after walking, he unexpectedly heard a strange noise. In the dense mountain forest, Wei He slowed down his speed, leaped up, and gently jumped onto the branch of a tall tree, hiding his aura. He used the concealment skills he had not used for a long time to hide himself.
Ahead in the forest, a team of people was slowly walking past. The team was led by government soldiers of the Dayue Dynasty, with soldiers as the main force and monks as assistants. A three-meter-tall young general in white armor carried a huge black curved saber on his shoulder, using the pinkie nail of one hand to pick food from his teeth, leading the way forward at a neither fast nor slow pace.
Under the guard of the soldiers behind, a long metal chain bound and connected a long line of prisoners. These prisoners wore gray-black prison clothes, were covered in blood, and their hands were bound by restraints connected by chains.
The most crucial thing was that the aura on these prisoners was extremely weak, and wisps of true qi aura faintly emanated from their bodies, but these auras were extremely weak, as if their cultivation had been broken.
The place where a true qi martial artist stores true qi was completely broken and injured, and this kind of injury was called breaking cultivation. After breaking cultivation, it would cause the true qi martial artist to stay in the same spot forever for the rest of their lives, and nursing such an injury back to health also required a long period of recuperation.
If this kind of injury happened during the peak period of a true qi martial artist, it could be repaired by carefully nursing it with secret techniques and treasures. But right now... that was out of the question. Once cultivation was broken and one could not get proper recuperation afterward, it would definitely have a serious impact on future cultivation, true qi, and physique.
Wei He could see that this was Dayue people escorting prisoners. Dayue now issued a martial code, listing many true qi martial arts techniques as taboos. Those who practiced true qi before would have it abolished and would not be pursued further. But if they wanted to continue practicing true qi secretly afterwards, that would violate a major taboo and lead to direct arrest and imprisonment.
Wei He guessed that this group of people should be the martial artists caught by Dayue who were practicing true qi in secret.
Soon, this group of people slowly walked past and disappeared deep into the forest.
Only then did Wei He jump down and continue on his journey. Those people had no relation to him by blood or friendship, so naturally he would not casually step forward to save anyone.
Following the direction with human traces all the way, Wei He planned to find a place where people lived first and ask for directions to make sure he had not walked the wrong way. After all, there was no mobile phone location navigation nowadays. Walking outside relied entirely on the stars in the sky and asking people.
Along the way, he continuously encountered several more escort teams just like the previous one. Some were escorting true qi system martial artists, and some were escorting prisoners of unknown identity. Obviously, the opponents of the Dayue Dynasty were not only the true qi system, but also quite a few other rivals.
Advancing not long after, upon reaching the official road, Wei He did not need to hide and openly blended into the passing pedestrians to move forward.
Dispersing his protective true qi and relying solely on the divine strength brought by the Three Hearts Scripture of the Whale Flood Scripture, he covered his body with a layer of faint scale patterns. At this moment, pretending to be a true blood martial artist was more than enough.
Moving forward along the official road, soon, Wei He encountered another escort team.
However, this time it was different from the previous ones. The prisoners escorted by this team had a somewhat heavier true qi aura on their bodies.
It seemed because they were approaching a town, there were many pedestrians on the official road. Wei He and the others moved to the side to make space for the escort convoy. Like the other onlookers, Wei He also pretended to be curious, watching the long queue of prisoners being escorted past.
"Make way! Do not block the road!" The government soldiers clearing the way in front shouted loudly. The strong physique brought by true blood martial arts allowed them to act as loudspeakers without auxiliary tools.
At the front of the convoy were individual prison carts, each carved with many star array runes. The escorted people were also powerful in aura, including men and women, old and young.
Wei He glanced over and saw that in as many as five prison carts, the imprisoned prisoners were all people from the Wushi Sect. And it seemed they were even at the Zhenren level!
He sighed in his heart. The once high-and-mighty Wushi Sect had now fallen to such a state.
The prison carts holding the Zhenren passed by slowly, followed by a mess of large cages jumbled together. In each large cage, a bunch of people were lying across each other in disorder. A bunch of men, a bunch of women.
The aura of these prisoners was much weaker, but even if they were not Zhenren, they were at least true qi training martial artists. Moreover, many of them were poorly clothed, covered in blood, and weak in aura, and they were probably all people whose cultivation had been broken.
Outside the large cages, there were special hanging wooden boards with words written on them: Third-class meat people, five taels each, pick and choose at will, guaranteed fresh and alive.
"The goods this time seem pretty good! Wait a moment and wait early, so as not to be snatched away!"
"If only I had a VIP card from the Feiyun Tower, I could get priority in picking good goods. Bringing this kind of human cargo back would be a good laborer even after you get tired of playing with them!"
"Forget it, your qualifications for trading are not enough. Even if you have money, you cannot buy it. Aren't you afraid of having your throat cut by a slave in the middle of the night?"
Surrounding pedestrians discussed the related topics of this escort team with great interest.
Wei He listened quietly, feeling somewhat saddened in his heart about the fate of the true qi system martial artists in the current Dayue environment. He did not expect Dayue to act so ruthlessly.
Sighing in his heart, he stopped paying attention, turned around, and prepared to hurry toward the town, intending to see what changes had taken place in Dayue's cities nowadays.
However, suddenly, when his gaze swept across the second-to-last large cage, Wei He's footsteps paused and he stopped abruptly.
His gaze turned back, carefully looking into that large cage.
He saw more than ten women huddled together inside the cage like meat pigs with numb expressions, poorly clothed, and curled up. One of them actually looked somewhat familiar to him.
Looking closely, Wei He recognized the person's identity.
It was actually Jiang Su, whom he had not seen for many years!
This was the senior sister who left Yunzhou with him back then, arrived in Taizhou, and even apprenticed to the Wushi Sect. Although her appearance was still clear and pretty, she was covered in wounds everywhere, and a much younger girl was tightly pressed against her side.
Their appearances were similar, except that the younger girl's legs were slenderer and she was taller.
"Jiang Su..." Wei He sighed secretly.
Flashbacks of memories from when he was still practicing martial arts at the boxing academy welled up in his mind.
If he had not run into her, that would be it, but since he had run into her, he could not help but extend a helping hand.
Of course, directly making a move to rob the prisoners was impossible. There was no need to cause such a big commotion. But disguising his identity and spending money to buy a person was still doable.
Anyway, when Wei He came to Dayue this time, he actually had another goal, which was to see if he could dual-cultivate true blood martial arts. If he could dual-cultivate true blood martial arts, he could utilize the epoch-making blood weapon technology invented by the Dayue Dynasty.
With true blood and true qi dual cultivation, by that time, he would step into an unprecedented top-tier realm.
Of course, true blood martial arts most urgently required bloodline true blood, which he did not have. But the Three Hearts Scripture gave him a trace of the possibility of becoming true blood.
Therefore, he wanted to try.
Under this premise, saving an old friend on the side could also be considered fulfilling a portion of their past fellowship as fellow disciples.
