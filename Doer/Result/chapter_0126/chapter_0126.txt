The noise of fast strikes filled the woods as two fighters traded hits.
Song Qing from Plum Blossom Villa was strong. His kicks were heavy and sharp, drilling straight through Wei He’s Rain-Covering Force.
Both were Martial Masters, respected men in Xuanjing City. Yet here they were, fighting near an unknown pond over something almost silly.
After a dozen moves, Song Qing paused. His inner force spun fast, gathering into his right leg. A tight swirl of air kicked up dirt and grass around his boots.
"You have some skill," Song Qing sneered. "But if this is all you’ve got, you lose today!"
He whipped his leg forward, driving three swift kicks straight ahead.
Bang! Bang! Bang!
Wei He blocked all three. Before he could reset his stance, a side kick slammed hard into his right arm.
Thump.
Wei He stumbled back, spit up a line of blood, and used the force of the hit to throw himself into the thick trees. Then, silence.
He had no choice but to back off. They were too close to the main road and Xuanjing City. Too many people passed by here, making it a bad place to fight to the death.
"Weak. Lucky he runs fast," Song Qing said, landing softly. Disdain covered his face.
"How was he?" a masked woman in green asked as she walked out from the trees.
She had been hiding the whole time, waiting for the fight to end. She wore a green dress, carried a thin curved sword on her back, and had cold, killer eyes above her white mask.
"Nothing special," Song Qing said, chest out. "His force is weak, far below a real master. In a real fight, I could take his head in fifteen moves."
"Should we track him down?" she asked.
"No need. Heavenly Seal Sect is already on edge after losing a master. We shouldn't make noise. Besides, he's too weak to be the one who took the goods. It was someone else." Song Qing shook his head.
"Fine."
The two of them and their men left the pond, vanishing into the forest.
A moment later, Wei He stepped out from the shadows. He looked at the direction they went, thinking deeply.
That guy's kicks were decent, but nowhere near enough to actually hurt him. He was about as strong as the Human-Faced Tiger.
Wei He had held back because he felt a second person hiding in the dark, but couldn't pinpoint her exact spot. Plus, he needed to keep up his disguise. Everyone thought he was a weakened master who had lost his inner force. He couldn't draw attention.
So the snakes are coming out. Plum Blossom Villa, huh...
Word was that Plum Blossom Villa had two masters running the place, a husband and wife team. It was likely the two he just met.
From what Wei He knew, Xuanjing City used to have only a few dozen masters divided among the three big families. But over the past year, crowds of rogue fighters flooded the city, muddying the waters. Plum Blossom Villa was one of these new forces, taking over local gangs to build a name.
Interesting. Someone high up must be pulling their strings...
This proved he had to stay hidden.
He could have wiped them all out right there, but three things stopped him. First, Song Qing was a leg specialist and moved faster than him. Second, facing two masters at once made a total kill risky. Third, the main road was too close, making poison hard to use undetected.
So, he swallowed his urge to crush them, held back his poisons, and let them walk.
Once he was sure they were gone, Wei He turned and rushed toward the Heavenly Seal Sect.
Heavenly Seal Sect, Ten Thousand Green Courtyard.
"Long time no see, Qingqing. How have you been?"
A fair-skinned man in a clean white martial outfit walked through the archway.
"Ah, Senior Brother Zhou. I was practicing my moves and didn't hear you come in. My bad," Wan Qingqing said, pulling back her stance.
"Why so distant, Qingqing? We grew up together. Why act like strangers now?" The man sighed.
This was Zhou Yugui, the only son of Vice Sect Master Zhou Shun. He was one of the top young talents in the sect, standing side by side with Wan Qingqing.
"Just tell me why you are here, Senior Brother," she replied coolly. After everything that had happened, she saw right through him.
"Don't worry, I'm not here to talk you into anything," Zhou Yugui smiled. "Yumo and Bufan said it's been too long since we all hung out. They asked me to get us together, just like old times."
Hearing those two names, Wan Qingqing’s face softened.
"It really has been a while. Where are we meeting?" She had been stressed lately and wanted a break with real friends.
"At Jing River Cloud Bay Dock, on Yumo’s boat. We haven't picked a time yet. You choose."
"Let Yumo pick. It's her boat."
"Sounds good."
Zhou Yugui saw her guard was still up. He knew she was worried he would bring up the marriage proposal from the Lishan Sect leader's son.
He wasn't in a rush. Pushing it himself would only make her fight back. But if her close friends brought it up, she would start to doubt her own choices.
The Lishan Sect was rising fast, unlike the falling Heavenly Seal Sect. If he could help seal this marriage, his father would gain a strong ally for the future fight over the Sect Master seat.
Tianya Library.
Wei He stood before the old bookshelves, digging through dusty texts for power-draining methods. He focused on methods that used rare beast parts, which were usually kept in old, damaged books.
It didn't take long for him to find two options he could use.
The first was the Three Elements Reset. It used the organs of three different rare beasts to force the inner blood into a strange state, allowing a fighter to drain their force without damaging their foundation.
The problem was that those three beasts had been extinct for a hundred years. Even back then, they were super rare. The creator only pulled it off by pure luck, got wrecked by the beasts in the process, and died shortly after.
Wei He set it aside as a backup.
The second method was called Cloud Smoke.
This one was even stranger. It required the flesh of a rare Cloud Smoke Bird as a catalyst. It turned the inner blood into smoke, letting it evaporate out of the body without harm, while even boosting the user's base vitality slightly.
The catch? The Cloud Smoke Bird was only mentioned in ancient myths. No living person in any of his books had ever seen one.
If I want to fake it, Cloud Smoke is the best choice. Someone probably made it up, but the theory sounds real enough to pass.
Wei He made up his mind, picked up the Cloud Smoke book, and began to memorize it.
It was short, only about three thousand words, along with a single foundation chart. It detailed how to move the inner blood and react it with the bird's flesh. The chart helped the user track bad blood left inside the body.
This unique chart was likely the only reason the book was stored on the second floor.
"Now I just need to wait for the Break-Through Bead to fill up," Wei He thought, feeling hopeful.
He had the theory down. Now it was just a waiting game.
He put the book back, left the library, and headed straight for the Thousand Bats Pavilion to check on his sister, Wei Ying. Since she lived in a single room, he didn't stay long, just checked that she was safe before leaving.
He rested at home for the night and headed back to Xuanjing City the next morning.
Then, he waited.
Time flew by. Xuanjing City stayed quiet. Neither the sect, the government, nor the three big families made any big moves.
Wei He was happy with the peace. He spent his time studying the Break-Through Bead.
Two months passed in a flash, and the bead was finally full again. During this time, he acted like an injured master recovering at the branch, occasionally walking around the area.
Oddly enough, the stash of goods he had hidden seemed completely forgotten. No one mentioned the stolen weapons either.
The sect only sent a few old masters to ask him about Zhao Xinzheng. Wei He answered their questions smoothly.
Soon, the sect handed down its verdict: Zhao Xinzheng plotted with outsiders to steal branch goods. Traitor to the sect.
And that was it. The whole thing was swept under the rug like a simple case of a missing person.
As for Master Yao Hanlin, the sect tried harder, sending search parties to the site multiple times. But they found nothing. The wolves had eaten everything, leaving behind only scraps of clothes and shoes.
Wei He wasn't worried. He needed time to grow anyway.
He had thoroughly mastered the Cloud Smoke method in his head. Now that the Break-Through Bead was ready, it was time to test if his plan would work.
