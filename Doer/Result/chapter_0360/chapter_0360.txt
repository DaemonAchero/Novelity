Wei He scanned the information about the Lou family in his mind. He glanced at the other three candidates, whose faces had slightly changed, and waved his hand dismissively.
"If you can't give me a good reason to hire you, leave."
"Senior brother, do you know that I am a cousin of Senior Brother Ni Anhong?" A young man with arrogant eyes spoke up. "If you take me in, you will definitely earn his friendship..."
"Get out," Wei He frowned.
Who was Ni Anhong? Why should he care?
To use connections and even throw around subtle threats was pure stupidity.
Leave alone the fact that Wei He didn't even know this Ni Anhong. Even if he did, he would never hire someone this dumb as his attendant. Wasn't this guy just using someone else's name to piss people off everywhere?
Seeing Wei He wave his hand again, the three had no choice but to leave.
The arrogant young man glared at Wei He once more on his way out. He was clearly pissed off that Wei He ignored Senior Brother Ni Anhong's name.
Fool.
The other two sneered in their hearts.
They also knew that Ni Anhong was one of the grand masters in the sect. For so many years, the Xuanmiao Sect only had a dozen or so grand masters, so everyone knew their names well.
This young man was named Ni Shucheng, but no one knew how he was actually related to Ni Anhong. He kept using Ni Anhong’s name to get hired by several true masters using his strength and background, only to be fired every single time.
Now he wanted to try his luck with Wei He. The other two didn't even know what to say to that.
With the three gone, only Lou Shengyue remained.
Wei He looked at her closely for the first time.
His first impression of her was pure. Simple, without scheme, with a faint innocence in her eyes. She was clearly protected very well growing up. Like a pig just let out of a sty, curious about everything outside.
"Does your Lou family really know where to find the Black Line Whale?" Wei He asked again. The whale's oil was key to his next level of cultivation, so there could be no mistake.
"We really do," Lou Shengyue nodded. "We lost a lot of men and paid a heavy price, but we still couldn't capture it."
Her parents had hired many experts and even asked true masters to move out to get that whale. Sadly, just as they were about to get it, things went wrong.
"What went wrong? Tell me directly," Wei He said, reading her expression.
"It was like this," Lou Shengyue quickly explained their trouble without hiding anything.
It turned out the Black Line Whale was not in the waters near the Xuanmiao Sect. They had to sail deeper into the ocean, which meant going around a large zone of dangerous winds.
The Lou family spent a lot of time and men to finally find a Black Line Whale in a calm sea area. They were about to catch it when a man calling himself Taoist Sanyun stopped them.
Taoist Sanyun claimed all the Black Line Whales nearby were raised by him. To hunt them, they had to get his permission first.
The Lou family refused. The whale had no mark on it and didn't obey his orders, so how could he prove he raised it?
A fight broke out. The Lou family suffered heavy losses, and their big ship with over a hundred people was seized on the spot. The few people sent back were badly injured.
On their second trip, the Lou family asked two true masters to sail out together and fight Taoist Sanyun again.
They still suffered a crushing defeat.
After returning, the Lou family tried to gather info, but could find nothing useful. The sea area where they found the whale was too far from the Xuanmiao Sect, and there were no old records of it.
"How can you be sure it was a Black Line Whale?" Wei He asked.
Lou Shengyue listed the features of the whale one by one. Wei He knew instantly that she was right. It was indeed what he needed.
Only this Taoist Sanyun...
A thought suddenly hit him. He remembered Senior Sister Yao Wan also saying she was going far away to help a friend with some business.
"By the way, have a lot of grand master senior brothers and sisters been leaving the sect recently?" Wei He asked out of nowhere.
Lou Shengyue paused, then thought about it with a frown.
"Yes, that's true." As a martial arts genius, she had been training hard inside her family. Even after breaking through, she didn't have many mundane troubles compared to others, so she hadn't noticed it before.
Wei He felt a chill in his heart.
After the trap set for him by the Guanci Sect during Zhou Muqing's event, he knew the Wu State was secretly leaking into the Xuanmiao Sect.
Now so many experts were leaving one after another. What was going on?
He didn't know what First Senior Sister planned to do. Nor did he know how the Xuanmiao Sect would handle this situation. But he didn't believe the sect was unaware of the Wu State's infiltration under these conditions.
Besides, he had already warned Senior Sister Yuanduzi about it before.
Yet under such a dangerous situation, many grand masters were still leaving...
With a heavy heart, Wei He stopped asking questions.
"Go back and prepare. I will go with your family's ship next time. If it is truly a Black Line Whale, I will give you the position."
"Yes! Thank you, Senior Brother!" Lou Shengyue was overjoyed.
She wouldn't be this happy for anyone else's attendant position, but Wei He was different. He was a genius master who stood equal to the seed disciples, someone First Senior Sister herself valued highly.
Becoming his attendant meant having a indirect link to First Senior Sister Yuanduzi. It promised a great future, both now and later.
...
Outside the Xuanmiao Sect, on a small island in a certain sea area.
A secret cave door slowly opened. A man with an ordinary face walked out. He wore a Bagua robe with a purple-gold beast pattern on his back. Invisible force fields lightly wrapped around his body to protect him.
This man was Xiao Li, Taoist Yudan, one of the grand masters from the Lock Mountain branch of the Xuanmiao Sect.
Walking out of the cave, Xiao Li looked at the distant sea.
"They haven't arrived yet?" he asked softly.
"They should be here soon," a woman's voice sounded near his ear.
"This operation will lure out nearly half of the grand masters and seed disciples from the inner mountain. Although it's done in batches, the numbers are quite high. Won't Yuanduzi notice?" Xiao Li asked with worry.
"We have studied the Xuanmiao Sect for a long time. Their travel schedules, daily routines, and weaknesses are all recorded and well understood. Don't worry. As long as they don't leave all at once, it will be fine. Controlling them one by one in batches like this is the safest way," the woman replied.
"Besides, the Great Yuan forces have basically given up on this area to focus all their power on Taizhou. The Xuanmiao Sect can't hold out alone. Even if she knows, she can't change anything. What's the point?" the woman added.
"Where is the surprise attack army now?" Xiao Li asked again.
"Fifty thousand troops in total are attacking in groups. They will gather near Zhentao City on Tianhai Island and then charge up the mountain. With help from inside, five Buddha Lords from the Temple, plus THAT person... the Xuanmiao Sect will surely fall this time!"
The woman's voice was full of strong confidence.
Xiao Li had nothing to say to that.
Fifty thousand troops with star formations combining their force was enough to fight a Grandmaster. Add the True Blood experts from the Temple, five Buddha Lords, and that person...
With this kind of power, destroying the current Xuanmiao Sect was almost too easy.
Still, thinking about how he had lived here for so many years, seeing this familiar land about to be destroyed soon brought a slight stir to his heart.
He was human, not a rock. He had feelings too.
"What's wrong? Feeling sad? Can't bear it?" the woman laughed.
"A little. But the people I care about won't care about me if they find out the truth. They will only hate me," Xiao Li sighed.
"Since you know that, don't overthink it. Just stick to the plan," the woman's voice slowly faded away.
Xiao Li had a complex look in his eyes. He looked up at the dark, cloudy sky, turned around, and went back into his cave, vanishing from sight.
Half a month later was their agreed date of action. It would also be the end of the Xuanmiao Sect.
...
Inner Mountain, Black Cliff.
Below the wild edge of the cliff was a vast sea of clouds.
Yuanduzi stood alone at the edge. Her eyes, covered by a blindfold, looked quietly down at the deep abyss, waiting for something.
Time passed slowly.
About fifteen minutes later, deep within the clouds floating in front of the cliff, a voice slowly reached her ears.
"Yuandu, is something wrong?"
It was the voice of Xiao Ling, the founder of the Lock Mountain branch.
"Master, I brought the Blue Eye Ointment. It should help with your injuries," Yuanduzi said coldly.
"No need. I can heal my own injuries. There is no need to waste a miracle medicine like the Blue Eye Ointment," Xiao Ling answered in a heavy voice.
"Then do you need me to make other preparations?" Yuanduzi asked again.
"Keep everything as it is."
Yuanduzi fell silent for a moment, then said, "The coalition army sent news asking our sect to move to another state. The whole Haizhou has become the main target of the Wu State's full attack. I wonder if Master has any advice for me."
"...Very well," Xiao Ling seemed to speak with a faint sense of helplessness. "Go work out a route for the move and bring it to me to check. Once it is confirmed, we can leave."
"I understand," Yuanduzi replied respectfully with her head bowed.
Xiao Ling's voice slowly disappeared.
After waiting for a long time, Yuanduzi slowly stepped back and left Black Cliff.
"First Senior Sister, are you really going to move the sect?"
On the mountain path toward Yuntai Peak, Qingmeizi suddenly appeared on the side, staring closely at her.
"We have no choice," Yuanduzi said softly.
Qingmeizi had no words to offer. Facing the Wu State's army alone, how could the Xuanmiao Sect survive? Even top sects like the Wushi Sect or Jinlian Sect would be helpless in this situation.
To put it simply, the core of the Xuanmiao Sect was just the founder and the three of them, along with a dozen grand masters and around two hundred true masters. Everything else was just an outer layer. As long as the core stayed alive, they could rebuild the sect quickly anywhere.
It was just that the seal... and the holy land...
"Before we leave, we will strengthen the seal one last time," Yuanduzi said calmly.
"..." Qingmeizi stayed silent.
He had been hunting down the Guanci Sect true masters lately, and things were going smoothly with good news everywhere. He thought the situation would slowly get better, but he didn't expect it to crash down so fast...
Yuanduzi looked at Qingmeizi, her body slowly fading and disappearing as she moved away at high speed.
