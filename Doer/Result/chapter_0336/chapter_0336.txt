Zhou Muqing stopped questioning the two Mo family teenagers and looked outside.
A heavily built man with pale yellow eyebrows walked in. A long scar cut diagonally across his cheek, making him look hideous.
As soon as he entered, his eyes fell on the two corpses on the floor. "Good. Very good. I didn't expect anyone in Quanan County to have the guts to kill my people."
"It's the Four-Armed Black Ape, Qin Zhong!" the Mo family boy gasped in terror.
The moment that title was uttered, the martial artists watching the scene grew pale. They slipped out through the side doors, not daring to stay a second longer.
The boy and girl from the Mo family turned deathly pale as well, especially when they saw the man's eyebrows were light yellow.
"Even the Four-Armed Black Ape joined the Guangci Sect?" Wei He thought, shaking his head.
The Guangci Sect from the State of Wu was spreading far too quickly.
Before he left, his senior brother Chang Xuezhong had warned him to avoid trouble with them if possible.
It was a pity.
"You've got nerve," the Four-Armed Black Ape said. He did not charge in. Instead, he stared at Zhou Muqing with caution and pointed a finger at the group.
Zhou Muqing looked back at Wei He, waiting for his command. They hadn't come out to cause trouble, so every choice was up to him.
"Since the Guangci Sect comes from Wu, I assume they cultivate the Path of True Blood?" Wei He asked in a quiet voice.
"Yes. Those three earlier were the same," Zhou Muqing nodded. "Their bodies are extremely tough. They don't use internal force. They rely purely on raw strength, speed, and lethal moves."
That explained why her chopsticks, laced with internal force, had barely done any damage earlier. These True Blood fighters from Wu had remarkably dense muscle and bone.
"If you dare, wait right here!" the Four-Armed Black Ape growled. "You attacked my Yellow Brow lineage and killed our men in broad daylight. Just you wait!"
He had sharp eyes and knew Zhou Muqing was trouble. Rather than stepping inside, he turned on his heel and fled.
Zhou Muqing rushed out the door, ready to give chase.
"Come back," Wei He called out.
"Master?" She turned back, confused. Weren't they supposed to clear up trouble quickly? Why stop now?
Wei He shook his head. "No rush. This is our territory, so nothing major will go wrong. It's a good chance to see how this True Blood system compares to our True Force."
He meant it.
The Guangci Sect dared to act so wild in Haizhou because they had backing. But with the Xuanmiao Sect ruling Haizhou, they wouldn't dare send high-level figures here out of fear of getting wiped out.
These sect members were just small infiltrators, nothing to fear. They were perfect subject material for studying the Path of True Blood.
Wei He knew he would fight the State of Wu sooner or later. He wanted to use this chance to study their path early.
He glanced at the three dead Yellow Brows on the floor. They were just small fries who wouldn't know much anyway. He wanted to see what bigger fish he could hook.
"Let's go," he said, standing up on purpose. "We should leave before their reinforcements arrive, just so we don't get trapped."
Zhou Muqing understood his plan instantly. She raised her voice so others could hear. "Yes, Master. Those three were strong, and their backers must be far stronger. We can't fight a whole crowd. Leaving now is best."
Wei He nodded and led the way out.
Outside, the sun was bright, and a soft flower scent drifted in the wind. A crowd of locals had gathered around the diner. Seeing them come out, some people cheered.
"You two should run!" someone shouted from the crowd. "If their people get here, it'll be too late!"
Wei He cupped his hands and smiled. "Thanks for the warning."
He said no more and walked toward the city gate with Zhou Muqing.
"Benefactors, please wait!"
A girl's voice called from behind. It was the young girl from the Mo family. She wore the outer robe Zhou Muqing had given her. Tear tracks covered her face, but her eyes were firm.
Her brother followed closely, carrying their unconscious father. He looked like he wanted to speak, but held back.
"If you have nowhere to stay, you can come to our Mo Family Manor!" the girl called out loudly.
She knew that if Wei He's group just walked away, they could easily escape the mess. But the Mo Family Manor couldn't run. When the Guangci Sect came looking, her family would take the fall.
They had only fought earlier to protect themselves. But now three people were dead, and they were members of the Guangci Sect. Those vicious cultists would never drop it.
The girl's mind worked fast. She had to keep these two around to take the blow for them. Otherwise, her family manor would be wiped out, just like the families before them.
Zhou Muqing was a former leader who knew human nature well. A young girl's simple scheme was clear as day to her.
Self-preservation was normal, but these kids were saved by them, yet they wanted to drag them down just to protect their own property. Such plotting was disgusting.
Zhou Muqing ignored the girl and caught up with Wei He.
The girl's face turned even paler. She could only grit her teeth and watch them leave.
A wave of resentment rose in her heart. If these two hadn't killed those men, there might still be room to negotiate with the Guangci Sect. Now three men were dead, and her family was tied to the incident. They were facing total ruin.
If not for these two killing people, they wouldn't be in this mess.
"Brother," the girl turned to her brother. "We need to go back, pack our things, and leave right now! We can't stay here!"
"Alright!" the boy agreed, nodding hard.
Carrying their father, they hurried back the way they came. They had to pack and leave while the two strangers drew the Guangci Sect's attention away.
On the other side of town, Wei He and Zhou Muqing walked slowly toward the city gate.
Before they could get far, a group of Yellow Brow men blocked the road.
They stood on the main road out of town. Every one of them was tall, broad, and fierce.
"Master, they really came," Zhou Muqing said, looking back.
Wei He looked up at the sky. "End it quickly. Don't waste time."
"Yes."
Zhou Muqing stepped down from the carriage and walked toward the men.
"Kill!!"
The men charged first, swinging heavy steel blades without fear for their lives.
Sadly, they were only low-level members, slightly stronger than average fighters. They were no match for a half-Real fighter like Zhou Muqing.
Her body blurred into a streak of blue light, weaving through the crowd in a single breath.
Slice after slice echoed.
Fresh blood sprayed as every single thug dropped their weapon, clutching their bleeding left arms.
Zhou Muqing flicked the blood off her blade and glared at them. "Get lost. Don't let me see you again."
In the back, Wei He shook his head. Zhou Muqing was good at everything, but her heart was too soft. Leaving these small trash alive served no purpose.
"Let's move," he said, not bothering to lecture her.
Since they were fishing anyway, leaving more survivors meant the news would spread faster.
Outside Quanan County, in a quiet, green forest.
A built man with yellow eyebrows wore black martial clothes. A large triangular pattern was embroidered on his back. In the middle of the pattern sat a three-eyed figure on a black lotus, its eyes half-open with a gentle expression.
This was the Three-Eyed Black Lotus Lord, worshipped by the Guangci Sect.
"Master," a sunken-eyed scholar with a white face said, fanning himself with a folding fan. "More martial artists from Dayan fell for the trick in town. They really are stupid beyond words."
"How did it go this time?" the Yellow Brow man asked calmly.
"These ones have some skill. Your disciples couldn't stop them and lost. So..." the scholar smiled.
"Then I will handle it myself," the Yellow Brow man said. He was the most arrogant figure in this area, the Yellow Brow Arhat of the Guangci Sect.
He commanded eighty disciples and ran wild in Quanan County. Even the local authorities couldn't control him.
"This is a key moment. Nothing can ruin the White-Robed Buddha's plan."
The Yellow Brow Arhat looked toward the distant border. If this went smoothly, they might crush the Xuanmiao Sect's ability to interfere in the conflict between the two nations.
For this grand scheme, the Holy Sect had sent many masters into Haizhou.
There were ten masters at his level alone. These ten True Blood Arhats hid their true power, pretending to be ordinary fighters scattered across the land, all to keep the plan moving forward.
As long as the border post didn't leak news, they could drag this out further.
And this was just Haizhou. Similar moves were happening in Taizhou and Zhongzhou.
"Lead the way," the Arhat ordered. "We'll cut them off. Useful for nothing, failing to deal with two travelers!"
His only joy these days was finding new ways to torture weak Dayan martial artists. If not for this mission, he wouldn't be hiding here, laying low for months.
"Tsk, tsk, tsk..."
A voice drifted down from the tree canopy above.
The Arhat frowned and looked up. "Who are you?"
He hadn't even noticed someone getting this close.
Sitting on a branch was a woman in red, holding a long sword with a calm look on her face.
It was Yao Wan.
She looked calm, but her mind was anything but peaceful.
'Good thing I came back in time,' she thought. 'If this guy ambushed little Wei while he's at a critical stage of Sense Anchoring, it could cause big trouble...' She felt lucky she had returned.
The eldest senior sister had paid her again despite being busy, asking her to protect Wei He until he finished his Sense Anchoring.
If he was hit by an ambush from someone like this and failed his Sense Anchoring, he could suffer a terrible backlash.
Yao Wan looked down at the Arhat and shook her head.
'Thank me, little Wei He,' she thought. 'If not for me, you might have taken a huge hit today.' She knew Wei He's limit was equal to someone at the second Sense Anchoring stage. But right now was a sensitive time. He couldn't easily close off his heightened senses during a fight.
When real masters fought, they closed off most of their senses to avoid mental interference and deadly spirit winds. Fighting while exposed to those threats was suicidal.
If a True Blood master at the Transformation realm ambushed him, it would be dangerous.
Luckily, she was here.
