A splash broke the silence.
Wei He rose from the dark water. He leaped to the shore and landed with absolute balance.
With a sudden burst of force, the water on his skin vaporized into mist.
Three months had passed. His Break-through Bead was full again. It was time to pick a new art to boost his Rain-Covering Force.
Breaking through the next level of the Rain-Covering Cloud Art would take way too long. Finishing eighteen cloud lines one by one was a grind. If he wanted to get stronger in real combat right now, he had to upgrade his Rain-Covering Force directly.
He crossed the ground in a few fast strides and entered his wooden cabin. On the desk sat a stack of books.
The top book had a pale green cover with five dark words written by hand: Shadowless Distortion Art.
This was the leg art manual he had looted from the corpse of the Thousand-Armed Demon, Ruan Qinghong.
It had been a long time since Wei He last found a manual on a body. He could not help but feel a strange sense of fortune.
Most people knew the safest place for a secret manual was anywhere but on themselves. But Ruan Qinghong was arrogant. He believed the safest place in the world was his own body. So he carried it everywhere.
In the end, that arrogance gave Wei He a free prize.
Wei He flipped through it. The manual was not complete, but it only lacked the secret catalyst needed to break through bottleneck realms—just like the eyes of the Spring-Origin Bird needed for the Rain-Covering Cloud Art.
This Shadowless Distortion Art worked the same way. The catalyst was only needed at the absolute moment of breakthrough.
More importantly...
Wei He scanned the pages. A faint poisonous scent rose from the paper, but his White Jade Art made him completely immune to this type of poison, so he ignored it.
The Shadowless Distortion Art was born from the God-Distorting Force of the God-Distorting Cult. It was mainly a leg art focused entirely on raw speed.
That was not even the best part. The best part was that this manual was far more complete than anything he had found in the Horizon Building!
In Wei He’s hands was a full path: from entry, to force conversion, to bone forging, all the way to organ training!
"Not bad. The Flying Dragon Art is getting outdated for my level anyway. It has reached its ceiling. Without converting to internal force, it just wastes a slot in my Prime Blood."
A martial artist's capacity for different arts was set by their Prime Blood limit.
A normal fighter could only cultivate five arts at most. Even if the arts complemented each other, they could not break this limit.
This was a rule Wei He had tested and confirmed over many years.
Now that his Whale-Flood Art had broken through its second layer, forming two blood sacs, his total Prime Blood was far beyond normal people. He did not have to care about the five-art limit.
"Since it can convert to internal force, I need to test if this leg art clashes with my Rain-Covering Force first."
With the Autumn Deer Art as a safety net, Wei He opened the Shadowless Distortion Art. He started testing the first layer using his earlobe.
Back then, Ruan Qinghong used this leg art alone to match Wei He’s top speed—a speed built from three layers of stacks: Rain-Covering Force's speed effect, Whale-Flood Art's raw power, and the Flying Dragon Art.
It was obvious that this art was vastly superior to the Flying Dragon Art.
Wei He channeled his Prime Blood and followed the instructions to begin the first layer trial.
Since he already had a foundation in other force-converting arts, converting Prime Blood into Stamp Blood was extremely smooth.
Part of the Stamp Blood traits in the Shadowless Distortion Art overlapped with the Flying Dragon Art, which saved him a lot of time.
This trial lasted three full days.
For three days, Wei He stayed inside the cabin. When he was hungry or thirsty, disciples brought food to his door.
After three days, the results came out.
"As expected, it doesn't work..." Wei He touched his slightly damaged left earlobe and let out a quiet sigh. Nothing in this world was that easy. The odds of a newly looted organ-training manual perfectly matching his current set were just too low.
Still, the experiment was not a total waste. At the very least, he gained a solid grasp of how this direction-bending force worked.
If he ever ran into cultists using God-Distorting Force again, he could counter them with ease.
Next, he started testing the other leg arts he had taken from the Horizon Building.
Before he could test for a few more days, new trouble arrived at his door.
"Sect Master! A battle challenge from the Lishan Sect!"
Inside the cabin, Wei He looked at the disciple kneeling on one knee. His brows furrowed.
The disciple offered a pure white letter with both hands. On the front, bold black ink read: To Sect Master Wei He of the Thousand Poisons Sect. From Yu Guangyan.
"Yu Guangyan... Hall Master of the Hundred Weapons Hall?" Wei He instantly recalled the name.
The true core of the Lishan Sect was not the faction of Yan Junshan. It was the Hundred Weapons Hall.
The Lishan Sect was famous across Taizhou for weapons mastery.
Yet almost none of the masters Wei He had met so far—including Yan Junshan and Yan Jiuhai—actually used weapons in battle.
Now, a real weapon master had finally stepped out.
Wei He took the letter, opened it, and read: Yu Guangyan of Lishan Sect Hundred Weapons Hall presents: On the first of May, at Fusu Mountain outside Juxia City, we settle our grudge in battle. After this fight, dead or alive, win or lose, all debts between the Lishan Sect and the Thousand Poisons Sect are cleared forever.
Do you dare to fight?
"Yu Guangyan... head disciple of the Sect Master, Master of the Hundred Weapons Hall... Trying to use me as a stepping stone? A bright young genius indeed. Ha." Wei He let out a cold laugh.
Rumor had it that this head disciple was handsome, upright, and insanely talented.
Nurtured by endless resources, he had reached the Organ Training realm at just twenty-eight years old.
He had built his fame by slaying renowned evil masters in the wilderness and single-handedly destroying the Black Bell Dock under the Katori Sect.
His talent was on par with someone like Wang Shaojun.
It seemed that in the eyes of these righteous heroes, Wei He had become nothing more than a perfect target to polish their blades.
Wei He felt a flash of annoyance.
Before crossing over to this world, he too had dreamed of being a righteous hero—wearing white robes, carrying a sword, looking sharp and noble.
It sounded cliché, but who wouldn't want to look cool?
Unfortunately...
He thought it through. If the Lishan Sect waged an all-out war right now, the current Heavenly Stamp Sect...
They stood no chance.
"What a nuisance..." Wei He sighed. The martial world was always like this. Endless grudges, flowing back and forth until one side was completely wiped out.
"I barely beat Yan Junshan. If this guy knows I killed Yan Junshan and still dares to challenge me, he must have a trump card."
He put the letter away and waved his hand for the disciple to leave.
His Silk-Twisting Force was still not fully mastered, and his strength had not made a major leap. Accepting this fight now was risky.
Wei He fell silent.
Yet if he refused the challenge, the Lishan Sect could use it as an excuse to launch a full attack.
They were one of the five major powers in the region, far stronger than the Heavenly Stamp Sect in their prime.
They had much more than just Yan Junshan.
Yan Junshan was only ranked third in Lishan. Above him were six Hall Masters, and above them sat the supreme leader, Yu Qingyan.
All of them were Organ Training masters. Yu Qingyan was even beyond that level—far stronger than the Heavenly Stamp Sect ever was.
Back in the day, Yu Qingyan was already powerful enough to crush Shangguan Ji before he was injured. Her current power was terrifying to imagine.
Wei He could beat Yan Junshan, but he was not stupid enough to overestimate himself.
If a full war broke out, the outcome for his Wanqing Sect was obvious.
"I can't take this fight," Wei He decided. He resolved to stay hidden in Black House Mountain.
Still, hiding forever was not a long-term fix. If Lishan sent a group of elite masters to raid their base, his sect would still be crushed.
...
Taizhou. Juxia City.
The headquarters of the Lishan Sect was the most striking landmark in the city.
It was a giant, dark-red tower twelve stories high.
At its peak hung wind chimes that constantly hummed in the breeze. Each floor below had a grand landscape painting on its outer walls.
Eleven floors held eleven paintings of famous mountains.
The tower was named the Twelve Mountain Hall. The eleven paintings plus the final floor representing Mount Li made twelve.
On the top floor, a giant woman nearly four meters tall, covered in heavy muscle and fat, sat cross-legged on the floor. A group of handsome men and pretty young boys surrounded her, feeding her wine and fruit.
More than a dozen young men tried their best to make her laugh.
Leaning against the wall behind her was a massive, eight-angled bronze hammer. The handle was three meters long, the hammer head was as big as a basin, and a heavy metal chain thick as an arm trailed from its end.
Just looking at the weapon gave off a suffocating weight.
"Sect Master, Young Master Yan requests an audience," a woman in a white robe reported from the doorway, kneeling on the floor.
The fat woman lazily grabbed a cluster of grapes, stuffed the entire thing into her mouth, chewed twice, and swallowed.
"Let him in." Her voice was surprisingly clear and sweet, like a ringing silver bell, despite her giant frame.
"Yes."
Moments later, a young man in white entered. He had sharp features, a confident air, and a long sword at his waist.
Seeing the decadent scene inside, he frowned slightly. But knowing his mother’s nature, he pushed through without a word.
"Mother." He bowed deep.
"What is it? Speak," the giant woman said lazily.
"It is about Xuanjing City. Yan Junshan is dead, and Jiao Ning fled back in shame. Our sect's name has taken a hit. I plan to use this Wei He as a target, kill him personally, and restore our prestige," Yu Guangyan said calmly.
"Are you sure you can win?" Sect Master Yu Qingyan pushed away a young man leaning against her. Her wild, yellowish eyes stared down at her son.
The massive size difference between them looked like a giant standing over a child.
"Seventy percent chance. That is enough. I am on the verge of a breakthrough. Using Wei He as a stepping stone will push me straight toward Mind Perception!" Yu Guangyan declared.
"Go then. But to guarantee success and claim Xuanjing at the same time, I will send the Six Halls with you." Yu Qingyan grinned.
"And I will go personally too. If I knew Shangguan Ji was just faking his strength last year, I would have wiped them out back then."
To her, the local wealthy families were nothing. The only minor threat was Wei Chizhong of the Red Flame Army.
Even then, she only worried about her disciples, not herself. No one else in her sect had her level of cultivation.
