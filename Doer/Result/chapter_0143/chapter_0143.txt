Near Mt. Zhongling, deep in the woods.
A gang of outer-circle goons from the Tianyin Sect stood guard, surrounding the area.
Zhou Yugui wore a pure white robe, holding a paper fan. He stood quietly, watching Old Inspector Xiahou Ji search for clues.
Xiahou Ji climbed up trees one moment and crawled on the ground the next, sniffing the dirt and checking every mark. He clearly had some special trick for tracking people.
After messing around in the woods for a long time, he finally looked up.
"It should be right here. Just as I thought. When someone hides their face to do dirty work, the first thing they want to do is wipe their tracks and blend back in. So this guy did the same thing. He came straight back to where he started so no one would suspect him," Xiahou Ji said seriously.
"You mean you found it?" Zhou Yugui asked, a spark of hope rising in his chest.
This time, Yan Junshan's only son was badly hurt. Yan Junshan hated Zhou Shun, the man who brought up the whole idea in the first place. If Zhou Shun hadn't pushed so hard for it, Yan Jiuhai wouldn't be half-dead right now.
Before this, Yan Jiuhai was a nobody. His name would never have reached his enemies' ears if not for this mess.
Zhou Shun and his son knew Yan Junshan was just taking his anger out on them, but they had to swallow it.
Looking back, the attacker that day seemed to hit Yan Jiuhai on purpose just to draw attention away from something else.
Zhou Shun and the Lishan Sect had both checked. Yan Jiuhai had never killed anyone who matched the attacker. The guy didn't even care about women, so that was easy to rule out.
Since Yan Junshan couldn't find anything on his end, he began to suspect the Tianyin Sect set the whole thing up. He thought someone had worn a disguise and made up an excuse just to ruin his son. The more he thought about it, the more real it felt, and the more he hated the Tianyin Sect.
Zhou Yugui thought the exact same thing. The timing was just too weird.
Why did Yan Jiuhai get attacked right now of all times? It was way too convenient.
Because of this, all the plans made by Zhou Yugui and his father were ruined. They got nothing out of it except the hatred of a dangerous man.
So this time, he had to find the truth and hunt down the real killer.
"Found it!"
Xiahou Ji suddenly yelled from a distance.
He seemed to have found a huge clue. He was squatting on the ground, holding a filthy, pitch-black cloak in his hand.
"Wait, is that... the clothes the guy wore that day?" Zhou Yugui froze for a second, then felt a wave of wild joy. He rushed over as fast as he could.
"Mr. Xiahou, what did you find?!" he asked quickly.
"Just as I thought..." Xiahou Ji didn't even turn his head. He stared closely at a mark on the ground.
"This is where the killer changed his clothes. He tossed them into the weeds right here without even bothering to hide them. He must have been in a huge rush to get away, so..."
Xiahou Ji stopped talking midway. He leaned down lower to look under the grass.
If he stopped hiding his tracks here and walked straight out to the main road, it'll be easy to check his footprints and guess his weight and size, Xiahou Ji thought to himself.
He pushed the grass aside and saw a faint footprint in the corner. He leaned in closer to look.
Suddenly, his eyes shrank!
"Mr. Xiahou?"
Zhou Yugui moved a step closer, looking at the crouching man with confusion. "Did you find something important?"
Xiahou Ji slowly stood up, still clutching the black cloak.
Pfft!
His whole body shook. He spat out a huge mouthful of blood and fell straight backward.
Zhou Yugui gasped and rushed forward to catch him.
Then he stopped dead in his tracks.
Xiahou Ji lay on the ground, twitching as blood poured out of his nose and mouth.
Zhou Yugui felt a cold chill run down his spine.
"Who's there?!"
He looked around wildly, but there was no sign of anyone.
"Young Master! Step back! It's poison!" a smart guard shouted, realizing what was happening.
Zhou Yugui snapped out of it and backed away fast.
Luckily, he was already a force user. The raw power inside his body flowed constantly, blocking out outer forces. Otherwise, he would have been hit too.
Looking at Xiahou Ji's still body on the ground, Zhou Yugui felt his head go numb.
How long had it been since the attack happened? How on earth did Xiahou Ji get poisoned after all this time?
Was the poison sitting on that cloak the whole time?
Xiahou Ji's life was cheap, but with him dead, the lead was completely gone.
The local law office was a complete joke now. Where was he supposed to find another master tracker on short notice?
Once time passed, the wind and rain would wash away every single trace left in the woods.
BOOM!
Furious, Zhou Yugui smashed his fist into a tree trunk. The bark shattered and flew everywhere.
"Young master, Xiahou Ji is dead."
A guard covered his mouth and nose with a wet cloth, using a long stick to poke the body. There was no breath left.
Zhou Yugui's face was dark. He never expected the killer to be this nasty.
After so much time, was this poison left recently, or was it set back then?
This killer was far too cruel and careful. If they ever ran into each other...
The thought made Zhou Yugui's skin crawl.
"Go bring the best poison expert in the city right now. I will tell my father about this myself."
This whole thing was way bigger than he thought. He had to hand it over to his father and take it slow...
Wei He slowly finished his training, pulling his inner force back into his body. He opened his eyes and looked at Old Man Jiuying, who was busy with some small glass dishes across the room.
"Any progress?" Wei He asked in a deep voice.
The room was packed with cages. There were all kinds of live creatures inside, from small bugs and tiny animals to larger wild pigs.
Wooden shelves hung on the walls, filled with small glass bottles. Simple names and code numbers were written on them in red ink.
Jiuying stood in front of a strange machine made of glass and metal. He carefully mixed a few drops of plant juice with a strange powder.
Pfft!
The powder burst into flame. A nasty, thick smoke filled the air before the mixture turned into a black, hard blob.
The fire died down as Jiuying put it out.
He sighed.
"Failed again. The trick you gave me helped us make some progress, but we still can't get the mix right."
"That's the hardest part," Wei He nodded.
"We worked out five ways to use poison over the past few days: Bait, Set, Plant, Spread, and Hide."
"We are good at the first four. It's the last one that's giving us trouble."
Jiuying nodded in agreement.
"Hiding means locking the poison deep inside your own force. But our force is tied to our own bodies. How can we lock a poison inside it without hurting ourselves, then release it when we attack? If it were weak poison, fine. But if it's the real deadly stuff, we can't take it either. Putting it in our force is just suicide."
"That's true..." Wei He agreed.
What they were trying to figure out was a path people had been hunting for ages: Poison Skills.
The old way of using poison was to force your body to build up a small shield against different toxins over time. That way, you could use poisons that wouldn't kill you, but would kill your enemies.
However, because the human body had its limits, those old poison skills were always weak. They couldn't even scratch a real martial master.
Wei He and Jiuying were trying a totally different path.
They wanted to fuse the poison directly with their inner force, without hurting their own flesh at all.
The old poison skills weakened you and cut your life short. After all, forcing your body to adapt to poison consumed your own life energy.
If Wei He and Jiuying pulled this off, they could wipe out all the self-damage while gaining a terrifying edge in battle.
"Forget it, no need to rush, kid," Jiuying said, putting down his tools.
"I caught a nice beast earlier. I'm planning to turn it into a Meat Farm. Want to come take a look?"
"A Meat Farm? Sure!" Wei He had never seen one in person.
He had heard about Meat Farms for years, but he had no clue what they actually were or how people made them. This was a great chance to learn.
"Follow me then," Jiuying nodded.
He walked over to a locked chest, pulled out his core beast item, and left the house with Wei He. They walked through the deep woods for about half an hour.
Soon, they stopped in front of a cave shaped like a gourd.
Jiuying went in first.
Wei He followed right behind him.
Without making a sound, both of them let out a tiny, invisible cloud of poison dust from their bodies, dusting the entrance of the cave.
The poison dust would stay active for twenty-four hours. As long as it didn't rain heavily, any living thing that walked in or out would get coated in it.
This was just one of their safety steps.
As they walked inside, they casually touched the cave walls or lowered their heads to talk, releasing even more fine poison dust into the air.
Different types of poison formed layers of hidden traps, stopping anyone from barging in uninvited.
The wind couldn't blow these powders away. Only a heavy rain could wash them clean. Otherwise, people just had to wait for the poison to rot away on its own.
A short moment later, Jiuying led Wei He into the deep end of the cave.
Deep among the dark rocks, a thin trickling stream made soft splashing sounds.
A giant male deer, three meters long and two meters tall, lay on the ground. It looked like it was resting with its eyes shut. Heavy chains and thick ropes were wrapped tightly all around its body.
Sensing the two men coming close, the giant deer snapped its eyes open. Its bloodshot eyes glared straight at Jiuying.
MOOO!!
It let out a deafening roar that made the whole cave shake and hum.
"This beast is called a Stone Deer," Jiuying said. "It's super fast. Normal martial artists can't even catch its shadow. Only those who train their leg skills to the limit have a chance to grab one. I had to use poison and set a trap to pull it off this time."
