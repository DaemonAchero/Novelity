The next morning.
About a hundred nautical miles from Yin Xu Island.
Waves crashed under the scorching sun. A long-haired figure dressed in black dashed across the water at extreme speed.
A faint, strange aroma drifted from his body. It was a special lure, made to drive True Beasts wild with instinct.
Splash!
Water erupted. A giant, squid-like sea beast burst from the waves. It had massive pincers on its head, stretched over ten meters long, and its tail was packed with suction-covered tentacles.
"A Thousand-Legged Hunter," the figure murmured. He flashed aside, dodging the beast’s strike, and landed lightly on the water. His face was cold and calm.
It was Wei He.
"According to the records, these beasts rank between All-True Three and All-True Six. A Hunter King can reach All-True Six, but normal ones are just All-True Three. The beast tide just retreated, leaving stragglers behind. Using the lure now is perfect."
Pleased, Wei He leaped into the air. Shifting his body against the pull of gravity, he drifted effortlessly through a forest of lunging tentacles.
He darted straight through a tiny gap and thrust a palm forward.
Thump.
His hand hit right between the beast's giant pincers—dead center on its head.
BOOM!
A wave of overwhelming, pure force burst outward as dark grey ripples.
The ten-meter monster shrieked in agony. Its body violently shrank and curled, twisting into a ball of flesh just two meters wide in seconds.
At the same time, a cool wave of mental energy rushed into the acupuncture points in Wei He's mind.
"Not bad. If I seal about fifty more beasts at this level, I should have enough energy to finish the fifth layer of the Xuan Lock Technique."
Wei He calculated his progress, dug out a silver star core from the beast, and tossed it into his pouch. Then he sped off toward deeper waters.
Monsters like the Thousand-Legged Hunter were kings of their own territories on the surface ocean. To find more All-True sea beasts, Wei He had to keep changing location.
In the past, regular fishermen could never catch or even see these creatures. The beasts would stir up massive waves, flip fishing boats, and devour the crew. They were a nightmare to deal with.
In a way, Wei He was clearing out monsters for the common people.
His feet slapped against the water as he rushed forward, quickly arriving at a new area. He slowed down, letting the lure's scent spread into the air.
Soon, a massive shadow loomed under the surface beneath his feet.
BOOM!
The ocean burst open. A giant black turtle with a massive shell shot out from the water, snapping its jagged, needle-filled jaws right at Wei He.
It didn't look like a normal turtle. It had eight jointed legs like a spider.
Before it could reach him, vision blurred for the beast as a heavy palm slammed into its forehead.
THUD!
The spider-turtle's defensive aura shattered instantly. Its body contracted violently, rolling up into a three-meter ball of flesh wrapped in cracked bits of shell.
Feeling another strong rush of mental energy, Wei He smiled.
He waved his hand, using the gravitational pull of his inner energy to rip the shell away from the meat.
Oddly enough, he noticed a gap between the turtle's flesh and its shell. It was a complete vacuum.
"No... not a vacuum. It's an empty space held together by inner energy..." Wei He looked closely and realized the truth.
"Wait... inner energy?"
A thought flashed through his mind.
With a wave of his hand, a silver star core flew out from the shell into his palm. He ignored the core, standing still on the water with a frown, thinking deeply about the idea that had just popped into his head.
"If inner energy can be used as a buffer zone, couldn't I use my gravitational pull to do the exact same thing?"
A stroke of inspiration struck him.
Inner energy buffers could still get contaminated, but gravitational pull was completely safe.
The first thing he thought of was his newly acquired martial technique: the Three Hearts Sutra.
With a thought, he drove his inner energy, spinning it rapidly according to the manual's instructions.
Soon, threads of energy began to form inside the right side of his chest, meticulously building a brand-new heart made entirely of energy.
This was the first step of the Three Hearts Sutra. It wasn't hard for him.
Constructing a heart wasn't difficult for a poison expert like Wei He, who had dissected countless living creatures. The tricky part was using inner energy to copy a real organ.
"Next, I need to pick a True Beast and take its heart to complete the second one."
Wei He looked down at the spider-turtle. "I'll use this one as an experiment."
The spider-turtle was rated between All-True Three and All-True Five. It looked like trash in front of Wei He, dying in a single hit, but that was only because he crushed it with sheer force. Under normal circumstances, without his sealing power, its tough skin and hard shell made it one of the hardest beasts to fight outside the Misty State.
Wei He pointed his finger. The flesh of the meatball split open, melting away into a bloody tunnel under his corrosive energy.
At the end of the tunnel, a blood-red heart was beating slowly.
Wei He closed his eyes, recalling the patterns and text from the stone tablet. The tablet had over ten illustrations and thousands of words, detailing step-by-step how to steal a True Beast's heart.
After thinking for a moment, Wei He reached out. Fine grey-black threads sprouted from his fingertips.
The threads cut through the blood vessels around the heart, lifted it gently, and sealed the bleeding with energy before pulling it out.
Soon, a blood-red heart as big as a human head was floating in front of him.
He pulled a small jar of black paste from his medicine pouch, poured it on his hands, and rubbed it evenly over the entire heart.
Then, he pressed the spider-turtle heart against the left side of his lower back.
With a swipe of his fingernail, he cut open his skin and pushed the entire heart inside his flesh.
With a head-sized heart stuffed into his back, he looked like a hunchback. But he didn't care. He closed his eyes and activated the energy heart in his chest, slowly pulling the mysterious essence out of the beast heart and transferring it into his energy heart.
Before long, the beast heart on his back shriveled up, shrinking until it completely dried out and died.
Wei He dug it out, tossed it away, and watched his skin heal up instantly. He licked his lips in satisfaction.
"I've only finished one-tenth of the build. That means I need at least nine more spider-turtles to complete the basic prep for the first heart."
"Once that's done, I can use a Blood-Coagulating Skin Fiend to wrap the heart and begin the first level of foundation building. Those fiends are extinct, but my Breaking Orb can simulate one."
Having mapped out his plan, Wei He erased the scent of blood from his body and resumed his search for more spider-turtles.
Seal the beast, cut out the heart, advance the technique, and repeat.
Over the next two hours, Wei He searched almost a hundred miles of ocean. He ignored anything below the All-True level, but even so, he managed to seal over thirty All-True sea beasts left behind by the tide.
He was still a bit short of the bottleneck for the fifth layer of the Xuan Lock Technique, but he had gathered all the spider-turtles he needed.
With the essence of ten turtle hearts harvested, Wei He returned to the main ship at Yin Xu Island, eager to use his Breaking Orb to force a breakthrough in the Three Hearts Sutra.
If it didn't work well, he could always use his safe skill-dispersing method to wipe it away.
Late at night.
Inside a luxury cabin on the Grand Mystery Sect's ship, docked at Yin Xu Island.
Wei He sat cross-legged on his bed, eyes closed in deep focus.
His skin was a dark grey. Veins surged across his body, popping out before disappearing again in a continuous cycle.
A quarter of an hour later, he opened his eyes.
"The prep is complete. Now I just need the Blood-Coagulating Skin Fiend as a primer to start the first level of foundation building."
"Anyone else would be stuck here forever without that skin fiend. They would have to purge the beast essence from their bodies to avoid corrupting their cultivation. But... I'm different."
Wei He focused on the Breaking Orb in his chest while visualizing the first level of the Three Hearts Sutra.
Break!
With a thought, the Breaking Orb shattered. A cool rush of air flowed rapidly through his body.
All of it gathered at the energy heart he had built.
The air solidified on the heart's surface, turning into a layer of writhing, dark-red matter that looked like fresh blood and meat.
If Wei He had ever seen a Blood-Coagulating Skin Fiend, he would have recognized this as its core flesh essence.
This layer of dark-red flesh turned his energy heart into a real, breathing heart of flesh and blood—a tiny version of the spider-turtle's heart.
As the heart took shape, new blood vessels grew from its edges, linking directly into his body's circulatory system.
It was like a heart bypass, except Wei He had added an entire extra heart to his body.
Thump-thump.
Thump-thump.
Thump-thump.
The two hearts synced up, beating in a shared rhythm.
Wei He let out a long breath and opened his eyes.
"It worked. The first level of the Three Hearts Sutra is complete."
The technique had three layers in total, building one new heart with each layer. Every heart served as the center for a whole new network of blood vessels and veins.
This was how the technique forcibly reshaped a cultivator's body structure. It didn't upgrade what was already there; it built an entirely new system from scratch.
Because of this, only All-True experts with extreme self-healing abilities were qualified to cultivate it. Normal people would be devoured by the beast flesh or killed by body rejection.
"The start of the first level is done. Now I just need to build out the new blood vessels around this heart."
Wei He felt a flash of excitement. Even in ancient times, almost no one could cultivate this art.
What kind of power boost would this second heart, built from ten spider-turtle hearts, give his body?
Theoretically, the Three Hearts Sutra only stole hearts to upgrade a person's physical base. But looking at its design, it was clearly an evil, demonic path. Stealing hearts was bound to alter the user.
According to the records, taking a beast's heart could grant the user some of its unique abilities. A True Beast's heart was the incubator for its star core, making it far more special than the heart of a normal animal. Stealing it was bound to bring mysterious benefits.
As for what those benefits were, that depended on luck.
