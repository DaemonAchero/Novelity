"I told you long ago, the people you hang around with are no good... Just look at them," Guan Fulin sighed.
"People change," Guan Yi shook her head. "They might not be doing great right now, but who knows what the future holds for them?"
"That's for the future to decide," Guan Fulin said, completely speechless.
He looked at the chaotic mess in front of him and felt even more helpless.
Those guests who actually had good connections left their gifts, turned around, and walked away immediately.
They simply could not stand being around such low-class people.
These freeloaders came to a birthday banquet just for food and drinks without bringing a single gift. They were clearly here to disgust everyone.
Only Guan Yi held her patience, welcoming every single one of them inside and treating them well.
"Cheng Shaojiu from the Cheng Family Escort presents ten gold pens!"
Finally, someone Guan Yi knew actually brought a gift to the door.
Guan Yi was instantly delighted. She quickly stood up to greet him.
At the main entrance of the restaurant, a somewhat old carriage slowly pulled to a stop. The curtain lifted, and a gentle-looking man with slightly graying hair stepped down.
Seeing Guan Yi greeting him at the door, the man smiled and cupped his hands.
"Sorry I'm late. Please don't mind me. It took me a lot of effort to find these gold pens on the way here."
The man had a weathered face and warm eyes. Compared to years ago, he looked much more mature.
"Brother Cheng, as long as you're here, it's fine. I was just wondering why no one from the Cheng family showed up yet. I was relying on your gift to save my pride today," Guan Yi said helplessly.
Cheng Shaojiu knew what she meant and felt just as helpless.
He used to be just like Guan Yi. But later, when his escort agency ran into deadly trouble, none of those so-called friends were willing to help him.
From that moment on, he understood that you must be careful about who you choose as friends.
Not all kindness brings a reward.
The two went inside the restaurant and sat at the main table on the second floor.
After chatting casually about recent events in the city, Guan Yi shifted the topic and brought up the letter she received earlier.
"Maybe an old friend was passing by and decided to check on you," Cheng Shaojiu replied.
Speaking of which, he met Guan Yi back when they were both in Feiye City.
Back then, Wei He stood up for the Cheng family. Guan Yi, who was watching Wei He closely, met Cheng Shaojiu because of that.
When Wei He was mentioned, Cheng Shaojiu shared a few funny stories.
"Back then, Little He's family was very poor, but he trained extremely hard. When others were eating and playing, he was training. When others were resting, he was training. No matter what, he always put martial arts first."
He laughed.
"Let me tell you, I went to his house once and ate with him. His second sister, Wei Ying, made a really delicious meat dish.
At that time, there was a terrible famine everywhere. Everyone was starving. Wei He hadn't made much progress in his training yet, and he couldn't go outside the city to hunt, so where could he get meat?
As I ate, I thought it tasted great, so I asked his sister where the meat came from. Guess what she said?"
"What did she say?" Guan Yi asked with a smile.
Stories about Cheng Shaojiu and Wei He were her favorite things to hear. Every time she met Brother Cheng, they would talk about these things.
"His sister pointed to a hole in the corner of the wall and told me that Little He needed to eat a lot for his daily training. So every day, she left a tiny bit of food at home to catch rats.
At first, there weren't many rats. But slowly, she learned the trick and used sweeter food to lure them in. Soon, she was catching more and more rats."
Guan Yi immediately burst out laughing.
Wei He was truly miserable back then.
Before he made a name for himself in martial arts, his family was so poor they could barely eat. It was the absolute rock bottom of his life.
Both of them sighed with emotion as they recalled the past.
Who could have guessed that Wei He would later reach such terrifying heights?
He not only saved Cheng Shaojiu, but he also saved the Guan brother and sister.
"It has been so many years now. I wonder how Little He is doing. And our other martial brothers..." Cheng Shaojiu felt a wave of nostalgia for the old days.
Back at the martial art school, all they had to worry about every day was training and having fun. Their families and master protected them from all danger, keeping them safe and carefree.
Compared to now, life back then was so much easier.
"I sent someone back to Feiye City once, but... the martial art school was gone. I heard all the old members had left," Guan Yi replied, feeling regretful.
"If Wei He is still alive, I'm sure he's doing well right now," she whispered softly.
"You certainly know him well." Suddenly, a voice came from the side.
At the table where the two were sitting, a person had somehow appeared out of nowhere.
The person wore a bamboo hat with a black veil over their face, along with common gray traveling clothes. With one hand, he picked up the wine jug and casually poured himself a drink.
"Who are you...!?" Guan Yi was startled. Before she could finish her question, the person slightly lifted his hat, revealing the face beneath it.
Even though it had been years since they last met, she recognized him at a single glance.
"Wei He!?" She was about to stand up with pure joy on her face.
However, Wei He quickly pressed a hand down on her.
"Stay calm. Don't let anyone know I was here."
Wei He instructed in a quiet tone. Meeting Guan Yi right now was not a good thing for her.
The Xuanmiao Sect had already broken off from the allied forces, and he had offended far too many people.
If he revealed himself publicly, everything would be fine while he was here, but after he left, it would bring immense trouble to the Guan family.
Beside them, Cheng Shaojiu was equally shaken. He hurriedly looked at Wei He.
When he saw the familiar face under the veil, he was so excited he almost shouted out loud.
However, he quickly suppressed the urge.
"Little He! You really are alive! And not just alive, I bet you are doing the best out of everyone who came from Feiye City!" Cheng Shaojiu kept his voice low, but he was still trembling with excitement.
"Brother Cheng." Wei He smiled and extended his hand, giving Cheng Shaojiu a firm, tight handshake.
After so many years, seeing his childhood friend again filled his heart with surging emotions.
When he was still in Feiye City, he was only sixteen years old when he started learning martial arts. Now, in the blink of an eye, he was already over thirty...
Time showed no mercy. Over ten years had passed in a flash.
Wei He looked over Cheng Shaojiu. Compared to their time in Feiye City, he had aged a lot. But because he practiced martial arts, he still looked decently preserved.
It was just that managing the escort agency had made him much more steady and grounded than before.
"Since you're here, don't leave just yet. Stay for a few days. After the feast ends, come over to my house and meet your sister-in-law and your goddaughter, Cheng He. As her godfather, you've barely let her see you since she was little."
Cheng Shaojiu said with excitement. His daughter, Cheng He, was offered to Wei He as a goddaughter since she was an infant. Now that they met, he naturally wanted to drag him back home to meet her.
"Same for my place! You saved my life and my third brother's life back then, and I never got to thank you properly. Now that we meet again, you must give me a chance to repay you!" Guan Yi laughed, her mood extremely bright.
Wei He looked at Guan Yi. The once pretty and delicate young lady of the Guan family had now matured significantly. Her looks and figure were like a fully ripe peach, deeply attractive.
Yet, those clear, watery eyes still held the same curiosity and purity from back then.
He laughed and shook his head: "I am traveling on business this time and happened to pass by, which is why I sent you a letter. I didn't expect to run into Brother Cheng as well."
He thought about it. Sensing the real realm was the same anywhere, so he might as well stay here. Once he got the Fruit of Life, he could go into closed-door cultivation right here to complete his breakthrough.
As for the second realm-sensing, his internal power had already broken through. He simply needed to stay in the super-sensing state outside and let the ghost wind blow over him for a month to complete the transformation of his force.
The three of them had a wonderful conversation. Wei He agreed to stay at the Cheng residence for a while, allowing Cheng Shaojiu and Guan Yi to act as proper hosts for their reunion.
After the banquet, Wei He offered his birthday gift. It was a little trinket he had used long ago, a Poison-Clearing Bead that could ward off many poisons.
To the current him, with the Zhenfa Technique protecting his body, he was not only immune to all poisons, but he could also coat his own force with deadly toxins.
However, since these toxins had little effect on true experts at the Real Person level, he rarely used them.
In truth, anyone below the Real Person level who came across the toxins inside the Zhenfa Technique would surely die.
The Poison-Clearing Bead was useless to Wei He, but it was extremely valuable to Guan Yi.
Right after, Wei He followed Cheng Shaojiu back to his residence to stay. Although Guan Yi invited him passionately as well, she was an unmarried woman after all. Letting a man stay at her house would ruin her reputation if rumors got out.
As for the three heads, Wei He chose not to show his face at all. He simply let Gong Huan take his place to host the public execution.
He hid inside the Cheng family residence, chatting with Cheng Shaojiu about the past every day, and occasionally bonding with Cheng He.
The teenage Cheng He had grown into a slim, lovely, and cute girl, but she possessed a fierce temper. All she cared about was swinging weapons and fighting.
Whenever she went out, she got into a small fight every three days and a big fight every five days.
Wei He spent his time entering the real realm for long periods to finish his second realm-sensing, while occasionally guiding Cheng He so she wouldn't cause huge trouble for her family outside.
Time flew by in a flash.
Before anyone realized it, over a month had passed.
The Xuanmiao Sect had already finished the public execution of the three heads, and after that, all personnel of the Xuanmiao Sect completely withdrew from the Yunzhou Prefecture.
When the top experts from the Wanfei Palace and the Wushi Sect arrived and found no one there, they had nothing to say.
They wanted to cause trouble, but the people of the Xuanmiao Sect were already gone, leaving them to vent their anger at empty air.
As for Wei He, after finishing his second realm-sensing, he found time to have Han Xiangqi lead him into the Han family treasury. There, he obtained part of the Fruit of Life and clues regarding the Three-Eyed Dove.
Originally, Wei He planned to return to the sect to officially cultivate the Five-Turn Dragon Breath as his trump card.
After all, the Xuanmiao Sect possessed complete resources. To cultivate secret techniques, returning to the main sect was naturally the best choice.
However, a sudden message sent from the Xuanmiao Sect forced him to give up his original plan.
"What?! Senior Sister isn't at the sect? She went to the Wu State??!!"
Inside a guest room of the Cheng residence.
Wei He suddenly stood up, staring at the person in front of him with a face full of shock and disbelief.
Standing before him was none other than Senior Sister Yao Wan, who had been recovering for a long time.
"Why would I lie to you? Senior Sister actually left the sect before you even departed. The person left behind was just a body double," Yao Wan sneered softly.
"Those scumbags thought we could only defend ourselves passively. Who would have guessed that Senior Sister would dare to strike first?"
"Then why are you, Senior Sister Yao..." Wei He was confused. If it was meant to be a secret, why tell him so suddenly now?
Wouldn't it be better to keep it a complete secret?
"Because the job is already done, of course," Yao Wan looked at him, feeling exasperated.
"Senior Sister went to assassinate the Emperor of Wu. Though she didn't fully succeed, she took the chance to sever one of his arms and heavily wound him.
Right now, the entire Wu military is driven insane.
Before, their massive army pressed against our borders to wipe out our Xuanmiao Sect, but now, Senior Sister immediately paid them back in full!"
Yao Wan looked radiant as she spoke.
Both the Great Yuan and Great Wu were nations built on martial power.
The Emperor of Wu was surrounded by countless experts, including two top-tier Grandmasters guarding him personally, alongside endless numbers of formidable martial artists. There were also massive star arrays protecting the palace layer by layer.
Under such desperate odds, Senior Sister Yuandu Zi actually managed to sever his arm and heavily injure him.
This showed just how terrifyingly brutal her strength was.
"Doing this, won't the Wu army put even more focus on our Xuanmiao Sect?" Wei He asked with a frown.
"They won't dare," Yao Wan laughed. "Two top Grandmasters, three layers of star arrays, and dozens of peak experts could not stop Senior Sister alone.
In the entire Great Wu, apart from National Teacher Moduo, no one else can give the Emperor of Wu a sense of safety. Therefore, he will definitely recall National Teacher Moduo back to the capital.
Without Moduo around, the Wu army loses its core strength at the top level. Combined with their long supply lines and heavy burdens, the war is about to enter a new stage."
"Besides, if the Wu army wants to drag us down with them, we are already prepared. The Xuanmiao Sect has long prepared sea ships and cleared out all surrounding hidden forces. Once a fight breaks out, with Senior Sister lurking in the shadows, who among the Wu army's high-ranking officials apart from Moduo would dare to sleep peacefully?
If not for the suppression of the star array back then, Senior Sister would have slaughtered everyone inside the Wu imperial palace!"
Yao Wan laughed out loud with pure satisfaction.
After laughing for a good while, she swallowed her saliva.
"Oh right, I almost forgot the main business. I came to tell you to return to the sect for relocation. It's not the previous Tianhai Island, but another place."
"Hmm? Relocation?" Wei He froze.
"Not quite a relocation. Our Xuanmiao Sect never had just a single site to begin with. This time, it's simply to guard against revenge from the Wu State, so we are completely retreating into the deep ocean," Yao Wan smiled. "It's just that this time, you must hone your cultivation properly back at the sect."
Relocating a whole sect required a massive amount of manpower, and encountering enemies along the way would bring endless trouble.
Because of this, all personnel outside had to be recalled. It was easy to foresee that during this relocation of the Xuanmiao Sect, all kinds of wicked monsters and hidden enemies would surely creep out.
