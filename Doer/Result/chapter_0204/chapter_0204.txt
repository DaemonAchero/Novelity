“The rumor is true,” Xie Dong smiled, continuing, “The last strain of dragon blood still exists. It was kept in the Dragon-Phoenix Hall. But when I left the capital, the Grand Commander had already taken it. No one knows where it is now.”
“Then what about the dragon blood in Dongzhou that the righteous and demonic sects are fighting over...?” Yuchi Yan asked, curious.
“Only a Master could take dragon blood from the Grand Commander. As for what’s really going on, people at our level wouldn’t know.” Xie Dong shook his head.
“Is dragon blood really that miraculous? Can it truly rebuild a person's body, boost power overnight, build the ultimate foundation, and open the path to the peak realm?” Yuchi Yan asked.
“It can. But only someone with a true dragon bone can use it. Anyone else dies instantly.” Xie Dong explained, then sighed. “Forget it. I hid here in Taizhou just to stay out of this mess.”
“True. Let’s not talk about it. Drink.” Yuchi Yan raised his glass.
Xie Dong politely declined, chatted a bit more, and left.
Yuchi Yan felt tired. Even as a Viscera Refining expert—one of the top fighters in Xuanjing City—dealing with the local factions took everything he had. Every word was a trap. Every statement had hidden motives.
Once the guests left, he rested, told the servants to clean up, and walked toward the inner yard.
He walked down the garden path, ready to sleep, when a soft voice called out from behind.
“Second Granduncle.”
He turned. It was Yuchi Qi. She looked troubled and pale.
“Little Seven? It’s late. Why aren’t you sleeping?”
“Second Granduncle, I have a problem,” Yuchi Qi begged softly. “Grandpa isn’t here, so I can only ask you.”
“Is it about your martial arts?”
“No... something else,” she shook her head, looking around nervously. Guards were everywhere, and snipers watched from the roofs. “Please follow me. It’s not safe to talk here.”
Yuchi Yan nodded. “Lead the way.”
As they neared her private yard, Yuchi Yan waved his hand, signaling his bodyguards to wait outside.
Three figures stepped back into the shadows: River Palmer, Seven Swords, and Mountain Bow. They were top guards assigned to him by Yuchi Zhong.
Outside, Mountain Bow grumbled, “Old Yan is going into his grandniece's yard this late? He should know better.”
“He isn't interested in women,” River Palmer said softly. “He has his reasons.”
“Reasons my ass!” Seven Swords growled, resting a massive sword on his shoulder. “If something feels wrong, grab her and beat the truth out. Wasting time!”
BANG!
A violent crash echoed from inside the yard, followed by the sound of rapid, deadly blows.
“Enemy!”
The three guards reacted instantly, sprinting toward the yard less than a hundred meters away.
Suddenly, a massive figure burst from the shadows, blocking their path.
“Assassin!” Mountain Bow yelled, leaping backward while drawing a massive bow.
The intruder was too fast. His speed surpassed even a Bone Refinement leg-specialist. Like a black hawk, he lunged straight for Mountain Bow’s throat.
The other two guards roared and attacked from behind.
Mountain Bow swung his heavy iron bow directly at the intruder’s face, trying to buy just one second for his brothers to strike.
The three guards had a killer trick that let them fight Viscera Refining masters. It was called the Sun Air Blade—a forbidden technique where they stabbed their own pressure points to unleash massive bone energy. For ten seconds, all three could power up to the Viscera Refining level.
Wei He felt the sudden explosion of power. His eyes narrowed.
Just before his finger hit Mountain Bow's iron bow, Wei He stopped dead in mid-air.
Defying gravity, his body dropped straight down, escaping the three-way trap. The moment his feet touched the ground, he activated his Flying Dragon Skill, triple-stacking his speed, and bolted.
“Stop him!” River Palmer yelled, throwing two short daggers packed with bone energy. They whistled through the air like missile strikes.
Wei He slammed his foot into the ground, blasting a small crater, and sidestepped the daggers.
THWACK! THWACK!
Two heavy arrows shot straight for the spots where Wei He was supposed to land. Mountain Bow had predicted his dodge.
Instantly, Wei He’s body pulled back like a charging rhino suddenly yanked in reverse by an invisible rope.
The arrows missed, hitting a stone wall behind him and blasting it into rubble.
“Yuchi Zhong’s top guards. Impressive,” Wei He muttered.
Like a giant bat, he spun in mid-air and dove at Seven Swords.
Seven Swords roared, swinging his massive blade, creating a wall of sharp wind.
He knew this assassin was a nightmare. The guy wasn't forcing a direct fight. He was just wasting time. Once their ten seconds were up, the backlash from the forbidden technique would kill them.
Worse, the assassin could ignore momentum, making his movements impossible to read.
Right before hitting the sword light, Wei He veered right, floating away into the night.
A crumpled paper note flew out from Yuchi Qi’s yard. Wei He caught it smoothly in mid-air.
“Second Granduncle!!” A girl’s scream echoed from the yard.
The three guards froze. They turned and rushed inside.
They found Yuchi Qi kneeling on the ground, crying beside a body.
It was Yuchi Yan.
His chest was completely blown open. Most of his internal organs were gone. The yard was wrecked.
Only thirty seconds had passed since the guards first heard the fight.
Thirty seconds to murder a Viscera Refining master.
“How...?” River Palmer whispered, stepping back in horror.
On the other side of the estate, Wei He unrolled the paper note while moving like a shadow.
It was the map to the secret vault.
