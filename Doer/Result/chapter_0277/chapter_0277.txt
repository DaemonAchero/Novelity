On Mount Yueqing, thick clouds wrapped around the forest.
Yan Jiuzhen held a tree branch, showing two disciples how to deal with blades using fist techniques.
As head of the Jiuzhen Hall, he had personal disciples who followed him everywhere. But he was busy, so he rarely had time to teach them.
"If someone thrusts a sword straight at your chest, use the technique called Mountain Twist," Yan Jiuzhen said, showing the moves. "This move turns real force into nothing. It deflects the sword and leaves room to attack or pull back. Whether you fight or defend is up to you. It works for both."
The two disciples watched without blinking, standing completely still.
"Brother Jiuzhen, it has been a long time since I saw you teach so seriously."
A thin old man in a loose robe walked out from the trees, carrying a jar of wine. His hair hung down loosely.
"Ah, Brother Qinghu," Yan Jiuzhen said, turning to face him and telling his disciples to practice on their own. "Why are you here in my yard instead of staying in your Qinghu Hall?"
The old man was Song Qinghu, a hall head at the same rank as him.
"I just finished some business down the mountain," Song Qinghu said with a smile. "On my way back, I heard news about Su Zhao from the Farmers faction. I heard you are in charge of catching him. Here, take this."
Song Qinghu tossed a tightly folded paper card across.
Yan Jiuzhen caught it, snapped the thin string with his inner force, and opened the note.
After reading a few lines, his brow tightened.
"Lu Yunbai failed," he said heavily.
"Lu Yunbai is an outer manager and a Viscera Refining master," Song Qinghu smiled. "He gathered a group to attack the Thousand Poisons Sect, but they were driven back. That sect has real strength."
"If you came to mock me, please leave," Yan Jiuzhen said bluntly. "If I lose my temper, things could get ugly."
Song Qinghu laughed. "I just find it interesting. I heard the sect master, Wei He, was once a outer member of our Mountain Return Fist Branch. When he returned from Yun Prefecture, he wanted to enter the inner court, but you rejected him because of his poor talent. Is that true?"
Yan Jiuzhen looked calm. "Yes, that is true."
"Now, Wei He is the top fighter of the Thousand Poisons Sect. His power is at least at the Viscera Refining level. Looking at his records, his movement skills are top tier. Even among Viscera Refining masters, he is rare. It seems you made a huge mistake back then, Brother Jiuzhen," Song Qinghu said, grinning.
"Even if he reached Viscera Refining, without breaking into the Sense Stage, everything is an illusion. Too many people are stuck at the Sense Gate in Taizhou," Yan Jiuzhen said coldly. "He was lucky to get this far, but without entering the Sense Stage, his success is just a reflection in water. It will disappear sooner or later. Besides, the Sense Stage takes no luck."
"What do you plan to do?" Song Qinghu asked.
"Since the Thousand Poisons Sect rejected our offer, we will destroy them," Yan Jiuzhen said softly.
"Wei He is the only disciple sent by your senior brother. Aren't you afraid..."
"I gave him a chance," Yan Jiuzhen cut him off, turning to look at the sea of clouds in the distance. "Since I surpassed the Viscera Refining stage, I learned that everything outside of true strength is fake. Down the mountain, danger is everywhere. Only by climbing higher can we find a way to survive."
"Cold and ruthless," Song Qinghu said. "When will you deal with them?"
"Tomorrow. The place is easy to defend and hard to attack, so bringing too many people is useless. Lu Yunbai and I will go together, take down Wei He and Su Zhao, and return."
"I am curious too. Mind if I tag along?" Song Qinghu smiled.
"Suit yourself. With you along, it will be safer. We will strike fast like thunder and crush them before the Anti-Nest Alliance can react. Once they are gone, we return immediately," Yan Jiuzhen replied.
"I am interested in their poison-locking methods. I want to bring some back to study," Song Qinghu said.
"Fine. Aside from that, they have dozens of poison formulas. You can have them all. I only want the records from the Tianya Tower," Yan Jiuzhen said.
"Good, then we go together tomorrow," Song Qinghu laughed, leaped high, and drifted away into the distance.
In a quiet town in Taizhou.
A two-story wooden boat drifted along the White River.
The boat was painted red with silver patterns and carved with flowers and leaves. Yellow lanterns glowed dimly under the night sky.
You Fu sat on the upper deck, holding a wine jar in one hand, drinking silently.
He looked out at the water. The curved moon reflected on the ripples, and fish swam near the surface.
"Master, it is late. Why not rest?" Mo Shiyan walked out from the cabin behind him and placed a light blanket over his shoulders.
"No need," You Fu sighed softly. "I wonder if Qier will blame me after this."
"Zhenqi respects you the most," Mo Shiyan smiled. "Even if she finds out, she will only complain a bit. Besides, you are only pressing Wei He, not doing anything evil."
"Pressing him?" You Fu took another drink. "I do not plan to save him right away."
Under the moonlight, Mo Shiyan's smile froze.
"Master, you...?"
"You think I gave Su Zhao to Wei He so he would feel forced to protect him, using Wushi Sect to push him into joining us, and then I would save him immediately, right?" You Fu said calmly.
"Is that not your plan, Master?" Mo Shiyan asked back.
"It was," You Fu nodded. "But without feeling true pain, a man will never learn."
He stood up. His huge body still looked like a solid iron statue under the thin blanket, full of pressure.
"When he has no backer, no strength to resist, and makes an enemy out of Wushi Sect, my help will carry true value."
"Aren't you afraid Wei He will hate you?" Mo Shiyan was confused.
"I can find many excuses, and he has no choice. When the time comes, I am the only one he can beg for help. A little bit of anger does not matter." You Fu drank the last drop, crushed the jar into a ball, and threw it into the river. He turned and walked inside.
Mo Shiyan remembered her own past.
To sharpen her skills, You Fu had thrown her into a brothel. Her power was sealed, leaving her with no way to fight back or escape. She had to use her mind to survive every day. If she lost her purity, she would lose her chance to return and stay a courtesan forever.
The same happened to Zhenqi. You Fu saw her talent, yet he let people hunt her for thousands of miles across several years. Only after she proved her strength and mind did he accept her as a disciple.
Compared to that, this trial for the Thousand Poisons Sect was nothing.
Still...
Mo Shiyan recalled the look on Wei He's face.
'Would a man that proud really bow down and beg for help as Master expects?' 'What if he does not ask for help... what then?' A cold feeling ran down her spine. She already knew the answer. If Wei He did not ask, You Fu would never lift a finger.
He was cruel enough to do it.
There was a reason people called them the Demon Faction.
Night fell.
Wei He stood on top of the Tianya Tower, the highest point of the Thousand Poisons Sect.
Wan Qingqing stood behind him with worry on her face, holding a bamboo flute. She had wanted to play a song to ease her mind, but what Wei He told her ruined the mood.
The two stood in the cold wind under the crescent moon, just like back in the early days at Heavenly Seal Sect, when they were still normal martial siblings.
"Do you really want to disband the sect?" Wan Qingqing still found it hard to believe.
There were other ways to solve this trouble, like handing Su Zhao over. A single favor was not worth paying such a high price. You Fu should understand that.
Why did they have to jump straight to disbanding the sect?
"It is not really disbanding, just leaving this place and hiding," Wei He sighed. "The Thousand Poisons City draws too much attention now."
He saw through You Fu's goal, and he knew why he ended up in this situation.
It was all because he refused to pick a side.
The Taian Army, the Demon Faction, and Wushi Sect were the three big forces in Taizhou. The Taian Army and the Demon Faction had to team up to stand against Wushi Sect.
The Thousand Poisons Sect was the only faction that stayed neutral.
Because of this, the matter with Su Zhao was bound to happen sooner or later.
"Su Zhao looks like the core of this danger, but he is just a spark," Wei He said quietly. "A tall tree catches the wind. Our neutral position forces the other factions to clear things up early."
Wan Qingqing thought it through and understood.
"You mean You Fu did this on purpose?"
"Intentional or not, it does not matter now. What matters is that he is forcing me to pick a side."
Wei He stood up, lifted Wan Qingqing, and placed her on his shoulder.
"So I will handle the current crisis first, then change our names and hide. The wealth and resources we saved will last us a long time."
Wan Qingqing went silent. Over the years, she had grown used to life in the Blackwood Mountains. A sudden change was hard to accept, but she knew there was no other way.
"When do we leave?"
"Tonight. The situation is dangerous, and staying in Thousand Poisons City will bring trouble," Wei He said softly. "In a moment, you and Xiao An leave first."
"Where to?"
"Jinzhou. Someone will lead you there. Do not worry about the rest. Most of our resources and money will be moved to the hidden base deep in the mountains. That is where Jiu Ying and I tested medicines before. I expanded it, so it is safe and hidden.
The rest of our people will split up, change their faces, and scatter across different provinces. We will stay in touch using insect incense."
