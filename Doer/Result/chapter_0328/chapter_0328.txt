"If you're sure you want this trait, we can look for a stronger individual among the Face-Birds. Their traits should be better than this one," Chang Xuezhong suggested.
"Alright. Thanks for the help, Senior Brother," Wei He nodded.
The two walked around the lake, but no new birds appeared. They decided to stay in a small village by the shore, waiting for another chance to hunt.
Two days later.
They headed deeper toward the center of the lake.
The vast lake was completely frozen, covered in white. Snow blew through the cold air. Wearing straw raincoats, they walked step by step across the ice.
Every now and then, they stopped. Wei He threw a few punches into the empty air, then stooped down to pick up invisible items before moving on. To regular people, a Grandmaster hunting looks like a madman talking to ghosts. Regular people can't see True Beasts, but they can see Grandmasters.
To Chang Xuezhong, however, Wei He was leaving a trail of beast corpses behind, heading straight for the center of the lake where the ghost wind was strongest.
Dozens of Face-Birds circled above the ice, watching Wei He like hawks. These creatures had gray-brown feathers, human heads, and bird bodies. Their cries were harsh and ugly, like quacking ducks. But they dived fast, had incredible stamina, and packed heavy force. Their only weakness was low defense—their power was roughly at the First Perception level.
Wei He was patient. He possessed endless stamina and moved forward step by step, staying totally grounded.
"There should be a whole flock here, including a leader," Chang Xuezhong said in a low voice. "Junior Brother, if you kill the leader, you'll get the strongest trait."
"Understood," Wei He nodded. He looked around, but saw only white snow. Yet his super-sensory left hand felt sharp sound waves vibrating against his skin. Sound was just vibration, so his enhanced hand picked it up easily.
Soon, they reached the center of the lake.
There was a massive ice hole, likely cut by local villagers for fishing. To normal people, this place was safe. To Grandmasters, it was a nest of hidden beasts.
"This is it," Chang Xuezhong said, staring at the huge hole. "Junior Brother, move closer and touch the edge of the hole."
Wei He did as he was told. He crept forward and used his left hand to feel around. Soon, his hand hit a hard column of ice right at the edge of the empty hole.
Wei He opened his eyes wide. He saw nothing in front of him except a wide ice hole. But his left hand clearly felt a thick pillar of ice, a meter wide, blocking his path.
This weird gap between his eyes and touch made his mind go blank for a second. But he quickly pulled himself together.
Cold wind howled. Snow danced in the air.
Suddenly, a shout came from behind.
"Junior Brother, the leader is right on top of that ice pillar!!"
Wei He’s heart chilled. He swung a fist upward with brutal force.
His massive physical power, backed by Return Force, slammed into something heavy.
BOOM!
A blast of cold wind blew across the ice. The ice beneath his feet cracked. He quickly leaped to the left.
From that single strike, he gauged the creature's strength. It was close to the power he was currently showing. Aside from high stamina and deep force, these birds didn't have much else going for them.
In the real world, a massive bird—two meters tall with a five-meter wingspan—was swooping down toward Wei He.
Wei He dodged the sharp claws by a hair, then whipped his left hand out, chopping the bird's leg. The monster groaned in pain and staggered back into the air.
"What a tough beast!" Chang Xuezhong knew how hard Wei He could hit. Even Double-Headed Turtles, famous for defense, couldn't take two of his punches. Yet this leader took a full blow and could still fly. Its body was as tough as someone with Second Perception defense.
QUACK!
Another harsh cry echoed from the distance.
A second giant bird, just as large, dived straight for Wei He.
"Not good! The leaders come in a pair!" Chang Xuezhong's face changed. "Junior Brother, I'll lure the second one away. Finish yours fast!"
Leaving those words behind, he charged at the second bird. That second leader was even larger, with a bright red crest on its head, looking far more vicious.
"Alright!" Wei He called back.
Without his Senior Brother watching, he could finally unleash his full power. Holding back all the time was exhausting.
However, neither of them had strong detection skills, so neither noticed something watching them from the snowstorm far away.
It was a giant bird that looked like a massive eagle. It stood four meters tall with a wingspan over ten meters. Its wings looked like they were forged from steel, sharp and hard. Its eyes gleamed with human-like cunning as it watched Wei He from a distance.
This beast was called a Wild Eagle, the natural predator of Face-Birds. It fed on them. Seeing one leader drawn away and the other locked in a fight with Wei He, the Wild Eagle’s sharp mind instantly realized this was a golden chance.
It just had to wait until both sides beat each other bloody, then swoop in to finish them both.
According to the sect's records, a Wild Eagle was a high-tier beast that required Third Perception to hunt. Since Second Perception was the Realm of Transformation, this eagle was above that level. Combined with its flight, it was extremely lethal.
Seeing its chance, the eagle flexed its wings.
SWOOSH!
The massive black shadow rose into the sky, gliding like a huge bomber plane. Energy surged through its steel feathers as it dived straight toward Wei He's back.
SHING!!
A flash of light sliced through the storm.
The giant eagle's head slid off its neck, spurting dark blood. Its huge body shattered without a sound, turning into black smoke and fading away.
Yao Wan crouched on the ice, slowly putting her sword back into its sheath. She stood up and looked back at the fading smoke.
"What a pain..."
Despite the hassle, she looked at Wei He in the distance and felt her efforts were worth it. If Wei He grew up safely, he would become a vital pillar for the sect.
Faint pride warmed her heart. She flashed forward, caught a falling Star Stone out of midair, and vanished.
On the lake, Wei He shifted his body to the left. Covered in Return Force, his hands locked onto the bird leader's legs like iron clamps.
Immense force burst from his body, pinning the monster in midair. No matter how hard it thrashed, it couldn't break free.
Wei He glanced around. Seeing no sign of his Senior Brother, his eyes darkened.
RIP!
With a brutal jerk of his arms, he tore the giant bird leader completely in half.
The sharp screech cut off instantly. A rain of blood splashed across the white ice.
"I really need to unlock my eye sense soon. Hunting blindly like this is a joke..." Wei He sighed.
Acting out a hard fight with a dumb bird was embarrassing. Screaming out loud while putting in zero effort felt terrible.
He tossed the bird's remains aside and stepped forward to check for the Star Stone. Suddenly, he snapped his head back.
Behind him, a man and a woman holding short knives were creeping up. They stared at him in utter horror.
Wei He looked at their terrified eyes, then at the torn corpse on the ice. His gaze shifted.
"You... can see this?"
The man and woman froze. Cold sweat poured down their backs.
They were rogue cultivators out to hunt Face-Birds together, hailing from outside Haizhou. Since Face-Birds were relatively weak, they were easy targets.
They had seen Wei He struggling against the bird and planned to sneak up, kill the monster, and rob the man. But the moment they got close... they watched this "struggling" man casually rip the beast in two with his bare hands.
Their souls nearly left their bodies. Trembling under Wei He’s cold gaze, they forced a painful smile.
"Brother..."
Before they could finish, Wei He leaped forward like a bolt of lightning.
"Blame yourselves for seeing what you shouldn't have."
His cold voice struck their ears.
A distance of ten meters meant nothing to him. With his raw physical strength combined with Raincover Force, his speed was terrifying.
One step. One leap. One strike.
He appeared right in front of them.
BOOM!!
He slammed a hand onto each of their heads, smashing them down. The ice shattered, creating two massive holes.
The two outer-state hunters vanished beneath the dark water.
Wei He quickly absorbed the bird leader's trait, picked up the six ounces of Star Stone, and dumped the bird meat into the ice hole. Then, he sat down on the ice, panting heavily as if he had drained all his energy.
Killing those two was like stepping on bugs. Even the bird leader was helpless against him. This made him wonder just how strong he really was.
To win, you must know yourself and your enemy. He lived by this rule. If he didn't know his own limits, he might run into a real monster and realize too late that he couldn't win.
'Now that my trait is set, I need to hunt alone next time and push my limits.' Moments later, a limping figure appeared on the lake. It was his Senior Brother, Chang Xuezhong.
Having barely shaken off the second bird leader, Chang Xuezhong felt a wave of relief when he saw Wei He sitting safely in the distance. He waved from afar, thinking to himself how lucky they were that nothing went wrong while he was away. If his junior brother died on his first hunt, he wouldn't be able to face Senior Sister Yao.
"Thank goodness! Did you succeed? Are you alright?" Chang Xuezhong exhaled deeply as he limped closer.
"I did it. Thank you for protecting me, Senior Brother," Wei He stood up and bowed sincerely.
If he hadn't wrapped this up, being watched every second would have driven him crazy.
"Good, good..." Chang Xuezhong sighed. If this went on any longer, his own bones would break. This kid kept picking more dangerous spots every time.
"Once your trait is locked in, go back and cultivate hard. You can come out to hunt alone later to stay sharp," Chang Xuezhong added. "The world is getting messy. Our Daoist sects will eventually clash with the Great Wu's True Blood. Getting used to real blood and death early will save your life."
Wei He paused. "Did you hear something, Senior Brother?"
If those words came from Senior Sister Yuanduzi, it would be normal. But coming from a simple man like Chang Xuezhong, it meant trouble was brewing.]
