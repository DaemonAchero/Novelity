The war drums beat like thunder.
A dark army covered the land like a swarm of ants. They rushed forward toward the white walls of Bianzhou like a flood.
Ladders slammed against the high walls. Vanguard soldiers climbed up fast, moving like apes, and fought onto the top of the wall.
"Li Tong of Bianzhou was born with rare blood," a strategist said. "He is very strong. He is one of the few generals in the Yuan State who knows both warfare and governing."
"Too bad he came from a rogue cult. He works hard, but he lacks capable people around him. A single tree cannot hold up a house."
A black flag moved in the strong wind. It showed a large character for "Wu".
Under the flag stood a square formation of heavy cavalry. At the very front was a huge general built like a small mountain.
At his feet sat two iron balls that looked like meteorites. A heavy chain as thick as an arm linked the two weapons. The rest of the chain wrapped around his big body.
He did not ride a horse, but he was taller than all the riders around him.
This man was Tang Yan, a top general of the Wu State.
His face was hidden inside a thick black helmet. He watched the siege in front of him while listening to his strategist.
"Xiangcheng is a vital city for Bianzhou," Tang Yan said. "Li Tong came here himself to guard it. If we break through, how should we handle him?"
The strategist was an old man with white hair. He held a feather fan and smiled. "General, why not give him a chance when he runs out of strength? We can try to make him surrender."
"You mean a single combat challenge?" Tang Yan asked.
"Yes," the old man nodded. "Li Tong is surrounded. He lost many men in past battles and suffered heavy damage. If you give him a chance and promise to pull back our troops if he wins, he will take the bait."
"Also, Li Tong is the spirit of his army. His men trust him completely. If you defeat him in front of everyone, his army will collapse even if he refuses to surrender. That will lower our losses when we take the city."
"Third, Li Tong knows he has no future left. If you promise to treat him and his men well, then..." The strategist stopped talking, but his point was clear.
"You are right," Tang Yan said. "This is a good time to recruit him. But the State Master ordered us to take all resources from the Yuan State. All soldiers and warriors must become slaves. Even if I take Li Tong in, I cannot treat him too well."
The Wu State had no room for wild martial artists. Their ruler controlled everything, and their generals crushed all enemies.
Their troops trained together for killing. Their power was far beyond the weak Yuan State. They would never allow rogue martial artists to waste valuable resources.
"That is easy," the strategist laughed. "First, make him feel safe. Once we use him to conquer the Yuan State, we will have plenty of land and resources to give him."
...
Night fell slowly.
The people of Haibai Town were set free. They went home, packed their things, and fled toward nearby towns.
The attack by the White Skulls scared away their last hope. They knew they would die if they stayed.
Haibai Town became a complete dead zone.
Wei He left the town with the Song brothers, feeling unsure about the situation. The White Skull bandits left with him.
Since he spent his poison energy to control these bandits, he wanted to get full value from them.
He was no longer the leader of the Wan Du Sect back in the wild mountains. Back then, poison plants grew everywhere in the Black House Mountains, so he had endless resources. Now, making poison was not easy.
Poison was not magic. It took a certain amount to drop a warrior.
Wei He used his own inner poison to replace half the cost, but his poison force needed outside materials. He could not make pure poison out of nothing.
The poison in his energy came from outside sources he put into his body. Every time he used some, it got weaker.
To make use of these bandits, Wei He ordered them to spread out across the empty plains.
They would act as hidden informants to watch the area.
This place sat between the Wu army and the rebel forces. Having these men controlled by his core disciples gave him free labor.
Best of all, they cost nothing, and it did not matter if they died.
After getting the Picture of the Bright Moon and Whale, Wei He made someone find another small border town nearby.
There, he taught Song Shixiong a full basic martial art from the Tianyin Sect called the Heart Lock Seal.
When mastered, this technique produced its own force and could reach the Third Blood level.
This basic force was fully compatible with all nine core techniques of the Tianyin Nine Attacks.
It was the best basic skill in the Tianyin Sect. It used Taoist ideas, had little violent energy, and was perfect for building a solid base.
Since Wei He was teaching them real martial arts, it meant the painting was real.
Song Shixiong mixed a liquid and wiped it across the surface. The true painting appeared. Wei He saw at a single glance that something extra was hidden inside.
He could not tell what it was right away, but when he looked at the picture, the blood bag of his Whale Flood Technique twitched inside his body. It felt like a spark touched it.
This proved the picture was the real thing he had hunted for years. It was the key to breaking through to the third level.
The secret on the picture was the key to the Whale Breath State.
Wei He stayed in the town for half a month. He taught the Song brothers the full Heart Lock Seal, gave them some money, and left quietly with the picture.
Other followers would take the brothers to a safe place later.
With the picture in hand, Wei He went home and trained day and night.
Time passed quickly, and winter was ending.
Inside Weifang City.
White paper money was tossed in front of a large house. Priests inside chanted scriptures to help the dead pass on.
In the yard, a group of people wore black cloth on their arms. They lined up quietly to pay their respects in the main room.
A realistic picture of Jiu Ying hung in the middle of the room. An incense burner and food sat on the table before it.
Yuan Bao knelt on the side with a blank face. Every time a guest stepped forward, he bowed back like a wooden doll.
Soon, a strong young man walked into the house.
His skin was fair and clean. His face looked ordinary, but he had a strong air around him.
His steps were steady and light, showing he was a trained warrior.
Many people present knew martial arts, so no one paid much attention to him.
The man wore a clean white robe and boots made from wolf skin. Two large men followed right behind him.
This was Wei He, who came right after hearing the news.
He looked at the picture of Jiu Ying hanging high on the wall. He felt sad and sighed in his heart.
The old man held on for so many years, but this time it was real.
When his time came, he just passed away.
What surprised Wei He was Jiu Ying's social circle. The old man did not have many friends in life. Yet many strangers stood in the room alongside the members of the Wan Du Sect.
There were merchants, farmers, blacksmiths, doctors, and even military officers.
People came from all walks of life.
They all came for the same reason: Jiu Ying had saved their lives in the past.
Whether that was true or not did not matter.
Wei He did not care to look into it.
When he walked in, he saw Lin Lin, the young girl from next door, talking softly to a few old men.
The old men treated her with care, like real family. They clearly knew each other well.
"Uncle Jiu, you had a good end," Wei He said softly to the picture. "So many people came to see you off, and you have a good student to pass on your craft. Take care of yourself down there."
To outsiders, he was just a retired military officer who was close to Jiu Ying. They played chess together, so he kept his words simple.
Only Wei He knew his feelings were real.
When he stepped out of the room, he saw a middle-aged woman in the yard looking at him.
She had a strong and commanding presence, like someone used to high power. She nodded slightly at Wei He with a friendly look.
Wei He glanced at the three guards beside her. At this distance, he could hear their strong heartbeats, heavy breathing, and rich blood flow.
All three were at least Martial Masters.
Their physical bodies alone showed their high level.
When a warrior trained inner force, their body grew stronger too.
The change was small, but Wei He had seen enough to spot the signs of each stage.
Of course, this only worked for standard techniques. He could not tell if someone used a rare or hidden energy type.
Wei He just nodded back to her friendly look.
He did not want to get involved with the power behind Jiu Ying. Those people hid deeper than he did, and their work was far more dangerous.
He was just a regular man who wanted a peaceful life. He did not want any trouble.
His power was stuck at the Organ Refining level. He needed plenty of free time to train, strengthen his organs a second time, and prepare for the next step.
He wanted no part in their business, but the other side thought differently.
"Are you Master Chen?" A strong middle-aged man stepped forward. He looked like a leader and kept his eyes fixed on Wei He. "Can we talk in private?"
"Sorry, I am in a hurry," Wei He said calmly. "Also, I am not interested in your business. Please do not bother me."
He made his point clear.
These people lost their contact when Jiu Ying died, so they wanted to use Wei He to reconnect the line.
Wei He had no wish to join their mess.
The man choked on his words and could say nothing. He watched Wei He turn around and walk away with his men.
Wei He got into his carriage outside and told the driver to go.
He rubbed the back of his left hand gently.
"Next, I need to understand the Whale Breath State and fully unlock the super sense in my left hand."
Super sense could be very dangerous. He planned to break through the Whale Flood Technique first before trying it.
