Three days later.
Near the Parash Archipelago, a single small wooden boat moved slowly against the current. It pushed through the waves, heading toward the scattered islands on the outermost edge of the chain.
In the endless blue ocean, the white boat sliced through the water like a fish. It had no sails, yet it shifted direction on its own and sped toward the outer islands.
Wei He stood in the boat with his hands behind his back, looking into the distance.
Far out on the water, between the white foaming waves, armored ships were slowly passing by.
"I need to find that small island first, settle down, and build a shelter. I can hunt star cores in my spare time and use the star array to cultivate safely when I rest. Two birds with one stone."
Most importantly, this was where Wei He had tracked down clues about the Dragon Lock Wood.
Dragon Lock Wood had the effect of soothing the energy of True Beasts, but it was almost useless to humans. Because of that, even though it was rare, it was not valuable. Nobody bothered to harvest and sell it.
Wei He had used the strength of his Wandu Sect to contact a local rogue group in the Parash Archipelago. He got the clue about the Dragon Lock Wood from one of their Grandmasters.
To his surprise, the exact location was unknown. There was only a rough general area. On local maps, that entire zone was marked as a high-risk region.
No wonder the Lou Family and the Wandu Sect could not find it even after searching for years.
If someone went to a high-risk area without enough strength, they would die. The Wandu Sect's martial power was simply not enough. As for the Lou Family, they were stronger, but they would never send their Grandmasters to risk their lives for him.
Wei He pulled back his thoughts and looked at the island ahead. It was now the size of an egg.
The island looked jagged and dark, like a piece of moldy black cake dotted with faint patches of dark green.
Before he even reached the shore, Wei He caught a strange scent in the air. It smelled like dry, rotten meat.
The small boat shot straight toward the edge of the island, leaving a pale white wake behind it.
"Hey!" A voice called out from a large ship on his right.
Wei He turned his head. The voice came from a wide black ship built with round mushroom-like structures on its deck.
The speaker was a chubby young boy standing by the railing.
"Brother! Ansha Island is entering its Boiling Phase! Going there now is dangerous!" The boy shouted out of kindness.
Wei He noticed two middle-aged noblewomen walking up to the railing. Disturbed by the noise, they began to scold the boy in low voices.
The boy was only in his teens. He broke free from a woman's grasp, looking annoyed.
'It must be a pleasure cruise, with his family protecting him so he can see the world,' Wei He thought.
The ship was huge, twice the size of an ordinary boat. It was wide, well-guarded on both sides, and had movable cannon mounts on the deck. A ship like this was not something a normal family could afford.
Seeing the boy getting scolded, Wei He smiled slightly and used sound transmission from a distance.
"Thanks for the warning. I know what I'm doing."
The Boiling Phase was a term used by locals to describe the patterns of sea beast attacks. They had learned that every island cycled between a Boiling Phase and a Dormant Phase.
During the Boiling Phase, sea beast attacks were three times more frequent and intense than usual. During the Dormant Phase, attacks dropped to less than half the normal rate.
Throughout the year, the timing of the Boiling Phase across the Parash Archipelago was completely unpredictable. Locals could only spot it by observing changes in the beasts' behavior. Certain signs appeared right before an outbreak, which helped people figure out the cycle.
After sending his reply, Wei He quickly left the large ship far behind. His small boat accelerated in a straight line without stopping.
Soon, he reached a shore filled with jagged rocks.
He leaped forward, turned around, and lifted the white boat with one hand. Walking across the waves, he charged onto the beach.
The white boat weighed several hundred pounds, but it felt like straw in his hand. His grip did not even crack or twist the wood from the pressure.
Traveling dozens of meters took only a few splashes of water. Wei He landed on the shore with the boat resting in his hand.
The sun was setting, and twilight was falling.
This outer island was completely empty. There were no signs of human life and very few footprints on the beach, only countless tiny crab holes.
"Fewer people means more True Beasts. It's dangerous, but it also means more star stones. I'll find a hidden spot to rest first."
Holding the boat, Wei He tapped his toes on the ground and floated lightly toward the island's forest.
The forest was dead silent. There were no insect sounds, no animals, and no birds. It felt as if nothing lived here.
Yet, Wei He sensed that something was off.
He quickly found an empty cave, tossed the boat inside, and dragged over several large rocks and logs to block the entrance. He left only a tiny crack for air.
Then, he gathered dry firewood and used a flint to start a fire. That was enough to set up camp.
He sat alone inside the cave.
A fire burned in front of him, and a cool breeze blew softly through the gap in the rocks.
The sky visible through the crack changed from pale yellow to golden red, then to a dim crimson, before finally turning completely dark.
Wei He watched the fire. The dry twigs and branches turned yellow, then black under the heat, before turning to ash.
Crackling sounds echoed from the fire.
Snap.
A thick log supporting the pile burned through and collapsed.
Wei He reached his hand into the flames, grabbed the hot wood, and adjusted its angle. He squeezed it to check its hardness; it was already burnt through.
Pulling his hand back, he looked at his black, soot-stained fingers.
"Without realizing it, I've reached this level... Heat that normal people fear like a tiger feels no different from normal things under my protective force."
Wei He felt a touch of emotion.
He pulled out dried meat, dried vegetables, and dried fruit from his bag. He set up an iron pot, poured in some water, and began to boil soup.
Before cooking, he pulled out the star array given to him by his senior sister, Yuanduzi: the Heavy Moon Heavenly Wolf Array.
The array stone was gray, carved from rock. The shadow of a massive black wolf on its surface had eyes that glowed with a faint green light. Odd, crawling symbols and lines surrounded it.
Wei He stood up, found a flat spot in the cave, and placed the array stone on the ground.
He then pulled a black star core out of his pouch.
It was about the size of an egg and weighed around three pounds. It was a rare item he had gotten after killing a powerful True Beast in the past.
Normal True Beast star cores usually weighed less than a pound. Stronger ones reached two pounds. A three-pound black star stone could only be taken from beasts whose strength matched a Quanzhen master.
Such beasts were rare unless one went close to the gale zone. Wei He had rarely seen them.
He set the star core on the array stone and tapped it gently. A thread of Huanzhen force stripped away the outer impurities.
The pure star stone instantly touched the surface of the array.
Hiss...
The black wolf on the array stone began to wriggle rapidly. The surrounding symbols and patterns spun faster around it.
An invisible wave expanded outward from the center of the array.
Wei He pressed his hand against the stone, tracking the wave's range. It moved at a steady speed, giving him full control over its reach.
Once the wave covered the entire cave, Wei He poured another thread of Huanzhen force into the stone.
The wave stopped, perfectly fitting the space.
Wei He withdrew his hand and returned to the fire. Activating the array set it to full strength, including its training boost.
It was a bit wasteful, but he did not know how to adjust it, and he had plenty of star cores anyway. With his strength, hunting for star cores was easy.
He rested for a moment and ate some food.
Outside, night had fully fallen.
Wei He put down his soup bowl, stood up, and walked to the entrance.
He pushed the large stone aside, revealing the dark island outside.
Thick clouds blocked the moonlight. Apart from the faint fire light from the cave, the entire island was pitch black.
Wei He's eyes twitched slightly as he entered his super-sensing state.
The darkness before him faded into a gray-black hue, allowing him to see. His vision caught light far better now.
'If I had to give this a name, should I call it low-light vision?' Wei He thought.
He walked out of the cave and slid the stone back into place.
He pulled out a whale-oil lamp he had prepared, struck a flint, and lit it with a spark.
The lamp burned with a dim light, illuminating a radius of about ten meters. This was only because his super-sensing eyes could adapt to the dark.
Thump. Thump.
Right after he lit the lamp, two heavy footsteps sounded from ahead.
Wei He looked up. At the edge of the light, a gray-black lizard crawled out from the dark forest. It was three meters long and over a meter tall.
The lizard was covered in rotting sores, giving off a foul smell. Half of its gums were exposed.
It took two more heavy steps forward. With every step, thick black fluid dripped from its body.
"So many True Beasts? This place really is dangerous."
Wei He did not expect to meet one the moment he stepped onto the island.
He looked closely at the creature.
"Rotting all over and oozing pus, and you're a giant lizard. I'll just call you a Pus Lizard."
He kicked the ground.
A stone shot out, striking the lizard directly in the head.
Bang!
The lizard's head blew apart. It twitched forward a few times before collapsing to the ground, dead.
Wei He walked over and waved his hand, tearing open the corpse. With a flick of his wrist, a dark star core flew out and landed in his palm.
He tossed it in his hand.
"Half a pound at most. Too weak."
Putting the star core away, Wei He held the lamp up and walked slowly toward the dark coast.
In the dark, wind howled, carrying the faint sound of a woman laughing. It was the work of the Nightingale Wind.
At his current strength, a wind like this was no trouble at all.
He walked straight toward the coast he remembered.
A short moment later, two more Pus Lizards lunged at him from the dark. He killed them instantly.
This kind of beast relied on poison and disease in close combat. Against Wei He, who did not even let them get close, they were as helpless as babies.
Soon, he had crossed half the forest.
Suddenly, a dark red line shot out from the dark side like lightning, aiming straight for his head at extreme speed.
