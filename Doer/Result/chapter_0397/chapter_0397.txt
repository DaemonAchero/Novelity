Time passed, and years flew by.
Since the Incident at the Ruins of the Shadow Beast, Wei He returned to the sect with Yuanduzi and had been training inside ever since.
After what happened, he did not even go out to search for the Three-Eyed Dove. Instead, he paid outsiders to search for it.
As for Yao Wan, after she got back, the sect doctors removed the poison from her legs and stopped it from getting worse.
Sadly, the poison left lasting damage. Her legs could not heal on their own, and no medicine could fix them.
The strange poison was buried deep inside her bone marrow and nerves. There was no way to repair it.
Wei He went to check on her, but he was helpless too.
Yao Wan was left disabled. She usually had to use a wheelchair to get around.
A master of swordsmanship had suddenly lost the ability to walk normally.
For a True Virtuous master like her, she could use her True Energy to power her movement when needed. It did not cause too much trouble in daily life.
However, in real combat, her strength was heavily damaged.
Wei He had no fix for this either.
He had looked at the Thousand-Face Demon Lord's poison. He even got a small piece of a sample from the Ghost Face.
It was only after getting that sample that he understood how nasty it was.
It was not a pure poison. To be exact, it was a special toxic bug.
When it bit her, the bug ate away many nerves, flesh, and bone marrow in Yao Wan's legs. It destroyed her body's natural healing power and left behind bad waste that ruined her bone marrow.
The current medicine of the Xuanmiao Sect could only clear the poison. They could not reattach or fix the destroyed nerves and bone marrow.
After losing her strength and becoming disabled, Yao Wan's personality slowly became quiet and cold. Even when Wei He dropped by to visit, he often did not know where she went.
He heard people say that Yao Wan went out often to ask about the Thousand-Face Demon Lord. She also went often to check on the two younger family members left behind by Zhong Yuanlin.
Zhong Yuanlin's family was nearly gone. Years ago, they lost contact because of war. When they were found again, only two kids in the Zhong family were still alive. The rest died from a plague.
Only two people in the whole Zhong family survived.
Yao Wan had saved Zhong Yuanlin, and Zhong Yuanlin had saved Yao Wan. Cause and effect were set by fate.
Besides sighing, Wei He did not know what to say.
At first, the Great Yue Dynasty did not affect the Xuanmiao Sect much.
But as time went on, Great Yue was a massive empire. Now that it had united the world, it had plenty of power to target the Xuanmiao Sect with ease.
The Jinlian Sect defected to the enemy. The Wushi Sect went missing and hid from the world. Only the Xuanmiao Sect was left to stand alone among the Taoist lines.
On top of that, Yuanduzi had attacked and heavily injured Emperor Dingyuan's father, leaving him disabled. Naturally, all eyes were now fixed on the Xuanmiao Sect.
After this event, the Yao family behind Yao Wan acted even lower key.
Wei He stayed behind closed doors to train hard. He ignored the outside world, focusing only on breaking through the second layer of the Xuan Lock Energy to reach the third.
And with that training, eight years passed in a flash...
...
...
...
Dang... Dang...
A deep, long bell ring woke Wei He from his training state.
This was the sound of the Qinghan Bell.
Wei He opened his eyes slowly and let out a long breath, venting all the bad air stored in his chest.
"The Qinghan Bell sounds smooth and rings only twice. Something good must have happened in the sect."
He raised his eyes. His sight seemed to pierce through the stone door, seeing the ancient bell far on the mountainside.
The Qinghan Bell had a special meaning in the Xuanmiao Sect. It was only struck to tell everyone when major events happened.
Because it sat on a slope between the Inner and Outer Mountains, and had a star formation to boost the sound, both Inner Sight Real Persons and Outer Mountain disciples could hear it clearly.
The long bell sound struck twice in total.
It meant something good happened, though it was not a top-tier event.
Wei He had been in the Xuanmiao Sect for ten years now. He knew these common rules well.
Though he kept his distance from many disciples of his level, he still had to join many sect events.
For example, sect missions required his help from time to time.
Luckily, they rarely needed him. Plus, Wei He was strong and had his Five-Turn Dragon Breath as a hidden card. Everything went smoothly.
Whenever he faced danger, his hidden power let him solve it without real harm.
Over the years, Wei He slowly made a name for himself in the sect.
Xu Ran was punished to reflect in isolation for thirty years inside the Inner Mountain because he ran away and left others to die.
As one of the Three Heroes of Lock Mountain, he could no longer keep that title.
The empty spot among the Three Heroes naturally fell onto Wei He's head.
Wei He stood up slowly and looked at the neatly set items inside the cave, which Lou Shengyue had organized well.
On the bed to the side lay the Xuan Lock Skill secret book, which he had flipped through until it was almost ruined.
It had been eight years. His Five-Turn Dragon Breath reached the fourth turn. He needed one last push to finish the final turn.
His Whale Flood Skill had also reached the final step, the fourth layer.
His pure physical power reached an unheard-of thirty-two times its original amount.
A wave of his arm could bring close to seventy thousand jin of force.
For True Blood warriors, this might be nothing. Those monsters who trained their physical bodies, like the Buddha Son Black Cross back then, could hit with over one hundred thousand jin of force.
But for True Energy masters who were weak in raw force, this power was godly.
After all, they focused on True Energy, not raw strength.
"This is it. The Whale Flood Skill can go no further," Wei He sighed.
At this level, the Whale Flood Skill had reached the absolute limit designed by its creator.
There was no path further down. The fifth layer was just an idea. Even the creator did not know if it could be trained.
Yet, Wei He was already satisfied with this skill that had helped him so much.
It was just an old book he found back in a plain group like the Sky Seal Sect. To work this far was already amazing.
Besides, with nearly seventy thousand jin of force boosted by the Five-Turn Dragon Breath...
With raw power alone, even if Black Cross stood before him today, Wei He could crush him with one punch.
Of course, he rarely used such hidden tricks. Wei He was still on the path of True Energy. There was no turning back.
He pushed open the stone door and walked out of the cave.
The honeycomb caves of the Inner Mountain were no longer as quiet as before.
After the Xuanmiao Sect moved, many Real Persons were called back. Since they just arrived overseas, they faced countless problems and threats.
This made many Real Persons crave power, so more of them began to train hard.
The water supply point at the Taoist Nature area was finally fixed by the sect after they chased away some gold-furred monkeys that kept causing trouble.
Yes, there were still gold-furred monkeys on these mountains, though not the old lazy ones from before.
Hearing the Qinghan Bell, many training masters stepped out of their caves, walked down the mountain, and headed toward the big square near the Dragon Bay and Morality Palace.
Wei He closed his door behind him, tapped his toes, leaped light as a feather, and floated down.
Lou Shengyue stepped out from a cave behind him and quickly followed.
"Master, you are finally out." The once simple and pure young girl had lost her greenness, becoming steady and focused.
"Yes. Do you know what the good news is?" Wei He landed, kept a steady pace forward, and asked casually.
"It should be about the First Dao Seed, Senior Brother Cai Menghuan. Earlier, Senior Brother Cai used a single sword to kill the Three Evils of Heavy Sea, who were three True Virtuous villains. After returning to the sect, he had an epiphany and went right into closed-door training. It has been two months. Could it be that Senior Brother Cai broke through?"
When Lou Shengyue mentioned Cai Menghuan, her tone showed deep respect and admiration.
It was true. Among the younger generation in the Xuanmiao Sect, no one spoke of Cai Menghuan without feeling this way.
Ever since this First Dao Seed took off five years ago, he broke through a stage every two years. Now he had passed the older generation like Yao Wan, reaching the second step of True Virtue.
Wei He felt deeply moved as well.
Cai Menghuan, who was only at the third level of Sense Setting eight years ago, had reached such a high level today, catching up to older peers like Yao Wan...
Such speed fit his name as the top genius of the Xuanmiao Sect in hundreds of years.
In just a few decades, he stepped into the highest group in the current Xuanmiao Sect.
Wei He looked back at himself.
He was about to finish the third layer of the Xuan Lock Skill. This layer focused on nourishing the mind and transforming the Xuan Lock Energy to cause a quality change.
Wei He knew that his current speed was normal.
Usually, taking ten years for one level of Sense Setting was standard speed for a master.
Cai Menghuan was just too fast.
He built up strength over time and exploded. He was slow at first, but grew faster the further he went.
This was how top geniuses acted.
In eight years, many people were still worried about the Ghost Wind creeping in and whether their power could hold off the next Sense Setting.
Wei He had worked hard every day, yet he was only near the end of the third layer, preparing to break through.
Every master needed full prep for a breakthrough because the fail rate was very high. Once you failed the first time, your odds dropped lower next time, and your power would get hurt.
Yet Cai Menghuan moved like he was on cheat pills, jumping one step every two years, rushing forward like crazy.
Wei He sighed in his heart and pulled his thoughts back.
By now, he and Lou Shengyue had joined the other Real Persons and their servants. Like a flood of black dots, they gathered on the big square.
As Real Persons, Wei He and his group had a special viewing area. They did not stand down below with the crowd.
Wei He stood in the fourth row.
The first row at the very front held spots for the three Founders, with one saved for Yuanduzi.
These four stood tall, representing the strongest peak of the entire Xuanmiao Sect—four Grandmasters.
The second row held three faces Wei He did not recognize, all with white hair, both men and women.
Though he did not know them, he had heard of them.
These three were grand elders who returned to the sect only in recent years. They were from the same generation as the three Founders, possessing high rank and great power.
They were not Grandmasters, but they had reached the ninth step limit of True Virtue.
They had gone out to explore the True Realm years ago and returned long after receiving the summons.
When they came back, they brought lots of records on True Beasts from the True Realm, which reportedly shocked the whole sect.
The third row held the Chief Masters of the three branches and the three Dao Seeds: Xuan Zhen, Xuan Ning, and Cai Menghuan.
Yuanduzi was no longer a Chief Master. The Chief spot for Lock Mountain was now held by a tall, handsome man.
Wei He did not know him and guessed he might be an Inner Mountain master who had been in quiet training.
The Chief of Ghost Head was still Baihezi, and the Chief of Qianliu was still Qingmeizi.
The fourth row held the experts of the three branches. Senior True Virtue experts like Huansongzi stood in this row.
Behind these senior experts stood the Three Heroes of each branch.
The Three Heroes of Lock Mountain were no longer the same three as before.
Today, it was Wei He, a cold woman with a beauty mark by her mouth, and a warm, middle-aged fat man.
