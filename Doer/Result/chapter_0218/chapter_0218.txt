Night Eagle Tower was more than a dark info hub and kill-for-hire squad. Deep money and power tied into it. No one knew how much dark trouble it would pull out later.
At the same time, the Wang family led the Akakage Army's top fighters to hunt down small and medium gangs around the land.
In just half a month, more than half of the ten-plus gangs were wiped out. The rest tried to drop into the dark and break into tiny pieces to hide.
It was no use. They were tracked down one by one and killed with ease.
A group called Liu Ye played a huge role in this.
No matter how deep a target hid, Liu Ye found them like it was nothing.
Gangs were dug up. Big rich families were pulled out by the roots. As time went on, more families saw where the wind blew and gave up, joining the winning side.
The Wang family grew like a snowball, bigger and faster every day.
Tons of secret martial art books were taken. Wei He took his cut, copied many of them, and put them in Tianya Tower.
As the hunt went on, not every targeted group was pure evil. Because of this, the names of the Wang family and Wanqing Gate started to stink.
Wei He did not care.
For two whole months, when he was not training, he was out with Zhu Chen, clearing out all kinds of martial arts groups.
His cloud marks grew to twelve. Only six were left before he hit eighteen.
When all eighteen cloud marks linked up for the Cloud Bone body, he planned to use the Realm-Breaking Bead with the best medicine to make a perfect breakthrough and build the strongest base.
Far Chess Town.
A light rain fell.
Wei He sat in a low-key chess house. He listened to the quiet shouts of players and the crisp tap of pieces on the boards.
He was tall and thick. Usually, he would stand out, but these days, two-meter-tall fighters were everywhere.
In this chess house alone, five or six men were just as big.
Wei He wore a hat and rubbed some herbs on his face to change his look.
No one could tell who he was.
Even Wan Qingqing would need to get right up to his face to know him.
Outside, the rain kept falling. Warm tea sent thin steam into the air, filling the hall with a quiet scent.
Wei He let out a soft sigh.
In these weeks, he had led teams to wipe out eight forces.
In over half of them, only a few people were bad. Most were innocent.
Yet the big wheel rolled on. Nothing stopped it.
To pull up every root, Zhu Chen would wave his hand and order a full house killed.
Too many died. Too much blood flowed. Even a man as cold and steady as Wei He felt tired of it.
Last time, he saw Zhu Chen order the death of kids only a few years old. Wei He could not take it anymore and had to walk out to clear his head.
In two months, he saw hundreds die.
Many were innocent, even good people.
It did not matter to Zhu Chen. His face stayed cold. Anyone who did not bow was wiped out.
Sitting in the chess house, listening to the crisp clicks of the game, Wei He felt his mind slow down.
Once he settled, the quiet talk around him slipped into his ears.
"No way out for them," a man nearby whispered. "The Zhang family is rich, but look at who they pissed off. The Akakage Army belongs to the Wangs now. They hunt and kill all over. Say one wrong word, and blades drop on you. The Zhangs are done."
"Maybe not," another whispered back. "This morning, I saw the Zhang family's second young mistress take a line of carts to the Wang house. Probably giving gifts to ask for grace."
"Useless. Wang Shuyi leads the hunt herself. No one's word works. They picked the wrong person at the wrong time. That second young master could have picked on anyone, but he had to mess with Wang Shuyi while she was in plain clothes. He begged to die."
They talked on and on.
The Wang family had a foul name now, but their power was too vast. No one could stand in front of them.
Even the Zhou and You families stepped back to stay out of the way.
Wei He did not care at first.
He had sat here for a while and heard lots of talk. Rich families getting crushed like this was nothing new.
The Wang family was looking for any reason to wipe out mid and small groups.
The big bosses above wanted to end all small forces, pull all resources to the top, and move them easily.
The Wang family was chosen to lead this area. They were the main family backed by the top.
Now, Wang Zhihe had made a deal with the You and Zhou families. The three joined hands, with the Wangs on top. Add the army, and the whole land was turning into a wall of iron.
Wei He leaned back in his seat and rested. He got up to leave.
Once in a while, he put on a fake face and went out alone. It was his way to catch a breath.
To find a place where no one knew him and rest like a normal guy.
No eyes watching him, no big hopes placed on his shoulders. Just him, clear and free.
He stood up, but his feet stopped. He heard a name in the chatter.
The rich Zhang family was falling. Five out of ten tables were talking about it.
Wei He walked out of the chess house and followed the street signs toward the Zhang house.
He passed a few shop streets and turned into a quiet dead-end lane.
At the end stood a big yard with red doors, white walls, and black tiles. The main gate was open wide.
People were running in and out, carrying stuff out to ox carts parked outside.
In the yard, women in servant clothes and fine dresses held small kids, watching with hopeless eyes. Some cried softly and wiped tears.
The tiny kids knew nothing and looked around with bright eyes.
The older kids knew a bit, their eyes full of fear.
On the board above the gate, two words were cut in gold and black: Zhang House.
Wei He stood on the street outside, looking in from far away.
The workers moving things kept quiet and moved fast. They only spoke to ask what to take and what to leave.
A lead manager held a small book, writing down every item taken out.
"First Lady, Second Lady, I'll count all this as fourteen thousand taels of gold notes. How's that?" the manager called out.
"We... we agreed on sixteen thousand before," a soft, weak woman stepped up and said.
"Some of your stuff is old and chipped. We have to pay to fix it up before selling. We have to drop the price," the manager said.
"But..." The weak woman tried to speak, but another woman pulled her back.
"Sister, let me handle it," the second woman said in a low voice.
She stepped forward right by the gate.
She wore a light green dress with a silver-edged belt. She was tall, with an air that did not belong to common folk.
Wei He knew her at a glance.
The woman was about to speak when she saw Wei He standing far down the street.
Both froze.
She snapped back quickly, blocked the manager, and spoke fast. She pointed out every item, fought over every detail, and called out the cheap price.
The manager could not beat her words and had to stick to the old price.
Once done, she ran out of the yard to find the man standing nearby.
She walked around, but he was gone.
She hit her hands together in regret.
That man looked just like her old friend from the sect.
Her home was in deep trouble now. If she could reach that friend, things might get better.
It felt shame to ask, but for her child, face meant nothing. She was ready to throw it away.
On the next street over.
Wei He walked slow, heading away from the Zhang house.
He did not meet Yang Guo. Even though she was the first woman he met when he came to Sky Seal Gate.
Long ago, when she married into the rich house she wanted, all ties broke.
When Sky Seal Gate fell apart, she stayed hidden. She played it safe and stayed away from the hunt.
She was not there through the bad times, so now that Wanqing Gate was up, she had no right to share the power.
"Wait!" A light figure ran up from behind. With a few quick leaps, she landed right in front of Wei He and blocked his way.
"Something wrong?" Wei He looked up at Yang Guo, his face cold and calm.
Years ago, the two of them trained by the lake. The image was still clear in his head.
Looking at her now, the tall young girl was gone. Her hair was tied up, her body softer and more full.
"You... are you Wei He?!" She was not sure before, but up close, she knew him through the simple fake face.
"Long time no see," Wei He nodded. "Just passing by. Don't mind me."
Yang Guo opened her mouth, but no words came out.
The Wei He today was the Master of the Ten Thousand Poison Sect, a close friend of Wang Gao, the top young lord. His power was vast. A word from him held the power of life and death.
He was worlds away from the unknown boy who joined the sect years ago.
"Can I... buy you a drink?" she asked softly.
She knew a married woman inviting a young man alone was wrong.
But she had no other way.
All the men in the Zhang family were locked in prison. They tried everything and asked everyone, but got nowhere.
The family they crossed was the Wang family. The rising, unstoppable Wangs.
Meeting Wei He was like catching a rope while drowning.
She had tried sending letters to him before, but every note sank into the sea without a word back.
Now she ran into him on the street.
"Sorry," Wei He shook his head. They had no reason to meet alone.
He stepped around her and walked on.
"But... I really have no way out..." Her voice broke into a cry behind him.
Her child was just over one year old. If they could not pass this wall, then...
Wei He stopped and turned around.
She was an old friend after all. He let out a sigh.
"Tell me. What happened?"
