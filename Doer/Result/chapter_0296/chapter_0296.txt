Wei He felt a bit curious, but quickly stopped caring.
His relationship with the Song brothers was over. He gave them money, taught them martial arts, and sent them to a safe place.
They were even now.
The Song brothers ran to Weifang City on their own and got dragged into trouble, but that was out of his control.
So, suppressing his curiosity, Wei He did not bother with it and kept eating his fried tofu.
On the other side.
The Song brothers went up to the restaurant with Wang Zui and sat in a private room to eat.
Once they stepped inside and locked the door and windows, the panic on their faces disappeared instantly.
Looking at the confused Wang Zui, the older brother Song Shixiong bowed with his hands together.
"Brother Wang, we had to make a scene to get close to you. Forgive us if we offended you."
"You... two?" Wang Zui realized the whole thing was just an act.
"You helped us before, so we came today to warn you," Song Shixiong said in a serious tone. "Brother Wang, you need to be careful. We got news that the White Tiger Gang, the Green Dragon Gang, and the Black Hand Society are all targeting your Wang family. They want to move against you."
"!!" Wang Zui was shocked.
However, he was still skeptical.
"This... brothers... my family has no connection with them at all. Why would they target us?"
"We are not sure either. We just heard rumors on the road and came to warn you. Whatever happens, you should prepare in advance," Song Shixiong said quickly.
"But... this..." Wang Zui could not believe it.
His family was just a regular wealthy family in Weifang City, nothing special. Why would three brutal gangs target them?
What did they want from the Wang family?
"I heard the Wang family recently got an item. It seems to be a martial arts manual." Song Shizhen, who had been silent, finally spoke up, catching Wang Zui's attention.
"A manual? Could it be...!?" Wang Zui suddenly remembered something.
"Did you think of it?" Song Shixiong asked immediately.
"Yes... my grandfather found a secret scroll while studying ancient books. It is called the Xuan Tian Manual," Wang Zui frowned. "But the content is full of Taoist terms. It is not a martial arts manual at all."
"That matches our news," Song Shixiong replied. "We found out that all three gangs are working together under one person.
Which means there is a single mastermind behind all of them!"
"Brother... do you mean Lu Weisheng of the Treasure Light Pavilion?!" Song Shizhen reacted and shouted in surprise.
"Lu Weisheng?!"
Wang Zui was shocked, and he finally believed the brothers.
Lu Weisheng had tried to buy the Xuan Tian Manual not long ago, but his grandfather rejected him.
He was one of the few people who knew about the scroll.
"But Boss Lu... is just the owner of a shop. How could he command three entire gangs?" Wang Zui was still confused.
"Because Lu Weisheng has another name that very few people know."
Song Shixiong said seriously, "The Spear King of Weifang."
"He is the Spear King of Weifang?!" Wang Zui's face turned pale.

What does he mean?
Wei He was speechless.
It seemed Lu Weisheng came here for a specific reason.
The man hid his true self well. Wei He thought he was just a rich businessman, but he had a hidden background.
Wei He looked around and saw people clearing the streets. It was a large operation.
"Alright then. I only came for the tofu. Now that I am done, I will head back." He nodded, bowed to Lu Weisheng, and walked away without hesitation.
Lu Weisheng watched him leave.
A man behind him frowned.
"Master, why didn't we kill him? It is too suspicious for him to appear at this time and place."

"Yes...!" The man lowered his head in shame and stepped back.
"Besides, he is one of the few cultured people I know who likes ancient paintings. If I kill everyone, where will I find like-minded friends?"
Lu Weisheng sighed.
In an era where everyone practiced martial arts, few people cared about paintings and calligraphy.
That was why he advised Wei He to leave.
"Alright, everyone is here. Go bring Wang Zui. Tell the Wang family to choose between their grandson's life or the Xuan Tian Manual."
"Understood, I will arrange it now," the subordinate replied quickly.

On a rocky plain ninety miles outside Weifang City.
A campfire burned brightly next to a few ox carts. Around ten people sat together, cooking soup in an iron pot.
These people looked strange. Some were nearly three meters tall, others were foreigners with golden hair, and one woman had messy hair and only one arm.
Despite their odd looks, a faint scent of incense floated around them.
Every now and then, everyone looked over at a white-haired old man sitting nearby.
The old man had a bump on his forehead and a long beard. He looked healthy and long-lived, but his eyes were filled with sadness and hatred.
"Elder Dong, the clues point to Weifang City, but if we march straight in, we might attract too much attention. After all, that is the territory of the Wanfei Palace," the one-armed woman said.

"I have already prepared fake identities for us. We will wait outside in disguise. Once our insider sends a signal, we will finish the mission, then find our target."
