“What is the point of fighting back? The Three Primary Peaks possess absolute strength. Even if you escape this time, there will always be a next time,” Qiao Ye murmured, an unreadable look in her eyes.
“The harder you resist now, the more you will suffer later. Instead of that, it is better to accept it earlier...”
She lowered her head, closed her eyes, and softly recited her Daoist scriptures.
“If you truly have the skill, then keep resisting. Prove to me that your choice was not wrong!”
In her heart, the Three Primary Peaks were invincible, standing high above everyone else, unmatched in all of Taizhou. Wei He resisting them was like an insect trying to stop a carriage. He would surely be crushed into dust.
“Just like Senior Brother Zheng back then. If he had not fought back, he would not have met such an end. Those who resist never end up well! Just wait, Wei He... You and your master will share the exact same fate. It is only a matter of time...”
Her hands tightened without her realizing it. Sharp fingernails dug deep into her palms, but she felt no pain at all.
Unlike the heavy silence in Qiao Ye’s inner courtyard, the main outer courtyard was in total chaos.
The outer courtyard was divided under many managers, and each manager handled numerous smaller departments. Every department had its own building to handle daily business.
Inside the Dao Robe Department, Zhang Qi slowly ended his stance, finishing his hard daily training.
He was now a full Martial Master. Compared to the wretched user who had fled all the way to Taizhou alongside Wei He years ago, he was now handsome, confident, and full of energy.
“Senior Brother Zhang, someone named Jiang Su is outside looking for you. She says she comes from your hometown and wants to talk,” a lower disciple said with a smile.
“Jiang Su? Got it, thanks,” Zhang Qi replied. The name felt distant, bringing back old memories.
Back when he was just an ordinary nobody who knew nothing, Jiang Su was already a powerful expert of the Two Bloods Hall. But now he had broken through to Martial Master and was about to enter the inner courtyard, while Jiang Su had barely reached Martial Master herself. They were now equals.
In fact, when it came to status, talent, and aptitude, he was superior to her in every single way. Sister Jiang had no hope of moving further up, but he could keep climbing! He could get even stronger!
A surge of absolute confidence rose in his chest.
He tidied his clothes a bit and walked out of the training grounds. From afar, he saw the curvaceous, attractive figure of his former Senior Sister standing right before the gates of the department.
“What brings you here, Sister Jiang?” Zhang Qi asked as he smiled and walked over.
“Junior Brother Zhang...” Jiang Su turned around, watching the young man walk toward her.
This was the younger brother of her former Junior Brother Zhang Lu. He had grown so fast within the Wushi Sect, even passing her to become a rising star about to enter the inner courtyard.
Yet, as he gained power so rapidly, his old honesty had vanished. He had turned into a ruthless man who would use any means necessary just to get stronger.
“I heard you are court–ing Senior Sister Liu Haoying?” Jiang Su asked in a low voice.
Liu Haoying was the daughter of Liu Zhenyuan, the head of the Seventh Inner Courtyard. She had a nice name, but her face and body were a total nightmare. She was massive, rough, and domineering. One punch from her could kill two bulls.
That was not an exaggeration. She had literally punched straight through two bull heads before. That was the main reason why she remained unmarried for so many years.
Zhang Qi had good aptitude and good looks. His reason for pursuing such an older, brutal female warrior was painfully obvious.
“If you have something to say, Sister Jiang, please say it quickly,” Zhang Qi said, completely unbothered. Ever since his brother died, he knew that power was the only thing that mattered in this world. To gain power, marriage was just another tool.
Jiang Su was pretty, but beauty was not strength. It could not feed him. Under a real fist, no matter how pretty someone was, they would still turn into meat paste. His will was like iron.
“It is not about me. Do you know about the Myriad Poison Sect?” Jiang Su asked with a frown.
“You mean the Poison Sect Master who shares the same name as our old Senior Brother Wei?” Zhang Qi paused.
“Yes,” Jiang Su nodded. “The Outer Courtyard Chief Grandmaster wants us to drop by. He probably thinks that big figure is the same Wei He who came here with us.”
“Alright.” Zhang Qi nodded, his mind spinning fast as he calculated how to use this chance to get close to the Chief and gain some benefits.
“Zhang Qi,” Jiang Su spoke again suddenly.
“What is it?”
“What if... that person really is Senior Brother Wei? What do we do?” Jiang Su’s voice trembled with doubt and fear. Deep down, even she found it hard to believe.
“We will be dragged into it,” Zhang Qi said with certainty. “But whether that turns out to be a good thing or a bad thing depends entirely on our attitude.” He did not care about how his former senior brother was doing now.
He quickly ran scenarios in his head. If the Poison Sect Master really was Wei He, how should he act to carve out the biggest profit for himself?
“You!” Jiang Su understood his implication. If they were truly the same person...
Her heart was filled with both shock and deep fear.
Wei He had beaten two Courtyard Heads to death, injured two Grandmasters, and angered a massive number of people within the Wushi Sect. If he was that person, everyone connected to him would face brutal revenge from those angry factions.
If she were alone, it would be fine, but she had the Jiang family behind her. Over the years, some members of her family had come to Taizhou to settle down using her influence. If they were dragged into this mess, years of hard work would be destroyed in a single day.
Both of them fell silent. Neither knew what kind of fate awaited them at the upcoming meeting.
Spring faded and autumn arrived.
The Myriad Poison Sect broke into small squads and fled in different directions. Protected by many loyal experts, Wei He and his family finally reached Jinzhou safely. They bought a compound in Weifang City ahead of time and settled down.
Everyone scattered and blended into the massive crowd of the city.
As masters of poison, the members of the Myriad Poison Sect naturally had ways to block tracking methods. They also had secret ways to send messages over long distances.
The sect’s experts changed their names and altered their faces, completely taking on new identities. Some integrated into Jinzhou, some went to other provinces, and some even joined the military as officers.
The once famous Myriad Poison Sect rapidly retreated from the light into the dark, turning into a hidden underground force.
However, while such a force was secret, the number of core members had to remain small. Wei He only kept the elite experts and dismissed the rest, cutting ties completely. This made it much easier to stay hidden. Even the Wanfei Palace did not know the whereabouts of the Poison Sect’s current experts.
As for the Wushi Sect, before they could make their next move, they were suppressed by the combined forces of the Nest Extinction Alliance and the Taian Army.
The Wushi Sect, which had sent many experts to Dragon Blood and Lake Tai, suffered a sudden nighttime attack by numerous peak masters just half a month after Wei He left.
A grand battle broke out at the Three Primary Peaks. Nobody knew who won or lost, but after that day, the Wushi Sect went quiet, while the Nest Extinction Alliance and the Taian Army also lost their aggressive edge.
The wild storms sweeping across Taizhou seemed to instantly settle down.
Under these circumstances, Wei He brought his family, took a new name, and lived a peaceful life in Weifang City.
Jinzhou had no war. The air was peaceful. Once they hid within ordinary life, there were no more sect rivalries, no more martial arts battles, and no more political fights between military factions.
Wei He finally got to enjoy the quiet, normal life he had wanted for so long.
He slowly adjusted to the super-sense state of his left hand while teaching his son every day. Occasionally, he would take his wife out to enjoy the sights and rivers. Guarded secretly by many hidden masters, they were completely safe and free.
They had no lack of food or clothing. They could do whatever they wanted. They played chess every day, listened to music, tasted local foods, and collected rare treasures. Such a life made Wei He truly blend into his new identity.
The identity he created for himself was a retired military official from another province. Right now, there were plenty of retired officials hiding their names. Most of them had money and martial skills, living without worry, making it the perfect cover.
In truth, Wei He had originally planned to act as a retired scholar official, but his massive body did not allow it, so he had to play a military officer instead.
Leisurely days passed like flowing water, and a year went by in the blink of an eye.
“Hurry! Come on, it is your turn, Little An!”
Three year old Wei An was at the main gate, playing jumping games with several neighbor kids. A group of little kids were yelling and shouting wildly.
The game was simple. They did not draw lines on the ground. Instead, they placed a wooden crate on the floor, and the kids took turns jumping over it. If someone failed to jump across, they had to sit inside the crate and let the others jump over their head.
Though Wei An was only three years old, he was already as tall as a four or five year old. His two arms were packed with hard muscle, proving clearly that he was Wei He's son.
After a few rounds, a handsome, skinny boy failed to jump over and was immediately shoved into the crate by the group.
Being shoved into the wooden crate was the ultimate embarrassment for these children. The skinny boy’s face turned bright red, but he had to stay there until the next kid failed.
Inside the courtyard, Wei He and Wan Qingqing stood near the door, watching the children at the gate.
“The one sitting in the crate, is that the young son of Member Wang next door?” Wan Qingqing asked softly.
“Yeah, his name is Wang Xinde. It is a nice name, but the little guy has a fierce temper, just like Wei An,” Wei He chuckled.
“That is why they play so well together,” Wan Qingqing smiled as well.
“Let them play, just make sure Little An does not hurt anyone. I need to visit Ninth Uncle later,” Wei He said, his smile fading. “I am afraid his body will not survive this winter.”
“Ninth Uncle...” Wan Qingqing went silent. Ninth Shadow had worked hard in his old age, giving their family immense support. Now he had no descendants to see him off, only one young apprentice who was still too small.
“How nice would it be if we could keep living like this forever... Ninth Uncle is so skilled in medicine, can he really not cure himself? If he needs anything, we can give him whatever we have at home,” Wan Qingqing whispered.
“It is not a matter of medicine. His time has simply come,” Wei He sighed.
He enjoyed this current peace, but he knew it was only temporary. The enemy army was pushing at the borders, rebels were rising everywhere, and every province was fighting internally while trying to team up under extreme pressure. The world was far too chaotic.
So while his life looked peaceful on the surface, he had never stopped training for a single second.
By now, he had mastered the ability to train every second, anywhere, at any time. The internal force within his body moved constantly, driving his circulation. Various special force traits enhanced each other, forming countless training routines that only he could use.
Over the past year, he had also gradually adapted to the super-sense state of his left hand.
Next, what he needed to do was keep this state stable, slowly immersing himself into reality, contacting reality, merging into reality, and digging out the deeper power hidden within his body.
This process would take a long time, as it was a total transformation of his internal force. He planned to spend more than ten years to break through this realm.
“By the way, the White Pine Merchant Group is holding a small meeting in a few days. They should have a new batch of items for auction. I heard there are many paintings and calligraphy pieces. Boss Xiao heard you like landscape paintings, so he brought in quite a few. You should go take a look. You might find something you need,” Wan Qingqing said.
“Paintings?” Wei He had already attended these private auctions many times and was quite experienced.
“Alright, I will go take a look when the time comes.”
