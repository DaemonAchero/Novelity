The sea looked as if it were covered in a thick layer of grey dust. Its waves kept crashing and rolling just like any other day.
Yet, the crushing weight that felt like a falling mountain was gone.
Grandmaster Modo had vanished.
Wei He snapped back to his senses and instantly looked toward First Sister Yuan Duzi.
Almost everyone else did the exact same thing.
Thankfully, Yuan Duzi was still standing in her spot.
That meant they hadn’t actually fought.
Phew…
Wei He heard a wave of deep sighs ring out around him. It was the sound of absolute relief.
The battle between Modo—the National Teacher of the Guangci Cult—and Yuan Duzi—the Sect Master of the Xuanmiao Sect—was no longer just a duel between two people. It was a clash between two nations and two peak martial arts systems.
Neither of them could afford to lose.
If Yuan Duzi lost, the entire Xuanmiao Sect would face total destruction.
If Modo lost, it meant the True Blood system of the Wu Country was inferior to the True Force system. For a state religion like the Guangci Cult, which relied entirely on faith to hold power, such a defeat would be catastrophic.
More importantly, if neither could kill the other on the spot, the survivor would face a nightmare. The loser would spend the rest of their life launching endless, deadly ambushes in the shadows.
One was the number one fighter on the Dao Registry. The other was the Grandmaster of Wu. Who could possibly stop either of them if they chose to become assassins?
That was why Modo did not destroy the Xuanmiao Sect today. The sect was Yuan Duzi’s only weakness. If he destroyed it, he would create a monster—the strongest fighter in the Great Yuan with zero remaining restraints.
It was the same reason Yuan Duzi chose not to kill the Emperor of Wu back then.
A fight to the death would only destroy both sides. No one would walk away a winner.
Standing by the shore, Yuan Duzi stared into the distance for a long time.
Once she was sure Modo was truly gone, she remained silent. Her body suddenly dissolved into black smoke, vanishing into the True Realm.
Only after both giants were gone did the crowd on the beach slowly begin to leave.
From start to finish, the three Ancestors who had returned to the sect never showed their faces.
Ancestor Suoshan had a good reason; he had been injured during an earlier fight and was hiding to heal his wounds. Even now, he stayed behind closed doors every day. But the other two Grandmasters also refused to step outside. Their silence made people uneasy.
This tense meeting had ended without a bloodshed. The only loss was suffered by the Ghost Head Lineage's Chief, Baihezi.
The largest Xuanlan Snake she had raised was dead.
From a distance, Wei He watched her walk step by step into the cold seawater. She stood beside the floating corpse of her giant snake, crouched down, and gently touched its icy scales. She didn't say a single word.
"Let's go," Duan Yanhui whispered, pulling Wei He back to reality.
Wei He let out a long, heavy breath.
He had a bad feeling. The Guangci Cult and the Wu Country would never let this go. Grandmaster Modo’s arrival today was a clear sign of what was to come.
The previous attack that severely wounded the Wu Emperor had forced Wu to pause its aggression, but it also created an unforgivable hatred.
The Wu Emperor was the supreme ruler of a nation. How could he tolerate a threat that could take his life at any moment?
Perhaps Modo's visit today was pushed by the Wu Emperor himself.
"Don't overthink it," Duan Yanhui said, trying to comfort him. "Those monsters are far above our level. With First Sister Yuan Duzi and the three Ancestors around, they will hold up the sky if it falls. Don't worry, Wu won't dare to move recklessly."
Wei He nodded.
He knew that with his current power, worrying was completely useless.
"Come on, let's pack up and leave as soon as possible. Haizhou... can't be saved anymore. This land is no longer our home," Duan Yanhui sighed, turning to walk up the mountain.
Wei He could tell that Duan Yanhui saw the hopeless situation just as clearly as he did. But like he said, when you are weak, knowing the truth changes nothing.
After a long silence, Wei He took one last look at Senior Sister Baihezi crouching in the ocean. He sighed quietly and turned to leave.
...
One month later, Haizhou fell.
The Xuanmiao Sect fled deep into the overseas islands, cutting off all ties with the mortal world.
The Jinlian Sect surrendered to Wu. The Wushi Sect of Taizhou packed up its gates and vanished into hiding.
Chen Zhan, the last loyal lord of the Great Yuan Dynasty, gathered two hundred thousand troops to make a final stand. His entire army was wiped out at Yiling in Dongzhou.
Chen Zhan fled for his life with the young imperial prince, Li Rong. During their desperate escape, both fell off a steep cliff. No one knew if they lived or died.
With that, the royal bloodline of the Great Yuan was completely cut off.
The Great Wu Empire officially took control of both lands.
The following month, the Wu Emperor stepped down due to his severe injuries. Crown Prince Sima Ling took the throne as Emperor Dingyuan. He changed the nation's name to the Great Yue Empire, abolished the nineteen old provinces, and redrew the land into nine strategic territories.
The new era was declared.
The Great Yue Dynasty was born, reaching a size and power never seen before in history.
On the ninth day of the first month of the Great Yue Era.
Emperor Dingyuan, Sima Ling, bathed and chanted scriptures. He offered sacrifices to heaven and earth at the main temple of the Guangci Cult, praying for good weather and peace across his new empire.
"The Great Yuan has fallen. Only a few rats like Li Zhouzhao are still running around. The nine lands are mostly under control," Emperor Dingyuan said in the deep inner shrine. "National Teacher, do you have any news on that woman from the Xuanmiao Sect?"
Emperor Dingyuan wore a pitch-black dragon robe. His massive five-meter-tall body radiated terrifying pressure. Yet, he knelt respectfully before a cold, solemn statue of the Buddha, his hands pressed together.
Modo stood beside him, slowly rolling a string of prayer beads through his fingers. His lips didn't move, but the low sound of chanting echoed softly through the air.
"Your Majesty..." Modo began.
"I am the supreme ruler of Great Yue," Emperor Dingyuan interrupted, his voice dropping heavy and cold. "Yet every day, I can only watch my own father suffer in pain from the wound she gave him."
He turned his eyes toward Modo. They were as huge as copper bells, filled with dense, blood-red veins.
"You are my teacher and the National Teacher. Tell me the truth. If you fight Yuan Duzi face-to-face, what are your chances?"
Modo went quiet. He remained silent for a long moment before answering.
"Ninety percent chance... that we both die."
Emperor Dingyuan fell silent.
That was a cost he could not pay.
He slowly rose from his cushion, his massive shadow covering the floor.
"Order the Imperial Martial Institute to gather every single piece of information on Yuan Duzi. I don't believe anyone in this world has no weaknesses. Yuan Duzi is strong, but she only relies on absurd speed. If I use the power of the entire empire, I will find her lethal flaw sooner or later! Once we find a way for other Grandmasters or military formations to track her, she will die!"
Modo's face stayed calm. His eyes showed no joy, no sadness, and no fear.
...
Meanwhile, far across the ocean, at Jujun Island.
Clang!
Wei He raised his hand, blocking a sharp sword slash from the side. Bare skin met cold steel, making a loud metallic ring.
He spun around and struck backward, his bare palm smashing against another blinding blade.
In a fraction of a second, his hands moved like lightning, blocking a storm of incoming sword attacks.
Beside him, Yao Wan’s hands were a blur as she unleashed countless sharp strikes.
However, her strength was now far behind Wei He's. She was only at the low level of the True Realm, relying entirely on her elite sword skill to keep her name on the registry.
She could only spar with Wei He when he was in his normal, casual state.
After a short while, Yao Wan stopped her sword, breathing a bit heavily.
"I'm done. You're getting more monster-like every day. I wonder if anyone in the whole sect can even beat you now," she complained. She felt like she was just playing around with a child, except she was the child.
The gap between them was too big. Sparring gave her no real practice at all. She had wanted Wei He to help her sharpen her sword skills, but the overwhelming defeat only made her feel helpless.
Wei He slowly pulled his hands back and smiled.
"You were the one who asked me to help, Senior Sister. Besides, you are wrong. There are plenty of people stronger than me in the sect. Senior Brother Huansongzi is definitely one of them."
Since moving overseas, Wei He spent every day training in the deep mountains of Jujun Island.
The environment here was almost identical to Tianhai Island, built using the exact same layout.
Because of this, transitioning here was seamless. Everyone quickly adapted to the new island life. When the Xuanmiao Sect was back on Tianhai Island, they rarely interacted with the outside world anyway, only trading with Zhentao City for supplies. Now, they simply changed their trading routes.
"Fine, fine, whatever you say," Yao Wan said with a helpless laugh. "But since I brought you out here and couldn't even use you for practice, my favor was spent for nothing."
She paused and looked at him.
"Since you're already here, help me with another favor instead."
"What is it?" Wei He asked casually.
"Our sect only kept a small force here in the past, so we didn't cause much trouble," Yao Wan explained. "But now, our entire sect has moved over. The impact on the surrounding area is massive. There were already local sea forces and strong sects living near these waters."
"Are any of them stronger than the Xuanmiao Sect?" Wei He asked with a faint smile.
"Not really, but we can't look down on them either," Yao Wan smiled back. "You just reached the Second Sense and entered the Transformation Realm. You won't be able to push for the next stage anytime soon; it will take years. You might as well take on some sect missions, gain some real experience, and test yourself."
Her smile faded, replaced by a serious look.
"To be honest, the surrounding forces aren't our biggest problem. The real threat is the unstable Ghost Winds out at sea. These winds can turn violently harsh at any second, and when they do, hordes of powerful True Beasts follow them. You need to prepare yourself for long battles against both the beasts and the winds."
She suddenly stopped, coughing violently into her hand. Sweat beaded on her forehead.
Wei He narrowed his eyes. Something was clearly wrong with her.
"Senior Sister, you..."
"I'm fine. Just caught a cold from the ocean breeze, and my old wounds haven't fully healed yet... Don't worry about it," Yao Wan lifted her head and forced her usual bright smile.
"If you say so," Wei He nodded.
Everything on this new island was foreign. He didn't have many close friends, but Yao Wan was definitely one of them.
"Senior Sister, if you ever need help, just tell me. Don't hide it. If I can help, I won't hesitate."
Yao Wan waved her hand, laughing softly.
"Don't worry, I won't forget a top expert like you."
She knew very well that Wei He's true power put him in the top ten of the entire Xuanmiao Sect. He was far stronger than she was.
