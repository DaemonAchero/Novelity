Black House Mountain was covered in heavy toxic mist all year round. It was packed with poisonous bugs and dangerous beasts. Most of them were immune to poison and carried deadly venom of their own.
At the edge of the mountain, inside a forest of purple-red needle pines.
The Wanqing Gate group used purple-red branches to build a large fenced enclosure. Following Wei He’s instructions, they hung harvested black poison thorns and vines all over it.
Inside the ring of fences sat several newly built wooden huts.
For these martial arts masters, chopping trees did not require axes or knives. Building huts was as easy as stacking blocks.
Between the huts, someone had even cleared a small plot of land and planted some fruits and vegetables.
In the open space between the huts, Wei He and Wan Qingqing stood facing each other, slowly adjusting their breathing.
The remaining disciples of the Wanqing Courtyard stood in a circle around them.
Pan Rui was pushing a wooden wheelchair. On it sat Wan Ling, whose injuries had not yet fully healed.
"The meat fields for both sects are set up now. But we still need a large amount of grain to feed them," Wei He said calmly. "Senior Sister, if you want to go into the city with me, I need to see if you have recovered from your injuries. A quick test is necessary."
"...I understand." Wan Qingqing nodded, looking at Wei He with a complicated expression.
Although Wan Ling had stayed quiet, Wan Qingqing had heard all the rumors coming out of Xuanjing City over the past few days.
Wei He had acted alone. He poisoned over five hundred soldiers of the Red Sun Army and forced back the military commander, Zhou Xingtong.
Such a massive event was almost hard to believe.
But as a martial artist, it was easy to disguise herself, change her look, and head out to check if the news was real.
So right now, she knew the truth. It really was Wei He who stepped in and saved the entire Wanqing Gate, especially her and her mother.
Because of this, her feelings toward Wei He were extremely mixed.
"Alright, let's begin. Master, please witness this." Wei He nodded slightly to Wan Ling.
Faced with Wei He's gaze, Wan Ling felt a bit awkward, but she still nodded in agreement.
"Okay."
A soft breeze blew past, dragging a trail of mist between them.
A thin ribbon of mist drifted slowly right between the two.
Swoosh!
They moved at the same time. Two shadows crashed together instantly.
Bang!
The impact scattered the surrounding mist.
Wei He blocked left and right with just one hand. Powered by his internal force, his footwork was faster than Wan Qingqing's.
He handled her attacks with effortless ease. With simple shifts of his body, he blocked every single strike she threw.
After exchanging more than ten moves, Wan Qingqing grew surprised. She could clearly feel that Wei He was much stronger than before.
It was not just his hitting power, but his speed and defense.
In the past, whenever she threw an attack wrapped in force, Wei He had to focus entirely and use an equal amount of force to cancel it out.
But now, he did not seem to care how much force she used at all. He just blocked casually to the left and right, leaving no openings whatsoever.
They were both using the same Heart-Shattering Seal moves. One was attacking and the other was defending, making it look like an intense fight.
Yet Wan Qingqing could clearly feel how easily Wei He was holding back.
"Watch out!" Unwilling to lose, she pushed her bone force out from her entire skeleton, flooding it into her arms.
Whoosh!
The force swirling around her strikes suddenly surged. Both its density and killing power became far stronger than before.
Bang!
Yet this time, Wei He still caught her right fist with a single hand.
Fist met palm. The force wrapped around their limbs clashed, squeezed, and canceled out.
Wei He's body trembled slightly. He felt a faint trace of bone force seep into his palm. But because it was so weak, it caused him almost no damage at all.
His heart settled. He knew that his Rain-Covering force, enhanced by the Iron Ridge Armor, was already equal to the bone force of a Bone-Forging master.
Right now, even though his formal realm had not reached Bone-Forging, his actual combat strength was already at that level.
However, he could not show off this hidden card directly. There was no way to explain the logic behind it.
So he deliberately took several steps back, pretending his force was lacking, and looked at Wan Qingqing.
"Senior Sister seems to have recovered well."
"Thank you for holding back," Wan Qingqing said, raising her cupped fists.
"That should be enough. Time is short, we leave soon," Wei He nodded.
"Good!"
The rest of the Wanqing Gate disciples nearby, led by Pan Rui, stepped forward.
"Courtyard Chief, Brother Wei saved all our lives. We are not ungrateful people. If there is anything we can do to help, just give the order!"
Wan Ling nodded and looked at Wei He, letting him respond himself.
Wei He had saved the entire Wanqing Gate on his own, so he had to accept this gratitude himself.
"If you want to help, brothers and sisters can gather herbs and catch exotic beasts for us. I will give you a list of what we need shortly. These items will be very helpful for our future growth," Wei He answered seriously.
"Got it! Leave it to us!"
"Easy job!"
Several female disciples spoke up at once.
"Brother Wei, please be careful on this trip. Put your safety above everything else!" Pan Rui urged earnestly.
"I will."
For now, the Wanqing Gate people were settled. But feeding and clothing so many people required effort. The meat fields also needed a huge supply of grain. All these supplies needed new sources.
That was why Wei He planned to meet with Wang Shaojun and find a way to solve this problem.
After packing their gear, Wei He and Wan Qingqing left the camp, came out of Black House Mountain, and headed straight toward Xuanjing City.
They had both changed their clothes. Their faces were disguised with simple plant juices.
For Wei He, who spent years studying poisons, this kind of disguise was effortless.
They soon arrived at the east gate of Xuanjing City.
To the right of the gate was a board where wanted posters were posted.
Wei He had changed his hairstyle. He also used simple makeup tricks to alter his eyes and eyebrows.
By using dark powder as eye shadow, his eyes looked much larger than before. He had trimmed his eyebrows to look neat and sharp.
Add a new hairstyle, a bit of stubble, and a change of clothes, and he looked completely different.
The two of them walked up to the wanted posters.
The wanted poster for Wei He showed a fat-headed, big-eared man with a terrifying face drawn by an overly creative artist.
Compared to the current Wei He, they were two entirely different people.
"If anyone can recognize you from this, it would be a miracle," Wan Qingqing whispered.
Comparing the two, the man on the poster and the disguised Wei He looked nothing alike.
Looking closely, the current Wei He had a dark, cold vibe. His adjusted features actually gave him a strange, sharp charm.
He was still not handsome, but he looked far better than before.
"Luckily, there are no wanted posters for the others," Wei He said softly.
At the city gate, there were only wanted posters for him and five other people.
He was from Wanqing Gate. The other five were from other factions that had recently been wiped out by the three major families and the Red Sun Army.
While being hunted, those five had been ruthless. They either assassinated important figures or set fires in hit-and-run attacks, causing heavy casualties. None of them were to be taken lightly.
"Even if I were on that board, I wouldn't worry. I doubt anyone could recognize me either."
Wan Qingqing fixed her long hair. She had changed her look too. Her gentle aura was now turned into a slightly masculine, sharp look thanks to Wei He's rough makeup application.
If her old friends saw her now, they probably would not dare recognize her.
Not many people were watching the wanted posters. Most passersby only glanced at them before moving on.
Wei He noticed that more people seemed to be leaving the city than entering.
Occasional ox carts and horse carriages loaded with heavy bags were slowly driving out of the city.
The two entered the city and walked along the wide main street toward the nearest Yubei District.
Wei He had worked there for a long time. He knew exactly where the grain shops were.
With new identities, buying grain was easy.
In less than half a day, they ordered over a hundred bags of coarse rice. This grain came from nearby towns, so it was cheap, plentiful, and easy to buy without attracting attention.
After placing the order, they arranged for the grain to be delivered to a small town outside the city.
Wei He asked Wan Qingqing to go back first and bring people to pick up the food. He then took a turn and headed toward a different direction.
Before long, he arrived at Yuanqi District.
Inside an abandoned chess garden, Wei He waited quietly for a short moment.
Soon, another person walked softly into the garden alone. It was Wang Shaojun, dressed in gray outfit with a cloth hood over his head.
"Punctual as always," Wang Shaojun smiled.
"I need your help, so of course I came early." Wei He turned around.
The two looked at each other and shared a subtle smile.
"You need to buy grain, right? And other supplies? When I heard you were in trouble, I immediately gathered a batch of grain, spices, and daily necessities. I put them in Qinghe Town outside the city. Just go to my Wang family's grain shop there to pick them up. Show them this and mention my name."
Wang Shaojun threw a piece of black jade.
Wei He caught it firmly.
"Good. You're a true friend."
"You promised to help me even while injured. How could I not do this much?" Wang Shaojun chuckled.
"Speaking of which, we never had a chance to fight each other. I originally planned to have a real match with you to see who is stronger. Too bad... you are injured now," Wang Shaojun sighed.
"Did you... break through!?" Wei He froze. Sensing something, his eyes widened as he looked at him.
"You noticed," Wang Shaojun smiled softly. "I got lucky and made a breakthrough. I am stabilizing my realm right now. In about half a year, I will fully enter the Viscera Refining realm."
"..." Wei He suddenly felt like his recent breakthrough was a waste of effort.
Compared to this guy, he felt completely useless.
"Don't look at me like that. I was stuck at this level for years. If I couldn't break through, how could I face you and keep bragging?" Wang Shaojun joked.
"Fine. Let's talk business. Who is the target?" Wei He asked seriously.
"The Wild Goose Tower's push into Taizhou isn't news, but this time they sent real masters over. The Feiyu Hall and Ruyi Hall entered together. Both hall masters are Bone-Forging experts."
"What is your plan?"
"Walk up to their door and kill them all!" Wang Shaojun parted his red lips, speaking words packed with cruelty.
"No wonder you asked for my help. Breaking them isn't hard for you, but killing every last one indeed needs my help," Wei He understood.
"Don't worry. Wild Goose Tower made a huge mess this time. It's not just my personal matter anymore. If we succeed, someone in the Red Sun Army will take part of the pressure off your shoulders," Wang Shaojun smiled.
"Oh?" Wei He narrowed his eyes. "How so?"
"The situation in Taizhou is critical. The rebel army is closing in. To save its own strength, the Red Sun Army refuses to move, and the higher-ups are furious.
The whole land is in chaos now. Every state only cares about itself, and more lords are keeping their armies to themselves," Wang Shaojun sighed. "In comparison, the matter with Heavenly Seal Gate is a minor issue. The conflict between the Red Sun Army and the Taian Army in the capital city is the real problem."
"Where does your Wang family stand?" Wei He asked.
"The Taian Army, of course. The State Marshal and State Governor joined forces, and they have hidden strength behind them. Even the Wushi Sect is being pushed back step by step. They are the strongest force in Taizhou. My Wang family naturally stands with the strong."
