rules The island was split into three areas: the residential zone, the commercial zone, and the docks.
The Xuanmiao Sect was built on a central volcano, which didn't belong to any of these three zones.
Wei He’s manor sat in the residential area. If he wanted to go back to the mountain, he had to climb the volcano. As he climbed higher, he could look down and get a clear view of the whole island.
Usually, old people and children would take walks in the residential area, but now there were far fewer of them.
The commercial area was quiet too. A few shops had even shut their doors.
On the other hand, the docks were busier than usual, with more ships coming and going. Floating checkpoints had been set up on the water to inspect every single vessel.
Martial arts experts from the outer mountain were constantly flying across the sea surface, skimming the water as they moved between ships to run checks.
Before long, Wei He passed through the gate and returned to the inner mountain. Inside the underground palace of the Moming Temple, he found his senior sister, Yao Wan, eating food.
Inside the palace, besides basic trading stalls, there were a few small shops that cooked meat from True Beasts.
True Beasts were something ordinary people couldn't even touch. Unless you reached the Sense Perception stage, you couldn't even see or touch them, let alone cook them into meals.
The cooks working in these small shops were all True Masters.
A True Master cooking food—if word of this got out into the outside world, it would shock everyone.
But here, it was nothing special. True Masters lived a long time. Many who came here just to extend their lives ended up doing all kinds of random jobs over time.
Though the shop was small, it had private rooms.
Because True Masters had sharp senses, these rooms were built with special stone and fitted with sealed doors to keep sound from leaking out.
Wei He asked around among his fellow disciples from the Mountain Lock lineage before finally finding her here.
When he walked in, Yao Wan was sitting with another woman, eating.
"Come on in! Just in time to eat together," Yao Wan said. She reeked of thick medicine, and one of her arms was wrapped in bandages and hanging in a sling against her chest.
She was holding chopsticks with her good hand, devouring her food.
The other woman had the mark of the Ghost Head lineage on her body. She ate far more gracefully.
"You're in good spirits, Senior Sister. But your injury still hasn't healed?" Wei He walked over, pulled out a chair, and sat down.
"It's mostly healed, but I'm afraid of the Ghost Wind. I drained my internal force too much, so I have to use this method to buffer for now.
Anyway, why are you looking for me? A guy like you never looks for anyone unless you need something." Yao Wan had followed Wei He secretly for so long that she knew his personality inside out.
"You're joking, Senior Sister. Who is this?" Wei He looked at the woman beside her.
"This is Cheng Wan. You can call her Sister Cheng, or by her Daoist name, Wan Xuan. We've been friends for decades. She's a veteran of the Ghost Head lineage," Yao Wan introduced.
She then turned to the woman and pointed at Wei He. "And this is Wei He. The newcomer I was ordered to watch over in secret for a year by First Senior Sister."
"I've heard of him. The monster newcomer who killed two Sense Perception True Masters while still at the Organ Refining stage," the woman said with a soft smile. "Seeing you in person is even better than hearing rumors."
She smiled and nodded at Wei He.
"You praise me too much, Senior Sister. That was just luck," Wei He said helplessly. "By the way, I came here today to ask: Senior Sister Yao, after your eyes opened Superperception, what did the world look like to you? Can you tell me about it?"
"What? Did you get disgusted the moment you stepped outside?" Yao Wan immediately showed a gloating expression.
Cheng Wan, sitting nearby, didn't know whether to laugh or cry. Yao Wan was so old, yet she still acted like a child.
"It was indeed very surprising..." Wei He nodded.
"Of course it was. That's why we need to unlock Superperception in our eyes first. The eyes show you dangers and threats in a more concrete and clear way," Yao Wan chuckled.
"You won't find these things in books even if you search for them. Most of it is passed down by word of mouth in our sect. As your acting mentor, once I finish eating, I'll explain it all to you properly."
Yao Wan had felt embarrassed in front of Wei He earlier when his strength shocked her senseless. She had been holding in a breath of frustration.
Now, she finally had a chance to win her pride back from him.
Her mood brightened up significantly. As she invited Wei He to sit and eat, she poured the last two dishes on the table into her own large bowl.
Both Wei He and Cheng Wan were left speechless.
Yao Wan picked up the plate and licked every last drop of sauce clean.
"The ingredients in this shop are all True Beast meat. Top-tier knife skills, incredible flavor. Wei He, you need to learn to enjoy life.
You have hundreds of years ahead of you. If you just focus on bitter cultivation all the time, you won't last. You'll go crazy. You have to learn balance, you have to learn to relax."
She took the chance to brainwash Wei He while she ate.
"What's the point of only eating Fasting Pills? Those are just for occasional use when traveling outside. Sure, one pill lasts a full day, and it's rich in nutrients, but it tastes awful!"
The Fasting Pills Yao Wan mentioned were free items every official True Master could claim upon entering the inner mountain.
The outer view of the inner mountain had nothing, but inside the underground palace of Moming Temple, you could pick up free Fasting Pills made from blended True Beast blood and flesh.
These pills tasted terrible, so not many people took them. Most True Masters preferred going outside to enjoy real food.
Ordinary True Beast meat was nothing rare to them; it was everywhere.
Many True Beasts were massive. If you preserved them using special methods, a single beast could feed you for a long time.
And every time they did a sect mission, they could always get plenty of it.
Speaking of which, only now did Wei He realize that those True Masters he killed earlier didn't just vanish into thin air when they turned into ash upon death.
Instead, some of them fully mutated, merged into the real world, and became invisible.
Others were swallowed up by passing True Beasts and disappeared.
"Thank you, Senior Sister, but I just don't want to waste time on eating," Wei He answered politely.
"What do you mean waste time! Now that you've unlocked Superperception in your eyes, there are even fewer things you can eat.
Except for these few small shops in the inner mountain, you shouldn't even think about eating food outside. Just those floating specks alone will gross you out."
"Floating specks?" Wei He paused. "Are you talking about those fine hairs and powder I saw covering everything?"
"Exactly. That stuff is everywhere, all around us. What you saw is just the basic, surface-level layer. In some places outside Dayuan, people call them microorganisms.
It means tiny living organisms. I think that's a very fitting name."
Yao Wan quickly wolfed down the large bowl in front of her like a storm sweeping away clouds.
Only then did she pull out a silk handkerchief to wipe her mouth.
"Microorganisms are common. Some are harmful, some are harmless, so don't worry about them. What you really need to watch out for is the Ghost Wind." She raised her head, looking at Wei He with a serious expression.
"I take it you've already seen that white smoke the Ghost Wind pulls out of normal people?"
"Yes, I saw it. What is that?" Wei He nodded.
"That is Life Qi!" Cheng Wan spoke up from the side.
"Life Qi?"
"Let Cheng Wan explain this part. Her Ghost Head lineage focuses on arts that deal directly with Life Qi, so they know it best." Yao Wan gestured toward Cheng Wan.
"Then I'll trouble you, Senior Sister Cheng." Wei He clasped his hands together, ready to listen closely.
"You're welcome. Yao Wan and I have been close friends for decades, it's no trouble at all," Cheng Wan said with a calm, gentle smile.
"Life Qi is what our Daoist sect considers the total lifespan unit that every living creature is born with from nature."
She started by defining the concept clearly.
"Our physical bodies are containers, complex structures like wooden carriages or mechanical devices.
Such a body, from its materials to its structure, has a usage limit right from the moment of birth. This limit is our fate.
Fate determines the maximum amount of time our bodies can function. And what keeps the body running is this Life Qi."
Looking at the thoughtful Wei He, she smiled and continued, "This is why people often say things are 'predestined by fate'.
Fate is set by the heavens. Before stepping onto the path of martial arts, how long an ordinary person can live in their lifetime is set in stone long before."
"Life Qi is constantly being consumed every single second. It is the core power driving the body. When it drops, it means you are moving toward death."
"And that white smoke you saw being pulled away by the Ghost Wind was actually Life Qi working to resist the wind's damage."
"Can ordinary people be affected by the Ghost Wind too?" Wei He frowned.
"Of course they can. They just don't know it themselves. Being unable to sense it doesn't mean it isn't there.
Many years ago, a master from our Daoist sect proposed a theory.
He said: If a living creature were never disturbed by disasters and hazards from birth, its natural lifespan should actually be a thousand years long."
"Through careful research and exploration, that master discovered that the maximum limit of human life should be one thousand years.
However, after birth, all kinds of disasters, injuries, and daily wear and tear require Life Qi to block them.
Even simple things like changing yourself to adapt to your environment require consuming Life Qi.
That was why he put forward this idea."
"That makes a lot of sense..." Wei He nodded, mentally connecting this to the scientific knowledge from his past life.
Although he wasn't a biology major, he still knew plenty of basic common sense.
"So, this is the biggest difference between us True Force martial artists and the True Blood Path of the Wu Nation," Cheng Wan said softly.
"We try our best to extend and protect our Life Qi. Even if we replenish it, we rely on our own cultivation to rebuild it internally.
The True Blood Path, however, relies directly on outside objects. They fuse with the long-lived, powerful bloodlines of True Beasts. By blending those into themselves, they borrow the lifespan of other species, turning themselves into creatures that are neither human nor ghost."
Speaking up to this point, Cheng Wan sighed softly.
This was how her Ghost Head lineage viewed the world.
It was different from the other systems Wei He had heard about before, but all great paths led to the same destination, and they shared many core ideas.
"So, for us who practice martial arts and seek the Dao, the key isn't about constantly boosting our Life Qi. It's about reducing the waste of Life Qi and saving the lifespan that originally belonged to us," Yao Wan summarized from the side.
"Now then, do you have any other questions?" She looked at Wei He. "Since we've already started talking, we might as well cover all these basic facts right here. It won't take long."
"There is one more thing." Wei He nodded. "How long is the gap usually between each Sense Perception?"
"What? You want to do your second Sense Perception so soon?" Yao Wan was taken aback.
"Yes, I have that intention." Wei He nodded.
"Fair enough. For someone like you, the first two levels aren't really a problem." Yao Wan nodded. "This is simple. You just keep your Superperception turned on all the time. As for when you enter Sense Perception, that depends on you.
Everyone else tries to drag it out as long as possible. They wait until they can no longer suppress their super-senses and are forced into a second Sense Perception after being soaked in the Ghost Wind for too long.
You, on the other hand, are reckless. You just finished your first one, and you're already thinking about the second."
Yao Wan looked at Wei He as if looking at a monster.
"Do you think that given your current strength, you won't run into any danger?"
"..." Wei He stayed silent. He indeed held a bit of that thought in his heart.
"Do you have any idea?" Yao Wan's face turned dead serious. "The more times you complete Sense Perception, the more dangers you will be able to see...
You're only at your first Sense Perception right now, so you can only see the shallowest layer. The further you go, the deeper your contact becomes, and the greater the dangers you can touch will grow.
Many of those hazards are far more dangerous than the Ghost Wind!"
"Just talking won't change anything," Cheng Wan said softly from the side. "Since Wei He is a vital core disciple of the Mountain Lock lineage, why not ask Senior Sister Yuan Duzi to lend him a temporary sensory boost? Then he will see the point immediately."
"That... that's usually a treatment saved for those doing their fifth Sense Perception..." Yao Wan was a bit worried that Wei He wouldn't be able to handle it.
"We can let Senior Sister Yuan Duzi decide for herself," Cheng Wan replied softly.
At their level, the circle within the Xuanmiao Sect was actually very small, consisting of only around ten people in total.
Everyone they dealt with was an old friend or acquaintance. Wei He was a fresh face who had suddenly broken into their realm after many years.
While other newcomers at his level were still taking it step by step after receiving the core teachings, cautiously trying to achieve their first Sense Perception, he had already broken free from his original level.
He had jumped straight into the high-level circle.
"That works too. Maybe you really can try walking around freely with your Superperception turned on now," Yao Wan thought for a moment and agreed seriously.
