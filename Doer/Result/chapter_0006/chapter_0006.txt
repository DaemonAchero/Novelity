"Combat techniques are just ways to bring people down," Old Man Zheng said, walking through the yard with his hands behind his back. He circled around the sparring men, occasionally repeating the core ideas of the Return Mountain Fist. "The secret is simple: hit where it hurts, be fast, strike hard, and stay accurate."
It was just past noon, and cicadas buzzed loudly in the trees. Yet their noise could not drown out the shouts of the men practicing in the yard.
Wei He stood facing his Third Senior Brother, Cheng Shaojiu.
Old Man Zheng’s voice drifted over. "Return Mountain Fist has basic blocks, strikes, and kill moves. Right now, you learn the blocks first. Blocking means two things: stopping the hit or stepping out of the way."
"Stopping a hit is good, but moving away is better. Moving away is good, but hitting first is best. Keep that in mind. When you fight, think about how to end the enemy. Once the enemy is down, you will not need to block or dodge."
"The blocking stance looks simple, but it changes in many ways. Raise your hands into fists, keep your head down, tuck your chin, hold one fist slightly higher than the other to protect your face, and lock your elbows like a frame..."
The more Wei He listened, the more familiar it sounded.
He adjusted his stance according to the instructions, then looked up at Cheng Shaojiu.
He felt speechless inside.
This was the exact guard position used by mixed martial arts fighters in his past life.
The body stayed bent, and the arms protected the head and chest. The only difference was that this stance did not involve bouncing around.
Cheng Shaojiu took his position and smiled at Wei He with a grin. "Want to try a hit?"
Wei He ignored the provocation. He kept adjusting his posture, following the teacher's words to understand the real meaning.
This defensive guard had five variations, each meant to counter five different kinds of attacks.
Next came the dodging moves, which were mostly meant for dealing with armed opponents.
Which way to step, how to dodge safely, what to do after moving, how to strike back, and how to spot a weak point.
Old Man Zheng taught this big pile of set patterns to all the newcomers.
They were indeed standard routines.
Wei He's job was to practice these routines until they became second nature. When danger came, his body needed to move like lightning on reflex alone.
Only then could he win a real fight.
"Master," a small newcomer suddenly asked, "I heard Return Mountain Fist has an internal training method. Will you teach us that?"
Old Man Zheng stopped walking. He kicked the small guy hard in the butt.
"Internal training? You can think about that when you build your body to its absolute limit. You know nothing except rumors. That technique is for training internal force!"
He shook his head, walked back to his high chair with wide steps, and sat down without another word.
Nearby, Wei Ying quickly brought over hot tea and snacks.
Ever since Old Man Zheng noticed that Wei Ying was much more careful with her work, he had moved her to serve in the main yard.
Because of this, he also treated Wei He a bit better.
Wei He had worried that his sister might be mistreated, but after observing carefully, he realized Old Man Zheng did not care much for women. The old man only cared about money.
That brought some relief to Wei He's mind.
For the rest of the day, Wei He and the others practiced these routines over and over.
They worked on blocking patterns and dodging movements.
On the second day, they moved to attack moves.
There was still no sign of any kill moves. Old Man Zheng clearly had no intention of teaching those yet.
The defense and attack systems alone were enough to keep these uneducated men busy for a long time.
Wei He was only average in progress. Among those who joined at the same time, many had already given up, lost their money, and left early.
Others had better talent. They had finished the attack and defense moves and were already working on strengthening their fists and blood energy, moving much faster than him.
Wei He had thought life would go on like this peacefully, without sickness or trouble.
However, Old Man Zheng soon arranged a short, private contest with another martial arts teacher.
In local slang, this was called a Fist Match.
It was a common activity among martial masters in Feiye City.
The rules were simple. The masters did not fight each other. Instead, their students fought to show off their skills. Rich merchants and locals were invited to watch.
The goal was to prove which master was better at teaching.
"The other side is the Bat Fist school," said Cheng Shaojiu. As the Third Senior Brother, he had long finished the basics and was now hardening his fists. He had already reached the Bull Leather stage.
"Bat Fist?" Wei He asked quietly while practicing moves with him. "Have you fought them before?"
"They are old rivals," Cheng Shaojiu said casually. "We have a match every six months. Sometimes we win, sometimes they win. It is just a fight for reputation in the nearby districts."
"You are not fighting anyway, so what are you worried about? Only a few of us will go up."
He pointed his chin toward their First Senior Brother, Zhao Hong, who was standing nearby.
"Look, First Senior Brother is already at the Stone Skin stage. His fists hit like thrown rocks. He can shatter bones in one strike."
"Then there is Second Senior Sister, Zheng Hongxiu. She is the master's granddaughter. Her fists are also at the Stone Skin stage, though she is a bit weaker due to her natural frame. Still, she is much stronger than me."
"Second Senior Sister rarely comes here, right?" Wei He asked.
"Yeah, rarely. She lives in the other yard with the female disciples." Cheng Shaojiu nodded.
He slapped away Wei He's incoming right punch, then suddenly remembered something and signaled to stop.
"By the way, I got news about what you asked me to check. Last year, a group of stone sculptors went outside the city and disappeared. Nobody knows if they are dead or alive, but no bodies were found near the area."
"...Disappeared... Does this happen often?" Wei He kept his face calm, but his eyes turned dark.
"Now and then. It is a mess out there. Bandits, wild beasts, poisonous insects, and violent gangs. One wrong step and a person is gone." Cheng Shaojiu sighed as he spoke.
Outside the city wall was a taboo topic. The high walls of Feiye City separated people from the chaotic and dangerous world outside.
The inside of the city was chaotic too, but people could still find a way to survive. The outside was far more dangerous.
"Also, you asked me to keep an eye on Chen Biao. He joined the Horned Snake Gang recently, right? My cousin said the Horned Snake Gang fought with his Green Bamboo Gang yesterday over territory in Nanshan District. Many of their men got beaten badly. Chen Biao was among them. You might want to check it out."
Cheng Shaojiu's cousin was a low-level leader in the Green Bamboo Gang. Although he liked to brag, his news was usually reliable.
"Nanshan District? Thank you!" Wei He nodded seriously.
Cheng Shaojiu had helped him a lot lately. Even though Wei He helped him with his written homework in return, that small favor did not compare to what Cheng Shaojiu had done for him.
"No need to thank me," Cheng Shaojiu waved his hand. "To be honest, I know what kind of guy I am. I like a bit of this and a bit of that. I want to learn everything, but I am bad at all of it. My only good point is that I like making friends. When a friend needs help, if I can do it, I do it without a second thought!"
He patted his chest.
"Of course, I pick my friends. I do not waste time on boring people." He smiled and glanced toward a corner of the yard.
There stood a thin, dark-skinned young boy. Old Man Zheng had brought him in personally last month.
His name was Xiao Ran.
The name sounded completely different from the rest of them, carrying a quiet, educated feel.
More importantly, Xiao Ran was far more talented than anyone else in the yard.
He learned every move after seeing it once, and his body adapted to the training fast.
Old Man Zheng treated him like a hidden treasure. He often gave him extra lessons, treating Xiao Ran as his personal successor.
The way he treated Xiao Ran was completely different from how he treated ordinary disciples like them.
"Junior Brother Xiao Ran is not like us," Wei He shook his head.
When he first joined the school, he had also dreamed of being a genius. But reality had given him a heavy punch in the face.
His talent was no different from Cheng Shaojiu's. He was just an ordinary person in the crowd.
"That kid Xiao Ran... I asked him to drink with us last time, and he scolded me in front of everyone. He said I was wasting money and energy instead of training hard."
"I almost slapped his face right there!" Cheng Shaojiu still felt angry when he remembered it.
"He is only fifteen, right? Maybe he is just too young," Wei He said softly.
"Fifteen is not young! I was already engaged at fifteen!" Cheng Shaojiu grumbled. "Forget about him. Want to get a drink together after training?"
"I cannot. I have things to do," Wei He declined.
"Things to do? I know you well. Today is not the day you help me with homework, so you are free. What else could you have to do? Honestly, I hate fake people who claim to be busy training, but then sneak out behind everyone's back to visit brothels. We have plenty of those scum in this yard."
Cheng Shaojiu began to complain again.
"I really have something to do," Wei He shook his head.
"Tell me, what is it? If it is a big deal, I cannot help, but if it is small, I will handle it for you!" Cheng Shaojiu made a grand promise with a wave of his hand.
"It is nothing big," Wei He smiled faintly. "I just plan to take a walk around Nanshan District."
"Uh..." Cheng Shaojiu froze.
He had just shared news about Nanshan District, and now Wei He was heading straight there. The intent was clear.
Wei He had asked him to track Chen Biao before, and now that Chen Biao was injured, Wei He was going to Nanshan District...
For the first time, Cheng Shaojiu looked closely at his junior brother from top to bottom.
Before this, he had always viewed Wei He as a quiet follower. Wei He was quiet, kept secrets well, never gossiped, and never talked behind people's backs.
That was why Cheng Shaojiu always came to Wei He whenever he wanted to vent.
His impression of Wei He had always been quiet, tough, and minding his own business.
But just now, a strange feeling crept into his mind about this junior brother.
Both men stopped talking and went back to sparring in silence.
Cheng Shaojiu clearly had a newfound caution toward Wei He.
A junior brother who had seemed harmless had suddenly shown his sharp claws. It felt uncomfortable, and he could not adjust to it right away.
Wei He remained as silent as always.
He practiced silently, counter-attacked silently, and paid close attention to how to use force and react to danger.
Once the basic attacks and blocks were mastered, the real conditioning of the fists would begin. Bull Leather, Stone Skin, and Iron Skin were the three stages of Return Mountain Fist. They were the foundation of the school's reputation in the outside world.
They were its core strength.
The sky grew dark, and it was time to end the practice.
Cheng Shaojiu used a towel around his neck to wipe off his sweat and looked at Wei He, who was changing clothes.
He opened his mouth to say something, but stopped himself.
He had trained at Return Mountain Fist for a long time, but he had never met anyone like Wei He.
That quiet ruthless edge felt like a venomous snake hiding in a dark corner, waiting silently for the right moment.
And then, striking without warning.
Most other men were loud and superficial, acting rashly without thought.
Cheng Shaojiu pulled his thoughts back, but in that brief moment of distraction, Wei He was already gone.
