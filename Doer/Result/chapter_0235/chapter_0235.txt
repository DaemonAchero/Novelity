ROAR!
A wild roar tore through the woods behind him. Wei He did not look back. He ducked left, barely dodging a broken tree trunk flying past his head.
He and Zhu Chen had chased each other for nearly two hundred miles. Even if he knew the path back and was faster now, it would still take time. Thirty minutes at least.
Only one thought filled his mind: save them.
Qingqing and the others were facing a pack of deadly fighters. Even if the enemy wanted to take them alive, and even if those three Organ Bleeding masters were poisoned before he left, they could not hold out for long.
ROAR!
The three-headed fox roared again in the distance, followed by heavy thuds and crashes. The beast was locked in a bloody fight with that old eunuch, Zhu Chen.
Wei He said nothing. His shadow flickered through the heavy fog and vanished into the trees.
Splat!
A huge tear opened on the fox’s tail mouth.
Zhu Chen burst out with his blade, covered in blood. He tried to sprint away, but his legs were a bloody mess. The fox’s bite had shredded his outer defense. Worse, the acid in its stomach had eaten through his flesh. His skin and meat were half-melted, leaving exposed bone and raw tendons barely holding him up.
If he had not hardened his bones during his training, his legs would be useless right now.
Even so, his blood boiled. His eyes turned completely red as blood vessels popped across his whites.
"Wei He, you little bastard!" he screamed.
He swung his blade in rapid strikes, bashing back a fox head that lunged from the side. But as one fell back, two more snapped at him.
The three heads bit at him endlessly. Zhu Chen had crippled their tail, and the pure pain drove the beast into a killing frenzy.
Moments later, thick slime spilled from the slashed tail. The wound pulled itself shut and healed on its own.
Hiss!
The tail mouth joined the attack once more.
Thick poison fog sprayed from the beast’s noses and mouths, turning the forest into a dead zone. With his legs melted, Zhu Chen could not outrun it. Even with his Seven Stars Blade boosting his pace, his speed was shot.
He knew he had been played. He set his mind, swallowed the pain, and fought back.
Zhu Chen had faced death a hundred times. No master at his level was weak. He had only fallen into Wei He's trap because he did not know these inner mountains hid a Sensate beast. Otherwise, the Wan Poison Sect would be dead today, and Wei He would not escape either.
He forced himself to stay cool, watching for a chance to run.
Leaving the beast’s strength aside, its healing power was beyond any human. He could not win a war of attrition. He needed a way out.
Boom!
Wan Ling leaped into the air. She spun like a top and thrust her finger out.
A sharp burst of bone force shoved a attacker back ten meters. But as soon as one fell back, another took his place.
Xu Lu held her Nine-Node Sword with one hand and thrust forward in a flash. Cold steel gleamed like a cluster of stars.
A heavy surge of power exploded from the tip of her blade, slamming hard against Wan Ling's attack.
Xu Lu was known as the Nine-Node Sword because her force was split into nine layers. The first layer was average, but every layer after grew stronger than the last. By the ninth, she could hit with three times her normal power.
She was on her third layer now, and her force was rising fast. If her style were not focused on heavy defense, Wan Ling would have fallen already.
"Why keep fighting, Wan Ling?" Xu Lu asked. Her face was plain, her build was as thick as a man's, and her chest was flat. "The Wan Poison Sect is gone. Even if you fight to the death, it changes nothing. Keep yourself alive. You might still do what you want later. Why end it all here?"
Xu Lu liked women. Watching Wan Ling’s slender body twist in battle, showing glimpses of fair skin now and then, she felt it would be a shame to kill her. She slowed her strikes.
"A shame..." Wan Ling muttered.
Her secret technique was fully active, making her much faster than the two fighting her. She struck down another warrior, stepped lightly on the back of a running black panther, and leaped up.
"I won't get to see... Qingqing's baby born..."
Her body went soft. Her internal force drained away like a falling tide.
The side effect of her technique had hit.
Holding out this long was a miracle. She had trained in the Rain and Cloud Skill up to the seventh level, giving her a partial base in organ training. Any normal bone-forging fighter would have collapsed long ago.
"Ling, everyone must choose their own path," a faint voice echoed in her mind.
"Do not yield. Yielding leaves you no room to stand."
"Take our daughter and go."
Wan Ling closed her eyes. The man she once loved appeared before her again.
"Where can I go?" she asked in her heart.
"Somewhere they can't find you." The man stood in bright light, his face blurred.
"What about you?" Wan Ling asked.
"In a family, someone has to stand their ground." The man walked deeper into the light. "As long as you two live..."
His shadow vanished into the white glow.
Opening her eyes, Wan Ling finally understood what her husband felt back then.
Weak and empty, she fell from the sky like a stone. Below her, hundreds of beasts ran wild. The repellant powder on the fighters was fading.
A giant black tiger with two tails leaped up, opening its jaws to snap her in half.
"Yes... as long as you live... I have nothing to fear..."
Wan Ling looked at the faint stars in the night sky and closed her eyes again.
Splat!
Blood sprayed bright and red.
Deep in the woods, two fast shapes darted through the trees. Their fluttering dresses looked like white and purple flames.
Wan Qingqing spun around a trunk and slammed her palm backward.
Boom!
Her palm hit a purple umbrella.
Ouyang Zhao held the umbrella in one hand. With the other, she threw a handful of beads.
The beads flew out, but Wan Qingqing caught them with her wide white sleeve. Her sleeve puffed up like leather, sunk in, and snapped back.
Pop! Pop! Pop!
The beads blasted back and blew up in front of Ouyang Zhao.
Purple poison mist spread out. Both women backed off, landing on tree branches.
The night wind whipped their dresses.
"You think you can run?" Ouyang Zhao sneered.
"You think you can chase?" Wan Qingqing snapped back.
"Talk is cheap. The Wan Poison Sect is dead. Stop running and surrender. You might live," Ouyang Zhao said. "When Lord Zhu returns, you won't even get to die easy."
"Die?" Wan Qingqing stepped off the branch and dropped into the air.
Boom!
She kicked off a tree trunk, splintering the wood, and charged ahead like a ghost.
"I won't die!"
Their forces collided with a heavy boom. Fists hit fists, legs hit legs, clashing like bolts of lightning.
They traded dozens of blows in a second, too fast for normal eyes to follow.
Wan Qingqing’s Rain and Cloud Skill gave her sudden bursts of speed, and she pushed it to the limit.
Ouyang Zhao’s fingertips were sharp and tipped with purple venom. A single scratch would end the fight. She had cut Wan Qingqing several times, yet the venom seemed useless.
Her technique relied on poison, making her skin and bones toxic, but in pure combat power, it was weaker than the Rain and Cloud Skill.
Suddenly, Ouyang Zhao leaped back. "You have something that blocks poison?"
She looked at her hand. A cold cut ran across the back of it.
"Who knows?" Wan Qingqing turned and sprinted into the dark.
She took three steps before a thin wire caught her ankle. The wire yanked her back, sending her tumbling into the air toward Ouyang Zhao.
Ouyang Zhao pulled the silver threads tight in her hand, her eyes cold. "I told you, you can't run."
She jumped up, tossing more wires toward Wan Qingqing's limbs.
Clang!
A short dagger sliced through the threads.
Ouyang Zhao dodged sideways just in time as the blade grazed her cheek, nearly cutting her open.
"You...!"
Before she could speak, Wan Qingqing cut the rest of the wires and leaped away again.
Ouyang Zhao gave chase, but the moment she left the ground— Slash!
A cold gleam passed her chest, ripping her front open.
She dropped down, covering herself with one hand. "Who's there?!"
No answer came.
A white shadow flashed through the trees and vanished into the night.
Ouyang Zhao stood alone, her chest heaving. In the end, she did not chase. With no back up left, going after them alone was too dangerous.
