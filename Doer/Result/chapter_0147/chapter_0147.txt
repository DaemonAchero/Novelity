Tap.
Wei He stopped silently in front of the door. Through the thin wood, he could hear the noise inside. People were eating, drinking, and bragging.
He placed his palm lightly against the door and waited.
After confirming the people inside matched what the captured scout said, he focused and slowed his breathing.
His fingers slid gently along the gap of the door. Soon, he found a wider crack. He pulled a damp piece of paper from his robe and pushed it softly through the gap, then waited in silence.
Before long, muffled voices came from inside.
"What's that smell?"
"Did the wine go bad?"
"No, it's not the wine..."
Wei He struck out with a soft slap.
The lock snapped with a crisp crack. The door swung open.
Inside the room sat two inner disciples from Fushan Hall and several experts from Night Hawk Tower.
At the sound of the broken lock, everyone turned toward the door. Some stood up from the table, some reached for their weapons near the window, and others pushed their inner force to get ready for a fight.
Every eye locked onto the doorway.
The door was fully open, but the outside was completely empty. Nothing was there.
Their minds went blank for a second. After focusing so hard, this sudden lack of an enemy made them hesitate. It was like pulling hard in a tug-of-war only for the other side to suddenly let go.
In that brief moment of distraction, a dark shadow burst in from the left side of the door.
The shadow moved so fast that no one had time to react.
The two Night Hawk Tower fighters closest to the door only managed to raise their arms halfway before a massive force smashed into their foreheads.
It hit them like a charging rhino. Their bodies flew back into the air. Their inner defense crumbled before it could even form, and they slammed into the wall, sliding down lifelessly.
The shadow moved in a sharp z-shape, like a quick stroke of black ink, and instantly appeared in front of Zhao Shuilong, the last Night Hawk Tower master.
"Hah!" Zhao Shuilong knew he was in deep trouble. Without a word, he pushed his inner power to the limit.
He used the Three Phases Finger, a technique that hardened his fingers like iron to snap steel with ease. As he fired his inner power, his fingers thrust straight at Wei He with deadly piercing force.
Unexpectedly, Wei He was faster. His arm curved out in a flash, and his palm slammed into Zhao Shuilong's chest first.
BANG!
A dull thud rang out.
Zhao Shuilong's fingers stopped halfway in the air, frozen forever.
The gap in speed was simply too wide. Ever since Wei He mastered his Rain Cover Skill and unlocked the new Speed trait, he moved on a completely different level.
Wei He's figure blurred. He flashed past the last Night Hawk Tower expert, then lunged toward the two inner disciples from Fushan Hall.
The two disciples tried to strike him, but they were far too slow. Their attacks hit nothing but air as they chased behind him.
Seeing the Night Hawk Tower fighters go down in just a few breaths, panic seized them. They turned and sprinted toward the window.
Unfortunately, they only made a few steps.
Distorted air wrapped around Wei He's palms. With his Rain Cover trait active, he surged forward and caught up to them in the blink of an eye.
BANG! BANG!
Two heavy blows struck their backs. Massive physical force, backed by inner power, shattered their defensive energy instantly and knocked them flat onto the floor.
Wei He walked over and struck each of them in the head to make sure they were dead.
It was over.
Quiet returned to the room. Bright red blood slowly pooled across the floor.
From start to finish, Wei He barely made any noise.
First, he used drugs to slow down anyone below the master rank. Then, he combined his raw strength with his high-speed Rain Cover technique.
The result was total death. Not a single person escaped.
Wei He walked back to the door, closed it behind him, and pulled a newly crafted bottle of Corpse Melter liquid from his waist.
He had Jiu Ying to thank for this. Through their exchanges, he had gained many great recipes from the man.
Among them was this Corpse Melter liquid, as well as special mixed poisons that could even affect martial masters.
In the past, Wei He could never figure out the mixed poison he got from Wang Shaojun, as he didn't understand how it could affect a true master. His research had been stuck. But Jiu Ying's vast experience filled in those gaps.
Soon, the bodies on the floor began to rot from their wounds, quickly dissolving into pools of light yellow slime.
Wei He gathered all the looted valuables into a large bag. As he prepared to leap out the window, his feet suddenly paused.
At the edge of the window frame, a folding fan hung on a wooden hook caught his eye.
He didn't care much for it at first. With a quick glance, he saw the fan had hidden mechanisms, but such recognizable items were dangerous to keep. They could easily lead enemies right to his door.
So he planned to leave it behind.
He leapt toward the neighboring window. But halfway through, something crossed his mind. He paused mid-air, snatched the fan on his way out, flicked a stone to knock over the oil lamp, and vanished into the night.
Moments later, thick smoke poured from the room as bright flames spread, waking up the entire inn.
Workers and guests screamed, jumping out of bed to fetch water and fight the fire.
Far away, Zhou Yugui stood by the second-floor railing of a nearby building, watching the inn from afar.
When he saw the smoke and fire rising, he gasped in shock.
"Something really happened!" He recalled the man he had met earlier.
That muscular dark shadow, from his clothes to his cold vibe, felt exactly like the person behind the Yan Jiuhai case.
"I left my fan behind... I might be exposed now." Zhou Yugui couldn't understand why attacking the Wanqing branch had turned into such a mess.
Why did that giant dark shadow happen to show up right there?
He never believed in coincidences. The only explanation was that the Wanqing branch had a secret connection to that mystery man.
The thought scared him.
But the more he thought about it, the more it made sense.
The last time that shadow appeared was when Wan Qingqing was forced into a marriage proposal. That man had beaten Yan Jiuhai half to death, and Yan still hadn't recovered.
And this time, when the Wanqing branch faced danger, the shadow appeared again and wiped out every single attacker.
"He must be deeply tied to the Wanqing branch!" Zhou Yugui connected all the dots.
His gut told him he had uncovered a massive secret.
"I need to tell my father right away. If this is true, the Wanqing branch is dead for sure!" Zhou Yugui grew excited.
If this was real, Wan Ling would face the wrath of Yan Jianshan and all the other branch masters. Even Sect Master Shangguan Ji would demand answers.
Framing the sect and stirring up internal war was a unforgivable crime.
Neither Wan Qingqing nor Wan Ling could survive that blame!
Urgent and eager, Zhou Yugui turned and dashed toward the Tianyin Sect headquarters.
SLAP!
A hand reached out from a dark alley below and grabbed his ankle tightly.
Zhou Yugui went completely numb. He opened his mouth to scream, but it was too late. The giant hand yanked him violently into the alley.
Muffled struggles and desperate whimpers echoed from the dark, followed by a sharp CRACK.
Then, silence.
Moments later, Wei He stepped out of the alley. Carrying a bag filled with Zhou Yugui's clothes, he sped off toward the distant city limits.
He needed to find a place to dispose of these clothes completely.
With the bodies melted, the clothes gone, the room burned, and all chemical traces destroyed, he doubted anyone could ever track this back to him.
As Wei He grew stronger, his attitude toward the world had shifted. He was no longer the fearful, passive survivor he used to be. He was beginning to shape his surroundings actively.
Power gave him the freedom to live on his own terms.
After clearing everything up, Wei He returned to the Tianyin Hall and went back to sleep, acting as if nothing had happened.
With his raw strength and Rain Cover skill combined, ordinary martial masters were no match for him anymore. He could kill them with one hand.
Still, a master was a master. They were the backbone of any sect, and every single one of them was officially registered. They weren't nameless thugs.
Losing three Tianyin Sect masters and two Night Hawk Tower masters in a single night was a huge case.
Normally, even if a master ran into a Bone Forging expert, they would still have time to scream for help or flee. The protective inner energy was just too tough. Even a Bone Forging expert couldn't guarantee an instant kill.
And if you couldn't kill them instantly, they would scream and run.
Unfortunately for them, Wei He's combination of abilities was completely unnatural.
The second level of the Whale Flood Technique gave him physical strength on par with a Bone Forging expert. On top of that, his mastered Rain Cover Skill gave him extreme speed.
Combine that speed and strength with his deadly poisons and surprise attacks, and the result was total slaughter.
Daylight slowly broke.
Wei He practiced his martial arts in the yard as usual, blending the Heart Breaking Seal with the Mountain Return Fist.
BANG!
The yard door was kicked open violently.
A strong woman wearing the inner disciple uniform of Fushan Hall stepped in with a cold gaze.
"Wei He of the Wanqing branch?" Her brown eyes locked onto him with clear killing intent.
"So it's Senior Sister Lin, the head disciple of Fushan Hall. My apologies for not greeting you sooner." Wei He ended his stance and bowed politely. His face remained calm, as if she had walked in normally instead of kicking down his door.
Lin Shiling was tall, a full head taller than Wei He. Her bulging muscles looked like twisted tree roots. Her hair was braided into tight black rows that swung as she walked, making her look like a brutal warrior.
"Wei He, your Wanqing branch sure hides things well. Hand my brother back now and I might let this go. Otherwise..."
Lin Shiling cast a freezing glare over the rest of the yard. Ding Yang and the others felt a chill run down their spines, too terrified to move.
"What do you mean, Senior Sister?" Wei He asked with a puzzled look. "I only killed a few thugs last night, and they weren't even strong. What does that have to do with your brother?"
"Where are the thugs you killed?" Lin Shiling frowned, her voice sharp.
"In the back yard. See for yourself," Wei He replied calmly.
They walked to the back yard together.
A row of bodies lay on the ground, filling the air with the smell of blood. Two servants from Tianyin Hall stood guard nearby.
Lin Shiling stepped forward and waved her hand, blowing away the white sheets covering the corpses.
She scanned the faces, but her brother wasn't among them.
Yet she clearly remembered her brother going out last night with another Fushan master to handle the Wanqing branch matter. A whole night had passed, and he still hadn't returned.
"I don't care about your excuses. Hand him over today!" Lin Shiling guessed a terrifying possibility, her gaze turning increasingly dangerous as she stared at Wei He.
"Or not a single person in this building leaves alive!" She was losing her patience.
The nine branches were already on the verge of open war anyway. Her brother's safety was all that mattered, and she didn't care about the consequences anymore.
"Senior Sister Lin, we belong to the same sect. You can't find your brother, so you come here to throw a tantrum? That hardly seems fair," Wei He said in a heavy voice.
"A tantrum? If you don't hand him over, I'll beat you to death right here! At worst, I'll take a light punishment back at the sect!" Lin Shiling sneered. "Stop talking and hand him over!"
"Senior Sister, I haven't seen your brother. Who am I supposed to hand over?" Wei He countered.
"I'll break you first, then we'll see how tough your mouth is!" Anger flared in Lin Shiling's heart. She leapt forward, slamming a palm toward Wei He.
Her palm cut through the air, inner force wrapping around her arm like a sleeve of armor.
This was the unique trait of Fushan Hall's mastered inner force: Scale Armor.
Scale Armor boosted the user's defensive energy by two to three times compared to an ordinary Fushan warrior, making them insanely tough.
With her energy fully active, Lin Shiling charged at Wei He like a wild bear.
Wei He stopped holding back. The Rain Cover force instantly wrapped around his body, activating its special trait. He felt light as a feather.
He dodged to the left, slipping past her palm, and chopped his hand down onto Lin Shiling's left shoulder.
THUD!
Their inner energies clashed and canceled each other out.
Lin Shiling stood completely unbothered, while Wei He retreated quickly.
In just one exchange, he realized her total inner force was far greater than his, especially with her defensive trait active.
Using only his fourth-level Rain Cover skill, he couldn't pierce her defense.
If he stacked his raw Whale Flood strength, he could probably break through, but right now he was supposed to be a regular warrior who had just restarted his training. He couldn't use his hidden strength or poison here.
For now, he could only use the high speed from his Rain Cover skill to dodge her attacks and keep her at bay.
