For three whole days, Wei He stayed at the martial field trying to learn the technique of Fluid Reality from Yao Wan.
He still could not get the hang of it.
He understood how it worked in theory. But unlike standard fighters, he had far too much True Force to control.
To make matters worse, after his Whale Flood Art broke through, his vital blood kept increasing. It slowly turned into even more True Force.
This constant growth made his energy reserve swell further.
Before this, his force was thirty times larger than that of any normal cultivator at his level. Now, he did not even know the exact number.
He estimated it was at least fifty times as much.
Still, Wei He kept Yuan Duzi’s words firmly in mind.
Once a cultivator passed the Five Steps of True Reality, their reaction speed and power burst would jump to an insane degree. No matter how much energy Wei He stockpiled, if his reaction speed fell behind, he was just a huge meat shield.
Because of this, he did not care much about his newly added force.
What really consumed his thoughts was this new domain of Fluid Reality.
Yao Wan had already shared every bit of her insight with him before leaving the sect with a hunting team.
Wei He studied by himself in the sect for a long time, but he kept hitting a brick wall.
Out of options, he went to see Huan Songzi, a famous grandmaster in this field, hoping to get some answers.
The training field was spacious. Rows of pale grey trees grew evenly around it, connected by a mesh of silver metal wires. The netting formed a complete enclosure around the arena.
Wei He and Huan Songzi stood face to face. It was early morning, and the low sunlight hit their legs, making their stillness stand out even more.
Neither of them moved a muscle, like two stone statues.
"Not many people waste time trying to learn a skill like Fluid Reality. I never thought you would come to me for this," Huan Songzi said with a helpless sigh. "If you asked about anything else, I might give you some advice. But I haven’t mastered this domain myself, so what can I tell you?"
Noticing the disappointment on Wei He’s face, Huan Songzi changed his tone. "That said, even though I haven't learned it, I know how this realm works. Do you want to hear about it?"
"Please, tell me," Wei He said with a nod.
Huan Songzi was happy to build a good relationship with this junior brother who was favoured by Senior Sister Yuan Duzi.
He smiled and said in a calm, steady voice, "These martial move domains were first created by those fake masters. They were people who couldn't break through their limits and had to stay in the blessed lands, only able to step outside for short periods. They invented these techniques to bridge the power gap between themselves and true high-level masters."
He looked at Wei He, who was lost in thought, and went on. "It is a method of pushing physical moves to the absolute limit. You can think of it as a permanent special skill with no side effects. It works very well, allowing users to beat opponents above their rank quite easily. However..."
"The difficulty of learning it does not match the actual payoff. The value is terrible," Huan Songzi explained. "This domain only lets you bully people who are close to your level. If the gap is too large, you are dancing on a knife edge. A single mistake means death and total destruction. That is why masters who focus on this domain rarely die a natural death.
As the saying goes, good swimmers are the ones who drown. Because their combat power rises, they start viewing high-level kills as something normal. But in truth, their actual foundation cannot take that kind of stress.
Over time, dark injuries and deadly trouble always follow."
Huan Songzi’s expression turned serious. "Junior Brother Wei, do not get addicted to these minor tricks. Martial moves are only useful against mortals and low-level cultivators. Once you reach higher levels, your True Force is the core of everything."
"Thank you, Senior Brother. I understand," Wei He nodded.
"By the way, you broke through the third layer of the Mysterious Lock Skill, right? Want to give it a shot and see if you can beat me now?
My wife is heading out soon, and I have to go with her. If we miss this chance, you will have to wait at least a year for another," Huan Songzi offered, his serious face relaxing into a smile.
His wife was Chen Songzi, who was currently in charge of the sect patrol line.
"Then please guide me, Senior Brother." Wei He bowed slightly and accepted the offer.
This was not his first time sparring with Huan Songzi. But every time, Huan Songzi held back, never using the full strength of a Five-Step cultivator.
He had never even used his sealing force, spirit pressure, or innate abilities.
Although Wei He had also held back, he always felt that the power Huan Songzi showed was only a tiny fraction of his true strength.
Huan Songzi was a top representative among the younger Five-Step cultivators in the Xuanmiao Sect. Wei He really wanted to know how strong he truly was.
Through Huan Songzi, Wei He could figure out how far he was from a true Five-Step master of a top sect.
"Senior Brother Huan Songzi, Senior Sister Yuan Duzi once said that my reaction speed could never keep up with a real Five-Step master or above.
Since you are a Five-Step master, can you show me how fast a real one actually is?"
Wei He asked with deep sincerity.
"A real Five-Step master... when energy changes quality to a certain point, the speed boost is indeed very fast. We call this state the Ether Form," Huan Songzi explained. "To enter the Ether Form, you must use all your True Force to boost your body at once. That is why I never used it when we sparred before."
He paused, saw the earnest look in Wei He's eyes, and cleared his throat.
"Fine. Since you asked so sincerely, I will show you. Three seconds. I will only keep it up for three seconds. Watch closely."
"Alright!" Wei He nodded heavily. He quickly flexed his muscles, putting his whole body into a state of total readiness.
The area fell dead quiet.
Hiss...
Streams of greyish-black energy poured out from Huan Songzi, covering his entire body.
In an instant, he looked like he was coated in black mud. The mud scattered just as fast, and he vanished from where he stood.
"Watch out!" Huan Songzi’s voice seemed to come from far away.
Wei He opened his eyes wide and activated his super perception. The scene around him transformed completely.
Everything took on a pale grey-black tint. The light dimmed, and thread-like lines floated in the air.
Yet to Wei He’s shock, he still could not see any trace of Huan Songzi.
Whoosh!
The sound of rushing wind struck from his left.
Wei He tried to block, dumping massive amounts of True Force to cover his body like a tidal wave.
Sadly, just as his hand started to move, a sharp, terrifying force instantly tore through his defensive energy. The sharp tip stopped right against the side of his neck.
At that moment, the rest of his outer energy was still rushing over to reinforce his defense. But before it could get halfway, the fight was already over.
The gap in speed was simply too massive.
Wei He was completely speechless.
So this was the real power of a cultivator above the Five Steps?
"Honestly, you were just unlucky to face me," Huan Songzi’s body reappeared right in front of him. He held a short blade, its point resting against Wei He’s neck.
Clearly, he had broken through Wei He’s energy guard in a split second, leaving Wei He no time to block before ending the fight.
"I broke through to the Sixth Step six months ago. While I am not a freak like you, I can still fight people above my rank. Small gaps in energy can be covered by other factors, so don't compare me to ordinary masters."
Huan Songzi put his blade away and struck a cool posture, looking calm and proud.
Wei He stood there silently, carefully replaying that single strike in his mind.
The power gap between Five-Step masters and those below them was far too huge.
It was like night and day. The actual combat power went through a terrifying transformation.
This was totally different from the Black Cross Buddha he fought before.
Black Cross was tough, heavy, and possessed pure brute strength of over a hundred thousand pounds. But his speed was nowhere near this fast.
Compared to Huan Songzi, Black Cross was far weaker.
They were both masters, so why was Black Cross so much worse?
"Don't lose heart. You are still young," Huan Songzi began to comfort him like a kind elder. "Even though I beat you effortlessly without using my spirit pressure, my sealing force, or my innate ability, you are still very strong among cultivators of your generation."
Wei He felt a growing urge to punch him in the face.
"The further you go, the harder it gets to fight above your rank. That is completely normal," Huan Songzi said seriously. "In the early stages, those who can kill stronger enemies rely on huge energy reserves, secret skills, or special abilities.
But once you pass the Five Steps, masters start fixing all their weaknesses. At that point, a master has almost no blind spots left. It becomes a pure battle between monsters.
After the Fifth Step, a master's energy is so thick and dense that it hits the physical limit of what a human body can hold. Once your body hits that wall, you must rely on your spirit to keep improving.
Using the spirit to nourish the body and build a divine form for a grandmaster, that is the path of cultivation above the Fifth Step. Well, at least for us who use True Force.
When you reach that stage, everyone's energy capacity is maxed out at their body's limit. Once natural talent is fully drained, what people compete in is hard work, comprehension, and raw experience."
It was rare for Huan Songzi to speak this much.
"So that is why you slack off every day and still gain power?" Wei He said, speechlessly.
Huan Songzi burst out laughing.
"My body is already holding as much energy as it physically can. I am not like you. Hard training does nothing for me now. What I need now is mental training. The spirit!"
He tapped his own forehead.
"You need to keep your spirit relaxed and peaceful. That is the only way to last."
Wei He had nothing to say to that.
Afterward, he had a real trial against Huan Songzi's Sixth-Step sealing force and spirit pressure.
He was instantly defeated in every single round.
The massive lake of force inside his body was like a heavy, clumsy suit of liquid armor. It could not keep up with Huan Songzi's speed and explosive power at all.
Even when Wei He formed a thick energy shield right from the start, it proved completely useless.
Huan Songzi used a single move to compress his own force into a sharp needle, instantly piercing right through Wei He’s defenses.
Half an hour later, a bruised and exhausted Wei He said goodbye to Huan Songzi and returned to his cave.
He stood alone inside the dark cave.
He kept replaying every detail of his fight with Huan Songzi in his mind.
"If I use my secret skill, Five-Fold Dragon Breath, along with the monstrous force from the Whale Flood Art, I wonder if combining all that power would be enough to beat Senior Brother Huan Songzi?"
