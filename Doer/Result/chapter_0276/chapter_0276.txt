“In your dreams!!” The archer spat, gritting her teeth as she spun around and bolted.
Her core cultivation was clearly in leg techniques. She moved at a terrifying speed, but she was unlucky enough to run into Wei He.
Wei He smiled faintly. His body blurred as he shot forward, tearing through the air at nearly double her speed.
Within seconds, the gap between them shrank to a few meters.
Wei He thrust his hand out like lightning, reaching for her left shoulder.
Chii!!
A surge of hidden force suddenly shot up from below, aiming straight for his arm.
Wei He completely ignored it and forced his hand forward anyway.
Pfft!
The hidden force slammed into his arm, but it did zero damage. It dropped weakly to the ground, revealing a metallic cross-dart.
By then, Wei He’s heavy palm had already clamped onto the archer’s left shoulder. He squeezed hard.
Bang!
The archer’s protective force exploded. She let out a muffled groan and swung her bow backward like a club, aiming straight for Wei He’s face.
The heavy bow smashed into his face, but an invisible force stopped it dead in midair.
Thick, overlapping layers of defensive force surfaced and twisted, blocking every bit of the strike.
At such close range, the archer saw the impossibly dense layer of protective force with her own eyes.
Despair filled her gaze.
This time, numerous experts from both the unorthodox and demonic paths had joined forces to ambush Wei He. Almost every hidden power in Taizhou had gathered. They even had the newly joined Iron Flute Daoist and a mysterious master who provided items to bypass lethal poisons.
The main group ambushed Wei He, while a smaller team went straight into Myriad Poison City to kidnap people.
They thought their ambush was flawless and airtight.
They never expected Wei He’s raw combat power to be so far beyond their calculations. So many masters had hit him together, yet they could not even breach his protective force.
In a fraction of a second, countless frantic thoughts raced through the archer's mind, but she had already lost all power to fight back. Grabbed by Wei He’s massive hand, toxic force flooded her body, making her entire frame go instantly soft and limp.
“Junior Sister!!”
A man below roared in fury. He leapt into the air, swinging a heavy copper staff down toward Wei He’s head with both hands.
The copper staff carried a massive amount of internal force alongside raw, violent physical strength. Clearly, this man was another born with monstrous physical power.
“Not bad.” Wei He did not get angry. Instead, he chuckled as his other hand shot out from beneath his cloak.
Clang!
His palm caught the heavy copper staff firmly, then yanked the man downward with terrifying force.
The massive counter-shock numbed the man’s arms completely. Slammed toward the dirt by Wei He's throw, he groaned in agony and crashed hard onto the ground.
Wei He was just worrying about finding enough free labor. Now that these people were handing themselves over on a silver platter, they were the perfect candidates to fill the empty ranks of the Myriad Poison Sect.
Controlling them with poison would work just fine to keep up appearances for now.
He swept his gaze across the area, looking toward the remaining attackers who had ambushed him earlier, intending to capture them all.
Bang!
Suddenly, a firework shot straight into the night sky from inside Myriad Poison City, bursting into a brilliant blue flower.
Wei He’s expression changed slightly. This was the emergency distress signal designed by the Myriad Poison Sect. It was only used in dire situations. Using it now meant the core area of the sect was under attack.
He turned to leave immediately.
In an instant, a white figure suddenly appeared right in front of him, blocking his path.
It was not just one. To his rear, left, and right, three more figures clad in tight white robes and heavy combat gloves appeared, sealing every exit.
The old man leading them in front had a long, flowing beard, a deeply spiritual aura, and a tight hair bun on his head. On his white robe, a clear Chinese character for "Mo" was embroidered.
“The Mo Family?”
Wei He’s eyes darkened. Clearly, the small fry earlier were just appetizers—mere bait. The four standing in front of him right now were the true top-tier masters.
The four of them unreservedly released their power. Every single one was at the Viscera Refining level. Because they cultivated similar techniques, their powerful aura merged into one seamlessly, blending into the surrounding forest.
“I have long heard that the poison path of Taizhou is led by Sect Master Wei. Now that my Mo Family has just arrived, we want to see if the rumors are true,” the leading old man smiled gently. “I am Mo Huigu. Sect Master, please stay a moment.”
“You should know that a mere Mo Family trying to go against me is nothing short of seeking death!” Wei He said coldly.
“Naturally, it is not just my Mo Family,” Mo Huigu sighed, shaking his head. “The experts from the Mountain have spoken. If Sect Master Wei does not comply, the entire Myriad Poison Sect will be wiped out in the coming days.”
He looked at Wei He and added, “Also, Sect Master Wei, do not bother trying to use poison. The Purple Script Medicine King of the Wushi Sect is right here. No matter what poison you use, it will be completely useless.”
The Purple Script Medicine King...
Wei He’s eyes flickered slightly.
Indeed, under normal circumstances, the four people before him should have already suffered the effects of his poison. But right now, they showed no reaction at all.
Making a split-second decision, he stopped wasting time and charged forward like a bullet, aiming directly at Mo Huigu.
This old man was the head of the Mo Family, the strongest among them, and the central core of their combined formation.
“I’ve long heard that Sect Master Wei possesses legendary strength and superior movement techniques. Today, let me test them!” Mo Huigu laughed loudly as he raised both fists, striking out at Wei He’s shoulders.
By now, Wei He already had a clear idea of what was happening.
The previous deal between the Wushi Sect and himself had been completely broken by them.
Since that was the case, he had no desire to waste time here. He needed to return at top speed to stabilize the situation inside. Although he had backup plans, returning in person was safer.
Wei He shifted his body to dodge the dual fist strike, intending to break past them and head back toward the Myriad Poison Sect.
However, a strange and massive force suddenly pulled at him, forcibly tugging his body back to his original position.
“Under the Four-Direction Sealing Array, Sect Master Wei had better not think about leaving so easily!” Mo Huigu smiled.
Wei He flexed his arms. They felt as if thousand-pound weights were tied to them, making movement extremely difficult. His eyes turned deadly cold, and he took a deep breath.
“Is your Mo Family truly set on making me your enemy?”
“It is not that my Mo Family wants to make things hard for you, Sect Master. It is simply that the spots to go up the Mountain are far too rare. Do you know how many experts have gathered around this area right now, just waiting to catch people?” Mo Huigu said.
This was the first time Wei He had directly clashed with the Wushi Sect. It was not even a direct fight, just some small fish drawn in by the bait they threw out, yet it was already this troublesome.
He fell silent, looking off toward the distant Myriad Poison City.
Chii!
Soon, another bright red firework shot high into the sky and exploded.
A shower of red light, shaped like a lotus flower, slowly drifted down.
This firework meant that every part of his backup plan was running smoothly.
Seeing this, the coldness in Wei He’s eyes settled into a calm, quiet stillness. He lowered his gaze, landing it square on Mo Huigu.
“It seems your luck is very bad.”

Elsewhere.

Along the way, disciples of the Myriad Poison Sect charged out to kill them.

Pfft!
A heavy poison net shot through the air from afar, spreading wide to cover a large area.
Highly corrosive poisonous water and toxic smoke sprayed inward from all directions.
A few weaker men among the black-robed group failed to hold their breath in time. Inhaling a tiny trace of the toxic smoke, their limbs went soft and they collapsed onto the dirt.
The rest of the group never expected the Myriad Poison Sect to have such lethal defenses prepared, aside from Wei He himself.
It was not that there were too many top masters here, but rather that on top of all the toxic tricks, they actually had firearms!
And they were heavy, terrifying sniper rifles.
Bang!!
A sudden gunshot rang out from afar. A rifle bullet flying faster than three times the speed of sound was something even Wei He would struggle to dodge if caught off guard.
Another black-robed man fell instantly.
Having guns was bad enough, but the worst part was that every single bullet was coated in lethal poison!
Poison water, poison smoke, heavy rifles, and a hidden weapon master constantly throwing lethal ambushes from the shadows.
The black-robed group felt as if they were wading through deep mud. Every single step forward cost them heavy casualties.
On top of a nearby watchtower.
Xie Yan looked down at the struggling black-robed group below and let out a soft sigh.
“It’s all thanks to the Sect Master’s early preparations. Combining poison nets and toxic water with long-range rifle support makes our job so easy. We only need to stop them from rushing into our own ranks.”
Beside her, Wan Ling felt the same way.
The black-robed group clearly consisted of Bone Forging martial artists and even Viscera Refining masters, yet these basic tools and traps were suppressing them so completely that they could barely hold on.
“Even so, we can’t stop them from escaping completely,” Xie Yan sighed.
“That’s obvious. There are too many of them, and they came fully prepared. Killing more than half of them in a surprise attack is already a great result. Next, we’ll have to fight them face to face,” Xiao Qingyu said calmly.

One of them shattered the heavy gate with a single palm strike, leading the rest as they burst outside.
Seeing that they were about to escape with their hostage, Xie Yan leapt up gently, dropping down from the watchtower onto the high wall.
“Do you really think taking Su Zhao will save you? Without the unique antidote for the Myriad Poison Sect’s Locked Poison, he will die within three days.”
Below, the black-robed men were about to carry Su Zhao away when those words hit them like a bolt of lightning.
“Locked Poison!!?”
The leader of the black-robed men had bloodshot eyes. It was a sign that his organs were reaching their absolute limit after holding his breath for so long during intense combat.
“You actually poisoned Su Zhao!? And you used Locked Poison!?”
The fame of the Myriad Poison Sect’s Locked Poison was well known throughout Xuanjing for being utterly incurable. Naturally, they had heard of it before coming here.
They just never imagined the Myriad Poison Sect would be ruthless enough to poison Su Zhao directly!!
The reason Locked Poison was incurable was not just because the poison was complex, but because nobody knew how many types of poison were stacked together, or what order they were combined in.
That was the real nightmare.
Wei He had created an exceptionally terrifying and difficult method of poisoning people, and now, they were finally experiencing how horrible it was firsthand.
Because aside from Wei He himself, no one else on earth knew the types or sequence of the poisons locked inside.
Holding the unconscious Su Zhao, the group froze in front of the gate, their eyes burning with pure rage.
Yet at this point, they had no choice. The Myriad Poison Sect had shown its resolve: you can take the person, but without the antidote, Su Zhao dies.
They had planned this entire trap and lost so many men, only to be completely checked by this single move from the Myriad Poison Sect.
“You dared to poison Su Zhao himself! Are you really not afraid of having your entire sect wiped out!?” the black-robed leader growled in hatred.
“Wiped out? By the likes of you?”
From the dark woods behind them, a tall, burly figure drifted out slowly, stepping onto the grass.
The pale moonlight spilled down, illuminating his cold face. It was Wei He, who had just returned from the outside fight.
“Wei He!?” The black-robed leader whipped his head around and recognized him instantly. Not even the four masters of the Mo Family with their array could stop him!?

“Useless trash.”

Pfft! Pfft! Pfft! Pfft!!

Su Zhao was poisoned with a Locked Poison that only Wei He could cure. This meant that no matter what the Wushi Sect tried to do, they could never bypass him.
And the Wushi Sect’s sudden attack tonight had completely set Wei He’s mind.
Taizhou was not a place where the Wushi Sect could rule alone.
