Because Dong Ling died, a huge hole opened up in their plans for Jinzhou. The Master above lost a lot of profit. Because of this, Wei He finally caught the attention of the True Realm.
Two Masters from the Incense Sect and the Chaos God Sect were passing through Weifang City. When they heard about Wei He, they decided to join forces and kill him to clear the way for their sects.
They had done this many times before. The longer they lived, the smarter and richer their experience became.
Before making their move, they dug deep into Wei He's past fight with the two Masters from the Wushi Sect. They found out every single trick Wei He had, including his Tower State. With the huge networks they ran, every poison Wei He had ever used was listed on paper.
To the fully moving forces of the Incense and Chaos God Sects, the whole Myriad Poison Sect had no secrets left.
"We still need to be careful," the old man named Ximen said in a heavy voice. "In the past few years, Wei He showed great talent. He might have grown stronger again."
"No matter how talented he is, he cannot step into the True Realm without pill medicines or a sacred land to protect him," the man in the bamboo hat sneered. "I checked the details of his fight against the two Wushi Masters. If those two had not been afraid of mutating and had chosen to fight to the death, Wei He might not have walked back that day."
"Even so, caution hurts no one. This is the Wanfei Palace's ground. If he connects with those women, it will be a headache," Ximen said calmly.
"We are two True Realm Masters, not like those weak trash from the Wushi Sect. If we strike together and kill him fast, what is there to worry about?" the bamboo hat man asked, unable to understand.
"Fair enough. A mere Organ Refining martial artist cannot flip the sky," Ximen thought for a moment and nodded.
The Incense Sect was in a bad spot. Besides Ximen, only one Master stayed in Jinzhou to control everything. Everyone else had been sent to other states to fight off the Wu army that was pushing closer.
The Chaos God Sect faced the same problem. The Wu army was pressing hard, banning martial arts wherever they went. All martial artists had their interests crushed, and the Chaos God Sect was no exception.
On top of that, rogue martial artists from the Wu Kingdom were flooding into the Great Yuan. Most of them were desperate men pushed to the edge by the ban, starving for resources. As soon as they entered Great Yuan, they hunted down every rare beast they could find, causing chaos across the martial world.
So the Chaos God Sect was busy setting up traps for the Wu Kingdom.
The two Masters passing through Weifang City were Ximen Xing and Zhao Qing. A hundred years ago, they were top fighters who ruled across Taizhou and Jinzhou.
After going into hiding to train, they stepped into the True Realm and rarely showed their faces to the world.
True Realm Masters had to guard against disasters like the Ghost Wind every single day, while also watching out for attacks from True Realm beasts. Living in such a harsh world, you either moved forward or died. They simply had no time for normal world business.
If Wei He had not been acting so loud, they would never lower themselves to deal with a junior. Picking on the weak like this was embarrassing to talk about.
It was like a trained special soldier going to a kindergarten just to fight a little boy named Wei He. While the gap was not actually that big, that was how it looked to outsiders.
"Where is Wei He now?" Zhao Qing asked.
"I do not know. But if we grab his wife and child, he will show up. We can hide our true power and act like normal Organ Refining fighters coming for revenge. That way, he will drop his guard and rush over. When he thinks he has won, we strike hard and kill him in one hit." Ximen Xing built a simple trap in seconds.
"Sounds good. What if he runs?" Zhao Qing asked.
"I have a secret skill that boosts my speed for a short time. Plus, I can use my specialized skin technique to pin him down," Ximen Xing said coolly.
The two had worked together many times and had even killed Masters at the Second Sense level. They had answers for every type of enemy.
"Good. Let us go grab the people first," Zhao Qing laughed.
Wei He held his breath. He focused his mind into a sharp point and slammed it into the Breakpoint Bead inside his chest.
The bead cracked open. A flood of hot energy exploded out like a wild river, turning into tiny threads that rushed into his body.
He quickly pictured the key item needed for the third level of the Whale Flood Technique: the warm blood from the heart of a Karma Whale.
The Karma Whale was a terrifying sea monster over a thousand meters long. It was a giant monster that only lived in ancient myths.
Naturally, Wei He could not get real blood from such a beast, so he had to picture it in his mind. The energy from the Breakpoint Bead began to change, turning into the exact elements he needed to break through.
Hiss...
A massive amount of blood energy was turned into special mark blood, which then turned into red threads to weave the third blood bag.
As the weaving went on, the energy from the bead injected precise bits of the fake item into the process at set times.
Time passed slowly.
Wei He's skin turned pale, looking as if he had lost too much blood. This was caused by the huge amount of blood energy being drawn away to form the new bag.
This breakthrough took far more time and effort than the previous two levels.
It seemed the third blood bag brought a massive jump in quality. Wei He felt the first two blood bags release all their stored energy to help weave the third one.
The red threads were used up quickly, and the process began to slow down as his blood energy ran low.
Sensing trouble, Wei He snapped his eyes open. He reached into a bag of prepared pills, grabbed a handful of blood-replenishing pills, and shoved them into his mouth.
Though the Breakpoint Bead had never failed him, running out of blood energy could drag out the process, which would cause problems. He decided to use outside help to top it up.
He swallowed handful after handful of pills. Finally, ten minutes later: Whoosh!!
A warm wave of air pushed out from Wei He in all directions. It was not normal wind, but a wave of heat that felt like warm sunlight.
"It is done!"
Wei He's eyes shone bright. Cracking sounds burst out from all over his body.
His body grew taller and bigger on its own. Even his Bone Shrinking Skill could not hold back the sudden change. The Whale Flood Technique was slowly turning him into a giant.
Inside his body, the third blood bag now hung stable and strong, sitting right next to the other two.
"Third level... Whale Flood Technique..."
Wei He stood up. He pinched his fingers together, and a feeling of unstoppable raw power flooded his mind.
His physical strength had jumped by a terrifying amount.
It was so high that he did not even dare to use full force right now. He felt floaty and needed to adjust to his body as soon as possible.
"The power jump is huge... This level did not scale by four times exactly, but it is very close."
"Before, my strength was four times that of a normal Organ Refining fighter. Now, it multiplied by four again on top of that. That makes me sixteen times stronger than my normal self without the Whale Flood Technique... This manual is insane..."
Wei He took a step forward.
Crack.
A hole opened up on the floor. The stone plate in the room shattered under his uncontrolled weight and power.
Wei He quickly eased his strength. Luckily, with his high level of martial arts, getting used to raw power was not too hard.
After walking a circle around the room, he gained control over his output and stopped breaking things.
The next step was fine control, so he could use this power smoothly in actual martial moves.
But before that, Wei He walked out of the quiet room and went to the back garden.
Next to the garden pond sat a small rock hill made of a dense, heavy stone he had bought from another state.
This single rock weighed ten thousand pounds. It was brought here directly by heavy cart. Now, it was the perfect tool to test his physical strength.
Wei He walked up to the rock hill, wrapped his arms around it, and flexed every muscle in his body.
Puch! Puch! Puch!
Where his arms squeezed, chunks of stone broke into dust and fell to the ground.
"Up!"
The entire rock hill shook and was lifted right off the ground by Wei He.
The rock hill was not big because the stone was dense. Ten thousand pounds sounded like a lot, but it was really just three or four cubic meters of stone. Yet holding ten thousand pounds in his arms like that was terrifying.
He did not hold it for long. After testing his grip, he set it back down.
"They say those with Defiled Blood are born with monster strength. I wonder if I count as one now..." Wei He was pleased.
Even though ten thousand pounds of force was less than a heavy crane from his old world, and big trucks could carry more, he was a living human being.
Reaching this level as a human was impossible to imagine in his past life.
Clapping the dust off his hands, Wei He noticed another change.
His skin felt much tougher than before, almost like the copper skin from the Zhou Xing Copper manual.
"I picked the Whale Flood Technique just to extend my life, but it ended up turning into a strength skill. Could it be that those with Defiled Blood have such rich blood energy that it naturally gives them copper skin, iron bones, and monster strength?"
Wei He wondered to himself.
With his current strength, every simple move felt like swinging a heavy iron hammer. A slight mistake could crush someone's bones into pieces.
He needed to keep working on controlling his strength.
'Besides, my growth track is very clear to others. My power was nowhere near the Defiled Blood level before. If I show this massive jump in strength out of nowhere, it will bring trouble... People will easily guess that I have a way to build giant strength from scratch.' 'I must guard against this.' Wei He thought it through.
In his past fights, he could not hide his strength, so smart enemies could easily calculate his limits. If this sudden jump was exposed, everyone would get greedy for his secret.
Who would not want monster strength at the Defiled Blood level?
"It seems I must keep this as a hidden killer move and never show it lightly."
Once his thoughts were settled, he prepared to practice a set of Lock Heart Seal moves to get used to his body.
Just then, a maid moved swiftly down the hallway and bowed to Wei He.
"Master, someone is secretly digging for information about the mansion. Their goal is unknown."
"Hmm?" Wei He froze and turned around.
After he had killed so many people, someone still dared to target his family?
"Did you find out who they are?" he asked casually.
With all the top fighters he controlled using Lock Poison guarding the place, normal threats were nothing to worry about. To this day, he could not even remember how many people he had put under Lock Poison. Every year, he had to mix over two hundred portions of slow-release medicine just to keep them controlled.
And Wei He only used Lock Poison on true Martial Masters.
Counting it up, the area around the Chen Mansion was packed with slaves from the Myriad Poison Sect. Even a fly buzzing in would be spotted right away.
Hearing his question, the maid shook her head. "We only caught low-level scouts. They are weak, so there must be stronger forces behind them."
"Interesting." Wei He wiped the stone dust off his hands. "Change my clothes. Prepare the carriage."
He was going to handle this himself.
The guards around the Myriad Poison Sect were not top-tier. Since the enemy knew who he was and still sent people, it meant the incoming threat was beyond what normal Martial Masters could handle.
That was fine too. It gave him a chance to test out his new, explosive strength from the Whale Flood Technique.
He had heard that Tang Yan, a top general of the Wu army, used a pair of spiked iron balls on chains that weighed ten thousand pounds combined. When Tang Yan swung them, they moved like shooting stars, leaving only blurs before smashing through targets like cannonballs.
They killed men and crushed army formations effortlessly.
That kind of strength and speed took far more than just raw ten-thousand-pound power. A normal man could lift a hundred-pound object, but if you asked him to take a fifty-pound weapon and swing it so fast that it turned into a blur, no one could do it.
Not even with a ten-pound weapon.
