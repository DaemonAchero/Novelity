The beach.
Four Sky-Turning Rays moved at the speed of sound. They looked like four thin lines, spinning and attacking around Cai Menghuan and Wei He without stopping.
Loud snaps rang out as raw force splashed everywhere.
Sound travels at 340 meters per second. Wei He could only reach 160 meters per second at his absolute best. Without his trained reflexes and his protective energy to cushion the blows, he would have been killed instantly. Even so, he was barely holding on, struggling with every move.
His three Gravity Pythons circled around them. They stopped fighting from the front and moved to the sides to support him.
The rays were fast, but their attack spots were easy to guess. Beasts like these had no real brains. They were simple-minded, moving in straight lines and aiming only for vital spots.
Beside him, Cai Menghuan was much calmer. He used his copper flute to knock away the attacks with precise circles of energy. The energy formed spinning shields that blocked every strike.
Strange noise echoed whenever his energy vibrated. When the rays heard the sound, their movements turned clumsy and slow. That was why Cai Menghuan could handle two rays with ease.
Still, both men knew that fighting these beasts required a master at the Realm of Absolute Truth. They were proud of their own strength, but they were surprised to see each other match this level.
Cai Menghuan’s eyes flickered with shock. He had heard of Wei He before, but he had ignored him because Wei He’s rank was too low. He never expected Wei He to be this strong.
Clang.
Cai Menghuan knocked back two rays and noticed something bad. He quickly sent a voice transmission.
"Brother Wei, behind you!"
Wei He narrowed his eyes and glanced back. Handling two rays was already his limit. He had no extra focus to spare.
A body was flying toward them at high speed, hit by some massive force.
It was Ouyang Luo.
Ouyang Luo’s body was stiff. His back was turned as he crashed straight toward Wei He.
Wei He leaped to the left to dodge. Just as Ouyang Luo flew between Wei He and Cai Menghuan...
BOOM!
A loud blast shook the air. A bright red light burst from inside Ouyang Luo’s body. The red light turned into bright gold and exploded into a massive fireball, swallowing both Wei He and Cai Menghuan.
At that moment, a figure appeared right behind the fireball.
It was Hong Chan.
She looked frantic, wrapped in protective energy as she rushed into the flames, pretending to save them.
Then, two bodies flew out of the fire.
Cai Menghuan flipped in mid-air and landed firmly on his feet. His face was scorched black, but he had blocked the blast at the last second.
Wei He was in much worse shape. His body was burnt black, his energy was shattered, and his breath was weak. Blood poured from his mouth.
BANG.
He slammed into a black rock, smashing it to pieces.
The rays chased after him, swooping down to finish him off.
"Thunder Shadow Step!"
A sharp voice shouted. Cai Menghuan appeared in front of Wei He. He spun his copper flute, turning it into a wheel of shadows.
"Phoenix Flute!"
Buzz!
The shadow wheel gathered a huge mass of gray-black energy, growing to two meters wide. It vibrated loudly, sending out soundwaves that slowed the rays down. A deep, heavy roar echoed from behind the wheel.
The four rays crashed into it at full speed.
BANG! BANG! BANG! BANG!
The wheel shattered. Cai Menghuan turned pale and stumbled back.
"Brother Wei, run!" he yelled. His fingers blurred as he tapped the holes on his flute.
"Blood as the guide, path to nature!"
His energy vibrated again in that strange frequency.
"Secret Art: Deep Truth!"
A bronze phantom of a three-headed, six-armed god flashed over his body. Each hand held a copper flute.
WOOO!
Cai Menghuan pushed a wave of energy to throw Wei He far away to safety. Then he turned around and stabbed his flute forward.
Torrents of gray-black energy gathered in front of him, forming the shape of the six-armed god. It charged directly into the four rays.
BOOM!
A massive ball of gray-black energy exploded between them. For ten meters around, dense energy covered everything. Nothing could be seen.
Wei He was thrown through the air like a broken doll. He rolled across the ground, hitting the dirt far away.
Pfft!
He spat out another mouthful of blood. His face was pale as paper, and his eyes began to fade.
"Looks like I win," a voice said.
Hong Chan appeared in front of him, looking down at his broken body.
"To stay awake after my poisonous fireworks and block two rays... no wonder you killed my three students."
"A pity..."
Hong Chan sighed and pointed her finger at Wei He’s forehead. Black energy glowed at her fingertip, easily piercing Wei He’s weak defensive layer.
Pfft!
A ring of bright gray light exploded from Wei He’s body, blocking her finger completely.
"As expected, everyone on the hit list has one of these," Hong Chan said, pulling her hand back to wait.
When she killed Xuan Zhen and Xuan Ning earlier, they had the same protective barrier. It was strong, but it did not last long. She only needed to wait it out.
"Hong Chan! Are you insane?!"
Cai Menghuan roared from a distance. Black veins bulged across his face as he pushed his body past its limits.
On the ground beside him, two rays lay dead. The other two had fled.
Cai Menghuan moved with terrifying speed, hitting over 200 meters per second. In a flash, he stood between Hong Chan and Wei He.
"I am a Dao Child of the Xuanmiao Sect! I will not let any disciple die in front of me!"
His eyes were cold and firm, filled with absolute resolve.
He gripped his copper flute and lowered his stance.
"Hong Chan... you really are the spy for the Thousand-Face Demon Lord!"
"No... you ARE the Thousand-Face Demon Lord!"
He had figured out her true identity.
"Impressive," the Thousand-Face Demon Lord clapped slowly. "Too bad you are poisoned and badly hurt. What can you do now?"
With Wei He down, Cai Menghuan was the only obstacle left.
"If you want to die so badly, I will grant your wish!"
The Demon Lord’s face twisted, opening a wide mouth with sharp fangs.
"Thousand Strikes!"
He reached out his hand and clawed the air.
Sharp blades of energy spawned out of nowhere, slashing against Cai Menghuan’s defense. The blade-like energy came in a relentless wave, exploding repeatedly.
Cai Menghuan’s energy was destroyed. He was pushed back step by step, his eyes turning blood-red.
Just then, the invisible shield around Wei He faded away. Its time was up.
The Demon Lord smiled and vanished, entering the Misty State. Moving through the hidden dimension, he formed a massive three-meter halberd out of raw force.
'Secret Art: Sea Dragon Halberd!' He swung the weapon in dead silence.
A blade of force, ten times stronger than before, slashed toward Cai Menghuan. It gathered for a second, then exploded like a tidal wave, covering everything.
BUZZ!
A second invisible shield flared up around Cai Menghuan, scattering the attack. It was his own personal barrier.
"That makes two," the Demon Lord said calmly. He stepped around Cai Menghuan and reached for Wei He.
Clang!
Cai Menghuan’s copper flute actually blocked his speed.
"GET OUT!"
A wild, violent force exploded from the flute, knocking the Demon Lord’s hand away.
Even though Cai Menghuan was heavily injured, he should not have been able to block this attack.
The Demon Lord backed away quickly and looked at his hand. Fine cracks had opened on his palm.
"This is... Dragon Blood?!" His pupils shrank. He recognized the legendary item that had caused so much bloodshed in the Grand Yuan empire.
"You... actually drank Dragon Blood?!"
Real Dragon Blood did not just improve talent. It gave the user a terrifying bloodline ability: Dragon Shift.
Cai Menghuan’s forehead was bulging, growing a pair of small horns. Black scales covered his skin.
This was the base form of Dragon Shift. It boosted his speed, power, defense, dodge rate, healing, and mind by 80 percent.
Even the Demon Lord was stunned by such a massive power jump.
"No wonder you were chosen as a Dao Child... If you had another hundred years, you might have stopped me. But now..."
"You are still too green!"
The Demon Lord roared and dropped all restraint.
"True Body: Mirror Demon!"
A loud explosion shook the shore. Gray-black smoke erupted from his body, covering the area.
Out of the smoke rushed a silver figure, charging straight at Cai Menghuan.
"Today, you die!"
Cai Menghuan finally saw what it was: a four-meter-tall humanoid wearing armor made of silver mirrors. Its head was an irregular mirror crystal, and its skin reflected everything around it.
"DIE!"
Cai Menghuan knew this was a Grandmaster's True Body. He swung his flute with everything he had.
The moment their weapons clashed, an overwhelming force shattered the energy on Cai Menghuan’s flute. The force acted like a black snake, shooting up the flute, tearing through his arm, and aiming straight for his heart.
The Demon Lord went all out to kill Cai Menghuan instantly. He had fought geniuses before; he knew you could never give them time to adapt.
His first strike was meant to end it all.
