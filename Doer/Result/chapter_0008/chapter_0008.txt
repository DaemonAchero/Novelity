Wei He blew out a tired breath. He had trained three full rounds straight, stopping only when his hands turned bright red and couldn't take the pain anymore. He sighed and stepped back to let the next person take a turn.
"How is it going?" Cheng Shaojiu, the third senior brother, asked from the side. He was chewing on a piece of dried meat. It looked like a dried rat, with a skinny little tail sticking out of his mouth. It looked pretty gross.
"It's alright," Wei He nodded.
"Skin-grinding takes real willpower," Cheng Shaojiu smiled. "It hurts like hell. If it were anyone else, I’d worry they couldn't take it. But you? You'll be fine."
He leaned in closer, and his smile faded a bit.
"Chen Biao is dead. They found him a few days ago in his own yard."
He kept his voice low, clearly dropping a hint.
In these dark times, seeing a dead body was nothing special. Nobody cared if a low-life thug died.
But Chen Biao was different.
Cheng Shaojiu knew it, and Wei He knew it too.
"Oh," Wei He replied with a blank face.
"His two goons died with him," Cheng Shaojiu tapped Wei He on the shoulder, his expression a mix of emotions.
He had a whole new respect for how ruthless this younger brother could be.
Just a short while ago, he told Wei He that Chen Biao was injured. Now all three were dead. There was no way it was just a coincidence.
If anyone said Wei He didn't do it, he would never believe them.
Thinking back on how fast and cold-blooded Wei He acted, Cheng Shaojiu felt a chill go down his spine.
He had spent a long time at this martial arts school and seen plenty of young guys come and go. But he had never seen anyone with Wei He's vicious drive and instant action.
Still, people in his own family used similar methods, so he didn't judge.
"What a world we live in..." he sighed.
Wei He stayed quiet. He knew Cheng Shaojiu had guessed the truth. But without proof, nobody could do anything to him.
"Anyway," Cheng Shaojiu quickly changed the subject, switching back to his usual tone. "Pork prices in the city went up again. We martial artists need meat to stay strong. Sure, we can eat other meats, but eating stuff like this dried rat just doesn't feel right."
Wei He listened, throwing in a small response now and then just to show he was paying attention.
The two quickly moved on to other topics.
Before long, Eldest Senior Brother Zhao Hong walked into the yard with a straight face. Behind him were a few female students wearing martial arts robes.
"Master."
Most of these female students were built thick and heavy. Only one stood out. She had good looks, a slim body, full curves, and smooth, delicate skin.
Compared to her, even Wei He's second sister, Wei Ying, looked plain.
Most of the men practicing skin-grinding turned their eyes right toward her.
She even carried a clean, neat look that poor people simply didn't have.
Wei He noticed the group too and couldn't help looking at that main girl a couple extra times.
"The female branch closed down and merged with us," Cheng Shaojiu whispered from the side. "They said they didn't have enough people."
"Third Brother, you know everything around here," a man nearby leaned in and smiled. "Do you know who that pretty girl is?"
"Of course I do," Cheng Shaojiu nodded. "But don't get any ideas. Her name is Jiang Su. She is the second daughter of the rich Jiang family in town. She has plenty of money, and her talent was top-tier over at the women's branch. I'd say only Xiao Ran is slightly better than her. But the main thing is, she has a terrible temper." He shook his head, clearly not liking her much.
Wei He stood beside them listening in silence, not joining the talk.
These days, he was secretly looking for news about his oldest sister and his parents.
His oldest sister was in trouble because the Black Water Gang was losing members and slowly falling apart.
As for his parents, the only thing he got from Cheng Shaojiu was a confirmation that they were missing.
Other than that, he had no leads.
Because of this, he had zero interest in girls right now. All he thought about every day was training hard. Once he built up enough strength to protect himself, he would secretly track down his family.
No matter what, his parents and oldest sister had given him love and support when he first arrived in this world.
He didn't care about blood ties. He only cared about real bonds. Blood didn't matter to him, but he had to pay back this kindness.
"Look at that, everyone who went to talk to her got turned away," Cheng Shaojiu laughed, enjoying the sight. "That Junior Sister Jiang is not in our league. You guys better wake up."
Wei He looked over.
Sure enough, the brothers who tried to talk to her were all brushed off by her cold face.
Jiang Su looked left and right, then walked to a lonely corner by herself and started skin-grinding. It was hard to say why a girl like her would choose a martial art like Return Mountain Fist, which damaged her own skin.
Cheng Shaojiu and the others gossiped for a bit longer, then drifted apart when there was nothing left to say.
Wei He didn't care either. He rested for a while, then stepped up again for his second round of skin-grinding.
Over the past few days, he felt the Break-Through Bead on his chest getting closer to completion. The very last bit of black color was about to fill up.
Because of this, he felt a surge of hope, giving him extra energy as he trained.
Yet, the more he waited for that last spot of black to fill, the slower it seemed to go.
Time moved fast.
Days of painful training passed by. The black shade inside the Break-Through Bead grew darker and thicker, and the remaining white spot grew smaller and smaller.
At the same time, the skin-grinding phase entered a deeper stage.
According to Old Man Zheng's rules, students were only given two months for skin-grinding. If someone couldn't finish it within two months, they would never succeed.
It simply meant they lacked talent, and staying longer wouldn't lead to anything anyway.
There were six other men doing skin-grinding alongside Wei He.
Counting Wei He, that made seven in total.
Wei He kept his head down and trained like mad, not saying a single word.
Then came a quiet morning.
Third Senior Brother Cheng Shaojiu was unusually silent. It felt strange.
Wei He just took off his outer coat and pulled up his sleeves to train, but he saw two brothers who usually trained with him wearing normal clothes and carrying bags. They quietly slipped out through the side door.
"Why aren't they training at this hour...?" someone nearby asked in surprise.
"They're not training anymore. They won't ever come back," Cheng Shaojiu said in a plain voice.
Everyone around understood immediately and fell silent.
Only a new student who joined this month looked lost, not understanding what was happening.
Not far away, Old Man Zheng was showing off again, teaching the basic moves of Return Mountain Fist to the new recruits.
Every now and then, he whipped someone with a thin stick.
The new guys could only swallow the pain. Some ran to fetch snacks from the inner room and handed them over to please him.
Old Zheng kept dragging his feet and wasting time, just like he did when Wei He first joined.
Wei He stayed silent and counted how much time he had left for his skin-grinding.
Half a month. If he couldn't break through by then, he had no choice left. He would have to leave in shame.
With that thought in mind, he silently rolled up his sleeves, walked over to a wooden tub, and began a new day of skin-grinding.
Cheng Shaojiu was unusually quiet today. He walked over to a corner by himself and started sparring with others.
The large yard was split into three areas: one for beginners, one for skin-grinding, and one for real combat practice. White chalk lines drawn on the ground kept the areas strictly separated.
Days kept passing by.
The two-month deadline drew closer every day.
Soon, two more students couldn't take the mental pressure and left early.
And new faces joined the skin-grinding line to take their spots.
Wei He worked hard in silence, but the redness on his fists always faded away too fast. He just couldn’t turn his skin as tough as ox hide.
Only by reaching the ox-hide stage could a student stay and learn the real moves. Without this foundation, learning Return Mountain Fist was impossible, and staying around was useless.
Soon, there were only five days left.
Inside his own house.
It was late afternoon. Wei He returned from the yard, sat on a stool to rest, and was about to get up to eat.
With a soft click, the wooden door was gently pushed open.
His second sister, Wei Ying, walked in quietly. She was holding a small gray cloth bag.
Before she even stepped close, a strong smell of medicine drifted out from the bag.
Wei Ying quickly shut the door behind her and slipped the wooden bolt into place.
"Xiao He, this is for you!" She walked over and handed the small bag to her brother.
"Second Sister, what is this?" Wei He took it with a confused look. He opened the gray cloth bag, only to find another layer of gray cloth wrapped inside.
After untying three layers of cloth, he found a yellow paper packet at the center. On the outside of the packet, three large words were written in bright red ink: Blood Nourishing Powder.
The three words were clear as day.
"This is Blood Nourishing Powder!" Wei He froze. He often went to the pharmacy to buy coin-bug meat, so he knew this recipe very well.
It was a special formula meant to rebuild blood and energy. It worked wonders, but it was very expensive.
"Don't worry, I didn't steal it, and I didn't spend money on it," Wei Ying smiled, a hint of shy warmth showing on her flushed face.
"I clean up the medicine storehouse for Master Zheng every day. There are lots of broken pieces and leftover bits inside. I collected them piece by piece for a long time just to make this small packet. Just don't mind that they are crushed too small."
Hearing this, Wei He carefully opened the paper packet and looked inside through the gap.
Sure enough, the contents were all tiny bits and pieces, crushed very fine.
The largest piece was no bigger than a fingernail, and the smallest was the size of a grain of rice.
He couldn't imagine how much time and effort Wei Ying had spent just to gather this little packet of medicine.
Wei He fell silent for a moment, then gave a firm nod.
"I understand. Don't worry, I will definitely pass!"
"Mm, I believe in you!" Wei Ying nodded back with serious eyes.
They talked in low voices for a bit longer until dusk settled in.
Wei He then got up and walked Wei Ying back to her rented room near the martial arts school.
Back home, Wei He silently mixed the Blood Nourishing Powder with water and put it on to boil right away.
While it boiled, he pulled open his shirt at his chest and looked down at the Break-Through Bead.
The mark on his chest had turned completely black a few days ago.
A strange urge made him feel as if a balloon ready to burst was hanging on his chest.
With just a tiny poke, he could pop the balloon and unleash the power of the Break-Through Bead.
Yet he kept holding back. He wanted to rely on his own hard work to break through the skin-grinding stage of Return Mountain Fist.
But as the deadline drew closer and closer, the hope in his heart grew smaller and smaller.
"Sometimes, you just have to admit that people are born different."
Wei He watched the flames under the stove, placed the medicine pot onto the fire, and let it cook.
He knew that if he failed the skin-grinding test, he would have to leave the Return Mountain Fist yard, and his journey in martial arts would end right here.
