Threat Wan Qingqing looked at her mother. Unlike her mother, she had a mind of her own. The young man named Wan Xuetian had passed the blood test and proved to be her mother's brother.
Still, no one wanted to leave their peaceful life here and move to Central Continent so suddenly.
"Mother, what do you think?" Wan Qingqing asked. "If you do not want to go, my husband is here. No one can force you."
Wei He was the pillar and master of the house. On top of that, experts from the Ten Thousand Poison Sect hid nearby, protecting them in secret. If they chose to stay, no one could take them away.
"Yes, Grandma, it is nice here. I do not want to leave," Wei An chimed in with a pout.
"You... you do not understand." Wan Ling shook her head. Memories of her childhood flashed in her mind.
Back then, if her family had not helped her in secret, she could never have broken through the encirclement to reach the Heavenly Seal Sect with her daughter. She had lived without trouble for all these years only because the main family in Central Continent took the pressure and hid her location.
She had been the one who broke the rules. She fell in love, got pregnant out of wedlock, and broke a family marriage pact, bringing great shame to her family. Young and foolish at the time, she blamed her family for not standing up for her.
Now she realized her family had likely protected her from the shadows all along. Now that the family was in trouble and needed her to step up, if she hid away... how would she be any different from an animal?
Wan Ling only wept in silence.
Wan Qingqing and the others grew restless. The sisters Wei Ying and Wei Chun exchanged helpless looks. This was a private matter of the Wan family. Had they not shared the same large estate, they would not have come to ask.
"When will Xiao He return? Only he can make the call on this. Central Continent is too far away, and no one knows the real situation over there," Wei Chun said in a low voice.
"I am not sure. I sent people to Sky Sea Island, and he should be back tonight at the earliest. It will be bad if he is not there," Wei Ying said. She was far more decisive than she had been years ago.
Ever since the Demon Faction plotted against Wei He, she had cut ties with Zhen Qi. Going through so much had made her calmer and stronger.
Both sisters felt helpless. Whenever they mentioned Wei He, he felt distant. Their younger brother returned only a few times a year, and each visit was brief. Every time they saw him, his presence felt heavier and more dominant. Now that he had entered the Xuanmiao Sect, the gap between him and regular people grew wider. He was like a legend outside the Wei Estate, high above them and hard to reach.
While they discussed the matter, Wan Xuetian from Central Continent rested in a side courtyard of the Wei Estate.
He had traveled a long way because he found the last remaining bloodline of the Wan family. The Wan family had suffered a disaster. Only a few elders remained, struggling to hold on as the family line faced total extinction. In despair, they remembered a daughter who had fled to Tai Province long ago.
The family sent people to search for her. The trail led from Tai Province all the way to Sea Province. In truth, this was only because Wei He had entered the inner mountain of the Xuanmiao Sect and no longer hid his identity, openly using the Wei family name. Otherwise, Wan Xuetian could never have found them.
Looking at the white plum blossoms dusted with snow in the courtyard, Wan Xuetian felt worried. He feared the Wei family would not let her go. Wan Ling had built a life here and even had a grandson. No one would want to leave for Central Continent for no clear reason.
What was worse... the elders' true motive was not simple. If Wan Ling went back... she might face a grim fate.
The thought gave Wan Xuetian a headache.
"Awu, what do you think I should do?" he asked in a low voice.
"Wan Ling has fulfilled her duties here. One more or one less person makes little difference to this household. But the family is in deep danger. Weighing the two sides, I believe she will make the right choice," a deep voice came from somewhere in the dark.
"What will happen to Sister Ling if she returns?" Wan Xuetian asked again.
"What else? She still possesses the Linyin Blood, and her body can still bear children. She will return to pass down the bloodline for the family," the cold voice replied.
Wan Xuetian's mouth twitched. To think Wan Ling was at her age and still...
"Speaking of which, my niece Wan Qingqing also holds the pure Linyin Blood. It would be better if she went back instead. But... her husband is a Xuanmiao Sect disciple. We cannot take her," Wan Xuetian shook his head.
"Then you have only one choice," Awu replied.
"I know..." Wan Xuetian sighed. "If Wan Ling really refuses to leave, we must prepare a backup plan."
"I checked. The Wei family master, Wei He, is just a newcomer who joined the Xuanmiao Sect not long ago. He is not a big problem. We must act fast and take her away immediately. As long as we do not cause a major incident, the Xuanmiao Sect will not throw a fit over a mere new disciple, no matter how talented he is," Awu said gravely.
"I just hope she decides quickly. If enemy scouts track us here, things will get messy."
Before Wan Xuetian could think further, a servant entered the courtyard and bowed to him.
"The master has returned. He invites the honored guest to the East Study."
Wan Xuetian's heart jumped. The real test had arrived. If he could pass Wei He, no one could stop him from taking Wan Ling.
"Tell your master I will be right there."
Wan Xuetian hurried into his room, straightened his clothes, and followed the servant toward the East Study.
Inside the East Study.
Wan Ling, Wan Qingqing, and Wei He were all present. Wei He squeezed Wan Qingqing's hand to reassure her.
"I gathered information on the Wan family of Central Continent long ago. They are one of the five big families there, holding great power and resources. In truth, their foundation is far stronger than the old Heavenly Seal Sect. Their family master has always been at the Real Realm. Though I do not know why they seek Mother, this is Sea Province, the domain of the Xuanmiao Sect."
He left the rest unsaid, but his point was clear. If the other side failed to give a valid reason, he would reject them without hesitation. The Wan family was tough, but so was he.
Soon, Wan Xuetian walked in alone, guided by the servant.
The moment he stepped inside, his eyes swept over Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing before landing on Wei He, who sat in the main seat.
"Wan Xuetian of the Wan family," he bowed, keeping things strictly formal without claiming kinship.
"Wei He of the Xuanmiao Sect," Wei He stood up and returned the greeting.
Both men sat down.
"Master Wei, do you know that disaster will strike your family in a matter of days?" Wan Xuetian spoke with sudden intensity.
Wan Qingqing and Wan Ling gasped.
"What do you mean? My Wei Estate stays out of trouble, lives peacefully, and holds no grudges with others. Where would disaster come from?" Wei He remained calm and composed.
Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing held hands, staying silent and leaving the talking to Wei He.
"Master Wei, do you know why my Wan family suffered a break in our bloodline?" Wan Xuetian asked seriously.
"I do not. But no matter what, this is Sea Province, the land of my sect," Wei He replied without a change in expression.
"The Xuanmiao Sect is strong, but can Master Wei represent the whole sect?" Wan Xuetian shot back. "Since I have arrived, it means news of Wan Ling's location has already leaked out. Enemies will surely follow. Can Master Wei protect his family every single second?"
He continued, "To be honest, I felt people watching me along the way. The Wan family has many enemies in Central Continent. I was lucky to find Sister Ling first. If enemies from Central Continent get here first..."
Wan Xuetian paused before adding, "I know you are valued in the Xuanmiao Sect, and regular enemies dare not touch your family. But what about desperate outlaws who wander the world?
"The Dragon Blood War in Central Continent dragged down countelss noble families, ruining thousand-year legacies overnight. The blood feuds born from it are endless. Do you think those thousand-year families lacked True Masters at the Sense Realm? If they had them, why did enemies still dare to attack?
"You do not fear ordinary masters, but what if Sense Realm experts come? When that time comes..."
He stopped, but the danger in his words made Wei He hold his breath.
This was indeed Xuanmiao Sect territory, but Wei He was not important enough to have Sense Realm experts guarding his family. Unless... his own force guards reached the Sense Realm. Only then could they protect his family without raising objections.
Seeing Wei He fall silent, Wan Xuetian turned to Wan Ling. "Sister Ling, your identity is exposed. Leaving right now is the best way to help the Wei Estate. Otherwise, the Wan family's enemies will send endless waves of killers to wipe out our bloodline and destroy our last hope. Master Wei alone cannot stop them."
Wan Ling was the only fertile female left in the Wan main line. In the recent disaster, almost all the young generation with Linyin Blood had died. This meant the Wan family would fall from its position as one of the five big families and become ordinary. Discovering that Wan Ling still held Linyin Blood was their only hope for revival.
Once word got out, no enemy of the Wan family would let Wan Ling return.
"Has... has it really come to this?" Wan Ling's voice trembled.
She did not want to leave her daughter, son-in-law, and grandson. Yet the desperate, tearful eyes of her parents from years ago still haunted her memory.
"It is an absolute emergency!" Wan Xuetian declared gravely. "Others may not know, but Wei He should understand. As a member of a great family, I traveled light and alone to stay safe. Even so, I faced several assassination attempts along the way. In one attack, I even ran into a Sense Realm expert!"
"A Sense Realm expert!?"
Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing were not ignorant. They knew what level a Sense Realm expert represented, and both turned pale with shock.
"Yes... some people are willing to pay any price. The Ten Eagles of the Evil Path cost a fortune to hire, yet our enemies hired two of them to hunt me down," a ruthless glint flashed in Wan Xuetian's eyes.
Hearing this, even Wei He's expression turned heavy.
At any time, a True Master at the Sense Realm was a formidable opponent. Before fighting, no one knew their true strength. On top of that, Sense Realm experts possessed all kinds of strange abilities. With a surprise attack, they could turn the tables even against a stronger opponent.
Sizzle.
A small spark flickered under the dim afternoon light.
A few hundred meters from the Wei Estate, inside an abandoned courtyard.
Yao Wan stepped hard on two men whose limbs had been chopped off, stacking them together like logs.
She leaned down, took a deep drag from her pipe, and blew a smoke ring.
"So you are the famous Ten Chickens of the Evil Path?"
"We lost to your Peacock Sword, so we have nothing to say. But the Xuanmiao Sect owes us an explanation! Attacking rogue cultivators of the Evil Path for no reason and hurting us this badly... even the Xuanmiao Sect must give an answer!" one of the limbless men with a big beard yelled in anger.
Rogue cultivators of the Evil Path were not without backing. The Three Evil Grandmasters stood behind them. The man dared to speak so boldly because most of the Ten Eagles of the Evil Path had ties to those three Grandmasters.
"Heh," Yao Wan chuckled. "You knew I was from the Xuanmiao Sect, yet you were bold enough to target the family of our sect's disciple? Are you tired of living?"
She kicked the man in the rear.
"Do you know our sect motto? When a Xuanmiao disciple acts, we must teach the enemy the ultimate truth of the Mysterious and Subtle."
She let out a cold laugh. "Do you know what 'Mysterious and Subtle' means? Mysterious means dark and unknown. Subtle means just right."
"So, beating you until you are dark, unknown, and broken just right... that is the true meaning!"
Only Yao Wan could launch a sneak attack and explain it with such righteousness.
"You demoness! No wonder people say the Xuanmiao Sect is corrupt! You are a curse to the Daoist path! Today..."
Pfft.
Yao Wan sliced off his head with one strike. The head flew into the air, landed with a thud, and rolled on the ground.
"Go on, keep talking," she said.
The severed head glared at her, its mouth opening as if to speak, but no sound came out.
"Seriously, do you think it is easy for our Great Yuan to produce a True Master? Why do you all line up just to seek death?" Yao Wan sighed, shaking her head.
She did not want to kill them. The time and resources needed to train a True Master far exceeded those of ordinary worldly experts. But she could not stop them from throwing their lives away.
Every year across the provinces of Great Yuan, people broke through to the True Realm and entered the Sense Realm. The number of True Masters was large, and with their long lifespans, their numbers should only grow. Yet top-tier experts remained scarce.
That was because many True Masters could not control their desires after breaking through. They wanted to unleash their power and act like tyrants, only to get themselves killed. They became stepping stones for other experts, their bones forming the path to the top ranks of the Great Yuan Dao Register.
The hundred people on the Dao Register represented the absolute strongest in Great Yuan. The top twenty were Grandmasters, while the rest were ranked purely by actual combat strength, regardless of cultivation level.
Although Yao Wan sat near the bottom of the Dao Register, she was far beyond these two low-level experts who had only achieved First Sense.
She ended the other man with a quick sword strike, then turned to look in Wei He's direction. She shook her head in disbelief before vanishing from the spot.
She had never seen a new junior brother who was so good at drawing trouble.
