"Father... what is the point of you pacing around like this? Pacing won't change whatever happens to the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect."
Wang Yehe sat at the wine table, looking helplessly at his aging father, Wang Zhihe, walking back and forth in the small hall.
Wang Zhihe was old. Though martial arts had kept him strong, old combat injuries were finally catching up to him. He no longer had the strength to bear the burden, so he had passed the headship to Wang Yehe.
Wang Yehe was capable enough and held a good reputation in the martial world, but he still fell short compared to his son, Wang Shaojun.
"Wei He of the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect is on good terms with our family," Wang Zhihe said, his anxiety clear. "He is facing a major crisis now, so of course I am worried. If... if only..."
Because Wang Zhihe knew how powerful the Three Path Sect truly was, he was burning with impatience. He couldn't stand how Wang Yehe remained sitting there calmly, sipping tea, seemingly oblivious to the gravity of the situation.
"Father, Grandfather."
Wang Shaojun walked in from the outside, dressed in his official uniform. Seeing the two men in the hall, both pairs of eyes snapped toward him.
"How is it? Any news?" Wang Zhihe stopped in his tracks and asked.
Wang Shaojun took off his official robe and shook his head lightly.
"No news has come back yet. Wei He... it is said that he has already disbanded the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect. The entire Poison City is empty. Everyone is gone, leaving no trace."
"What about the Grand Masters from the Three Path Sect?" Wang Zhihe pressed.
"They went after him," Wang Shaojun said in a low, heavy voice. "Word is that two Grand Masters went down the mountain. No one knows if they caught up."
He didn't know how terrifying the Grand Masters on the mountain were, but since they dared to give chase after two Inner Hall leaders were killed, they definitely had the confidence to take Wei He down.
"Father, Grandfather, don't worry," Wang Shaojun added. "Even if the Grand Masters act, they won't kill him right away. Su Zhao is still afflicted with the Lock Poison, and no one else can cure it. They won't murder him. It's just..."
Wang Shaojun trailed off.
He and Wei He had been friends for years, yet he never imagined things would reach this point. If he had persuaded Wei He earlier to join the Taian Army together, perhaps none of this would have happened.
A heavy weight pressed on Wang Shaojun's heart.
The grand Ten Thousand Poisons Sect had collapsed just like that. The Three Path Sect was like a god towering over Taizhou, pressing down with a single hand to destroy families and sects. He wanted to help, but he was completely powerless. He didn't even dare to speak up in support, fearing it would drag the Wang family down with him.
It had been a long time since Wang Shaojun felt this helpless.
He looked up at Wang Yehe and Wang Zhihe. Deep down, were they worried about Wei He's safety, or were they worried that Wei He would bring disaster to the Wang family? No one could tell for sure.
Though it was likely the latter...
"It's here! News is here!" Wang Zhihe suddenly looked toward the entrance.
The other two, both organ-forging masters, looked toward the main gate at the same time.
A series of hurried footsteps echoed outside. Soon, a soldier rushed into the room and knelt on one knee.
"Reporting to the Lord, Master Chen sent an invitation."
"Chen Weijian?" Wang Shaojun let out a breath, thinking it was news about the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect. "Bring it here."
He cleared his throat. The soldier handed over the invitation. Wang Shaojun flipped through it casually and lost interest instantly. On a normal day, he might have read it carefully, but right now...
Seeing that it wasn't news about the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect, the other two also felt their hearts sink. They had to keep waiting.
None of the three had the mood to speak. They sat in silence, each consumed by their own thoughts.
"Report!"
Before long, a second guard ran inside.
"Commander You from the city prefecture requests an audience!"
"You Feng? Bring him in quickly!" Wang Shaojun commanded immediately.
"Yes, sir!"
The guard left. Soon after, a dark-skinned, heavily built general carrying a saber walked into the hall. He greeted Wang Zhihe and Wang Yehe first before turning his gaze to Wang Shaojun.
"Lord Wang, regarding the matter you asked me to look into, the fight between Wei He and the Grand Masters of the Three Path Sect, there is news."
"What? Really?"
All three members of the Wang family were shocked. They didn't expect You Feng to get the news before them.
"It is absolutely true," You Feng nodded. "My cousin works in Xuanjing and has some connections. I just received the report."
"What happened?" Wang Zhihe asked urgently.
Out of everyone present, he was the one who understood the terrifying strength of the Sense Realm and the Three Path Sect best. And the more he knew, the better he understood how massive this crisis could be.
You Feng saw six eyes staring fixedly at him, and a chill ran down his spine. These were three powerful organ-forging martial artists. Even though he was a military commander with decent skill, the pressure frightened him so much that his throat tightened, and he couldn't speak a word.
"Speak up, quickly!" Wang Yehe took a step forward, pressing him.
Frightened, You Feng took a step back, even more unable to talk. As a commander, his cultivation was only at the bone-forging level. Facing three top-tier masters, his aura was completely crushed. Combined with his lower official rank, he simply couldn't handle the oppressive atmosphere. His mind went blank.
"Father, don't rush him. Give Commander You a moment, you're scaring him," Wang Shaojun said, realizing the problem.
"I... I am fine," You Feng forced a weak smile and cupped his fists quickly. "According to reliable sources, the two Grand Masters searched Black House Mountain all night. Then, on the main road leading out of Taizhou, they caught up with Wei He. As for the fight..."
"How did it go?" Wang Shaojun couldn't hold back anymore either. His heart leaped into his throat as he stared intensely at You Feng.
Just like him, Wang Yehe and Wang Zhihe stared dead at You Feng's mouth.
In an instant, the combined aura of the three masters surged, making You Feng's heart beat wildly. He felt that if he didn't speak right now, he might get beaten to death.
He forced his trembling tongue to move and spoke in one breath.
"The details of the fight are unknown, but someone saw the two Grand Masters return injured, empty-handed. In other words, the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect likely fought off the two Grand Masters."
Silence fell over the small hall. All three men let out a long breath.
Listening to You Feng's words, the knot in Wang Shaojun's heart finally loosened. Strangely, he felt a mix of loss and relief.
Looking back, when he first met Wei He, that guy was weaker than him, only at the bone-forging stage. Step by step, Wei He had surpassed him and turned the entire Taizhou upside down...
"Is the news real?" Wang Shaojun asked to confirm.
"It is real. I verified it many times before coming to inform you," You Feng said quickly.
"To be able to beat back Sense Realm masters..." Behind him, Wang Zhihe had a complicated look on his face.
The Sense Realm was the ultimate dream for countless martial artists. Compared to the realm of grand perfection, which was too far out of reach, the Sense Realm was a stage they could realistically hope to touch.
However, being within reach didn't mean they dared to take that step. Once you entered the Sense Realm, you had to keep advancing. If you fell back, you died. There was no second path.
Most importantly, how old was Wei He? How could someone his age reach that level? Or was there a powerful master backing him up?
"Perhaps he really is a mixed-blood..." Wang Zhihe sighed softly.
Wasn't Zhou Xingtong an example? His mother had a mysterious background, and his ancestors likely contained mixed-blood warriors. That was why Zhou Xingtong inherited immense strength and copper-like skin. From the looks of it, Wei He might be the same.
"With the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect disbanded, Wei He probably won't appear before us ever again," Wang Shaojun sighed in a low voice.
"This is fine too..." Wang Yehe let out a long sigh. That single phrase carried countless hidden thoughts.
The Ten Thousand Poisons Sect had become a thorn in Taizhou's side. Its complete disappearance was the best outcome, as it meant the Wang family wouldn't be dragged into the mess.
Wang Shaojun stayed silent.
Perhaps this was for the best, both for Wei He and for the Wang family.

Yueqing Mountain.
"Starting today, hunt down Wei He and the remaining members of the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect with full force. Issue the Three Path Order."
"All disciples descending the mountain must gather any information regarding the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect. If anyone learns of Wei He's whereabouts, do not seek death by engaging him. Report to the Three Main Peaks first to claim your reward."
An order passed down from Yunya Peak set the entire Three Path Sect ablaze with commotion.
The names "Ten Thousand Poisons Sect" and "Wei He" became the hot topics across the entire sect.
Two Grand Masters had joined forces to go down the mountain, only to return empty-handed and wounded. Word was that they faced Wei He head-on and were accidentally injured by him.
A mere master of a mid-tier sect down the mountain was able to fight off two Grand Masters by himself and even injure them!
To the many disciples who had always viewed the Three Main Peaks as a sacred land, this shock was no different from a meteor crashing right outside their front door.
If powerful figures from the Taian Army or the Nest Destruction Alliance could fight a Grand Master, that was understandable. But for a mid-tier sect to suddenly produce a master capable of fighting off two Grand Masters was unbelievable.
The entire Three Path Sect was thrown into an uproar.
Master Qingye sat cross-legged inside her small hall in the Inner Court, a tray of calming incense burning before her.
"Master! Master!"
Suddenly, a young girl with pigtails rushed anxiously into the hall.
Her name was Su Ruoxue, a personal disciple Qingye had raised for years. She possessed rare talent and a bright, lively personality, making her deeply loved by Qingye.
"What is it? Can't you calm your spirit and speak peacefully?" Qingye said calmly, reaching out to snuff the incense.
"It's about that demon Wei He from the Ten Thousand Poisons Sect, the one who killed Master Jiuzhen and Master Qinghu!" Su Ruoxue said breathlessly. "He... he... this time... the two Grand Masters who went down the mountain were injured by him too! The whole mountain is talking about it now!"
Snap.
The finger Qingye used to snuff the incense trembled, breaking the stick in half.
"Is this... true or false?" She raised her eyes and looked sharply at her beloved disciple.
"It's true! The two Grand Masters went to the Zishu Pharmacy to treat their poison. Everyone knows!" Su Ruoxue said hurriedly.
"...How is that possible?!"
Qingye had already viewed Wei He as a dead man. Two Grand Masters had gone down the mountain together using the vast network of the Three Path Sect as their eyes, backed by their own terrifying strength. A clean strike should have been guaranteed. There was almost no chance of failure.
Yet now... Wei He had actually fought off those two Grand Masters?!
"Did the Taian Army or the Nest Destruction Alliance step in to help?" She immediately thought of that possibility.
"No... it was just him. The Three Main Peaks issued the Three Path Order! It is targeted only at Wei He. They told us that if we meet him, we shouldn't engage. We should run away immediately and report back so the Grand Masters can hunt him down personally! Only those legendary big shots down the mountain ever get this kind of treatment! He is... so strong! If I had that kind of strength, maybe..."
"Enough!!"
Qingye cut her disciple off with a sudden, sharp bark.
Her face turned pale, her mind tossed in storming thoughts.
Before this, Wei He's resistance was nothing more than a small disturbance in her eyes. Even if he killed Yan Jiuzhen, his surrender to the sect was only a matter of time. But now, his actions had shattered her expectations again and again, causing a long-held belief deep in her heart to crack.
She remembered the past, back when Senior Brother Zheng was still around, and even earlier, when she first joined the mountain.
As a child, she came from a mid-tier sect. Later, under the pressure and dominance of the Three Path Sect, that sect collapsed. The masters in her sect couldn't fight back and bowed their heads, sending their young talents to the Three Path Sect.
Back then, she had thought about fighting back too. Unfortunately, she didn't dare, nor could she.
Later, after losing her original martial arts and rebuilding her strength, she was pushed around by everyone on the mountain. It was Senior Brother Zheng who couldn't stand it and protected her, allowing her group to return to a normal life.
Back then, how similar were they to today's Ten Thousand Poisons Sect!
