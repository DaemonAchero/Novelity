As the Gravity God slowly sank into his acupoint, Wei He felt a gentle, warm sensation spread through the back of his head.
For a moment, his entire brain felt strangely full and solid.
With a thought, he checked his Breakthrough Bead. Sure enough, it was completely empty.
Time to start filling it up all over again.
Wei He raised his hand and pushed a tiny trace of Truth Seeking Energy to his fingertips.
Because it came from the acupoint holding his divine spirit, and because there was so little of it, the energy was completely clear and invisible.
Wei He could only tell it was there through his mind.
"Doesn't feel much different from the first layer," he muttered with a slight frown.
Then, he brought his finger close to the side of his desk.
The small items resting on the wood—a tiny pen, some paper, a water cup—began to slide toward his hand. They moved slowly, pulling closer and closer.
It was as if his hand had turned into a magnet.
"I knew it! Before you reach the fourth layer of the Xuan Lock Skill, you can't use the divine spirit inside you. It just sits in the acupoint, building up power.
"But training the Xuan Lock Energy slowly takes on the traits of that god. It makes your body stronger in whatever area that god controls.
"I stored a God of Gravity. So now, my energy is starting to pull things in?"
Wei He tested his new power like a child with a fresh toy. He kept sending out tiny strands of Truth Seeking Energy to check how strong the pull really was.
He split his total energy into one hundred equal parts.
The test showed that just one part of energy could create a pull of over a hundred pounds.
And if he used all one hundred parts at once...
Wei He stood up and left his cave.
He walked deep into a desolate forest and stopped in front of a massive slab of blue rock.
The rock was over twenty feet long and looked like a giant stone table. Moss and dark stains covered its top.
On its side, someone had carved words: Deep Sea Weight Stone.
Marks covered every inch of the stone. Deep fist dents, palm prints, and cut marks from heavy weapons.
It was obvious that disciples from the inner mountain had used this rock for target practice for years. It was battered, broken, and beaten to a pulp.
Wei He stood three feet away from the stone. He checked his surroundings to make sure no one was watching, then raised his hand and channeled his energy.
Sizzle...
Invisible currents of air began to hum around him.
The blue stone did not budge.
Wei He pushed harder, sending out strand after strand of Truth Seeking Energy.
One ten percent... thirty... fifty... eighty... full power!
Wind howled through the trees. The energy in his palm gathered so tightly it looked like a black screen.
Dense ripples, like raindrops falling into a pond, washed across the black surface.
The pull grew wild. Dirt, grass, small pebbles, broken twigs, mud, and bushes were dragged off the ground and flew straight toward him. Even dark, nameless junk flew through the air.
Everything rushed toward Wei He.
In seconds, a massive ball of trash gathered in front of his palm.
The mountain of junk slammed into his hand, forming a thick, ugly shield.
Yet the blue rock remained frozen in place. It hadn't moved an inch.
Wei He cut off his energy and drew it back into his body.
Thump!
The giant ball of trash dropped to the dirt, punching a small hole into the ground.
"Relying only on pulling force isn't enough. This move is too weak right now. It tops out at around ten thousand pounds of force. It can't even move a heavy rock.
"For a low-level Real Master, this boost would be amazing. But for me..."
It was useless.
After seeing the terrifying, raw strength of the Black Cross, Wei He wasn't impressed by a simple ten-thousand-pound pull.
Still, this was only the beginning. The Xuan Lock Energy would keep growing. This was only the second layer. The third and fourth layers would boost his power even more.
Just look at Senior Sister Yao Wan. Her main skill was meant for sealing things, yet her sword moves were ridiculously lethal.
Wei He tried a few more times. To his disappointment, the stone didn't move. Instead, because he was too light, the force dragged his own body forward toward the rock.
"Wait... that's not bad. I can use this pull to snap into sharp turns and speed up my footwork!"
The idea hit him like a lightning bolt.
This was just like Spider-Man's webs, except his webs were completely invisible!
His eyes lit up. He reached out with his mind and locked his pull onto a thick tree trunk far away. His body shot forward like an arrow.
He began casting his force rapidly with both hands, flying through the forest at terrifying speeds.
It was noon, and bright sunlight flooded the woods.
Wei He didn't use any of his Rain Cover Energy effects. He moved purely on the pull from his stored divine spirit, leaping through the air over and over.
To his delight, the pull activated instantly. A ten-thousand-pound force dragged him forward at insane speeds.
He was already moving at more than half his normal top speed.
The best part was the control. He could change directions instantly. Whether he wanted to pull something to him or pull himself to a target, he didn't need to push off the ground. There was no tell, no warning, and no sign he was about to move.
Raising his hands was just a habit to make aiming easier.
In reality, he could keep his hands at his sides. Wherever he focused his energy, the gravitational pull would drag him in that direction.
If he combined this invisible pull with his own physical strength and movement techniques, his speed would reach a level he had never touched before!
Wei He held his breath. He flexed every muscle in his body and triggered the special traits of his Rain Cover Energy. At the same time, his Truth Seeking Energy gave him another natural speed boost.
With four power boosts stacking at once...
He planted his feet and charged forward.
SWISH!!
In less than a second, Wei He crossed a massive distance.
He shot from the blue stone and landed next to another rock over four hundred feet away.
Two old monkeys were sitting on that stone, quietly grooming their fur.
When Wei He suddenly appeared out of nowhere, the monkeys shrieked in terror and scattered into the trees.
Wei He stood there, shocked by his own power. After losing his leg techniques when he wiped his old skills, the Truth Seeking Energy had barely kept his speed equal to his old limit.
But now, with this gravitational pull added to his Whale Flood Skill's raw force...
His explosive speed had shattered his old limits!
This speed was fast enough to be a deadly trump card.
When he fought full-fledged masters before, none of them moved this fast. Maybe experts who had passed the fifth step could beat this, but he hadn't seen anyone do it yet.
He spent a bit more time practicing in the volcanic woods on the mountainside before returning to his cave, satisfied.
Next, he resumed training the Whale Flood Skill.
With the Black Thread Whale Oil in hand, he smoothy started the fourth layer.
His progress was steady, and his physical strength began to climb once more.
This brought a sense of peace to his heart.
Now that he had broken through to the second layer, it was time to go down the mountain and complete his second sensory lock.
He had to do it.
If he didn't want to end up as a crippled Real Master, he had to leave the holy land and let the cruel ghost winds smash against his body and sharpen his mind.
The ghost wind was a deadly threat, but it was also the ultimate forge.
He trained for a few hours. In the afternoon, Wei He walked out of his cave to find Senior Sister Yao Wan and tell her he was leaving.
"You broke through? To the second layer already? Well, with your foundation, the first two layers aren't hard. It makes sense you moved so fast."
Yao Wan wasn't in the inner mountain. She was at the Moral Palace on the outer mountain, training newcomers.
Two old men with white hair were standing respectfully behind her, watching her demonstrate sword forms.
Over the past six months, the Moral Palace had seen a massive rush of experts. Ever since the Xuan Miao Sect beat back the Wu army's surprise attack, countless martial artists had fled here for safety.
The buildings that were destroyed in the war had been rebuilt quickly.
Martial artists were fast at tearing things down, but they were just as fast at building them back up. In six months, Tianhai Island had become even busier than before.
"If you want to leave the mountain, you don't need to tell me," Yao Wan said, looking puzzled. "Didn't Senior Sister Yuan Duzi ask Senior Brother Huan Songzi to protect you?"
Besides, Wei He was much stronger than her now. He only looked weak on the surface because his official level was low.
"Senior Brother Huan Songzi... is almost impossible to find," Wei He said, speechlessly. "And Chief Senior Sister is too busy. I didn't want to bother her."
"Fair enough. I'll pass the message to them later," Yao Wan nodded.
"Senior Sister Yao, who is this brother?" one of the white-haired newcomers asked, looking quite pleased with himself. He wanted to make a connection with Wei He.
"Him? Didn't you mention him when you were talking earlier? He is one of the top geniuses Chief Senior Sister is personally training—Wei He, a Tao Seed candidate," Yao Wan said casually.
Wei He had no interest in wasting time with new recruits. He exchanged a few polite words and turned to leave.
As he walked away, he heard the two men whispering behind his back.
"This Senior Brother Wei seems pretty arrogant..."
"I heard he spends all his time training in isolation. He probably just doesn't like talking to people."
Yao Wan said nothing. She watched Wei He's back and shook her head softly.
Those two had no idea how the real world worked.
A genius at Wei He's level didn't need to care about what two newcomers thought.
The fact that he even bothered to nod and reply politely was already more respect than they deserved.
Wei He packed his things. He planned to head home and stay with his family for a while.
He walked out through the Xuan Cha Jade Wall of the inner mountain.
As he walked down the stone stairs, he saw a group of men and women in Moral Palace robes walking up toward him.
The Moral Palace was where the sect accepted experts who already had strong backgrounds.
Seeing them climb the stairs meant an inner mountain master was bringing new disciples into the holy land.
"This is the gate to the inner mountain. Further up, you'll see the key to entering the holy land: the Xuan Cha Jade Wall.
"Remember, if you want to see the inner world, you must pass through that wall," a mature female voice explained from the front of the group.
Wei He looked down. A female priest was leading two young men up the steps.
When they met on the narrow path, the female priest bowed her head politely to salute Wei He.
Wei He bowed his head back in respect.
Time moved so fast. He was already close to his second sensory lock. Looking at these two newcomers reminded him of the day he first joined. A soft sigh escaped his chest.
He cleared his mind, passed the group, and hurried down the mountain toward Dragon Bay.
"Junior Brother Wei, hold on a second!" Huan Songzi's voice called out from behind him.
Wei He turned around. A shadow flashed on the stone path, and Huan Songzi appeared out of nowhere.
"Is something wrong, Senior Brother?"
Huan Songzi stank of heavy alcohol. He let out a loud burp, stepped closer, and said, "Chief Senior Sister knows you're going down the mountain. She has a mission for you."
"Where is Chief Senior Sister?" Wei He asked.
"She's still at the Liyuan Abyss Prison. A few real beasts escaped, and she has to catch them. She can't leave right now. Everyone else in the sect is out on missions, so since you're free, you can take this off her hands.
"As for your reward... Chief Senior Sister found a lead on that Three-Eyed Dove you were looking for," Huan Songzi said, letting out another foul burp.
The Three-Eyed Dove? That was the core ingredient he needed to train the Five-Turn Dragon Breath secret technique!
The Five-Turn Dragon Breath could multiply his strength five times in an instant.
Wei He's heart leaped with joy. He had searched everywhere for clues without luck. He didn't expect the Chief Senior Sister to find a trace so fast.
His spirit lifted. "Alright. Tell me about the mission."
Since he was taking the sect's protection, guidance, manuals, and resources, it was only right for him to work for them.
Besides, he had been living and training in the inner mountain for free for a long time. It was time to stretch his legs.
