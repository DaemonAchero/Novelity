"I remember a while back, quite a few strange beasts migrated here from the distant mountains. Could this guy be one of them?" Wei He recalled that he had never heard of stone deer appearing near Xuanjing City before.
"Likely so." Jiuying nodded. "Alright, enough talk. Let's make the meat farm. From now on, my meat farm stays here. You help me guard it, and I'll give you a third of the harvest. Deal?"
"Deal." Over these days, Wei He had figured out the old man's background. Jiuying had no family left, nowhere to go, and simply wandered around without any ties.
Besides, he had already reached the limit of his martial path. His vital blood was fading. He didn't crave this beast meat for strength; he just used it to fuel his drug research.
So Wei He didn't hesitate to accept.
After all, he needed these resources right now.
Before he could react, Jiuying darted forward like a ghost. He threw a cloud of medicinal powder straight over the stone deer's head.
The stone deer thrashed and roared, but it was useless. Soon, its mind went foggy, and it collapsed onto the ground.
"The drug only lasts a short time. We must move fast!" Jiuying pulled out the grey, spherical parasitic seed. He stepped forward and sliced open the deer's belly with a light flick of his palm.
Blood poured from the slash, and he immediately stuffed the seed inside.
Then, something bizarre happened.
The seed seemed to come alive. It suddenly squirmed and flattened out. From the sphere, blood-red tendrils sprouted like veins and bored deep into the deer's body.
Moo!
Even while unconscious, the stone deer let out a agonizing wail.
Its body began to swell and expand, as if filled with air.
Soon, the belly where the seed was planted grew bigger and bigger, turning into a massive sphere that was almost as large as the rest of its body.
It groaned and thrashing weakly, but the corruption of the seed was far too terrifying. Before long, the stone deer went completely still.
Gradually, its bones began to melt. Aside from its antlers, the entire stone deer dissolved into a pile of pure, squirming flesh in less than an hour.
It was a huge, writhing mass of raw meat.
If one end of this flesh hadn't still carried the branch-like antlers unique to the stone deer, Wei He would have thought he was hallucinating.
"Sigh... No matter how many times I see it, this parasitic seed really is cruel," Jiuying sighed.
"Is it done?" Wei He asked in a deep voice.
"Done. This meat farm is set. Water it and feed it on a schedule, and it will grow a steady supply of beast meat," Jiuying nodded.
"Feed it? With what?" Wei He asked, curious.
"Whatever the stone deer used to eat," Jiuying replied.
Wei He looked at the large pile of writhing flesh before him. For some reason, he felt a wave of nausea.
Suddenly, a dark thought crossed his mind.
"Has anyone ever used martial artists as meat farms?"
"In the past, yes," Jiuying answered. "Later, they were hunted down and wiped out completely. Even the demonic sects find this taboo. No one who practices martial arts can tolerate the thought of being turned into a meat farm, having their flesh carved off over and over again."
Wei He nodded silently.
He watched Jiuying step forward, use a small knife to carefully cut off a large chunk of meat, sprinkle some powder on the wound to stop the bleeding, and walk back.
"Let's go. This is enough to feed us for a while."
Wei He looked at the fresh chunk of meat in the old man's hand—it weighed at least ten pounds, and the muscle fibers were still twitching slightly.
His thoughts about these seeds and meat farms grew deeper.
This technique felt strikingly like an accelerated, simplified version of lab-grown meat he had read about in sci-fi novels in his past life.
He suddenly felt an urge to research how it all worked.
However, he hadn't even settled down yet. He had no time to waste studying things outside of martial arts.
When I have time and the chance in the future, I'll test it out, Wei He thought to himself.
...
Time flowed silently.
In the blink of an eye, another half a year passed.
While Wei He focused on building up his force, the Heavenly Seal Sect also underwent notable changes.
Ever since Wan Qingqing left and Yao Hansheng became the head student of Wanqing House, Yao Hansheng began challenging the top students of other branches.
He won some and lost some, but he won far more than he lost. Slowly, Yao Hansheng built a name for himself across the Heavenly Seal Sect.
Meanwhile, Chen Lin and Zhou Xingsu—the two geniuses who had drawn much attention in Wanqing House—were approaching the breakthrough to the Force Realm.
Chen Lin came from a martial family. Guided by her experienced parents, everyone assumed she would pass smoothly.
Unfortunately, fate was cruel. Chen Lin hit a wall during her breakthrough, tried to force her way through, and lost control of her force. She damaged her own blood veins, ruining the nerves in her left hand.
From then on, her left hand lost most of its flexibility, turning into a massive weakness in her martial path.
When the news broke out, many people sighed in regret.
On the other side, Zhou Xingsu, whose family ran an inn, also got injured during his attempt and failed to become a Force Master.
Fortunately, he didn't push too hard. He only drained his vital blood, meaning he could try a second time later.
By late August, the weather in Taizhou turned cold again, and rains became frequent.
A heavy rain every five days, a light drizzle every two. The sky was always overcast with thick, dark clouds.
Amid the slant drizzle.
Inside an upper-floor private room at Yuxing Tavern.
Yao Hansheng, Sun Yi, and Qian Rui sat together. All three looked silent and grim.
Scattered wine jars covered the floor around them. They had clearly been drinking heavily.
This was the scene Wei He saw when he pushed the door open.
Today, Qian Rui was hosting a feast specifically for Yao Hansheng and Sun Yi. Since they were on Wei He's turf, Wei He naturally had to drop by for a drink to show respect.
However, it seemed he had arrived at an awkward moment.
"Senior Yao, Brother Sun, Brother Qian, you came to my place without letting me know? If no one had mentioned it, I would have been blamed for being a bad host," Wei He said with a slight smile.
"Ah, Junior Wei." The three quickly stood up.
Yao Hansheng was calm, as he wasn't close with Wei He to begin with. But Qian Rui and Sun Yi were different.
Qian Rui was Wei He's desk partner when they first joined Ziju House, while Sun Yi entered Wanqing House in the same batch.
Both knew him well.
Seeing that Wei He had already reached the Force Realm and seemed to be living well, they looked at their own failures and felt a wave of bitterness.
"Brother Wei, it's been a while. You haven't changed at all since you first arrived," Sun Yi sighed.
Wei He smiled softly. "How could I change? Every day is just training or handling petty chores. I'm bored out of my mind. What changes could there be?"
He sized the three up, especially Qian Rui and Sun Yi.
Both looked far more weathered than they had years ago. Qian Rui used to be a handsome man who had even joined Zhao Wanzhu's Yuanyin Society. Now, he looked exhausted, his old charm gone.
Sun Yi had two new scars on his face. His eyes were sharper, older, and far more world-weary than before.
The four sat down and chatted for a bit. Only then did Wei He learn why Qian Rui was hosting today.
He was planning to leave the inner court of the Heavenly Seal Sect.
"My vital blood isn't growing anymore. I know my talent has hit its limit. Continuing to train is just a waste of time and money. I'd rather settle down early, build up a business for my family, and give them a return for all the years they supported my training."
Qian Rui sighed. "I've practiced martial arts since I was a kid and spent vast amounts of my family's wealth. Going back now, while I still have some strength, I can at least offer protection for the family business."
"If I don't make it next year, I want to go back too," Sun Yi sighed. "I've set up an escort agency on the side. So if Brother Qian has nothing to do, you can join me and make a name for ourselves."
"If either of you face any trouble in the future, make sure to find me! As for what happened to Senior Sister Wan, if I hadn't been away back then, I would have..." Yao Hansheng was still filled with rage whenever he recalled that incident.
He had been away on a mission at the time, escorting the sect's cargo convoy. The round trip took over two months, making it impossible to rush back in time.
When he returned and heard that Senior Sister Wan Qingqing had been backed into a corner without a single person standing up for her, he nearly exploded with anger.
He could understand Wei He, who had lost his cultivation and would have been useless anyway. But the other two Wanqing House martial artists, Ding Xiaohe and Bai Rui, made his blood boil.
Afterward, he used spars as an excuse to beat those two up repeatedly for months. The two women now took a detour whenever they saw him, which showed just how fiery his temper was.
"Thank you for your justice, Senior!" Qian Rui raised his glass and drank three cups in a row to show his gratitude.
"Our Wanqing House is already looked down upon by other branches because of the pressure from the Lishan Sect. If we don't stick together, we'll become loose sand and get bullied by everyone," Yao Hansheng said seriously.
"Indeed," Sun Yi agreed. "A few days ago, I heard our house leader was targeted during the inner meeting again. They wanted to cut our funding to patch up the Huaqi House... It's outrageous!"
"The other branches have always been like this. If our house leader didn't have the full support of another friendly leader, I wouldn't have even gotten this branch post," Wei He agreed.
"Our funding is already the lowest, yet they still demand this..." Yao Hansheng shook his head helplessly.
He looked around at the people present. He knew their backgrounds well and had heard about their character.
The incident with Wan Qingqing had acted as a mirror, exposing everyone's true nature.
A thought struck Yao Hansheng, and he spoke up: "We all know each other's character. Rather than mixing with ungrateful people in other branches, why don't the four of us form an alliance? We can support each other like brothers. That way, we won't have to worry about getting stabbed in the back or having no one to turn to in critical moments. What do you three think?"
Hearing this, Sun Yi and Qian Rui were overjoyed.
They respected Yao Hansheng immensely. After the incident with Wan Qingqing, another reason he kept challenging the top students of other branches was because those branches kept pressing down on Wanqing House.
Martial artists were used to settling things with fists, and minor friction often turned into fights.
Wanqing House's force technique was weak in direct combat, so they suffered heavily in inner fights.
Yao Hansheng was different. He had achieved Great Completion in the Force Realm and reached the fourth layer of the Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Art. Combined with his constant fighting experience, his real combat power was terrifying.
He had actually won far more than he lost against the other top students.
With such a powerful big brother shielding them, Sun Yi and Qian Rui were naturally thrilled.
Wei He, however, felt helpless. He had only come over to offer a toast and politely pass through...
"To be honest, I've also sworn brotherhood with Sun Qi, the new disciple. She treats me as her older brother. Now that the four of us are forming a bond, we can introduce her later. With more people, we have more strength. As long as I, Yao Hansheng, have any strength left in the future, I won't let anyone bully you!" Yao Hansheng swore earnestly.
"Big Brother!" Qian Rui was deeply moved. He stood up and bowed straight down.
Sun Yi's blood ran hot, and he bowed down as well.
Only Wei He remained standing.
"I..." He was just about to politely decline.
"Junior Wei, are you worried about your lost cultivation?" Yao Hansheng said heroically. "You underestimate me too much. Our Heavenly Seal Sect is growing weaker by the day. If we don't stand together, we'll just be carved up like meat by outsiders."
He stepped forward, grabbed Wei He, and pulled him right out of his seat.
"Come! Today, we substitute blood with wine! If you refuse, it means you look down on me, Yao!"
Since things had reached this point, Wei He had no choice.
He was expecting to break through to the fourth layer of the Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Art within a few days. He hadn't expected this sudden drama to unfold.
However, the three in front of him were already dead drunk, and Yao Hansheng had indeed protected the disciples of Wanqing House multiple times. His character was solid.
As for Sun Yi and Qian Rui, if Yao Hansheng valued them, they clearly had their own merits too.
Thus, Wei He went along with it and settled the sworn brotherhood with the three of them.
Of course, it was just a formal title. If the three turned out to be unreliable later, he would act as if it had never happened.
If they proved trustworthy, it would simply give him a few more useful connections.
