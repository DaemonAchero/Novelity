The plague was slipping out of control. Any more chaos, and the supply line would collapse.
Two deputy generals served under the Commander of the Chijing Army. Xie Dong was one of them. He was a massive man with a thick beard and sharp eyes, a spiked meteor hammer resting at his hand. He came from the capital, not the Yuchi family.
On the other side sat You Jianduan of the You family. He sipped warm wine from a cup, hiding his face behind his sleeve. He was now the face of the family's businesses ever since his sister, You Muxuan, was killed by Wei He.
"The plague has spread beyond our control," Xie Dong continued. "All five outer counties are down. I asked the capital to lock them down completely. If we cut them off, the supplies might hold. But we are running out of workers."
"We should think about a way out," someone sighed.
Silence fell over the room. Locking down meant abandoning those five counties to the plague. Tens of thousands of lives were on the line.
The room held the top strength left in Xuanjing City: three Viscera-Refining masters and four Bone-Forging warriors. Every night, they met at the Yuchi estate to manage the crisis. Rising prices, food shortages, panic, and a losing war were tearing everything apart.
Outside the estate, a silhouette dropped silently over the high wall.
A patrol walked past. Seconds later, the shadow slipped through the gap behind their backs. Green-eyed war hounds sniffed the air, felt something, but found nothing. They moved on.
The intruder was huge, over two meters tall, wrapped in a black cloak. He glided from shadow to shadow like a bat. The patrol gaps were only a few seconds wide, but that was all he needed.
He landed inside the inner garden, stepping under the roof of a pavilion. Guards moved along the rooftops nearby, but none saw him.
The moonlight caught a pale face. It was Wei He, fresh from the Wanqing Sect. He knew the Nine Strikes of the Heavenly Seal manual was in Yuchi Zhong's hands. He needed a guide to find it, someone close enough to the old commander to know where he hid his secrets.
That person was Yuchi Qi, Yuchi Zhong's favorite granddaughter.
Wei He waited. Soon, a maid walked down the corridor carrying a heated tray of midnight snacks.
He darted out.
A moment later, the maid blinked. Her head felt dizzy, like she had lost her mind for a second. She checked her tray. The food was still fine. Relief washed over her, and she hurried along.
Wei He now knew where to go. He slipped through several courtyards until he reached a private house with a small pond. The gates were closed, but light leaked through the windows. Voices came from inside.
Wei He dropped into the courtyard without a sound.
The moment his feet touched the ground, the voices stopped.
"Breaking into the Commander's estate? You have nerve," a smooth, gentle female voice called out from the bedroom.
Wei He was surprised. His footwork was top-tier, yet she noticed him instantly. Her senses were far too sharp.
Still, he did not panic. Yuchi Zhong was away. No one else here could stop him from leaving unless they were a Viscera-Refining master who specialized in speed. Those were rare.
"The night is clear. I could not sleep, so I took a walk," Wei He said calmly, eyes fixed on the door. "I did not expect a master like you in this place."
The wooden door slid open.
A woman stepped out. She wore a black dress with a high split, long white boots up to her thighs, and a black veil over her face. She looked to be in her thirties, her body toned and dangerous.
She was not Yuchi Qi.
"You are not Yuchi Qi. Who are you?" Wei He asked.
"Not your concern. Leave now. This is not a place for you."
"I am not leaving empty-handed," Wei He took a step forward.
She had not shouted for guards. She was hiding here, just like him.
Her eyes went cold. "You break into a young girl's yard at night and talk like this? One shout from me, and the guards will kill you."
"Try it," Wei He smiled. "I am only taking back what belongs to me."
"Master...?"
Another woman stepped out of the bedroom. She was younger, in her late teens, holding a short sword. Her face looked like Yuchi Zhong's. This was the real Yuchi Qi.
"Interesting," Wei He started to say, but the veiled woman moved.
She darted forward, two dark daggers flashing in her hands. Her blades slashed toward his neck with terrifying speed.
As she struck, a strange, thread-like force wrapped around the air. It dragged at Wei He's body, trying to pull him off balance.
Clang! Clang!
Wei He traded several blows and leaped back against the wall. His expression turned heavy.
He recognized that force.
"The Chaos Cult?" he muttered.
If not for his massive strength from the Whale Power Technique, those invisible threads would have ripped his posture apart. Her force matched his own fully strengthened Rain-Covering Force. She was holding back, too.
She was a Viscera-Refining master from the Chaos Cult. And she was standing in Yuchi Zhong's home, acting as his granddaughter's master.
Wei He did not care about their secrets. He only wanted the manual.
"I do not care what you are doing here," Wei He said. "Tell me where the Nine Strikes manual is, and I leave."
The veiled woman paused. She knew she could not finish him quickly. She turned to the girl. "Qi'er, do you know where it is?"
"No," Yuchi Qi replied. "Grandpa never told me. But one person definitely knows."
"Who?"
"Yuchi Yan."
