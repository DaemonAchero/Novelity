Yao Wan was thrown out of the dark hall by a massive force, escaping the grip of the black smoke. When she landed, her legs went weak and gave out under her.
The smoke was clearly no ordinary trap.
She scrambled to her feet and looked back. "Wei He!"
"Sister, go first! I'll be right behind you!" Wei He called back without turning around. His muscles bulged as he locked himself in a raw contest of strength against the stone face.
He pushed his energy to the limit, combining his raw power and gravitational force. Yet, the stone face was still dragging him forward step by step.
"This place isn't a ruin at all!" Wei He caught sight of something from the corner of his eye. Beneath the stone face lay a pile of fresh human bones.
The clothes on those skeletons belonged to the Haining Alliance. The masters from that alliance had already been swallowed whole by this creature long before they arrived.
The thick black smoke swirled around like a living beast, trying to drown him and bind his limbs.
"You're asking for death!" Wei He snarled. Being dragged forward pushed his inner rage to the breaking point.
His eyes flashed with violent intent. He planted his feet, and his skin turned a deep black-red. Dark, chain-like marks spread across his body.
He took a deep breath that sounded like a giant monster drawing air into its lungs. It was the power of his five-turn dragon breath.
"You want to pull me? Go ahead!"
"DIE!"
Wei He roared at the ceiling, surging with explosive strength, and yanked his arms back with all his power.
Snap! Snap! Snap!
Under the massive strain, the black snake hair snapped one by one.
Wei He stepped forward and drove his right fist straight into the air like a heavy spear, bringing down a wave of crushing force.
BOOM!
The stone face quickly used the black smoke to form a massive torso and reached out with a giant hand to block the strike.
It miscalculated Wei He's power. The moment their hands met, the creature's arm shattered into dust.
Wei He's fist plowed right through, smashing straight into the creature's face. Small white fragments of its mask shattered into the air, and Wei He grabbed a handful of them.
"RAAAAH!" The stone face twisted in agony. A flash of purple light erupted, and more smoke poured out from behind it, forming dozens of black arms that lunged at Wei He.
They grabbed nothing but empty air.
Wei He was already gone. He appeared on its left flank and slammed another fist into its side, breaking several arms off.
Without slowing down, he vanished and reappeared on its right, above it, and behind it. His movement was faster than ever before. Supported by his massive strength and gravitational force, he moved as light as a feather, flickering around the stone face over ten times in a single second.
Every single strike landed with full power, shattering its arms one by one.
Bang! Bang! Bang!
Under the non-stop heavy strikes, the stone face was forced backward again and again.
Its raw power was strong enough to crush high-tier masters, and its smoke contained a lethal poison that could melt skin. But against Wei He, none of that mattered.
His five-turn breath, combined with his energy, allowed him to match its brute strength. As for the poison, his body had long been built to resist all kinds of toxins. Even if this poison was deadlier than usual, his high resistance rendered it completely useless. He had been standing in the toxic black smoke for minutes without feeling a thing.
The man and the monster traded brutal blows deep inside the ruins, beating each other at close range. Two forces of massive physical strength collided, sending shockwaves of energy crashing through the hall.
BOOM!
The ground in the center of the island trembled violently. Two figures burst through the dirt, soaring straight into the sky.
Even mid-air, Wei He and the stone face continued to trade blows. Sparks flew between them from the sheer speed and force of their strikes.
Yao Wan crawled out of the underground ruins and made it back to the surface. She watched the brutal fight from a distance, her heart in her throat. She had no idea what that stone face was, but she prayed she could find a way to help Wei He.
Zhong Yuanlin was dead. Xu Ran had run away. She was the only one left who could stand by his side.
She tried to push down her grief and think of a plan, but before she could do anything, the battle in the sky came to an end.
Wei He was simply too fast. He broke the stone face's arms again and again. Healing once or twice was easy for the monster, but doing it twenty times took a heavy toll. Its arms began to heal slower and slower.
It was already slower than Wei He, relying purely on its extra arms to keep up. Without them, it lost its edge completely.
Heavy fists slammed into its back, sending clouds of black smoke scattering across the sky.
Realizing it was losing, the stone face turned to flee. But Wei He reached out and grabbed it by the edge of its mask.
"Die!"
Wei He threw a wild, all-out fist straight into the center of the face. This strike carried every bit of power he had. If it hit, it would smash the monster to pieces or leave it heavily wounded beyond repair.
"Hold."
A soft voice echoed from the distance.
Before the sound even reached his ears, Wei He's arm froze in mid-air. An invisible, massive force locked his body in place, leaving him unable to move a single inch.
Terrified, the stone face broke free and flew toward a tall man standing nearby, who wore silver half-armor.
The creature shed its massive body and floated next to the man's hand like a tame pet, letting him stroke its mask gently.
The man's face was blurry, covered by a strange distortion that blocked anyone from seeing his features. He looked at Wei He with a hint of praise in his eyes.
"If that fist had landed, you would have destroyed my mask."
He stood on the green grass and took a few slow steps forward.
"I left my pet on this island to rest. Yet you came uninvited and tried to kill my little friend."
"It's a good thing I noticed something was wrong and came early. Otherwise..."
A silver shimmer flickered near the corner of his eye. Looking closer, it wasn't light, but fine silver fish scales.
"Tell me... how should I handle you two?" He narrowed his eyes, smiling with a cold, bone-chilling tone.
"Who do you think you're handling?"
A voice suddenly cut in from the side.
The smile on the man's face froze. He turned his head toward the sound, but there was no one there.
His expression changed instantly. He turned around to look behind him.
A figure in a black dress stood silently less than five meters away. He hadn't noticed her approach at all.
Cold terror gripped his heart. He grabbed the mask and tried to dash ten meters forward. But halfway through his sprint, an invisible force grabbed his entire body.
"Sect Master Yuandu! This is a mistake! I..."
Bang!!
The man's body was squeezed into a bloody mist on the spot.
Along with the mask, both were crushed by the giant force, leaving behind nothing but a shower of blood that rained onto the grass.
The top-tier expert who had just backed Wei He and Yao Wan into a corner was wiped out in a single breath.
Yuandu turned to look at Wei He, a hint of approval on her face. "You did well using the branch I gave you to call me. If you had been a moment slower, you both would have been in real danger."
Wei He let out a long breath. In his other hand was the broken twig Yuandu had given him earlier. He had snapped it the second things went wrong, which was why she arrived so fast.
"Senior Sister... Zhong Yuanlin is..." Wei He said in a low voice.
"Perhaps that was her fate," Yuandu said softly, remaining silent for a moment. "Also, if you ever run into that man again, turn around and run immediately."
Wei He nodded, but felt confused. "You mean... he's not dead?"
He had just watched her crush the man to dust with his own eyes.
"That was just a double," Yuandu explained. "He specializes in a secret technique that controls others and turns them into his puppets. He's been famous in this region for years. People call him the Thousand-Faced Demon."
"Thousand-Faced Demon..." Wei He truly realized how dangerous this land was. Or perhaps their sect's arrival had pushed the local forces to band together against them.
"Don't overthink it. He isn't just targeting us," Yuandu sighed. "He's one of the most notorious rogue masters in the area, feared by both dark and righteous paths. You were just unlucky."
She kept the rest to herself. According to her reports, the Thousand-Faced Demon had deep ties with the Great Yue Empire. The empire had never stopped trying to destroy their sect, and this demon was just one of their pawns.
The Great Yue Empire was far stronger than any single nation had ever been.
Yuandu looked over at Yao Wan, who seemed to have lost her spirit, and then at Cheng Wan, who had just crawled out from the underground ruins.
If Wei He hadn't been on this trip, not only would Zhong Yuanlin have died, but Yao Wan and Cheng Wan would have been wiped out too. Except for Xu Ran, no one would have escaped.
Wei He had saved both of their lives.
"As for Xu Ran, I will deal with him when we return," Yuandu said. "But don't expect much. Running from a fight will only get him light punishment. He must have recognized the mask's identity, which was why he ran without even trying to fight."
"I understand," Wei He nodded.
After this, Xu Ran's reputation in the sect would be completely ruined. Even if Yao Wan didn't seek revenge, she would never keep his cowardice a secret.
Wei He sighed inwardly, looking at Yao Wan, who was silently crying.
Zhong Yuanlin and Yao Wan were extremely close. Yao Wan had even saved her life in the past. Their bond had always been strong, but now it was completely shattered.
Zhong Yuanlin was dead, Xu Ran had fled, and Yao Wan was left broken.
"Let's go back," Yuandu said quietly. "I'll send people to clean up this place. A few masters from the Haining Alliance died here too, so we need to inform them."
"Senior Sister," Wei He called out, thinking of something. "Has this kind of thing been happening often lately?"
Yuandu remained silent for a second. "More than you think. This is the fifth attack since we moved here. We lost three masters in the previous ones."
"Let's go..." She didn't want to talk about it anymore. She took a step forward, her body turning into black smoke before vanishing completely into the air.
"Thousand-Faced Demon...!"
Yao Wan wiped her tears, gripped the hilt of her sword, and stared angrily at the ruins, making a silent vow in her heart.
