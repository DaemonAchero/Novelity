Clang!
"Xuan Zheng" twisted his face into a dark sneer and ripped out a short sword.
"No time left, move!" Next to him, "Xuan Ning" grabbed his arm, shaking his head.
"But little brother..."
"Move!"
Xuan Zheng's eyes turned red with rage, but he forced himself to back off.
In a split second, both vanished. The bloody mess on the ground in front of Wei He vanished too.
Wei He lunged forward, but his hand caught nothing but empty air.
Two more Ethereal Realm monsters? Cold warning bells rang inside him.
He had only won against that fake Lou Shengyue because he struck first from the shadows, and his current power level was far above his past self. Once he realized the fake was much weaker than Huan Songzi, he had turned the fight into a test.
During the clash, he quietly bled his gravity force into the air, slowing his enemy down.
By the time the trap sprung, the assassin's speed had dropped without them even knowing it. That was when Wei He unloaded everything, crushing the target in a single hit.
From start to finish, it was a plan built on surprise, testing, and tight defense.
If the assassin hadn't been hurt by the first strike, Wei He knew he couldn't have kept them here. The Ethereal Realm was just too fast. If they wanted to run, they could run.
Unless he tagged them with his force first.
Too bad. The assassination failed, and the hunters became the hunted.
"In the world of adults, there are no second chances," Wei He muttered, staring at the crater of blood. "You make a mistake, you die."
His giant body rapidly shrank back down to his normal six-foot disguise.
The last splash of blood turned into black smoke and washed away into the wind. It wasn't spatial travel; it was a cheap trick to erase their tracks.
Wei He didn't chase. He knew he couldn't catch them.
Even if he did, fighting two Ethereal experts head-on was suicide. He didn't know their real strength or their hidden moves. His gravity net trick only worked if it landed on the first try. If they caught on and kept their distance, they could bleed him dry from far away.
Honestly, if that assassin had fought him face-to-face from the start, things wouldn't have ended so quickly.
They aren't just coming for me.
The realization settled deep in his chest.
Far away, he heard the heavy footsteps of the sect's True Masters rushing over. This round was over.
But the Thousand-Face Demon Lord had already broken Sister Yao Wan, and today they went after his bodyguard, Lou Shengyue.
What about next time? When they realize he is too hard to kill, will they go after his family?
"I am still too weak... The grandmasters can handle the old monsters, but below them, there are still too many people who can kill me..."
With a sudden burst of movement, he disappeared into the dark.
He didn't need to stay. The temple priests would clean up the mess. Right now, he needed to check on the real Lou Shengyue and the two brothers.
As he moved, he crushed the jade token at his waist.
A warning signal. The fastest way to alert the whole sect.
Moments later, Wei He reached the stone wall and slipped into Lou Shengyue's cave.
She was collapsed on the floor, out cold, but alive. Smelled like sleep poison.
Wei He checked her pulse. The attacker hadn't tried to kill her, probably fearing a death-curse would set off the sect's main alarms early.
He cured her poison, then sprinted straight down the mountain.
At Dragon Bay Docks.
Dozens of True Masters and elite warriors were gathered, their positions locking into array formations. They stood ready to spark their star cores at a second's notice.
Wei He's arrival, along with his broken warning token, caught everyone's eyes. But when they saw who it was, they looked away.
As a top-tier disciple, Wei He had privileges. He didn't need to take orders from common commanders.
Leading the frontline was a senior brother from the Ghost Head Branch. His face was calm, even burning with a dark excitement, like he was eager to see if their battle array could cut down a Grandmaster.
Wei He took a spot in the back and stared out over the water.
All across the mountain, other hidden experts were quietly stepping out from their closed-door training. Not everyone was built for frontline war.
Countless sharp eyes locked onto the sea.
Far out on the water, heavy fog rolled like thick smoke. Two massive figures were tearing each other apart inside the mist, throwing up wild ocean waves.
Seeing that the fight wouldn't end anytime soon, Wei He turned and rushed back to his residence.
BANG!
He kicked open the gates of the Wei Mansion and charged into the main hall.
The entire family was huddled inside.
His parents, his two older sisters, Wan Qingqing, Wan Ling, and Wei An were all gathered in fear.
"Father!"
Wei An stood up first. He was sitting next to his mother, shielding a young girl behind him.
"What's happening out there, Father?" Wei An was only in his twenties, now over six feet tall and built like an ox. But his rank was too low to understand this level of danger.
Without Wei He's cheat item to break his limits, the boy's talent was limited. If Wei He hadn't forced his own body to evolve years ago, he would have been stuck at the bottom too.
"Nothing for you to worry about," Wei He said, looking across the room. "Stay inside. Don't go out."
His old parents stood up with shaky legs, their mouths opening to ask questions, but no words came out.
The sect had ordered total lockdown. Everyone was waiting in terror for news.
Wei He's presence gave them a moment to breathe, but he knew words couldn't erase the fear.
"It's fine for now," Wei He said softly. "But all of you need to move down to the basement. If things go wrong, you'll be safe there."
"Husband... did someone break through the defenses?" Wan Qingqing asked in a low voice.
"Yes. But the Patriarch is here. The sky won't fall."
The Xuan Miao Sect was a giant. It was usually quiet. When a small problem happened, no one cared. But when a giant sect like this went on lockdown, it meant the world was tearing at the seams.
Wan Qingqing knew better than to ask for details. She nodded, gathered the family, and led them toward the underground shelter.
Every house on the island had a shelter connected to the sect's deep underground tunnels. If the island fell, they could hide in the main bunkers.
Once his family was secured, Wei He left the house.
As a core True Master, he had to take his place in this fight.
Halfway down the path, he ran into patrol squads. They were setting up checkpoints, searching every face, checking every pass.
His earlier warning token and the attack on the brothers had thrown the sect into chaos.
Checkpoint after checkpoint lined the roads, but without a central commander, the orders were messy and conflicting.
Patriarch Yundu and the three ancestors were not here. The sect was at its weakest point in history.
Usually, the inner affairs were run by the main halls. The man in charge of internal security was Xuan Ning.
And right now, Xuan Ning was lying somewhere in a coma, close to death.
No one knew if the killer was still inside the gates. Panic spread like wild fire.
If monsters like Xuan Ning and Xuan Zheng were taken out quietly, what chance did the rest of them have?
Everyone was afraid.
Wei He watched the mess as he walked down the mountain. With the main leaders down, the substitutes were useless. Orders overlapped, guards argued, and fear hung thick in the air.
Wei He sighed inwardly.
These masters spent their lives sitting in quiet caves or enjoying their high status. When real disaster hit, they didn't even have a backup plan.
He reached the bay.
Below, arrays of True Masters held their weapons ready. Huan Songzi was among them, standing tall with a full-figured, beautiful woman at his side. They stood close, their bond clear despite the tense air.
Cheng Wan stood further back in the Ghost Head array alongside ordinary masters.
He saw many familiar faces. Masters from the Lou family, Cai Menghuan, Wang Xi, Duan Yanhui, Ouyang Jing, Liang Huyao, Ouyang Luo...
Even Ouyang Luo's bodyguard, Shangguan Xing, had finally reached the Mind Perception rank.
As Wei He scanned the crowd, he caught her looking at him.
This girl had shared a courtyard with him when they first joined the sect. Now, they lived in two completely different worlds.
When she realized Wei He was looking at her, Shangguan Xing forced a weak, pleading smile.
She had bet everything on Ouyang Luo back then. Part of her regretted it deeply now. If she had stayed with Wei He like Zhou Muqing did, maybe her life would be different.
But there were no "what ifs" in this world.
Even Zhou Muqing, who made it to the same rank later... look how that ended.
Wei He felt a cold flicker of emotion, then let it go.
Beside her, Ouyang Luo noticed the eye contact.
His face remained blank, but his mind burned with anger.
He had entered the inner mountain at the same time as Wei He. Now he was at the Second Perception rank—a feat he achieved through family power, but also through his own raw effort.
And Wei He? That guy was just a dumb brawler who got lucky and caught the Master's eye.
Wei He got top-tier resources from day one. Everything was handed to him. Yet after using up mountains of pills and treasures, Wei He was only at the Third Perception rank—just one step ahead of him.
Why him?
Ouyang Luo had always been arrogant. To him, he was clearly the better man. But the world gave everything to Wei He and left him with scraps.
