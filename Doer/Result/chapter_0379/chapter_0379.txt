The Lotus Cult was once a branch of the Demon Faction. But over many years, they completely forgot their original path.
When the founder created the sect, it was about natural harmony and balance. Now, it had turned into a foul way of stealing power from others.
In the beginning, the Lotus Cult was actually a proper, righteous Daoist sect.
Then it went wrong.
Qi Lianzi was one of them. Years ago, he broke away from the main Demon Faction to stand on his own. He wanted to bring back the original glory of the Lotus Cult.
Unfortunately, the ancient teachings were long lost.
He decided on a single path: push his own strength to the absolute limit first. Only then would he use this evil power to restore the righteous way.
To reach that level, he needed massive resources. It was the dark night before the dawn.
As long as he survived this phase, all the danger and the sacrifice of the captured female masters would be worth it.
He just never expected things to go wrong so fast.
Qi Lianzi whipped his long robe around and looked at his disciple.
"Where did they disappear?"
He did not believe his two junior brothers were caught. They probably just ran far away and lost contact.
Still, since his disciple brought the news, he would go check it out himself.
"At the Poplar Inn! That's where the women from Wanfei Palace were staying. But by now, they must be back at the Xuanmiao Sect camp. That Wei He took everyone there earlier. Second and Third Uncles are definitely locked up there too."
"Stay here and guard the things in the back. I will see for myself."
Qi Lianzi said no more. He strode out of the temple and vanished into the night air.
His disciple could not even see how he left.
"Let me see just how arrogant this Xuanmiao Sect really is."
Wei He was grabbing anyone he saw. It was beyond overbearing.
Before this, Qi Lianzi could easily walk away and hunt somewhere else.
Now he had no choice.
If he lost both junior brothers at once, the power of the Lotus Cult would collapse overnight.
They could not afford such a loss.
He had to go.
Xuanmiao Sect Outpost.
Ling Xian'er, Shangguan Tong, and now Zuo Qing stared at each other in silence. They looked over at the other captives, along with the group of beautiful women from Wanfei Palace.
Those people were clearly not real masters. Why did Wei He capture them too?
Night was falling.
A large crowd stood in the yard, but few dared to speak. Everyone's energy was sealed tight. No one was stronger than anyone else here.
From a small room nearby, blood-curdling screams rang out from time to time.
Soon after, someone would be carried out covered in blood.
Then, men from the Ten Thousand Poison Sect would pick the next person from the crowd and carry them inside to repeat the process.
Nothing was scarier than the unknown.
No one could see what was happening inside that room. They only saw people walk in, only to be carried out half-dead and bleeding heavily.
One by one, the faces of everyone in the yard turned pale.
Wei He sat quietly in the corner of the yard.
His skilled men handled the interrogation. He only needed to guard the prisoners and wait for Three Faces to take the bait.
Right now, almost every female master in the entire Yunzhou Mansion was gathered in this single yard.
If Three Faces really feared nothing, he would definitely strike this outpost.
He would never give up his prey just because of one man.
Either way, they had to fight to find out.
In the Realm of Masters, raw cultivation level was not everything.
A higher rank only meant your inner energy was stronger, like having better armor and weapons in battle.
To actually hit an enemy, you still needed martial arts, secret techniques, and correct timing. You needed strategy.
Battles were never won by simply comparing who had a higher rank.
If that were true, people would just show their rank and walk away without fighting.
Wei He had often killed enemies stronger than himself in the past. Now that he had reached his current level, he understood this truth better than anyone.
He sat in silence, waiting for the interrogation results.
He was also waiting for Three Faces to launch a surprise attack.
"Senior Brother Wei, I understand your plan now. You are forcing Three Faces to reveal himself. But if we are all sealed up like this, we have no power to fight back. If something goes wrong..." Ling Xian'er tried to negotiate for better terms.
Wei He merely gave her a cold glance, then looked down and ignored her completely.
Three Faces was cunning. If he wanted to strike, he must have gathered information about Wei He's true power already.
He would come fully prepared.
The wind howled softly.
Twenty minutes passed in total silence.
Wei He remained cross-legged on the ground, completely still. His inner energy naturally blocked the cold wind around him.
There was no protection array here, so the cold wind was much harsher than by the sea.
Wei He had to keep his senses sharp at all times, blocking the wind while watching every corner of the yard.
"Do you really think Three Faces will come?" Shangguan Tong asked. She finally understood the plan after Ling Xian'er explained it to her.
This Wei He from the Xuanmiao Sect was using all of them as live bait!
Ling Xian'er glanced at Wei He sitting in the corner and shook her head gently.
"I don't know."
Before leaving her palace, she had heard of the Xuanmiao Sect.
People said their disciples were peaceful and balanced. Who would have thought such a monster would come out of that place?
She knew a little about Wei He's past. He started in the Wushi Sect, moved to the Tianyin Sect, and finally joined the Xuanmiao Sect through Wanfei Palace.
She never expected him to return with such terrifying strength.
In this vast city, no one could stop him, and no one dared to try.
"So we just wait here like this?" Shangguan Tong sighed.
"Three Faces will definitely come. We just don't know when. Given how wild he is... he might already be hiding right next to us," Ling Xian'er said in a low voice.
Shangguan Tong fell silent. Most of the top masters in the city were locked up right here. Where else could Three Faces go?
Crack.
A sudden noise came from outside the yard. It sounded like footsteps moving fast across the roof tiles.
Wei He's expression did not change. He stayed motionless in his spot.
Gong Huan and Zhao Yuxi stood nearby, watching everything carefully.
Gong Huan looked at Wei He. Seeing him sit like a stone statue, he forced himself to stay calm.
He remembered Wei He's warning: as long as they stayed within his sight, they were completely safe.
Tap.
The footsteps stopped right outside the right wall of the yard.
Separated only by a single wall, the person outside stood still, as if checking the situation inside.
Outside the wall, a heavy, dull-looking man stood like a stone, waiting for someone to chase him out.
If Wei He rushed out to fight him, he would turn and run immediately, drawing the master away.
Then, his partner hiding in the shadows could sneak in, rescue their brothers, and capture the female targets.
Yet, no matter how much noise he made outside, the yard remained dead silent.
Wei He sat in his spot with his eyes closed, waiting.
There were no insect chirps in the yard, only the heavy breathing of the captives.
By now, Qi Lianzi had already slipped into the yard, waiting for his chance.
He was waiting for someone to leave the group to use the latrine.
That would be his moment.
He wanted to draw Wei He's attention away and split his focus.
In truth, Qi Lianzi was just as anxious as the prisoners.
He had hunted too many targets, angering all the big sects. Stronger ancient masters could arrive at any moment.
If he did not act now, he would lose his chance forever once those monsters arrived.
He had to strike fast, save his two junior brothers, and get out.
If he could snatch those three captured female masters along the way, even better.
Using a secret stealth technique from his cult, Qi Lianzi completely hid his presence and crept through the yard like a shadow.
By switching between real and hidden spaces, he melted right under Wei He's nose.
Ordinary masters were far worse at detecting subtle presence than true ancient masters.
With such a high-level stealth skill, even Wei He's sharp senses were completely tricked.
It was this exact trick that allowed Qi Lianzi to survive hunters far stronger than himself for years.
It was the true secret behind the survival of the Lotus Cult.
Hidden deep in the shadows, Qi Lianzi waited patiently. He merged into the darkness, making no sound at all.
Time slowly passed.
Wei He did not move an inch.
However, a few prisoners finally stood up and went to the back under the guard of the Poison Sect, needing to relieve themselves.
Jin Chi and his brother Mo Yilian exchanged a fast glance and began to gather their hidden strength.
They had lost to Wei He earlier, but that did not mean they were weak.
Because Wei He had low energy rank, the seals he placed on them were slowly being pushed open by their own hidden power.
They were getting their strength back. They only stayed still because they feared Wei He sitting right next to them.
'When the noise outside draws Wei He away, we strike together. Grab the women and run,' Jin Chi sent his voice straight into his brother's ear.
Mo Yilian nodded slightly.
Working together, as long as Wei He was gone, no one in this yard could stop them.
They would grab Shangguan Tong and the others and break out immediately!
As for their senior brother Qi Lianzi, both of them had absolute trust in his power.
Qi Lianzi was once a true registered master of the Dao. Even if he could not kill a junior like Wei He, he could easily distract him.
The air in the yard grew colder and heavier with tension.
Gong Huan and Zhao Yuxi swept their eyes across the area, searching for any small trace of danger.
They were sweating inside.
Wei He had captured so many people at once and sealed all their powers. If Three Faces broke in now, these helpless people would be easy meat for him.
