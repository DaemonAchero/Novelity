Deep in Shaoyang Mountain.
Late at night, when everything was quiet, moonlight filtered through the canopy, lighting up small patches of the dark forest.
Wei He moved carefully. He was fully dressed in black gear, taking light and silent steps as he moved deeper into the woods.
The second level of Flying Dragon Skill was truly impressive. Paired with his custom boots, his footsteps were completely silent, fully hidden by the rustling leaves in the night wind.
With plenty of blood energy fueling it, the Flying Dragon Skill gave him total control over every muscle in his feet and calves. Walking felt like lightly tapping nails into the ground, barely making a sound.
This was the first time Wei He had gone this deep into Shaoyang Mountain.
In the past, he only went to the front side of the mountain and stopped near the top. But this time, he crossed the peak and moved quickly down the dark, shadowy side.
What Ding Hai said last time had been stuck in his mind like a thorn. He kept thinking that the Shaoyang Sect might be linked to his parents' disappearance.
After holding back for a long time, he was finally fully prepared to investigate.
Leaves slapped around him as Wei He ran fast, dark bushes and trees passing by quickly.
He could smell dry grass and dead leaves, mixed with the occasional scent of rotting meat and fruit.
He moved for several minutes.
Suddenly, Wei He stopped in his tracks and ducked behind a dead tree, standing completely still.
Right ahead in the forest, through a gap in the trees.
A square, high-walled compound sat quietly in the deep woods, shaped like a small earth fort.
Torches were lit around the perimeter, their flames flickering in the wind.
A few strong men in white clothes were slowly patrolling toward Wei He's direction, carrying lanterns and weapons on their backs.
These men had dull faces and lifeless eyes. Their movements were stiff and their bodies looked somewhat bloated.
Soon, they passed close to Wei He and moved toward the other side.
Wei He stepped out lightly from behind the big tree and examined the earth fort carefully.
The fort was about twenty meters long and ten meters wide. It couldn't hold many people, yet guards were patrolling this late at night.
Wei He recognized their clothes. They were the uniform of ordinary Shaoyang Sect disciples.
Unlike the Shaoyang Sect disciples he had killed before, these people were clearly a tier lower. For some reason, none of them spoke a word, and even their breathing was steady and flat, without any change.
He crept closer to the fort, quickly grabbed a few rats from the bag behind him, and threw them forward.
He had caught these rats in advance. As soon as they hit the ground, they scattered in all directions and disappeared into the dark.
However, not a single rat ran toward the fort.
Wei He pulled out a few more rats and threw them closer to the fort.
This time, out of four rats, one ran toward the fort and scurried along the outer wall.
Wei He moved his feet, quickly following the rat with light steps.
He was extremely fast. Tapping his feet in quick succession, he made only a tiny rustling noise like a rat as he slipped into a patch of shadow by the fort.
Soon, a patrol was drawn over by the sound of the rat. Shortly after, the squeal of a rat being killed echoed in the air.
Wei He didn't make a rash move. He came here to catch someone with status to get answers. Catching these outer guards would yield no clues.
It might even raise the alarm and ruin his plans.
He wanted to succeed in one go, catch them off guard, and find out the truth.
The patrol remained silent. After killing the rat, they marched off with neat steps, slowly leaving the side of the fort.
Wei He pressed his back against the wall, using a fresh wooden stick he picked up to probe the ground step by step as he moved along the wall.
Before long, he made a full circle, but he couldn't find a single entry point. Left with no choice, he crept toward the main entrance.
The entrance had a heavy round wooden door. Two chubby men in white stood on either side like wax statues, completely motionless.
Wei He stopped quietly, then kicked off the ground hard. His body shot forward like a bat, flashing past behind one of the chubby men.
With a quick series of taps, his body turned and swept lightly behind the other chubby man.
Thud.
The two chubby men fell to the ground dead without making a sound.
Wei He grabbed one of the bodies and rolled it toward the front door.
Thump, thump, thump!
A series of dull thuds sounded, and two rows of nail-sized holes appeared on the body.
Wei He narrowed his eyes, recalling the speed of those hidden weapons, and felt relieved.
If they were all at this speed, it didn't matter how many hidden weapons there were.
He stepped forward and pushed his palm lightly against the wooden door.
The Five Ridges Palm struck the door softly, sinking deep inside without making a sound.
Wei He broke the door lock with both hands and pulled the door open outward.
Inside, two huge, chubby men in white were playing dice. Sensing something was wrong, they roared and charged at Wei He together.
Their arms swung forward, executing two different Shaoyang Palm techniques, attacking Wei He one after the other.
"Too slow."
Wei He met them with both hands, unleashing his Five Ridges Palm force.
With two dull thuds, the huge pushback drove the two chubby men back repeatedly, their faces turning bright red like pig liver.
Holding their hands, they retreated fast, trying to shout for help.
Unfortunately, it was too late. Wei He was too fast. The moment they stepped back, he closed the distance and chased them with a palm strike to each of their chests.
The Flying Dragon Skill made him far faster than them.
Soon, another patrol rushed over. As they reached the door and were about to enter, Wei He threw a handful of lime dust into their faces, blinding them.
Moving in with a flash of palm strikes, several men fell to the ground on the spot.
"I originally planned to sneak in, but look at this now," Wei He sighed. He picked up two corpses and threw them into the fort one after the other.
One flew through the air, and the other rolled on the ground, instantly triggering a string of hidden traps.
After clearing the traps, he moved forward step by step with extreme caution.
It seemed this fort only had these few people. It was merely a lookout post.
Walking to the center of the fort, he saw a small iron cage sitting on the floor made of packed black mud.
Inside the cage was a person.
A curled-up, filthy man covered in hair.
"What is this?" Wei He frowned. Why was someone locked up in what looked like a guard post?
Why was this man kept here?
"Who are you?" he asked in a low, muffled voice, using his energy to disguise it. "Why are you here?"
The man in the cage remained still and silent, as if he were dead.
However, Wei He could clearly hear his breathing. He knew the man was still alive.
Moreover, though the man looked weak, his heart beat with incredible power, similar to martial artists he had heard who reached Second Blood status.
Wei He narrowed his eyes and began searching the inside of the fort slowly. A fort this big couldn't possibly hold only this many people.
Yet after making a full loop, he was surprised to find that there really were only this few people here.
Returning to the cage, Wei He thought for a moment, then grabbed the cage with both arms and lifted it upward.
The entire cage was lifted easily, revealing a stone trapdoor with a pull ring underneath.
It was an entrance to a hidden passage.

Whimper...
A few green-eyed, black-furred wolves suddenly lowered their heads to the ground, their noses twitching frantically.
"An intruder!"
Behind the wolves, several white-clad men holding leashes spoke in low voices.
"Someone actually dared to break into the Shaoyang Sect's Earth Tower?"
"The Earth Tower gave no reply. They must have all been taken out. Inspector, what do we do now?" one person asked, looking back.
Behind them stood a huge man almost three meters tall. He held a black spiked warhammer in one hand, his eyes wide as bronze bells, and his blood energy rolling like a flood.
"The two inner disciples didn't respond, so they are definitely dead. Follow me and kill him!"
The giant man licked his lips.
"Also, tell the other two Inspectors to head over as well. That person got away last time, but he will definitely die this time!"
"Yes!"
Several inner disciples quickly agreed in unison and scattered to leave.
Inspectors were top experts of Third Blood status, second only to the Sect Leader. Last time the intruder escaped because only one Inspector was present, but this time was different.
With three Inspectors moving together to hunt him down, victory was certain.
'As long as that person gets misled by the tunnel, he likely won't realize the fort is actually a trap cage. Once our men arrive, we can lock him inside the fort without any effort at all!' The giant man hoisted two warhammers onto his shoulders and charged toward the fort, his large body gliding lightly across the ground.
...
...
...
Wei He frowned as he looked at the open passage on the ground.
The passage was pitch black and looked extremely deep and long. But there were no signs of people coming in or out, no footprints, only scratch marks.
He looked around the area once more. Clearly, something was hidden here.
The fort wasn't big. Inside were just a few tables and chairs, a cage, some torches on the wall, and a copper burner.
He walked up to the torches and checked them one by one.
After confirming that every torch was normal, he checked the tables and chairs.
Soon, the furniture showed no issues either, leaving only the copper burner.
Wei He walked up to the copper burner and looked closely. Gathering his blood energy, he slowly rubbed the surface of the burner.
"Huh?" Suddenly, he spotted something wrong.
The copper burner didn't have a single stain, scratch, or smell of incense ash on it.
Could it be...
A thought flashed through Wei He's mind. He quickly looked at the tables, chairs, and the inner walls around him.
The walls were the exact same way.
Wei He moved slowly through the fort step by step, soon arriving back at the hidden passage.
He knelt down slowly on one knee, wiped his hand across the ground in front of the passage, and brought it to his nose to smell.
A faint smell of iron and blood drifted up.
"Could this be!?" Wei He's heart skipped a beat.
BANG!!
Suddenly, the main door of the fort was slammed open. Several chubby men in white marched inside carrying shields.
Behind them, the three-meter-tall giant held a spiked warhammer, his massive belly completely blocking the doorway.
"Where is he?"
The fort was completely empty now, with no one inside. Only a row of corpses lay stretched out on the floor.
"The dogs didn't catch a scent. He ran!" a group of white-clad chubby men holding green-eyed wolves shouted from the perimeter.
"Useless trash!" The giant slammed his hammer onto the ground. The surrounding men felt their feet go numb and staggered back several steps, not daring to make a sound.
