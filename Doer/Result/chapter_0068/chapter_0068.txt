The Guan family convoy pushed forward, leaving the city gates behind and drifting past the five-mile mark.
Just past that mark, three massive figures stood dead center on the main road.
One of them was Chen Jun, known as the Broken-Chain Spear.
The blood-energy surging from the three men fused together, creating a crushing pressure that no ordinary human could match. Even worse, behind their backs, countless metal javelins were strapped and ready to draw.
The convoy ground to a slow halt.
Guan Ye peeked through the curtain gap. She saw Chen Jun blocking the path, and she quickly recognized the two men beside him.
"The Tiger Gate Hand and the Three-Element Wind-Chasing Blade?!" Guan Ye’s face drained of color.
The poison setup and heavy shield formation she had designed for the Guan family could only handle two opponents of the three-refinement blood stage. A third one...
They had never even planned for that. The Guan family was rich, but they were merchants, not warlords. If they built a force that was too strong, the Seven-Family Alliance would never allow it.
Now, three three-refinement masters were attacking together. This was nothing short of an all-out strike by a major faction to wipe out the Guan family.
It was not just Guan Ye who went pale. Beside her, Guan Qing and the two head managers turned ghost-white as well. Dealing with two was already deadly; dealing with three was impossible.
"Move! Leave no one alive today!" Chen Jun yanked a javelin from his back, took aim, and threw it.
Swish!!
...
Half an hour later.
Dark clouds choked the sky, heavy with sudden cracks of thunder and lightning.
Eight or nine miles outside Feiye City, deep in a barren mountain range.
Chen Jun panted heavily as he leapt through the brush, hunting down the few surviving members of the Guan family.
Guan Ye and her core group were fleeing on horseback. The rest of the convoy had been torn to pieces by the initial charge of the three masters. The shield line and poison traps had been pinned down by long-range specialized javelins thrown by the other two experts. Her best guards had been cut off and engaged by Chen Jun’s disciples.
Everything she had taken pride in was stripped away like layers of an onion, leaving her isolated and helpless. The battle formations meant to stop two masters crumbled like paper against three.
They only stayed ahead because Chen Jun had tripped a trap earlier, taking a poisoned wound that slowed his pace.
Suddenly, a horse collapsed with a loud crack, its front legs snapping on impact.
Guan Ye and the others were thrown off, crashing hard into the dirt.
As horse screeches mixed with human groans, Guan Qing scrambled up without a word. He grabbed his sister's arm and dragged the survivors straight toward the dense mountain woods.
The woods were pitch-black and thick, perfect for hiding.
Above them, black clouds rolled in and the wind roared, making the air suffocatingly tense.
Chen Jun walked up to the groaning horse, holding his chain spear. He gave it a casual thrust to end its life, then swept his eyes across the ground. He found the footprints instantly.
"No horses left. Let's see how far you can run!"
His eyes flashed with malice, joy, and relief. His long-held dream was about to come true, and the feeling was beyond words.
Spear in hand, he blurred into motion, darting down the trail of footprints.
Before he went far, he froze. His chain spear whirled like lightning to form a defense before him.
Clang! Clang! Clang!
Three faint chimes sounded. Three poison needles, thin as cattle hair, bounced off his spear and bit into a nearby tree trunk.
"Cheap tricks," Chen Jun sneered. Looking down, he saw tiny poison-trigger boxes hidden in the mud. He swept his spear, crushing them all to pieces.
With a short pause, he resumed the chase.
Flash!
A streak of lightning split the sky, painting the entire barren mountain in a blinding white.
Guan Ye and Guan Qing gasped for air as they stumbled through the forest.
"I... I can't keep going!" Guan Ye had been raised in luxury. She couldn't handle this level of pure exhaustion. She clutched her stomach, close to throwing up.
"You have to! Get up if you don't want to die!" Guan Qing snapped, jerking his sister upward.
"I will carry the Young Lady!" Yang Jie gritted her teeth. She knew a bit of martial arts, so her stamina was far better than the rest.
Guan Qing didn't argue. He loaded his sister onto Yang Jie's back, and the group pressed deeper into the dark forest.
"That old dog got poisoned too. We just need to stall. If we last until the poison breaks him, we win!" Guan Ye muttered angrily from Yang Jie’s back.
She thought of how confident she felt leaving the city, only to hit Chen Jun's trap. She thought of her guards getting wiped out one by one, leaving her to flee like a rat. Everything she had worked to build was gone.
The more she thought, the angrier and sadder she got, until tears spilled down her face.
"Don't worry, Young Lady. Didn't you hide a secret stash in advance? Most of the Guan family assets were converted to gold notes and hidden there. Why not tell me where it is now? Just in case you die, at least the secret won't die with you." Dong Danian's voice suddenly sounded, cold and calm.
The other three froze, instantly backing away from Dong Danian.
"Dong Danian, do you know what you are saying?!" Guan Qing roared in shock.
"I know exactly what I am saying," Dong Danian replied with absolute composure. "Aren't you curious why the help Chen Jun brought was just enough to break our formation's limit?"
"It was you?!" Yang Jie gasped, staring in disbelief.
"Correct," Dong Danian nodded slightly. "The Guan family's business is too big to just pull out of Feiye City. Chen Jun isn't the only one backing me. So, Young Lady, you..."
Pfft!
A short blade stabbed out from the side, punching straight through Dong Danian's neck.
His eyes bulged. He reached up with both hands to clutch his throat, but his strength vanished instantly.
Thud.
His body collapsed, revealing a figure standing right behind him.
The person wore a bamboo hat and a full face mask, wrapped in pitch-black clothes that blended seamlessly into the dark forest.
"Who are you?!" Guan Qing stepped back, startled.
Guan Ye, still on Yang Jie’s back, felt a strange sense of familiarity from the stranger.
"You?!"
She opened her mouth to speak.
Snap.
The black-clad man brushed past her in a flash.
A dark, hard piece of medical residue was tossed high into the air. It landed right in front of Guan Ye, and she caught it instinctually.
"You... could it be...?!" Guan Ye stared at the remnant in her palm, her mind clicking as she turned back sharply.
Without saying a word, the black-clad man sped up, turning from a walk into an absolute sprint.
BOOM!!!
He slammed both palms forward, clashing head-on with Chen Jun, who was bursting out from the trees.
A ring of fine dust exploded between the two.
"Run!" Guan Qing snapped out of it first. He grabbed his sister and Yang Jie, turning to flee.
No matter who that savior was, this was their only chance to get away.
The three tumbled through the brush, running as fast as their legs could carry them, quickly vanishing into the thick woods.
"Who are you? The Guan family is already finished. Why jump into this muddy water?" Chen Jun took a step back, staring intensely at the masked man.
That palm hit was simple, but it carried the raw weight of a three-refinement master. Chen Jun was poisoned and injured. Fighting a full-power master now would let the Guan family survivors slip away.
The black-clad man remained silent. He leaned low and brought his palms up, one in front and one behind, taking a starting stance.
"You seek death!" Chen Jun's eyes raged. His chain spear shot forward like a venomous snake, biting toward his target.
The two instantly clashed again.
BOOM!
BOOM!
BOOM!!
Yet for some reason, every single hit exploded with a cloud of dust.
How could a single set of clothes hold this much dust?
Chen Jun felt deeply frustrated as they fought. If he were at full strength, this would be easy. But with the poison slowing his blood and cutting his power by thirty percent, plus this weird dust everywhere, he didn't want to drag this out. He tried to slip past the masked man to resume his chase.
But his path was blocked instantly.
They collided once more. Spear shaft met palm, and another small ring of dust exploded.
"Where is all this dust coming from?!" Chen Jun felt something was wrong.
He hadn't put any real power into that last strike. He had just blocked an incoming attack.
Suspicious, he threw a feint, sweeping his spear tip sideways.
The move looked heavy, but it was light and hollow, holding no real force behind it.
BOOM!
The black-clad man slapped his palm forward anyway.
After the loud impact, another burst of dust blew out.
This time, Chen Jun saw it clearly. The sound was actually coming from the guy's mouth!
And the dust was being thrown out on purpose, even when the spear didn't hit him hard at all!
He was making sound effects and throwing dust by hand!
"You...!!" Chen Jun stood dumbfounded. He had never seen someone so utterly shameless in his entire life.
Thinking back, half of those loud impact sounds during the fight made no sense. Now he realized this guy was making fake fight sounds the whole time!
"What are you...!!" Chen Jun suddenly felt a wave of horror, but it was too late.
His legs went soft. Dizziness hit his head, and his blood surged out of control. He had been poisoned again without noticing.
And it wasn't just ordinary poison.
Wei He backed away over ten meters instantly, dodging the lingering toxic powder. He placed his hands behind his back and let out a long sigh.
"A thrilling battle today. It is a pity your skills fell short, senior."
"You...!!!" Chen Jun was already burning with rage, and hearing those words almost made him choke on his own blood.
He pointed a shaking finger at Wei He, stumbled forward a few steps, and collapsed face-first onto the dirt.
Hit by two different poisons, running miles at full speed, and then engaging in heavy combat—his blood circulation had carried the toxins everywhere. Staying conscious this long was already a miracle.
Even after hitting the ground, Chen Jun wasn't dead yet. He just twitched uncontrollably, unable to move a muscle.
"Safe travels, senior."
Wei He flicked his wrist, sending a Shaoyang Sect throwing dart flying.
Pfft.
The dart slammed straight into Chen Jun's forehead, crushing the skull and embedding itself deep inside.
Chen Jun, a famous three-refinement master, died a pathetic death all because he dropped his guard against a series of sneaky poison attacks.
Standing over the body, Wei He gained a fresh understanding of this ruthless world.
"I can never let my guard down, anywhere, anytime. Thank you for the lesson, senior."
He bowed deeply toward the corpse.
