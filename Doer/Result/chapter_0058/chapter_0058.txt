Wei He peered through the gap in the window. Outside lay another piece of mutated beast meat.
Over the past few days, Jiang Su had sent him twenty jin of different beast meats.
Her connections were far better than his. Deer meat was not the only rare food she could buy. By scraping together every resource she had, she managed to pull off this impressive haul.
Wei He used to hold a grudge against her. Now, seeing how hard she worked to make amends, his anger slowly faded.
He moved away from the window and pulled open his collar.
The Breakthrough Bead on his chest was still missing just a tiny bit.
Sighing, he picked up a piece of dried turnip and chewed on it slowly.
'Still, I will break through in a few days... I got much more beast meat than expected. This lets me maximize my digestion. The moment I digest one meal, I can eat the next.' He now ate six times a day, stuffing himself with beast meat without stopping.
As he chewed, a sharp scream from Wei Ying echoed outside.
Bang!
The door burst open. Wei He rushed out like an arrow and caught sight of a shadow falling over the courtyard wall.
Then came the sound of stumbling footsteps running away.
Wei He noticed fresh blood smeared across the sharp stones on top of the wall.
He had specifically bought sharp stones and set them along the ridge to stop thieves.
It seemed his trap worked.
Just then, the Breakthrough Bead absorbed another surge of blood energy. It flashed faintly and crept forward a tiny step.
Wei He did not care about that right now. He ran straight over to Wei Ying, who was sitting on the ground, pale with fear.
"Sister, are you okay?" He pulled her up.
"I... I'm fine. But that person just now was just a kid! His belly got cut open... so much blood!" Wei Ying yelled in shock.
"A kid?" Wei He frowned.
"Go check on him, please! He was so small, only half my height, and so thin. His belly was cut open, I'm scared he might..." Wei Ying begged, unable to bear it.
"Stay in the yard." Wei He nodded. He unlocked the gate and stepped outside.
He walked up to the wall and stared at the puddle of blood. He followed the trail for a short distance before it disappeared completely.
Judging by the amount of blood, if that thief was truly just a small child, he would not last long in this harsh world.
Wei He shook his head. The boy was likely dead.
If Wei He found him, he might have survived. Since he ran off, death was certain.
To protect his house, Wei He had soaked every sharp stone on the wall in poisoned water.
He got that toxic powder off a dead bandit. The drier it got, the more lethal it became.
Back in the yard, Wei He explained the situation. Even though Wei Ying felt pity, there was nothing they could do without a trace.
This was how the world worked. People died every single day.
Right now, half the districts in the outer city were completely empty. Massive crowds had fled to escape disease and starvation.
Whole families dragged themselves away, chasing a tiny, foolish dream of hope.
Wei He returned to his bedroom. He planned to keep training, but seeing his sister so shaken, he changed his mind.
Living alone without anyone to talk to for too long would drive anyone crazy.
Besides, gathering blood energy did not require absolute silence.
He stayed in the yard and practiced his Mountain-Returning Fist techniques. During breaks, he trained his Dragon-Soaring Steps, which focused mostly on leg movements.
Yet, he felt an uncomfortable sensation, like someone was watching his yard from the shadows.
Less than a hundred meters outside the Wei family yard.
A few skinny teenagers stared at the house with dark, hateful eyes.
"Fifth is dead, Boss," one boy said calmly.
"If he's dead, he's dead. He was careless. I told him to poison them, not make noise. He almost ruined our job. Good thing we're young so people don't suspect us much, or this contract would be wasted," the boy called Boss said coldly.
His name was Wang Ziyang. His parents had named him Wang Ziyang with a softer character, but he hated it because it lacked power, so he changed it himself.
In these outer city districts, he was one of the few ruthless child bosses. He would do any evil deed just to get a bite of food.
In the beginning, he felt a bit guilty, especially when poisoning good people who showed him pity. But after doing it over and over, he grew numb.
To stay alive and fill his stomach, nothing was off-limits.
This time, he took a job from a rich client. The task was to secretly drug the pretty girl in the Wei yard and drag her away.
Those powerful men loved that kind of prize. Wang Ziyang had helped them pull off this trick many times before.
He always took advantage of being a child because people lowered their guard around kids.
He never expected this family to put poison on their wall!
Fifth only suffered a scratch on his stomach, but soon after returning, he dropped dead...
Wang Ziyang's eyes grew darker. He glared at the yard, plotting all kinds of vicious schemes.
The bounty offered by the big shot was huge. If he got it all, he might never have to starve again.
"Fourth, you watch the back of this yard every day, right? Doesn't that woman ever go out?" he asked.
"It's weird these past few days. She never steps out. They even get water from a private path behind the house with high walls. It's too open, we can't get close..." Fourth answered.
"Is that so? No rush. She has to buy food and oil eventually. We wait," Wang Ziyang sneered.
"Boss Yang, maybe we should drop this... I asked around, and this yard belongs to a powerful man too. If we mess with him..." a tiny girl whispered nervously.
"So what if we mess with him? I, Wang Ziyang, am going to do big things and make big money! If I can't even handle this, how can I..."
Bang!
A rock the size of a fist flew out of nowhere and slammed hard into Wang Ziyang's forehead.
He collapsed instantly. After a few twitches, blood dripped down his hair into the dirt, and he blacked out.
The other kids screamed and turned to run.
But the alley exit was already blocked by several large, muscular men.
Both ends of the alley were completely shut down. All these men wore the same gang mark.
"You brats have real guts, targeting Officer Wei's home. Do you think our Iron Heart Gang is just for show?" A bald brute rushed forward and kicked one teenager hard, flipping him onto the ground.
"Boss Yang is dead! Fight them!!" The rest of the kids pulled out sharp tools like sharpened stones, rusty scissors, and crude metal shivs, rushing forward like wild dogs.
"Beat them down and drag them away," the bald man ordered with a wave of his hand.
The gang members charged in. With swift punches, kicks, and wooden clubs, they easily smashed the weak kids to the dirt.
Wei He stepped into the alleyway, watching the gang of teenagers with a cold face.
This was the brutal world they lived in. Plenty of kids possessed hearts far more evil than adults.
Did they really think he left his family unguarded?
Did they think all those small gangs he joined were just for title?
With a bit of silver and food, he could easily hire men to guard his home day and night.
Even when Wei Ying went out to buy groceries or oil, he had these gang members keep an eye on her.
In times like these, as long as you provided food and pay, men willing to risk their lives were everywhere.
These thugs knew every trick in the book and had plenty of free time, making them far more useful than expensive bodyguards.
"Master Wei, I saw that kid trying to climb your wall earlier, so I dropped him with a stone. Now these little bastards actually dared to plot against your family. They must be tired of living! Don't worry, I will handle every single one of them later."
Wei He nodded. He did not ask how they would handle them, nor did he care.
As long as it was not someone he cared about, the lives of others meant nothing to him.
The bald man ordered his men to drag the kids away, then turned back and bowed deeply to Wei He.
"Master Wei, you helped me back before the New Year, and that was my greatest luck. Don't worry, I will keep a tight watch here. Nothing will go wrong again!"
"Good," Wei He replied simply.
"Master Wei, don't pity those brats. They used people's kindness to trick and kill over ten people. They are infamous around these districts. That leader, Wang Ziyang, ruined countless families. He got the men killed and sold the women to brothels. His heart is blacker than ours!" the bald man added.
None of those kids would ever leave his hands alive.
Wei He merely nodded, saying nothing more.
He waved his hand and turned back toward his courtyard.
Behind him, the bald man and his crew bowed low, watching him leave.
Creak.
The yard gate swung open.
Wei He walked inside and went straight into his bedroom. He checked his chest once again.
The round pattern on his skin was now pitch black.
The final piece he had waited months for was finally complete.
Wei He took a deep breath, his heart pounding with excitement.
He locked the door, closed his eyes, and recalled the moves of the Five Mountain Palms.
Focusing his mind on the Breakthrough Bead on his chest, he felt the dense energy packed inside like a tight balloon. He gathered his intent like a sharp needle and stabbed right into it.
Pop!
In an instant, a massive hot wave burst from the bead, rushing into every corner of his body.
The bulk of this burning energy poured directly into his arms and hands.
A moment later.
Bang!
The door was kicked open.
Wei He's figure blurred. In the blink of an eye, he vanished past the courtyard gate.

Xiao Ran stepped out of the Hundred Flowers Brothel. As he walked out of the outer city, he let out a drunken burp, still smiling as he recalled the soft skin and sweet perfume from earlier.
Suddenly, a cold shiver ran down his spine. He snapped his head up.
A dark shadow darted toward him like lightning. A heavy palm slammed down toward his head!
"Who are you?!" Xiao Ran roared, surging his blood energy to throw a lethal punch back.
Boom!!
Fist met palm.
Xiao Ran's arm shattered bone by bone. His body flew into the air, but before he could land, the dark shadow closed in and smashed a second palm straight into his chest.
Pfft!!
His chest collapsed inward. He blasted backward and slammed violently into a dirt wall behind him.
The wall shook, leaving a deep human-shaped dent.
Xiao Ran's eyes bulged with pure disbelief. He glared at the shadow, trying to speak, but the light in his eyes faded rapidly.
His head slumped to the side. Dead.
The dark shadow stepped forward, wiped Xiao Ran's body clean of valuables, turned around, and melted into the dark alley.
