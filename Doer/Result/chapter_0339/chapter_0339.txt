Deep in the pine forest, trees snapped and collapsed one after another from heavy palm strikes.
Strangely, while you could see the trees falling, you couldn't see who was doing it. Only two fast shadows moved back and forth through the woods.
Rainbow-colored sword light flashed every few seconds. Waves of living True Energy spread out from the sword blade.
Yao Wan forced down the blood rising in her throat. Her internal energy drained quickly as she used her Lock Energy technique.
At her level, this Lock Energy could instantly seal an enemy's blood and power with a single touch. But it had a limit. If her power wasn't strong enough to block the enemy's force, the seal would fail and bounce back on her.
The Mount Lock branch of the Xuanmiao Sect was powerful, but its backfire was deadly when things went wrong.
That was Yao Wan's exact problem. She tried to seal her enemy's power, but the Three-Faced Bodhisattva's blood power was too massive. Yao Wan couldn't seal it or even slow it down. The moment her energy shattered, the rebound gave her severe internal injuries.
Seeing the danger, Yao Wan acted fast. She swung her sword, and a bright white light flashed out of nowhere behind her.
It was a quick trick she made by mixing her own energy with a special powder. This trick was meant to blind the strong senses of True Blood fighters. It used blinding light to mess up their senses for a split second so she could escape.
As the light exploded, a strange burning smell filled the air. Yao Wan pulled back, ready to run.
"You think you can run?!" The Three-Faced Bodhisattva laughed loudly. She took a deep breath, her body swelling and growing a size bigger.
BOOM!
The ground behind her burst apart. She launched forward like a cannonball, rushing straight at Yao Wan.
"Cloud Flash!"
In an instant, white air swirled around her fingertips, bringing a sharp, lethal threat. The spinning air on her ten fingers worked like electric drills, turning at high speed.
Ten fingers acted like ten drills, all aiming straight for Yao Wan's chest.
"Die!!" The Three-Faced Bodhisattva grinned wildly. She simply closed her eyes to ignore the blinding light, striking forward using her memory and pure instinct.
A True Blood general was much faster and stronger than a True Energy martial artist of the same level. They had massive bodies, huge power, and skin as tough as iron.
To a True Energy martial artist, they were high damage, high armor, and high health tanks.
To a True Blood fighter, a True Energy user was high damage, low armor, and low health. They were glass cannons, fragile and easy to break.
That was why the True Blood system looked down on the True Energy path.
Right now, the fight between Yao Wan and the Three-Faced Bodhisattva proved it. Yao Wan kept dodging and weaving, not daring to take a single direct hit.
True Energy users were just too weak against True Blood users. At the same rank, a True Energy user had to slowly wear down their target over a long time to win. But a True Blood user only needed one chance to land a flurry of punches. Once their speed and power exploded together, a True Energy user couldn't block it at all.
BOOM!
They clashed again. Grass bent, leaves flew everywhere, and the ground was full of deep craters.
Yao Wan leaped backward, landing on the ground and looking ahead.
"As expected, you are a master listed on the Dao Records. Three-Faced Bodhisattva of the Guangci Sect... I think I remember you."
Failing to escape, she realized her enemy was far tougher than she thought.
"Looks like... I won't be able to leave today unless I beat you."
Once she accepted this, Yao Wan actually calmed down.
Leaning her back against a tree trunk, she held her sword with one hand. Strands of True Energy began to flow out from her body into the air.
"In that case... let's enter the True Realm!"
CLANG!
A sharp sword sound rang out.
Yao Wan let her energy cover her body without holding back. In the blink of an eye, her body vanished from reality, as if erased by an invisible cloth.
"Heh... You think you can win just because you entered the True Realm?" The Three-Faced Bodhisattva sneered. The dark patterns on her skin turned black quickly.
In less than half a second, her body grew darker and darker until she turned completely into a cloud of black smoke and drifted away.
With that, the deep forest fell completely silent.
BOOM!!
Suddenly, a shockwave exploded in the woods. It felt as if an invisible object had just burst open.
The wind swept across the area, blowing wildly through the forest. Grass, branches, leaves, and even dirt and rocks were pulled into the wind, forming a strange giant snake made of debris.
The debris snake spun at high speed, tearing through the forest like a wild beast.
...

On the other side.
Wei He tossed the Guangci cultist in his hand onto the ground like trash.
"Get ready. We are leaving right now to report this," he turned and ordered Zhou Muqing.
"Yes," Zhou Muqing replied, understanding how serious it was.
If the Guangci Sect had taken over the outpost at the border of the Xuanmiao Sect, it meant they were starting their true attack on Haizhou.
Such an important matter had to be reported right away.
Without waiting, both turned and ran away from Black Pine Town.
The cultist lying on the ground took a big breath of relief, thinking he had survived. But before he could feel happy, a burst of energy exploded inside his chest.
Gah!
Blood poured out of his mouth.
Then, more hidden energy blew up inside his chest one after another.
BOOM BOOM BOOM BOOM!
In a flash, his whole chest burst open. The massive amount of True Energy he left inside worked like a set of landmines, killing him instantly.
Wei He moved fast through the woods.
Zhou Muqing followed behind him, wanting to ask a few questions that were on her mind.
Suddenly, a loud explosion echoed in the distance, sounding like something had just blown up.
Both stopped in their tracks and looked toward the sound.
"Should we go check? Or should we just light a signal flare for help?" Zhou Muqing asked with a serious look.
"No. Lighting a flare right now will only make them act early. It won't bring enough allies in time. If people arrive one by one, they will just be killed," Wei He said calmly.
"Then our only choice is to go to another outpost and use their long-distance message tools to contact the main sect!" Zhou Muqing suggested.
"If this outpost fell, the others might not be safe either," Wei He said steadily.
He had been through many crises, so he stayed calm under pressure.
"Then what do we do?!" Zhou Muqing asked grimly.
"Going back now is too late. We split up! You go back to deliver the message, and I will draw the pursuers away on another path!" Wei He said firmly.
"No, let me draw them..."
"You can't. You are too weak, so the Guangci Sect won't care about you. They will still send most of their force after me," Wei He cut her off.
He narrowed his eyes and added gravely, "That Three-Faced Bodhisattva is very strong. Since that man trusted her so much, she is likely one of the main leaders here."
"For such a big plan, their leader must be very powerful. Splitting up like this is the best choice."
He snapped his head to the left.
"They are here. Move!"
With a loud shout, Wei He charged toward the left.
Zhou Muqing clenched her fists tightly. It had been years since she last felt this useless because of her lack of strength.
But Wei He was right. Even if she tried to draw the enemies away, she couldn't do it. The only choice was to follow his plan.
Grit her teeth, she ran toward the other direction at full speed, not daring to stop for a second.
SWISH SWISH SWISH SWISH!!
On the other side.
Wei He had only taken a few steps before several figures suddenly appeared in front of him.
Several bald, muscular men leaped out from the woods, roaring as they rushed at him.
"Capture him! Don't let anyone from the Xuanmiao Sect escape to spread the news!"
A giant, seven-foot-tall bald man stood nearby in the woods to block his path, speaking in a cold voice.
"Kill!!"
Figures charged at Wei He from the front, spreading out like a fan.
Wei He stood where he was, carefully sensing how they differed from True Energy martial artists.
He had heard of the True Blood system before. It was a path that focused purely on raw speed and power. But these people in front of him...
They didn't seem to rely on just speed and power. Their aura felt somewhat similar to the wild beasts in the mountains.
There were six figures leaping toward him. Two were focused on heavy strength, two on high speed, and two had cold, sharp energy covering their palm blades.
It was clear they trained in different ways.
Of course, this was not what shocked Wei He. What really surprised him was that all six were top experts extremely close to the Realm Master level.
The threat they gave off was almost the same as the Organ Tempering masters from the True Energy system.
This meant they could likely match the Organ Tempering masters of Great Yuan.
If that was true, it explained why the Wu State dared to invade the Yuan State. With so many top experts, anyone would grow ambitious.
"Whatever, no point overthinking it," Wei He's eyes sharpened.
He had planned to come out to train, but ran into this mess instead. Well, it was fine. He wanted to test his true limits anyway, and this was a good chance.
Since they were still in Xuanmiao Sect territory, as long as he stalled for a bit, help would arrive. Even if Zhou Muqing got caught halfway, it didn't matter as long as he made a big enough scene here.
Either way, the enemy definitely underestimated his true power. By the time they realized how strong he really was, he would have made enough noise to alert the Xuanmiao Sect.
Thoughts flashed through his mind like lightning. Once his plan was set, Wei He looked up at the six men leaping at him.
He raised his hand.
His right arm split into six fast strikes like lightning, meeting all six attackers head-on.
BOOM BOOM BOOM BOOM!!!
A series of explosion-like blasts roared out.
While the six men were still in mid-air, massive amounts of True Energy poured into their bodies. They exploded on the spot, turning into clouds of blood mist.
"Huh?" The bald leader froze. Before he could react, his vision blurred.
Wei He's face grew huge in his eyes, then instantly turned pitch black.
BOOM!!
Without any chance to fight back, the leader's head was smashed to pieces by a single punch, killing him on the spot.
Wei He squatted down, quickly searched the body for loot, then stood up and walked forward.
He wasn't moving too fast or too slow. He was intentionally waiting for more pursuers to come.
After clearing out those few, he kept moving forward.
But after taking only a few steps, another figure suddenly appeared in front of him.
Blood leaked from her mouth, her face was pale, and her energy was unstable.
It was Yao Wan, whom he had just separated from a short while ago.
"Follow me!" Ignoring Wei He's shocked look, Yao Wan grabbed his arm and yelled sternly.
"That Three-Faced Bodhisattva is too strong to fight head-on! I can't hold her off alone. We have to go back right now!"
Holding Wei He, she gave him no time to argue. She leaped into the air and sped away toward the distance.
