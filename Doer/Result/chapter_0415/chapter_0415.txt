The Barren Sea.
This was a vast and desolate stretch of water near Yuanxi. With too much water and too few fish, local fishermen rarely came here to cast their nets, giving the area its name. Outside the occasional foreign merchant ship passing through, it was completely dead.
Right now, a group of Grand Yue Dynasty Real-Blood warriors stood on the deck of a massive five-story warship. They were watching the distance, waiting for the arrival of the Xuanmiao Sect.
The ship was over sixty meters long, its sides bristling with rows of cannon ports and arrow slits. Massive oars extended from both sides of the hull, rhythmically beating against the waves to push the vessel forward.
Moduo stood on the deck wearing a plain white monk robe. His bronze bald head was covered in strange black stripes. Compared to the other Real-Blood masters around him, who stood two to three meters tall, Moduo was only about five feet tall. Standing in the middle of them, he looked like a child.
Yet, not a single person dared to look down on him.
This warship was officially traveling under the banner of a diplomatic mission to the Chu Kingdom in the Yuanxi Archipelago.
"Is it true there are over eighty countries in Yuanxi?" asked a fat monk next to Moduo. He was over ten feet tall and patted his massive belly with a doubtful expression. "A tiny place like this has eighty countries? Sounds like nonsense. Added together, they probably aren't even half the size of our Grand Yue."
This fat monk, whose eyes and nose were almost buried in rolls of fat, was Garan Kuna, the Future Buddha. He was the second-strongest master on this trip, right below Moduo.
"It does not matter," another thin monk said in a low voice. He stood seven feet tall and looked like a dried-up corpse. "Our target was never Yuanxi anyway."
This was Duo Ming, the Increasing Buddha. He was one of the strongest fighters in the Guangci Sect's Buddha Hall. Like the fat monk, he was a Grandmaster, but because Moduo’s fame was so overwhelming, the outside world barely knew their names. To outsiders, the Guangci Sect was just Moduo, the invincible State Teacher.
Neither of them cared about such empty fame. They only cared about one thing: could Moduo achieve their goal today?
That goal was to crush Yuanduzi.
Before leaving on this trip, everyone on board had purified their bodies using Blood Artifacts, boosting their raw strength and resilience. Emperor Dingyuan had sent them here specifically to test if Moduo, combined with other top experts, could kill Yuanduzi.
The name Xuanmiao Sect was a thorn in the Emperor’s side. Every time he visited his sick father, the Supreme Emperor, and saw the old man twisting in pain, he was reminded of the old assassination attempt. He swore to pay that blood debt back sooner or later.
"I do not know," Moduo said, shaking his head. "Yuanduzi is a pillar of the Daoist sects. She may have grown stronger over these years. Until we clash, I cannot predict the outcome."
"The Xuanmiao Sect ship has arrived," a nearby monk reported respectfully. They were using specialized long-distance scopes, which, combined with their enhanced eyesight, allowed them to see far beyond normal human limits.
"Are they here?" Moduo looked into the distance.
A few tiny black dots had appeared on the blue horizon. On the lead ship, though human figures were not yet visible, one could already feel a heavy, suffocating pressure washing over the water.
"Daoist martial artists really love to put on a show," the fat monk sneered. "Their force spreads out much wider than ours."
"Get ready," the thin monk said without emotion. The veins across his body began to bulge, and his frame expanded violently as his muscles swelled.
Moments later, the Xuanmiao Sect fleet stopped three hundred meters away. The two massive ships faced each other across the quiet blue sea.
Yuanduzi stood at the bow of her ship, looking across at Moduo.
"It has been years, Moduo. You seem even shorter than before," Yuanduzi said with a hint of surprise.
"Greetings, Benefactor Yuanduzi," Moduo replied quietly. He was not a man of many words. Having spent centuries in bitter cultivation to reach this level, today's battle was just a small moment in his long life. Still, he felt a faint trace of curiosity toward her.
"Years have passed, yet time has left no mark upon you," Moduo sent his voice across the water. His voice did not sound like a private whisper; it hit like rolling thunder, shaking the decks of the ships and causing the ocean surface to ripple.
"Cut the talk, Moduo. I know you spent years mastering the Mara Golden Body just to counter my cultivation," Yuanduzi said, refusing to waste time. She stepped off the deck and landed on the water, standing on the ocean surface as if it were solid ground. "Let us finish this quickly."
"Very well." Moduo did not argue either.
Neither of them liked small talk. They moved fast and hit hard. There was no need for excuses or rituals, as both sides knew exactly why they were here.
"Moduo of Guangci. Please instruct me." Moduo pressed his palms together, bowed his head, and closed his eyes.
"Heiyin of Xuanmiao. Please instruct me." Yuanduzi spoke her true name. Yuanduzi was merely her human name, and Kunpeng was just a title given to her by outsiders. From the beginning, her true self was named Heiyin.
Their eyes met. Their hidden spirits clashed sharply across the open water.
"True Form: Heiyin Kunpeng!"
"True Form: Thousand-Handed Tathagata!"
Two roars exploded at the exact same time. The ocean shattered under a terrifying pressure, sending massive waves and sea mist flying into the air. Two gigantic, monstrous shapes shot into the sky, tearing through the water and clashing violently in the clouds.
While Yuanduzi was away, the Xuanmiao Sect continued to operate as usual. After driving back the Thousand-Face Demon Lord, the internal atmosphere of the sect had quickly returned to peace.
On an open field outside the Wuming Temple of the Mountain Lock Lineage, most of the True Realism masters had gathered. Every master who could make it in time was present.
Gong Yu and Bai Hezi sat on simple wooden chairs. In front of them, a heavy two-meter-high arena was being built.
A pack of small mole-like beasts was busily carrying heavy stones to set up the platform. These creatures were a recent addition to the sect. A newly risen genius master had figured out a special language to communicate with them, and by offering them fair pay, the sect could hire them to build basic structures. While they could not build complex temples, building a simple fight ring was easy work.
Before long, the arena was complete, and the mole construction crew scurried away.
The Mountain Lock lineage was fully assembled. Wei He stood in the crowd.
Normally, because he received the highest level of treatment in the sect and had an unlimited supply of high-grade beast meat, he did not need to participate in internal competitions like this.
However, word had leaked out about his previous fight with the assassin. Most of the masters assumed Wei He had simply gotten lucky. They believed he had been assigned the weakest killer due to his low rank, and that the assassin had slipped up, allowing Wei He to land a cheap surprise hit.
In their eyes, Wei He was just a lucky kid. Only a few top-level leaders knew how strong he actually was.
This time, Wei He did not plan to back down. The leader of the upcoming sea-monster purge would hold massive power, which would directly affect his future plans and freedom. He wanted that spot. He decided to fight for it, using only his normal form, without relying on his secret cards or special transformations. He wanted to see for himself how strong his base form really was.
The Xuanmiao Sect followed a relaxed, natural philosophy, so its members were usually quite lazy. But today, under Gong Yu’s strict orders, the Mountain Lock Lineage had to hold this internal tournament to pick their lead figure.
The rules reflected the sect's casual nature.
"Get up there and fight! As long as you win, any method is allowed," Gong Yu announced loudly. "However, you cannot use one-time tricks. Whatever you use here, you must be able to use repeatedly when we face the Haining Alliance and the rogue fighters out there."
She scanned the crowd. Most of the masters had blank expressions, looking thoroughly bored. They clearly had no interest in proving who was stronger.
Gong Yu added, "Also, our Ancestor has approved special rewards. The top three fighters from each of the three lineages will get to enter the secret vault and pick one treasure! And the top twenty will receive an upgraded array disk."
The moment those words left her mouth, eyes lit up across the field.
Most people knew they had no chance at the top three, but the top twenty was a different story. Almost everyone felt they had a shot.
The Mountain Lock Lineage had grown significantly. Over eighty masters were present, though most were low-tier fighters who had joined the sect after it moved to Yuanxi. They had originally been rogue cultivators who joined for the resources and safety, inflating the sect's numbers.
Out of these eighty-plus masters, only nine had reached full mastery. Xu Ran was currently locked away in solitary confinement as punishment, so she was absent. Most of the others were unfamiliar faces to Wei He.
Of the original "Three Heroes of Mountain Lock," Zhong Yuanlin was dead, Yao Wan was crippled, and Xu Ran was grounded. The old group was completely gone. Today was the perfect chance to pick three new representatives.
Gong Yu had a strongman keep order. Between the eighty-plus masters, their servants, and the crowd of spectators, nearly two hundred people were gathered around the ring. The atmosphere was loud and bustling.
After a short discussion, Gong Yu and the elders decided on the simplest way to rank everyone.
"True Realism masters have immense stamina and recover fast, so we will keep it direct," Gong Yu sent her voice across the crowd. "This will be a king-of-the-hill style fight. Whoever stays in the ring the longest and wins the most matches gets the highest rank. Once the top twenty are left standing, anyone can challenge them one last time."
With the rules set, Gong Yu looked around. "The Mountain Lock tournament begins now. Who wants to go first?"
Her bright eyes swept over the crowd, waiting for the first volunteer.
With two hundred people crowded around a crude stone ring, it looked less like an elite sect tournament and more like a cheap street brawl. But this was Gong Yu’s style. She cared only about results, not fancy appearances.
The noise slowly died down. After a brief silence, a heavily built female master nearly six feet tall leaped onto the stage.
"I am Ding Lei," she said, as grey energy instantly burst around her body to form a dense layer of defensive force. "Please, fellow disciples, show me what you have!"
