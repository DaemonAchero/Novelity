Feiye City. The inner core. The Castellan’s Manor.
Swish!
A white blur sliced through the heavy guard zones and stepped into the inner compound as if no one was there.
"Who goes there!? How dare you trespass!"
Figures from the Enshrined Hall launched into the air, leaping off rooftops to intercept the intruder.
The Enshrined Hall gathered over sixty percent of the local rogue fighters. It was a collection of the finest martial artists in the Feiye region. Even the weakest among them was an Ingress Master.
Right now, a swarm of these masters closed in from every direction.
Fists, feet, and cold steel rained down from all angles. In a split second, at least eight distinct forces hit the white robe simultaneously.
Even a Bone Forging Master would die in a trap like this. A Bone Forging Master’s special force was strong, but it could not cover the whole body at once. Most of their skin remained at a normal strength, protected only by basic force. Only the spot where their special force traveled would become hard as iron.
Yet this deadly trap meant nothing to the intruder.
She flipped mid-air, accelerated straight ahead, and slammed into the first martial artist.
Bang!
She did not fall back. Using the reaction force from that single palm strike, she shot skyward and burst straight through a window of the highest tower in the compound—the Red Goose Tower.
The tower was nine stories high, rebuilt by Hong Daoyuan after a fire to mark his control over Feiye City. The nine levels mirrored his deepest ambition, and the name held its own secret meaning.
On the eighth floor, Hong Daoyuan stood with his hands behind his back, clad in a heavy black silk robe. He quietly watched the white figure crash into the room.
He stood alone, resting one hand on a heavy iron spear stuck into the floorboards. The thick outline of his muscle bulged through his black robes.
"A master from the prestigious White Jade Cap, and you still resort to midnight raids instead of the front door?" Hong Daoyuan said calmly.
Whoosh!
The female taoist in white robes landed smoothly a few meters away from him.
She felt a bit confused. The defense was tough at first, but the guards completely backed off halfway through. Clearly, this man had ordered them to stand down.
The Enshrined Hall had a few decent fighters. If they had all jumped her, she would have wasted quite some effort.
Still, now that she was here, it did not matter.
"You are Hong Daoyuan, Lord of Feiye City?" She glared at him, her voice cold.
The man before her looked young, over two meters tall, with a massive frame that gave off a natural pressure. Even without sensing his true strength, one felt an instinct not to move recklessly.
"I am. If you came through the main gate, I would have welcomed you with tea. But this entrance is a bit rude, don't you think?" Hong Daoyuan looked completely relaxed, as if everything was under control.
"Since you know I am from the White Jade Cap, you know why I am here," she said, her voice dropping lower.
Rumors said Hong Daoyuan was a rare genius backed by a mysterious master. Unless necessary, she did not want to start a full war with him.
"For the Chongsheng Cup?" Hong Daoyuan asked.
"Work with me. Once we get it, you take thirty percent," she said directly.
"Too little."
"Do not be greedy. Even if we get the cup, solve the puzzle, and find the vault, there are countless traps to deal with."
"I only want twenty percent," Hong Daoyuan smiled. "For the remaining ten percent, I want your help to trade for enough firearms from the provincial capital."
"Firearms... Deal!" She agreed on the spot.
"Very well. Next, I will order my men to check everyone who made contact with old man Zhuang recently. Person by person, place by place, we will sift through them all," Hong Daoyuan smiled softly.
Behind him stood more than just the Hong Family. The Shaoyang Sect, Qingdu Faction, and Mountain-River Gang had all merged under his command. Without their combined power, he could never have filled the Enshrined Hall with so many experts, nor could he have driven out the Xiangqu Cult.
The carriage rolled smoothly along the path.
The forest trail was rough. Deep holes, thick tree roots, steep rocks, and dense trees blocked the way.
Fortunately, Three Poisons Monk and the Spotted Leopard cleared the path up front.
The ride was surprisingly smooth. Whenever they hit a huge rock or pit, the monk would turn around, lift the carriage with his bare hands alongside White Dou, and carry it across.
Inside the carriage, Wei He looked at the sleeping Wan Qingqing, then closed his eyes to focus on his cultivation.
His Silk Force had already seeped into his vital organs, strengthening one-third of them.
This progress was great. At this rate, he would reach the Organ Cultivation stage in less than a year. He faced no bottlenecks; once his progress maxed out, he could use the Break-Through Orb to force a perfect breakthrough.
After practicing the Silk Force for a while, he let his organs rest and switched to the Golden Joy Five Elements Technique. This art could be trained up to the Bone Forging level and directly determined his movement speed, so it was worth his time.
Right now, he had organized his martial arts into three main categories.
First: The Sky Seal Nine Hits, centered around the Rain-Covering Force. Now that all the sub-techniques had generated force, the Rain-Covering Force could officially be renamed the Sea-Covering Force.
The Sea-Covering Force was the root of his strength and his core key to reaching Organ Cultivation. It contained all the forces of the nine core arts, taking its name from how the vast sea receives a thousand rivers.
Second: The supporting arts that boosted the Sea-Covering Force, collectively called the Cloud-Sea Codex. This included the Golden Joy Five Elements Technique, the Iron Ridge Armor, and various secret skills. Their main purpose was to boost his core force and patch up his weaknesses.
Third: Auxiliary skills, grouped under the Whale-Flood Master Manual. This contained the Whale-Flood Art for blood energy, the Master Art for poison resistance and power, the Autumn Deer Art for mental simulation, the Five Spirits Breath Art for hiding aura, and the Cloud Smoke Dissipation for discarding force.
This third category contained many random skills, representing the practical essence of everything he had learned.
Three categories is still a bit messy, Wei He thought. Boosting and auxiliary can be combined into one. Boosting is just another form of support anyway.
He pondered for a moment.
I will just combine them into the Cloud-Sea Codex.
From now on, I only need to find new manuals to upgrade different functions inside the Cloud-Sea Codex.
His thoughts became clearer. An overly complex system would dilute the advantage of his Break-Through Orb. He needed to cut away the useless branches.
Ideally, he would create one ultimate art that combined every single function. But right now, his foundation was not deep enough for that.
Sea-Covering Force for core cultivation. Cloud-Sea Codex for upgrades. The upgrades cover speed, physical body, poison resistance, and utility skills.
Using his modern mindset from his past life, Wei He began to measure these traits with clear numbers.
If I set a normal master's base stats as the average baseline, I can turn all my skills into raw numbers. That way, I can easily measure and organize everything I learn.
The idea clicked in his mind.
Right now, his core was strong, but he had too many side techniques with confusing names and mixed effects. In a fast battle, a second of hesitation over which skill to use could cost him a chance to kill. Grouping and measuring them by raw function would make his future path far cleaner.
In the future, I do not need to care about skill names. I can just label them as Speed Boost Level X.
Using a basic master as the baseline, I will always know exactly where I stand.
Every path of knowledge eventually leads to a unified system. Building a system of his own and fitting all external things into it was an inevitable step.
Thump.
A faint noise came from outside, and the carriage came to a slow halt.
"What happened?" Wei He asked, his mind pulled out of his thoughts.
"Sect Master, two groups are fighting up ahead. Looks like two factions from the same organization," Three Poisons Monk reported respectfully outside.
"Tell them to pause their fight and let us pass first," Wei He said carelessly.
"Yes, sir."
Three Poisons Monk answered and looked ahead.
On a gravel patch beside a small stream, over twenty people were locked in a bloody fight. Most were weaklings who had not even reached the martial master level.
To someone like him, who had traveled through Taizhou and seen real power, these people were complete trash. Even the two masters leading each side were weak. Their force was tiny, their moves were simple, and their forms were full of dead spots. They posed zero threat.
"Starting now, capture every martial master you see. I will handle them myself," Wei He added from inside the carriage.
On this trip back to Feiye City, he knew he would likely run into the groups that held grudges against his old Master Zheng. Having more pawns on hand was always useful.
"Understood!" Three Poisons Monk grinned, looking at the two fighting masters with pure malice.
Whenever he suffered, seeing others suffer made him feel much better. The more people that got dragged down, the happier he felt. It proved that he wasn't weak; his boss was just terrifying.
Look at White Dou—a Bone Forging Master brought along as a common laborer. What could a basic master like him do?
It wasn't his fault. It was an act of god.
In the snow, Wei Chun squatted in a corner, using freshly melted snow water to wash dishes.
Her mother, Li Cui, was inside the house talking with her father, Wei Tang. She didn't know what they were saying, and she didn't care. Her mind was elsewhere.
When she went out to work today, she saw government men asking questions around an antique shop near the old ruins. They were searching for someone.
On her way home, she also saw gang members holding portrait scrolls, asking passersby if they had seen the person drawn on them. Anyone with useful clues could claim a golden coin.
The sight filled her heart with a growing sense of dread.
