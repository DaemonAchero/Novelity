Wen Lian was furious. Everyone was riding the new reforms to grab market share, but her best friend wouldn’t budge, staying entirely in her shell.
Was now really the time to lay low?
A single step behind today meant years spent catching up tomorrow.
She nagged and pleaded for a long time, but Lin Xiaoxiao did not care at all.
Enraged, Wen Lian turned on her heel.
"Fine! I’m going back!"
She took a few steps, then glanced back over her shoulder. Lin Xiaoxiao was simply waving her off with a gentle smile.
"Get some good rest," Lin Xiaoxiao said softly.
"...Argh! You’re driving me crazy! What good is cash if it just sits at home doing nothing? What a waste!" Wen Lian clutched her hair in frustration.
"If you need it, I can lend it to you," Lin Xiaoxiao smiled.
Wen Lian’s anger instantly drained away at the sight of her friend's warm smile.
"I’m leaving!" She grumbled.
Recently, she saw her father’s business make massive gains by aggressively taking over market territory. The profits were far higher than before, so she had rushed over to push her friend to do the same.
Instead, she got a completely indifferent look. It set a fire in her chest.
Lin Xiaoxiao listened to everything that man named Wei said. She didn't expand her business, didn't recruit martial experts, and only cared about her own tiny piece of land. She lacked all ambition. It was infuriating.
"I'm out of here!" The more Wen Lian thought about it, the angrier she got. She left the estate, got into her carriage, and headed back.
She rode all the way to the Wen family mansion in Bear Mountain Town.
The Wen family was one of the top merchant households in the area. Their massive compound spanned two full streets.
It had over thirty buildings of various sizes, complete with lush gardens, ponds, and artificial rock walls. They employed over a hundred servants and guards.
Right now, inside the Crimson Crane Pavilion, the largest main building of the mansion.
On the second-floor balcony, Master Wen Shang was standing beside a heavily built man who wore a white eyepatch over one eye. Together, they looked down at the gardens below.
Wen Shang was over sixty. He was plump, friendly, and known for his peaceful, charitable approach to business.
Because he donated so much to charity, many outside called him "Wen the Philanthropist."
Yet right now, sweat kept dripping down both sides of his soft, cheerful face.
He lowered his head, holding a handkerchief, nodding constantly as he wiped away the sweat.
The cloth was nearly soaked through, but he didn't even notice.
"Lord Han, your request is truly impossible. We already donated all our stored grain before. We've exhausted every supply line, but we really can't find any more food or meat..." Wen Shang begged softly.
"That's not my problem," the one-eyed man said coldly. "I run Bear Mountain Town, and I must fill the required food rations. If we fall short and the higher-ups blame us, neither of us can afford the consequences."
His name was Han Yuzhong. He was the newly appointed Director of the Guangji Department in Bear Mountain Town.
The Guangji Department handled trade and supply storage for the city. It directly oversaw most of the Wen family's business operations.
"Besides, I checked the records," Han Yuzhong continued, his tone laced with threat. "When Wang Chenghao was in charge, you donated this much every year. Now that I take over, you offer me scrap change? Do you think I'm easier to fool than Wang Chenghao?"
Wen Shang cried out bitterly in his heart.
Just a few days ago, the former director Wang Chenghao was caught in a massive corruption bust and stripped of his rank.
A whole wave of officials fell with him, replaced by a fresh batch of new leaders.
The network of connections the Wen family had spent years building collapsed overnight. They had to start all over again from scratch.
"Lord Han, please forgive us! Our family really has nothing left to give," Wen Shang pleaded desperately. "The market is almost dry. We offered double the price, but we still can't buy any grain!"
"I don't care," Han Yuzhong sneered. "You made so many big moves recently and swallowed up so many profits. You think you can walk away without giving me real benefits?"
Wen Shang was speechless.
They had seized the chance to snatch up market share, and they indeed gained heavily.
But that had nothing to do with stored grain! In fact, those moves tied up a massive amount of cash. Now, Han Yuzhong was using it against him.
Several of the family's recent trade deals were too aggressive, stepping right into grey legal areas, and Han Yuzhong held all the evidence.
If they couldn't satisfy him, the Wen family was finished.
Wen Shang’s mind raced as he scrambled for excuses to persuade the official.
Then, he noticed the silence beside him.
He looked up and saw Han Yuzhong staring down into the garden, his mouth slightly open.
Wen Shang looked down too.
His daughter, Wen Lian, had just walked through the front gate. She was scolding a servant for failing to sweep away fallen flower petals.
Wen Lian had just turned twenty. She had a full, curving figure and striking, fiery beauty. She loved wearing bright red, looking like a hot chili pepper.
Han Yuzhong watched her every move from the second floor, his eyes narrowing as he found her more captivating by the second.
"Who is that to you?" he asked, narrowing his single eye.
Wen Shang’s heart skipped a beat, but he answered, "That is my daughter, Lian'er. Lord Han..."
"Ah, Niece Lian," Han Yuzhong’s voice softened, a slick smile spreading across his face. "I hadn't seen her before. I didn't realize she was such a beauty. We should spend more time together."
"This..." Wen Shang felt a sharp pain in his chest. He knew Han Yuzhong wanted his daughter.
Han Yuzhong didn't care much for money, but he was obsessed with women. Wen Shang had already sent him several beauties before, but now, the man wanted his daughter...
Wen Shang struggled internally. Yet, if the family couldn't cross this hurdle, there would be no future for any of them.
He reconsidered. She was old enough to marry anyway. In times like these, marrying a powerful man like Han Yuzhong wasn't the worst fate.
Han Yuzhong was lustful, but there were no rumors of him abusing his wives or concubines.
Wen Shang gritted his teeth, thinking of his two sons and another daughter whose futures depended on the family.
If one daughter could secure the safety and growth of the whole household, it was a price worth paying!
At the evening banquet.
Wen Shang deliberately called Wen Lian over to join them for drinks.
The more Han Yuzhong looked at her, the more pleased he became.
Later that evening, Wen Shang arranged for Wen Lian to walk with Han Yuzhong along the river near the mansion, aiming to foster feelings between them.
The twilight sky was dark and gloomy, with distant thunder rumbling in the air. Rain was coming.
Along the riverbank, Han Yuzhong walked ahead while Wen Lian followed behind, flanked by guards at a short distance.
"I don't know why my father made me walk with you," Wen Lian said coldly, breaking the silence. "But if you think this will win my heart, you're dead wrong!"
Han Yuzhong paused, then let out a laugh.
"Lady Lian has quite the spirit. I like that about you. A girl should have some backbone. Submissive women are far too boring."
"What does my spirit have to do with you?" Wen Lian snapped back. "Let me make this clear: matchmakers lined up for me could stretch all the way to Yubei Town. If you have any self-awareness, stop embarrassing yourself."
As she walked and barked at him, her foot caught on a stone. She stumbled with a sharp cry, falling forward.
"Are you alright?" Han Yuzhong moved fast, reaching out to catch her waist and pull her into his arms.
"Ah!! What are you doing?! Disgusting!" Wen Lian screamed. Feeling his big hand on her waist made her stomach turn.
Slap!
She swung her hand and struck Han Yuzhong across the face, then scrambled backward.
The mild concern on Han Yuzhong’s face froze. He touched the burning cheek where she hit him.
Slap!
Without warning, he lashed out with a vicious slap of his own, sending her spinning.
"Hold her down."
Two guards rushed forward, grabbing her arms on both sides.
"What are you doing?!" Wen Lian shrieked.
Before her voice could carry, Han Yuzhong rained down a relentless shower of slaps against her face.
Slap! Slap! Slap! Slap!
He struck her left and right with merciless force.
"Send word to Wen Shang," Han Yuzhong said, wiping his hands. "Tell him I'll discipline his daughter for him tonight. Take her away!"
The heavy blows knocked Wen Lian unconscious. Her screams died down, her head slumped forward, and blood trickled from the corner of her mouth.

The meat was pure Stone Deer meat.
It came from his one-third share of the monthly production from Jiuying.
A single Stone Deer farm yielded over two hundred pounds of refined meat each month. His portion was just enough to add solid nutrition to his family's meals.
Besides, his sister Wei Ying ate barely a tenth of what he did, which was almost negligible.
Ever since Zhou Shun died, the other branch factions of the Heavenly Seal Sect had gone completely quiet. They stopped causing trouble, clearly terrified by the ruthless display of force.
The Wanqing Gate finally entered a peaceful period of rest and growth.
Aside from practicing in the Western Mountains, guiding other disciples, and teaching Lin Xiaoxiao’s younger brother Lin Yuan, Wei合 spent his days studying poison recipes. Occasionally, he dropped by Hu Zicheng's place to check on the newly built martial academy.

With Wei合's current wealth, the academy's small expenses meant nothing to him. Furthermore, Hu Zicheng had local family support, so Wei合 didn't need to worry about operations.
He only needed to drop by once in a while to guide the kids' martial arts.
"Sigh..." Wei合 scraped the last bite of rice from his bowl, set it down, and stared at the empty seat across from him in silence.
His sister was out hanging around with Zhen Qi again. She was rarely home these days.
He used to dislike it, but seeing her skin turn smooth and her health improve so much, he couldn't bring himself to stop her.
Knock! Knock! Knock!
Sudden, rapid knocks rattled the courtyard gate.
Wei合 stood up and walked over. This courtyard was much smaller than their previous house in Heavenly Seal Town, taking only a few steps to reach the front door.
He pulled the gate open. Standing outside was Lin Xiaoxiao, out of breath and drenched in sweat.
Her usually neat, elegant dress was messy, showing how desperately she had rushed over.
"Brother Wei, it's an emergency!" Her chest heaved as she spoke with absolute gravity.
