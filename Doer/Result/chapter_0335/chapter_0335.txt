Wei He did not pick a fixed spot to begin his sensing. Instead, he prepared his supplies and decided to do it on the road.
Zhou Muqing was with him to help.
He planned to complete his first sensing step while traveling to the border outpost. Doing it this way saved time, and keeping his super-sensing active did not waste much of his inner strength.
The two were in no rush. They moved slowly and steadily along the road.
Wei He used his eyes and hands as his focus points for this first sensing step, hoping to speed up the process.
As they traveled deeper toward the border of Haizhou, the weather grew warmer, and the snow began to melt.
One day, they arrived at a county town along their route.
They entered the town, picked a small restaurant at random, and sat down to eat.
Wei He was dressed like a traveling martial artist. He wore a bamboo hat that hid his face.
After ordering a few simple dishes, he looked around. He felt a strange tension in the air.
The restaurant was small, with only two floors.
The first floor had six tables, and most of them were full.
Behind Zhou Muqing, a few people were whispering. When they saw Wei He and Zhou Muqing sit down, they lowered their voices even more.
Normal people could not hear what they were saying, but to a master like Wei He, their words were as clear as someone reading out loud.
"This is the third time this month," a man whispered. "Three entire families wiped out. Not a soul left alive, and blood red handprints were smeared all over the walls. Who could be so cruel? They did not even leave the farm animals alive..."
Three people sat at that table: a middle-aged man with a broadsword on his back, and two young teenagers.
The teenagers, a boy and a girl, wore practical martial arts clothes. They had clean, handsome faces and looked full of confidence.
"Father," the handsome boy asked softly. "Besides our Mo Family Village, the Baiqing Sect, and the Cold Sky Twin Blades, who else is joining this gathering? That Ghost Hand creature comes and goes like a phantom. If we do not have enough people, we will never find him."
"Ghost Hand comes from the Guangci Cult. He is a demon from their order," the middle-aged man replied with a slight smile. "With a cause like this, many strong fighters will answer the call. I heard famous men like Iron Palm Xiao Long and Hero Li Gonghao are already on their way here."
"This is a good chance for you two to open your eyes," the father added. "You get to see what real, top-tier fighters in the martial world look like."
"Father, do you know when Hero Li will get here?" the young girl asked in a pure, eager voice. "I heard he chased a Guangci demon across a thousand miles and killed several of them with his bare hands. His power is amazing. If I could meet him, this trip would be worth it."
"Soon, soon," the father smiled. "He should arrive with the men from the Sea Lantern Office. After all, his name is registered with them. He is not a rogue traveler like us."
His Mo Family Village had some fame in their local area, but outside that region, nobody cared who he was.
Compared to the famous masters joining this gathering, his village was just an ordinary group.
If his kids had not nagged him non-stop, he would not have brought them here.
Still, with so many fighters gathering, any Guangci demon who heard about it would be scared out of his mind and run away overnight.
The three ate and chatted about the famous figures of Haizhou.
As they talked, three men at another table suddenly slammed their chopsticks down.
Pak!
"What did you just say?" one of them barked.
The three men looked different from the local Da Yuan people. They had pale yellow eyebrows and high cheekbones, clearly marks of foreigners.
The man who spoke used broken Da Yuan words, staring at the Mo family with fierce, bloodthirsty eyes.
"We just killed a few fools outside who dared to mess with our holy cult, and now we run into three more. You Da Yuan people are brave. You are not afraid to die!"
The speaker had a wild mop of yellow hair, broad shoulders, and thick muscles. Copper rings encircled his arms. His thick eyebrows framed pale eyes, and he radiated a terrifying presence.
He stepped toward the Mo family.
The three turned pale and stood up. The father grabbed the hilt of his blade, looking at the intruders with a mix of shock and rage.
"You are from the Guangci Cult!"
Everyone in the restaurant turned to look.
Some customers quietly stood up, planning to slip out before a fight broke out.
The manager and two waiters hurried over, trying to calm things down while hinting that they had government connections.
The Guangci cultists shoved the manager aside and walked right up to the Mo family table, ready to strike.
Nearby, Wei He and Zhou Muqing frowned.
They had only stopped for a meal, yet trouble had found them anyway.
"Is the fight between the Guangci Cult and the Haizhou martial world really this bad?" Zhou Muqing whispered, her brow furrowed. "It is happening out in the open now. Does the government not care?"
"They are just playing both sides," Wei He said calmly. "The local officials are unsure. Seeing the Wu Kingdom growing stronger, it is normal for them to act this way."
Ordinary people had their own rules. Now that he was a True Master, he did not want to meddle in minor conflicts.
If he stepped in, the fight would scale up, creating trouble no one wanted.
While the two kept eating, the fight between the Mo family and the three cultists broke out.
The outcome was decided in a single exchange.
The father was no match for them. The moment he tried to channel his power, the yellow-haired man grabbed his arm and snapped it like a dry branch.
The yellow-haired man lunged forward and drove his fist straight into the father's chest.
Crack!
The heavy hit crushed the man's ribs. He flew backward and spat out a mouthful of blood.
"Father!"
The two teenagers panicked. In a flash, the cultists pinned them down.
One of the cultists laughed and squeezed the young girl's cheek.
"Senior Brother, this girl has soft skin. Killing her now would be a waste. Why don't we take her back so everyone can share her?"
"If you want to play, do it here," the yellow-haired man sneered, picking up the fallen father and taking his purse. "If we take her back, someone will nag us about it."
He drew his knife, planning to cut off the father's head right there as a trophy.
"These Da Yuan people are as weak as chicks, yet they love to talk big. They are asking to die."
Meanwhile, his companion began tearing off the girl's clothes as she screamed and cried.
Wei He and Zhou Muqing frowned deeper.
Only one other table of customers remained on the first floor. Seeing this scene, one of them could not hold back any longer.
A man tried to stand up to intervene.
His friend quickly pulled him back, whispering in a rush, "Are you crazy? That man is one of the eighty-eight disciples under the Yellow-Eyebrow Arhat! Can't you see their yellow eyebrows? The Yellow-Eyebrow Arhat protects his own at all costs. If you save those kids today, they will escape, but what happens when they hunt down your parents? What happens to your brothers and sisters?"
The man's chest heaved with rage, but he could only watch as the cultist tore away the pretty girl's outer jacket.
She screamed and tried to break free, but the cultist grabbed her ankle and dragged her back.
Beside her table, Wei He let out a quiet sigh.
He had not wanted to get involved. But right in front of his eyes, in broad daylight, these men were committing such a vile act.
Worse, these Guangci cultists kept insulting all Da Yuan people.
If he had not seen it, he would have ignored it. But since it happened right in front of him...
"Muqing," Wei He said softly, without moving himself.
"Yes!" Zhou Muqing said.
She had been unable to stomach the sight for a while, holding back only because Wei He was the leader.
Their trip was meant for his sensing step, a critical task, so they did not want to cause trouble. That was why she had endured it until now.
Now that Wei He gave the word, she pulled out a wooden chopstick, broke it into three pieces, and snapped her wrist backward.
Swish! Swish! Swish!
Three sharp sounds echoed through the room.
The three pieces of chopstick slammed deep into the chests of the three cultists. They grunted and stumbled back.
The men pulled the wooden pieces out of their chests, bringing out streams of blood, and turned their glares straight onto Zhou Muqing.
"Someone actually has the guts to fight back? Kill them!" The yellow-haired man threw the chopstick piece aside as if it were nothing and charged at her first.
Zhou Muqing was surprised. Her attack had failed to put them down.
The wooden pieces had been infused with her inner strength, yet they caused no serious damage.
Even though she had thrown them casually, it was strange that her inner force had no real effect on them.
Her eyes turned cold. She stood up and turned to face them.
"You three spoke with filth and committed violence in public. Now let us see what you..."
Before she could finish her sentence, the yellow-haired leader lunged at her. His arms, heavy with copper rings, slammed toward her chest.
Aiming such a move at a woman was a blatant insult.
Anger flared in Zhou Muqing's heart. Her right palm lashed out like a streak of lightning, striking the yellow-haired man's chest before his move could land.
The strike was blindingly fast and totally unexpected. The man had no time to block or react.
Boom!
The force of her angry palm sent the yellow-haired man flying several meters back. He crashed to the ground and rolled over.
Yet he was still breathing. Blood poured from his mouth as he glared at Zhou Muqing.
"You... how dare you... how dare you?!"
Zhou Muqing did not care about his words. Since she had started the fight, she meant to finish it.
She struck out two more times, snapping the chest bones of the remaining two cultists. With no chance to escape, they vomited blood and died on the spot.
By now, people from the second floor had come downstairs. They and the restaurant manager stood frozen in shock.
Zhou Muqing had destroyed all three cultists with a few casual moves, leaving two dead and one dying. Her power was terrifying.
The manager stepped forward, his voice trembling with genuine worry.
"Fighters, you... you should leave quickly! If Yellow Zhiying's master comes looking for you, it will be too late."
"Yellow Zhiying's master is the famous Yellow-Eyebrow Arhat. He moves like the wind. Even the Sea Lantern Office failed to catch him several times and lost men in the process."
The manager meant well. Seeing that they had saved the kids, he wanted them out of danger.
"Do not worry, Manager. We know what we are doing," Zhou Muqing said, walking over to the Mo family.
The father was on the verge of passing out, while his son and daughter knelt beside him, crying.
When they saw Zhou Muqing approach, the two teenagers bowed to thank her for saving their lives.
Zhou Muqing helped the father sit up and began treating his wounds while asking a few simple questions.
As the murderers lay dead on the floor, the teenagers' fear slowly faded, replaced by deep worry for their father.
While they talked, Wei He suddenly raised his head and looked toward the main entrance.
Just a moment ago, he felt someone pass by outside.
That person radiated a mass of vital energy far beyond that of an ordinary fighter, far surpassing even a Bone-Forging master.
That heat and presence were impossible to hide.
"Muqing. Someone is here," Wei He said.
He finished the last bite of food in his bowl, wiped his mouth, and stood up.
