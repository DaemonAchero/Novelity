“Just like the stories say, this kid has a special force that blocks Real Qi,” Song Xiange said. He shook his hand, changed his palm into a fist, and raised it.
Invisible force rose from his fist like thin smoke.
“If this were a normal fight, we might face you fairly. But we are out of time, so…”
Song Xiange’s eyes went cold. He flicked his right fist up and down, aiming straight at Wei He from far away.
“Got you!”
An invisible line of force shot out like a hidden dart, striking Wei He’s body.
This force was sharp and dense. The moment it hit, it bit into Wei He’s shield like a needle.
“Again!” Song Xiange threw another punch, shooting a second line of force that stuck into Wei He.
Wei He was busy fighting Song Ting. He had no time to worry about these sneaky attacks.
The two men traded blows on the dirt road, blasting holes of all sizes into the ground.
Trees along the path snapped on contact. Rocks shattered into dust.
Song Xiange’s forced lines stuck into Wei He’s shield like iron nails, refusing to come out.
Wei He gathered his Bone Force into his palms and pushed Song Ting back with a lightning punch. He stepped back a few paces, shaking his body to shake off the foreign energy.
“Explode!” Song Xiange shouted from a distance.
The trapped force inside Wei He’s shield suddenly burst.
Ice-cold energy went off like a bomb inside his shield, messing up his internal loop.
“What is this?!” Wei He was shocked. He had never seen someone use energy like this before.
If they thought this trick could defeat him, they were dead wrong.
His Whale Flood skill kept his blood pumping with endless power. His stamina far exceeded normal men.
He dropped his shield completely for a split second, then instantly built a new one.
It took less than a second to reset his form and clear the stun.
Song Ting rushed in again, dropping a heavy fist from above like a falling mountain.
BOOM!
Wei He reached out with both hands and caught the fist.
He twisted it hard clockwise. At the same time, his right knee snapped upward, aiming right for Song Ting’s elbow joint.
This was a deadly move from his Heart-Breaking Seal.
If it hit, Song Ting’s shield would never survive this three-way strike.
Hands and knee hitting all at once had enough power to smash any defense.
They had fought long enough to know they were at the same level of raw power.
If two fighters had the same strength, and one used a single hand while the other used both hands and a knee, the single hand would lose every time.
And Wei He was slightly faster. Since Song Ting had no flaws, Wei He forced one open by turning the man's own move against him.
“Interesting,” Song Ting laughed instead of panicking.
His arm shook, and his force turned slippery. Wei He could not hold on, and the arm slid right out of his grip.
Without a grip on the arm, the killing move failed.
Song Ting did a light backflip and landed smoothly.
“You are so young, yet you have such skill and force. Are all young men down the mountain as good as you now?”
“You dodged it, didn't you, senior?” Wei He smiled.
“I didn't dare take that hit,” Song Ting said. “If I took even a small scratch, the poison inside your force would infect me. That is truly dangerous.”
Song Ting had seen right through it. While others thought Wei He used separate poisons, Song Ting realized the poison was mixed inside the force itself.
“Let’s go again!”
Song Ting bent his back low.
“Since my force is not as raw as yours, let me show you what true martial technique looks like!”
The moment he finished speaking, he vanished.
In a split second, Song Ting appeared at Wei He’s side, throwing a heavy hook right at his temple.
BOOM!
Wei He raised his arm to block and tried to counter. But when he struck Song Ting’s forearm, it felt like grabbing a slippery fish. His power slid harmlessly off the skin.
“What?!” Refusing to believe it, Wei He threw a quick pair of palms straight at Song Ting.
BOOM! BOOM!
Two heavy thuds sounded.
These were not the sounds of Wei He hitting Song Ting. They were the sounds of Song Ting hitting Wei He in the chest.
Wei He’s hands had slipped off Song Ting’s defensive shield once again.
“Again!”
Facing this for the first time, Wei He gritted his teeth. Knowing his shield could take a few more hits, he put everything into his hands, gathering his Bone Force to strike like crazy.
With his speed, he threw over ten moves in a flash.
Yet all ten moves slid harmlessly off Song Ting’s body, finding no solid ground.
After a quick exchange, the two pulled apart again.
Wei He looked down at his chest. His shield there had been punched through, though it was quickly fixing itself.
He had almost been crippled by Song Ting just now.
“What kind of move is that?” He took a deep breath, knowing he was in trouble.
He couldn't even beat Song Ting quickly, and Song Xiange was still watching from the side.
If they teamed up, he would be dead.
What confused him was that Song Ting’s force hadn't gotten stronger, yet it worked ten times better.
“Whether it is a special force effect or a secret skill, your body and energy should leave a trace, but I see nothing,” Wei He said calmly.
“Do you know the four realms of martial moves?” Song Ting smiled faintly.
“This is called the Flowing Water Realm!”
He shifted his foot and charged at Wei He again.
Wei He stopped hiding his true power.
He activated his Tower State. His muscles swelled, his energy expanded, and his body grew even taller than before.
His large hands swept forward. A terrifying black force wrapped around his palms like thick smoke.
“DIE!” Wei He roared, thrusting his palm against Song Ting’s fist.
BOOM!!
This time, under the full power of his Tower State, Wei He felt it clearly. The moment his power touched the enemy, Song Ting used an insanely clever trick to slide his power away.
At the exact moment of impact, Song Ting seemed to predict where Wei He was pushing and at what angle.
He simply went with the flow, dodging the deadly hit cleanly.
It looked like Wei He hit him head-on, but in reality, Wei He hit nothing but empty air.
Not only that, but his own immense power dragged him forward. Song Ting even gave him a light push to help him along.
With both forces combined, Wei He lost his balance.
“It's over!” Song Ting smiled, launching a left fist straight up toward Wei He’s waist.
STAB!!
His fist was suddenly caught. Wei He’s left hand grabbed it tight.
Even though Wei He had been falling forward from the push, a brand new wave of power surged from inside his body the moment before he hit the dirt.
It came from the two blood sacks of his Whale Flood skill.
This new power stabilized his weight instantly, bringing him back to a solid stance.
“This time, you lose!”
Wei He changed his move like lightning, swinging his right fist around toward the back of Song Ting’s head.
Even Song Ting did not expect this.
Tower State combined with the raw strength of the Whale Flood skill pushed Wei He’s speed, power, and burst to his absolute limit.
A deadly reverse hook.
Massive dark force spread wild through the air like black smoke.
“Halt!”
Just as Song Ting’s face changed and the fist was about to crush his head, Song Xiange roared from the side.
A sharp, ice-cold surge of force hit Wei He, locking his body in place for a brief moment.
Between masters, a brief moment was more than enough.
That split-second pause made Wei He’s fist miss its target.
Song Ting ducked under his arm, spun around, and drove a fist straight for Wei He’s eye.
He had almost died just now. Terrified, his easygoing mood vanished, replaced by pure malice.
In an instant, the attacker became the defender.
Wei He growled, using his raw power to break free from Song Xiange’s hold. He slammed both hands together, trying to catch Song Ting’s incoming fist.
“Halt!”
Song Xiange’s voice echoed again, freezing Wei He’s body one more time.
BOOM!!!
He couldn't dodge it this time.
Song Ting’s fist hit Wei He right in his right eye. His Real Qi erupted like a geyser.
He held nothing back, pouring every drop of power into his fist.
He had lowered his guard for a second earlier and almost died for it. He could not afford another mistake in a fight to the death.
“A pity… If this were a one-on-one, I would be no match for you,” Song Ting thought with a sigh.
BOOM!!!
The heavy punch landed square on Wei He’s head. The heavy force and the destructive nature of the Real Qi shredded Wei He’s protective shield layer by layer.
After the two forces canceled each other out, the leftover power broke through everything and hit Wei He’s eye directly.
PUNCH!
A grunt of pain came from Wei He, but at the same time, Song Ting let out a sharp scream.
The two split apart and froze.
Blood leaked slowly from one of Wei He’s eyes.
Song Ting had a deep hole bleeding in his stomach.
“What… is this?!” Song Ting was used to Wei He’s fist speed and force rhythm.
This sudden surprise attack caught him completely off guard, leaving him no time to defend.
Because this hit was not a palm or a fist. It was a leg strike.
While Song Ting put all his power into his attack, his own defense opened up. Wei He kicked straight through his weak shield, leaving a bloody hole in his stomach.
It was this kick that saved Wei He’s eye from exploding.
“I forgot to tell you… I don't just use palm strikes!!” Wei He let out a beastly roar and charged forward again.
This was the most dangerous battle he had ever fought. If he hadn't used his kick—strengthened by his Five Elements technique—to wound his enemy, his head would have been blown off.
The speed and power he was so proud of were just ordinary to these masters.
His massive force and his Tower State were nothing more than a slightly stronger monster to them, something they knew how to handle.
The active force and secrets of these masters gave them crazy speed, power, and strength.
And it wasn't just that—even their body recovery was far beyond that of normal humans.
