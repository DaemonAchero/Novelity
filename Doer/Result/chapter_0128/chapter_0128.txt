Three small birds fell dazed into Wei He’s hand. Their bodies were burning hot and acting strange, trying to fly but failing. It didn't look like drunkenness.
He tossed the birds aside and picked up his wine jar. He turned around, planning to head back inside, but stopped dead in his tracks.
On the open ground outside the bamboo grove stood a pale green paper umbrella. A traditional landscape with wild egrets was painted on it.
A man stood beneath the umbrella, staring at him with a calm gaze.
"Did you see that?" The man raised the edge of his umbrella, showing a fair, delicate, and handsome face.
"Of course." Wei He was surprised. The military arms smuggling case had barely settled, and here he was, running into trouble again so soon.
Wei He shifted his body instantly, taking a fast step to the left. Behind him, a row of silver needles silently pinned into a bamboo stalk.
"Silencing the witness?" Wei He was startled. He hadn't even accused the man yet, but this guy was bold enough to strike first.
"Just testing my hand," the man smiled gently. "This isn't a good spot. The bamboo grove is too small, and the street is right past it."
"And when you and I walked in, plenty of people saw us," Wei He added.
He couldn't guess the man's true strength yet. But judging by the power behind those needles, anyone who wasn't used to dodging ambushes would have been hit. Of course, whether the needles could pierce his body-protecting force was another story, but it proved the man was no weakling.
More importantly, the man clearly saw his Heavenly Seal Sect inner court robes. He also saw that the rain didn't even wet Wei He's clothes. That meant the stranger knew who he was—a martial master, a force-user.
"Tell me, do you think I'll kill you right now?" the man in green asked softly. He seemed to be talking to himself, yet direct to Wei He.
"Are you sure you can beat me?" Wei He asked back.
"I don't know," the man replied. "You are strange. You must be using a technique to hide your blood energy. You look like an ordinary master, but you feel different. It's like you train in multiple arts, but training in multiple arts shouldn't get you this far..."
Wei He was chilled to the bone. This man could read so much off him.
"From the start until now, you've been spreading poison continuously," Wei He countered smoothly. "Four types of poisonous liquid, three types of poisonous smoke. Odorless, colorless—a compound poison. You knew I was a force-using master, and you knew it was raining, yet you still used it. That means you're confident your poison works on martial masters, or at least breaks through force protection. Too bad your delivery is sloppy."
The man's smile vanished. He was clearly shocked by Wei He's words.
Silence fell over the bamboo grove. They stood in a tense standoff.
Neither was sure if they should make a move. Since they couldn't see through each other, neither could guarantee a win. Unknowns brought caution.
The rain slowed to a light drizzle, replaced by a cool wind. The two men stayed as still as statues.
After a long pause, the man finally spoke.
"How about a trade?"
"Deal," Wei He agreed instantly. "I want your compound poison, the one that works on martial masters."
He had been waiting for the man to offer.
"..." The man's expression twitched. He felt like he had been tricked, but there was no way Wei He could have planned every step. It had to be a coincidence.
"...Fine," the man nodded. "You keep quiet about what you saw earlier."
"Deal," Wei He said without hesitation.
"...Aren't you afraid I'll find a chance to kill you later?" The man was surprised Wei He trusted his word so easily.
"Are you that confident you can kill me instead of me killing you?" Wei He asked. "You can try right now if you want."
"..." The man fell silent again.
"You're very confident," he finally said.
"So are you," Wei He said calmly. "It's my first time meeting someone who knows nothing about his opponent yet boasts about killing him."
"Aren't you from the Heavenly Seal Sect?" the man asked back.
"..." This time, Wei He stayed quiet. Those words meant too much. Did it mean he could kill anyone as long as they were from the Heavenly Seal Sect?
"Can you defeat Shangguan Ji?" Wei He asked.
"..." The man went quiet once more. "Not yet. But in the future, yes."
"..." Wei He was speechless. Bragging was truly free.
"Whatever the future holds, as long as you can't beat Shangguan Ji right now, you can't beat me," Wei He said flatly.
Silence fell between them again as the cool breeze swayed the bamboo.
Before long, a figure staggered toward them. It was a pretty singer dressed in a short pink skirt and thin silk veil. She was heavily drunk, leaning against a bamboo stalk and dry-heaving onto the ground.
She couldn't throw anything up, but something remained stuck in her throat. Having no choice, she knelt on the ground, holding her neck and reaching into her mouth to gag herself. After trying for a while with no luck, her face flushed red and she burst into tears.
"Dad... Sob... Mom... It hurts so much..."
"I want to go home... I don't want to stay here..."
She tried to stand up, but after a few wobbly steps, her legs gave out and she collapsed into a faint. A faint red stain began spreading beneath her, coloring the soil.
Both men turned their attention to the girl.
The man in green stayed silent for a second, then walked over slowly and held his umbrella over her.
"She's going to die," he said quietly. "My poison is still floating around here. There's no cure."
Wei He walked up behind him, slightly surprised. He didn't expect a guy like this to care about a cheap singer's life. But she belonged to the Yuxing District, after all.
He thought for a moment and said, "She won't die. I cleared your poison long ago."
"..." The man fell quiet once more, his pride taking a hit. He raised his head to look at Wei He. "She's bleeding."
"Her period came," Wei He said.
"You mean her heavenly cycle?"
"..." Wei He wanted to say he misspoke, but he couldn't take the words back now. "Where I come from, we call it that," he claimed with a straight face.
"..." They stared at each other.
"Where you come from, do they also call skin pig hide?" the man suddenly asked.
"What, you don't believe me?" Wei He put on a shocked look.
Seeing his expression, the man in green actually started to doubt himself. Could people really call it that in his hometown?
"Fine." He knew he couldn't win an argument against this guy. He tapped his foot on the ground, gently lifting the girl's body into the air with a soft kick.
A gentle wave of inner force wrapped around the girl, landing her safely onto the back kitchen hallway nearby. Soon, some servant girls spotted her and quickly carried her off to the medical clinic.
Inside the dark bamboo grove, the two watched the servants leave before pulling back their gazes.
"Didn't expect you to have a good heart," Wei He said. "I view you a bit differently now."
"To be honest, I wouldn't care even if you talked. I just didn't want to waste time hunting you down later, so I used poison," the green-clothed man turned around and said. He looked at Wei He with growing interest. "You're an interesting guy. Can you teach me how you cleared my poison?"
"Not for free," Wei He replied.
"Fair," the man nodded. "Guess we don't have a real reason to fight then."
"And?"
"So, you can come find me later to make the trade," the man said calmly.
"Are you going to leap into the sky, fly away, and drop your cool name right now? Do you think that looks stylish?" Wei He suddenly cut in.
"..." The man lost count of how many times he had been stunned today. His face felt hot.
"Read fewer hero novels and you'll act more normal," Wei He advised.
"Then what should I do?" the man asked back.
"I don't know."
"You don't know?"
"I don't know what you should do, but you can copy me," Wei He said.
"..." The man was completely speechless this time. He felt he had just met the thickest-skinned person in the world.
"Forget it. I'll state my name. I am Wang Shaojun, known in the martial world as one of the Three Young Masters of Xuanjing," the man said seriously.
"I am Wei He. Branch Leader of the Heavenly Seal Sect," Wei He answered directly.
Even if he didn't state his name, the man could easily look him up. Wei He had become a known figure lately, drinking and eating at Yuxing District every day. Too many people recognized him.
Wang Shaojun?
Wei He didn't expect to meet the guy he had just heard was a useless extra among the Three Masters. He doubted the rumors were true. A guy this sneaky couldn't just be an extra. If the rumor was real, it meant one of two things: First, this guy was hiding his true power on purpose.
Second, the other two were so strong that they completely outshined Wang Shaojun.
Wei He leaned toward the first guess.
"Remember your word. Teach me that clearing method when you come," Wang Shaojun said seriously.
"You dare use poison without knowing how to cure it? Aren't you afraid of poisoning yourself?" Wei He said, helpless.
"I carry anti-poison treasures on me."
"..." Fine, another rich master. Anti-poison treasures were something Wei He had never even heard of.
"Remember to find me. If you don't, I'll go to the Heavenly Seal Sect myself," Wang Shaojun left with those final words before vanishing.
Wei He naturally agreed. Wang Shaojun's compound poison was a massive prize for him. If he could figure out how it managed to affect force-using masters, his own combat tricks would jump to a whole new level.
He could clear the poison only because every toxin had a basic chemical structure. He simply used general methods to destroy that structure, like high heat or strong chemical environments.
Being able to destroy a poison didn't mean he knew how to make it. Or rather, he wasn't truly curing the poison—he was just breaking it apart.
