Boom!
Boom!
Boom!
Zhou Xingtong took heavy steps on the ground. Every step launched his body forward, flying low through the air.
He was born with divine strength and bronze skin. Even without armor, traveling like this was easy for him. He used all his power perfectly.
Power, especially explosive power, easily turned into speed.
He was not as fast as martial artists who specialized in leg techniques, but he was close. His raw power gave him a huge advantage when running straight ahead.
Armor was a burden for someone at his level. The higher a warrior went, the less useful weapons and armor became. Most armor was just for decoration or to protect weak soldiers. When a warrior of his rank wore armor, it was mostly just to show off status.
Splatter.
He stepped into a puddle, sending water, mud, and rotten leaves flying everywhere. The puddle instantly turned into a wet hole.
Zhou Xingtong slowed down fast, sliding forward a few steps before stopping.
"Who is there?! Get out here!"
He stared straight ahead with cold eyes. Thick fog filled the air. Between the dark shadows of the trees, he heard faint breathing.
Tap, tap.
A shadow walked out of the fog.
It was a strong black leopard with two black horns on its head. The leopard was over four meters long. Its muscles moved smoothly as it walked without a sound. It was a mix of power and beauty.
"A Horned Leopard?"
Zhou Xingtong charged forward suddenly. The black leopard panicked, turned around, and ran into the deep forest, disappearing in a flash.
Zhou Xingtong laughed out loud, finding it funny.
After this small distraction, he sped up again.
Above him, he heard the faint cry of a purple falcon, guiding his path.
nearby trees grew taller and thicker. The fog became heavier. Zhou Xingtong took out a light green pill, put it under his tongue, and kept moving.
After a few steps, he stopped again.
A figure stood quietly on a high tree branch, looking down at him.
The figure was big, two meters tall, which was large for a normal person. He had long black hair falling over his shoulders and wore a wide black cloak. Standing in the dark, his face was hard to see.
"Zhou Xingtong?" the man said in surprise.
"Are you the little rat that killed my men earlier?" Zhou Xingtong licked his lips, his eyes turning wild.
"Rat? Heh..." The figure jumped down lightly and landed on the ground, revealing a calm, plain face.
It was Wei He, who had just parted ways with Wan Ling and the others.
"Want to fight?" Wei He looked at Zhou Xingtong, who was a head taller than him.
"Fight? You nobody! Who do you think you are!"
With a loud explosion, Zhou Xingtong stomped his foot. Dirt and grass exploded behind him as he rushed at Wei He.

West Mountain, outside the Wanqing Sect base.
Wooden houses were on fire one after another. Heavy smoke rolled out as flames swallowed everything. The fire lit up the night sky in red.
The crackling fire and the messy night wind made Wang Shaojun feel uneasy.
"Something went wrong," Wang Shaojun muttered. He looked around the base and saw many tidy footprints and horse tracks on the ground.
"The Chijing Army must have chased after them."
Wang Shaojun thought for a moment. He had just made a plan with Wei He to kill the experts of Guiyan Tower, but now this happened.
After pausing, he took a mask from his coat.
It was a fancy silver mask with cloud patterns. It fit tightly against his face, highlighting his handsome features.
He put on the mask, pulled out a small copper mirror, and checked his appearance by the firelight. He fixed his hair, tying it up neatly with a hairpin.
Once he was sure he looked fine, he took out a pair of dark red daggers. He held one in each hand and swung them around to check his grip.
"Old Wei, if you die, it means you were unlucky. If you survive, it depends on your luck. Zhou Xingtong is not an easy guy to deal with..."
With a sigh, Wang Shaojun ran off in the direction of the horse tracks, disappearing into the dark night.
CLANG!!
Wei He thrust his palm out like lightning, hitting Zhou Xingtong's forearm.
Zhou Xingtong's skin was bronze and looked like real flesh, but the impact sounded like two heavy pieces of metal hitting each other.
When Wei He practiced Huishan Fist, his fists made a similar noise, but this was different.
His current power came from the fourth level of Rain Cover Power, mixed with the fifth level of Flying Dragon Skill and his speed boost. The impact from his high speed sprint and stacked energy was far beyond normal martial artists. Even a Bone-Forging master could not ignore this strike.
"Is that all?" Zhou Xingtong shook his arm, and a swirling wave of air blew out from his skin.
Using only his protective energy, he easily blocked Wei He's strike.
"I thought you were strong, but you are just trash."
He casually watched as Wei He jumped backward, landing lightly on a tree trunk in a squat.
"Is that so?" Wei He thought. He had just tested the waters and now knew that without using the Whale Flood Method, he might not even break Zhou Xingtong's defense.
This guy's skin was ridiculously thick.
Hiss...
Wei He took a deep breath. A large amount of air and fog rushed into his mouth and nose like thick streams.
The power of the Whale Flood Method began to unleash as his muscles relaxed, filling his whole body.
Wei He had always held back his strength in fights to avoid raising suspicion. After all, he had done many big things using his extreme strength.
But now, he did not need to hide anymore.
Crack, crack...
His muscles began to swell up.
His veins filled with blood, growing tough and thick.
Wei He's body expanded slightly. A massive amount of blood energy poured out from the two blood pouches of his Whale Flood Method, spreading through his entire body.
Phooey...
Wei He exhaled deeply. A thick wave of white air blasted out from his nose and mouth like a giant snake, blowing away the cold fog in front of him.
Rain Cover Power and Iron Ridge Armor Power worked together on his arms. The two complementary energies wrapped around each other, forming an even stronger defense on his forearms.
"Again," Wei He said from above. He jumped down, diving toward his opponent.
His hands were held like blades, chopping down with all his force.
Blood energy surged into his arms, making them swell even larger, looking almost non-human.
"Come at me!" Zhou Xingtong called out. He was no fool. Just from the sound of the wind and the heavy booms, he knew this strike was powerful.
However, he had never met an opponent stronger than him.
He raised both palms, swinging them from left and right toward the center. He wanted to trap Wei He right in the middle and crush him in one move.
Layered Sound Power vibrated through his body. Energy rolled like ocean waves, flowing from his body to his arms like a rushing stream.
BOOM!!!
Their four heavy arms smashed into each other.
Their arms squeezed and deformed, swelling with blood until their skin turned dark red.
HUMM!!!
The clothes on their arms exploded at the same time, tearing into shreds that flew in all directions.
The ground under Zhou Xingtong's feet sank down. His feet were pushed deep into the dirt by the heavy weight.
A continuous stream of massive force pressed down on him from above.
"This strength..." Zhou Xingtong widened his eyes.
Sizzle!
His upper body expanded in a flash, and his arms turned jet black.
"Still too weak! Hahahahaha!!!" His face suddenly turned wild as he burst out laughing. He grabbed toward Wei He with both arms. "Did you think I was surprised, you fool?!"
When Wei He let go, his full-power palm blade chopped down hard, hitting Zhou Xingtong right in the middle of his forehead.
BANG!!
Not a single scratch.
Zhou Xingtong grinned savagely and reached out, grabbing for Wei He like lightning.
Layered Sound Power vibrated over his palm. Wherever it touched, everything became loose and weak. It could make any martial artist lose their focus and energy instantly.
Slash!
His palm barely grazed the side of Wei He's face, cutting off a strand of black hair.
Wei He leaned back to dodge. His body slid to the side, rolling a few times before landing next to a large tree.
"Oh? You dodged it?" Zhou Xingtong turned around, sneering at Wei He. "Your strength is pretty good. But too bad. Do you think strength is all I rely on?"
Wei He narrowed his eyes and turned to stare at him.
In that quick exchange, he had used all his strength. But compared to Zhou Xingtong, he was still a bit weaker.
"You were hiding your true power before?" Wei He asked in a deep voice.
"I wasn't hiding it, I just didn't need to use that much force," Zhou Xingtong said. He tore off his ruined shirt, revealing a powerful chest that looked like it was made of bronze.
"You are strong enough to give me some fun." He walked forward slowly, then suddenly sped up.
He lowered his arms. As his palms vibrated, streams of energy gathered together.
Slice, slice.
A massive amount of energy extended out from his fingertips by several centimeters.
The invisible energy formed blade shapes, attaching to the ends of his palms.
The energy twisted the air, vibrating at high speed, creating two airflow blades that looked like chainsaws.
"This is the peak form of my energy, called the Invisible Whip. It cuts through things better than weapons."
Zhou Xingtong jumped up, flipped his body in midair, and chopped down at Wei He with both hands.
Swish!
Wei He dodged to the side, avoiding the hit. But the tree trunk next to him was not so lucky. The bark and trunk were sliced clean through in an instant.
Zhou Xingtong chased him closely. Vines and grass on the ground could not slow him down at all.
His palms acted like sharp blades. Combined with his heavy strength, whatever he touched broke apart.
"All you know how to do is run? Weren't you acting tough just now?!"
Zhou Xingtong spread his arms, swinging them like a windmill.
Everywhere he went, tree trunks and rocks were sliced in two.
Wei He dodged left and right, stepping back again and again. Fine streams of powder spilled out quietly from where he passed, spreading into the air.
"You really are strong," Wei He said as he jumped back, landing lightly on an open spot. "But winning a fight takes more than just strength."
Wei He put his right hand behind his back, clenched it into a fist, and then opened it.
Puff.
A tiny bit of powder flew out from his open hand, floating into the air with the wind.
This was his final mixed poison meant for martial artists.
Zhou Xingtong bent his back slightly, a savage smile appearing on his face.
"Is that so? Let me see how you plan to beat me then!"
"Marching Kill Technique! Phantom Form!"
He reached into his pouch, grabbed something, and threw it out.
Puff!!
A large cloud of thin metal needles shot out. The steel needles were packed with bone energy, making their hardness and speed far greater than normal hidden weapons. They reached Wei He in the blink of an eye.
Normal moves could only put bone energy into a few weapons at most. But his Phantom Form allowed him to spread bone energy evenly into a vast number of tiny needles.
He mixed needles filled with bone energy with normal ones. The real and fake strikes blended together, making them impossible to tell apart.
To the naked eye, the needles thrown with Phantom Form all looked like they carried the same energy. But if someone assumed they were all the same, they would make a huge mistake.
Swish swish swish swish swish!!
The dense pack of steel needles arrived right in front of Wei He.
Caught off guard, Wei He lowered his body and slid across the ground, dodging the needles.
His raw speed was not much faster than Zhou Xingtong's, but his ability to stop and turn was incredible, as if gravity had no effect on him.
Taking advantage of a brief opening, Wei He closed in on Zhou Xingtong's side, slamming both palms hard into his lower back.
Thump thump!!
Two heavy sounds exploded out.
Zhou Xingtong's body shook slightly, but he turned around as if nothing happened and threw a palm back at Wei He.
One was fast, and the other was strong. They exchanged dozens of moves in an instant.
Zhou Xingtong kept getting hit, but it was useless. His bronze skin was so tough that even the Organ-Training experts of the Heavenly Seal Nine couldn't break it.
Wei He's raw power from the Whale Flood Method was only equal to a normal Bone-Forging force. Even with the boost from Flying Dragon Skill and Rain Cover Power, it reached the peak of Bone-Forging power at best.
As they fought, Wei He realized he had miscalculated. This guy was extremely difficult to deal with.
Except for his eyes and ears, Zhou Xingtong did not bother to defend any other body parts, letting Wei He hit him freely.
Even if someone wore heavy iron armor, they couldn't take this many direct hits. Yet Zhou Xingtong remained completely unharmed, without even a scratch on his skin.
While Wei He felt annoyed, Zhou Xingtong was just as frustrated.
His main cultivation method, the Marching Kill Technique, was built for direct, forward attacks. It crushed everything on the battlefield. Even when facing fast targets, he could use his Phantom Form hidden weapons to kill them.
But the guy in front of him was a monster.
Even martial artists who focused on leg techniques had to deal with inertia when stopping or turning, needing a moment to adjust their blood and energy.
But Wei He didn't need to do that.
His raw speed wasn't the fastest Zhou Xingtong had ever seen, but his movement was the most bizarre.
Moving front, back, left, right, up, and down, he was like a ghost with no weight, flashing around softly and quickly.
Yet, if anyone thought this guy lacked power, the heavy blows landing on Zhou Xingtong's body reminded him that a single careless mistake could cause serious damage.
He wasn't afraid of hits to normal spots, but he still had to protect his eyes, nose, and ears.
The two fought back and forth in the forest, running through trees and snapping them in half.
The ground was soon covered in holes, looking like a battlefield hit by bombs.
Zhou Xingtong couldn't grab his target, and Wei He couldn't break his opponent's defense. But neither of them wanted to give up.
"If you have the guts, fight me head-on! Let's see who falls first!" Zhou Xingtong roared in anger, smashing a palm through a pine tree and pulling his hand out.
"If you have the guts, catch up to me first and then we can fight!" Wei He replied coldly.
After saying that, he suddenly felt something was off, but in the heat of battle, he didn't have time to think about it.
After fighting this monster several times, his hands, arms, and chest were starting to hurt.
Now he finally understood how the Heavenly Seal Nine felt before.
Since the fight started, he had landed at least twenty strikes on Zhou Xingtong, but none of them worked!
What confused him more was that the mixed poison he used hadn't taken effect after all this time.
"Do you really think I can't catch you?!" Zhou Xingtong suddenly stopped, taking a deep breath into his chest.
Streams of Layered Sound Power gathered toward his throat like small rivers.
"KILL!!" A massive roar exploded like thunder.
Wei He's whole body went numb, and his movements slowed down for a moment.
Right then, Zhou Xingtong stepped forward rapidly, slamming a palm toward his chest.
"DIE!" A second roar shook the air again.
Strange enough, Zhou Xingtong's movement was slightly slower for some reason.
Just as his palm was about to hit Wei He, Wei He reacted in time. He leaped to the left, flipping several times in midair to dodge the strike.
Cough, cough...
Zhou Xingtong stood in place. He wanted to chase after him, but he couldn't help lowering his head and coughing.
Without him realizing it, the temperature in his arms had dropped significantly compared to before.
"You poisoned me?!" He stared at Wei He in shock and anger. How could this poison break through his Poison Protection Bead?
