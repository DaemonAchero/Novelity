A streak of light shot past at the speed of sound. Even Wei He could not catch up with it.
Yet, missing it did not mean he had no way to deal with it.
Wei He waved his hand and sent out five gravity pythons. The black pythons coiled around him, forming a small zone of pulling force.
"Get close to me," he growled.
The rest of the cultivators heard him and hurried over.
Zhao Yin and Meng Chunhan were the strongest of the group, but they still could not match the speed of the sky ray. Their bodies were covered in bloody cuts. All they could do was protect their vital spots to stay alive.
As the group gathered together, the sky ray turned around and charged straight at Wei He.
Whoosh!
The moment the streak of light entered the range of the gravity pythons, the heavy force dragged at it.
Wei He clenched his hand. A surge of heavy energy poured out, clamping down on the area with massive force.
The invisible field slowed the sky ray down until it almost stopped in midair.
Before the beast could react, Wei He reached out and grabbed it.
Bang!
Dark energy wrapped around the creature and exploded.
Wei He knew the beast was tough, so he held nothing back. The force shattered the creature's defenses, tearing its wings to shreds and leaving deep, bone-showing wounds across its body.
Wei He grabbed its head and pressed down hard.
With a series of sharp cracks, the sky ray froze.
He used the pulling force of his energy to turn the creature's brain into liquid.
Even through a thick skull, the gravitational force could reach inside its body. Without its protective energy layer, the beast's strong body was useless against the force.
Wei He pulled his hand back. A new idea crossed his mind.
If he could destroy a beast's brain from the inside like this, could he use the same trick on a True Blood warrior?
He thought about it and realized it would be hard. True Blood warriors did not just rely on pure physical power. They carried a unique kind of energy. Without that special energy, normal power could not even touch a true beast.
Setting the thought aside, Wei He pushed his energy out to seal the dying beast.
Under the sealing power, the flat body of the ray twisted and crushed inward. Bone and skin cracked under the immense pressure.
Within seconds, the giant ray was squeezed into a tight ball of flesh.
Right after, a cool stream of air flowed back into Wei He from the seal.
He felt the divine force inside his mind grow stronger, and he felt a rush of satisfaction.
"Dig out the star core and clean it up," he said, tossing the ball of flesh to Meng Chunhan.
Zhao Yin and Meng Chunhan stood in shock.
The beast had just run wild through their ranks, hurting anyone it touched. Yet in Wei He's hands, it was handled like a cheap toy.
Only now did they truly feel the huge gap between Wei He and themselves.
"Leave it to me, Junior Brother," Meng Chunhan said with a firm nod.
Taking charge of such a rare beast corpse showed that Wei He trusted him. The body was extremely hard, requiring special tools and methods to cut open and extract the core.
"Take care of the fallen brother's body. Keep it safe and send it back to his family," Wei He added.
"Understood," Meng Chunhan replied.
He did not know the dead man well, but casualties were part of a beast tide. Once you step into battle, death is always near. Everyone knew the risk before coming here.
Wei He nodded and walked away. He sat down on a nearby rock near the beach, waiting for the next strong beast to show up.
By now, the tide had lost its threat.
With Wei He and Cai Menghuan guarding the island, no beast could step across the line.
Wave after wave of beasts attacked, but the strongest among them was just a giant sea lion beast.
The sea lion had no eyes. It relied on dark tentacles all over its body to sense its surroundings. It had high defense and fast healing, which would give other cultivators a hard time.
Yet under the hands of Cai Menghuan and Wei He, the creature was ripped apart in seconds.
The beast tide lasted for two months. By the time it cleared, Cai Menghuan had earned 132 points, while Wei He got 135 points.
Their scores were close.
Compared to other factions like the Haining Alliance, they ranked seventh and eighth.
After turning in the clear star cores, each of them received a drop of Sea Essence.
However, the end of the beast tide did not mean their work was done.
...
The sun shone bright in the sky.
The ship of the Xuanmiao Sect moved slowly across the blue ocean, heading toward the central area of the Twin Eye Islands.
All the factions that had fought in the tide were gathering there.
Normal beast tides were small, and high-level masters could clear them easily. Yet the elders used these events to test the younger generation, so they did not wipe out the beasts right away.
The rewards were meant to drive young disciples to fight harder and grow stronger.
Although the Sea Essence was a top prize, outsiders could also win it. The Xuanmiao Sect and Haining Alliance had invited independent masters and other groups to join. As long as a fighter was under two hundred years old and below the master rank, they could take part.
Fierce competition made the battle far more intense.
Standing at the front of the ship, Wei He looked out into the distance as the sea breeze hit his face.
A giant, grayish-white port was coming into view.
Half of the port was built into the side of a mountain, while the other half sat in the bay.
"Yinxu Port is the largest island here," Meng Chunhan explained from the side. "This is where everyone gathers after the tide ends."
"Yinxu... what a name..." Wei He thought back to the ancient legends of his past life.
"It is said to be taken from an old book," Meng Chunhan continued. "After the tide, people come here not just to count their scores, but also to exchange ideas. You cannot grow in martial arts by hiding behind closed doors. So every group comes here to trade goods and talk strategy. Since different islands face different beasts, people often find rare items they need here."
Wei He nodded in silence.
Meng Chunhan smiled and added, "Now that you have killed a real master, Junior Brother Wei, many people will come to challenge you to friendly matches. No one will dare fight you to the death, but plenty will want to test their skills against yours."
Wei He remained quiet.
Still, testing his skills against others was not a bad thing. Seeing different styles would help his own growth, as long as the opponents were strong enough.
Besides, with so many people gathered in one place, he might find items he needed in the market.
His biggest problem right now was his weak talent, so he was looking for rare herbs, special recipes, or even broken manuals.
He had made up his mind on the way here.
To him, incomplete manuals were the most useful. Just like the Whale Flood Skill he found long ago, he still used it today, and it had helped him more than any other technique.
His main goal for this trip was to find broken manuals that suited him.
Soon, the ship pulled into the port.
A crowd was already waiting on the dock.
At the front was a group wearing tight blue outfits with white lines.
Though the clothing looked like battle gear, it covered very little skin. The men were fine, but the women wore simple tops and short bottoms that hugged their bodies tight, showing off their curves.
Wei He looked at them and felt a strange sense of familiarity.
It looked just like the short sleeves and shorts from his previous world.
"The people from the Haining Alliance are here," Meng Chunhan warned in a low voice. "Be careful. We do not fear a fair fight, but watch out for their dirty tricks."
Wei He gave a small nod.
The ships docked one by one. Cai Menghuan walked down first, trading polite words with a tall, broad man on the shore while waiting for Wei He to follow.
Once everyone was off the ships, the group set off toward the main hall in the center of the islands.
They walked in a long line, taking their time to look at the scenery instead of using fast movement techniques.
The line stretched for dozens of meters. Guards from the island stood along the sides to keep order.
People lived on these big islands year-round, so they had their own guard forces. However, compared to major powers like the Haining Alliance or the Xuanmiao Sect, these guards were weak and could only handle crowd control.
Wei He kept his eyes open as he walked.
The disciples of the Lock Mountain branch followed right behind him. Some of them used to be talkative, but they had picked up Wei He's quiet habits. Seeing their leader stay silent, the disciples did not dare make a sound.
This made the entire Lock Mountain team walk in cold, heavy silence, casting a dark and sharp pressure over the street.
The fighters from the Haining Alliance instinctively took a few steps back to keep their distance.
"Are you Senior Brother Wei He from the Lock Mountain branch?"
A woman stepped out from the right side and called out to Wei He.
"I am. What is it?" Wei He turned his eyes toward her.
She looked to be in her mid-twenties, with bright golden hair, green earrings, and a striking body. Her fitted top barely held her chest in place, swaying slightly with every step she took.
