Wei He didn't expect this.
You Fu had handed him a hot potato.
The info Wei He got at first was incomplete. You Fu only asked him to protect Su Zhao for a month. He probably just wanted to hide the guy here briefly, but something went wrong along the way. The news leaked, and now the Wushi Sect was at his door.
Wei He's mind raced with thoughts.
'If the Wushi Sect truly acted without rules, they wouldn't come to negotiate first. They must be holding back because of something.' 'Su Zhao was safe with You Fu. That means Wushi Sect either couldn't find him before, or they feared You Fu's own strength.' 'And You Fu, a Minggan realm expert, was eager to send Su Zhao away. He fears the Wushi Sect, or some other trouble. Both sides have reservations... which means both have powerful backers.' 'Wushi Sect has the Three Peaks. But what is behind Alliance Leader You Fu?' Wei He cleared his thoughts and looked at the men in front of him. He understood their angle now.
The Minggan experts of the Wushi Sect wouldn't move easily. Otherwise, the whole Taizhou Region would be ruled by their names, not some Viscera Refining or Bone Forging fighters.
This made things easier to handle.
If his guess was right, the strongest Wushi Sect fighters active in Taizhou were Viscera Refining at best. As long as he played his cards right, he wouldn't draw out any Minggan masters.
He knew what to do.
"How should I address you, leader?"
, The masked man at the front paused briefly before answering.
"Lu Yunbai."
"Brother Lu, do you know who handed Su Zhao to me?" Wei He asked.
"What are you saying? You want to use the Nest-Extinguishing Alliance to pressure us?" Lu Yunbai sneered.
"Not at all," Wei He smiled. "But Leader You is also a Minggan expert. Do you really think he would just leave such an important person here without any backup plan?"
"There must be a trap. I know Leader You; he isn't that foolish. Don't you think he considered the possibility of the Wushi Sect coming for the guy?"
Lu Yunbai fell silent. He understood Wei He's point.
Indeed, if he were You Fu, he would never give Su Zhao to a minor junior without a backup plan. To think You Fu did nothing would be self-deception.
"I respect the Three Peaks, but my hands are tied here," Wei He sighed.
"What is your point, Sect Master Wei?" Lu Yunbai noticed his tone softening.
"Can you make the decisions for this operation?" Wei He asked.
"I am in charge of this raid. Speak freely." Lu Yunbai wasn't lying. The seniors of the inner court and the main peaks only cared about cultivating and big decisions. Detail work was left to outer court managers like him. Bringing Su Zhao back was entirely his call.
"Good," Wei He smiled. "Leader You told me to hold Su Zhao for one month, then hand him over to be taken to Central Province. Since there is a clear transfer date, let's make a deal. Wait until the month is up and I hand him off. Then you can make your move."
"How is that any different from taking him now?" Lu Yunbai frowned. "It just wastes time and adds risk."
"It's very different," Wei He smiled.
"First, if you force it now, I can't answer to Leader You, so I will fight you. Second, after a month, I will send my men to help you intercept him. By then, You Fu will lower his guard, making it much easier for you."
"The news is already out. The longer we wait, the more dangerous it gets," Lu Yunbai shook his head.
"That risk falls on us and Leader You. As more forces watch him, his pressure grows. When the time comes, other factions will tie him down. With my help, your success rate will be much higher," Wei He urged.
"Sounds good. But why should I trust you?" Lu Yunbai asked back.
"Brother Lu, why does everyone want to enter the Inner Court and the Three Peaks?" Wei He asked with a knowing look.
Lu Yunbai smiled.
"Breaking through to the Minggan realm on the Three Peaks is much safer than down here. Many old fighters stuck at the Viscera Refining realm want to go up. A single success adds ten years of life."
He looked at Wei He with a half-smile.
"You are young, so you might only know that much. Let me tell you about the other benefits."
"Please do," Wei He said seriously. He actually knew nothing, but he was happy to act the part.
Lu Yunbai waved his hand, telling his masked men to spread out and guard the area.
Once secure, he continued.
"You only know it's easier to reach Minggan up there, but there is more."
"I'm listening."
Lu Yunbai smiled proudly.
"Our sect stands above Taizhou not just because of Minggan breakthroughs, but because of the Wanhua Jade Field."
"Wanhua Jade Field?" Wei He frowned. He had never heard of it.
"Yes. It's a top-tier flesh field on the Three Peaks. It can simulate the flesh of almost any strange beast. No matter what art you practice, as long as you get up the mountain, you won't lack beast flesh for your training. That's why so many experts want to go up. It means longer life and higher cultivation. With guidance from our top masters, who could resist?"
Lu Yunbai grew fanatical as he spoke.
Wei He took a deep breath.
This flesh field was incredible. He didn't expect the Wushi Sect to hide such a treasure.
"Can it simulate any beast flesh?"
"Not everything. It needs a sample to start," Lu Yunbai explained. Extremely rare beast flesh couldn't be simulated, but he kept that to himself to make the sect sound better.
Even so, it was impressive.
Wei He was tempted. Easier breakthroughs, endless beast flesh, and guidance from top masters. The Three Peaks really were a sacred land for martial arts in Taizhou.
If he didn't have his Break Through Bead to skip bottlenecks and beast flesh requirements, he couldn't resist either.
"If I help, will I really get a spot on the Three Peaks?" Wei He asked.
Lu Yunbai smiled. No warrior could resist this reward.
"This spot was set by the higher-ups specifically for Su Zhao's capture. If you help us take him, you will get in."
Wei He fell silent for a moment.
"Fine. Since you can decide, we have a deal. Give me one month, then I will help you take Su Zhao."
"Why not now? Do you think we fear You Fu?" Lu Yunbai frowned. If Wei He agreed, why wait?
"Brother Lu, if your seniors can handle Leader You, I have no problem. But my Wandu Sect is large, and I can't risk my people's lives on just your word."
Wei He's point was simple: he wouldn't bet everything on empty words. If the Wushi Sect could suppress You Fu, everything was fine. Otherwise, if You Fu came for revenge later, what would the Wandu Sect do?
"Indecisive!" Lu Yunbai snorted. He was annoyed, but he didn't dare make promises for the seniors. You Fu wasn't just the leader of the alliance; he had the Demon Faction behind him. Both sides had strong backers, so no one could act recklessly.
"Fine. One month. You must help me take him then. If anything goes wrong, don't blame me for being ruthless," Lu Yunbai said coldly. "For the Wushi Sect to wipe out your Wandu Sect, it would only take a few elders coming down for tea."
"I understand," Wei He nodded solemnly.
He meant what he said. He really intended to hand Su Zhao over in a month. Whether the Wushi Sect or someone else took him by then was none of his business. He couldn't afford to mess with the Wushi Sect yet. Doing this much for You Fu's promise was already his limit.
As for the spot on the mountain, he took it as talk. Believing it fully would be foolish.
Lu Yunbai's group left quickly.
Wei He stood in place for a long time.
Now that all his real arts were at the Bone Forging stage, his combined power was more than twice as strong as before. Against a Minggan expert like Zhu Chen, he didn't need to run anymore. He might even have a chance to kill one in a head-on fight.
Still, without a real battle, he couldn't be sure of his true limit.
With the Wushi Sect at his door, he had to move his plans forward a bit sooner.
