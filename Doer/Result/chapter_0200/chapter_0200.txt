"I’ll give you the real Taisui Sutra and the complete Silk Force! Don't kill me!" Yue Xiaodie shrieked. She was young, and she did not want to die yet. She still had so much to experience and enjoy.
"I lied to you earlier. Once you are poisoned by my venom, you won't remember what you said or did. You’ll just be completely confused," Wei He said, looking at her carefully.
"So you really were lying to me from the start..."
Yue Xiaodie froze, then flew into a rage. "You lied to me!?"
"It seems you had strict poison resistance training. Very few of my drugs work on you," Wei He said calmly. "Alright, spill it. How do I cultivate the Silk Force?"
"If I tell you, will you let me go!?" Yue Xiaodie dropped her easy attitude and asked with absolute seriousness.
"Of course. I keep my word," Wei He nodded.
"I can tell you ninety percent first. I’ll keep the last bit until someone comes to pick me up, then I’ll tell you the rest!" Yue Xiaodie tried to negotiate.
She could tell this man was not easily fooled. He was a truly cunning old fox.
Usually, in a life-or-death fight with poisons, most people acted straight. Anyone who played mind games was already impressive. After all, overthinking in a fight could easily get a person killed. The more complex a scheme was, the easier it was to fail. So most people killed quickly or ran away without dragging things out.
People like Wei He, who constantly calculated and twisted everything, were rare.
"How about this: the Silk Force needs a medicinal primer to work. You can keep the primer recipe for now and tell me the rest in full," Wei He said coldly.
The primer was the key to any martial art. It was short, but vital.
Yue Xiaodie thought about it and agreed. The primer was what made the practice actually work. Without it, one could not master the true state of Silk Force. The primer was mostly a hallucinogenic drug anyway.
"Fine," she agreed quickly.
"And you must say it all in one breath, as fast as you can. No pauses," Wei He’s eyes darkened. "If you pause once, I break one of your limbs."
Yue Xiaodie tensed up and swallowed hard. "What if I make five mistakes?"
"I can always break your neck," Wei He replied.
Yue Xiaodie did not dare to play tricks this time. She knew that a cruel, experienced person like Wei He would ruthlessly attack if he had even a single doubt.
Right then, one spoke and the other took notes. Soon, she recited the full cultivation method for Silk Force.
"What about the Taisui Sutra?"
"I only know up to the Bone Forging stage. The rest is secret, locked away in the Wanfei Palace. Do you still want it?" Yue Xiaodie answered honestly.
"Speak."
Then came the second recording. This time was much simpler, as Yue Xiaodie did not dare to pull any stunts.
Once he finished writing, Wei He nodded. This second recording matched the first without a single pause, though there were five contradictions. That meant she had lied in five places earlier, starting right at the beginning.
"Looks like my drugs really didn't affect you at all..." Wei He noted.
Yue Xiaodie had nothing to say.
She watched Wei He carefully check the cloth record, roll it up, and tuck it away.
"Can you let me go now? We have no grudges or conflicts of interest. Behind me is the Wanfei Palace, the strongest force in Jinzhou. You don't want to make an enemy of such a large power over me, right?" she tried to convince him.
"True," Wei He nodded, agreeing with her logic.
"However, I forced you to reveal so many secret martial arts. If you go back and tell your elders to send killers after me, wouldn't I lose out?" he said seriously.
"I swear, today's matter will never..." Yue Xiaodie raised her hand to the heavens and swore a lethal oath.
"Besides, how do I know if what you gave me this time is real or fake? What if it's still fake?" Wei He asked.
"What do you want then?" Yue Xiaodie was helpless.
"How about this," Wei He said as an idea struck him. He clapped his hands. "Come back with me first. Once I successfully cultivate the Silk Force, I will let you go. That way, I'll know you didn't lie to me. Deal?"
"No!" Yue Xiaodie panicked. "We are strangers with no enmity! You can't just do this to me for no reason!"
"It's settled," Wei He said. "You still have your martial skills, and I can't watch you every second to stop you from running. So..."
Swoosh!
He struck out suddenly, hitting both of Yue Xiaodie's legs in two quick blows.
His speed was insane. With his divine strength, high speed, and Flying Dragon Skill stacked together, his sudden burst surpassed ordinary Viscera Refining masters. Yue Xiaodie, who was only at the Bone Forging stage, could not match him at all.
She barely had time to step back and raise her force before his palms hit her.
Snap! Snap!
Yue Xiaodie let out a painful groan as both her legs snapped like tree branches. She collapsed to the ground, using her hands to push herself up. But Wei He struck two more lightning-fast palms.
Snap! Snap!
Her two arms broke as well.
"There. Now you can't run away or play any tricks."
Wei He pulled his hands back, satisfied. This girl was ruthless and loved to deceive people, so taking precautions made him feel much safer.
He pulled a tiny, milky-white insect egg from a poison pouch at his waist. Pinching Yue Xiaodie's jaw open, he flicked the egg inside.
Cough, cough!
The girl gagged and swallowed the egg, which was the size of a soy bean.
"What was that!?" she screamed, lying broken on the ground.
"A very cute little bee," Wei He said. "As long as you don't run, it won't build a nest or collect honey in your stomach. Don't worry."
Yue Xiaodie felt a mix of rage and terror. She began to curse and scream hysterically.
Wei He quickly used her own socks to make a gag, tying her mouth shut. But soon, she bit through the cloth using her inner force.
Annoyed by the noise, Wei He struck her neck a few times, knocking her out cold. He scooped her up with one hand and headed back.
As for offending the big forces of Jinzhou, Wei He did not really care. His Wanqing Sect was hidden deep inside the toxic mist of Black House Mountain. Ordinary factions would not easily find it. Besides, whether Yue Xiaodie's words were true or false was still up for debate. If he let a few words scare him off, he wouldn't deserve to be the Master of the Wanqing Sect.
Carrying Yue Xiaodie as extra loot, Wei He returned to where the hunter was waiting.
When the hunter saw him come back carrying a beautiful girl with broken limbs who had passed out, he swallowed hard and kept his mouth shut.
Wei He offered a brief explanation. "I ran into people fighting in that cave. This girl was caught in the crossfire and severely injured. I couldn't bear to see her die, so I'm bringing her back to save her life."
"I see. You are truly kind, Master Wei," the hunter relaxed. Then he asked, "Wait, did someone discover that cave? It was so well hidden. It shouldn't have been found easily. Plus, fierce beasts show up there from time to time."
"Hidden? Fierce beasts?" Wei He paused. "Is the place you mentioned... a cave entrance blocked by a huge white stone covered in moss and vines, with white pine trees outside?"
"White pines? There are no white pines there, just lots of purple flowers. There is a stone blocking the entrance, but it consists of several dark green rocks, not white ones," the hunter shook his head.
Wei He realized he had entered the wrong cave.
"Can you take me there now?" he asked in a deep voice.
"Alright. A fierce beast lives there, so I didn't want to go at this time. But if we hurry, we can leave before it returns," the hunter agreed.
The two men, along with the unconscious Yue Xiaodie, moved forward quickly. They soon passed the cave where the fight had just taken place.
As they walked, Wei He noticed Yue Xiaodie waking up. He struck her a few more times to keep her unconscious.
The hunter did not notice. Guided by the small squirrel on his shoulder, which squeaked and pointed the way, he led them forward. Soon, they stopped at the bottom of a slope, in front of a plain cliff wall.
"We are here," the hunter said, pressing his hand against the wall.
The wall sank inward. A thick layer of vines and leaves formed a natural curtain, hiding the cave entrance at the bottom of the slope.
Wei He was amazed. He struck Yue Xiaodie a few more times to make sure she stayed asleep, then carried her into the cave.
The inside had the exact same structure as the previous cave, with an inscription carved at the far end. However, the text here was completely different. It described an entirely different technique.
It was a Secret Skill.
Wei He raised his torch, burned away the cobwebs, and studied the text carefully.
There was a preface at the top: 'Fatally injured, I know my death is near. I leave this scroll, using true and false traps to confuse intruders. If a destined person reaches this place, you may inherit my legacy and learn this technique.' The text was split into distinct sections.
'If you master this skill, I hope you will fulfill my last wish: kill a man named Shangguan Canglu.' Below that was the full Secret Skill.
Seeing the name Shangguan Canglu, Wei He sighed. If he remembered correctly, that was the grandfather of Shangguan Ji from the Tianyin Sect. The man had been dead for over eighty years, his bones long turned to dust.
"Is this the right one?" the hunter asked. He did not understand these high-level techniques, which required at least a Martial Master level to learn. Since it was a secret technique using inner force, a low-level martial artist like the hunter could not make sense of it.
"Yes, this is what I came for," Wei He nodded. He quickly read through the full text.
The technique was called the Silk Crimson Force. It had nothing to do with the Silk Force despite sharing part of the name. Though complex to cultivate, it allowed a martial artist's force to become as thin and tough as silk, multiplying its piercing power several times for a short duration. It also required specific drugs and the flesh of rare beasts as catalysts.
'If I gather the ingredients, this will pair perfectly with my Rain Force as the core Secret Skill of the Wanqing Sect,' Wei He thought, satisfied.
The Wanqing Sect lacked Secret Skills. In fact, the current Tianyin Sect lacked them entirely, likely because Shangguan Ji had taken all the best ones with him.
This trip was definitely worth it. He got Yue Xiaodie, who knew the Silk Force, and he gained this Silk Crimson Force Secret Skill.
Wei He decided to rename it the Wanqing Crimson Force, making it a standard technique for his sect.
He quickly copied the technique, destroyed the stone carving, and returned to the Wanqing Sect. He locked Yue Xiaodie in a room, assigning staff to handle her meals and needs. Then he left her alone and focused entirely on studying the Silk Force.
Unsurprisingly, Yue Xiaodie had not lied about the cultivation method this time. It was real.
With the help of the Breakthrough Bead, Wei He spent just half a month reaching the final barrier, which only needed the medicinal primer to break through. It was a high-level trick that relied on true guidance. Without a teacher, one would never figure it out, but once taught, enlightenment came instantly.
With the direction clear, Wei He used his high comprehension and the Breakthrough Bead to reach the final step.
All he needed now was to force his way through the barrier using the bead.
Once the Silk Force was mastered, he could focus on reshaping his bone structure to cultivate the eighteen cloud patterns required for the sixth layer of the Rain Gathering Technique.
With that, he would definitely break through to the Viscera Refining stage within two to three years!
