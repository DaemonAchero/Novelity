Li Jue was tall. He stood a good head above Wei He, making him one of the tallest men in the yard.
Yet he barely had any visible muscle. From a distance, he looked lean and harmless.
He was a simple, honest man who worked himself to the bone every single day. He was always among the first to arrive and the last to leave. But for some reason, his progress just stayed stuck.
The crowd gathered around Xiao Ran, who stood in the center. Xiao Ran slowly gathered his blood energy. His fists turned a faint shade of black and swelled noticeably larger than anyone else's. The skin on his knuckles carried a dark, dull grey shine.
This was the Stone Skin stage. At this level, a warrior's blood energy was several times tougher than ox hide.
Wei He had often measured the gap between himself and Cheng Shaojiu, who was also at the Stone Skin level. The difference in strength, speed, burst power, and stamina was massive.
"Isn't Xiao Ran still under nineteen?" someone whispered from the crowd.
The mood in the yard instantly grew heavier.
"Good! Very good!" Old Man Zheng stroked his beard and laughed, clearly thrilled. "You truly are the best talent in this yard!"
He stood up and led Xiao Ran into the inner room to speak with him in private.
Senior Brother Zhao Hong stepped in to clear the area, shooing the rest of the students back to their spots to keep training.
Wei He walked back to his position. He pulled his collar open and checked his chest.
The pattern of the Break-Through Bead was only half full. This was even after he ate the silver-kissed black snake meat from the Cheng family every single day.
He felt the energy was accumulating much slower than he had expected.
'The snake meat still has the same nutrients. It must be my body that changed. Now that I'm stronger and my blood energy is richer, the energy needed for the next bottleneck is naturally higher. But at this rate, who knows how long it will take?' Wei He sighed. Still, with the Break-Through Bead in his possession, he was already far ahead of everyone else. He was content.
Xiao Ran's breakthrough did not just stir up the martial arts yard. It sent waves through the outside world as well.
Over the next few days, wealthy merchants, rich households, house managers, and gang leaders flooded the yard for Xiao Ran.
Xiao Ran turned them all down.
Seeing that they could not recruit him directly, these people changed their tactics. They invited him out every day for heavy drinking, grand feasts, and music.
Time and again, luxurious carriages waited outside the yard just to pick Xiao Ran up after practice.
Many of the other disciples watched with deep envy.
For a time, Xiao Ran was riding high on success. He trained with a constant smile on his face.
The martial arts yard sat in the small district of Shiqiao, where news traveled fast. Xiao Ran's name spread across the neighboring streets in no time.
Already a busy man, Xiao Ran was now buried in social invitations and was rarely seen around the yard anymore.
Wei He stayed calm. He kept to his own pace, practicing his moves and refining his skin day after day.
After becoming a full disciple, his monthly tuition dropped significantly. Occasionally, he visited the Cheng family compound to spar with Cheng Shaojiu, slowly building his physical strength and real combat experience.
The progress was slow, but it was steady and visible.
More importantly, the Break-Through Bead was slowly filling up. Seeing this clear path ahead gave Wei He a deep sense of inner peace.
However, peaceful days rarely lasted.
Not long after Wei He retrieved his custom spiked brass knuckles from the Li family blacksmith, news came from the Cheng family.
A job had come up, and they needed him on board.
Wei He felt it was time. He had been eating the Cheng family's food and taking their money for a while without doing any heavy lifting. It was time to pay his dues.
At the break of dawn.
Inside the main compound of the Cheng family.
A large banner bearing the name "Yonghe Escort Agency" snapped and curled in the morning wind.
Under the flagpole, about twenty guards gathered. They all wore matching grey-and-white long robes with grey waist sashes and short blades strapped to their sides.
Cheng Shaojiu stood at the front alongside a middle-aged man with a thin mustache.
This man was a bit shorter than Cheng Shaojiu, but his fingers were unusually thick and heavy. It was not a deformity, but the clear mark of a specialized martial art.
He wore the same guard uniform, but his waist sash was jet black.
His face was cold and strict as he cleared his throat.
"I am Cheng Kai, one of the three Chief Escorts at Yonghe. Friends on the road call me Cheng Iron Hands."
He flexed his hands and tapped his palms together. A sharp sound like striking wood against stone echoed through the yard.
"Some of you here are veterans, and some are newcomers. We stick to the old rule: one veteran paired with one newcomer. First, let me lay down the rules of our agency."
He coughed, spat a thick wad of phlegm onto the ground to his left, and ground it into the dirt with his boot.
"Rule one: On the road, rookies obey the veterans completely.
Rule two: Rookies must follow the Three Nots. Do not rush, do not grab, and do not blabber.
Rule three: This trip is long. It will take at least half a month to go and return. The route is set. Everyone must settle their personal affairs before we march out. No letters along the way, no leaking information, and no chatting with strangers without permission!"
Chief Escort Cheng Kai was clearly a seasoned veteran of the road, rattling off strict regulations without hesitation.
Wei He stood silent among the guards, listening closely.
His expression remained calm. He fully understood and respected these strict rules.
No wonder the pay was so good. A single run took half a month, and the farther they went, the deadlier it got. Without high pay, no one would risk their life for this.
Once Cheng Kai finished speaking, he began pairing the veterans with the rookies.
Led by Cheng Shaojiu, Cheng Kai walked directly toward Wei He.
"Little Brother Wei He, you'll be coming with me personally," Cheng Kai said with a warm smile, his tone remarkably friendly. "Shao Jiu has talked about you plenty, but I've been out on runs and never got the chance to meet you. I'm your third senior brother's uncle, so feel free to call me Uncle Cheng."
Wei He quickly cupped his hands in respect. "Uncle Cheng. Please look after me on this trip."
"Don't worry about it. Escort work sounds complicated, but once you get a few runs under your belt, it becomes second nature. Just watch, listen, and learn. With your sharp mind, you'll get the hang of it in no time," Cheng Kai said kindly.
"Thank you, Uncle Cheng," Wei He replied.
Cheng Shaojiu gave Wei He a subtle look from the side.
Wei He caught the signal instantly.
As soon as Cheng Kai stepped away to handle other matters, the two slipped into a shadowed corner.
Under the cover of the shadows, Cheng Shaojiu pulled a small porcelain bottle from his shirt.
The yellow ceramic bottle had no label. A light shake revealed the sound of liquid inside.
"Here is the snake venom water you asked for. I collected a good amount for you. If my family wasn't in the snake meat business, you couldn't get this much anywhere else."
"Thank you, Senior Brother!" Wei He took the bottle gratefully. With this, his custom spiked brass knuckles could finally be put to full use.
"We are brothers, don't mention it. But this stuff isn't cheap. If you tried to buy this outside, it would cost you a fortune. Use it wisely," Cheng Shaojiu whispered, flashing five fingers.
Wei He understood.
Because Cheng Shaojiu was the young master of the family, he could get this at a dirt-cheap base cost. When Wei He offered to pay for it earlier, Cheng Shaojiu had simply waved his hand and given it to him for free.
Cheng Shaojiu spent money like water and did not care about a small amount like this.
Wei He kept this favor close to his heart.
To make poison effective in battle, it had to be fast and lethal.
The silver-kissed black snake was an extraordinarily venomous beast. The refined venom liquid was usually kept strictly for internal use by the Cheng family and was rarely sold outside.
Getting a small bottle for free was a massive favor. Outside, even five hundred taels of silver might not buy a bottle like this, assuming you could even find a seller.
With the poison safely stowed away, Wei He joined Cheng Kai's unit. After a brief final check, the convoy of escort wagons slowly rolled out of the Yonghe Escort Agency and headed toward the city gates.
Once outside the city gates, the guards raised their large agency banner high.
Two riders took the lead, and two guarded the rear. In the center were horse-drawn wagons filled with cargo, surrounded by armed guards on foot.
Wei He walked near the front beside Cheng Kai. Cheng Kai rode a horse, while Wei He walked alongside him.
Riding a horse was not meant for comfort, but for height, allowing a better view of the surrounding terrain. Only guards with the keenest eyesight were assigned to ride up front.
The convoy moved at a steady, fast pace without stopping. Within half a day, they had covered ten miles.
Along the road, Wei He caught sight of Mingde Temple in the distance.
The temple was partially hidden in the mountain forests, its outlines faint against the deep mountain fog.
Incense burners smoked at the foot of the mountain, and crowds of pilgrims continued to visit, seemingly untouched by the killing he had committed there earlier.
"They say ten miles out is a completely different world. It turns out that's true," Cheng Kai said, leaning over from his horse to talk to Wei He.
"Have you ever been beyond the ten-mile mark, Little Brother Wei?" he asked with a faint smile.
"Never," Wei He shook his head. "Uncle Cheng, why is it so dangerous beyond ten miles?"
"Bandits and outlaws," Cheng Kai sighed. "Ten miles out marks the boundary agreed upon by the Hong Family Fort and One-Ear. Within ten miles, the Hong Family Fort keeps things safe. But beyond that line lies One-Ear's domain. If you want to pass through, you pay the toll."
"Hong Family Fort? One-Ear?" This was the first time Wei He had heard these names.
"Sounds strange, doesn't it? You'd think the city garrison would handle the borders, but instead, it's the Hong family," Cheng Kai explained.
"The Hong family was originally a local noble clan. Generations of their family served as high officials, and they held great prestige. But later, a new military commander took over Feiye City, and for some reason, he clashed with the Hong family.
The conflict grew fierce. Eventually, both sides compromised. The Hong family withdrew from the inner city and built a massive earthen fortress outside, naming it the Hong Family Fort.
However, because the Hong family had helped so many people in the past, their connections ran deep. Even the city garrison didn't dare push them too far, choosing to look the other way."
"And who is One-Ear?" Wei He pushed further.
"One-Ear is a notorious bandit lord in these parts. He has massive numbers, strong men, and swift horses. Within a hundred miles, he is the top dog," Cheng Kai said, raising a thumb.
"When the Hong family built their fort, they fought One-Ear a few times. One-Ear suffered heavy losses and retreated. So, both sides drew a hard line at the ten-mile mark."
He paused, as if remembering something crucial.
"Ah, one more thing. Beyond the ten-mile line, it's not just One-Ear you need to watch out for. There are also the Two Sects from the legendary Three Gangs and Two Sects. If you ever run into them in the future, do not provoke them. Yield to them immediately."
"Understood," Wei He nodded.
Cheng Kai did not elaborate on the names of the Three Gangs and Two Sects, and Wei He did not press the issue. Now that he knew the basics, he could gather more details down the road.
