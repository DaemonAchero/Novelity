The True World On the other side, Shangguan Xing stood next to Ouyang Luo. She was going over their plan, but her voice died down as she looked toward Wei He.
She stared in disbelief. She thought her eyes were playing tricks on her, but after checking again and again, she had to accept the cold truth.
"How... how is this happening?!"
"How else?" Ouyang Luo sneered, his eyes locked onto Wei He as a strong desire for battle flared inside him. "The guy in your yard is named Wei He. No wonder he made it to the top brass's radar, just like me."
He had heard about Wei He's record. Before joining the sect, Wei He had fought off two First-Grasp experts from the Wushi Sect on his own. That was on par with Ouyang Luo's own achievements. Now, Wei He was likely even stronger. Ouyang Luo really wanted to see who would win if they fought.
"Still, Level Seven... heh." Ouyang Luo shoved the stunned Shangguan Xing aside and leaped toward Wei He.
SWOOSH!
A powerful protective force erupted from his body, sweeping through the air. He had reached Level Seven as well!
Their eyes met, both reading the sudden seriousness in each other's gaze.
"I used to wonder how you got picked as an Inner Mountain Seed alongside me," Ouyang Luo grinned. "Looks like you actually have some skill..."
Wei He glanced at him, didn't bother to reply, and turned his eyes to Shao Qingzi.
"Good! Excellent!" Shao Qingzi had not expected these two fresh top talents to hit Level Seven so quickly. This kind of talent was equal to Wan Wenchun, the chief disciple from the main branch.
The crowd went wild. Main branch or outer branch, everyone broke into loud chatter. Hitting Level Seven in less than a year meant these two could challenge Wan Wenchun directly, even if they were just re-cultivating from the organ-refining stage.
Usually, the sect would only get one or two inner seeds over several years. This time, the outer branch gave birth to two seeds at once. With Wan Wenchun included, three people were entering the Inner Mountain this year. It was a massive breakout.
"After this is over, you two will follow me into the Inner Mountain and go to the Mo Ming Temple," Shao Qingzi said, growing happier the more he looked at them.
In recent years, the main branch had fewer talents while the outer branch kept growing. This sudden explosion of talent was great news. He pushed down his excitement, sent voice messages to a few deacons to arrange things, and then told Wei He and Ouyang Luo to go back to their spots.
The rest of the test continued. With their Level Seven cultivation exposed, those two were already locked in for the Inner Mountain and didn't need to fight anymore. But the others still had to finish.
When Wei He returned to his spot, Liang Huayao was staring at him with burning eyes, looking like he wanted to speak but held back. Wei He instantly knew Liang Huayao was aiming for his attendant slot.
"Brother Wei..." Liang Huayao said anxiously. "Name your price! I will do whatever it takes!"
"What can you offer in return, Brother Liang?" Wei He asked back.
He wouldn't hand over a spot just because they lived in the same yard and knew each other. Liang Huayao was too slick, calculating, and practical, unlike Zhou Muqing. Zhou Muqing spent all her time on martial arts and was simple at heart, which made her easy to control. Liang Huayao was a different story.
Wei He's question cooled Liang Huayao down. He carefully thought through what he had and what Wei He needed, only to realize he had nothing of real value to offer.
Zhou Muqing walked over openly and stood beside Wei He, claiming her spot. If she didn't mark her territory now, those sly organ-refining veterans would pull all kinds of tricks.
Wei He gave her a look and said nothing. Having one reliable person was enough. He could use the remaining attendant slot to trade for actual benefits.
With the two outer seeds settled, the rest of the matches held little interest for Shao Qingzi and the leaders. An hour passed quickly, and the test ended.
The crowd began to disperse. However, a large group of people gathered around Wei He and Ouyang Luo, mostly from the outer branch. Many didn't say a word. They just stepped up, bowed, and handed over freshly written notes containing their highest offers.
Ouyang Luo collected his notes and glanced at Wei He. Something felt familiar. He then looked at Shangguan Xing beside him and saw her pale face and nervous eyes.
"What? Are you scared? Or do you regret it?"
"Well, it makes sense. You shared a yard with him. If you were smarter and built a good relationship, you could have gotten an attendant slot as easily as that girl."
Ouyang Luo loved fighting, but he wasn't dumb. A fool couldn't reach this level of martial arts or survive this long. He saw right through her.
Shangguan Xing stayed silent, biting her lip hard while forcing a weak smile onto her face. "I don't regret it. I'm just surprised. Besides, I calculated everything beforehand. You are my best choice, Brother Ouyang."
"Fair enough," Ouyang Luo laughed.
Shangguan Xing smiled back, but looking at Zhou Muqing beside Wei He, she was lying to herself if she said she didn't regret it. Only she knew what price she had paid just to get a spot from Ouyang Luo.
Shao Qingzi watched the scene with a smile and said nothing. The sect had no rules against these trades, and a simple attendant spot was no big deal anyway. Once the two gathered all the notes, he clapped his hands to disperse the crowd.
"Let's go. I'll take you to the Inner Mountain. You can pick your attendants later. Right now, we need to confirm your identities so you can come and go as you please."
"Thank you, Senior Brother," Wei He and Ouyang Luo bowed together.
"Next, you should think about which lineage you want to join," Shao Qingzi said with a smile.
The three left the martial ground and walked along the path until they reached a long set of stone stairs. Wei He looked up at the mist-covered mountain peak. This was a different gate from the one he visited before, but both led to the Inner Mountain.
"Let's go."
Shao Qingzi tapped his foot and floated upward. Ouyang Luo and Wei He used their movement techniques and followed closely, racing up the steps.
The gray-white stairs blurred beneath their feet. Soon, they landed on a jade platform surrounded by white mist.
The moment he landed, Wei He felt a sudden shudder through his entire body. He snapped his head toward a cliff wall on the right.
The wall was a bit taller than a person and had an irregular shape. Its surface reflected a soft white glow, with the vague, delicate curves of a woman flowing inside it. It seemed to emit a strange energy nonstop.
"This is the Xuan Cha Jade Wall. To enter the Inner Mountain sanctuary, you must pass through here once. Remember this: if you don't pass this wall next time, you won't truly enter the sanctuary." Shao Qingzi warned them.
"Understood," both replied quickly.
They walked past the wall, took ten more steps, and the mist slowly faded away. The sky grew dark without them noticing.
"Keep up," Shao Qingzi said, picking up his pace.
Wei He and Ouyang Luo exchanged looks and ran after him.
After a few seconds, the sky grew even darker. A old, dark-gray taoist temple slowly appeared ahead. Lanterns flickered inside, casting shadows of people sitting by the windows.
Shao Qingzi stopped at the main gate and cupped his hands. "Senior sisters, I have brought the new outer branch seeds to the Inner Mountain. Please verify them."
"Thank you for your hard work. Let them in," a gentle female voice came from inside.
Wei He and Ouyang Luo stepped forward and walked through the gate as Shao Qingzi gestured.
Before stepping inside, Wei He looked around.
Everything was pitch black. There were no stars, no moon, and nothing in the sky. Outside the temple, there was nothing but the cold dirt beneath his feet. Total darkness surrounded them in every direction, and only the faint light from the temple barely lit the ground outside.
Wei He turned his head to look behind him. The path they had just walked on was now swallowed by the dark.
"Come inside, Wei He," the gentle female voice called out again. "You don't have super-sensory eyes, so there are many things you cannot see."
Wei He felt a sudden chill, and endless theories raced through his mind. But right now, the only way was forward!
After struggling for so many years, he was finally... finally close enough to touch the truth.
Looking at the mysterious temple ahead, Wei He took a step inside. His crisp footsteps were the only sound in the quiet building.
Wei He and Ouyang Luo entered the main hall one after the other.
Inside, three figures wrapped in black taoist robes sat in a triangle formation, facing the two newcomers. At the top of the triangle sat the blindfolded senior sister Wei He had met before—Yuan Duzi.
"Faster than I expected," Yuan Duzi smiled at Wei He.
Wei He instantly realized her identity and bowed deeply. "Outer branch Wei He greets the three ancestors!"
Beside him, Ouyang Luo bowed respectfully as well. "Outer branch Ouyang Luo greets the three ancestors."
"Wei He, Ouyang Luo, it is time for your choice. Pick one of the three lineages for your future path," Bai Hezi said with a light laugh from the side. "Mine is the Ghost Head lineage, focusing on endless transformations."
"Mine is the Flowing Stream lineage, focusing on ice-cold torrents," Qing Meizi said plainly.
"Mine is the Mountain Lock, focusing on heavy locks and endless peaks," Yuan Duzi added.
Wei He raised his head and looked at the three people in the hall.
The lanterns flickered around them. For a brief moment, he noticed the shadows on the floor. The shadows cast by the three taoists looked like massive, terrifying monsters, squirming on the ground with the flickering light like living creatures.
Wei He's heart skipped a beat. He lowered his head to think and carefully examined their shadows. Yuan Duzi's shadow was clearly much larger than the other two.
Remembering how Yuan Duzi had given him the healing salve earlier, he made his decision.
"I choose the Ghost Head lineage!" Ouyang Luo spoke first.
"I choose... the Mountain Lock lineage!" Wei He declared.
HISS...
In an instant, everyone in the hall except Yuan Duzi turned into black smoke and dissolved into the air.
Wei He was startled. He looked up, only to see Yuan Duzi smiling at him.
"Don't be nervous. I simply blocked your senses from the others. You are still in the same spot."
"Now, it is time for you to see the real world..." Yuan Duzi stood up.
"You weren't supposed to be taught by me directly, but you and Ouyang Luo are the best talents the sect has seen in years. So, I am making an exception."
She walked down the steps until she stood right in front of Wei He.
"Wei He... have you ever seen the true world?"
She reached out her hand—a flawless, jade-like hand—and held it in front of him.
"Take my hand."
Wei He looked at her hand. An intense dread spread from his chest to his whole body. His internal force surged uncontrollably.
Then, he reached out.
Slowly, step by step, his hand moved toward hers. Compared to hers, his skin looked like rough stone, while hers was like fine jade.
CLASP.
Yuan Duzi grabbed his hand tight.
BOOM!
A massive explosion went off in Wei He's mind. His head spun, his body went weak, and the sound of thunder roared in his ears.
Everything in front of him blurred rapidly, turned chaotic, and then snapped back into sharp focus.
CRACK!
A flash of lightning exploded outside the temple, lighting up everything for a brief second.
