Night wind howled. Cold air dried the sweat on Bai Dou’s skin.
"Sir, you might have the wrong idea," Bai Dou said, keeping his voice low. "I’m just an ordinary man living in the hills."
"Ordinary?" Wei He took a deep breath, catching the faint scent of poison in the air. "Does an ordinary man use odorless poison? Does he—"
Clang!
A flash of cold steel stopped inches from Wei He's face, held motionless by an invisible force.
"Thousand Shreds!" Bai Dou leaped backward. A bright white light flashed from his hands.
Looking closely, the monk Three Poisons realized it wasn’t light. It was a dense cloud of silver daggers.
Shi-shi-shi-shi-shi!
A storm of dark weapons slammed into Wei He. It was useless. The strikes were too weak, stuck firm in his protective forcefield.
"If this is all you have..." Wei He looked disappointed.
"Heart Piercer!"
A deep yell echoed out.
A streak of dark red shot like a meteor through the cloud of weapons, cutting straight for Wei He's throat.
This strike was on another level. It burst with several times the speed and force of the rest.
Even Wei He was caught off guard. He only reacted after the point shredded through his barrier.
Lightning quick, he snapped two fingers together.
Crack!
"Double Piercer!"
Shi!
Wei He’s eyes narrowed. Before he could raise his other hand, a second red blade burst out from the first knife he was holding.
At point-blank range, he couldn't dodge.
Pfft!
The faint red light died down. Everything went quiet, save for the howling wind on the cliff.
Wei He looked down at the red, diamond-shaped blade buried slightly into his skin.
"Interesting." He looked up, eyes showing genuine interest for the first time. "Bone Forging strength. To think a master like you was hiding in these mountains."
Three Poisons was just a peak martial artist. But this thrower, supposedly a weakling, was at the Bone Forging stage.
Wei He plucked the knife out and licked the poison off the blade.
"Good blend... shame it's still too weak."
After completing his White Jade and Divine Law arts, common low-grade poisons meant nothing to him. Only unseen, ultra-dense toxins could affect him, and even then, only in huge doses.
Tasting poison was how he trained his Divine Law technique. The more he tasted, the stronger his art grew, and the deadlier his own aura became.
This art, made with Jiu Ying, was a masterpiece. It required no cultivation of raw force, serving only as a secondary path. It suited Wei He’s massive blood reserves and fast breakthroughs perfectly. With his Breakthrough Bead, he could level up far faster than normal people, giving him time to train this poison path.
In truth, only Wei He could train it. Even Jiu Ying couldn't, simply because normal martial artists lacked the time to cultivate a second path.
"What do you want?" Bai Dou’s muscles tensed. He knew he was outmatched.
The cliff looked like a dead end, but he had hidden thick ropes along the rocks. He could drop down anytime.
Even so, his heart pounded. His full-power Double Piercer had only managed to scratch the man’s skin. He had used that exact move to kill a renowned Organ Refining master before. Yet against this man, it failed. Even the poison did nothing.
Is he even stronger than an Organ Refining master?
"What is your goal?" Bai Dou asked.
Wei He tossed the knife aside, a small smile on his face. "No one hides in dangerous mountains for no reason. Join my sect, and I can—"
"No need. Leave," Bai Dou cut him off. He was confident his movement speed could get him away before the man flipped out.
Wei He paused, then relaxed. "If you refuse..." He smiled. "...then I'll just use force."
He blurred, lunging at Bai Dou.
Bai Dou was fast, shooting backward instantly. They moved like lightning, neck and neck.
"Rain Cover!" Wei He activated his speed boost.
His pace surged, hand reaching out again.
Bai Dou remained calm. His body twisted like a snake to dodge the strike, his veins turning blue as his speed surged too.
Both were surprised by the other's sudden speed. Bai Dou, however, was now slightly faster, weaving toward the cliff edge.
Wei He cut him off again and again.
Bai Dou pulled another hidden knife. Just then, Wei He vanished from his sight.
"Arc Flash!"
Wei He activated a second, explosive boost. Rain Cover gave sustained speed; Arc Flash gave massive, short bursts.
With both active, his speed doubled. He became a blur.
BOOM!
A body crashed into the stone house behind them.
Before Bai Dou could hit the wall, Wei He caught him mid-air and slammed him down into the dirt.
BOOM!
The ground caved in. Bai Dou lay in the crater, coughing blood, unable to move.
Wei He’s hand gripped his neck tight. A single squeeze would end him.
Bai Dou’s hidden card, his freshly broken-through Bone Forging power, was useless here. His defensive force had torn like paper.
"Had enough?" Wei He asked.
"Yes," Bai Dou gasped.
He wanted to live.
"Eat this." Wei He dropped a purple pill into his mouth.
It was a Lock Poison pill he had made. The poison was a complex trap. To cure it, a doctor had to know the exact formula and layers. One wrong attempt, and the whole thing would blow up inside the body like a landmine.
Bai Dou swallowed it without hesitation. The loser had no rights.
"Kill them!"
Elsewhere in the dark night, dozens of figures in white pushed forward with swift sword thrusts, surrounding a small group of gray-robed men.
The white-robed attackers had numbers, but they were getting slaughtered. Every exchange left one of them dead on the ground.
Out in the fields outside Feiye City, stacks of harvested straw were sliced to pieces as the fight raged.
The gray-robed men were Iron Wolf’s group, sent by Good Man Zhou. Their attackers were from the Ancient Sword Sect, led by the brothers Gao Ling and Gao Xiu.
The brothers wore white metal gloves. Each strike cut through the air with a sharp whistle.
The Ancient Sword Sect fought by treating hands as blades, focusing purely on lethal lunges. They rarely chained attacks; instead, they lunged, disengaged, and lunged again. It packed massive power, but if dodged, left them open.
Iron Wolf’s group knew this weakness. Though outnumbered, their four masters held the upper hand against the lower-tier crowd. Only the two brothers posed a threat.
"Hand it over, or none of you leave tonight!" Iron Wolf barked.
"Go! Get it back to the sect!" Gao Xiu yelled to his brother.
Gao Ling turned and ran.
The Star Cup was on him. If he made it back to the sect, their seniors would keep it safe. They had paid a heavy price to kill that old monster for it, only to get ambushed by Iron Wolf's crew.
Just wait, Gao Ling thought. Once we crack the cup's secret, we'll wipe you all out.
He sprinted into the dark.
Seconds later, a sharp scream echoed from the woods where he had fled.
It cut off instantly.
A female taoist walked out from the shadows. She wore a white robe and a jade crown, a long sword strapped to her back.
She looked cold, blood slowly dripping from her fingertip.
