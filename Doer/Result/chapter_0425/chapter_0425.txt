Profound Sect.
Cloud Terrace.
A rare feast was taking place right here.
Yuanduzi held a wine cup in her hand. She was the host of this gathering.
Beside her stood the two elders who guarded the sect. Around them were nearly ten foreign Grandmasters.
This was a gathering of Grandmasters.
If anyone here remembered the old days of the Great Yue Dynasty, they would realize that most of these Grandmasters were once part of the Great Yuan Daoist sects.
Jinlian Sect, Wushi Sect, along with stray Grandmasters and smaller factions.
So many powerhouses had gathered in one place for only one reason.
"Fellow Yuanduzi, you know very well why we came here today," a bald old man said in a deep voice, walking over to her with a glass of wine.
"Great Yue burned the bridge after crossing it. They broke our deal and launched a sudden attack on our Daoist sects. As the leader of the Daoist path, won't you do something about it?"
The old man, Xiang Cheng, was the representative for the visiting Grandmasters.
"Xiang Cheng, I am glad you all came to visit me," Yuanduzi said calmly. "After all, few of us old people are left. No one knows how many of us will still be around in the future."
"However, my Profound Sect left Great Yue long ago. We no longer take part in the affairs of the mainland. The only promise and help I can offer is to meet you offshore and cover your retreat."
Yuanduzi spoke in a peaceful tone. She did not seem to care that the allied forces had abandoned Profound Sect in the past.
The reason was simple. Years ago, the Daoist sects had united to apologize to Profound Sect and paid a huge amount of compensation.
Part of that compensation was Dragon Blood.
Last time, after the Dao Child was ambushed and his foundation was damaged, Yuanduzi gave the Dragon Blood to him, Cai Menghuan, to help him recover.
Beyond the Dragon Blood, Profound Sect gained far more benefits as the leader of the Daoist sects.
That was why these sects came to ask for help now.
"Back then, Great Yue made its ambition clear," Xiang Cheng said. "If our Daoist sects had not joined forces, the entire Great Yue would belong to the Buddhist factions by now. The Eastern Sect, the holy land of Buddhism, backs the Guangci Sect. Their evil intentions are clear to everyone."
"Now Great Yue released the Martial Codex, placing our martial arts on the banned list. They are trying to cut off our Daoist roots completely!"
Xiang Cheng was one of the founding grandmasters of the Jinlian Sect, holding a high rank among the nine grandmasters of the Nine Hearts.
He came today hoping Yuanduzi would help move Jinlian Sect's branch members overseas.
"Fellow Yuanduzi, I heard you fought with Moduo, the State Teacher of Guangci Sect, some time ago. Who won?" another man asked softly.
This man had white hair, long eyebrows, and a white robe. His expression was cold and calm, like a true immortal.
His name was Gongsun Shan, representing the three main peaks of the Wushi Sect.
The three main peaks of Wushi Sect had three Grandmasters in total. All three, including Gongsun Shan, had sparred with the three elders of Profound Sect years ago. Back then, they had won by a narrow margin.
However, time passed. Hunted by Great Yue for so many years, they lived a far worse life than Jinlian Sect.
The three Grandmasters were fine, but their sect disciples had been cut down by ninety percent.
Without disciples supplying resources over the years, most Real Persons of the three peaks turned into crippled garbage. Once they left the blessed land, they could only wait to mutate.
As a result, Wushi Sect fell sharply in power. If not for the Grandmasters guarding it, it would have dropped into a second-rate sect.
They fell to the same level as smaller Daoist branches like the Ziyi Gate and Tianshan Gate.
When speaking of Great Yue, they hated the dynasty more than anyone else in the Daoist path.
"I merely won by a small margin," Yuanduzi replied calmly.
"In that case, my Wushi Sect has prepared everything. Next month, we will rely on your help," Gongsun Shan said seriously.
"It is fine. We belong to the same Daoist root. It is what we should do," Yuanduzi nodded.
Anyone could speak nice words. In a place like this, she would not say out loud how much Wushi Sect paid for this retreat.
Recently, many sects inside Great Yue bought islands overseas through Profound Sect's channels to prepare their move.
If Yuanduzi had any ambition, most of the Daoist path would have fallen under Profound Sect's control by now.
"Thank you, fellow Daoist." Gongsun Shan raised his cup.
This drinking toast was a custom that spread from overseas in recent years. It was quick and simple, so people slowly picked it up.
Seeing Jinlian Sect and Wushi Sect finish their talks, the remaining smaller sects moved forward. They discussed their retreat with Yuanduzi and Yan Wujiu.
Faced with Great Yue's Martial Codex, the Daoist sects gave up all hope of staying. They were ready to move away completely.
Just then, a dark gold swallow swooped down from outside Cloud Terrace. It glided into the hall, slowed down, and landed gently on Yuanduzi's shoulder.
The swallow was only the size of a palm. It stood on her shoulder, using its beak to clean its feathers.
"A Gold Signal Swallow? Using a True Beast messenger means something huge has happened."
The Grandmasters present had lived for three or four hundred years. Their knowledge far surpassed normal people. They recognized the bird at a single glance.
This type of swallow flew at extreme speeds, rumored to be faster than sound.
It possessed decent strength, allowing it to fly through the True Realm where True Beasts frequently appeared.
Because it traveled within the True Realm, normal people could not see it, and ordinary Real Persons could not stop it.
Only high-tier Full Real Persons or Grandmasters could catch it.
Furthermore, the swallow usually flew at an altitude of over ten thousand meters.
Even Grandmasters and high-tier Real Persons would not constantly stare at the sky ten thousand meters above them to hunt a bird.
Therefore, this was in theory the safest way to send messages in the entire True Realm.
What confused everyone was simple: with Profound Sect's current status, what news could possibly be important enough to use a Gold Signal Swallow?
Yuanduzi stayed calm. She reached out, took the swallow, and pulled a small paper roll tied to its leg.
She opened the tan paper roll and scanned it quickly. Her face changed slightly, looking quite shocked.
"Take a look, teachers," Yuanduzi said. She threw the paper roll toward Yan Wujiu and the other elder.
The two elders took turns reading the note.
Their expressions became strange, mixed with surprise and shock.
The three exchanged glances and quickly reached an agreement.
"Someone come!" Yuanduzi clapped her hands softly.
A Real Person rushed into the room and bowed deeply.
"Pass down my order. Starting today, cancel the wanted list and investigation on the Thousand-Face Demon Lord. Also, promote Real Person Wei He from the Lock-Lifting Mountain line to the sect's Dao Seed, making him the secondary Dao Child."
Yuanduzi spoke slowly. Her words did not just stun the messenger.
Even the surrounding Grandmasters were shocked.
"Our Dao Child, Cai Menghuan, joined forces with Dao Seed Wei He. Yesterday, they were ambushed. During the fight, they successfully killed the ambushers, slaying the Thousand-Face Demon Lord of the Demon Path."
"Pass this news down to the entire sect!"
Yuanduzi's voice was steady, carrying a hint of relief and satisfaction.
The Thousand-Face Demon Lord was a big trouble that had stirred up constant mess for Profound Sect in recent years.
Now, he actually died at the hands of two disciples.
Although the letter mentioned that Xiao Ling had already wounded the Demon Lord, a Grandmaster was still a Grandmaster.
How strong was a Grandmaster? Before this, no one below the Grandmaster rank had ever killed a Grandmaster.
Now, regardless of their past talent or future path, Cai Menghuan and Wei He became famous in an instant.
When Yuanduzi's voice echoed, all the Grandmasters stood frozen.
A Real Person killed a Grandmaster?
They wondered if they had heard it wrong. Even if two people joined forces, the enemy was still a Grandmaster.
The gap between a Grandmaster's Dharma Body and a Full Real Person was like a giant cliff.
They cultivated entirely different forces.
A Grandmaster controlled True Qi, while a Real Person only used True Force.
Qi and Force were two completely different levels of power.
The Grandmasters turned to look at Yuanduzi and Yan Wujiu.
They wanted to confirm if they had misheard.
Unfortunately, Yan Wujiu was smiling so wide his face almost split.
"Yes, the news is real. The Thousand-Face Demon Lord was indeed killed in a surprise attack by our Dao Child Cai Menghuan and Dao Seed Wei He."
"Of course, the Demon Lord was already heavily injured, and he suffered another sneak attack during the fight. But..."
No one cared to listen to the rest.
The mental shock of this news was second only to the release of Great Yue's Martial Codex.
Profound Sect had Yuanduzi to shock the world, and now two amazing geniuses emerged to take over the succession.
With such successors, Profound Sect had a clear future. Compared to them, the other Daoist sects were in a sad state.
Even Jinlian Sect, which was doing the best among them, showed signs of running out of talented youth. Their high-level power remained, but the lower disciples were failing.
Only one top genius Real Person, known as a monster, was holding up the next generation of Jinlian Sect.
Otherwise, they could not compare with Profound Sect at all.
"Wei He? Is he the same Wei He who was the leader of the Ten Thousand Poison Sect in Taizhou?" Gongsun Shan from Wushi Sect asked urgently.
Yuanduzi knew that Wei He had created the Ten Thousand Poison Sect in the past.
She had investigated her fellow countryman thoroughly.
Her answer was direct and clear.
"Wei He did found the Ten Thousand Poison Sect back then, but that was just a child playing around."
"It... was really him?" Gongsun Shan was stunned.
He seemed to think of something, shook his head, and fell silent. He turned and walked away silently.
If he remembered correctly, this Wei He had wanted to join Wushi Sect at the beginning.
However, because the people on the mountain took bribes and refused to do their jobs, they did not let him enter the inner court.
By the rules, with a branch recommendation, Wei He should have been allowed inside to cultivate.
Yet, the inner court head back then was too cold-hearted. He gave no respect to Wei He's master, broke the rules, and chased Wei He down the mountain.
