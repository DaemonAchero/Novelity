Answer: Chapter 125
"You Rong, Zhou Xingtong, and Wang Shaojun are the top stars of the three big families. But... Wang is just packing material. The real monsters are You Rong and Zhou Xingtong. Both are pure talents with insane strength. They are almost thirty now. Ten years ago, they had already reached the peak of Jin power. God knows how strong they are today. People just know that if these two step in, any problem gets fixed."
Ding Yang sounded excited as he rambled on about rumors and stories of You Rong and Zhou Xingtong.
Wei He listened with patient ears. Now that the trouble inside his group was cleared, and with a fresh stash of stolen wealth in his pocket, he felt no panic at all.
He had snagged over ten thousand taels in gold notes off Zhao Xingtang alone. That guy clearly carried his entire life savings on his body. On top of that, the hidden stash of rare beast meat was worth thousands more. A true goldmine.
Of course, he could not touch that meat stash yet. He had hidden it well and moved it in secret, but he could not risk someone using strange magic or tracking skills to hunt down the loot.
Truth was, Wei He had thought about stealing the army weapons at first. But that stuff was too hot to handle. Handing it over to authorities was too risky. The best move was to make everyone believe a third party stole it all. Who that third party was depended on who the mastermind suspected.
If anyone found the medicine stash and tried to camp out to catch the thief, the Soul-Seeking Incense Wei He left behind would take effect.
The incense worked two ways: as a poison, or as a scent. By using the sleep habits of specific bugs, he could tell if anyone nearby had touched the scent.
Wei He did not want to get dragged into this mess, but he refused to be played for a fool either. Unmasking the puppet master was one of his goals. If nobody picked up that stash, he would just bring it home for himself later. By then, no one would ever know he killed Zhao Xingtang. They might suspect him, but suspicion was not proof.
As for the secret letter, it was packed with code. Without a key, it was useless junk to a layman like him. He planned to hold onto it until he found the right chance to trade it for something good. To get maximum value, he had to figure out who was selling army gear and hand the letter to their mortal enemy.
Wei He pulled his mind back to his own strength.
Right now, he was only at the basic Jin stage—pretty much a total rookie who just started learning inner force, like in those old storybooks. He had plenty of hidden tricks, but his actual inner power was still weak. He needed to grind harder.
Earlier, his Rain-Covering Jin lacked force and thickness, which was why it failed to completely block Yao Hanlin's Nine-Rivers Jin. If not for the raw brute strength granted by his Whale-Flood Technique, that hit would have broken him. Luckily, his monstrous strength turned the tide in the end.
While listening to Ding Yang talk, Wei He reviewed his last fight.
Weak Jin power was a real problem compared to old veterans. Also, he needed to trash his extra martial arts ASAP. Except for the Flying Dragon Style, both Five-Ridge Palm and Mountain-Return Fist were useless clutter now.
He decided to make a plan to strip away those two useless styles.
"Wait... if a skill to strip power is still a martial art, why don't I design a perfect one myself? Something that strips bad power without hurting my bones and core?" Wei He had a sudden spark of inspiration.
Looking at the Whale-Flood Technique, he realized the Breakthrough Pearl worked like a cheat code. Every tier of the Whale-Flood Technique normally required the flesh of long-extinct rare beasts as a catalyst. Yet, the Pearl bypassed all of that.
Did this mean he could design a power-stripping method that used the most terrifying, top-tier beast as a catalyst, completely cutting out the nasty side effects?
"No... it goes deeper than that! This trick works on ANY martial art!"
Wei He snapped awake. THIS was the true, terrifying way to use the Breakthrough Pearl!
He remembered reading through stacks of ancient books in the Heavenly Cliff Tower. They mentioned legendary extinct beasts—creatures of insane power, living in forbidden lands, rare and bloodthirsty.
Martial arts built around catalysts from those beasts were without a doubt the most vicious, dark, and unstoppable arts in history. Normal fighters would get crushed into paste trying to hunt those beasts, let alone get close.
But if he could find those ancient manuals...
"Chief? Hey, Chief?"
Ding Yang's voice snapped Wei He out of his trance.
"What is it?" Wei He asked, blinking.
"I... I finished talking. So..." Ding Yang looked lost. Seeing his Chief zone out made him think he had talked too much and annoyed his boss. He stood there, sweating nervously.
"You can go. Also, I'm heading back to the Thousand-Bat Water Pavilion in a bit. Watch the branch while I'm gone. Save any business until I get back."
Wei He could not wait to rush back. Solving his mixed-blood problem opened up endless ideas. If his theory worked, he could build insane combinations of power.
For instance, breaking through the fourth layer of Rain-Covering Jin required the eye of a rare Spring-Origin Bird. But what if he used the eye of a far deadlier, far more vicious monster of the same element as the catalyst instead? Would that make his Rain-Covering Jin infinitely stronger?
What if he found an art that used supreme, nightmare-tier beasts from start to finish—just like the Whale-Flood Technique—and trained it as a side style? Once mastered, he would be...
The thought made his blood boil.
Once Ding Yang left, Wei He packed his things, left the Tianyin Hall, and rushed out of the city.
The moment he stepped out the main gates, several figures slipped out from the dark alleys and fell into step behind him. They wore plain clothes, blending seamlessly into the crowd.
It was late afternoon, and plenty of people were out walking in Yubei District. The safety in Xuanjing City was night and day compared to Feiye City. You could almost feel a hint of peace here.
In the crowded streets, even the poor were fed and healthy, wearing clothes with few patches. Mixed among them were bare-chested, heavily muscled fighters.
Wei He picked up his pace, cutting through the main roads dividing the districts and heading straight for the city exit.
Horses and carriages zipped down the main road. Plenty of martial artists were sprinting along the edge on foot, just like Wei He. Normal people scrambled to get out of their way.
To outrun a horse carriage on foot, a fighter needed to be at least at the Three-Blood stage, or a master of special leg techniques. The latter were rare, but the former were everywhere.
Once Wei He hit the main road and accelerated, the trackers behind him could only watch in despair as he left them in the dust.
However, the moment he cleared the city gates...
A new team picked up his trail. This time, they were blatant and made no attempt to hide.
Wei He picked up on them instantly. His eyes narrowed. Without a word, he veered off the main road and dove into the dark forest to his left.
Between Xuanjing City and Tianyin Lake lay a massive, dense forest. The paths were narrow and shaded, twisting deep into heavy greenery where light barely hit the ground.
Three men chased after him.
The leader wore copper rings on both arms and a red headband that whipped in the wind. The two behind him wore pale gray combat robes, each stamped with a giant plum blossom design on the back.
All three were built like heavy tanks—not as massive as Wei He, but clearly deadly fighters.
Seeing Wei He turn into the woods, the three swung their direction and charged straight into the trees behind him without hesitation.
"Where is he?" the red-headband man growled.
"Still ahead!" One man dropped to his knees, pressing his ear flat against the dirt.
"Keep pushing!"
They sprinted forward, bursting into a clearing with a deep water pool. At the far end of the pool, a small waterfall crashed down from the rocks above, filling the air with a steady roar.
A tall figure in black robes stood by the water, quietly waiting for them.
The man was built like a stone wall, half a head taller and far broader than the three trackers.
It was Wei He.
"You three have been sniffing my heels for a while now. What do you want?" Wei He looked down at them with cold, heavy eyes.
"Tianyin Gate, Yubei Branch Chief, Wei He?" The red-headband man stepped forward, his voice low.
"That's me. And who are you?" Wei He asked casually. He knew the drill. In the martial world, unless you were pulling a cheap ambush, you stated your name before a fight so your opponent knew who beat them.
Names and fame were what these fighters killed for. Now that he was in this world, he played by the rules.
"I am Song Qing of Plum Blossom Manor. I heard you beat the bosses of the White Snake and Black Water gangs. Word is you can fight. I came to test that."
Song Qing threw out a name no one had ever heard of—at least, Wei He hadn't.
"Friend, we don't know each other. Is there any need to jump straight to blood?" Wei He offered calmly.
He knew about Plum Blossom Manor. They were a shady, half-underground force controlling the towns around Xuanjing City. They did every filthy job imaginable short of outright rebellion, and they had been recruiting heavy hitters lately.
"We don't know each other, true. But I just don't like your face. Take this!"
Song Qing exploded forward. He cleared fifteen feet in a single leap, moving at terrifying speed—a rare leg-style master.
Before his body even landed, his leg whipped around like a heavy battle-axe, whistling through the air straight for Wei He's waist.
The sharp, air-cleaving screech alone proved how lethal the kick was.
Wei He's eyes went dark. He dropped his stance low, thrusting both palms straight out to meet the strike.
The Rain-Covering Cloud Style was his core cultivation, but its secret hand seals were saved for finishing moves. Most of the time, Tianyin fighters used the Heart-Breaking Seal for regular combat. Despite its name, it was a basic set of palm strikes and footwork—simple, direct, and violent. In Wei He's eyes, it was no different than his old Mountain-Return Fist.
His eyes stayed sharp and cautious as his palms slammed out to block the deadly kick.
