A few months passed in the blink of an eye.
Wei He focused on training the Rain Covering and Cloud Gathering Art, and his hard work paid off. He was quite smart, so he picked up the basics quickly. However, his progress in building up Blood 印 (Blood Seals) was slow. Perhaps he needed to store energy in his Breakthrough Bead while practicing his martial arts at the same time.
He decided to eat exotic beast meat faster. The water bear meat he earned soon went straight into his stomach. On top of that, he got his monthly supply of Threaded Blue Fish meat. Thanks to all this food, Wei He finally managed to fill the First Layer of the Rain Covering and Cloud Gathering Art to its limit. He was just one step away from spreading the Blood Seals across his entire body.
This final step was tricky. It did not just test how well he could control the Blood Seals. It also required him to have a large total amount of Blood Seals in his body. Simply put, the first step depended on natural talent. If a person had decent talent, they could gather enough Blood Seals.
The second step depended on control. A fighter had to guide those Blood Seals to cover their body in precise amounts. This tested pure control and accuracy.
The first step needed natural talent. The second step needed hard training and sharp understanding. A person could not miss either one. This was why the Rain Covering and Cloud Gathering Art was so hard to master.
"Out of money again..." Wei He sighed deeply.
He had clearly reached the limit of the First Layer. The skin on his hands had turned a faint shade of white, which was the main sign of this art. The higher a fighter's skill grew, the fairer their skin became. In time, it even made a person look better. This was one of the reasons why Master Wan Ling and Senior Sister Wan Qing were so attractive. They had great figures, and their fair skin added to their charm.
"At least the Breakthrough Bead is almost full again. I used up a third of it for the Flying Dragon Art last time, but it recharged pretty fast this time."
Wei He felt a bit relieved.
He pulled open his collar and looked at his chest. The Breakthrough Bead was about ninety percent full. It needed just a tiny bit more energy to turn completely pitch black. When it was full, he could use it to break through the First Layer and step into the Second Layer.
"Still, there are over ten inner disciples in our Wan Qing Yard. A few of them have been stuck at the First Layer for over a year. If I break through too fast, I will stand out too much."
Wei He felt worried. His natural talent was mediocre, and training two arts at once made his body potential even worse. If he broke through faster than normal disciples, it would make no sense to anyone. But staying stuck on purpose was a waste of time.
"The only way is to make people think I just got lucky. I need to find a reasonable excuse for my breakthrough."
Wei He began to count the ways a disciple with poor talent could make a believable breakthrough.
Rare medicines.
Help from a powerful master.
Or pretending to be a genius with a special body type?
"Is Wei He here?"
A clear voice came from outside the courtyard gate.
It was Yang Guo. She was a local from Tianyin Town, and her family lived nearby. Last time Wei He punished his neighbors, Wang Qibing and his son, he had asked Yang Guo to help look into their background.
"Coming!" Wei Ying answered. She wore an apron and ran out of the kitchen to open the gate.
A young woman stood outside. Her long hair was tied in a single bun, and she wore a white headband across her forehead. The headband was made from the soft, tough hair of a special beast. It looked neat and elegant.
Recently, many female disciples in the Tianyin Sect began wearing this kind of headband. It looked nice, and it kept long hair from flying around during fights. Even some male disciples started trying simple versions. However, the men used plain rope knots instead of fancy patterns. Yang Guo wore a headband with an S-shaped pattern.
"Wei He! Sister Yang Guo is here. Come quick!" Wei Ying called out warmly. Ever since Wei He took care of the neighborhood troubles, she got excited whenever a female friend visited him.
A long time had passed, and Wei He showed no interest in starting a family. He lived alone with her all the time. She worried that she was holding her brother back. Many times, she had thought about moving out on her own.
"Coming," Wei He said as he stood up and walked to the gate.
"Let's go to the inner yard together," Yang Guo said with a smile, touching her headband. "Didn't you want to see if the Thousand Bats Pavilion has any new jobs?"
Wei He sighed. He wanted to tell Yang Guo that the headband looked odd on her. But seeing how happy she was while touching it, he kept his mouth shut.
"I can't go today. Our Wan Qing Yard has a gathering later. Someone from the inner yard came to tell me this morning."
"A gathering? What kind?" Yang Guo asked in confusion. Her branch was the Arc Light Yard, which was the First Yard. Wei He was in the Wan Qing Yard, the Third Yard. They learned different skills, but their yards were next to each other. Since they got along well, they often walked together.
"A rich new disciple with good connections is buying a big dinner for all the inner disciples," Wei He explained simply.
"Knowing you, you usually hate these events, right?" Yang Guo asked in surprise.
"I didn't plan to go, but Senior Sister Wan is going. They say even Master Wan Ling will show up," Wei He said helplessly.
When the top leaders were attending, skipping the event would make him look rude and foolish.
Yang Guo understood. "That's rough. I think I heard about that new guy. His name is Zhou Xingsu, right? His family runs inn businesses. They own more than half of the inns in Xuanjing City and the nearby towns."
"You are well informed," Wei He nodded.
Zhou Xingsu was not the only rich newcomer. Before him, a wealthy girl named Chen Lin joined. Her parents were real Martial Masters who controlled inner force. She had high talent and top potential. With endless resources from her family, she reached the Third Blood stage at eighteen and entered the inner yard.
Zhou Xingsu and Chen Lin were the two most famous newcomers in recent months. New members joined every month, but these two stood out the most and received the most attention from the branch master.
"When are you leaving?" Yang Guo asked.
"It is at Xiaoyue Restaurant in Xuanjing City. I will head over soon," Wei He replied.
"So rich! Xiaoyue Restaurant..." Yang Guo said with envy. "That's a Zhou family property. I heard their famous dish, the Nine Treasure Soup, gives massive energy. It is made from nine beast meats and dozens of rare herbs. One bowl costs hundreds of gold coins!"
Yang Guo had no major hobbies other than making money.
"If you like it, why not come along? I can bring you as my guest," Wei He offered.
"No, no, that's not right," Yang Guo waved her hands quickly. "You have a great personality and you are reliable, but I just want to find a rich man. I don't want to live a life of squeezing every penny anymore."
She let out a tired sigh.
"Alright, I'm off. I have late classes, and I promised to deliver letters with my friends later."
She turned and left without looking back. Delivering letters was a hard and risky job, but it paid well. Outside the safe city walls, wild beasts, monsters, and poisonous bugs were everywhere. Only skilled fighters could stay safe while delivering messages. That was why messenger work paid so much. Plus, messengers carried large bags with hundreds of letters, making the total reward quite high.
After Yang Guo left, Wei He said goodbye to Wei Ying and headed toward Xuanjing City. At his speed, he reached the side gate of the city in less than fifteen minutes.
Farmers were busy planting seeds in the large fields outside the walls. Merchant wagons moved in and out of the city gates. Wei He wore the light blue outfit of an inner disciple of the Tianyin Sect. Soon, he arrived at Xiaoyue Restaurant in Chiheng District.
Along the way, regular citizens moved out of his path. Gang members avoided him in fear. Even armed fighters bowed their heads slightly and looked away.
The Tianyin Sect had declined, but it still held a strong reputation in Xuanjing City. Regular people stayed away from anyone who looked strong and wore martial arts clothes. The sect had hundreds of fighters, including inner force users and bone-forging experts. Regular people did not dare to cause trouble for them.
Xiaoyue Restaurant had five floors and stood over twenty meters tall. It was a famous landmark in Xuanjing City. The new disciple, Zhou Xingsu, hosted the dinner on the third floor.
Wei He showed his invitation and followed a waiter up to the third floor.
As soon as he stepped up, he heard people talking loudly.
"...The country grows weaker every day. Corrupt officials rule, natural disasters strike everywhere, and hunger spreads. Bandits and rebels grow numbers daily. Life is getting harder!"
"There are two hundred thousand rebels holding Chenzhou City right now. The government cannot stop them, so they just let them be. I heard a huge wave of refugees is heading our way."
"It is all because of that Incense Gathering Sect. They start fires everywhere, and the common people suffer the most!"
"Why talk about that? We are just single individuals. Even if we had supreme martial arts like our Master, could one person turn the whole situation around?"
"Not necessarily! If I had that power, I could not stop an army head-on, but I could assassinate the enemy leaders. I could make them live in fear every night!"
"Do you think the Incense Gathering Sect is weak? Their special skills are deadly! Do you think their three grandmasters are just for show?"
"So what? Should we just sit still and do nothing?"
"We are regular people. Doing our job and not hurting others is good enough. Even if you have some fighting skills, do you really think you can beat veteran army generals?"
"Why couldn't we? We practiced martial arts since childhood. Are we worse than generals who carry old injuries from war?"
When Wei He reached the floor, the whole room was full of heated debates.
The Wan Qing Yard had rented the entire third floor. Disciples sat or stood at three large tables, arguing until their faces turned red.
"To be honest, I went to Taizhou before and sparred with several army officers there," a tall man stood up and said solemnly.
Wei He recognized him. His name was Yao Hansheng, and he was one of the top three inner disciples right below Senior Sister Wan Qing. His family had good connections, and several of his brothers served in the provincial army. Only he had joined the Tianyin Sect to learn sect martial arts.
"How did it go?" someone asked.
Yao Hansheng shook his head. "The two deputy generals I fought had similar strength to me. Both were at the Blood-Driven stage. Yet, both of them defeated me within thirty moves."
