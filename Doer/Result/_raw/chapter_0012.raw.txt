Chapter 12: A Test?
"A test?" Wei He felt confused.
"That's right. The Gapless Gathering is a rare opportunity. Jiang Yan comes from a influential family, and he even uses his family's power to hire experts to spar with us. So if you can, try your best to join in..."
Cheng Shaojiu was truly thinking for Wei He's sake. He felt that with Wei He's mindset and vision, it would be a shame if he was held back by his weaker talent.
Wei He smiled softly. A test? Just a bunch of kids playing games.
Although the gathering sounded very tempting, he had his own plans.
"Thank you for recommending me, Senior Brother. But I know my talent is poor. I won't force my way in and embarrass myself." He answered calmly.
"Hey, don't act on impulse. If you can gain some real combat experience from this, it will be very good for your future," Cheng Shaojiu urged.
"I'm not acting on impulse. I just have my own plans. Thank you for your kindness, Senior Brother." Wei He rejected him again politely.
Cheng Shaojiu tried to persuade him a few more times, but it was useless. In the end, he went back and told Jiang Yan and the others.
"It seems this Junior Brother Wei knows his own place," Xiao Ran smiled mockingly.
"I've seen plenty of people like him. They just try to climb up any ladder they see. Senior Brother Cheng was probably annoyed by him constantly begging, and couldn't say no, right? But if he thinks he can squeeze into our circle like this, he is dead wrong." Jiang Yan spoke with a calm smile, acting like his father and older brother.
"True. Senior Brother Cheng is too polite, but we don't have that problem," Xiao Ran agreed from the side.
He looked down on these talentless trash. Why bother learning boxing when you are so stupid that simple moves take forever to learn? Wouldn't it be better to go back home and farm?
Jiang Su made no comment. She didn't even care to look, focusing on practicing her skin-grinding technique.
Cheng Shaojiu had nothing to say and could only smile bitterly. He wanted to explain that Wei He was not that kind of person, but looking at their faces, explaining would be a waste of breath. They simply looked down on Wei He. Instead of letting Wei He come in and get insulted, it was better to drop it, lest someone got angry and caused trouble.
Still, he sighed softly in his heart, feeling sorry that Wei He lost this chance.
One should know that this gathering was a great opportunity to gain experience fighting experts. The gathering even had connections with core disciples of other martial masters. It was hugely helpful for seeing different martial arts and increasing combat knowledge.
Unfortunately...
Wei He did not feel it was a pity at all.
After talking with Third Senior Brother Cheng Shaojiu, and before the formal work at the Cheng family began, he took on a short-term escort job. He planned to go out and broaden his horizons first.
This was a temporary job from Changfeng Escort Bureau. The pay was good: five hundred taels of silver for a one-way trip, plus one bag of rice and ten catties of smoked pork. It would take only one day.
The job was to guard goods from the inner city to a small earthen fort near Mingde Temple outside the city.
The reason he took this job was that it passed by Mingde Temple, which was where Wei He's parents had gone missing. No matter what, he planned to go and investigate.
He was worried about the danger of leaving the city alone. But if he followed the large team of Changfeng Escort Bureau, the safety was much higher.
Wei He had checked into it. Jobs at Changfeng Escort Bureau were not like television dramas in his past life, where every delivery ran into trouble. For a large group like Changfeng, trouble happening once in ten trips was rare. Many bandits and mountain robbers would give them some face. Passing through only required paying a small toll.
So he did not think he would definitely run into danger this time. The chance of trouble was very small.
Coincidentally, Wei He also planned to use this chance to see for himself what the supposedly dangerous world outside the city really looked like.
Three days later.
Wei He put on the short outer cape prepared by Changfeng Escort Bureau. The grey cape had the words "Changfeng" written on it. It looked cheap on fabric yet neat. Since they were only hired temporarily, it was impossible to make custom outfits for everyone. Instead, this short cloak could fit anyone, tall, short, fat, or thin. It could also be recycled for future use.
The wide city gate arch of Feiye City slowly passed above Wei He's head.
He followed behind the long convoy of the escort team, carrying a rough short blade at his waist. This was not used for fighting, but for clearing bushes, weeds, and poisonous insects.
The rumbling sound of wooden wheels kept drifting from the front.
Wei He pulled out a water bag from his chest, then took out a paper package. He picked a small piece of golden dried meat from it, put it into his mouth, and chewed carefully.
This was the Silver-Kissed Black Snake meat he had just drawn in advance from Senior Brother Cheng. Although it was dried and had lost some nutrients, eating a piece the size of a palm was enough for a full meal. It was very good at keeping hunger away.
Over the past few days, Wei He had unhesitatingly drawn a month's worth of food rations, which was the single paper package in his chest. According to Cheng Shaojiu's estimate, this package of dried meat could last at least a month and a half.
He just could not imagine that at Wei He's eating speed, it would be gone in ten days at most.
Wei He took a sip of water, swallowed the meat in his mouth, pulled open his collar, and looked at his chest.
The black color of the Realm-Breaking Bead showed signs of appearing again. An area the size of a grain of rice had started to change color.
This Silver-Kissed Black Snake meat really had extraordinary effects. Before, if he kept eating money-bug meat, it would take at least two weeks to reach this level. Now, it only took three days to see the change. The time gap was shortened by a lot.
"Not bad, not bad. If I keep this up, I might be able to fill it up again in two months." Wei He's heart was full of expectation.
After experiencing the blood-energy of the Ox-Hide level, he became even more curious about the next realms of blood-energy transformation.
At this time, the escort convoy moved slowly and steadily out of the city gate, heading along the grey official road toward the distance.
Wei He took the chance to walk alongside the convoy, looking around outside the city.
Outside Feiye City.
Not far from the city walls were neatly cut fields. In the distance, farmers could be seen whipping oxen to plow the land. The fields were like cut blocks of tofu, extending block after block toward the end of the horizon along the official road.
Outside the fields were dense mountain forests and hills. Further past the hills were continuous ranges of mountains.
Chen Shiniu, an old guard from the bureau, walked on Wei He's right. He was an elder specially arranged to help him gain experience.
Seeing Wei He's curious expression, he smiled.
"Did you think the outside of the city was full of danger everywhere, and people wouldn't even dare to step out of the city gates?"
"A little," Wei He nodded. He was indeed a bit surprised.
"Outside the city is very dangerous, but not here. At least within ten miles of the city wall, it is safe. Once you go past ten miles, heh heh."
Chen Shiniu squinted at the official road ahead. The wrinkles on his face shook loosely as he walked.
"When I was young and took my first delivery job, I thought the same as you. Outside the city was full of mountain bandits, wild beasts, and poisonous bugs, extremely dangerous!"
"But after coming out, I realized. Whether it is dangerous or not depends entirely on who you are."
"Uncle Chen, what do you mean by 'who you are'?" Wei He asked whenever he didn't understand.
"For example, our Changfeng Escort Bureau is one of the three big bureaus in Feiye City. We have connections with various local areas, and we have shared drinks with many powerful families and gang leaders. Everyone gives us some face. So walking on this road, our main worry is wild beasts and poisonous bugs. As long as we prepare against those, it is actually very easy."
Chen Shiniu smiled, "And look at these surrounding fields. Don't be fooled by all these farmers working hard. In truth, ninety-nine percent of these fields do not belong to them."
"Then whose are they...?" Wei He asked along.
"The masters in the inner city," Chen Shiniu replied, his expression filled with an indescribable envy.
"The inner city is a good place. All kinds of luxuries, fine wine, good tobacco, they have everything. It's just that everything costs money."
"Now I say, money isn't working that well anymore," another guard nearby came over and shook his head. "Last year, one tael of silver could buy a jar of sheep-bone wine. Now one tael can only buy half a jar."
"Isn't that right? Money gets worth less every year," another guard sighed in agreement.
"That's why the master gives us part of our pay in rice and meat now, to keep everyone from complaining, right?" Chen Shiniu smiled.
"The master is righteous."
"Yes, indeed, the master is very generous."
Instantly, voices praising the Changfeng Escort Bureau rose around them.
Wei He listened silently and stopped talking, acting merely as an observer. At the same time, he kept his whole body on alert, watching the surroundings.
To deal with this escort mission, he had inserted a hard wooden board against both his chest and back. This was to protect against long-range arrows or flying stones. As long as he was not hit in vital areas on his head, the wooden boards would absorb the impact and lower his chance of injury significantly.
The convoy moved forward. Since it was a short trip, they could go and return in a single day, covering a distance of no more than ten miles. It was right on the border of the city's defense zone.
Wei He stayed alert the whole way, but there was no place to use his skills. Everything outside the city was peaceful.
From time to time, groups of rich young men out for a stroll could be seen on the official road. There were also occasional city patrol soldiers passing by with their troops.
The former were fine, looking no different from what he saw inside the city. But the patrol soldiers he saw made Wei He go silent.
These patrol soldiers were all thin and sallow, walking weakly. Some held weapons in their hands, while others were completely empty-handed. They wore yellow undershirts with very old, cheap grey leather armor over them, which only protected their chest and back, leaving the rest bare. Wei He even saw that some of the shoes these soldiers wore were worn-out cloth shoes showing their toes!
Only the squad leader leading them wore something slightly proper, but even that was very limited. His weapon was whole, his leather armor was newer, and he wore a pair of black cloth boots. That was all.
Relying on soldiers like these, Wei He could not believe that the safety of Feiye City could depend on them.
The team kept moving forward. The handcarts escorted in the middle occasionally made shaking sounds as they ran over stones on the ground.
Gradually, as the time approached late afternoon, the escort team finally reached their destination.
According to the rules, they had to rest locally for a short while, resupply, and then head back.
Wei He took advantage of this break to greet Chen Shiniu, then headed toward Mingde Temple alone.
Since it was considered safe within ten miles of the city, how did his parents vanish at Mingde Temple?
Now that he was here, Wei He intended to investigate quickly.
