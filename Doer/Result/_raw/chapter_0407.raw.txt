Great Yue Dynasty. Central State Armory.
Giant furnaces dozens of meters tall stood near the wide Baishui River like massive buildings. Flames roared inside them, occasionally spewing bright red fire out of the tall chimneys. Below, cart after cart of coal and fuel was dumped into the flaming mouths. Craftsmen drenched in sweat bent over rows of workbenches, carefully polishing metal parts.
On a metal platform nearby, a few white-haired old men watched the busy furnaces from above. Among them was Chen Chaogang, the current head of the Armory and a legendary master artisan famous across the land.
Chen Chaogang was over two hundred years old. His hair and beard were pure snow-white. He held the highest rank among the old men. He had witnessed the rise of the Great Yue Dynasty from its early days, and now he was the Emperor's most trusted master craftsman.
In this realm, "Master Artisan" was a supreme title given to the ultimate masters of the craft. It was an official rank granted directly by the court.
"Did the prototype succeed?" Chen Chaogang pulled his beard and asked in a low voice.

"If this works, blood tools will raise Great Yue to a whole new level," Chen Chaogang sighed with emotion.
Since its early days, Great Yue had been searching for ways to strengthen True Blood martial arts. Selective breeding among high-blood families was one way, but it caused too many deformed children.
Now, after years of endless spending and research, they finally found a method to purify True Blood inside the body to the maximum limit.
