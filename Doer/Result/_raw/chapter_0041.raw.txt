"So you mean, if I do not go, I cannot buy this poison powder?" Wei He frowned.
"Not really. Why are you so stubborn? It looks tense outside, but a real fight is still far away," Cheng Shaojiu shook his head. "Three years ago, Hong Family Fort and the Seven Coalition had non-stop conflicts. Everyone thought a war was coming then, but nothing has happened yet."
Wei He stayed silent. He just wanted to travel light. In truth, Feiye City was becoming a place he no longer wanted to stay in for long.
Cheng Shaojiu looked at his expression and knew he was not interested at all. He could only wave his hand.
"Fine, fine. Since you refuse, I will send them a reply and still get the poison powder for you."
"Thank you, Brother Cheng," Wei He nodded solemnly.
"We are brothers, why say that?" Cheng Shaojiu did not mind. "Back to practice."
"Mm."
Wei He returned to his spot, where Ouyang Zhuang was already waiting. He began to guide his moves carefully.
Time flew by.
More than two months passed in a flash. Wei He went nowhere. He only registered his name with a few small gangs in the city to get some food and meat each month. His main focus was staying with Master Zheng, relying on the old man's herbs to boost his blood and qi.
Master Zheng had a type of meat called Red Ground Fish. It tasted awful, almost like human waste, but its medicinal effect was only a bit weaker than Spotted Deer meat. It was a rare item for nourishing blood and qi.
Wei He accepted Master Zheng's conditions and became the core student he truly focused on training. He was now a true close disciple, groomed to be the future successor of the 回山 Fist Academy.
During these two months, the Three Gangs, Two Sects, Hong Family Fort, and other small forts and wealthy families all tried to win Wei He over. They offered valuable terms. Hong Family Fort offered resources no worse than Jiang Yan's, but they also required him to move his family into the fort. That meant demanding his total loyalty unto death.
Wei He thought it over for a long time and refused. He only chose to affiliate with a few small gangs.
Besides that, under Master Zheng's advice, Wei He agreed to become a guest advisor for the Qingdu Sect, one of the Three Gangs and Two Sects. This status was no different from the temporary contract with Yonghe Escort Agency. They paid him a small fee normally, and if they needed his help, they would hire him at an extra price. He was basically an exclusive mercenary.
After setting all this up, Wei He practiced hard every day. His progress in understanding the moves was decent thanks to his modern mindset. However, his progress in gathering blood and qi was very slow.
After eating the Red Ground Fish, his speed was faster than eating Silver-Bite Black Snake meat alone. Eating both together definitely speeded up his blood and qi production. But even so, after two months, his progress was still slow. Right now, the Breakthrough Bead was only filled to two-tenths, which was one-fifth.
As for his blood and qi, he had only formed the red mark of the first Nine-Cloud Flower. He still needed to condense it further to finish the first flower. This was just the first and easiest flower, yet it took so long.
Wei He finally understood why Master Zheng said this step was very time-consuming.
As the situation grew more troublesome, Wei He became very unhappy with his blood and qi progress. To speed things up, he decided to find a way to get more blood and qi tonic herbs. But every group kept their tonics secret and guarded their breeding methods. The only way to get powerful tonics without taking them from others was to hunt for them.
Wei He knew the danger outside the city. But not everywhere outside was dangerous. Otherwise, how could the people bringing food in and out stay alive?
He spent three days going out of the city daily, walking around Feiye City to check the situation. With his current power and reaction speed, as long as he stayed careful, there was no real danger.
Then he watched the city gate for a few days and found an old hunter who often brought game to sell. He paid fifty jin of mixed grain flour to hire him. He asked him to explain the surrounding areas and take him around the huntable spots near Feiye City to prepare for his hunt.
After several life-and-death fights, Wei He gained some confidence in his strength. Rather than relying on outsiders, it was better to be self-reliant. That was his thought.

Ten miles south of Feiye City, past a plain area.
Here stood a range of light green mountain forests called Mount Shaoyang. White stones dotted the steep and rough hills, and the plants grew thick. Though the mountain was not high, few people visited it. It was full of poisonous bugs and wild beasts, so normal old hunters dared not rush inside.
At the foot of Mount Shaoyang, a middle-aged man wrapped in gray animal fur stood near the entrance. Behind him followed a tall, strong young man in tight clothes.
The two came to the entrance and stopped. A faint path stretched into the woods, covered in wild grass. Even in such hot weather, a chill oozed from deep inside the path.
"This is Mount Shaoyang. The huge forest area south of Feiye City belongs to Mount Shaoyang. There are bears, tigers, leopards, and wild boars inside. You can hunt whatever you want," the middle-aged man said.
"Can we still meet big beasts now?" the tall young man asked in a deep voice.
He wore dark green clothes, with several small bags tied to his waist, a short blade on his thigh, and a long cloth bag on his back. This was Wei He, who had prepared for days.
The man in front of him was the old hunter Ding Hai, the one who brought back the most game among those he had seen.
"Big beasts? That depends on luck. Nowadays, when you hunt, you must avoid the forts of the big families first. Do not enter the forts or the places with signboards around them. Otherwise, you will get into trouble."
Ding Hai walked forward, pulled out a chopping knife from his leg, and began clearing the wild grass along the path.
"Be careful and follow me. There are many poisonous bugs here. The recent drought reduced them a bit. In the past, no one dared to come close."
Wei He nodded and followed, listening to Ding Hai speak as they walked.
"I do not know why you must come out to hunt by yourself. But if you want to catch anything, first, you must be patient. Bring dry food, a water bag, a waterproof backpack, medicine for injuries, basic snake poison medicine, spider antivenom, and if possible, some bait."
"Bait?"
"Yes, something to spread a scent and lure beasts when you cannot find any game." Ding Hai pulled a dark brown, meat-like piece from his bag. It was only half the size of a palm.
"Also scent-masking powder, beast dung, and some ropes," Ding Hai added.
He led Wei He along the path. Before long, they reached a dark, yellowed forest. Yellow fallen leaves covered the ground like a thick carpet. The trees above were bare with few leaves, and the air smelled dry.
"It has not rained here for a long time. Many trees and animals died of thirst. So you need good luck to meet anything. As for whether it is a big beast, that depends on your timing," Ding Hai said softly.
"Alright."
Wei He watched him crouch down to search the ground. Soon, under a big pine tree, he found a track left by an unknown animal.
"In this place, besides watching out for beasts, you must watch out for people," Ding Hai stood up, chose a direction along the track, and walked slowly.
Wei He followed close behind, listening carefully.
"Watch out for what people?"
"People around here," Ding Hai smiled. "Mount Shaoyang is split into outer and inner areas. In many outer spots, you often run into experts coming out to hunt for fun. Once you meet those people, run fast."
He walked to a tree trunk, checked the direction again, and made a mark on the bark with his short knife.
"Also, there is a restricted zone here. If you come to hunt, remember one thing, never come near dusk."
"Why?" Wei He asked.
"Because dusk is when the Shaoyang Sect people come out to hunt. But... hehe," Ding Hai let out a cold laugh. "They do not hunt animals. They hunt people."
"People!?" Wei He was startled. "What do you mean?"
"To survive outside the city, you must follow rules everywhere." Ding Hai paused.
"From morning to noon, the Hong Family Fort guards patrol the roads. It is quite safe then. As long as you do not crash into their main troops, you will be fine."
"From noon to dusk, after Hong Family Fort troops go back, it is time for the two sects to act. The Qingdu Sect and the Shaoyang Sect are bullies outside the city. They often roam near Mount Shaoyang. Qingdu Sect people are fine. If you run into them, stop immediately, lower your head, and do not speak or run. They will ignore you."
"But if you meet Shaoyang Sect people, if they are in a good mood, they might spare you. If they are in a bad mood, you will definitely be captured."
Ding Hai cut down the wild grass in front of him with his knife and continued.
"Though Shaoyang Sect people do not catch everyone they see. They only come out once in a while, and their numbers are small. You will only meet them if you have very bad luck."
Wei He nodded slowly.
"What about night?" he asked.
"Night?" Ding Hai gave him a strange look and turned back. "Night is for wild beasts. You cannot see anything. Do you dare to wander around with a torch?"
"I see."
Wei He understood. Suddenly, he thought of his elder sister Wei Chun and his parents who disappeared earlier. Was their disappearance linked to this Shaoyang Sect?
"Brother Ding, do you know about the group of stone carvers who went missing outside the city almost two years ago?" he asked in a low voice.
"I know. They were heading to Mingde Temple back then. I even saw their group on the road. There were quite a few people," Ding Hai replied casually.
"What? You saw them!?" Wei He felt a surge of hope in his heart. "Then do you know how they vanished?"
"How they vanished?" Ding Hai turned around with a deep sneer on his face. "Do you know what time I saw them? Dusk!"
"Are you saying it was the Shaoyang Sect?" Wei He raised his voice slightly.
"I did not say that. But it is the most likely case. That day, I saw those white-robed people from the Shaoyang Sect roaming around from afar. They stood out clearly," Ding Hai replied.
