Wei He slowly took off his mask, black clothes, hood, and coat.
He also pulled off the spiked metal gloves. He had taken these from the fat guy at the Shaoyang Sect last time. After repairing them, they were perfect for sneaky ambushes—one hit, one kill.
As he took off the gloves, he noticed something odd. The newly set spikes had been smashed and broken in a whole row.
Surprised, he reached out and lightly pinched the iron part of the glove.
This was no ordinary pinch. He routed all his blood energy through his body, tensing up as if using his full strength just to hold a tiny cup.
Silently, his fingers sank in. He had lightly pinched a dent into the thick metal layer.
Wei He stayed quiet.
He put the gloves down and clapped his hands together.
Clang.
His palms felt like hard iron, making a sharp, metallic sound when they hit.
"Is this the Third Blood energy? Is this the fourth realm of the Five Ridge Palm?"
Clear River, Cloud, and White Mist—these were the five realms of the Five Ridge Palm.
The first stage was Clear. Only by mastering this could one truly enter the path and grow their blood energy enough to move on.
The second stage was River. This compressed the grown energy until it transformed, breaking through the limits. This was the First Blood breakthrough, reaching the Ox Hide level of the Mountain-Turning Fist.
The third stage, Cloud, was the Stone Hide level, or Second Blood.
The fourth stage, White, was the Iron Hide level, or Third Blood.
"Right now, my Mountain-Turning Fist has only condensed one Nine Glow Flower. To gather enough blood energy for the Breakthrough Pearl, I completely stopped my skin-refining progress."
Condensing a Nine Glow Flower took a massive amount of blood energy.
A human body produces blood energy every single moment. Daily output depends on a person's health and the quality of their food.
Just like a cow making milk—if it gets poor rest or bad food, the milk dries up.
It was the same for martial artists.
Daily blood energy was limited. Using it for one technique meant starving another. Training in multiple styles always slowed a person down.
So Wei He had put the Mountain-Turning Fist on hold, which let his Breakthrough Pearl charge up much faster.
"Now it’s done. Third Blood..." Wei He carefully felt the changes in his body.
His blood energy surged like a roaring river. His body felt as refreshed as if he had just woken up from a deep sleep. His mind was sharp, focused, and crystal clear.
His thoughts moved fast, without a single hitch.
Beyond how he felt inside, the physical changes were even clearer.
Wei He walked over to his wardrobe. He lifted the entire heavy wardrobe with his arms and set it flat onto his bed.
Underneath where the wardrobe had been, he found a metal ring on the floor and pulled it up.
A large square slot opened, revealing a hidden compartment made of dark wood.
Inside were all kinds of poison powders, hidden weapons, daggers, and money pouches.
Wei He pulled out the items he had looted from Xiao Ran's body to inspect them.
A silver-trimmed money pouch, a small green porcelain bottle, a small silk handkerchief with a lotus pattern, and a book filled with dirty, explicit art.
"This kid... tsk tsk." Wei He tossed the silk and the dirty book into the hidden box. Then he picked up the silver pouch, pulled open the drawstring, and tipped it upside down over the floor.
A hundred-liang gold note fell out, along with a few gold leaves, two tiny gold nuggets, and several small wooden tokens used to prove identity in certain groups.
"Poor guy," Wei He shook his head. He picked up the gold note, smelled its faint scent, and gathered the gold leaves and nuggets. He tossed the wooden tokens into the hidden box to burn later tonight.
"Little He, time to eat!" His second sister called out from the courtyard.
"Coming." Wei He stood up, grabbed the porcelain bottle, quickly shut the hidden box, and moved the heavy wardrobe back into place.
He took a long breath and stretched his hands.
Walking out of his bedroom, Wei He passed through the yard and paused where he usually practiced the Five Ridge Palm.
He looked at the large black stone. Usually, a full-power strike from him would only leave a shallow dent on it.
This was no ordinary stone. It contained tough minerals, making it extremely hard and resilient.
The Mountain-Turning Fist Academy used the exact same stone. Wei He had found quite a few on Shaoyang Mountain and carried a large one home.
The oval black stone was covered with his old fist and palm marks.
Because he struck downwards at an angle, the dirt behind the stone had been packed solid over time.
Wei He looked at the palm print he had left yesterday.
It was over an inch deep, with clear palm lines visible. It looked impressive enough.
Suddenly, a thought struck him. He lightly slapped his left hand onto the black stone.
His blood energy surged. Pushing it to the max, he activated the fourth realm of the Five Ridge Palm. An invisible force naturally gathered in his hand and slammed into the stone.
Boom.
The black stone sank suddenly, shoving deeper into the dirt behind it.
Wei He raised his left hand and stared at the web of cracks on the rock in shock.
This black stone was famous for its toughness. Almost no one could shatter it; at most, people just left deeper dents.
You usually judged a fighter's strength by how deep the dent was.
Yet his light slap had shattered the stone.
In the center, his palm mark was over three inches deep, completely buried in the surface.
Spreading out from the print were countless tiny, spider-web cracks.
Wei He suddenly understood. No wonder when he killed Xiao Ran earlier, Xiao Ran had tried to fight back with all his power, but Wei He hadn't felt a thing.
It had felt like hitting a block of soft tofu. There was zero resistance.
The guy had just flown backward like a trash bag, slammed hard against the wall, and stopped breathing instantly.
"What terrifying power...!" Wei He was shocked inside. After breaking through to Third Blood with the Five Ridge Palm, his power was more than double what it used to be.
Before, a full-power strike only made a one-inch dent. Now he reached over three inches and left shattered cracks all around it.
"No wonder... Third Blood warriors are so rare, while Second Blood fighters are everywhere. The difference in status is massive. So that's why... that's why!"
Wei He finally saw the truth.
The gap between Second Blood and Third Blood was like night and day.
Before breaking through, he couldn't beat Xiao Ran head-on because he lacked blood energy.
After breaking through, a single slap crushed Xiao Ran to death before the guy could even react.
No wonder Master Zheng was so obsessed with having a Third Blood disciple in his academy.
"Little He, hurry up! The food is getting cold," Wei Ying called out, poking her head out from the room.
"Coming," Wei He pushed the thoughts aside and walked inside.
After the breakthrough, all the power of the Five Ridge Palm gathered in his hands. His palms were now as hard as iron, immune to ordinary blades. This was his greatest secret weapon now. Each strike carried an explosive impact.
If a Second Blood fighter got touched by it, they were ruined. If they took a direct hit, they died.
On top of that, Wei He could feel his body changing. The massive surge of new blood energy was constantly washing through his frame, strengthening him from the inside out.
His digestion was better, his focus was sharper, and his body was pushing out impurities every moment. The speed and quality of his blood energy production were far higher than before.
Just as Master Zheng had once mentioned, breaking through to Third Blood added twenty years to a person's lifespan. Their physical peak was no longer capped at twenty years old, but pushed all the way to forty.
It was a total transformation.
Inside the room, Wei He sat cross-legged. He looked at the meal on the table: yam and duck soup, stir-fried dried tofu with celery, and braised rabbit with green beans.
"Where did we get the duck and rabbit?" he asked, puzzled. He hadn't hunted anything recently.
"Oh, your junior brother Ouyang Zhuang brought them over," Wei Ying explained with a smile. "He said he met a trapper at the market and bought a dozen ducks and rabbits in one go. So he sent some to us."
Wei He looked at the meat in his bowl. This was a generous gift. Ouyang Zhuang was certainly thoughtful.
He picked up his rice bowl and started eating.
After just a few bites, someone pounded wildly on the courtyard door.
"Who is it?" Wei He stood up to open it.
"It's me! Senior Brother Wei, open up! Something terrible happened!" It was Li Jue's voice.
Li Jue was an older student at the academy. He was stuck at the Ox Hide level and had run out of potential, though he refused to give up.
While he wasn't close with Wei He, they chatted occasionally.
Wei He unlatched the door and pulled it open.
"What happened?"
He looked at Li Jue, whose face was pale as a sheet.
"It's... Xiao Ran! Xiao Ran... he was beaten to death!!" Li Jue shouted in panic. There was no grief in his voice, only sheer shock.
To ordinary disciples, Xiao Ran was an absolute genius, a future Third Blood master. He belonged to a higher class than them, a future leader like Master Zheng.
And now...
He was dead?
Who was brave enough to do that? Weren't they afraid of Master Zheng's wrath?
"What? Xiao Ran was beaten to death?!" Wei He froze, his voice rising sharply.
"Take me there! Quick!" He called out a quick word to his sister, shut the door, and rushed off with Li Jue toward the Mountain-Turning Fist Academy.
The academy grounds were packed with disciples. Xiao Ran had been the strongest student in the entire place, yet he had been killed right inside the city.
If a master like Xiao Ran could be beaten to death so easily, what if the killer targeted the rest of them? It would take zero effort to wipe them out one by one.
In the large courtyard, everyone stood in a quiet circle, staring at Xiao Ran's corpse in the middle.
The body rested on a wooden stretcher, covered by a thin white cloth.
Xiao Ran's parents knelt beside him, pale and broken. They couldn't say a single word, just clutching the white sheet with trembling hands.
He was their only son, a young man with a bright future, and now he was suddenly gone.
Zheng Fugui stood nearby with his hands behind his back, staring at the corpse in a daze.
He had been counting his money earlier, feeling happy that business was picking up. Before the joy could settle, this nightmare news hit him like a brick, leaving him completely stunned.
A few of Xiao Ran's bad companions stood nearby, crying quietly.
They weren't weeping for Xiao Ran—they were crying because no one would pay for their meals or back them up in fights anymore.
In that sense, they were the only ones crying besides his parents.
None of the other disciples felt any grief. Some were even secretly glad, though no one dared to show it.
Xiao Ran was hated at the academy; no one liked him. But he had been the face of the Mountain-Turning Fist Academy, the most talented student among them all.
