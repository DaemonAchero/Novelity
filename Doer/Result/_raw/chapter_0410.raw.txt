Chapter 410
Wei He had an idea in his mind.
Reaching his current level, his mastery of the Righteous Way Method had already hit its theoretical limit.
When he and Jiuying first created this technique, their only goal was to use poison to strengthen their blood and prolong life.
Instead, his blood barely grew, but the technique became more poisonous the more he practiced it.
Last time, Wei He got some active fragments from a ghost mask under the Thousand-Face Demon Lord.
The poison inside was so strong it could even knock down someone at Yao Wan's level.
In a short time, it killed the nerves in Yao Wan's legs, making them impossible to heal.
When he got those poison fragments, Wei He was moved and spent years studying them.
In the art of poison, his knowledge and experience were now a whole era ahead of this world.
Combining basic science from his past life with years of quiet research, Wei He finally got results and added them to his technique.
"Pity, this poison runs out after use. I can only coat my full body force three times at most. That is my limit."
Wei He used his limited materials to dilute and multiply the mix, barely making enough poison.
After all, he had too much force in his body. Covering it all at once took a huge amount of poison.
Though strong, the Righteous Way Method could not create poison out of nothing. Once used, he had to refill it from outside.
While this was troublesome, the biggest benefit of the technique was that using various poisons made him immune to all poisons.
That was the real prize.
For example, after blending the Demon Lord's ghost mask poison into his technique, it could no longer harm him.
Since he was still in the sect and might spar with others, Wei He did not poison everything yet. He only coated one giant python force as a hidden trump card, hiding it among normal ones.
With that done, he went back to training his grip on the Gravity Net.
He had to move and fight freely while controlling the net at the same time.
That way, when enemies entered the net, it would trigger slow-downs, seals, and poison.
His massive force would seal and delay their power, stopping them from using burst skills to break free.
Then, he could strike once to kill them and end the fight.
This entire flow was the ideal combat method Wei He had envisioned.

Outside the Xuanmiao Sect.
On a deserted, wild sea.
Blue waves rolled up and down, with strange fish occasionally swimming by.
Sunlight could not fully pierce the thin fog over the water.
Whoosh.
A blurry figure ran across the waves from afar, slowed down, and stopped on the water.
The figure stood on the sea as if on flat land. The rolling waves under his feet were pressed down by a strange force.
A calm, round surface over one meter wide formed beneath him.
The figure stood inside this circle. The sea below was like a mirror, completely still.
After a short wait, three figures trod the waves from the distance.
They were faint in the fog, their faces and bodies completely hidden by heavy clothes.
"Greetings, Teacher."
The three bowed with respect to the first figure.
"Good. Great Yue sent more good stuff this time. If we complete this job, you won't need to worry about cultivation resources for the next thirty years. You will also get a star array that boosts training speed by fifty percent," the blurry figure said softly.
"Master, please tell us. With such a high reward, is it the same as last time?" one of the three asked in a deep voice.
"Almost," the figure nodded. "This time, the Xuanmiao Sect made a secret protection list. I brought you here to assassinate at least two of them."
"That will be very hard. Even with our power, killing those target elites is extremely tough. They all carry life-saving trump cards," the tallest man among the three spoke honestly.
"If it were easy, the pay would not be this high," the figure said calmly. "I called you here because you have the power to do it."
"Is the list you mentioned about Cai Menghuan, Xuan Zhen, Xuan Ning, and Wei He?"
The second person spoke up, naming secrets known only to the inner mountain of the Xuanmiao Sect.
This voice belonged to a woman.
"It is. Once done, you don't need to hide anymore. Just leave the Xuanmiao Sect," the figure said.
"But Yuan Duzi and the three grandmasters are always guarding the inner mountain. We have no chance to strike," the woman said.
"An opportunity will come soon," the figure whispered. "Top experts from the Great Yue Dynasty will arrive in Yuanxi shortly. When that happens, Yuan Duzi won't sit still, and at least two of the other three grandmasters will show up. As for the last one... I will hold him off."
"Understood," the woman nodded.
"Also," the figure added, "if you succeed, I will keep my promise and let the three of you go."
The three trembled slightly. If their master had not controlled them with poison, they would have left Yuanxi long ago.
With their power, anywhere else was better than staying here.
Sadly, things did not go their way. The poison controlling them was very harsh, and only the teacher before them had the cure.
"Teacher, is that true?" the woman could not help asking.
She was not naive, but though her teacher was good at poison, he always kept his word.
If any other expert said this, she would never believe it.
But this person might actually mean it.
The other two also tensed up, staring at their teacher for an answer.
"It's true, because after this, I will leave Yuanxi too," the figure chuckled lightly. "I will live under a new identity, and taking you along would just be a burden.
We have been together for over a hundred years. You did so much for me, and as master and disciples, I never mistreated you, nor did you betray me.
After this task, we will part ways peacefully."
The words sounded sincere, and the three believed him a bit more.
"I understand. We accept the mission," the woman said firmly.
"Good. Divide the targets among yourselves. I don't care how you do it, I only want results."
After leaving those words, the figure turned and glided away, disappearing into the distant sea in a flash.
The three remained on the spot.
They talked through voice transmission, carefully discussing when and how to strike, and how to escape after the kill. All of this required careful prep.
Of course, the key was waiting for the Great Yue experts to arrive and draw away the grandmasters.

Wei He trained hard every day, getting used to controlling the Gravity Net while moving, attacking, and defending freely.
Luckily, he had trained a lot for this moment before. Now, he was just adjusting to the higher difficulty.
In half a month, he managed to move naturally while keeping the net active.
He just needed some time to test its real combat power.
Just as he planned to apply to go outside and test the net on an opponent, someone from Cloud Terrace arrived to summon him.

"Greetings, First Senior Sister."
Inside Cloud Terrace.
The three key people protected by the Xuanmiao Sect—Xuan Zhen, Xuan Ning, and Wei He—stood side by side, bowing to Yuan Duzi in the main hall.
Then they bowed to Yan Wujiu, the ancestor of the Ghost Head line sitting beside her.
No one else was around.
Yuan Duzi looked a bit tired, but her eyes were still calm and deep like the ocean.
With her around, the Xuanmiao Sect stood firm as a mountain, and no one dared to cause trouble.
"I called you here to give you something," Yuan Duzi waved her hand. Three boxes next to Yan Wujiu opened at once.
Three items flew out, guided by force, and landed right into their hands.
"These three items are for your self-defense," Yuan Duzi paused. "Ancestor Yan Wujiu made them himself using many rare materials. They can protect your life for thirty seconds in a crisis."
Yan Wujiu coughed a few times and stood up.
"This Three-Star Array is called 'One Line', meaning it leaves a line of hope for you.
You don't need to load star cores or maintain it daily. Just keep it on you.
Keep in mind that the array activates when your force is close to breaking. So, if you meet top experts who can kill you instantly, fight with everything you have. That gives you more chance to live and triggers the array so we can save you."
Xuan Zhen looked at the item, unable to tell how it was a star array.
If Ancestor Yan Wujiu hadn't told them, they would have thought these were three plain black pearls.
The "One Line" array looked just like a nail-sized black pearl, with no other features.
"Alright, keep them close. You three are our main concern, though the Dao Seed has one too. You may leave now," Yan Wujiu waved his hand.
"Thank you, Ancestor. Thank you, First Senior Sister," the three bowed quickly.
"By the way, starting today, Wei He's resources will be raised to the highest tier. I am just informing you, don't spread it around," Yuan Duzi added.
The three were stunned.
Xuan Zhen and Xuan Ning were shocked and confused.
Wei He was completely puzzled, not knowing what had happened.
They quickly snapped back, bowed, and left with doubts in their minds.
With the "One Line" array, they had one more life-saving card.
Thirty seconds might be useless for normal people, but for true martial experts, it was enough to run a long way.
The three left Cloud Terrace together and split up at the gate to head home.
Since Xuan Zhen and Xuan Ning were brothers, they lived close to each other and walked together.
"In the past, weren't top resources only for us two and Senior Brother Cai? Why did Junior Brother Wei get them today too? He even got the qualification..." Xuan Ning was confused.
The two of them had top talent and high cultivation levels.
They had always been the core of the sect, receiving top treatment one tier above normal geniuses.
In the past, major events only involved the three of them. Now, Wei He was added to the group.
"With his talent and level, his growth speed over the years shouldn't reach our circle. Maybe First Senior Sister favors him," Xuan Zhen said calmly.
Wei He became famous by fighting above his level, catching the attention of the higher-ups.
However, fighting above one's level was something he and his brother could do too.
Though not as extreme as Wei He, real combat had a lot of exaggeration unless seen with one's own eyes.
"Why care so much? Who dares speak up against First Senior Sister's favor?" Xuan Zhen pushed the thought aside.
"Just curious," Xuan Ning shook his head. He didn't mind Cai Menghuan catching up so fast, but he didn't believe there was a second Cai Menghuan in the sect.
Even so, seeing top resources go to Wei He made Xuan Zhen feel a bit sour inside.
They had worked endlessly for their status, and many others in the sect had far higher realms than Wei He.
A few of Xuan Zhen's close friends had that qualification, yet none had his luck.
Still, since First Senior Sister Yuan Duzi had spoken, no one dared to raise objections over wasted resources.
Even though Wei He's talent could only take him to the lower tier of the realm at best.
