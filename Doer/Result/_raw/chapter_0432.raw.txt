Wei He’s raw physical strength had reached seventy thousand jin after the Whale Inundation Skill’s boost. Now, in just one day, it had jumped by another ten thousand jin.
This was clearly the work of the Three Hearts Skill.
He could feel the second heart constantly pumping fresh nourishment into his body. It was building a brand-new circulation network inside him.
"Interesting."
Wei He felt a slight stir of anticipation. He wondered how far this boost could go.
Once he finished his daily quota of seals, he went straight back to the Yin Ruins. He had to wait for the jury, formed by the Haining Alliance and the Xuanmiao Sect, to give the final verdict and hand out the rewards.
A good thing like Sea-Anchoring Elemental Essence was rare. If he could get it, he would.
The next day.
Every single Real Person on Yin Ruins Island gathered at one side of the ocean.
A massive fleet of ships tied together, deck to deck, hull to hull, forming a vast wooden floor over the water. From far away, it looked like a patched piece of land sitting on the blue sea.
Hundreds of Real Persons from all over Yuanxi were there, wearing all kinds of clothes. They were all participants in the Beast Tide Tournament.
The crowd gathered around a large multi-deck ship in the middle. The place was noisy and full of excitement. Every eye was fixed on the top of that ship.
Up on the top deck, a square opening slowly slid back. A high stage supported by a metal frame rose into the air. It kept going up until it stopped thirty meters high.
With the height of the ship and the stage combined, everyone around could clearly see what was happening up there.
Two old men at the peak of the Whole Real Realm—the top leaders of the Haining Alliance and the Xuanmiao Sect—stood quietly on the platform. One wore black, the other white. Their robes stood out clearly against the sky.
As the two appeared, the noise around slowly died down. Everyone waited for them to speak.
After a short pause, a voice echoed out.
"The beast tide has ended. Following the rules of past tournaments, we have spent the last few days calculating the scores and verifying the results.
Next, we will read the top twenty rankings from the bottom up. Tied rankings will receive the exact same rewards."
The old man used no speaker. His voice spread across every ship in the distance through sheer force.
Wei He stood on the Xuanmiao Sect's ship. Behind him were many Real Persons from the Lock Mountain branch. Some looked calm, while others exchanged quiet messages. Every face carried a trace of relief now that the tide was over.
Wei He turned his head slightly toward another ship.
Over there, Daoist Cai Mengpuan was pinned between two women with large chests. The three of them were whispering about something, but no one knew what was going on.
The two women were clearly two more of his unknown "sisters."
This guy just had too many sisters.
Over the past few days, Wei He had seen plenty of women fighting for his attention. But even though Cai Mengpuan was a total player, these women still willingly hovered around him.
Wei He had to admire that kind of skill.
Noticing Wei He's gaze, Cai Mengpuan cast a pleading look toward him.
Wei He quickly pretended not to see it.
He listened to the rankings being called out while feeling the continuous changes in his body.
Yes, he had realized this morning that his pure strength had increased by at least another ten thousand jin. The transformation brought by the second heart was still going on without stop.
This change was rough and fast. Only a Real Person's strong self-healing ability could take it. Anyone else would have died from internal bleeding within seconds.
The benefit of the Three Hearts Skill was clear, but it came with a huge appetite that annoyed Wei He. Since yesterday, he had been eating more than double his normal amount.
By now, the voice on the stage reached Wei He's name.
"Xuanmiao Sect, Wei He. Seventh place."
"Xuanmiao Sect, Cai Mengpuan. Sixth place."
The two were right next to each other. With their strength, they should have ranked higher. But they had been attacked by a Grandmaster and had to spend time healing.
Besides, that Grandmaster's attack had scared off most of the strong beasts around the island. It was normal that they didn't get many points.
"Third place, Haining Alliance, Wan Guyi."
"Second place, Seven Ghosts Path, Shangguan Qiulin."
"First place, Haining Alliance, Sun Fengzhu."
Wei He narrowed his eyes in surprise.
He had heard of Sun Fengzhu and Wan Guyi from the Haining Alliance. Both were top-tier Daoists. Since the alliance had so many member sects, they had far more Daoists than the Xuanmiao Sect.
Sun Fengzhu had another title: she was the direct disciple of Mo Nian, the Deep Sea Dragon King.
Mo Nian was the only top Grandmaster in all of Yuanxi who could fight on equal ground with Yuanduzi of the Xuanmiao Sect. A disciple trained by him was naturally far stronger than an ordinary Real Person.
What surprised Wei He was the second place.
Where did this Seven Ghosts Path come from? He had never even heard the name Shangguan Qiulin before.
"Seven Ghosts Path..." Wei He narrowed his eyes.
Next was the award ceremony. He wanted to see what gave this Seven Ghosts Path the right to stand on the same level as the Xuanmiao Sect and the Haining Alliance.
"Now, will the top twenty please come up to receive your rewards," the old man from the Xuanmiao Sect shouted from the stage.
Wei He stepped forward. He leaped lightly into the air, turning into a thin line as he charged toward the high platform.
At the same time, nineteen other figures did the same. Twenty people shot toward the platform together.
Just then, among the twenty figures, a strange man covered in pitch-black robes and dragging faint black smoke suddenly reached out.
Sizzle!
In an instant, endless white threads shot out from his black robes. The thick web of white threads flew straight toward the other Real Persons rising to the stage.
The threads looked soft and weak, and a cluster of them flew right at Wei He.
He frowned. He tapped his finger into the air, and an Attraction Serpent formed out of nothing, snapping its jaws at the threads.
Pfft!
The serpent passed right through the white threads without touching them at all.
Wei He's face changed. He twisted his body to dodge, barely avoiding the wrapping threads.
With a few backflips, his movement ignored momentum under the pull of his attraction force. He backed away smoothly, dodging the rest of the threads completely.
Thump.
Wei He landed safely back on the deck of his ship.
He looked up again and saw that more than half of the eighteen other Real Persons were wrapped in the white threads. They hung in midair like giant white cocoons.
The attack came out of nowhere. Not only did Wei He fail to react in time, but even the two top leaders on the stage were caught off guard.
By the time the two old men snapped back to reality and leaped into the air toward the black figure with furious roars, the figure jumped lightly. It turned into a cloud of black smoke in midair and flashed to another open spot.
"How rotten. Do you see? This is the strength of Yuanxi today. Weak, backward, and trapped in the past."
The black figure's voice was loud and clear, intentionally letting everyone hear. He floated in midair, using the white threads tied to the surroundings to hold his balance.
A uproar broke out among the Real Persons below.
To think someone dared to cause trouble on such an important occasion.
There were at least six Grandmasters gathered here, including those from the Xuanmiao Sect and the Haining Alliance. There were also many top experts at the peak of the Whole Real Realm.
Starting trouble here meant facing all these powerful opponents alone. It was practically suicidal.

"Who dares to act so wild here?!"

"Too bad. If I were the me from a hundred years ago, I might have to surrender. But now..."

"If you want them all dead, go ahead and attack!"
"Shangguan Qiulin, what is your real goal?!"

Shangguan Qiulin struck out, throwing three palms into thin air.

"If you want your people back, bring the Abyss Eye to the Black Feather Abyss... We will be waiting for you there."

As the crowd fell into chaos, the Real Persons leaped up and gathered around their respective Grandmasters.
Xiao Ling looked at Cai Mengpuan and Wei He with a solemn face.
"This is an old debt the Haining Alliance owes. It has nothing to do with us, so we shouldn't get involved. We are returning to the sect right now. Don't delay. The Sea-Anchoring Elemental Essence will be given to you internally by the sect."]
