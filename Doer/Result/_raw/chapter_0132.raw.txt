Chapter 132
"To be honest, I have to thank you. You helped me locate all the ambush spots around here," Wei He said with a faint smile.
Unlike his earlier messy state, he was now as quiet as a hidden, massive beast. The terrifying sense of danger oozing from him made even the birds and insects afraid to make a sound.
Around them, even the falling leaves seemed to drop more gently.
"You think you can beat me?! No!! Impossible!!" Song Qing yelled in frenzy, finally pulling his right leg free.
He gathered all his strength and poured out his best ultimate moves.
Countless leg shadows rushed madly toward Wei He, but every single strike was blocked by a single hand. With just a light flick, the heavy leg shadows popped and dissolved like fragile toys.
Song Qing was deeply terrified, unleashing every bit of his power. But against Wei He, he was as weak as a toddler.
Thump.
His last kick was caught by Wei He once again.
"Alright, time to end this."
"You...!!" Song Qing looked up, only to see a fist expanding rapidly in front of his face.
BOOM!!
The protective energy around his body shattered like porcelain. Under the blow, his hard skull caved in, cracked, and exploded inward.
Song Qing flew backward, slamming hard into a tree trunk as wide as several men combined.
He spit out blood and froth, his eyes losing focus. He struggled to say something, but couldn't utter a word.
In a flash, a silhouette darted over.
One punch.
BANG!!!
With a loud crash, Song Qing's chest and stomach were instantly caved in and pierced through.
Wei He's arm went straight through him, slamming deep into the tree behind, pinning him to it alive.
With a wet squelch, Wei He pulled his hand back out.
"Sorry, I haven't quite gotten used to my new strength yet. First hit was a bit too light, so I had to throw a second one."
A rain of dry, red and yellow leaves fell from above, mixing with the blood dripping onto the ground. It was hard to tell the red of the leaves from the red of the blood.
On a high branch not far away, hidden in the thick leaves, a masked, shapely woman was trying hard to hold back her anger and grief.
A single second of hesitation had kept her from jumping down to team up with Song Qing. Now, the fight was already over.
It was too fast.
So fast she didn't even have time to react before everything ended.
She watched her husband get pinned to a tree with a single punch, his body twitching, eyes fading, covered in blood.
The same Wei He who seemed so helpless just a moment ago had suddenly bared vicious teeth, biting Song Qing whole and tearing him apart.
The huge contrast left not only Song Qing helpless, but even her, hiding in the dark, unable to process it.
It was all like a nightmare.
If this was a dream, please let her wake up!
Among the leaves, the woman held her breath and closed her eyes, forcing down the pain in her heart. She knew she had to stay hidden. Only by surviving could she seek revenge later.
When Song Qing's fading eyes looked toward her, she read the clear refusal in them.
He wanted her to stay hidden, not to get caught!
As long as she could escape and expose Wei He's true strength, stronger experts would eventually come to kill him!
As long as she wasn't found, she could...
She opened her eyes.
Right in front of her face was a man's face. It was Wei He, staring right at her.
Her skin crawled. She flipped backward off the tree, sprinting away at full speed.
Before she could take two steps, a sharp pain shot through her scalp. Her long hair was grabbed from behind.
Then, the world spun upside down.
BOOM!!!
The woman tried to raise her energy to block, but it was smashed to pieces.
Her arms crossed over her chest, along with her chest itself, were struck by Wei He's mid-air fist.
No special energy was used, just pure, brute force like a tidal wave, crushing all resistance.
Spurt!
The woman's torso collapsed inward. Just like Song Qing, her arms, chest, and spine were all punched straight through.
A huge burst of flesh and blood erupted from her back, spraying across the ground.
"You... you..." Hanging on Wei He's arm, the woman struggled to speak.
Her eyes held hatred and pain, but it was over. Even a Martial Master couldn't survive such a horrifying wound. Her entire chest was ruined. No miracle medicine could save her now.
"The power was just right this time," Wei He said calmly, pulling his arm back. The blood and splatter were blocked by his protective energy, coming off clean with a light shake.
The woman's body slid to the ground, motionless.
"That wraps it up." Wei He glanced around and felt no other threats.
The ambush set up earlier consisted of only these people.
Now, it was time to loot the bodies.
Two Martial Masters and six ambushers. They should carry a decent amount of money and items.
Wei He moved quickly, searching the corpses one by one. He didn't bother cleaning up the bodies. This time, he hadn't used a single technique or energy style from Heavenly Seal Sect. He had crushed them purely with brute force and his fists.
Anyone investigating the scene would only find out that the killer had immense strength, and nothing else.
Packing everything into a single bundle, Wei He walked into the cave. The hidden supplies he left inside were still untouched. The smoked meat was still fresh.
He tossed the bundle onto the cart, grabbed the handles with both arms, and lifted the entire cart of herbs and meat. Turning around, he used his Flying Dragon Footwork and darted out of the cave.
Outside, he flicked his toe, sending an unconscious man on the ground flying onto the cart.
This was the single survivor he intentionally left alive to interrogate about their background.
The cart weighed over a thousand pounds, yet he carried it easily as he ran.
He didn't take the goods back to Xuanjing City or Heavenly Seal Lake. Instead, he headed off in another direction.
Earlier, he had found a hidden cave nearby, likely an old bandit stash. It was the perfect spot to hide his extra loot.
After running for over ten miles with the load, Wei He reached the cave and hid the goods and herbs inside. Then, he tossed the survivor onto the ground.
Inside, the cave was dark and dry, covered in dry grass and twigs. There was a faint animal smell, as if wild beasts had stayed here before.
The cave had a teardrop shape, narrow at the entrance and wide inside.
As soon as Wei He dropped the man, he felt the man wake up.
"Open your eyes and tell me who is backing you."
The man on the ground stayed still.
"I'll count to three. If you don't open your eyes, you'll never open them again," Wei He said coldly.
Finally, the man sighed, pulling himself up.
"You're the boss. Ask whatever you want."
Knowing he was dead either way, he didn't struggle or shout.
Wei He said nothing. He flicked his finger, scattering a faint puff of gray dust in front of the man's eyes.
Soon, the man's eyes rolled back, his mind growing hazy.
Only then did Wei He begin his questioning.
"Who do you work for?"

He just hadn't expected the Heavenly Seal Sect to be bold enough to get involved in smuggling military arms.
