Yunzhou Prefecture, the Prefectural Commander's Mansion.
Inside the Huaihai Hall.
Maids stood against the wall, holding plates of fresh dishes, ready to serve at any second.
On the hall walls, carved with cloud patterns and exotic beasts, hung specialized metal weapons and armor.
The Prefectural Commander, Sun He, belonged to a great local family. He loved collecting ancient weapons and armor.
Usually, looking at these collections during meals always put him in a good mood.
But now, Sun He put down his ivory chopsticks with a deep sigh.
"What time is it now?"
"Master, it is a quarter past the Hour of the Sheep," the housekeeper in black answered softly from the side.
"The Hour of the Sheep already..." Sun He looked out the window. The sun shone brightly outside. Summer had come again.
The temperature was starting to rise.
Ever since the last Governor was assassinated, the new one proved totally useless. Under heavy pressure from the Wu army, he spent every day drinking and partying, ignoring all duties.
Now, all matters regarding Yunzhou Prefecture and the nearby cities fell entirely onto Sun He's shoulders.
Sun He felt exhausted, but he had to hold on.
He knew that if he collapsed, the remaining territory of Yunzhou would shatter from within, even without pressure from the Wu army.
People were panicked, money was low, and food was scarce. Frequent battles with the Wu army produced countless wounded soldiers. The demand for medicine was huge, far beyond what they had.
On top of that, sending men to join the Coalition forces had drained their strength even more.
Thinking of this, Sun He felt a sharp headache.
"That Three-Faces creature in the city... has he been caught yet?" he asked again.
"Master, many forces are hunting him down. But no news has come out yet."
The housekeeper replied. He was a veteran who had followed Sun He for over ten years, deeply loyal, which earned him the right to learn about government affairs.
In the past, Sun He would never allow a housekeeper to touch state matters.
But now, he simply had no one else to rely on.
"There is no other way... The army suffered heavy losses, and most of our forces must guard the defenses. A small pest like Three-Faces is not our main priority."
Sun He shook his head helplessly.
Who could have imagined that a grand state army now had so few men left? Only four experts at the Viscera Refining stage remained, and two of them were civil officials.
A dozen or so at the Bone Forging stage might seem strong to small factions, but this was the main state force. They were supposed to control the entire region.
This little military power was far from enough.
Worse yet was the Wu army camp not far outside the city. Although the main Wu forces gathered at the Sishui Plains to face the Coalition, the Coalition's situation was likely not good either.
It was precisely because Sun He sent his best generals to join the Coalition that the state army now lacked top fighters.
"By the way, I heard the Master of the White Jade Crown took action against Three-Faces too?" he asked suddenly.
"Yes. Two female experts under his command have been killed," the housekeeper answered.
Sun He was speechless. Chaos ruled the city. Three-Faces acted like a wild trouble-maker, daring to attack anyone.
A few days ago, Sun He played chess with the Master of the White Jade Crown. Even a person of such gentle nature could not help but step into the fight personally.
It showed how far Three-Faces had pushed things.
"Shangguan Tong is a gentle man, but when decisions must be made, he can strike without mercy. With him acting, this matter should be solved.
"However, I heard experts from Wanfei Palace, the Chaos God Cult, and the Xuanmiao Sect have also entered the city. Is this true?" Sun He asked coldly.
"Yes. Three-Faces has no fear and attacked members of these three factions as well. All three have sent people. Wanfei Palace's team has arrived, led by Ling Xian'er.
"The Chaos God Cult's situation is unknown. The Xuanmiao Sect's people have not arrived yet," the housekeeper answered respectfully.
"Ling Xian'er..." Sun He rubbed his temples. Though he was a True Realm expert, dealing with someone of Ling Xian'er's level was still difficult.
She was one of the Ten Flowers in Wanfei Palace.
The Ten Flowers were chosen through a public ranking based on overall combat strength among all disciples.
Those who made it into the Ten Flowers received monthly resources and benefits far beyond ordinary disciples.
Because of this, disciples fought fiercely for these spots, sometimes even causing deaths. All kinds of dark tricks were common.
Ling Xian'er ranked ninth among the Ten Flowers. Though her rank was low, she was still a True Realm Transformative Realm expert.
"Troublesome..." Sun He sighed. Just as he was about to speak again, a soldier rushed in through the doors and fell to his knees.
"My Lord, urgent news!"
"Speak," Sun He said, sitting upright as his heart tightened.
"True Master Yang Ling of the Xuanmiao Sect was just found dead in a water well near the Zuixin Building!"
"...!!" Sun He stood up abruptly.
Earlier, when an incognito True Master from the Xuanmiao Sect was killed, he had not known about it. That expert hid her presence so well that no one knew she possessed the power of a True Master.
But this time was different.
This was a real, confirmed death of a known True Master. When Yang Ling entered the city, she had met him in person. She was a very strict and quiet woman.
And now, she was... killed?!
Sun He felt a chill go straight through his heart.
Without a doubt, he knew things had blown completely out of hand...
The Xuanmiao Sect was a top faction in this world. The death of a True Master would never be let go lightly.
"This is bad..." Sun He took a deep breath, rapidly thinking about how to face the coming storm.
The Xuanmiao Sect had just beaten back the Wu army and held immense momentum. With a True Master killed, they would fight back hard to keep their reputation.
The whole Yunzhou Prefecture was about to fall into complete chaos.

Behind him, the leopard's head exploded, and its body dropped dead.

"Are you holding up alright? Do you need to rest?" He glanced back at Lou Shengyue.
"No need, Master. Just focus on your path and pay no mind to me," Lou Shengyue said obediently.
She knew her role as a servant. Her duty was to serve Wei He and remove trouble, not create it.
Wei He nodded and looked toward the city ahead.
He did not intend to see Guan Yi right away. He planned to finish his business before visiting his old friend.
After so many years, when he read the investigation report earlier, Wei He could not help but laugh.
Guan Yi was still unmarried after all these years.
That guy loved the path of poison so much that no one dared to offer him a marriage proposal.
With only a few minutes of distance left, Wei He prepared to charge through in one burst.
Suddenly, a faint tremor came from the pouch at his waist.
He pulled out a transparent small bottle.
This was a new communication method created by the Xuanmiao Sect for members traveling outside, called the Signal Bug.
It was said to be inspired by the Wu Country.
When Wei He first saw it, he realized it was simply an otherworldly version of a radio.

"Hm?!"
Wei He knew that these three bugs were paired with three others inside the city.
These bugs were bound to the energy of the three True Masters in the city, making them semi-real beasts. The death of one bug here meant the person carrying it inside the city had died...
Wei He's good mood instantly vanished, turning dark and heavy.
Catching up from behind, Lou Shengyue saw the dead bug inside the bottle, and her expression changed.

Inside the city, near the Zuixin Building.
Beside a deep water well, two Taoists stood over the corpse of a woman in Taoist robes. Their faces were filled with rage.
One man knelt on the ground, his hands trembling as he tried to touch the corpse's face.
Yet he did not dare. He was afraid to get too close.
The man was named Gong Huan, the official partner of Yang Ling.
Now, his wife was dead, murdered and humiliated. The blood inside Gong Huan felt like it was going to explode.
Such humiliation! Such deadly hatred! He could barely control himself.
Beside Gong Huan stood a woman named Zhao Yuexi. When Yang Ling was attacked, she and Gong Huan were outside investigating the area.
They never expected that Yang Ling, a Transformative Realm expert, could not block the ambush of Three-Faces.
Zhao Yuexi felt deep rage, but even more, she felt fear.
Her strength was lower than Yang Ling's. If Yang Ling could not stop Three-Faces, she would be completely helpless if that attacker came for her next.
Beside them stood Han Xiangqi, her eyes bloodshot as she clenched her fists tightly.
Her heart was filled with guilt.
If she had not acted so recklessly that day, perhaps Yang Ling would not have been ambushed and killed.
Even if Yang Ling could not defeat the attacker, she would have had enough time to send a help signal.
Because of her stubbornness, Yang Ling's focus had been stuck on keeping her safe.
And that led to this tragedy.
"Do not blame yourself. Even without you, if Three-Faces targeted Senior Sister Yang Ling, she could not have stopped him," Zhao Yuexi comforted her in a low, grim voice.
"Our main priority now is to leave this place immediately. We must meet with our backup team. With just the few of us, we cannot block Three-Faces."
She feared she would end up like Senior Sister Yang Ling.
If Three-Faces could kill Yang Ling, he could definitely kill her too.
However, almost a century of hard training and travel prevented her from panicking like a normal girl. She could still analyze the situation with a calm mind.
"Do we know which senior brother or sister is coming to reinforce us? How many people?" Gong Huan wiped away his tears and stood up, holding the corpse.
"Not sure. The message bug hasn't arrived yet. The backup team is likely traveling faster than the bug," Zhao Yuexi said gravely. "We must leave this place as fast as possible."
"No. We cannot leave!" Gong Huan said with pure hatred. "Leaving now is the real danger. Inside the city, forces like Wanfei Palace and White Jade Crown are keeping things under control. If Three-Faces shows his head, he will be surrounded and killed. But if we go outside the city and get ambushed, we won't be able to stop him."
