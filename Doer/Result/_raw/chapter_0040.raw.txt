Answer: Chapter 40
Zheng Fugui smiled. Hands behind his back, he walked around the inner courtyard, occasionally moving his feet to loosen up.
"In Feiye City, the Seven-Family Alliance is the strongest, with the Hong Family Fort leading them. Next are the Three Gangs and Two Sects, followed by our Martial Arts Academies. Below us are the minor gangs, like the Thief Gang."
He pointed at Wei He and continued, " The actual most dangerous man in this city is Ou Chen, the head of the Ou family and the city warden. Before Hong Daoyuan rose up, Ou Chen was number one."
He gave a thumbs-up.
"Ou Chen..." Wei He noted the name, ranking him alongside Hong Daoyuan in his mind.
"Ou Chen's Golden Money Force is vicious and deadly. Touch it and you are crippled; take a direct hit and you die. Once you reach the Force Realm, your skin and flesh are trained to the limit. Then your body naturally creates a unique force based on your martial arts."
"Ou Chen's force comes from his family's lineage. It is extremely dominant," Zheng Fugui sighed.
"Old Zheng, how does one cultivate this force?" Wei He asked, curious.
"Cultivate force? Break through to Iron Skin first, then fully grasp the concept of the Mountain-Returning Fist. Combine both to raise your blood energy to the next level. Only then can you manifest force. Though our Mountain-Returning Force is weaker than the Ou family's Golden Money Force, we have our own edge. Ours is far easier to master!"
Zheng Fugui said proudly, "The Ou family has over a hundred members, but only one ever mastered the Golden Money Force."
"Then Master, how many do we have?" Wei He asked.
"Our Mountain-Returning Fist..." Zheng Fugui fell silent for a moment before stopping. "We used to have many."
"..."
"Now, all you need to know is that as long as I am alive, our school will not fall," Zheng Fugui said, regaining his pride.
He was old now, and his blood energy had drained significantly. But as a former Force Realm expert, even with weakened blood energy, beating a few peak Third Blood Energy fighters was no problem.
Wei He picked up on this and was secretly surprised by the old man's real strength.
Force Realm experts. They truly were the top fighters in Feiye City.
"Master, are the other academies like ours?" Wei He asked again.
"Not necessarily. But anyone allowed to register with the warden's office and open an academy must at least reach the peak of Third Blood Energy," Zheng Fugui nodded.
He glanced at Wei He.
"Stop daydreaming, kid. Cultivate the Nine Cloud Flowers first and lock in your blood energy. Once you bloom nine flowers and reach Iron Skin, you can drastically slow down your physical decline. That extends your peak blood energy until you turn forty. Only then are you qualified to push for the Force Realm."
"I understand," Wei He nodded.
After Zheng Fugui's explanation, he finally had a clear picture of the city's martial arts world.
"Just know that you are no longer a mere errand boy. You have some power at the bottom level now. The strongest leaders in small gangs are usually only at peak Second Blood Energy."
"Alright, stop staring at the sky. Back to work!" Zheng Fugui said sternly.
'It's aiming too high...' Wei He corrected him silently.
Before he could think further, Zheng Fugui swung a fist at him.
Constantly sparring like this was the fastest way to understand the movement concepts. This part relied entirely on comprehension and mental sharpess. The skin-hardening and blood energy build-up afterwards depended purely on talent.
All three parts combined made up the Stone Skin training method.
After more than two hours of private training in the inner yard, Wei He walked out. He saw Cheng Shoujiu yawning as he put on fresh clothes and talked to new recruits.
Seeing Wei He approach, Cheng Shoujiu waved.
"Morning, Xiao He."
"Brother Cheng, what happened to you? Why are your dark circles so heavy?" Wei He asked in shock.
Cheng Shoujiu's eyes were puffy with dark circles beneath them. He clearly lacked sleep.
He waved his hand and sighed, "Don't ask. Yesterday I was practicing hard and had a sudden breakthrough. I was so happy that I celebrated with two drinks. Then I went to listen to music with some brothers and stayed up half the night."
"..." Wei He was speechless.
That excuse could only fool a child.
Their blood energy was several times stronger than regular people. Staying up for one night could not make someone look like a wreck. It was ridiculous.
Feeling awkward, Cheng Shoujiu scratched his head and chuckled without saying more.
Ever since Wei He motivated him that night, he had trained hard for a bit. But his enthusiasm quickly faded.
Take two days ago for instance.
He worked hard all morning and had a small realization. Overjoyed, he decided to reward himself. He unsealed a jar of fine wine and drank it clean with friends.
When he woke up sober, his friends were gone and it was already night. Thinking it was too late, he decided to wake up early tomorrow to train instead, believing a clear mind would help him learn faster.
So he went to sleep early.
The next morning, he woke up to practice, but his stomach grumbled. He thought he should eat first; sharp tools make good work, and training on a full stomach felt more grounded.
So he ate a lavish breakfast.
After breakfast, the sun came up and blinded the training field.
Cheng Shoujiu felt the sun was too harsh. Prolonged exposure was bad for skin, caused excessive sweating, and lowered efficiency.
He decided to wait until it cooled down. So he closed his eyes and rested, waiting for the heat to pass.
When the sun finally softened, his concubine brought his newborn daughter to visit, along with his other kids.
A happy family needed time together.
So he played with his kids and enjoyed a nice lunch. He could not just ignore his own children, could he?
After lunch, an old friend came by to invite him for drinks.
They had not met in a long time, so he could not refuse. He went along for wine and entertainment. Refusing a friend who came to invite him would look bad.
So he went, played until evening, and bumped into another group of friends who invited him again.
He had just attended one friend's gathering. Skipping this one would make it look like he looked down on them.
Left with no choice, he went out again for drinks and girls until dusk, leaving the venue completely drained.
When he got home, he forced himself to train for half an hour. Realizing it was midnight, he had to sleep, or he would never wake up early tomorrow.
The next day, he woke up early to train again, but heard a friend was in trouble and needed help. He rushed over with men to help.
After spending half the day resolving it, the friend insisted on taking him out to drink as thanks, hiring four women to keep them company.
By the time he got home, Cheng Shoujiu could barely stand. After resting a bit, his uncle called him to help organize the warehouse.
He had ignored family duties for days, so feeling guilty, he went to help in the warehouse.
That night, an escort leader from the agency fell gravely ill. As the young master, he had to bring gifts and visit.
On his way back, he met another old friend who dragged him out for drinks again.
Thinking back on this, Cheng Shoujiu let out a long sigh while looking at Wei He's strange gaze.
He felt a deep, unspoken frustration.
With nowhere to vent it, he could only sigh.
"By the way, Xiao He, now that you broke through, your status is different. Someone came to me asking to play matchmaker for you."
Cheng Shoujiu smiled and lowered his voice. "It is the daughter of the boss of Guan Pharmacy. I had someone check her out secretly. She is stunning and well-built, perfectly fitting your taste."
"Besides, Guan Pharmacy is no small business. In Stone Bridge Town, only my family and the Jiang family rank above them. If you marry Miss Guan, you will never worry about money again. They might even cover your martial arts resources."
He babbled about the Guan family's influence, mentioning their guard force and their trade links with outside forts. They had deep roots.
If this worked out, Wei He's life would change completely.
Unfortunately, Wei He was not interested at all.
He had Master Zheng's promise for blood energy resources, and he could secure the rest by joining a faction. He had no desire to rely on in-laws.
Besides, marriage in these dark times made no sense. Feiye City was growing unstable, and outer districts were decaying. Families were consolidating toward inner districts and forts.
If war broke out, getting married would only add attachments.
He could carry his second sister on his back and run. With a wife and in-laws, he would be trapped.
"Brother Cheng, how many Nine Cloud Flowers have you formed now?" Wei He suddenly asked.
"..." If you do not bring this up, we are still friends.
Hit where it hurt, Cheng Shoujiu shut his mouth immediately.
He would never admit he had only formed five flowers so far.
Only by reaching nine flowers could one achieve Iron Skin and enter the Third Blood Energy realm.
When Wei He joined, Cheng Shoujiu had barely broken through to Stone Skin. Two years had passed since then.
Now Wei He had reached Stone Skin too, while his own progress remained sluggish.
"Keep trying. I have already reached peak blood energy in Stone Skin. You should know it takes at least five flowers to hit peak," Cheng Shoujiu said, keeping a calm face.
"Senior Brother is truly impressive," Wei He nodded in admiration.
"Speaking of which, I found one of those poisonous powders you were looking for. Though finding it was how I got in touch with the Guan family," Cheng Shoujiu added.
"Oh? You found it?" Wei He was thrilled. At his current level, quicklime was no longer enough. Skilled fighters could react fast enough to block their eyes.
If it missed the eyes, quicklime lost most of its lethality.
That was why he asked Cheng Shoujiu to find a stronger alternative.
"Only the Guan family holds this stock right now. If you want it, you must buy it yourself. Those were their exact words," Cheng Shoujiu answered.
He was genuinely looking out for Wei He. Given Wei He's potential, pushing past Stone Skin was nearly impossible.
It was time to plan for the future and settle down.
Connecting with a wealthy family like the Guan family was a great path for long-term security.
