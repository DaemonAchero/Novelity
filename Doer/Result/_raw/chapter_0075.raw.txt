After eating, Wei He walked Jiang Su back home. On his way back to his own place, he saw Mr. Yuan Zhouchi again.
It was noon, and the sun was burning hot.
The old man sat inside a small stone pavilion on the street corner. He held a large flat basket filled with water plants he had just pulled from the river.
His hands kept moving the plants around so the sun could dry them evenly. Every now and then, he took a sip from a water bag beside him.
This old man used to teach in the main hall. People respected him greatly back then, and many rich families hired him to teach their children.
Now, he wore clothes covered in thick patches. His beard, once gray, was completely white.
His eyes seemed weak. He sat hunched over on a cracked stone bench, carefully picking out the rotten parts of the water plants.
Wei He slowed down and stood there for a moment, watching the old man work.
Maybe because Wei He stayed too long, Yuan Zhouchi slowly looked up and stared at him closely.
Wei He smiled back. He did not want to disturb the old man's life, so he walked past him.
At first, Wei He wanted to leave some money or goods for his former teacher. But then he remembered how proud and stubborn the old man was. Looking at his stern face, which had not changed at all, Wei He gave up the idea.
Though the teacher had fallen on hard times, his breathing and color were fine. He was not sick.
Wei He picked up his pace. He crossed a large part of the outer city and headed toward Stone Bridge Town.
When he reached home, his sister Wei Ying was drying pickled vegetables in the yard. The whole place smelled strongly of pickles.
Wei He had told her earlier that he would leave the city soon. Because of that, Wei Ying was already packing their things into bags and bundles.
"Sister, did anyone come looking for me before I got back?" Wei He asked suddenly.
"No," Wei Ying put down her basket, looking confused. "Nobody came all morning. Besides, we live so close to the academy. Even if someone wanted you, they would go there."
Wei He stood in front of his bedroom door. He looked down at the broken hair stuck in the door gap.
He stayed silent for a second. Then, he slipped one hand into his back pocket while gathering his energy into the other. He reached out one finger and gently pushed the door open.
Creak...
The wooden door opened slowly with a sound.
Inside the room, a figure in pure white stood still, facing away from him.
The person had a thin back. The veins on his exposed hands stuck out clearly. His five fingers looked like chicken claws, dry, hard, and covered in thick calluses.
"Who are you...?" Wei He started to ask.
Before he could finish, the figure leaped through the open window and vanished into thin air.
Wei He's face turned cold. He jumped out the window right after him, chasing the figure at full speed.
Someone had snuck into his home. Did they think he had no temper? Breaking into a house like this was a warning. It meant the stranger could sneak in and kill his family at any time.
Wei He knew that showing his real strength would attract unwanted eyes, but he did not expect them to act this fast.
The two ran one after the other through the quiet, cold alleys.
After a short while, the figure ahead stopped. He stood quietly on a dry riverbank.
On his right, the river water flowed slowly.
With his hands behind his back, the man turned around. He wore a plain white mask.
The mask had holes only for the eyes. The rest was completely blank and white, giving it a creepy look.
"I heard news from the Huishan Academy this morning," the man said in a raspy voice, clearly altering his sound on purpose. "Another young talent broke through his limits and entered the path of martial arts. I felt very pleased, so I came to see you. Please forgive my sudden visit."
This man was about the same height as Wei He. The wind blew against his loose white robe, showing his thin frame.
It was hard to tell how such a thin body could run as fast as Wei He.
No... it was not the same speed.
Wei He felt a chill in his heart. The man had kept the exact same distance and speed the whole time. That did not mean he was as fast as Wei He. It meant he was faster.
He had been holding back just to wait for Wei He.
"Who are you really?" Wei He asked in a cold voice. "You did not use the front door. Instead, you climbed my wall and broke my window. Do you not think that is rude?"
"My apologies," the masked man replied. "I have special reasons, so it was not easy for me to walk through the front door. If someone saw me, it would only bring you trouble."
"Is that so?" Wei He's heart sank. He began to guess who this person was.
Before he could guess further, the man revealed his background himself.
"In three years, you went from an ordinary person to reaching the third level of Blood and Blood-Energy. Your talent is truly amazing. Sadly, you never found a famous master. I wonder if you have heard of..."

At Huishan Academy.
The academy was usually quiet, but many people had gathered today.
There were so many guests that Master Zheng of Huishan Martial Arts expanded the event to the inner courtyard.
They opened the space between the rooms so both courtyards could welcome the guests.
The disciples smiled as they greeted one visitor after another.
Zheng Fugui sat tall and firm on the main chair in the inner courtyard. On his left and right stood his two main disciples, Jiang Su and Zhang Lu.
Zhao Hong led the rest of the disciples to welcome guests at the front gate.
"East Mountain Town, Bat Fist is here!"
A disciple shouted at the top of his voice to announce the guests, making it easy for the people inside to welcome them properly.
"South Mountain Town, Wind Leaf Leg is here!"
"Red Stone Town, Bamboo Fist is here!"
The representatives from the three towns that were close with Zheng Fugui had all arrived. Each group was led by three inner disciples who came to offer their congratulations.
Zheng Fugui had asked his old friends to bring some people over. He wanted to make the event lively and show off how strong his academy's new genius was.
"Nine Knot Town, Leopard Fist is here!"
"Raging Mountain Town, Iron Evil Fist is here!"
"White Horse Town, Through-Arm Fist is here!"
"Yunsu Town, Flying Bear Sword is here!!"
At first, Zheng Fugui only smiled and stroked his beard calmly when he heard the names.
But when he heard the last name, his expression changed slightly, and his eyes narrowed.
In the Martial Masters Alliance, the name Flying Bear Sword meant something big. It was not a normal martial arts school.
The Flying Bear Sword held almost one third of the power among all martial arts schools in the outer city.
Old Man Yun, the master of Flying Bear Sword, was the strongest martial master in Feiye City. Under him were two top fighters, Yun Daochun and Zhou Lai. Both were great masters at the third level of Blood-Energy, trained by Old Man Yun himself. Both also held important jobs in the Three Gangs and Two Factions.
Of course, a small event like this would not bring those two famous masters here.
To be fair, Huishan Academy did not have that kind of influence.
Besides, those two were busy figures with important duties. They had no time to show up for a junior who had just made a small breakthrough.
Still, even if it was just a second-level disciple, it was still someone from Flying Bear Sword.
The smile on Zheng Fugui's face froze for a moment before turning into a proud look.
Even though Huishan Academy ranked low in martial matches year after year, so what? Half of those schools with better martial arts had never trained a single disciple to reach the third level of Blood-Energy.
Out of all the towns in the outer city, only a few schools had ever trained a master of the third level. Now, even Flying Bear Sword, a school he barely worked with, had sent someone to congratulate him.
So, he, Zheng Fugui, was not bad at all!
I cannot beat you old guys, but that is fine. My disciple can beat your disciples, and that is enough!
Soon, teams from different martial schools were led into the inner courtyard.
They stood in two rows on the left and right, looking very grand. It felt like the time Zheng Fugui visited Flying Bear Sword.
Out of more than ten martial arts schools, seven had come today. Three were his friends whom he invited, while the other four came on their own.
Luckily, all of them brought gifts.
Zheng Fugui looked across the visitors. The people from Bat Fist, Bamboo Fist, and Wind Leaf Leg all smiled politely. They were all on his side.
He knew their inner disciples well. They were neither the strongest nor the weakest, just right.
However, the other four schools were different.
Leopard Fist, Iron Evil Fist, and Through-Arm Fist all seemed to follow the lead of the Flying Bear Sword team.
The person leading Flying Bear Sword today was none other than Zhou Jihan, a famous young talent.
Zhou Jihan was just eighteen years old, one year younger than Wei He. He was handsome, tall, and carried himself with a calm and steady air, though there was a slight pride between his brows.
Standing next to him were Hu Zhen'an from Leopard Fist and He Hong from Through-Arm Fist. One was tall and the other was chubby. Both were well-known young talents in the city.
The three of them formed their own small group around Zhou Jihan, much like Xiao Ran's old group.
After everyone paid their respects to Zheng Fugui, they took their seats and waited for the event to begin.
"Brother Zhou, do you really believe someone in Huishan Academy made a breakthrough?" asked Hu Zhen'an of Leopard Fist. He had a small mustache and a large, strong build. His arms were much thicker than everyone else's.
As soon as he arrived, he looked closely at the other inner disciples of Huishan Academy. After one look, the little bit of hope he had before completely disappeared.
"Can people of this level really produce a breakthrough? Did they make a mistake? Did they mistake a fake rise in blood energy for a real breakthrough?" Hu Zhen'an shook his head slightly.
A fake rise in blood energy happened when someone was born with a unique body. Their bodies reacted differently when practicing martial arts.
For example, some people turned red when drinking wine, while others turned pale. It was just a natural difference in body type, making the body react differently to the same task.
Martial arts worked the same way.
That was why Hu Zhen'an had such doubts.
"Do not speak carelessly," Zhou Jihan said calmly. "Master Zheng of Huishan Academy is a real martial master who can use Jin energy. He is famous in the outer city. An old master like him would never make such a mistake."
Although Zhou Jihan was the youngest among the three, he was the strongest and carried the most presence. Added to the fact that Flying Bear Sword was much stronger than the other schools, he naturally became their leader.
"Brother Hu has a point, but I do not think a real martial master would make that mistake," He Hong from Through-Arm Fist said in a calm tone. "Let us wait and see. Sometimes, a pearl can be found even in a pile of mud. No need to rush."
"True."
Time passed minute by minute, and the scheduled start time was fast approaching.
Yet, Wei He was still nowhere to be seen.
Zheng Fugui grew anxious inside. He sent someone to Wei He's house to check on him. But the messenger returned quickly with news from Wei He's sister, Wei Ying. She said Wei He had already left early in secret, and no one was at home.
"This stubborn kid, why did he run off at a time like this?" Zheng Fugui sat in his chair, barely able to sit still anymore.
