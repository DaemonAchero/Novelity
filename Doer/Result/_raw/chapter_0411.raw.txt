Chapter 411: A Month Later...
The May weather grew hotter by the day. Sunlight burned bright, casting wide streaks of golden light across the ocean.
Over at the Longwan Dock on Jujun Island, massive three-deck ships flew the flags of the Xuanmiao Sect. One by one, they slowly sailed out of the bay.
The ships were filled with the sect's top experts for this journey.
Yuan Duzi stood near the front, her hands behind her back as she looked out at the distant sea.
Beside her were two accompanying masters, Yan Wujiu and Xiao Ling.
The rest were three top experts from the Inner Mountain. They belonged to the same generation as Yuan Duzi, but their names were rarely known because her brilliance had outshined them for years.
Still, being unknown did not mean they were weak.
They were at the peak of the Quanzhen realm. Staying alive to their age was proof enough of their strength. In the realm of reality, those who were not strong enough had long since mutated or died.
With the sect's deep background, experts like them were not limited to the few who showed up at the big ceremony. All three branches had accumulated immense power over many years.
The sect hid at least five or six more high-level experts like them. Usually, these people stayed far away, guarded the Deep Abyss Prison, or spent their time in closed-door research. They barely talked to outsiders.
Yet this time, Yuan Duzi had no choice but to bring out these last hidden assets of the sect. This trip was not something she could handle completely on her own.
"Moduo asked to fight me directly this time. His blood weapon must have grown stronger," Yuan Duzi said calmly.
"Are you sure you can handle it?" Xiao Ling asked in a deep voice, clearly worried.
Yuan Duzi was the pillar of the entire Xuanmiao Sect. If anything went wrong with her, the whole sect would fall into chaos.
"Back then, we were evenly matched. I was only slightly stronger than him. Now... it is hard to say," Yuan Duzi said, narrowing her eyes. "However, I can still force a draw."
Xiao Ling sighed.
None of the three had ever expected the sect to reach its current size and glory. If they had relied only on the three of them, bringing the sect to this level would have been impossible.
And yet, right now...
"Moduo is traveling with the Future Buddha, the Zengxing Buddha, the Dashizhi Bodhisattva, and the Da'enhui Bodhisattva from the Guangci Sect," Yuan Duzi reminded them. "Their two main leaders are masters, so our two grandmasters will have to deal with them. As for the rest, I will leave them to you, Junior Brother Yuguangzi."
"Do not worry, leave it to us," Xiao Ling nodded calmly.
Against Moduo, he had no confidence at all. But against the others, as one of the three leaders of the sect, he still held strong confidence.
"We are fine on our end as well. We only need to hold the line and avoid defeat," Yuguangzi said. He was one of the hidden cultivators, belonging to the same generation as Yuan Duzi.
...
The ships drifted further into the distance.
Moduo's delegation had arrived at Yuanxi and set up camp on a isolated island not far from the Xuanmiao Sect.
On that island, Moduo personally wrote a challenge letter and sent it to the sect, demanding to fight Yuan Duzi once more.
This was no secret. The entire sect knew about it.
Shortly after the fleet left, a white wooden canoe drew near Jujun Island from the side of the ocean.
Standing quietly on the canoe was a scholar in a milk-white robe. His long hair flowed in the wind, topped with a green jade crown. Phosphorescent light flickered lightly around the corners of his eyes.
It was the Thousand-Faced Demon Lord, the rogue dark master that the Xuanmiao Sect had hunted for years.
"This place will do." He stomped his foot lightly, stopping the canoe, and stared at Jujun Island from afar.
At this distance, he believed the master left behind to guard the sect would definitely sense him.
Of course, with only one master remaining inside, that person might not even dare to step out.
But it did not matter.
A relaxed smile appeared on his face. What he was doing right now was an open trap.
He would wait here for a hundred breaths. If the remaining master did not come out by then, he would start destroying the sect's trade ships and hunting down the disciples outside.
Naturally, the remaining master knew this was bound to happen.
Soon, a long roar echoed, followed by the urgent ringing of alarm bells. The entire Jujun Island erupted into motion.
Uninvolved people rapidly evacuated. Nervous Taoist cultivators spread out across the area, setting up defenses around the island.
The Inner Mountain experts stayed at the center, forming an even star array to prepare for any sudden attack from the Thousand-Faced Demon Lord.
Ships in the surrounding waters quickly sailed away from him, fearing they might get caught in the middle. Ships further out got the message and stopped completely, drifting helplessly on the open water.
Such was the terrifying presence of a true master.
Before long, a figure rushed out from Jujun Island, speeding across the waves toward the Thousand-Faced Demon Lord.
The speed and force were far beyond any regular Quanzhen expert. Every time the person stepped on the water, high columns of water erupted into the air.
"Brother Fengran, you are still as energetic as ever after all these years." The Thousand-Faced Demon Lord did not move. He even smiled slightly as he waited for the other man to close the distance.
"You still dare to come?!" The arrival shouted before his body even reached the spot. He launched a direct attack without stopping.
Power wrapped around his simple straight punch, turning it into a wild storm of violent energy.
The power swept up the sea below, strangely twisting the water into dozens of chain-like streams that rushed toward the Thousand-Faced Demon Lord with cold air.
With a single punch, the sea around the Demon Lord was instantly locked down by a fluid array. Each stream of water carried heavy energy, creating a trap full of deadly moves.
"Good timing. No one will disturb us today, so let me taste the grand moves of the Xuanmiao Sect."
The Thousand-Faced Demon Lord abandoned his old habit of running away on sight. This time, he met the attack head-on. His right palm turned pitch black, and a massive shadow of a giant whale flashed behind his back as he charged forward.
Boom!!
Their fists collided.
Two massive streams of internal power blew up like two barrels of gunpowder hitting each other.
A massive shockwave spread out in all directions, leaving a clear circular crater on the surface of the sea. Water was crushed outward, unable to return for a long time, forming a giant bowl shape in the ocean.
...
At that exact moment, inside the Xuanmiao Sect.
Three figures were quietly approaching three key targets on the hit list from different directions.
Xuan Zhen stood on a cliff, staring into the distance at the battle on the sea. There were no fellow disciples around him, only one of his female guards standing quietly behind him.
He was originally at his residence reuniting with his family, but he never expected such a major event to break out suddenly.
Besides, the Thousand-Faced Demon Lord showing up out in the open meant he definitely had a goal in mind. Many people had already realized this.
"The most likely scenario is a distraction to draw the grandmaster away, leaving the mountain defenceless. But the star array is already active. Even if another master comes, they will not break through the array anytime soon."
With dozens of experts relying on massive resources and star stones, the top-tier array was extremely strong once activated. Any enemy martial artist who entered would suffer heavy suppression.
Even if a master entered the mountain, their strength would be severely weakened.
Suppressed by a giant star array and surrounded by a group of peak experts forming a battle formation, even a master could die here.
After all, the sect still had hidden cultivators. These people usually had no presence, but when the sect faced a life-or-death crisis, they would definitely explode with full force.
Because of this, Xuan Zhen could not figure out what the enemy was really planning.
"Big brother, are you not going down to join everyone?" Xuan Ning walked up rapidly from behind.
"No. There are too many people below and leaders are already directing them. I will only cause trouble if I go down. What about you? Why are you coming up here instead of staying below?" Xuan Zhen turned around and asked in confusion.
"I have some doubts," Xuan Ning frowned. "The Thousand-Faced Demon Lord is clearly pulling the tiger away from the mountain. But our star array is active, and they cannot break through it anytime soon. So what is he really trying to do?"
"No matter his goal, as long as we stay calm and react to changes as they come, we will not make a mistake," Xuan Zhen said heavily.
"True," Xuan Ning nodded. "Actually, I guessed another possibility."
"Are you talking about a traitor?" Xuan Zhen narrowed his eyes. He had guessed it as well, but he simply had not said it out loud earlier.
"Yes. As long as it is a traitor who has passed the array check, they will not be suppressed by the array. They can move freely without raising suspicion. Achieving their goal would be very simple, would it not?" Xuan Ning said with concern.
"That is true, but... we still have hidden cultivators around. What can traitors do? Even if they destroy a few key spots, they cannot succeed. Unless..." Xuan Zhen paused, suddenly realizing something.
"Yes... unless they are targeting specific people," Xuan Ning frowned. "If the Demon Lord has another helper targeting key people in the sect for assassination... for example, targeting Senior Brother Cai Menghuan..."
The two looked at each other, realizing the same terrifying point at the same time.
"Not good, get to Yuntai Terrace fast!" Xuan Zhen immediately dashed down the cliff.
Pfft!
At that exact moment, Xuan Ning behind him struck without warning. A short sword appeared in his hand out of nowhere, thrusting forward with extreme precision.
The blade pierced through Xuan Zhen's protective energy without resistance.
It cut through his clothes, skin, and muscles, with the tip of the blade barely poking out from his chest.
Blood dripped onto the ground, drop by drop.
Xuan Zhen lowered his head in disbelief, looking down at his chest wound.
"Big brother... do not blame me... after all, I am your beloved younger brother..."
The 'Xuan Ning' behind him no longer had his usual hesitant and indecisive personality. Instead, a weird smile covered his face.
Meanwhile, the female guard standing nearby remained completely cold, watching the whole scene without any emotion.
"Are you done? If you are done, move fast. Put on his clothes and token, then head to the next spot," she said coldly.
"It will be quick. These two brothers cannot die just yet. This token has a sensing function. If they die, an alarm will sound right away. Tsk tsk, the Xuanmiao Sect has acquired quite a few good technologies from those foreigners recently. Even our master has not managed to get his hands on stuff like this."
'Xuan Ning' pulled out the short sword and supported Xuan Zhen, who was slowly falling unconscious from the poison, laughing with a relaxed expression.
"Stop wasting time. Assassination is easy. These three may be key elites, but their alertness is low. With our poisoned weapons, a single scratch is enough to take them down. The real difficulty is how we get out after killing them," the woman said calmly.
"I know. Leave it to me, do not worry," 'Xuan Ning' let out a few strange laughs.
