Under the dark sky, deep inside the Black House Mountain Range, a group moved through the night along the main road toward Jinzhou. They had carriages and ox carts with them.
On the surface, they looked like an ordinary merchant caravan.
Yet inside several carriage cabins sat members of the Thousand Poison Sect.
Wan Qingqing, Wei An, and Wei He's parents and oldest sister were all here.
Wan Ling was also among them. She disguised herself as a normal team leader and stayed close to the cargo wagons, watching the surroundings carefully for danger.
The caravan used the name Jinhoo Store, a famous big business in Jinzhou.
This was a backup plan Wei He had arranged long ago. The real owner behind Jinhoo Store was Wei He himself.
Inside the carriage, the old couple, Wei Tang and Li Cui, did not know the full truth, but they still worried about trouble at Thousand Poison City.
Wan Qingqing stayed by their side, whispering comforting words.
Wei An sat quietly off to the side, playing with a rattle drum.
"It is so late, yet we still have to travel. When will Little He join us?" Wei Tang asked in a low voice.
"He will come soon. My husband is handling the rest of the supply arrangements. If chaos breaks out, it will be much harder for him to pack everything up later. Time is tight, so he sent us ahead first," Wan Qingqing said softly.
"That's right, Dad. Little He is not the same boy he used to be. Thousand Poison City is huge. Without him around to manage it, the whole place might stop running tomorrow. So don't worry," Wei Ying added.
Zhen Qi sat on one side and held Wei Ying's hand without speaking.
They had already told the parents about their relationship. They had expected strong opposition from the old couple.
Instead, the parents' reaction brought them both relief and sadness.
Wei Tang and his wife did not care much about how their daughter settled her life. All their attention was focused on Wei An.
Because of this, Wei Ying and Zhen Qi could now sit together openly.
While comforting the old couple, Zhen Qi looked out the carriage window.
She had a bad feeling. She felt their enemies would not let them leave so easily without a fight.
Swoosh!
Suddenly, a slight noise came from the woods beside the carriage.
It was very quiet, easily drowned out by the rolling wheels. Most people did not hear it at all.
But Zhen Qi heard it.
Wei Chun had trained hard in martial arts over the past few years, so her senses were sharper now. She heard it too.
The two traded glances and stayed alert.
To keep a low profile, this caravan did not bring many guards.
At a critical moment, the few of them would have to step up and carry the weight.
Around the caravan, deep in the dark woods, the moonlight shone bright.
Groups of masked men in black stood in the shadows, watching the caravan slowly leave.
"That is the group! I saw Wan Qingqing, wife of the Thousand Poison Sect leader, get inside with Wei He's son, Wei An. If we capture them, the masters of the Wushi Sect will give us massive rewards!
I heard the Wushi Sect holds countless secret techniques in their Thousand Law Pavilion. You could read there for a full year and not finish even half the books.
If this succeeds, even if we cannot change our own path, our children might build a brand new powerful family!"
The masked man spoke with excitement.
Few people could resist such a temptation.
To hunt down the Thousand Poison Sect, the Wushi Sect offered high rewards: spot on their mountain, rare energy seeds, secret techniques, land titles, and territorial rights.
These rewards drew many desperate fighters looking for luck.
Everyone knew the Thousand Poison Sect was dangerous, but the Wushi Sect was far stronger.
With the Wushi Sect leading the charge, many top experts gathered here tonight. The chance of success was extremely high.
They only had to deal with a few young members and average guards.
"Enough talk. Who goes first?" one black-clad man said coldly.
"My men's martial style is not built for the first strike, so..."
"Then I will go! You are scared of a few old people and kids!" another man sneered.
Everyone wore masks and hid their main weapons. They wanted to hide their true identities so the Thousand Poison Sect would not track them down if they failed.
As the caravan moved further away, one group of black-clad men dashed out of the woods and rushed toward the wagons.
With only Wan Qingqing and Wan Ling protecting the group, the caravan looked like a tiger with its claws pulled out—completely helpless.
"Kill!!"
In an instant, men in black dropped from the trees along both sides of the road like heavy rain, leaping toward the caravan.
Many among them were high-level martial masters.
Decades of masked attackers rushed a few dozen caravan members, but in the blink of an eye, the attackers were sliced down to the ground.
Caravan members charged forward one by one, stabbing every fallen attacker to finish them off.
The leader of the caravan was a seventy-year-old man with a full white beard.
He stepped forward, grabbed a masked man in each hand, and slammed them together. Both men suffered crushed spines on the spot, spitting blood as they collapsed dead.
"There are more in the woods!" a caravan guard shouted.
"Charge in and wipe them out!"
A group of caravan members roared and plunged into the trees, catching the remaining ambushers in seconds.
In less than ten breaths of time, a hundred masked attackers were completely wiped out by these simple-looking caravan guards.
The guards looted the bodies, piled the spoils onto the wagons as new cargo, and kept moving forward.
Wan Ling looked at them with a satisfied nod.
These guards were actually powerful martial artists captured by the Thousand Poison Sect and forced into servitude. Some had even tried to ambush Wei He recently, including Bone Forging and Organ Refining experts.
This single caravan carried six Organ Refining masters, six Bone Forging experts, and twelve peak Martial Masters.
Every single one was controlled by a Lock Poison, forced to serve Wei He.
Among them were the four members of the Mo family, who had changed their appearance.
They had tried to kill Wei He before, only to be captured and poisoned with the Lock Poison.
Now, they had no choice but to work as bodyguards.
The Lock Poison was special. If Wei He did not reveal the specific ingredients and exact order himself, no one could cure it.
It was not just poison; it was a lock with a secret code.
Even the Medicine King of the Wushi Sect was helpless against it.
Unwilling to die, these men hid their faces and acted as guards for the Thousand Poison Sect.
After tonight, almost every expert in Taizhou outside the three main factions who tried to attack Wei He had been poisoned and turned into slaves.
Some had chosen to die rather than submit, but Wei He had simply crushed those firm-willed men to death.
After the one-sided slaughter, the caravan returned to normal and headed toward Jinzhou.
With fresh loot and riches from the dead attackers, Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing felt much better.
Inside the carriage, Wei An tried to poke his head out to look.
Wan Qingqing covered his eyes with her hand.
"Do not look," she whispered.
"Why, Mother?" Two-year-old Wei An could speak simple words now.
"You are too young," Wan Qingqing said softly.
Across from them, Wei Tang held Li Cui's hand tightly. The two old people kept their eyes closed, their bodies trembling slightly, but they stayed quiet.
In this chaotic world, they understood the rule: kill or be killed. There was no other choice.
The wheels kept rolling into the distance.
BOOM!
Suddenly, from behind them in the direction of Thousand Poison City, a red signal rocket shot into the sky and exploded violently.
Wan Ling and the others turned back to look.
The blood-red light bloomed like a red plum blossom in the night, shifting from dim to bright, then scattering, falling, and fading away.
Just like the brief, explosive rise of the Thousand Poison Sect over the past few years.
"Little He will be fine," Li Cui said softly.
"Of course he will. He said he would catch up soon," Wei Ying replied with a smile.
Watching the fading light in the sky, everyone offered a quiet prayer in their hearts.
