Although the Guan family had weakened recently in Yunzhou Mansion, they were still stable because they had stored plenty of supplies, medicine, and poison earlier. They were still among the richest people in the area.

Guan Ye was the fourth daughter of the third branch, under Guan Yude.
Guan Yude liked women and spent his time playing around outside, but over all these years, he only had four children.
The first son was Guan Wei, the second was Guan Fulin, the third was Guan Qing, and the fourth was Guan Ye.
Before the war started, the children had been given resources to build their own power outside. The eldest son was meant to inherit the main family business, so the other branches had to build their own paths outside.
Guan Yude had no talent, but his eldest son Guan Wei was decent and built a good reputation for their branch.
The other three children showed no results. After losing all their money and resources, they had to come back home.
With over 180 people in the family, there were many young people like Guan Fulin, Guan Qing, and Guan Ye.

Ever since she failed her business in Feiye City and returned home, she was forced to stay inside, reflect on her mistakes, and learn ordinary things to prepare for marriage.
She was forbidden from touching poison or mechanical tools, which she loved.
A few years ago, she almost lost her life. Now that she was back, her family did not allow her to cause trouble.
"Fourth Miss, there is a letter for you. It came from Haizhou," a servant girl walked in with small steps and said softly.

Still, she stepped forward and took the letter.
She had never been to Haizhou, but she had made many friends in the past. Maybe one of her friends had gone there and sent a letter.

She had not been this crazy in the past, but ever since she was shocked by that person named Cheng in Feiye City, she had suddenly woken up.

How could she make real friends like that?

However, the Guan family had plenty of money, so wasting a bit did not matter. What she wasted, he could earn back.

"Fine, fine. I know you have a big name, Fourth Miss," Guan Fulin said a few more words and left.

So as long as his sister stayed home and avoided going out, she would be safe. Besides, the feast would be held near the house, making it easy to protect her.

'I am very happy to know Miss Guan Ye is still in Yunzhou Mansion.
It has been years since we last met. I do not know how you are doing now. On your birthday in a few days, I will come to visit you in person. Forgive me if I disrupt your day.'

The letter suddenly said someone would visit without even giving a name.
Still, Guan Ye felt happy. Even if the person gave a name, she might not remember it, because she had made far too many friends over the years.
