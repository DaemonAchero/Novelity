Inside a wide indoor martial arts hall at the Jiang family estate.
In a corner of the black dirt floor, a stick of incense was burning. Thin smoke curled up and slowly drifted out the window.
Jiang Yan, wearing tight martial arts clothes, stood respectfully in front of a tall man with long eyebrows, listening to his guidance.
"Your Huishan Fist is still no good. Your strength, timing, and reactions are all lacking. If not for your inability to learn our family's martial arts, why would you go to the outer city to learn such a mediocre fist technique?"
The long-eyed man had graying hair. He was even taller than Jiang Yan, built like a solid wall, making people fear him just by looking.
He was Jiang Yan's father, Jiang Donggui, and one of the only three Third Blood masters in the Jiang family.
It was because of his teaching that Jiang Yan understood how hard it was to break through to Third Blood, far better than Xiao Ran or Jiang Su.
"Father, with my talent, is there really no chance for me to break through?" Jiang Yan asked, unwilling to accept it.
"You eat mutated beast meat every single day. Your blood qi gathers fast, and someone points out every key step for the breakthrough. Yet you are stuck at the barrier, unable to move. Can you make it or not? Don't ask me. Ask yourself!" Jiang Donggui said coldly.
Jiang Yan had been stuck at the threshold of Third Blood for a long time. Every time he tried to break through, he failed.
If not for the Jiang family's vast wealth and rich resources, someone with his talent would not even have maxed out Second Blood by now.
"I am almost twenty... I guess I really can't do it..." Jiang Yan sighed.
Martial arts was not his only path. He practiced only to make one last try, hoping for a sliver of hope. Sadly, it seemed he truly lacked the talent.
"Breaking through to Third Blood is extremely hard. In the whole Feiye City, across all the outer martial arts academies, if all Second Blood students combined can yield one breakthrough every three years, that is already lucky," Jiang Donggui said calmly.
"So few?" Jiang Yan knew it was hard, but he didn't expect it to be this rare.
"Feiye City is a huge place with a population of a million. Yet all Third Blood masters added together do not exceed thirty. That is the result of years of accumulation. Is it hard or not?" Jiang Donggui snorted.
"Once you break through, it is like a carp leaping through the dragon gate. One Third Blood master can easily beat five Second Blood masters combined. Do you know why?"
"I do not know." This was the first time Jiang Yan seriously listened to his father explain these secrets. He straightened his posture and answered gravely.
Jiang Donggui did not answer right away. He walked slowly to a black iron pillar rooted in the ground near the wall.
"The reason is... force!"
Swish!
In an instant, his right hand shot out like lightning. His five fingers lightly swept across the arm-thick iron pillar.
Without a sound, a deep finger mark appeared on the hard iron pillar.
Without making any noise, he cut through solid iron using only his fingers!
Jiang Yan was shocked. He had never seen his father show his true power before. He knew his father was strong, but he never had a clear idea of how strong he actually was.
Now, he finally understood the huge gap between Second Blood and Third Blood.
He walked up quickly and touched the mark.
It was at least two centimeters deep! The edges were completely smooth, as if cut by a sharp blade.
If Jiang Yan used all his power, he could barely bend the pillar, and he would even get hurt from the force bouncing back. He was nowhere near this level.
"To reach Third Blood, your talent, willpower, and blood qi must all hit your body's limit to break the chains and enter a new stage. Do you understand now?" Jiang Donggui asked.
"I understand..." Jiang Yan nodded heavily. "No wonder a Third Blood master gets twenty more years of life..."
"Third Blood sounds like just one step up, but this step is a total change, a transformation that breaks your own body's limits."
Jiang Donggui swung his palm again.
Pfft.
The iron pillar in front of him was cut clean in half. The top piece flew off and rolled onto the ground.

Outside the city, at the Mo Fort of the Qingdu Sect.
The three gangs and two sects all had their own stone forts outside the city. Though not as big as the Hong Family Fort, they served as bases and retreat routes.
The Qingdu Sect's Mo Fort was one such place. It served as both the sect's main base and the backing for Wuxing Town.
Compared to this place, their branch in Feiye City was just a small reach to show their presence as one of the major factions.
Mo Fort was round, surrounded by tall walls with shooting holes and basic watchtowers. Inside, it could hold over a hundred families.
The people living here were all core members of the Qingdu Sect and their families.
Around Mo Fort were yellow-green fields with small canals letting river water flow slowly along the edges.
It was afternoon. Under the lead of a instructor, members of the Qingdu Sect were practicing martial arts together outside the fort. The formations were neat and orderly.
Soon, a sharp whistle echoed from Mo Fort. A black eagle swooped down from the sky and landed inside.
Shortly after, the main gate opened. A few people came out and waited outside.
The man leading them was Zhou Yan, one of the four hall masters of the Qingdu Sect, in charge of the outer hall.
Zhou Yan was over forty, with a red face and a scholarly look. Seeing a single rider approaching on the main road in the distance, he walked up to greet him.
"Brother Zheng, long time no see. How have you been?"
The visitor rode a black horse. He had a stooped back and a thin face. It was Zheng Fugui, who usually stayed at the Huishan Fist Academy.
"Kid Zhou, it really has been a long time." Zheng Fugui got off the horse and carefully checked its hooves to make sure it hadn't stepped on anything sharp.
This black horse had been with him for many years and was now thirty-five years old. Every time he went out, he was very careful, fearing it might get hurt.
Zhou Yan was in no rush, standing by with a smile. Once Zheng Fugui was done checking and sure the horse was fine, Zhou Yan spoke up.
"Brother, do you have important business for this trip?"
"To be honest, I came here to find work for a few of my useless disciples."
Zheng Fugui came to place Zhao Hong, Jiang Su, and Wei He, who had no hope of breaking through.
The situation now was complex. These three were all at the Stone Skin level. With no hope of breaking through, their strength would not grow and would only fade with age.
Because of this, they had to plan ahead for their future, living, and families. If they didn't try hard while young to earn money and assets, life would only get harder as they grew older.
Neither the Seven Family Alliance nor the Hong Family Fort were good people. A war between them would happen sooner or later. If the disciples chose to go on their own, that was fine. But since they were still by his side, he had to find them a safe place.
The Qingdu Sect had always stayed neutral and had a strong background. It was the perfect choice for a retreat route.
"Since it's your request, brother, say no more. Please come in!" Zhou Yan laughed out loud.
Surrounded by the others, the two walked into Mo Fort.

Wei He held a brush and wrote quickly on the dry yellow paper. His movements were large, but the words he wrote were neat, small, and clear.
He was copying a manual.
He wrote down the key parts of the Flying Dragon Skill himself and stored them in another secret place.
This way, he kept a broken copy on himself, hid another broken copy at home, and used his own unique code. Safety was greatly increased.
This was a lesson Wei He learned from the bandit leader who gave him the Five Peaks Palm.
"As the saying goes, there is always something to learn from others. It really makes sense."
Wei He finished the last word, carefully hung the paper to dry, and looked at his work.
On the yellow paper, he had written a bunch of weird symbols that only he could read. Not to mention, these symbols were split into two parts and kept in different places.
By now, many papers like this were hanging on a string by the bedroom wall.
The weather was dry, so they dried fast.
Wei He picked up some steamed meat beside him and stuffed it into his mouth. Even though it only had a salty taste, the natural flavor of the meat made it taste decent.
"It just smells a bit gamey..." Wei He sighed.
Well, it wasn't just a bit gamey, it was very gamey.
These past two days, he locked himself in to train, and eating the Flower Deer meat almost made him throw up. But the Breakthrough Pearl was getting close to full, just missing a tiny bit.
He sighed, about to stand up and practice his fist to help his stomach digest the food.
From the courtyard gate outside, another knock came.
"Who is it?" Wei Ying asked loudly and carefully.
"It's me," a faint, somewhat familiar female voice came.
Though Wei He didn't recognize the voice, it sounded familiar, like a junior sister he often saw at the academy.
It was just that this junior sister was huge, almost as big as his eldest sister, Wei Chun. So he didn't have much of an impression of her.
There were too many junior sisters like that in the academy, all tough enough to work with iron.
"Senior Sister Jiang asked me to bring this over." The big junior sister honestly dropped a bundle, turned around, and ran off.
By the time Wei Ying opened the door, the person was already gone. She poked her head out and looked around, but still saw no one, so she had to close the door.
"Little He, that big brother from last time sent meat again..."
"..." She might not look like one, but she really is a girl... Second Sister, calling her that...
Wei He was speechles inside.
However, he knew Wei Ying didn't mean it on purpose, but really thought that was a man.
To be honest, if he met her for the first time, heard that rough voice, and saw how developed the chest muscles were for both men and women in the academy, it was normal not to tell if she was a man or a woman.
"Got it. It's Senior Sister Jiang's idea, just keep it," Wei He replied from the room.
He had told Wei Ying a few days ago that he was going to train in isolation, but Wei Ying didn't understand what that meant at all.
From time to time, she would still run over and ask a few things.
Like, "Do you need your chamber pot changed?"
"I'm taking your blanket out to dry. Even if you're training, you need to rest."
"Didn't you just start training this morning?"
"Aren't you going to change your clothes?"
"Come out and take a bath! I set up the hot water for you. Eat some night snack after your bath before going back to train."
Words like these took Wei He a lot of effort to explain to her what training in isolation actually meant. Only now did Wei Ying stop bothering him so much.
"This is already the third time... Your Senior Sister Jiang is really good to you..." Outside came the sound of Wei Ying unwrapping the bag.
"Oh, it's that strong-smelling meat again. It looks very expensive from the package. Compared to the dried pork I bought before, the color looks way better..."
Wei Ying started handling the new meat, talking about the details of how it compared to the meat she usually saw.
Ever since Wei He stopped talking during his training, she formed a habit of muttering to herself, as if talking to herself or to Wei He.
