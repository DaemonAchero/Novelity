The fire spread across the town, turning half of it into a sea of flames.
Shadows crept out from hiding places, running toward an open path left at the back of the town.
Under the dark sky, the orange glow lit up the muddy ground. Wooden buildings burned. Townsfolk screamed and ran in panic. Rows of women and children knelt in the main square, trapped and being questioned.
Nearby, flocks of crows watched with blood-red eyes. They waited for the humans to leave so they could feast on the dead.
"Moon and Giant Whale?" Dong Baihua opened an ancient scroll and looked closely at the painting.
It showed a massive blue whale rising out of a dark sea under a crescent moon. On its right cheek, a row of glowing eyes sat tight together, looking like blue pearls stuck to a rock. Above, heavy clouds hid massive mountains, and flashes of lightning cut through the dark sky.
The painting was well made. It had a strange, deep mood to it. But on top of the main picture, someone had added random lines and weird colors. These extra strokes made the painting look messy. The blotches were supposed to look like waves and fog, but they ruined the view.
"Not bad," Dong Baihua said, rolling up the scroll.
She was strong enough to tell that something secret was hidden inside the art. But it was hidden too deep to figure out right away. She had seen things like this before. During the last dynasty, artists loved hiding secrets in their work. Back then, people believed that if a secret was too easy to learn, no one would care about it. So masters hid their martial arts tricks inside common items to pass them down. That trend almost wiped out many real techniques because no one could find them.
"Boss!" a bandit shouted, pulling her back to reality.
"What is it?" Dong Baihua tossed the scroll to a female bandit who carried her loot.
"Some unknown monster is killing our men! The other leaders are too slow to catch him. We can't stop him... The Second Boss asked for your help..." The bandit spoke with extreme caution.
Dong Baihua was known for her brutal temper. One wrong move, and she would rip out your eyes or cut off your arms. Everyone walked on eggshells around her.
"Too slow? Sounds like a real master," Dong Baihua laughed. "Fine. I will hunt him down myself. Once I catch him, we can all enjoy his flesh!"
She opened her hand. Two men rushed over, carrying a giant bow made of heavy horn.
With the fire reflecting in her eyes, she jumped high into the air and landed on the highest rooftop in town. From the top of the three-story building, she scanned the streets below.
In another part of town, Wei He stepped out of a paint shop.
He looked up at the growing fire.
At first, he wanted to check the pawnshops and art stores as fast as possible. Putting out a fire took too much time. If the scroll he wanted got burned while he was busy playing hero, he would lose everything. The fastest and best choice was to grab the art first and check it himself.
Trying to force the bandits to hunt for the picture would also waste time while the fire burned. Besides, there were only a few wells nearby. There was no water to stop a fire like this.
But after checking several places, Wei He felt the scroll was not here.
The fire kept growing. He had already searched every shop in town, but he found no clues.
"Where else would they keep paintings? Any private collections?" Wei He asked, looking back.
Zhou Miaoke nodded quickly, trembling with fear.
"Yes! Many rich families collect art here. But no one knows what they hide inside their homes, so..." She stopped, realizing she was useless to him now.
"So every single house could have it?" Wei He narrowed his eyes.
"Yes... yes..." Seeing the dark look on his face, Zhou Miaoke felt her heart drop.
"And... no one knows where they hide their secrets..." she added fast. "But I can lead the way! I know which rich families own art!"
"Which means I need more hands to search..." Wei He had moved too fast. His own men were still far behind, and he had no one to order around.

"Damn you! You can run, but you can't hide! Try running now, you little trash!"
