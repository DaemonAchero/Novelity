Chapter 260
In the new district of Feiye City, government soldiers were clearing the streets. They dragged out every broad-shouldered, muscular woman they could find. Women built like that stood out, so the guards rarely missed their targets.
Wei Chun was pulled out too. She stood in line with a dozen other well-built women, her heart pounding with fear. She had no idea what was going on.
Down the street, one family tried to hide their daughter. The guards burst inside, dragged her out into the dirt, and beat her family bloody. For poor folk, a single day out of work meant no food on the table. If a worker fell ill from exhaustion, the whole family would starve.
"Eight here in the East Tenth District."
"Five in the South Second District."
"Good enough. Load them up and head back to the inner city."
The soldiers quickly checked their numbers. They needed to bring these women to the city magistrate's mansion so a young beggar could point out the one who had been meeting with Master Zhuang. As they chattered about recent events, none of them noticed the subtle shifts occurring at the top of Hongyan Tower above them.
A wooden prisoner cage sat on the roadside. The soldiers forced the strong women inside one by one, preparing to haul them off.
Pfft!
A streak of red light shot high into the sky and exploded into a bright crimson flare.
As the smoke dissolved, dozens of shadows surged out from every side alley, racing toward Hongyan Tower. They wore black clothes, red ribbons tied around their heads, and curved silver blades strapped to their backs.
"Rebirth in bliss! Scent across the sea!"
They chanted in a wild, hypnotic rhythm, unsheathing their blades with crazy eyes as they charged.
"It’s the Xiangqu Cult!"
"Inform the Enshrinement Hall! Fast!"
The arrogant soldiers scattered like rats, scrambling for cover. But the cultists ignored them entirely. They only drew their swords to hack down anyone blocking their path. Common guards were not their target.
Wei Chun had not been loaded into the cage yet. She stared at the chaos, her mind going completely blank.
"Move!"
Someone grabbed her arm and dragged her into a sprint.
"Those guards accidentally blew a high-ranking cultist's cover," the person whispered urgently. "Run while it’s chaos!"
"Who are you?!" Wei Chun gasped.
The person had a thick black beard, yet a noticeably full chest. It was impossible to tell if this was a man or a woman.
"I am Zhuang Feng. Do you still have the Star Cup my grandfather gave you?"
Wei Chun played dumb immediately. "I don't know what you're talking about."
"Fair enough," Zhuang Feng grinned without offense, pulling her along faster. "Let's hide first and stay alive."
Wei Chun nodded and kept her mouth shut.
Across Feiye City, district after district collapsed into violence. The Xiangqu Cult seemed to have received a unified signal, pouring out from hidden corners toward Hongyan Tower.
In response, the martial masters of the Enshrinement Hall moved out. Most of them were former gym instructors now hired as high-paid guards. They clashed head-on with the cultists in a bloody street war.
At the top of Hongyan Tower, Wei合 stood by the window with Hong Daoyuan and Daoist Bai Yi behind him. They looked down at the burning city.
"Why is the Xiangqu Cult acting up now?" Wei He asked softly. "What were you two discussing before I got here?"
Daoist Bai Yi hesitated, keeping quiet.
Hong Daoyuan, however, did not care about the secret cup. He was the head of the Hong family and the magistrate of Feiye City. A hidden underground treasure meant little to him.
"It’s the Star Cup," Hong Daoyuan said calmly. "It holds a map to a vast fortune. But that is minor. Master, do you know that my Hong family also answers to someone higher?"
"I didn't." Wei He turned around, eyeing him closely. "Tell me."
Hong Daoyuan’s expression did not change. "Feiye is still a proper city. My family couldn't hold it alone against the cult without my teacher’s backing."
"And who is your teacher?" Wei He felt a slight chill. Anyone capable of training a bone-refining genius like Hong Daoyuan was no ordinary fighter.
"My teacher respects strong martial artists. He will come to visit you soon," Hong Daoyuan said with absolute seriousness.
Wei He frowned. From anyone else, this would sound like a threat. But Hong Daoyuan spoke without a trace of malice or disrespect.
"Setting that aside, what is happening down there?" Wei He turned back to the window. The city guards and the cultists were locked in a deadly struggle.
"I am unsure," Hong Daoyuan replied neutrally. "The cult has been planning something for a long time. Something must have triggered this outburst."
"If you don't step in, can your guards hold them off?" Wei He asked.
"No. Our strongest fighter is a former Grandmaster from the Qingdu Sect. The rest are weak. Meanwhile, the Xiangqu Cult has a bone-refining master stationed right near this city."
"Forget it," Wei He pulled out several rolled-up portraits from his robes and tossed them backward. They landed flat in front of the two men. "These are my parents, my sister Wei Chun, and my companions. Find them and protect them. Bring them to me."
Daoist Bai Yi was the strongest man in the local sect, but right now, he and Hong Daoyuan exchanged a quiet glance. They suppressed their urge to fight back.
Wei He was watching them like a hawk. They couldn't send a secret message even if they wanted to. But the cult’s sudden attack was a blessing. The bigger the mess, the sooner Hong Daoyuan's teacher would notice and step in. Once a real expert arrived, dealing with this mysterious man in black would be much easier.
Wei He did not care what they were plotting. Once his poison entered their veins, their lives belonged to him.
Down below, the cultists breached the tower's outer defenses. Hongyan Tower was protected by three rings: common guards, elite Hong family soldiers, and the red-robed martial experts. The cultists were cutting through the first two rings like paper.
"Bai Dou," Wei He commanded. "Go down and quiet things down."
"Yes, Master," a young man's voice called from outside the hall.
"You two can leave now," Wei He told the other two.
"...Understood." Hong Daoyuan and Bai Yi bowed, inwardly chilled, wondering how strong the youth outside really was. They left the top floor, leaving the room completely empty.
Wei He stared down at the blood bath below and sighed. When your strength reached a certain level, crushing people became effortless and boring. It took away the thrill of the struggle.
"To think the famous Master of the Ten Thousand Poison Sect would travel all the way from Taizhou to a backwater town like Feiye. How rare."
An old man with a goat beard appeared silently behind Wei He in the empty hall. He wore simple robes like an old schoolteacher, but his eyes were a natural, dark red. He stared at Wei He with deep caution.
The Ten Thousand Poison Sect was famous across Taizhou. Though this old man operated in Yunzhou, his spies kept him informed.
"So you are Hong Daoyuan's teacher?" Wei He turned around.
Two other withered, skinny old men stepped out from the shadows beside the goat-bearded elder.
"Master Wei," the goat-bearded elder said gravely. "The Hong family is a piece on our cult's chessboard. Barging in like this is foolish. The imperial court is corrupt, building strongholds and turning martial artists into puppet soldiers. People of the martial world should cooperate."
"Do you know Guiyan Tower? Do you know Jinyu Hall?" Wei He asked. He had killed plenty of cultists before.
"Wei He, do you think defeating those trash from Guiyan Tower makes you untouchable?" another old man sneered.
"There is no three-headed beast to save you here!" the third warned coldly.
Wei He smiled softly. "You did your homework."
"The three of us combined can form the Yuenyin Formation," the goat-bearded elder threatened. "Three minds act as one, three bodies move as one. Wise up, Wei He. Cure the Hong family, leave now, and we can part ways smoothly. Otherwise, do not blame us for being ruthless."
"The Yuenyin Formation?" Wei He stretched his fingers, cracking his knuckles. "I heard three elder brothers in the cult use that. You were friends with Elder Ruan Qinghong, weren't you?"
"You are well-informed," the goat-bearded elder replied coldly. "But that is in the past."
"Interesting," Wei He clapped his hands lightly. "Let me guess. The people hiding outside were set up by you in advance. I count thirty-two breaths. And those cultists fighting below have barely suffered any real casualties after all this time. How amusing."
This trip was truly an eye-opener. The Hong family was not only tied to the Wild Spirit Cult, but also connected to the Xiangqu Cult and other forces. The news about driving away the cult was just a show.
All these masters had gathered here today for one single reason: to take his life.
"It seems your news isn't as fresh as you think," Wei He murmured, pulling a pair of thin gloves from his pocket and fitting them onto his hands.
They didn't even know he had slaughtered thousands of people back in Taizhou. He knew the Hong family wouldn't submit easily, but a final struggle was just what he expected.
