Chapter 384: A Ruin?
Hearing the word "ruin," Wei He felt a slight stir in his heart.
Miss Tao continued to speak to Song Shizhen through her inner voice.
"Brother Zhen, the person who invited you is a powerful senior from the Wushi Sect. Neither of us can fight him. If I fail to bring you back, even I will be punished on the spot."
She was playing the sympathy card. Tears welled up in her ugly face, somehow giving off a fragile and helpless look.
Song Shizhen’s heart softened.
Truth be told, back when the two brothers were just starting out, it was Miss Tao’s strength that helped them avoid a lot of trouble and solve many hard problems.
Now that she was in trouble, standing by and doing nothing when they actually had the power to help would make them ungrateful hypocrites.
"What do you need me to do?!" Song Shizhen was, after all, more responsible than his older brother. He took a step forward and gritted his teeth.
"If you can solve the relic array puzzle set by that senior, all the past trouble you two caused will be forgiven. On top of that, you will receive ten thousand taels of gold as compensation!" Miss Tao felt a rush of joy, her tone growing even gentler.
Wei He listened from the side, a few thoughts coming together in his mind.
Wushi Sect, ruins, and an array lock—putting these key facts together naturally sparked his interest.
He had felt something was off earlier. Shi Heng from the Wushi Sect had appeared far too suddenly, far too fast.
It was as if he was already right around the corner and rushed straight over.
Now it was clear. The Wushi Sect must have found a ruin nearby and sent people to check it out, only to get blocked by a star array they couldn't solve.
Then Song Shizhen somehow showed a bit of talent and helped them make some progress, which ended up dragging him into this mess.
Wei He slowly put the pieces together while listening to them talk.
He was originally about to leave, but hearing about a ruin nearby—and that the Wushi Sect had sent a Dao Seed level figure like Shi Heng to take charge—meant this ruin was definitely not ordinary.
Wei He was growing quite interested.
The deadline for the Xuanmiao Sect's relocation still gave him some time. He could use this gap to check out what this ruin really was, and why it was important enough for the Wushi Sect to send one of their Dao Seeds to guard it.
Of course, he definitely wasn't going there just because Shi Heng had been rude to him earlier and he wanted to pick a fight.
He really just had a sudden whim to see what the ruin looked like.
By now, Song Shizhen had reached an agreement with Miss Tao. He cupped his hands to thank Wei He and bade farewell, ready to leave with her.
Wei He waved his hand dismissively and let him go.
Even though he was interested, he would never show it on the surface. Quietly following the two would be enough to lead him right to the ruin.
It would also be a good chance to test Shi Heng and see where he got the nerve to act so arrogant in front of him.
It came back to the same point.
For someone from the Wushi Sect to act cocky in front of him was simply courting death.
Years ago, he had left the Wushi Sect in shame because he was too weak to push back.
Now that he had gained a bit of strength, swallowing his pride again would make him worse than a coward.
Besides, his Senior Sister had explicitly told him to act without any restrictions.
If he still hesitated and held back, he would be completely wasting her good intentions.
Near the Yunzhou Capital, a continuous mountain range stretched across the land.
The mountains had a strange shape, resembling a giant hourglass—thick at both ends and narrow in the middle.
The locals called it Gong Mountain.
Deep inside Gong Mountain, there was an area covered by a vast stone forest.
On a slope covered with gravel and crushed stone, workers had dug out a neat, gray-black entrance to an underground tomb.
The entrance looked like the gate of a grand estate, complete with a main door, guardian stone beasts, and a hanging plaque.
However, everything here went against the rules of the living world.
The main door was painted a pale, ghastly white, with faint, eerie patterns visible on its surface.
The guardian beasts were not stone lions, but two terrifying stone bats.
As for the plaque hanging above, the writing on it was a pattern of characters that no normal person could recognize.
To ordinary people, it just looked weird and ugly. But to a real cultivator, it radiated danger and threat.
Around the tomb, members of the Wushi Sect guarded every direction, staying alert against any suspicious approach.
Not far off, under a stone pillar, Shi Heng sat on a long chair with his eyes closed, quietly cultivating while waiting for something.
"Senior Brother, we have been trying to break this star array for over a month and still have no clue. Staying here is just draining our energy. Should we invite some stronger star array experts to take a look?"
A man with a goat beard spoke up with a worried frown.
His name was Zhao Xu. Before Shi Heng arrived, he was the leader in charge of this location.
Now that a Dao Seed like Shi Heng had arrived, Zhao Xu’s authority had dropped to second place.
Shi Heng remained calm. He had gathered plenty of information before coming here.
His main task for this trip was to secure this Shadow Beast ruin.
Handling just three faces certainly didn't require a top expert like him, who could fight above his level against full Real World powerhouses.
What truly drew a heavy hitter like him was the ruin itself.
A Shadow Beast ruin.
The most core element here was the Shadow Beast itself.
Shadow Beasts were a type of True Beast. Their bodies were pitch black, as if made of condensed smoke.
They were fierce by nature, and their appearance always called for blood.
Most importantly, the star stones inside a Shadow Beast were star cores.
This was a higher quality crystal. Unlike regular star stones that served as disposable fuel, star cores were a far more durable and powerful energy source for star arrays.
A single Shadow Beast ruin could continuously produce Shadow Beasts, which meant a steady yield of star cores.
For ordinary real-world cultivators, every single Shadow Beast possessed terrifying power and was a complete nightmare.
But for a major faction like the Wushi Sect, this was a massive resource mine!
Shi Heng came in person just to confirm the details of this ruin.
If it really was a Shadow Beast ruin, the Wushi Sect would send more experts to lock down the area, explore deeper, and move the core of the ruin's star array—the Shadow Beast Star Disk—back to the Three Main Peaks.
The Shadow Beast Star Disk was the source of the entire ruin. As long as they possessed it and provided enough nourishment, they could endlessly produce Shadow Beasts, hunt them for star cores, and use them for a long time.
Of course, to ordinary people, this place was nothing more than a haunted tomb.
Normal people could not perceive the Shadow Beasts hidden in the true world, nor could they touch the Shadow Beast Star Disk within reality.
"We must get the Shadow Beast Star Disk this time. The star core supply on the Three Main Peaks is running dry. If this newly discovered ruin can produce a star disk, it will be a huge achievement for me..." Shi Heng said in a deep voice.
"So, the star array must be broken. Didn't you say earlier that the mortal named Song Shizhen had some talent in star arrays and happened to solve one of the hard parts?"
"Yes, I have already sent someone to fetch him. Song Shizhen was originally just an ordinary martial artist. Who knew he would accidentally solve the array puzzle we put a bounty on? He does have some talent. Later on, we might give him some resources to train him," Zhao Xu said seriously.
"Good," Shi Heng nodded.
He stared at the ruin's entrance, then suddenly thought of something and turned to ask, "By the way, has the Wanfei Palace completely pulled out?"
The Wanfei Palace also had a share in discovering this ruin; in fact, they were the ones who found it first.
For unknown reasons, they gave up on developing it alone and chose to cooperate with the Wushi Sect instead.
Now, it seemed something went wrong inside the Wanfei Palace. Their main force had fully withdrawn, leaving only a few people behind to guard the area.
"They have left. Ling Xian'er and Quan Lanying, who arrived later, left the Yunzhou region yesterday," Zhao Xu replied quickly.
"I heard their palace master, Xia Linghua, was ambushed and severely injured outside earlier. Looking at it now, it seems to be true. The Wanfei Palace lacks depth and overall strength compared to our Wushi Sect. A sect founded by mere merchants falls apart at the slightest storm. How laughable," Shi Heng said cold-bloodedly.
"Senior Brother is absolutely right. The Wanfei Palace is nothing to worry about. We only need to keep an eye on the Xuanmiao Sect. The leader of the Xuanmiao Sect is Wei He, known as the Black Hand Poison Heart. We just need to be careful not to let him discover this ruin and mess up our plans," Zhao Xu said, letting out a breath.
"The Xuanmiao Sect just got lucky to have a Black Mark Kunpeng. As for the rest, they are just ordinary, mediocre fools," Shi Heng said indifferently.
"It's fine if Wei He stays away. But if he dares to come and mess things up, for the sake of the Black Mark Kunpeng, I won't kill him. I'll just break one of his arms and legs as a lesson."
As one of the top three disciples in the Wushi Sect, many full Real World experts in the sect were no match for him, let alone a simple Sense Fixed cultivator from an outside faction.
Moreover, after checking the details later, he found out that Wei He was only a second-stage Sense Fixed cultivator.
And he had only just reached the second stage.
When he learned the truth, a wave of intense shame and anger surged in Shi Heng's heart.
He couldn't believe he had been intimidated by a nobody earlier.
Since when did he, Shi Heng, get scared off by a guy at the second stage of Sense Fixed?
He had always considered that freakish monster from the Jinlian Sect to be his only real opponent.
Who would have thought a mere second-stage Sense Fixed cultivator managed to freeze him in place and make him hesitate to act...
Wuu...
Suddenly, deep within the barren stone forest to the right of the ruin, thick black smoke rolled and spread.
A low beast roar drew closer.
A True Beast resembling a long snake, its head covered with countless tiny red eyes, crawled toward them through true perception.
A ghost wind howled as the beast's body, over thirty meters long, slithered toward Shi Heng.
"A Ghost Eye Dragon...!" Zhao Xu and several other real-world cultivators present turned pale.
Among True Beasts, the Ghost Eye Dragon was one of the most troublesome.
It possessed immense strength, and its scales were far tougher than other beasts of the same tier.
This kind of beast usually slept deep underground and would only awaken and emerge when it sensed an extremely dense build-up of true energy.
The appearance of this Ghost Eye Dragon was clearly drawn by the Shadow Beast ruin here.
"This Ghost Eye Dragon has lived for at least a thousand years! Look at the number of eyes on its head—each eye represents a hundred years!" a Wushi Sect cultivator said, his voice trembling.
Just from his courage, one could tell he was a low-tier cultivator who barely forced his way up using blessed lands.
"An ordinary Ghost Eye Dragon usually takes a full Real World powerhouse to handle completely. Right now..." Zhao Xu instinctively looked at Shi Heng.
He was already calculating his retreat. If Shi Heng couldn't hold it off, he planned to turn and run immediately.
After a hundred years of painful cultivation, he wasn't about to throw his life away for the sect here.
"A bunch of trash!" Shi Heng's usual aggressive temper exploded.
Looking at these cowards made his chest burn with rage.
"Get out of the way!"
He stood up, and an invisible force burst from his body.
The surrounding Wushi Sect cultivators and ordinary fighters were knocked back, scattering to clear the space.
Staring at the rapidly approaching Ghost Eye Dragon, he opened his right hand. Flecks of gray-black energy gathered rapidly in his palm.
The faint phantom of a mountain flashed behind him.
Shi Heng's eyes changed rapidly, covered by a strange layer of yellowish-white color.
"Die!!"
The ground beneath his feet shattered.
Boom!
Boom, boom, boom, boom!
A rapid series of heavy footsteps sounded like muffled thunder.
"Heaven Opens, Earth Shakes!"
Shi Heng grew faster and faster, the mountain phantom flashing behind him repeatedly as more energy gathered in his hands.
Man and snake charged toward each other, colliding violently in the blink of an eye.
Rumble!
Rolling black smoke exploded out, impossible to tell whose energy it belonged to.
Rocks on the ground blasted apart, and a fierce wind swept across the area.
Slowly, the force pulled back and rushed back into Shi Heng's body, revealing the scene.
He was standing with one foot on the giant Ghost Eye Dragon's corpse. The entire upper half of the dragon's body had been crushed by brute force, turned into a bloody, unrecognizable mess.
"Clean it up and keep guarding!"
Shi Heng ordered coldly, glaring at the group of trash around him. If he didn't need people to guard him while he cultivated, he wouldn't even bother talking to these useless fools.
