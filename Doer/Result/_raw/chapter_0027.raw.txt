Answer: Chapter 27: The Place Got Empty
Wei He could see it right away. At least a third of the people in the yard were gone.
"I haven't been paying attention lately. What happened?" He walked Ouyang Zhuang to a corner and started their usual daily practice.
While setting things up, he kept looking around in confusion.
A strange silence and restlessness hung over the people left in the yard.
Ouyang Zhuang looked left and right. When he was sure no one was near, he leaned in and whispered.
"A senior sister told me that most of them are senior brothers who got stuck and couldn't break through."
"Even so, why would they all leave at the same time?" Wei He knew that if you couldn't make a breakthrough after a long time in this yard, you had to find another way to live. After all, you had to pay tuition every month.
Though the fee wasn't huge to them now, the main issue was that most of their time went into training. They had very little time to earn money.
As they grew older, sitting around and draining their savings wouldn't work forever.
So many of them took side jobs outside. Just like Wei He, who worked part-time at the Cheng family's Yonghe Escort Agency.
"I heard the Hong Family Fort outside the city is recruiting soldiers," Ouyang Zhuang said with envy. "The pay and benefits are amazing. If you do well, you can directly become a squad leader or a unit leader."
"They say anyone at squad leader rank gets special medicinal meals that boost vital blood, plus top fighters to guide them face to face."
Wei He understood.
It made sense for those who had been stuck for so long to leave. Twenty was the peak age for a martial artist. Once you passed it, your stamina slowly dropped, and it would be too late.
Speaking of which, he was almost eighteen himself...
"How good can those meals really be? They're still risking their lives," Wei He scolded. Seeing the envy in Ouyang Zhuang's eyes, he quickly urged him to get back to training.
All day, Ouyang Zhuang was restless and couldn't focus at all. Wei He, on the other hand, stayed calm. He followed his routine, practicing moves, rubbing his skin, and resting.
None of this threw off his pace.
Still, after hearing Ouyang Zhuang's words, he was a little curious about how strong the experts in the army really were.
In the evening, near the end of training, Cheng Shaojiu finally showed up late. He had spent the whole day handling his family's escort agency business.
Wei He wanted to ask, but since Cheng Shaojiu didn't mention it, there was a reason for it. Wei He wasn't in a rush.
Now that his vital blood was almost full, his biggest goal was to save up enough resources and make his breakthrough.
Cheng Shaojiu entered the yard and froze when he saw how few people were left. But someone quickly explained the situation to him.
He thought for a moment, then went into the inner room, likely to speak with Old Zheng.
A short while later, he came out and waved at Wei He.
"Little He, are you done?"
"Almost." Wei He nodded. He hung the sweat towel from his neck onto the rack behind him and walked over.
"Brother Cheng, what is it?"
As they got closer, they started calling each other by friendlier names.
"Nothing much. Come with me to the inner city in a bit to relax." Cheng Shaojiu patted Wei He's shoulder, but quickly pulled his hand back in disgust.
His palm was covered in Wei He's sweat.
"The inner city?" Wei He paused. He had never been there.
"Never been there, right? No problem, I'll show you around!" Cheng Shaojiu smiled.
"Okay." Wei He kept it short. He had never gone to the inner city mainly because he heard it was super expensive, and partly because he didn't have the time or energy.
After cleaning up, Wei He gave Ouyang Zhuang a few notes on his training, took a shower, changed clothes, and got into the carriage with Cheng Shaojiu.
The carriage drove all the way toward the inner city entrance.
The inner city and the outer city were two completely different worlds.
High walls separated the inner city, making it a city within a city, totally cut off from the outside.
They passed through the entrance, which was its own small gate. Once inside, Cheng Shaojiu didn't wander around. He took Wei He straight to a three-story restaurant.
It was called the Hundred Flowers Building. The name was as common as it gets, but the style was different.
As Wei He walked in, he saw a young girl standing by every table. They wore white dresses that showed their shoulders and legs, serving wine and food to the guests.
"How is it? Heheh, today I'm showing you something new!" Cheng Shaojiu patted Wei He's shoulder proudly and led him to a seat by the window.
A waiter came over immediately.
This waiter was also different. She was a pretty woman, at least twenty-five, wearing a tight red dress that showed off her curves.
Cheng Shaojiu skillfully ordered a few dishes, then looked at Wei He.
"What do you want? Some wine?"
"No. As martial artists, we shouldn't drink much." Wei He had been busy gathering vital blood lately and was terrified of anything messing up his progress. He didn't dare touch alcohol.
"Fine, no wine then. Bring us a pot of Nine Fortunes Green Tea," Cheng Shaojiu ordered with a wave of his hand.
"Yes, young master. Would you like any girls to serve you?" the woman in red asked with a smile.
"No need, just us two eating," Cheng Shaojiu waved her off.
The woman went downstairs and vanished down the steps. Cheng Shaojiu only looked away after she was completely gone.
He picked up a teacup to wet his dry throat, but realized the tea hadn't arrived yet, so he put it back down.
"Sigh..." He let out a long sigh.
"Why are you sighing, Brother Cheng?" Wei He asked in surprise. He looked around. Girls were serving, tables were full of good food, and everything seemed fine. What was there to worry about?
"Little He... you and I stay in this Feiye City all the time and rarely go out, so we don't know what's going on," Cheng Shaojiu said quietly, frowning.
"I came here tonight and booked a spot early just to see if the famous Tiger Hunter, Hong Daoyuan from Hong Family Fort, is as good as they say."
"Hong Family Fort? Hong Daoyuan?" Wei He was confused. Weren't they here to relax? Why bring up the Hong family all of a sudden?
Cheng Shaojiu saw his confusion and explained further. "Last night, soldiers from Hong Family Fort clashed with the tax team of the Seven Family Alliance. Seven tax collectors died, and one was hurt. The survivor ran back to report to the Ou family of the Seven Family Alliance."
"Now things are tense between them. No one knows how they talked it out, but rumor says Hong Family Fort is sending their strongest young fighter, Tiger Hunter Hong Daoyuan, into the city to meet Lord Ou Chen."
"What does this... have to do with us?" Wei He was speechless.
"You don't get it. The Hong Family Fort has been building a private army for years and getting stronger. Their ambition is out in the open now. Killing the tax team was proof of that."
"But the Seven Family Alliance doesn't dare go to total war either. Out of fear, both sides made compromises. I guess Hong Family Fort is sending Hong Daoyuan into the city to show off their power." Cheng Shaojiu lowered his voice.
"Show off their power?" Wei He felt the problem was getting big.
If it reached the level of a power show, Hong Family Fort might be planning a rebellion at any moment.
"Just wait. According to the time, he should be here soon, maybe fifteen minutes," Cheng Shaojiu said, glancing at a water clock nearby.
The clock was sealed inside a large piece of glass to keep people from touching it. Black stone bowls sat like steps, water dripping slowly from the edge of one bowl to the next.
Wei He had always trained in isolation, so this was the first time he felt genuine curiosity.
Outside Feiye City, there seemed to be a completely different, chaotic world.
Hong Family Fort was a powerful force that kept the peace around Feiye City. And Hong Daoyuan was known as the strongest of the young generation.
Just how strong was he?
Wei He couldn't help but wonder.
"The people from the Three Gangs and Two Factions are here too," Cheng Shaojiu suddenly muttered under his breath.
Wei He froze and followed his gaze.
At the corner of the second floor stairs, groups of people were filing up toward the third floor.
They came wave after wave, wearing matching gang uniforms with gang symbols on them. All of them were men in black, built wide and strong, clearly not people to mess with.
Every black-clad man had a white mountain pattern on his right sleeve.
"Those are Shanchuan Gang members. The leader is at least a Black Mountain level expert," Cheng Shaojiu explained.
"Shanchuan Gang doesn't usually hang around our district. They are split into White Mountain, Black Mountain, and Green Mountain at the top. White Mountain is just regular members. Black Mountain is where you need to be careful. They've broken through their vital blood at least once."
Wei He felt a chill in his heart and nodded repeatedly.
He noticed that in the lead group, three men had the Black Mountain symbol on their sleeves.
Besides the Shanchuan Gang, the rest were smaller gangs. Cheng Shaojiu briefly mentioned them and dropped it.
They were all here just to watch the show, same as them.
This was the first time Wei He had close contact with other gang forces.
He had always kept his head down and trained, ignoring the outside world. This was the closest he had ever been to the Three Gangs and Two Factions.
"Don't worry. None of these gangs dare act up in the inner city. This is Seven Family Alliance territory. Except for Hong Family Fort, no other group dares cause trouble. Do you think the Ou family is afraid to kill?" Cheng Shaojiu said calmly, as if he was used to it.
Wei He understood, but he also grew curious about the true military and noble family forces of this era.
Even outer city people like them could reach the first stage of vital blood by using a tiny bit of blood-boosting herbs.
The children of the Seven Family Alliance in the inner city had far better resources. How far could they go?
And what about the Hong Family Fort, which made even the Seven Family Alliance cautious? How strong were they?
Wei He suddenly felt a strong surge of anticipation.
He started to understand how Cheng Shaojiu felt.
In fact, any martial artist would find it hard to skip a moment like this.
After all, Hong Family Fort usually kept a low profile.
"He's here!" someone nearby suddenly called out in a low voice.
"Junior Sister, look. That's the Tiger of Feiye. He's the strongest young fighter in the whole city!"
Wei He looked over. At some point, a handsome young man and a woman carrying swords had sat down at the table ahead of them.
The man was tall, well-built, and handsome.
The woman wore a white veil, her beautiful eyes shimmering.
They had been sitting there silently, barely noticeable. Only when they stood up to look out the window did Wei He realize they were there.
But Wei He didn't have time to stare at them now. Down below the window, the clashing of weapons and the heavy thud of horse hooves rang out.
