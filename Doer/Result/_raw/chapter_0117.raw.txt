Answer: Chapter 117
Xiao You lost her only backing. The money she spent on bribes was completely gone, and her body had been used for days by those men.
She was sure Wei He was behind this. But just as her hatred reached its peak, a piece of news broke out.
It was a statement from Wei He to clear his name.
The statement read: Regarding the White Snake Gang incident, he was merely a normal Third Blood martial artist. Even if he was strong for his level, it was impossible for him to kill an Entry-Force martial artist surrounded by so many people. He simply lacked the strength to do it.
In fact, while a Third Blood warrior might defeat a weak Entry-Force fighter, almost no one had ever heard of one killing an Entry-Force fighter surrounded by a crowd without letting them escape.
After hearing this, Xiao You checked through multiple sources and reached the exact same conclusion.
She got confused. Could the person who killed her father be someone else?
Her mind was in chaos. Though she kept training in the outer yard, her unstable mind messed up her blood energy. No matter how talented she was, her progress was terrible.
Money flowed out of her hands like water.
Two months passed in the blink of an eye. By the time Xiao You made up her mind to focus on training first and search for the truth later, she made a shocking discovery.
She was broke.
Most of the savings her father left behind had been stolen by a local thief gang one day. The rest of her money was completely wasted on bribes. She didn't even have enough to pay her monthly fee for exotic beast meat.
To earn money, she had no choice but to take on outer yard missions.
Unfortunately, she was a pampered rich girl who had never suffered in her life. The sudden change was too much for her, and things went wrong immediately.
During a mission, an exotic beast badly wounded her. She was barely breathing when a few kind fellow disciples carried her back.
When her younger brother Xiao Li rushed over, all he saw was his sister's pale, sickly, and confused face.
Without money to pay the fee, she couldn't stay in the outer yard much longer anyway.
In desperation, Xiao Li quietly packed up and led his sister away from the Tianyin Sect. They vanished without a trace.
In just two short months, the Xiao siblings went from being willing to give up everything for revenge to losing all their money, suffering severe injuries, and watching all their hopes turn to dust.
"Junior Brother Wei, do you have anything to say about this?" Hu Zicheng stared at Wei He, who was sitting across from him.
On the table between them lay a full report on the movements and recent state of the Xiao siblings.
When Hu Zicheng first learned that Xiao You ended up disappearing, his heart skipped a beat. A cold chill ran down his spine.
He immediately gathered all available intelligence on the brother and sister.
Looking at the series of events that seemed like pure luck, there were simply too many coincidences. The siblings lost everything step by step and fell into the abyss, never even learning the truth.
And Wei He accomplished all of this without even meeting them once.
Hu Zicheng was certain this was Wei He's doing. Now, no one even knew if the siblings were dead or alive.
He couldn't speak for others, but ever since he fought Wei He that night, he had felt the dark, suppressed murderous intent hidden deep within the man.
He didn't believe in that many coincidences. The fate of the Xiao siblings had to be Wei He's doing.
"I feel very sorry for them," Wei He said calmly, pulling his gaze back from the papers. "I told you long ago that I didn't kill Xiao Yurong. Someone else did. By the time I got there, he was already dead."
Do you take me for an idiot?
Hu Zicheng was speechless. He only felt that the man in front of him was fake, crafty, and ruthless enough to pull it off.
"Junior Brother Wei..." He opened his mouth to say something, but didn't know how to word it.
The last time he met a person like this was many years ago.
Back then, Hu Zicheng was still young. He had just reached Third Blood in his early twenties, full of energy and ready to build a great career in martial arts.
Then, over a tiny matter, he offended a senior disciple.
As a result, he suffered bad luck everywhere for no clear reason. He was constantly framed, blamed, and stressed out. The mental pressure ruined his focus, causing him to fail his breakthrough attempts again and again. That was why he ended up stuck as a nobody today.
Seeing a similar type of person again, Hu Zicheng felt terrified, but he also felt a strange impulse.
Staring at the files on the table, Hu Zicheng stayed silent for a moment before a bold idea rushed into his mind.
He bit his lip, braved the sharp, blade-like glare from the other side, and forced himself to speak.
"Junior Brother Wei, I am getting old, and my time to leave the sect is coming soon. I plan to open a boxing hall in a county town outside the city as a small business. I wonder if you might be interested..."
Hu Zicheng blurted out everything in one breath.
He was sure a person like Wei He would achieve great things in the future. So even though he was scared, he saw this as a rare chance. Just like the man who ruined him back then, that person had already risen straight to the top, leaving him far behind.
Facing the same kind of monster again, Hu Zicheng decided to place his bet without hesitation.
"A boxing hall?" Wei He smiled and was about to refuse when he suddenly paused. It seemed he thought of something.
He pondered for a moment. "How is the location?"
"I already bought it. Land in the countryside is cheap. It's about as big as the outer yard, definitely large enough!"
"Can the surrounding area support a boxing hall?"
"I checked, and it won't be a problem. My family supports me there, so we won't have trouble with the government or the local gangs. It's just that I'm returning alone without being a Force Master, so I can't hold down the fort by myself. But if you join me, Junior Brother, it will definitely work!"
Since Wei He had broken through the hardest second level of the Rain Cover Cloud Gathering Technique, reaching Entry-Force was practically guaranteed for him. It was only a matter of time. In fact, all inner disciples who broke through the second level were bound to reach Entry-Force eventually.
So Hu Zicheng was not wrong about that.
"You plan to rely solely on teaching boxing to make a living in the county?" Wei He asked again.
Hu Zicheng froze for a second, then chuckled.
"You might not know this, Junior Brother, but a large number of refugees flooded into our Taizhou recently. Many of them know martial arts, which made the local security worse. So I thought about raising our own people from a young age. That way, when our families need help in the future, we will have extra power to protect them. Otherwise, we won't be able to help them in time when trouble strikes."
His words hit right at what Wei He was worrying about.
Over the past two months, Wei He had been practicing the third level of the technique. His progress was steady and smooth. He estimated that he could reach full mastery in less than a year.
The third level wasn't difficult. It was just slow, hard work. He needed to turn all the Blood Marks across his body into Force without missing a single spot, creating a full protective force field to become a Force Master.
This required fine control, comprehension, and precision rather than innate bone talent, which happened to be Wei He's strong suit.
He calculated that at his current speed, he would reach Entry-Force in half a year at most. That was much faster than other disciples who reached the second level, likely thanks to his above-average comprehension.
In the Wanqing Yard, the only disciples with better comprehension than him were Wan Qingqing and another female disciple.
However, no matter how strong he became, his second sister, Wei Ying, was always his main worry.
Hu Zicheng's idea gave him a great chance. He could train a group of his own people to protect his family and friends.
"Alright. If I have time, I'll go check it out," Wei He agreed after thinking it over.
In chaotic times like this, adopting orphans who could barely survive and raising them to be loyal guards was a good plan.
As his martial arts level got higher, his training cycles would only get longer. His sister was growing older too, and she couldn't live with him forever. She would eventually marry and start her own family.
Hearing him agree, Hu Zicheng was overjoyed. The anxiety on his face vanished completely.
"Then it's a deal!" He quickly went over the details of the boxing hall and what Wei He should keep in mind.
They discussed for a while and both felt the future looked bright. Feeling much more at ease, Hu Zicheng stood up and took his leave.
When he walked out of the tavern, his legs were still a bit weak, but his mind felt solid.
Before coming here, he had researched other information about Wei He, and the more he dug, the more terrified he felt.
Wei He's journey was filled with way too many coincidences and luck.
If he hadn't seen Wei He's true side, he might have believed it. But after their encounter that night, he knew this man could never have made it this far on pure luck.
Behind those apparent strokes of luck were too many hidden motives and calculated moves.
But now, things were fine. He was on the same team as Wei He now, so he didn't need to be afraid anymore.
Thinking of this, Hu Zicheng felt great. Teaming up with the person he feared meant other people would be the ones living in fear from now on!
He laughed internally, an unsuppressible smile spreading across his face as he walked away with big steps.
Wei He came down from the tavern shortly after.
This was Xiongshan Town. They had randomly picked a small two-story tavern for a quick meeting.
The tavern was called the Wanmin Tavern.
The name sounded grand, but the building was run-down and shabby. The owner was a skinny, slow-moving old man, and the few waiters were all old, deaf, and blind. Business was completely dead.
However, the environment was very quiet.
Usually, places like this were full of hidden masters, where weak-looking bosses and waiters turned out to be legendary experts.
But after watching a waiter get slapped by a customer until his mouth bled without fighting back, Wei He was convinced. There weren't that many hidden masters in the world living such miserable lives.
He stood up and helped clear up the conflict. His inner yard disciple uniform put huge pressure on the customer, so the issue was resolved easily.
To show his thanks, the owner gave Wei He a 40% discount, which was the lowest price he could give without losing money.
After thanking the owner, Wei He stepped out of the tavern. Outside, the night fog was thick, and it was getting late.
Next, he needed to focus entirely on breaking through to Entry-Force and stepping into a whole new realm.
Everything was ready. He had plenty of money and meat, and no worries holding him back. The only thing he needed was time.
Wei He sighed, picking up his pace as he headed home without looking back.
Time flew by, and six months passed in a flash.
Half a year was neither too long nor too short.
Wei He focused entirely on his training, ignoring everything else. The six months passed like a single week, flying by in the blink of an eye.
Thanks to his strong control and comprehension built from practicing multiple martial arts, he quickly cleared the hurdles of the third level. Everything fell into place naturally, bringing him right to the final step.
He had turned all the Blood Marks in his body into Force. Now, he only needed one last step: connecting all the Force together using the artistic mindset of the foundational chart.
This single step was what set different martial arts sects and techniques apart.
