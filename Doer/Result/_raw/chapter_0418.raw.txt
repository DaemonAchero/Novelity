'Wei He, you were lucky before, but your luck ends now. Don't blame me... I have no choice.'

Passing the message would let him get rid of his rival, while secretly watching from the shadows to see who was pulling the strings. That way, he could kill two birds with one stone and keep himself safe.

A few days later.
Yuan Duzi and the senior elders returned. The emissaries from the Great Yue Dynasty set off to visit other small nations, and everything seemed peaceful again on the surface.
However, the common folk did not know that this was merely the quiet before the storm. The sea beast tide was about to break.
During this time, the sect assembled a defense force under the command of Cai Menghuan. They boarded a fleet of ships and set sail toward the Twin-Eye Archipelago, the birthplace and closest point to the origin of the tide. The Haining Alliance was also sending its own masters to reinforce the area.
Large double-deck ships slowly left the harbor, loaded with food, fresh water, and battle supplies.
"Starting today, these ships are our mobile homes," Cai Menghuan announced on the main deck. He stood with the leaders of the other two branches, looking out over the endless ocean as they discussed their route. "The islands out there have almost no land to stand on. If we want a safe place to rest, we must stay on the water."
Cai Menghuan was gifted with exceptional talent, but he did not act arrogant. He looked like an ordinary scholar, gentle and polite to everyone. He kept a respectful distance from people, neither too close nor too distant. He came from the Qianliu Branch and led their forces. Wei He was the leader of the Lock Mountain Branch, while the Ghost Head Branch was led by a woman named Hong Chan.
"Brother Cai is so powerful. Please take good care of me during this trip," Hong Chan said with a cute smile. She looked like an innocent girl next door, but nobody believed she was harmless. A woman who could defeat multiple realm masters to become the leader of the Ghost Head Branch was anything but gentle.
Wei He simply nodded to show he understood. He stayed quiet.
He was still getting used to the Star Array he had reclaimed a few days ago. Instead of picking rare weapons, armor, or secret pills from the sect treasure vault, he chose to add a new function to his Star Array. Weapons and pills could not boost his current combat power quickly, and he already had his own poisons and healing formulas.
The best choice was to upgrade his gear directly.
He chose to add a defense function. The Heavy Moon Timber-Wolf Array given to him by Yuan Duzi had great detection abilities, but its defense was weak. Adding a defensive layer fixed his only weakness.
Even though running the defense mode would burn through Star Stones much faster, the upcoming battle against the sea beasts was the perfect place to earn a fortune in Star Stones. Besides, he could turn the defense mode off whenever he wanted.
After finishing their plan, the three leaders returned to their own ships.
Normally, Sect Masters would not join a beast tide defense mission. However, because of the threat posed by the Thousand-Face Demon King, an ancestral master was secretly following the fleet to keep order and prevent total disaster.
Wei He stepped back onto the Lock Mountain ship. The other two top disciples traveling with him were Meng Chunhan and Zhao Yin.
Zhao Yin looked cold and mysterious. White bandages covered his entire body beneath a white Daoist robe, leaving only his sharp eyes visible. Counting the three of them, the Lock Mountain Branch had sent ten people in total.
Once back on board, Wei He did not bother chatting with the rest. He passed the duty of managing the crew to Meng Chunhan and locked himself in his room to meditate.
He needed to focus on his sensory perception training. Now that he had mastered the fourth layer of the Xuan Lock Technique, he needed to let his deep force interact with real world Qi to complete four perception steps. Finishing this early gave a master huge benefits, extending their lifespan and keeping their physical body at its peak for much longer.
The three ships moved slowly away from the sect, cutting through the waves toward the Twin-Eye Archipelago. Because they had to stay alert for sudden attacks by sea beasts hidden beneath the water, the fleet moved at a cautious speed.
