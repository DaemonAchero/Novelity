Five days later.
A thin drizzle fell over the forest.
Dressed in black, Wei He moved like a water dragon across the empty glade. His palms struck, retracted, and vanished in fluid motion as he sparred with the air. At his level, combat forms mattered more than ever. Force was like heavy armor and a sharp weapon, but actually hitting a target required form. Against an opponent at the same level with similar force traits, skill was the tiebreaker. Even if your force was weaker, landing five hits a minute meant winning.
Wind howled around his palms. Wei He bounded back and forth, shifting speed, as white steam rose from his skin. The heat and circulating force from his rushing blood turned the rainwater into vapor as fast as it hit him.
He drove both fists forward, pulled back, and finished the routine with the Heart-Breaker Seal.
He exhaled a long breath. White steam pierced the rain like a sword.
"Finally mastered," Wei He muttered, feeling the Rain-Covering Force surging within him.
Ever since he reached the third layer of the Rain-Covering Force, he had been working on the fourth. The hardest part of this stage was stacking enough force; the bottleneck itself was small. Normally, he would have tried to break through on his own. But he was out of time.
Ever since he stumbled upon other branches of the Heavenly Seal Sect teaming up with the Kaori Cult to traffic weapons, he felt a bad storm coming. The sect was doomed. He had no idea what the Sect Master and Deputy Masters were thinking when they allowed it—maybe the money was just too good to refuse. But if it ever got out, the sect was done.
To make matters worse, things inside were rotting fast. Friction between the branches was escalating, and Sect Master Shangguan, along with his two deputies, turned a blind eye.
Wei He had two choices.
One: stay, climb fast, grab power, and protect himself.
Two: pack up and flee the coming mess.
He chose the first. Taizhou was peaceful, the best place to settle down. Even though the Great Yuan Empire was vast, war was breaking out everywhere else. There was no safer place than Taizhou right now.

It was the top catalyst mentioned in the Rain-Covering Cloud Manual. It infused his force with a biting coldness that would slowly freeze and slow down an opponent during a long fight, leaving them wide open.
As the scalding energy surged through him, emptying the bead, Wei He felt his skin, muscle, and membrane vibrating, slowly changing at the core.
Time slipped away.
