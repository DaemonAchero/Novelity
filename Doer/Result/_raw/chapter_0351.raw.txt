“Forget it, Sister Ling. You should go back.”

Clearly, she was the very last person with the Linyin Bloodline in the Wan family. If he let her go, and the clan's bloodline ended in the future, the Wan family would truly sink into a completely ordinary family.

Wan Ling understood completely and went quiet as well.

“Alright, say no more. Go grab some food and water in a bit and head back quickly. Even if our Wan family has fallen this low, we don’t need to hold back a single Bone Forge martial artist!” Wan Xuetian said with a forced smile.
Wan Ling wanted to speak, but stopped.

around Central Province, the Wan family had plenty of enemies, and the Xue family was one of them. This time, the leader of the Wan family hadn't just screwed over his own weak clan members, he had also screwed over the Xue family. The Xue family suffered many deaths and injuries because of it.

“Let’s go!”
Without looking back again, she carried the two kids and rushed straight into the deep forest.
Suddenly, loud, sharp shouts tore through the air behind them.
“Surround them all!! Don’t let a single one get away!”

“Some of them are splitting up to run! Chase them down!!”
Wan Ling’s back instantly went cold and numb. She knew someone had locked onto her. Without saying a single word, she pushed her speed to the absolute limit and ran wildly into the deep woods.
