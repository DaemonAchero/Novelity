A piercing howl tore through the mountain forest, shaking the trees around the edge of Water-Gathering Valley.
In the fading mist, Zhu Chen flipped himself off the ground and scrambled toward the exit. It was not a charge anymore. It was a desperate escape.
His legs were badly crippled from a surprise attack. If he had not been holding a god-tier bone blade, he would have died inside the belly of the fox's tail long ago. Even so, escaping the three fox tails had cost him dearly. He had used his strongest secret technique three times in a row, not to fight back, but to get out of the valley.
His weapon was heavily corroded. Even as a Sense-Inscription master, triggering his secret technique three times while bleeding out put him at death's door.
Thump! Thump! Thump!
Heavy footsteps stopped behind him.
Deep inside the thick fog, the long necks of the three-headed fox danced in the air. It stood at the valley's edge and roared violently at Zhu Chen, but it did not cross the line.
From above, the small patch of ground between Zhu Chen and the beast aligned with the stone tablet at the valley entrance. It was a boundary, a restriction the three-headed fox could not break.
Zhu Chen sat on the ground, most of the flesh on his legs stripped down to white bone. His face was pale as a corpse. He was running on empty. Without immediate medical care, he would die here.
He looked back at the roaring beast that stayed behind the line and finally let out a breath.
"I was right... That thing can't leave the valley. Out of all places in Taizhou, I had to run into a mutated Sense-Inscription beast."
He knew he had won his gamble. The beast was bound by territory.
Breathing easier, he reached into the pouch behind his back, pulled out a small vial, and swallowed a medicinal pill. It was a top-tier imperial medicine for severe injuries. For the next two hours, as long as his head was intact, his heart was beating, and he did not bleed out, his condition would stabilize.
"Just wait," Zhu Chen growled under his breath. "Next time I come back, you're dead."
It had been years since he last felt this kind of despair. Today, he tasted death again.
Gathering his inner force into his legs to stop the bleeding, Zhu Chen forced himself up. He needed to get back to Xuanjing City immediately, mobilize his men, and call the Taian Army for backup.
The Sect Master of Thousand Poisons had lured him here to fight the monster, hoping they would destroy each other. He was probably waiting nearby right now.
Calculating his odds, Zhu Chen grit his teeth and ran in a different direction, steering clear of Xuanjing City. He knew Wei He would be waiting outside the valley to intercept him. Of course, Wei He might have rushed back to save the rest of the Thousand Poisons Sect instead, which would buy him time.
After running only a dozen meters, Zhu Chen froze.
His eyes locked onto a figure standing on his left. Wei He had been waiting for a while, staring at him with a cold, blank face.
"Lord Zhu," Wei He said. "Look at you."
Zhu Chen stayed silent. He knew he could not leave without getting through Wei He first.
"Thanks to you, I'm slightly hurt. But taking down a traitor like you is still easy."
His voice was raspy and broken, his throat damaged from the fight with the beast.
"That's a shame," Wei He said, looking at the bloody man. "A master like Lord Zhu is reduced to making jokes. My Myriad Green Sect fell to this state because of you. Tell me, how should I thank you?"
From the beginning, Zhu Chen had assigned a suicide mission to his sect, intending to sacrifice all of them as scapegoats.
Wei He stared at the battered expert before him.
"Any last words?" Wei He asked softly.
Zhu Chen had to die.
Because of him, fewer than forty people remained in the Myriad Green Sect. Their base was destroyed, and they were forced to live in the shadows forever unless the Great Yuan Empire collapsed.
The murderous intent in Wei He's heart grew razor-sharp.
"Do you regret it?" Wei He asked in a deep voice. "Regret putting my sect in that position?"
Zhu Chen laughed. "Regret? My only regret is that I didn't hunt you down alone before going after your sect. I underestimated you, kid."
His eyes were cold, devoid of remorse.
"Wei He, if it wasn't for this mutated beast, you and your sect would already be dead by my hands. Cough! Cough!"
He coughed violently and spat out a mouthful of blood.
"Go on," Wei He said softly. "Keep talking. When you're injured, you should talk more to keep the blood flowing."
He took a slow step forward.
While talking, he had used up the last of his Martial Master Poison, spreading it into the air. Fearing Zhu Chen might still have enough strength to turn the tables, he had mixed in a heavy dose of blood-activating medical herbs.
Any physician knew that when a body had open wounds, you had to wait for the bleeding to stop completely before taking blood-activating medicine. Even with Zhu Chen's strong body and inner force blocking his arteries, he could not stop every internal bleed in a few seconds.
On the surface, his legs seemed fine, but internally, blood was still leaking into his body.
The blood-activating medicine was not a poison, but right now, it was far deadlier than any venom. It bypassed all anti-poison artifacts and techniques because it was pure medicine.
It worked.
Zhu Chen looked shocked. He was wearing high-grade anti-poison charms, which should have rendered even Martial Master Poison useless. Why was he collapsing so fast?
He felt his lungs filling with fluid and blood. His heart sank as he glared at Wei He.
Even his anti-poison beads and pills failed to stop this. The name of the Thousand Poisons Sect Master was well earned.
"Thinking you were poisoned?" Wei He smiled. "Too bad your anti-poison gear was too strong. My poison didn't work on you. But luckily, another drug did. Want to guess what's making you bleed from the inside?"
Wei He closed the distance step by step.
"You..." Zhu Chen tried to speak, but only a gurgling sound came out of his throat.
Suddenly, his vision blurred from the massive blood loss. Instinctively, he threw himself into a side roll.
Thud! Thud! Thud!
Three silver throwing knives slammed into the tree trunk behind him, their blades coated in dark blue poison.
"As expected of a Sense-Inscription master," Wei He's voice drifted from all directions, impossible to pin down. "To react like that in your state... impressive."
Zhu Chen's vision cleared slightly. Wei He was gone from his line of sight.
Without hesitation, Zhu Chen leaped toward the trees, retreating toward Xuanjing City. Even with ruined legs, his powerful arms allowed him to swing through the tree branches at high speed.
However, he was already slower than Wei He when healthy. Now, he stood no chance.
As soon as he leaped into the air, a sharp whistle pierced the forest.
A hail of rocks slammed into his back like crossbow bolts. Normally, he could dodge them easily or shatter them with his inner force defense, even if they were packed with bone-force.
But now, his eyes went dark, and another mouthful of blood burst from his lips.
His lungs had failed. He was holding his breath, but even a master could not last long without air. He needed to break away from Wei He, hide, and bleed out the bad blood to heal.
Wei He gave him no chance.
After spitting blood, Zhu Chen grabbed a branch and swung forward again.
More fist-sized rocks blasted from behind. He dodged a few, but had to tank the rest.
Fortunately, his Sense-Inscription trait was skin touch, giving him enhanced sensation and a layer of skin defense like an armor technique. Taking a few dozen hits like this would not stop him.
As long as he reached Xuanjing City, he could force the three major families to help him push Wei He back. If they wanted to keep their standing in the city, they had no choice. That was his only chance to live.
Zhu Chen said nothing. He was ruthless to others, but even more ruthless to himself. He used his arms like an ape, swinging wildly through the trees.
Another spray of sharp stones flew at his back. To save energy, he chose to take the hit directly.
Pfft!
A hidden throwing knife mixed in the rubble pierced deep into his left back.
The sudden pain snapped his dizzy mind awake, forcing him to push his speed to the limit.
"Wei He!" Zhu Chen roared. "You think you've won? Sun-Day Willow-Night has more than one Sense-Inscription master! If I die here, you and your whole sect won't make it out of Taizhou alive!"
"Don't worry about us," Wei He said calmly.
Another wave of heavy stones shot toward him.
Zhu Chen glanced back, confirmed there were no hidden blades this time, and rolled in mid-air. He pulled the knife from his back and threw it behind him.
The knife collided with the stones, exploding with inner force and knocking them off course.
Evading the strike, Zhu Chen sprinted again. But after a few steps, he felt a crushing wind behind him.
He looked back.
A massive boulder the size of a millstone was spinning through the air, crashing straight toward his back.
