Located in a quiet southern corner of Xuanjing City, few people knew what Listening Bamboo Cottage was actually for. It was not open to the public, nor did it allow outsiders to spy inside.
Inside this small bamboo forest within the city stood a neat two-story green bamboo house. Heavy security guarded the perimeter.
On the second floor, new rich merchants from around the city gathered for the Purple Bamboo Meeting, hosted by the Heavenly Seal Sect. As the largest power in the region, the sect held this event for one reason: to let their inner court disciples find wealthy sponsors.

"The choices for this meeting are set," she said. "We have eight inner court experts from the nine branch courts. Their wooden name tags are right before you. If you want to back someone, hand their tag to the servant."
