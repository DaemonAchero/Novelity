It was unreal. The Incense Sect was surviving against the Seven-Alliance of the Inner City for so long. They even kept producing talented fighters.
Wei He couldn't help but think of the Hong Clan Fort, which had been quiet all this time. He suspected the Hong Clan Fort was secretly backing the Incense Sect. Otherwise, where did the Incense Sect get so much food when they didn't farm or work?
After resting in the yard, Wei He ate more beast meat. He compared the bear meat from the Incense Sect with the meat from the Shaoyang Sect, using the test methods he got from Guan Ye.
He tested the meat on rats first to make sure there was no poison. Only then did he start eating it over the last few days. Still, this small amount of meat wouldn't last him long.
Holding the dried meat, Wei He bit it off in a few mouthfuls, chewed it down, and washed it down with water. His strong stomach soon felt warm and comfortable.
After finishing his portion, Wei He stood up and went into his room. He expertly pulled a bundle from a hidden spot under the wardrobe.
It was wrapped in brown cloth, covered in light yellow powder that gave off a strong smell.
"It has been two months, sixty days. That is one full cycle for the breaking powder. The poison on this should be gone."
Wei He estimated the time, put on gloves carefully, and put the bundle into a wooden tub he prepared.
This was his loot from Chen Jun, the Broken-Spear.
Chen Jun was covered in poison. When Wei He searched the body, he saw two poisonous dart wounds that smelled rotten before they even fought. The back of the body had another sharp smell, likely a third poison. Plus the mixed poison powder Wei He threw during the fight, Chen Jun was basically a walking poison bag!
So Wei He had quickly picked out the items with gloves, sealed them in a leather bag, and rinsed them with rain on his way back.
At home, he followed a simple method Guan Ye taught him. He crushed the bark of the Iron-Gray Tree into powder and rubbed it inside and outside the bag. The powder slowly broke down most toxins and made them harmless over time.
Wei He shook everything out of the bundle. He took the tub out to the river and washed the items over and over. Only then did he return to his room to inspect his haul.
He used chopsticks to pick up the items one by one, setting them on the floor.
A black money bag with silver edges, a ledger book, a dark green jade piece, a group of small porcelain bottles wrapped in wood, and three faded gas masks.
Wei He picked up the masks first.
Chen Jun had clearly prepared these to fight the Guan family, but some poison had destroyed them. The parts over the mouth and nose turned from white to pale green with gray edges. Blue spots covered the rest.
The poison spots were different on each mask. It showed how brutal the battle was before Chen Jun pushed the three Guan members to the edge.
Wei He sighed and threw the masks aside. Next were the bottles.
These wooden-wrapped porcelain bottles were labeled: Snake Poison Pill, Spider Poison Pill, Grass Poison Pill, Poison-Calming Pill, and more.
There were about ten bottles, but most were empty. Some had no stoppers left. One could imagine how desperate the fight was. Chen Jun probably threw the stoppers away and poured the pills into his mouth.
Wei He shook his head and threw the bottles aside. He checked the rest: the jade, the ledger, and the money bag.
The jade felt cool and valuable, but it was useless in these times. It was not as good as a piece of beast meat. He set it aside and picked up the ledger.
He flipped it open, but the pages were soaked and ruined. Nothing could be read. He threw it away.
Finally, the money bag.
This was the main reward. Wei He calmed his mind. The savings of a Third Blood-Refinement master should not disappoint him.
Using chopsticks and a small knife, he undid the string and poured out the contents.
Clank.
A few small gold notes fell out, along with some thin gold leaves, and a pink clear stone that looked like amber or crystal.
Wei He looked at the gold notes. Their value was judged by size and thickness, and damaged ones lost value. He didn't need to read the numbers to guess their worth.
Passing over the gold notes, he focused on the pink stone. He picked it up with chopsticks to look closer. It was the size of a quail egg, shaped unevenly, with cloud-like patterns inside. It looked very nice.
"If a Third Blood master kept this with his gold notes in a money bag, it must be some kind of rare currency. I will keep it for now."
Wei He knew he lacked high-level knowledge, so he did not rush to sell it.
Cleaning up the leftover trash, Wei He felt a bit disappointed. He had waited so long, but got so little from Chen Jun's body.
When everything was done, Wei He sat cross-legged on his bed to adjust his blood energy. He recalled the foundation chart of the Mountain-Returning Fist. Lately, he felt he understood the chart in a new way.
After reaching Third Blood in the Five-Ridge Palm, he understood the force of Third Blood much better. Day by day, he began to see how this force was born and how it moved.
Looking back at the Mountain-Returning Fist chart, he had a strange realization.
He came from a modern world, so he had broader vision and richer imagination than people here. Comparing his Third Blood force with the current blood flow of the Mountain-Returning Fist, Wei He felt he might break through without using the Skill-Breaking Pearl.
"The Pearl should be saved for bigger moments, like pushing Five-Ridge Palm higher!" Wei He felt this strongly.
Over the past days, he kept studying the chart and felt he had grasped the core of the Mountain-Returning Fist.
The foundation chart was not about mountains or rivers. It was about the flow of blood energy.
Wei He thought it over, then stood up to practice the fist moves in his room. His blood energy filled his fists, turning them light gray.
As he practiced, the scenes from the chart turned into pictures of his own blood flow in his mind.
Where to speed up, where to slow down, where to be gentle, where to push hard. The blood energy moved, split, and looped, going faster and faster under his new understanding.
Wei He focused all his mind on controlling the blood energy in his body. He felt that if he drew the full path of this blood flow, it would form a picture just like the foundation chart.
Time passed.
Suddenly, a loud crash sounded in his head like thunder.
BOOM!
His body shook. All his blood energy rushed into his fists like twin tidal waves, unstoppable and wild.
Pfft!
A quiet pop sounded, as if something broke inside.
Wei He felt a sharp pain in his hands. Then the blood energy flowed back, twice as fast as before. The fast blood flow raised his heat quickly, making his skin burning hot.
A deep feeling of relief spread through his whole body.
He raised his hands and saw his fists had turned dark gray.
He knew the Mountain-Returning Fist had reached Third Blood!
"With Third Blood force as my base, and knowing how force works, my starting point is higher than most. My talent might be plain, but my mind is not weak!"
Wei He stood straight. He felt two different types of altered blood energy inside his body, separate yet balanced.
The blood energy of the Five-Ridge Palm was quiet.
The blood energy of the Mountain-Returning Fist was fierce.
One quiet, one fierce, they filled each other's gaps and made him feel stronger. He could feel his total blood energy growing fast after this second breakthrough.
"Too bad I have no other martial arts near a breakthrough limit. Otherwise, I could push further and feel the key to the next stage." Wei He sighed.
He opened his hands. Under his blood energy, both martial arts generated forces that pressed on his arm bones like grinding stones. The process gave him a strange itchy feeling.
"Like Master Zheng said, every breakthrough needs time to rest so the body can adapt. Since I don't need to spend blood energy on the Mountain-Returning Fist now, I can focus on the Flying Dragon Art."
The Five-Ridge Palm showed no sign of reaching its fifth stage limit yet. The Mountain-Returning Fist just broke through, so it was far from its limit too. These two took too long. By the time they were ready, the Pearl might fill up twice.
The Flying Dragon Art was the closest option.
"Good. I can test how the Pearl works when I use it on a lower-level art after growing stronger. Will it consume all progress at once like before, or only use a part of it?"
Wei He still did not fully understand his main trump card, the Skill-Breaking Pearl. It built up slowly and was too precious to waste on tests.
After his breakthrough, Wei He felt the blood flow of the Mountain-Returning Fist. Its movement was much bigger than the Five-Ridge Palm.
"The Mountain-Returning Fist is only mid-tier in Feiye City, yet it is so much stronger than the Five-Ridge Palm. I wonder how many grand martial arts exist in this world."
Wei He felt a spark of hope inside.
Third Blood added twenty years to a person's life. What about stronger arts? What about higher realms? Maybe one day, long life was possible.
He walked into the yard and practiced his moves slowly to speed up his blood flow and shape his body.
He stayed home for three days straight. Only when he gained full control over his blood energy and felt his body adaptation slow down did he change clothes and head to the martial school.
