Wen Lian lightly hopped onto the carriage.
"Go, go, go! Hurry up! We're about to be late!" she urged her driver.
"Young Lady, the road ahead is blocked. We have to take a detour for now," replied the honest female driver.
"Huh? Blocked? Why?" Wen Lian was confused. "Isn't this Mount Qi Road always empty?"
"It seems like a few small gangs nearby fought for the chief position, causing a lot of deaths and injuries. Now the government officers are clearing the corpses," a guard outside the carriage said, enjoying the drama.
"Fighting for chief? Their leader died?" Wen Lian froze.
"Yeah, I heard four of them died in a single day yesterday, all beaten to death. Some say the killer was an inner yard master from the Heavenly Seal Sect."
That guard loved to brag and chat with his brothers after work, so he was always well informed about this gossip.
"In a single day?" Wen Lian was stunned. She suddenly remembered yesterday when Wei He pointed at the map and asked questions.
"Among those dead gang leaders, are there the White Snake Gang and the Black Water Gang!?" she asked in a hurry.
"Yeah. Those two gang chiefs were said to be Force Master experts who dominated this area for over ten years. Who would have thought they would drop dead so suddenly," the guard sighed.
"I heard about this too. Some said a single person from the Heavenly Seal Sect's inner yard did it alone, but how could that be possible? The White Snake Gang and Black Water Gang had so many people, with so many Blood Three leaders. Even if one or two couldn't win, a group attack would beat him to death.
Others said they were drugged and knocked out. That's even more impossible. So many people, knocked out at the same time, without any reaction at all? Too fake.
How high must one's poison skills be to do that?
In my opinion, the most popular rumor now is that insiders from these two gangs betrayed the two chiefs to steal power. As for the news about the Heavenly Seal Sect inner yard, it's all fake news leaked on purpose."
,The guards spoke with great certainty, but Wen Lian fell into silence and doubt.
Even now, she could clearly recall the way Wei He pointed at the map yesterday, asking for the names of those small gangs.
"Could it really be him? But that makes no sense? He's just a Blood Three martial artist, while those were two Force Masters... and there were so many gang members..." Wen Lian's family was very rich, and her father was one of the undisputed big merchants in Xuanjing City. So she was not completely ignorant about martial arts.
'If it's really him...' Wen Lian suddenly felt shaken inside.
'Impossible...! Absolutely impossible! He's not a god, how could he take on so many people alone. It must be a coincidence. He just got lucky... pure luck.'
No matter how Wen Lian thought about it, it felt impossible.
"Lianzi!" Suddenly, Lin Xiaoxiao's voice came from the side of the carriage.
Wen Lian's thoughts were interrupted, and she looked toward the carriage door. She saw Lin Xiaoxiao handing over a letter.
"Look," Lin Xiaoxiao said seriously.
Wen Lian looked at the letter, then looked at her best friend's serious face.
She reached out, took it, and slowly opened it.

Rustle.
Wei He softly flipped a page, reading the book in his hand.
A light cold wind blew in through the window on the first floor of Tianya Building, scattering a few fine ink orchid petals onto the floor.
Outside the window, the thin tree branches swayed slowly, their green buds just showing their tips.
The cleaner quickly came up with a broom and wooden shovel, sweeping away the dark red orchid petals.
A moment later, Wei He put down the book.
This was the fifth journal he had read, recording the life of a self taught martial artist.
This man was a Great Force Master at the absolute peak of Force Training, very strong, but because he couldn't resist learning other arts when he was young, he lost the chance to go higher despite his strength.
Many things recorded in this man's biography were very helpful to Wei He.
Now the matter at Rushui Market was resolved.
When Wei He left, he couldn't help but wipe out two small gangs on the way. He found out they had also taken money to target Rushui Market, so he killed their leaders.
Sadly, the gain from those two small gangs wasn't big. Only two thousand gold notes.
Combined, he earned a total of ten thousand gold notes.
However, this kind of action that broke order and stability could only be done once due to the Rushui Market incident.
He couldn't do it again later.
As soon as Wei He returned, he sent five thousand gold notes to Hall Head Wan Ling and told her about the matter.
Because a senior teacher had to step up to cover for him, he had to give up something to gain something.
To avoid trouble, Wei He decisively gave away half his earnings.
To his surprise, the gold notes were returned by Wan Ling without taking a single coin. She also told him to stay away from such shady moves and focus on training. She would handle the rest.
Wei He still remembered her final words clearly.
'Don't bother me with such small matters in the future. Neither the Zhou family nor my Heavenly Seal Sect cares. Just focus on your training.'
The deaths of two Force Masters from the White Snake Gang and Black Water Gang were just small, unimportant matters to the Heavenly Seal Sect and the Zhou family.
Only through these words did Wei He truly feel the benefits and dominance of being part of a huge sect.
"The Heavenly Seal Sect is only the last of the five big powers, yet it has such overbearing style. No wonder the Wushi Sect had that attitude back then," Wei He realized in his heart.
Of course, understanding was one thing, but holding a grudge was another. They were separate things.
He stood up and put the book back where it was.
He went to the stairs and walked up toward the second floor, step by step.
Walking up the wooden steps, Wei He noticed fine words carved on both edges of the staircase.
Words like 'Calm', 'Ask', 'Trust', 'Guard', and 'Peace'.
He walked up tens of steps to the second floor, and every single step had a different word.
All of them were carved in wild, dragon-like shapes.
"Those were warning words carved by the third generation Master of our sect. They warn all disciples who enter the second floor of Tianya Building to watch themselves, control their desires, and hold onto their inner heart when facing powerful martial arts, so they can walk further."
To the right of the second floor entrance, at a wooden desk with a reclining chair, lay a thin old man with white hair.
The old man wore a large black robe and held a pipe in his hand. He hadn't lit it, just keeping it in his mouth to take fake puffs.
"Greetings, Senior," Wei He cupped his fists.
"Mm, go on. To anyone who notices the steps, I'll explain a sentence depending on my mood. You're no genius, you have no special encounters, and I'm no legendary master. Alright, read fewer storybooks and go," the old man pointed to the somewhat empty second floor.
Wei He didn't know whether to laugh or cry. He nodded seriously, gathered his thoughts, and walked into the second floor hall.
The hall had the same design as the first floor, filled with concentric circles of bookshelves.
However, there were only five rings of bookshelves here.
The shelves were packed with various yellowed books. On the sides of the shelves hung head-sized wooden signs showing their types.
'Offense', 'Defense', 'Speed', 'Nourish', 'Incomplete'.
Without hesitation, Wei He walked straight toward the Defense section.
Stacking as much Force defense as possible to keep himself safe was his core goal.
Looking across the dense books on the shelves, every single one was a martial arts manual. Wei He scanned them with a glance.
'Dao Lotus Body', 'Golden Light Arm', 'Three-Method Hardening', 'Clean Body', 'Iron Lock Art', 'Stone Lock Art', 'Copper Wall Art'...
He picked up Clean Body at random and flipped through it. They were all Force Training martial arts, but they only went up to Force Training at most.
The concept and design of this art only reached the level of Force Training.
Concept meant the creator believed the human body could only reach this far, so everything ended at what he thought was the finish line.
There was nothing after that.
The Force trained from Clean Body was called Clean Force. It focused on purity. After entering Force Training, it protected the whole body like a thick coat of cotton, soft and tough, hard to pierce, and extremely good against blunt hits and similar force.
Wei He put down Clean Body and picked up Pure Yang Art. This one relied on training Pure Yang Force. After entering Force Training, the Force across the body became extremely hard, giving strong defense and counter shocks, but it was hard to master. It required staying a virgin, and it made one's temper wild, requiring mind methods to stay stable.
Wei He picked through them one by one, treating them like precious treasure.
These arts were different from the ones he had pieced together before.
All of these were complete Force Training martial arts. They included full sets of content, from basics, body charts, and foundation diagrams, to Force Training mind methods.
Every manual was the fruit of human wisdom, holding years of effort.
None of the arts Wei He trained, whether Flying Dragon Art, Five Ridge Palm, or Mountain Return Fist, could go into Force Training anymore.
So these extra arts became his hope right now.
'Total human Blood Energy is limited. In theory, training can quickly boost the total amount, but time is limited, and the speed of Blood Energy growth has a cap.'
Wei He analyzed it in his mind.
'A person can train five extra arts at most. Dividing Blood Energy into five parts will still leave enough for Stamp Blood. Otherwise, if you train too much, Stamp Blood will be weak, and even if you reach Force Training, your power will be weak.'
'So the core goal is to increase total Blood Energy first. The total Blood Energy of the whole body.'
Looking at it from this angle, the choices became clear.
Very few martial arts could increase total Blood Energy.
Wei He searched quickly and soon pulled out a manual.
On the cover, written in messy handwriting, were three words: Whale Flood Art.
Wei He flipped through four bookshelves quickly. Among them, only twelve arts could increase total Blood Energy.
And out of those twelve, this one suited him best.
Its trait was: the training process was simple and easy, but breaking through bottlenecks was extremely hard and dangerous, requiring the flesh and organs of different rare beasts as catalysts.
This art had five levels in total. Once mastered, each level doubled the total Blood Energy from before.
In the end, at full mastery in Force Training, the body's total Blood Energy would increase to thirty-two times its starting amount, massive and heavy like a giant whale and wild floods, making every move hold massive power.
Yet while this art was strong, nobody could train it because many of the catalysts, the flesh and organs of different rare beasts, could no longer be found.
Those required beasts had been extinct for many years, leaving no trace. People who trained the Whale Flood Art couldn't even finish the first level, so it was left here to gather dust for years.
