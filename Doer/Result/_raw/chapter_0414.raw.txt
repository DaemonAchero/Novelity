So what if he could fight? What good was combat power if your cultivation topped out at the lower tier of the True Reality Realm?

Anyone who didn't reach the upper tier of the True Reality Realm would constantly face the dark reality of corruption. It would only get stronger and stronger over time.

Everyone held a different view.

People questioned whether he was actually qualified to receive such high treatment.
Wei He spent every day in isolation, completely unaware of this noise. Yet, the whole sect was already boiling over it.

Senior Baihezi took the cultivators from her branch and whispered instructions through sound transmissions.

Her raw power was extremely formidable. If Yuanduzi hadn't crushed everyone with absolute dominance back then, Gong Yu would have taken the Chief position years ago.

Wei He followed orders and headed over to the Suoshan branch array camp.
Since he didn't know anyone, he could only stand at the very back of the group, quietly listening to Gong Yu's sound transmission up front.
The structure of the Xuanmiao Sect was loose by nature, and most cultivators rarely faced strict rules.
So when the surprise attack hit, many were flustered, not knowing where they were supposed to stand.
With the Grandmasters absent this time, everyone panicked during the chaos, leading to the mess that had just unfolded.

Unlike Yuanduzi's relaxed and effortless style, Gong Yu had a cold and strict tone, even though she possessed a stunningly attractive face.
