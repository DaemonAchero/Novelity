Chapter 49: The Fall of Yonghe
After asking about the agency, Wei He went to visit Cheng Zhengxing, who was bedridden with illness.
Unfortunately, Wei He couldn't help with their current troubles.
Cheng Zhengxing was a veteran martial artist who had reached the Third Bloodline level. Though old, his body was strong and his skills were real. If even a man like him was brought down by this crisis, everything now rested on young Cheng Shaojiu.
Wei He had asked Cheng Shaojiu multiple times, but the answer was always the same: No problem.
It seemed Cheng Shaojiu had things under control.
Relieved, Wei He put the matter aside and returned to his routine of hunting in the mountains.
He had paused his progress on the Nine Cloud Flowers technique. His Five Ridge Palm was already mastered, so all he needed now was to fill his Breakthrough Bead again.
As he expected, stopping all bloodline-draining exercises allowed his bloodline energy to build up twice as fast as usual. He could clearly feel a steady stream of energy flowing into the bead, filling it up.
Two days later.
Wei He walked down the mountain empty-handed. He hadn't seen a single animal or bandit. It was pure bad luck.
Usually, if game was scarce, he could at least rob a few bandits for food. Today, there was nothing.
He rested briefly at the Natural Abode before walking over to a local drinking house called Guest House.
The name sounded ordinary, but the place wasn't. It was technically a tea house, but poor people often came here to put price tags on themselves, hoping someone would buy them to save them from starvation.
In times like these, though, people barely had enough to feed themselves. Nobody wanted an extra mouth to feed.
Wei He pushed open the cloth curtain and immediately noticed a long-haired girl sitting in the corner.
She sat on the cold floor with red, swollen eyes. Her gray patched dress was rough, but it couldn't hide her smooth skin and delicate features.
The usual customers were gathered around, staring at her intently instead of ignoring her like they normally did. Yet nobody offered to buy her. The air was tense and quiet.
Wei He sat at an empty table and ordered a pot of tea.
"What's with that girl?" he asked the tea girl serving him.
"A tragic story," the girl sighed.
"Tell me."
The server looked at Wei He carefully. Seeing he might actually have the money to buy her, she lowered her voice and explained.
"Her name is Shangguan Lin. She comes from a family of scholars. Her grandfather was once a government official, but the family fell on hard times. Her parents offended a manager from the Blood Cloth Gang, and now the whole family is destroyed. She has no choice but to sell herself to survive."
She sighed again. "Nobody dares to buy her because they're afraid of that gang manager. Anyone who becomes a manager in one of the Three Major Gangs is strong and has plenty of men. Plus, the Blood Cloth Gang is brutal..."
She stopped there, but the message was clear. Men wanted the pretty girl, but nobody wanted to cross the gang.
Wei He understood.
He had met a manager from the Blood Cloth Gang before, back when the local factions tried to recruit him. That manager was only at the Second Bloodline level, just like Wei He was now.
He was surprised that a mere manager held so much terror in the outer city.
He thought about the core disciples at his own martial arts school. Compared to them, it seemed he had actually become a figure of some importance without realizing it.
"If you want to buy her, sir, be careful," the tea girl warned kindly. "Even the local Copper Mountain Gang and Green Wolf Gang don't dare cross the Blood Cloth Gang."
"I see. Thanks," Wei He smiled.
Copper Mountain Gang? Green Wolf Gang? Those tiny groups only had a dozen ordinary men. Did they even count? Their bosses were just tough guys who knew basic fist techniques and reached First Bloodline through raw luck and violence. Such small gangs appeared and vanished all the time in these dark times, popping up like weeds.
While the girl was attractive, Wei He had no interest in buying her.
He wasn't looking for a fragile girl. He wanted a female fighter who could protect his older sister. Finding someone like that was hard. He planned to hire one of his senior or junior martial sisters from the school once the time was right.
A shadow flickered in his mind.
Not the right time yet, Wei He thought, sipping his tea.
His main goal here was to gather information on mutated beasts. Having hunted in the mountains for a long time, he had enough experience to target one.
The Black Madman beast he encountered during that night on the bridge still haunted his memory. To avoid dying, he needed absolute preparation and information before making a move.
He waited a little longer, but no news about beasts came up. He finished his tea and stepped out of the house.
Right outside, he bumped into Ouyang Zhuang, the newcomer he was training, walking by with a servant.
