The test that happened every two months was about to start again.
The four people in Wei He's yard put on their robes. They walked toward another part of the outer mountain: Dening Hall.
Dening Hall sat on the other side of Dragon Bay, right across from Daode Palace.
All around the hall were buildings used for tests. There were also offices for managers and the guards who enforced the law of the sect.
Usually, nobody liked to go to the other side of Dragon Bay. But everyone had to go when it was time for the test.
Wei He packed his things. He left Daode Palace with Liang Huyao, Zhou Muqing, and Wang Lu. They walked down the mountain path toward Dragon Bay.
Many other people were going to the test too. More than thirty people walked slowly down the path. Most of them were old, but a few looked young or middle-aged.
Wei He used his Bone Shrinking Skill a little to make himself taller. He looked forward and backward at the crowd.
"There are at least two other groups taking the test with us," he said softly.
"Those two groups don't have many people left," Liang Huyao explained with a nod. "Most gave up and left. Only the ones who still have hope joined our group."
Liang Huyao sighed. "In our group, no one stands out except Ouyang Luo and Feng Han." He was still stuck at the third layer and could not make any progress.
He still had the healing medicine, but he could not move forward at all. Now he finally understood. Most of the time, failure was not caused by a lack of energy, but a lack of talent and understanding.
The gap between geniuses seemed small when everyone was a top fighter, but in a place like this, the gap became huge.
Wei He said nothing. He did not tell anyone that he had reached the seventh layer.
Zhou Muqing stood next to him, feeling helpless inside. She had already checked and knew Wei He had reached the seventh layer. Yet this guy was still hiding it and acting like nothing happened. She had no idea what he was thinking.
The group walked through the mountain path and passed the soft sand of Dragon Bay. They climbed back into the forest and walked toward the big training ground near Dening Hall.
Today, there was not just their test, but also a grand tournament for the direct disciples.
The training ground was already packed with people. The direct disciples in light robes and the outer disciples like Wei He in gray robes stood in two clear groups.
Wei He followed the crowd to his spot. It felt a bit like doing morning exercises in a school yard from his past life.
Soon, three old men with white hair and long beards stepped out onto a small building that looked like an incense burner. They rang three bronze bells, each with a different sound.
The ringing mixed together and echoed across the entire training ground, loud and clear in everyone's ears.
"Now, Master Shao Qingzi will speak to everyone!"
The three old men stepped back.
An old man with white hair and a big beard suddenly appeared on the high platform in a flash.
"Today is the day of our Xuanmiao Sect's inner mountain test," he announced. "Thirteen direct disciples and thirty-two outer disciples will take part. When the test ends, the tournament will begin. The winners will get rewards based on their rank. Also, Senior Brother Luo Yan will be the judge today..."
He went on and on, explaining the tournament rules and the rewards for the winners.
The tournament had nothing to do with outer disciples like Wei He. Still, everyone waited patiently.
Wei He looked at the sect leaders on the platform, but his mind drifted to the blindfolded woman who gave him the medicine before. He wondered who she really was.
He already knew how the inner mountain was set up. It had three parts: Black Cliff, Cloud Terrace, and Wuming Temple.
Wuming Temple was for new inner disciples and fighters who had not reached the Real Realm yet. Cloud Terrace was for those at the Real Realm or higher. The highest place, Black Cliff, was where the grandmasters of the three branches lived.
Looking at the speaker on the platform, Wei He guessed this Shao Qingzi must come from Wuming Temple.
Suddenly, Wei He felt someone staring at him.
He turned his head to look.
Far across the field in another group stood a tall, strong man with sharp, wild eyes looking right at him.
Ouyang Luo?
Wei He recognized him, but he did not know why the man was staring at him. He knew very little about this guy and had no idea what he wanted.
"Brother Wei..." Zhou Muqing whispered beside him.
"Wait, change how you call me!" Wei He raised a hand to stop her.
Zhou Muqing was confused for a second, but she changed it right away. "Lord Wei then?"
"That works," Wei He nodded. "Go on."
"Ouyang Luo might have gotten some news from the inside," Zhou Muqing said, her tone quick to show respect. "He might have found out about your real strength."
Now that she was his guard, she knew her place. She only got this chance because of Wei He, so she had to show the right respect.
"What do you know about this Ouyang Luo?" Wei He asked. He had been training hard and paid little attention to the outside world.
"He is a super genius," Zhou Muqing explained softly. "His bones, talent, and energy are all top tier. Before he came to the mountain, he beat a strong fighter to death in a head-on fight."
"Oh? He is that strong?" Wei He was surprised. The man's story sounded a bit like his own past fights.
"Yes, very strong," Zhou Muqing nodded. "And that's not all. He fought four people at the same time and won!"
Wei He was shocked.
Fighting four at once was a big deal. Even though Wei He could do it too—he had killed two leaders from the Wushi Sect in seconds—he knew how hard it was and how much power it required.
"There's more," Zhou Muqing continued. "He once fought an inner mountain master and beat him. I only heard about it, so I don't know if it's true, but people say the higher-ups already have their eyes on him."
Wei He understood. He decided to treat Ouyang Luo as a bigger threat in his mind.
Without a doubt, this guy had his own hidden tricks. Anyone who could beat higher-level fighters was not normal.
Wei He had his secret glass orb and layers of defensive energy, but he wondered what this Ouyang Luo relied on.
As they talked, Liang Huyao and Wang Lu overheard them from nearby. They felt strange seeing how polite and respectful Zhou Muqing was toward Wei He. It did not make sense to them.
Soon, the speech on the platform ended, and the tournament was about to begin.
Wei He had no interest in watching. He closed his eyes and started practicing his energy right where he stood, not wanting to waste time.
Seeing him do this, the others closed their eyes to practice too. No one who made it this far lacked focus.
The main matches were only for the direct disciples, so the outer disciples just had to wait.
After more than an hour, the direct disciples finished their matches, and it was finally time for the outer disciples.
Compared to the direct disciples, the outer fights were much more fun to watch. The outer disciples were all experienced fighters from the outside world with complete skills, making their battles much more exciting.
Wei He did not care about the other fights. No matter what happened, he knew he would pass the test today.
The sun rose higher in the morning sky. The temperature went up, and the fog began to thin out.
Pair after pair of outer disciples went up to fight. The rules stated they could only use the Seven Wonders Skill. The winner would get a higher rank.
Even though high rank brought no immediate reward, it could catch the attention of the inner mountain masters. Some said there were hidden rewards too.
Wei He did not care about any of that.
After waiting for a while, he finally heard his name called.
"Group Eleven: Wei He versus Situ Chen!"
Wei He opened his eyes slowly and looked at the open space in the middle of the field.
After all the previous fights, the ground was full of holes and bumps. But it did not affect him at all.
Wei He jumped forward, did a few flips in the air, and landed smoothly in the middle of the arena.
A man jumped out from the other side. He was a very tall man with a pale face.
Standing across from him, Wei He guessed the man was at least nine feet tall. Yet this Situ Chen was just tall and thin, looking like a bamboo stick from afar.
Ring!
A bell rang from the side.
The old judge called out in a loud voice, "You can only use the Seven Wonders Skill. You lose if you give up, pass out, or cannot fight back!"
Wei He rolled up his sleeves and felt many eyes on him.
He glanced around and saw Ouyang Luo and another handsome, tall man staring at him closely. Zhou Muqing, Liang Huyao, and Wang Lu were watching him too.
No one else paid attention to Wei He. Most people were looking at Situ Chen instead. On the surface, Situ Chen looked much taller and stronger, pulling all the attention.
"Let's get this over with quickly. I still need to go eat at Dragon Bay," Situ Chen said calmly, looking at Wei He.
It seemed he was quite famous among the outer disciples.
Wei He did not even bother to speak. Reaching the seventh layer meant his Seven Wonders Skill alone was at the peak of the Organ Refining level.
As for his opponent, the guy had not even reached the fifth layer, meaning his skill was only at the third level of blood.
How big was the gap between the peak of Organ Refining and a third-level blood fighter?
The result was obvious without even thinking.
"Now... let the test begin!" the judge shouted.
Situ Chen charged forward instantly, taking big strides as he threw a heavy punch toward Wei He's chest.
Whoosh!
A gust of wind suddenly blew outward from Wei He's body.
An invisible layer of energy covered him completely.
Bang!
Situ Chen's fist stopped dead in mid-air, as if it hit a wall and was frozen by a powerful force.
A wave of air spread slowly between the two of them.
"Do you still want to fight?" Wei He stood calmly in place without even raising his hand.
Whoosh!
On the high platform, Shao Qingzi suddenly stood up from his seat.
"This is... the seventh layer peak?!"
The old judge's eyes narrowed. How long had this group of outer disciples been here? How could someone already reach the seventh layer peak?
"Seventh layer?!"
The outer disciples standing nearby did not realize it at first. But soon, people started to understand that a monster who mastered the seventh layer of the Seven Wonders Skill had appeared among them.
As the crowd realized what happened, a wave of shock spread through the field.
The news spread outward like ripples in water.
This group had joined the sect less than a year ago, yet someone had already trained the Seven Wonders Skill all the way to the seventh layer peak.
Liang Huyao opened his mouth, his mind going completely blank.
He reached the seventh layer already?!
The same Wei He who lived in his yard?!
And he himself was still only at the third layer...
He looked at Zhou Muqing, then back at Wei He. Even with all his life experience and calm nature, he was so shocked by this news that he did not know how to react.
It was like finding out that the normal guy living next door was actually the richest man in the country.
The gap was just too big for him to understand.
He thought Wei He was just like him, but Wei He had suddenly rushed straight to the seventh layer peak.
