Beside Taizhou City, there was a mountain called Mount Yueqing.
The main gate of the Wushi Sect sat right on this mountain.
It was noon, and a large crowd had already gathered at the foot of the mountain, waiting for the sect's entry test to begin in the afternoon.
As the undeniable giant force of Taizhou, the Wushi Sect attracted people every day who came to try their luck with the test.
If you passed, you could join the sect and become one of them.
For many martial artists who hunted everywhere for beast meat and lived meal to meal, this was a massive temptation.
At the registration point at the bottom of the mountain, a long line wound around several times.
The people in line were of all ages. Some were in their thirties, while others were as young as eleven or twelve, brought along by their parents.
The midday sun beat down mercilessly.
Beside the line, an old priest in his fifties with white robes and tied-up hair led five young men and women forward.
This group looked completely out of place compared to the people in line, and their energy was noticeably different.
The five people behind the old priest all had sharp eyes and full spirit. One look was enough to tell they were skilled martial artists.
Wei He was among them, walking in third place.
Like him, the other four held tokens and were recommended directly from separate branch posts.
In other words, the other four were also martial artists who had broken through three blood peaks.
Standing close to them, Wei He could feel their fierce blood power.
However, their blood power felt like it had only broken through recently. It was still lively, and their body changes were not even finished yet.
When Wei He first entered Taizhou City, he quickly found an inn to settle Jiang Su, Wei Ying, and Zhang Qi. Then he asked around and found the entry point for the Wushi Sect.
Just as Old Man Wang had said, the Wushi Sect was very famous in Taizhou City. When Wei He asked about it, helpful locals immediately pointed the way.
Wei He did not waste a single second. He used gold bills to clear his path, went straight to the registration point, and presented his token.
When the priests at the registration point saw the token, they did not waste words either. One priest stepped out and added him to this small group to head up to the main gate together.
"With tokens, you are guaranteed to enter. You skip the test step, but..." the old priest leading the way spoke as he walked.
"You must go to the Outer Court first. After that, we match you to a teacher based on your token. If a teacher wants you, then everything goes smoothly. Congratulations, you jump straight over the Outer Court into the Inner Court, and you become my junior brothers.
But if no teacher wants you, you go right back to where you came from. Do you understand?"
The old priest explained at a slow pace.
"Understood!"
The five of them answered in unison.
Along the mountain steps, the priest walked fast and did not wait for them.
Fortunately, they were all three-blood martial artists. Even without special leg arts, pushing their blood power was enough to keep up.
About ten minutes later, the old priest brought the five of them to a cluster of taoist buildings with white walls and black tiles.
"This is the Outer Court. Go in by yourselves. Match your token to your area, and do not give it to anyone else.
Different tokens mean different branch areas, and different branch areas belong to fixed Court Heads," the old priest explained casually as he brought them to the entrance.
"Thank you, Senior Brother, for leading the way," a man with a full beard and a bold look bowed and thanked the old priest.
The others snapped back to reality and quickly bowed as well.
"Nothing to thank me for," the old priest said coldly. "Go inside and wait. Since you all have tokens, the Court Heads will come see you in person. Just show them your best when the time comes."
These three-blood martial artists were different from the tested applicants outside. They had already passed long tests at local branch posts, and their strength was built on the foundation moves of the Wushi Mountain-Returning Art.
Because of this, they bypassed most of the early checks and only needed a final review to enter the Inner Court without grinding in the Outer Court.
That was why the old priest had a gentler tone with them.
Once he delivered the people, he turned and left.
A young priest at the door stepped forward to guide the remaining five.
Without a word, the young priest led them into the buildings. After twisting through several hallways, they came to a wide courtyard.
In the middle of the courtyard sat an old man with white hair and a white beard. He sat on a large stone, holding a fly-whisk, eyes closed as he rested his mind.
The old man wore a light green robe. He looked like an immortal, with a gentle and natural aura that was clearly beyond normal people.
"Senior Brother Han," the guide priest bowed softly. "Five more candidates recommended by the branch posts have arrived."
"Bring them up," the old man, Senior Brother Han, said coldly without opening his eyes.
"Hand your tokens to me, then line up to show Senior Brother Han your Wushi Mountain-Returning Fist one by one. Use your full blood power. Do not hide anything," the guide priest warned Wei He and the others carefully.
The five agreed, gave their tokens to the guide priest, and stepped forward to begin.
Wind rushed through the courtyard as fist strikes punched the air with loud pops. The five lined up and showed their skills with everything they had.
On the side, the guide priest took the tokens and wrote registration notes on them with a small brush.
These tokens could not be faked. Each token came from a different place and carried hidden marks.
Because of this, the guide priest could not cheat or swap them.
After writing down the information, he left in a hurry and passed the tokens to another priest who was waiting.
Then came a long chain of hard checks, matching tokens to areas and reporting them to the Court Heads.
Three-blood martial artists were different from the new beginners lining up outside. Reaching three-blood status already proved a lot on its own.
So many useless tests were skipped.
Soon, the bearded man among the five was the first to finish demonstrating his Mountain-Returning Fist.
The old priest sitting on the stone squinted at the bearded man.
"Your blood power is steady, and your foundation and talent are both decent. Looking at your records, your family lives in this city and has some wealth?"
The bearded man quickly said, "Yes! My family has some wealth, and my father is the leader of a gang in a nearby city with thousands of men. Every month, he can send me a steady supply of gold bills."
"Alright. Next," the old priest pulled out a red wooden plaque from his robe, made a quick scratch on it, and tossed it to the bearded man.
The mark on the plaque clearly read: Bone Quality: Medium.
The second person was a tall, thin man. He said nothing, stepped forward, and slowly performed the Mountain-Returning Fist.
With his full three-blood power pushed to the maximum, he fought as if he were killing enemies on a battleground. He carried a faint smell of blood, looking even stronger than the bearded man.
Yet the old priest frowned instead.
"Stop," he called out before the man could finish.
"Go," he took out a plaque, made a mark as usual, and threw it over.
The words carved on the plaque read: Bone Quality: Medium-Low.
The tall man looked upset, but he did not dare say a word. He took his plaque and stepped back.
The old priest did not care. This was actually part of the test too.
Besides him, three other testers were hidden nearby. They would judge these people from different angles, and each person would receive four scores in total.
The marks on the plaques might be the same or different because they measured different things.
Hiding this detail from the newcomers was a secret test of their patience and mind.
Soon, the third and fourth candidates stepped up to perform.

"All of them used rare pills to force their break throughs..." the old priest shook his head in disappointment.
It was possible to use rare pills to pass the three-blood stage, but such pills were very rare and expensive. They required meat from many different beasts to mix, and they had time limits. People from small places could never see or touch them.
But what kind of place was Taizhou City? Supplies were rich here. The six million people in the city district produced many martial artists, and beast farms were everywhere. What kind of pill could they not make?

Looking at it now, his fist power was the strongest, and his force output was the highest. He should perform well.

His foundation was deep, he was not the oldest here, and he had plenty of real combat experience. His set of moves was fierce, explosive, smooth, and natural, far better than the four before him.

He frowned toward the edge of the courtyard. A servant quickly ran over holding a stack of red plaques.

His stroke this time looked larger than the ones before, clearly writing different words.
He threw the plaque to Wei He.
