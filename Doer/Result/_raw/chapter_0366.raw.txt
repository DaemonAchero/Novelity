Chapter 366: Black Cliff
On the empty edge of Black Cliff, in the inner mountain of Xuanmiao Sect.
A tall man wearing a golden wolf mask slowly walked out of the distance. His body flickered, vanishing and reappearing with every unhurried step along the winding path.

Behind him followed four massive monks in black robes.
At the front was Lord Dingyan. He stood six meters tall, looking like a mountain of pure meat. Yet despite their huge weight, all four monks moved without making a single sound. Silent as ants.
Just the five of them. Nothing more.
