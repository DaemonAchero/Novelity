Year Two of Qingsong, May.
Mountains beyond mountains, towers beyond towers. Six gilded characters were carved into a massive boulder near Juxia City.
Just like Heavenly Seal Sect back in the day, the dozens of towns around Juxia City were tied to the massive Lishan Sect. Some were run by the families of Lishan experts, some were branch sects, and others were servant sects.
Fushan Tower was one of those servant sects. They only took in students after Lishan Sect finished picking the best ones. That way, every youth who wanted to learn martial arts in Juxia City found a place.
"This is Fushan Tower."
At dusk, two pale, expressionless figures in black ran down the road beside the boulder. They were a man and a woman, around thirty or forty years old, both built with unnatural strength.
Their eyes swept over the carved stone.
"Finish it quick," the man warned. "Fushan Tower's Heavy Sound Power is tricky. It counts as a real art. Be careful."
"Got it," the woman said.
They picked up the pace, passed the boulder, and vanished into the dark road.
"Sect Leader! Emergency!"
Inside Twelve Mountain Towers, Yu Qingyan was putting on her heavy battle armor with the help of several male servants.
A masked woman in silver scale armor leaped through the window, landing on one knee. She was part of the Silver Scale Squad, an elite unit answerable only to the Sect Leader. Every member was a master of leg arts, built purely for swift news delivery.
"Speak," Yu Qingyan frowned. The Silver Scale Squad rarely showed up. When they did, it was never good news.
"Half an hour ago, someone found Fushan Tower empty," the agent reported fast. "They checked inside. All twenty-one people are gone without a trace."
"Fushan Tower..." Yu Qingyan's brow tightened.
Lately, small sects had been vanishing one by one. Some vanished into thin air, while others allegedly dissolved on their own. The smallest were just martial arts schools, while the larger ones were proper formal sects like Fushan Tower.
These incidents had been overshadowed by the fall of Heavenly Seal Sect, so no one paid much attention. Yu Qingyan hadn't cared much before, but this was different. Fushan Tower was close to her territory. They paid tribute every year and provided men when needed. Their loss hurt Lishan Sect's bottom line.
Yu Qingyan picked up her small arm shield. It was a block of dark metal as thick as a palm, heavy as a boulder. She flicked her wrist, shaking the floor. Luckily she was on the first floor, or her sheer weight in armor would have caved the ceiling in.
"Tell Liu Qingtang to send men," Yu Qingyan said coldly. "I want an answer in two days."
"Understood." The agent vanished in a flash.
"Guess my trip to Xuanjing will have to wait..." Yu Qingyan cracked her wrists and grabbed her battle hammer.
Wanqing Sect Grounds, Heavenly Seal Hall.
Wei He sat at the head of the main hall. On either side sat Xie Yan, Wan Ling, Wan Qingqing, Du Han, Xiao Qingyu, and Grandma Zeng with her husband.
Only three of the Nine Seeds remained, but Grandma Zeng and her husband had finally stepped out of hiding to fill the empty seats.
"Lishan Sect sent a battle challenge. What do you think?" Wei He looked around.
"Ignore it," Du Han spoke up. He used to serve the Chijing Army, so he was eager to prove his loyalty now.
"Why?" Wei He asked.
Du Han organized his thoughts. "Just think about how Heavenly Seal Sect fell in the first place."
"Go on," Wei He nodded.
Du Han stood up and cupped his hands to the room. "My Red Earth Court surrendered to the Zhou Family to survive. During that time, I learned secrets we never knew before. The collapse of Heavenly Seal Sect wasn't just because Master Shangguan was injured. It was what the Chijing Army, the Three Great Families, and the new Governor all wanted."
"What do you mean?" Xie Yan asked.
"Master Shangguan was injured, but didn't he act far too soft these past few years?" Du Han said. "He didn't just act soft. He stopped fighting back entirely."
The room fell quiet. It was true. Shangguan Ji had gone entirely passive in his final years.
"Lishan Sect sent this challenge for two reasons," Du Han continued. "First, to avenge Yan Junshan. Second, for greed. Heavenly Seal Sect left behind a massive power vacuum. Small factions are fighting over it, and Lishan Sect wants the whole pie. But the Chijing Army and the Three Families crushed us specifically to prevent one group from ruling everything. They prefer small weak factions fighting each other. They will never let Lishan Sect take over Xuanjing."
"So your point is to refuse?" Wei He asked.
"Refuse," Du Han nodded. "As long as we stay in Xuanjing and don't expand, the Chijing Army and the Three Families become our shield. They won't allow a second Heavenly Seal Sect to rise. We stay hidden in Black House Mountain. They won't risk sending elite fighters in here."
The room murmured in agreement.
"Good. I will ignore the challenge," Wei He said. "Now for the next topic. I plan to gather the complete Heavenly Seal Nine Arts so we can all cultivate them. Any objections?"
"All of us?" Xiao Qingyu stood up, shocked.
"Yes. As long as everyone shares what they have."
Wei He had no interest in hoarding secrets. Even if he handed over the manuals, few could master them anyway. Holding onto old rules while the sect was in ruins was suicide. "I will collect all nine manuals and merge them back together. We have three courts here, plus returning disciples from the other branches. We can piece it back."
He paused. "Though, the manuals above Bone Forging for the other six courts were likely taken by the Chijing Army. Only the Court Heads knew those higher levels."
Excitement rippled through the room. The Nine Arts had always been the main strength of Heavenly Seal Sect. If Wei He pulled this off, Wanqing Sect had a real future.
"I will give the full Cloud Rain Manual," Wan Ling said, standing up first.
"I will give mine too," Xiao Qingyu joined her with a quick smile.
"Count me in," Du Han agreed.
"I will talk to the disciples from the other courts to see what we can recover," Xiao Qingyu added.
Xie Yan looked on with envy, but she knew her place. The Nine Arts were built for the nine core branches, not for outsiders like her.
Soon, representatives from the remaining six courts brought their manuals into the hall. Hearing that they could learn the complete Nine Arts, no one hesitated.
