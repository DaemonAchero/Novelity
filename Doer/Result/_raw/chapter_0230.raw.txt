Wei He opened his eyes. He felt a speed of cultivation he had never experienced before.

"I can start Viscera Training now," he thought. "Using my own training alongside the bead will save time."
He was finally on the same level as his master, Wan Ling.
He stayed in the forest for a few more days to stabilize his state. Once he ran out of food, he stepped out. The giant python he killed earlier was now just bones, surrounded by the skeletons of other beasts he had hunted. His poison resistance let him eat almost any beast without worry.
He hunted a black-striped leopard and carried it back with one hand.
As he got close to his cabin, he saw a masked man in green clothes standing at his door.
Wei He stopped. "Who are you? Why are you here?"
"Are you Sect Master Wei He?" The man bowed. His inner force showed he was a martial master as well.
"I am. What do you want?"
"Master Mo sent me to deliver a message."
The man tossed a letter, turned, and leaped away.
Wei He caught it mid-air with his inner force, letting it drop softly to the ground. He checked it for poison with powder, put on gloves, and opened it.
'July 1st. The Alliance Master will arrive. If you wish, meet at Boat No. 1 on the Peng River in Yishuang County in one month to plan against the nest.' — Mo Shiyan.
Wei He crushed the paper into dust with his force and let the wind carry it off.
Mo Shiyan was gathering people. A lot of strong fighters would come.
Wei He looked at his own strength. Reaching the sixth layer did not raise his direct power much, but his speed was far faster now. The speed effect of the Rain Cloud force stacked with his Five Elements step, making him faster than before.
"With this speed, I can handle those three foxes. I can deal with Zhu Chen too."
He decided to go. He could not beat Zhu Chen alone, so he needed allies.
He walked back to pack his materials. He needed his main base to craft certain items.

"Yes, sir!" The man turned and ran off.

He might not be able to kill Zhu Chen yet, but if anything happened to his sect, everyone else involved was going to die.
