Chapter 312: The Inner Mountain
Inside the quiet Mo Ming Temple, three Daoists sat in a triangle. They looked through wooden plates floating in a basin of water. Each plate contained the secrets of a candidate.
Their selection process was simple and cold. It matched the Great Spirit Sect, though their numbers were far smaller.
The Inner Mountain held three main branches.
Daoist Qianliu taught the Hundred Cycles Real Manual.
Daoist Suoshan mastered the Xuan Lock Technique.
Daoist Guishou commanded the Yin Forging Manual.
Three grandmasters ruled these branches. All of them had broken past the Realm of Perception, rising to higher, deadlier heights.
Yet the mundane duty of picking fresh blood fell to their chief disciples. Though called disciples, every single one was over two hundred years old.
Today, those three chief disciples sat in the hall.
Qianliu’s chief, Qing Meizi, wore white robes and a feather crown. Aura sharp and heavy. He was nearly three hundred years old, yet his face looked barely thirty.
"Among the direct disciples, four stand out," Qing Meizi said softly. "The top four are already adapting to the true world. Soon, they will finish the Seven Marvels Manual."
Across from him, Bai Hezi spoke up. She was the chief disciple of Daoist Guishou. Soft eyes, gentle voice, like a harmless woman.
"I prefer the outsiders who brought their own martial arts," she said. "Some carry massive stockpiles of resources. One even found an artifact that counters the Ghost Wind."
"Their numbers are too small," said the third disciple, Yuan Duzi. Her voice was cold.
She was the chief of the Xuan Lock branch, and the strongest of the three. A black silk blindfold covered her eyes, yet she saw everything. Her body and face were flawless. Her lectures were always the most crowded in the entire sect.
"Outsiders have age and weak potential against them," Yuan Duzi added. "Few will ever reach the Perception Realm."
"We will see how they react after the next sermon," she said, looking down at the floating plates. She didn't touch them.
Right now, no one—neither direct disciples nor outsiders—had mastered the Seven Marvels Manual.
All three sighed. Every year, fewer people made it into the Inner Mountain. And those who did usually froze at the lowest level, unable to push further.
Without new blood, the Xuan Miao Sect was dying.
It was not just them. Across the Great Yuan Empire, every sacred ground faced the same decay. War, plague, and disasters had wiped out the population. Fewer people meant fewer geniuses.
Now, the invasion of the Wu Empire forced them into constant battles. The sect's growth slowed to a crawl.
They knew the path led to ruin, but they were powerless to stop it.
The next morning.
Wei He opened his eyes. He had spent the entire night working on the Seven Marvels Manual.
The manual was simple. The steps were written down in extreme detail. He hadn't fully unlocked it yet, but reaching the First Layer wouldn't take long.
He stepped outside. Today was the first sermon for newcomers, and he planned to listen.
In the courtyard, Shangguan Xing was whispering with Liang Huoyao.
"Brother Wei, you came at the right time!" Shangguan Xing said, her face full of shock. "Brother Liang mastered the basics in a single night!"
"That fast?" Wei He was surprised.
"It's nothing," Liang Huoyao said smoothly, brushing it off. "I am just rebuilding my base. Basic techniques move fast at the start."
"Don't be so humble," Zhou Muqing said as she walked out of her room, smiling softly. "Your talent and bone structure are top tier. If not for our age, you would match the best direct disciples in the sect."
"Top-tier talent? That is impressive," Wei He agreed.
Even after reshaping his body, his own bone structure was only upper-middle tier. Liang Huoyao was indeed a monster.
Still, nobody here was a naive youth. They said nice words, but none of them truly cared. They had all reached the Organ Refining Realm. A small head start at the bottom meant nothing.
Only the ones who survived to the end mattered.
Days passed. Every seven days, the sect handed out a dark ointment called Sea Paste.
Rubbing it on the chest and back was mandatory for cultivating the Seven Marvels Manual. It worked just like the medicinal baths from his old sect days.
Wei He felt like he was back at the Sky Ink Sect. Eat, sleep, train. Nothing else.
Half a month later, the entire Wei family arrived at Haizhou and settled in Zhentao City. Wei He went down to meet them, spent time with his wife and son, and then returned to the mountain to train.
Rebuilding a base as an Organ Refining master was fast.
A month flew by.
Wei He mastered the basic movements. He began converting his original force into the sect's specific Qi.
Zhou Muqing and Shangguan Xing also mastered the basics.
Meanwhile, Liang Huoyao proved his talent. In just over a month, he broke through the basics, hit the First Layer, and was already approaching its limit.
Every layer of the Seven Marvels Manual left a mark on the body.
Once a layer was complete, a mark like a tree branch would appear on the back.
Beyond that, the manual was useless in combat. The Qi it created provided only a tiny boost to speed and strength.
But nobody learned it to fight. They used it as a key.
In a clearing near Dragon Bay, outside the Moral Palace.
Wei He moved slowly through his forms.
The manual was an ugly mix of palms, fists, and kicks. A messy beast with zero practical combat value.
Yet as he trained, Wei He felt a strange depth behind its simple moves.
He finished the sixty-four forms of the set and let out a long breath. Tomorrow, he would hit the bottleneck and force his way into the First Layer.
"Brother Wei! So you were here." A tall, thin Daoist with a full beard walked over from the path.
"Brother Shen. Something wrong?" Wei He asked.
The man was Shen Yifei, a neighbor from the nearby courtyard. Both were practical, quiet men, and they had become quick friends.
"Ouyang Luo from Court One just broke into the Second Layer!" Shen Yifei sighed. "They say his talent, bones, and blood are all top rank. Plus, he has a rare body type. His speed is terrifying."
Wei He stayed silent.
With his secret skill pearl, he would catch up eventually. Slow conversions at the start didn't bother him. He just needed to reach the bottleneck first.
"How is Liang Huoyao doing in your yard?" Shen Yifei asked.
"Close to the Second Layer as well," Wei He said.
"Monsters, all of them," Shen Yifei groaned. "Oh, by the way, I saw Shangguan Xing walking with Ouyang Luo earlier. She was asking him for advice."
Wei He said nothing.
"Nothing to say?" Shen Yifei nudged him.
"I barely know her," Wei He replied coldly. "Besides, I have a wife and a son."
"Word is her family was slaughtered recently," Shen Yifei said, lowering his voice. "Over a hundred people dead. She only came here to hide."
"And?"
"And Zhou Muqing is getting close to Liang Huoyao. You're the only one left standing alone." Shen Yifei shook his head.
"If you spent less time gossiping and more time training, you would be at the First Layer by now," Wei He said.
"Tch, you're no fun."
Shen Yifei wanted to say more, but he noticed Wei He pause and look behind him.
He turned around. A woman stood on the path holding a food box, smiling at Wei He.
She wore black robes, but she had altered them to fit tight around her chest, waist, and legs. She knew exactly how to use her body to draw eyes.
"Shangguan Xing?" Wei He raised an eyebrow. "Do you need something?"
"Can't I come just to see you?" She stepped past the surprised Shen Yifei, stopping right in front of Wei He.
"I saw your file," she said, her cold face breaking into a bright, striking smile. "Taking on two opponents alone. Impressive."
"I had no choice," Wei He said flatly. He knew what she meant. News of him killing the two masters had finally spread. Even with help hiding it, the truth had leaked.
At least his family was safe now.
"Free for dinner?" she asked naturally, showing no grief for her dead family. "Brother Ouyang wants to meet you."
Wei He frowned. Ouyang Luo was not just a strong candidate. He was rumored to be connected to a high-ranking elder in the sect.
"I don't know him," Wei He said.
"Don't decline so fast," she said, raising an eyebrow. "Brother Ouyang has deep ties in the Inner Mountain. Knowing him will only help you."
"Skip the dinner," Wei He cut her off, annoyed by the games. "What does he actually want?"
Shangguan Xing glanced at Shen Yifei, then looked back at Wei He.
"Brother Ouyang wants you to give him half of your Sea Paste allowance for the next year. He will pay you in gold."
Wei He stared at her.
Sea Paste was required to cultivate the manual. If he gave away half, his own progress would grind to a halt.
"I don't need money," Wei He said.
"If you give it up, Brother Ouyang will pull you up once he enters the Inner Mountain," she said seriously.
Beside them, Shen Yifei kept his mouth shut.
This was the brutal reality for outsiders. With low pass rates and limited resources, most people gave up on training and looked for a powerful master to serve.
Shangguan Xing had realized she was too slow. So she immediately offered herself to the fastest climber on the mountain.
