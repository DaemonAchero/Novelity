Chapter 22
"Looks like even though I eat Silver-Kissed Black Snake meat every day, the other disciples aren't slacking off either. Everyone has their own way to build up blood energy."
Wei He felt more and more that agreeing to Senior Brother Cheng back then was the right choice.
In about half a year, he hadn't fallen behind in his blood energy progress, and he was also constantly charging the Break-Through Bead.
He grabbed a towel to wipe his sweat and looked at his chest. Only a quarter of the Break-Through Bead pattern was left to fill.
"No matter what, I need to polish my blood energy to the limit first. Only then can I use the Break-Through Bead to go further." Hope burned inside Wei He, and his eyes grew firm.
Suddenly, Cheng Shaojiu rushed in with a happy look on his face. He walked over to Wei He and whispered.
"Xiao He! There's a chance!"
"??" Wei He looked blank. A chance for what?
Seeing his reaction, Cheng Shaojiu felt speechless.
"Did you forget? Finding you a wife!" he reminded in a low voice.
"I picked three suitable women from good families nearby. First, we'll exchange your basic info to see if you like any of them. If it works out, then it's a deal!" Cheng Shaojiu said with a smile.
He looked left and right, making sure no one was paying attention, then kept talking.
"I hired an artist to draw portraits. You can look at them yourself in a bit. If you like one, I'll send your details over. You're my junior brother and my best friend. Don't worry, I definitely picked the absolute best for you!" He gave a serious thumbs-up. "But you need to clean yourself up a bit too, so the girls don't look down on you."
"Don't you think it's a bit early..." Wei He said.
"Not early at all! Look at me, I've been married for almost two years. How is it early?" Cheng Shaojiu laughed.
Wei He was silent. He didn't actually mind the idea. He just felt his current life was good and didn't want to change it.
However, carrying on the family line was indeed a huge deal in this era.
After thinking it over, he decided to let things take their natural course and let his senior brother make the arrangements.
A few days later, Cheng Shaojiu brought three portraits of young women for Wei He to look at.
He would check if he liked any of them first, then express interest to see if the other side wanted to meet.
It wasn't like the ancient times from Wei He's past life, where people married without ever seeing each other. Women here had a much higher status.
Wei He chose the one who looked the gentlest. Then, a matchmaker was sent to deliver the letter.
This woman was named Zhang Wanruo. Her family had some wealth and ran a restaurant business.
Because they had done a lot of business with the Cheng family, Cheng Shaojiu had included her as an option.
Zhang Wanruo looked weak on the outside, but she was sharp and decisive inside. At just fifteen, she had already started managing part of the family business.
When she received the letter from the matchmaker, she was enjoying the flowers in her backyard.
The area where the Zhang family lived wasn't in the chaotic slum where Wei He used to stay, but in the relatively peaceful Red Stone Town.
Guards and gang members patrolled the streets day and night to ensure shops and market stalls could run smoothly. It was the most commercial town in Feiye City.
The Zhang family's restaurant business was fairly famous here. It wasn't the strongest, and couldn't match the Cheng family's status in Stone Bridge Town, but it was no small household either.
"The letter from Young Master Cheng says this Wei He broke through his blood energy at seventeen, became an official disciple of the Returning Mountain Fist, and bought a house with a yard with his own money. He has good character, is loyal, honest, and reliable. He only has one older sister left, no parents to take care of, and good future prospects..."
A maid named Hongmei read the contents of the letter carefully from the side.
Zhang Wanruo fit her name well. She was small and delicate, wearing a light green cotton dress, with clear features and black hair down to her waist.
Except for her slightly dark skin and rough hands from running business outside, she looked every bit like the top choice for a good wife and mother.
Hearing this information, Zhang Wanruo frowned slightly.
"Since he is Old Zheng's personal disciple at the Returning Mountain Fist, he shouldn't be bad. By the way, Hongmei, do you know how long it took Wei He to break through his blood energy?"
As a business person, she knew a little about martial arts. She knew that the earlier one broke through, the greater their potential.
In current times, strength was the best guarantee for safety, so she couldn't be careless.
Houses and property didn't matter. The plague was bad, many people were dying, and the grain towns outside were hit by a locust swarm. Right now, houses were the cheapest things around. Many people were trying to pack up and leave.
The maid Hongmei quickly pulled out another letter.
"I already asked around for you, Miss. I paid someone to get this news from other insiders at the Returning Mountain Fist Compound."
She opened the paper and read word by word.
"This Wei He is quiet and secretive. Others can't tell what he's thinking. His blood energy breakthrough happened barely on the last two days, just barely passing. His potential might already be used up. He comes from the most chaotic and dangerous slum in the outer city. He has one older sister, while his parents and oldest sister are missing. In the compound, he talks little and rarely hangs out with others."
This letter was very objective and realistic, filling in all the details Cheng Shaojiu had left out.
Zhang Wanruo frowned even tighter after listening.
"Breaking through on the last two days means his future potential isn't great. Young Master Cheng didn't mention this at all in his letter."
"Then Miss...?"
"Refuse it. I, Zhang Wanruo, am not so desperate that I'll just marry anyone at this level," Zhang Wanruo said with a wave of her hand.
Even though she rarely got marriage proposals because she showed her face in public for business, she hadn't fallen so low as to marry just anyone.
Although this Wei He was an official disciple of the Returning Mountain Fist and barely matched her social status, his potential was just too weak.
She planned to wait a bit longer. She had already waited this long anyway.
"Alright, Miss. I'll go tell the matchmaker right now." Hongmei put away both letters and turned to leave.
On the other side, after hearing he was rejected, Wei He didn't take it to heart.
His mind was entirely on how to speed up his blood energy build-up. As for romance and marriage, he just let nature take its course.
Although having children was important, there was no rush if he didn't meet the right person.
Cheng Shaojiu, on the other hand, had been full of confidence at first. Being rejected made him lose face.
He brought a whole stack of new portraits to Wei He. But since the first attempt failed, Wei He lost interest and politely turned them all down.
Over a month passed quietly like this. Finally, the Break-Through Bead was almost full. It would probably complete naturally in a short time.
Wei He focused all his energy on boosting his blood energy as fast as possible. Once his blood energy reached the limit, he could break through to the next level, Stone Skin.
From how Xiao Ran was treated, it was clear there was a big gap between Bull Hide and Stone Skin.
The difference was clear in both wealth and social standing.
However, building up blood energy couldn't be done overnight. It required constantly expanding and training the body.
On the martial arts field of the Cheng family.
Wei He and Cheng Shaojiu stood facing each other.
They were more than five meters apart, standing on bare yellow dirt.
Compared to neat stone tiles, natural dirt ground didn't need repairs. If it got damaged, a few scoops with a shovel fixed it easily.
That was why the real practice ground behind the Cheng family used this method.
As for the stone tile practice ground shown to Wei He at the start, that was just for show.
"Senior Brother, I've always had a question. How big is the actual combat gap between one rank of blood energy? Could you help me test it?"
Wei He had been thinking about how to speed up his blood energy build-up lately.
Because he lacked a clear comparison, he didn't know how much blood energy was needed to reach the limit of Bull Hide.
So he spoke directly to Cheng Shaojiu, who had always gone easy on him during practice.
"One rank of blood energy makes a massive difference in real fight power. Since you want to feel it carefully, I'll show you a bit. Just don't blame me if you get beaten up badly."
Cheng Shaojiu laughed. Constantly holding back while sparring with Wei He wasn't much fun anyway.
Right then, he shook his hands, and a greyish-white layer naturally appeared on his fists.
This was the sign of the Stone Skin level. Once blood energy reached a certain point, it caused a physical change in the fists.
"Watch out!"
As soon as the words left his mouth, Wei He felt a sudden gust of wind hit his face.
The distance of several meters between them seemed to vanish. Before he could even react, his senior brother was gone from his sight.
Luckily, Wei He was no novice. He had some real combat experience. He covered his head with both fists, bent his body, and shifted quickly to the left.
No matter what, he had to move first so the opponent couldn't lock onto his position.
Bang.
At that moment, a heavy fist landed right on Wei He's right shoulder.
Even though shifting left took away some of the impact, the gap in speed and power still shattered his defensive guard instantly.
"I'm going to hit your right shoulder," Cheng Shaojiu's voice came again.
Wei He's vision blurred. He couldn't see anyone at all, so he could only pull his fists back to cover his head.
He waited for the next punch to come. Yet the moment the thought crossed his mind, his right shoulder was hit again.
With a thud, Wei He fell backward onto the ground, losing all his strength to move.
Just two hits. Two moves.
He was completely defeated. There was no chance to drag out the fight or use any secret moves.
The gap in reaction speed and explosive speed was just too massive.
Cheng Shaojiu stood still again, looking at Wei He with a smile.
"How was that? Just so you know, I only used half my power. Otherwise, you would have stayed down on the first hit."
Wei He's chest heaved as he pulled himself up from the dirt.
"Impressive!" He squeezed out the words through his teeth.
"If it's impressive, then train hard. Sooner or later, you'll reach my level too," Cheng Shaojiu laughed.
Suddenly, he remembered something.
"By the way, Xiao He, you need to come support me in a while. I plan to take another concubine." The corners of Cheng Shaojiu's mouth curled up uncontrollably as he spoke, looking full of pride.
"Another concubine?" Wei He was speechless. Weren't you just talking about finding me a wife? How come now...
"Speaking of which, it's fate. This new sister-in-law of yours was someone I set my eyes on while looking for your marriage match. I'll definitely give you plenty of wedding beans then," Cheng Shaojiu said proudly.
Because refining sugar was complicated and expensive, people here mostly used wrapped fried beans as small gifts during weddings.
"..." Wei He had nothing to say.
He knew Senior Brother Cheng was already married. He didn't just have a wife, but also two concubines, and now he was getting a third.
The main wife had given birth to a son, and the two concubines each had a daughter. Now he was taking a third one...
"Right, Senior Brother, who is stronger between Cheng Jing and me?" Wei He suddenly asked.
Last time, his fight with Cheng Jing was interrupted by that monster.
He was still a bit curious about it.
"Cheng Jing? The Cheng Jing from your last escort mission?" Cheng Shaojiu suddenly recalled. "She's not ordinary. If you fight her, you probably won't make it. You'll lose within thirty moves."
