Chapter 355
"The nature of gravity... is definitely far more complex than I thought. But..."
Wei He sat cross-legged inside the cave, fully aware of his limits. His understanding of gravity was barely a surface-level grasp, based on basic modern science.
Yet that tiny bit of knowledge was more than enough to crush everyone in this era.
He didn't need to fully comprehend gravity. He just needed to understand it better than anyone else. Besides, no one would ever think to project a deity of gravity into their mind. People of this age couldn't even fathom a concept as advanced as universal gravitation. Anyone who could grasp basic weight and gravity was already considered a genius.
"The best part about gravity is how easily it scales from weak to strong. Unlike many deities whose power has a hard limit, gravity transitions smoothly. An apple falling is gravity. Two planets colliding is gravity. Even a collapsing star turning into a white dwarf is gravity."
After careful thought, Wei He chose universal gravitation.
Nothing in history could outlast it. No one even knew how long gravity had existed. On top of that, it was real and everywhere. Its force could be tiny or massive, making it perfect to control, with boundless future potential. According to modern physics, gravity could even warp time and space.
"It's settled then!"
Wei He focused his mind. In his thoughts, the deity of gravity took the form of a dark, twisted wave of water. It had no fixed shape, which matched his personal view of gravity. Gravity had no physical form to begin with. It was just a phenomenon, a description of how everything in the universe interacted. It was merely a concept defined by humans.
Therefore, this deity belonged to the type that unleashed as much power as the energy poured into it.
Once the image was set, Wei He began infusing his mental force into it. A simple shape wasn't enough. To crush and seal external forces, it needed sheer intimidation. Gravity itself wasn't a violent monster, but the disasters it caused went far beyond any beast.
Wei He poured in thoughts of vastness, infinity, domain, and absolute control. At the same time, he guided his Real Energy through the thirteen core points in his body.
Just as he expected, the energy flowing through the thirteen points transformed rapidly. For every ten units of energy, at least nine were converted. The converted energy turned slightly darker in color, though no other huge changes were visible yet.
"Realism really boosts the conversion rate. But the concept of gravity is too vast. Even if the initial force of my gravity deity isn't high, it will still take a massive amount of Real Energy to finish the process. Otherwise, it won't create that terrifying, infinite pressure."
Wei He knew what he was getting into. He wasn't entirely sure if he would succeed, but with his current strength, testing it out didn't hurt. Besides, he could already crush top experts at the Real Truth level, so he had nothing to worry about.
He threw himself completely into converting his energy. However, his reserves were massive, almost thirty times that of an average expert at his level. The vast energy moved through his body like thick, burning mercury. Even with high efficiency, the process would take time.
Hours passed by quietly.
Suddenly, a faint knock sounded from outside the cave.
"Who is it?" Wei He opened his eyes and asked in a low voice.
He remembered placing a 'Do Not Disturb' sign outside. If someone knocked anyway, it meant urgent business.
"Master, it's me," Zhou Muqing's voice came from outside.
"Zhou Muqing?" Wei He paused, then quickly collected his energy and stood up.
He opened the door. Standing outside was Zhou Muqing, looking vastly different from before. Most surprisingly, she had actually reached the Mind Sensing realm.
"Master, I am sorry. I was ambushed on the road earlier. After a desperate escape, I passed out in a hidden spot. I only returned here after my wounds healed."
Her explanation matched what Wei He had assumed.
"It's fine as long as you're back. What about your strength...?" Wei He asked, a bit curious.
To him, reaching Mind Sensing wasn't a huge deal. She might have hidden in a corner of the inner mountain to break through, which was normal enough. But breaking through before even returning to the sect was impressive.
"It was pure luck, all thanks to the Mysterious Heart Pill you gave me. I faced life and death back there," Zhou Muqing said with a trace of lingering fear.
She quickly changed the subject and said seriously, "By the way, Master, on my way back, I ran into your mother-in-law."
"Wan Ling? Didn't she leave for the Central Region? How did you run into her?" Wei He frowned.
"I caught the Chaos Cult hunting down Lady Wan Ling on the road, so I stepped in and saved her. I have already sent her back to the Wei Manor."
"Hunting her?!" Wei He's gaze turned cold and sharp.
"Those Chaos Cult bastards really are everywhere. Let's go check on her!"
He didn't hesitate. He had been unhappy when Wan Ling decided to leave for the Central Region, but it was her choice, so he hadn't stopped her. But now, the Chaos Cult had attacked his family. That changed everything.
He immediately left the inner mountain with Zhou Muqing and headed back to the Wei Manor on Tianhai Island.

Inside the Wei Manor.
Wan Ling wore a green dress while her daughter, Wan Qingqing, applied medicine to her injured foot.
"Now that you're back, don't leave again. Stay here with us. Bring those two kids along and live a peaceful life," Wan Qingqing said softly. "With my husband guarding the Profound Sect, no one will cause trouble. Isn't it better to enjoy a quiet life?"
Wan Ling's eyes dimmed. Thinking of the Xue family hunting her convoy and the grim fate of the rest of the Wan family made her heart ache. But looking at her daughter and her young grandson, Wei An, standing quietly nearby, guilt washed over her. She had run off on her own, only to make her family worry endlessly.
Bang!
The door was pushed open.
Wei He stepped inside with wide strides. After greeting his wife and son, his eyes locked onto Wan Ling.
"Mother! Are you alright?!"
"Wei He... I'm fine. It's just the Wan family... it's gone..." Wan Ling's eyes turned red.
No matter what, that was the place where she grew up. Now it was completely destroyed.
"Was it the Chaos Cult?" Wei He asked.
"It's dangerous outside right now, Wei He! Don't do anything reckless!" Wan Ling panicked and tried to stand up despite her injuries.
"I know my limits. Don't worry, Mother," Wei He said calmly. He waved his hand, signaling the maid outside to bring in fresh medicine. "This is high-grade ointment from the inner mountain. It works much better than normal medicine. You should try it. You can't buy this outside."
Without waiting for her response, he nodded to Wan Qingqing. "Qingqing, take good care of Mother. I'm stepping out for a moment."
He turned and left, ignoring Wan Ling's calls behind him.
Leaving the room, Wei He gathered details from Zhou Muqing about the attack. The attacker was a peak Viscera Training expert. Ordinary fighters from his Thousand Poison Sect were useful for gathering information, but useless against an opponent of that level. He had to handle this himself.
"Master, are you going after the Chaos Cult?" Zhou Muqing asked softly, waiting in the courtyard.
"Yes. Do you have a lead?" Wei He's tone toward her was noticeably warmer after she saved Wan Ling. She had protected his family when it mattered.
"I do. I got information from that Chaos Cult fighter," Zhou Muqing said seriously. "He came to Sea Region because the Chaos Cult is holding a small high-level meeting nearby. Many core members are attending."
"Interesting... Where?" Wei He asked coldly.
"At Qingfeng Lake in the Five Spirits Snowfield," Zhou Muqing answered. Deep in her eyes, hidden from everyone, a flicker of guilt flashed by.
"Qingfeng Lake?" Wei He narrowed his eyes.
In the past, the Chaos Cult seemed like a terrifying giant to him. But now, they were nothing special.
Back when he was at the First Mind Sensing stage, he had already killed Three-Faced Bodhisattva and three top experts from the Wu State. Now that he had completed the First Mind Sensing stage, his energy had evolved. It was far more corrosive and explosive.
Since the Second Mind Sensing stage required a long wait, and the second layer of the Mysterious Lock Technique needed a completed deity projection, he was currently stuck in a bottleneck anyway. He was still pouring energy into his deity, so taking a short trip to test his strength was perfect.
Without prior warning, the Chaos Cult couldn't possibly mobilize top-tier experts to trap him. He estimated that only high-stage experts among the Nine Steps could pose a threat to him now.
"Stay here and wait for my return," Wei He ordered.
"Master, I have broken through as well. I might be able to help you. Besides, you might not know the exact path to Qingfeng Lake. I explored the entire Sea Region while you were in secluded cultivation," Zhou Muqing urged quickly.
Wei He turned to look at her and paused.
"Fine."
Having someone around to carry gear wasn't a bad idea.
Without delay, the two packed lightly and left the Wei Manor.
Meanwhile, by Qingfeng Lake.
The frozen lake was smooth as a mirror. A few pure white snow foxes tumbled and played on the ice.
Two hill-like figures stood by the lake, quietly watching the cold wind blow the snow around.
One of them was a woman with a completely shaved head, tattooed with a massive black spider. Her muscles were so heavily built that she lacked any female curves, looking more like twisted tree roots.
"The Profound Sect's total lockdown strategy is working well. Our people can't slip inside anymore, and most of our hidden spies have been cleared out. That Yuan Duzi really has some skill," the bulky man beside her spoke in a deep voice.
