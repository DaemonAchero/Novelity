Outside Black House Mountain.
By the stone bridge, Zhu Chen slowed his horse to a stop. He looked at the far side.
"Across here is the Thousand Poison Sect main base, right? The Thousand Bat Pavilion was just a cover. The real base is deep in those woods. Is the exact location confirmed?" he asked softly.
A masked woman in a purple dress beside him said, "Yes. It hasn't moved. The ones trying to scatter and run came back, too. We've surrounded the whole area."
"I planned to let Wang Shuyi handle this herself later," Zhu Chen said. "Plans change. Someone wants to gather people and cause trouble. To stop internal chaos, I must clean up inside before killing the outer enemies."
Rebel forces were closing in. Spies were everywhere. He had to wipe out the Thousand Poison Sect fast and clean. Only then could he deal with Li Tong of the Katori Sect.
Besides, Wang Shuyi had been acting strange ever since she was kidnapped and brought back. Her every move was different. That ruined his original plan.
Then spies got new news: in one month, a troublesome person would arrive in Xuanjing.
To save his strength for that person, Zhu Chen sped up his timeline. He would clear all internal threats right now.
That included destroying the Thousand Poison Sect.
Once decided, speed was everything.
Strike before they notice. Kill the fighters. Capture the martial masters and send them to the prefectural city.
On top of that, he planned to catch Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing as hostages to force Wei He's hand.
This had two benefits. First, it pinned all the blame on the Thousand Poison Sect to calm the public anger. Second, holding the people Wei He cared about most meant Wei He couldn't fight back freely and would have to serve them.
Zhu Chen knew how to deal with these martial artists. He saw right through them.
He looked back at the small group behind him. Only ten people, but all were top elites from the local Sun-Seeking Willow-Night branch.
Apart from himself, there was Cold Poison Master Ouyang Zhao, Double Fists Xu Fei, Nine-Joint Sword Xu Lu, and the newly sent Nine-Fingers Zhang Yeyun.
Except Ouyang Zhao, the other three were Organ-Refining masters. The remaining six were Bone-Forging masters. They were the core of the organization, the leaders of every branch in the region.
They gathered today for one purpose: total annihilation of the Thousand Poison Sect.
To be safe, Zhu Chen also brought in the You and Zhou families. They sent fighters to block every escape route.
Fish already in the net could not be allowed to jump out.
As for the Wang family, they were kept inside the city. The Wangs were close with Wei He, and Zhu Chen couldn't risk them ruining things.
"Let's go," Zhu Chen said calmly. "After this, the masters we catch should last us a while."
He dismounted and put on prepared gloves.
Ouyang Zhao threw a handful of fine powder into the air. It drifted onto everyone.
"This sticks to your force and blocks poison for two hours," Ouyang Zhao said.
"Two hours is plenty," Zhu Chen said.
He leaped forward like an arrow, and the others followed close behind, rushing straight into Black House Mountain.
...
In the forest, a boy wearing a straw hat and carrying a basket asked curiously, "Master, what is the martial world?"
He was around eight years old, walking behind a saber-carrying man in gray.
The man thought for a moment. "It's just a crowd of martial artists."
"When do we get there? Do we pay a fee?"
The man smiled, then stopped smiling. "If you are strong, you don't pay."
"How strong is strong?"
The man stayed silent. He didn't have an answer.
Suddenly, he stopped and looked ahead.
On the path, a young man in white stood by a rock with a folding fan. He wore a silver crown and had a sharp, cold look in his eyes. His fan was pure white, without a mark on it.
The gray-clothed man blocked his disciple. "Stay back," he whispered.
The boy backed away. Before they took a few steps, a strong wind blew past.
A tall black shadow landed in front of the white-clothed man like smoke. He had wild long hair and a massive build.
"Wang Shaojun," the shadow said. It was Wei He.
"Are you here to help me, or stop me?"
Helping meant killing the Sun-Seeking Willow-Night people, which would make the Wang family traitors to the city. Stopping meant watching his own sect die.
Wang Shaojun gripped his fan tight. He looked down and said nothing.
"Answer me!" Wei He roared.
Leaves shook loose from the trees and fell around them.
"You shouldn't have come back," Wang Shaojun said softly.
"That's not up to you," Wei He said.
He walked straight past him.
"If I were you, I wouldn't have come either."
"Going back is suicide!" Wang Shaojun called out.
Wei He didn't answer. He turned into a blur and vanished into the trees.
Wang Shaojun stood still. He wanted to chase, but forced his feet to stay put. He understood Wei He's message: stay out of this to protect himself and his family.
He gripped his fists hard.
He jumped up into the trees and sped away in another direction.
The man and the boy were left alone.
"See that?" the man said. "That is the martial world. People kill or get killed."
"Master... I want to go home and farm," the boy muttered.
The man slapped the boy's head. "Farm for what? Learning martial arts keeps you alive. Come on, pick herbs. Don't go that way."
...
Trees blurred past.
Wei He ran at full speed. His endurance was high, allowing him to cover great distances with ease.
He tapped his foot on a trunk to spring forward, but a huge saber blocked his path.
The copper saber was three meters long, held by a giant man.
"You...?" Wei He stopped and looked closely.
The man had wild hair, dark skin, three gold rings in one ear, and massive muscles.
"Zhou Xingtong?" Wei He recognized him. It was the Zhou family's copper giant who had been missing for a long time.
"My family guards this path," Zhou Xingtong said heavily. He was no longer the wild, arrogant man he used to be.
"I don't care about your family," Wei He said coldly. "Are you stopping me?"
"I promised Lord Zhu," Zhou Xingtong said, resting his saber on the ground. "Get past my blade first."
"Do you know what you are doing?" Wei He's voice was ice cold.
Zhou Xingtong stayed silent. Blocking Wei He meant becoming deadly enemies forever.
"This has nothing to do with your family," Wei He said. "You can't catch me anyway."
Wei He burst forward with explosive speed, turning into a shadow that swept past the huge blade.
Zhou Xingtong gripped his handle, ready to strike, but hesitated. He suddenly remembered the day he fought to retrieve his master's body. He looked just like Wei He did now.
"Let him go," a middle-aged scholar walked out from the dark. "His life is his own choice. Lord Zhu knows our speed can't hold him back."
Zhou Xingtong turned away without a word. Wei He was already gone.
His sound force could have stopped Wei He, but he chose not to use it.
