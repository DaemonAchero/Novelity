Answer: Chapter 137:
Wei He walked inside the gates of Zhongling Manor, blending into the crowd with Zhang Lingxu and Meng Qing.
The main entrance had no actual doors. It was just a large stone archway shaped like a traditional gateway.
Inside, a wide main path snaked deep into the estate, splitting into dozens of small trails like a spiderweb. The crowd at the entrance was thick. Wei He and the others had to stand in line for fifteen minutes before reaching the front.
They paid the entry fee, stated their business, and slowly headed toward the sector reserved by the Heavenly Ink Sect.
Their path ran along the edge of the vast Peacock Lake. In the bright midday sun, the water shattered the light into colorful waves that danced against both banks. From a distance, it looked as if the whole lake had put on a vibrant robe.
However, shortly after they entered, a commotion broke out ahead in the sect's reserved area.
A large crowd dressed in Heavenly Ink Sect robes came rushing toward them.
Zhang Lingxu quickly ordered her servants to step aside, and Wei He's group followed suit.
"What happened?"
"No idea. Looks like something huge went down."
"People from the Gas Transformation Court, Arc Light Court, and Ten Thousand Green Court are all here!" Zhang Lingxu quickly recognized several faces in the crowd.
"Let's go, follow them!" Wei He said as a thought crossed his mind, tugging the two women along.
Understanding his signal, they gave quick orders to their servants and followed Wei He, sticking to the tail end of the group.
The Heavenly Ink Sect members moved in a hurry. Led by Chang Kuan and another disciple, they soon arrived at Shanhe Building.
The building, which was already missing a chunk of its structure, was now a mess being cleaned up.
Yan Jiuhai and his son were nowhere to be seen. Only overturned tables and broken chairs remained on the scene.
On the second floor, a massive gap gaped wide. It wasn't just the outer wall that was gone; two inner walls behind it had been blasted straight through as if hit by a heavy projectile, opening a clear line of sight to the sycamore tree inside.
A circle of curious onlookers gathered below the hole.
Zhongling Manor never lacked regular tourists. Though the Heavenly Ink Sect's martial tournament to win the young mistress's hand was a big deal, it only drew a fraction of the total visitors. Most people were just there to enjoy the famous scenery.
Zhang Lingxu eagerly squeezed through the crowd to ask around, returning a moment later with a gleam in her eyes.
"Guess what happened?" she said, full of excitement.
Meng Qing shook her head softly, unsure of what to say.
"Martial artists fought, didn't they?" Wei He frowned. "Judging by the damage, it was no small fight."
"Yep! And it was a major contender for the tournament—the son of Yan Jianshan from the Lishan Sect. Supposedly, he ruined a girl's purity years ago and killed her to keep her quiet. The girl's father finally came to track him down for revenge."
In just a short moment, Zhang Lingxu had gathered almost the entire truth.
Wei He looked up at the masterpiece he had created not long ago.
Shanhe Building would have to foot its own repair bill this time. With Yan Jiuhai so badly injured, the manor wouldn't dare ask the Lishan Sect for money. At the same time, the Heavenly Ink Sect was one of the five major powers, so the manor couldn't afford to offend them either. They could only swallow the bitter loss in silence.
By now, the rest of the Heavenly Ink Sect members had also learned the story from the crowd. They looked at each other in shock.
Standing in the crowd, Wei He spotted Wan Qingqing.
She stood staring at the damaged building, her eyes hollow, lost in thought. An inner court junior brother beside her kept babbling on, but she did not seem to hear a word.
Wei He excused himself from Zhang Lingxu, who was still talking non-stop, and walked over at a steady pace.
"Senior Sister Wan."
"Junior Brother Wei, you're here too?" Wan Qingqing snapped back to reality and gave him a faint smile.
"Yeah, I just arrived. I ran into a couple of fellow disciples on the way, so we decided to walk together for safety," Wei He nodded. He offered a small smile. "I originally planned to join your tournament. My strength isn't much, but I thought I could at least help you block a few pesky flies. Who would have guessed something like this would happen out of nowhere?"
He let out a sigh. "The world really is getting more chaotic by the day."
Wan Qingqing seemed to understand his point. She looked at him with a warm, appreciative gaze.
"It's fine. I appreciate the thought."
She did not pry any further. Since Wei He said he was delayed on the road, she took his word for it.
"Senior Sister, you don't look too good. Is everything alright?" Wei He asked, slightly puzzled.
He had already crippled the main culprit, so why did she still look so down?
"I'm fine. I just remembered a few unpleasant things, that's all." Wan Qingqing was certainly relieved about Yan Jiuhai, but the earlier candidate screening had left a deep scar on her view of her fellow disciples.
"Qingqing."
A few people walked over from the side. Among them were Zhou Yugui, the white-porcelain-like Shu Yumo, and another handsome man with sharp eyebrows named You Bufan.
As the three approached, Shu Yumo opened her mouth to speak, but Wan Qingqing cut her off.
"You're here," Wan Qingqing said before anyone else could. "Thank you all for coming to support me today."
She smiled, and while her expression was as gentle as ever, there was a sudden cold distance in her voice.
During the previous screening, Zhou Yugui had been present the entire time, yet he had shown no intention of stepping up to help her.
"Bufan, it was a long journey for you. Things are a bit complicated today, so I'll have to trouble you to take Yumo back home later." She looked at You Bufan with a serious face.
"No problem," You Bufan replied. He had no idea what had happened, but his sharp instincts told him that something subtle had changed in his close friend.
He wanted to say more, but Wan Qingqing spoke up first again.
"Junior Brother Wei has something to discuss with me, so I won't stay to keep you company. I'll take my leave first," she said with a polite smile.
Wei He caught her cue instantly and chimed in. "Then I must trouble Senior Sister for a bit of your time."
"Don't mention it. Let's go."
The two walked off in harmony, heading toward the shores of Peacock Lake.
Along the water ran a small stone path built for visitors, dotted with occasional viewing pavilions. This park-like scenery suited Wei He's taste quite well.
Wan Qingqing led the way, with Wei He following close behind. They soon reached a quiet, secluded spot by the lake.
"Junior Brother Wei," Wan Qingqing slowed her pace and turned around. "Thank you for earlier."
"It was nothing. But those people..." Wei He hesitated, as he did not recognize the group.
"It's fine. I was just naive," Wan Qingqing smiled bitterly. "Honestly, I have to thank you for the letter you gave me before."
"Oh? I'm glad it helped." Wei He did not ask for details. He had written it on a whim, but it seemed some people were even lower than he had expected.
"I thought I had many friends, but when real trouble hits, I realize I never had a single true friend to begin with," Wan Qingqing turned toward the water, watching the colorful light reflect off the surface, and sighed.
"Some of them think they hide it so well. But whenever they lie, they always make small habits they aren't even aware of, all while thinking they've fooled everyone."
Wei He remained silent, knowing she had been deeply hurt by the truth.
Hard times reveal true hearts; that was simply human nature.
He did not interject much, simply listening as she rambled on about her childhood memories with Zhou Yugui, Shu Yumo, and You Bufan.
Across the lake, a team of repair workers led by manor staff was hauling materials toward Shanhe Building. The ant-like hustle on the other side formed a stark contrast with the quiet stillness of the two standing here.
After talking for a while, Wan Qingqing suddenly stopped and turned to look at Wei He.
"By the way, Junior Brother Wei, how long has it been since you reached the Third Layer?"
"I don't quite remember. It's been a while." Between his chaotic routine and his daily research on combining medicine with martial arts, Wei He had lost track of time. "Probably close to a year," he answered.
"Even though it's been a year, you spend all your time practicing behind closed doors. You probably don't know much about the techniques of other courts or sects, right?" Wan Qingqing smiled softly.
"Want to spar?" She raised her hands, setting up the opening stance of the Heart Severing Seal.
"Sure." Wei He knew she needed an outlet to vent her feelings. He hadn't expected Senior Sister Wan to hide such suppressed emotions under her gentle exterior.
The two stepped back to create space.
Wei He raised his hands into the exact same opening stance of the Heart Severing Seal.
After a brief pause, both rushed forward at the same time. Driven by their inner force, their four palms clashed like fluttering butterflies, moving so fast they almost turned into blurs.
Rain-Covering Force against Rain-Covering Force.
Wei He's version of the force was still unrefined. Facing Wan Qingqing, who was far stronger than him, he immediately fell into a disadvantage.
He instantly adjusted his strategy. Instead of taking her attacks head-on, he shifted to side strikes and deflections.
The Heart Severing Seal focused on speed, density, and precision, making it a perfect fit for the Rain-Covering Force.
Their inner force spilled out into the air. After dozens of fast exchanges, Wei He gradually lost ground.
Without using the monstrous strength of his Whale Strength Skill, he was completely outmatched in speed, power, and inner force density.
Bone Forging seemed to possess a unique physical enhancement, allowing a woman with a much smaller frame like Wan Qingqing to completely overpower him in raw physical capabilities.
Suddenly, Wei He shifted his technique. He switched to the Mountain Return Fist, powering it with his Rain-Covering Force.
The sudden shift caught Wan Qingqing off guard. Her palm struck empty air, and a sudden hook punch from Wei He came speeding toward her face.
Wan Qingqing locked her waist and unexpectedly pulled out a different palm strike that was not part of the Heart Severing Seal.
That single move broke the dam. Neither of them restricted themselves to official sect techniques anymore, throwing in all kinds of different moves.
Once a martial artist reached the Inner Force stage, every talented fighter would study various moves and secret arts. This allowed them to create an edge when their inner force levels were close.
In truth, unless the difference in force was overwhelming, fights between martial artists were usually decided by other factors—such as secret arts, weapons, or mindset.
When inner force levels were similar, neither side could easily kill the other, as protective inner force was hard to break through with raw hits alone.
Wan Qingqing had spent years analyzing different martial styles on her own, while Wei He had trained in multiple martial arts himself. Both were naturally familiar with various moves.
After fighting for quite a while, Wan Qingqing suddenly gave a soft shout.
"Watch out!"
She stopped holding back, unleashing her Rain-Covering Force that was right on the verge of the Bone Forging tier.
Surges of inner force poured out along her arms like a rushing river, wrapping her limbs in a faint, invisible current of air. The massive density of force distorted the surrounding air, making the light bend around her hands.
"Take a good look! This is the Fourth Layer of the Rain-Covering Force!"
Wan Qingqing burst forward with a quick leap. Her palms doubled in speed, striking Wei He several times in the chest like lightning.
Wei He could not react in time. His hands were only halfway back to defend when the strikes hit him.
His heart sank.
The Fourth Layer was the very level he had yet to master. Reaching it was the final step before pushing toward Bone Forging.
He used to think the gap between the Third and Fourth Layers was small, but now he realized the Rain-Covering Force was a style that scaled heavily in the late game.
The more force you accumulated, the faster and stronger it became.
He had always felt that in terms of raw impact, the Rain-Covering Force was inferior to the skills of other martial artists outside the sect. You had to find an enemy's weakness before delivering a decisive blow, which felt a bit impractical to him. In short, before finding a flaw, the skill didn't boost fighting power by much.
Now, he saw its true power.
"I win this one," Wan Qingqing pulled her hands back and stepped away, standing still with a smile.
"Thank you for the guidance, Senior Sister," Wei He said politely, carefully feeling the few strands of Fourth Layer force that had seeped into his body. This was the best possible chance to study the Fourth Layer from the inside out.
Sending inner force into another person's body without causing harm took immense skill and energy.
He knew this was her way of thanking him for the letter. This short exchange alone would save him months of hard work.
A moment later, Wei He snapped back from his thoughts. He had a feeling that it wouldn't take long for him to break through to the Fourth Layer.
This layer was mostly about building up raw inner force, with very few conceptual hurdles. With Wan Qingqing's direct guidance today, his progress would be even faster.
"No need to thank me. Moving from the Third to the Fourth Layer isn't complicated; it just requires accumulating a massive amount of force. If you run into issues in the future, you can always come find me."
After the intense match, Wan Qingqing seemed to have let off some steam, and the knot in her heart had loosened up quite a bit.
"Alright. Thank you, Senior Sister," Wei He nodded earnestly.
