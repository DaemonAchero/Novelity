Chapter 29
Wei He said no more. He just kept grinding his blood and qi.
Ouyang Zhuang knew how his senior brother was. The man was usually silent, hated idle talk, and cared about nothing except hard training. So, Ouyang Zhuang said nothing either and joined him in the grind.
By noon, after a quick meal, Wei He walked back to his usual spot near the wall, standing right under the hot sun.
He stared at the sandbags, black stones, and medicine tubs he trained with day and night. He just stood there, silent for a long time.
He really wanted to rely on his own power to break through. But deep down, he knew the truth. His talent was totally average. Reaching this current level was already his absolute limit. Without the Break-Through Bead, he would likely stay stuck right here for the rest of his life.
But what if? What if he could break through on his own by some miracle?
Wei He was deeply torn inside.
He could not help but hesitate. The Break-Through Bead was just too hard to fill up. It took him so long just to get one charge. He really could not afford to waste it.
More importantly, he wanted to know if his talent was truly that bad. What if he had some hidden potential he had not noticed yet?
After a long silence, Wei He shook his head. He decided to push himself for two more days.
He took a short nap, and soon the crowd in the yard began another round of brutal training.
In the huge yard, new faces were getting fewer. Old faces were disappearing too.
Wei He trained hard all afternoon, but he felt zero progress in his blood and qi. Nothing moved at all.
Bad mood heavy on his mind, he walked back to his family’s small compound.
His second sister, Wei Ying, was taking in the dried laundry with a wooden tub. She happened to grab her own underwear and skirts. When she saw Wei He walk in, she quickly hid her inner clothes under her long skirts.
She touched up her long hair, tried to hide her red face, and turned around.
"You're back? Dinner is ready. Sister Zhang from next door came over to chat for a bit, so I got delayed a little."
"It's fine. I'll go change." Wei He shut the door behind him and walked to his bedroom.
To keep proper distance, his room and his sister's room were on opposite ends of the small house. Even so, it was only a dozen steps across the yard, and they could easily hear any noise.
Wei Ying brought out the food and set the small wooden table under the eaves in the middle of the yard.
It was almost dark. Poor families always ate fast using the last bit of daylight, so they could save money on lamp oil. Even though they had a bit more money now, they kept this habit.
Sitting under the roof edge, Wei Ying picked up her bowl and looked at her brother.
"When Sister Zhang was here earlier, she mentioned something. I know it has nothing to do with you, but I thought I should tell you anyway."
"What is it?" Wei He asked, looking up. "Is someone trying to find you a husband again?"
"No, of course not!" Wei Ying said, feeling embarrassed. "It's about that girl in your yard, Li Huanhuan. Sister Zhang told me to keep an eye on you so you don't get close to her."
"Li Huanhuan?" Wei He thought for a second before he remembered who that was.
She was the new girl who took over Wei Ying's job to care for Old Zheng for free. She was pretty, and while her figure was not as nice as his sister's, she was still quite attractive.
He did not remember much about her because he only cared about his training. Besides, that girl liked to hang around rich, high-status disciples anyway. They had zero interaction.
"What about her?" Wei He asked, taking a bite of scrambled eggs and tomatoes.
"She... Sister Zhang said she has messy relationships with several men in the yard..." Wei Ying felt bad talking about someone behind their back.
"Messy relationships?" Wei He was surprised for a moment, then understood.
"Yeah, not good ones. Sister Zhang saw her coming out of different disciples' rooms more than once. Just be careful and stay away from her," Wei Ying said softly. "True or not, it's better to keep your distance."
"I know." Wei He recalled that Li Huanhuan indeed acted a bit too close when greeting some of the men.
In a yard full of muscular guys sweating and training every day, with their blood running hot, it would be extremely easy for a woman like her to get what she wanted.
Wei He made a mental note to watch out.
He picked up his bowl and started shoving rice into his mouth again. But after just a few bites, someone knocked on the main door.
Knock, knock, knock.
"Is anyone home?" A thick male voice came from outside.
"Who is it?" Wei He stood up and walked to the door.
"I am the younger brother of your neighbor, Xu Chun. Please open up," the man yelled.
Wei He narrowed his eyes. He paused, then looked back and signaled Wei Ying to hide in the inner room.
Wei Ying got the point right away. She quietly stood up, slipped into the inner room, and shut the door tight.
Only Wei He was left in the yard.
He stood before the door, pulled back the wooden latch, and opened it.
A large, burly man stood outside. He had a rough beard and wore a white top with sleeves rolled up, showing thick arms.
Seeing Wei He open the door, the man narrowed his eyes and scanned Wei He up and down.
"Sorry to bother you. My sister is Xu Chun. I am Xu Wei. A few days ago, my sister was beaten badly at home. Now she is blind, can't see a thing, and can't take care of herself. So I want to ask the neighbors, did you hear anything weird coming from next door that day?"
This man had slow, deep breathing, a steady heartbeat, and smooth, strong muscles rather than bulky ones.
Most importantly, his eyes kept darting around, as if he was checking to see if anyone else was nearby!
Wei He's alarm went off instantly.
"Noise next door? Didn't hear a thing. I'm going to sleep soon, leave if you have nothing else," he said, acting annoyed while trying to shut the door.
"Hold on."
The man calling himself Xu Wei slapped his hand against the door, stopping it from closing.
"We are neighbors here. My sister is blind now. I just came to ask a simple question. You can't even give me a few seconds to talk?" Xu Wei's face went cold.
His sister was just trying to rest at home and stopped causing trouble for the cult, yet this still happened to her.
"I don't know what you are talking about. Take your hand off!" Wei He glared at him.
"Heh, my friend, you know something, don't you?" Xu Wei let out a cold laugh.
They were so close to each other. One side was getting beaten up, and the other side heard nothing at all? How was that possible? He was clearly hiding something.
"I know nothing. Get your hand off!" Wei He raised his voice.
"Why should I? Why are you so nervous? Don't tell me... you were the one who beat up my sister?!" Xu Wei recalled the state of his sister's eyes.
The doctor told him someone threw quicklime into her eyes, blinding her after a long time. But his sister rarely went out and mostly burned incense at home. She never pissed anyone off, so why would anyone throw lime at her?
Xu Wei's eyes swept down and caught sight of the quicklime pouch hanging at the back of Wei He's waist.
"It WAS you!!!" His eyes went wide as saucers, and he lunged forward wildly.
He threw a harsh Palm of the White Lotus straight at Wei He's chest.
At the exact same time, Wei He pulled back his right fist, packed it with full power, and smashed it straight back at him.
Fist met palm head-on.
BANG!
The violent hit pushed both Wei He and Xu Wei a step back. Surprise showed on both their faces.
Wei He felt a huge wave of power surge from the man's palm, easily stopping his punch. This was a fully powered Mountain-Turning Punch, yet he could not push through.
Xu Wei was equally shocked.
He did not use 100% of his power in that strike, but he did use 60%. Yet this guy blocked it cold.
As an Incense Master of the Incense-Taking Cult, he knew right away that this guy was a tough nut to crack. The kid's strength was not far off from his own.
He stepped back and lowered his stance.
"So you are a disciple of the Mountain-Turning School. My bad, must be a mistake. My sister has stick marks all over her body. The attacker was good with a short staff. Now her old injuries are acting up and she is hurt badly. If you know anything, please tell me the truth. I will reward you properly later!" Xu Wei said seriously.
"I really don't know. I was training in my yard that day, hitting the sandbag very loudly. I had no idea what happened next door," Wei He softened his tone, knowing this guy was trouble.
"For real?" Xu Wei asked, his eyes relaxing a bit, thinking he might have made a wrong guess.
"For real," Wei He answered firmly.
"...Fine. Sorry for bothering you then." Xu Wei took a hard look at Wei He, turned around, and walked away quickly.
After leaving Wei He's front door, he made a turn and walked into a dark alley.
Xu Wei slowed his pace. Two men were already waiting in the alley, both wearing gray-white clothes and round straw hats.
"Incense Master? How did it go?" one whispered.
"Not good. That yard belongs to an official disciple of the Mountain-Turning School. He is strong. I can't take him down alone without him running away. Just to be safe, go back and tell Incense Master Shangguan to come help me out."
Xu Wei's eyes flashed with pure bloodlust. "No matter what, that kid has a lime pouch on his waist, yet he lied to my face saying he heard nothing. Is that even possible? At this distance, even if my sister wasn't screaming, he would have heard something. Especially with sharp senses like his!
It was definitely him! Or at least someone close to him. Before my sister fell sick, she went to his family's bun shop, and he kicked her out. This little bastard held a grudge and did it behind my back. Did he really think I couldn't figure it out?"
Xu Wei was no idiot. He put the clues together and instantly found the most likely suspect.
"Now go and come back fast! Bring Incense Master Hu along too. I will watch this spot so he can't run. Anyone who messes with our Incense-Taking Cult has no idea how to spell death!" Xu Wei said coldly.
"Got it!" The two turned around and ran off toward the other end of the alley.
With three Incense Masters working together, there was no way that kid would survive!
A dark rage burned in Xu Wei's chest.
On the other side.
Inside the yard.
Wei He shut the door and backed up two steps. The more he thought about it, the more wrong it felt.
When that man left, his eyes were shifting and his fists were clenched tightly. He was clearly not letting this go.
