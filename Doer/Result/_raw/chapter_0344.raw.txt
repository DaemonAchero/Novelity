"I'm fine, Senior Sister. None of this blood is mine," Wei He said, shaking his head.
Seeing the deep concern in Yao Wan's eyes, he felt a bit surprised, but having someone care about his safety warmed his heart.
"What about you, Senior Sister? How are you holding up? Can you still walk?" he asked, frowning.
"I'm alright, but I can't fight again anytime soon. Otherwise..." Yao Wan shook her head with a bitter smile.
She couldn't remember how many years it had been since she fought this hard. But as she looked at Wei He, her determination grew stronger.
It was all worth it.
The Xuanmiao Sect hadn't seen a top-tier genius in a long time. If Wei He could grow into one, it would be a blessing for the entire sect.
"What do we do now? Go straight back?" Wei He asked.
"Take a detour. We need to send word back to the sect as fast as possible. Once we reach any medium-sized town, we can use the sect's outpost to send an urgent message. But that's just the usual way. With our speed, running all the way back won't take long anyway. Instead of wasting time looking for an outpost, we go straight back to the headquarters!"
"Got it!" Wei He nodded.
He hadn't expected to run into such a mess right after coming down the mountain. But things were already done, and there was no point dwelling on it.
The two immediately locked down their direction and moved out at full speed.
In less than half an hour, they reached Zhentao City, crossed the sea by boat, and made it back to Tianhai Island.
Finally, they reported the attack on the border outpost to the higher-ups.
...
"What?! The outpost at Black Pine Town was hit too?!"
Qing Meizi, the Chief of the Qianliu Lineage, stood up abruptly from his seat, his face stiff with shock.
They were inside the Main Hall of the Xuanmiao Sect.
Qing Meizi stared at Yao Wan and Wei He. Both were covered in blood, having had no time to clean themselves up.
On both sides of the hall stood the senior figures of the sect's three lineages, who had just rushed over.
In the entire sect, there were only about a dozen True Realm masters, and Five Locks Mountain alone accounted for five of them, not including Yuan Duzi.
Now that everyone was gathered, Yuan Duzi frowned slightly as she looked at Qing Meizi, who had lost his usual composure.
"It seems the Wu Army wants to distract us," Yuan Duzi said, her voice heavy. "Even if this isn't their main force, their size alone is enough to cause us real trouble."
"The main force isn't the problem..." Qing Meizi said, his expression dark. "A lot of new patrol officers went to Black Pine Town before this, and none returned. The reports claimed they were killed by True Beasts..."
The meaning behind his words was crystal clear.
Yuan Duzi and Bai Hezi, who stood nearby, both shifted their expressions.
"Without a traitor inside hiding the truth, something this big couldn't have stayed quiet for so long!" Qing Meizi's eyes turned sharp as blades as he scanned the dozen True Realm masters in the hall.
Everyone felt a chill, instantly understanding his point.
"Right now, the priority isn't finding the traitor, but handling the threat in front of us! The Wu Army is advancing in two columns. In Haizhou, there are only three cities capable of holding out. Combined, our troops only number around three hundred thousand. No one knows how well they will perform against the Wu forces."
Qing Meizi's voice turned harsh.
"Yuan Duzi, Bai Hezi, junior brothers and sisters, does anyone have a plan?" His gaze swept across the crowd.
"As far as I know, the Lifo Temple hasn't deployed any Grandmasters," Yuan Duzi spoke up quickly. "Relying only on the White-Robed Buddha and General Yuan Han, taking Haizhou and devouring our sect is impossible."
"Then what is their true goal...?" Qing Meizi frowned.
Moving a massive army cost huge amounts of supplies every day. The Wu Kingdom wouldn't waste food and resources for nothing. Since they launched this attack, they were bound to have a plan to deal with the Xuanmiao Sect.
Otherwise, an army without top-tier powerhouses would fall apart quickly against assassinations by True Realm masters.
They did possess grand formations meant to deal with Grandmasters, but the cost to use them was far too high to trigger easily.
While the leaders debated military strategy, Yuan Duzi signaled another disciple to lead Yao Wan and Wei He away so they could rest and tend to their wounds.
Wei He and Yao Wan followed the master out of the hall, both letting out a quiet sigh of relief.
At the very least, they had delivered the warning before everything blew up.
"By the way, where is my servant?" Wei He suddenly asked.
"...I didn't see her," Yao Wan replied, looking blank.
The three walked along a long corridor lined with thick stone pillars. Thinking about Zhou Muqing, Wei He stopped, feeling a bit worried.
"If you're worried, go to the Outer Mountain later and have someone look for her," Yao Wan said weakly.
"Alright..." Wei He agreed, turning his eyes back to her.
Just now, they hadn't told the whole truth inside the hall.
They only claimed that Yao Wan had risked her life to kill one of the Three-Face Buddhas under the White-Robed Buddha, and that both of them were injured before making a run back to report.
As for the other four who died by their hands, they pretended to know nothing about them.
This was Yao Wan's idea.
The goal was to erase all witnesses and buy Wei He as much time to grow as possible.
Everyone who saw them fight was already dead. As long as they kept their mouths shut, even Xu Dingchun—the White-Robed Buddha himself—would never believe that a newly ascended True Realm disciple had wiped out his five top lieutenants.
To be honest, if Yao Wan hadn't seen it with her own eyes, she wouldn't have believed it either.
It was best to let this secret rot in their hearts. Later, they could quietly explain the truth to Senior Sister Yuan Duzi in private. Since Yuan Duzi managed the sect's resources, slanting support toward Wei He would be effortless once she knew the full story.
They followed the master out of the corridor and stopped outside the heavily guarded main hall.
"Go back and get some good rest. Don't worry about anything else for now, just focus on completing your Sense Fixation. The rest will be handled by Master Senior Sister and the others," Yao Wan comforted him.
Wei He nodded. His task was done.
Now that the news was delivered, there was nothing left for him to do. While his actual combat strength was impressive among True Realm fighters, a giant like the Xuanmiao Sect was never short of powerful masters.
Not to mention the three Lineage Chiefs.
Qing Meizi, Bai Hezi, and Yuan Duzi were all terrifying monsters far above the rest. Yuan Duzi in particular had dominated the sect for over a century, easily crushing any junior disciple in just a few moves. Her true depth remained completely unknown.
With such a powerhouse guarding the sect, Wei He felt much safer. If the sky fell, taller people were there to hold it up.
He packed his things, said goodbye to Yao Wan, and headed toward the Outer Mountain.
Since his Sense Fixation wasn't finished, he planned to stay at his own place for now, waiting for Zhou Muqing to return while continuing his training.
Next, his eyes were about to enter full Super-Sensing.
Once he pushed past this stage, he would move on to the second level of the Xuan Lock Force.
After all, while people used the number of completed Sense Fixations to measure a True Realm master's strength, what truly decided power was cultivation level, total energy reserves, and secret techniques.
Only deep energy could handle more Sense Fixations and allow force to undergo a true qualitative leap.
Clearing his mind, Wei He posted a search task at the Outer Mountain and then shut himself inside his house on Tianhai Island to cultivate in total isolation.
Half a month passed in the blink of an eye.
Outside, the world was changing fast.
The Xuanmiao Sect joined forces with the Haizhou military, sending out almost all their top fighters. A combined force of three hundred thousand soldiers clashed brutally with the Wu Army across the frozen barrens of Haizhou.
The Xuanmiao Sect had the home advantage, long used to the biting cold of Haizhou.
The Wu Army, however, suffered greatly.
Even though the Lifo Temple later sent three more Buddha Masters alongside the White-Robed Buddha, taking control of separate squads, only the high-ranking commanders were immune to the freezing cold. The ordinary soldiers couldn't take it. Their siege weapons constantly broke down in the deep snow and ice.
Taking advantage of this, Qing Meizi laid out a series of traps to lure the enemy in. Working together with Yuan Duzi, Bai Hezi, and reinforcements from the Wanfei Palace, they caught a break.
In one massive clash, they heavily wounded General Yuan Han, the three Buddha Masters, and a large group of Lifo Temple commanders, killing several on the spot.
Wei He knew nothing of these intense battles.
Ever since he delivered the message, he had ignored everything outside, keeping his doors locked to focus entirely on his training.
A month flew by.
Finally, he reached the final step.
Sealing—locking away the perception that had stayed in a state of Super-Sensing for so long.

Clang... Clang... Clang...
The distant sound of a bell woke Wei He from his deep meditation.
He slowly opened his eyes and looked around the quiet room.
The space was empty except for a painting hanging on the wall opposite him—a painting of a bright moon and a giant whale, representing the Whale Flood Art. He loved the calm mood of the painting, so he used it as his focus tool during meditation.
"It should be time..."
Wei He looked up. A ray of bright sunlight fell from the room's only air vent.
He reached out his hand, letting the light rest on his palm.
During this past month, he hadn't stepped outside once, all to wait for this final moment. Leaving the room risked running into True Beasts or creating unnecessary trouble.
"It's almost time to seal it..." Wei He glanced at the hourglass sitting in the corner.
There were only a few minutes left before the sand ran out.
As the final moment approached, Wei He took a deep breath, filling his chest with air.
Then, he stood up slowly.
Having stayed in the Super-Sensing state for a whole month, his eyes could now maintain it smoothly without flickering during movement.
Wei He had always been curious about what the world looked like under Super-Sensing.
Now, he could finally experience it himself.
His Realizing Force swept through the room regularly, so no strange creatures could exist inside. His eyes saw nothing unusual here.
Driven by curiosity, Wei He adjusted his clothes and walked to the door.
He reached out, grabbed the handle, and unlocked it.
This was his first time using pure Super-Sensing eyes to look at the real world.
He wanted to see what the true world actually looked like.
With a mix of curiosity and tension, Wei He pulled the door open.
Creak.
