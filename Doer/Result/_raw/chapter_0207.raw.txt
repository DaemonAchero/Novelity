Between the dark green sea of trees, a pale yellow dirt road wound like a ribbon. It slowly crossed rivers and hills, stretching toward the distant horizon.
A brass donkey bell jingled softly with every step, making a crisp sound.
Tang Huanhuan and Yuchi Qi rode their small donkeys without any rush, taking their time along the road.
By now, Xuanjing City was far behind them. They were no longer anywhere near its main roads.
Back at the city, the Yuchi family of the Chijing Army was likely in complete chaos.
Sorting out the fires and fixing the destroyed supply line were their real priorities. They could not afford to waste endless time and manpower hunting two people down.
"If I were in charge of the Chijing Army," Tang Huanhuan said with a faint smile, "I would use the large, slow main forces to fight the fires. Then I would form an elite squad to hunt down the arsonists."
"Too bad," Yuchi Qi laughed beside her. "The only person with the rank and authority to make that call—Yuchi Yan—is dead."
She paused, then asked, "Teacher, where are we heading next? Are we meeting up with the others at the place you mentioned before?"
"Yes, no need to rush. The other decoy teams will draw the pursuers away. I set up three decoys—more than enough to pull away most of their experts. Anyone left over won't be a match for us anyway."
Tang Huanhuan stayed completely calm. Everything was under control.
They traveled for a full day and night. Reports from the three decoy teams confirmed that the Chijing Army was in total disarray. They had not even managed to send out a proper search party yet.
"There is a tea stall ahead," Tang Huanhuan said, looking into the distance. "Past that is open wilderness. It's probably the last tea stall around, so let's rest, get some water, and eat before moving on."
Ahead by the road, right next to a relay station, stood a simple tea shed.
An old man and woman were inside boiling tea, while two burly men in tight outfits sat drinking and chatting.
Nearby, a muscular man in a sleeveless vest squatted on the grass, chewing on food with a single-edged saber resting by his hand. Judging by where he stood, he belonged to the stall as well.
The two women did not mind. A wilderness tea stall like this could not survive without some self-defense. Beasts or thugs would have destroyed it otherwise.
Because of that, prices at these stalls were usually high.
They dismounted, led their donkeys to the shed, and tied them to a wooden post.
"Vendor, bring a pot of tea, a few tea eggs, and some wild herb cakes."
Tang Huanhuan was used to traveling and ordered the standard tea stall set. The herb cakes were not made of grass, but wild greens and dried tofu mixed with coarse grains to make stuffed cakes.
"Coming right up!" The old woman came over with a cheerful smile, quickly wiped the table with a rag, and invited them to sit.
She showed no fear toward their martial arts attire. Stalls like this dealt with all kinds of travelers and preferred doing business with martial artists. When served well, these customers gave generous tips, often leaving the change behind if they were in a rush.
Soon, the tea was served alongside the snacks, including a small plate of black melon seeds.
These were processed watermelon seeds, glossy black, and rich with a deep aroma that instantly stirred their appetites.
Just then, a merchant caravan arrived along the road, heading toward Xuanjing City.
The caravan brought four ox carts, heavily loaded with unseen goods.
Two middle-aged men wearing round hats decorated with jade walked into the tea stall with a sigh. They sat in a corner and began talking softly about business.
The caravan hands sat or squatted on the nearby grass, eating their dried rations and drinking water.
Yuchi Qi poured tea for her teacher and herself. Then she spread out two handkerchiefs and split the melon seeds evenly between them.
She picked up a seed, about to eat it.
"Wait!" Tang Huanhuan suddenly snapped.
"What is it?" Yuchi Qi froze, looking across at her master.
Tang Huanhuan narrowed her eyes. She picked up a single black seed and dropped it straight into her tea.
Sizzle!
The seed reacted instantly with the liquid, releasing a plume of sharp, white steam.
"It's poison!"
Yuchi Qi slammed her hands down and stood up.
The moment she stood, the atmosphere in the stall shifted completely.
"This stall is a trap!" Yuchi Qi glared at the old couple boiling tea.
The two old people looked totally confused, as if they had no idea what was happening.
Yuchi Qi immediately channeled her inner force and charged forward.
Bang!
The two burly men sitting on the right suddenly leaped up, lunging at Tang Huanhuan. They swung their weapons straight for her waist.
"Looking to die!" Tang Huanhuan stood up and struck out with both hands, taking on both attackers at once.
Clang! Clang!
The moment their weapons clashed, she felt something off. Their strikes lacked power, as if they barely put any effort into the attack.
The two men used the recoil to slip away to the sides, refusing to engage her further.
Wrong!
Her heart chilled. She spun around.
She saw her disciple, Yuchi Qi, charging the old couple.
Halfway there, both old people grinned and tossed aside what they were holding.
One grabbed a rag, snapped it open, and threw it. The cloth expanded to the size of a basin, blocking Yuchi Qi's vision.
The other bent low and kicked off the ground, shattering the earth beneath his feet. He lunged toward Yuchi Qi from beneath the floating cloth.
Their coordination was seamless.
"Not good!"
Tang Huanhuan realized instantly that these two were no ordinary peasants. Both were Bone Forging martial artists.
She tried to fall back to save her disciple, but the two merchants from the caravan suddenly sprang into action. They leaped at her from left and right, striking at her shoulders.
Mid-strike, two clouds of white powder burst from their hands, blinding her completely.
The powder reeked with a sharp stench—deadly poison.
In Xuanjing, the most famous poison user used to be Elder Ruan Qinghong of the Chaos God Sect. But after he died at Guiyan Tower, a new poison master had risen to fame: Wei He, the Sect Master of the Thousand Poison Sect.
The realization flashed through Tang Huanhuan's mind.
Through the white haze, a scream rang out.
Tang Huanhuan blasted her inner force outward to clear the powder. She saw Yuchi Qi collapsed on the ground, motionless.
She made a split-second decision to retreat. Leaping into the air, she snapped the wooden support pillar of the tea shed and shot outward.
At the same time, she flicked her wrist, releasing a shower of shiny silver needles behind her.
Painful grunts came from inside the white cloud. Some had been hit.
Tang Huanhuan broke out of the haze and sprinted straight for the dense forest beside the road without looking back.
Only the thick trees could give her a chance to escape.
She did not dare stay and fight. In that brief exchange, she sensed at least three Bone Forging masters and one Viscera Refining expert watching her from the shadows like a predator waiting for her to stumble.
Given the attack style and the sheer number of experts deployed here, only a few factions fit the bill.
The most likely one was the Thousand Poison Sect!
Tang Huanhuan bit her lip hard and pushed her movement technique to its limit.
She did not run far before a figure stepped into her path, waiting patiently.
It was a middle-aged woman with a calm face, wearing a pale red cloak and pure white gloves.
"Xie Yan of the Thousand Poison Sect?" Tang Huanhuan stopped abruptly. She knew she could not leave without striking this woman down.
"So it really is you!" she said coldly. There were only a handful of Viscera Refining experts in Xuanjing, and the Gold Jade Hall had thoroughly investigated them all before coming.
Xie Yan said calmly, "By order of the Sect Master, I am here to block your path. Vice Hall Master Tang, since you have traveled so far, why not come to our sect as a guest?"
"You think you can stop me alone?" Tang Huanhuan sneered. "If Wei He were here himself, I might be wary. But just you?"
"My strength is indeed lacking," Xie Yan replied, unaffected. Among Viscera Refining martial artists, she was on the weaker side.
"What if you add us?"
Figures emerged from the forest behind her.
Almost all the top experts of the Thousand Poison Sect had arrived.
Aside from Wan Ling, the old couple Elder Li and Elder Ceng, Du Han of the Red Earth Yard, Wan Qingqing of the Wan Qing Yard, and Xiao Qingyu of the Zheng Lin Yard were all present.
Only the martial artists below the Bone Forging stage had been left behind. In a fight of this level, ordinary martial artists were nothing more than cannon fodder.
Even so, the lineup was terrifying.
Five Bone Forging masters and one Viscera Refining expert, all surrounding a single target.
Because Bone Forging masters had protective inner force, they could take at least one hit from her without dying. As long as she could not kill them in a single blow, they could catch their breath and attack again.
A war of attrition.
With Xie Yan leading the assault, if they coordinated properly...
"Kill!"
Xie Yan gave the order.
Everyone flared their inner force. Led by Xie Yan, the six of them split into two groups and rushed Tang Huanhuan.
With three people per group, they covered every angle, rotating their attacks wave after wave to wear her down completely.
Facing this setup, even someone as confident as Tang Huanhuan felt her face turn pale.
She was not Wei He. She lacked his lethal poison methods and his terrifying speed that defied numerical disadvantage.
Though she was fast, her movements followed predictable patterns. And if her path could be predicted, she could not dodge a coordinated ambush.
Sunlight poured through the trees, warm and bright, but all she felt was a deep, penetrating cold.
"Do you think she can break out?" Wei He stood on a high branch, casually poking at a chick in a nearby bird's nest.
"She should be fine," Wang Shaojun said, setting down his book.
Beside him stood two black-clothed experts who did not look like natives of Taizhou. They were Bone Forging masters he had personally recruited, forming his private force.
Against an ordinary martial artist, throwing extra bodies was useless. But this joint hunt of Tang Huanhuan was a perfect chance to train his men in a real fight.
"Besides, you told them not to kill her, didn't you?" Wang Shaojun looked up.
"Right," Wei He nodded. "The Chaos God Sect has too many hidden tricks. If she panics and uses a deadly technique, losing even one of my people would be a waste. Besides, she had a clear direction while traveling this way. She must be meeting someone."
"So..."
"So, do you want to go down and test yourself?" Wei He smiled.
"So that was your plan all along." Wang Shaojun laughed softly, thinking it over. "Fine. I could use a little exercise."
"Don't push her too hard. Just bleed her a bit," Wei He warned.
"Understood."
Wang Shaojun smiled, set his book aside, and vanished into the forest.
Haa... haa... haa...
Tang Huanhuan clutched her right chest as blood seeped out slowly between her fingers.
The attack had been aimed at her heart, but she used her speed at the last second to shift her body, taking the hit on her right side instead.
This was not her first time being surrounded, but it was the first time against so many high-level experts.
With a Viscera Refining master leading the hunt, unless she could kill a Bone Forging master instantly, she was stuck in a battle of attrition.
In just a short moment, more than half of her inner force was gone. She knew that if this continued, she would die here.
She had traded injuries to wound two of them and break past the encirclement.
Golden beams of sunlight cut through the forest canopy, spreading warmth across the leaves.
Yet where her hand pressed against her bleeding chest, Tang Huanhuan felt only an icy, bone-chilling cold.
