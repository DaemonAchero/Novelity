Answer: Chapter 369
"A genius like Wei He cannot be judged simply by his number of sense-anchorings," Yuan Duzi said, shaking her head. "Going from one to five anchorings is just a basic increase in force quality. For Wei He, the boost from these realms is not as huge as you think."
"What do you mean, Senior Sister?" Wei He asked quickly.
"It is simple," Yuan Duzi smiled. "No matter how much force you have or how high its quality is, you still need to hit your target. At this stage, whether you have anchored once or five times, you still cannot beat someone at the Five Steps of True Perfection."
"Do you mean that after the Five Steps, a person's speed becomes extremely fast?" Wei He seemed to understand.
"Correct. If you want to match stronger opponents, you must either improve your movement technique or speed up the reaction of your True Return Force. The anchoring stage focuses entirely on building force quality. Your force quality is good enough now. What you lack is reaction speed. You are too slow."
Yuan Duzi did not talk about cultvation realms. She analyzed pure combat instead, making everything crystal clear to Wei He.
"Also, past the Five Steps, a martial artist does not just get faster force speed. Their body digs deeper into its hidden bloodline, showing signs of partial mutation. The body changes rapidly. Healing power, speed, and reaction time shoot up to a high level in a short time. So..."
Yuan Duzi looked at Wei He.
"Work hard on your cultivation. Every stage of the five anchorings is a hurdle. It will not give you a huge power jump, but it will extend your life and keep you alive. Once you get there, focusing on your techniques might bring great results."
She did not actually believe Wei He had more potential than the prime Dao Seed of the Golden Lotus Sect. Wei He was a combat genius, but his cultivation speed was only above average. He had little advantage over true geniuses and showed no world-shaking talent. He was only ahead because of his massive blood gift, which let him go a few steps further than other True Ones during the anchoring stage.
Yuan Duzi knew very well that Wei He held the upper hand among his peers for now. But later, those Dao Seeds would cultivate faster and faster, while Wei He's weak talent would slow him down until he hit his limit. That would be the real gap.
Of course, right now, Wei He could be used as a fighter close to the level of a Great Bodhisattva—just below a Buddha. That was already high-level power in any major sect. That was why Yuan Duzi remained calm after learning his limits. Wei He's future was not as bright as the Dao Seeds, but for now, he was much stronger than the rest.
"Wei He, I have a method to test your limits. Do you want to try it and see where your bone talent stops?" Huan Songzi asked suddenly. He was curious how far Wei He could go in the future.
Wei He was curious too. His bone talent was poor at first. He used the Breakthrough Bead to force his way through, rebuild his body, and raise his talent to above average. He wanted to know his true limit now.
"Can it really test that?" he asked.
"Of course," Huan Songzi said. He was playful and had no arrogant attitude. But he was no weakling. On Lock Mountain of the Xuanmiao Sect, his strength was second only to Yuan Duzi, far stronger than the Three Heroes. In the whole sect, he ranked fourth, right behind the three Chief Disciples. He had reached the Fifth Step of True Perfection five years ago. That was why Wei He could not defeat him no matter what. He was extremely close to the Buddha level. If he were not bad at direct kill-fights, he could be a Buddha in the Wu Country based on his realm alone.
Soon, Huan Songzi recited a chant. Wei He memorized it and slowly moved his force along the path. Threads of True Return Force moved through unused veins and channels in his body. Everything went smoothly at first, but when he hit the sixth barrier, he could not push any further.
"The sixth barrier... That is good enough," Huan Songzi nodded.
"Senior Brother, what does the sixth barrier mean?" Wei He asked in a deep voice.
"It means your bone talent can only support you up to the entry level of True Perfection," Huan Songzi explained. "But do not worry. You already have power close to the Fifth Step. What is there to fear? As long as you do not fight the Buddhas of Wu Country, you can go anywhere in the world. Even if you cannot break your limit, you can crush normal True Beasts easily. Arhats and Bodhisattvas are no match for you. Also, since your cultivation will get slower and your limit is fixed, training is a waste of time. Why not slow down and enjoy life? Let those genius Dao Seeds worry about big problems. Right?"
Huan Songzi was not good with words. He kept repeating that Wei He had bad talent, would cultivate slower, and had no future, so he might as well enjoy life instead of wasting time training.
Yuan Duzi smiled helplessly at the side and turned to leave.
"Tsk tsk, see that? Senior Sister is going to check on that kid Cai Menghuan again," Huan Songzi muttered, watching her back with envy.
"Cai Menghuan?" Wei He paused. He felt he had heard the name before.
"Yeah, the prime Dao Seed of our sect, one of the main pillars for the next generation. Much stronger than Xuan Zhen and Xuan Ning. He comes from the main line, so it is normal you do not know him," Huan Songzi shook his head. "That guy trains super fast. A normal True One takes thirty years on average to break through the first layer of the main art. That kid took only two years."
"Two years!?" Wei He was shocked. He reached his level quickly because the first two layers were just about gathering force, and his huge blood reserve gave him a shortcut. But that kid built everything from scratch. That speed was terrifying.
"He is naturally gifted and had some lucky encounters. He has already completed three anchorings, doing one every three years. He is the biggest hope of our sect. Many compare him to that monster in the Golden Lotus Sect, seeing him as our only hope against them. So normal people like us should not compare ourselves to monsters like that, or we will lose confidence." Huan Songzi patted Wei He's shoulder.
"Alright, go back and train hard... Oh wait, go back and enjoy life. Training will not do much for you anyway. Let's go have a drink together."
Wei He wanted to rip his mouth open. But after thinking for a moment, he shook his head. "No thanks. I have some problems in my training to look into."
He had no time to waste with Huan Songzi. The sect seemed safe for now, but the Great Yuan Dynasty was falling. Lots of resources and people were being taken, and the Allied Army was struggling. The fate of the empire was being eaten away by the Wu Country. Under such conditions, he was not sure how long the three Patriarchs and Senior Sister could protect the sect. The best way was to raise his own strength as much as possible.
"Fine, fine. You are no fun at all, living like a monk who only knows how to train," Huan Songzi sighed, disappointed he had no drinking partner. "I'm off then. Luckily I met another good friend recently, or life would be too boring."
He had clearly found someone else to drink and play with. With a few quick movements, he vanished from the field.
Once everyone left, Wei He headed back to his honeycomb cave in the outer peak. At first, he found the cave empty and boring. But after staying there longer, he grew used to it. Sometimes, when he went elsewhere, he felt the world was too noisy compared to the quiet cave.
He left the Cloud Terrace and reached his cave. He lit a stick of incense to calm his mind, drank some water, and cleared his body of waste. Then he sat down with cross legs to meditate.
Threads of dark grey force drifted out like smoke. This was his current True Return Force. After half a year, he had turned all his force into the second-layer Deep Lock Force. He had also reached the peak limit of visualizing the Attraction God. But the conversion took longer than he expected.
The second layer of Deep Lock Force was the start of God Visualization. After changing the force, one needed to sink the god into the acupoints to store power, using it only during battle. Wei He had finished converting his force, but sinking the god was still stuck. He tried to break through on his own, but he felt he was still a bit short.
Today's fight with Huan Songzi made him impatient. Huan Songzi beat him easily without even using his sealing power. If he used the sealing effect of the Deep Lock Force, Wei He's force might get sealed completely in just a few moves. Even though Huan Songzi was one of the strongest people in the sect and ranked in the top thirty of the Great Yuan Records, Wei He still hated being defeated so easily.
Today, his talent limit was tested as the entry step of True Perfection. Both Huan Songzi and Yuan Duzi thought this was his cap. But Wei He did not think so. He knew his talent was terrible from the start, and he fixed it step by step through hard work—and the Breakthrough Bead. He was sure he could find another way around this so-called limit.
And now, it was time to break the wall. With his foundation, breaking through the first two layers should not be too hard, so doing it now would not look strange.
Wei He closed his eyes and summoned the Attraction God in his mind. This time, he did not try to control it himself to sink it into his acupoints. Instead, he moved his focus to the Breakthrough Bead on his chest.
'Do it.'
He liked to try hard on his own first. When he failed, he used the bead to help. That way, he could see where he made mistakes and fix his own cultivation path. He could not rely entirely on the bead. If it ever vanished, he would become useless overnight.
Snap!
A sharp sound rang in his mind. Wei He felt a surge of heat in his chest.
A massive rush of hot energy poured out from the bead's patterns, driving straight into the dozens of acupoints where the god was held. Soon, the heat turned into net-like threads that wrapped around the Attraction God ahead of time.
The nets sank slowly and touched one of the acupoints, taking root like plants in the surrounding flesh. Then, more heat poured into the acupoint, coating its inner wall to strengthen the flesh.
A few minutes later, under the pull of the net, the Attraction God lowered bit by bit until the force inside the acupoint caught it and pulled it deeper. Silent and quick, it sank into the acupoint and disappeared.
"So I was missing the steps inside the acupoints," Wei He realized.
His god visualization was completely different from others. According to the secret books, others only needed to build a net out of True Return Force to pull the god into the acupoints. But his Attraction God held ninety-nine percent of his massive force. His total force was at least thirty times larger than a normal True One at the same level. Nourishing a single god with that much force created a huge result. That was why his normal net could not move the Attraction God.
The bead's operation gave him new inspiration. It made him realize that cultivation did not mean following books blindly. He needed to use his own brain and find his own ways.
