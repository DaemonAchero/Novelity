Wei He went stiff. Wasn't this just asking him to marry into her family and take her name?
Even though he was a soul from the modern world who respected women, he was the only son of the Wei family. If he married into the Wan family as a matrilocal husband...
And all his kids had to take the surname Wan...
He could not accept this.
If his path ended here, if his martial arts stopped growing and his future was dead, maybe he would say yes.
But he had a massive future ahead of him. If he took this deal and married into her family, his dead parents would turn in their graves from anger.
His sister Wei Ying would feel awful too.
In this dark era, being a man who married into a woman's family was seen as a shame.
No matter the reason, the moment people heard it, they would think the man had no pride, no skill, and was just a pathetic free-loader.
"I'm sorry, Master. This... I cannot accept," Wei He went quiet for a moment, then gave a firm, clear refusal.
"If you agree, all the money of the Wan family and the two meat fields will belong to you. Both Qingqing and I will protect your path in martial arts," Wan Ling said seriously.
The more Wei He refused, the more she thought he was a man of high honor and pride.
After all, just by saying yes, he would gain a strong backer and a beautiful, gentle wife. Yet Wei He wasn't swayed by the huge reward at all.
"I am sorry," Wei He lowered his head, refusing to say another word.]
