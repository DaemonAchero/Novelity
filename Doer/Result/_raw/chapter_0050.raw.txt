He lifted his stained red thumb, holding it in midair over the paper. Yet, he could not force it down.
"Young Head, please," the man in the black coat urged again.

In this single day alone, he had three ruined families to handle. The Yonghe Escort Agency was just one of them.

But he had no choice.
The enemy was the Zhao family—the second strongest among the Seven Alliance. In all of Feiye City, only the Ou family was bigger than them.
Closing his eyes, he grit his teeth and forced his finger down.
Creak.

Wei He stood at the door, out of breath. He stared directly at Cheng Shaojiu in his seat, as well as the crowd of people around him.

One copy of the Wuling Palm could get at least five hundred taels of gold, even without its core diagram or medicine recipe.
Of course, if that still was not enough, he could just head back to the mountains later. With a bit of luck, he might bring back a few hundred more taels.
Now that he was skilled, Wei He knew the mountain forests outside the city like the back of his hand. It was just like his own home.
"You..." Cheng Shaojiu felt that the best thing he had ever done in his life was becoming friends with Wei He!

Tear!

At this point, the money on the floor was more than enough for the escort agency to get through this crisis.

On Shaoyang Mountain.
Wei He let out a long sigh as he looked at the thick, dead trees in front of him.
After several months, he was finally back here to look for money again.
The only reason he came back here was because on the other mountain peaks, most bandits would run far away the moment they saw his outfit. They would not even let him see their faces.
He did not even get a chance to get close, let alone make money.
In truth, he could understand why. After all, counting them up, the bandits who had died by his hands were no fewer than a few dozen.
And every single one of them was an elite.
With a physique like his, normal bandits without real strength would never dare to rob him.
So the ones he targeted were all the confident ones.
"I just lent that money to Brother Cheng, and now I do not even have money for groceries. Pure impulse, pure impulse."
Wei He felt a bit of regret in his heart. If he had known, he would have kept a dozen taels of gold to get by.
Now he did not even have money for food for the next half of the month.
In a moment of hot-headedness, he had thrown out even his gold beans.
He walked carefully along the thick dry grass on the mountain, heading toward the cave Ding Hai had left him before.
That place was not a natural shelter, but a temporary spot Ding Hai used when hunting.
Wei He planned to go there first to see if he could bump into other hunters.
That cave was known to many hunters, not just him.
Everyone had a silent agreement to leave some common tools inside as spares to help each other out.
Following the open trail, Wei He kept his ears open, moving swiftly and quietly.
He kept his noises as quiet as possible.
His reason for going up Shaoyang Mountain again was a rumor he heard at the winery. They said strange beasts had been appearing on Shaoyang Mountain lately, and people often saw members of the Shaoyang Sect hunting them.
Strange beasts.
The moment Wei He heard the name, he kept it firmly in mind.
He changed his outfit, no longer wearing his masked disguise, prepared all his gear, and went up the mountain once more.
Even though he had killed a Shaoyang Sect member, weeks had passed. Who could possibly know he was the killer?
Who?
Back then, besides the tall grass and trees, no one had seen a thing.
Even the only group that had met him—those bandits—were all dead now.
So in theory, he was still just an ordinary hunter.
After quietly moving through the mountain for half an hour.

A bandit with a gray-white headscarf and a blade was squatting next to a corpse on the ground, seemingly looting it.
Wei He stopped his steps and recognized the gray-white headscarf.
He had killed plenty of bandits recently, so he knew how these groups were split up.
Anyone wearing a white headscarf was a worker under the Shaoyang Sect's control.
So the guy in front of him should be the same.
He thought for a moment and decided not to act rashly, slowly backing away.
If there were other scouts nearby, it would get troublesome.

The shape of the pouch stuck out clearly. No matter how he looked at it, those looked like gold ingots...
"Take this!"

The coin pouch and various random items were instantly in his hands.
Before the white dust hit the ground, he darted into the bushes on the other side and vanished.

Even worse than himself.
