The dark forest echoed with the clash of steel and flesh. High above the dirt, Xie Yan and Xiao Qingyu darted through the branches. They traded swift blows with two deadly fighters: Xu Fei, the Two-Fist Terror, and Zhang Yeyun, known as the Nine-Finger Reaper.
Down on the ground, three Bone-Forged commanders led dozens of men. They trapped the last twenty members of the Wanqing Sect in a tight circle.
Among these survivors were Martial Masters and blood-refining disciples. Dead bodies already littered the soil. Some belonged to the sect, others to the rival faction, Sun-Seeking Willow-Night.
Without Ouyang Zhao to stop them, clouds of poison powder drifted through the air. The mist could not defeat a true master, but it killed ordinary fighters fast.
The Wanqing Sect had become a cult of venom. Wei He had trained every survivor in the toxic arts. They tossed deadly dust, toxic fumes, and poisoned water without hesitation.
They drank their own antidotes and drowned the battlefield in filth. It became a pure contest of who could survive the poison longer.
Ouyang Zhao’s anti-toxin pills were weak. They were nowhere near as good as the medicine Wei He created. That was the only reason the sect was still breathing.
Yet, they were at their limit. The trap was closing.
The enemy only hesitated because they wanted to avoid casualties. Cornered beasts fight dirty, and dying warriors always take enemies down with them.
"Finish them off!" Xu Fei roared from the trees. "Don't let Wan Ling or Wan Qingqing escape! If Lord Zhu gets back and sees them alive, he will have our heads!"
Xu Fei was Lord Zhu’s right-hand man. With Zhu gone, he was in charge.
"You have a big mouth, Xu Fei," Zhang Yeyun spat back. "If you're so brave, go fight them yourself. Stop barking orders at me."
Anyone could see the sect members were on their last legs. A little patience would yield a safe victory. Only an idiot would rush in and eat a desperate, lethal counter-attack.
Zhang was only sent here to help. Zhu wasn't his boss. Why risk his life for zero reward?
"If you keep slacking and mess this up, how will you explain it to Lord Zhu?" Xu Fei shouted.
Everything had gone wrong with this raid.
First, Wei He had returned. That was part of the plan—a trap to draw him out and kill him. But no one expected the beast bait.
They had brought masks and pills to counter the poison. Yet the Sect's poison burned through skin on contact. Then came the beast bait, drawing packs of wild monsters straight to them.
Wei He had lured Lord Zhu away while the beast horde tore into their ranks. It took everything they had to regroup and trap these survivors.
"I came here to fill a spot, and now you want me to do the heavy lifting?" Zhang laughed mockingly. "Why don't you do the dirty work yourself?"
"You!" Xu Fei's face turned red.
He couldn't force Zhang. In terms of sheer skill, Zhang was meaner and stronger. In a cold-blooded spy network, the loud ones were always the ones who could back it up.
Xu Fei looked down at his troops. They were dragging their feet too, waiting for the sect to bleed out.
Up in the trees, the pressure was falling entirely on Xu Fei. Zhang was just pretending to fight, barely throwing a punch.
"Fine! Fine!" Xu Fei hissed. "Zhao Yi! Zhong Desheng! Help me kill them now, and I'll get you both promoted when we return! Zhang Qingren! Help me, and I'll settle your issue today! I swear it!"
Down below, the three commanders exchanged glances. They stopped slacking and charged with full force.
Screams split the night.
Xiao Qingyu's eyes burned with rage. She tried to dive down to help, but Xu Fei cut her off.
"I guess... this is where we die," Xie Yan whispered.
She blocked a strike from Zhang, leaped backward, and landed on a branch.
She looked down at the dead.
Once, the Tianyin Sect had ruled this land supreme. Now, only a handful of broken fighters remained.
"Sect Leader... I failed you," she muttered.
Her hand reached for a small black metal orb in her belt. It was her ultimate escape card. If she used it now, she could get away alive.
She froze. Then, she pushed the orb back into her belt.
Her eyes hardened. Dying here wasn't a bad end. If she ran, where would she go?
This world was cruel. She had fled too many times. Every flight had stained her mind with doubt and fear. Once you look for an escape hatch, you stop growing.
That was why her cultivation had stalled. She was weaker now than Wan Ling.
"I won't run anymore," she growled, raising her fists. "Living like a rat means nothing. Have I ever truly fought with everything I have?"
Down below, another scream tore through the air.
Xie Yan's eyes locked onto Xu Fei.
"Die!!"
She shot forward like an arrow.
Her spirit flared as her internal energy surged into her knuckles, twisting the air around her hands. She let go of her fear, her doubt, and her hesitation.
Her internal force exploded, surging past its normal limits.
Her fist cut through the falling leaves, wrapping itself in a vortex of compressed air.
Xu Fei didn't expect this sudden surge. He raised a single hand to block, using only standard force.
Their fists crashed.
BANG!
Shockwaves canceled each other out.
Xu Fei's eyes went wide. He gasped as blood spurted from his mouth.
"You..."
"I..."
Xu Fei tried to turn around.
"I... how..."
PLUG! PLUG! PLUG! PLUG!
Four violent cracks echoed inside his body. Energy burst out from his limbs, spraying dark blood. His internal organs were ripped into raw meat.
Xu Fei collapsed and fell from the trees like a stone.
Behind him stood a thick, imposing figure, slowly pulling back a palm.
It was Wei He.
He had grabbed Xu Fei's arm from behind, shattered his defenses, and killed the master in a single joint strike with Xie Yan.
"Sect Master?!" Xie Yan gasped.
Xiao Qingyu and Zhang Yeyun froze in terror.
Wei He was supposed to be fighting Lord Zhu. Yet here he was.
Did that mean Lord Zhu was dead?
"No way!" Zhang Yeyun's heart slammed against his ribs. He let go of the branch and backed away.
He knew he couldn't fight Wei He. Even in the same realm, Wei He was a monster who fought top-tier commanders.
Sweat ran down Zhang's neck. He needed to run. But Wei He was faster.
Wait, Zhang thought, panic racing through his mind. He used a sneak attack to kill Xu Fei. He might be badly injured from his fight with Lord Zhu! I can make it!
Zhang turned and ran. He pushed his movement speed to the limit, abandoning his men without a second thought.
Below, the enemy fighters panicked. They stopped attacking and backed away.
Seeing their leader run, the troops broke rank. Dozens of men scattered into the dark woods.
"You all..." Wei He watched them run. "Can go to hell."
He clenched his fist.
Far across the forest floor, the fleeing men suddenly collapsed one by one. They hit the dirt, seizing.
Wei He had already spread his lethal tracking scent. The faster they ran, the faster the poison stopped their hearts. He had only waited this long to make sure none escaped.
Zhang's figure vanished into the distant trees.
Wei He looked at Xie Yan, who was still shaking, and at Xiao Qingyu, who could barely stand. The surviving members looked up at him, eyes filled with hope.
Wei He didn't speak. He turned around.
In the thick fog behind him, a shadowy figure in white stood silently, watching.
"I have to go," Wei He said softly. "But I will return."
The white shadow remained silent, then turned and dissolved into the mist.
Wei He leaped into the air and vanished into the night, hunting down Zhang Yeyun.
Tracking a poisoned target was easy.
He had saved his people. Now, it was time for revenge.
He would tear down every enemy base in the city. He would slaughter every single spy.
He would show the world that the Wanqing Sect was still alive.
And that Wei He was still breathing.
