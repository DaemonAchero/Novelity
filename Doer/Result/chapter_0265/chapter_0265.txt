Year Four of the Qing Dynasty.
Central Province fell into utter chaos. The imperial palace burned.
Grand Marshal Li Zhouzhao led eight thousand elite troops in a sudden raid on the court. He executed the chief eunuchs, Zhu He and Zhou Changjun, and set the palace ablaze. A thousand years of imperial heritage crumbled into ashes in a single night.
That same year, rebellion erupted in the border province of Yizhou. The commander Xu Shinian was assassinated. His son, Xu Yan, threw the city gates open and invited the foreign Wu army north. The realm shook to its core.
In Xuanjing City, inside a tranquil courtyard, soft zither music flowed like a clear stream.
Wei He and Wang Shaojun sat opposite each other, placing black and white stones on a Go board. Nearby, a songstress sang in a low voice.
A water-driven bamboo timer struck every minute, making a crisp sound.
Not far away, Wan Qingqing sat with Wang Shaojun’s wife, Xie Xue. They talked about how to raise children and what to watch out for.
Xie Xue had already given birth to a healthy baby girl. At six months old, she was already toddling around.
Wan Qingqing’s belly grew bigger by the day. Her labor was near. But as a Bone-Forging martial artist, she knew her body well. Her strength kept the pregnancy from being too harsh.
Wei He pulled his gaze back and placed a black stone.
"You named your daughter You'ning. Do you hope her childhood stays peaceful and safe?"
Wang Shaojun smiled weakly. "Just a wish. In times like this, no one knows how long Taizhou can stay stable. Right now, the Wu Shi Sect and the two provincial leaders are locked in a standoff. But with the imperial court gone, those two leaders lost their main support. Who knows what comes next?"
"Setting that aside, what happened in Yizhou?" Wei He asked.
He was no longer blind to the outside world. Through the Wang family's network and his own spies, he kept close tabs on current events despite living in the mountains.
Wei He knew that closing his eyes to the world meant death.
His family was safe, healthy, and happy. He would let no one ruin this peace.
"Yizhou sits right on the border," Wang Shaojun sighed. "Past it lies the Kingdom of Wu. It is vast, even larger than our empire, but mostly snow mountains and grass plains. Little land can be farmed. That is why Wu always stares at us like a hungry wolf."
He paused, then added, "Now that we are tearing ourselves apart, neighboring countries won't miss the chance. Border skirmishes are everywhere now."
"When a nation falls apart, every trouble comes crawling out," Wei He said.
The empire was a piece of fat meat. Anyone could bite it without fear of revenge. The local lords only cared about their own territories.
"We can only live one day at a time..." Wang Shaojun placed a white stone. "You lost again."
He captured a huge group of Wei He's pieces, leaving the board empty.
"I am truly bad at this," Wei He laughed. "By the way, how did you handle your sister, Wang Shuyi?"
Wang Shaojun shook his head. "Since the Meteor Bandit died, she lost all hope. She became a nun."
"You trust her that easily?" Wei He asked.
Only a fool would believe she had that kind of self-control.
"Of course not," Wang Shaojun said cold-faced. "We crippled her martial arts first. Letting her live out her life in a temple is kind enough."
Wei He was not surprised. She had lost her honor and her mind. Left unchecked, she would bring disaster.
Suddenly, Wan Qingqing gasped, clutching her stomach. "Little He, something is wrong. I think it's time!"
Wei He's heart leaped. He instantly vanished from his seat and appeared beside her.
"Deliver the baby here! Going back to your sect will take too long!" Wang Shaojun yelled.
"Fine!" Wei He agreed.
Servants rushed to fetch midwives, hot water, and towels. Wang Shaojun quickly cleared a room for her. Wei He sent men to bring medical tools and notify his family at the sect.
Soon, two female doctors and midwives arrived.
Inside, Wan Qingqing screamed in pain. Outside, Wei He stood like a statue, his fists clenched tight.
"Relax. She is a martial artist. She can handle a birth," Wang Shaojun said with a faint grin.
Seeing the terrifying master of the Ten Thousand Poison Sect sweating in fear was a rare sight.
"I am not nervous," Wei He muttered. "I am just happy."
He stared at the room, his mind drifting far away.
"Got a name yet?" Wang Shaojun asked.
"Wei An if it's a boy," Wei He replied softly. "Wei Rong if it's a girl."
Moments later, a sharp baby cry pierced the air.
A midwife walked out, holding a tiny, wrinkled baby wrapped in warm cloth.
"Congratulations, Lord Wei! It's a boy!"
Wei He took the baby into his arms. Looking at the small red face, he felt a strange warmth fill his chest.
"From today on, your name is Wei An."
"My son."
Yuya Peak of the Wu Shi Sect.
Sharp white stone pillars stood straight like ivory needles against the sky. For a hundred years, each pillar belonged to a top master of the peak.
Daoist Liu Zhenyuan climbed the sheer cliffs, swift as a leaf in the wind. He stopped before the tallest pillar.
He bowed deep. "Liu Zhenyuan, Chief of the Seventh Court, requests an audience with the Peak Master."
The pillar stretched over a hundred meters high into the clouds.
Liu Zhenyuan waited quietly, keeping his breathing steady. The Peak Master was an old monster who spent years in solitude. Even as a Chief, Liu Zhenyuan dared not show disrespect.
After a long hour, a distant sigh drifted down from the clouds.
"Liu Zhenyuan... Speak. What brings you here?"
"The imperial court failed to create Mutated Blood warriors, and their hideouts were destroyed," Liu Zhenyuan reported. "I thought peace would return. But a new disaster has struck."
"What disaster?"
"Wu forces invaded our borders. Their generals are all Mutated Blood warriors. Their power is terrifying."
Liu Zhenyuan continued, "General Tang Yan of Wu slew five of our best commanders in battle. He is only at the Viscera Refining stage, but his strength is absurd. He wields twin meteor chain hammers weighing five thousand pounds each. His skin is like iron, immune to blades, and his speed is unnatural."
"Another Mutated Blood..." The Peak Master snorted with annoyance. "One Chen Taihe was headache enough. Now this Tang Yan."
"Mutated Blood warriors are born with monstrous strength and iron bodies," Liu Zhenyuan said. "We cannot judge them by standard martial ranks..."
"Wu's army must pass Li Tong's domain before reaching us," the Peak Master interrupted coldly. "And even if they come, Commander Chen Taihe is also a Mutated Blood monster. Let those beasts kill each other."
He scoffed in disgust. "We train hard for decades, yet these freaks surpass us in a few years. There is no fairness in this world."
The voice faded, leaving the mountain top silent.
"Farewell, Martial Uncle," Liu Zhenyuan bowed and turned to leave.
