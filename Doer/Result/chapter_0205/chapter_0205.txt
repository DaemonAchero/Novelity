Inside the Yuchi mansion, under the red roofs, stood a small yard hung with black wind chimes.
A shadow flashed. Wei He crossed the rooftops and landed softly on the yard wall.
As he dropped down, the wind from his movement shook the chimes. A light sound echoed through the air.
"So the secret vault is here?" Wei He looked around.
The place looked like a simple garden. A three-story tower stood in the middle. The most unusual thing was the black wind chimes on every corner of the roofs. They were made of porcelain, metal, steel, and bone. Every single one was black.
No one was in the yard, but the ground was clean. Someone swept it often.
Wei He pulled out a paper note. It showed the location of the vault and how to open it.
"Interesting. Getting this much information so fast..."
He did not care how it happened. He only wanted the full technique of the Nine Marks of Heaven. Nothing else mattered.
He moved quickly, slipping into the tower at the center of the yard.
On the first floor, three statues of white-veiled goddesses with only one arm sat on the altar. He did not know what gods they were.
Incense, candles, fruit, and meat lay on the table. Light smoke rose and filled the room with a mild scent.
Wei He walked behind the statues and tossed out a handful of white dust.
He waited for it to settle on the floor, then threw out a red dust.
When the two powders touched, many small footprints appeared on the ground. One set was very fresh. It led from the hall to the back of the statues and stopped right next to the wall.
"Here it is." Wei He walked over and stepped on the floor three times, just like the note said.
Click.
The floor cracked open. Behind him, a half-circle entrance appeared on the ground, leading down.
Wall lamps lit the tunnel. Every few steps, a bowl of incense ash sat on the ground, used for burning offerings.
"Yuchi Zhong was actually a believer." Wei He checked the entrance, then stepped inside.
Swish! Swish! Swish!
Sharp noises rang out. Six small arrows shot from both walls without warning. They were fast.
Wei He could not react in time. The arrows hit him. But since he only took one step forward, only his leg got struck.
The arrows hit his shin. His body protection power broke them into pieces. They bounced off the stone wall and fell to the ground.
"If a normal martial artist stepped in, they would be dead."
Wei He felt his leg armor almost get pierced. His inner force was as strong as a master who trained their inner organs. That meant even strong fighters could not block these arrows. They could only dodge.
He slowed down and walked carefully deeper into the tunnel.
Elsewhere in the mansion, news of Yuchi Yan’s death spread fast. Torches lit up, turning the night bright as day.
Yuchi Yuansha changed her clothes quickly and rushed out.
A large crowd of elite fighters gathered in the main courtyard. Yuchi Yuansha wore heavy brown armor. She was nearly nine feet tall and held a huge two-hundred-pound silver blade. In the torchlight, she looked like an iron statue.
She stared down at her brother’s corpse. His eyes were wide open. Her eyes grew wet, but she swallowed her tears and refused to let them fall.
"The mansion is locked down," she said. "The killer took less than fifteen minutes from entry to murder. Yuchi Qi, tell me. Who did this?!"
She looked up, her gaze freezing cold as she stared at Yuchi Qi.
Yuchi Qi knelt near the body, crying.
"It was... Wei He from the Thousand Poison Sect!" she sobbed. "I saw it myself! Second Uncle was talking to me when his face went pale. Then someone dropped from above and punched him..."
"Poison first, then a strike. That is how the Thousand Poison Sect works," a military officer said with a frown.
"Why did he attack us? Why kill my brother?" Yuchi Yuansha demanded.
"The secret vault!" Yuchi Qi screamed. "I remember now! His helper asked me where the vault was before they left. I said I didn't know, so he took something from Second Uncle's body! It must be the clue to the vault!"
"The vault!" Yuchi Yuansha realized. "He wants the other manuals of the Nine Marks of Heaven!"
Everything made sense now.
"Wei He killed Yan Junshan," Yuchi Yuansha said. "He knows poison, and he has help. We cannot take him lightly. You three, come with me to the vault. Send orders to the three big families in the city to help us. Lock the city gates! I want him dead tonight!"
"Kill!" the men shouted back.
The three generals agreed at once. Their leader was killed in his own home under their watch. It was a massive disgrace, and only blood could wash it away.
Wei He walked forward slowly.
The tunnel was long, lit by oil lamps along the walls. He walked down a hundred steps and triggered over ten traps. Poison arrows, gas, acid, spikes. The place was a showcase of deadly traps.
Finally, he reached the end. A small orange room opened up before him.
Racks were built into the four walls, filled with gold, jewels, weapons, armor, and pill bottles.
Wei He ignored them all and looked at a small purple bookshelf in the corner.
About ten books sat on it. Some were made of leather, some of bamboo, some were scrolls, and others were made of strange leaves.
He stepped forward and saw five volumes marked with the Nine Marks of Heaven. They were bound in matching leather. They sat in the center of the shelf, clearly valued above the rest.
"Only five? One is missing..."
Wei He remembered that Shangguan Jue was not killed. She had left with her brother. That meant even the army only had five volumes here. Even if he took all of these, he could only get eight volumes in total, not the full set.
He picked up the five books. Suddenly, he caught a faint smell.
He quickly pulled a white powder from his belt and flicked it over the books.
The smell vanished at once.
"Just weak smoke poison." He tucked the books into his clothes, then grabbed the rest of the manuals on the shelf. He wrapped them along with gold notes in a heavy cloth bag, turned, and left.
He moved much faster on the way out, ignoring the deactivated traps.
In seconds, he cleared the tunnel and stepped out behind the statues.
Footsteps rushed toward the building.
Boom! Boom!
The doors and windows shattered. Heavy-armored guards burst into the room, rushing at Wei He. At the same time, a rain of crossbow arrows flew toward him, covering every spot in the room.
A single arrow had the power of a martial artist's full strike. A hundred of them together could kill even top experts.
Wei He stayed calm. He curled his body into a ball and dropped right in front of an incoming heavy guard. The guard's body blocked the rain of arrows for him.
They clashed in mid-air.
Wei He's hand moved like a butterfly, lightly tapping the side of the guard's blade to push it away. He rolled through the air, grabbed the guard with one hand, and charged out.
The broken door shattered completely.
Using the guard as a shield against the incoming arrows, Wei He burst outside.
Immediately, five thick nets made of snake-skin rope fell from above. The material was far stronger than normal rope. Swords could not cut it, and even strong fighters could not tear it apart easily.
It was made to trap experts like him.
Before the nets could touch him, a spray of black liquid shot out from Wei He's body.
Pfft!
White smoke rose as acid ate through the ropes. Wei He threw the heavy guard into the weakened net, smashing right through it.
He flew out like a bird, touched the ground lightly, and leaped up onto the top of the three-story tower.
The wind chimes rang wildly under his feet like falling rain.
Wei He turned around and looked down at the crowd below.
The cold wind blew through his black robes. He stood there, completely calm.
"You didn't go after the killer, but came for me instead. Do you really think I killed Yuchi Yan?" He realized their mistake right away.
Below the tower, Yuchi Yuansha glared up at him, her eyes red with rage.
"Wei He, you won't leave this city alive today," she growled. "The army is here, and the three families are ready. Even with your poison, you die tonight!"
