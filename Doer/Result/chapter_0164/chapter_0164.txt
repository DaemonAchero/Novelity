Deep in the dark forest.
You Muxuan brought her black blade down with everything she had.
Behind the blade, nine bone-force shadows burst out one after another. Heavy, swift, and lethal, they slammed down like thunder.
Swish!
Clang!
Wei He moved like a flash. He slapped the side of the black blade, using the force to slide away like a ghost.
In a single instant, his body flexed like a snake, changing directions three times before landing right behind You Muxuan.
"You took my Nine-Shadow Soul Poison," Wei He said softly. "The faster your blood and force flow, the deeper it goes."
He did not strike. He just stood quietly behind her.
Around them, no other soldiers were left standing.
Out of more than two hundred gunners and heavy horsemen, Wei He had poisoned most of them. The rest escaped because his poison ran out, but that was enough.
You Muxuan roared in madness, spinning around to slash at him again.
Wei He floated like a dry leaf in the wind, easily stepping clear of every swing.
It was not just that he was too strong. You Muxuan's speed and reaction had dropped hard from the poison.
Meanwhile, Wei He had reached the fifth level of the Flying Dragon Skill. Paired with his high-speed trait and the raw power of the Whale Flood Skill, his short bursts, quick turns, and stops were completely flawless. He moved like a phantom.
The Flying Dragon Skill gave him high stamina and speed caps. The Rain Force gave him quick short-distance bursts. The Whale Flood Skill let him turn and stop without losing balance.
At the fifth level, his speed had reached a brand-new stage. He moved in and out of sight as if breaking the laws of nature.
Even if You Muxuan were not poisoned, she could never catch him now.
After dodging a few more swings, Wei He stopped and looked at her.
"I remember now..." You Muxuan let out a dark laugh.
"You... are... Wan Ling's martial arts disciple... Just a plain disciple...!"
Her body shook. Shadows and double images filled her sight.
"Who knew... Wanqing Court... kept a monster like you..."
She propped herself up with her blade and shield, fighting to stay standing.
"Just wait... Zhou Xingtong will avenge me! He will tear you to pieces! He will wipe out Wanqing Court, and kill you, Wan Ling, Wan Qingqing, all of you..."
Slice.
Wei He cut off her head with one move. He held it in his hand, his face blank.
"As long as no one sees your face, no one will know you were ugly," he whispered. "So, thank me."
He stepped forward, about to check her body for loot.
"Wei He!!"
A sudden cry came from behind him.
Wei He froze and turned around.
Deep in the dark forest, Wan Ling stood holding a bloody short blade. She stared at him in shock.
"You!?" Her eyes were full of doubt, confusion, and horror.
Wei He was stunned for a second, then relaxed and showed a soft smile.
"Master. Can you keep this a secret for me?"
Wan Ling stared at him. She looked at the headless body behind him, then at the piles of dead soldiers all around.
She had just been worried about his safety. She had turned back to save him.
Now, the Wei He in her mind overlapped with the Wei He standing among mountains of corpses.
She could no longer tell which one was real and which was fake. Her mind went completely blank, filled with a loud, chaotic noise.
Xuanjing City, Wang Family Manor.
"What!? The Chijing Army moved against the Tianyin Sect!?"
Wang Shaojun slammed the table and stood up, his face full of shock.
"What, you didn't know?" His uncle Wang Zhihuan stood by the window with a white jade cup in his hand, looking calm.
"I didn't... When did this happen?" Wang Shaojun pressed down his panic.
If the Chijing Army moved against the sect, the outcome was obvious. The only question was how many could escape. An imperial army and a martial arts sect were not even on the same level.
"Just an hour ago. General Yuchi Zhong feigned a banquet for the twelve branch leaders, then struck. He cut them off while they had no time to gather their shadow fighters," Wang Zhihuan smiled.
"What was the result?" Wang Shaojun asked in a low voice.
"Out of the nine top leaders in those twelve branches, five died, one surrendered. Vice Sect Master Xie Yan escaped, and Shangguan Ji is missing. They also found hidden guns at several branch bases and shot dozens of guards. That is a major crime," Wang Zhihuan shook his head.
"Smuggling weapons... Who knew the Tianyin Sect was so bold? I met Shangguan Ji years ago. He was gentle and polite, not the type to do such things."
Wang Shaojun was silent.
He was worried about Wei He, but then he remembered Wei He had never shown his true strength. The army would definitely misjudge him, so Wei He should be safe.
"Why? Do you have friends in the Tianyin Sect?" As a high-ranking member of the Wang family, Wang Zhihuan saw through his nephew right away.
"Yes, so I am a bit worried," Wang Shaojun did not hide it.
"Then there is nothing we can do. The Zhou family's bronze man has moved. Even You Rong cannot stop him," Wang Zhihuan shook his head.
"Zhou Xingtong? That guy is indeed terrifyingly strong," Wang Shaojun nodded.
In chaotic times, talented people rose quickly through war. Zhou Xingtong was one of them.
Wang Shaojun had once fought him in disguise. His bone-forging force had hit Zhou Xingtong, but the man had taken it as if it were nothing.
"Earlier in the provincial capital, another big name showed up. A man named Zhang Renhong. They say he stood alone at the pass and saved Xie Minzhi from the Xiangqu Cult's assassins. He killed thirty assassins alone, including five martial masters and one bone-forging expert, then left unharmed.
The Governor called him a man of absolute loyalty and supreme courage, worth a whole city!"
"Worth a city? That is a high praise," Wang Shaojun was moved.
"Indeed. In these dark times, heroes arise everywhere. Zhou Xingtong and Zhang Renhong are both born talents. I wonder when you plan to make a name for yourself, Shaojun," Wang Zhihuan laughed.
"Not yet," Wang Shaojun shook his head. "In terms of strength, I am behind them. I still need time."
"Victory is not decided by strength alone. You do not need to look down on yourself."
"I am not looking down on myself. There are too many hidden talents in this world, like stars in the sky. Even in Taizhou alone, there are hidden dragons. How could I be arrogant?" Wang Shaojun thought of Wei He and sighed.
"Now that Zhou Xingtong has moved, how did the Tianyin Sect react? How is Wanqing Court?" he asked again.
"Not sure, but Wanqing Court was not among the branches whose leaders were killed. You don't need to worry too much. Fate is unpredictable, and our Wang family cannot step into this," Wang Zhihuan advised.
"I know." Wang Shaojun understood. At this point, he could only hope Wei He would survive on his own.
The purge was an order from above. A single Wang family could not change it.
"I will go get some fresh air," Wang Shaojun stood up with a sigh and walked out.
"If you are worried, go take a look yourself. Our family still has enough influence for that," Wang Zhihuan called out behind him.
Teams of red-armored soldiers moved fast, piling up the corpses from the ground.
Medical units wearing cloth masks dragged the bodies onto carts one by one, taking them away to be burned.
Zhou Xingtong stared at the bloody ground with a dark face.
Late at night, the foul smell of blood still hung in the air.
Over a hundred gunners, plus You Muxuan and three martial master assistants. A bone-forging general and three strong officers, all dead right here.
And You Muxuan was a key member of his own division!
"Did you find out how they died?"
Zhou Xingtong looked at the old men examining You Muxuan's headless body.
"It was poison. A rare, high-level poison that works even on martial masters. It seems to be made from over thirty different toxins," a white-haired old man answered with a frown.
"Poison..." Zhou Xingtong's face grew darker.
If a general died in a fair duel, he would say nothing, maybe even respect the enemy's skill.
But poison... To lose his general to cheap tricks...
Bang!
Zhou Xingtong whipped a small tree next to him.
The trunk snapped instantly, sending leaves and branches crashing to the ground.
"Wanqing Court alone does not have this power. They must have had help from a poison expert along the way. So..." the old man added.
"I understand. Thank you, Elder Zheng." Zhou Xingtong looked at his soldiers.
"From now on, everyone put on poison masks! The Wanqing Court base is nearby. Follow me to wipe them out!" he ordered in a low voice.
Out of the nine main branches, besides Du Han of Red Land Court who surrendered, only three were left.
Shuang Qingyu of Zhenglin Court, Shangguan Jue of Yuanbing Court, and Wan Ling of Wanqing Court.
Shangguan Jue had joined Shangguan Ji and disappeared. Shuang Qingyu disbanded her people right away and vanished.
Wan Ling's side was the only one that caused this much trouble and death.
"Wanqing Court's base is on the West Mountain. We came as soon as we got the message, so there must be people who haven't escaped yet. If we go up now, we can still catch them."
After betraying his own side, Du Han of Red Land Court was eager to prove his worth.
"I have been to the West Mountain base and know the paths well. I will lead the way!" He stepped up to the front.
"They killed so many of my men. I will make sure not a single soul is left alive in Wanqing Court!" Zhou Xingtong growled.
"Move!"
He grabbed his steel whip and marched forward, with lines of gunners following close behind.
Meanwhile, on the West Mountain base.
Wan Ling and the others were rushing their retreat. Everyone in Wanqing Court packed their food and gear, leaving the mountain in groups.
Night was turning into dawn. The forest was thick, and while guns were lethal in open space, martial masters could easily dodge them in dense trees if they were not trapped in formation.
The steep, thick parts of the forest were bad for guns, making it the best place for Wanqing Court to hide.
