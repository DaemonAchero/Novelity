Jinyu Hall was a branch of the Chaos God Sect. Though the sect had countess branches, its core was built on criminals and outcasts.
If the Evil Path was just losing one's way, the people of the Chaos God Sect were truly rotten to the core.
Those who joined were always cruel, ruthless, and extreme.
The group from Jinyu Hall sitting here was no exception.
And to lead a bunch like this—to make them obey from the bottom of their hearts—you had to be even more ruthless than them!
Dong Ling thought this way, and he acted this way too.
The group boiled meat soup, watching the hot steam drift away with the wind.
Soon, a man drew his blade. The silver light of the edge reflected the firelight.
"I really don't know what we're afraid of. What is this Wanfei Palace anyway? Just something started by a bunch of women. You people from Yuan are too soft, mostly weak trash. Your minds aren't strong enough, and your blades aren't sharp enough."
This man was a foreigner with long, loose golden hair.
He held a short knife in a reverse grip. In an instant, the blade flashed.
The meat in the pot was split into eighteen pieces.
Another flash of the blade, and the eighteen pieces turned into thirty-six.
The eyes of the others shrank. They had seen this foreigner use his blade before, but his speed this time was faster than ever.
Hearing his arrogant tone, everyone except Dong Ling turned red with anger, yet no one said a word.
They had fought him before over this, and every one of them had lost completely and miserably.
His name was Mailisi. He came from the Kingdom of Jilu, a place even further than the State of Wu. With his blade skills and the special internal energy in his body, he was extremely strong.
Even in Jinyu Hall, he was the top fighter right below the few big bosses at the very top.
"We know the blade skills of Jilu are great. But if Mr. Mailisi thinks he can fight a hundred men alone, then take my words back. You can enter the city on your own," Dong Ling said coldly.
"Heh heh heh." Mailisi glanced at Dong Ling, let out a cold laugh, and stopped talking.
His role was just a representative, collaborating with Jinyu Hall.
Not far away, merchant caravans occasionally passed by. The sound of horses, wagons, shouting, and loud laughter filled the air, forming the unique feel of Jinzhou.
An old driver passing by sang out loud as he drove his wagon: "Three measures in the morning, seven in the evening. Fifty for one go, no need for ten times.
Troubled heart, aching head, cut away your troubles and all is well."
Dong Ling and his group watched the passing caravans with cold eyes. These merchants were all desperate men risking their lives for profit.
For big money, they were merchants when nothing happened, but could turn into bandits whenever needed. The song had a hidden meaning behind it.
The night was deep. Traveling in this kind of environment, it was clear this caravan carried some dangerous goods.
Dozens of wagons passed by before the caravan slowly moved into the distance, leaving deep wheel tracks behind.
Yet, after the caravan left, several figures in black night-suits appeared by the side of the road out of nowhere.
The figures looked toward Dong Ling's side from afar, then turned and chased after the caravan.
It looked like they were tracking that merchant group.
"Are those scouts?" the one-armed woman asked, confused.
"Probably. This area is famous for being messy. It's normal for someone to target a caravan that big. As for who wins or loses, it depends on their skill," the giant man, almost three meters tall, answered casually.
Dong Ling picked up a wooden stick and poked at the campfire, making the fire burn brighter.
But after poking it a few times, he thought back to the eyes of those men in black, and the more he thought, the more wrong it felt.
"No! Those men in black just now... they weren't looking at the caravan, they were looking at us!!"
He stood up suddenly, reaching for his waist.
Seeing this, the others stood up too, pulling out their weapons and getting ready.
"Good senses," a voice praised softly from the surrounding night.
From the high grass all around, figures rushed out from every direction, surrounding the group tightly in an instant.
"Who goes there? We are just passing merchants with no money to spare. Please show mercy," Dong Ling called out loudly.
The other side had a lot of people and they moved well, making Dong Ling's group watchful and unwilling to fight blindly.
After all, this was the land of Wanfei Palace. If they caused a big scene, trouble would surely follow.
"People of Jinyu Hall?" A large figure suddenly drifted down from the distance, landing softly and looking over.
The figure was Wei He.
The experts of the Myriad Poison Sect were spread out right now, each managing their own territories, so they could not move easily.
Because of this, every branch had set up a hidden院, keeping a group of fighters who used weapons every day.
The hidden院 was just starting and lacked top experts, so he had to do things himself.
As for the experts he controlled with poison before, he sent them to other places to handle other businesses. They stayed out of contact normally, only linking up in secret when needed.
This way, the power of the Myriad Poison Sect was split into small pieces. Except for a few people, no one knew how many people or experts the sect really had.
Wei He looked at the Jinyu Hall group in front of him.
There were fifteen people in total. Aside from ten dull-looking, lifeless followers, only those five had a fierce aura. They were clearly the core of this group.
"Who are you? Knowing we are from Jinyu Hall and still showing up, you have some guts," Dong Ling, as the leader, said coldly.
"Is your name Dong Ling?" Wei He smiled.
"You...!?" Dong Ling felt a chill in his heart. The man even knew his name, which was a bad sign.
Just as he was about to ask who the man was, the figure flickered and disappeared right in front of him.
The night was lit only by the fire and the moon, making it hard to see. Now that the man suddenly vanished, the group immediately pushed their internal energy to full power, staying on high guard.
"A few weak dogs think they can ambush your grandfather!!?" The three-meter giant roared. His arms, covered in beast-hide gloves, opened wide as he charged at one of the men in black.
He had the cultivation of a Martial Master. As his energy surged together with his huge raw strength, he erupted.
His aura was like a wild tiger. Just his presence made the two men in black feel numb on their scalps, unable to move.
But halfway through his leap, while still in the air, a person appeared right in front of him. It was Wei He.
Wei He raised his right hand and slapped the giant's chest like lightning.
It looked like a gentle slap, but the overlaying energies within his Overlying Sea Force were no small matter. Even a basic strike was far beyond what a mere Martial Master could take.
Boom!
With a loud crash, the giant's chest caved in. He flew backward, breathing out his last breath while still in the air.
Wei He gave a light laugh. His body moved like a ghost, appearing right before the one-armed woman in a blink. He flicked his finger.
Before the woman could scream in anger, her head spun like a top, turning a full three hundred and sixty degrees.
With a loud crack, her neck bones shattered, completely breaking her neck. Her body fell to the ground, losing all life.
The next person was even weaker. Seeing this, he tried to run, but before he could cover even a few meters, Wei He cut him off and struck him down to the dirt.
Dong Ling roared in fury. As an expert at the peak of Organ Refining, his strength was second only to the Hall Master. How could he not see they had hit a wall this time?
The enemy's movement was something even he could not see clearly. The man's power was terrifying.
He roared continuously, his internal energy running through his Settling River Skill in layers, spreading outward like water ripples.
"Scatter..." Before the word left his mouth, Wei He was already standing in front of him.
In a rush, Dong Ling gathered all his force and pushed both palms forward.
Layers of air-twisting energy slammed toward Wei He's chest and stomach alongside his palms.
Boom!!!
Amidst a dull impact, Dong Ling's face froze in disbelief.
A bloody hole appeared between his eyebrows, even though his full-force palms had indeed hit Wei He.
Sadly, his desperate strike accomplished nothing, leaving the opponent completely unharmed.
The man had stopped his full-power hit using only his defensive energy.
"What a waste... practicing martial arts for decades, only to die in this middle of nowhere. Truly not worth it," Wei He sighed. He pulled his hand back and looked at the last person.
The foreigner with golden hair and blue eyes.
Wei He had saved him for last on purpose because the man's hair and eye color felt familiar.
This was the first time since coming to this world that he saw someone who looked like a European from his past life.
The foreigner's whole body was tense, his eyes filled with a fearless, brutal light.
He held a short knife that was vibrating in a strange rhythm, as if building up a special ultimate move.
"Interesting," Wei He smiled slightly.
At his current level, an Organ Refining expert was not much stronger than a normal person in his eyes.
Only those at the Mind Sensing realm could catch his attention.
It had been a long time since his battle with the two Mind Sensing experts of the Wushi Sect, and he had not been standing still.
Though he made little progress in raw energy and strength, his skill level had improved quite a bit.
The four realms of skill had opened a whole new world for him.
"Ah!!!" The foreigner finally finished his setup. He raised his knife above his head and charged at Wei He with a wild scream like a fool.
A large amount of special force gathered on his blade, a force that surprised even Wei He a little.
That amount was equal to the combined full power of two Bone Forging masters.
It was only half a step weaker than Dong Ling's final move just now.
"A decent secret technique. Too bad it's useless against me. Your footwork is too poor."
Wei He dodged like lightning, avoiding the blade, and casually slapped the foreigner on his back.
Pfft.
The foreigner spat out a mouth of blood and crashed face-first onto the ground.
"He doesn't have defensive energy on his body?" Wei He pulled his hand back, a bit surprised.
This foreigner's martial arts were strange. His attack power was extremely high, but his defense was weak.
Still, dealing with this group from Jinyu Hall cleared one thing off his list.
Aside from this group, there were probably no other enemy Organ Refining experts around.
"Alright, check the bodies and clean them up," Wei He clapped his hands and ordered, turning to leave.
"Yes!"
The surrounding men in black bowed in response.
Clap, clap, clap...
Before Wei He could walk away, rhythmic clapping sounded from nearby.
"Such fast footwork, such ruthless methods. One strike, one kill. As expected of the top expert who was once famous in Taizhou, Myriad Poison Sect Master Wei He."
Wei He's heart turned cold. He turned quickly and looked toward the sound.
Others might not tell where it came from, but he could hear clearly that it came from his front-left.
There was a patch of knee-high grass over there.
Though the land here was vast, high grass and shallow water pools were everywhere.
The voice came from one of those grass patches.
"Who are you? Why not show yourself?" Though Wei He was on guard, anyone who could hide from his senses was no weakling, certainly far better than trash like Dong Ling.
The men of the Myriad Poison Sect around him spread out in secret, holding the poison bags at their waists, ready to throw poison at any moment.
To their surprise, a figure in a pink dress floated out gently from the grass, landing steadily on the flat ground ten meters away from Wei He.
The newcomer was actually a beautiful woman who looked only in her early twenties!
She wore a simple dress, carried no weapons, and showed no powerful aura at all.
If they had not seen her leap over ten meters with their own eyes, no one would believe she was a martial artist.
Her arms and body were soft and slender, showing no signs of hard training at all.
The woman wore a black veil with her long hair tied high up. Her fair, long legs showed now and then beneath her red dress.
She stood barefoot, yet her body floated slightly off the ground without touching it, as if an invisible force held her feet up from the dirt.
