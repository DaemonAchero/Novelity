Yao Hanlin was an old martial master who had cultivated force for many years, holding a mid-tier position in the Heavenly Seal Sect.
Wei He calculated his strength based on the resistance he met from the force. Yao was weaker than the Human-Faced Tiger, Xiao Heng.
If Xiao Heng was the benchmark unit of measurement among the force martial masters Wei He had faced: Yang Guo never went all out, but he was likely far below Xiao Heng, worth half a Xiao Heng at best.
Wan Qingqing possessed force equal to at least three Xiao Hengs based on her raw power in their previous clash. Her accumulation was formidable, though her actual combat ability remained untested.
The Yao Hanlin in front of him possessed about three-quarters of Xiao Heng's total force.
As for the White Snake Gang Leader, Xiao Yurong, he was even weaker than Yao Hanlin, barely worth mentioning.
Wei He sighed to himself. Rumor had it that rogue martial artists possessed weaker force than sect disciples.
Now, that rumor seemed inaccurate.
Sect disciples lived in luxury year-round, lacking real combat experience. Even if they developed force, they remained hothouse flowers.
If this had been the Human-Faced Tiger, he would never have let himself be ambushed and killed so easily.
'Or was the Human-Faced Tiger just an exception?' Wei He inspected his surroundings again. A short distance away, the green-robed man was trying to sneak off.
Without a word, Wei He used his Flying Dragon Technique to catch up. He killed the man in a few moves, and complete quiet returned to the woods.
He made sure no one escaped. The surrounding forest stood dead silent.
Yao Hanlin and the green-robed man had likely felt uneasy and followed behind to check. In the end, they stumbled upon Wei He in the act of killing.
"Don't blame me, you just had bad luck," Wei He sighed.
He didn't know when, but he had formed a habit of sighing after killing his opponents.
Next was cleaning up the mess.
He searched the bodies. The two ox carts had spooked and run off, but Wei He easily herded the black oxen back. Then he scattered a large amount of scent-masking powder.
For a force master like him, controlling two black oxen was simple. The oxen moved slowly, so he could easily adjust their direction.
The real issue was the cargo, and going back with all these dead bodies was not an option.
The most troublesome part was Yao Hanlin's death.
This was no ordinary person, but a genuine force master of the Heavenly Seal Sect, not a rogue like Xiao Heng.
The Heavenly Seal Sect kept its number of force masters to around thirty-odd people. They would definitely investigate thoroughly.
Before leaving, Wei He used his Five Peaks Palm to silently beat the corpse into raw pulp.
That way, wild wolves and beasts could eat every bit, bones and all.
Fortunately, it was night time. No one could see clearly from a distance, so he didn't need to worry about spies.
Wei He led the two carts away until he found a hidden ravine cliff. He dumped all the military weapons over the edge.
He then merged the valuable herbs and rare beast meat onto a single cart, hid it in a cave, and set the oxen free.
He set simple poisonous hunter traps at the cave entrance, sprinkled beast-repellent powder for safety, and finally returned to Xuanjing City.
The next day, Wei He acted as if nothing had happened, practicing his martial arts and checking reports as usual.
Without Zhao Xingzheng's help, his efficiency dropped significantly.
He was waiting for the situation to boil.
The goods were gone and people were dead. The interested parties would inevitably step forward.
Moreover, the city gate guards had seen him go in and out during that time. It was very likely that someone would suspect his connection to Zhao Xingzheng's death.
Days passed.
Wei He did not have to wait long.
On the fifth day, a visitor arrived at his door.
"Rudder Leader Wei, I have heard so much about you," the man said in the meeting room.
Wei He sat side-by-side with the visitor.
"Brother Chen is too polite. We are both Rudder Leaders serving in Xuanjing City. We should stay in touch and support each other more in the future."
"When I heard your name last time, I was surprised that the Wanqing Court produced a junior brother with such strong skills. Later, I heard you were actually researching drugs, which was quite unexpected."
The visitor was Chen Zi'ang, the Rudder Leader of the Bear Mountain Town Heavenly Seal Hall. His opening words carried a cold tone.
"What is so unexpected to Brother Chen? Is it admiration or disgust? Speak directly," Wei He said, cutting straight to the point without wasting words.
"Let's not talk about that," Chen Zi'ang replied. He was clearly suspicious because of the recent rumors about the White Snake Gang.
On top of that, Wei He needed large quantities of poisons and drugs for experiments, which was hard to conceal. It was normal for him to slip up.
"My sudden visit today is because someone found a murder scene in the suburbs yesterday. The victim wore Heavenly Seal Sect clothes. Because wild beasts heavily chewed the body, we are still trying to identify him.
Additionally, important items were lost. Deputy Sect Master Zhou is furious and is personally looking into it.
In a few days, an investigation team from the main sect will arrive. They will come to your place first, then mine. Rudder Leader Wei, you must receive them with care."
Chen Zi'ang seemed to be warning him, but his eyes were fixed on Wei He's expression.
It looked like someone really suspected him. After all, Zhao Xingzheng vanished shortly after Wei He took office.
As the Rudder Leader, he was indeed suspicious.
Wei He looked heavy-hearted and nodded.
"I understand. I will definitely be careful. Rest assured, Brother Chen."
"Don't blame me for blabbing, Brother Wei," Chen Zi'ang sighed. "Sometimes, if a person takes what they shouldn't take, they might trigger a disaster at any moment without knowing how to handle it. What do you think one should do?"
He seemed to be asking, but also hinting.
"Did Brother Chen take something... and feel worried?" Wei He asked softly.
"Haha, not at all. A friend of mine met a similar situation before, and my advice to him was to give it back. Find a way to return it to its original owner," Chen Zi'ang smiled and replied.
"So that's how it is. That is indeed a good way," Wei He agreed.
"Alright, I won't disturb you any longer. Farewell." Chen Zi'ang stood up and cupped his hands.
"Farewell." Wei He cupped his hands and saw him out.
He watched the man leave the yard until he disappeared and the gate closed.
Wei He turned around.
He hadn't had enough time to clean up the bodies earlier because there were too many and it took too long.
It was normal for them to be found.
He originally thought Zhao Xingzheng was only colluding with government officials to sell weapons from the armory.
Now it seemed that many people in the Heavenly Seal Sect were involved too.
The Chen Zi'ang in front of him was one of them.
The man almost directly told him to return what he shouldn't have taken to its original owner.
Wasn't he just after that letter?
But was Wei He someone who was easily tricked?
He was sure that if he made any unusual move, they would confirm his guilt as an accomplice, followed by a swift and brutal strike.
'With my current strength, where do I stand in the whole Xuanjing City?' Wei He estimated his power. If he went all out against Wan Qingqing at full strength, who would win?
Judging purely from the power Wan Qingqing had shown so far, he could catch her off guard, but he couldn't tell who would win after that.
Wei He guessed he had a higher chance of losing.
"Xiao Ding, come here," he called out.
Since Zhao Xingzheng was dead, Wei He promoted a new person to take his spot, one of the outer court subordinates.
The man's name was Ding Yang. From Wei He's observation, he was the most isolated among the five.
He was simple, honest, and quiet.
So Wei He promoted him to assistant to help manage minor matters.
"Rudder Leader, you called for me?" Ding Yang quickly walked in and lowered his head.
"Tell me, where does our Heavenly Seal Sect stand in Xuanjing City? Who can we mess with, and who should we avoid?" Wei He asked gently.
"Don't be afraid of saying the wrong thing. I'll back you up," he added.
Zhao Xingzheng had obvious problems, so it was hard to tell if his previous words were true. Wei He needed to ask someone else.
"Answering the Rudder Leader," Ding Yang looked around, saw no one else, and lowered his voice.
"Our Heavenly Seal Sect has nine branches. In Xuanjing City, except for the Three Great Families, we fear no one."
"That's it?"
"That's it," Ding Yang nodded.
"Are the Three Great Families really that strong?" Wei He asked further.
"Yes. The Three Great Families refer to the You Family, the Zhou Family, and the Wang Family. They represent three major forces in Xuanjing City," Ding Yang answered, stopping after just one sentence.
"Which three forces?" Wei He was forced to keep asking.
"I'm not entirely sure, but I heard people say the You Family is the strongest among the local gentry. Old Master Zhou is the head of the Xuanjing Chamber of Commerce. Old Master Wang is the famous scholar Wang Zhihe, who has many students serving as officials in Xuanjing City," Ding Yang explained again.
"The Gentry You Family, the Merchant Zhou Family, the Official Wang Family..." Wei He quickly summarized. They were simply representatives of three major forces.
"Even so, our Heavenly Seal Sect shouldn't be afraid of them, right?" He felt confused.
"It's like this. Because of their vast wealth and power, the Three Great Families gathered many resources, warriors, and secret manuals. They built their own castles outside the city and raised livestock farms. In terms of top-tier strength, our Sect Master Shangguan is indeed the strongest in Xuanjing City.
However, they have too many other experts. Our Heavenly Seal Sect simply cannot afford to maintain that many martial artists..." Ding Yang finally explained clearly, no longer giving short answers.
They lived in the city year-round, so they felt this difference most strongly.
"So every time we clash with the Three Great Families, if we bring ten people, they can call twenty people of the same level."
Wei He suddenly understood.
This was the result of chaotic times. Many martial artists fled everywhere, while wealthy noble families had deep pockets to recruit far more martial artists than a remote sect like the Heavenly Seal Sect.
It took the Heavenly Seal Sect over ten years of hard work to cultivate a dozen Three-Blood experts.
Those big families could probably hire the same number in a few days. Although their loyalty differed, the sheer gap in numbers was overwhelming.
"The Three Great Families aren’t even the most powerful force in Xuanjing. The strongest is the Red Scenery Garrison," Ding Yang finally opened up completely.
"It is said that Commander Yuchi Zhong of the Red Scenery Garrison fought our Sect Master ten years ago. He lost by half a move after a hundred rounds, and no one has seen him fight since. He is definitely one of the top martial experts in Xuanjing City."
"What about the Three Great Families? Are there notable talents among them?" Wei He asked.
"This... Rudder Leader, didn't you know? Last month, You Rong, the eldest young master of the You Family, challenged the head of Fushan Court, one of our Nine Seeds of Heavenly Seal, and only lost by a single move.
Also, Zhou Xingtong of the Zhou Family set up a martial ring and challenged masters from various boxing schools. He won every match and crushed all his opponents easily. These stories are widely known." Ding Yang looked confused.
Wei He showed a blank face; he truly didn't know.
Aside from researching drugs, he spent all his time cultivating martial arts, visiting his second sister, and looking after Rushui Workshop.
Even then, he always felt he lacked time.
