"Kill!!!"
Black cavalry flooded the hillsides like a raging storm. Mixed with siege infantry and heavy war engines, they rushed toward the sparse watchtowers on the border.
Under the fluttering war flag of the Wu Kingdom, a four-meter-tall giant in pale white plate armor stood looking toward the distant fortress.
At the edge of his vision, behind the watchtowers, soldiers inside a run-down border fortress were frantically closing the gate and pulling up the drawbridge.
It was too late.
The black armor cavalry smashed into the half-raised drawbridge and poured into the fortress.
Curved blades and long spears flashed. Blood sprayed into the air as screams of panic rang out, mixed with the explosions of guns and clashing weapons.
The wind carried a heavy stench of blood.
The giant commander had blood-red eyes under his horned helmet. He pulled his gaze back from the distance.
"Tang Yan took the lead and got lucky. Nobody mess up this time."
"Does Commander Yuan Han think the Xuanmiao Sect in Haizhou is an easy target?" A bald man in white robes spoke slowly from his right side.
The man had white skin and a face full of rough muscle. He was over three meters tall, which should have made him look massive, but next to the commander, he looked almost short.
He was Xu Dingchun, the White-Robed Buddha. He was one of the Hundred Buddhas of the Lifo Temple, and he led the local assassination forces for this operation.
"With Brother Xu here, we are working together. The Xuanmiao Sect is caught off guard. How many cities can they possibly hold? Are they going to make their Air-Master Grandmasters step in?"
Yuan Han narrowed his blood-red eyes.
"Besides, even if a Grandmaster shows up, so what? Grandmasters are still human. They have limits."
"That is true," the White-Robed Buddha agreed.
In this world, being at the absolute top of individual strength did not mean you could do whatever you wanted.
Grandmasters were strong, but they were not invincible.
The Wu Kingdom had the Ten Thousand Blood Array. If a Grandmaster dared to slaughter regular soldiers on a massive scale, activating this array after a short delay could force a true calamity into existence—a Blood Calamity.
"If the Ten Thousand Blood Array could trigger faster, combining the power of ten thousand men would make even a Grandmaster back down," Yuan Han said coldly.
Back when the Yuan Empire was at its peak, three Grandmasters fell because of it. Now that they were clearly falling apart, things would only be worse for them.
Boom!
A loud explosion came from the top of the distant fortress wall.
A group of martial masters jumped off the wall, breaking through the perimeter to flee into the distance.
"Brother Xu, do you know who is leading that group?" Yuan Han frowned.
Xu Dingchun looked closely.
"The border fortresses of Haizhou are full of soldiers. That person should be Cui Rong, the Four Dragon Spear of the Haizhou Yuan Army."
"Cui Rong is only at the Mind Sense realm, but he must have Zhenren masters from the Xuanmiao Sect protecting him. I will leave that to you, Brother Xu," Yuan Han said.
"No problem." Xu Dingchun nodded and whispered a few orders to a subordinate next to him.
The man left quickly, leading several people to chase down the fleeing group.
"Once we take this place, we can move closer to the three cities of Haizhou. According to the plan, hitting all three fronts at once is best. But I wonder how things are going in Taizhou and Zhongzhou? Any news?" Xu Dingchun asked in a low voice.
"We should get news today after we take this fortress." Yuan Han paused, about to speak again, when a messenger ran up to Xu Dingchun and whispered in his ear.
After a few words, Xu Dingchun's face changed.
Yuan Han frowned, worried something had gone wrong.
For this invasion, the Wu Kingdom had raised a million troops, pushing forward on three fronts to hit Haizhou, Taizhou, and Zhongzhou all at once.
Zhongzhou was the key. It was the symbol of power for the Great Yuan. Taking it meant taking true legitimacy.
Taizhou held the Wushi Sect, one of the main pillars of the Taoist sects in Great Yuan. Its influence was massive, so it had to be destroyed.
Haizhou was the weakest, but since it was near the sea, it controlled the ocean supply lines. That was why it was picked as a target too.
On all three paths, True Blood Commanders led the troops, while the Hundred Buddhas of the Lifo Temple moved with them.
Led by State Master Modo, they moved to crush all resistance in Great Yuan.
This was a war for the fate of the nation. Failure was not an option.
Yuan Han looked off into the distance at the fleeing commander. He met a pair of eyes filled with rage and killing intent.
"Yuan Han, do you dare to fight me face to face?!" Cui Rong roared from far away.
His internal force carried his voice across the battlefield.
The two Taoists behind him were both Full-Realization masters from the Qianliu branch of the Xuanmiao Sect.
One was Zheng Shaowen, and the other was Xu Nan. Both were ordered to protect Cui Rong and defend the border fortress.
Sadly, the battle was already lost.
Hearing Cui Rong shout, both of their faces changed color.
In a situation like this, Cui Rong still wanted to win through a one-on-one duel. Leaving aside whether he could win, the routing soldiers around them would not give them the chance to do something so foolish.
"How foolish," Yuan Han said, raising a hand.
Two men behind him brought up a heavy black short spear.
He grabbed it with one hand, turned his wrist, and leaned back.
Swoosh!
The short spear tore through the air with a high screech, flying toward Cui Rong like a falling star.
The tip of the spear heated up rapidly from the air friction, giving off a sharp, burnt smell.
"Dodge!" Before Cui Rong could react, Zheng Shaowen behind him panicked. He pulled his blade and slashed forward like lightning.
It looked like a normal slash, but his True Force stacked in layers like heavy waves, pouring into the blade to block the spear.
Clang!
A massive shockwave exploded from the blade.
Zheng Shaowen's body shook hard, and blood nearly surged up his throat.
Bang!
A soft hand pressed against his back, pouring a steady stream of True Force into him.
Seeing things turn bad, Xu Nan had moved in time, channeling her force into her senior brother's body.
The main reason the Xuanmiao Sect could seal powerful beasts was that the cultivated force from their three branches could be borrowed and shared temporarily.
Because their force shared high similarity, it was standard practice to borrow strength from fellow disciples in times of need.
That was what happened now.
Combining their strength, the two of them barely managed to stop the spear.
The spot where the spear tip hit the blade was less than ten centimeters from Cui Rong's face.
His face turned dead pale, and his body froze completely. All his previous bravery vanished.
"Retreat!" Zheng Shaowen shouted. He and his junior sister protected Cui Rong as they pulled back.
In the distance, Yuan Han smiled slightly. He picked up another short spear and looked at Xu Dingchun.
"What do you think?"
"Your strength is truly impressive, Commander," Xu Dingchun said with a smile. Whether it was the True Blood or True Force system, both had the hurdle of Full-Realization.
At this stage, the deeper your foundation was in the past, the stronger you would be after entering Full-Realization.
This held true for both systems.
The Nine Steps of Full-Realization meant digging deep into one's own secrets at every single step.
Cui Rong was just a common Mind Sense fighter, barely at the First Sense level. Provoking Yuan Han was pure suicide.
Xu Dingchun was about to say more when someone approached from behind again, whispering into his ear.
Swoosh.
Xu Dingchun flicked his white sleeve, his expression darkening.
"My apologies, Commander Yuan. Something went wrong with my arrangements elsewhere. I need to go deal with it."
"The situation here is set. Just remember to join me for victory drinks later, Brother Xu," Yuan Han smiled.
"I will." Xu Dingchun nodded, cupped his fists, and walked away quickly.
The moment he turned around, his calm face collapsed into rage.
It took him years to raise his Five Master Followers.
Among those five, the Three-Faced Bodhisattva was the strongest, groomed to be his direct successor.
He never expected this.
"What happened? How could the Three-Faced Bodhisattva die?! Who killed him?! A Full-Realization master from the Xuanmiao Sect?!" Xu Dingchun suppressed his rage and asked in a harsh whisper.
The messenger was one of his personal secret guards.
The structure of the Lifo Temple used Buddhas, Bodhisattvas, and Arhats as its backbone, with secret guards acting as the hands and eyes.
As True Blood experts, the masters would not bother to learn tracking, scouting, or investigation.
Those minor tasks belonged to the guards.
The guard wore a blue suit, a black mask, and a matching short white cape, with only his eyes showing.
"It happened near Heisong Town. Two inspectors from the Xuanmiao Sect showed up, but one of them had a Full-Realization master acting as a bodyguard. We did not dare get too close, but we saw the Three-Faced Bodhisattva get beaten to death head-on."
"Beaten to death in broad daylight?!" Sorrow flashed through Xu Dingchun's eyes. He was human, after all. He was both a teacher and a friend to the Three-Faced Bodhisattva, and their bond ran deep.
He had no children of his own, and now his favorite successor was dead.
"Where is the killer now?!"
"On the official road between Heisong Town and Zhentao City. They seem to be trying to break through to deliver a report," the guard said quickly. "Our people have moved ahead to block them."
"Use the Sky Thunder Bombs. Tell the remaining Four Masters to gather and kill those two! Block every single path out of there. I want to see what kind of person managed to kill my successor!"
Xu Dingchun clenched his fists, dark veins bulging around his eyes.
...

...
On the official road of Haizhou.
Two figures ran at high speed toward the distance.
Wei He was in front, and Yao Wan followed behind.
Ever since Wei He blew up earlier, they had stayed in this strange, uncomfortable silence.
Yao Wan had a hundred questions she wanted to ask. After barely surviving, she wanted to know why Wei He was so strong.
Yet their relationship was not close enough for her to ask about his secret cards.
On top of that, no matter how she checked, Wei He only felt like a beginner who had not even finished his First Sense. She had no idea how he generated so much True Force earlier.
On the road, their bodies moved so fast they were practically shadows.
"How do you plan to report this to the sect?" Yao Wan finally could not hold back and asked.
"Are you going to tell them you went out on patrol, ran into a True Blood General at Full-Realization, and accidentally beat him to death with two punches?"
Wei He stayed silent.
"You think you can just hide it? You think everyone else is dumb?" Yao Wan said, shaking her head.
"If you promise to keep it secret, Senior Sister, I will owe you a favor," Wei He said softly.
Even while moving at high speeds, their words carried clearly.
Wei He understood that he was on a completely different level compared to normal masters now.
Because of various reasons, the potential he accumulated was too huge, making his total True Force unbelievably massive.
Once he finished Mind Sense and stepped up layer by layer, even he did not know how far he could go.
Being one step ahead made you a genius. Being ten steps ahead made you a rare monster.
Being so far ahead that others could not even see you?
That was changing species.
So if he did not want to be captured and researched like a prize animal, keeping a low profile was his best choice.
Besides, if enemies learned about your hidden cards, you could no longer crush the weak in small fights, and winning would become much harder.
"How long do you plan to hide it?" Yao Wan asked again.
"At least until I have a little bit of power to protect myself," Wei He said after a moment.
Yao Wan stared at him. You call the power to kill a Full-Realization True Blood general 'having no power to protect yourself'?!
She had never seen someone so shameless in her entire life.
