"Should I pick... science, or religion?"
Wei He got up and paced back and forth inside the cave.
Anyone else might not have this kind of problem, but he was different.
The many gods of Earth that had existed for thousands of years, combined with the great modern science concepts, gave him a nice dilemma.
"Wait!"
After walking for a bit, he suddenly realized something.
"I don't even know the difference in power and difficulty between a completely fake god and a real one. I should test both to see which one gives me the best value for my effort."
He made a fast decision. He sat down crossed-legged, closed his eyes, and began to imagine a completely made-up, twisted god in his mind.
'First, let's give it nine heads. The more heads, the scarier it is. Right... nine human heads are the most terrifying. Most of my enemies will probably be humans anyway.' 'Next, it needs a rotting, twisted body. Let's add some colorful puss-filled blisters to trigger people's fear of tiny holes. That will really spark terror. Viruses and rotting sicknesses are things all living creatures fear.' 'Then, it needs wings around it. Let's go with twelve pairs. The more wings, the higher its status...' Wei He used his modern imagination to the fullest.
Soon, a terrifying monster with a grotesque face, nine heads, a human body, and pus leaking all over it appeared in his mind.
"I'll call it the God of Decay and Rot."
Now that he made up a god, he immediately started pouring his mental energy and True Power into it.
The method was simple: keep visualizing it, and dump all his fear, authority, cruelty, and dominance into the image.
He brainwashed himself this way, while using his True Power to feed the image.
To feed it, he had to make his True Power flow around more than ten special points in his brain, forming a loop.
These points were the core technique of the Xuanmiao Sect's god visualization, called the God Heart Points.
According to the secret books, a god that was successfully visualized would sleep in these points for a short time.
Nurturing them over time would cause a small part of his True Power to change its nature.
This changed True Power would stay hidden in these points, waiting for the user to draw it out and unleash it.
When all the True Power in the body turned into this new form, it would mark the arrival of the fourth layer of the Mysterious Lock.
That was when the sealing power would appear in all his strength.
Wei He spent half a day visualizing, imagining, and tinkering, but the fake god was terrible at changing his True Power.
He tried hard for a long time, but not even a tiny thread of his power changed.
He had no choice but to give up on this fake god.
"Normally, how fast a god changes your power, and how strong that new power becomes, depends on how real it is, how many people believe in it, and how old its history is."
"A made-up god has none of these things."
He looked down at the twelve god drawings in the secret book.
"Let's try these instead."
A visualized god could be thrown away.
Without a supply of mental energy and True Power, the visualized god would quickly grow weak and vanish.
Wei He picked another drawing at random.
This time, he tried a god named the Three-Eyed Sky Emperor.
This god was good at watching and seeing through thick fog. It was a god built for searching and tracking.
Wei He quickly built the god's image in his mind, then poured in his mental power and triggered the points.
This time, things were totally different.
He could clearly feel that for every ten units of True Power passing through the points, one unit turned into the new power.
"Hmm... Looks like a lot of people believe in this Three-Eyed Sky Emperor."
The difference between the two tries was huge.
Wei He tried a second fake god just to be sure.
It was the same as before—zero power changed. But for the twelve pictures in the book, every single god could change about one-tenth of his power.
This rate was very high.
That meant if a hundred units of power passed through the points in a day, ten units of new power would be made.
"No wonder the secret book keeps these twelve pictures. They must be the twelve easiest gods to form."
Wei He understood now.
'Next, time to try the gods of Earth...' Wei He closed his eyes and relaxed his body. The first one he picked was a famous Taoist god from Earth.
The first choice was Pangu.
Wei He closed his eyes, then suddenly opened them again.
He couldn't remember what Pangu looked like... He completely forgot the details.
'Whatever, I'll just make one up that looks close enough.' He closed his eyes again and quickly built a super strong, massive giant covered in dark gold markings.
Then he pushed his True Power into the points...
Nothing happened...
"Alright... Either the image was too far off, or something else is wrong."
Wei He felt helpless and switched to another one.
"Next up, Nuwa."
A human head and a snake body. He couldn't go wrong with that.
Sad to say... the result was the same.
His points didn't react at all.
After thinking for a moment, Wei He picked up the secret book and read it carefully again.
He suddenly noticed a key detail.
None of the gods in the twelve drawings were super powerful.
All of them had very simple, practical powers.
Things like making a punch 30% stronger, or making eyesight 50% sharper.
Meanwhile, the Taoist gods on Earth could move mountains and empty oceans effortlessly...
"Okay... Looks like if a god is too far from reality, it won't work either..."
Wei He was speechless.
He realized his current strength wasn't enough to back up that level of power. He couldn't even build the empty frame for it, so the visualization failed.
He thought it over and switched ideas again.
"Then let's try science..."
Science had a lot of choices.
The best choice was the sun above his head.
The sun was real, and Wei He generally understood how nuclear fusion worked.
Understanding the basic idea, plus being able to look up at it anytime, made it easy. He didn't need small details; he just needed to imagine a ball of light.
Wei He tried again.
This time, it actually worked!
Even though it wasn't much—only one unit changed out of a hundred—it still worked!
Once he took the first step, the next steps would follow.
"It seems how real something is matters way more than any other condition. Even if the sun's power is far beyond most gods, its realness makes up for it."
Wei He now understood.
Then he tried another real image.
It was a huge mountain range—the Black House Mountains.
He had lived in the Black House Mountains for a long time, so he knew them well, both the outside and inside details. He had walked through more than half the area while picking poisonous herbs.
So visualizing the Black House Mountains should work in theory.
Nobody said a god had to be a living creature.
Wei He focused his mind, calmed his breath, and soon built the image of the Black House Mountains in his heart.
Then he used his True Power to hit the points.
This time, after just one pass, more than half of the True Power changed.
Out of ten units, six units were completely converted.
However, this newly converted power seemed useless. Aside from feeling a bit heavier, it had no other effect.
Wei He flipped through the secret book, but found no helpful notes about this.
Making up his mind, he stood up, put on his cloak, left the cave, and rushed toward the Cloud Platform.
The Cloud Platform was in the Inner Realm, so he had to pass through the Xuanchai Jade Wall first.
Ever since Wei He unlocked his vision, he hadn't walked around the Inner Mountain yet.
The Inner Mountain was pitch black everywhere.
There was no light and nothing to see, except for a few temples and the Cloud Platform standing clearly in the dark.
Guided by his badge, Wei He walked all the way to the Cloud Platform, which glowed with a soft white light in the dark.
This place was split into three branches and three areas. Normally, nobody came here to pay respects for no reason.
After all, this was where the high leaders often showed up.
The Cloud Platform had three big gates.
The gate for the Mountain Lock Branch was wide open right now. When Wei He arrived, two groups of people were standing face-to-face, refusing to back down.
One group looked angry, and True Power was already flowing in their hands, ready for a fight.
The other group looked cold and distant, completely ignoring their opponents.
"This is the Xuanmiao Sect. It is no place for you to act wild," the cold group said softly.
"Fine! What a great Xuanmiao Sect! I've seen enough today!" The angry man pulled back his power, stared hard at the other group, turned around, and walked away.
Several people left with him. None of them seemed to be from the Xuanmiao Sect.
They were visitors from other sects coming to pay their respects.
When Wei He walked into the Mountain Lock gate, the cold-looking people saw him. Their expressions softened, they nodded gently at him, and turned to leave.
Wei He felt confused, but he never liked gossip, so he didn't care what happened.
He went straight through the main gate, walked past several buildings, and quickly found Big Senior Sister Yuan Duzi in a jade training ground.
Yuan Duzi was chatting comfortably with a white-haired old man. She looked relaxed, as if it was just a casual talk.
Noticing Wei He from afar, both of them turned their heads to look over.
Wei He stepped closer and bowed.
"Wei He greets Big Senior Sister and Senior Brother."
"You're here?" Yuan Duzi waved her hand, telling the old man he could leave.
The old man gave Wei He a friendly smile, turned around, and left.
"What brings you here? Do you have questions about your training?" Yuan Duzi asked him gently.
"Senior Sister, I am about to visualize a god and enter the second layer of the Mysterious Lock. But I still have questions about picking a god," Wei He said seriously.
"What questions?"
"It's about the link between made-up gods and real gods, and what key things control how strong a god can be."
"That is actually just one question," Yuan Duzi smiled slightly. "With a made-up god, it is easy to imagine something too powerful. That takes too much mental power and True Power, making it impossible to finish, and the change speed will be way too slow."
"As for a real god, its power might not be enough. Even if you finish changing your energy, it won't be very useful. The boost it gives is too weak.
You should know that if it is too weak, you won't be able to stand against the Ghost Wind when you reach the fourth layer."
"That is indeed my problem," Wei He nodded.
"You need to understand that the goal of visualizing a god is to get usable power for yourself.
Everything must focus on this point. Gods are just symbols for external images. You only need to focus on three things: What power do you want? Can you master it? How long will it take to master? That's all."
