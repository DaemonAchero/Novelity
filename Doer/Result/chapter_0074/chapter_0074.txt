"Is it because you're too prideful?" Wei He guessed.
"No." Jiang Su turned her gaze away and walked forward slowly. "It's because my father gave my oldest sister to a famous master as a concubine."
She paused, then continued, "My sister was weak and helpless. Every time her husband's family bullied her, she could only endure it. Our family's big business needed that master's backing. So she had no choice but to bear it."
"..." Wei He could pretty much figure out the rest.
"She raised me with so much love when I was small. We were very close. But every time I saw her get mistreated, forced to keep pleasing that man, my heart broke. I swore to myself that if I ever reached that level one day, I would make sure no one could touch her again. Because then, everyone would know she had me as a sister!"
Jiang Su's emotions suddenly ran high.
Wei He could only walk beside her in silence.
Everyone had their own story, whether it was Jiang Su or Guan Die. If you did not get close to someone, you would never understand what they went through.
Just like him. In Jiang Su's eyes, he was probably just a poor kid who got lucky with a breakthrough, maybe with a bit of kindness and loyalty, but nothing more.
None of them knew how hard he worked every time he snuck out to make money and hunt rare beast meat.
"Wei He, tell me," Jiang Su said after a quiet pause. "If I had agreed when Master tried to arrange our marriage, what would have happened?"
Wei He thought about it carefully.
"Nothing would be different."
"Why?"
"Because your legs are short."
"..."
Jiang Su was speechless, then suddenly burst out laughing.
She pointed at him, unable to find words. Her laughter grew louder and louder until tears streamed down her face, and she still could not stop.
Red Stone Town, the Zhang Family Restaurant.
Zhang Wanruo stared at the account book with a icy cold expression. Last month's profits had dropped again.
Population in Red Stone Town was shrinking, and customers were getting scarce. The restaurant was on the verge of collapsing.
She was holding on with everything she had, but all she got in return was bad news after bad news.
"Miss, have some tremella soup..." Her maid, Hongmei, brought over a bowl of watery soup.
There was tremella inside, but barely any. Just a tiny yellow scrap floating in the middle of plain broth with a few grains of rice. It looked completely unappetizing.
Zhang Wanruo closed the ledger and let out a long sigh.
"Hongmei, what if I sell the restaurant, leave Feiye City, and head to the provincial capital to join my uncle? Would things be better?"
"Miss... don't lose heart. We'll find a way," Hongmei comforted her.
Selling the business and leaving Feiye City sounded easy. But in this current climate, who would buy a half-dead restaurant?
Even if they managed to sell it and held gold notes in hand, who could guarantee they would make it to the provincial capital alive?
Before, the inner city kept basic order. The Zhang family could rely on their weak guard force to get by.
But as the situation worsened, the Seven-Family Alliance stopped caring about the outer city. Their shadow war with the Xiangqu Cult was getting hotter and spilling into the open every day.
Order in the outer city now depended entirely on raw brute force.
Each town area was kept in check mostly by local martial arts schools. Meanwhile, the gangs that bled the common people dry ended up becoming the last line of defense for order.
Red Stone Town relied on the Xiangzhu Boxing Academy to keep a lid on things.
Many thugs and troublemakers only held back because they feared the eyes of the Xiangzhu Boxing Academy.
"Sister! Sister!" A short boy wearing a melon-skin hat suddenly burst through the door, yelling as he ran.
"Good news! I just got good news!"
"What good news? Look at you running like that. Slow down, take it easy before you collapse," Zhang Wanruo said, catching the boy and wiping his sweat with a look of worry.
"It really is good news!" The boy was Zhang Songhao, her younger brother and the heir to the Zhang family.
He said with pure excitement, "I was hanging out with my friends just now, and I heard people talking! Someone at the Huishan Boxing Academy in Stone Bridge Town reached the Iron Skin level! He's a top master now, on the same level as Fifth Master Yao of the Mountain River Gang!"
Fifth Master Yao was the senior manager of the Mountain River Gang to whom the Zhang family had to pay monthly protection money. He was a real giant who ruled five whole town districts.
"He's incredible!" Zhang Songhao danced around with joy. "Do you know who that person is?!"
Zhang Wanruo and Hongmei both shook their heads blankly. They were not familiar with Stone Bridge Town, let alone the Huishan Boxing Academy.
A big figure capable of breaking through to Fifth Master Yao's level was not someone they could ever hope to cross paths with.
"It's the guy who sent a matchmaker for you back then! Remember? He took a liking to you and hired a matchmaker to come over! It's him!" Zhang Songhao clapped his hands and laughed out loud.
"Is that true?!" Zhang Wanruo was shocked and stood up abruptly.
Her pretty eyes went wide. She could hardly believe what she was hearing.
"Of course it's true! I asked around so many times! Hahaha! I told them that guy used to propose to my sister, and none of them believed me! Sister, you don't need to worry anymore! He sent a matchmaker to our doorstep back then, so he definitely liked you a lot. Now all you have to do is show a little interest, and he'll definitely become my brother-in-law!"
Zhang Songhao grinned. "A big boss like him can fix a tiny issue like our restaurant with a single word. After that, we can choose to go to the provincial capital or stay here. Whatever we want!"
"Oh my, this is such wonderful news!" Hongmei was both surprised and delighted. "That Master Wei He even used the Cheng family to propose back then. Who knew he would become so powerful today!"
She turned to her mistress.
"Miss, this is a massive blessing! With Master Wei's current achievement, you could become the wife of an influential man just like Fifth Master Yao! Ah, when that happens, the family business won't even matter anymore. You won't have to stress every day over plans and budgets."
"Exactly, sister! I already made a promise to invite my friends over for drinks when you two get married! It's been ages since I tasted alcohol. This is our big chance!" Her brother, Zhang Songhao, was trembling with excitement.
"I'll show those guys what a real master looks like!" Zhang Songhao clenched his fists, full of hope.
"Sister, say something! Why are you just standing there in a daze?"
"Yeah, Miss. This news is a huge blessing. Why don't you look happy at all?" Hongmei asked in confusion.
Zhang Wanruo stayed silent and slowly sat back down.
"You're both dreaming. Now that he's made it to the top, he won't look down at someone like me."
"How is that possible? Wei He was the one who asked for your hand first, but you said no. The worst we have to do now is send a matchmaker over ourselves, right?" Zhang Songhao said carelessly.
"That's right, Miss! You have no idea. Plenty of men who make it big still hold feelings for the girls they used to like. As long as you take a small step forward, won't he fall right into your lap?" Hongmei joined in with a smile.
Zhang Wanruo could only give a bitter smile. Listening to the two of them talk endlessly about a bright future, she only felt a growing sense of emptiness inside.
They did not know what Third Blood meant. They did not know what Fifth Master Yao represented. But just because they were ignorant did not mean she was.
A man in that position with that kind of strength in the outer city of Feiye City only had to wave his hand, and countless people would throw themselves at his feet.
Among them were plenty of women far prettier than her.
Ignorance breeds boldness. That was exactly what this was.
Zhang Wanruo stopped talking. She turned her face toward the window, watching the sun slowly set behind the mountains. There was disappointment in her heart, but also resolve.
Perhaps if she had accepted his proposal back then, her current trouble would be solved. But if a person relied entirely on others for help, they would never amount to anything in life anyway.
As for rejecting Wei He back then, she might feel a trace of regret, but not much, and she did not regret her choice.
Because no matter when it happened, given Wei He's conditions at the time, her personality would never let her accept.
'Am I really unable to pull through this crisis without relying on someone else? I don't believe it!' The emotions in Zhang Wanruo's eyes slowly faded, replaced once again by her original firmness.
Inner city, Zuihua Brothel.
"Back then, Jiang Yan, Xiao Ran, Cheng Shaojiu, and I sat right here talking about everything. We were even discussing you and Cheng Shaojiu. We used you as an example to joke around, while Jiang Yan explained how to win people's hearts."
Jiang Su pointed at a nearby dining table with a smile, proving that was where they had sat.

The two of them sat down at another table by the window and ordered food and drinks.
"Yeah. Back then, we laughed at your poor background, thinking a little favor from Cheng Shaojiu was enough to make you completely devoted to him. Looking back now... the ones who deserve to be laughed at are us," Jiang Su said with a self-mocking tone, downing a cup of tea in one gulp.
"Completely devoted?" Wei He smiled. "I was just returning a favor."
"You two were equals. Senior Brother Cheng said so back then, but we didn't believe it. Now I do." Jiang Su nodded. "Back then, no one could tell you had great ambitions hidden in your chest."
Now that she knew a bit more about Wei He, she understood his nature better. He was not someone who would follow another person, nor could he ever be tamed.
He seemed plain and quiet on the outside, but inside he was deeply proud and held himself in high regard. The only way to connect with him was through equal and genuine treatment.
"Master leaves Feiye City the day after tomorrow. Have you made up your mind yet?" Wei He asked casually. He reached out with his chopsticks to pick up a piece of food. It was stir-fried dried tofu with celery.
The savory dried tofu paired with the crisp, slightly bitter celery tasted quite delicious.
"I've decided," Jiang Su nodded. "I'm leaving, but the Jiang family stays."
"Hmm? Just you?" Wei He was startled and raised his head.
"No. My father already threw his lot in with the Wang family of the Seven-Family Alliance. He can't leave now. So I'll take a few relatives with me as a branch of the family and follow Master. Consider it leaving a spark of hope for our bloodline." Jiang Su spoke calmly.
"Makes sense," Wei He agreed.
"Aren't you worried at all?" Jiang Su glanced at him. "Your celebration ceremony is later this afternoon. Martial artists from all the surrounding towns will send their disciples to congratulate you. There will definitely be sparring tests."
"Not many martial arts schools are on good terms with Master, and I've memorized all of them. As for the rest, I'll just deal with them as they come. I'll play it by ear," Wei He said indifferently.
Since joining the Huishan Boxing Academy, he had rarely seen people from other martial arts schools. This would be a good opportunity.
