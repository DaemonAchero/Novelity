Wei He had a question in his mind.
If he thought of something, he did it.
He closed his eyes right away. When he opened them again, his breath became long and steady. Two air currents swirled like whirlpools into his lungs, then slowly blew back out.
The stuff inside the cave was blown all over the place.
Wei He's whole body turned dark red, and countless faint marks appeared on his skin.
He ignored all of this and raised his hand.
A single swing.
Swish!
His arm showed up in another spot in the blink of an eye. The speed was so fast that even Wei He could barely see it himself.
Still, he frowned.
Because even at this speed, he felt he was still slower than Senior Brother Huan Songzi.
Both were super fast, but Huan Songzi was not only a step faster, he could also go deeper into the True Realm. He could hide his body so that ordinary True Masters could not see him at all.
An unseen True Master with extreme speed.
And he had the power to break through Wei He's defense at any moment.
Wei He just thought about fighting him for a second, and the hairs on his arms stood up.
No matter how he ran the fight in his mind, he was always the one getting killed in a second.
If Huan Songzi went all out and used his ethereal state, Wei He would not live past one second.
This answer was like a bucket of ice water poured all over his head. It completely washed away the small bit of pride he had after his breakthrough.
"Thinking back, old Black Cross was much nicer at the same level..."
Black Cross was tough and hit hard. What was the point of running fast? Fighting face to face was what real men did.
"Looks like the real big power spike of the True Force system comes after the Five Steps of True Realm. I need to be careful from now on."
Wei He took a deep breath and turned off his Fifth Turn Dragon Breath state.
In this mode, he could boost his power four times over. But the power boost was too big. When he moved or attacked, he had to use a lot of True Force to shield his own body.
Otherwise, he would tear his own muscles apart.
After all, huge strength has huge backlash.
Once he knew his speed and power limits, Wei He packed his bags quickly, told Lou Shengyue what to do, and walked out of the cave down the mountain.
Next, it was time for him to go out and lock his senses.
Breaking through his skill only meant he had the power to face the ghost wind during sense-locking.
To fully finish his third sense-locking, he had to go down the mountain into the outside world, let the ghost wind blow on him, and make his force stronger.
Also, with three sense-lockings, he was ready to use star arrays to speed up his training.
As long as he could handle it, a True Master could use a star array to boost their training speed.
Of course, if the growth stayed solid or not depended on how strong their base was.
As a top Taoist sect, Xuanmiao Sect gave out this benefit too.
But Wei He knew that the basic array plate from the sect only boosted speed by ten percent. That was the worst kind, and it burned through star cores fast.
So he thought about where he could get a custom star array plate instead. A good, right-fit array plate could save a True Master decades or even a hundred years of hard work if used well.
It was very important. He had to take it seriously.
Before that, though, Wei He planned to go to his First Senior Sister to get the free star array first.
Over the years, he had learned about the market for star arrays. Normal star arrays were cheap, but those that boosted training speed cost a lot.
Even for the ones given out by the sect, you could not get one without paying over a hundred star stones.
The ones with better effects cost crazy money as you went up the line, and you could not even buy them with money.
At an auction held by the Haining Alliance before, a Cloud-Phoenix Array that boosted speed by seventy percent was sold for a huge price of ten pounds of gold star stones.
Black, gray, clear, silver, gold, and star cores—the ranks of star stones were clear as day.
Each rank was ten times the last. Ten pounds of gold star stones was a full year's loot for many small sects.
Putting his thoughts away, Wei He walked down the mountain path.
Since Xuanmiao Sect moved to Jujun Island, it was out in the deep sea. The ghost winds in the True Realm were always changing.
Sometimes a spot was a weak wind zone in the morning, then turned into a strong wind zone by the afternoon.
Strong beast monsters were everywhere in the deep sea, and sea beast attacks happened once or twice every year.
A sea beast attack meant monsters from the sea came onto the islands to hunt land creatures.
So Xuanmiao Sect set up many patrol teams to keep the area around Jujun Island safe.
Because of this, the need for star arrays among the sect and its True Masters went up fast.
A lot of star arrays were broken and needed fixing every year.
This made star arrays even rarer, and their prices went sky-high.
Inside Yuntian Palace.
Yuan Duzi held a book in her hand. Her eyes were blank, as if she was reading, or maybe just lost in thought.
Noticing Wei He's arrival, she slowly put the book away and got up from her bed.
Yuntian Palace was like a small palace. It had rooms for playing, martial arts, meetings, and rests.
Yuan Duzi wore a shoulderless black dress with a pure white fur shawl, like a fox tail that just covered her shoulders.
Walking out of her bedroom, she looked up at Wei He standing in the yard.
"You broke through? Good." Yuan Duzi nodded slightly. Eight years to lock senses three times was a normal speed.
The reason she noticed Wei He back then was that he had a huge amount of Primordial Blood from birth, just like her.
Looking at him now, the Primordial Blood inside Wei He was even bigger.
Yuan Duzi could faintly smell the scent of her own kind on Wei He.
It was the bloodline of the Peng Fish.
Right now, the Peng Fish scent on Wei He was almost at its peak.
Because of this, Yuan Duzi looked at Wei He with a much softer gaze.
She did not expect that the Peng Fish tribe, dead for so long, still had blood left in this world.
She pushed her thoughts down and waved one hand. In the corner of the room, a wooden drawer opened by itself. An item flew out and landed right in her palm.
"Now that you hit three sense-lockings, you can use a star array to speed up your training if you are able to. Of course, this costs money. The money used between True Masters is star stones.
As a sect disciple, you can get a free Star Gathering Array Plate from Xuanmiao Sect. It will speed up your daily training a bit.
If you are not happy with this standard array, you can go find a better one yourself, as long as you can pay the star cores."
Yuan Duzi's words made things clear to Wei He.
So this was to force True Masters to keep hunting sea beasts.
He took the star array she handed over.
"I understand. Thank you, Senior Sister."
"What I said just now is the standard for normal disciples. This one is my own personal gift to you. It is called the Heavy Moon Heavenly Wolf Array."
Yuan Duzi explained softly.
"This array uses ten ounces of black star stones every month. It speeds up force conversion by thirty percent. It also has basic warning and hiding features. It is much better than the ones given by the sect."
Wei He was shocked. A thirty percent boost made it a top-tier item among Star Gathering Arrays.
A small array plate like this was worth at least a few thousand black star stones if sold outside. Ordinary True Masters could never dream of getting one.
He sighed in his heart. If a rogue fighter outside wanted an array plate like this, they had to kill and loot, or owe a huge favor to ask an array master to make one. They would also have to pay a big price and collect materials everywhere.
It was nothing like a big sect where a senior sister gave away a good one just like that.
He knew the sect's standard Star Gathering Array only boosted speed by ten percent.
He was planning to search everywhere for a custom one, but now he had a good one right in his hands.
Senior Sister Yuan Duzi was really generous.
"Thank you for the gift, Senior Sister!" Wei He took the array plate carefully and looked closely at it.
A lifelike black giant wolf was carved on the plate. Unlike normal wolves, this wolf had six legs.
Around the wolf, there were many blurry black symbols that looked like they were moving around.
The array plate itself felt like a living thing.
This solved his big problem. He did not need to raise money for a custom star array anymore.
"I know you need to go out and hunt star cores, but be careful outside. The sea area has been changing a lot lately. Strong wind zones move very fast. Keep an eye on your surroundings at all times. If things look bad, leave right away."
Yuan Duzi warned him.
Facing a natural disaster like a strong wind zone, even she was helpless.
Any level of beast monster could show up in a strong wind zone, and their numbers were endless.
Even if she stayed in a strong wind zone for too long, she would be in danger.
"Wei He understands."
Wei He nodded with a serious face and bowed.
"If you want to hunt, the areas near the sect have been cleared out. In the Yuanxi region, there are three good places left," Yuan Duzi said softly.
"First, go southwest for about nine thousand six hundred miles to a big island named Black Candle.
Second, if you can swim well, go north by sea for six thousand miles. You will see a sea trench named Gui Xuan. It is bottomless. As long as you do not go down past two miles, you will be fine.
Third is the Poluosha Islands. It has many species and is the main front line for sea beast attacks. Even out of season, many beasts and monsters come out of the sea to attack people.
However, besides watching out for beasts, you must also watch out for people there.
It is close to the Haining Alliance. Our relationship with them is just average. If you go, you better bring someone with you."
Yuan Duzi explained slowly.
Wei He thought it over. Besides hunting for star cores, he also needed to do his sense-locking on this trip.
That meant he needed time to rest and do nothing else but lock his senses.
So the best choice was the Poluosha Islands.
People gathered there, so there would definitely be safe places to rest.
Also, if he got the chance on this trip, he needed to look for the key item to finish his Fifth Turn Dragon Breath—Dragon Lock Wood.
The final turn of Dragon Breath needed a strange substance inside Dragon Lock Wood.
Without it, he could not finish the last step.
Wei He had looked everywhere for it before, but found no clues.
The Poluosha Islands had lots of people and True Masters gathered to fight sea beasts. It was the best place for news.
If he could not find Dragon Lock Wood there, his chances anywhere else would be even lower.
He made up his mind.
Wei He bowed to Yuan Duzi, took his leave, and left Yuntian Palace.
After that, he went home to talk about the blind date with the Yao family, then gave some pointers to Wei An and his nine other disciples before leaving.
With everything ready, he got onto the Lou family's boat alone and headed toward the Poluosha Islands.
He could not take one boat straight to the islands.
If he did that, everyone would know he was from Xuanmiao Sect.
Then a bunch of secret enemies targeting Xuanmiao Sect would come after him.
To avoid trouble, Wei He took the Lou family's boat to a small island halfway there.
He got off the boat, changed his clothes, removed all marks on his body, and got onto a normal big boat heading for the Poluosha Islands.
For a True Master, nothing was safer than hiding among normal people.
