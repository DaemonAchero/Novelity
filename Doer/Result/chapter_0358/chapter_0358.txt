A bleak war horn sounded, its low cry drifting over the camp.
In the unseen spiritual layer, grey and black smoke rose like living things. They intertwined to form massive arrays tens of meters wide. These small arrays linked together, weaving a far larger ritual pattern.
In an instant, the entire camp was enveloped by a vast array of black smoke.
Yet in the real world, the soldiers noticed nothing. The military camp functioned as normal. Guards stood watch, patrols moved along their paths, troops trained, and interrogators worked. Everything was in order.
A fast horse burst through the gates, galloping deep into the rows of grey tents.
Urgent battle reports were passed up the chain of command, landing straight on the desk inside the central command tent.
Inside, the grand alliance of Dayuan sat in state. Nine lords had gathered, each flanked by their top generals.
Among them was Chen Ling, the military commander of Tai Province. Behind him stood a Master from the Wushi Sect and a high-ranking general from the Taian Army.
Beside them was the commander of Jin Province, a long-bearded elder backed by experts from the Wanfei Palace and his own general.
The other seven provinces were set up the same way. Even Yun Province had sent their representative.
The man sitting in the main seat held ultimate command over them all.
He was Li Zhouzhao, the Grand Commander of Zhong Province.
Li Zhouzhao looked refined and elegant, with a long flowing beard and narrow, sharp eyes. He was tall and built like a mountain. Born from a thousand-year-old noble family, he was a master of the arts and a famous calligrapher who had created his own widely practiced script.
The Li family was one of the top three powers in all of Zhong Province.
More importantly, a figure in a grey robe with silver trim stood behind him.
This figure wore a plain white mask with only two eyeholes, showing a pair of faint golden eyes. Nothing else could be seen—no face, no skin. Every inch of his body was hidden under the robe and mask.
Yet even standing completely silent behind Li Zhouzhao, the terrifying pressure radiating from him weighed like a mountain on the experts of every province, leaving them gasping for breath.
"The state of Wu has deployed a million troops, striking from three paths. Most of Zhong Province has fallen. Tai Province and Hai Province are barely holding on. What strategy do you all have?"
Li Zhouzhao spoke in a calm, deep voice, sweeping his gaze across the room.
The allied forces had fewer than six hundred thousand men in total. After all, no lord could send every soldier away; they needed troops to defend their own homelands. Gathering this much force at the edge of the Sishui plains was already their absolute limit.
Six hundred thousand against a million...
Worse, the enemy was led by Grandmaster Modo of Wu, a monster who had defeated several Grandmasters on his own.
Silence fell over the tent. Representatives from each province searched for a solution.
"Wu broke the taboo first by letting Grandmaster Modo step onto the battlefield. Since they crossed the line, why shouldn't we pay them back in kind?"
A massive man with blonde hair and a full beard spoke up with a harsh voice.
His name was Xu Shaowen, a top general from Wan Province. The previous commander of Wan Province—his uncle, who was like a father to him—had been assassinated by the experts of Wu's Temple of Worship. He had brought his army here for revenge.
"I have heard that you have old ties with the Jinlian Sect of the Taoist order, Commander Li," Xu Shaowen said, turning his gaze. "If you could invite a master from the Jinlian Sect to descend the mountain..."
He suddenly looked toward another corner.
"Also, Lord Ouyang of Huan Province, can you ask the experts from the Eastern Sect to step in and fight Wu? As far as I know, Huan Province is the sacred birthplace of the Eastern Sect of Buddhism, isn't it?"
The commander of Huan Province was a thin man named Ouyang De. A Buddhist expert stood behind him.
Hearing the question, Ouyang De shook his head gently.
"Before coming here, I visited the Eastern Sect five times. Unfortunately... the only Grandmaster at the ancestral hall is on the verge of death. As for the other Grandmasters..." He shook his head and stopped talking.
Clearly, it was a dead end.
Grandmasters stood above the mortal realm. They were not easily ordered around. Grandmaster Modo of Wu was an anomaly.
"Must the fate of our Dayuan Empire truly rely entirely on the Taoist sects? How ridiculous!" the commander of Ting Province spat angrily.
"Everyone knows Wu worships Buddha. The Guangci Sect used to be a branch of Buddhism. Why would they ever help us?" another man sneered.
Li Zhouzhao tapped his hand lightly on the table, instantly bringing order back to the room.
"I have already convinced Grandmaster Jinding of the Jinlian Sect to come help. With him working alongside the Wushi Sect and the Xuanmiao Sect, our Grandmasters can stall the Temple of Worship led by Modo."
He paused for a second.
"As for their Grandmasters personally assassinating our leading generals, we have already set up the Star Array. Under the pressure of our massive army, even a Grandmaster will not be able to escape easily if they dare enter the array."
Grandmasters were immensely strong. But they were not invincible.
The Star Array gathered the bloodthirst and mental force of the vast army, forging an ocean of martial intent.
This created an invisible forcefield within the spiritual inner layer. Any expert stepping into this field with hostility would instantly face the crushing pressure of hundreds of thousands of soldiers.
Even a Grandmaster would see their strength drop sharply under such weight. If the alliance seized that moment to swarm them, the Grandmaster could genuinely die.
Because of this, no expert who could touch the spiritual inner layer would lightly sneak into a large army to kill a general.
Instead, many martial experts fought duels in front of the lines. Those duels tested not just the strength of two men, but the momentum of two armies' Star Arrays. Without an army's spirit behind them, top experts suffered too much suppression to use their full power.
"You do not need to worry," Li Zhouzhao added. "With the Star Array active, any Grandmaster who sneaks in will be weakened to the point where they are weaker than a normal martial artist. The Temple of Worship can only harass us from the edges."
Everyone understood this logic. Otherwise, they wouldn't have gathered their armies here in the first place.
"Right now, Tai Province and Hai Province are being crushed by the Wu army. The enemy has set up bases and taken cities in both places. Should we save Tai Province first, or Hai Province? Any suggestions?"
Li Zhouzhao looked at the crowd, his brows slightly drawn.
Which side to save was a dangerous choice. The alliance had limited troops. Splitting six hundred thousand men against the three hundred thousand enemy troops on either side gave them little chance of winning.
"I believe we should save my Tai Province first," Chen Ling, the commander of Tai Province, stated. "Hai Province has only three cities. It is vast but sparsely populated. Even if Wu takes the whole place, they won't get many supplies.
On the other hand, my Tai Province has numerous cities. If it falls, it will give Wu a massive supply base."
His reasoning made complete sense.
"Furthermore, out of the three Grandmasters of the Xuanmiao Sect, one was heavily injured and returned to base, while another is severely wounded with broken strength. Only one remains standing... They can no longer affect the overall war. So..."
"Chen Ling, are you burning the bridge after crossing it?!" a man shouted angrily before he could finish.
"Not at all," Chen Ling raised his hand to cut him off. "The Xuanmiao Sect acted with honor before. Their three Grandmasters came straight to Zhong Province to fight the Guangci Sect. I respect that."
In truth, the three Grandmasters of Xuanmiao Sect had only gone there originally to snatch dragon blood. They were forced to team up against the enemy when the Guangci Sect launched a sudden attack.
Still, they had bled for Dayuan. If the alliance abandoned them now just because two were injured, the great sects behind the allied armies would lose faith in the leadership.
"My point is... we abandon Hai Province, but we do not abandon the Xuanmiao Sect!"
Chen Ling paused, looking at the thoughtful faces around the room, and smiled slightly.
"Put simply, we will gather our resources to compensate them, then ask the Xuanmiao Sect to relocate their entire sect!"
The tent went dead silent.
Deep down, everyone knew saving Tai Province over Hai Province was the right choice. If Wu took Tai Province, their momentum would become unstoppable. Besides, the Wushi Sect was far stronger than the Xuanmiao Sect.
Faced with such a choice, anyone would choose Tai Province.
Chen Ling's proposal sounded fair. It was just asking the Xuanmiao Sect to move to a new location, and everyone would pool money to pay them back. No big deal.
Yet from the perspective of the Xuanmiao Sect, asking a thousand-year-old sect to abandon its holy ground was asking them to dig up their own roots.
It was simply their bad luck that among the three major Taoist branches, Xuanmiao was the weakest.
As for the ancient true beast sealed beneath the Xuanmiao Sect, Chen Ling did not bring it up at all. He wasn't ignorant; he ignored it on purpose.
The Xuanmiao Sect could never truly move. If they left, the seal array would collapse, freeing the ancient beast. Once freed, its absolute hatred would drive it to destroy the Xuanmiao Sect first.
Moving away meant suffering a crushing blow. Staying meant certain death.
Everyone in the tent understood this ugly truth, which was why no one spoke.
In the end, Chen Ling voiced the final decision himself.
"Lord Chen makes a good point," Li Zhouzhao sighed. "We can only wrong the Xuanmiao Sect this time..."
"Can the Dongzhou Academy and Taiyi Academy send people to help the Xuanmiao Sect move?" He looked coldly at the representative from Dongzhou.
The representative gave a bitter smile and shook his head.
Taiyi Academy was built on the backing of Taoism and Buddhism. After their grand scholar lost a debate at Taihu Lake years ago, he had vanished. The academy was falling apart, and many masters had already left.
As for Dongzhou Academy, most of their experts had been wiped out or crippled in the earlier battles in Zhong Province. They had no manpower left to spare.
"Then it is settled," Li Zhouzhao said flatly. "Next, let us discuss our march route..."
Just like that, in a brief moment inside a war tent, the fate of the ancient Xuanmiao Sect was decided.
Everyone left the conversation with heavy, dark thoughts.
