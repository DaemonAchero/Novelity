Ouyang Zhao felt a sudden chill.
She had seen beast bait before. But judging by the deep vibration shaking the ground, the sheer number of beasts coming this way was insane.
She leaped up, landing lightly on a high tree branch, and looked into the distance.
Deep in the woods, a dense, blood-eyed tide of beasts was tearing through the forest, rushing right toward them.
Flies, runners, crawlers, jumpers—every kind of beast was there. Wolves, wild boars, black bears, tigers, giant pythons. They looked like ordinary animals, but they moved like a dark wave, flooding the forest.
Worst of all, this was the Black House Mountain Range.
Nothing that survived here was without venom.
"Beast horde! Fall back!!" Ouyang Zhao roared. Her voice echoed for a hundred meters, making the ears of every fighting warrior buzz.
Moments later, a grey-black wave of beasts surged past the base of her tree.
The horde was like a dark ocean. In seconds, it swallowed the entire Wanqing Sect base, drowning all the fighters in a sea of claws and teeth.
Neither the Wanqing Sect members nor the Sun-Seeking Willow-Night assassins could stand against it.
The fight stopped dead. Everyone scrambled up into the trees to survive. But the branches were already crawling with tree pythons and deadly insects.
The intense battle was forced to a sudden halt.
Thousands of beasts rushed into the camp, flooding every spot where the powder had been scattered. They devoured the battlefield like a dark wave.
Strangely, the Wanqing Sect members carried something on their bodies that forced the beasts to turn away, leaving them untouched.
"Move!"
Wan Qingqing barked a low command, leading her people along the edge of the horde to escape.
The remaining dozens of sect members leaped from branch to branch. Not a single beast attacked them.
The Sun-Seeking Willow-Night assassins were not so lucky. The beasts went wild, swarming them from all sides.
In seconds, the tables turned.
Still, it was only temporary. These lured beasts could tear apart normal fighters, but they couldn't stop true Bone-Forging or Organ-Refining masters.
Even so, the beast tide leveled the playing field for the outnumbered Wanqing Sect.
"Go, fast! The bait won't last long!" Wan Ling yelled to her friend Xiao Qingyu, dodging a passing green python. "Once we're safe, Wei He can break free too!"
"Someone has to stay and hold them off," Xiao Qingyu said flatly. "You and Qingqing go. They want you two."
"I can't go..." Wan Ling smiled weakly.
Her force surged out violently. Her energy turned razor-sharp, far deadlier than before.
She was using a secret technique: the Wanqing Extreme Dark Force. It boosted her force's piercing power to pair with her Cloud-Rain Art, allowing her to stand against Organ-Refining masters for a short time.
Otherwise, she had no chance against their overwhelming strength.
But every secret technique had a price.
Xiao Qingyu stayed silent, taking one last long look at Wan Ling. Then, she turned and leaped into the dark trees.
"Go!"
This was their only chance, bought by their Sect Master Wei He.
,The Wanqing Sect split into two groups. One group, knowing they couldn't escape, ran a different way to draw enemy fire.
The main group ran for their lives, trying to break out of the zone.
Out of more than a hundred people, only a few dozen remained. Even with the repellent, some slipped, fell into the horde, and were trampled to death under red-eyed beasts.
This move hurt both sides badly.
"Catch them!"
The ten masters from Sun-Seeking Willow-Night, including Xu Fei, weren't stopped by mere beasts for long. They slaughtered the creatures around them, broke free, and gave chase.
The beast tide surged beneath Wei He and Zhu Chen.
A few mindless beasts snapped at Zhu Chen as they passed, but he killed them effortlessly, dropping their bodies into the dark below.
Wei He was covered in the special repellent, so the beasts ignored him completely. He ran fast through the high trees, dragging Zhu Chen behind him.
He had no intention of fighting Zhu Chen to the death. His only goal was to keep Wan Qingqing and the others alive.
The Wanqing Sect had taken heavy losses, but their enemies were losing even more in the chaos.
"Is this your little trick?" Zhu Chen sneered, leaping across the branches close behind him.
He understood Wei He's plan now.
Wei He refused to fight. He just kept running.
Zhu Chen had thought his men could easily wipe out the rest of the sect without him. But with this beast tide, he knew that if Wei He escaped today, the whole ambush was a failure.
"A trick, maybe. But it works," Wei He said calmly.
Even at full speed, his breathing was steady, his voice quiet in the rushing wind.
"And now?" Zhu Chen said, his tone turning cold. "How long can you run? Do you really think I can't touch you?"
He was growing impatient. In just a few months, this man's speed had grown terrifyingly fast.
Before, Zhu Chen could have knocked him down from a distance with thrown force and hidden weapons. But now, every force projectile he threw was blocked head-on.
It made no sense.
Even top Organ-Refining masters like Wang Zhihe would be broken and injured after taking a few hits from his range attacks.
Yet Wei He just took the hits, his defensive force rattling slightly, and kept running.
How could a Bone-Forging fighter have a defensive force stronger than an Organ-Refining master?
Zhu Chen made a split-second choice. He stopped abruptly, turned, and dashed toward the escaping Wanqing Sect members instead.
If he couldn't catch Wei He, he would take Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing.
Seeing his intent, Wei He stopped cold and turned back to intercept him.
Zhu Chen was forcing a fight, and Wei He had no choice but to take it.
"Not running anymore?!" Zhu Chen laughed, striking with palm and hand-blade at the same time.
His force burst forth, a flash of white like a silk ribbon slicing through the dark.
Clang!
They collided and broke apart instantly.
Wei He threw both palms out, layers of force surging through his bones. Two compressed vortexes of dense energy formed in his palms, hitting Zhu Chen’s hand-blade directly.
Just as he expected, a sharp, concentrated force cut through his layers of defense like a blade.
If his force hadn't been unnaturally thick, that single hit would have cut him in half.
Knowing the danger, Wei He didn't linger. He struck once and backed off instantly, relying on his thick force shields to absorb the damage.
Zhu Chen blinked, surprised.
"You came prepared. To take my hit and walk away unhurt... I underestimated you."
Zhu Chen stood still on a branch, looking at Wei He across the dark space.
"The court needs men right now. Join us again, Master Wei. I will give your sect a fresh start."
"Do you really think I'd fall for that twice?" Wei He sneered.
They were far from the main battle now. Thick fog surrounded them, hiding everything in white gloom.
"If you refuse, none of your people will leave this forest alive," Zhu Chen sighed.
"You should have said that earlier," Wei He said coldly. "Now, it's too late."
"It's not too late if you say yes now," Zhu Chen said, lowering his right arm. "Men as fast as you are rare, even among our ranks."
"Pass," Wei He said softly. "If I live through today, I will kill every last one of you."
Zhu Chen sighed softly.
"Then I have to use this..."
From his sleeve, a semi-transparent bone blade slid into his hand. It was thin as a cicada's wing, glowing with a faint, deadly chill.
"This is the Seven-Star Blade, one of the grand art techniques of the Great Yuan."
Zhu Chen held the blade up, his eyes cold and solemn.
"You should feel honored to die by it."
"Honored?" Wei He’s face remained blank. "Catch me first."
He slammed his foot into the branch.
BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!
Thick clouds of poison powder exploded around Zhu Chen, blasting away the fog and filling the air with deadly dust.
"Tricks..." Zhu Chen sneered, pouring his force into the bone blade. Seven glowing spots appeared on the edge.
"Seven-Star Method!" He raised the blade high. The seven spots spun like high-speed engines, making the blade hum violently.
"Die!!" he roared, leaping forward.
BOOM!!!
A massive shadow tore up from below.
A giant, dark mouth slammed shut!
Pfft!
Zhu Chen was swallowed whole, snapped up in a massive jaw of razor teeth.
The long, unnatural body of the Three-Headed Fox settled silently on the trees behind them, breathing out a long, heavy hiss.
Hssshhh!
Thick fog poured from its three massive heads.
"Finally..."
Wei He let out a long breath. Without looking back, he turned and disappeared into the dark forest.
All that poison had just been to blind Zhu Chen—and feed him to the fox.
