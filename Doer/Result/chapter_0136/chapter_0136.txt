Wan Ling looked around at the crowd.
"So, who is willing to step up and act as the screener for my daughter?"
Silence fell over the room. Everyone hesitated. This meant real combat with live weapons, and one mistake could lead to severe injury.
Fortunately, Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing had already chosen three people beforehand. This question was just a formality.
Following their prior agreement, Wan Ling looked over at three specific men in the crowd. These three had agreed to act as screeners. After all, you couldn’t just put anyone up there; the screeners needed real strength to filter out as many weak challengers as possible. Otherwise, Wan Qingqing would run out of stamina in a long sequence of back-to-back fights.
But as Wan Ling looked over, she noticed something was wrong.
The three men shifted their eyes away and lowered their heads. They stayed completely silent, breaking their promise. They were supposed to "voluntarily" step forward right now, but for some reason, not one of them spoke up.
"Is anyone willing?" Wan Ling asked again, raising her voice.
The martial artists gathered here included members from the Wanqing Branch and other branches. Some people were still on the road because of the long distance.
Still, nobody answered.
Wan Qingqing looked around the room. The inner court disciples she had taken care of in the past were all shrinking back like turtles, afraid to meet her gaze. The three men her mother hired were also looking down, dead silent.
She knew many people here simply wanted her to submit, give up her stubbornness, and marry into the Lishan Sect. But she never expected so few people would actually stand by her side.
The atmosphere in the hall turned cold and quiet.
If no one stepped up as a screener, Wan Qingqing would have to fight every single opponent herself. That was a huge disadvantage.
Even though she was a Martial Master who had mastered force control and was currently forging her bones, a person’s internal force was not bottomless. Internal force was tied directly to blood vitality and physical stamina. Once drained, it grew thin and weak, requiring time and rest to recover.
Worse yet, everyone knew her strength and reputation. Anyone who dared to step up to challenge her today would definitely be a Martial Master. No one below that level would bother trying.
Facing a streak of Martial Masters would wear Wan Qingqing out fast, no matter how strong she was.
She could try to delay the fights to get rest, but delaying brought its own risks.
Wan Ling knew Vice Sect Master Zhou Shun didn't come here just to watch. Battles between Martial Masters were not decided by internal force alone; combat instinct and tactical adaptability mattered just as much.
In theory, Qingqing was stronger than Yan Jiuhai. But if her condition dropped, the mental fatigue would build up even if she got physical rest. If the other side figured out her fighting style and prepared a counter-move, the outcome would become unpredictable.
Now that the three pre-arranged screeners had backed out, there was no doubt who was behind it: Yan Jiuhai.
"If really no one is willing, then..." Wan Ling felt helpless. She was about to make the announcement.
Snap.
Wei He stepped on a dry leaf, crushing it as he looked up at a three-story building painted in red lacquer.
Inside Zhongling Manor, there were two main places to eat: Baiwei Building and Shanhe Building. The former was a regular, affordable restaurant, while the latter was a luxury spot reserved for wealthy martial artists.
Shanhe Building was shaped like a massive hollow cylinder. Dining tables were set up in rings around a giant parasol tree right in the middle. Across its three floors, it could fit over three hundred guests.
Wearing a long black cloak, Wei He stepped forward.
He didn’t care about political schemes, messy traps, or complicated local situations. He didn't care about anyone else's plans; he only cared about his own.
If you kill the source of the trouble, everything gets quiet. Doesn't it?
Suddenly, he slammed his foot into the ground and shot straight up into the air.
On the second floor of Shanhe Building, near a window, Yan Jiuhai was halfway through chewing a braised chicken foot.
Suddenly, a voice roared like thunder.
"Yan Jiuhai! Pay for my daughter's life!!"
In an instant, a massive black figure burst up from below, charging straight at him with terrifying speed.
Yan Jiuhai flinched and looked up, confused by the masked attacker rushing at him. He stood up quickly and glanced around at the other guests on his floor, assuming someone sitting near him was being hunted by an enemy.
He had barely stood up.
BOOM!!
On the second floor of Shanhe Building, Yan Jiuhai was thrown straight through the wall, smashing hard into the trunk of the massive tree in the central courtyard.
An unstoppable force poured into his raised right arm. Before Yan Jiuhai could even realize what was happening, his world spun. The single punch blasted him through two wooden walls and slammed him right into the tree.
Before he could recover, Wei He leaped across the open air, giving him no space to breathe.
The two collided against the tree trunk, exchange fast blows that echoed like deep explosions.
Yan Jiuhai was, after all, a peak fighter halfway through bone forging. His instincts kicked in. He pushed off the trunk and landed heavy on the first floor.
"Who the hell are you?! I don't even know you!" he screamed.
"You ruined my daughter's honor and killed her to hide it! Today you pay in blood!!" Wei He roared like a tiger and lunged again.
They clashed once more. Tables, chairs, wooden pillars, and decorative screens were instantly smashed to pieces by their stray internal force. The other guests scattered in panic, though a few brave, high-level fighters stayed nearby to watch the show.
The more Yan Jiuhai fought, the more terrified he became. The attacker barely bothered to defend, relying instead on raw, terrifying strength. After just a few dozen blows, Yan Jiuhai's arms felt numb and ready to break.
And that was after his bone forging had hardened his arms significantly. Any normal Martial Master would have been beaten into paste already.
He had only come here to grab a quick meal while waiting for his father, Yan Junshan, to arrive. He never expected to be ambushed like this.
Even though he misjudged the attacker's strength, his father was close by. They were supposed to meet here, eat, and then head over to the martial contest in the afternoon. As long as he held out a little longer, his father would arrive and kill this maniac on the spot.
Yan Jiuhai used his footwork to dodge and weave, but after just a few steps, the attacker caught up again.
"Focus!" Yan Jiuhai channeled his blood vitality and internal force, boosting his power by several leaps in a fraction of a second. He raised his arm to block.
BANG!
Wei He threw another palm strike, slamming straight into Yan Jiuhai's elbow.
Seeing his guard still up, Wei He raised both arms high and brought them down like heavy blades, chopping repeatedly.
Three strikes in a row.
BANG! BANG! BANG!!
Yan Jiuhai's protective internal force shattered completely.
"Father! Save me!!" he screamed out in terror.
WHOOSH!
A heavy palm stopped just inches from his face. The sudden wind made him squeeze his eyes shut.
When he opened his eyes again, the attacker was gone. Not far off, a figure in a pale yellow robe was flying toward him at top speed.
"Hai'er! Are you alright?!" The yellow-robed old man caught his son.
"I... cough!" Before Yan Jiuhai could speak, he spat out a mouthful of blood. His eyes rolled back, and he passed out cold.
On the wall right next to them, a line of words was written in deep marks: A daughter's death must be paid in blood!
"Hai'er! Hai'er!!" The old man's face twisted in shock. He quickly tapped several key pressure points on his son's body to stop the internal bleeding. Then, he scooped him up and dashed toward the main hall of Zhongling Manor, where the local doctors were posted.
Meanwhile, on the other side of the estate.
Wei He quickly shed his black cloak and mask while running. He leaped into a dense thicket of trees in the woods, and by the time he stepped out, he was back in his original clothes.
Using his Flying Dragon movement technique combined with his massive strength, he moved like a passing shadow. In less than thirty seconds, he was back at the bush he had left earlier.
He strolled out nonchalantly, shaking water off his hands with a relaxed expression, looking like someone who had just taken a quick bathroom break in the woods. Then he walked back toward the carriage waiting by the road.
Inside the carriage, Zhang Lingxu and Meng Qing were eating snacks and chatting happily, waiting for his return.
Inside the building.
Wan Ling looked at the crowd with a feeling of helplessness.
Her backup plan had fallen apart at the last moment. The inner court disciples who had received so much help from her daughter were all keeping quiet, clearly pressured or bought off beforehand.
She knew these people were scared of the Yan family's power in the Lishan Sect, and they were also scared of the pressure from inside the Tianyin Sect to force this marriage.
There were simply too few voices willing to stand up against it.
One look at Vice Sect Master Zhou sitting nearby said it all. The old man wasn't here to enjoy the view; he was clearly backing Yan Jiuhai. His son, Zhou Yugui, was close friends with Yan Jiuhai, making their intentions blatantly obvious.
"Don't worry. Trust Qingqing, she can handle it. We can just give her longer rest periods between fights and treat it as real combat practice," Xiao Qingyu said softly, squeezing Wan Ling's hand.
At this point, that was the only way to look at it.
Wan Ling looked down at her daughter standing in the hall, feeling a pang of sadness.
Wan Qingqing wasn't looking at her mother. Instead, she swept her eyes across the inner court disciples with a faint look of hope and plea. These were junior brothers and sisters she had personally guided and cared for. But every single one of them lowered their heads, avoiding her eyes.
The hope in her eyes slowly faded into disappointment.
She suddenly thought of Junior Brother Wei He, who had told her to contact him if she ever needed help. The message had been sent out long ago. With his speed, if he had run full throttle, he should have arrived by now.
Yet he wasn't here. The message behind his absence was painfully clear.
They were all afraid.
The light in Wan Qingqing's eyes slowly dimmed.
Then she took a breath and thought about it from their perspective. If she were in their shoes, asked to take on massive pressure and danger for a senior sister who had only shown her small favors, she might hesitate too.
Accepting the harsh reality, Wan Qingqing offered a calm, bright smile.
"I'm fine, Mother. Let's just do it this way."
Wan Ling saw the change in her daughter's expression and felt her silent disappointment. She wanted to say something to comfort her, but everyone in the hall was waiting for her official decision.
"In that case, we will—"
"REPORT!!"
A loud shout from outside the main entrance cut Wan Ling off.
A Tianyin Sect disciple burst inside, drenched in sweat. He cupped his hands to bow to Wan Ling and Zhou Shun, breathing so hard he could barely keep his balance.
"What happened, Chang Kuan?" Wan Ling asked, puzzled. This disciple had stayed behind to work for the Wanqing Branch.
Disciples of the Tianyin Sect had the choice to leave or stay after completing their training. Those who stayed were given various administrative duties. Chang Kuan was one of them.
"Branch... Branch Leader! Outside... outside!!" Chang Kuan pointed outside, his face pale with panic.
"Outside!... Something... happened...!!" He was frantic, but he couldn't get a full sentence out.
"What actually happened?" Wan Ling frowned.
"It's... it's... the... the... opposite...!" Chang Kuan pointed at the door, sweating harder the more he tried to speak.
"The... the restaurant..."
Push.
Another sturdy Tianyin Sect disciple shoved Chang Kuan aside as he rushed in.
"You stuttering like that isn't helping anyone, get out of the way!"
The sturdy disciple cupped his hands quickly.
"Branch Leader, Vice Sect Master! Over at Shanhe Building across the courtyard, Senior Brother Yan Jiuhai of the Lishan Sect was suddenly attacked by a mysterious man in black while having lunch! He was beaten unconscious and is currently in critical condition!"
"What?!" Wan Ling stood up in shock. Her face went pale, not out of concern, but out of pure disbelief.
Beside her, Zhou Shun's expression turned dark as he stood up.
"Are you sure this news is true?" he asked in a heavy voice.
"Every word is true, Vice Sect Master! You can go see for yourself; a massive crowd has already gathered at Shanhe Building. They say Senior Brother Yan ruined the honor of the attacker's daughter and killed her, so the man came seeking blood revenge!" the disciple reported quickly.
"Let's go!"
Zhou Shun leaped forward, flashing across the hall, and disappeared from sight in a second.
Wan Ling activated her internal force and rushed out right behind him.
Only then did the rest of the room wake up from their shock, scrambling to follow them out.
