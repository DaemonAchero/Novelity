A white seagull spread its wings and glided over the ocean. It touched the water lightly, then climbed high into the sky and flew off into the distance.
Below the bird, a large black ship moved fast across the blue sea. It left a long trailing wake of white foam behind its stern.
"I managed to use my agility to shake him off, and luckily they didn't chase after me in time. Otherwise, we would all be dead right now."
Wei He sat cross-legged on the deck. His face was pale, making him look badly injured.
After killing Black Cross, he had prepared quickly, swam underwater to catch up with the ship, and climbed back onto the deck.
That Black Cross was indeed dangerous. His strength, speed, explosive power, and reaction time were higher than anyone Wei He had ever met before.
Earlier, Wei He had killed the Three-Phase Bodhisattva and the five master monks who chased him later. He had roughly measured their power back then.
Compared to them, this Black Cross was a whole level stronger.
He was far stronger than Senior Sister Yao Wan, who couldn't even beat the Three-Phase Bodhisattva.
"The man with the black cross on his forehead must be a major figure in the Wu State. It's a pity we stayed for too short a time to get much information," Wei He sighed, shaking his head.
He had gone all out this time.
Although his Real Force energy was deep, the enemy's strength wasn't just raw power. It carried a strange, special aura.
That aura felt somewhat similar to the corrosive nature of his Real Force.
He had never seen this kind of aura on any opponent he fought before.
Lou Chengxun brought over some healing medicine for internal use, a guilty look on his face.
"It was us holding you back. Otherwise, with Junior Brother Wei's strength alone, you could have easily escaped."
He was not surprised that Wei He got away so easily. After all, Wei He was a personal disciple of First Senior Sister Yuanduzi. Would she let him out here without giving him some life-saving tools?
He was just worried that Wei He would blame them for not fighting to the death to buy time.
"It's not your fault. The Wu State has been planning this for a long time. This wasn't an accident; we just happened to run into them."
Wei He stood up, took the medicine pills, smelled them, and popped a few into his mouth.
Even though he wasn't actually hurt, he still had to put on a show.
As for these people from the Lou family, they were neither family nor close friends. Expecting strangers to risk their lives for him was out of the question.
He had lived for so many years, and he had never been that naive.
"I found it!!" Lou Shengyue rushed out from the cabin door, holding a small booklet in her arms and shouting loudly.
"I found the file on that Black Cross! He's a top-tier fighter on par with our Great Yuan Dao Registry masters, officially recorded in the books! And his identity is huge!"
She ran over to Wei He and Lou Chengxun, flipped the booklet to a specific page, and handed it over.
"Look, this man is one of the six Buddha Disciples of the Hall of Buddha Worship in Wu State! His master is Kasyapa Buddha, one of the top Buddha Masters in the hall!"
"He goes by the title Black Cloud Monk. As a Buddha Disciple, he is one of the most likely candidates to break through and become a Buddha Master! On top of that, he has killed higher-level Dao Registry masters many times, and he knows Great Yuan's culture very well. That's why he was specifically listed." Lou Shengyue explained in detail.
"Black Cloud Monk..." Wei He thought back to the moves that Black Cross used earlier. No wonder the man was so strong.
However, it didn't matter now. The man was already dead. Whatever identity he had was completely meaningless.
"Why would Wu State send such a powerful figure out to the deep ocean?" Lou Chengxun asked in confusion.
"No matter what, we will stick to the original plan and keep hunting the whale. Then we'll send one person back to report to the sect," Wei He said in a heavy voice.
"Keep hunting the whale?!!" Lou Shengyue and Lou Chengxun both cried out in shock. "But Wu State has already set a trap here. If we keep hunting, isn't that..."
Lou Chengxun couldn't help speaking up.
"Don't worry. I stole this from that Black Cloud Monk just now," Wei He smiled slightly. He pulled an item out from his sleeve.
It was a long, black object that looked like a whistle.
"Is this... a Whale Whistle?!"
Lou Chengxun was a man of experience and recognized the item at a single glance.
A Whale Whistle was a special tool used to attract and affect whales.
The sound it made was very close to the mating call of whales, so it was often used to draw them near.
However, the whistle in front of them looked a bit different from normal ones.
Wei He nodded.
"Since the Wu State set up an ambush in advance, going back along our original route means we'll likely run right into them. But if we send a small boat back instead, it will have a much better chance of slipping through their lines unnoticed.
The ocean is vast. They can't lock down every single spot. A big ship can't pass through, but a small boat should have no problem."
He paused for a second. "So, our main ship will keep circling around and use the whistle to draw out the Black Line Whale, hunting it according to plan.
In this situation, the enemy is strong and numerous, while we are few and weak. We shouldn't focus on fighting head-on, but on finding a chance to send word back.
A small boat carries fewer people, is harder to spot, and has a much higher chance of success."
After hearing this, the two members of the Lou family nodded, agreeing with his reasoning.
If they fought head-on, their group could never beat the ambush force from Wu State.
However, sneaking back in small numbers using a hidden boat gave them a good shot at making it.
"Then we will follow Junior Brother Wei's plan. We'll keep circling and find a good spot to hunt the whale!" Lou Chengxun said firmly. "I'll go arrange the men right now to head back and report."
The Wu State had set up an ambush on the sea, and this news had to be reported back as soon as possible.
The men on the ship were all loyal servants of the Lou family. With a big reward, there would surely be brave men willing to take the risk.
After picking out three small boats and the right men, all that was left was to wait for the right moment.
They were still far from Xuanmiao Sect, so they had to get as close as possible before lowering the small boats.
Wei He immediately started studying how to use the Whale Whistle.
Thump.
Suddenly, a dull thud came from inside the ship cabin.
A Taoist dressed in white stepped out casually, holding a sailor in his hand.
Plop.
He tossed the knocked-out sailor onto the deck and looked over at Wei He.
"Caught a spy for you guys. No need to thank me."
The surrounding sailors burst into a commotion. This man had popped out of nowhere, grabbed their crewmate, and was talking about things they couldn't understand.
Some of them grew wary and reached for their weapons.
However, Lou Chengxun raised his hand and stopped them all.
"Senior Brother Huan Songzi?!" Lou Chengxun took one look, recognized the man, and called out in surprise.
"You know me?" The white-clothed Taoist was surprised.
"Of course I do. Senior Brother Huan Songzi is listed among the sect's Quanzhen masters. We have heard your name for many years," Lou Chengxun said with a bitter smile.
He looked at Wei He, then at the Taoist, and instantly understood.
It seemed this man was here to protect Wei He too.
Wei He frowned at the sailor on the ground, then studied the white-clothed Taoist opposite him.
"And this is...?" He already had a rough guess in his mind.
"You guessed right. I was sent by First Senior Sister to meet up with you." The Taoist had thick eyebrows, big eyes, and a broad square face, looking like an open and direct person.
He rubbed his hair, feeling a bit embarrassed.
"I was hiding in the cabin searching for the spy earlier, but I almost let something happen to Junior Brother Wei instead. Good thing you had the great items given by First Senior Sister, so nothing went wrong. Otherwise I would really..." He sighed with a guilty face.
Wei He and Lou Chengxun were both stunned.
So this guy had been hiding on their ship all along, moving right under their noses without them ever noticing?
His hiding skill was truly incredible.
Huan Songzi saw their confusion and chuckled.
"I have a unique stealth method, so don't worry about it. Even among those True Blood barbarians of the Wu State, very few can spot me. That includes their Buddha Masters!"
There was a hint of pride in his tone.
"Alright, enough small talk. Junior Brother Wei, blow the Whale Whistle quickly. Once we hunt the Black Line Whale, I will personally head back to deliver the news. The rest of you stay outside and wait for word." Huan Songzi urged.
This arrangement was very reasonable. With a Quanzhen master like him who had top-tier stealth skills and far greater power than anyone else here, the dangerous task of returning to report became much easier.
Wei He nodded, but his eyes stayed fixed on the man.
"It's a pity. If Senior Brother Huan Songzi had stepped in earlier, maybe we could have captured that Black Cloud Monk..."
He was testing the man to see if he knew his true strength.
"Junior Brother Wei is right. Blame it on me drinking too much..." Huan Songzi slapped his forehead in frustration.
It seemed he probably didn't see the moment when Wei He killed the Black Cloud Monk.
Still, one couldn't rule out the possibility that he was just pretending.
"However, that Black Cloud Monk is extremely strong. Even if I stepped in, I might not have been able to keep him here. One on one... I'm not confident I could beat him," Huan Songzi said, his face turning slightly serious.
"His level isn't that high, but his combat ability is terrifying. Rumor says he once entered the Wu State's Merit Gold Pool. His huge strength carries a special corrosive power, which is no joke."
"He really was tough," Wei He smiled bitterly. "When I fought him, if he hadn't been overly cautious trying to capture me alive, I wouldn't have had the chance to escape his hands."
"Junior Brother Wei is already very impressive," Huan Songzi sighed. "Alright, time is ticking. Full speed ahead. Once we hit a good spot, I will jump off and head back to report. However, you guys shouldn't go back for now. Just wait on the sea for news."
He looked closely at Wei He.
"In fact, based on the intel we gathered earlier, even if we head back now, it might already be too late."
"What do you mean, Senior Brother?" Wei He froze, his expression changing. A bad feeling rose in his heart.
His family was still on Tianhai Island. If something happened to the sect on the island...
He didn't even dare to imagine that outcome.
The two from the Lou family also changed color, knowing things were bad.
"Is the Wu army going to attack our Xuanmiao Sect?!" Lou Chengxun's voice trembled.
"Your families on the island were arranged long ago, so don't worry," Huan Songzi shook his head and laughed.
With such a major crisis approaching, he was actually in the mood to laugh.
"Is Senior Brother Huan Songzi not worried about the sect?" Wei He asked in a deep voice.
"First Senior Sister made plans long ago. Our worrying won't change anything. The sect might not hold out, but I trust First Senior Sister," Huan Songzi pulled back his smile and said seriously.
"She won't let us down."
Wei He felt a bit more at ease.
He picked up the Whale Whistle, circulated his force to clean off all the residue, put it to his lips, and blew hard.
No sound came out.
Only an invisible vibration spread out rapidly from the whistle, sweeping far into the distance.
He needed to hunt the Black Line Whale fast and finish the task.
Since he couldn't step into the clash between the sect and the Wu army, he had to focus on what he could do first.
Right now, his Whale Surge Skill was stuck. Once he got the Black Line Whale oil, he could keep cultivating.
The reason he felt so helpless now was simply that he was too weak.
As long as he kept growing stronger and training hard, there would come a day when he would no longer feel as helpless as he did today.
As if reading Wei He's mind, Huan Songzi shook his head.
"Relax. Speaking of which, you guys probably don't know how First Senior Sister became the acting Sect Master in the first place, right?"
As soon as these words came out, Wei He and the two from the Lou family all locked their eyes on him, waiting for the rest.
From his tone, whatever Huan Songzi was about to say next had to be connected to the crisis facing the sect right now.
"Wasn't the position passed down to Senior Yuanduzi by the three patriarchs?" Lou Shengyue asked curiously.
