The Underground of Stone Lotus Island.
Tunnels ran like an ant nest, stretching and meeting at the very bottom in front of an oval door.
The pale white door was round with an S-shaped crack down the middle. Its edges glowed with a faint purple light, framed by a special material that looked like purple crystal. In front of the door sat two purple stone bat monsters, identical to the statues Wei He and the others had seen before.
Just as the people from the Haining Alliance and the Xuanmiao Sect landed on the island, a thin stream of white smoke slowly crept through the crack in the door.
The white smoke passed through the heavy door, traversed the deep dark tunnels, and glided past stone halls and hidden rooms filled with statues. Soon, it reached the deepest point of the ruin.
At the very end of this beast ruin was a circular wall carved with massive purple patterns. Right in the middle was a faint face. It looked human, but dense clusters of black snakes wrapped around its cheeks like a thick mane.
Hisss...
The white smoke slowly settled onto the stone face and sank into it.
Gradually, the face twitched. The black snakes around its cheeks began to move like living hair, letting out low hisses. The face slowly opened its eyes, and thick white smoke oozed out from its mouth, eyes, and ears.
The smoke spread and soon covered the entire stone hall. An indescribable vibration rippled through the whole ruin.
This white smoke was packed with a deadly poison, far stronger than anything a normal beast could produce.
This human-faced beast had lived here for over a thousand years. It was vastly more powerful than the creatures found in ordinary ruins. To it, even normal Real Masters were nothing more than food.
Over the centuries, it had taken many shapes and created many types of structures to lure outside creatures into its lair, all so it could devour them.
And now, it smelled fresh prey once again...
The thick white smoke quickly pulled back, sucked right into the face's mouth and nose.
Suddenly, the face on the wall sensed that someone had arrived outside the ruin and was trying to break in.
It seemed to think for a moment, then came up with an idea.
Its entire face began to melt and shift. In seconds, it turned into a complex purple disc covered in strange symbols and patterns.
If any Real Master were here, they would instantly recognize it as the Beast Star Disc.
No one would ever guess that a creature could fake the Star Disc so perfectly. If an outsider touched it without knowing the truth, it would cost them their life.
After finishing its disguise, the face stopped moving completely, turning back into a plain stone carving.
No one knew that this ruin did not contain a real Star Disc at all. The whole thing was just a trap set by this monster.
...
At two different entrances of the ruin.
Duan Chongsheng and his group from the Haining Alliance stood before one of the large doors.
They whispered to each other using voice transmission. Then, Duan Chongsheng stepped forward and gently tapped the door with his palm.
Bright purple light patterns instantly spread out from where his hand touched.
Hisss.
The stone door slowly slid open, revealing a deep stone path inside.
"I will take the lead. Senior Brother Zhang, junior brothers and sisters, please follow me," Duan Chongsheng transmitted his voice softly.
Everyone nodded.
Duan Chongsheng tapped his toes on the ground and floated inside like a leaf. The rest followed right behind him in a single line.
However, as Zhang Qianhe entered last, he felt something was wrong. He stopped and looked around, but could not find anything out of the ordinary.
Seeing the others moving further ahead down the dark path, he pushed his doubts down, raised his guard, and quickly caught up.
Shortly after they disappeared into the darkness, another group arrived at the open door.
This time, it was Yao Wan, leading Xu Ran and Zhong Yuanlin.
"This is the place. It seems the Haining Alliance got in before us. Let's hurry. Last time we were here, we cleared the inner formations, killed the beasts, and explored more than half of the area. This time, we will finish the rest," Yao Wan said calmly.
During their last visit, they had spent several days inside. That was because some of the arrays in the ruin only weakened at specific times, and they had to wait for those weak moments to break through.
That was why she was not worried about the Haining Alliance taking the Star Disc first.
Today was different. She had chosen this specific day because her calculations showed that the star formation would be at its weakest right now.
"Yuanlin, you know the most about star formations among us. If we run into another one, we'll be counting on you," Yao Wan smiled.
"No problem, leave it to me," Zhong Yuanlin nodded, his eyes scanning the area with a serious look.
"Also, be careful. The beasts here aren't that strong. The strongest one we met last time was a step weaker than me, so they are no real threat, and we already got plenty of Star Cores. But we must watch out for the Haining Alliance. Since Duan Chongsheng brought a team, he must have called in new helpers. Who gets the Star Disc in the end depends on who wins that fight," Yao Wan warned carefully.
"Don't worry. With the three of us working together, we won't have to fear anyone unless we run into a Dao Seed like Senior Brother Xuan Zheng. But someone like him has plenty of ways to get Star Cores. He wouldn't risk his life coming to a place like this to hunt," Xu Ran smiled gently, looking calm.
For the Xuanmiao Sect, Dao Seed Masters had endless ways to get Star Cores. They did not need to fight to the death in minor ruins just for a single Star Disc.
To people at that level, the resources given by the sect were far greater than the tiny amount of cores a Star Disc could produce each year. It was like a rich man who earned thousands a month; he would never risk his life for ten dollars.
Of course, the giant ruin Shi Heng found back then was an exception. That was not an ordinary ruin.
Yao Wan and Zhong Yuanlin knew this well, which was why regular ruins rarely attracted Dao Seed experts.
The dangerous thing about a Dao Seed expert was not just their raw power, but their ability to defeat enemies far above their rank. On top of that, they carried powerful secret trump cards given by their sects.
If you fought one, failing to kill them meant a terrible end, and actually killing them brought even bigger trouble. They were best avoided.
"In that case, let's head in together. We won't take the main path. Ruins like this have many crisscrossing tunnels, though most are blocked by star formations. Since the Haining Alliance went through this door ahead of us, we need to take a different route if we want to pass them," Zhong Yuanlin suggested.
Yao Wan and Xu Ran agreed. The three activated their movement skills and rushed into the stone tunnel at high speed.
Before long, they arrived at a large stone hall.
In front of them were five split paths, each blocked by thick black smoke.
Right now, the smoke at one of the paths looked slightly dim and thin.
"They must have taken this one," Zhong Yuanlin pointed at the thin smoke.
"No need to rush. The layout here looks like a nine-position life-or-death array. Taking that path is not the best choice. Follow me."
She ran some quick calculations in her mind, then flicked tiny streams of inner force into the smoke of the other four paths.
By reading the vibrations in the smoke, Zhong Yuanlin quickly picked a route.
"This way is faster! Move," she called out and darted into another path.
The other two did not waste time and followed right behind her.
Once inside, Zhong Yuanlin quickly found new star arrays blocking the way. Though they were somewhat tricky, they were still within her ability to handle.
She broken them one after another with ease, surprising Yao Wan and Xu Ran, who both looked at her with growing respect.
Zhong Yuanlin felt great, but a small doubt rose in her heart. This trip was going almost too smoothly.
BOOM!!
Wei He casually threw a palm strike, shattering a bird-headed beast made of black smoke into thin air.
The destroyed beast left behind a dark purple, rough crystal that fell to the ground.
He flicked his finger, and a invisible force lifted the crystal into his hand.
"The Star Cores here are quite large."
He had seen Star Cores before, but none were this big.
On the other side, Cheng Wan easily finished off another beast and picked up its core.
"That's why we are fighting the Haining Alliance for this place. Junior Brother Wei, let's pick up the pace."
"Right," Wei He nodded. He wasn't sure if it was because they picked a different entrance or something else, but the star arrays they met along the way were unusually weak. After a few hard strikes, the arrays simply broke apart like old, broken gear.
"Maybe the Haining Alliance went through here earlier and broke these arrays once. That's why they seem so weak now," Cheng Wan guessed.
"That's possible. If so, we really need to move faster," Wei He agreed.
The two walked down the long stone path, looking at the endless darkness ahead.
Wei He pulled out a neat whale oil lamp.
He wrapped his force around the lamp to protect the flame, called out to Cheng Wan, and suddenly burst forward with full speed.
Cheng Wan followed closely behind.
With two Real Masters moving at top speed, they expected to reach the deepest part of the ruin very soon, big as it was.
Inside the long, dark tunnel, the yellow light of the oil lamp moved forward rapidly.
As Wei He ran, he carefully sensed his surroundings.
In the real world, the walls and floor of the ruin were covered in thick layers of living dust and fungus that slowly wriggled around.
It was as if it was constantly raining inside the tunnel, with living specs falling non-stop.
Whenever these specs hit their protective force, they were instantly burned away and killed, keeping the masters clean and unpolluted.
They ran along the long stone path for over ten minutes, but the way ahead remained dark and curved, with no end in sight.
Not only that, ever since they sped up, they hadn't run into a single star array or beast.
"Stop!" Wei He halted abruptly, frowning as he looked around.
"I'm afraid we fell into some kind of maze loop. We've been running in circles."
Cheng Wan didn't know the word maze, but she understood the second part.
"I can fix this," she said. She took a small clear bottle from her pouch and opened it.
A small white bug with four wings slowly crawled out.
It had a sharp head, a straight body, and a thick patch of black fur on its tail, looking like a tiny microphone with wings.
"Dongdong, please lead the way. I need to find the spot with the highest energy," Cheng Wan spoke gently as she stroked the little bug.
As if it understood her words, the bug buzzed its wings, flew up into the air, and headed deep into the tunnel.
