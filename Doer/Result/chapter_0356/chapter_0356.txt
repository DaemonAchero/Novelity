In the current Xuanmiao Sect, both the outer and inner mountains held at least a dozen people she had effortlessly brought under control.
She didn't just breach their lines; she turned the people inside directly into her own puppets.
Bola was like a real venomous spider, constantly spinning web after web, slowly wrapping her threads tight around the key figures of the sect.
"Relax. Zhou Muqing saved the kid's relatives and already earned his trust. This time, we're using the Chaos God Sect's name as bait to drag him out. Once we get our hands on a Dao Seed level talent like him, the Xuanmiao Sect will be full of fatal holes in our eyes. Now that he hit his first Sense Anchor, under the Dayuan power system, he can probably go toe to toe with a normal second or third stage user. "Don't worry about it. I'm a Bodhisattva level powerhouse. A mere Arhat tier kid, who would still be bottom tier trash on our side, no matter how tough he is, how strong can he really get?" Bola snapped dismissively.
Wei He was a top tier genius of the Xuanmiao Sect.
She, too, was a top genius within the Guangci Sect.
On top of that, she was fifty years older than Wei He, with fifty more years of brutal training under her belt. Just recently, she breached the Full Truth threshold and stepped into the Bodhisattva position.
If she couldn't even deal with a green horn who just hit his first Sense Anchor, she might as well be dead weight.
"Do not drop your guard. I also got word that Wei He always has a high level master protecting him from the shadows," the man frowned deeply.
Bola burst out laughing.
"Your news is way behind, my friend. My informant made it crystal clear. The person guarding Wei He is named Yao Wan, a high tier master from the Dayuan Dao Registry, extremely dangerous. However..."

A brutal, savage look washed over her face.
"While he has no guard during this gap, it's the perfect time to drag him down and rip out deeper secrets from his head."

He had other business to tend to, so he bade Bola farewell and turned around, walking away into the cold.
As for Wei He, with Bola—a full Bodhisattva level master—on site, paired with Zhou Muqing's sneak attack from the inside, a rookie with only one Sense Anchor had zero chance of escape.
Left alone, Bola raised her hand and suddenly squeezed her fist tight.
BANG!

She let out a soft laugh that grew louder and louder, echoing cold and harsh in the empty space.

Snowflakes danced across the heavy sky, spinning as they fell onto the earth.
Across the vast, frozen wasteland.
Wei He and Zhou Muqing moved at blinding speed.
In the endless expanse of pure white, they were just two tiny black specks rushing forward, tearing across the snow at an insane speed of over sixty meters per second, a speed far beyond what any normal human could ever imagine.
If Wei He was running alone, he could easily push his speed past a hundred meters per second.
But with Zhou Muqing dragging behind, he had to dial it back a bit.
At their level, bringing along normal weaklings was completely useless.
Just like right now, if he really wanted to, he could put on the full grand display of the Thousand Poison Sect Leader.
A whole squad of elite martial artists clearing the path ahead, carrying a fancy sedan chair while he rested inside.
But what was the point?
A trip that took only a few hours of fast running would take until tomorrow if he put on a big show.
He was no idiot.
Striking fast and moving alone was always the best way to kill.
"Ever since I passed the Sense Anchor, I haven't really used these eyes to take a good, hard look at the real world. This is a good chance to check it out."
Before long, the two paused to take a quick rest. Wei He stared into the endless snow plain and spoke softly.
"Did you anchor your vision too, sir?" Zhou Muqing asked.
"Yeah. Having better eyes is just more convenient," Wei He said with a sigh in his heart.
At this moment, his eyes were no longer like those of a normal human. Countless dense red blood threads moved like living worms, constantly squirming and wrapping around his pupils.
In his field of vision, the outside world was not just a dead, silent ice field.
On the contrary.
It was not lonely at all. In fact, it was quite lively and terrifying.
Inside the True Inner World.
Taking the rock Wei He was leaning against as the center, deep red ruined walls and broken stone ruins were scattered all over the vast white snow.
The air was a thick, gloomy gray, filled with endless floating dust and particles that gathered and scattered at random.
A few dry, dark green trees stood like twisted demons, stretching their sharp branches out in all directions.
In spots where the snow hadn't covered the floor, one could see dark green soil underneath, breathing out a thick, disgusting reek of filth.
Aside from all that, the most prominent sight was a herd of snow white cattle slowly passing across the snow.
These cattle only existed inside the True Inner World.
They looked filthy, not much different from normal wild bulls. The only difference was that the flesh on their bodies had already begun to rot away.
Some bulls even had hollow, dried up black holes where their eyes should be. The fur on their backs had long lost its shine.
Their entire bodies oozed out faint gray mist.
This gray mist floated ten meters above the herd, gathering into a massive, blurry, twisted face in the sky.
That monstrous face distorted and cried out in pain, silently releasing violent wave after wave of unsettling soundwaves that disturbed the mind.
Even though Wei He didn't have enhanced hearing to catch the sound, his left hand had anchor level touch, allowing him to feel the faint vibrations of the soundwaves.
That was how he knew the giant face was wailing.
After resting for a moment and watching the cattle herd fade into the distance, he pulled out a map and checked it.
"How much further?"

"Just a little bit more. We're already in the area. Please be careful, sir."

"That's for the best. If there are too many strong masters inside, we pull out right away. If there are only a few, we can wipe them out completely," Zhou Muqing nodded.

After moving forward for another few minutes, an irregular white frozen lake appeared right in front of their eyes.
Right beside the lake stood a two story wooden building.
The surface of the building was covered in a thick layer of snow powder. Long, sharp icicles hung down from every corner of the curved roof.
From a distance, one could barely see faint yellow light flickering from within the building.
"Stay here. I'll go check it out," Wei He instructed. Without waiting for her reply, he burst forward like an arrow, vanishing on the spot.
Zhou Muqing felt a spike of panic in her heart. She wanted to say something, but there was no time.
Seeing that Wei He was gone, she slid her hand behind her back, quietly slipping a tiny green bottle out of her sleeve.
She popped the bottle cap open, and a bright green flying bug silently flew out, hiding within the cold wind and snow as it zipped toward the distant building.
This was her secret contact method with the Guangci Sect person.
When she went back to report earlier, she was caught by the Guangci Sect masters instead. To save her own skin, to break her limits and extend her life, she surrendered to the enemy without a single thought.
Now, as long as she lured Wei He over and warned the other side in advance, her job would be mostly done.
Soon, the bug flew away into the storm.
Yet the building remained dead silent.
A few massive guys walked out from inside, looking around in confusion. Seeing nothing out of the ordinary, they turned around and retreated back inside.
Zhou Muqing waited with deep patience.
Time ticked away, minute by minute.

Only two words were written on it: Where's he?

Wei He's voice suddenly rang out right next to Zhou Muqing's ear.

If she had opened that paper just a second later, Wei He would have caught her red handed.
If her identity as a rat was exposed, only one thing waited for her.
Death.

If the Guangci Sect managed to capture Wei He this time, working together with him in the future would make her cover even safer.

If they just left like this, her mission would end in failure, and she would be dragged back into a living hell of punishment.

Sadly...
