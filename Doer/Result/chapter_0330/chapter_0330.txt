Xuanmiao Sect, Inner Mountain Unknown Temple.
In the dark night, a few Taoist temples shone like torches. They were the only lights in the area. The light did not reach far, but it gave anyone walking by a strong sense of safety.
Inside the Ninth Unknown Temple, Wei He followed his senior sister, Yao Wan.
Under the dim lamplight, they walked through a trapdoor in the back hall and entered an underground tunnel.
They followed the path straight ahead. For the first time, Wei He reached the real main hall of the Unknown Temple. This was also the meeting place for the Inner Mountain Masters.
"You only need to come here once. Remember the way in and out," Yao Wan said at the end of the tunnel.
She threw a stone badge to Wei He, looking a bit impatient. Then she pushed open a heavy black iron door.
Behind it was a large underground plaza, as big as a football field.
Inside, simple houses made of black stone stood in rows. People walked between them, coming and going like a small underground town.
Wei He looked around carefully.
The cave ceiling above was shaped like a giant dome. The ground and walls were covered with glowing blue moss that lit up the area. The stone buildings were built nicely, some tall and some short.
Yao Wan pointed at the people walking around.
"These are the Master Warriors of the three branches. Most of them are new like you, here for less than thirty years. All the Unknown Temples link up to this place. You can stay here if you spend Star Stones. You can also buy pills, items, and secret skills. The Pill Hall and Weapon Hall leave practice items here for people to trade."
Wei He walked in with her. Along the way, many Masters greeted Yao Wan.
"Greetings, Senior Sister Yao."
"Good to see you, Senior Sister Yao."
"Stay well, Senior Sister."
"Haven't seen you in a while, Senior Sister Yao. You look even better today."
Men and women, every Taoist they met greeted Yao Wan with a bit of fear in their eyes.
Yao Wan just nodded to some and ignored others, looking cold. She clearly held a high place in the Unknown Temple.
Wei He watched this with curiosity. This senior sister, picked by Master Yuan Duzi to teach him, seemed very strong.
Yao Wan led Wei He around the plaza. Then she stopped in front of a wide five-story building and pushed the door open.
"Lian Xiang'er! Any free rooms left?" Yao Wan yelled out.
Behind the front desk, several senior brothers and sisters were standing with new disciples, blocking the counter.
Hearing her voice, they all turned around and stared at Yao Wan and Wei He.
"Senior Sister Yao is bringing a new person too?" A middle-aged man frowned and asked in a deep voice.
"If you can bring people, why can't I?" Yao Wan said with annoyance. She waved her hand. "Move, move! Get out of my way!"
She walked over rudely and pushed past the four or five people at the counter, taking the front spot.
The people she pushed could say nothing. Their faces looked dark, but they stayed quiet. If anyone else cut in line, they would fight back. But few dared to mess with Yao Wan.
The Inner Mountain of Xuanmiao Sect looked peaceful, but there was still fighting and rivalry between the three branches.
Yuan Duzi was the strongest person in the Lock Mountain branch, as well as the head of the whole sect. No one doubted that.
Below Yuan Duzi were the "Three Heroes of Lock Mountain", the three strongest Masters in that branch.
Yao Wan was one of them.
Her fame was not just talk. She earned it with her blade. She cut down wild beasts, she cut down enemies, and she even hurt quite a few of her own sect members.
She seemed normal usually, but if someone made her mad, she exploded like a barrel of gunpowder.
Besides the dozen people above the Realm Stage, there were plenty of Masters in the three branches.
The Inner Mountain seemed to have few people, but that was only counting the official Masters. If you counted the Master Warriors too, the number was three times larger. There were about two hundred people in total.
A few were real Masters, while most were just weak warriors using the sacred lands to extend their lives.
With all three branches combined, the plaza ended up looking like this.
Yao Wan slammed her hand on the counter.
"Any free rooms? Give me one."
The woman behind the counter was an old woman with white hair. But when she saw Yao Wan, she quickly stood up and bowed.
"Lian Xiang'er greets Senior Sister Yao."
"Enough with that. Just give me a room quickly," Yao Wan said with a frown.
"Yes, yes. Please wait a moment, Senior Sister." The old woman was respectful. Even though she looked old, she was younger than Yao Wan in terms of entry, so she was still a junior.
She quickly found an open room, wrote down the name, and handed over a token.
Yao Wan threw it to Wei He.
"Go inside. Absorb your Amber Bead and stabilize your power before coming out. It is hard to get a spot here, so do not waste time."
"Thank you, Senior Sister!" Wei He took the token. He was a bit confused, but seeing the jealous looks from the people around him, he knew this was something good. So he thanked her right away.
Yao Wan nodded.
"Qing Ping, the top disciple of the main line this year, joined the Ghost Head branch. Bai Hezi will surely give him special training. Do not make me look bad!"
"Yes!" Wei He agreed. He still did not fully understand, but he took the benefit first.
Holding the room token, Wei He went up the stairs on the right and soon reached a room on the fourth floor.
He used the key attached to the back of the token to open the door.
The room was completely empty. There was only a cushion on the floor, an incense burner, and a bucket of clean water. Nothing else.
Yet, for some reason, the air inside felt a bit different from the outside world.
Wei He felt his heart move. He understood that this small difference was probably why everyone wanted these rooms.
He stepped inside, closed the door, and sat down with his legs crossed. He pulled out the Amber Bead and prepared to absorb it.
Downstairs, Yao Wan waited outside, feeling bored. She was squeezing a Star Stone in her hand, twisting it around like soft clay to pass the time.
She had little patience, but because the Master Senior Sister asked her to guard Wei He, she had to stay.
The main hall had a steady stream of people coming and going.
Most Masters and warriors stayed in the sacred lands and only came out here to live and trade.
Few people failed to recognize Yao Wan. Strong fighters from the three branches were rare, and Yao Wan was even listed in the Great Yuan Dao Records.
Soon, news about Yao Wan bringing Wei He spread.
Wei He was so important to the Master Senior Sister that she sent Yao Wan, one of the Lock Mountain Three Heroes, to teach him personally. This was much higher treatment than any other new person got before.
This underground hall opened and closed at set times. People who needed things would time their visits to buy materials.
Right now, many Masters and warriors of Xuanmiao Sect were gathered here. The news about Wei He learning directly from Yao Wan spread fast to all three branches.
Luckily, the core idea of Xuanmiao Sect was to follow nature. Though many were curious about Wei He's talent and wanted to make friends with him, they learned he was busy absorbing his talent power to prepare for his sense stage. So they decided not to bother him.
As Yao Wan waited downstairs with nothing to do, a few people walked by quickly. They carried bags and rushed toward the exit tunnel.
The leader was a middle-aged woman. She had a long face that was not pretty, but her eyes were sharp and fierce. She clearly was not someone to mess with.
Yao Wan looked at her for a second. Neither spoke, and they walked past each other.
The woman led her group away toward the tunnel.
Yao Wan's face grew heavy.
That woman was Daoist Ming Ding, one of the strong fighters from the Qianliu branch. They had fought before, and Ming Ding only lost to her by one move during the internal competition. Her strength was real.
More importantly, the top senior brother of Qianliu, Qing Mei'zi, was in charge of dealing with the Wu Country invasion.
The three branches had their own jobs. Lock Mountain guarded the inner seals and helped other sects fix theirs. Qianliu handled outside wars and held the land. Ghost Head took care of defense and supplies.
Because of this, Qianliu had the most people and the strongest overall force.
Since even a top fighter like Ming Ding was rushing off, something bad must have happened.
A handsome Taoist nearby explained to her, "Senior Sister Yao has been outside lately, so you might not know. Qianliu already sent three top fighters with a large group to the border. They say the Guanci cultists from Wu Country killed over a hundred people from the Sea Lantern Office. They even ambushed and badly hurt the general of the Dinghai Army. These Guanci cultists are far worse than the Xiangqu Sect used to be. They do not even follow the rule that Masters should not mess with normal people. Are they trying to ruin everyone?"
"The Guanci Cult is not from our Great Yuan land, so why would they follow our rules?" Yao Wan said coldly. "I hope the Qianliu group gives them a hard lesson."
"Our Xuanmiao Sect follows nature and peace. Many of us are not good at fighting. I am afraid..." The handsome Taoist shook his head and stopped speaking.
"Afraid of what? Boy, which branch are you from? You talk half sentences on purpose to tease me. Do you want to die?" Yao Wan hit the Taoist on the head with her palm, feeling annoyed.
She did not hit Wei He because the Master Senior Sister was watching him, but she had no patience for anyone else.
"Do not be angry, Senior Sister. I just fear this trip will be dangerous. The Guanci cultists knew Haizhou is our area, yet they still came to make trouble. They must have come prepared. Going out in a hurry might end badly..." The handsome Taoist spoke slowly, shaking his head.
"Senior Brother Qing Mei'zi is super strong, so he must know this too. Since he still sent people, he must have a back-up plan. This is not for you kids to worry about." Yao Wan hit him again, making the Taoist smile helplessly.
Outside, a storm was coming.
Inside the room, Wei He was at the key moment of absorbing his talent power. This new talent seemed to bring him far more than just a simple boost...
