"If you want to grab him, do it yourself," Xu Chengtao scoffed, rolling his eyes at the woman. "I just want to make friends with a powerhouse like him. Do you really think the Xuanmiao Sect hasn't given him good stuff? Secret techniques or hidden cards—if he actually uses them, neither of us might walk away."
He had been a Dao Seed for nearly a century, stuck at the fifth step of Full Truth for a long time. His next step was finding a way to break through and trigger a qualitative change.
Beyond the fifth step lay a completely different realm.
"Forget it. Since he already left, let's head back too. The Gale Belt could cover Wuhui Island at any moment. We shouldn't stay."
"Let's go." The woman nodded. She glanced at the giant deep pit Wei He had left behind after digging out the Dragon Lock Tree.
"Only the small piece of tree heart in the middle of the trunk is actually useful," the woman said, completely speechless. "Why did he dig up the whole tree? Isn't it too heavy?"
"Maybe he wants to replant it back home?" Xu Chengtao guessed, hesitating.
As a Star Array Master, he had seen all kinds of rare materials and used Dragon Lock Wood before. This stuff was brutally heavy. A tree like that easily weighed well over a hundred thousand jin combined.
Elsewhere.
Wei He was gasping for air as he dragged the heavy trunk forward. If not for using gravity to share the weight, every step would have sunk him deep into the dirt, leaving him completely unable to walk.
"Only someone like me, with power and stamina far above my level, could pull this off. Any other Real Person would have been worked to death."
It was exhausting, but getting the material meant success.
Wei He gathered his strength and continued retracing his steps.
Soon, he made it back to the underground cave entrance. Looking at the dark opening, he tightened his grip on the dragged trunk and let out a long breath.
He didn't feel tired walking down the long tunnel before, but now...
Recalling the man he just met, Wei He was deeply impressed.
'Xu Chengtao of the Haining Alliance... definitely not an easy guy to deal with. And he's one of the Dao Seeds.' If Xu Chengtao hadn't shown up this time, Wei He wouldn't have found a way to deal with the immortal treeman. He wouldn't have gotten that golden star core either.
It was an unexpected bonus.
At this point, every goal of his trip had been fully achieved.
He found the Dragon Lock Wood, completed his Perception Anchor, and even scored a piece of golden star core. It wasn't much—just a small piece weighing around half a jin at most—but it was still great.
Wei He was very satisfied with the outcome.
...
...
...
A few days later.
A sea vessel slowly pulled back into the dock at Xuanmiao Sect's Jujun Island.
Wei He stepped off the ship onto the pier, looking annoyed.
He had dragged that giant trunk for a whole day before the outer parts began turning gray and rotting away on the second day. Only the core in the middle retained a faint glow.
Only then did he realize that only the core was useful. Everything else was just useless growth.
Thinking about how he had nearly killed himself hauling that giant log, Wei He felt deeply speechless.
Still, the haul was great overall.
He had already shrunk back down to his disguised six-foot height.
However, the moment he stepped off the ship, he felt the atmosphere on the dock was wrong.
Disciples from the Moral Palace and Dening Hall of the Outer Mountain were working with peripheral disciples, setting up several checkpoints to screen everyone at the pier.
Every disciple looked dead serious and grim.
"Everyone off the ship, step this way! State your purpose at the Xuanmiao Sect, how long you're staying, and where you're going!"
A disciple in red robes shouted nearby, directing the crowd into designated lanes.
The pier had been temporarily split into open passages using wooden boards. Passengers leaving the ship queued up obediently, moving through the lines to pass the inspection one by one.
Wei He pulled out his waist badge and passed through the check point quickly. He grabbed a nearby taoist disciple and asked with a frown.
"What happened? Why are things suddenly so strict?"
"Senior Brother Wei?!" The disciple recognized the badge and bowed respectfully. "Half a month ago, Senior Brother Cai Menghuan—the new Dao Child chosen by the Sect Master—was ambushed at Longwan inside our own sect! Right now he's severely injured, unconscious, and being treated!"
"Cai Menghuan?!" Wei He was shocked. Cai Menghuan was said to have reached Full Truth already and was a top talent capable of fighting above his tier. Yet he was ambushed and heavily wounded inside the sect's own grounds.
No wonder security was so tight.
Worse, this happened right after First Senior Sister Yuan Duzi named him a Dao Child.
This was a direct slap to the sect's face!
"Do they know who did it?" Wei He asked in a low voice.
The disciple shook his head. "I don't know. Senior Brother Wei, you're a Real Person. You might hear more if you ask the masters in the Inner Mountain."
Wei He let him go, leaped up into the air, and rushed toward the Inner Mountain.
A short while later.
At the Cloud Immortals Terrace, he saw Cai Menghuan lying pale and unconscious on a bed.
Beside the bed, two elderly Real Persons with white hair were slowly pouring their power into him to speed up his recovery.
Yuan Duzi had a dark face as she talked with Senior Brother Qingmeizi.
Huan Songzi and several other Real Persons whom Wei He had never seen before stood quietly to the side.
Seeing Wei He enter, Huan Songzi quickly gestured for him to come over.
Wei He crept over quietly and stood beside Huan Songzi.
"What happened?" He couldn't believe it. Just half a month ago, Cai Menghuan was riding high and full of pride, and now he was lying here half-dead.
"It was the Thousand-Faced Demon King," Huan Songzi sent a voice transmission. "Sigh. Brother Cai was so talented. I didn't expect..."
Wei He stayed silent.
The Xuanmiao Sect had been hunting the Thousand-Faced Demon King for years, yet the demon had turned around and nearly killed their Dao Child right under their noses.
Looking at Cai Menghuan on the bed, Wei He felt a growing sense of danger.
"Don't worry. Right after it happened, First Senior Sister spent a huge amount of resources setting up defenses across the sect. The three Grandmasters were also furious. They've come out of seclusion and split into two teams to hunt down the Thousand-Faced Demon King personally!" Huan Songzi whispered.
Wei He didn't reply.
In a head-on fight, the Thousand-Faced Demon King was definitely no match for Yuan Duzi.
The real terror was that this guy was extremely strong and didn't care about honor or face at all.
A Grandmaster who threw away his pride and focused entirely on slaughtering juniors was a nightmare.
Add to that his terrifying disguise skills—hardly anyone in the entire sect could see through them.
This left the whole Xuanmiao Sect living in fear.
If the Thousand-Faced Demon King disguised himself and ambushed any of them like he did to Cai Menghuan, no one could stop him.
This was the absolute pinnacle of an assassin's threat.
Wei He sighed inwardly.
He was believing that old saying more and more: the tallest tree in the forest gets broken by the wind first.
Cai Menghuan stood out the fastest and grew taller than the rest, so he got hit first.
"I also suspect the Dayue Dynasty is behind this," Huan Songzi added darkly. "The Thousand-Faced Demon King alone couldn't pull off something this big."
"Does First Senior Sister have a plan?" Wei He asked.
"She plans to build a massive warning array across the entire island to identify people through their internal power. Sigh, it's just bad timing. First Senior Sister was busy negotiating grain and fruit imports with big merchants from the Serakra Federation. The grain supply around Yuanxi had already been swept clean by wealthy merchants from Dayue. Otherwise, there's no way the Demon King could have escaped so easily after injuring him," Huan Songzi replied.
Wei He had nothing to say to that.
He looked toward Yuan Duzi, sensing that the Xuanmiao Sect was slowly growing anxious and restless.
Dayue didn't attack directly. Instead, they used their massive influence and dirty tricks from the sidelines to grind away the sect's peace bit by bit.
Perhaps when Dayue felt the time was right, they would make their final move.
The Dayue Dynasty had been making small moves for years.
If Yuan Duzi weren't sitting at the center holding the sect together, any other faction would have been crushed by that massive empire long ago.
Soon, Qingmeizi took his leave. Yuan Duzi looked grim as she warned Huan Songzi, Wei He, and the others to stay safe and avoid leaving the mountain unless necessary. Then she threw herself back into treating Cai Menghuan.
The Real Persons left slowly.
Under these circumstances, no one was in the mood to chat. They quickly returned to their lodgings and laid down warning arrays.
Even though they knew an array couldn't stop a Grandmaster, buying a little bit of time until Yuan Duzi arrived brought them some peace of mind.
Wei He returned to his cave with his loot to continue training.
Once the Star Array was set up, the cultivation speed boost was noticeable. He had also researched the rare flower he brought back. It was a valuable herb called the Gold Thread Lotus, famous for healing internal injuries.
Wei He quickly checked the ancient books and processed it into a medicinal powder to maximize its effects.
He named the blend the Sun Jade Spirit-Nourishing Powder. It worked wonders for healing body and mind injuries.
He made five portions in total. After trying one and seeing its great effect, he delivered one portion to Yuan Duzi as a return gift for her array plate.
He kept the remaining three portions, waiting for the right price.
Just because he didn't need them now didn't mean he wouldn't later.
After preparing the medicine, Wei He plunged back into hard cultivation. With resources fully stocked, he closed his doors and focused entirely on training.
In the blink of an eye, time flew by.
Three whole years passed before Cai Menghuan barely managed to recover from his injuries and resume training under Yuan Duzi's guidance.
However, that injury had left behind deep hidden issues in his body.
To break through to the next level, he had to clear those issues first, which cost him far too much time.
Advancing through the Full Truth realm was brutally hard to begin with.
After taking such a heavy blow, Cai Menghuan's progress slowed down even further.
Another three years passed in a flash.
Six years in total. Cai Menghuan finally nursed his body back to health, regained his normal training speed, and slowly showed hints of his former talent again.
The Xuanmiao Sect had gradually returned to peace. Aside from the three Grandmasters returning empty-handed without finding the Thousand-Faced Demon King, life returned to its usual routine.
Wei He remained completely focused on his secluded training. Boosted by the Star Array and fueled by burning through star cores without care for the cost, his progress speeded up significantly.
After six years, he was finally about to max out the fourth level of the Xuanlock Power.
Once he reached the bottleneck, he could use the Breakthrough Bead to force his way through smoothly.
Truth be told, the Perception Anchor stage didn't boost his raw combat power by much. The entire stage was about refining the quality of his True Power rather than improving his reaction speed.
Wei He's True Power was already far beyond ordinary people, so making it denser didn't change the game much.
What truly decided victory or defeat against higher-level foes was still the reaction speed of his power.
Just as he was gathering materials and preparing to fake a secluded session to make his breakthrough official, trouble broke out again.
Only this time, the trouble didn't hit the Xuanmiao Sect—it hit the Dayue Dynasty.
