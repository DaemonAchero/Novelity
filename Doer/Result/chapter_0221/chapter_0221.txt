Clear music like a fresh spring spread through the building.
The crowd gathered on both floors to watch a beautiful woman play. Her thin, white fingers plucked at a lute-like instrument.
The crisp beat mixed with the other musicians' backing. It made the song sound pure and light.
Flute notes, bamboo rhythms, and the quiet humming of a young girl joined in.
The whole Shuyun House fell silent. No one dared make a sound to break the magical music.
Soon, the song ended.
The player stood up slowly, smiled, and bowed to the crowd before leaving the stage.
An announcer stepped up to take over.
Backstage, a few young, pretty women walked over to meet the player.
"What's the situation?" she asked softly.
"We found their location. That dog has four top fighters under him," a girl replied softly, handing her a piece of paper.
The player took it and glanced down.
The paper clearly listed: Cold Poisoner Ouyang Zhao, Double Fists Xu Fei, Nine-Node Sword Xu Lu, and Blue Frost Fist Zhan Heng.
"The first two are Xuri rank masters. The last two are Liuye rank. These four are Zhu Chen's key men, brought directly from the prefectural city. Three are at the Viscera Refining stage, and one is at the Bone Forging stage," a pretty girl explained seriously.
"Four men... three Viscera Refiners... impressive. Did you find the info on the people I asked about before?" The player walked into her private room and flicked her wrist, using her inner force to shut the door behind her.
"Yes. We made a ranking list for Xuanjing City based on battle records and overall strength. The top ten fighters are all here."
Another girl handed over a second piece of paper.
The player read it carefully.
The top three were the old patriarchs of the Three Great Families, all veteran masters from the previous generation.
Wang Zhihe was among them.
Wang Shaojun and Wei He were ranked fifth and sixth.
"Right now, the only top master left in Xuanjing City who hasn't been caught is Sun Zhu, the master of the Decision Hall," the girl whispered.
"Sun Zhu... ranked fourth?" The player raised an eyebrow. "What are his feats?"
"Just one battle. He was enemies with Zhou Rong from the Zhou family. He fought Zhou Rong and two other Viscera Refining masters all by himself, then walked away unharmed while injuring all three of them," the girl answered.
"Good strength. Zhu Chen won't be able to catch him unless he acts personally," the player nodded. "Now that our alliance is forming, we should bring in masters like him. To deal with Zhu Chen, we need more power."
"We thought about contacting him too, but his movements are unpredictable. He lives alone with no family. He doesn't even care about his position as Hall Master," the girl said.
"Then no rush. We wait for the right time and reach out to Wei He of the Thousand Poisons Sect first," the player smiled. "Wei He has a bad reputation, but he only targets people who are already evil. He has his own lines. People are fooled by rumors into thinking he is pure evil, but he isn't. He is someone we can recruit."
"But... the Thousand Poisons Sect headquarters is heavily guarded. Their deadly poison array makes it hard to get close," one girl said, troubled.
"No rush. We will stay here a bit longer. An opportunity will come." The player smiled.

Heavy rain poured down.
Inside the Thousand Bat Water Pavilion.
Wei He stood on the balcony with his hands behind his back, letting rain hit his body.
His inner force blocked the rain automatically, but it couldn't stop the small stream of water flowing under his feet.
The bat carvings on the roof spread their wings, trying to block as much falling rain as possible.
Water collected and dripped fast from the tips of the bat wings.
Looking into the distance, Wei He seemed calm, but he was thinking about his next move.
He had thought about dropping everything to hide deep in the Black House Mountains.
The mountain range was huge, but it could only hide him from normal soldiers. If elite masters came to hunt him down, he still couldn't escape.
If Zhu Chen sent all four of his top fighters together, the Wanqing Sect would be doomed.
Once a fighter reached the Viscera Refining stage, their force flowed through their bones. Normal poisons became useless against them.
Only rare, martial-artist-grade poisons worked.
Targeting a Viscera Refining master with poison was already hard.
Their senses were too sharp, and their control over inner force was too precise.
If anything felt off, they would notice right away and take an antidote.
Small doses of poison did almost nothing to them.
To make matters worse, Zhu Chen had just brought in a poison expert—Cold Poisoner Ouyang Zhao.
Wei He didn't know how skilled the man was, but if he could lower the danger of the mountain, Zhu Chen's group could attack the Wanqing Sect with ease.
This was why Wei He felt troubled.
"Still worried about what happened?" Wan Qingqing walked up behind him.
"Yeah." Wei He sighed.
Ever since he came to this world, he worked hard to build a safe place for himself and the people around him.
Yet even at his current level, he still couldn't find peace.
"Can we move to another state?" Wan Qingqing asked.
"We can't run," Wei He shook his head. "Zhu Chen is watching me. The moment I move, he will strike."
"If I knew this earlier..." Wan Qingqing started, but stopped herself. If Wei He hadn't built a good relationship with the Wang family, the Wanqing Sect might have been wiped out long ago.
"Don't worry, I will handle it," Wei He comforted her. "Get ready to retreat into the deep mountains if needed. I'll take care of the rest."
Wan Qingqing nodded. She trusted Wei He completely.
After she turned and left, Wei He flipped over the railing. His toes tapped lightly against the edge, launching him onto the roof of the pavilion.
The sloped red tiles formed neat, curved lines.
Wei He stood on the roof ridge and looked out.
In the distance, green fields were split into neat squares, blurred by the sheet of rain.
He took a deep breath of cold, wet air to clear his head.
'I can't improve my Cloud Patterns much in a short time. Even if I find the Moon Whale Map, my Whale Hong technique won't boost my power right away.
So, there is only one way to gain power fast.' Wei He raised his right arm.
He originally planned to break through on his own later, but to face the threat of Zhu Chen, he had to boost his strength immediately.
'The only thing I can raise quickly is the other eight arts of the Nine Sky Seals.' Hum.
His right arm vibrated slightly. Nine completely different forces gathered on his arm, forming a strange ring.
Though eight of the forces were only at the Three Blood level, they were already showing their power.
Wei He clearly felt his arm growing stronger, lighter, faster, and tougher.
He flicked his finger forward.
His fingertip struck a few raindrops, sending them flying.
Some drops exploded like fireworks, scattering the surrounding rain.
Some accelerated sharply, piercing through the air like needles before disappearing.
Some hardened like solid stone, flying far before breaking apart.
Each force had a different effect.
This was the power of the Nine Sky Seals.
One strike carried nine different forces, allowing him to adapt to any fight.
Speed, pierce, scatter, explode, harden, heat, chill, confuse, and heavy strike.
These nine effects came from the nine individual arts.
'All my arts are at Three Blood now, and the Breakthrough Bead is full again. It's time to try a complete breakthrough for the Nine Sky Seals.' If all nine arts reached the Force stage, his defensive force would multiply.
This was what he needed.
He closed his eyes and used his mind as a needle, trying to trigger the bead in his chest.
He pictured the Nine Sky Seals as a single martial art.
Pfft!
He tried to pierce the bead with his mind.
To his disappointment, the bead didn't move. It stayed still near his heart.
He froze.
He tried again, driving his mind toward the bead.
The bead trembled slightly, then went still again.
'It won't work?' Wei He frowned.
He guessed that breaking through eight arts at once took too much energy, and the bead couldn't handle it.
"Then I'll have to break through one by one." Helpless, Wei He shifted his focus to the first art: the Shadow Mist Art from the Arc Light House.
This art focused on speed, just like the Rain Cloud Art. But there was a difference.
The Shadow Mist Art was built for sudden bursts, while the Rain Cloud Art was built for sustained speed.
The Shadow Mist Art gave quick, short bursts of speed that were even faster than Rain Force.
The Rain Cloud Art acted like a long line, staying active until inner force ran out, though its top speed was slightly slower.
Wei He picked this art first to boost his speed, making it easier to handle danger.
He pierced the bead with his mind.
This time, it worked.
Wei He felt the bead break apart into streams of heat, flowing through his body.
The required material for the Shadow Mist Art was the feather of a Golden-Winged Eagle, a rare beast. That was the minimum requirement.
Wei He decided to swap it for the strongest beast of the same attribute he had found in ancient books.
Curious, he tried to imagine the feather of a mythical Garuda bird instead.
Whoosh...
The heat from the bead retreated immediately, returning to his chest in its original shape.
'As expected. Does it fail because fake things don't exist, or because the bead doesn't have enough energy for that level?' Wei He couldn't tell.
