"I'm fine either way," Xiao Qingyu said with a smile. She actually quite liked life on Black House Mountain.
She didn't care much, but Wei He had to consider her opinion. Not because she was Wan Ling's close friend, but because after receiving the shared Nine Strikes of the Heavenly Seal, she had made rapid progress. Word was she was pushing into the Viscera Refining stage just recently.
"Head Xiao, how is your push into Viscera Refining going?" Wei He asked.
"Thank you for sharing the full Nine Strikes, Sect Master," Xiao Qingyu smiled. "With a bit more time, I should be able to stabilize it."
The others were shocked, then overjoyed. This was huge news. It meant the Wanqing Sect had gained another top-tier expert.
The Sect Master was not technically at the Viscera Refining stage, but his actual combat power was no different from one. Add Xie Yan, and now Xiao Qingyu. Three top fighters. They could now match half the strength of the old Heavenly Seal Sect. The current Wanqing Sect was no weaker in raw power than any of the Three Great Families.
Everyone offered their congratulations to Xiao Qingyu, their eyes full of hope for the future.
"Alright," Wei He said. "The Wang family needs time to stabilize the area and take over power. After the Chijing Army was crushed, Yuchi Zhong died in battle. What about Zhou Xingtong? Any news on him?"
"Zhou Xingtong is missing. No trace of him," Xie Yan replied. "They say he was like iron and bronze on the battlefield. Even after being surrounded for a long time, he only suffered light injuries. In the end, he took a hit from the rebel general Li Tong, got heavily wounded, and fled. But before leaving, he stole his master's body. A man of real character."
Zhou Xingtong had worked with Yuchi Zhong to kill several of the Nine Seeds back then. He was a sworn enemy of the Heavenly Seal Sect. Everyone present hated him to the bone, yet the room fell dead silent at the news.
Even Du Han, who had been crushed and suppressed by Zhou Xingtong in the past, had to admit the man was a tough bastard. Taking a heavy blow from an enemy general just to steal back his master's corpse took guts. An enemy, yes, but one worthy of respect.
Clap, clap, clap.
Wan Ling clapped her hands gently. "Alright, let's talk about something lighter." She looked around the room and rested her eyes on Wei He with a smile. "Everything is moving in a good direction, but there is one thing we need to settle quickly."
Everyone looked over, waiting for her to continue.
"That would be Sect Master Wei's marriage," Wan Ling smiled.
A few chuckles broke out. It was true. Wei He was almost twenty-six now and still unmarried. For a normal man his age, his kids would already be seven or eight years old. Yet Wei He didn't even have a wife or a concubine.
Wei He was caught off guard. He hadn't expected the topic to turn to him, especially coming from Wan Qingqing's mother, Wan Ling. It made things awkward. After all, Wan Ling used to want him to get together with Wan Qingqing.
"I used to think Qingqing and Little He would work out, but neither had any interest, so I dropped it," Wan Ling smiled. "Now the timing is right, and I have an idea. I wonder if Little He is willing?"
"Just say it, Master. No need to be polite," Wei He said helplessly.
"Alright," Wan Ling smiled. "I hear you are close friends with Young Master Wang Shaojun of the Wang family. Why not take it a step further? Pick a woman from the Wang family and form a marriage alliance."
"That's an option." Wei He nodded. He wasn't against an alliance. He didn't have anyone he liked right now anyway, so he was fine with trying. Feelings could be built over time.
"There is another choice," Wan Ling added. "Remember Yue Xiaodie from the Wanfei Palace whom we locked up?"
"Huh? What do you mean?" Wei He blinked, caught off guard.
"That girl is sharp. She's yelling to make a deal with you," Wan Ling said.
"A deal? Sounds like someone from Jin Prefecture." Wei He nodded. "What deal?"
"She says she's willing to hand over all the Wanfei Palace arts and secret techniques she knows. But on one condition: you have to talk to her yourself."
"Talk to her myself? Fine." Wei He was fairly curious about the Wanfei Palace. The art Yue Xiaodie had recited before was only one core technique, and he didn't know how many flaws it contained. Now that she was willing to spill everything, including other arts, it was worth a chat. After all, it was a major power that once ruled a whole prefecture. It had value.
Soon after the meeting ended, Wan Ling led Wei He down to the dungeon behind the compound.
The dungeon was dug deep underground, made of hard ironwood and metal cages. It was built specifically to hold special prisoners like Yue Xiaodie.
Ever since her limbs were broken and she was poisoned with bugs, Yue Xiaodie had been on her best behavior.
Deep in the dungeon, under the glow of torches, Wei He saw Yue Xiaodie again.
The girl sat cross-legged on the cold ground, munching on a chicken wing. Seeing Wei He walk in, she swallowed hard.
"You're here?" Yue Xiaodie stood up and wiped her hands. "It's been so long. My family must have tried to rescue me and failed, right?"
"You're smart," Wei He nodded. "What do you want to say?"
It seemed Wanfei Palace had a set routine for saving captured members, with specific time windows. Yue Xiaodie had figured out her rescue had failed based on the time passed, so she finally gave up and wanted to deal.
"I want to say there's no real hatred between us," Yue Xiaodie said seriously. "If you let me go, Sect Master Wei, I'll convince my family not to come after you."
"No hatred?" Wei He laughed softly. "I killed your top guard. You call that no hatred?"
"Just a dog. Who cares if he died?" Yue Xiaodie sneered. "He was just a slave bought by the Palace, not a real person."
Wei He frowned. He disliked that attitude. Even though he had a bad reputation outside, he never viewed people as dogs.
"You called me here just to say this?" Wei He grew impatient. If this was all, she was wasting his time.
"Of course not." Yue Xiaodie cleared her throat. She was trying hard to stay calm. The fear Wei He had put in her before wasn't easy to shake off. "I have a way to help you find Shangguan Ji and his group."
"Oh?" Wei He paused, his expression turning cold and serious. "How?"
"Simple. If I use the Wanfei Palace intelligence network, we can track almost anything in the nearby prefectures. We might be weaker than the Wushi Sect in raw power, but when it comes to news..." Yue Xiaodie trailed off, her meaning clear. Their intelligence network far outmatched the Wushi Sect's.
"You want me to set you free first, and then you'll help me find them?" Wei He asked.
"If you're willing, that's best," Yue Xiaodie smiled.
"I'm not." Wei He shook his head.
The smile froze on her face. "Fine. Then I have another way."
"What way?"
"I'll send word back to my mother and ask her to find the clues. Once she has them, we trade, and you agree to my condition. How about it?"
"Deal. Better to resolve a grudge than keep it. You should have said that from the start." Wei He thought it over and found it reasonable.
Yue Xiaodie brightened up. "I knew you weren't a bad guy! You really are a good person!"
Her idea of a "good person" seemed a bit twisted.
"Anything else?" Wei He asked.
"One more thing," Yue Xiaodie said quickly. She moved close to the bars and stared at him with wide eyes. "Do you want to have a child with me?"
"..." Wei He froze, unable to process the question.
"You're so strong, smart, and brutal. A child between us would definitely be strong," Yue Xiaodie giggled. "Maybe my mother would even like you, and our kid would have a better chance of surviving in the Palace."
Wei He felt that this Wanfei Palace place was completely out of its mind.
"Well?" Yue Xiaodie looked at him with expectation. "Oh, you probably don't know, right?" She smirked. "When I was little, I had to train like crazy just to survive among all the other kids. Wanfei Palace is no normal place. How about this? If I find the clues for you, you promise to have a child with me!"
"You don't want to go back?" Wei He asked back.
"If you agree, why would I go back? I have food and drink here, you shield me from danger, and I don't have to worry about getting killed. Isn't that great?"
Wei He was speechless. He stared at the girl. She was weird. Her ability to adapt was terrifyingly strong. Locked up, poisoned, yet she acted like she was on vacation. What kind of place was Wanfei Palace to produce a person like her?
He shook his head and pushed the thought aside. "Pick another condition. I won't agree to that."
"Then... we'll talk later," Yue Xiaodie smiled.
Wei He looked at her closely and was surprised to find she really wasn't going to push it further. He took a token from her as a mark for the message to Wanfei Palace, then left the dungeon.
After sending the message off to Jin Prefecture, Wei He went back into hard training.
A few days after the Chijing Army fell, the remaining soldiers trickled back to Xuanjing. Zhou Xingtong returned alone, heavily wounded, and was taken away by the Zhou family.
The Wang family officially took over the mess left by Yuchi Zhong, absorbing power on all fronts. Wei He sent his men to cooperate closely with them. Cleaning out enemies, removing threats, and wiping out the remnants of the Yuchi family went smoothly.
With the Wang family's backing, the Wanqing Sect made its official return. With three top experts, the sect quickly swallowed up part of the Yuchi family's resources and territory, becoming the fourth major power in Xuanjing, standing equal with the other three families.
By this time, Wei He's Cloud Gathering Bone structure had reached the eighth cloud pattern. He was almost halfway to eighteen.
At the same time, the Xiangqu Cult army pressed close. Without the Chijing Army to block them, the rebel forces were less than forty miles from Xuanjing, setting up camp to rest.
At that exact moment, the Taian Army deployed over eighty thousand troops to face Li Tong's rebel army head-on. The two massive forces stood in standoff on the plains outside Black House Mountain.
