Days later.
A bronze incense burner let off a thin line of straight smoke. A thick, mint-like scent spread through the small, windowless quiet room. Only a few narrow air vents pierced the walls.
Wei He sat cross-legged. On the wall in front of him hung a large banner with a single word painted on it: Stillness.
His breathing was deep and slow. His left hand twitched non-stop. The skin and flesh wriggled like living things.
Choosing his left hand for sensory enhancement was a decision he made while walking with his family days ago. Since then, he followed the Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Art. He washed his left hand with special medicine, step by step, and kept using inner force at special frequencies to train the nerves in his hand every second.
Enhancement had two stages.
First was Opening. Opening the senses until they reached the state of Super-Sensing.
This step was fast. As long as a warrior's inner force met the standard and their internal organs were over half-enhanced, they could try it.
Second was Sealing.
It meant putting a safe lock on the opened senses.
This was the real trouble. Records showed the sealing process was extremely dangerous. Many organ-refining masters failed right here. Once they failed, their inner force ran wild, destroying their bodies from the inside out.
So Wei He made every preparation he could.
He told no one he was already in the late organ-refining stage and trying to break into the Sensing Realm.
He kept it secret for one reason. He was too fast.
With the Break-through Bead, the twelve small organ-refining hurdles were just a matter of time. He did not need to suffer like other masters who spent decades stuck on a single barrier.
No one was supposed to level up this fast in the organ-refining stage. It had nothing to do with talent. It took rare resources and endless time.
"Records show the fastest genius took over fifteen years to go from organ-refining to Sensing," Wei He thought. "If I go too fast, people will watch me. Standing out too much will kill me. Wasting a bit of time is the safest choice."
Wei He opened his eyes. Today's training was done.
Three strict sessions every day—morning, noon, and night—were mandatory. The rest of his time was free. He could stay with his family, handle sect business, or take a walk.
He stood up and blew softly.
A tiny puff of inner force put out the burning incense tip with perfect accuracy.
This mind-calming incense was rare and expensive, newly made by Jiu Ying. Even the Thousand Poison Sect had little stock left. It helped him focus, so he used it sparingly.
Wei He patted the wrinkles off his robes and walked to the door.
Suddenly, he froze.
He stood stock-still. His left hand trembled. The skin writhed.
The skin on the back of his left hand felt something passing by in the empty space beside him.
It felt warm, soft, and somewhat furry. But the fine hairs felt alive, like they were crawling on their own.
For a tiny second, his left hand touched that thing.
Just a light touch. Less than two seconds.
Wei He raised his left hand.
A bright red mark burned into the back of his hand, like a hot iron had pressed against his skin. It stung with sharp pain.
"I can actually feel a trace of the truth now?" Wei He was shocked, but also thrilled.
He was shocked by the unknown thing beside him. He was thrilled because his left hand was finally changing.
"Senior You Fu was right. Once internal organ strength hits a limit, Super-Sensing just happens naturally. But what was that thing..."
Wei He felt that the truth behind Super-Sensing might be full of danger.
With a single thought, he wrapped his left hand in his protective inner force. Over ten layers of inner force covered his skin completely.
Earlier, he had pulled back his inner force to let his skin adapt. That was why he felt it.
Now, with his hand covered again, he felt much safer.
"The records say inner force can only shield a tiny area. As Super-Sensing grows, I will keep touching strange real things. Inner force cannot block everything."
Wei He stayed on high alert. But this was a step every master had to take.
Compared to others, he was much safer.
Most masters needed luck, rare insights, or high-stakes fights to break through the twelve hurdles. He only needed to wait until the Break-through Bead filled up, then give it a light nudge.
The only cost was waiting three months for the bead to charge. That was nothing to him.
Wei He pushed the door open and stepped outside.
The moment he walked out, strange air currents brushed against his left hand.
Even with his protective inner force on, his left hand felt completely bare, exposed to the open air.
Wei He narrowed his eyes and moved his left fingers.
A thick cloud of cold, grain-like particles drifted past his hand. Some touched his skin. Many had tiny legs, squirming around.
The particles kept bumping into his skin, rolling away into the air.
Wei He stood still and looked down at his left hand. There was nothing there. Just empty air.
He could see nothing. He could hear nothing.
"Is this real sensing, or just a trick of the mind?"
He stood by the doorway, looking up at the hanging flower baskets along the hallway. These decorations were everywhere in the sect.
Now, he had a weird gut feeling about them.
He raised his left hand and slowly reached out toward a branch sticking out from a basket.
The branch was thin and green, covered in fresh, lively leaves.
Wei He touched a leaf gently.
A completely different texture hit his brain.
To his eyes, the leaf was smooth and soft.
To his left hand, the leaf was covered in thick fuzz, and a cold, tiny liquid flowed inside it like blood.
"It really is different..."
Wei He pulled his hand back and looked at his palm.
He remembered You Fu's warning: When exploring the truth, go slow. Be careful.
Wei He held his left hand up toward the light and spread his fingers.
The skin on his five fingers was turning slightly clear. He could almost see the blood flowing and the outlines of his muscles beneath.
"So this is Super-Sensing..." Wei He sighed.
Suddenly, his face turned pale.
Someone was holding his hand!
Ice-cold fingers slid right between his own, interlocking with his, holding his hand like a lover!
Wei He saw no one.
But his skin felt it clearly. The hand was freezing cold, with thick knuckles and rough skin.
It did not use much force, but Wei He could not shake it off!
What is this?!
Wei He felt his left hand losing control. Clear black finger marks rapidly bruised onto his skin.
This was no illusion. The finger marks were real proof.
A freezing chill raced from his hand up into his forearm.
Wei He snapped. He did not hesitate. All his inner force surged into his left hand and blew outward.
BOOM!
A dull bang rang out.
His left hand broke free from the cold grip.
Wei He pulled his hand back into his sleeve and clenched his fist.
He stood on the spot, taking deep breaths, waiting and sensing the area. Only after confirming nothing was near did he walk away quickly.
He recalled an old ghost story he had read about handshakes. He thought it was just a fairy tale. Now...
Wei He rushed straight to the Tianya Building, the sect's library.
He had built a copy of the library here in the Thousand Poison Sect. With plenty of money and slave labor, it cost him almost nothing to make.
Only a dozen disciples were inside. Ignoring their greets, Wei He walked straight up to the third floor, entering his private reading room.
He locked the door, sat at his desk, and pulled out an old book of strange tales.
He flipped the pages fast until he found the story.
The story started with a warning: Do not spread your fingers forward for no reason, especially when you are alone. It counts as an invitation.
Below was the text: A scholar named Xu Yu could feel things ordinary people could not. One night, while practicing calligraphy, his hand grew tired. He raised his hand and spread his fingers to stretch.
The room was empty. The night was dark. He stretched his palm forward into the dark.
An unseen hand grabbed his.
Xu Yu panicked, but could not pull away.
The next day, people found him dead in his room. His body was already rotten, and his arm was ripped clean off, nowhere to be found.
Wei He closed the book.
He looked down at his left hand. His skin was back to normal. The black finger marks were gone.
It felt as if the icy grip had never happened.
"My hands are already much larger than average," Wei He thought, his heart sinking. "How big was the hand that made mine feel small?"
The truth was far more dangerous than he thought.
Organ-refining and Sensing were just one rank apart, but they belonged to two totally different worlds.
"I am already running into trouble before I even finish. Once my Super-Sensing fully opens, things will only get worse."
