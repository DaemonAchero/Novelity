"If I want to take the route of a divine attendant, what do I even have to trade?"
Her looks?
She had beauty, sure, but it was nowhere near enough.
Viscera Refining experts were not naive young disciples. No matter how simple they acted, they had seen every trick in the book.
With real power, what kind of beauty couldn't they get?
"I need something decisive..." Zhou Muqing thought, searching for her own edge.
She came from the Clear Stream Sect and served as its vice sect leader. Creating a new technique based on her sect's foundation proved her comprehension was top-tier.
She quickly found a direction.
"First, I need to find a new, better target. Too many people are clinging to Ouyang Luo and Feng Han. Joining them now means I've lost the upper hand. I won't get a spot."
Decision made, she stood up, packed the tea set, and returned to her room to prepare.
It was time to build her advantage.
Two months passed in the blink of an eye.
Sizzle...
In the courtyard, Zhou Muqing poured tea with one hand.
Wei He sat across from her. Both of them sighed.
"It's too hard," Zhou Muqing said, feeling choked with frustration. She was stuck on the second layer, unable to break through the barrier to the third. It left her speechless.
She had never doubted her talent. Growing up, everyone called her a genius. But this situation made her doubt everything.
"Yeah... it really is too hard..." Wei He agreed deeply. "This technique isn't meant for ordinary humans."
Even with the secret trick, it took him over two months to reach the seventh layer and complete the Seven Subtle Truth Art.
The difficulty was unreal.
"Indeed." Distressed, Zhou Muqing drained her teacup in one gulp like it was water.
"Why did they put so many obstacles in this technique? So many paths seem useless, wasting energy and time." Unhappy, she muttered her frustration.
"Maybe it's a test of patience for future generations?" Wei He suffered just as much. Viscera Refining was fine, but the bone rebuilding before it was a nightmare.
The Subtle Truth Body was compatible with the Cloud-Gathering Bones, and its bone-forging areas didn't clash with it.
In fact, it didn't clash with most bone structures.
The bones needed for the Subtle Truth Body were extremely rare and obscure.
And like the earlier techniques, it was broadly compatible.
However, it had one massive problem.
It was fragmented.
A piece here, a piece there. Some were the size of a sesame seed, requiring training on just a tiny spot of a single bone.
It was painful to identify them all.
Wei He drained his mental energy on it, leaving him exhausted.
"Here, have a drink." He raised his cup and tapped it against hers.
Over these past months, sharing tea had brought them closer.
Zhou Muqing drank it down, her mind dark.
If she failed to break through, she had less than a decade to live. In seven or eight years, her hidden injuries would flare up, and she would die a gruesome death.
She desperately needed a target, someone with a high chance of entering the blessed lands.
Yet, all the newcomers to the Moral Palace were plain and unimpressive.
Besides, unless someone revealed their cultivation progress, outsiders couldn't guess it.
You could only know during official assessments.
Two months of searching yielded zero hope. Zhou Muqing was ready to give up.
Instead of wasting time on phantom hopes, she thought it was better to go home and enjoy her final years.
"Wei He, do you want to go back?" she asked softly while drinking.
"No," Wei He replied instantly.
"Why?"
"In the eyes of outsiders, I'm an evil demon with enemies everywhere. Why would I go back? Having my family with me is enough," Wei He said plainly.
"I see... I kind of envy you..." Zhou Muqing forced a weak smile. "At least you live freely."
Thinking of her dead-end search, her heart grew bitter.
Her cultivation was stuck, her goal was gone, and she had poured her entire life into martial arts.
In the end, this was what she got.
She wasn't willing to accept it.
Not at all.
Her entire existence was tied to this path. When others rested, she trained. When others played, she trained.
She gave up everything, only to end up here.
"Life... martial arts... it's just too hard..." Bitterness welled up inside her.
"Yeah... too hard..." Wei He thought of the future. The further he walked, the greater the dangers and harsh realities he had to face.
Even if this path brought longevity, it held little joy.
Because there was no end in sight.
Both felt a heavy weight on their chests and silently downed another cup.
After emptying his cup, Wei He began thinking about how to split his two attendant spots.
On the martial path, family came first.
However, his wife Wan Qingqing had hit her limit; she hadn't even reached Viscera Refining, so she couldn't make it.
His mother-in-law was the same, unable to meet the basic requirement.
No one else in his sect showed the potential for a breakthrough.
Entering the blessed lands required candidates who could potentially become true masters. Otherwise, these spots would go to waste.
Wei He ran through names in his head, but found no one suitable.
He thought of Wang Shaojun, but the distance was vast. Whether Wang Shaojun would abandon everything to come to Haizhou was unknown.
'Whatever, I'll hold a public selection later. Pick a few Viscera Refining experts from the Moral Palace who actually have a chance. If I get stronger later, there will be plenty of opportunities.' He sighed, annoyed by these distractions.
Then he looked at Zhou Muqing in front of him.
During their time together, he saw that her nature was pure and driven purely by martial arts, without malice. She was a solid candidate.
"I plan to leave the mountain," Zhou Muqing said quietly.
"You're... giving up?" Wei He was surprised.
"My time is near... Rather than wasting my last days here, I'd rather go back and enjoy life." Zhou Muqing had finally accepted it.
A trace of defeat showed in her beautiful eyes.
"Are you... really sure?" Wei He asked, his voice low.
"...I'm sure... I threw my whole life into martial arts, and only now do I realize how many people I let down, how much love I ignored," Zhou Muqing said softly. "I want to use my remaining time to go back, repay them, and thank them."
Wei He had spent enough time with her to know she was soft on the outside but tough inside. They got along well.
He originally planned to invite her to be his attendant, but seeing her spirit broken, he sighed.
"Actually, there's still hope... If..."
"It's fine, I've made up my mind," Zhou Muqing interrupted softly.
Wei He felt disappointed. Knowing her mind was set, saying more was pointless.
Breaking through in martial arts relied heavily on willpower. Without that fire and drive, she would never make progress again.
"Alright... A toast to you!" He raised his cup.
"Thanks." Zhou Muqing nodded.
She drank the tea in one gulp and slammed the cup down.
"I plan to leave tomorrow."
"So fast?" Wei He froze. They were friends now. He didn't expect her to leave so soon.
"The sooner, the better. A clean break brings peace," Zhou Muqing smiled calmly.
"Fair enough..." Wei He nodded. Her personality was easygoing, able to let go of things lightly.
Once someone like her decided, putting an attendant spot in front of her wouldn't change a thing.
Seeing her resolute eyes, Wei He sighed internally and stopped talking.
"Have a safe journey."
"Thanks," Zhou Muqing smiled. "If you ever need anything, visit me in Bianzhou. If I'm still alive, I'll welcome you warmly."
"If I get the chance, definitely!" Wei He nodded solemnly. He respected those who faced death with peace.
Precisely because he couldn't do it himself, he admired it even more.
Zhou Muqing cupped her fists in farewell and stood up to leave.
"What a shame. If you stayed, Sister Qing, I could have given you a spot as an attendant. I don't have anyone suitable around me anyway," Wei He lamented.
"But since your mind is set, I won't say more..."
He picked up the teapot and poured himself a full cup.
"I know someone as firm as you won't change her mind easily."
BAM!
Suddenly, a pair of small hands slammed violently onto the stone table in front of him.
Deep palm prints sank into the stone. With loud cracks, web-like fractures spread rapidly from her palms.
Crash.
The table shattered into piles of stone dust on the ground.
Wei He blinked, utterly stunned. He slowly raised his head.
Zhou Muqing had turned back and was leaning over him.
Her eyes were wide, and her upper body trembled slightly, as if holding something back.
"Actually, I am not firm at all. Can... can you repeat what you just said?"
"..." Wei He was speechless, his brain temporarily short-circuiting.
Two minutes later.
They replaced the table with a wooden one.
Zhou Muqing stared at Wei He with a complex look. She never imagined that right when she hit rock bottom, everything would change so fast.
Nor did she expect that Wei He, who always kept a low profile, had secretly rushed to the seventh layer of the Seven Subtle Truth Art, fulfilling the sect's strict condition.
This meant that compared to Ouyang Luo and Feng Han, Wei He was the true top hidden genius.
"Are you really deciding to make me one of your attendants?" Even though she got a clear answer earlier, Zhou Muqing still couldn't believe it.
She asked once more.
"Yes. I can't find a better peak Viscera Refining expert around me. Bringing someone with low cultivation inside would be a waste. So I decided to choose someone nearby to help me," Wei He nodded.
"...That's... really reckless of you..." Zhou Muqing didn't know how to describe Wei He.
Such a precious attendant spot, yet he handed it out so casually.
One spot was worth a fortune to those with connections.
Even someone like Shangguan Xing was willing to give herself up and work endlessly for Ouyang Luo just for a chance.
Even then, getting a spot wasn't guaranteed.
"I understand..." Zhou Muqing suddenly realized something, her heart trembling. Looking at Wei He now, her gaze shifted slightly.
She felt she understood why Wei He gave such a precious spot to her.
What reason would drive a man to give a woman something so valuable?
What else could it be besides that?
Zhou Muqing believed she had guessed the truth.
