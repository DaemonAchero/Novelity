“Show mercy!”
A sharp, urgent voice echoed from the distance.
Before the person even arrived, the sound vibrated through the air, striking the ears of everyone present.
Wei He shifted his posture. Instead of striking again, he turned toward the source of the voice.
A white figure blurred across the field, landing softly just five meters in front of him.
It was a woman. Her long legs, narrow waist, and defined curves gave her a striking presence. Her long hair was tied simply with a white ribbon, giving her an elegant, untamed look.
“Long time no see... Brother Wei.”
The woman let out a small breath of relief. Seeing Wei He hold back his strike, she quickly spoke up.
“Yue Xiaodie?”
Wei He did not expect to meet an old acquaintance here.
He felt nothing for her. Back then, she had feelings for him, but he was already married to Wan Qingqing. Besides, Yue Xiaodie had been cruel and unpredictable back then. She had a naive side, but they were never a match, so he had turned her down repeatedly. He never thought he would cross paths with her again after returning to Yun State.
“Brother Wei, is there a misunderstanding between us? We can talk this through. There is no need to fight like this,” Yue Xiaodie said, her eyes fixed on him.
“Two real masters of my Xuanmiao Sect are dead,” Wei He said coldly. “The last corpse bore marks of the Taisui Sutra. Xiaodie, out of respect for you, I won't kill anyone today. But until the suspicion is cleared, I am taking her with me. If you want to save her, find the truth first, then come look for me.”
Wei He did not waste more words. He tightened his grip and swung his hand.
Boom!
A sudden blast of wind exploded outward, forcing the women to cover their eyes. By the time the wind settled, Wei He and Ling Xian'er were already gone.
“In three days, I will be waiting at the Xuanmiao Sect branch. If you cannot give me a clear answer by then, do not blame me for being ruthless.”
Wei He’s voice drifted back from far away, growing fainter until it vanished completely.
Yue Xiaodie felt a heavy pressure in her chest. Ling Xian'er was the strongest fighter leading the Wanfei Palace group this time, a master of the Transformation Realm. Yet even she was dragged away like a helpless captive.
As a junior disciple, Yue Xiaodie knew she only got this far because Wei He gave her face. She had no power to settle this alone. Her only choice was to report back to the sect and ask her mother, the Second Palace Master, for help.
Inside a quiet courtyard in Tongning District.
A wooden plaque hung above the main gate with bold black characters painted on a red background: Xuanmiao.
Under the shadow of a large tree, Ling Xian'er sat slumped against the trunk. Her body was completely limp, her energy sealed, but her eyes remained icy as she glared at Wei He.
Wei He, Zhao Yuexi, Gong Huan, and Han Xiangqi stood nearby.
Yang Ling's body had completely sunk into the True World. Filled with grief, Gong Huan had buried her deep in the soil of the True World, granting her a proper rest. Sinking into the True World did not mean absolute destruction; it simply meant normal senses could no longer perceive her presence.
Back at the Yun State branch of the Xuanmiao Sect, Gong Huan put all his hopes on Wei He, desperate to avenge his wife. After seeing Wei He overpower Ling Xian'er in a single move, Gong Huan knew that following Wei He was his only chance to get justice.
“Ling Xian'er. One of the Ten Flowers of Wanfei Palace. A master of the Transformation Realm.” Wei He took a slow sip from a cup of warm tea.
“Do you have anything to say for yourself?” He glanced down at her, his voice heavy.
Ling Xian'er forced herself to stay calm and pushed back. “Senior Wei, strength rules this world, so I call you Senior. Since I lost to you, I have nothing to say about my defeat. But don't you think it's reckless to blame Wanfei Palace based on a few faint marks on a corpse?”
Her words made sense. Suspicion was one thing, but striking without concrete proof seemed overly aggressive.
The other three shared the same thought, their eyes shifting to Wei He, waiting for his answer.
Wei He set his teacup down.
“If not for the help Senior Xia Linghua gave us in the past, do you think you would still be breathing?” he said softly. “Our sects used to have a good relationship. But when the Wu army attacked, Wanfei Palace stood with the coalition and turned a blind eye. That old debt of gratitude is already cleared.”
When the coalition abandoned the Xuanmiao Sect, Wanfei Palace offered no objection. Now that trouble had landed on their own doorstep, they wanted to talk about old friendships?
What a joke.
Seeing Ling Xian'er open her mouth to speak again, Wei He flicked his finger. A streak of force locked her throat, sealing her voice completely.
He turned to Gong Huan. “Who else do you suspect? Speak.”
Gong Huan felt a chill run down his spine. He could sense Wei He’s cold intent, but with his wife dead, he had nothing left to fear. He nodded fiercely.
“Someone saw Three-Face escape into the main headquarters of the White Jade Crown before vanishing. I suspect he has people inside. They also lost two people recently, but those were low-level members. It could easily be a trick to throw us off.”
The other two gasped.
Han Xiangqi was a local and knew exactly how dangerous the White Jade Crown was. They were the true underground rulers of the city, controlling a massive network of thugs and killers. Their leadership, made up of the Crown Master and the Three Feathered Crowns, could gather an army of elite killers in an instant.
The women of the White Jade Crown were notorious fanatics. Once they started killing, they stopped at nothing. Rumors even said they had powerful sects supporting them from the shadows.
To attack them based on a single line of suspicion...
“Senior Wei... Should we investigate a bit more first?” Zhao Yuexi asked nervously.
“Stay here and watch the place. I will be back soon.”
Wei He had no intention of wasting time. He still needed to find a spot to complete his second sense-lock. He preferred to cut through the mess quickly.
His Senior Sister had given him total authority. If he hesitated now, he would be betraying her trust.
The current Xuanmiao Sect did not need to fear anyone.
“Also, I don't know the layout of the city. Lead the way.”
Wei He stood up, grabbed Zhao Yuexi by the collar, and vanished into a gust of wind before anyone could react.
He was heading straight for the White Jade Crown headquarters, leaving the others staring at each other in stunned silence.
Meanwhile, near the site of the earlier clash.
Among the crowd of watching warriors, a lean, muscular man with a plain face and a single blade on his back quietly slipped away. He navigated through the narrow alleyways of the city.
Soon, he stopped in front of a small, run-down temple and knocked lightly on the door.
Knock. Knock.
“Come in.” A weak, raspy voice came from inside.
The man stepped inside without a word and locked the door behind him.
Inside stood a shrine dedicated to an unknown deity. The statue had three heads, six arms, a high crown, and a black-and-red Daoist robe carved with patterns of mountains, rivers, and beasts.
Below the statue, a withered, white-haired old man sat cross-legged on a mat. He had his back to the door and his eyes closed, but his breathing was deep and strong, completely unlike his frail appearance.
“Jin Chi, you're back?”
“Senior Brother, someone from the Xuanmiao Sect did come. Just a young kid who recently broke through to become a real master. His movement speed and raw power are impressive though, almost on par with someone who has cleared three or four sense-locks.”
The dull expression on Jin Chi's face instantly shifted into a cunning, wicked grin.
“Xuanmiao Sect... They are strong, and I have heard of this Wei He. He is young. At most, he relies on combat experience equal to three or four sense-locks. Once I regain my full power, he will be nothing more than a bug I can crush with a finger.”
The old man turned around to face him. “Did you leave the traces like I told you to?”
“Of course,” Jin Chi chuckled darkly. “That kid Wei He has a fiery temper. He grabbed Ling Xian'er on the spot, and no one knows where she is now. I didn't even need to use the rest of my plan. They started fighting right away.”
“The city army, the White Jade Crown, Wanfei Palace, and the Xuanmiao Sect... Quite a few experts have gathered in this tiny Yun State city,” the old man said calmly. “Good. The remaining female experts here should be enough to help me fully restore my cultivation.”
“As for the remaining female masters, Wanfei Palace has one left, and the Xuanmiao Sect has two—that Zhao Yuexi and the captured Ling Xian'er. All of them fit your requirements,” Jin Chi grinned. “Then there is the Master of the White Jade Crown. She has been disguised as a man for years, but she possesses stunning beauty and carries a rare bloodline. If we catch her too, you won't just recover your strength, Senior Brother. You might even break through to a higher level.”
“The White Jade Crown headquarters has an array that detects energy. It's risky to force our way in,” the old man noted. “However, with the clues I left behind, Wei He will likely charge right in and tear the place apart.”
“I have already set everything up,” Jin Chi nodded. “Wei He is just a simple-minded musclehead. Give him a small trail, and he will rush in blindly, clearing the path for us for free.”
“Good. Once we capture the Master of the White Jade Crown and I regain my peak strength, I will handle Wei He and consume whatever resources the Xuanmiao Sect has left.”
To the old man, ordinary sense-lock masters were nothing more than cattle waiting for slaughter. Once he returned to his full power as a former True Master, he could take on anyone the Xuanmiao Sect sent his way.
The headquarters of the White Jade Crown was located in Xu Family District.
The area took its name because most of the local businesses belonged to the Xu family. The Xu family was a massive clan with deep roots across the city.
However, the real force behind the Xu family was the White Jade Crown. Almost every business the Xu family owned served to generate wealth and resources for them.
At the center of the district stood the headquarters: a massive structure known as the Star Phase Building.
On the surface, the Star Phase Building was famous across Yun State as the Wanbao Tower, a place known for housing rare treasures. The main building rose sixteen stories high, surrounded by a maze of connected halls and towers.
It functioned as a market for rare goods and a high-end pawnshop, while also serving as the base for the Three Feathered Crowns—the top martial artists of the White Jade Crown. All three were female experts who had mastered organ cultivation.
Because they were women, many had been married off to powerful figures over the years. This web of political marriages was one of the main reasons the White Jade Crown held so much influence in the region.
At that moment, on the fifth floor of the building, Crown Master Shangguan Tong stood with his hands behind his back, looking down at the wealthy merchants and officials gathered for a grand banquet.
