Snap.
Wei He landed softly on top of a wooden building. From a distance, he quietly watched groups of bandits burning, killing, and looting the town of Huibai.
The White Skeleton bandits were easy to spot. Every single one had white human skulls hanging from their necks. Because the skulls were large, most men carried only one. Anyone carrying two or more was a giant, at least two meters tall.
"Over here! Search everywhere! See if you can find any grain!"
Orders drifted through the wind from far away.
Wei He stood on the roof and looked around. He had reached Huibai Town, but it was much bigger than he expected.
The town was filled with circular compounds. A main building sat at the center of each compound, surrounded by high walls, watchtowers, arrow slits, and heavy iron doors. Smaller houses crowded around these walls for protection. People naturally wanted to live near safe spots, and the owners of the main buildings liked having extra meat shields around them.
Looking down from above, Wei He saw circles of all sizes. Between those circles lay a chaotic mess of wooden huts.
"This is going to be hard to find," he muttered. He scanned the shops below, but most signs were gone or smashed to pieces. Finding the Xu Pawnshop or the Wang Art Shop was nearly impossible.
"I need a local to guide me."
To him, bandits were just a normal part of this brutal world. He didn't want to meddle, nor did he have the time. The town was already almost empty. Unless they crossed his path directly, his own goals came first. If he had spare time after finishing his task, his good nature might move him to wipe them out.
Wei He jumped down from the roof and began moving through the alleys to find someone.
...
"That's all you found?!"
At the town's main intersection, Dong Baihua, the chief of the White Skeletons, glared at her lieutenants. Her eyes overflowed with malice and violence.
Dong Baihua was a stunning woman with snow-white skin and a killer body. Her long legs and high hips showed off every curve. She wore gray tight pants, black boots, and a sleek red dress that trailed down to her heels. Her full chest bulged against her clothes. With her hair down, she would look like a bold, gorgeous noble lady.
Yet none of the bandits, not even the most ruthless commanders, dared to stare at her body. It wasn't because they didn't like women. It was because too many men had already died for looking at her.
"Half an hour," Dong Baihua said coldly. "I'm giving you half an hour. If you don't bring back enough grain, you can crawl into the kitchen yourselves tonight."
Her voice wasn't loud, but every bandit shuddered. They knew what she meant. If there wasn't enough food, their own flesh would be served on the table.
In the White Skeleton gang, cannibalism was common, and Dong Baihua enjoyed it the most. The martial arts she practiced required human blood as fuel. That was how all those white skulls around their necks came to be.
"C-Chief..." a bearded commander stammered, shaking with fear. "We really can't find any more grain! But... we did find some other valuables!"
"Fine," Dong Baihua said with annoyance. "If we run out of food, we'll just capture more people for storage. Though eating the same meat every day gets boring."
She looked around at the ruined, empty houses of Huibai Town.
"Once we clear this batch, there won't be anyone left anyway. Burn the whole place down. It's annoying when these two-legged sheep play hide and seek."
"But... Chief, if we burn it down, where will we..."
"Just do what I said." Dong Baihua gave him a sharp glare. The bandit's face turned pale, and he shut his mouth immediately.
...
Inside the pawnshop.
The Song brothers watched in horror through the cracks as the side building caught fire. Bandits were patrolling right outside, so they couldn't run. The bandits were deliberately using the fire to smoke people out.
"Brother... what... what do we do?!" the younger brother, Song Shizhen, gasped. His voice shook so hard he could barely speak, and sweat poured down his forehead from both heat and terror.
"Don't panic!" Song Shixiong grit his teeth, staring at the flames.
They were hiding in the wood storage room, surrounded by the study, bedrooms, and hallway. The fire was spreading fast from the study because of all the paper and books. The flames roared higher by the second.
The brothers were trapped. At least three White Skeleton bandits were waiting outside. If they ran, they would be caught instantly. If they stayed, they would burn alive.
Outside, the three bandits chatted in a dialect the brothers couldn't understand.
"AHHH!"
A sharp scream tore through the air. A bandit dragged a woman out of the house next door and threw her onto the dirt.
"Force some water down her throat to flush her out," the bandit chuckled. "The meat will taste much better!"
"Nice. She has soft skin. Where did you find her?"
"Under the bed. There was a baby with her, a few months old, but it was already dead and stinking. She was still holding it in her arms. Make sure you scrub her clean later."
They calmly discussed how to cook her.
The woman collapsed on the dirt, barely holding herself up. She clutched a pillow to her chest, her eyes blank and dead. She had already lost her mind.
Inside the room, the Song brothers heard everything.
"That's... that's Aunt Yinhua from next door... Master Zhang's second wife..." Song Shizhen swallowed hard, terrified. He grabbed his brother's sleeve tightly, refusing to let go.
"It's okay... we'll be fine..." Song Shixiong whispered softly to comfort his brother, though he was really just praying to himself.
The room grew hotter. The flames crept closer.
Outside, more hiding locals were smoked out by the growing fire, only to be grabbed by the bandits.
"Ah!" Song Shizhen suddenly gasped. He clamped a hand over his mouth to muffle his own voice.
"Brother, look! Look over there!" He slapped his brother's arm furiously, pointing to the corner of the wood room.
Song Shixiong turned around and saw heavy smoke rising from the back corner.
"It's burning from behind too! We're dead!" Song Shixiong was stunned. Were these bandits trying to burn the whole town to ashes?
Both brothers were carrying huge packs, which made running impossible.
"Drop the packs! Take only food, water, and gold! Leave the rest!" Song Shixiong ordered instantly.
They tore open their bags, grabbed their rations, and stuffed a few gold coins and leaves into their pockets. Then they dumped the rest on the ground.
A pile of items rolled across the floor. Among them was their family treasure, the Moonlight and Whale Scroll.
Song Shixiong stared at the ancient painting. They had hoped to sell it for gold and live a good life. But fate was cruel, and now they had to abandon it.
"Get ready to run!" Song Shixiong said through grit teeth, forcing himself to look away from the painting.
Song Shizhen didn't reply. He just squeezed his brother's hand tightly. It was life or death now.
The fire raged closer, and thick smoke filled their lungs.
Unable to take it any longer, Song Shixiong cracked the wooden door open with one hand while holding his brother with the other. Hearing the bandits talking nearby, he pulled the door open and burst out, sprinting toward the back exit.
"Keep quiet!" he hissed.
They moved as fast and quietly as they could, but the bandits were trained martial artists. Their senses were far sharper than normal men, and they instantly picked up the footsteps over the sound of the crackling fire.
"Heh, two more came out. I'll go get them," a bandit with a blade sneered. He sped toward the back door with fast, practiced footwork.
Meanwhile, the brothers ran for their lives, panting heavily. The streets in this part of town were empty since the main group of bandits hadn't arrived yet. The path ahead was clear.
As they ran, Song Shixiong noticed something. His brother was holding his hand with one arm, but his other hand was clutching a scroll.
It was the Moonlight and Whale Scroll. The boy had picked it back up before running out.
"This is... father's most important thing! We can't lose it!" Song Shizhen cried out. He was terrified and exhausted, but his grip on the scroll was like iron.
"Just run! Don't worry about that now!" Song Shixiong didn't have time to argue.
They sprinted madly toward the town's exit.
...
BANG!
The main door of the Xu Pawnshop blew inward. The heavy wooden plank shattered into pieces and slammed onto the floor.
A tall figure walked slowly through the doorway, looking at the burning building.
"Did it get destroyed?" Wei He felt a cold drop in his stomach.
He had searched for the Moonlight and Whale Scroll for years. If it was reduced to ashes right now, his Whale Flood Technique might never break through for the rest of his life.
Without delay, Wei He rushed toward the study, the most likely place for a painting. He smashed the door with one palm and stepped inside.
Thick smoke filled the room, blinding the eye. But Wei He could still see a pile of scrolls and books on the floor, all burned into black charcoal.
His face turned grim. A terrible feeling washed over him.
"Hahaha! There's another live one here! What luck!"
Outside, a woman screamed in terror, followed by a man's rough voice laughing.
Wei He looked at the ashes on the floor, turned around, and walked out.
In the courtyard, the weak woman he had captured earlier was cornered by two muscular bandits wearing skull necklaces. She was frozen in terror.
Her name was Zhou Miaoke, the second daughter of the wealthy Zhou family nearby. He had pulled her out to serve as his guide.
The two bandits were built like bears. Standing over the delicate Zhou Miaoke, they looked like wild beasts facing a tiny lamb.
Wei He was already furious after finding the burnt study. Seeing this scene made his blood boil.
Swish!
His body blurred. He closed the distance in an instant and struck out with his palm.
BANG! BANG!
The two bandits were only basic Blood-Refining fighters. They were no match for him. Despite their massive two-meter frames, they were like paper toys under Wei He's palm. He struck them both right in the waist.
Before they could even speak, their ruthless grins froze on their faces. Their bodies snapped in half at the waist, blowing apart into four bloody chunks that slammed into the wall, turning into a red mess.]
