Dayue Dynasty, Haizhou.
The blue ocean was flat as a mirror, looking like a giant sapphire. White clouds moved slowly across the sky.
This was a quiet bay in a hidden corner of Haizhou. Where the curved coastline met the water, there was a stretch of fine golden sand.
A group of people stood on the beach, looking out at the sea. There were old and young among them, but most were middle-aged. Although their clothes were a messy mix—some in rags, some in clean robes, some looking like rich men, and others like plain farm women—every single person carried a quiet, serious air.
"They are here!" someone suddenly called out.
Far out on the water, two massive ships slowly appeared. They were three stories high with a few people standing on the decks. A giant character for "Xuan" was printed on the sails.
"The Xuanmiao Sect truly keeps its word," a white-haired old man sighed. He turned to a young man beside him. "Xinjin, when we reach the overseas lands, keep a low profile. With your talent, it won't be long before you lead our Jinlian Sect back to the top!"
"I understand, Patriarch!" the young man said with a sharp nod.
"Our sect had nine core leaders, but to cover our retreat, only five are left alive... Such a heavy loss. The Dayue Dynasty..." The old man bit his lip. He wanted to curse, but only let out another long sigh.
The Dayue Dynasty was at the peak of its power, growing stronger every day. Even if they hated it, what could they do? How could they fight such an enemy?
He remembered the bloody battle against the True Blood Buddha Lords. Those Buddha Lords were already terrifying, especially the top ranks. After being powered up by blood artifacts, their strength became even more insane. On top of that, new faces had appeared—top fighters who had risen in recent years.
Compared to Dayue, which kept churning out powerful masters, the Jinlian Sect and other Taoist sects were slowly fading away.
Just then, a sharp crane cry pierced through the clouds.
Everyone looked up and saw three giant red-crowned cranes break through the cloud layer. They glided down and landed softly on the sand right next to the crowd. The huge cranes flapped their wings, took a few steps to slow down, and stopped.
Yuan Duzi, Cai Menghuan, and Wei He slid off their backs.
"Sect Master Jinlian, it has been decades. I hope you have been well," Yuan Duzi said plainly.
The white-haired old man quickly stepped forward to greet her. "I am ashamed that Fellow Daoist Yuan Du came in person just to escort us."
Back when the Jinlian Sect decided how to treat the Xuanmiao Sect, he had argued for them a bit within the alliance, but he hadn't stood his ground. Thinking back now, he felt deeply ashamed. If he had stayed firm back then, he might not have had to pay such a huge price today just to get Xuanmiao Sect's help to escape.
"It is nothing. We are all part of the Taoist path, so helping is only natural," Yuan Duzi smiled, completely skipping the fact that she had bled them dry of resources for this trip.
She talked with him for a moment, then frowned. "Why hasn't the Wushi Sect arrived yet?"
"Based on the distance, they should have arrived earlier than us," Jinlianzi said, equally confused.
"Maybe they ran into a Dayue hunting party on the way. Dayue is far more ruthless toward Wushi Sect than they are toward us," Jinlianzi sighed. "Wushi Sect doesn't have many grandmasters left. After being ambushed several times, they took heavy losses. So this time..."
Yuan Duzi pondered for a moment, then turned back to look at Cai Menghuan and Wei He.
"Can you two handle part of the escort mission?" she asked softly.
"Yes," Wei He and Cai Menghuan looked at each other and bowed.
Even though the Jinlian Sect group looked beaten down, they were still led by a grandmaster, so there wasn't much danger here.
"As for Wushi Sect, their main group should be fine," Yuan Duzi said calmly. "However, when I contacted them earlier, they had split into three groups. They are coming in three separate teams, which makes a lot more noise than Jinlian Sect."
"That is true," Jinlianzi nodded. "Before I came, I heard news from my men in the rear. Dayue's official organization, Yuelong, has already locked onto one of Wushi Sect's groups."
"Yuelong?"
"Yes. It's an official group built to balance the Buddha Hall. It is extremely powerful. Though it was formed only a few years ago, its leader is not Modo, but another terrifying top-tier grandmaster," Jinlianzi sighed.
As time went on, the Dayue Dynasty only grew stronger, while people like them were forced to run for their lives, abandoning everything they had built. The bitterness was beyond words.
"I received news that Wushi Sect's main force is also led by a grandmaster. However, on their way here, they plan to stop by Hua Shan Valley, Zongnan Valley, and Yubian Town to dig up the treasure they buried earlier," Jinlianzi said heavily.
"Those places..." Yuan Duzi froze, then shook her head. "I'm afraid that won't work. Our Xuanmiao Sect spies sent word that Dayue agents are watching those spots. Someone inside Wushi Sect probably leaked the news. If they go there, it will be a trap."
"Then we should order them to come straight here. We will wait another half hour. If they don't show up, we leave on our own. What do you think, Fellow Daoist Yuan Du?" Jinlianzi suggested.
"Agreed." Yuan Duzi was not one to hesitate. She wasn't going to waste time waiting here forever just for Wushi Sect.
"I will send them a message right now. If they skip those three spots, they should be able to get here very fast." Yuan Duzi reached out her hand, and a pale gold beetle materialized in thin air.
The beetle vibrated its round wings and buzzed down onto her fingertip.
Yuan Duzi quickly wrote a small note, tied it to the beetle's leg, and set it free.
Once that was done, she looked at Cai Menghuan and Wei He.
"Wushi Sect has three groups. Two are led by grandmasters, and the third was scheduled for us to pick up. Since you are familiar with them, Wei He, it makes sense for the two of you to go. Move out."
"Yes!" the two answered right away.
Wei He bowed, exchanged a glance with Cai Menghuan, and both turned into blurred shadows, rushing into the distance.
Before arriving, while still on the crane's back, Yuan Duzi had already given them the maps and details for the mission. All they had to do was go to the meeting point, wait for the group, and bring them back. If there was trouble, they would give them a hand. It was a simple job. If they failed this, they wouldn't deserve to be called Dao Seeds anymore.
The two left the bay and moved fast through the quiet, thin mountain forest toward the agreed location.
The trees in the forest were tall and straight, sticking out of the ground like long, thin wooden spikes. A thin layer of mist drifted between the trunks.
Cai Menghuan ran ahead while Wei He followed behind, chatting via voice transmission as they moved.
"Junior Brother Wei, I heard you tried to join Wushi Sect back in the day?" Cai Menghuan asked casually.
"I was only in my late teens or early twenties back then. What did I know?" Wei He said plainly. "Actually, I came from a branch of Wushi Sect originally. But things happened, and I ended up in the Tianyin Sect... Later, through my ties with Wanfei Palace, I joined Xuanmiao Sect..."
He had long let go of the past. Since Cai Menghuan asked, he shared his past experiences bit by bit.

Near Yubian Town.
Deep in the forest, a group wearing heavy grey cloaks stepped softly through the trees. They halted and peered toward the town under the cover of the branches.
"Master sent a message. We are to give up on digging for the treasure and head straight to the rendezvous point," a person in the group reported in a low voice.
"Are you sure it's from Master?" Another person pulled back her grey cloak, revealing a pretty, strict face.
It was Master Linhua from Yuya Peak, one of the three main peaks of Wushi Sect.
Her team had purposely split off to come here, aiming to dig up the sect resources buried near Yubian Town.
But now... they had reached the spot, only to get a message saying it might be a trap and ordering them to leave immediately.
The treasure was right in front of them, ready to be taken. Leaving now left a bitter taste in Master Linhua's mouth.
While she hesitated, one of the disciples nearby pulled down her hood and spoke up.
"Master, since the higher-ups gave up on this treasure, if we just act fast, dig it up, and leave right away, wouldn't all these resources belong to our own group once we get back? Isn't that better than blindly following orders and walking away empty-handed?"
Under the hood, the girl had a quiet air, but her face carried a sharp, attractive edge. It was Qing Ye, a former inner court leader of Wushi Sect.
"Qing Ye... that is too risky," Master Linhua frowned slightly.
Though she was tempted, a message from the three main peaks usually meant real danger.
"Maybe the Xuanmiao Sect is just rushing us because time is tight," she added in a heavy tone.
"So what? Wushi Sect paid a massive price to get Xuanmiao Sect to help us. Making them wait a little won't kill them," Qing Ye said as if it were obvious.
"If there really is an ambush in Yubian Town, we will all be in danger," Master Linhua shook her head. She decided to stick to the order and fall back.
"If Master is worried, why not wait until the Xuanmiao Sect experts arrive? The moment they show up, we make our move and dig up the treasure. If the Buddha Hall is watching, the Xuanmiao Sect experts will draw their attention and take the heat off us."
Qing Ye pushed again.
"Master, we are not going overseas for a short trip. We are going there to rebuild the sect. Rebuilding takes massive resources. Without money, we can't take a single step. For the sake of our future, even if we annoy the escort from Xuanmiao Sect a little, it doesn't matter. We can just send them a gift later to smooth things over."
Master Linhua was clearly swayed.
She quickly made her choice.
"Fine. We move fast and dig up the buried goods."
"Yes!" the group answered in unison.
"Master, I actually have a better idea," Qing Ye spoke up once more.
"Oh?" Master Linhua narrowed her eyes at her sharpest disciple.
"Didn't Xuanmiao Sect give us a tracking token to find us? We can just drop that token inside Yubian Town. That way, the Xuanmiao Sect experts will test the waters in town for us. If there are no enemies, great. If there is an ambush, they will take the hit, and our sect won't lose a single person."
