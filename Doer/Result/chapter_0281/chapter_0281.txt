Wang Shaojun rushed back to the royal estate.
As soon as he stepped off his carriage, he saw his father, Wang Yehe, coming out in a hurry, alongside his grandfather, Wang Zhihe.
They were escorting a middle-aged woman in official robes. Her face was sharp, her eyes grim, and she held a scroll made of silk in her hands.
"Leader Wang, the State Governor asks you to come to the official office at once!"
"What is so important that Lady Gong had to come herself?" Wang Shaojun felt his heart sink. She was one of the top fighters loyal to Governor Zhao. She was a woman, but her strength was dangerous.
"At four this morning, Wei He, the master of the Myriad Poison Sect, killed two inner院 masters of the Wushi Sect at Black House Mountain!"
She spoke each word with heavy force.
"His strength seems to have passed the Visceral Strike stage and reached Mind Sense!"
"What?!" Wang Shaojun stopped dead in his tracks. His eyes went wide.
"Passed to Mind Sense?! Impossible!!"
He knew Wei He well. Wei He had never gone through the usual steps to reach that level, let alone kill two inner院 masters at once. There had to be a mistake.
"Impossible? Hundreds of people saw it with their own eyes. In front of everyone, he killed two masters in just a few moves. That kind of power is far beyond the organ-refining level.
The Wushi Sect will be furious. How they will react, and how many people will come down from the Three Main Peaks—these are things the two leaders must care about," the woman said quickly.
"I understand... but Wei He cannot be at Mind Sense. I am sure of it..." Wang Shaojun felt his mind spin.
He could not get it. Just a short while ago, Wei He was only a little stronger than him. How could he suddenly become this...
"If he is not at Mind Sense, there is only one reason left," the woman said with a dark voice.
"A Blood Disrupter..." Wang Zhihe added from the side.
"Blood Disrupter..." This was the first time Wang Shaojun had ever heard that name.

On the White River, You Fu stood alone on a small wooden boat, drifting slowly down the stream.
On the right bank ahead, a man in a gray robe and a bamboo hat sat cross-legged with a sword in his arms, waiting for him.
"Brother, it has been years. Your aura has changed again," You Fu said with a smile, touching his eyepatch.
"When you get close to it, you cannot control it. When you cannot control it, you change. Are you not the same?" the man in the hat replied calmly.
They were tens of meters apart across the river, but their voices carried clearly over the water like waves.
"Tell me, why did you ask to meet me this time?" the man asked.
You Fu leaped into the air. His toes tapped the water surface, and he glided across the river to land right beside the man.
"Right now, the Wushi Sect is pinned down by the Dragon Blood issue. Over half the force of the Three Main Peaks is gone. If a few more brothers help out, we might actually pull off something big."
"Just us is not enough. The Three Main Peaks have many strong fighters. They are not easy to deal with," the man shook his head.
"Those cowards who only dare to hide on the mountains are not worth comparing to us," You Fu laughed. "Why else would I contact you from so far away through our private channels?" With the governor's army, if they could convince the other branches, they could take over the whole Tai State right now.
"That is true. The states are standing on their own now. It is the best time for us to act."
Before the man could finish, a masked person suddenly appeared on the riverbank behind him. Kneeling on one knee, the person threw a black tube toward them.
You Fu caught it.
"Don't worry, it is my person." He crushed the tube and took out the letter inside.
The letter clearly recorded the fight involving Wei He last night.
"Just as I expected," You Fu said quickly after reading it. He rubbed the paper into dust and let it float away in the wind.
"It seems we will have a new little friend very soon." He was very pleased with Wei He's action.
Killing two inner院 masters in one go meant that no matter what state the Wushi Sect was in, they had to react. Otherwise, they would lose all respect and suffer even greater losses. And to take down the current Wei He, no ordinary person would do.
Only the people from the Three Main Peaks could do it. But those experts could not come down in large numbers. That gave them their chance.
"It seems you are scheming against someone again," the man in the hat said softly.
"I am just making him choose a side sooner. It is not really a scheme. That young man is very strong and has great potential. I think highly of him," You Fu smiled.
"Is he a Blood Disrupter?"
"Probably not. But do you know what a Blood Disrupter really is?" You Fu asked.
"I have never listened closely to the details. Can you explain it directly, Brother You?" the man asked in doubt.
"Blood Disrupters have insane raw talent. The martial art energy they train in is just extra to them, not essential. Most importantly, they have always been the main weapon used by the court to crush martial artists.
They appear with no pattern. They start quiet, training in basic fighting skills, but suddenly become famous all over the world in a single day.
The word 'Disrupter' means they have no set pattern or tradition. Yet they are the strongest tool the government has against the martial arts sects," You Fu sighed.
"Then why don't we act first and take them in as disciples?" the man asked back.
"Some do, of course. But Blood Disrupters care more about practical combat moves, not energy cultivation. Their strength grows peak high as they get older. They are too hidden and hard to find.
Most of the so-called strange legends among the common people come from these Blood Disrupters."
You Fu shook his head. "At first, I thought the kid I picked was also one of them, but looking at it now, he seems to be something else."
"What do you plan to do?" The man thought for a moment and guessed who You Fu was talking about.
There were only a few young experts You Fu had been close to recently. It was easy to guess.
"The experts from the Three Main Peaks will surely come down the mountain now. When he can no longer hold on, I will step in to save him. That is how you win someone over," You Fu laughed.
"Aren't you afraid your plan might fail?"
"It won't. Everything is under control. Even if his strength went a bit beyond my math, this is an open trap he cannot avoid."

At Mount Yueqing, in the Wushi Sect.
Dong... Dong... Dong...
The heavy old bell was rung slowly.
The Wushi Sect had not rung the main mountain bell for a long time.
But this time, the bells for both Jiuzhen Court and Qinghu Court rang at the same time.
The sound echoed through the mountains, wave after wave.
Disciples who were working or sparring all stopped what they were doing and looked toward the inner院.
Crowds of disciples rushed toward the main martial square.
Groups of people wearing gray and white robes with hair tied in knots moved like streams of water, pouring into the massive center square.
Of the seven inner院 masters, only five showed up today.
Leading them was the head master, Liu Zhenyuan.
His face was cold, his eyes sharp, holding a report letter that had just been brought up the mountain.
The other four masters, including Master Qingye, stood quietly behind him.
In front of all the masters, on a high platform in the square, lay two bodies covered in white cloth on wooden frames.
Faint blood soaked through the white cloth, leaving a light smell of blood in the air.
"Yesterday, the masters of Jiuzhen Court and Qinghu Court went down the mountain together to track down Su Zhaoyi. Sadly, they were killed!
Inside the Myriad Poison Sect, they were poisoned and killed by the sect master, Wei He!" Liu Zhenyuan shouted with a cold voice.
"I have reported this to the Three Main Peaks. In a few days, experts from above will come down to capture Wei He!
Now, whoever is willing to go down the mountain and hunt down the rest of the Myriad Poison Sect, step forward!" Liu Zhenyuan yelled.
Hundreds of inner院 disciples in the square looked at each other.
Most of them could not believe it. They had not even processed what happened.
The rest quickly weighed the risks and stayed quiet.
The Myriad Poison Sect could kill two court masters on the spot. They were not weak. As for the experts from the Three Main Peaks, no one knew how strong they would be or how many would come.
Before things were clear, no one wanted to pick a side.
For a moment, only the personal disciples of Yan Jiuzhen and Song Qinghu stood out, shouting that they wanted revenge.
But altogether, they were only about ten people. Their voice was weak.
The flaw in how the Wushi Sect ran things was showing up clearly.
Everyone only cared about themselves. They were cold to each other and only wanted to reach the Three Main Peaks.
A flash of disappointment showed in Liu Zhenyuan's eyes, but he was used to it.
"Very well. Tomorrow, you will follow the people from the Three Main Peaks down the mountain. Someone will inform you when it is time."
After saying that, he turned his sleeves and walked away, ignoring the rest.
The bodies were surrounded by people from Jiuzhen Court and Qinghu Court, and carried away by members of the Yan and Song families.
Unlike the cold reactions of the other monks, these two families cried out loud with deep sorrow, forming a sharp contrast.
Watching the two groups slowly leave, the remaining court masters stayed silent.
Master Qingye stepped forward slowly, standing where Yan Jiuzhen's body had just lay.
She still found it hard to believe that two court masters had died at once.
It was not that people never died in the Wushi Sect, but that usually happened in the outer院 or on the Three Main Peaks.
Outer院 disciples died because they often fought with people down the mountain.
Peak experts died because they were at the core of the power game across the Great Yuan empire, fighting high-level wars.
But dying in the inner院, and losing two masters at the same time, was a whole different story.
The inner院 ruled over the local affairs of Tai State.
Two masters dying meant that the Wushi Sect's control over Tai State was in deep trouble.
It meant their foundation of power here was shaking.
And even more...
Qingye never thought that the person who killed Yan Jiuzhen and Song Qinghu would be Wei He!
"Wei He of the Myriad Poison Sect... I remember he was once one of Senior Brother Zheng's disciples, right?" Master Konghe asked beside her in a low voice.
As fellow masters, seeing two of their own killed so suddenly felt terrifying. They were working together just a few days ago, and now they were dead. A mixture of anger and fear filled every master's heart.
Because it meant that since Wei He could kill Yan Jiuzhen and Song Qinghu, he could kill them just as easily.
"Yes..." Master Qingye replied softly.
"Back then..." She stopped midway. In her mind, she remembered the day when she and Yan Jiuzhen were choosing new disciples. Because of his poor talent, they had rejected Wei He's name tag.
Who could have guessed that things would change so much? The tiny disciple whose tag they threw away back then...
Now had become this...
"Another exception, it seems..." another master said in a low voice, sighed, turned around, and walked away.
