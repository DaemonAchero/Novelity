“No matter what, I was wrong to accuse you this time.” Zhou Shun nodded. “However, if you get any news about Yugui, inform me immediately. You will be rewarded heavily,” he added in a deep voice.
“Alright,” Wei He nodded.
After saying that, Zhou Shun turned around, jumped lightly onto the courtyard wall, and disappeared into the distance within the blink of an eye.
Wei He let out a long breath. He only relaxed once Zhou Shun had fully left.
“Senior Sister Lin…” He looked over at Lin Shiling.
To his surprise, Lin Shiling had already left as well. He had been so tense just now that he did not even notice her leave.
Only the people from the Tianyin Hall were left in the backyard.
Wei He wiped away the blood from his chest and mouth. The feeling of his protective energy being smashed apart was terrible.
He had suffered serious internal injuries. It would take him at least a month or two to recover.
He had really suffered an unfair misfortune this time.
When he killed the target to silence him, he never expected Zhou Shun to arrive so quickly. He did not even have time to carry out his plan to move the crime scene.
Wei He did not linger. He quickly returned to his room to pack his things.
Yao Hansheng was currently guarding the two meat fields that belonged to the Wanqing Yard. The Yard Master wanted him to head over there immediately. It was very likely that the meat fields were in trouble and needed extra hands.
Wei He packed his things for a while: preserved meat, money notes, clothes, and various medicine pouches.
Suddenly, he jerked his head up.
A figure in green was standing right outside his window.
It was Zhou Shun.
No one knew how long he had been standing there, watching Wei He.
“Deputy Master Zhou?! You haven’t left?!” Wei He was startled, almost dropping the item in his hand.
“No. I remembered I had some business left, so I came back to check,” Zhou Shun said calmly, sweeping his eyes across Wei He’s room.
He raised his hand and tossed a pill over.
“This is my special Blood Stabilizing Pill. It can stabilize your internal injuries. Take it quickly.”
Wei He caught the pill and looked at it.
It was a pale green, crystal-clear bead that looked like glass. It gave off a faint musky scent.
“Thank you, Deputy Master Zhou,” Wei He thanked him sincerely.
“Do you hate me in your heart?” Zhou Shun asked.
“I wouldn’t call it hate, just a bit of resentment,” Wei He said candidly. “But if I put myself in your shoes, I would probably be even more anxious if my son went missing. With your strength, your actions were already gentle. So I have nothing to hate.”
Zhou Shun looked at Wei He closely for a moment. He saw an honest face and clear eyes. The young man really did not have much hate in him, only a quiet sense of understanding.
Zhou Shun fell silent. Suddenly, he felt that his suspicion just now had been a bit too much.
He reached out his hand and pulled the pill back from Wei He’s palm. Then, he flicked a different pill to him.
“I grabbed the wrong one just now. Take this one instead.” He gave no further explanation, turned, and left.
The first pill was actually not a healing medicine at all, but his signature slow-acting poison, the Yinhong Pill.
After Zhou Shun left, the wooden window frame creaked and swung back and forth for a long time before stopping.
Wei He sighed. Luckily, he liked to use sudden attacks himself, so he had not shown any flaw just now.
He then packed his things quickly. Fortunately, he had not brought back stolen goods like the folding fan. Most of the items in this room had already been moved to his hidden place with Jiuying.
Otherwise, if Zhou Shun had found anything just now, Wei He would have been dead.
Zhou Shun really lived up to his reputation as a former scholar: he was naturally paranoid. Rumors said he was once a successful imperial scholar who turned to martial sects after failing in politics.
Wei He used to doubt it. Why would a top scholar run off to be a deputy master of a sect? Even though it held high status, it was far more dangerous and painful than being a government official.
Now, it made sense. This man acted without restraint. Being an official came with too many rules, so being a deputy master was much easier for him.
After packing his things, Wei He quickly left the Tianyin Hall and rushed toward the Wanqing Yard’s meat fields.
The meat fields of the Wanqing Yard were built deep in the Western Mountains, not far from Tianyin Lake. Normally, inner disciples of the Wanqing Yard took turns guarding them.
Each yard was not made up of only a few inner disciples. Every year, the inner disciples rotated. They could only stay for nine years, after which they had to choose to leave or stay.
A good number of disciples chose to stay.
Many of those who stayed became sect managers, overseeing different departments or holding various roles.
Now that they gathered deep in the Western Mountains, there were actually over thirty people.
Light rain began to fall through the lush forest.
Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing looked at the gathered group from the Wanqing Yard. Their faces looked dark.
There were actually over forty disciples left in the Wanqing Yard, ranging from old to young. Most were at the Third Blood stage, as that was the basic requirement to enter the inner yard.
This was the network and foundation the Wanqing Yard had built over many years.
Yet, only thirty-something people had shown up. This meant the rest had chosen to abandon the Wanqing Yard.
“Two days ago, during the internal meeting of the Nine Yards, the Huguang and Huaqi Yards fought bitterly over the mining area. The other yards all took sides to help. Sect Master Shangguan did nothing, which caused the conflict to escalate. Now, every yard is growing apart.”
Wan Ling simply mentioned the main reason why the yards had reached this state.
She looked at the people spread out before her.
Yao Hansheng and Wei He were present, but the other two martial masters, Ding Xiaohe and Bai Rui, were nowhere to be seen.
Needless to say, those two must have joined other yards and branches.
Ding Xiaohe and Bai Rui had been stuck at the third layer of the Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Art for a long time. Their progress was slow, not because their talent was bad, but because the beast meat they earned was poor in quality.
Simply put, they lacked nutrients.
Even someone as strong as Wei He needed financial aid from Lin Xiaoxiao, along with his own side jobs and one-third of the medium meat field at Jiuying's hidden place.
Only with so much support could he barely maintain his current cultivation speed.
Under normal conditions, people like Ding Xiaohe and Bai Rui were the common norm.
In reality, if you did not fight and steal, where would you get so much beast meat to raise your blood energy?
Relying solely on the small monthly beast meat ration from the sect was far from enough.
At this point, the thing holding them back was just one word: money.
More importantly, they could not eat any random beast meat like Wei He to raise their blood energy.
Wei He did not have to worry about bottleneck barriers breaking or not.
Normal martial masters who trained in the Rain-Covering Cloud-Gathering Art had to rely on beast meat of the same attribute to cultivate.
Otherwise, when they reached a bottleneck, a tiny bit of impurity could make their life a living hell.
Wan Qingqing went silent for a moment, then said with understanding, “Ding Xiaohe and the other girl were approached by the You family long ago. Their First-Line Green meat fields produce far more than ours. The fact that they stayed this long shows they were already being loyal.”
Wan Ling remained silent.
Since Ding Xiaohe and Bai Rui made that decision, they had already given up on reaching the fourth layer of the art.
Clearly, they did not even know if they could ever reach the fourth layer, so making this choice was normal.
She calmed her breathing and looked at the crowd.
Wan Qingqing, Yao Hansheng, and Wei He—these three martial masters were the final foundation of the Wanqing Yard. The rest were all at the Third Blood stage.
Usually, once someone became a martial master, few stayed behind. Most were recruited by outside forces.
Sect disciples were different from gang members.
Most came from rich backgrounds with solid family networks. They had decent family businesses to support them.
They did not put the sect yard first; instead, they cared more about their own families.
So, being able to keep this many people was already good. After all, the Wanqing Yard was not strong.
“You all did very well.” Wan Ling looked at everyone, feeling a sudden wave of bitterness in her heart.
“Yard Master, those who stayed are either people with no attachments or people who have made up their minds. Stop wasting words and give us our orders. What should we do?” Yao Hansheng said with a broad grin.
The reason he stayed was simply that he wanted to. He was unwilling to run away alone during a crisis.
Although he had the Yao family to look after, he had two older brothers taking care of things at home, so he had no worries.
“That’s right, Yard Master. Everyone who stayed knows what they are doing. Say no more and make the arrangements now.”
“We are all here, so give us your orders. On my way here, I saw people from other yards already fighting at the mining area.”
“Yes, stop being sentimental and tell us what to do!”
Disciples from the Wanqing Yard spoke up one after another.
Those who stayed at a time like this were truly loyal. The oldest among them was in his nineties, while the youngest was in his early twenties.
Not far away, many family members who had been brought along felt deep emotions watching this scene.
Although the Wanqing Yard had few people, its current unity was something the other eight yards could never match.
Wan Ling showed a warm smile. Though most people in front of her were women and only a few were men, their courage was no less than any man's!
“Alright then. We currently have three martial masters with force energy: Qingqing, Hansheng, and Wei He. You three…” She suddenly thought of something and turned her beautiful eyes onto Wei He.
“Wei He, you took a palm strike from Zhou Shun earlier. How is your injury?”
She had only glanced at him briefly before and saw no major issue, so she had not paid much attention.
Now that she recalled it, she asked.
“I suffered some internal injuries, but it’s no big deal. Just give your orders, Yard Master,” Wei He said in a deep voice.
“Luckily, I managed to break through recently and entered the fourth layer of the Rain-Covering Force. My energy built up a vibrating resonance, giving me rapid movement speed. So my previous injury is not that heavy,” he stated honestly.
“What? You broke through?” Wan Ling was stunned.
She knew very well how long it usually took to go from the third layer to the fourth layer of the Rain-Covering Force.
Yet Wei He did it so fast…
Qingqing had mentioned before that Wei He seemed highly compatible with the Rain-Covering Force, so his progress was very fast.
She had not taken it seriously at the time. She had seen many geniuses, but how many could stay a genius forever?
Take Zhou Xingsu, Chen Lin, and Sun Qi, who were not here today. They were all geniuses too, yet they were stuck at the bottlenecks right now, unable to move forward or break through quickly.
“Did you… really break through?!” Wan Ling asked again, disbelief clear on her face.
Wan Qingqing was equally shocked. She still remembered clearly how long it took her to get from the third layer to the fourth.
And how long had Wei He taken? He had entered the sect less than four years ago! He was only twenty-two years old now! Yet he had rushed all the way from the first layer to the fourth layer of the Rain-Covering Force!
This speed proved he was truly compatible with the Rain-Covering Force!
“It's true.” Wei He nodded seriously. “If the Yard Master does not believe me, you can test it yourself.”
