Far off, Yao Wan and Wei He walked by. Xuan Zhen nodded to them from afar, a quick greeting, and kept talking.
"He found a big pack of one-eyed three-clawed squid on the way," Yao Wan said casually. "Nasty stuff. Xuan Zhen probably took a sect task to clear them out."
Wei He nodded, looking at the crowd. Half were True Men, half were Strength Warriors. Most had good gear standard for True Men.
Yet he did not know a single face there.
They walked through the jade wall, entering the strange temple inside the dark realm.
Near Temple Number One, on the dark open ground, another crowd of True Men gathered.
They held large bags, packing up their goods stored in this realm, ready to leave.
Yao Wan walked over, greeting a few people at the front.
"So? Want to come with us?" She turned to Wei He. "This is my second-to-last group. If you don't go now, you'll have to take the very last one."
Wei He looked around.
The inner realm was always dark and cold.
Light shone only near the temple, showing small spots of empty ground.
Outside, grey-black mist floated everywhere. Strange, ugly plants grew from the ground.
This inner world was much cleaner than the outside.
Aside from a few raised beasts, no weird monsters roamed here, making it feel vast and empty.
"Made up your mind?" Yao Wan asked again.
"It will take my family at least two days to pack," Wei He said.
"No problem," Yao Wan replied. "With your status now, why worry about hands? Just get the Lou family to send help."
"Alright then," Wei He thought for a second and agreed.
They were moving to Juju, a large island in the deep sea, thousands of miles from the Great Yuan coast.
It was not alone. A cluster of islands surrounded it, all inhabited. Most were descendants of fishermen who fled from various lands over time.
Juju was no random choice. The Xuanmiao Sect had set up a base there long ago.
"Once it's set, go back and finish your affairs," Yao Wan sighed. "Leaving Great Yuan this time... who knows when we can ever return."
"Is Sister Yao sad to leave a young lover behind?" a female True Man joked.
"Get lost! You dare tease me now? Want to die?" Yao Wan made a move to draw her sword, making the woman run off with a laugh.
Two other acquaintances came over, pulling Yao Wan aside to debate what to pack and what to throw away. Space was limited, so choices had to be made.
Wei He watched her deal with the others. He said a quick goodbye and turned to leave.
He spent all his time training hard, never taking part in sect events. He barely knew anyone and could not fit in.
Leaving the inner realm, Wei He went down the mountain, crossed the Virtue Palace, and returned to his manor on Tianhai Island.
People moved in and out of the manor. Many things were already packed away.
His son, Wei An, was nine now, five feet tall, and built strong like a bull.
Right now, he circled Cheng He, a girl brought back from Yun Prefecture, trying hard to please her.
Cheng He was pretty. Though a few years older than Wei An, their natural builds made them look the same age.
Seeing Wei He enter, the two kids quickly stepped up and bowed.
"Greetings, Father." "Greetings, Master."
"Alright. How is the packing?" Wei He asked, sitting in the main chair as a servant brought tea.
"All packed," Wei An spoke clearly, sounding like a grown man. "The Lou family sent two big ships yesterday. Mother, Grandmother, and Grandfather are all trying to stuff things inside."
"How is your study?" Wei He asked.
"The Sea of Clouds skill you taught... it's too deep. I've barely entered the door..." Cheng He answered softly, looking nervous.
Wei An stood beside her, eyes wandering. Clearly, he had been playing around and done nothing.
Wei He shook his head inside.
The boy was good when small, but grew harder to control as he aged. Wei He could not watch him every second.
The Sea of Clouds skill was the core base he built from his own combined codex.
With his current height in martial arts, looking back at basic blood-force skills was simple. He took the best parts of his life's work, mixing the Nine Strikes with other secret books to create this new art.
He turned the codex from a hard path—once relying only on the Break-Through Bead—into a path anyone could learn.
Of course, this change had a cost. Many rare herbs were gone, so its power fell far short of what Wei He used himself.
In real fights, its force matched the full Nine Strikes, nothing more. No extra boosts, but one could practice it all the way to the Mind Impression stage.
He sat in the hall, testing the two kids for a while. Soon, Wan Qingqing came back after hearing the news, along with Wei Ying and Wei Chun.
"Husband, everything is packed. Is the date set?"
Wan Qingqing looked even more full and striking now. She held Wei He's hand and sat beside him. Only a trace of her old, sharp spirit from the Tianyin Sect remained in her eyes.
"Yes. Get ready. We leave the day after tomorrow." Wei He watched the hall fill with people.
His close family, their relatives, the Wan family, their relatives, his disciples, their families, and the Lou family of Strength Warriors.
Outside stood the core experts of the Thousand Poison Sect and the old Wanqing Sect, all with families of their own.
Without noticing, the manor had grown full of voices and noise.
Soon, Wan Ling came back with Wei He's parents, chatting and laughing in peace.
Wei He took a glance. In a flash, dozens of people stood before him.
All these people relied on his strength. If he rose, they lived well. If he fell, they died.
He used to feel he was walking alone, cutting through thorns with nothing to tie him down.
Looking back now, he realized he had gathered so many ties without knowing it.
"Husband? Husband?"
A soft voice pulled Wei He out of his thoughts.
"I'm fine. Just lost in thought," Wei He smiled, clearing his mind. "What is it?"
He looked at the two women standing before him with heads bowed. They looked plain, carried a bit of martial skill, and had the air of shopkeepers.
Wei Chun stood to the side with a serious face. She was no longer as close as before, holding a touch of plea and fear as she waited for his call.
As his power grew and his status in the sect rose, Wei He's word at home carried total weight. Add in his long training in isolation, and he rarely saw them.
Slowly, the old family members stopped acting bold around him.
Even his big sister, once the most loud and wild, had grown careful.
Wei He sighed inside, though he knew this was bound to happen.
Losing interest, he looked at the two women and nodded.
"We have room. Bring them along."
That opened the gates.
Next, his father, Wei Tang, came over, asking to bring a rich merchant friend named Tang.
His mother, Li Cui, wanted to bring her close friends too.
Wei He did not mind. In the end, he gave everyone two spots to bring whoever mattered to them.
He did not expect leaving Great Yuan to make them so happy.
There was no sadness about leaving their home behind. Instead, it felt like a holiday, everyone full of joy.
He left the manor.
Walking alone to the shore of Dragon Bay, he looked into the distance.
The setting sun dyed the sea in gold and red.
Standing there, Wei He could not calm his heart. Memories of Yun Prefecture, Taizhou, and the quiet days in Jin Prefecture flooded his mind.
Now that he was truly leaving this land, old pictures kept rising in his head.
Far off, a large ship sailed from the direction of Zhentao City. On the deck stood his senior brother, Chang Xuezhong.
Chang was laughing with two others, broad and warm, slapping each other's shoulders.
Further away, more ships sailed toward the island. Every ship carried the family and friends of a True Man disciple.
"Wei He? Junior Brother Wei?"
A voice came from behind.
It was a warm, gentle voice of a man.
Wei He turned around.
The speaker was a handsome scholar, looking much like Wang Shaojun in his youth.
"It really is you." The scholar smiled and walked closer, stopping on the sand to his right, looking out at the ships.
"Senior Brother Huan Songzi spoke of you before, calling you a real combat genius among our young generation. I wanted to visit you, but kept delaying until today."
He looked back at Wei He.
"I am Duan Yanhui, a True Man of the Qianliu branch. I heard you slapped Shi Heng of the Wushi Sect hard in Yun Prefecture. That was truly great to hear." His tone carried clear warmth.
"That fool Shi Heng met me years ago at Qingtang Mountain. We had a small fight, and he hurt me badly. My junior sister and I had to heal in the sect for three years before getting better.
I thought only our three Dao Seed senior brothers could match him. I didn't expect you to step up and give him a hard slap."
Duan Yanhui laughed out loud, clearly thrilled by Shi Heng's loss.
"Is Shi Heng that strong?" Wei He asked softly. "I didn't fight him directly. We missed each other."
"Beside the three Peak Masters of Wushi Sect, he ranks in the top five," Duan Yanhui nodded. "He rarely drops out of the top five. He is a top Dao Seed who might become a Peak Master one day."
"It's good you didn't fight him. Otherwise..." Duan Yanhui did not think Wei He could win either.
He had seen Shi Heng's power. The man was a monster, killing stronger enemies as easily as eating.
Wei He said nothing.
He did not think he was weaker than Shi Heng.
In the current Xuanmiao Sect, aside from Huan Songzi, the three Chief Disciples, and the three Ancestors, he would have to fight the rest to know who was stronger.
Experts above the Fifth Step of Full Truth were rare in the sect outside the Ancestors and Chiefs.
Once his secret technique was complete, his power would explode again.
Of course, the Five-Turn Dragon Breath could not be used like a normal move; it was a final card.
The total weakness that followed its use was far too deadly.
