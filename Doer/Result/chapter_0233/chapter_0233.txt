Torches crackled in the dark, casting a bloody red glow over the estate.
Wan Qingqing squeezed her fists tight. She stared up at Wei He standing on the roof, her chest tight with mixed emotions. Joy, worry, fear, and a desperate wish for him to run.
Yet, the moment he stepped into view, the crushing fear of facing Zhu Chen vanished. No one wanted to die, and Wan Qingqing was no different. She had simply buried her terror deep inside. But now, looking at Wei He, that fear was completely gone.
"Wei He!" she screamed, stepping forward. "You shouldn't have come back!"
On the roof, the dark figure turned. Wei He looked down at her with calm eyes and offered a subtle smile.
Then he moved.
He shot into the night like a shadow, leaping toward the tree line.
Zhu Chen’s eyes narrowed. He didn't chase Wei He. Instead, he lunged straight toward Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing. To a master like him, a distance of tens of meters was just a second away.
Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom!
Before Zhu Chen could hit the ground, the high tree branches exploded. Clouds of greyish-white dust burst into the air, raining down until a dense fog of poison covered the entire Wanqing Sect.
Seeing the deadly rain descend, three Viscera Refinement masters from the Sun-Seeking Willow Night squad leapt into the sky.
"Blast it away!" Xu Fei shouted, swinging his heavy fists.
The three masters struck at once, creating a gust of wind to blow the poison away.
Suddenly, a dark silhouette flashed past them.
Wei He was like a giant bat, his speed terrifyingly fast. He flashed three times in mid-air, slamming his palms into the three masters.
Bang! Bang! Bang!
Shockwaves rippled through the air. Like birds with broken wings, the three grandmasters were smashed straight into the dirt.
Wei He flipped through the air and landed lightly on a thick branch. He looked down at Zhu Chen, his face completely emotionless.
"Lord Zhu," Wei He said softly. "Guess how many of your people just got poisoned?"
Zhu Chen looked up from the ground. "You really think we can't cure your poison?"
"It's not about confidence. It's about time," Wei He replied. "Before I came, I thought about threatening you with the lives of these Sun-Seeking Willow Night experts. But I know what kind of man you are. Then I thought about using your closest henchmen. But it seems you don't care about them either. If one dies, you just get another."
The white poison dust drifted down around Wei He like snow.
Even though the imperial troops wore anti-poison gear, they shrank back in terror. The Sect Master of the Ten Thousand Poison Sect had murdered thousands of elite soldiers with his toxins. His name alone made their blood run cold.
"So, I kept thinking... what do you actually care about, Lord Zhu?" Wei He said, his voice smooth. "And now, I think I found it."
"And what is that?" Zhu Chen asked coldly.
"People think you're afraid of the rebel army taking the city," Wei He smiled softly. "But I think you're scared of ruining the big plan back at the provincial capital. They need martial artists, and they need time. You stayed here to clear out threats just to buy them time."
Zhu Chen clapped his hands slowly. "You are correct. It seems killing you first really is the right choice. Given time, you will become a monster."
"Thank you for the compliment," Wei He chuckled. "Oh, I forgot to mention. Before I came here, I sent a letter to the rebel army. I told them Xuanjing is empty, and you are not in the city."
"You dare?!"
Zhu Chen's pupils shrank. With a roar, he burst into the air, shooting straight at Wei He like an arrow. Invisible force surged around his body like giant snakes. His right hand gripped the hilt of his saber, pouring massive energy into the blade.
"I just wanted to see if you really don't care," Wei He said calmly. He didn't fight back. Instead, he glided backward, effortlessly keeping his distance.
The two blurred through the dense forest, one chasing, one retreating.
"Those three experts who just traded blows with me... they were Viscera Refinement masters," Wei He said as he circled a massive ancient tree. "Guess if they're poisoned or not?"
Boom!
They spiraled down the trunk of the massive tree. Zhu Chen’s palm slammed into the wood. His bare hand cut deep, clean gashes into the trunk like a sharp blade.
"Poisoned or not, you die today!" Zhu Chen screamed, throwing another palm strike.
A flash of white light shot from his hand. It grazed Wei He's back and sliced deep into the tree trunk.
Crack!
The giant tree, thick as two men, was severed completely and began to fall.
Yet Zhu Chen was still too slow. He spat out wave after wave of white blade-light, but he couldn't catch Wei He. The poison master's speed had increased again. Even with Zhu Chen's high-level footwork, he couldn't close the gap.
"Take this!"
Zhu Chen waved his arm, throwing a handful of black iron caltrops.
Packed with raw power, the iron spikes blasted into Wei He's back.
Puk! Puk! Puk!
Wei He’s defensive force shook wildly, almost breaking under the hit. A simple thrown weapon carried enough force to shatter defenses beyond the Viscera Refinement level. If he fought Zhu Chen head-on, he would die in seconds.
The two sprinted through the dark woods. Zhu Chen attacked relentlessly, coming dangerously close each time, but Wei He slipped away every single time. He gave Zhu Chen hope, only to snatch it away.
In seconds, the two vanished into the deep forest.
Back at the estate, the Wanqing Sect disciples were left facing the imperial forces.
The three grandmasters who had blocked Wei He earlier felt a sharp itch in their throats. Something was wrong.
They looked over at Ouyang Zhao, their poison expert.
"I don't know yet! Grab the targets first!" Ouyang Zhao shouted, her face pale behind her veil.
If she couldn't identify the poison, they needed hostages. Catching Wei He's family would force him to stop. Lord Zhu's main goal was to kill Wei He anyway. Without him, the rest of the sect was useless.
The three grandmasters gritted their teeth and charged toward Wan Ling and Wan Qingqing. The remaining six Bone Forging experts charged the other disciples like wolves entering a fold.
Ouyang Zhao took a few steps, then retreated. Her hands trembled as she dug through her pouches, desperately mixing liquids and powders. As a poison master, creating remedies on the fly was a basic skill.
Yet none of her anti-toxins worked. Sweat broke out on her forehead.
Wan Ling stepped forward to meet the incoming masters, followed closely by Xiao Qingyu. Though facing three Viscera Refinement experts, Wan Ling used a secret technique to boost her power, clashing head-on without falling back. Xiao Qingyu and Xie Yan held off the other two, barely holding the line.
Meanwhile, the six enemy Bone Forging experts swept through the weaker disciples. One strike, one fallen disciple.
Then, they froze.
The energy in their bodies was leaking out fast. A sharp, needle-like pain began to spread across their skin.
The poison worked on contact. The pills they ate before the fight were useless.
Terrified, the six men pulled out emergency anti-toxin pills, ripping off their face masks to swallow them. But the moment their masks came off, their vision went blurry.
Panic erupted.
The battlefield devolved into chaos. Soldiers and spies quickly realized the airborne dust was deadly. The three main grandmasters had to use their inner energy to suppress the poison in their organs, keeping their breathing shallow.
They couldn't fight at full power. If they pumped their blood too fast, the poison would kill them faster.
The six Bone Forging experts also held back, terrified of pushing their bodies too hard. They didn't need to kill everyone anyway. They just needed to capture the main figures and wait for Zhu Chen to return. If they accidentally killed the hostages, they might never get the cure.
Shouts, clashing steel, and painful screams filled the night.
Wan Qingqing fought like a madwoman. Dressed in white, her aura flared as she darted through the enemy rank like a streak of light.
The imperial experts tried to take her alive to use as leverage against Wei He. Combined with their fear of the poison, they pulled their strikes, resulting in a chaotic, messy stalemate.
A short distance away, Ouyang Zhao was finally figuring out the ingredients of Wei He's poison. Relief washed over her. She was close to mixing the cure.
Suddenly, a low rumble echoed from the deep woods. It grew louder and louder.
"What is that?!" She snapped her head toward the sound.
She sniffed the air, and her eyes widened in sheer horror.
"Damn it... the powder... it has beast bait in it!!"
