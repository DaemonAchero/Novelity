"Die!"

Clang!

Using only two fingers, the Bodhisattva pinched the blade with ease, locking it in place.
Streams of True-Yield Qi, moving like living things, poured endlessly from the blade into her.
She grinned, dark armor-like markings spreading across her entire body.
"Too weak."

Chirp! Chirp! Chirp!
The force blades spread outward from the sword like a peacock opening its tail.
Swish!
A splash of blood bloomed.
The Bodhisattva's face changed slightly. She stepped back and looked at the cut on her hand.
"Peacock Sword?" Since they came to Haizhou, they had naturally investigated all the top experts of the Xuanmiao Sect.
Peacock Sword Yao Wan was a listed Dao-rank expert, so she was already on their record.
"I didn't expect to meet you here. A Quanzhen expert... interesting." A smile touched the corner of the Bodhisattva's mouth.
"That works out fine. Saves me the trouble of hunting you down later. Killing you right here is for the best!"

"DIE!!"
Both her arms swelled up a notch. Her skin turned black like iron as she swung her arms forward in a wide hug.
Every motion of her arms dragged a wave of distortion along with it.
It was not from force distortion, but from the heat radiating off her body, twisting the air like a furnace.
Her body temperature spiked far beyond normal. Her blood boiled, and thick steam burst out from every pore.
Her embrace clashed head-on with the opening Peacock Sword.
Clang! Clang! Clang!
Countless sharp impact sounds rang out.
Pure physical power collided with inner force, and neither side gave ground.
Yao Wan's face changed sharply. She had not expected to meet another Quanzhen level expert here.
She had heard that the Wu military was strong, that the Guangci Sect alone could suppress both the Katori and Chaos Spirit sects while holding down the Great Yuan lords.
Hearing it was one thing. Facing a True Blood expert from Wu in real combat made her realize how absurd it was.
This was just a small scouting unit sent by Wu, yet it already had a Quanzhen level True Blood expert in its ranks.
She could only imagine how many Wu experts had invaded the other provinces, especially Taizhou, where the Wushi Sect was under the heaviest pressure.
Realizing things were bad, Yao Wan thought of Wei He, who was rushing over. She made a split-second decision to pull back.
She could not let this monster hurt Wei He.
No matter how strong Wei He was, the Quanzhen realm was beyond his reach. If he ran into her, he would die for sure.
She tapped her feet and darted away like a swallow.
Though she was not a leg-technique master, under the boost of True-Yield Qi, her speed was no slower than a regular leg-technique martial artist.
"Trying to run?!" The Bodhisattva raised her eyebrows and stomped her foot. Explosive force sent her leaping into the air, chasing right after Yao Wan.
She did not specialize in movement skills either, but as a True Blood cultivator with huge power, her straight-line pursuit speed was just as fast.
The two vanished from the courtyard in the blink of an eye.

These two in front of him did not look strong either. They would probably be handled in a single moment.
He thought for a second and answered, "It's probably just people sparring inside the post. Inspector, we are bored in these deep mountains, so we have to find ways to pass the time."
"Makes sense." Wei He nodded, his brow smoothing out a bit.
"This way, please." Zhang Runian kept walking.
Wei He and Zhou Muqing followed behind him.
After a few dozen meters, they turned a corner and saw a huge mansion with a wide back garden.
"We are here," Zhang Runian said. He walked up and knocked gently on the door ring.
Thump, thump, thump.
No answer came from inside.
He frowned, then knocked again.
Thump, thump, thump.
Still no response.
"Looks like no one is here right now. They probably went to the other spot," Zhang Runian turned around and said in a heavy voice. "How about I take you to the other place they usually go to?"
"Sure." Wei He nodded.
The three left the mansion and walked out of town.
They exited from the side and had not walked far when Zhang Runian suddenly stopped on a slope near a stream.
"We are here." He turned back to look at Wei He, his eyes flashing.
"Here?" Wei He looked around.
Sure enough, two men walked out from the tall reed grass nearby.
The man in front had long arms and long legs. He wore a humble smile, his eyes darting back and forth between Wei He and Zhou Muqing.
"I am Huang Yu. Greetings, Inspectors." He stepped forward with a warm smile, bowing with hands cupped.
"Mister Huang is too polite. We came on orders to inspect..." Before Wei He could finish his words, a sudden blast of wind blew into his face.
His vision blurred. The man who was just in front of him vanished and reappeared right at his side.
At the same time, a pair of iron-like hands struck hard against the center of his chest with terrifying force.
BANG!
No inner force, just pure speed and raw strength.
The high-speed Huang Yu was like a heavy truck, slamming full body into Wei He's chest.
A loud explosion rang out.
Both of them flew back like cannonballs, crashing into the dark pine forest behind them.
Countless branches and bushes snapped and tore apart.
Zhou Muqing stood frozen, unable to react before Zhang Runian vanished. Another man was rubbing his fists, closing in on her.
Her eyes went cold. Realizing it was a trap, she drew a knife and a sword in a flash. Her muscles tightened as her force surged.
On the other side, inside the pine forest.
Huang Yu pressed both fists against Wei He's chest, dragging them back through the trees at high speed.
During the rush, a cruel smile appeared on Huang Yu's face.
This full-power strike from his second-turn realm, delivered in close combat, was something a second-sense master could not take without gathering massive force to defend.
If even a second-sense master could not block it, then this guy in front of him...
Grab.
A hand suddenly clamped around his wrist.
Huang Yu's pupils shrank. He jerked his head up.
All he saw was a pair of purplish-red eyes covered in fine cracks.
"You..."
BOOM!

A massive palm strike packed with scary True-Yield Qi slammed into the side of his head.
Huang Yu's body flipped and flew out, hitting the trunk of a giant tree.
The trunk shook, wood chips and cracks exploding out from the middle.
Massive amounts of True-Yield Qi flooded his bones and flesh. Everything inside him went soft and dissolved under the corrosion of the force.
If it were a normal amount of True-Yield Qi, he might have survived, but the sheer volume in that palm strike was way too big.
In his daze, he felt as if he had been struck by a Quanzhen realm master.
That endless flood of force made it impossible for his trained body to hold up for even one second.
It was like a pile of grass trying to stop a flash flood.
No matter how well built the grass was, it was nothing more than thin paper against the flood.
Pfft!
Huang Yu rolled onto the ground, his flesh and bones collapsing fast under the massive force.

Draped in black mist, he looked like a ghost or a god.

Not at all!

A very, very light slap.
How strong could twenty percent force be?

Zhou Muqing was still out there. It would be bad if she got surrounded.
Out of the pine forest.
